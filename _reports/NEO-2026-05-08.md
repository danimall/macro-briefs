## Executive Summary

NeoGenomics is a specialized oncology diagnostics company generating $727M in annual revenue (2025), growing at 10-11% organically, with a portfolio of increasingly high-value tests (NGS, MRD, liquid biopsy). Q1 2026 revenue of $186.7M (+11.1% YoY) and raised full-year guidance to $797-803M demonstrate operational momentum. Adjusted EBITDA reached $43M in 2025 and is expanding. However, the company carries $342M in convertible notes maturing January 2028 with only $146M in cash and continues to run GAAP net losses of ~$70-100M annually. The equity will almost certainly face dilution or unfavorable refinancing terms within 18 months.

The growth story is genuine: secular tailwinds from precision oncology, MRD monitoring expansion, PanTracer MolDX approval, and community pathology channel penetration provide visible revenue catalysts. Analyst consensus targets of $11-25 (median $14) imply approximately 54% upside. But the capital structure creates a binary risk that dominates the equity's risk/reward profile until addressed. At current levels, the stock offers upside from earnings momentum and potential re-rating toward profitability milestones, but the 2028 maturity wall requires monitoring as a potential thesis-invalidating event.

## Company Overview

NeoGenomics operates cancer-focused testing laboratories across the United States and United Kingdom, employing approximately 2,500 people including 135+ MDs and PhDs. The company positions itself as a non-competitive partner to community pathologists — a differentiated model that avoids the channel conflict inherent to competitors like Guardant Health or Natera, who sell directly to oncologists.

Revenue sources include: clinical cancer testing (billed to clients, commercial insurance, and government payers), pharmaceutical development services (clinical trial support), and oncology data solutions. No single client exceeds 10% of revenue. The test menu spans cytogenetics, FISH, flow cytometry, IHC, NGS panels, and the RaDaR molecular residual disease platform acquired through Inivata in 2021.

The company operates in a single reportable segment and recently rationalized its portfolio by disposing of Trapelo Health and writing down InVisionFirst-Lung assets, concentrating resources on community oncology testing and MRD — where growth visibility is strongest.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** Revenue has compounded at approximately 13% annually from 2022 ($510M) through 2025 ($727M). Q1 2026 delivered $186.7M (+11.1% YoY), with full-year guidance of $797-803M implying ~10% growth. Growth is driven by volume increases and mix shift toward higher-value tests (NGS, MRD, PanTracer LBx).

**Profitability Trajectory:** Gross margins have expanded from 36.9% (2022) to 43.3% (Q1 2026), reflecting operating leverage and test mix improvement. Adjusted EBITDA improved from -$48M (2022) to +$43M (2025) to a $9.0M run-rate in Q1 2026 ($36M annualized). However, GAAP losses persist at -$17.1M in Q1 2026 (improved from -$25.9M year-ago). Stock-based compensation of $41M/year and $30M in annual intangible amortization represent the primary gap between adjusted and GAAP profitability.

**Balance Sheet:** Cash of $146M as of March 31, 2026, declined from $367M at end-2024 due to $201M repayment of the 2025 convertible notes. The remaining debt is $342M in 0.25% convertible notes maturing January 2028 (conversion price $85.75 — irrelevant at $9/share). Current ratio of 4.26x provides adequate near-term liquidity. Goodwill of $524M and intangibles of $279M dominate the asset base ($803M combined vs. ~$1.18B market cap).

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was $5.2M in 2025 and -$8.1M in Q1 2026 (seasonal working capital). Capex of $27M (2025) and guided $30-35M (2026) produces negative free cash flow. The company is likely burning $5-15M per quarter in cash (varying by seasonal working capital patterns), suggesting $100-120M cash remaining when the 2028 notes mature.

**Critical Capital Structure Math:** $342M notes due January 2028. ~$100-120M estimated cash at maturity. Gap of $220-240M must come from: (a) equity issuance (~25-27M shares at $9 = 20% dilution), (b) new debt at likely 6-8% given loss-making profile, (c) asset sales, or (d) a strategic transaction.

## Growth Analysis

NeoGenomics benefits from durable secular tailwinds:

1. **Rising cancer incidence** and proliferation of targeted therapies requiring companion diagnostics
2. **MRD monitoring growth** — RaDaR platform positioned in one of the fastest-growing segments of molecular oncology
3. **NGS adoption in community settings** — NEO's core channel where it has established relationships
4. **PanTracer LBx MolDX approval** (Q1 2026) — enables broader reimbursement for liquid biopsy comprehensive genomic profiling
5. **Epic integration** — expands test ordering access through the dominant EHR platform

The pharma services segment (clinical trial support) faces near-term headwinds from biotech funding constraints, but this is a smaller contributor to overall growth. Management's guidance raise following Q1 2026 provides confidence in near-term trajectory.

Analyst estimates show positive revision momentum: current-year EPS consensus rose 13.8% over the last 30 days and next-year by 14.2%. While absolute EPS remains small ($0.18 current year, $0.36 next year), the direction is unambiguous improvement.

Peer context: Profitable health care services peers trade at 11-18x P/E, but NEO is in a different category as a pre-profit growth company. NEO's discount reflects persistent losses, refinancing risk, and smaller scale.

Analyst consensus target of $14 (median) implies 54% upside from current levels. The target range of $11-25 reflects wide uncertainty around the capital structure resolution and profitability timeline.

The valuation is not expensive on revenue or forward EBITDA, but the 2028 refinancing creates a ceiling on re-rating until resolved. If management successfully refinances without excessive dilution and EBITDA continues expanding, 2-3x revenue ($12-18/share) is reasonable. If refinancing is dilutive (20%+ dilution), per-share upside compresses significantly.

## Competitive Landscape

NeoGenomics occupies a differentiated niche: the only scaled, cancer-focused reference lab that operates as a non-competitive partner to community pathologists. Community oncology groups (which control a growing share of cancer treatment) are reluctant to send specimens to labs that also compete for their patients' testing volumes.

Competitive positioning:
- **Versus Guardant/Natera:** NEO lacks the direct-to-oncologist liquid biopsy brand but benefits from not threatening pathology relationships. As MRD and CGP adoption grows in community settings, NEO's channel should capture share.
- **Versus Quest/Labcorp:** Large reference labs have scale advantages in logistics and payer contracting but lack oncology specialization depth and community pathology relationships.
- **Versus Tempus AI:** Emerging competitor with AI/data-driven approach but less established laboratory infrastructure.

Risks to competitive position include: (1) large labs developing specialized oncology offerings, (2) Guardant/Natera expanding channel partnerships with pathology groups, and (3) CMS reimbursement changes that reduce testing volumes industry-wide.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **2028 Convertible Note Maturity (18 months):** The most material risk for equity holders. Refinancing at current loss levels likely requires significant equity dilution or high-cost debt. A failed refinancing could force a distressed transaction.

2. **OIG Investigation (ongoing since 2021):** $11.2M reserved as "minimum probable loss" with no estimable range of additional liability. Healthcare fraud cases have produced outcomes well above initial reserves. Exclusion from Medicare/Medicaid (13% of revenue) would be catastrophic.

3. **Persistent GAAP Losses:** $451M accumulated deficit. Even with improving adjusted EBITDA, SBC of $41M/year and amortization of $30M/year mean GAAP profitability remains 2-3 years away.

4. **Goodwill Impairment Risk:** $524M goodwill at a ~$1.18B market cap. Management already performed quantitative impairment testing in Q3 2025 — the margin above carrying value is thin.

5. **Reimbursement Headwinds:** PAMA rate cuts (15% cap for 2026-2028) create direct revenue per-test pressure that must be overcome by volume and mix improvements.

**Mitigating Factors:**
- Revenue growth trajectory provides confidence in demand trends
- Adjusted EBITDA improving ($43M 2025, likely $55-60M in 2026)
- No near-term liquidity crisis (4.26x current ratio)
- Patent litigation resolved (RaDaR ST)
- Goldenberg securities class action dismissed with prejudice (though plaintiff has appealed)

## Investment Thesis

- Revenue grows 10-12% through 2027, reaching $880-900M
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion to 8-10% by 2027 ($70-90M)
- PanTracer LBx and RaDaR gain traction as reimbursement expands
- Successful refinancing of 2028 notes with manageable dilution (5-10% new equity + smaller debt facility)

**Bear Case ($4-6, -34-56% downside):**

- 2028 note refinancing requires 25-30% equity dilution or forces strategic sale at depressed valuation
- OIG investigation produces settlement well above reserves ($50M+) or operational restrictions
- Goodwill impairment charge wipes out book equity
- PAMA reimbursement cuts compress gross margins below 40%

## Conclusion

NeoGenomics presents a genuine revenue growth story with improving profitability in a structurally attractive end market. The 11% organic growth, expanding adjusted EBITDA, and positive analyst estimate revisions (+14% over 30 days) provide a credible 12-month growth thesis. The company's positioning as a non-competitive partner to community pathologists supports continued share gains in molecular oncology testing.

However, the equity's risk/reward is dominated by the 2028 convertible note maturity — a $342M obligation against declining cash with no announced refinancing plan. This creates a binary outcome that caps potential upside (through likely dilution) while creating meaningful downside tail risk (distressed refinancing or forced sale). The OIG investigation adds a secondary binary risk with unknown magnitude above the $11.2M reserve.

At $9.07, the stock is not expensive (1.7x 2026E revenue) and offers upside if the capital structure is resolved favorably. But initiating a position requires accepting that the refinancing outcome — likely determined in the next 6-12 months — will define equity returns more than operating performance. This is a HOLD for existing investors with a clear growth thesis but insufficient clarity on capital structure resolution for a new BUY recommendation. Monitor for refinancing announcement as the primary re-rating catalyst.
