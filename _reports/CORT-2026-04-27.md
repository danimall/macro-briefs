## Executive Summary

Corcept Therapeutics faces a critical transition. The March 25, 2026 FDA approval of Lifyorli (relacorilant) for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer — nearly four months ahead of the PDUFA date — provides a credible replacement revenue engine as the legacy Korlym franchise faces irreversible generic erosion following the February 2026 Federal Circuit ruling in favor of Teva. The stock has recovered from its December 2025 low of ~$34.80 to ~$46 but remains 49% below its October 2025 high of $91, reflecting the market's attempt to price in both the Lifyorli opportunity and the compounding legal and commercial risks.

The financial profile is shifting from high-margin single-product economics to investment-phase platform economics. SGA expenses surged 60% in FY2025 to $449M (58.9% of revenue) as the company built its oncology commercial infrastructure, compressing operating margins from 20.3% to 5.9%. Management has guided for further SGA increases in 2026. With the current quarter consensus at -$0.14 EPS and full-year 2026 at $0.41, the ~56x trailing P/E is largely irrelevant — what matters is the trajectory of Lifyorli adoption, the pace of Korlym erosion, and whether the ALS and Cushing's pipeline assets deliver.

The risk stack is unusually dense: ongoing DOJ investigation (4+ years), securities class action litigation, Teva antitrust trial scheduled for January 2027, the Cushing's CRL, mifepristone political exposure, and 13.6% short interest. The balance sheet ($532M cash, no debt) provides a cushion, but the sheer number of open legal and regulatory vectors warrants caution. Rating: HOLD with a growth rationale. The pipeline optionality is genuine, but the stock demands monitoring of specific execution milestones before upgrading.

## Company Overview

Corcept Therapeutics is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused exclusively on cortisol modulation. Since 2012, its sole commercial product was Korlym (mifepristone) for hyperglycemia secondary to Cushing's syndrome. On March 25, 2026, the FDA approved Lifyorli (relacorilant) in combination with nab-paclitaxel for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer — a first-in-class selective glucocorticoid receptor antagonist (SGRA) in oncology. Lifyorli is priced at $37,900 per 28-day cycle and has been added to NCCN Guidelines as a preferred regimen.

The company has discovered and patented four structurally distinct series of selective cortisol modulators comprising over 1,000 compounds. The pipeline includes relacorilant for Cushing's syndrome (CRL received; working with FDA), dazucorilant for ALS (Phase 2 showed 84% mortality reduction; pivotal Phase 3 planned), miricorilant for MASH, and nenocorilant in solid tumors with immunotherapy. An EMA regulatory decision on Lifyorli for ovarian cancer is expected Q4 2026.

Corcept employs a specialty pharmacy distribution model — over 99% of revenue flows through pharmacy collections. The company recently transitioned from Optime to Curant as its primary specialty pharmacy partner after Optime was unable to meet demand.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue of $761M grew 12.8% YoY, driven by 37% volume growth that offset an ~18% average selling price decline from the authorized generic launch. Q3 2025 revenue of $207.6M (+13.7% YoY) showed similar dynamics: 42.5% volume growth offset by 20.2% price compression. Management noted that revenue would have been "materially higher" absent supply constraints at Optime.

**Margins:** SGA rose from $280M (41.5% of revenue) in FY2024 to $449M (58.9%) in FY2025 — a 60% increase reflecting Lifyorli pre-launch commercial infrastructure buildout. Operating income collapsed from $137M to $45M (operating margin 20.3% → 5.9%). R&D was stable at $255M. Cost of goods sold remains negligible at 1.7% of revenue, reflecting specialty pharma pricing economics.

**Earnings:** Net income fell from $141M to $100M in FY2025. Trailing EPS of $0.82 reflects the compressed-margin transition year. Current quarter consensus is -$0.14 EPS, with FY2026E at $0.41 — a 50% decline from FY2025 as SGA ramps further while Korlym erosion accelerates. FY2027E of $1.61 reflects analyst expectations that Lifyorli revenue will begin to offset these headwinds.

**Balance Sheet:** Cash and marketable securities of $532M at year-end 2025. No long-term debt. Current ratio of 2.92. Altman Z-Score of 4.69 (safe zone). Debt-to-equity of 0.29. Retained earnings of $643M. The company is self-funding all operations, pipeline investment, and share repurchases from product revenue.

**Cash Flow & Capital Allocation:** Operating cash flow declined from $198M to $142M, with FCF margin of 18.6%. The company repurchased $246M of stock in FY2025, a significant acceleration from $38M in FY2024. The $200M formal repurchase program was nearly exhausted by September 2025.

**Earnings Surprises:** 3 beats in 4 quarters, with the Q4 2025 miss (-34.6% vs. consensus) coinciding with CRL-driven uncertainty. The Q2 2025 beat of +51.6% was the standout quarter.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis centers on three vectors with distinct timelines:

**Near-term (0-18 months): Lifyorli oncology launch.** With NCCN preferred regimen status, Lifyorli targets approximately 22,000 new ovarian cancer patients annually in the US. The ROSELLA trial demonstrated a 35% reduction in death risk (HR 0.65, p=0.0004) with median OS of 16.0 vs. 11.9 months. The drug was available within one week of approval. The pace of formulary access, specialty pharmacy distribution ramp through Curant, and payer adoption will determine whether FY2027 revenue estimates are achievable.

**Medium-term (18-36 months): Pipeline expansion.** Dazucorilant for ALS showed an extraordinary Phase 2 signal — 84% reduction in mortality at 300mg (HR 0.16, p=0.0009) — despite missing its primary endpoint (ALSFRS-R). FDA granted Fast Track and orphan drug designation. If the pivotal Phase 3 (planned 2026) confirms these results, this would be a transformative asset in a disease with no meaningful life-extending therapies. Relacorilant's path forward in Cushing's syndrome is uncertain post-CRL, but the GRACE trial did meet its primary endpoint; the company is working with FDA on additional evidence requirements.

**Long-term (3+ years): Platform optionality.** Phase 2 programs in pancreatic cancer (TRIDENT), cervical cancer (STELLA), prostate cancer, and MASH (miricorilant MONARCH) expand the addressable market. The CATALYST study finding that 23.8% of difficult-to-control diabetics have hypercortisolism suggests a large undiagnosed patient population for cortisol modulation.

**Key headwind:** Korlym revenue erosion is accelerating. Teva's generic is on the market, with Sun and Hikma generics pending. The authorized generic strategy trades price for volume retention but cannot prevent margin degradation. FY2026 will be the trough earnings year as Korlym declines intersect with peak SGA investment.

## Valuation Assessment

At $46.06, CORT trades at ~56x trailing EPS ($0.82) and ~112x FY2026E EPS ($0.41). On FY2027E of $1.61, the forward P/E is ~29x. The market cap of $4.9B against FY2025 revenue of $761M produces a P/S of ~6.4x, within the range for commercial-stage specialty pharma but not cheap.

Analyst consensus targets: low $44, median $60, mean $67.40, high $110. Four of six covering analysts rate the stock Buy or Strong Buy; two rate it Hold. The wide target range reflects genuine uncertainty about Lifyorli's commercial trajectory and pipeline outcomes.

The valuation framework depends entirely on which year you normalize to. If Lifyorli ramps to $400-500M in peak US revenue (consensus-adjacent) by FY2028 while residual Korlym contributes $300-400M, total revenue could reach $700-900M with restored operating margins of 25-35% as SGA leverage kicks in. At 25x those earnings, that implies a $75-100 range. If Lifyorli disappoints or uptake is slower than modeled, Korlym alone on an eroding base might support a $20-25 stock.

The current price essentially assigns credit for base-case Lifyorli adoption but no value to the ALS asset, MASH, or pipeline oncology expansions. That pipeline optionality is real but unpriced. The stock also embeds no discount for the DOJ investigation, securities litigation, or Teva antitrust trial. The net of these offsetting factors suggests the stock is approximately fairly valued with a slight upward skew from pipeline optionality.

## Competitive Landscape

Corcept has a genuine competitive moat in cortisol modulation. No other company has a comparable portfolio of selective cortisol modulators. The four structurally distinct series comprising 1,000+ compounds create both patent protection and developmental optionality that would take a decade to replicate.

**In Cushing's syndrome:** The competitive set includes Recordati's osilodrostat (expanded label April 2025), Xeris's levoketoconazole, and generic ketoconazole — all cortisol synthesis inhibitors with a different mechanism of action. Teva's generic mifepristone is the most direct competitive threat, but it competes on price, not mechanism. The authorized generic strategy partially addresses this.

**In ovarian cancer:** Lifyorli is first-in-class as an SGRA in oncology. Competing therapies in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer include bevacizumab combinations, PARP inhibitors (limited to BRCA+), and antibody-drug conjugates (mirvetuximab soravtansine). Lifyorli's NCCN preferred regimen status and survival data provide a strong commercial foundation. The risk is that the ovarian cancer treatment landscape evolves faster than expected with competing novel mechanisms.

**Platform trajectory:** The competitive position is strengthening as the company transitions from a single-product franchise dependent on an unprotectable molecule (mifepristone) to a multi-product platform with proprietary compounds, even as the transition creates near-term financial pain.

## Risk Assessment

**Generic erosion (HIGH — partially priced).** Teva's generic mifepristone is on the market; Sun and Hikma will follow. Korlym pricing will continue compressing. The market has partially priced this, but the rate of erosion in 2026-2027 could exceed current estimates.

**DOJ/NJ USAO investigation (MODERATE — underpriced).** Open for 4+ years regarding Korlym sales practices, HCP relationships, and reimbursement. The NJ USAO stated Corcept is "not currently a defendant," but the investigation's duration and scope (potential criminal/civil violations) create meaningful tail risk. Resolution could range from nothing to a significant settlement or criminal referral.

**Securities class action (MODERATE).** Allegheny County Employees' Retirement System case covers the period when management allegedly concealed FDA warnings about the Cushing's NDA. Lead plaintiff deadline April 21, 2026. The prior Melucci case settled for $14M (insurance-covered), suggesting Corcept has experience managing these, but the CRL-driven price decline was substantially larger.

**Teva antitrust trial (MODERATE).** Scheduled January 2027. Teva and four health insurers allege antitrust violations in Corcept's specialty pharmacy distribution model. An adverse ruling could result in substantial damages and force changes to the distribution model.

**Cushing's CRL path forward (MODERATE).** The FDA's December 2025 CRL cited need for "additional evidence of effectiveness." The GRADIENT trial's primary endpoint (SBP improvement) was not statistically significant. The path to approval may require additional clinical work, adding cost and time. This indication remains strategically important for protecting the Cushing's franchise post-generic erosion.

**Lifyorli commercial execution (HIGH).** The entire near-term investment thesis depends on Lifyorli launch trajectory. Specialty pharmacy transition risk (Optime → Curant), payer adoption timelines, and competition from existing ovarian cancer regimens all affect uptake speed. Any disappointment in early prescription data would pressure the stock materially.

**IRA / OBBBA pricing pressure (MODERATE).** Management has stated that the Inflation Reduction Act's Medicare inflation rebate provisions and Part D redesign are expected to "materially reduce revenue and profits in 2026 and beyond." Korlym currently enjoys orphan drug exemption from Medicare price negotiation, but this could change if relacorilant is approved for a broader indication.

**Short interest at 13.6% of float** with 5.9 days to cover indicates meaningful institutional bearish positioning. This creates both risk (informed bears see something) and opportunity (short squeeze potential on positive catalysts).

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 55.6% is 33.7 percentage points below trailing 1-year historical volatility of 89.3% — the options market is pricing significantly less uncertainty than the stock has recently exhibited. This is consistent with a post-event period (FDA approval resolved, CRL absorbed) where realized volatility was dominated by discrete shocks that may not repeat at the same frequency. The put/call OI ratio of 1.13 is modestly bearish. The flat term structure (55.6% near-term vs. 57.6% at 6-month) suggests the market sees no specific near-term catalyst to drive outsized moves. Call-heavy skew across all tenors (particularly -6.6% at 6-month) indicates some institutional call accumulation, possibly reflecting pipeline optionality bets.

The cheap IV relative to HV creates potentially favorable entry timing for long positions via options, but the single-stock binary risks (DOJ, antitrust trial, Lifyorli data) mean that cheap vol could reprice rapidly on any negative headline.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($70-80, +52-74% upside)
Lifyorli launch exceeds expectations with rapid formulary access and strong prescriber uptake, reaching $150M+ in FY2026 revenue. Korlym erosion is gradual rather than precipitous as the authorized generic retains volume. The CATALYST study finding drives increased Cushing's screening, partially offsetting generic pricing pressure. Dazucorilant Phase 3 in ALS confirms the Phase 2 mortality signal, adding substantial pipeline value. DOJ investigation resolves without material financial impact. Operating margins begin recovering in H2 2027 as SGA leverage materializes. EMA approves Lifyorli for European markets in Q4 2026.

### Bear Case ($25-30, -35-46% downside)
Lifyorli uptake is slower than modeled due to specialty pharmacy distribution challenges, payer pushback at $37,900/cycle, or competitive displacement. Korlym erosion accelerates as Sun/Hikma generics launch, compressing revenue faster than Lifyorli can replace it. DOJ investigation results in a meaningful settlement or criminal referral. Teva antitrust trial produces adverse judgment requiring distribution model changes and damages. Dazucorilant Phase 3 fails to confirm the Phase 2 signal. FY2026 operating losses are larger than expected.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Lifyorli quarterly revenue exceeds $30M within the first two reported quarters post-launch, demonstrating rapid formulary access and prescriber adoption
2. DOJ investigation formally closed or resolved with immaterial financial impact
3. Relacorilant Cushing's path forward clarified with FDA acceptance of supplemental data (avoiding full new trial requirement)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Lifyorli quarterly revenue below $15M after two full commercial quarters, indicating fundamental adoption problems
2. DOJ investigation results in criminal indictment or settlement exceeding $100M
3. Teva antitrust ruling forces material changes to specialty pharmacy distribution model before Lifyorli reaches commercial scale

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (Q1 2026, expected May 2026) will provide first meaningful Lifyorli launch data. The January 2027 Teva antitrust trial is the next major legal milestone. EMA decision on Lifyorli expected Q4 2026.

## Conclusion

Corcept Therapeutics is in a transition where the legacy Korlym business is in terminal decline and the replacement growth engine — Lifyorli in oncology — has just received regulatory validation. The balance sheet is clean, the pipeline optionality (particularly dazucorilant in ALS) is real, and the competitive moat in cortisol modulation is durable.

However, the risk stack is among the densest in small/mid-cap pharma. At ~$46 with FY2026E EPS of $0.41, the stock requires substantial faith in FY2027-2028 earnings recovery that depends on flawless Lifyorli execution. The margin of safety is thin.

HOLD with a growth rationale. The pipeline optionality and Lifyorli launch trajectory justify maintaining exposure, but the concentration of legal, regulatory, and commercial risks argues against adding until the first few quarters of Lifyorli revenue data provide visibility into whether the transition is working. Monitor the May 2026 earnings report for initial launch metrics.
