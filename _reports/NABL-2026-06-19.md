**Research Date: June 19, 2026**

## Executive Summary

N-able sells an AI-powered cybersecurity, data-protection, and IT-management platform to Managed Service Providers (MSPs), VARs, and distributors, who in turn serve roughly 500,000 SMB and mid-market organizations through a channel-only model. Spun out of SolarWinds in July 2021 at roughly $13, the stock now trades at $3.06, down 61% over the trailing twelve months and 76% from the spin price. The central question for the stock is whether that decline marks a business in structural decline or a still-growing franchise whose multiple has compressed faster than its fundamentals.

The evidence favors the latter. The operating business continues to grow and generate cash: ARR reached $548.0M as of 3/31/26 (+11.2% YoY), subscription revenue grew 13.4%, net revenue retention re-accelerated to roughly 106% from ~101% a year earlier, and the share of ARR from >$50K-ARR partners climbed from 58% to 62%, confirming up-market traction. Q1 2026 GAAP operating income recovered to $12.5M (9.3% margin) from $1.8M (1.5%), operating cash flow was $17.5M, and adjusted EBITDA was $36.7M (27.5% margin). The GAAP net loss narrowed to -$0.6M. Net leverage is ~1.8x with maturities pushed to 2032 via a November 2025 refinancing. None of this describes terminal impairment.

What keeps this a HOLD rather than a BUY is forward momentum and signaling quality, both of which are negative. Consensus EPS estimates are falling across every horizon (current-year $0.49→$0.41 over 90 days, ~15%; current-quarter down ~27%). Management put goodwill on impairment watch after the stock decline, with $1.0B of goodwill against $799M of book equity and a $576M market cap, a non-cash overhang already reflected in the price. Insiders bought zero shares on the open market through the collapse, and the buyback sat idle in Q1 2026 despite $45M of remaining authorization. A ~$60M deferred-consideration payment for Adlumin comes due in November 2026. The risk/reward is genuinely balanced, and the honest classification is uncertain.

## Company Overview

N-able operates a single software segment in which ~99% of revenue is subscription. The product set spans remote monitoring and management (its RMM heritage), data protection/backup, and, following the November 2024 acquisition of Adlumin, a Washington D.C. security-operations/SIEM-XDR platform, security operations and "cyber resilience." The Adlumin deal is the strategic pivot: it moves N-able up-market from fragmented SMB tooling toward larger, stickier MSP partners and higher ARR per customer.

Revenue is geographically diversified (US ~48.5%, UK ~10.4%, rest-of-world ~41% in Q1 2026), which creates meaningful EUR/GBP/CAD translation exposure. The company remains tied to SolarWinds through intercompany OEM, IP, and trademark agreements, though the dollar amounts are immaterial (~$0.5M of quarterly revenue).

N-able is a controlled company. Private-equity sponsors Thoma Bravo and Silver Lake, carried over from the SolarWinds take-private, retain board influence under a Stockholders' Agreement. Sponsor-affiliated entities continue to receive director-equivalent equity grants. This structure matters for the take-private optionality discussed in the risk section.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and ARR.** Q1 2026 revenue was $133.7M, up 13.1% YoY from $118.2M. The qualitative tell is the up-market mix shift: customers with >$50K ARR grew to 2,710 (+13%). Three-year revenue growth of ~37% has been increasingly acquisition-aided (FY2025 revenue $516.4M vs FY2024 $457.7M), and organic growth is decelerating, but the franchise is intact and the mix is improving.

**Net revenue retention.** The recovery reverses the trend disclosed in the FY2025 10-K, where NRR had fallen to ~103% from 110%. The recovery was aided by pricing/packaging changes, so durability is the open question; distinguishing a structural demand recovery from a one-time pricing benefit is the single most important fundamental input.

**Profitability and the GAAP-vs-adjusted wedge.** FY2025 net income swung to -$17.0M from +$31.0M in FY2024, driven entirely by Adlumin: $74.8M developed-technology amortization plus $5.4M customer-relationship amortization, transaction costs, deferred-consideration interest, and higher debt-service cost. The recovery is already visible in 2026, helped by the roll-off of $6.3M of Q1 2025 transaction costs (only $0.2M in Q1 2026). On an adjusted basis the picture is healthier: 22.2% non-GAAP operating margin. The wedge between GAAP losses and strong adjusted EBITDA is structural: ~9%-of-revenue stock-based comp ($11.8M/quarter), acquired-intangible amortization, and a punitive 114.7% effective tax rate (cash tax on foreign income against largely unbenefited US losses). The 27.5% EBITDA margin should be read with the SBC dilution in mind.

**Cash flow and balance sheet.** Operating cash flow was $17.5M in Q1 2026 (down from $19.7M), and with ~$4M capex, FCF conversion is solid. Cash stood at $117.8M against $399M of term-loan debt; net leverage ~1.8x is manageable for a recurring-revenue software business. The November 2025 refinancing (Amendment No. 2) upsized the term loan from $336M to $400M, extended maturity to 2032, lowered the spread, and pushed the revolver to 2030, de-risking the maturity profile at the cost of slightly more leverage. The balance-sheet vulnerability is goodwill: $1.01B of goodwill plus $60M of intangibles against $1.40B of total assets and $799M of equity; goodwill is ~72% of assets, and market cap now sits below book equity.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on the up-market security pivot. Adlumin moves N-able from RMM tooling into security operations, where ARR per customer is higher and switching costs are greater. The >$50K-ARR cohort reaching 62% of ARR is direct evidence the strategy is converting, and the NRR recovery suggests the installed base is expanding spend again.

Two facts temper the growth case. First, organic growth is decelerating into low-double-digits and the headline three-year growth has leaned on M&A. Second, and more important for forward returns, consensus estimates are falling across every horizon: current-quarter EPS $0.13→$0.10 (-27% over 90 days), current-year $0.49→$0.41 (-15%), next-year $0.56→$0.48 (-14%). Falling estimates are the most reliable negative forward signal in software, and they argue against treating this as an earnings-inflection story. Management's own framing, per sell-side notes, that guidance "implies stronger execution in 2H2026," confirms the second half is a show-me period rather than a confirmed acceleration.

The earnings track record is mixed: two beats (Q2 and Q3 2025 by 22% and 42%), one in-line (Q1 2026), and one sizable miss (Q4 2025, -41%). The Q4 miss followed by an in-line Q1 alongside falling estimates suggests deteriorating visibility, not improving momentum.

## Valuation Assessment

On trailing GAAP the stock screens at a negative P/E (-54.6x) because of Adlumin amortization, so trailing multiples are uninformative. On a forward basis, $0.41 current-year EPS against a $3.06 price implies ~7.5x forward earnings, and the EV/sales is roughly 1.6x ($576M equity + $399M debt - $118M cash = ~$857M EV against ~$535M annualized revenue, i.e., Q1 2026 revenue of $133.7M × 4). Both are well below the systems-software peer median P/E of 19.9x (PRGS 16.2x, QLYS 19.9x, PATH 17.6x). The peer set is heterogeneous (CVLT trades at 80x, ATEN at 53x), but the cheaper, slower-growth comps (PRGS at 16x, also down 52% from its 52-week high) are the right reference, and even against those N-able is discounted.

The screen is cheap, but cheapness alone is not a buy signal; it frequently marks a value trap, and the discount here is doing work. The market is pricing falling estimates, the goodwill-impairment overhang, controlled-company governance, and the Nov 2026 deferred-consideration cash claim. For the multiple to re-rate, the company needs to stabilize estimates and demonstrate that the NRR recovery is durable. The lowest sell-side target ($5.75, Scotiabank, Sector Perform) sits 88% above the current price, and Needham's $6.50 (Buy) implies more than a double; that gap is wide enough that any confirmation of the 2H execution thesis would close it, but the targets themselves predate confirmation and reflect the same show-me uncertainty. I assign a balanced valuation score: genuinely cheap on forward metrics, with no identified near-term catalyst to force the re-rating.

## Competitive Landscape

N-able competes on two fronts. In MSP IT-management tooling it faces ConnectWise, Kaseya/Datto, NinjaOne, and Atera in a mature, crowded market. In security operations, Adlumin places it against far larger SIEM/XDR vendors. Its differentiation is the integrated, channel-delivered, multi-product platform purpose-built for the SMB/mid-market MSP segment: a sticky, recurring base reached through a partner-first model rather than direct enterprise sales.

The competitive trajectory is mixed but tilting positive at the margin. The up-market mix shift and NRR recovery suggest the platform is deepening within its existing channel rather than losing share. Against that, peer total shareholder return has tripled since the spin while N-able lost ~40% (per the proxy: $100 invested at spin grew to $170 for peers vs $60 for NABL), which the market reads as N-able being a laggard in the software/security cohort. The channel model is double-edged: efficient distribution and stickiness, but one step removed from end demand and exposed to SMB IT-spending cycles that management repeatedly flags.

## Risk Assessment

**Goodwill impairment (sentiment overhang, non-cash).** Management added language to both Note 4 and Critical Accounting Policies stating that the Q1 2026 stock decline could trigger an interim impairment test, with any charge potentially "material." Market cap below book equity is a textbook impairment trigger. This is real, but it is non-cash and does not affect liquidity, debt covenants, or the recurring-revenue base. I treat it as a sentiment overhang already reflected in the 61% drawdown rather than a thesis-determining catalyst; the sources do not support any covenant or liquidity linkage.

**Leverage and rates.** $399M floating-rate term loan at ~6.42% SOFR; a 100bp move is ~$4M of annual interest. The Nov 2025 refinancing extended maturities to 2032 and lowered the spread, so refinancing risk is low through the medium term.

**Adlumin deferred consideration.** ~$59.7M was payable on the second anniversary (Nov 2026) plus up to $15M contingent earn-out, a real cash claim against ~$70M annual OCF, and the reason debt was upsized. Liquidity around that payment is the key near-term cash-flow watch item, though cash on hand ($117.8M) plus the revolver covers it.

**Governance and signaling.** Controlled-company structure concentrates influence with Thoma Bravo/Silver Lake. The pay-for-performance disconnect is stark: TSR fell ~40% since spin and FY2025 net income swung negative, yet CEO total comp rose to $7.75M from $6.40M. Three Item 5.02 (officer/director change) 8-Ks in ~14 months point to elevated leadership turnover during the strategic pivot (the underlying filings were inaccessible and remain unconfirmed). Insiders bought no shares on the open market through the collapse, and the buyback was idle in Q1 2026 with $45M authorized; management is not signaling conviction with its own capital, a mild negative.

**FX.** ~41% of revenue is non-US; Q1 2026 carried a $13.0M negative FX translation hit to OCI and a $2.2M FX swing in other expense.

### Options Market Signal

The reported ATM IV reading of 2,545.8% (versus 51.6% in the prior snapshot) is a corrupted thin-options artifact on a low-priced micro-cap and is non-informative. Short interest is low at 4.3% of float with 1.9 days to cover, indicating the bearish case is uncrowded and the stock is not a consensus short. There is no usable options-implied signal here.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The NRR recovery proves durable through 2H 2026, the up-market mix continues toward 65%+ of ARR, estimates stabilize and resume rising, and the goodwill-impairment test passes or the charge is taken and dismissed as non-cash. At ~7.5x forward earnings the stock re-rates toward the cheaper peer band (12-15x forward), and the take-private optionality (sponsors who already own a control stake, a depressed price below book equity, and 2032 maturities) becomes a live floor. Bull target $6.00, roughly the analyst median and ~96% above current.

**Bear case.** The NRR recovery proves to be a one-time pricing benefit, organic growth decelerates toward high-single-digits, the goodwill impairment is taken and confirms market skepticism, the Adlumin earnout strains liquidity, and estimates keep falling. The multiple compresses further on a no-growth, leveraged, controlled-company software laggard. Bear target $2.25, ~26% below current.

The asymmetry tilts modestly favorable from a washed-out base, with downside to the bear case smaller than the upside to even the lowest sell-side target, but the catalyst path is unconfirmed and the forward signals (falling estimates, no insider buying, paused buyback) argue against pre-positioning.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

This is a HOLD with an uncertain rationale: the evidence does not support a confident growth thesis (estimates are falling) or an income thesis (no dividend), and the business is too intact and the price too washed out to justify an AVOID.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Two consecutive quarters of NRR sustained above 105% with management attributing it to volume/expansion rather than pricing, confirming durable demand.
- Current-year EPS estimates stabilizing or turning positive (the current $0.41 consensus stops falling for two reporting cycles).
- Buyback restart at these levels or open-market insider purchases, a capital-allocation signal that management views shares as cheap.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- NRR rolling back below 100%, or organic ARR growth decelerating below high-single-digits, indicating the up-market thesis is stalling.
- A liquidity strain around the Nov 2026 Adlumin deferred-consideration payment that forces additional debt or equity issuance.
- Estimate cuts accelerating (current-year falling another 15%+) alongside a guidance reduction.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (Q2 2026, expected early August) is the natural checkpoint; it tests the NRR durability question and management's 2H execution claim directly. The Nov 2026 deferred-consideration payment is the second checkpoint.

## Conclusion

N-able is a decelerating-but-growing, cash-generative MSP security-and-management software vendor whose stock has fallen 61% on a combination of falling estimates, an Adlumin-driven swing to GAAP losses, and an explicit goodwill-impairment watch. The drawdown reflects valuation compression on an intact franchise (ARR +11.2%, NRR re-accelerating to ~106%, 27.5% adjusted EBITDA margin, positive cash flow, manageable 1.8x leverage with maturities to 2032) rather than structural impairment. At ~7.5x forward earnings against a 19.9x peer median, the stock is cheap, and the bearish case is not actionable as a short: short interest is only 4.3%, downside to a $2.25 bear target is ~26%, and live take-private and buyback optionality cap the downside off a washed-out base.

Cheapness without a catalyst is a value trap until proven otherwise, and the forward signals are negative: estimates are falling across every horizon, insiders bought nothing through the collapse, the buyback sat idle, and a ~$60M cash claim arrives in November. The call is HOLD with an uncertain rationale: the risk/reward is balanced, and the path to a higher rating runs through two quarters of durable NRR, estimate stabilization, and a capital-allocation signal that management itself believes the stock is mispriced.
