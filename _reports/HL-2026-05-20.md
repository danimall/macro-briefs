## Executive Summary

Hecla Mining has executed a dramatic financial turnaround, transforming from $653M in long-term debt and an $84M net loss in 2023 to completely debt-free status with $322M net income in 2025. The company now operates as a pure-play silver producer (post Casa Berardi gold mine divestiture) with consolidated AISC of $11.28/oz against silver prices near $80/oz, generating extraordinary margins.

The stock has declined 52% from its January 2026 high of $34.17 to approximately $17, broadly tracking silver's retreat from $118 to ~$80. At the current price, the $11.5B market cap implies roughly 11-12x annualized free cash flow at current silver levels. This is not cheap for a commodity producer operating at peak margins — it prices in sustained elevated silver but offers limited margin of safety against a price correction. The most recent quarterly earnings (Q1 2026) missed estimates by 10%, reversing three consecutive beat quarters, and current-year estimate revisions have turned negative (-13.6% over 30 days).

The investment case for HL is fundamentally a bet on silver prices remaining above $60-70/oz. The company's cost structure, jurisdictional quality, and balance sheet position it as the highest-quality vehicle for that bet. Without conviction on silver's forward direction, there is insufficient basis for a directional recommendation.

## Company Overview

Hecla Mining (founded 1891) is the largest silver producer in the United States and Canada, operating three mines:

- **Greens Creek (Alaska):** Polymetallic mine producing silver, gold, zinc, lead. FY2025 revenue of $612.8M (44% of total) with $322.6M gross profit. Operates at negative AISC of -$2.36/oz silver due to by-product credits.
- **Lucky Friday (Idaho):** Deep underground silver mine achieving record 5.3M oz production in 2025. Revenue $306.6M with $133M gross profit. AISC $21.98/oz.
- **Keno Hill (Yukon):** Acquired 2022, still in ramp-up phase. Produced 3.0M oz silver in 2025 at exceptionally high grades (29 oz/ton). Not yet at commercial production status.

The company completed the sale of its Casa Berardi gold mine to Orezone Gold for up to $593M (closed March 25, 2026), pivoting to a pure silver focus. On April 9, 2026, Hecla redeemed its remaining $263M Senior Notes, achieving zero debt.

Revenue grew approximately 53% from 2024 to 2025, with $417M of the $493M increase attributable to price appreciation (silver realized price: $28.58 → $45.25/oz; gold: $2,403 → $3,490/oz). This price sensitivity is the defining characteristic of the investment.

### Balance Sheet — Transformed

- **Altman Z-Score: 3.16** (safe zone)
- **Current Ratio: 2.72**
- **Debt/Equity: 0.37** (as of year-end 2025; now effectively zero with April 2026 redemption)
- **Cash + investments: $349M** at year-end 2025, plus incoming Casa Berardi proceeds

The elimination of ~$50M/year in interest expense flows directly to the bottom line. The balance sheet is appropriate for a highly cyclical commodity producer.

### Cost Structure

Consolidated silver AISC of $11.28/oz provides enormous margin at current silver prices (~$80/oz). Greens Creek's negative AISC (-$2.36/oz) means it generates profit at any silver price. Even if silver falls to $30/oz, Lucky Friday at $21.98/oz would still operate profitably, and consolidated margins would remain positive.

### Cash Flow Quality

FCF of $310M on $322M net income = 96% conversion rate. Operating cash flow of $563M substantially exceeded net income, indicating high-quality earnings with minimal accrual manipulation.

## Growth Analysis

### Volume Growth Is Limited

Hecla's growth story is primarily a price story. Silver production guidance for 2026 is approximately flat at 15-16M payable ounces across the three mines. Keno Hill is guided flat vs. 2025 as management prioritizes permitting and infrastructure over throughput expansion.

### Keno Hill Optionality

The upside case for production growth centers on Keno Hill reaching its 440 tons/day permitted capacity (currently ~297 tpd). At 29 oz/ton grades, full throughput would meaningfully increase company-wide production. However, permitting challenges (complicated by the Victoria Gold heap leach failure in 2024), power supply issues (Yukon Energy turbine failures), and water management concerns create execution risk that management has explicitly flagged, including care-and-maintenance as a contingency.

### Nevada Exploration

The Midas and Aurora properties in Nevada represent longer-term optionality. Historical production of 2.2M oz gold + 27M oz silver at Midas, existing infrastructure (1,200 tpd mill in care & maintenance), and 2025 drill results confirming mineralized structures provide a development pathway. This is a 3-5 year catalyst, not near-term.

### Estimate Trajectory — Turning Negative

Current-year EPS estimates have been revised down 13.6% over 30 days and 18.6% over 90 days. Next-year estimates are more favorable (+7.4% over 30 days, +31.4% over 90 days), suggesting analysts expect silver price recovery. The Q1 2026 miss of -10.1% broke a three-quarter beat streak and may signal that the rapid price-driven earnings expansion has peaked.

## Valuation Assessment

At $17.14 with trailing EPS of $0.49, the stock trades at ~35x trailing earnings. Forward estimates suggest FY2026 EPS of $0.87 and FY2027 EPS of $1.25, implying forward P/E multiples of ~20x and ~14x respectively.

The critical question is whether current silver prices (~$80/oz) represent normalized or peak conditions. At $80 silver:
- Estimated annual FCF: ~$400-500M (based on 2025 margins adjusted for lower average price)
- Market cap / FCF: ~23-29x
- EV / FCF: ~22-27x (no debt, $350M+ cash)

Compared to Materials sector peer median P/E of 18.7x, Hecla's ~35x trailing appears elevated. But this comparison is inappropriate — the peers (Westlake, Avery Dennison, RPM, Crown Holdings) are not commodity producers with equivalent operating leverage. Against precious metals peers, silver miners routinely trade at premium multiples reflecting reserve optionality and inflation-hedge demand.

The median analyst target of $24.50 (range $13.50-$32.00) implies 43% upside, but analyst targets in mining equities tend to embed optimistic commodity assumptions. The low target of $13.50 implies meaningful downside if silver corrects further.

At current prices, the stock is fairly valued assuming silver sustains $70-80/oz. It is cheap if silver moves to $90-100 (Deutsche Bank's target). It is expensive if silver returns to $40-50/oz range.

## Competitive Landscape

### Structural Advantages

1. **Lowest-cost producer in the Americas.** Negative AISC at Greens Creek is essentially unmatched in the silver sector.
2. **Jurisdictional quality.** Operating exclusively in the US and Canada eliminates emerging-market political risk that affects competitors (Mexico, Peru, Bolivia).
3. **Debt-free balance sheet.** Few commodity producers can claim zero debt with $350M+ liquidity.
4. **135-year operating history** in the same mining districts provides geological knowledge advantages.
5. **~37% of US/Canadian silver production** from three mines gives meaningful market position.

### Competitive Vulnerabilities

1. **Scale.** At ~15M oz annual silver production, Hecla is mid-tier globally. Pan American Silver and Fresnillo produce significantly more.
2. **Single-commodity concentration.** Post Casa Berardi, revenue diversification is reduced (though Greens Creek still generates significant gold/zinc/lead by-product revenue).
3. **Customer concentration.** Three customers = 62% of revenue. With China eliminated by tariffs (no shipments since March 2025), the buyer base has narrowed further.

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

**1. Silver Price Collapse (HIGH PROBABILITY, HIGH IMPACT)**
The 2025 revenue increase was 88% price-driven. If silver retreats to $40/oz (2024 average), revenue approximately halves and net income falls to 2024 levels (~$36M). The stock would likely trade back to $6-8 range. With silver already having corrected from $118 to ~$80, the question is whether this represents a floor or a waystation.

**2. Keno Hill Permitting Failure (MODERATE PROBABILITY, MODERATE IMPACT)**
Management explicitly states care-and-maintenance is an option if permitting or economics deteriorate. The Victoria Gold incident has created a hostile regulatory environment in Yukon. Power supply reliability is also uncertain. Failure here eliminates the production growth narrative.

**3. Casa Berardi Contingent Consideration Risk (MODERATE)**
$321M (54%) of the $593M sale price is deferred or contingent on Orezone's operational success. The 65.7M Orezone shares (~$112M) are illiquid. If gold prices decline or Orezone fails to execute, Hecla receives substantially less than headline consideration.

**4. Dilution History**
Share count grew 17% (557M to 652M) from 2022-2025 via ATM issuances. While proceeds were used productively, the pattern signals management willingness to issue equity.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV of 82.2% significantly exceeds historical volatility of 71.0%, with IV in the 87th percentile. The term structure is in backwardation (82.2% near vs. 66.8% far), pricing imminent stress. Put/call OI ratio of 0.85 is balanced, suggesting institutional positioning is neutral rather than directional. The implied move of ±5% on nearest expiry indicates the market expects continued high-amplitude daily moves. The rich IV environment makes both call and put spreads relatively expensive, reducing tactical entry quality.

## Conclusion

Hecla Mining is a high-quality silver vehicle: the best balance sheet in the sector, the lowest costs, and the most defensible jurisdictional profile. The financial transformation from 2023 to 2026 is genuinely impressive. However, the investment proposition at $17 is fundamentally a commodity price bet: own HL if you are bullish on silver, avoid if you are neutral or bearish.

The 52% decline from highs reflects silver's correction from $118 to ~$80, not any deterioration in company-specific fundamentals. The business is better positioned today than at any point in its history. But with current-year estimates declining, the Q1 miss breaking the beat streak, and insider selling of $9M+ without any open-market purchases, near-term momentum has shifted negative.

The analyst consensus (median $24.50 target) implies substantial upside, but is predicated on silver assumptions that may not hold. At ~35x trailing and ~20x current-year estimates for a commodity producer at peak margins, there is insufficient margin of safety for a high-conviction BUY, and insufficient evidence of fundamental deterioration for an AVOID. This is a HOLD pending clarity on silver's next directional move and Keno Hill's operational trajectory.
