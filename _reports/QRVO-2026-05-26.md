## Executive Summary

Qorvo is trading at $108, roughly 6% below the implied $114 deal value from the pending Skyworks Solutions acquisition (0.960 SWKS shares + $32.50 cash per share). The FTC issued a Second Request on February 5, 2026, extending the regulatory timeline and introducing genuine antitrust risk given the direct competitive overlap in cellular RF between the two companies. Both shareholder bases approved the deal in February 2026, and the outside date extends to October 2027.

The standalone business has undergone a genuine operational transformation: gross margins expanded from 36.3% (FY2023) to 45.9% (FY2026) through exiting mass-market Android, selling unprofitable assets, and narrowing focus. FY2026 FCF reached $680M on $3.68B revenue. However, organic revenue has been flat-to-declining for three years, Apple represents 50% of sales (and rising), and the CSG segment remains unprofitable.

The investment question is whether the merger arbitrage spread (5-6%) adequately compensates for the binary risk. With 9-12+ months of duration, potential deal failure would send the stock to $80-95, implying 15-25% downside from current levels. The asymmetry is unfavorable for new money at $108.

## Company Overview

Qorvo designs and manufactures RF, analog, and power management semiconductors across three segments. The Advanced Cellular Group (ACG, 69% of revenue) supplies RF modules and components to Apple (50% of total revenue) and Samsung (10%). The High Performance Analog segment (HPA, 19%) serves defense, infrastructure (DOCSIS 4.0, 5G base stations), and industrial markets. Connectivity and Sensors Group (CSG, 11%) addresses UWB, Wi-Fi, and IoT applications but remains loss-making.

The company employs approximately 5,200 people and holds ~2,700 patents, with proprietary GaAs, GaN, BAW, and SAW process technologies. It holds DoD Category 1A "Trusted Source" accreditation for its defense products.

## Financial Analysis

Gross margin expanded 960 bps over three years (36.3% FY2023 → 45.9% FY2026), driven by exiting low-margin mass-market Android, divesting non-core assets (SiC power, China assembly operations, MEMS sensing, North Carolina fab), and focusing on premium/flagship content. Q4 FY2026 reached 48.9% GAAP gross margin.

FY2026 operating cash flow of $809M and FCF of $680M (18.5% FCF margin) on declining capex ($129M vs. $200M+ historically). Cash on hand reached $1.22B against $1.55B in long-term debt ($850M due 2029, $700M due 2031), yielding near-net-debt-neutral status.

Current ratio of 3.24x, debt/equity of 0.74x, Altman Z-Score of 2.81 (grey zone but stable). Inventory reduced from $797M to $554M, reflecting discipline.

FY2026 revenue of $3.68B is essentially unchanged from FY2024 ($3.77B) and FY2023 ($3.57B). The margin improvement has come entirely from mix shift and cost reduction, not top-line growth.

Buybacks totaled $536.7M in FY2026 at ~$81 average — below current price, creating per-share accretion. Share count declined from 102M to ~92.6M over three years.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are muted and uncertain:

**Positives:**
- HPA segment growing 11% with 26.8% operating margins, benefiting from defense spending (phased array radar, electronic warfare, "Golden Dome") and DOCSIS 4.0 infrastructure upgrades
- 5G RF content per device is structurally increasing as higher frequencies, carrier aggregation, and MIMO architectures require more complex front-end modules
- UWB adoption in automotive (digital keys) provides a long-term driver for CSG

**Negatives:**
- Total company revenue declining 7% YoY in Q4 FY2026 and flat over three years
- ACG faces structural headwinds from Qualcomm/MediaTek integrating RF into their chipsets, potentially disintermediating standalone RF suppliers in mid-tier designs
- CSG remains unprofitable at -10% operating margins with declining revenue (-10.8% YoY)
- No forward guidance provided due to pending merger — investors are operating blind
- Estimate revisions show current-quarter EPS rising modestly (+10.3% over 90 days) but next-year estimates declining (-2.7% over 90 days)

The next-year estimate decline suggests the street views the current margin trajectory as peaking rather than accelerating.

## Valuation Assessment

At $108, QRVO trades at 29.4x trailing P/E on $3.62 EPS, and approximately 15.7x forward FY2027 earnings ($6.90 consensus). Skyworks trades at 34.3x — QRVO's multiple is cheaper, but Skyworks has a dividend yield (3.4%) and QRVO does not.

**Merger arbitrage framing dominates valuation:**
- Deal value at current SWKS price (~$85): 0.960 × $85 + $32.50 = ~$114
- Current QRVO price: $108
- Spread: ~5.6% ($6/share)
- Annualized (assuming 12-month close): ~5-6%

**Standalone valuation if deal breaks:**
- Forward P/E of 12-14x on $6.90 consensus = $83-97
- From $108, this implies 10-23% downside
- The floor is supported by $1.2B cash, $680M FCF generation, and demonstrated margin improvement

The risk/reward arithmetic: 6% upside if deal closes (with 9-12 months of duration and SWKS stock price risk) versus 15-25% downside if it fails. This is not a compelling asymmetry for new capital.

The stock's 40% appreciation from its May 2025 lows has largely priced in both the operational improvement and the deal premium. The median analyst target of $98 sits below the current price, with only the high target ($128) reflecting full deal completion plus upside.

## Competitive Landscape

Qorvo operates in a consolidating RF semiconductor market where scale and integration matter. The combined Skyworks-Qorvo entity would be the dominant non-Broadcom RF supplier, which is precisely what creates the antitrust concern.

**Competitive position is mixed:**
- Apple concentration (50%) provides revenue stability but extreme customer dependency
- Exiting mass-market Android conceded volume to Chinese competitors (Vanchip, Maxscend) permanently
- HPA's DoD Trusted Source accreditation creates a moat in defense, but this segment is only 19% of revenue
- Qualcomm and MediaTek's RF integration efforts represent a structural threat to the standalone ACG business model

The 5-year TSR data: QRVO returned 88.61 (indexed from 100, i.e., negative total return) while the peer group returned 495.89. This reflects years of sub-optimal portfolio decisions that management has only recently begun correcting.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Deal Break Risk (PRIMARY):** FTC Second Request on a merger combining the #2 and #3 cellular RF suppliers with substantial Apple overlap. Skyworks agreed to divest up to $100M in revenue but the FTC may demand more. Break probability is difficult to quantify but non-trivial — perhaps 25-35%. If the deal breaks, the $100M regulatory failure fee from Skyworks to Qorvo partially cushions but doesn't prevent a 15-25% stock decline.

**2. Apple Concentration:** 50% of revenue from a single customer that has historically in-sourced components (modem development). Any loss of content, program delay, or pricing pressure would be material. This risk exists in both deal-close and deal-break scenarios.

**3. Tariff/Trade Exposure:** China is 13% of revenue; broader Asia is 34%. U.S. tariff escalation has already impacted HPA customers ($9M revenue decline). Rare earth export restrictions could affect manufacturing.

**4. SWKS Stock Price Risk:** The deal consideration is majority stock (0.960 shares). At SWKS $85, deal value is $114. If SWKS falls to $75 (it was recently at $83 after a post-earnings selloff), deal value drops to $104.50 — below QRVO's current price. The deal has no collar or price floor.

**5. Duration/Opportunity Cost:** Even if the deal closes, 9-12+ months at 5-6% gross return with binary downside risk is an uncompetitive risk-adjusted return in a 4%+ risk-free rate environment.

### Options Market Signal

Options are pricing significant uncertainty: ATM IV of 54.6% vs. 33.8% historical volatility (91st percentile within the chain). The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 54.6% > 6-month 47.5%), consistent with binary event risk. The put/call volume ratio is extremely low (0.03) and OI ratio is essentially zero — this is highly unusual and likely reflects the merger arbitrage community's positioning via the stock rather than options. The -4.0% put/call skew (call-heavy) suggests some participants are positioned for deal completion or SWKS stock appreciation.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($114-128):** Deal closes on schedule. SWKS stock recovers to $90+ as combined entity synergies become visible. QRVO shareholders receive $114+ in consideration. Timeline: 9-15 months.

**Bear Case ($80-95):** FTC blocks the deal or demands remedies unacceptable to Skyworks. QRVO trades back to standalone valuation of 12-14x forward earnings. The standalone business is fundamentally improved but lacks a growth catalyst and faces Apple concentration risk. The stock settles in the low $80s-mid $90s.

**Base Case ($108-114):** Deal closes but SWKS stock remains range-bound at $83-87 due to its own growth challenges. QRVO shareholders receive $112-116 in mixed consideration over the next 12 months. Net return of 4-6% — barely compensating for duration and binary risk.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers to BUY:**
- FTC formally clears the merger (removes primary risk; remaining spread becomes risk-free return)
- QRVO stock drops to $90-95 (widens spread to 15-20%, creating genuine risk/reward asymmetry even with deal uncertainty)
- SWKS stock recovers to $95+ (increases implied deal value to $123+, widening spread materially)

**Downgrade triggers to AVOID:**
- FTC files a complaint to block the merger
- SWKS stock falls below $75 (implied deal value drops below current QRVO price — makes the entire spread negative)
- Evidence of Apple content loss in next product cycle

**Reassessment timeline:** FTC decision expected by Q4 2026 or Q1 2027. Monitor SWKS quarterly earnings (next: August 2026) for stock price trajectory. Any regulatory news flow from FTC should trigger immediate reassessment.

## Conclusion

Qorvo is a merger arbitrage play where the underlying business has genuinely improved but the stock at $108 offers limited risk-adjusted return. The 5-6% spread to deal close over 9-12+ months, with 15-25% downside if the FTC blocks the merger and meaningful SWKS stock price exposure, does not clear the hurdle for new capital deployment. The 16 analyst HOLD ratings (vs. 4 buys and 1 sell) confirm the market views this as a holding pattern. The operational turnaround (gross margins from 36% to 49%) provides legitimate downside support if the deal breaks, preventing this from being an AVOID. However, without the deal closing or the stock pulling back to create a wider spread, there is no compelling reason to initiate a position here.

**Recommendation: HOLD** — classified as "uncertain" given the binary deal outcome that dominates all fundamental considerations.
