## Executive Summary

Cytokinetics achieved FDA approval of aficamten (MYQORZO) on December 19, 2025 for symptomatic obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, U.S. commercial availability from January 27, 2026, European Commission approval in February 2026, and partner-led approval in China. The first nine weeks of selling produced $4.789M of net product revenue. The launch's flow metrics are strong: more than 425 unique prescribing HCPs and roughly 1,100 patients prescribed through April, over 1,400 REMS-certified prescribers, more than 70% of dispensed patients on paid scripts with sub-two-week bridge conversion, and over 40% of Q1 prescriptions originating from low-volume or first-time HCM prescribers. Against an entrenched incumbent, that breadth this early is the right shape of evidence.

The problem is the price against the arithmetic. At $78.61 the reported-share equity value is $9.78B; adding $911.6M of convertible principal, $275M of drawn Royalty Pharma term loan and $539.1M of revenue-participation carrying value, less $1,073.3M of cash and investments, puts enterprise value near $10.65B (approximate — there is possible overlap between the disclosed $138.8M of RPI liabilities at fair value and the revenue-participation carrying values). Bristol Myers' Camzyos, four years into its launch, reported $416M in Q2 2026, a ~$1.66B annualized run rate. Cytokinetics is being capitalized at roughly six times the incumbent's annual revenue while booking 1.2% of the incumbent's quarterly volume. That gap is bridgeable over years if the claimed >30% new-to-brand exit share is accurate and durable — new-patient flow share converts to installed-base share slowly — but the market has already paid for the conversion working out, plus a non-obstructive HCM label whose effect size is the weakest part of the story.

Three things keep this a HOLD rather than a BUY. First, financing: $145.5M of Q1 operating cash use against $1,073.3M of liquidity, with FY2026 opex guidance of $830–870M implying ~$216.5M per quarter for the remaining nine months, roughly 8% above the Q1 run rate. The Royalty Pharma Tranche 7 ($175M, repayable at 190% of principal) expires roughly one year after approval, and the February 2025 Jefferies ATM has never been used. An equity raise in the next two to three quarters is a live possibility that management has effectively pre-announced by framing Tranche 7 as the default "unless we are able to meet our financing requirements through more favorable funding sources." Second, the ACACIA-HCM effect size (KCCQ-CSS least-squares mean difference of 3.0 points, peak VO2 difference of 0.67 mL/kg/min) sits below conventionally cited clinical-importance thresholds, and the Q1 10-Q added a risk factor conceding that regulators' subjective reading of "minimally important clinical difference" governs the nHCM pathway. Third, the newly issued MyoKardia/BMS patent claiming beta-blocker discontinuation plus myosin inhibitor administration overlaps precisely with the MAPLE-HCM monotherapy positioning that carries a November 14, 2026 PDUFA date.

## Company Overview

Cytokinetics is a South San Francisco specialty biopharmaceutical company organized around one scientific franchise, small-molecule modulation of muscle contractility, with 673 employees at year-end 2025 and a single reportable segment.

The commercial asset is aficamten, a cardiac myosin inhibitor sold as MYQORZO, retained by Cytokinetics in the U.S. and Europe. Japan is licensed to Bayer (€50M upfront received in 2024 and recognized in 2025; €10M clinical milestone in 2025; €10M earned in Q1 2026 on first U.S. commercial sale; up to €70M of further first-sale milestones, up to €490M of commercial milestones, and tiered royalties described as high-teens to low-thirty percents). China, Taiwan and Hong Kong are licensed to Sanofi (up to $160M in milestones, $25M earned to date; tiered royalties in the low-to-high teens).

The pipeline behind it: omecamtiv mecarbil in the ~1,800-patient Phase 3 COMET-HF trial in heart failure with severely reduced ejection fraction, enrolling through 2026; ulacamten in Phase 2 AMBER-HFpEF; CEDAR-HCM in pediatric and adolescent oHCM with adolescent cohort enrollment targeted for Q4 2026; and early-stage CK-089. Management frames the U.S. and European oHCM opportunity as potentially exceeding $5 billion by 2035 with a belief that MYQORZO can take greater than 50% of cardiac myosin inhibitor share. These are company estimates supported by internal market research, not independently verified.

Commercial differentiation as management describes it is regulatory rather than molecular: a more flexible echocardiogram monitoring window (two to eight weeks, adaptable) and a REMS that does not require drug–drug-interaction counseling. That distinction matters for prescriber friction, and it can narrow if the incumbent's REMS requirements are relaxed as real-world safety data accumulate.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue composition requires care.** FY2024 revenue was $18.5M and FY2025 was $88.0M, both entirely collaboration, license and milestone income — no product revenue existed before Q1 2026. The FY2025 step-up was driven principally by recognition of Bayer's €50M upfront ($52.4M) and Sanofi milestones. Q1 2026 total revenue of $19.355M breaks into $4.789M of product sales, $2.637M of collaboration revenue and $11.929M of license/milestone income triggered by the first U.S. commercial sale. The one-time milestone is 2.5 times the product revenue. Any model anchored on total revenue misstates the launch's current scale by a wide margin.

**Cost structure has fully arrived.** Q1 2026 R&D was $95.525M (down 2.8% year over year) while SG&A rose 82.8% to $104.896M, taking combined operating expense to $200.4M from $155.6M. Net loss was $206.0M, or $(1.67) per share on 123,273K weighted-average shares (the arithmetic reconciles: $206.0M ÷ 123,273K = $1.671). Stock-based compensation was $30.4M.

**Gross margin is an artifact, not a signal.** Cost of goods of $0.160M against $4.789M of product revenue implies a ~96.7% margin. Pre-approval inventory was expensed to R&D as incurred, so cost of sales will stay artificially depressed until zero-basis inventory is consumed; total balance-sheet inventory is only $3.997M. Steady-state gross margin will be materially lower.

**Losses have escalated every year for five years:** $(215.3)M in 2021, $(389.0)M in 2022, $(526.2)M in 2023, $(589.5)M in 2024 and $(785.0)M in 2025. The FY2025 figure includes a $121.2M debt-conversion expense from the September 2025 note exchange; excluding it, the loss was approximately $663.8M, still ~13% above FY2024. The escalation is not explained away by the one-time charge.

**Liquidity and burn.** Cash, equivalents and investments fell from $1,217.3M at year-end 2025 to $1,073.3M at March 31, 2026. Operating cash use was $145.5M in Q1 (versus $131.6M in Q1 2025). Working capital was $640.3M. FY2026 guidance, reaffirmed on the Q1 call, is $830–870M of combined R&D and SG&A including $120–130M of stock compensation. Q1's $200.4M annualizes to $801.7M, below the low end of guidance; hitting the $850M midpoint requires $649.6M across the remaining nine months, or roughly $216.5M per quarter — about 8% above the Q1 pace, driven by the German launch build, five additional HTA dossiers and nHCM regulatory work.

Naive runway is $1,073.3M ÷ $145.5M ≈ 7.4 quarters. That figure is too generous: guidance implies opex rises, working capital builds during a ramp, and roughly $20.2M of term-loan principal and interest is payable in 2026. It is also arguably too conservative on the revenue side. A normalized case with H2 opex near $216M per quarter and combined product plus partner revenue offsetting $25–40M per quarter puts net burn at roughly $160–190M per quarter and runway at 5.6–6.7 quarters, landing in mid-to-late 2027. Both are sensitivities on disclosed inputs.

**The capital structure is the least appreciated part of the story.** Stockholders' deficit widened from $(659.6)M at year-end to $(826.6)M at March 31, 2026 against a $3,692.8M accumulated deficit. Convertible principal totals $911.6M — $21.1M of 4.00% 2026 Notes (currently convertible, classified current), $140.5M of 3.50% 2027 Notes and $750.0M of 1.75% 2031 Notes — with aggregate fair value of $1,295.2M. Dividing principal by the disclosed antidilutive share counts implies conversion prices near $10.53, $51.07 and $68.42 respectively; all three series are in the money at $78.61, and the 2026 Notes' fair value of $132.7M on $21.1M of principal (roughly 6.3x par) confirms these now trade as equity substitutes.

The Royalty Pharma package carries embedded costs that function like high-yield debt:

- **Multi-tranche term loan:** $275M drawn, repayable at 190% of principal per tranche, implying up to ~$522.5M. Tranche 7 of $175M remains available and expires roughly one year after FDA approval (approximately December 2026). Drawing it takes total draws to $450M and implied repayment to ~$855M.
- **Aficamten revenue participation:** 4.5% of worldwide net sales up to $5.0B cumulative, then 1%. Carrying value $339.6M. At $2B of annual worldwide net sales, the 4.5% tier costs ~$90M per year and ~$225M cumulatively before the tier steps down.
- **Omecamtiv mecarbil loan (2024):** $100M drawn, with all three disclosed repayment scenarios requiring payment well above principal. A COMET-HF failure by June 30, 2028 triggers 22 quarterly payments totaling 227.5% of principal — roughly $227.5M owed on a failed trial. The heart-failure program's downside is a write-off plus a fixed liability.

One footnote movement warrants flagging: the imputed interest rate on the aficamten revenue-participation liability rose from 23.5% at year-end to 26.0% at March 31, 2026. Under effective-interest accounting, a higher rate against a given carrying value is arithmetically consistent with a raised internal forecast of future royalty payments. It is equally consistent with a change in expected payment timing or a technical remeasurement on transition to commercial status. Worth confirming in the Q2 10-Q; not established.

**Dilution, stated both ways.** Convertible notes underlie 15,716K shares — 12.6% of the 124,432,565 shares outstanding, equivalent to 11.2% ownership dilution post-issuance. All 30,639K antidilutive instruments (options 11,783K, RSUs/PSUs 3,085K, ESPP 55K, notes 15,716K) equal 24.6% of shares outstanding and 19.8% ownership dilution. These are maximum contingent figures: the option pool includes out-of-the-money grants, PSUs carry unmet conditions, and the 2027 Notes were not convertible as of March 31, 2026.

**A note on the quoted market cap.** The $10.7B figure in market data does not reconcile with reported shares: 124,432,565 × $78.61 = $9.78B. Adding the 10,962K shares underlying the 2031 Notes gives 135,394,565 shares, which at $78.61 equals $10.64B — close enough to suggest the provider embeds conversion of the in-the-money 2031 series. The difference is roughly 9% and matters for any per-share calculation.

The Altman Z-Score of −0.75 is dominated by the $3.69B accumulated deficit, deeply negative EBIT and negligible sales relative to assets. The model was calibrated on profitable manufacturers, so its discriminating power here is limited. It is nonetheless flagging three true facts: the company cannot self-fund, equity is negative and leverage is high. The correct solvency question is refinancing and dilution risk on a 2027–2028 horizon, not near-term insolvency.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case is the strongest dimension and rests on four legs.

**Launch execution in oHCM.** Prescriber breadth is the metric that matters most this early: 275 unique prescribers in Q1 rising to 425+ through April, more than half from the high-volume HCM segment, with 2.4 prescriptions per prescriber (2.6 among high-volume writers). Patient count went from ~680 at quarter-end to ~1,100 through April. More than 1,400 HCPs are REMS-certified, a leading indicator of future writing capacity. Q1 detailing reached only ~40% of the low-volume/first-time segment, meaning the sales force has not yet covered the cohort already generating a large share of scripts. Payer access is progressing: comparable access for ~90% of Medicare lives with Medicare parity expected in Q2, 50% of commercial lives by early Q3, commercial parity targeted by end of 2026.

**Geographic expansion.** German launch in Q2 2026 with the full commercial team already hired, six EU HTA dossiers submitted and five more planned, a Swissmedic filing, a Health Canada decision expected in H2 2026, and partner-led progress in Japan (Bayer), China, Hong Kong and Taiwan (Sanofi).

**Label expansion.** The MAPLE-HCM sNDA carries a November 14, 2026 PDUFA date and, if approved, supports monotherapy positioning against metoprolol — the message management says would accelerate community-cardiologist adoption. ACACIA-HCM met both dual primary endpoints in nHCM on May 5, 2026, opening a population with no approved therapy. CEDAR-HCM addresses pediatric and adolescent oHCM.

**Category validation.** Camzyos establishes that cardiac myosin inhibition is a multi-billion-dollar class. That is the ceiling argument and simultaneously the timing caution: it took the incumbent roughly four years to build that base.

Estimate revisions are positive but modest in magnitude — next-year consensus loss narrowed from $(4.97) to $(4.69) over 90 days (+5.8%), current year from $(6.58) to $(6.31) (+4.1%), with slight 30-day slippage on the out-year. This is directionally supportive of the launch tracking to plan; it does not change the valuation math because there are no earnings to capitalize.

The earnings surprise record is mixed and not very informative for a company at this stage: two beats and two misses in four quarters, with the Q3 2025 figure distorted by the $121.2M debt-conversion charge.

## Valuation Assessment

There is no meaningful P/E. Trailing and forward EPS are both negative ($(6.54) trailing), so the peer P/E median of 39.8x is not a usable benchmark; the closest analogues in the peer set are similarly loss-making (SMMT at −8.2x, MDGL at −34.7x), which tells us only that the cohort is valued on pipeline and launch expectations rather than earnings.

The relevant framing is enterprise value against realistic peak sales, net of the Royalty Pharma skim and prospective dilution.

Against ~$10.65B of enterprise value:

- Camzyos annualizes to roughly $1.66B on Q2 2026, so CYTK trades at about 6.4x the incumbent's current run rate while generating 1.2% of the incumbent's quarterly product revenue.
- If MYQORZO reaches $2.0–2.5B of CYTK-booked annual net sales in the U.S. and Europe by the early 2030s, the current EV represents roughly 4–5x that unadjusted peak, or higher once probability weighting, the 4.5% RPI participation and 11–20% prospective dilution are applied.
- Partner royalties from Japan and Greater China add value but on a tiered high-teens-to-low-thirties (Bayer) and low-to-high-teens (Sanofi) basis on partner-booked sales, not on CYTK revenue.

Two adjustments push against the price. The RPI aficamten participation takes 4.5% of worldwide net sales off the top until $5.0B cumulative — and the filings leave unresolved whether that 4.5% applies to partner-booked sales in Bayer and Sanofi territories, which would make the economics meaningfully worse than a simple deduction from CYTK's own revenue. Second, the financing clock implies either Tranche 7 at 190% repayment or equity from an untouched ATM, most likely within two to three quarters.

Analyst targets are uniformly above spot: low $83 (+5.6%), median $105, mean $107.8 (+37.1%), high $146. Twenty of twenty-two ratings are Buy or Strong Buy with sentiment stable. That distribution is corroborative but not independent — sell-side targets on a launch biotech are built from the same peak-sales models that support the current price, and the lowest target sitting only 5.6% above spot indicates the bearish end of the analyst range sees the stock as roughly fairly valued.

My base case is $92 (+17.0%), reflecting the oHCM franchise ramping through 2027 with Medicare and commercial parity achieved, MAPLE approved, and partial credit for nHCM. That sits between the lowest analyst target and the median, and below the consensus mean, because I discount for the RPI participation and near-certain dilution. Bull case $130 (+65.4%) requires ACACIA data at ESC that reframes the effect size favorably, an nHCM label without a restrictive LVEF-monitoring burden, MAPLE approval unencumbered by the MyoKardia patent, and share conversion from flow to installed base tracking above 30%. Bear case $52 (−33.8%) follows from an nHCM regulatory setback, MAPLE promotional constraints, slower stock-share conversion, and an equity raise executed into weakness.

That distribution — 17% to base, 65% to bull, 34% to bear — is positive in expectation but not asymmetric enough to justify new capital at $78.61. A valuation score of 5.0 reflects a price that is defensible on a successful path and offers no margin of safety on a contested one.

## Competitive Landscape

Cytokinetics is the challenger, and its trajectory is improving. The claimed >30% new-to-brand exit share, prescriber breadth into the low-volume segment, and sub-two-week bridge-to-paid conversion all describe a product taking new-patient flow from an incumbent. That is the right pattern; the caveat management itself supplied is that the exit-share figure derives from internal analysis of projected syndicated data rather than audited script data.

Three competitive developments cut the other way.

MYQORZO's advantages — flexible echo monitoring, no drug–drug-interaction counseling in the REMS — are features of the approval, not the molecule. If BMS secures REMS relief for Camzyos as real-world safety accumulates, a meaningful portion of the pitch erodes. The current status of the Camzyos REMS is not verified here and is a specific, checkable item material to the >50% share thesis.

A competitor patent lands on the next label expansion. The Q1 10-Q disclosed an Issue Notification for U.S. Patent 12,616,697 to MyoKardia (BMS), expected to issue May 5, 2026, claiming a method of treating HCM or HFpEF patients on beta-blocker therapy comprising discontinuation or reduction of the beta blocker plus administration of a myosin inhibitor, including MYQORZO. MAPLE-HCM is a monotherapy-versus-metoprolol study with a November 14, 2026 PDUFA date, and management identified MAPLE data as the lever for expanding into community cardiology. Method-of-treatment patents are difficult to enforce against a manufacturer absent an induced-infringement theory tied to labeling and promotion, validity is untested, and no suit has been filed in the filings reviewed. The overlap is specific and lands at the moment of label expansion.

The third entrant has been funded. Servier agreed on June 1, 2026 to acquire Edgewise Therapeutics' muscular dystrophy business for $1.55B upfront and up to ~$2.6B in total. Edgewise retains EDG-7500, its cardiac sarcomere modulator, which reported positive Phase 2 CIRRUS-HCM topline in both obstructive and non-obstructive HCM on June 16, 2026 — though shares fell roughly 10% because efficacy fell short of investor expectations. The funding constraint that would normally throttle a small third entrant has been removed. Commercial competition is unlikely before roughly 2030, but the duopoly window is finite, and it matters most for the long-tail nHCM opportunity partly reflected in today's price.

Distribution concentration must be described precisely. Three specialty pharmacies accounted for 45%, 31% and 24% of Q1 gross product revenue — 100% combined. These are customers of record under a limited-distribution model, not end-demand concentration; demand originates from 425+ independent prescribers. The genuine exposure is operational: a disruption at any one of three nodes interrupts revenue recognition immediately.

## Risk Assessment

**nHCM effect size and label risk (highest).** ACACIA-HCM hit both endpoints, but the KCCQ-CSS least-squares mean difference was 3.0 points (placebo itself improved 8.4) and peak VO2 improved 0.67 mL/kg/min. Conventionally cited importance thresholds run near 5 points for KCCQ-CSS and 1.0–1.5 mL/kg/min for peak VO2; both endpoints fall below. The Q1 10-Q added a risk factor stating that regulators' interpretation of "minimally important clinical difference" is subjective, that no universal standard exists, and that FDA or EMA may find the benefit insufficient. Management declined all analyst questions on the results call. The counter-argument is legitimate: nHCM has no approved therapy, and consistency across a patient-reported measure, an objective functional measure, physician-assessed NYHA class and NT-proBNP supports a real effect even at modest magnitude.

**nHCM safety.** LVEF below 50% occurred in 27 patients (10%) on aficamten versus 1% on placebo, with two serious heart-failure adverse events associated with reduced LVEF and 3% interrupting treatment for LVEF below 40%. Mechanistically plausible — without an outflow gradient to relieve, reducing contractility offers less offsetting benefit — and directly relevant to labeling, REMS scope and prescriber willingness.

**European exclusivity is unresolved.** Generics [UK] Limited (Viatris/Mylan) opposed EP 3740481, the aficamten composition-of-matter patent expiring 2039, with an oral hearing scheduled for June 2026. The outcome was not located. Additional oppositions target EP 3999180 (polymorphs, 2040) and EP 4370116 (compound for use, 2042). U.S. protection looks firmer: patent 10,836,755 to 2039 with a term-extension application filed February 4, 2026, plus 12,370,179 to 2042.

**Supply concentration.** Single-source contract manufacturers across raw materials, API, finished product and packaging, with key registered starting materials sourced from China and a dual-sourcing program expected to take several years. Non-cancellable materials commitments were $17.2M. In the current tariff and supply-security environment this is concentrated risk with no near-term remedy beneath a global launch.

**Litigation.** *In re Cytokinetics Securities Litigation* (N.D. Cal.) alleges Section 10(b) and 20(a) violations over aficamten NDA timeline statements for a December 27, 2023 to May 6, 2025 class period; an amended complaint filed March 10, 2026 added the CFO. A derivative action, *Pohlmann v. Blum*, was stayed April 16, 2026 pending the motion to dismiss. At this market capitalization such matters are typically resolved within D&O coverage, but the outcome is unresolved.

**Financing with a hard clock.** Tranche 7's $175M availability expires roughly December 2026 at 190% repayment; the alternative is the untouched ATM. Either path is costly.

**COMET-HF asymmetry.** A failed trial by June 30, 2028 still obligates approximately $227.5M under the RP OM Loan. The downside is a write-off plus a fixed liability.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility is 49.2% against 56.2% one-year realized, a −7.0-point spread placing IV in the 6th percentile of the current chain. That is unusually cheap given a known calendar: Q2 results are imminent (the Q2 10-Q was not on file as of this analysis), and three late-breaking ACACIA-HCM abstracts including a Hot Line primary-results presentation are scheduled for ESC Congress 2026 in the customary late-August window. IV has compressed from 59.6% to 49.2% since July 19 while the price moved only −1.7%, so the market has grown more complacent into a denser event calendar. The term structure is in contango (49.2% near, 59.9% at the September 18 tenor pricing a 16.2% move, 54.2% at six months), which locates the event risk in the September expiry rather than August. Put/call volume of 0.12 and open-interest ratio of 0.33 are call-tilted; the data source characterizes the near-term skew of −2.4% as balanced, and I make no directional inference from the sign. Separately, short interest of 19.4% of float with 6.6 days to cover is genuinely two-sided: favorable ESC data creates squeeze fuel, an unfavorable reading validates a large positioned short base. For a holder, the cheap near-dated volatility argues for buying protection rather than trimming into the readout.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $130 (+65.4%)

ESC Congress responder analyses reframe ACACIA-HCM: the proportion of patients achieving clinically meaningful KCCQ improvement, combined with consistency across four endpoint types, satisfies FDA that the effect is real despite a modest mean difference, and an nHCM label arrives in 2027 without a prohibitive LVEF-monitoring burden. MAPLE-HCM is approved on November 14, 2026 and the MyoKardia patent proves unenforceable against promotional use, unlocking community cardiology where over 40% of current scripts already originate despite only ~40% detailing coverage. Commercial parity by end-2026 converts the >30% new-to-brand flow share into installed-base share through 2027–2028, and CYTK-booked U.S. plus European sales approach $2.5B by the early 2030s with Japan and China royalties on top. The company funds the gap through a single equity raise at or above current levels rather than drawing Tranche 7, avoiding ~$333M of incremental repayment obligation. COMET-HF success in 2027–2028 adds a second franchise the current price barely credits.

### Bear Case — $52 (−33.8%)

FDA finds the ACACIA effect magnitude insufficient or demands a restrictive label with mandatory serial echocardiography that mirrors the 10% LVEF-reduction signal, removing the nHCM leg of the valuation. The MyoKardia patent constrains MAPLE-based promotion, or BMS asserts it and forces a settlement that limits the monotherapy message. Camzyos REMS relief narrows the differentiation that underpins the >50% share claim. Flow share proves less durable than the internal projection implied, and installed-base conversion runs slower than models assume, with product revenue reaching only $250–400M in 2027. H2 opex accelerates to the guided $216M per quarter, burn stays near $170M net, and the company raises equity in a weak tape — 10–15% dilution at a lower price — or draws Tranche 7 and adds ~$333M of implied repayment. The EPO opposition invalidates or narrows EP 3740481, threatening European exclusivity from 2039 backward through generic-preparation dynamics. Multiple compression on a launch biotech with negative equity and 19.4% short interest does the rest.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** The 12-month growth thesis is real and catalyst-dense, but the risk/reward at $78.61 does not support new capital.

**Upgrade triggers to BUY:**
1. ESC Congress ACACIA-HCM presentation delivers responder analyses showing a clinically defensible proportion of patients above KCCQ MCID thresholds, *and* FDA accepts an nHCM sNDA without a restrictive-monitoring signal in the review timeline.
2. Q2 2026 product revenue above roughly $22–25M (a sequential ramp from $4.789M across a full quarter with Medicare parity), with new-to-brand share confirmed at or above 30% using third-party script data rather than internal projections.
3. Financing resolved on favorable terms — an equity raise completed above $80 or the Tranche 7 window allowed to lapse in favor of cheaper capital — which would remove the single largest overhang.
4. A price near $60–62 on unchanged fundamentals, where the base case offers roughly 50% upside against the bear case.

**Downgrade triggers to AVOID:**
1. An FDA Complete Response Letter or refusal-to-file on the nHCM submission, or a label requiring monthly echocardiography.
2. MAPLE-HCM PDUFA on November 14, 2026 producing a CRL, or BMS filing suit on Patent 12,616,697 in a way that constrains promotional claims.
3. Q3 or Q4 product revenue growth decelerating sequentially, or the FY2026 opex guide raised above $900M without a corresponding revenue offset.
4. An equity raise executed below $65, or Tranche 7 drawn in full (implying total term-loan repayment near $855M and signalling that equity markets were unavailable at acceptable terms).
5. Confirmation that the EPO revoked or materially narrowed EP 3740481.

**Reassessment timeline:** immediate on the Q2 2026 10-Q and earnings call (which should disclose the first full quarter of MYQORZO revenue, initial German contribution, the RPI imputed-rate movement, and any guidance revision for incremental nHCM spend); again at ESC Congress 2026 in late August; and decisively at the November 14, 2026 MAPLE PDUFA date.

## Conclusion

The score trajectory here has been declining — 5.5 in April, 6.4 in May on the ACACIA readout, 6.0 in June, 5.7 in July — and this review takes it to 5.3. Price has moved only −1.7% since the prior look and remains 114.9% above the 52-week low. The downgrade reflects three specific developments in the intervening period, all pointing the same way. The MyoKardia patent lands directly on the MAPLE monotherapy positioning with a November PDUFA date. Servier's $1.55B upfront payment to Edgewise removed the funding constraint on the third HCM entrant. And Camzyos at $416M in Q2 2026 quantified the revenue gap: $4.789M versus a $1.66B annualized incumbent, against an enterprise value of roughly $10.65B.

The bull argument is sound in structure. Flow metrics lead stock metrics in specialty launches, and CYTK's flow metrics are good — prescriber breadth into the low-volume segment while only 40% of that segment has been detailed, sub-two-week bridge conversion, over 1,400 REMS certifications. If the >30% new-to-brand share is durable, revenue convergence follows over years. The problem is that the price already pays for it, and the financing structure taxes the outcome: 4.5% of worldwide net sales to Royalty Pharma until $5.0B cumulative, a 190%-repayment tranche whose window closes around December 2026, an untouched ATM, and 11–20% ownership dilution from contingent instruments before any new issuance.
