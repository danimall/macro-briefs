## Executive Summary

EQT Corporation is the only large-scale integrated natural gas producer in the United States, holding 28 Tcfe of proved reserves across the Appalachian Basin and operating a vertically integrated model (upstream + midstream) that delivers industry-leading cost structure at $0.09/Mcfe LOE. The company generated approximately $2.95B in non-GAAP free cash flow in FY2025, beat earnings estimates in all four reported quarters, and trades at a valuation discount to natural gas peers.

The investment case rests on three legs: (1) structural demand growth for US natural gas via LNG exports (4.5 MTPA committed) and data center power requirements in EQT's backyard, (2) the ongoing Equitrans integration creating permanent cost savings and pipeline optionality (MVP Southgate, MVP Boost), and (3) a balance sheet deleveraging trajectory targeting $5.0B total debt, with Q1 2026 already achieving $6.0B. Against these, near-term estimate revisions for the current and next quarter have turned negative (-20% and -25% over 90 days respectively), the stock carries binary commodity price exposure with 93% natural gas concentration, and valuation metrics require careful sourcing given mixed market data.

At $58.72, the stock offers meaningful upside to analyst consensus targets, with downside supported by investment-grade credit, rapid deleveraging, and substantial proved reserve backing (though reserves do not constitute a hard valuation floor). The risk/reward profile favors ownership at current levels, though the magnitude of near-term estimate cuts warrants close monitoring of Q2 guidance.

## Company Overview

EQT operates three segments following its July 2024 merger with Equitrans Midstream:

- **Upstream**: Development and production of natural gas from ~4,264 productive wells, primarily in the Marcellus Shale (93% of proved reserves). FY2025 production: 2,382 Bcfe at an average realized price of $3.19/Mcfe.
- **Gathering**: 1,995 miles of pipeline infrastructure plus 0.2 Bcf/day processing. FY2025 operating income: $836M.
- **Transmission**: 950 miles of transmission pipeline plus 44 Bcf storage capacity including ownership in the Mountain Valley Pipeline. FY2025 operating income: $375M.

The company holds approximately 2.3 million gross acres and estimates 30+ years of drilling inventory at current pace (~4,000 gross undeveloped locations). This acreage position, combined with integrated infrastructure, creates a structural advantage in delivering molecules at lower all-in cost than pure-play competitors who pay third-party gathering fees.

## Growth Analysis

**Demand Catalysts:**

1. **LNG Exports**: EQT has committed to 4.5 MTPA of long-term offtake agreements plus 2.0 MTPA of tolling capacity (expected 2030-2031). At full realization, 6.5 MTPA represents approximately 0.85–1.0 Bcf/day of feedgas demand at international pricing — a significant premium to domestic Henry Hub. Some facilities have not reached FID, creating execution risk but also optionality.

2. **Data Center Power**: EQT is geographically positioned in Appalachia where significant data center construction is underway. Management has publicly stated that natural gas underpins AI growth. The company's integrated pipeline network enables direct service to power generation facilities.

3. **MVP Pipeline Infrastructure**: The Mountain Valley Pipeline reached in-service in June 2024, providing 2.0 Bcf/day capacity to Southeast markets. MVP Southgate ($370-430M) and MVP Boost ($400-540M) pending FERC approval would add another 1.15 Bcf/day by mid-2028.

4. **Production Growth**: 2025 volumes of 2,382 Bcfe grew 7% year-over-year as curtailments declined from 107 Bcfe to 14 Bcfe. The combination of Olympus Energy assets and reduced curtailments provides a volume tailwind without requiring aggressive capital spending growth.

**Estimate Trajectory — Mixed Signal:**

Full-year 2026 estimates have risen (+16.6% over 90 days to $4.82) on the strength of the Q1 beat. However, current quarter and next quarter estimates are declining (-20.3% and -24.9% over 90 days), reflecting lower near-term natural gas strip prices. This creates a divergence: the annual number is improving on the back of a strong Q1 already reported, while the forward quarters are deteriorating on commodity price weakness.

## Competitive Landscape

EQT holds a structural advantage as the only large-scale vertically integrated natural gas producer in the US. While Expand Energy has claimed the title of largest independent natural gas producer by net daily production (based on its 2025 10-K), EQT's integrated midstream ownership differentiates its business model. Key competitive dimensions:

1. **Cost Structure**: $0.09/Mcfe LOE is among the lowest in the industry, enabled by Appalachian geology, operational scale, and combo-development techniques. Operating costs in Q1 2026 were $1.09/Mcfe (excluding depletion and capital costs).

2. **Integrated Infrastructure**: The elimination of ~$1.25B in intercompany gathering/transmission fees (previously paid to Equitrans) creates a permanent cost advantage versus competitors who pay market-rate midstream fees.

3. **Scale & Reserves**: 28 Tcfe of proved reserves and 30+ years of inventory provide visibility that smaller producers cannot match.

4. **Pipeline Access**: MVP Mainline (2.0 Bcf/day to Southeast) plus Equitrans transmission network provides market access that partially insulates from Appalachian basis differentials.

5. **Trajectory**: The competitive position is improving. Each MVP expansion (Southgate, Boost) widens the market access advantage. LNG commitments lock in premium demand. Data center proximity creates a locational advantage that cannot be replicated by Gulf Coast producers.

The primary competitive vulnerability is the 93% natural gas concentration. In a sustained nat gas bear market (sub-$2.50 Henry Hub), EQT's earnings decline faster than diversified peers. However, the low cash operating cost structure provides resilience at depressed prices, as evidenced by the company's ability to remain profitable in FY2024 when NYMEX averaged $2.30. Management has indicated breakeven economics in the $1.50–1.75/Mcfe range on a cash-cost basis, though this excludes depletion ($0.92/Mcfe in Q1 2026) and maintenance capital requirements ($2.07–2.21B guided for FY2026).

## Risk Assessment

**1. Natural Gas Price Volatility (High)**
With 93% natural gas reserves and relatively light hedging (collar strategy rather than fixed-price swaps), earnings are highly sensitive to Henry Hub movements. A $0.50/MMBtu move in average realized price translates to approximately $1.2B in annual revenue and $1.5-2.0/share in EPS. The current negative estimate revision trend for Q2-Q3 2026 reflects this sensitivity.

**2. Securities Class Action — Settled (Medium)**
EQT recognized approximately $134M in net litigation expense in FY2025 related to a securities class action. The company's 10-K discloses that a $167.5M settlement was agreed at mediation in May 2025 and paid in full during Q3 2025, with approximately $16M recovered from insurance. This matter is resolved and does not represent an ongoing contingent liability, though the cash impact was material in FY2025.

**3. LNG Execution Risk (Medium)**
Some committed LNG projects have not reached FID. Delays or cancellations would leave EQT with excess pipeline capacity and stranded takeaway commitments. Successful execution would provide long-term demand visibility.

**4. Midstream JV Obligation (Medium)**
The $3.41B remaining Base Return obligation to Blackstone creates a senior claim on midstream cash flows. In a downturn scenario, this obligation persists regardless of commodity prices, potentially constraining capital allocation flexibility.

**5. Geographic Concentration (Low-Medium)**
All operations in a single basin creates exposure to regional disruptions (regulatory, weather, infrastructure). The MVP project history demonstrates how permitting challenges can impose multi-year delays and billions in cost overruns.

## Conclusion

EQT offers an asymmetric risk/reward profile at current levels. The stock trades at a P/E discount to peers while delivering industry-leading cost structure, 4 consecutive earnings beats, and multiple structural demand catalysts (LNG, data centers, pipeline expansion) that peers lack. The approximately 7–8% FCF yield provides capital return support, and investment-grade credit with rapidly declining leverage limits downside.

The primary risk is near-term natural gas price weakness, reflected in negative Q2-Q3 estimate revisions. However, the full-year estimate trajectory remains positive, the low cash cost structure supports profitability at depressed nat gas prices, and the options market's elevated pricing creates an implied binary outcome that may resolve favorably. The majority-Buy analyst consensus and mean target of approximately $68 (16% upside) align with the fundamental case.

Maintain BUY with a 12-month base case of $68 and awareness that commodity volatility will create interim drawdowns. Position sizing should reflect the natural gas concentration risk — this is a 2-3% portfolio position, not a core overweight.
