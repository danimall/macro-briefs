**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Ormat Technologies trades at $138.14, up 93% from its 52-week low of $71.63, driven by the company's emergence as a primary beneficiary of data center baseload power demand. The Google 150MW PPA announced in February 2026 and a series of storage project completions have repositioned the investment thesis from niche geothermal utility to AI infrastructure power provider. Q1 2026 delivered record revenue of $404M (+76% YoY) and a 40% EPS beat ($1.30 vs. $0.93 estimate).

The fundamental challenge is valuation: ORA trades at 67x trailing earnings and approximately 62x FY2026 consensus ($2.22), roughly 3x the utility peer median P/E of 21.5x. Near-term EPS estimates have declined 35-44% over the past 90 days for the current and next quarters, and insiders — including the CFO and multiple directors — sold substantially all of their holdings in May 2026 at prices below current levels. Free cash flow remains deeply negative (-$285M in FY2025) as the company invests $675M annually.

The growth story is real: geothermal baseload power is genuinely differentiated for data center demand, the company's 2028 capacity targets imply 25-30% capacity growth, and energy storage is scaling rapidly. But at current prices, the thesis requires near-flawless execution and no valuation compression — a high bar given negative earnings momentum and a rising rate environment that structurally challenges high-multiple utility equities.

## Company Overview

Ormat Technologies is the world's only vertically integrated geothermal energy company, headquartered in Reno, Nevada with manufacturing in Israel. The business operates across three segments:

- **Electricity (70% of FY2025 revenue):** 35 geothermal, solar PV, and recovered energy plants with 1,340MW installed capacity, achieving 84% capacity factors under long-term PPAs averaging ~14 years remaining term.
- **Product (22% of revenue):** Proprietary Ormat Energy Converter (OEC) manufacturing and EPC services, with $352M backlog concentrated in New Zealand (68%).
- **Energy Storage (8% of revenue):** 19 battery storage facilities totaling 415MW/1,038MWh across U.S. wholesale markets, the fastest-growing segment at +109% YoY in 2025.

The company holds 182 active patents on organic Rankine cycle technology and ~434,000 acres of U.S. geothermal leases across 34 prospects. Key customers include SCPPA (17.8%), NV Energy (13.8%), and KPLC Kenya (11.9%).

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** Revenue grew from $734M (2022) to $990M (2025), a 10.5% CAGR. Q1 2026 revenue of $404M was inflated by the $105M Topp 2 plant sale; normalizing for this, underlying revenue was approximately $299M, still representing solid organic growth. Management guides FY2026 revenue of $1.11-$1.16B (+12-17%).

**Profitability pressure:** Despite 35% revenue growth from 2023-2025, net income remained flat at ~$124M each year. The core electricity segment gross margin compressed from 36.6% to 28.5%, driven by curtailments (277,923 MWh lost in 2025 vs. 121,299 in 2024), aging plant maintenance, and higher depreciation. Trailing EPS is $2.02, essentially unchanged from $2.08 in 2023.

**Balance sheet:** Total debt stands at approximately $3B post-Q1 2026 (including $1B in new convertible notes). Net debt/Adjusted EBITDA was 4.36x at year-end against a 6.0x covenant limit. Current ratio of 0.81 is below 1.0. The Altman Z-Score of 1.22 sits in the distress zone, though this is characteristic of project-financed infrastructure rather than imminent credit risk.

**Free cash flow:** Operating cash flow of $335M minus capex of $620M produced -$285M in FY2025 FCF. The 2026 capex budget of ~$675M implies continued negative FCF. This investment profile is necessary for the 2028 capacity targets but creates dependence on continued capital market access.

**Adjusted EBITDA:** The more relevant profitability metric for a capital-intensive utility grew from $482M (2023) to $582M (2025) and is guided to $615-$645M in 2026, representing steady mid-teens growth.

## Growth Analysis

**Data center demand thesis:** The Google 150MW PPA through NV Energy's Clean Transition Tariff is the signature event. Geothermal's 84% capacity factor provides 24/7 carbon-free baseload power without storage pairing — a structural advantage over intermittent renewables for data centers requiring guaranteed uptime. PPA prices for new geothermal now exceed $100/MWh (up from $60-80 historically), and the CD4 blend-and-extend at +27% pricing confirms repricing power.

**Capacity growth targets (2028):**
- Electricity: 1,650-1,750MW (from 1,340MW today, +23-31%)
- Storage: 950-1,050MW (from 415MW today, +129-153%)

**Energy Storage:** Grew 109% in 2025 with gross margins reaching 59.1% in Q1 2026 under favorable PJM merchant pricing. The Shirk 80MW/320MWh project reached COD in March 2026. Texas tolling prices are declining, however, and merchant revenue is inherently volatile.

**Indonesia expansion:** Four tender wins (122MW potential) position Ormat in the world's largest untapped geothermal market. Long-term optionality.

**Estimate trajectory is negative near-term:** Current quarter consensus fell from $0.39 to $0.25 (-35%) over 90 days; next quarter from $0.42 to $0.23 (-45%). Full-year 2026 consensus declined more modestly from $2.37 to $2.22 (-6%). This pattern suggests lumpy quarterly recognition (the Q1 Topp 2 sale pulled revenue forward) rather than structural deterioration.

## Valuation Assessment

ORA trades at 67x trailing P/E and approximately 62x the FY2026 consensus of $2.22. On FY2027 consensus of $2.49, it trades at 55x. The utility peer median P/E is 21.5x.

**Premium justification required:** At 3x peer multiples, Ormat needs to deliver growth dramatically above the utility sector. FY2026 guided Adjusted EBITDA of $615-$645M against an enterprise value of approximately $11.5B ($8.5B market cap + $3B debt) implies an EV/EBITDA of ~18x, elevated but less extreme than the P/E suggests given tax credit benefits suppressing reported taxes.

**Analyst targets:** Mean $135.45 (2% below current), median $135, range $122-$152. The stock has already surpassed the mean target, which typically signals that consensus has not yet adjusted upward to reflect the recent rally. However, given declining estimates, this may reflect genuine valuation concern rather than stale targets.

**Downside math:** At $138, the stock prices in multi-year execution of the data center thesis, storage scaling, and PPA repricing with no margin for error. A reversion to even 40x forward earnings ($2.22) implies $89 — a 36% downside. The upside case requires sustained multiple expansion in a rising rate environment.

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat strength: High.** Ormat's position is genuinely differentiated:
- Vertical integration from exploration through manufacturing to operations creates a knowledge barrier that takes decades to replicate
- 60+ years of accumulated geothermal expertise with proprietary ORC technology
- Existing geothermal lease portfolio (434,000 acres) provides development pipeline
- Long-term PPA contracts with creditworthy counterparties reduce revenue volatility

**Competitive trajectory: Stable to improving.** The data center demand wave is expanding the addressable market specifically for Ormat's core competency (baseload carbon-free power). EGS technology (if proven) could massively expand the market beyond hydrothermal areas.

**Threats:** Fervo Energy and other EGS-focused startups are competing for geothermal leases with venture capital backing. Binary technology competitors (Turboden/Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Baker Hughes) compete in the product segment. Storage is highly competitive with low barriers to entry.

## Risk Assessment

**Geothermal resource depletion:** Multiple plants show measurable temperature decline — McGinness Hills (5°F/year), Don A. Campbell (3-4°F/year), Platanares (4-5°F/year). The Brawley plant was already impaired ($7.2M). This is analogous to production decline in oil & gas and represents the single most company-specific risk.

**Curtailment headwind:** Lost generation more than doubled from 121,299 MWh (2024) to 277,923 MWh (2025). Grid congestion from renewable build-out is forcing available geothermal offline — a structural problem that worsens as solar penetration increases.

**Sovereign collection risk:** $29.5M overdue from Kenya (KPLC) and $20.3M from Honduras (ENEE). KPLC's request for "more favorable rates" on existing PPAs threatens 11.9% of revenue.

**Israel operational risk:** Manufacturing and senior management located ~26 miles from Gaza. Prior conflicts disrupted product segment operations.

**Negative FCF dependency on capital markets:** Continued access to debt markets at reasonable rates is essential for the growth plan. The convertible structure ($1.17B outstanding) creates potential dilution if shares remain elevated.

**Rate sensitivity:** At 67x earnings and 0.35% dividend yield, ORA behaves as a long-duration equity. Higher long-term Treasury rates create a structurally higher discount rate for this type of asset.

- EGS technology proves commercially viable, expanding addressable market by 10x
- Storage segment scales to 1,000MW+ by 2028 with sustained high margins

- Energy storage margins normalize as Texas tolling prices decline further

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Price retracement to $105-110 (45-50x FY2026 consensus) providing better risk/reward entry
- A second hyperscaler PPA announcement confirming the Google deal wasn't one-off
- FY2026 estimate revisions stabilize and reverse to positive trajectory

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- FY2026 guidance cut below $2.00 EPS or $580M Adjusted EBITDA
- Evidence of accelerating resource depletion requiring >$50M in aggregate impairments
- Curtailments exceeding 350,000 MWh annually
- Share price exceeding $155 (>70x forward) without corresponding estimate upgrades

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (likely late July/early August). The near-quarter estimate cuts create a setup where either the company beats substantially (validating the growth thesis) or misses (confirming near-term deceleration after Q1's Topp 2 pull-forward).

## Conclusion

Ormat Technologies occupies a genuinely unique competitive position with real structural tailwinds from data center demand for carbon-free baseload power. The Google PPA, storage scaling, and PPA repricing collectively support a compelling multi-year growth thesis.

However, at 67x trailing earnings and 62x FY2026 consensus — with estimates declining, insiders broadly selling, FCF deeply negative, and the rate environment structurally unfavorable for high-multiple utilities — the current price embeds exceptional execution with no margin of safety. The analyst consensus mean target of $135 sits below the current price. The stock has appreciated 93% in 12 months, and the risk/reward from here is symmetric at best.

HOLD with growth-hold rationale. The thesis is correct but the price is ahead of the fundamentals. A pullback to the $105-115 range or a material positive catalyst (second hyperscaler PPA, FY2026 estimate upgrades) would warrant reassessment.
