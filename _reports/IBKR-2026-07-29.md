**Research Date: July 29, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Interactive Brokers is one of the highest-quality franchises in the brokerage industry: a fully automated, low-headcount electronic broker with global direct-market connectivity, a sophisticated/professional client base, and adjusted pre-tax margins near 77%. Consolidated net income grew from roughly $1.64B in 2021 to approximately $4.36B in 2025, and consensus EPS estimates have risen across every forecast window over the past 90 days (+9.3% current year, +12.0% next year). Q2 2026 delivered a 6% EPS beat on record trading activity, expanding margin lending, and stronger net interest income. This is a compounder with genuine operating leverage and a moat that competitors cannot cheaply replicate.

The issue is price and mix. At roughly $91 (down from a $97.84 52-week high set in July), IBKR trades at about 28.5x next-year consensus EPS ($3.19) and roughly 33x current-year ($2.72), a wide premium to the brokerage peer median near 20x. A large and growing share of the record profitability is net interest income earned on client cash, margin loans, and securities lending, which is directly tied to the level of short-term rates. The market's higher-for-longer bias currently supports this line, but the same sensitivity that drove earnings up is a symmetric downside lever if the Fed shifts to cuts. Near-term options are pricing stress: front-week implied volatility is 56.8% versus 38.1% realized, the term structure is in backwardation, and near-dated put demand is elevated.

We rate IBKR a HOLD (growth_hold). The 12-month growth thesis is intact and supported by rising estimates, secular account growth, and emerging optionality in crypto and prediction markets, which is why this is a hold-for-appreciation rather than a hold-for-yield. The premium valuation, the two-sided rate cycle, degrading analyst sentiment (one persistent strong-sell rating plus a recent hold downgrade), and the meaningful open-market insider selling by a senior executive keep the risk/reward balanced rather than compelling. We would become more constructive on a pullback toward the low-to-mid $70s or on confirmation that the rate environment stays elevated through 2027.

## Company Overview

Interactive Brokers is a global electronic broker providing execution, clearing, settlement, custody, and financing across equities, options, futures, currencies, bonds, funds, and increasingly crypto and event/prediction markets, spanning 150+ markets in 30+ countries. The platform was built over decades by founder and Chairman Thomas Peterffy around direct connectivity to global exchanges, a structural advantage because establishing direct links to non-US venues takes years and heavy engineering investment.

The customer base skews toward active traders, professional and institutional clients, introducing brokers, hedge funds, and financial advisors, a higher-value, higher-margin segment than the mass-retail free-trading apps. Two profit engines drive the model: commissions on very high trading volumes, and net interest income earned on client cash, margin lending, and securities lending. In the current rate environment, net interest income has become the dominant earnings driver.

IBKR operates under an Up-C / dual-class structure. The publicly traded Class A shares represent a minority economic interest in the operating partnership (IBG LLC); Peterffy and affiliates control the majority via high-vote shares and IBG Holdings. This is why the publicly floated market capitalization (~$38.5B on the Class A count) is far smaller than the fully consolidated enterprise. A 4-for-1 forward split effective mid-2025 explains the observed share-count jump from ~108M to ~441M Class A shares.

## Financial Analysis

IBKR's financial profile is exceptional for a financial firm. Adjusted pre-tax margin reached roughly 77% in 2025, a level reflecting the automated, low-marginal-cost platform. Revenue growth flows to the bottom line with minimal incremental cost, producing industry-leading operating leverage.

- **Earnings trajectory:** Consolidated net income roughly 2.7x'd (2021 to 2025), a ~28% CAGR. Q2 2026 revenue was ~$1.90B with EPS ~$0.69, ahead of the $0.65 consensus.
- **Earnings surprise record:** Three beats and one small miss over the last four quarters (Q2 2026 +6.0%, Q4 2025 +7.8%, Q3 2025 +3.1%, Q1 2026 -2.2%). The pattern supports reasonable confidence in forward estimates.
- **Balance sheet quality:** As a broker-dealer, IBKR carries a large but low-risk balance sheet. Client assets are segregated, the firm avoids proprietary directional risk, and it maintains excess regulatory capital well above requirements. Standard industrial-company bankruptcy screens (Altman/Piotroski) do not map to a broker's balance sheet and their failure to compute is not a red flag.
- **Capital allocation:** Modest dividend (~0.4% yield), limited buybacks (constrained by the controlling structure), and the mid-2025 4-for-1 split. Capital return is measured rather than aggressive.

The central financial vulnerability is earnings mix, not solvency. A large share of profit is net interest income, which is a direct function of short-term rates and client cash balances. This is the single most important variable to monitor: aggressive Fed cuts would compress the highest-margin revenue line. The market's higher-for-longer bias currently favors this line, which tempers the near-term compression risk but does not eliminate the medium-term asymmetry.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on three legs. First, secular account and client-asset growth: IBKR continues to add sophisticated and international traders, and near-zero marginal cost per incremental account means each new client is highly accretive. Second, the rate-driven net interest income tailwind, which is durable while rates stay elevated. Third, emerging optionality in crypto and prediction/event markets, an underappreciated growth vector that also introduces new regulatory exposure.

Estimate revisions are the clearest forward signal and they are uniformly positive: current quarter +11.1% (30d) / +12.3% (90d), next quarter +10.0% / +12.2%, current year +8.4% / +9.3%, next year +10.9% / +12.0%. Consensus now sits at $2.72 for the current year and $3.19 for next year, with sell-side commentary framing a ~25% EPS growth outlook. This is a clean, broad-based upward revision pattern, though it falls short of the >30% inflection magnitude that would flag a systematic underestimation. The revisions are best read as consistent, positive earnings momentum that the market has largely absorbed rather than a hidden inflection.

The key qualifier: a portion of the revision strength reflects the rate environment feeding net interest income. If the composition were more commission-driven and less rate-driven, the durability of the growth would be higher. As it stands, the growth is real and secular at the account level, but cyclically amplified by rates at the earnings level.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$91, IBKR trades at roughly 33x current-year EPS ($2.72) and ~28.5x next-year EPS ($3.19). Against the brokerage peer median P/E of 20.0 (RJF 14.2, EVR 20.0, SF 13.9, SNEX 23.0, HLI 21.8), that is a substantial premium. The peer set is heterogeneous (full-service wealth managers and advisory boutiques) and none is a clean comparable for a fully automated global electronic broker. More apt reference points (exchanges and high-quality brokers) sit in the low-to-mid 20s, which still leaves IBKR at a premium.

The premium is partially justified. On ~25% expected EPS growth, next-year 28.5x implies a PEG near 1.1, reasonable for a high-margin compounder with a durable moat. Analyst targets (mean $106.97, median $109.50, high $122, low $70) imply ~18% upside to the mean. On a high-momentum stock with rising estimates and a PEG near 1.0, consensus targets can lag reality and act as a floor, which argues against reading proximity to targets as full valuation.

Two offsets keep the valuation score modest rather than high. First, a meaningful slice of the earnings base is net interest income at cyclically elevated rates; on cycle-adjusted (normalized-rate) earnings, the multiple is richer than it looks. Second, the premium to peers is wide and vulnerable to multiple compression if the rate tailwind reverses or if trading volumes normalize from elevated levels. This is a fairly valued high-quality name that does not clear the bar for a valuation-driven buy.

## Competitive Landscape

IBKR's moat is durable and improving in its chosen segment. Global direct-market connectivity, the lowest cost and highest automation in execution, and a focus on professional/international clients differentiate it from US mass-retail apps (Robinhood) and full-service wealth managers (Schwab, Morgan Stanley/E*Trade). Direct connections to non-US exchanges take years to build, which raises the barrier to entry against the professional and international active-trader base.

Trajectory favors IBKR: it is gaining share among active and international traders, its near-zero marginal cost per account produces operating leverage that payment-for-order-flow retail competitors cannot match on the professional segment, and product breadth (crypto, prediction markets) is expanding the addressable base. The competitive position is strong and the trajectory is positive, which supports a high-quality score. We temper it modestly because the professional/active-trader segment is inherently volume-sensitive and because competitive positioning has historically been a less reliable predictor of forward returns than the core financial and growth signals.

## Risk Assessment

- **Interest-rate sensitivity (dominant swing factor):** Net interest income is now a core profit engine. A dovish Fed pivot would directly compress the highest-margin revenue line. This is the single largest determinant of the earnings trajectory, and the rate path is genuinely two-sided.
- **Market-activity dependence:** Commission revenue tracks trading volumes and volatility. In calm markets, both fall. Earnings are pro-cyclical to market participation.
- **Founder control / key-person risk:** Peterffy's majority voting control via the Up-C structure limits public shareholder influence and creates key-person dependence. This is a permanent governance feature, largely priced, but a genuine constraint on external accountability.
- **Regulatory / multi-jurisdiction complexity:** Operating in 30+ countries exposes IBKR to overlapping regulators. Expansion into crypto and prediction/event markets adds novel and evolving regulatory risk.
- **Technology / operational risk:** A fully automated platform means outages, cyberattacks, or software errors could cause outsized reputational and financial damage.
- **Margin-lending credit risk:** Growing margin loans expose IBKR to client default in a sharp drawdown, mitigated by automated real-time liquidation.
- **Sentiment:** Analyst sentiment is degrading, with one persistent strong-sell rating across all periods and a recent hold downgrade against nine buy/strong-buy ratings. This is a divergence to track rather than an alarm.

Idiosyncratic risks are moderate and mostly priced, with the rate cycle the one variable that could move the stock materially in either direction. The company is clean on event risk: no restatements (the recent 10-K/A is a clerical Item 9A controls correction), no major litigation, no acquisitions, and a quiet 8-K cadence.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing near-term stress. Front-week ATM IV of 56.8% sits well above 38.1% realized volatility (rich by ~18.7 points), IV percentile is 76%, and the near-dated put/call skew is elevated at 8.3% with a put/call volume ratio of 2.14. The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 56.8% > six-month 40.3%), which typically signals an imminent catalyst or hedging demand. The intermediate tenors carry negative skew (call-heavy at the 1m and 3m expiries, -7.3% and -5.3%), so the medium-term positioning is constructive even as near-dated hedging is defensive. Combined with the IV expansion from 38.3% to 56.8% while the price fell from $96 to ~$86-91, the market is pricing elevated uncertainty around the recent pullback. This confirms our cautious near-term stance and argues against chasing at current levels; it does not point to a structural bearish view. Short interest is low (2.4% of float, 2 days to cover), so there is no squeeze setup and no crowded informed-short signal.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (~$122, ~34% upside)
Rates stay elevated through 2027, sustaining record net interest income. Secular account and asset growth continues at a double-digit clip, trading volumes remain robust in a volatile macro backdrop, and crypto/prediction-market expansion adds incremental revenue. Estimate revisions keep drifting higher, EPS grows ~25%, and the multiple holds near 38x next-year EPS as the market rewards the quality and durability. This tracks the analyst high target.

### Bear Case (~$70, ~23% downside)
The Fed shifts to a cutting cycle, compressing net interest income and reducing the highest-margin revenue line. Trading volumes normalize from elevated levels, dampening commissions. The premium multiple derates toward ~22x next-year EPS as the market repositions IBKR as a rate-cycle beneficiary rather than a secular compounder. This tracks the analyst low target.

### Base Case (~$100, ~10% upside)
Rates stay firm-to-modestly-lower, net interest income holds broadly, and account/asset growth carries EPS to the ~$3.19 next-year consensus. The multiple settles around 30x next-year EPS as the market splits the difference between the quality premium and the rate-cycle discount.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

- **Base case target:** ~$100 (30x next-year EPS), ~10% upside from ~$91.
- **Bull case target:** ~$122 (38x next-year EPS), ~34% upside.
- **Bear case target:** ~$70 (22x next-year EPS), ~23% downside.
- **Timeframe:** 12 months, keyed to the rate path (FOMC decisions), quarterly trading-volume and net-interest-income disclosures, and continued estimate revisions.
- **Upside/downside:** +10% to base, +34% to bull, -23% to bear — balanced-to-slightly-favorable but not asymmetric, consistent with a hold.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. A pullback toward the low-to-mid $70s (near the analyst low target), which would restore asymmetric upside on unchanged fundamentals.
2. Confirmation that the rate environment stays elevated through 2027 (net interest income guidance reaffirmed/raised), removing the primary bear lever.
3. Acceleration in estimate revisions above +30% over 90 days combined with sustained double-digit account growth, signaling a genuine underestimation rather than steady momentum.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. A confirmed Fed cutting cycle with management guiding net interest income materially lower.
2. Estimate revisions turning negative for two consecutive quarters, or a quarterly EPS miss accompanied by decelerating account growth.
3. A material deterioration in trading volumes (DARTs) that is not offset by net interest income, indicating the commission engine is stalling in a calmer market.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings and the following FOMC decision, whichever comes first.

## Conclusion

Interactive Brokers is a high-quality, structurally advantaged franchise with best-in-class margins, secular account growth, and consistently rising estimates. Those strengths keep it in the hold-for-appreciation camp rather than a name to avoid. At ~28-33x forward earnings, a wide premium to its peer group, with a large share of record profitability tied to a rate cycle that is genuinely two-sided, the risk/reward is balanced rather than compelling. Near-term options are pricing stress, analyst sentiment is degrading at the margin, and a senior insider made meaningful open-market sales in early 2026. The declining score trajectory (7.1 to 6.3) reflects a premium multiple meeting a maturing rate tailwind, not fundamental deterioration.

We rate IBKR a **HOLD (growth_hold)**. Own it for the compounding and the durable moat, but wait for a better entry — either a pullback toward the low-to-mid $70s or confirmation that the rate environment stays elevated — before adding at a premium multiple into a two-sided rate cycle.
