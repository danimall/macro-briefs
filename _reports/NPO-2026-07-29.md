**Research Date: July 29, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Enpro is a two-segment industrial-technology company built around proprietary sealing products (Garlock, STEMCO, Technetics) and advanced surface technologies (NxEdge, LeanTeq, Alluxa, Technetics Semi) that serve semiconductor capital equipment. The investment case rests on a real operational improvement that the headline financials obscure: FY2025 GAAP net income fell to $40.5M from $72.9M and the trailing P/E screens at 153x, but both figures are artifacts of a one-time $67.2M non-cash pension settlement loss taken in Q4 2025 when the company terminated its frozen U.S. defined-benefit plan. On the metrics that reflect the business, results improved: operating income rose to $161.6M, adjusted EPS to $7.91 (from $6.96), adjusted EBITDA to $277.6M, and free cash flow to $159.2M (13.9% of sales). Q1 2026 extended the trend, with revenue up 10.9% to $303M and the AST segment margin expanding to 23.3%.

The growth engine is Advanced Surface Technologies, now ~36% of revenue and driven by leading-edge semiconductor demand for outsourced cleaning, coating, and refurbishment of chamber components embedded in the supply chains of Applied Materials and ASML. Management raised FY2026 guidance to 10-14% sales growth and $8.85-9.50 adjusted EPS. Estimate revisions are positive but modest (current-year consensus up 3.4% and next-year up 4.8% over 90 days), which places NPO in the durable-compounder category rather than the sharp-inflection category where estimate momentum drives multiple re-rating.

The constraint is price. At ~$314, NPO trades near 34x FY26 midpoint adjusted EPS and ~30x next-year consensus, a premium to the 28.2x peer median that already discounts the semiconductor tailwind. Analyst mean target of $359 implies ~14% upside, and the low target ($305) sits below the current price. Layered on top is a genuine, unreserved tail risk: an emerging Water Valley, Mississippi trichloroethylene litigation cluster (13 suits, ~531 plaintiffs, filed Feb-May 2026, no reserve accrued) and a ~24% single-customer concentration in AST. We rate NPO a HOLD (growth_hold): a quality business worth owning through the cycle, but priced such that near-term appreciation is capped and asymmetry is absent at the current multiple.

## Company Overview

Enpro (spun off from Goodrich in 2002) generated ~$1,143M of revenue in FY2025 across two segments:

- **Sealing Technologies (~64%, $732M)** — Garlock (gaskets, dynamic seals, single-use biopharma componentry), Technetics (metal/mechanical seals plus the "Process Analytics" gas/liquid sensing group), and STEMCO (commercial-vehicle wheel-end and suspension). Roughly two-thirds of segment revenue is aftermarket/recurring, supporting a ~32.9% segment AEBITDA margin.
- **Advanced Surface Technologies (~36%, $412M)** — NxEdge, Technetics Semi, LeanTeq (Taiwan-based semiconductor part cleaning/refurbishment), and Alluxa (optical filters/thin-film coatings). Segment AEBITDA margin ~20-23%.

FY2025 end-market mix: semiconductor 32.1%, general industrial 26.2%, commercial vehicle 14.7%, aerospace 8.1%, power generation 6.2%, food/biopharma 6.7%, oil & gas 6.0%. About 43% of sales are international. The competitive premise is applied-engineering know-how and specified positions in critical applications where cost-of-failure dwarfs product cost, reinforced by high customer-qualification barriers in AST.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue.** $1,099M (2022) → $1,059M (2023) → $1,049M (2024) → $1,143M (2025, +9.0%, of which 7.6% organic). Q1 2026 accelerated to +10.9%, led by AST organic +11.1%.

**Earnings quality.** The GAAP/adjusted divergence is the central analytical point. GAAP net income reflects the pretax non-cash pension settlement, an accounting cleanup that recognized actuarial losses previously deferred in AOCI, not an operating loss. Adjusted EPS rose to $7.91 (2025) from $6.96 (2024) and $6.54 (2023). Q1 2026 adjusted EPS of $2.14 was up ~13% year over year and beat consensus by 1.7%. The earnings-surprise record is 3 beats, 1 miss over four quarters, with beats ranging from 1.7% to 4.1%, a solid but not standout track record supporting reasonable confidence in current consensus.

**Margins and cash flow.** Gross margin ~42.6%, reported operating margin ~14.1%. Segment margins are stable-to-expanding (Sealing 32.9%; AST expanded to 23.3% in Q1 2026 from 21.9%). Cash conversion validates the adjusted-earnings story: FY25 operating cash flow of $201.2M (up from $162.9M) and FCF of $159.2M, with Q1 2026 OCF nearly double the prior-year quarter. FCF conversion of adjusted net income is comfortably above 90%, indicating high earnings quality.

**Balance sheet.** Total debt fell to ~$605M after a Q1 2026 $50M revolver paydown (funded by repatriating foreign cash). Net leverage is modest and well within the 4.0x covenant, with interest coverage above the 2.5x minimum. The company refinanced in 2025, issuing $450M of 6.125% senior notes due 2033, redeeming the $350M 5.75% 2026 notes, repaying the term loan, and moving to an $800M revolver (2030, ~$630M available). Goodwill ($1,065M) plus intangibles ($824M) equal ~71% of assets, a function of the acquisition-led strategy; the Altman Z of 2.38 (grey zone) is driven by this intangible-heavy balance sheet and the pension-depressed EBIT, not liquidity stress (current ratio 2.32).

**Tax.** The Q1 2026 effective rate dropped to 19.2% (from 24.3%), aided by H.R.1 (bonus depreciation, domestic R&D expensing) plus share-based payment benefits. This is a recurring cash-tax and EPS tailwind into 2026.

Financial health scores well: strong FCF generation, appropriate leverage for the business model, disciplined debt reduction, and clean cash conversion. The single caution is the intangible-heavy asset base tied to serial M&A, which we address under Management Quality.

## Growth Analysis

Growth is durable rather than explosive. FY26 guidance implies double-digit top-line and mid-teens EPS expansion, driven primarily by AST semiconductor demand at advanced nodes. The mechanism is structural: as chipmakers migrate to leading-edge processes, the volume of precision chamber components requiring outsourced cleaning, coating, and refurbishment rises, and Enpro's qualified positions in the AMAT/ASML supply chains capture that flow. A new Arizona facility is being built to add capacity, with timing flagged as an uncertainty.

The Sealing segment provides a defensive, aftermarket-heavy cash base while management builds higher-growth adjacencies: the AMI (Jan 2024, $209M), Overlook (Oct 2025), and AlpHa (Nov 2025) acquisitions assemble a "Process Analytics" sensing platform and single-use biopharma exposure, offsetting persistent commercial-vehicle OEM weakness at STEMCO.

Estimate revisions are positive but moderate: current-quarter flat, next-year up 4.8% and current-year up 3.4% over 90 days. This is meaningfully below the +30% velocity that signals a mispriced inflection; consensus is nudging estimates higher in line with delivery, not racing to catch a re-rating. We weight the growth thesis as confirmed and durable, but not as an independent BUY trigger.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$314 (down from a 52-week high of $390 and up ~48% over the trailing year), the trailing GAAP P/E of 153x is meaningless given the pension charge. The relevant metrics:

- **Forward P/E on FY26 midpoint ($9.18):** ~34x
- **Forward P/E on next-year consensus ($10.56):** ~30x
- **Peer median P/E:** 28.2x (MIDD 21.5, GTES 28.2, ESAB 23.6, JBTM 38.2, FELE 30.7)

NPO trades at a clear premium to the peer median on current-year earnings and roughly in line on next-year. With ~12-15% EPS growth, the PEG is ~2.0-2.7, full for an industrial even one with a semiconductor kicker. This is a quality name priced for quality.

The stock has declined ~8-12% since the prior review at ~$350, which modestly improves the entry (from ~38x to ~34x FY26 EPS) on unchanged-to-improving fundamentals. That is a directional positive, but it does not create asymmetry: the analyst mean target implies ~14% upside, the low target sits below the current price, and only 3-4 analysts cover the name. On inflection stocks with cheap forward multiples, consensus targets act as floors; here, at 30-34x forward with modest revision velocity, the targets more likely reflect the same growth extrapolation embedded in the price. We score valuation below neutral because the multiple expanded ahead of the estimate revisions, leaving little margin of safety and limited upside to fair value.

## Competitive Landscape

The AST franchise sits in a favorable structural niche. Outsourced cleaning, coating, and refurbishment of advanced-node chamber components carries high qualification barriers, and Enpro is embedded in the supply chains of the dominant equipment OEMs. Competition across precision machining, cleaning, and coatings is fragmented with no dominant player, and the AST margin expansion signals improving pricing and mix. The trajectory is positive: rising semiconductor share of revenue, margin expansion, and capacity investment.

Sealing Technologies is a mature, defensive cash generator competing on brand and specified positions rather than price, sustaining ~33% segment margins. The strategic pivot toward Process Analytics sensing and single-use biopharma via M&A is a coherent effort to raise the growth profile and dilute commercial-vehicle cyclicality.

The competitive position is improving, but two structural features cap conviction: AST is tied to volatile wafer-fab equipment capex, and a single customer represents ~24% of total consolidated sales. Concentration is the engine of current outperformance and the largest single vulnerability.

## Risk Assessment

- **Customer concentration:** One AST customer accounts for ~24% of consolidated net sales. Loss or material reduction would be immediately impactful.
- **Water Valley TCE litigation (highest-value new item):** Between February 24 and May 4, 2026, thirteen lawsuits were filed in Yalobusha County, Mississippi by a combined ~531 alleged former employees/family members over trichloroethylene exposure at a predecessor facility (1972-1996). Two suits (104 plaintiffs) name Enpro entities directly; 150 workers'-comp petitions have been filed, and plaintiffs' counsel has signaled additional non-employee claimants outside workers'-comp exclusivity. Enpro has accrued no reserve, citing an early stage. This is an open-ended tail risk with an expanding claimant pool.
- **Legacy environmental liabilities:** 19 sites, $41.3M reserved, including the Lower Passaic River Superfund site (Enpro's $5.9M share tied up in a Third Circuit appeal) and eight Arizona uranium mines on Navajo Nation land.
- **Semiconductor cyclicality:** AST is exposed to sharp capex downturns; the current tailwind can reverse.
- **PFAS/PTFE regulatory:** EU REACH restrictions on PFAS/PTFE could impair Sealing product lines; PTFE is a critical raw material.
- **Operational/geopolitical:** Palmyra, NY (Garlock) union work stoppage since Feb 16, 2026; Taiwan/Singapore geographic concentration in AST; China rare-earth dependency; a newly added Q1 2026 risk factor around Iran-conflict disruption to helium supply for semiconductors.
- **Governance follow-ups:** Two 2026 Item 5.02 director/officer-change 8-Ks could not be verified; a potential executive transition warrants monitoring.

These are specific, largely idiosyncratic risks that are not fully priced, particularly the unreserved litigation and the customer concentration. We score risk below neutral.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility is 55.8% against 37.3% one-year historical volatility — rich, at the 81st percentile of the current chain, with a ±11.4% implied move on the nearest expiry. The term structure is in backwardation (55.8% near-term vs 43.3% at six months), which the market uses to price imminent stress, most likely the upcoming Q2 print. The put/call volume ratio of 2.00 alongside a low put/call OI ratio of 0.17 and a balanced -2.3% skew suggests near-term hedging/positioning rather than a durable directional bear stance. IV jumped 4.1 points as the stock fell 8.2% into this snapshot. For a holder, the elevated near-term IV argues against chasing and favors patience around the earnings event.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case ($420):** Semiconductor demand at advanced nodes stays strong, AST organic growth sustains above 15% with margins holding above 23%, the Process Analytics/biopharma platform compounds, and the H.R.1 tax tailwind and disciplined deleveraging lift adjusted EPS toward $12 in the outer year. Multiple holds at ~35x on continued semi momentum and the Water Valley litigation resolves without material reserve. Analyst high target $420.

**Bear case ($240, ~-23%):** The semiconductor capex cycle rolls over, AST organic growth turns negative, and the multiple de-rates toward the low end of the peer range (~26x) on flat EPS near $9.20. A material adverse development in the Water Valley litigation or a reserve requirement compounds the derating. Commercial-vehicle weakness persists and drags Sealing.

The base case sits between: durable double-digit revenue growth, mid-teens adjusted EPS growth, and a modest re-rating toward the analyst median as consensus catches delivery, but capped by the already-full starting multiple.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold). Timeframe: 12 months.**

- **Base case target: $345** (~+10% from ~$314). Reasoning: ~33x next-year consensus adjusted EPS of $10.56 = $348, consistent with the analyst median of $356 and reflecting continued semi momentum without further multiple expansion.
- **Bull case target: $420** (~+34%). ~35x on outer-year EPS approaching $12 on sustained AST strength; matches the analyst high.
- **Bear case target: $240** (~-23%). Semi cycle rollover + de-rating to ~26x on flat ~$9.20 EPS, or a litigation-driven derating.
- **Upside/downside:** ~+10% to base, ~+34% to bull, ~-23% to bear — balanced, not asymmetric.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Pullback into the low-$260s (~28x forward, in line with peers), which would restore asymmetry.
- Next-year estimate revision velocity accelerating above +10% over 90 days (currently +4.8%), signaling a genuine inflection.
- AST organic growth sustaining above 15% with segment margin holding above 23% for two consecutive quarters.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Semiconductor capex cycle rolling over — AST organic growth turning negative.
- Loss or material reduction of the ~24% AST customer.
- Water Valley litigation expanding materially (requirement to accrue a large reserve, or claimant pool growing into the thousands).
- A guidance cut or a break in the beat cadence.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next natural checkpoint is the Q2 2026 earnings release (early August), which the options market is already pricing as a volatility event.

This is the fourth consecutive HOLD (5.2 → 6.4 → 6.4, now 5.9). The step-down from 6.4 reflects a fuller multiple expanding ahead of estimate revisions and a more explicit weighting of the newly disclosed Water Valley litigation and rich near-term volatility, not a deterioration in the business; Q1 confirmed the operational trajectory and the guidance raise. The fundamentals continue to improve, and the price prices most of that improvement.

## Conclusion

Enpro is a well-run industrial-technology compounder with a real semiconductor tailwind, strong free cash flow, a repaired balance sheet, and a coherent M&A strategy, all masked in the headline financials by a one-time non-cash pension charge that a naive screen misreads as deterioration. On the numbers that matter, the business is accelerating. The market has largely figured this out: at ~34x forward adjusted EPS, a premium to peers, with ~14% upside to the analyst mean and an unreserved litigation tail plus a 24% customer concentration, the risk/reward is balanced. We would own it through the cycle for the quality and the semi exposure, but we require either a pullback into the low-$260s or a sharper acceleration in estimate revisions before upgrading to BUY. **HOLD.**
