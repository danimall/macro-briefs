## Executive Summary

Warner Bros. Discovery is currently priced as a merger-arbitrage instrument, not as an operating company. Under the February 27, 2026 agreement with Paramount Skydance (PSKY), each share converts into **$31.00 in cash plus "ticking consideration" of $0.00277778 per day** elapsed after September 30, 2026 through closing. Larry Ellison and an affiliated trust have guaranteed $45.72 billion of the merger consideration. Shareholders approved the deal on April 23, 2026; the European Commission cleared it with remedies on July 22; the UK CMA declined a Phase 2 referral on August 6. What stands in the way is domestic litigation: twelve state attorneys general (July 13) and the WGA (July 14) filed Clayton Act §7 suits in N.D. Cal., a TRO issued July 20, and on July 24 the defendants agreed not to close until the earlier of five days after a merits determination or June 1, 2027. On August 4 the court set a single 12-day trial for **March 2-19, 2027**.

The arithmetic is straightforward. A March 24, 2027 close (five days post-trial) delivers $31.486 per share, a **17.6% gross return** from $26.78 over 229 days, roughly a 29% IRR. A June 1, 2027 close delivers $31.678, **+18.3%** over 298 days, roughly 23% annualized. Solving for the market-implied completion probability — P × $31.49 + (1−P) × break price = $26.78 — yields **59% at a $20 break price, 65% at $18, and 70% at $16**. At P = 70% the market is implicitly marking break value at $15.80. My own standalone estimate, built off LTM Adjusted EBITDA of roughly $8,768M and net debt of $29,033M, is $14.65 at 7.5x and $16.40 at 8.0x, with a possible $2.79/share addition if PSKY's $7.0B reverse fee applies on an antitrust failure and a materially worse outcome if it does not. The market's handicap and mine are close enough that I see no exploitable edge.

What has changed since my last review in late June is the dominant variable, and it changed for the worse. The spread is essentially unchanged at an unchanged price, but the time to resolution has stretched from a matter of weeks-to-months into a contested seven-to-ten-month path gated by a single district judge, and the merger agreement's End Date of March 4, 2027 arithmetically precedes the end of the trial that gates closing. The operating stub deteriorated on the lines that would set break-case value: Q2 revenue fell 11% to $8,717M against roughly $9.2B consensus, Studios Adjusted EBITDA fell 89% to $96M, domestic linear subscribers declined 10%, and domestic affiliate rate increases decelerated to +1% from +3% in FY2025. I am moving the overall score to 4.2 from 4.7 and maintaining HOLD, classified as uncertain: the position is a defensible carry for existing holders with a specific closing catalyst, but the payoff distribution (+18% versus roughly -44%) requires a completion probability above about 69% at a $16 break, and I cannot assert that with confidence.

## Company Overview

WBD operates three reported segments as of June 30, 2026:

- **Global Linear Networks** — FY2025 revenue $17,656M (−12%), Adjusted EBITDA $6,412M (−21%). Domestic and international cable networks including TNT, TBS, CNN, Discovery, Food Network, TLC.
- **Studios** — FY2025 revenue $12,619M (+9%), Adjusted EBITDA $2,545M (+54%). Theatrical, television production and licensing, home entertainment, games, consumer products.
- **Streaming** — FY2025 revenue $10,876M (+5%), Adjusted EBITDA $1,370M versus $677M in 2024. 131.6M subscribers at 12/31/25 (domestic 59.2M, international 72.4M); global ARPU $6.92, down 11%. The 10-Q does not disclose Q2 2026 subscriber counts, so these are year-end figures and should not be read as current.

The 2025 plan to separate Streaming & Studios from Global Linear Networks is **contractually suspended**: PSKY's closing obligation is conditioned on WBD not completing the separation or declaring a separation dividend. The structure survives in the debt stack — the borrower under the new first-lien term loans is Discovery Global Holdings, Inc., formerly WarnerMedia Holdings — which matters for the break case.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue.** FY2025 revenue $37,296M, down about 5%. H1 2026 $17,610M, −6% (−7% ex-FX). Q2 2026 $8,717M, −11%. The Q2 composition: Distribution $4,950M (+1%), Advertising $1,724M (−22%), Content $1,828M (−26%), Other $215M (−10%).

Two discrete items explain nearly the entire decline, and they differ in kind. First, the NBA rights lapse: management quantifies a $414M Q2 advertising reduction and a $547M H1 reduction, against cost-of-revenue reductions of $760M and $1,107M. The net effect on Adjusted EBITDA is therefore **positive roughly $346M in Q2 and $560M in H1**. Global Linear Networks Adjusted EBITDA fell only 4% in Q2 on a 17% revenue decline because the rights fee vanished alongside the revenue. That is a one-time reset, not a margin trend; domestic linear audience delivery fell 17% in the quarter, and audience delivery is what prices affiliate renewals. Second, the film slate comp: Studios revenue fell 39% to $2,328M, theatrical product revenue −46%, against a prior-year quarter carrying *A Minecraft Movie* and *Sinners*. *Supergirl* grossed $126M worldwide. Studios Adjusted EBITDA fell to $96M from $863M.

**Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was flat in H1 2026 at $4,082M versus $4,058M**, +0.6% on a 6% revenue decline. Streaming contributed +$318M and intersegment eliminations +$200M, offsetting Studios −$251M and Global Linear Networks −$225M. LTM Adjusted EBITDA is approximately **$8,768M** (FY2025 $8,744M + H1'26 − H1'25).

**GAAP earnings are dominated by non-recurring items and should not be used for valuation.** FY2025 operating income was $738M; a $2,945M gain on debt extinguishment from the June 2025 tender offers drove pre-tax income of $1,639M and net income of $727M ($0.29 diluted). FY2024 was a −$11,311M loss including a $9,147M non-cash goodwill impairment of the linear reporting unit. H1 2026 shows an operating loss of −$2,232M including the **$2,800M Netflix termination fee expense**, which PSKY paid to Netflix in cash on WBD's behalf; WBD expensed it, added it back as non-cash in operating cash flow, and carries it in accrued liabilities as reimbursable to PSKY in certain termination circumstances. Reading the H1 operating loss as a cash event would be an error. Q2 2026 delivered operating income of $237M and GAAP diluted EPS of $0.06 against a reported consensus loss near $0.10, a beat as reported, though I cannot confirm the consensus was compiled on a GAAP basis.

**Cash flow.** FY2025 operating cash flow $4,319M, capex $1,231M, **free cash flow $3,088M** (8.3% of revenue), against cash taxes of $1,926M and cash interest of $2,295M. H1 2026 operating cash flow was $640M versus $1,536M, capex $544M, **H1 FCF $96M**. Do not annualize the half. The ~$908M YoY H1 deterioration decomposes largely into working capital swings — prepaid/other assets (−$1,081M), payables/accrued/deferred revenue (−$692M), partly offset by receivables (+$738M) and a smaller content-payables drag (+$787M) — layered on a weak theatrical slate. FY2025's $3.1B is the better normalized anchor; on 2,506.7M shares that is $1.23/share, a 4.6% FCF yield at $26.78.

**Balance sheet (6/30/26).** Total assets $97,248M, of which goodwill $25,861M and net intangibles $25,922M. Total liabilities $63,253M; WBD equity $32,838M; accumulated deficit −$14,279M. Cash $3,369M, down from $4,566M. Current assets $12,539M against current liabilities $16,143M — **current ratio 0.78**, down from 1.06, driven mainly by the $2.8B Netflix accrual and $1,493M of debt moving current.

Debt is $32,402M face. The June 4, 2026 First Lien Credit Agreement provided $13.0B USD plus €1,717M term loans maturing 6/4/2033 at SOFR/EURIBOR+250, weighted average 5.95%, secured on substantially all assets, with **no financial maintenance covenant**; proceeds retired the $15B bridge in full. Senior notes total $17,694M at 3.92%/4.35%/5.17% by maturity bucket. The $4.0B revolver is undrawn (covenants: 3.00x minimum interest coverage, 4.50x maximum leverage, in compliance). Maturities through June 2027 total under $1.5B.

Derived leverage: **net debt $29,033M, 3.31x LTM Adjusted EBITDA** (3.70x gross). Derived interest run-rate: term loans ~$875M plus notes ~$804M, approximately **$1.68B** versus H1's $2.52B annualized pace. Refinancing the bridge into 2033 paper should reduce cash interest by several hundred million dollars annually, an improvement that will not appear in trailing screens until H2 2026 results. That is the most underappreciated positive in the standalone case.

Two balance-sheet items warrant attention. The 10-Q discloses senior note fair value of $15,218M against $17,694M face, about **86% of par**; the legacy unsecured notes have been structurally subordinated by the new first-lien layer, corroborating the 2025 downgrades by all three agencies which cited "an increase in secured debt." (The disclosed figure includes accrued interest, and some notes run to 2062, so duration explains part of the discount.) Separately, **$3,900M of receivables are derecognized** under a revolving program (facility reduced to $4,000M, extended to June 2027) with $2,238M of gross receivables pledged; withdrawal of that facility would be a working-capital event of roughly $3.9B.

An Altman Z-Score of 1.25 nominally sits in the distress zone. Three model-fit problems argue against reading it as a solvency verdict: the EBIT/assets term uses FY2025 operating income of $738M, suppressed by $5,684M of largely non-cash purchase-accounting amortization; the equity term is on book value (at market, equity/liabilities is ~1.06 versus 0.571 book); and the model was not designed for a balance sheet with $51.8B of goodwill and intangibles. What the screens legitimately flag is thin GAAP profitability, a sub-1.0 current ratio, and 3.3x leverage on a business whose largest segment is structurally shrinking. What they do not establish is imminent distress, given sub-$1.5B maturities through mid-2027, an undrawn revolver, and no maintenance covenant on the largest tranche. Financial health scores 5.0.

## Growth Analysis

Growth is negative outside one segment. Revenue declined 11% in Q2 and 6% in H1. Advertising fell 22%. Domestic linear subscribers fell 10% in Q2 (−9% in FY2025) while domestic affiliate rate increases decelerated to **+1% in Q2 and +2% in H1 from +3% in FY2025** — volume decay is accelerating while the pricing offset weakens. The 10-K states plainly that linear subscriber declines "are expected to continue," and that increased digital and FAST inventory "has also limited our ability to demand higher rates... or even the same rates that we previously charged."

Streaming is the working engine: Q2 revenue topped $3B, +10%, with Adjusted EBITDA +75% to $512M (+63% ex-FX), and H1 Adjusted EBITDA of $950M versus $632M. But the growth is international volume rather than price. FY2025 international subscribers grew 21% against domestic +4% while global ARPU fell 11% to $6.92. Streaming SG&A rose 14-16% in 2026 on HBO Max launch marketing. Profit will not scale in proportion to subscribers when incremental subscribers carry structurally lower ARPU.

Consensus estimates are moving the wrong way. Current-quarter EPS has been revised to −$0.12 from +$0.01 both 30 and 90 days ago; next quarter to −$0.08 from +$0.03; next year to −$0.05 from +$0.01. Current-year −$1.23 is dominated by the Netflix fee expense and is not economically meaningful, but the quarterly and next-year cuts are not. The four-quarter surprise record is 2 beats, 2 misses, including a −222.6% miss in the December 2025 quarter; visibility is poor, which matters because Studios is hit-driven and just demonstrated an 89% single-quarter EBITDA swing.

Management characterized 2027 as potentially HBO's strongest year and said there will be no DC strategy change after *Supergirl*; the *Superman* sequel was described favorably. These are second-hand characterizations from press coverage, not primary transcript, and they are forward assertions rather than evidence. Growth prospects score 3.8.

## Valuation Assessment

Two valuations coexist and must be kept separate.

**Deal-contingent value.** At $26.78 against $31.486 (March 24, 2027 close, 175 ticking days at $0.00277778 = $0.486), the gross spread is 17.6%, roughly a 29% IRR over 0.63 years. At the June 1, 2027 standstill limit, 244 days of ticking gives $31.678, +18.3%, roughly 23% annualized. For a dedicated arbitrage book those are attractive returns on a deal with an Ellison guarantee behind $45.72B of consideration and three of four major regulatory hurdles cleared or reported cleared.

**Standalone value.** At $26.78, EV is $67.1B equity plus $29.0B net debt = **$96.1B, or 11.0x LTM Adjusted EBITDA of $8,768M**. At the $31.00 deal price EV reaches $106.8B, **12.2x**. (The widely quoted $110-111B headline equals equity at $31.00 plus *gross* debt, not an EV/EBITDA-comparable figure.) No standalone linear-heavy media asset trades at 11x. Peer P/E medians of 17.9x (FOX 16.0x, FOXA 17.9x, NXST 36.6x, TGNA 16.3x) are unusable here because WBD's GAAP EPS is negative and noise-dominated, but the peer group's multiples are not consistent with 11x EBITDA on a business with a −12% revenue segment representing nearly half of revenue and roughly 60% of segment EBITDA. On 7.5x LTM Adjusted EBITDA, equity is $36.7B, **$14.65/share**; on 8.0x, $41.1B, **$16.40**. The pre-bid trading level in early September 2025 was $12.54 before the first Paramount reports, and the 52-week low is $10.76.

**Reconciling the two.** The market's implied completion probability spans 59% (break at $20) to 70% (break at $16), with $15.80 implied at P = 70%. My break estimate is $15-18 as a range: 7.5-8.0x standalone EBITDA, plus $2.79/share if PSKY's $7.0B reverse fee applies to an antitrust failure, less an unfavorable outcome if instead WBD owes $3.0B plus up to $1,528M of junior-lien reimbursement and the $2.8B Netflix fee — up to $7.33B, roughly $2.92/share. Which trigger maps to which fee is the single most consequential term I could not verify, and it swings break-case value by roughly $5.70/share.

Probability-weighted: at P = 68% and a $16 break, expected value is 0.68 × $31.49 + 0.32 × $16 = **$26.53**, marginally below spot. At P = 72% and $17, expected value is $27.42, +2.4%. Breakeven at a $16 break requires P ≈ 69%. My handicap sits at 65-72%, which places fair value in a band that straddles the current price. This is a fair-value situation, and it is why the valuation score is 4.5 rather than the 6.5 I carried in June — not because the price moved (it did not, $26.64 to $26.78), but because the same spread must now be earned over seven to ten months through a contested trial rather than over weeks through routine clearances. A discount that exists only because a court might block the transaction is not a margin of safety.

Analyst targets (low $26.00, high $31.25, mean $29.82, median $31.00) are deal-anchored rather than independent fundamental work, and the distribution — 2 buy, 16 hold, 1 strong sell, with sentiment characterized as degrading — reflects that. I do not treat them as confirmation.

## Competitive Landscape

The competitive trajectory is deteriorating on the metrics that matter for renewal economics. Domestic audience delivery fell 17% in Q2 and 25% in FY2025; the NBA has gone to NBCUniversal, and while the near-term financial effect is positive, affiliate renewals price off delivery. Management's own list of goodwill-trigger watch items opens with affiliate rights renewals for both linear and streaming.

Sports strategy has been rationalized downward: NBA gone, the Venu Sports JV with ESPN and Fox abandoned in January 2025 after the Fubo suit (settled for $220M total, $55M WBD share), leaving the NCAA Men's Tournament through 2032 shared 50/50 with CBS under a loss-cap arrangement ($76M recorded in H1 2026). That CBS partnership is itself part of the antitrust problem — the merger would consolidate both sides of a major sports-rights joint venture, which is close to what the state complaint describes.

Studios retains a top-tier IP library with severe quarter-to-quarter variance; games are becoming a swing factor in both directions (+45% Q2 revenue on *LEGO Batman*). Streaming is genuinely improving in profitability but on international volume with 11% lower ARPU. Competitive position scores 4.2 — the trajectory is negative in linear, positive in streaming, with linear still carrying the majority of segment EBITDA.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Single-judge antitrust risk on a compressed calendar.** Both the state AG and WGA cases sit with the same N.D. Cal. judge, tried together in 12 days beginning March 2, 2027, with closing barred until five days after the merits determination. The product-market theory — combining two of the largest basic-cable portfolios and two major studios — is not frivolous. The WGA's monopsony theory on writing services is comparatively novel §7 territory and adds outcome variance. [unverified] The nearest precedent frequently cited for merger challenges brought by state AGs after federal clearance is the failed T-Mobile/Sprint challenge, but I cannot confirm that specific comparison from the provided sources; a completion probability above 50% is defensible on the clearances obtained but is not established as high.

**2. End Date versus trial calendar.** The merger agreement's End Date is March 4, 2027, "subject to an extension to June 4, 2027 in certain circumstances." The trial ends March 19, 2027. Closing on the base End Date is arithmetically impossible. The deal's survival past early March depends on the extension operating as the parties expect. Whether it is automatic, unilaterally invocable, or requires mutual consent is unverified. A mutual-consent construction would hand either party a walk option at precisely the moment litigation risk peaks.

**3. Break-fee ambiguity.** PSKY owes WBD $7.0B (~$2.79/share) in specified circumstances; WBD owes PSKY $3.0B plus up to $1,528M of junior-lien reimbursement plus the $2.8B Netflix fee in others. The direction on an antitrust-failure path is not established from available sources.

**4. Junior Lien Exchange Offer.** WBD must cause the exchange of certain notes for junior-lien paper; failure triggers a one-time payment of $100 per $1,000 principal, capped at $1,528M. PSKY has committed to pay while the deal lives. On a break, the deadline becomes the later of December 30, 2026 or 90 days post-termination, and the obligation becomes WBD's own near-term cash item.

**5. Goodwill.** After the $9,147M linear impairment in 2024, $25,861M of goodwill and $25,922M of intangibles remain. No indicators were identified in H1 2026 and the FY2025 annual test was qualitative. A break plus a linear re-rating is a plausible impairment path.

**6. Nokia RAND litigation.** The English High Court ordered an interim license payment in June 2026 in a global rate-setting proceeding on H.264/H.265 standard-essential patents, trial late 2026. WBD recorded an immaterial liability and states the eventual adjustment cannot be reasonably estimated — an uncapped, unquantified cash exposure on streaming delivery.

**7. Governance.** CEO FY2025 Summary Compensation Table total of $165.0M (3.2x FY2024) and "compensation actually paid" of $636.1M against five-year TSR indexed at 96 versus a peer group at 228. CAP is a mark-to-market construct that captured the takeover premium rather than realized cash, and the SCT increase may reflect retention economics negotiated around the sale process, but the juxtaposition is a legitimate pay-for-performance issue. More unusual is a **CEO excise-tax reimbursement agreement entered during H1 2026** — a §280G gross-up created after the merger agreement was signed, unquantified, and largely abandoned as a practice among large-cap issuers.

**8. Insider activity.** No open-market purchases in the trailing window. Zaslav exercised 2,089,876 options at $10.16 on July 13, 2026 and sold the same shares at $27.22 plus 94,906 more; Perrette sold 659,120 at $27.42; director Gould sold 600,000 at $27.35, a roughly 71% reduction in his position; Merchant and Campbell also sold. Every sale was at a 12% or greater discount to contractual deal value. Option-expiry mechanics on a $10.16 strike, 10b5-1 pre-commitment, tax funding, and concentration management are all plausible explanations and I do not attribute motive. What is factual is that nobody with inside knowledge bought at a discount to the deal price, which is the trade one would expect from an insider viewing completion as near-certain. Combined with the gross-up and the pay-versus-TSR record, management quality scores 4.0, with an offsetting credit for retiring $17.7B of principal at a discount in June 2025 and for extracting a $31.00 price from a competitive process that started near $12.50.

**Two overhangs cleared:** the NBA-disclosure securities class action was dismissed without leave to amend on March 30, 2026, and the consolidated derivative action on June 15, 2026.

Risk factors score 3.2. This is a binary with roughly one-in-three failure probability and 35-45% downside, plus an unverified fee direction worth $5.70/share and an unverified End Date mechanic.

Short interest of 2.6% of float and 2.9 days to cover indicates the position is populated by arbitrage longs rather than shorts; there is no crowded bearish view to squeeze, and no informed short community corroborating a break thesis.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $31.68 (+18.3%)

DOJ has reportedly already cleared the transaction, the EU cleared with remedies, and the UK CMA declined a Phase 2 referral, leaving only state and private plaintiffs. PSKY raised full-year guidance on August 4, said it "fully expects" the merger to close, and its CEO is "absolutely open" to settlement while expecting to win. A negotiated remedy — divesting overlapping cable networks or theatrical distribution assets — could resolve the case before or during trial and pull closing forward, raising the IRR. Ellison's $45.72B guarantee removes financing risk on the consideration. If the deal closes on the June 1, 2027 outer limit, holders receive $31.678 for a 23% annualized return, and an earlier settlement-driven close pushes the IRR toward 30-40%.

### Bear Case — $15.00 (−44%)

The court enjoins the merger in March 2027, or the End Date extension requires mutual consent and a party walks. WBD reverts to a standalone media company with 3.3x leverage, a current ratio of 0.78, revenue declining 6-11%, domestic linear subscribers falling 10% annually with affiliate rate increases down to +1%, and a Studios segment that just posted an 89% EBITDA decline. At 7.5x LTM Adjusted EBITDA, equity is $14.65. If the fee direction runs against WBD — $3.0B company fee plus up to $1,528M of junior-lien reimbursement plus reimbursement of the $2.8B Netflix fee, roughly $2.92/share — plus a fresh goodwill impairment against $25.9B of carrying value and a resumed separation into a weak linear tape, the low end of the range is nearer $13. The 52-week low of $10.76 was set 11 months ago with a worse capital structure but also without $7.3B of contingent fee exposure.

### Base Case — $27.50 (+2.7%)

Probability-weighting completion at roughly 70% against a $16 break gives $26.85; adding a modest allowance for a settlement-driven earlier close and for ticking accrual gives $27.50. That is fair value within the error bars of the inputs, which is why this is a HOLD rather than a directional call in either direction.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (hold_rationale: uncertain).** The outcome turns on a single district judge's merits ruling that I cannot handicap more precisely than the market already does.

- **Base case target: $27.50** (+2.7% from $26.78). Derived as 0.70 × $31.49 + 0.30 × $16.00 = $26.85, plus allowance for settlement-accelerated closing.
- **Bull case: $31.68** (+18.3%). Full consideration including 244 days of ticking at a June 1, 2027 close; $31.486 (+17.6%) at a March 24, 2027 close.
- **Bear case: $15.00** (−44.0%). 7.5x LTM Adjusted EBITDA standalone with an unfavorable fee outcome; range $13-18 depending on which break fee applies.
- **Timeframe:** resolution is calendar-defined. Trial March 2-19, 2027; closing barred until five days after the merits determination or June 1, 2027 at the latest. The nearest interim decision points are Q3 results (early November 2026, which should show the first full quarter of reduced cash interest) and any settlement or divestiture announcement.

**Upgrade triggers (to ACCUMULATE):**
1. Public confirmation that PSKY owes WBD the $7.0B reverse fee on an antitrust-failure termination, which raises break value to roughly $18-20 and lowers the breakeven completion probability from about 69% to the mid-50s.
2. A settlement with the state AGs and WGA, or a court-approved consent decree with divestitures, that removes the injunction and permits closing before March 2027.
3. Confirmation that the End Date extension to June 4, 2027 is automatic or unilaterally invocable upon a pending legal restraint, removing the calendar-conflict walk option.
4. A price below $24 with the deal record otherwise unchanged, which would imply a completion probability under 52% at a $16 break — a level I would consider too pessimistic given the clearances obtained.

**Downgrade triggers (to REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. An adverse pre-trial ruling — denial of a motion to dismiss on the licensing-market theory, or an unfavorable market-definition ruling — that materially raises injunction odds.
2. Public confirmation that the End Date extension requires mutual consent, or any indication either party is preparing to invoke termination in March 2027.
3. Evidence the fee direction runs against WBD on an antitrust failure, cutting break value toward $13.
4. Operating deterioration that lowers the break-case floor: a second consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue decline with Adjusted EBITDA turning negative year over year, a goodwill impairment charge, or withdrawal/non-renewal of the $4.0B receivables facility ahead of its June 2027 expiry.
5. Q3 or Q4 2026 free cash flow that fails to recover from the H1 $96M level once working capital normalizes and the lower interest run-rate lands, which would undermine the $3.1B normalized FCF anchor.

**Position management note.** Given near-term IV at 11.4% against 42.1% realized, and a six-month tenor at 25.1%, downside protection spanning the trial window is priced well below realized volatility. Holders who wish to retain the arb payoff while capping the break tail can do so cheaply; that structural observation does not change the fundamental rating.

## Conclusion

WBD's price is set by a contract and a courtroom, and both are legible. Shareholders are entitled to $31.00 plus roughly $0.49-$0.68 of ticking accrual, worth 17.6-18.3% from $26.78, on a transaction that has shareholder approval, EU and UK clearance, reported DOJ clearance, and a $45.72B personal guarantee behind the consideration. Against that, the parties have agreed not to close until five days after a merits determination in a 12-day trial that begins March 2, 2027, before a single judge in a plaintiff-selected venue, and the agreement's base End Date falls before that trial ends. The market prices completion at 59-70% depending on the assumed break price; my own handicap of 65-72% against a $15-18 break produces an expected value that straddles the current price.

The operating stub does not rescue the situation. Consolidated Adjusted EBITDA was flat in H1 only because the NBA rights fee disappeared alongside NBA revenue — a $560M one-time benefit against a 17% decline in domestic audience delivery and affiliate rate increases cut to +1%.
