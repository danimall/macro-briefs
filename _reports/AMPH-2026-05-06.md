## Executive Summary

Amphastar Pharmaceuticals is a specialty injectable and inhalation pharmaceutical company undergoing a painful product mix transition. Its legacy high-margin injectable glucagon and epinephrine products are eroding rapidly (-36% and -25% YoY respectively in FY2025), while its growth driver BAQSIMI (acquired from Eli Lilly in 2023) grew 12% organically but carries higher marketing costs and lower incremental margins. Operating income fell 32% in FY2025 and management has explicitly guided to continued gross margin compression in FY2026.

The stock trades at 11.5x trailing earnings, which appears cheap on a headline basis. However, consensus estimates have been revised down 9-10% over the past 90 days, the company missed Q4 by 22%, and the forward earnings trajectory is declining. At $23.43, the stock sits near the bottom of its 52-week range ($17.03-$31.26) and just above the Jefferies downgrade target of $22. With 18.1% short interest, options pricing 128% near-term IV (vs 50% realized), and no identifiable near-term catalyst to reverse margin compression, the risk/reward is unfavorable over a 12-month horizon.

## Company Overview

Amphastar is a vertically integrated biopharmaceutical company specializing in technically complex generic and proprietary injectable, inhalation, and intranasal products. It manufactures most APIs in-house across facilities in California, Massachusetts, France, and China. Key products include BAQSIMI (nasally-administered glucagon, 26% of revenue), Primatene MIST (OTC epinephrine inhaler, 15%), injectable epinephrine (10%), injectable glucagon (10%, declining), and lidocaine (8%).

The company has approximately 1,976 employees and generates ~$720M in annual revenue. Three wholesale distributors (McKesson, Cencora, Cardinal Health) account for 65% of revenue. The company went public in 2014 and remains founder-controlled, with CEO Jack Zhang and COO Mary Luo holding ~26% beneficial ownership.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue was $719.9M, down 2% from $732.0M in FY2024. Adjusting for the elimination of a $19.2M Lilly TSA revenue pass-through, product revenue grew approximately 1%. This stall follows strong growth of +29% (FY2023) and +14% (FY2024), both driven by the June 2023 BAQSIMI acquisition.

**Profitability — Deteriorating Rapidly:**
- Operating income: $140.4M (FY2025) vs. $205.4M (FY2024), a 32% decline
- Net income: $98.1M vs. $159.5M, a 39% decline
- Gross margin: 49.5% vs. 51.1% (160bps compression)
- Operating margin: 19.5% vs. 28.1% (860bps compression)

The profitability collapse is driven by: (1) glucagon injectable revenue falling 36% from pricing competition, (2) epinephrine multi-dose vial revenue declining 25%, (3) a $23.1M legal settlement, (4) R&D spending increasing 16% to $85.8M, and (5) selling/marketing spending increasing 16% to $43.9M for BAQSIMI commercialization.

**Q4 FY2025 Miss:** Adjusted EPS of $0.73 missed consensus of $0.94 by 22%. Glucagon injectable declined 45% YoY in Q4 alone. This was the first miss after three consecutive beats, signaling deteriorating visibility.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Cash and short-term investments: $282.8M
- Total debt: $610.4M (D/E ratio 1.07x)
- Current ratio: 4.02x
- Operating cash flow: $156.1M (down from $213.4M)
- Free cash flow: $121.2M (FCF yield ~11.6% on current market cap)

The balance sheet is adequate but burdened by acquisition debt. The Altman Z-Score of 2.32 places the company in the grey zone. Up to $575M in additional milestone payments to Lilly could be triggered by BAQSIMI sales thresholds, representing a significant contingent liability.

**Share Repurchases:** $75.6M repurchased in FY2025 (2.9M shares at ~$26.1 avg). At current prices the buyback is modestly accretive, but capital allocation to buybacks vs. debt reduction is questionable given the leverage profile and declining earnings.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term trajectory is negative.** Consensus FY2026 EPS is $3.25 (adjusted), down from $3.56 just 90 days ago (-8.7% revision). FY2027 consensus is $3.46, down from $3.83 (-9.7%). Current quarter estimates have fallen 11.2% in 90 days. These are accelerating downgrades, not stabilization.

**Product-level dynamics:**

| Product | FY2025 Revenue | YoY Growth | Outlook |
|---------|---------------|-----------|---------|
| BAQSIMI | $185.4M | +46% reported; ~12% organic | Positive but decelerating |
| Primatene MIST | $108.7M | -3% | Stable; HFC regulation risk |
| Epinephrine | $70.6M | -25% | Continued competitive pressure |
| Glucagon injectable | $69.1M | -36% | Terminal decline expected |
| Albuterol | ~$15M | New launch | Ramp but competitive market |
| Iron Sucrose | Launched Aug 2025 | New | Incremental contribution |
| Teriparatide | Launched Dec 2025 | New | Small market opportunity |

The fundamental challenge: glucagon and epinephrine are high-margin legacy products (~60-70% gross margin implied) that are structurally declining. BAQSIMI is growing but carries co-promotion costs (MannKind revenue share), higher marketing spend, and milestone liability. Every dollar of glucagon injectable lost may require $1.50-$2.00 of BAQSIMI revenue to replace on an operating income basis.

**Pipeline optionality is real but distant:**
- Biosimilar insulin (interchangeable designation): addresses $1.7B+ market but no timeline to approval
- Licensed proprietary peptides (AMP-105, 107, 109, 110): early-stage, years from revenue contribution
- Ipratropium bromide HFA: approved Feb 2026, planned Q2 launch — incremental but not needle-moving

Management guided to lower gross margins in FY2026. Without a specific catalyst to reverse this trajectory — a large new approval, a BAQSIMI milestone trigger, or stabilization of legacy pricing — there is no identifiable 12-month growth inflection.

## Valuation Assessment

At $23.43, AMPH trades at:
- 11.5x trailing P/E (on $2.03 GAAP EPS)
- ~7.2x adjusted P/E (on FY2025 adj. EPS ~$3.25)
- ~6.4x EV/EBITDA (estimated)
- ~11.6% FCF yield on trailing cash flow

These multiples appear cheap in absolute terms. However, the market is pricing in continued earnings deterioration. If FY2026 adjusted EPS of $3.25 materializes and the market maintains ~7x adjusted P/E, fair value is ~$22.75 — essentially where the stock trades now. The consensus median price target of $28 assumes earnings stabilization that management's own guidance contradicts.

The valuation is not a catalyst. AMPH is cheap because it is getting cheaper. The downward estimate revision trajectory (-9% over 90 days with no deceleration) suggests consensus has not yet fully adjusted to the margin pressure management has flagged. A stock at 11.5x declining earnings with negative estimate momentum is a classic value trap risk.

Jefferies downgraded to Hold with a $22 target in April 2026 — the analyst low is $22, implying 6% downside even from current levels in the bear case.

## Competitive Landscape

Amphastar's competitive position is mixed:

**Strengths:**
- Vertical integration (in-house API manufacturing) creates cost advantages and supply chain control
- Technical manufacturing expertise in sterile injectables and inhalation products provides barriers to entry
- BAQSIMI is the only FDA-approved nasal glucagon — first-mover advantage in a growing category
- Established relationships with all major GPOs and distributors

**Weaknesses:**
- The injectable market is experiencing increasing competitive intensity from Viatris, Cipla, Lupin, Hikma, Fresenius Kabi, and others
- Glucagon injectable is being cannibalized from both generic competition and the therapeutic shift toward ready-to-use formats
- Epinephrine market adding competitors (generic auto-injectors, multi-dose vial alternatives)
- Albuterol market is highly competitive with larger players (Teva, Cipla, etc.)

**Trajectory:** Deteriorating in legacy products, stable-to-improving in BAQSIMI and new launches. Net competitive position is weakening over the next 12 months as legacy erosion outpaces new product ramp.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Continued Generic Erosion (HIGH PROBABILITY, OCCURRING NOW):** Glucagon injectable and epinephrine multi-dose vial collectively lost $62.7M in FY2025. Management has guided to continued pricing pressure in FY2026. There is no floor on this erosion.

**2. BAQSIMI Growth Deceleration:** If BAQSIMI's organic growth rate slows below 10% (currently ~12%), the margin math becomes untenable. Co-promotion costs with MannKind and expanded sales force spending create operating leverage in reverse if growth disappoints.

**3. Milestone Liability:** Up to $575M in sales-based milestones owed to Lilly creates a ceiling on BAQSIMI's profitability contribution. These milestones, when triggered, would create step-function charges.

**4. HFC Regulatory Risk:** Primatene MIST and albuterol (~$120M+ combined revenue) use hydrofluorocarbon propellants subject to AIM Act phase-down. Supply uncertainty and potential reformulation requirements.

**5. Insider Stock Pledging:** CEO and COO have pledged 3.6M+ shares as loan collateral. At current prices near 52-week lows, margin call risk is elevated, which could create forced selling pressure.

**6. China Operations & Tariff Risk:** Significant API manufacturing in Nanjing. Current pharmaceutical exemptions from tariffs could be removed under the current administration's escalatory posture.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing extreme near-term uncertainty: 128% ATM IV for the nearest expiry (8 days out) versus 50% 1-year realized volatility. This 78-point spread, combined with steep backwardation in the term structure (128% near-term → 73% 3-month → 49% 6-month), indicates the market expects a specific near-term catalyst that could move the stock ±19%. Put/call skew at 75.4% near-term confirms elevated institutional demand for downside protection.

The short interest at 18.1% of float with 7.8 days to cover represents significant bearish institutional conviction, well above the typical pharmaceutical sector average of 5-8%.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** The next earnings report (likely late May/early June 2026 for Q1 FY2026) will reveal whether margin compression is tracking worse than already-lowered consensus. The options-implied ±19% move on the nearest expiry suggests something is imminent — possibly an 8-K filing, product update, or guidance revision.

**Timing window:** 4-8 weeks to next earnings. The near-term IV (128%) suggests the market expects resolution within days, but the fundamental thesis operates over a quarter.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** 18.1% SI with 7.8 days to cover is elevated. This creates meaningful squeeze risk on any positive catalyst (pipeline approval, BAQSIMI upside surprise, acquisition bid). However, the stock has declined from $31 to $23 without a meaningful squeeze, suggesting shorts have been right and the borrow is manageable.

**Borrow and options signal:** The extreme IV premium (128% vs 50% HV) makes put protection expensive, increasing the cost of hedged short positions. Elevated put skew (75.4%) confirms bearish options positioning. The OI put/call ratio of 0.10 is very low, suggesting limited put open interest relative to calls — this could indicate short positioning is equity-based rather than options-based.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case target is $17-19 (52-week low territory) on continued margin compression and another earnings miss. Upside risk is $27-28 on pipeline news, acquisition bid, or short squeeze. From $23.43: downside to $18 = 23% reward; upside to $28 = 20% risk. Risk/reward of approximately 1.2:1.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** Despite genuine fundamental deterioration, the risk/reward is insufficient for an active short. The 18.1% SI means the trade is semi-crowded, the stock has already declined 25% from August highs, the 1.2:1 reward-to-risk ratio is below the 2:1 threshold, and the elevated borrow cost (implied by the IV/HV spread) increases carrying cost. The correct positioning is to avoid owning the equity rather than actively shorting it.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($28-30):**
- BAQSIMI growth accelerates beyond 12%, approaching $220-250M run rate by FY2027
- New product launches (iron sucrose, teriparatide, ipratropium) collectively contribute $40-60M incremental revenue within 18 months
- Glucagon injectable decline stabilizes as competitors consolidate
- Biosimilar insulin enters Phase 3 with clear regulatory path
- Stock re-rates to 9-10x forward adjusted EPS of $3.25-3.50 = $29-35

**Bear Case ($17-19):**
- Gross margins compress further than guided as glucagon and epinephrine erosion accelerates
- BAQSIMI growth decelerates to mid-single-digits as the severe hypoglycemia market matures
- FY2026 adjusted EPS comes in at $2.80-3.00 (below current $3.25 consensus)
- Stock trades to 6x bear-case EPS = $17-18
- Forced insider selling from margin calls on pledged shares creates additional supply

**Base Case ($20-22):**
- Estimates continue drifting lower through FY2026 as margin guidance proves accurate
- BAQSIMI grows 8-10% but doesn't offset legacy erosion on an operating income basis
- Market maintains 7x adjusted P/E on $3.00-3.10 actual earnings = $21-22

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria:** AMPH becomes interesting if: (1) estimate revisions stabilize or turn positive for two consecutive months, indicating the margin trough is being absorbed by consensus, (2) stock trades below $18 (6x trough earnings) with evidence of BAQSIMI acceleration above 15% YoY, or (3) a material pipeline catalyst emerges (biosimilar insulin filing acceptance, proprietary peptide Phase 2 data).

**Timeline:** The headwinds are structural and will persist through at least FY2026. The earliest potential inflection is Q3 2026 earnings if new product contributions begin meaningfully offsetting legacy erosion. Avoid for 6-9 months minimum.

## Conclusion

Amphastar Pharmaceuticals presents deteriorating fundamentals dressed in cheap-looking valuation multiples. Operating income declined 32% in FY2025, management has guided to further margin compression, consensus estimates are falling at an accelerating rate, and the options market is pricing extreme near-term uncertainty. The 18.1% short interest reflects institutional conviction that the decline has further to run. While the company's pipeline and BAQSIMI franchise provide long-term optionality, neither constitutes a 12-month catalyst sufficient to reverse the current trajectory. At $23.43, the stock is priced at approximately fair value for a business with declining earnings power, with more downside risk than upside potential over the next two to three quarters.
