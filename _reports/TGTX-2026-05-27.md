## Executive Summary

TG Therapeutics is a commercial-stage biotech executing one of the more impressive single-product launch curves in recent specialty pharma history. BRIUMVI (ublituximab) for relapsing MS has scaled from $92M (2023) to $607M (2025) to a guided ~$925M for 2026, representing sustained 50%+ growth in its fourth year of commercialization. The stock trades at ~14x forward P/E — a significant discount to biotech peers at a 30.6x median — and estimate revisions have accelerated sharply (+34% over the past 30 days for the current quarter), consistent with a company whose earnings trajectory the market is underestimating.

Against this, the operational reality is more complicated than the revenue headline suggests. The company has missed consensus EPS four quarters running (by 23-61%), generates negative operating cash flow despite reported profitability, carries $306M in extended-term receivables (50% of annual revenue), faces 25.5% short interest, and depends entirely on a single product in a market where Roche's Ocrevus commands dominant share with vastly greater commercial infrastructure. The subcutaneous Phase 3 program represents the company's most critical strategic initiative — failure would permanently disadvantage BRIUMVI against self-administered alternatives.

The risk/reward is favorable at current levels relative to the prior peak ($44.65) given the raised guidance and positive ENHANCE topline results, but the pattern of consistent EPS misses, cash conversion weakness, and elevated short interest argue against a high-conviction directional call until the company demonstrates operational leverage translating to actual cash generation.

## Company Overview

TG Therapeutics is a fully-integrated, commercial-stage biotech with 399 employees, focused exclusively on B-cell mediated diseases. The company's sole marketed product is BRIUMVI (ublituximab-xiiy), an anti-CD20 monoclonal antibody approved in December 2022 for relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis. BRIUMVI differentiates primarily on its 1-hour infusion time and twice-annual dosing schedule (every 24 weeks after initial loading doses).

The pipeline includes: subcutaneous ublituximab (Phase 3, >75% enrolled), simplified IV dosing (ENHANCE Phase 3, positive topline results May 2026), ublituximab in myasthenia gravis (enrolling), and azer-cel (allogeneic CD19 CAR-T for progressive MS, Phase 1). Manufacturing is entirely outsourced to Samsung Biologics and FUJIFILM Diosynth.

Ex-U.S. commercialization was licensed to Neuraxpharm in August 2023 for $140M upfront plus up to $492.5M in milestones and tiered royalties up to 30%.

## Growth Analysis

BRIUMVI is penetrating the largest treatment class in MS (anti-CD20), capturing share from both new starts and Ocrevus switches. The trajectory from $92M → $607M → ~$925M in three years positions the company to reach $1B+ revenue on a single product within 2027.

**Growth Catalysts:**
1. **Subcutaneous formulation (Phase 3, data 2026-2027):** If approved, opens self-administration channel competing with Novartis's Kesimpta. This is the single most important value driver beyond the base IV franchise.
2. **ENHANCE simplified dosing (positive topline May 2026):** Removes the Day 1/Day 15 loading complexity for new patients, potentially accelerating new starts.
3. **Myasthenia gravis expansion:** Opens a new $2B+ market, though early-stage.
4. **Azer-cel CAR-T for progressive MS:** High-risk/high-reward optionality. Allogeneic approach offers scalability advantages if efficacy demonstrated.
5. **Ex-U.S. milestones:** Up to $492.5M remaining from Neuraxpharm plus royalties.

**Growth Risks:**
- Revenue growth is decelerating (87% → 50%) as the base scales
- Penetration ceiling uncertainty: Ocrevus has ~$8B+ globally; BRIUMVI's addressable ceiling in the U.S. may be $1.5-2.0B at maturity
- Spending growth (R&D + SG&A combined growing 55-60%) is absorbing most revenue growth

**Estimate Revisions:** Current quarter EPS revised up +33.8% over 30 days (from $0.34 to $0.46). Next year revised +11.8% over 90 days. This constitutes a meaningful positive revision signal, though the 4-for-4 miss track record tempers confidence that these estimates will be met.

The critical question is whether those estimates will be achieved. Four consecutive misses suggest either: (1) consensus doesn't properly model spending ramp, or (2) revenue timing/collections create lumpiness. The +34% 30-day revision cycle suggests analysts are repricing upward, but the history warrants skepticism.

**Analyst targets:** Mean $48, median $50, range $17-$70. At $39.68, the stock sits 21% below median consensus. The wide range ($17-$70) reflects genuine disagreement about the terminal value of a single-product franchise.

## Competitive Landscape

BRIUMVI operates in the anti-CD20 MS treatment class, competing against:

1. **Ocrevus (Roche):** Dominant incumbent, ~$8B+ global revenue. Now available in both IV and subcutaneous formulations. Roche has overwhelming commercial infrastructure advantage.
2. **Kesimpta (Novartis):** Self-administered subcutaneous anti-CD20, growing rapidly. Already offers the convenience patients increasingly demand.
3. **Emerging BTK inhibitors:** Tolebrutinib (Sanofi), fenebrutinib (Roche) could eventually offer oral alternatives to infusion/injection.

BRIUMVI's competitive advantage — 1-hour infusion vs. Ocrevus's longer administration — is real but narrowing as Roche shortened Ocrevus infusion times and launched subcutaneous options. TG's ability to deliver its own subcutaneous formulation is therefore critical to long-term competitiveness.

The company's 399-person organization is executing above expectations against Roche's vastly larger field force, which suggests strong physician relationships and effective MSL/sales execution. Patent protection through 2042 (composition of matter) and biosimilar exclusivity through December 2034 provide meaningful moat.

## Risk Assessment

**Single-Product Risk (HIGH):** 100% of commercial revenue from BRIUMVI. Any safety signal, competitive displacement, or payer policy change directly threatens the entire enterprise.

**Cash Conversion (MODERATE-HIGH):** $306M in receivables on $607M revenue (50% of sales outstanding) while generating negative OCF. If collection cycles lengthen further or infusion center customers face financial stress, this becomes a liquidity event on a company with only $200M cash and $246M debt.

**Short Interest (HIGH):** 25.5% of float shorted with 14.9 days to cover. This is an extreme level suggesting institutional capital has a specific thesis about why consensus is wrong. The short thesis likely centers on: (a) spending growth absorbing all revenue gains, (b) EPS miss pattern continuing, (c) competitive pressure intensifying as Roche/Novartis respond, or (d) receivables/cash conversion never normalizing.

**Subcutaneous Execution (MODERATE):** Formulation challenges with high-concentration biologics are real. Failure would permanently lock BRIUMVI into the infusion center channel while competitors offer home administration.

**Regulatory/Pricing (MODERATE):** IRA drug pricing provisions, CMS GUARD/GLOBE models targeting MS drugs, and tariff rhetoric on imported pharmaceuticals all represent medium-term headwinds to pricing power.

## Conclusion

TG Therapeutics presents an unusual combination: exceptional revenue growth, attractive forward valuation metrics, and accelerating estimate revisions — offset by consistent earnings misses, negative cash generation, single-product risk, and 25.5% short interest representing significant informed capital betting against the consensus view.

The prior score trajectory decline (6.7 → 5.6) reflected the pattern of EPS misses and cash flow weakness. Today's elevated score relative to last month (6.1 vs. 5.6) reflects: (1) the raised 2026 guidance confirming the commercial trajectory, (2) positive ENHANCE topline results, and (3) the meaningful 30-day estimate revision acceleration. However, until the company demonstrates actual EPS delivery and cash conversion, the persistent miss pattern and elevated short interest create too much uncertainty for a BUY recommendation.

HOLD with growth orientation. The stock is worth owning if you believe operating leverage will emerge and SubQ succeeds, but the evidence of that emergence has not yet appeared in the cash flow statement.
