**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Angi Inc. is a home services marketplace that has deliberately destroyed its legacy business model without demonstrating a viable replacement. Revenue has declined 45% from $1.89B (FY2022) to $1.03B (FY2025), with Q1 2026 showing continued deterioration and a net loss of $9M. The company's "homeowner choice" transition eliminated advertising revenue entirely — from $312M in FY2024 to effectively zero — while proprietary revenue growth of 7% YoY is wholly insufficient to compensate. Active service professionals declined 22% YoY to 105,000, indicating accelerating supply-side attrition.

The stock collapsed 33% on May 6, 2026 following Q1 results that missed estimates and included the withdrawal of forward guidance. At $5.40, the stock trades at 12x trailing EPS of $0.94, but this multiple is misleading: consensus estimates for FY2026 have been slashed 62% in the past 30 days to $0.31, implying a forward P/E of 17.4x on rapidly deteriorating earnings. With $473M in senior notes maturing August 2028, declining cash balances ($245M and falling), and an AI pivot that management is executing while simultaneously cutting product development spend 61%, the risk/reward profile is severely negative.

## Company Overview

Angi operates a digital marketplace connecting homeowners with service professionals across 500+ categories. Formed in 2017 from the combination of HomeAdvisor and Angie's List under IAC's umbrella, the company became fully independent following IAC's March 2025 spin-off. Revenue derives primarily from lead fees paid by service professionals, with minor contributions from pre-priced services and memberships.

The company is mid-transformation: in January 2025, it implemented "homeowner choice" — allowing consumers to select their preferred professional rather than being automatically matched. This shift deliberately killed the high-volume network lead business (revenue down 72% in FY2025, another 56% in Q1 2026 YoY) in favor of higher-quality proprietary matches. The bet is that better consumer-Pro matching will create a healthier marketplace long-term. The interim cost has been catastrophic.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** Declining at double-digit rates since FY2023. Q1 2026 revenue of $238.2M fell short of estimates, representing a ~3% YoY decline — an improvement in the rate of decline from -13% in FY2025, but still negative.

**Profitability:** The company achieved operating profitability in FY2024 ($22M) and FY2025 ($65M) through cost-cutting, but Q1 2026 reverted to a $9.5M operating loss as marketing spend surged 18% YoY to $140M (59% of revenue). Adjusted EBITDA of $22.9M in Q1 was down from $27.7M a year prior.

**Cash Position:** Cash of $244.6M at March 31, 2026, down $60M from year-end 2025. Operating cash flow was -$18M in Q1 (seasonally weakest quarter, but concerning given the trajectory). At this burn rate, the cash buffer erodes materially before the August 2028 debt maturity.

**Debt:** $473M in 3.875% senior notes due August 2028, reduced from $500M through buybacks at a discount (fair value ~89 cents on the dollar, signaling credit concern). A $175M undrawn revolving facility provides liquidity but contains a 4.0x net leverage covenant.

**Balance Sheet Risk:** Goodwill of $890M vastly exceeds both market cap ($218M) and enterprise value (~$450M). A goodwill impairment test at year-end 2025 showed fair value exceeding carrying value by only $109.7M. Further deterioration will likely trigger a write-down.

**Capital Allocation:** Management spent $148.7M buying back stock in FY2025 at an average of $14.15/share. The stock is now $5.40. This represents ~$96M in destroyed shareholder value and a fundamental failure of capital allocation judgment, particularly given that the cash could have retired debt.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are severely challenged:

- **Revenue trajectory:** -28%, -13%, -13% in the last three full fiscal years, with Q1 2026 still negative YoY
- **Active Pros declining 22% YoY** to 105,000
- **Pro churn reportedly rising to 5.0%** — professionals are leaving faster than they're being recruited
- **Network revenue effectively zeroed out** — a $312M annual revenue stream (FY2024) is gone
- **Proprietary revenue grew 7% in Q1 2026** — insufficient to offset the collapse elsewhere

The company's stated growth path centers on an "AI-native platform" targeting "$5B in revenue opportunity." However, product development spending was slashed 61% to $10.4M quarterly. You cannot build a transformative technology platform while cutting engineering resources by more than half. Management's claim appears to rely on third-party AI tools rather than proprietary development, which limits competitive advantage.

**Estimate Revisions:** Consensus EPS for the current year was slashed from $0.82 to $0.31 in 30 days (-62%). Next year estimates fell from $1.24 to $0.70 (-44%). This velocity of downward revision signals fundamental deterioration beyond what current prices may reflect.

## Valuation Assessment

At $5.40 with trailing EPS of $0.94, the 12x P/E appears optically cheap versus the peer median of 47.4x. However:

- Forward P/E on revised FY2026 estimates ($0.31) = 17.4x
- Forward P/E on FY2027 estimates ($0.70) = 7.7x
- EV/Revenue on trailing basis: ~0.45x on $1.03B FY2025 revenue

The 7.7x forward P/E on next-year estimates would be genuinely cheap if those estimates stabilize. But estimates have been revised downward by 44-62% in 30 days with no sign of bottoming. The withdrawal of forward guidance signals management itself lacks visibility into earnings trajectory.

**EV/EBITDA:** On FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA of $140M, EV/EBITDA is approximately 3.2x. On a run-rate Q1 2026 basis ($22.9M × 4 = ~$92M), it's ~4.9x. Both look cheap, but EBITDA is declining while debt is fixed.

The fundamental valuation question is whether the business stabilizes before cash depletes and debt matures. At current cash burn rates and revenue trajectory, the enterprise value is increasingly dominated by the debt claim rather than equity upside.

## Competitive Landscape

Angi's competitive position is deteriorating:

- **Google's local services ads** are an existential threat — Google can match consumers with service professionals while capturing the search intent upstream
- **AI-powered search** (SGE and competitors) could reduce click-through to marketplace intermediaries entirely
- **Supply-side attrition** means fewer professionals to match with consumers, degrading marketplace quality
- **Thumbtack, TaskRabbit, Yelp** all compete for consumer attention and professional supply
- **Word-of-mouth and offline referrals** remain the dominant channel for home services — digital penetration is still low

The "homeowner choice" model is theoretically superior to the old spam-leads approach, but it requires scale on both sides of the marketplace to function. With Pros leaving at accelerating rates, the marketplace is shrinking rather than growing, creating a negative network effects dynamic.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Debt Maturity (August 2028):** $473M in notes mature in approximately 27 months. With cash declining and revenue falling, refinancing will be expensive or unavailable if fundamentals don't improve.

**2. AI Pivot Execution:** Management is betting the company on an unproven AI transformation while cutting the engineering team needed to execute it. The 12-month timeline announced in Q1 2026 creates a hard deadline for demonstrating progress.

**3. Continued Revenue Decline:** The rate of decline has slowed but hasn't stopped. If proprietary revenue growth cannot accelerate while network revenue stabilizes near zero, total revenue continues shrinking.

**4. Goodwill Impairment:** $890M in goodwill against $218M market cap. A write-down is probable within 2-3 quarters given current trajectory, which while non-cash, further impairs borrowing capacity and investor confidence.

**5. Management Turnover:** COO departed May 1, 2026. CFO was replaced. Multiple director departures in early 2026. Organizational instability during a critical transformation period compounds execution risk.

**6. Macro Sensitivity:** Home services spending correlates with consumer confidence and housing activity. High interest rates suppress remodeling and home improvement demand, creating a hostile macro environment for discretionary home services spending.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case:** Proprietary revenue stabilizes and grows 15-20% annually. AI tools reduce customer acquisition costs, allowing marketing spend to moderate. EBITDA recovers to $120M+ by FY2027. Debt trades back to par. Stock re-rates to 6-8x EBITDA ($450-600M EV) implying equity value of $5-9/share. Catalyst: two consecutive quarters of positive total revenue growth + improving Pro metrics.

**Bear Case:** Revenue continues declining as Pro attrition accelerates. AI pivot fails to differentiate the platform. Cash burns at $40-60M/year, reaching $150M by 2028. Refinancing is unavailable at reasonable terms. Company faces a restructuring or distressed debt exchange. Equity value approaches zero. Catalyst: missed debt covenant, further guidance withdrawals, Pro count dropping below 80K.

**Base Case:** Muddling through with flat-to-declining revenue, $80-100M EBITDA, and eventual refinancing at elevated rates (8-10%). Stock oscillates between $4-8 for two years until debt maturity provides clarity. No structural re-rating without revenue growth.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** August 2028 debt maturity creates a ~27-month countdown. Near-term catalysts include: (a) Q2 2026 earnings (likely July/August 2026) where continued revenue decline and marketing spend acceleration compress EBITDA further; (b) potential goodwill impairment recognition in Q2/Q3 2026; (c) any covenant breach on the revolving facility.

**Timing window:** 3-6 months for near-term catalysts (next 1-2 earnings). 18-27 months for the debt maturity catalyst.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** SI at 13.2% with 3.5 days to cover — elevated but manageable. Market cap of $218M means position sizes are small. Squeeze risk is moderate: any positive AI-related announcement could produce a 20-30% short squeeze in this low-liquidity name.

**Borrow and options signal:** Stock is low-priced and low-liquidity, which typically means moderate borrow costs. The 13.2% SI suggests shares are available. Without options data, IV signal is unknown.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case implies $2-3/share (50-55% downside). Squeeze risk to $7-8 (30-48% upside). Risk/reward approximately 1.5:1 to 2:1 depending on timing.

**Verdict:** **NOT SHORTABLE** — While the fundamental thesis is compelling, the $218M market cap creates liquidity constraints, the low absolute price amplifies percentage moves on minimal news, and the lack of a specific near-term dated catalyst (guidance was already withdrawn, estimates already slashed) means the thesis could take 12+ months to fully play out with meaningful carry and squeeze risk in between. The appropriate position is to avoid owning it, not to actively short it.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria:** (1) Two consecutive quarters of positive total revenue growth YoY; (2) Active Pro count stabilizing above 95K with declining churn; (3) Debt refinanced or retired below $300M; (4) Forward EPS estimates stabilizing or rising for 60+ consecutive days.

**Timeline:** Headwinds expected to persist 12-18 months minimum. The August 2028 debt maturity creates a hard deadline for business improvement. Monitor quarterly earnings for stabilization signals.

## Conclusion

Angi presents a case study in value destruction through misguided capital allocation and a business model transformation executed without adequate financial resources. The company spent $148.7M buying back stock at $14.15 when that capital should have been used to de-lever ahead of a revenue decline. It is now attempting an AI pivot while cutting the engineering team, withdrawing guidance while claiming a "$5B revenue opportunity," and watching its marketplace shrink on both the supply (Pros -22%) and demand (revenue still declining) sides.

At $5.40, the stock trades at 3.2x trailing EBITDA, which would be cheap for a stable business. Angi is not stable. It is a declining business with $473M in debt maturing in approximately 27 months, accelerating cost growth (marketing +18%), and an unproven strategic pivot. Consensus estimates have been cut by more than half in 30 days with no sign of stabilization. Management credibility is impaired by the buyback destruction and guidance withdrawal. No insider is buying stock.

The correct action is to avoid this name entirely until there is concrete evidence of revenue stabilization, Pro growth, and a credible refinancing path. The optionality embedded in the AI pivot thesis is real but unquantifiable and offset by the downside risk of debt maturity without adequate cash generation.
