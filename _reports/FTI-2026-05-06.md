## Executive Summary

TechnipFMC has executed one of the most impressive operational transformations in energy services over the past four years, taking adjusted EBITDA margins from 8.8% (2021) to 18.5% (2025) while simultaneously reducing debt by 60%, achieving investment-grade status, and retiring 11.3% of shares outstanding. The company's integrated subsea model (iEPCI) has created a structural competitive advantage in an oligopolistic market with only three Tier 1 contractors globally.

At $74.20, the stock trades at approximately 25.1x FY2026E earnings ($2.95) and 21.4x FY2027E ($3.46), with positive estimate revisions of approximately +5.9% and +4.7% respectively over the past 90 days. The trailing P/E of 28.6x is below the peer median (37.2x), which is skewed by elevated multiples at NOV and KGS. FTI's forward growth trajectory (17% EPS growth FY26→FY27) combined with its quality metrics (net cash, investment grade, approximately 1.5–1.6 years of backlog coverage against 2026 guided revenue) warrants a premium to cyclical OFS peers. The stock has appreciated 156% over the past year, reflecting fundamental improvement rather than multiple expansion detached from reality.

The key risk is the Iran conflict resolution scenario: a diplomatic deal could moderate oil prices and slow future FID activity, while continued conflict and elevated oil prices could accelerate deepwater investment. However, with 66.4% of backlog extending beyond 2026 and current projects already funded, near-term earnings are largely insulated from oil price movements.

## Company Overview

TechnipFMC is a UK-incorporated, Houston-headquartered global leader in subsea energy infrastructure. The company operates through two segments:

**Subsea (~89% of revenue):** Designs, manufactures, and installs subsea production systems, umbilicals, risers, and flowlines via its proprietary iEPCI integrated model. This segment generated $2.21B in Q1 2026 revenue (+14.1% YoY) at a 15.8% operating margin.

**Surface Technologies (~11% of revenue):** Supplies wellhead systems and pressure control equipment for onshore completions, concentrated in the Middle East (ADNOC, Saudi Aramco). Q1 2026 revenue of $284M with 13.0% operating margin.

The company's differentiation lies in vertical integration — according to company disclosures, it is the only subsea contractor offering combined subsea production systems (SPS) + subsea umbilicals/risers/flowlines (SURF) + installation in a single contract. This reduces operator cycle times by up to 12 months and costs by up to approximately 25% versus unbundled procurement.

### Balance Sheet Strength

TechnipFMC maintains a net cash position of $540M ($961M cash less $420M total debt). This is exceptional for a capital-intensive services company. Remaining private placement notes mature 2027-2033 at 3.75-4.0% fixed rates. The $1.25B undrawn revolver and $1.0B commercial paper program provide ample liquidity. Investment-grade ratings (BBB-/Baa2) from all three agencies confirm balance sheet quality.

The -$3.7B accumulated deficit is a legacy artifact from the 2017 merger-era impairments and has no bearing on current financial capacity.

### Cash Flow & Capital Allocation

Q1 2026 operating cash flow was $333M. Full-year 2025 operating cash flow was $1.76B, and with $317M in capex, free cash flow was approximately $1.45B. Capital allocation priorities are clear:

1. **Share buybacks:** $265M in Q1 2026 alone; $1.9B remaining authorization (~6.4% of current market cap)
2. **Debt reduction:** From $1.05B (2022) to $420M (Q1 2026)
3. **Dividend:** Negligible at $0.20/share (~0.3% yield)

Management's strong preference for buybacks over dividends suggests confidence in share undervaluation relative to intrinsic value.

### Contract Liabilities & Revenue Visibility

Net contract liabilities of -$1.06B indicate customers pre-fund projects, providing a working capital tailwind. Total backlog of $16.5B versus 2026 guided revenue of $10.35B–$10.90B provides approximately 1.5–1.6 years of booked revenue. 66.4% extends beyond 2026.

### Structural Growth Drivers

1. **iEPCI adoption:** Still in early stages of operator conversion. Each major operator converting to integrated procurement adds permanently to the addressable market.
2. **Subsea 2.0 cost reduction:** The configure-to-order platform reduces manufacturing cost up to approximately 25% and cycle time by as much as 12 months, improving project economics and enabling FIDs at lower oil prices.
3. **New frontiers:** Suriname, Namibia, Mozambique expanding addressable deepwater market.
4. **Carbon capture:** Early-stage positioning in subsea CCS (HISEP for Petrobras, Northern Endurance) provides optionality beyond traditional oil and gas.

### Growth Constraints

Vessel utilization across Tier 1 contractors is approaching full capacity. This supports pricing power but could limit volume growth without fleet investment. Engineering resource constraints may cap execution capacity in peak demand scenarios.

## Valuation Assessment

At $74.20, FTI trades at:
- 25.1x FY2026E EPS ($2.95)
- 21.4x FY2027E EPS ($3.46)
- ~12.5x EV/EBITDA on 2026E (~$2.4B EBITDA at 18.5%+ margins on ~$10B+ revenue)

**Versus peers:** The peer median P/E of 37.2x is skewed by NOV (83.8x) and KGS (75.1x). WFRD at 16.7x is the closest comparable pure-play OFS company, but WFRD lacks FTI's iEPCI integration, backlog visibility, and margin trajectory. The 25x forward P/E reflects a quality premium partially justified by structural attributes (net cash, investment grade, backlog depth, oligopoly position).

**Valuation math:** At $3.46 FY2027E EPS and a 25x multiple (maintaining current multiple on growing earnings), fair value is $86.50 — 16.6% above current. At a 23x multiple (assuming some multiple compression as growth decelerates), fair value is $79.60 — 7.3% upside. The analyst consensus median target is approximately $76, essentially the current price level, but high targets imply 21% upside.

**Case for underpricing:** The current multiple reflects residual OFS cyclicality discount. TechnipFMC's backlog coverage and contracted nature of revenue make it more comparable to an industrial compounder than a traditional cyclical. If the market reclassifies FTI's earnings quality as "infrastructure" rather than "cyclical services," a re-rating to 28-30x forward earnings is plausible, implying $97-104.

**Constraints on valuation score:** The stock is 4.6% below its 52-week high ($77.78) and has appreciated 156% in 12 months. Estimate revisions at +5.9% (90-day) are positive but moderate — not the 30%+ velocity that signals dramatic mispricing. The 28.6x trailing P/E is approaching mid-cycle levels for quality industrials, and a significant portion of the earnings catch-up trade has already occurred.

## Competitive Landscape

The subsea market is a structural oligopoly with high barriers to entry:

| Competitor | Subsea Backlog | Key Differentiator |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| TechnipFMC | $15.8B | Only integrated SPS + SURF + installation (iEPCI) per company disclosures |
| Subsea7 | $13.8B | Strong in conventional SURF, renewables exposure |
| Saipem | ~$12B subsea | Broader offshore construction; less pure-play |

**OneSubsea** (SLB/Aker Solutions JV) competes on subsea production equipment but does not offer integrated EPCI services. FTI therefore faces limited direct competition for its highest-margin product — the fully integrated project delivery.

The competitive position is strengthening: (1) operator preference for iEPCI is growing as cost/time savings are proven, (2) Subsea 2.0 creates manufacturing cost advantage versus competitors still using bespoke engineering approaches, (3) vessel constraints across the industry prevent new entrants, and (4) the backlog itself becomes a barrier — engineering resources committed to existing contracts cannot be redirected by competitors.

## Risk Assessment

### Geopolitical / Oil Price Scenario (HIGH IMPACT)

In a diplomatic resolution scenario, oil prices could moderate, slowing future FIDs for marginal deepwater projects. However, Subsea 2.0 economics make deepwater viable at lower price points than historical cycles, and the majority of current backlog extends beyond 2026 regardless of new order intake timing.

In a conflict persistence scenario with elevated or rising oil prices, deepwater investment could accelerate as operators seek supply security. However, FID decisions also depend on operator capital discipline, project readiness, service capacity, and long-cycle price assumptions — the link between oil prices and deepwater investment is not mechanical. TechnipFMC's own 10-K filed before the conflict referenced pre-conflict 2026 Brent forecasts of $50–$60/bbl, underscoring how rapidly the geopolitical landscape has shifted.

### Fixed-Price Contract Execution

Large iEPCI contracts are fixed-price. Cost overruns on any single project could materially impact quarterly results. Management notes legacy contracts (<10% of backlog) have been largely worked off, and newer contracts incorporate Subsea 2.0 cost structures. The risk is manageable but non-zero.

### Capacity Constraints

Full vessel utilization across Tier 1 contractors means FTI may face execution bottlenecks limiting revenue growth above guidance. This supports pricing but caps near-term volume upside.

### Insider Selling

CEO sold ~733K shares (~$46M) in March 2026; CFO sold ~$8M. While tied to option exercises (strike prices as low as $16.47) and consistent with long-tenured executives monetizing gains, the magnitude warrants monitoring. The CEO retained ~2.4M shares. Given that estimate revisions remain positive and Q1 2026 beat expectations, this selling pattern is consistent with routine diversification rather than a bearish signal.

### Options Market Signal

Based on options-market data (point-in-time and changes intraday), near-term implied volatility appeared elevated relative to historical volatility, with call-dominated flow suggesting the options market is pricing volatility but not directional bearishness. These observations should be treated as supplemental context rather than durable investment signals.

## Conclusion

TechnipFMC warrants a BUY rating at $74.20. The stock is 4.6% below its 52-week high ($77.78) and has run significantly, but the underlying business transformation is structural — margin expansion from iEPCI integration, Subsea 2.0 cost reductions, and legacy project roll-off compound as more revenue flows through the higher-margin framework.

The 25x forward P/E on FY2026E is full relative to the OFS peer group but cheap relative to the company's quality characteristics: net cash balance sheet, investment-grade credit, contracted backlog depth, oligopolistic market position, and 17% EPS growth into FY2027. The ongoing buyback program provides mechanical EPS support, and the company's recent pattern of exceeding expectations suggests estimates may remain too conservative.

The primary risk is geopolitical — specifically, whether Iran diplomatic resolution sustainably normalizes oil prices enough to slow deepwater FIDs. Even in this scenario, the contracted backlog provides 2+ years of earnings protection, and Subsea 2.0 economics lower the oil price threshold for project viability. The asymmetry favors maintaining the BUY: conflict persistence is incrementally positive for FTI's order pipeline, while diplomatic resolution has limited near-term earnings impact given existing backlog.

Position sizing: 2-3% given elevated valuation and geopolitical uncertainty. Reduce to 1-2% if oil falls below $90 sustained for 30+ days.
