## Executive Summary

Cboe Global Markets delivered a record Q1 2026 — $728.9M net revenue (+29% YoY), $3.70 adjusted EPS (+48% YoY, beating consensus by approximately 10-14%) — driven by structural growth in index options volumes and a geopolitical/macro environment that sustains hedging demand. The new CEO Craig Donohue is executing a textbook portfolio rationalization: divesting non-core assets (Canada, Australia, Japan, CEDX), cutting 20% of the workforce (~330 positions), and refocusing capital on the high-margin SPX/VIX franchise, event markets, and tokenization. Raised 2026 organic revenue growth guidance to "low double-digit to mid-teens" with $40-50M in annualized cost savings from the May 2026 workforce action (which, combined with earlier realignment initiatives, supports a broader reduction in adjusted operating expense guidance of ~$26M at midpoint) confirms accelerating fundamentals.

At the current price of approximately $281.65, CBOE has rallied roughly 40% from its 52-week low of $200.88. At ~20.6x consensus FY26 EPS of $13.65, the stock trades modestly below the peer median forward P/E. The analyst consensus 1-year target is approximately $288 (range $246-$352). While the business quality is exceptional and structural tailwinds persist, the current price largely embeds the Q1 beat, the guidance raise, and the cost savings. The stock is trading near the middle of its 52-week range ($200.88-$305.68), and while not egregiously overvalued, the risk/reward at current levels is balanced. I would be more aggressive on a pullback to $240-255 (18-19x forward) or on evidence that the volume trajectory is sustaining above Q1 levels through Q2.

## Company Overview

Cboe Global Markets operates the world's largest options exchange and the third-largest U.S. equities exchange. Five business segments generate $2.43B in net revenue (FY2025): Options (63%), North American Equities (17%), Europe & Asia Pacific (11%), Futures (5%), and Global FX (4%). The crown jewel is the exclusive license to list S&P 500 Index options (SPX) through December 31, 2032, which along with VIX products generates ~68% of net revenue from exclusively licensed or proprietary products.

The business model is asset-light (only $71M capex on $1.68B FCF) with operating leverage: incremental volume flows largely to the bottom line once fixed technology and regulatory costs are covered. Data Vantage (26% of net revenue) provides high-margin recurring revenue from proprietary market data products.

## Competitive Landscape

**Dominant Position in Index Options:**
Cboe's exclusive SPX license through 2032 creates a de facto monopoly on S&P 500 Index options. Combined with the proprietary VIX methodology, approximately 68% of net revenue comes from products with no direct exchange-traded substitute. SPX's structural advantages (European-style, cash-settled, Section 1256 tax treatment) create permanent differentiation vs. SPY options on competing exchanges.

**Erosion in Multi-Listed & Equities:**
U.S. equities market share declined from 11.4% to 10.0%, and net capture per 100 touched shares fell 32% YoY. The multi-listed options segment faces pricing pressure from 18+ competing exchanges. These segments contribute lower margins and face secular pressure from off-exchange venues. The CEO's strategic focus on proprietary products and Data Vantage represents correct prioritization.

**European Clearing Expansion:**
Cboe Clear Europe grew clearing share from 34% to 39.4%, with interoperability providing competitive advantage. European market share in equities grew to 25.0% from 23.6%.

**Emerging Threats:**
Event prediction markets (Kalshi-type) could compete for volatility exposure currently served by SPX/VIX. However, the institutional scale, regulatory framework, and embedded liquidity of SPX options create high barriers to substitution for professional users.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Volume Normalization Risk (Medium-High Probability):**
Q1 2026's exceptional volumes benefited from geopolitical crisis, tariff uncertainty, and CPI concerns. If any of these resolve (even partially), options ADV could decline 10-20% from peak, compressing revenue toward the lower end of guidance. This is the primary near-term risk at current valuations.

**2. SPX License Renewal (Low Probability, Existential Impact):**
Renewal in 2032 is six years away, reducing urgency. S&P has strong financial incentive to renew (significant royalty revenue). However, strategic shifts at S&P Global could alter the relationship's economics over time.

**3. Regulatory Implementation (SEC Tick Size/Access Fee):**
November 2026 implementation of new tick size and access fee cap rules could reduce U.S. equities transaction revenue. Management is already de-emphasizing this segment.

**4. Execution Risk — Management Transition:**
Complete C-suite turnover (CEO, COO, Head of Derivatives, Strategy) within 12 months creates integration risk. The 20% workforce reduction could result in institutional knowledge loss.

**5. Customer Concentration:**
Three clearing members account for ~71% of transaction fees. While these firms have no substitute for SPX access, counterparty consolidation could provide pricing leverage over time.

## Conclusion

Cboe is among the highest-quality financial franchises available: a monopoly on S&P 500 Index options with strong FCF generation ($1.68B on $71M capex, though 2025 was boosted by clearing-related working capital flows), structural tailwinds from secular options growth, and a new CEO executing correct strategic decisions with measurable urgency. The Q1 2026 results confirmed accelerating growth, and estimate revisions are strongly positive (+22% over 90 days).

At approximately $281.65, the stock trades at ~21x forward earnings — below the peer median and below its own recent historical range — which reflects either appropriate skepticism about volume sustainability or an opportunity if growth persists. The analyst consensus target of ~$288 suggests limited near-term upside on a consensus basis. The fundamental thesis is unambiguously positive, and the valuation is not stretched, but the volume-sensitive nature of the business and the degree to which Q1's exceptional results may normalize introduce uncertainty about the appropriate entry point.

This is a growth HOLD with a path to BUY on a pullback to $240-255 or on confirmed Q2 volume sustainability exceeding current consensus. The business deserves a premium multiple; the current price offers moderate but not compelling risk/reward for new positions.
