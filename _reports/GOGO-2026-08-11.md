## Executive Summary

Gogo is a levered residual claim on a business whose profitable half is contracting and whose growing half earns materially worse economics. ATG aircraft online fell from 6,730 to 5,731 in twelve months (-14.8%), ATG broadband service revenue fell 19.1% year over year, and ARPU declined 3.9%. That revenue runs across a network and a 4 MHz FCC spectrum license Gogo owns, so it converts at very high incremental margin. The replacement revenue, Gogo Galileo LEO service, is wholesale capacity purchased from Eutelsat OneWeb and delivered on electronically-steered antennas purchased from Hughes. The margin consequence is already reported: H1 service gross margin fell 460bp to 48.2%, H1 equipment gross margin fell 480bp to 5.8%, and Q2 equipment gross margin was 1.4% versus 13.7% a year earlier. Part of the equipment compression is likely ramp-related and should recover with volume; the service-side compression from substituting purchased capacity for owned spectrum is mechanical.

Two items make the reported numbers better than the underlying business. First, the FCC Supply Chain Reimbursement Program contributed $20.0M of H1 2026 pretax benefit against $8.1M in H1 2025, recognized as reductions of expense lines and as $8.5M of *service revenue* rather than as other income. Stripping the EBITDA-line portion, H1 Adjusted EBITDA fell from $115.7M to $87.0M (-24.8%) versus the reported -13.6%. The program's completion deadline is November 8, 2026. Second, Gogo's company-defined "Free Cash Flow" adds back both FCC proceeds and interest-rate-cap settlements. FY2026 FCF guidance of $65-85M becomes $17-37M of simple operating cash flow less capex once the guided $45M of FCC capex reimbursement and $3M of cap proceeds are excluded, a 4.0-8.8% yield on the equity rather than the headline 15.4-20.2%.

The August 6 print and revised guidance confirmed the deterioration rather than arresting it. Q2 revenue of $222.8M missed consensus by roughly $6.6M; GAAP EPS of -$0.01 missed by $0.08; FY2026 revenue guidance of $870-895M implies -1.7% to -4.4% against FY2025, with H2 revenue below H1 at both ends; and FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance of $175-185M against H1 actual of $107.0M implies H2 of $68-78M, a 27-36% sequential quarterly step-down. Management's disclosed incremental H2 items (full-year litigation expense raised to $22M from $8M, plus $5M of strategic investments) account for perhaps $6-7M per quarter of the roughly $15-19M quarterly gap. Consensus responded: current-year EPS estimates were cut 81.4% over 90 days, next-quarter 76.5%, next-year 22.5%. Net leverage on forward guidance is ~4.2x and rising despite $22.3M of H1 principal repayment, because EBITDA is falling faster than debt. This is the third consecutive AVOID on this name, and the evidence supporting it has strengthened.

## Company Overview

Gogo provides in-flight connectivity to business aviation and military/government aviation, reporting as a single segment following the December 3, 2024 acquisition of Satcom Direct. Q2 2026 service revenue of $191.3M splits by market into business aviation $151.3M (79%) and military/government $39.9M (21%), and by technology into satellite broadband $84.3M (43%), air-to-ground $60.0M (31%) and narrowband/other $46.9M (25%).

The three lines are economically distinct. ATG runs on Gogo-owned cell sites under an exclusive FCC license for 800 MHz ATG spectrum, carried as a $31.3M indefinite-lived intangible. Satellite broadband is a reseller model: Gogo buys LEO capacity from Eutelsat OneWeb and GEO capacity from third-party operators, and buys HDX/FDX antennas from Hughes. Military/government, largely the acquired SD Government business, sells SATCOM, cybersecurity and cockpit datalink software and is the only line growing.

Distribution runs through OEMs (Gulfstream, Dassault, Embraer, Pilatus were named in the Q2 release), dealers and MRO shops, and each airframe requires an FAA/EASA Supplemental Type Certificate. Management identified STC timing and OEM line-fit slots as the constraint that caused Galileo and 5G shipment delays and contributed to the guidance reduction. Installed base at 6/30/26: 5,731 ATG aircraft, 1,306 GEO, 184 Galileo.

The FY2024→FY2025 revenue doubling from $444.7M to $910.5M is Satcom Direct annualization, not organic growth; the deal closed December 3, 2024 and contributed roughly one month to FY2024.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and profitability.** H1 2026 revenue was $449.1M against H1 2025 of $456.3M (-1.6%). Adjusted EBITDA fell from $123.8M to $107.0M (-13.6%). FY2025 operating income was $114.1M on $910.5M of revenue; FY2025 net income was $12.9M, and trailing-twelve-month net income is approximately -$0.9M.

**The FCC overlay.** Q2 10-Q Note 4 quantifies the program's line-by-line benefit to net income: total pretax benefit $20.0M (service revenue +$8.5M, cost of service +$2.2M, cost of equipment +$2.3M, G&A +$0.8M, engineering/design/development +$6.2M, D&A +$0.05M) versus $8.1M in H1 2025. Ex-FCC Adjusted EBITDA of $87.0M should be read as a lower bound on underlying profitability, since the reimbursed costs would not all have been incurred absent the program; the gross expense is netted and therefore not disclosable from the filing. The directional conclusion holds: a finite government program with a November 8, 2026 deadline is contributing roughly $20M of pretax benefit per half-year and $45M of FY2026 guided capex reimbursement.

**Cash flow.** H1 2026 operating cash flow was $25.1M against $69.2M a year earlier; capex was $40.2M against $12.1M, leaving OCF-less-capex of -$15.1M. H1 was depressed by a $26.3M inventory build (finished goods +$18.4M, consistent with staging Galileo/5G hardware) and a $20.8M reduction in accrued liabilities. I am not annualizing H1: the company's own guidance implies H2 OCF of $57-77M, which requires a large working-capital reversal contingent on converting staged inventory into installed, cash-collected units through the same STC bottleneck that caused the shortfall. FY2025 OCF-less-capex was $65.1M.

**Capital structure — the crux.** Term debt principal is $825.9M: the 2021 Term Loan at $601.4M (SOFR + 3.75% + CSA) and the HPS Term Loan at $224.5M (SOFR + 6.00%). Both are pari passu, secured on substantially all assets, and both mature April 30, 2028. The $122M revolver is undrawn but carries a springing maturity to 90 days before the term loans under certain conditions. Cash is $63.1M, down from $125.2M at 12/31/25 after the $40.0M Satcom Direct earnout payment and $21.1M of HPS principal.

An important nuance in Gogo's favor: the only financial covenant is a 7.50x senior secured first lien net leverage test on the revolver, and it applies only if revolver drawings exceed 35% of commitments (~$42.7M) at a quarter end. With the revolver undrawn, there is effectively no maintenance covenant. The risk is concentrated at a single refinancing date rather than distributed across quarterly tests, which buys time but removes the early-warning mechanism.

Leverage: net debt $762.8M is 4.24x FY2026E Adjusted EBITDA midpoint of $180M, 3.79x trailing-twelve-month Adjusted EBITDA of ~$201M, and 5.3-5.6x my ex-FCC FY2026 estimate of $135-145M. Management stated a 2.5-3.5x target on the Q2 call; the company is above that and moving away from it. Cash interest coverage is ~2.6x on guided EBITDA and ~1.7x after gross capex. Interest-rate caps step down to $200M notional on 7/31/26 with the strike rising to 2.75% and terminate 7/31/27, so hedge protection erodes as the refinancing window opens. A 100bp move changes annual interest by ~$6.2M with caps, ~$8.3M without.

Credit marks at 6/30/26 were 92.8 cents on the 2021 facility and 96.7 cents on HPS, blended ~93.8 cents, stable to slightly better than year-end 2025. These marks predate the August 6 guidance cut and should not be treated as the lenders' current view.

**Equity quality.** Stockholders' equity of $120.7M contains $193.2M of goodwill and $225.0M of net intangibles, implying tangible book equity of roughly -$297.5M; deducting the $194.2M deferred tax asset gives roughly -$491.7M. Accumulated deficit is $1.176B, largely inherited from the commercial aviation business divested in 2020.

Two accounting items should not be misread. The Q2 GAAP net loss of $(2.0)M came on $6.8M of pretax income with an $8.8M tax provision (129.7% effective rate) driven by permanent differences on stock compensation and non-deductible officer pay; cash taxes paid in H1 were $1.9M against $15.1M of deferred tax expense. Pretax income also absorbed a $7.2M non-cash increase in the earnout liability. And the drop in additional paid-in capital from $1,472.2M to $1,288.3M with the disappearance of $196.4M of treasury stock was a retirement of treasury shares against APIC, a non-cash reclassification within equity, not a loss and not a buyback.

**Internal controls.** ICFR was ineffective at December 31, 2025 and remained ineffective at June 30, 2026, with the weakness located at Satcom Direct: inadequate IT general controls (user access, segregation of duties, program change management) and failure to design control activities providing reasonable assurance over reporting reliability. Remediation requires migrating off or enhancing legacy Satcom Direct systems through 2026. The acquired entity now generates the majority of revenue.

## Growth Analysis

There is no growth thesis to underwrite at present. The implied H2 revenue of $420.9-445.9M is below H1 at both ends while being -7.3% to -1.8% against H2 2025, an acceleration of the decline embedded in guidance that also assumes a Galileo/5G second-half ramp.

Unit economics: ATG aircraft online -14.8% (Gogo Biz 1,939→1,090; AVANCE 4,791→4,603; Gogo 5G 0→38), ARPU -$136 to $3,309, ATG service revenue -19.1%. GEO aircraft online was roughly flat at 1,306. Galileo aircraft online reached 184. Management's own MD&A states: "We expect service revenue to decline in the near term as a result of the expected decline in ATG services sold."

The two genuine growth vectors are Galileo and military/government. Galileo's problem is not demand but conversion: installations are gated by third-party STCs and OEM slots, and the product carries wholesale capacity cost and near-zero current equipment margin. Military/government at $39.9M of quarterly service revenue is growing but too small to offset a $60M ATG line shrinking 19%. A specific new input constraint appears in the Q2 10-Q: difficulty engaging suppliers for semiconductor memory (DRAM and NAND) "due to the surging buildout of artificial intelligence-related computing infrastructure." That connects Gogo's hardware ramp directly to the memory tightness now visible across device assemblers, and it raises the cost of the very units that already ship at 1.4% gross margin.

Estimate revisions are the clearest forward read and they are uniformly negative: current-quarter EPS $0.03 versus $0.14 ninety days ago (-78.6%), next-quarter $0.04 versus $0.17 (-76.5%), current-year $0.06 versus $0.34 (-82.6%), next-year $0.62 versus $0.80 (-22.5%). Coverage is one analyst on the estimate line and three on the recommendation line, so these are thin, but the direction is unambiguous. The earnings surprise record is 2 beats and 2 misses in four quarters, with the two misses being the two most recent reportable outliers (-114% in Q2 2026 on GAAP EPS and -1,009% in Q4 2025), which argues against high confidence in near-term visibility.

## Valuation Assessment

At $3.105, market cap is $420.8M and enterprise value $1,183.6M ($1,217.8M including the $34.2M earnout fair value). Against FY2026E Adjusted EBITDA that is **6.4-6.8x EV/EBITDA** (6.6x at midpoint), and 8.2-8.8x on my ex-FCC estimate of $135-145M. Net debt is 4.24x guided EBITDA.

Peer multiples are not usable on an earnings basis: GOGO's trailing and forward P/E are both -399x; SHEN is at -16.6x and SATS at -4.6x; only TMUS at 18.6x is meaningful and is a different business at a different leverage profile. The relevant comparison is that Gogo trades at roughly 6.6x EBITDA with 4.2x net leverage, meaning **about 64% of enterprise value belongs to lenders**. Each $10M change in EBITDA at a constant 6.5x multiple moves the equity by ~$65M, or $0.48 per share, a 15% swing on a $3.11 stock. The equity is a geared call on EBITDA stabilization.

The cheapness argument runs through free cash flow, and it does not survive decomposition. Headline FY2026 FCF guidance is a 15.4-20.2% yield on the equity. Excluding the finite FCC capex reimbursement and cap proceeds, the comparable figure is $17-37M, a 4.0-8.8% yield on a residual equity claim sitting behind $763M of net debt. That is not a margin of safety.

Analyst price targets of $7.00 low, $8.50 mean and $10.00 high (three analysts: 1 buy, 2 hold) imply 125-222% upside. These targets have not been refreshed against the August 6 guidance reduction and the 81% cut to current-year estimates; the sentiment trend is recorded as "stable" with no recent changes logged. Where estimates are collapsing and the multiple is on a shrinking EBITDA base, consensus targets are stale anchors rather than floors.

## Competitive Landscape

The durable asset is the licensed ATG spectrum and the terrestrial network built on it, plus 5,731 installed ATG aircraft and a 24/7 in-person support organization inherited from Satcom Direct. That moat sits on the declining line. In satellite, Gogo is a reseller with single-source dependency on Eutelsat OneWeb for LEO capacity and on Hughes for antennas, an integration and service moat rather than an infrastructure moat, and one that competes against operators who own their constellations and can price capacity at marginal cost.

The trajectory is what matters for scoring, and it is negative on three measures simultaneously: unit count (-14.8%), price (ARPU -3.9%) and margin (service gross margin -460bp). Mix shift toward the reseller business means that even revenue stabilization produces fewer EBITDA dollars.

SmartSky's antitrust complaint (W.D.N.C.) alleges Gogo "maintains an illegal monopoly over air-to-ground broadband inflight connectivity products and services and has blocked SmartSky from entering the market." Whatever its merits, the existence of a monopolization claim reaching trial on May 3, 2027 is an additional constraint on how aggressively Gogo can defend the ATG franchise commercially.

## Risk Assessment

**1. April 30, 2028 maturity wall against a declining EBITDA base.** $825.9M of pari passu secured term debt matures on one day; the revolver's springing maturity can pull it inside that date. There is no maintenance covenant to breach, so risk concentrates at refinancing. At 4.2x and rising, with hedge caps gone by 7/31/27, the refinancing is priceable but not assured on terms that leave the equity intact.

**2. ATG spectrum renewal, October 31, 2026.** The FY2025 10-K discloses that ATG licenses come up for renewal and flags specific uncertainty on the 1 MHz license, noting it is "not currently clear what level and length of service the FCC will find adequate" for a substantial-service showing. This is a near-dated binary affecting the ATG network, Gogo 5G and the $31.3M license intangible. I could not verify the outcome.

**3. FCC reimbursement program expiry, November 8, 2026.** Approved up to ~$334M; only $15.3M of capex reimbursement was received in H1 2026 with a $39.3M receivable outstanding at 6/30/26 (up from $27.8M). The program is simultaneously the largest near-term cash source, a P&L flatterer, and expiring.

**4. SmartSky litigation, exposure above accrual.** A Delaware jury found willful infringement on November 21, 2025 and awarded $22.7M; no judgment has been entered and nothing has been paid, and Gogo is challenging both verdict and amount after pre-trial rulings that invalidated one patent, denied lost profits and excluded SmartSky's damages expert. Willfulness permits enhancement up to treble (~$68.1M) plus fees. The antitrust suit seeks treble and punitive damages plus disgorgement, unquantified, with trial May 3, 2027. Total accrual is $10.5M against $120.7M of book equity.

**5. Unremediated material weakness** at the entity generating most of the revenue, two quarters running.

**6. Earnout dilution or liquidity drain.** Remaining cap is ~$184.6M with recorded fair value of $34.2M. At $3.105, the fair-value amount settled in stock would be 11.0M shares (8.1% of existing, 7.5% ownership dilution); the maximum remaining cap settled in stock would be 59.5M shares (43.9% of existing, 30.5% ownership dilution). Both depend on Satcom Direct gross profit through the four-year window, and Gogo may elect cash, as it did with the April 2026 payment, which consumed liquidity ahead of the 2028 maturities.

**7. Purchase commitments and supply.** Roughly $591M of total purchase obligations with approximately $341M payable within one year (Hughes HDX/FDX and Gilat), against $63.1M of cash, plus the newly disclosed DRAM/NAND sourcing constraint.

**8. Asset sale proceeds accrue to lenders.** The $26.4M Melbourne, Florida property has been held for sale across at least two reporting periods; both credit agreements require prepayment of 100% of net proceeds of certain asset sales.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case ($5.00, +61%).** ATG attrition decelerates as AVANCE holds and 5G units convert the Gogo Biz base; Galileo installations clear the STC backlog in H2, converting the $125.1M inventory position into shipped, installed and collected revenue and driving equipment margin back toward the 10-14% range; military/government continues compounding at 21% of service revenue; spectrum licenses renew on schedule in October; the SmartSky verdict is reduced or overturned on the pending inequitable-conduct and post-trial briefing, with the antitrust suit dismissed; FY2027 Adjusted EBITDA reaches $200M and the excess-cash-flow sweep takes net debt to ~$700M, delivering a 3.5x refinancing profile and a 7x multiple. This path exists, and the equity's gearing means it produces a large return. It requires four independent things to go right, at least two of which sit with third parties (FCC, FAA, courts).

**Bear case ($0.80, -74%).** The FCC program ends in November with no offsetting cost reduction on the $8.5M of half-year service revenue it generates; ATG service revenue continues declining ~19% while satellite revenue replaces it at half the gross margin, so FY2027 Adjusted EBITDA lands near $145M; net leverage runs above 5x into a 2028 maturity with the interest caps expired; the 1 MHz license renewal is contested; enhanced damages and fees on the Delaware verdict plus discovery in the antitrust case consume cash beyond the $22M litigation guide; and the refinancing arrives on terms requiring equity issuance or a debt exchange. At 64% of enterprise value already owned by lenders, modest EBITDA erosion compounds into severe equity impairment.

**Base case ($2.60, -16%).** Revenue declines low single digits, Galileo grows but dilutes margin, FY2027 EBITDA settles near $170M with the FCC benefit partially cost-neutral, the sweep reduces net debt to ~$735M, and the multiple stays at 6.4x. The equity is worth less than today.

The deciding evidence is the direction of ex-FCC EBITDA (-24.8% in H1) combined with the implied H2 step-down of 27-36% that management's disclosed incremental items only partially explain. Either guidance embeds substantial conservatism, or core erosion is accelerating; the disclosures do not permit distinguishing between them, and at 4.2x leverage the equity cannot afford the second interpretation.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst.** Three dated events cluster within four months: ATG spectrum license renewal on **October 31, 2026** (with the 1 MHz showing explicitly flagged as uncertain), FCC reimbursement program completion on **November 8, 2026** (removing ~$40M of annualized pretax benefit and the $45M capex reimbursement that bridges gross capex of $65M to net $20M), and the **Q3 2026 print in early November**, which is the first test of whether the guided H2 EBITDA of $68-78M and the required $57-77M of H2 operating cash flow materialize. Post-trial rulings on the SmartSky verdict could land in the same window.

**Timing window.** October through early November 2026, a tight two-to-six week resolution period, which is favorable relative to the usual 6+ month short.

**Short interest and squeeze risk.** 15.4% of float short, 12.07M shares, 4.9 days to cover. Elevated but not extreme. The stock sits 1.4% above its 52-week low of $2.98 after a 73.9% twelve-month decline, meaning much of the thesis is distributed. Analyst targets at $7.00-$10.00 against a $3.11 price create latent upgrade-driven squeeze risk if guidance is met, and a $421M market cap with a distressed-looking capital structure makes strategic or take-private speculation a live tail.

**Borrow and options signal.** Options confirm bearish positioning (P/C open interest 1.45, IV up 3.9pp to 86.5% on a 17% decline), which cuts both ways: the trade is not uncrowded and near-term implied volatility is expensive if expressed through puts. Cost of carry on a heavily-shorted $3 small cap is likely material.

**Downside target and risk/reward.** Bear case $0.80 implies +74% on a short. Upside risk on a clean Q3, spectrum renewal and reaffirmed guidance is a retrace toward the early-August $4.66 high, or -50%. That is roughly 1.5:1 before borrow cost, below the 2:1 threshold. The asymmetry is further compromised by the possibility that the FCC and FAA outcomes are simply favorable, resolving two of the three catalysts positively in the same window.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The catalysts are dated and the fundamental case is documented, but at 1.5:1 reward-to-risk with 15.4% short interest, the stock 1.4% off its 52-week low, stale consensus targets 125-222% above spot, and two of three catalysts controlled by regulators whose decisions could break favorably, the remaining downside does not compensate for squeeze and resolution risk. Avoid owning it; do not press it short.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria — what would change the view:**

1. **Two consecutive quarters of ex-FCC Adjusted EBITDA stability.** Specifically, Adjusted EBITDA less the Note 4 FCC benefit at or above $45M per quarter in Q3 and Q4 2026. H1 2026 ran $43.5M per quarter on that basis and falling.
2. **ATG attrition arrest.** Total ATG aircraft online flat or up sequentially, with AVANCE units stabilizing above 4,600 and ARPU no lower than $3,300.
3. **Equipment gross margin back above 10%** for two quarters, confirming the 1.4% Q2 print was ramp-related rather than structural Galileo economics.
4. **Refinancing progress.** Any announced extension, repricing or partial takeout of the 2021 or HPS facilities pushing maturities beyond 2029, or net leverage below 3.5x on trailing EBITDA.
5. **Resolution of the two near-dated binaries:** full renewal of all ATG licenses including the 1 MHz tranche, and entry of a Delaware judgment at or below the $22.7M verdict without enhancement.
6. **Material weakness remediated** with an unqualified ICFR opinion, and evidence of open-market insider purchases; none have occurred across the 25 most recent Form 4 transactions despite the decline from $4.26 in March.

**Price at which the risk/reward inverts:** below approximately **$1.60**, the equity would be capitalizing roughly $218M against $763M of net debt, or about 5.4x EV/EBITDA on guided FY2026, a level at which the bear case is largely priced and the refinancing option has genuine convexity. That is the level at which I would revisit, absent fundamental improvement.

**Expected duration of headwinds:** through at least Q2 2027. The FCC benefit rolls off in November 2026, the interest-rate caps terminate July 31, 2027, the antitrust trial is set for May 3, 2027, and the refinancing window opens in earnest during 2027 for an April 2028 maturity. There is no quarter in that sequence in which the reported numbers get structurally easier.

**Next reassessment point:** the Q3 2026 report in early November 2026, which will follow the October 31 spectrum renewal decision and coincide with the FCC program deadline.

## Conclusion

**AVOID.** This is the third consecutive AVOID on GOGO, and the bar for repeating it has been met by new evidence rather than by inertia: the August 6 guidance reduction, an 81.4% ninety-day cut to current-year consensus EPS, an implied H2 Adjusted EBITDA step-down of 27-36% that management's disclosed items only half explain, service gross margin down 460bp, Q2 equipment gross margin at 1.4%, and net leverage rising to ~4.2x despite $22.3M of debt repayment. The overall score falls to 3.2 from 3.8, with the declines concentrated in growth prospects (3.0), financial health (3.0) and risk factors (2.5).

The company is not in imminent distress: the revolver is undrawn, there is effectively no maintenance covenant, operating cash flow has been positive in every period reviewed, and lenders marked the paper at ~94 cents at June 30. The problem is the composition of the business and the arithmetic of a geared residual claim. Gogo is replacing owned-spectrum revenue that converts at ~53% service gross margin with resold satellite capacity that converts at ~48% and falling, while a government reimbursement stream that masks roughly half the EBITDA decline expires in three months, and while $825.9M of debt comes due on a single day in April 2028. Probability-weighted expected return from $3.105 is approximately -14%, with a base case of $2.60, a bull case of $5.00 and a bear case of $0.80 that would remove three-quarters of the equity.

The stock is cheap on headline free cash flow and on EV/EBITDA. Neither metric survives adjustment for the finite items inside it, and cheapness on a collapsing estimate base is the standard signature of a value trap rather than a mispricing. Do not own it. Revisit on evidence of ex-FCC EBITDA stabilization, ATG unit stabilization, or a price near $1.60.
