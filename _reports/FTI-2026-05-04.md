## Executive Summary

TechnipFMC has completed one of the most decisive operational transformations in the energy services sector over the past three years, moving from net losses in 2022 to over $960M in net income in 2025 while simultaneously deleveraging to a net cash position. The company's proprietary iEPCI model and Subsea 2.0 standardization platform have driven Subsea operating margins to 15.8% in Q1 2026 (from 12.8% a year prior), with management guiding toward full-year 2026 Subsea adjusted EBITDA margins of 21–22%. A $16.5B backlog representing approximately 1.7x annual revenue provides exceptional forward visibility across multiple years.

The primary tension in the investment case is valuation. At $74.88, the stock has appreciated 161% over the past year and trades at approximately 25x FY2026E consensus EPS of $2.96. This represents a meaningful discount to the peer group median P/E of 37.2x (though that median is skewed by low-earnings companies with inflated P/Es such as NOV at 83.8x and KGS at 75.1x). FTI carries net cash, generates strong annual FCF, is repurchasing approximately 3–4% of its float annually, and operates with structurally higher margins due to its integrated model. The current geopolitical environment—with operators accelerating deepwater developments as portfolio diversification—provides a multi-year demand tailwind. Management argues that FTI's integrated iEPCI model is differentiated in ways that competitors have not yet replicated at comparable scale.

I maintain a BUY recommendation with a base case target of $88-92, representing approximately 30x FY2026E EPS, supported by continued earnings momentum (estimates up 6.9% over 90 days), accelerating buybacks, and structural demand from energy security reorientation.

## Company Overview

TechnipFMC is the global leader in integrated subsea production systems, operating through two segments: Subsea (88.6% of revenue) and Surface Technologies (11.4%). The company's competitive differentiation centers on two proprietary platforms:

**iEPCI (integrated Engineering, Procurement, Construction & Installation):** A first-of-its-kind commercial model combining subsea production systems with umbilicals, risers, flowlines, and installation into a single contract. This eliminates interface risk between traditionally separate contractors, reducing project timelines and cost.

**Subsea 2.0:** A configure-to-order standardization platform that reduces equipment cost by up to 25% and delivery time by 12 months versus bespoke designs. This drives both margin expansion (lower engineering hours per unit) and faster project sanctioning.

The company holds an estimated 19% market share in the global subsea equipment and services market (per industry estimates), competing against OneSubsea (SLB/Aker Solutions JV), Subsea 7, Baker Hughes, and Saipem. Key customers include TotalEnergies, bp, Equinor, Petrobras, Shell, and ExxonMobil across deepwater basins in Brazil, Gulf of Mexico, West Africa, and emerging frontiers (Suriname, Mozambique, Namibia).

## Growth Analysis

**Revenue Growth Drivers:**

1. **Backlog conversion:** $16.5B total backlog (66.4% extending beyond 2026) provides contractual revenue visibility. Subsea revenue guidance raised to $9.2-9.6B for 2026.

2. **New orders:** Management targets $10B in Subsea inbound orders for 2026. The identified opportunity pipeline totals approximately $29-30B over 2 years, concentrated in Brazil, Gulf of Mexico, West Africa, and emerging basins.

3. **Geopolitical demand pull:** Energy security concerns and elevated regional risk perceptions are accelerating operator portfolio diversification toward deepwater projects. Operators facing regional concentration risk are sanctioning Gulf of Mexico, Brazilian, and West African projects at an accelerated pace.

4. **New energy optionality:** The HISEP project (subsea CO2 separation for Petrobras) and all-electric subsea CCS work in the UK represent potential new markets. These are currently immaterial to revenue but could become meaningful if carbon capture scaling continues.

**Estimate Revisions:** FY2026 consensus EPS has risen from $2.77 (90 days ago) to $2.96 currently (+6.9%), and FY2027 from $3.30 to $3.46 (+4.8%). The positive revision trend confirms the operational story is being validated quarter-over-quarter.

**Earnings Beat History:** FTI has beaten consensus EPS in all 4 reported quarters, with an average beat of 19.3% and a range of 11.8% to 37.1%. This consistent outperformance suggests either management sandbagging or analysts systematically underestimating the margin expansion trajectory. Both interpretations are positive for forward returns.

## Valuation Assessment

At $74.88 and ~$30B market cap, FTI trades at:
- 25.3x FY2026E EPS ($2.96)
- 21.6x FY2027E EPS ($3.46)
- ~4.3–4.8% FCF yield (based on FY2026 guided FCF of $1.3–$1.45B)
- ~15–16x EV/EBITDA (estimated, depending on whether trailing or forward EBITDA is used)

**Relative to peers:** The peer median P/E of 37.2x is misleading because it is heavily influenced by low-earnings companies with inflated P/Es (NOV at 83.8x, KGS at 75.1x). FTI's 25x P/E thus represents a meaningful discount to this skewed peer median. Weatherford at 16.7x is the most comparable pure-play, but Weatherford lacks FTI's integrated model, net cash balance sheet, or margin expansion trajectory. FTI's premium relative to Weatherford is justified by qualitatively superior business characteristics, but the magnitude of that premium (50%+ above WFRD) limits further multiple expansion.

**Absolute valuation:** At 25x forward earnings, FTI is priced for continued execution. The stock is no longer cheap on any traditional metric. The investment case rests on earnings growth (consensus expects 17% EPS growth from FY2026 to FY2027) and buyback-driven per-share compounding rather than multiple expansion.

**What could the market be mispricing?** The margin trajectory. If Subsea EBITDA margins reach the upper end of the 21–22% guided range and sustain at that level or above, FY2026 earnings could exceed $3.10-3.20 rather than the $2.96 consensus. At current price, that would represent 23-24x a higher-than-consensus earnings number—reasonable for a company compounding FCF at this rate with declining share count.

The valuation is the weakest pillar of the thesis. I score it 5.0/10, reflecting a stock that is fairly valued but not cheap, with upside dependent on further earnings beats rather than rerating.

## Competitive Landscape

**Structural advantages:**
- iEPCI remains differentiated. No competitor currently offers comparable end-to-end integration at scale. OneSubsea's 2023 JV formation (SLB + Aker Solutions) is a partial response but lacks FTI's installation fleet ownership, though this gap may narrow over time.
- Subsea 2.0 standardization creates a cost advantage that compounds over time as the CTO product library expands.
- Vertical integration through vessel ownership (including the Dofcon JV in Brazil) creates barriers to entry.

**Market share trajectory:** The company's estimated ~19% market share in subsea appears stable to growing, evidenced by the expanding backlog and increasing direct awards (non-competitive bids from operators specifically requesting FTI's integrated approach), though backlog growth can also reflect rising industry demand.

**Emerging basin positioning:** First-mover in Suriname (GranMorgu with TotalEnergies), Mozambique (TotalEnergies LNG), and developing Namibian basin exposure positions FTI in the highest-growth deepwater markets.

**Risk to competitive position:** OneSubsea's integration and other industry consolidation (such as Subsea7/Saipem combinations) could close the integration gap over 3-5 years. The company's own risk factors note competition and industry consolidation as ongoing risks.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Valuation risk at 25x forward:** With 161% appreciation over 12 months, the stock is priced for continued execution. Any quarter showing margin miss or order intake weakness could trigger 10-15% correction from momentum unwind alone.

2. **Oil price sensitivity (indirect):** While FTI's deepwater focus and long-cycle projects provide insulation from short-term oil moves, a sustained period of lower oil prices would slow FID sanctioning and compress the opportunity pipeline over 12-24 months.

3. **Fixed-price contract execution:** The backlog consists predominantly of fixed-price contracts. Cost inflation (labor, materials, freight) in a stagflationary environment could compress realized margins versus bid margins. The $1.06B in net contract liabilities (advance payments) represents execution obligations that must be met regardless of cost outcomes.

4. **Geopolitical project concentration:** Key projects in Mozambique (LNG), Suriname (GranMorgu), and other emerging basins carry above-average country risk. Force majeure events or political instability could delay execution.

5. **Trade policy/tariffs:** The company's 10-Q explicitly cites "significant changes or developments in U.S. or other national trade policies, including tariffs and the reactions of other countries thereto" as a known risk factor.

6. **Insider selling pattern:** CEO sold ~$46M and CFO sold ~$8M in March 2026. While both retain substantial positions (CEO ~$180M), the selling coincides with the stock's approach to all-time highs. Sales appear related to annual equity vesting events under a pre-established 10b5-1 plan.

**Options Market Signal:**

Near-term IV at 41.6% significantly exceeds 1-year historical vol of 30.1% (11.5pp spread), with the term structure in backwardation (41.6% near-term vs. 36.1% at 6-month). This reflects the market pricing elevated near-term uncertainty around the post-earnings period and broader macro volatility. Put/call skew at 7.1% shows modestly elevated put demand. However, the OI put/call ratio of 0.02 is extremely low, suggesting minimal structural hedging by institutions. The IV compression from 60.8% (April 17) to 41.6% (current) alongside +6.5% price appreciation confirms post-earnings uncertainty resolution. The remaining premium reflects macro/geopolitical volatility rather than FTI-specific concerns.

**Mitigants:**
- Net cash position eliminates refinancing risk entirely
- Backlog is largely contracted, reducing demand uncertainty
- Active buyback program supports shares on modest corrections, though the authorization is discretionary and subject to board/management judgment based on cash flows, liquidity, and market conditions

## Conclusion

TechnipFMC represents a structurally advantaged subsea franchise with validated margin expansion, exceptional backlog visibility, and a balance sheet that enables meaningful capital returns. The stock is not cheap at 25x forward earnings, and the 161% rally over 12 months means downside risk on any execution miss is material. However, the combination of rising estimates, consistent earnings beats (4/4 quarters, average 19% surprise), net cash position, approximately 3–4% annual share count reduction, and a geopolitical environment that structurally favors deepwater investment supports continued appreciation.

The key differentiator versus the broader OFS sector is FTI's integrated model and resulting margin profile. This company earns returns that look more like an industrial technology business than a traditional energy services provider. At $74.88, the risk-reward favors continued ownership with a 12-month target of $88-92.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The correction of the sustainable buyback rate from 5–6% to approximately 3–4% annual share-count reduction, and the use of official 21–22% Subsea EBITDA margin guidance (rather than 23%+), moderately reduces the EPS compounding trajectory supporting the bull case and target price.
