## Company Overview

Cboe Global Markets operates the world's leading derivatives exchange network across five segments: Options (63% of net revenue), North American Equities (17%), Europe and Asia Pacific (11%), Futures (5%), and Global FX (4%). The company's competitive moat centers on its exclusive license to list options on the S&P 500 Index (SPX) and its proprietary ownership of the VIX Index methodology.

The business model is highly asset-light with $71M annual capex against $1.68B in reported free cash flow. Revenue is transaction-driven (volume × revenue per contract) with a significant and growing recurring revenue component via Data Vantage ($635.5M in 2025, +10% YoY), which includes access and capacity fees, proprietary market data fees, and other revenue. The RPC differential is critical to understanding profitability: index options generate $0.924 per contract versus $0.066 for multi-listed options — a 14:1 ratio that makes proprietary product volume the overwhelming profit driver.

Under CEO Craig Donohue (former CME Group CEO, appointed May 2025), Cboe has executed a comprehensive strategic realignment: exiting Japan, selling Australia/Canada operations, winding down CEDX and corporate listings, cutting 20% of headcount, and planning entry into prediction markets. The workforce reduction lowered 2026 adjusted operating expense guidance to $838M-$853M (from $864M-$879M), amplifying already-strong operating leverage.

## Growth Analysis

**Secular Growth Drivers:**

1. **0DTE Options Adoption:** Zero-days-to-expiration SPX options represented ~50% of SPX volume on the April 14 record day. This product category barely existed before 2022 and represents genuine new demand from retail traders, institutional hedgers, and systematic vol-selling strategies. The growth trajectory remains steep.

2. **Index Options Volume Growth:** Market-wide options ADV grew 25% in 2025. Cboe's proprietary index options ADV grew 21%. The structural shift toward options for portfolio hedging, income generation, and directional expression has multi-year runway.

3. **Data Vantage Revenue:** $635.5M in 2025 (+10% YoY), comprising access and capacity fees ($408.7M), proprietary market data fees ($223.9M), and other revenue — a high-margin recurring revenue stream with limited volume sensitivity. Exchange data pricing power remains intact despite regulatory discussion.

4. **European Market Share Gains:** Cboe Europe Equities gained market share to approximately 25.0% in FY2025. Cboe Clear Europe gained share to approximately 39.4%. European operations provide geographic diversification.

5. **Cost Rationalization:** The 20% workforce reduction lowered 2026 adjusted operating expense guidance by approximately $26M (to $838M-$853M from $864M-$879M). Combined with the broader strategic realignment, this enhances operating leverage on incremental revenue growth, though royalty fees and other costs rise with index-product volumes (up 25% YoY in Q1 2026).

6. **Prediction Markets:** Planned product leveraging existing regulatory infrastructure. Addressable market is nascent but growing rapidly (Kalshi, Polymarket demonstrated demand).

**Growth Concerns:**

- Q1 2026 record volumes were partly driven by tariff-induced volatility (April 2026 tariff-related disruptions drove the 8.96M record day on April 14). If geopolitical uncertainty normalizes, volume growth may moderate.
- Multi-listed options market share is eroding slightly (30.3% from 30.8%) as competitors price aggressively.
- U.S. equities market share declined from 11.4% to 10.0% in FY2025 — low-margin segment but indicates competitive pressure.

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat Quality: Exceptional but Concentrated**

Cboe's proprietary index options and VIX franchise represent the highest-quality competitive moat in the exchange sector. The exclusive S&P 500 Index license cannot be replicated by competitors, and Cboe's proprietary VIX methodology gives it sole authority to list VIX options and futures. The 14:1 RPC advantage of index vs. multi-listed options quantifies the moat's economic value.

**Competitive Dynamics:**

- **Options:** Market share stable at 30.3%. No competitor can offer SPX. Multi-listed share faces pressure from MIAX, NYSE/Arca, Nasdaq.
- **Equities:** Market share declining (11.4% → 10.0% in FY2025). Highly commoditized, low-margin — less important to thesis.
- **Europe:** Gaining share in both equities (~25.0%) and clearing (~39.4%). Strategic priority.
- **Futures:** CFE dominates VIX futures. CME cannot replicate.

**Emerging Threats:**

SPY options (listed on multiple exchanges) provide similar economic exposure to SPX at lower contract sizes. While SPX has cash settlement and tax advantages (Section 1256), SPY options volumes have grown rapidly. This represents gradual erosion of the moat's exclusivity rather than a cliff — SPX retains structural advantages for institutional users.

## Risk Assessment

**Quantified Risks:**

1. **License and Proprietary Rights Concentration:** Options and Futures together represented approximately 68% of FY2025 net revenue; a significant but unquantified portion of this depends on the exclusive S&P 500 Index license and proprietary VIX rights. Cboe's 10-K states revenues are "dependent in large part" on these exclusive arrangements. While renewal risk is low given the symbiotic relationship (Cboe incurred $256.3M in royalty fees and other cost of revenues in FY2025, benefiting index providers), this remains the existential risk.

2. **Volume Cyclicality:** If geopolitical uncertainty moderates, vol could compress and with it options volumes. A 15% volume decline from Q1 peaks would reduce quarterly EPS by ~$0.40-0.50.

3. **Regulatory Headwinds:** SEC tick size/access fee cap rule implementation will reduce equities transaction fees. EU consolidated tape may affect European data revenue. These are medium-term margin compressors.

4. **Executive Turnover:** Nearly the entire C-suite refreshed in 12 months. While Donohue is experienced, execution risk on this scale of organizational change is real.

5. **Cboe Clear Europe Credit Facility:** €1.2B facility terminates June 26, 2026 — imminent renewal required. Low probability of failure but operationally critical.
