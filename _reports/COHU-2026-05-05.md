## Executive Summary

Cohu is a semiconductor test, automation, and inspection equipment company emerging from a deep cyclical trough (revenue declined 44% peak-to-trough from $813M to $402M) into an AI/HPC-driven upcycle. Q1 2026 orders grew 57% YoY, the company has secured "platform of record" status for AI processor testing at a major U.S. customer, and management has identified a $750M targeted pipeline across 15 customers. The proprietary active thermal control (T-Core) technology on the Eclipse platform positions Cohu for next-generation AI chip testing where thermal management is a binding constraint.

The fundamental story is compelling, but the stock's 200%+ rally from its May 2025 low of $15.34 to ~$49 has already discounted substantial recovery. The company remains unprofitable (Q1 2026 GAAP loss of $12.1M), faces ~22% potential dilution from deeply in-the-money convertible notes, carries 15.5% short interest, and trades at implied options volatility 22 percentage points above historical levels. With analyst consensus already at $57 (median $60) and the stock near its 52-week high, the risk/reward at current levels favors waiting for a pullback rather than chasing the move.

## Company Overview

Cohu, founded in 1947, operates as a single-segment semiconductor test and inspection equipment supplier. The company serves three core markets through its product lines:

- **Test Handlers (Eclipse platform):** Automated handlers with proprietary T-Core active thermal control for testing high-performance AI/HPC processors
- **Semiconductor Testers (Diamondx):** Mixed signal, RF, and power management device testing
- **Inspection & Metrology (Neon platform):** Six-sided optical inspection, particularly for HBM (High Bandwidth Memory)
- **Interface Solutions:** Test contactors and probe cards (consumable/recurring)
- **Software Analytics (PAICe/Tignis):** AI-driven process control acquired in January 2025

The business model generates 60% recurring revenue (interface, spares, services, software), providing meaningful downside protection against capital equipment cyclicality. Cohu operates with ~2,857 employees globally (75% Asia-Pacific), an installed base of 25,000+ systems across 280+ facilities, and ships approximately 90% of revenue internationally.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis rests on three pillars:

**1. AI/HPC Test Handler Demand ($650M pipeline):** Cohu's Eclipse platform with T-Core thermal control has achieved "platform of record" status at one major U.S. customer, representing an estimated $100M opportunity over three years. Five additional customers are in qualification representing ~$200M incremental opportunity. March and April 2026 saw sequential $30M orders from AI datacenter processor customers.

**2. HBM Inspection ($100M pipeline):** The Neon platform targets HBM3/4/5 inspection. Management forecasts ~$20M HBM revenue in FY2026 (~80% YoY growth) with total inspection at ~$70M.

**3. Software/Analytics Attachment:** Tignis (PAICe) subscriptions at ~$1.2M ARR growing to ~$3M in 2026. Low penetration (1.3% attachment) means significant upside as the installed base grows.

Estimate revisions confirm positive momentum: next-year EPS estimates rose 18.7% over 30 days and 14.3% over 90 days, now at $1.46. Current-year estimates at $0.58 represent significant improvement from losses but remain modest relative to the stock's valuation.

The silicon photonics opportunity, explicitly excluded from the $750M pipeline, represents additional optionality in AI optical interconnects.

- ~10.9x FY2025 revenue ($453M)

- ~85x FY2026 consensus EPS ($0.58)
- ~34x FY2027 consensus EPS ($1.46)
- Forward P/E of -39.2x (reflecting trailing losses)

Against peer comparables: PLAB trades at 20.5x, VECO at 81x, ACMR at 35.5x. Peer median is 35.5x. Cohu's implied FY2027 multiple of 34x appears roughly in-line, but the company must demonstrate it can actually deliver $1.46 in earnings — a significant step-up from the current $0.01/quarter run-rate.

The stock has rallied from $15.34 (May 2025) to $49.41 — a 222% gain in 12 months. Analyst consensus targets average $57.43 with a range of $50-65. At $49.41, the stock sits within 2% of the consensus low target ($50), offering limited margin of safety.

The market is pricing in the full recovery. If FY2027 EPS reaches $1.46 and the stock deserves a semiconductor equipment recovery multiple of 25-30x, fair value would be $36.50-$43.80. At 35x (generous growth premium), fair value is $51.10. The current price already embeds the optimistic end of the recovery trajectory.

## Competitive Landscape

Cohu's competitive position is improving from a low base. The T-Core active thermal control technology provides genuine differentiation for AI chip testing, where power densities are increasing exponentially and precise junction temperature control is critical for yield. Management has identified thermal heads as a supply chain bottleneck and is expanding Malaysian manufacturing capacity to address it.

However, Cohu competes against Advantest and Teradyne — substantially larger companies with deeper R&D budgets, broader product portfolios, and stronger customer relationships. In the test handler market specifically, competition from Asian manufacturers creates persistent pricing pressure.

The "platform of record" designation and sequential customer wins suggest Cohu is gaining share in the narrow AI/HPC test niche, but whether this extends to a durable structural position or remains a function of capacity-constrained demand is unclear.

## Risk Assessment

**Convertible Note Dilution:** The $287.5M convertible notes at $27.18 conversion price are deeply in-the-money at current prices. Full conversion implies ~10.6M additional shares, or ~22% dilution. Capped calls provide partial mitigation but dilution risk is material above the cap price.

**Execution Risk on Manufacturing Transition:** Cohu is consolidating manufacturing from Poway, CA to Asia while simultaneously ramping Eclipse production for AI customers. Thermal heads are a known supply chain bottleneck. Delivery delays during a demand surge could cede wins to competitors.

**Goodwill Impairment:** The Interface Solutions unit carries ~$110M in goodwill with "limited headroom." Any shortfall in IS recovery could trigger a material non-cash charge.

**Customer Concentration:** Top 10 customers represent 60% of revenue. Loss of any major relationship — particularly the "platform of record" customer — would be highly impactful.

**Short Interest at 15.5%:** While this creates potential squeeze dynamics, elevated short interest with 8.7 days to cover reflects institutional skepticism about the sustainability of the current valuation.

**China/Export Control Risk:** ~40-50 material export regulation changes in 2025 affecting semiconductor equipment. China-based customers are actively seeking non-U.S. alternatives, creating medium-term demand risk for Cohu's Asia-heavy sales base.

### Options Market Signal

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($55-65):** The AI/HPC test cycle proves durable and accelerates beyond current guidance. FY2026 revenue hits the high end ($566M+), gross margins return to 47%+, and FY2027 revenue approaches $700M with EPS of $1.50-2.00. The $750M pipeline converts at above-average hit rates. Silicon photonics and Tignis analytics become meaningful revenue contributors. Multiple expands to 35-40x forward earnings on demonstrated growth.

**Bear Case ($28-35):** The AI test cycle proves narrower than anticipated — concentrated in 2-3 customers with lumpy ordering patterns. Mobile and auto/industrial remain weak. Gross margins stay below 45% due to ramp costs. Convertible dilution weighs on per-share metrics. FY2027 EPS comes in at $0.80-1.00 vs. consensus $1.46. The stock re-rates to 25-30x on reduced estimates, implying $20-30 range. Short sellers prove correct about overvaluation.

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (late July/early August) is the next critical data point. The conversion from orders to revenue realization should be clearly visible in Q2-Q3 results.

## Conclusion

Cohu has legitimate positioning in the AI semiconductor test upcycle. The T-Core thermal control technology, "platform of record" wins, and 57% order growth are substantive. The $750M pipeline and $489M cash provide both growth runway and financial flexibility.

The problem is price. A 200%+ rally in 12 months has pulled forward substantial value creation that hasn't yet materialized in earnings. The stock trades at 34x FY2027 consensus EPS that requires a significant step-up from current quarterly run-rates. With 15.5% short interest, convertible dilution overhanging, rich implied volatility, and the stock within 2% of the lowest analyst target, the asymmetry favors patience. The underlying thesis is positive, but the entry point is not.
