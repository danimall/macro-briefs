## Executive Summary

Sempra is transitioning from a diversified energy holding company into a cleaner regulated utility growth story, anchored by its 80.25% stake in Oncor (Texas's largest T&D utility) and its California utilities. The pending $9.99B sale of 45% of SI Partners to KKR will reduce infrastructure/FX volatility and fund the $38.7B five-year capex plan. Oncor's rate base is compounding at ~17% annually, driven by explosive ERCOT load growth from data centers and Permian Basin electrification.

The fundamental issue is valuation. At 34x trailing earnings and ~18.3x FY2026E EPS of $5.11, Sempra trades at a substantial premium to the peer median of 18.9x. Even adjusting for Sempra's superior growth profile, the stock prices in significant future value creation. Two major unresolved risks — LA wildfire litigation against SoCalGas and credit rating negative outlooks from both Moody's and S&P — create meaningful downside skew that the premium multiple does not compensate for.

The stock has appreciated 25% over the past year, reflecting growing consensus around the Texas growth narrative. Analyst sentiment is strongly favorable (14 Buys vs. 5 Holds, zero Sells) with a median target of $105. We view this as a growth-hold: the thesis is directionally correct and worth maintaining existing positions, but new capital deployment at current levels offers insufficient margin of safety given identifiable binary risks.

## Company Overview

Sempra operates through three reportable segments:

**Sempra California** — SDG&E (electric and gas distribution, ~3.6M customers in San Diego) and SoCalGas (largest U.S. gas utility, ~21.3M customers in Southern California). Combined 2025 authorized rate base of ~$32B with ~9% growth CAGR.

**Sempra Texas Utilities** — 80.25% interest in Oncor, Texas's largest transmission and distribution system with 4.1M+ customers and 145,000+ circuit miles. Rate base ~$31.5B growing at ~17% CAGR, the fastest among major U.S. T&D utilities.

**Sempra Infrastructure** — Cameron LNG (existing 13.9 Mtpa), Port Arthur LNG (Phases 1 & 2, 26 Mtpa combined under construction), and ECA LNG (3.25 Mtpa, mechanical completion December 2025). Post-KKR closing, Sempra will retain only 25% economic interest.

Total assets reached $110.9B in 2025, up 15% from $96.2B, reflecting massive capital deployment across all three platforms.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement (FY2025 — GAAP vs. Adjusted)**

GAAP earnings fell 36% to $1.796B due to three non-recurring charges:
- $703M tax charge from SI Partners held-for-sale reclassification
- $464M after-tax wildfire mitigation program (WMP) regulatory disallowance
- $445M FX/inflation swing ($181M unfavorable vs. $264M favorable in 2024)

Adjusted ABP earnings were $3.154B, essentially flat with 2024's $3.158B. Normalized earnings are stable, not declining, while the $38.7B capex program begins to drive rate base growth from 2026 onward.

**Cash Flow**

- Operating cash flow: $4.565B (down 7% from $4.907B in 2024)
- Capex: $10.612B (up 29% from $8.215B)
- Free cash flow: -$6.047B
- Equity contributions to Oncor: $2.013B (up from $976M in 2024)
- Dividends paid: $1.603B

The negative FCF is structural for the current phase — Sempra is in a heavy capital deployment cycle that will not generate positive free cash flow until capex normalizes (likely 2028+). This is funded through the combination of KKR proceeds ($4.65B at close, $4.14B deferred), the $3B ATM equity program (~$2.6B remaining), retained earnings, and incremental debt.

**Balance Sheet**

- Total debt: $33.1B
- Debt-to-equity: 3.51x
- Debt-to-capitalization: 53% (up from 49% in 2024)
- Available credit facilities: $7.7B
- Credit ratings: Baa2/BBB+/BBB+ (parent), all with negative outlooks

The leverage is appropriate for a regulated utility (peer range typically 1.5-4.0x D/E), but the negative outlooks signal that rating agencies view the current capex cycle as straining credit metrics. A one-notch downgrade would raise financing costs by approximately 10-20bps on new issuances across the debt stack — material but manageable.

**Segment Profitability (FY2025)**

- California: $1.428B (-23% YoY, impacted by WMP disallowance)
- Texas: $861M (+10% YoY, Oncor growth + UTM mechanism)
- Infrastructure: -$160M (vs. +$911M, tax charge + FX)

Post-KKR, the earnings mix shifts to approximately 75% regulated/25% infrastructure, creating a lower-volatility earnings profile more consistent with utility peer multiples.

## Growth Analysis

**Oncor: The Primary Growth Engine**

Oncor's rate base is growing at ~17% CAGR, driven by:
1. Data center interconnection demand in Texas (ERCOT load growth)
2. Permian Basin Reliability Plan (765-kV transmission investment)
3. Industrial electrification and manufacturing reshoring
4. Population growth in DFW metroplex

The $47.5B five-year capex plan (potentially $57.5B with incremental projects) requires ongoing equity contributions from Sempra. At 80.25% ownership, Sempra's proportional equity injection requirement is approximately $7-9B over the period.

The Texas Utility Mechanisms (UTM) legislation, effective 2025, allows annual interim rate updates for T&D capital investments through 2035 — eliminating the traditional regulatory lag that compressed earned ROEs during heavy capex periods. This is a structural improvement in the Texas regulatory model.

**EPS Growth Trajectory**

Consensus FY2026E: $5.11 (implies ~18.3x forward P/E at current $93.67)
Consensus FY2027E: $5.53 (implies ~16.9x forward P/E)
Implied EPS CAGR: ~8% (2025 adjusted through 2027)

Management's stated target is 6-8% EPS growth from the 2025 adjusted base. Estimate revisions have been modestly positive (+2.1% for current quarter over 30 days) but flat for full-year figures (+0.1%), suggesting consensus already reflects the guided growth rate.

**LNG Contribution (Declining Post-KKR)**

ECA LNG Phase 1 first cargoes expected spring 2026 provides a near-term catalyst, but post-KKR Sempra's ownership drops to ~7-13% across the infrastructure portfolio. The reduced LNG exposure means less upside from a global energy crisis but also less volatility from tariffs, FX, and construction risk.

## Valuation Assessment

**Current Multiples**

| Trailing P/E | 34.0x | 18.9x |
| Forward P/E (FY26E) | 18.3x | ~17-19x* |
| Dividend Yield | 2.8% | 2.9% |
| P/B (implied) | ~1.9x | ~1.5-1.7x* |

*Estimated peer forward multiples based on growth-adjusted comparisons.

The trailing P/E of 34x is misleading due to the distorted FY2025 GAAP earnings. On a forward basis, Sempra trades at ~18.3x FY2026E, which is within the peer range but at the upper end. Given Sempra's superior growth profile (8% EPS CAGR vs. 5-6% for D, ED, XEL), a modest premium is warranted — but at current levels, that premium is already captured.

**DCF Sensitivity**

At 8% EPS growth, $5.11 FY26E base, and a terminal P/E of 18x (peer median) applied to FY27E EPS of $5.53, the 12-month fair value is approximately $99-100. Adding the dividend ($2.63) brings total return potential to ~9-10%. This is modestly attractive but insufficient for a BUY given the risk profile.

**What the Market Is Pricing**

The stock at $93.67 reflects: (1) successful KKR close, (2) Oncor UTM mechanism working as designed, (3) no material LA wildfire liability, and (4) continued investment-grade ratings. Any disruption to these assumptions creates downside from an already-discounted base.

## Competitive Landscape

Sempra's competitive position is strong and improving:

1. **Oncor** has no direct competitor in its Texas service territory and benefits from ERCOT's faster growth relative to other U.S. ISOs. The UTM mechanism provides regulatory support unavailable in most jurisdictions.

2. **SoCalGas** is the largest U.S. gas utility with a natural monopoly, though it faces long-term decarbonization headwinds from California's electrification policies.

3. **SDG&E** has lost commodity customers to CCAs but retains full T&D services and wildfire-related investment requirements that expand rate base.

4. **LNG platform** — while being partially divested — gives Sempra optionality in a structurally tight global gas market. Port Arthur LNG Phases 1 and 2 are among the lowest-cost new liquefaction capacity in the world.

Texas is gaining share of U.S. electricity demand growth, and Sempra owns the dominant T&D platform positioned to capture that growth. The strategic shift toward pure regulated utility is the correct call given the market's valuation preference for predictable earnings.

## Risk Assessment

**1. LA Wildfire Litigation (Severity: HIGH, Probability: Unknown)**

SoCalGas faces pending litigation related to the January 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires that destroyed ~16,200 structures. Management states it "cannot estimate the timing, costs, other impacts or ultimate outcome." While most SoCalGas infrastructure in affected areas is underground (reducing the likely causal link vs. electric utilities), the presence of active lawsuits creates a binary tail risk. Historical precedent (PG&E 2019 bankruptcy) demonstrates that California utility fire liabilities can reach existential proportions.

Mitigant: SoCalGas gas infrastructure is fundamentally different from electric transmission that causes sparks. The legal theory of liability is less obvious. However, the market may not fully differentiate this nuance.

**2. Credit Rating Downgrade (Severity: MODERATE, Probability: 30-40%)**

Both Moody's and S&P have negative outlooks. A downgrade would increase borrowing costs on the ~$38.7B in planned capex financing. The KKR transaction closing is the key event that either resolves or exacerbates this risk.

**3. KKR Transaction Execution (Severity: MODERATE, Probability of Failure: 10-15%)**

Mexican antitrust approval and lender consents remain outstanding. If the deal fails, Sempra's deleveraging plan is compromised, credit rating pressure intensifies, and the company retains higher-volatility infrastructure earnings.

**4. Securities Fraud Investigations (Severity: UNKNOWN)**

Multiple law firms announced investigations in March-April 2026. The basis is unspecified. These frequently amount to nothing, but they create headline risk and could indicate undisclosed material information.

**5. Tariff Impact on LNG Construction Costs**

25% tariffs on steel and aluminum, plus Chinese retaliatory tariffs on U.S. LNG, could increase PA LNG construction costs and reduce contracted demand for future phases.

### Options Market Signal

ATM IV of 29.6% is 10.2pp above 19.4% historical volatility — the options market is pricing a specific near-term event (likely wildfire litigation development or KKR transaction update). The term structure in backwardation (29.6% near-term vs. 24.4% at 6-month) confirms the market sees an imminent catalyst rather than persistent uncertainty.

Put/call skew at 17.6% is elevated, indicating directional hedging demand to the downside. Unusual call activity at the $100 strike (June 2026 expiry, 3.9x volume/OI) suggests some participants are positioning for upside past the near-term event.

The ATM IV collapsed 10pp intraday (from 39.6% to 29.6%) alongside a 3.4% price decline — this may reflect the resolution of a specific event premium (potentially earnings passing) with the price decline reflecting the earnings content itself.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($105-118)**

- KKR transaction closes Q2-Q3 2026, generating $4.65B cash and resolving credit rating concerns
- Oncor's rate case settlement approved at stipulated 9.75% ROE / 43.5% equity layer
- LA wildfire litigation dismissed or settled at immaterial cost (underground gas infrastructure liability theory fails)
- ECA LNG Phase 1 reaches commercial operations summer 2026
- EPS growth trajectory of 8% sustains through 2028-2030
- Re-rating to 20-21x forward as "pure regulated utility" story gains traction

At 20x FY27E of $5.53, fair value = $110 + $2.63 dividend = $113 total return target.

**Bear Case ($73-80)**

- LA wildfire litigation results in material liability ($2-5B+ range, comparable to PG&E precedent)
- KKR transaction delayed or restructured, credit downgrade occurs
- Oncor rate case settlement rejected, requiring refiling at lower ROE
- Rising rates increase cost of $38.7B capex program, compressing earned spreads
- Securities investigations reveal material disclosure deficiencies

At 14x FY27E (distressed utility multiple, cf. PG&E at 12.6x), stock = $77. If fire liability emerges as existential, equity value could be severely impaired.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Price declines to $82-85 (15x FY26E), providing >20% upside to $100+ fair value with sufficient margin of safety for wildfire risk
2. LA wildfire litigation meaningfully de-risked (cases dismissed, favorable preliminary rulings, or limited discovery scope that constrains liability)
3. KKR transaction closes AND credit outlooks revised to stable

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. LA wildfire litigation results in discovery suggesting multi-billion dollar liability exposure
2. Credit rating downgraded AND KKR transaction falls through
3. Oncor rate case settlement rejected with regulatory signal of lower future ROEs

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings (likely May 2026 for Q1 results already reported, then Q2 in August 2026); KKR close (Q2-Q3 2026); LA fire litigation updates (ongoing through 2026).

## Conclusion

Sempra has the best rate base growth story among large-cap U.S. utilities, driven by Oncor's structural position in Texas's expanding electricity market. The capital recycling through the KKR transaction is strategically sound and positions the company for cleaner earnings with lower volatility. Management is executing on a credible 6-8% EPS growth target supported by visible capex deployment.

However, at 18.3x forward earnings with negative credit outlooks and unquantifiable wildfire litigation risk, the current price reflects the bull case thesis without adequately discounting the downside scenarios. The 2.8% dividend yield provides limited cushion. We recommend holding existing positions for the growth trajectory while waiting for either (a) a price correction that provides margin of safety or (b) resolution of key binary risks before adding capital. The analyst consensus median target of $105 (12% upside) is achievable but does not offer sufficient reward for the risks at hand.
