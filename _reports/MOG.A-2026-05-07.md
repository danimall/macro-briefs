## Executive Summary

Moog Inc. is a precision motion controls specialist with 75% aerospace & defense revenue exposure, sole-source positions on multi-decade military platforms (F-35, MV-75), and a record backlog of $6B+ that provides 1.5x+ annual revenue coverage. The company is in the midst of a genuine earnings inflection: FY2025 diluted EPS of $7.33 (GAAP) and adjusted EPS of approximately $8.69 are accelerating toward FY2026 guided adjusted EPS of $10.60 (~22% adjusted EPS growth), driven by operating leverage on 13% revenue growth, pricing initiatives, and portfolio simplification. Q2 FY2026 reported adjusted EPS of $2.64, beating expectations and continuing a pattern of upside surprises.

At $316.57, the stock trades at approximately 30x FY2026 guided adjusted EPS — a premium multiple for a defense/aerospace company, but supported by 33% 12-month backlog growth, structural competitive moats, and emerging data center cooling revenue. The material weakness in Commercial Aircraft aftermarket contract accounting is a legitimate risk that caps conviction, but does not threaten the core business trajectory. Defense spending tailwinds, European rearmament demand, and AI-driven data center infrastructure buildout provide multiple growth vectors simultaneously.

The combination of accelerating earnings, record backlog conversion visibility, and a forward multiple of ~30x on guided earnings represents an attractive risk/reward for a business with this quality of competitive positioning.

## Company Overview

Moog designs, manufactures, and integrates high-performance precision motion and fluid control systems across four segments:

- **Space & Defense** (29% of FY2025 revenue, $1.11B): Missile steering, satellite avionics, spacecraft propulsion, defense turrets, launch vehicle thrust vector controls
- **Military Aircraft** (23%, $888M): Primary/secondary flight controls for F-35, MV-75 tiltrotor; tactical systems; aftermarket
- **Commercial Aircraft** (23%, $904M): Flight controls for Boeing 787, Airbus A350/A320/A330; growing aftermarket (37% of segment)
- **Industrial** (25%, $956M): Heavy machinery controls, flight simulation, energy, medical devices, liquid cooling pumps for data centers

U.S. Government contracts represent 38% of total sales. 64% of revenue is recognized over time on long-duration contracts. The company has ~5.7 million sq. ft. of manufacturing capacity globally.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** FY2022 $3.04B → FY2023 $3.32B (+9%) → FY2024 $3.61B (+9%) → FY2025 $3.86B (+7%). The deceleration in FY2025 reflects Industrial segment divestitures (-4% reported decline due to divestitures completed at the beginning of the fiscal year); underlying A&D growth remained 9-15%. Q2 FY2026 showed 13% revenue growth, confirming re-acceleration.

**Profitability:** Diluted EPS compounded at 16% over FY2023-2025 ($5.47 → $6.45 → $7.33). Segment operating margins cluster in 11-12.5% across all four segments. Gross margin compressed slightly to 27.4% from 28.1% due to loss of Employee Retention Credits and tariff pressures, but this is more than offset by volume leverage and pricing actions.

**Cash Flow:** OCF improved from $140M (FY2023) → $198M (FY2024) → $273M (FY2025). FCF of $128M (3.3% margin) is modest but reflects the working capital intensity inherent to long-duration defense contracts ($914M inventories, $744M unbilled receivables). Customer advances grew $82M in FY2025, indicating improving cash dynamics on new program starts.

**Balance Sheet:** As of the Q2 FY2026 period (March 2026), Moog successfully refinanced its capital structure. The company issued $500M of 5.50% senior notes due October 2034, redeemed the old $500M 4.25% notes due December 2027 in full on April 3, 2026, and extended its revolving credit facility and term loan maturities to February 2031. Pro forma for the redemption, the company has no significant maturities until 2031, with approximately $1.1B in unused credit capacity. The total liabilities-to-equity ratio was 1.22x at FY2025 year-end (debt-to-equity was approximately 0.47x based on ~$946M debt against ~$1.99B shareholders' equity). Altman Z-Score of 2.33 reflects working capital intensity rather than distress. Leverage is well within 4.0x covenant limits.

**Capital Allocation:** FY2025 saw $100M in buybacks (at ~$187/share — 40% below current price, indicating management recognized undervaluation), $145M capex, $41M acquisitions, and $36M dividends. Total shareholder returns (buybacks + dividends) of $136M.

## Growth Analysis

**Backlog as Forward Revenue Indicator:** Total backlog of $6.01B (+19% YoY in FY2025) with 12-month backlog of $3.0B (+20%) provides exceptional visibility. Q2 FY2026 set new records with 12-month backlog growing 33% YoY to $3.3B. This represents firm orders on defense contracts with multi-year execution timelines.

**FY2026 Guided Earnings Acceleration:** Management guided FY2026 adjusted EPS to $10.60 ± $0.20, representing approximately 22% growth over FY2025 adjusted EPS of ~$8.69 (based on FY2025 adjusted net earnings of $278.7M). This acceleration derives from: (1) operating leverage on 13%+ revenue growth, (2) completion of restructuring charges, (3) pricing initiatives flowing through, (4) portfolio simplification via divestitures of lower-margin businesses.

**Multiple Growth Vectors:**
- Defense spending: F-35 production ramp, MV-75 development, European rearmament creating "new defense pursuits serving European needs"
- Commercial aerospace: Boeing 787 and Airbus widebody production increases, aftermarket at 37% of segment revenue
- Data center cooling: Liquid cooling pumps for AI data centers represent an emerging growth vector with growing backlog specifically called out by management
- Medical devices: Market share gains in enteral nutrition pumps and CT components

**Earnings Surprise Pattern:** Q2 FY2026 adjusted EPS of $2.64 continued a pattern of upside results relative to expectations, consistent with management's track record of conservative guidance in recent quarters.

## Valuation Assessment

At $316.57, the stock trades at:
- ~43x FY2025 GAAP EPS ($7.33) — appears expensive on trailing
- ~36x FY2025 adjusted EPS (~$8.69) — still elevated on trailing
- ~30x FY2026 guided adjusted EPS ($10.60) — reasonable for a defense/aerospace grower with this backlog

The trailing multiple is misleading. FY2025 EPS reflects a period before the full benefit of pricing actions, volume leverage, and portfolio simplification. The forward multiple of ~30x on guided earnings — with ~22% adjusted earnings growth underway, 33% backlog growth, and consecutive upside results — reflects a premium supported by the quality of competitive positioning and growth visibility.

Comparable defense primes generally trade at lower forward multiples but with mid-single-digit growth. Moog's premium is justified by its superior growth rate (~22% adjusted earnings growth vs. mid-single-digit for larger primes), sole-source positioning, and emerging data center exposure.

The stock has appreciated approximately 84% YoY per April 2026 reporting. This appreciation is largely explained by fundamental improvement: ~22% adjusted earnings growth, record backlog, and accelerating revenue, though some portion likely reflects multiple expansion and sector re-rating in addition to earnings revisions.

## Competitive Landscape

Moog's competitive position is structurally advantaged:

1. **Sole-source positioning on safety-critical systems:** Once designed into a military aircraft or spacecraft flight control system, switching costs are prohibitive. Qualification testing, safety certification, and production tooling create barriers measured in years and hundreds of millions of dollars.

2. **Long-duration platform exposure:** F-35 has a projected service life through 2070+. Boeing 787 and Airbus A350 will be in production for decades. These positions generate recurring revenue through production, spares, and aftermarket for 30-50 year periods.

3. **Engineering depth:** Moog's precision motion control expertise spans hydraulic, electric, and hybrid actuation across extreme environments (space, deep sea, high-g military applications). This cross-domain capability enables sole-source wins that generalist suppliers cannot replicate.

4. **Portfolio shaping improving mix:** Divestiture of low-margin industrial businesses (motors, hydraulics) and acquisition of COTSWORKS (rugged optical components) demonstrate active management of competitive positioning toward higher-value markets.

5. **Data center differentiation:** Liquid cooling pump technology leverages existing precision fluid control expertise for AI infrastructure — a structural adjacency rather than a stretch.

## Risk Assessment

**Material Weakness (Moderate-High):** Ernst & Young issued an adverse opinion on internal controls due to inaccurate inputs in cost-to-cost revenue recognition for Commercial Aircraft aftermarket contracts. Management determined that Moog's disclosure controls and procedures were not effective as of FY2025 year-end due to the material weakness. Cumulative pre-FY2023 impact was $39M in retained earnings reduction. While management determined no individual period was materially misstated and the financial-statement audit opinion was unqualified, this raises legitimate questions about estimation quality across the broader contract portfolio. Remediation is underway but incomplete. The risk is that additional misstatements surface, though the company's characterization of this as a "distinct group" of contracts suggests localization.

**Refinancing Completed — Incremental Interest Cost:** The March-April 2026 refinancing adds approximately $6.25M in annual pre-tax interest expense on the higher coupon (5.50% vs. 4.25%). The refinancing execution risk has been resolved.

**Boeing Concentration (10% of sales, $263M receivables):** Boeing's production challenges, labor disruptions, and financial stress create customer concentration risk. A significant Boeing production cut or credit event would impact both revenue and receivables recovery.

**Tariff Exposure:** Management guides 110 bps of tariff headwind in FY2026. With manufacturing across 12+ countries and a complex global supply chain, escalating trade tensions represent ongoing margin pressure that may exceed current guidance.

**Fixed-Price Contract Risk:** 90% of over-time sales are fixed-price. Cost overruns on development programs can lead to contract loss reserves (currently $84M). New programs like MV-75 are in development phases where estimation risk is highest.

**Dual-Class Structure:** Class B shares carry 10x voting rights (one vote per share vs. one-tenth vote for Class A). Class A shareholders elect at least 25% of the board, with Class B electing the balance. With concentrated family/insider ownership, minority Class A shareholders have limited governance influence. This is a permanent structural feature.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($380-420, +20-33%):** Backlog conversion continues at 33%+ growth rates. FY2026 EPS exceeds guidance ($10.80+) on continued beats. European rearmament orders accelerate. Data center cooling becomes a recognized growth segment, attracting higher-multiple investors. Material weakness remediation completed without additional findings. Stock re-rates to 35-40x forward earnings on growth visibility. Target: 35x * $11.00 = $385.

**Bear Case ($240-260, -18-24%):** Material weakness investigation reveals broader estimation issues, triggering restatement. Boeing production cuts reduce Commercial Aircraft segment revenue significantly (stress-case assumption of 15-20% reduction). Tariffs exceed 110 bps guidance. Industrial segment growth disappoints as data center cooling fails to scale. Stock derates to 25x forward earnings on credibility concerns. Target: 25x * $10.00 = $250.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**

2. 12-month backlog growth decelerates below 10% for two consecutive quarters (would signal demand peaking)
3. FY2026 full-year EPS comes in below $10.00 (materially missing guidance would indicate execution problems)

## Conclusion

Moog is a structurally advantaged precision motion controls business experiencing a genuine earnings inflection driven by defense spending, commercial aerospace recovery, and emerging data center demand. At ~30x FY2026 guided adjusted EPS of $10.60, with ~22% adjusted earnings growth underway and 33% backlog growth, the forward valuation is reasonable for this quality of competitive positioning. The material weakness in internal controls warrants monitoring but does not threaten the core growth thesis. The 2027 refinancing wall has been eliminated through successful March 2026 capital markets transactions. BUY with a 12-18 month horizon targeting $370 base case.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The corrected adjusted EPS growth rate (~22% vs. previously cited 45%) and resulting PEG ratio (~1.35x vs. previously cited 0.67x) materially weaken the valuation argument supporting the price target and BUY recommendation.
