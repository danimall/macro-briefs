## Executive Summary

Allegro MicroSystems is the global leader in magnetic sensor ICs, commanding dominant market share in a structurally growing addressable market driven by vehicle electrification and ADAS adoption. The company is emerging from a severe cyclical downturn (FY2025 revenue -31%) with FY2026 revenue recovering to $890M (+23% YoY) and Q1 FY2027 guidance of $250M implying continued acceleration. The rejected onsemi acquisition offer at ~$35/share validates strategic value and provides downside optionality.

The fundamental recovery trajectory is intact and well-supported by multiple secular tailwinds. However, the stock at $51.37 has appreciated 171% from its May 2025 low of $18.17, now trades at the top of its 52-week range, and is priced at roughly 97x FY2027 consensus EPS of $0.53 (or ~53x FY2028E of $0.97). While this is a cyclical recovery stock that warrants forward-looking valuation, the current price embeds substantial growth execution that must occur over the next 12-18 months. Options markets are pricing extreme near-term uncertainty with 108.5% IV against 52% realized vol, and short interest at 12.7% suggests meaningful institutional skepticism at these levels.

The correct posture is to acknowledge the quality of the franchise and the validity of the recovery thesis while recognizing that risk/reward has shifted unfavorably after a 171% move. A pullback toward $38-42 would offer a more attractive entry.

## Company Overview

Allegro MicroSystems designs and markets sensor ICs and power ICs, operating as a fabless semiconductor company with outsourced manufacturing to UMC, TSMC, Tower, and Polar Semiconductor. The company ships ~1.5 billion units annually across 1,500+ products to 10,000+ customers globally.

**Revenue Mix:**
- Magnetic Sensors: 65% of FY2025 revenue (current sensors, position sensors including TMR/XtremeSense, speed sensors)
- Power ICs: 35% (motor drivers, voltage regulators, LED drivers, isolated gate drivers)

**End Market Exposure:**
- Automotive: 73.8% (e-Mobility, ICE, safety/comfort)
- Industrial & Other: 26.2% (data centers, clean energy, factory automation)

**Geographic Distribution:** Greater China 25%, Japan 21%, Europe 15%, U.S. 13%, South Korea 10%, Other Asia 12%

The company holds 1,942 active patents and acquired Crocus Technology's TMR (Tunneling Magnetoresistance) platform in October 2023 for $412M, adding the highest-sensitivity magnetic sensing technology available for EV and ADAS applications.

## Growth Analysis

**Secular Drivers:**
1. **EV/HEV content uplift:** BEV content per vehicle is ~2.5x ICE content for Allegro's products. Each EV powertrain requires current sensors, position sensors, and isolated gate drivers that ICE vehicles don't need.
2. **ADAS proliferation:** Sensor requirements expanding from premium to mass-market vehicles; radar, LiDAR, and camera subsystems all require magnetic sensing and power management.
3. **48V architecture transition:** Creates new power IC opportunities for 100V BCD technology (Allegro's proprietary platform).
4. **Data center power management:** Industrial diversification reduces automotive dependence over time.

**Recovery Trajectory:**
- Q4 FY2026: $243M (+26% YoY)
- Q1 FY2027 guidance: $250M midpoint (continued sequential growth)
- FY2027E consensus EPS: $0.53; FY2028E: $0.97
- 90-day estimate revision: +1.9% (current year), +2.8% (next year)

The recovery is confirmed but estimate revisions are modest (+1-3% over 90 days). This is consistent with a company returning to normalized growth from a cyclical trough rather than experiencing a demand surge that significantly exceeds expectations. Revenue remains ~15% below the FY2024 peak of $1,049M.

**Crocus/TMR Optionality:** TMR technology grew even during the FY2025 downturn, suggesting differentiated demand. As EV powertrains require higher accuracy sensing at lower power, TMR positions Allegro ahead of Hall-effect incumbent solutions from competitors.

## Valuation Assessment

**Current Valuation:**
- Market cap: $8.8B at $51.37
- FY2027E EPS: $0.53 → P/E: ~97x
- FY2028E EPS: $0.97 → P/E: ~53x
- Peer median P/E: 23.8x (CRUS 20.8x, SWKS 24.1x, QRVO 23.8x)
- EV/Revenue (FY2026 $890M): ~10.2x

At 53x FY2028 earnings, Allegro trades at more than 2x its profitable analog semiconductor peer group. The premium is justified by: (1) the cyclical recovery trajectory, (2) secular EV/ADAS growth above analog peer growth rates, (3) #1 market share in magnetic sensing, and (4) acquisition optionality (onsemi bid at ~$35 now 32% below market).

However, the premium is now extreme relative to what it was pricing 6 months ago. In November 2025, the stock traded at $23-24, implying ~45x FY2027E at the time — already a healthy premium. The subsequent 120% appreciation reflects legitimate recovery confirmation (beat-and-raise in Q4 FY2026, upgraded analyst ratings) but has pushed valuation to levels that require continued flawless execution.

Consensus mean target is $46.75, median $45.50, high $52.00. The stock is trading essentially at the highest analyst price target. While analyst targets on cyclical recovery stocks can lag, at 53x forward P/E, proximity to targets reflects consensus growth already priced rather than further upside.

**Normalized earnings power:** If Allegro returns to FY2024 peak revenue ($1,049M) with 55% gross margins and 20% operating margins (~$210M operating income, ~$1.00 EPS), the stock at $51 trades at ~51x normalized earnings. For a mid-single-digit revenue growth company at maturity (post-recovery), that remains expensive vs. the 20-25x range for analog/mixed-signal peers.

## Competitive Landscape

Allegro's competitive position is strong and improving:

**Dominant Market Share:** #1 globally in magnetic sensor ICs, a position built over decades through automotive qualification (2-4 year design-in cycles create high switching costs) and 1,942 patents.

**Technology Differentiation:**
- TMR (XtremeSense) from Crocus acquisition provides highest sensitivity/lowest power — critical for EV applications
- Proprietary 100V BCD for 48V architectures
- Isolated gate drivers (IGD) technology fills a key gap between sensing and power switching

**Structural Barriers:** Automotive IC qualification requirements (IATF 16949, AEC-Q100) create 3-5 year barriers to entry for new competitors. Design cycles of 2-4 years mean customer switching costs are very high once designed in.

**Competitive Threats:** Infineon and Texas Instruments compete in power ICs with scale advantages. China's domestic semiconductor push could eventually create regional competitors in magnetic sensing. However, near-term displacement risk is low given qualification timelines.

The onsemi bid validates that a major strategic player views Allegro's market position as irreplaceable — they were willing to pay ~$7B for the franchise.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Valuation Risk (HIGH):** At 53x FY2028 EPS, the stock prices in substantial recovery execution. Any disappointment in the growth trajectory — EV adoption slowdown, tariff disruption to automotive supply chains, or inventory re-accumulation — would compress the multiple toward peer levels (24x), implying 50%+ downside. This is the primary risk at current levels.

**2. Tariff & Geopolitical (ELEVATED):** U.S. tariffs on automobiles and auto parts (imposed March 2025) could slow OEM production volumes. China's antimony and rare-earth export restrictions threaten wafer fabrication materials. Greater China at 25% of revenue is exposed to retaliatory trade actions.

**3. EV Policy Uncertainty:** U.S. executive orders considering elimination of EV subsidies could slow adoption rates, directly impacting Allegro's highest-growth end market (e-Mobility content is 2.5x ICE).

**4. Operating Leverage Cuts Both Ways:** The FY2025 downturn proved that a 31% revenue decline produces an operating loss. With debt now at $345M (vs. $25M pre-Sanken buyback), the next cyclical downturn would be more painful.

**5. Customer Concentration & Automotive Cyclicality:** 73.8% automotive exposure + Greater China as largest single geography creates concentrated vulnerability to industry downturns.

**6. onsemi Bid Uncertainty:** The rejected ~$35 bid creates ongoing headline risk and governance distraction. The 10-K risk factors specifically mention costs and disruption associated with unsolicited acquisition proposals. A hostile proxy fight or tender offer could disrupt operational focus during the recovery.

- Gross margins recover above 55%, operating margins above 22%

**Base Case ($48-52):**
- Recovery continues along consensus trajectory (FY2027 $0.53, FY2028 $0.97)
- Automotive secular growth supports premium vs. peers but at current premium levels
- Stock consolidates after 171% run; time correction rather than price correction
- 53x FY2028E represents fair value given growth profile + acquisition optionality

4. Stock breaks above $55 without fundamental support (further multiple expansion without earnings delivery)

## Conclusion

Allegro MicroSystems is a high-quality franchise with dominant market share in a structurally growing semiconductor niche. The cyclical recovery is confirmed, the technology portfolio (TMR, IGD, 100V BCD) positions the company for continued automotive content growth, and the rejected onsemi bid provides both downside support and upside optionality.

The 171% appreciation over 12 months has already capitalized substantial recovery value. At 53x FY2028 consensus EPS, the stock requires continued flawless execution with no hiccups from tariffs, EV policy, or inventory cycles. Options markets pricing 108% near-term vol and 12.7% short interest both signal that sophisticated market participants question the sustainability of this valuation. Analyst consensus targets ($44-$52) are either at or below the current price.

HOLD with a growth_hold classification — the secular thesis is intact and the company remains well-positioned for automotive electrification, but entry at current levels offers insufficient margin of safety given the magnitude of cyclical and geopolitical risks. The preferred re-entry zone is $38-42, where valuation would offer a 35-40x FY2028 P/E consistent with the growth premium this business deserves.
