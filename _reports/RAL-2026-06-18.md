**Research Date: June 18, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Ralliant is a global precision-instruments and engineered-products company spun off from Fortive on June 28, 2025 (one RAL share per three FTV shares). It carries two segments with sharply divergent dynamics: a steadier, ~27%-margin Sensors & Safety Systems (S&SS) business (61% of revenue) riding grid-modernization and defense/space demand, and a more cyclical Test & Measurement (T&M) business (39%) anchored by Tektronix and Keithley that was the source of a $1.44B goodwill impairment tied to the EA Elektro-Automatik EV bet. The headline FY2025 GAAP net loss of -$1.22B and the resulting "distress" screens (Altman Z 0.32) are artifacts of that single non-cash writedown; on a cash basis the business generated ~$358M of free cash flow at a 17.3% FCF margin and is comfortably covenant-compliant.

The investable development is the Q1 2026 reacceleration. Revenue grew +11.0% (+8.8% organic), reversing FY2025's -4.0% organic decline. T&M returned to growth (+8.7% organic, operating loss narrowing from -6.3% to -1.5% margin) on communications, semiconductor-test, and AI-datacenter demand, while S&SS grew +8.8% organic on defense and sensors. Q1 adjusted EPS of $0.57 beat the $0.49 consensus, management raised FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance to $2.53–$2.69, and consensus EPS estimates have moved up roughly 13% on both the current and next fiscal year over the past 90 days. The track record is a clean four-for-four on quarterly beats. As an Information Technology name with a genuine organic-growth reacceleration and rising estimates, Ralliant sits in the only category where selection has historically added durable alpha.

The constraint is price. The stock has already re-rated ~45% from its February low of $37.27 to ~$67–68, trading at roughly 25–26x the midpoint of FY2026 adjusted guidance and ~22x FY2027 consensus. That is a full multiple for a high-single-digit organic grower with a recovering-but-still-loss-making T&M segment, a thin (~20%) cushion on the Tektronix indefinite-lived trade name, and management's own warning that missing revised T&M/EA projections could trigger further impairment. Near-term options pricing (154% 1-week ATM IV in backwardation) signals the market is positioning for an imminent event. The fundamental story is improving but the entry point offers no asymmetric edge. We rate Ralliant a HOLD (growth-hold) and would become buyers on a pullback toward the low-to-mid $50s, where the multiple compresses to a level the growth rate can justify.

## Company Overview

Ralliant operates ~7,000 employees, ~2,200 active patents, and serves 90,000+ customers across 90+ countries from its Raleigh, NC headquarters. It is the former "Precision Technologies" segment of Fortive, and it inherited the Danaher/Fortive lean-operating playbook now branded the Ralliant Business System (RBS), which management positions as its core operational differentiator.

**Test & Measurement (T&M)** — FY2025 sales $801.5M (39% of revenue). Brands include Tektronix, Keithley Instruments, Sonix, and EA Elektro-Automatik. Products span oscilloscopes, probes, source-measure units, semiconductor test systems, and high-power bidirectional power supplies. End markets are semiconductor, communications, diversified electronics, and EV/energy storage. This is the cyclical, higher-growth-potential, and currently lower-margin segment. It competes against the larger, better-capitalized Keysight, plus Rohde & Schwarz, Ametek, and Teledyne.

**Sensors & Safety Systems (S&SS)** — FY2025 sales $1,267.3M (61% of revenue). Brands include Qualitrol (grid monitoring), Gems Sensors, Setra, Hengstler Dynapar, Anderson-Negele, Dover Motion, and Pacific Scientific Energetic Materials (satellite/rocket ignition, defense pyrotechnics). End markets are utilities (grid modernization), defense and space, and industrial manufacturing. At ~27% operating margins, this is the cash engine. Competitors include Esco, Ensign-Bickford, Endress+Hauser, ifm, and Brooks Instrument.

The two-segment structure maps onto two of the strongest structural demand themes currently visible: grid/electrification capex (which Qualitrol serves directly) and the multi-front defense/space cycle (which Pacific Scientific's energetics serve). T&M's exposure to semiconductor and AI-datacenter test is the third theme, though that segment carries the most cyclical variability.

## Financial Analysis

The reported FY2025 figures require contextualization. The GAAP net loss and -57.2% operating margin trace almost entirely to the T&M goodwill impairment, written against the EA Elektro-Automatik acquisition after "slower-than-anticipated progression and recent reduction in industry forecasts of future EV adoption." Stripping that out, the underlying business is profitable and cash-generative.

- **FY2025 revenue:** $2,068.8M, -4.0% YoY (-4.1% organic), driven by a 15.3% organic decline in T&M partly offset by +4.6% organic growth in S&SS.
- **Gross margin:** 50.3% (vs. 51.6% in 2024) — structurally strong and indicative of differentiated, IP-protected products.
- **Free cash flow:** operating cash flow $397.6M; capex light at ~$39M (~1.9% of sales). On the current ~$7.7B market cap the ~$358M FCF is roughly a 4.6% FCF yield, reasonable but not cheap for the growth profile.
- **Balance sheet:** $1.15B term-loan debt drawn at separation (to pay Fortive $1.15B for contributed assets); $268M cash at Q1 2026. Reported D/E of 2.34x looks elevated, but EBITDA-based net leverage sits well within the 3.5x covenant. All $1.15B is floating-rate (effective ~4.8–5.0%); a 100bps move equals ~$6M of interest. The near-term maturity was refinanced in March 2026 (Amendment No. 2) into a 2029 tranche, removing the immediate refinancing risk.

On GAAP optics: Q1 2026 GAAP EPS of $0.39 appears weaker than the prior-year carve-out period (Q1 2025 GAAP EPS of $0.57), but the comparison is not apples-to-apples. The prior period carried no standalone debt interest (now ~$14.7M/quarter) and minimal public-company costs. Operating-margin compression to 12.7% from 15.3% is driven by ~$17.4M/quarter of new unallocated standalone public-company costs and dis-synergy compensation rather than operating deterioration. The Enterprise Productivity Program targets $50–60M in annualized savings by 2028 through a Group Purchasing Office and organizational simplification, the structural offset. The negative trailing P/E reported on data screens (-6.1x) is entirely a function of the impairment-driven GAAP loss and is not a usable valuation input.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis rests on the durability of the Q1 reacceleration across both segments.

**S&SS** carries the higher-conviction secular tailwinds. Qualitrol's grid-monitoring franchise is levered to the grid-modernization and electrification capex cycle. Pacific Scientific's energetics serve a defense and space buildout that remains structurally supported across multiple active fronts. At ~27% operating margins, growth here is high-quality and self-funding.

**T&M** is the swing factor. The EA/EV bet disappointed, but the broader franchise — Tektronix oscilloscopes, Keithley source-measure — is recovering on communications, semiconductor-test, and AI-datacenter demand. The FY2025 organic decline reflected a destocking down-cycle that the Q1 reacceleration suggests has bottomed, with comparisons now easy. T&M still competes from a scale disadvantage against Keysight, which constrains long-run share economics.

The estimate-revision picture is constructive: current-year EPS is up +12.9% over 90 days to $2.65, next-year up +13.1% to $3.07, with the current quarter revised up +18.0% over 90 days (+17.7% over 30 days). The four-quarter beat streak (3.7% to 18.1% surprises) supports confidence in the raised FY2026 guide. This revision velocity sits a notch below the +30% threshold that has historically marked the strongest inflection signals, and it is occurring on a stock that has already re-rated rather than on a depressed multiple. The growth is real and broad-based; how much of it the price now reflects is the open question.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$67–68, Ralliant trades at roughly 25–26x the $2.53–$2.69 FY2026 adjusted EPS guide and ~22x the $3.07 FY2027 consensus. The ~4.6% FCF yield and the high-single-digit organic growth rate yield a PEG well above 2 on near-term growth, which is full for a business of this cyclicality.

Peer comparisons are noisy because the sector is heterogeneous. The Electronic Equipment & Instruments cohort spans Vontier at ~10x, up through Cognex at ~73x, Novanta at ~115x, and Mirion at ~168x, with a peer-median P/E of 115 distorted by loss-making and high-multiple comps. Against that dispersion, Ralliant's ~22–26x forward multiple is neither cheap nor stretched; it sits roughly where a mid-quality, mid-growth precision-instruments compounder should trade once the impairment noise is removed.

The case against rewarding cheapness here is concrete. This stock is not mispriced low: it has appreciated 45% off its February low, the inflection is already in consensus estimates, and the multiple now fully captures the recovery narrative. Analyst targets bracket the current price tightly (mean $67, median $68, low $50, high $80), which on a stock at a full multiple signals the consensus optimism is largely embedded rather than lagging. Treating proximity to target as a "floor" applies to cheap inflection names; at ~25x forward with an active impairment tail, the more likely reading is that price and targets reflect the same recovery extrapolation, leaving asymmetry roughly balanced.

We anchor fair value near $63–66 on ~24x the FY2026 guide midpoint, modestly below the current price. A re-rating case to the high-$70s requires the T&M recovery to accelerate and the impairment tail to clear; a derating to the low-$50s would follow any T&M stumble or further impairment. The score reflects a full-but-not-egregious multiple with no identifiable mispricing in our favor.

## Competitive Landscape

The competitive trajectory is mixed but stabilizing. S&SS holds defensible positions in grid monitoring (Qualitrol) and defense energetics (Pacific Scientific), where switching costs, qualification requirements, and regulatory/security barriers protect share, and where the demand backdrop is structurally favorable. The ~27% margins and +4.6% organic FY2025 growth (against a declining T&M) demonstrate pricing power and end-market durability.

T&M is the weaker competitive position. The EA/EV impairment is an acknowledgment that the company overpaid for a franchise into a softening end market, and Tektronix/Keithley operate at a scale disadvantage to Keysight. The Q1 reacceleration and the narrowing operating loss suggest the franchise is regaining footing on the communications/semiconductor-test cycle rather than structurally losing share. The RBS operating system is a credible margin and working-capital lever, inherited and proven within the Fortive/Danaher lineage. On balance, the competitive position is stable-to-modestly-improving, supported by S&SS quality and a recovering T&M, but capped by T&M's structural scale gap and the questionable M&A underwriting that the impairment exposed.

## Risk Assessment

**Further T&M/EA impairment.** Ralliant paid ~$1.72B for EA in January 2024 and impaired $1.44B of related goodwill within ~18 months; remaining T&M goodwill is $847–901M, and the Tektronix indefinite-lived trade name passed its quantitative test with only a ~20% cushion. Management explicitly warns that missing revised projections could trigger additional writedowns. This is the single most material idiosyncratic risk and is not fully priced.

**M&A underwriting credibility.** The EA episode raises a genuine question about capital deployment discipline; the RBS model relies on serial acquisitions, and the most recent large deal was impaired almost immediately.

**Strategic handcuffs to mid-2027.** The Tax Matters Agreement restricts M&A and equity transactions for two years post-separation (through mid-2027) to preserve tax-free status, constraining the deal-driven growth model precisely when the equity currency is attractive. Uncapped indemnification obligations to Fortive also exist.

**Standalone cost ramp.** New corporate costs and lost Fortive scale-purchasing are real margin headwinds; the $50–60M productivity program is the offset but does not fully land until 2028.

**Tariffs and trade policy.** With 48.7% of sales ex-U.S., tariff escalation pressures input costs and demand. The IEEPA Supreme Court ruling and Ralliant's filed refund claims are potential un-modeled upside, with no recovery yet recognized.

**First-year SOX attestation** (pending with the second 10-K) and **defense/government budget exposure** (shutdowns, FAR/DFARS) are standard but worth monitoring.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing a near-term event. One-week ATM IV of 154.3% sits in steep backwardation against a 53.6% six-month tenor and a 54.8% one-year historical vol — an IV/HV spread of ~100 points and a 90th-percentile reading. The implied one-week move is ~6–7%. Skew is mildly call-heavy (-5.0%) and the put/call OI ratio collapsed to 0.02 from 0.08 since the prior snapshot, alongside the price move; positioning is not bearish, but the near-dated vol structure indicates the market expects an imminent catalyst (likely an earnings or corporate-action date). For a HOLD, this favors waiting for the event to resolve rather than chasing at the high.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The Q1 reacceleration is the start of a multi-year recovery: T&M rides the communications/semiconductor/AI-datacenter cycle back to its prior margin profile while S&SS compounds high-single-digit organic on grid and defense demand. The productivity program lands $50–60M, RBS drives margin expansion, the IEEPA refund provides un-modeled upside, and post-mid-2027 the company resumes accretive M&A. Estimates keep climbing past the current $3.07 FY2027 figure, and the stock re-rates to the high-$70s/$80 on FY2027 EPS approaching $3.30–$3.50.

**Bear case.** T&M's recovery proves a head-fake driven by easy comps; EV/communications demand softens, triggering a further impairment against the thin Tektronix cushion and the remaining T&M goodwill. Standalone costs prove stickier than guided, floating-rate interest expense bites, and the strategic handcuffs limit the growth model. The multiple compresses to ~18–19x on stalled estimates, taking the stock to the low-$50s, roughly a 22% decline from current.

The fundamental balance has improved materially since the spin, but the 45% rally has moved the price to where the bull and bear scenarios are roughly symmetric from here.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

Recommendation: **HOLD (growth-hold).** The growth thesis is intact and improving, but the current price offers no asymmetric edge.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- A pullback toward the low-to-mid $50s (~18–20x the FY2026 guide midpoint), which would restore asymmetric upside on an intact thesis.
- A second consecutive quarter of T&M organic growth ≥8% with the segment returning to positive operating margin, materially de-risking the impairment tail.
- Estimate-revision velocity accelerating past +30% over 90 days on confirmed end-market strength, or recognition of a material IEEPA tariff refund.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- A further goodwill or Tektronix trade-name impairment, or management language signaling one.
- T&M organic growth reverting to negative, indicating the recovery was comp-driven rather than demand-driven.
- A guidance cut below the FY2026 range, or net leverage approaching the 3.5x covenant.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (the near-dated options backwardation suggests an imminent event); reassess the full thesis on the Q2 2026 print and the realized ASR execution.

## Conclusion

Ralliant has executed a clean operational turn in its first year as an independent company. The "distress" screens are a false signal driven by a one-time EA/EV impairment; the underlying business generates strong free cash flow, holds 50%+ gross margins, and just delivered an organic reacceleration with a beat-and-raise. As an IT name with a genuine inflection and rising estimates, it sits in the most favorable selection category. The capital-allocation signal is constructive — buybacks executed near the lows at $42.40, authorization raised from $200M to $500M, a $100M ASR planned — though the EA impairment is a real mark against M&A discipline and a continuing balance-sheet risk given the thin Tektronix cushion.

The limiting factor is valuation. After the 45% move off the February low, the stock trades at a full ~25x forward multiple that already discounts the recovery, with analyst targets bracketing the current price tightly and near-term options pricing an imminent event. The risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric. We rate Ralliant a HOLD and would upgrade to BUY on a pullback toward the low-to-mid $50s or a second quarter confirming the T&M recovery and clearing the impairment overhang.
