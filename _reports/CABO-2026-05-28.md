**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Cable One is a broadband-focused communications provider serving approximately 1.0 million customers across 2.9 million passings in 24 U.S. states, concentrated in non-metropolitan markets. The company's stock has declined 97% from its 2021 peak of ~$2,200 to $55, reflecting a fundamental collapse of its rural broadband monopoly thesis as subsidized fiber competition overbuilds roughly 60% of its footprint.

The financial situation is approaching existential territory. Revenue declined 7.3% in Q1 2026 (accelerating from -4.9% in full-year 2025), residential data subscribers are eroding at ~13-14K per quarter with no stabilization visible, and the company faces a forced MBI acquisition that will add ~$1.4B in obligations (including $875-925M of debt maturing November 2027) to an already-stressed balance sheet. Post-MBI, total debt will approach $4.0-4.5B against a declining EBITDA base currently running at ~$733M annualized and falling.

The company generates meaningful cash flow ($278M FCF in 2025, $118M OCF in Q1 2026), which provides near-term debt service capacity. However, the combination of accelerating revenue declines, a 2028 maturity wall exceeding $1.6B, and debt trading at distressed levels (senior notes at 69 cents on the dollar) indicates the market is pricing material probability of credit impairment. At $315M market cap against $3.1B+ of debt, the equity is a levered call option on stabilization.

## Company Overview

Cable One operates under the Sparklight brand, providing broadband internet, video, and voice services to residential and business customers. Revenue is concentrated in residential data (60.1% of $1.5B total), with business data (15.3%), residential video (12.5%, declining rapidly), and other services comprising the remainder. The company offers Gigabit download speeds across all passings and has deployed multi-Gigabit service to 53% of markets, with DOCSIS 4.0 underway.

Historically, Cable One was positioned as a premium rural broadband consolidator with monopoly or near-monopoly positions in secondary and tertiary markets. The arrival of government-subsidized fiber competition (via BEAD, RDOF, and similar programs) has fundamentally undermined this thesis. Approximately 60% of Cable One's footprint now faces wired competition offering 100+ Mbps speeds, with additional builds planned.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory is worsening:**
- 2022: $1,706M
- 2023: $1,678M (-1.6%)
- 2024: $1,580M (-5.8%)
- 2025: $1,501M (-4.9%)
- Q1 2026 annualized: ~$1,412M (-7.3% YoY)

The acceleration from -5% to -7% indicates competitive pressures are intensifying. Residential data revenue declined 5.1% in Q1 2026 despite 0.8% ARPU growth, meaning volume losses are overwhelming pricing power.

**Adjusted EBITDA follows revenue down:**
- 2022: $912M
- 2023: $917M
- 2024: $854M
- 2025: $802M
- Q1 2026 annualized: ~$733M (-9.6% YoY)

EBITDA margin compression (from ~53% to ~52%) reflects operating deleverage as fixed costs spread over a declining subscriber base.

**Balance sheet is under severe stress:**
- Total debt: $3.12B (rising to ~$4.0-4.5B post-MBI)
- Cash: $166M
- Net debt: ~$2.95B
- Net leverage: ~3.8x trailing Adj. EBITDA (rising to ~5.0-5.5x post-MBI)
- Altman Z-Score: 0.62 (deep distress zone)
- Debt trading at distressed levels: Senior Notes at 69¢, Convertible Notes at 77.5¢

**2028 maturity concentration is extreme:** Approximately $1.6B+ matures that year (revolver facility, Term Loan B-4, 2028 convertible notes), and MBI's ~$900M of assumed debt matures November 2027. The company must refinance nearly $2.5B within 2 years against a deteriorating credit profile.

**Cash flow provides temporary oxygen:** FCF was $278M in 2025 and OCF was $118M in Q1 2026. Capex of ~$285M annually maintains the network. The dividend suspension saves $67M/year. At current trajectory, the business can service existing debt through 2027 absent the MBI acquisition. With MBI, the margin of safety narrows considerably.

## Growth Analysis

There is no organic growth story. Revenue is declining at an accelerating rate, subscribers are eroding consistently, and the competitive dynamics driving these losses (fiber overbuild, FWA) are structural and worsening.

Potential stabilization levers are modest:
- **Back-book price increases ($2-5/month):** May boost ARPU temporarily but risks accelerating churn in an overbuild environment.
- **DOCSIS 4.0 deployment:** Provides symmetric multi-gig speeds but fiber competitors already offer this.
- **MVNO mobile bundling:** Following Comcast/Charter playbook, but Cable One lacks the scale to negotiate comparable wholesale terms.
- **Value-tier customer acquisition:** Pivoting from premium-only to include price-sensitive customers, essentially conceding the market has commoditized.

Estimate revisions tell the story: next year's EPS consensus has fallen 37.9% over 90 days (from $35.24 to $21.90, single analyst). Current year EPS estimate declined 5.7% from $26.45 to $24.94 in the past 30 days. The direction is unambiguously negative.

## Valuation Assessment

At $55 per share with ~5.67M shares outstanding, the market cap is approximately $315M. With ~$2.95B net debt (rising to ~$3.8B+ post-MBI), enterprise value is approximately $3.3B currently.

- EV/EBITDA (trailing): ~4.1x on $802M 2025 EBITDA
- EV/EBITDA (forward, assuming ~$700M and post-MBI EV): ~6.5-7.0x
- Forward P/E: Negative (trailing EPS -$63.21); on $24.94 FY2026E, approximately 2.2x

The low EV/EBITDA appears superficially cheap, but the business is declining at 7-10% annually with massive leverage. At current decline rates, EBITDA reaches ~$600M by 2028 while debt remains ~$3.5-4.0B (net leverage >6x). The equity value is mathematically zero in a scenario where refinancing fails.

The peer comparison is of limited utility given Cable One's unique position (deep distress, rural cable overbuild concentration). Peers like ANGI and GOGO have similarly depressed valuations for structural reasons.

What would the stock be worth if the business stabilizes? If EBITDA stabilizes at $700M and the company can refinance at 7-8% weighted average cost of debt on $3.5B total debt, interest expense would be $245-280M, leaving ~$420-455M for maintenance capex, taxes, and equity. At 5x stable EBITDA with $3.3B net debt, equity value would be $200M or ~$35/share. At 6x, equity would be ~$900M or $160/share. The current price ($55) implies the market assigns roughly 20-30% probability to meaningful stabilization, a reasonable implied probability given the evidence.

## Competitive Landscape

Cable One's competitive position is deteriorating rapidly along multiple vectors:

**Fiber overbuild (primary threat):** ~60% of passings already face wired competition at 100+ Mbps. Government programs (BEAD, RDOF) are actively subsidizing additional fiber deployment in Cable One's core rural markets. These programs were specifically designed to eliminate the kind of broadband monopolies Cable One historically enjoyed.

**Fixed Wireless Access (secondary threat):** T-Mobile and Verizon offer home internet at $25-50/month in many of Cable One's markets, undercutting Cable One's ~$80 ARPU by 40-60%. FWA quality continues improving with each network upgrade.

**Scale disadvantage:** Comcast (30M broadband subs), Charter (30M), and Cox (7M) all face similar competitive pressures but possess dramatically greater financial resources, negotiating leverage (for mobile MVNO terms), and R&D capacity. Cable One's ~1M subs provide minimal scale advantage.

Cable One's historical competitive advantage was being the only high-speed provider in small markets. That structural advantage has been permanently eliminated by policy (government subsidizes competitors) and technology (fiber and FWA reach rural markets economically). There is no plausible path to restoring it.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary risks (not priced or underappreciated):**

1. **MBI acquisition creates a liquidity crisis in 2027:** The ~$900M of MBI debt matures November 2027. Cable One must refinance this at a time when its own creditworthiness is declining. If credit markets tighten further or revenue decline accelerates, this refinancing could fail or require highly dilutive terms.

2. **2028 maturity wall at $1.6B+:** Even excluding MBI, Cable One must refinance its revolver, Term Loan B-4, and convertible notes. Distressed debt pricing (69-77.5 cents) suggests the market does not view this as routine.

3. **Acceleration of subscriber loss:** Each quarter of additional overbuild completion (as BEAD funds are disbursed 2026-2028) creates new competitive pressure. There is no natural floor to subscriber erosion visible in the data.

4. **Equity dilution risk:** If refinancing requires equity issuance at current prices, even $500M of equity (a fraction of what's needed) would dilute existing shareholders by ~62%.

5. **Credit rating downgrade cascade:** If ratings agencies downgrade to CCC-range, covenant triggers, margin calls, and supplier credit terms could all deteriorate simultaneously.

**Potential (lower probability) upside risks:**

- BEAD disbursement delays extend Cable One's competitive window
- Interest rate declines ease refinancing
- Strategic acquirer values the physical plant above equity market pricing
- New CEO successfully stabilizes subscribers through bundling/pricing strategy

**Options Market Signal:**

Short interest at 17.3% with 4.4 days to cover reflects significant institutional bearish positioning but is not at extreme levels that would suggest a crowded short or imminent squeeze. The elevated short interest is consistent with the fundamental thesis: sophisticated investors are actively betting against the equity's viability.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($100-120):** New CEO stabilizes subscriber losses within 3-4 quarters through aggressive bundling (mobile MVNO + multi-gig), DOCSIS 4.0 restores temporary speed advantage, BEAD disbursement delays extend the competitive window, and interest rates decline enabling manageable refinancing. EBITDA stabilizes at $700-750M, leverage gradually declines below 5x, and the market re-rates equity to reflect going-concern value. Probability: 15-20%.

**Bear Case ($0-15):** Revenue decline accelerates to -10%+ as BEAD-funded competitors complete builds in 2027-2028. EBITDA drops below $600M. MBI refinancing in late 2027 requires highly dilutive equity or fails entirely. The 2028 maturity wall proves insurmountable at acceptable terms. The company enters restructuring, equity is wiped out or massively diluted. Probability: 40-50%.

**Base Case ($30-50):** Revenue decline continues at -7-8% but stabilizes modestly in 2028. Refinancing is achieved but at punitive rates (8-10%), creating an interest expense burden that eliminates FCF available to equity. The stock trades as a perpetual distressed situation with no dividend, no buyback, and no path to deleveraging. Probability: 30-40%.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** MBI debt refinancing (November 2027 maturity) and Cable One's own 2028 maturity wall. Q2 and Q3 2026 earnings will reveal whether revenue decline is accelerating further. MBI closing (~October 2026) will trigger the debt consolidation that makes the leverage math visibly unsustainable.

**Timing window:** 6-18 months. The immediate catalyst is MBI closing (Q4 2026), which crystallizes the debt consolidation. The structural catalysts are the November 2027 MBI maturity and 2028 Cable One maturities.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** 17.3% SI with 4.4 days to cover. Institutional ownership likely declining. Modest squeeze risk given the fundamental backdrop, though any takeover rumor or short-term rally could create 20-30% upside risk.

**Borrow and liquidity:** At $315M market cap, liquidity is declining (avg volume ~163K shares). The stock is sufficiently liquid for institutional shorts but carry costs may rise as borrow tightens.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case of $0-15 represents 70-100% downside from $55. Upside risk to $80-100 in a squeeze/stabilization scenario represents 45-80% upside. Risk/reward is approximately 2:1 to 2.5:1 in favor of the short over a 12-18 month horizon.

**Verdict: SHORT CANDIDATE** — The specific catalyst is the 2027-2028 debt maturity wall against an accelerating revenue decline. Suggested sizing: 1-2% of portfolio maximum given the illiquidity and squeeze risk. Stop-loss at $80 (46% upside risk). The short is most effective as a longer-duration position (6-12 months) with the trigger being post-MBI leverage consolidation.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria (to reconsider from AVOID):**
- Three consecutive quarters of residential data subscriber stabilization (net loss <5K/quarter)
- Successful refinancing of MBI debt at <8% weighted average cost
- Net leverage declining below 4.5x on trailing EBITDA
- Stock price below $25 with clear path to servicing debt (FCF/debt > 8%)

**Timeline:** The headwinds are structural and multi-year. The competitive overbuild pressure intensifies through 2028 as BEAD funds are deployed. The refinancing window opens in mid-2027 for MBI debt and throughout 2028 for Cable One's own maturities. Resolution (positive or negative) is likely within 24-36 months.

## Conclusion

Cable One represents a structurally impaired business facing accelerating competitive displacement, a forced acquisition that worsens its balance sheet, and a concentrated debt maturity wall within 2 years. The company generates real cash flow today (~$280M FCF), but the trajectory of decline (-7-10% annually) means this cash flow is shrinking while the debt burden is growing. At $55 per share, the equity is a highly leveraged option on stabilization, essentially betting that a declining rural broadband operator can refinance $2.5B+ of debt within 24 months while losing 5-7% of subscribers per year. The probability-weighted expected value is negative. Zero analyst Buy ratings, consensus at Hold/Sell, and 17.3% short interest confirm the professional investor community shares this assessment.
