## Executive Summary

Sun Country Airlines Holdings is a merger-arbitrage situation. On January 11, 2026, Allegiant Travel Company agreed to acquire SNCY for 0.1557 ALGT shares plus $4.10 cash per share, implying approximately $18.89 at announcement. The transaction has cleared all major regulatory hurdles — HSR early termination (March 16), DOT joint interim exemption (April 15) — with shareholder approval scheduled for May 8 and closing expected as early as May 13, 2026.

At the current price of ~$16.86-$17.05, SNCY trades at or slightly below implied deal value depending on Allegiant's stock price (at ALGT ~$83, implied consideration is approximately $17.02). The minimal spread suggests high market confidence in deal completion. The stock has appreciated ~50% over the past year, driven almost entirely by the acquisition premium. Standalone fundamentals are adequate but secondary to deal mechanics at this stage.

The appropriate analytical framework is spread analysis. The recommendation is HOLD for existing holders who are effectively running an arb position; the risk/reward is skewed toward small incremental upside at close versus moderate downside in the unlikely event the deal breaks.

## Company Overview

Sun Country Airlines operates a differentiated three-segment model: Scheduled Passenger Service (leisure-focused, primarily domestic), Charter (military, sports teams, casinos), and Cargo (CMI service for Amazon using 22 Boeing 737-800 freighters). The fleet totals 72 Boeing 737-NG aircraft based in Minneapolis-St. Paul.

The hybrid model allows dynamic resource sharing across segments, mitigating the severe seasonality that typically afflicts leisure carriers. The Amazon cargo contract provides revenue diversification and utilization optimization that most LCCs lack.

CEO Jude Bricker, a former Allegiant COO, has led Sun Country since 2017. His prior relationship with the acquirer facilitates deal execution but raises governance considerations around negotiation alignment.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** FY2022 $894M → FY2023 $1,050M → FY2024 $1,076M → FY2025 $1,127M. The ~8% CAGR is respectable for a mature domestic carrier, driven primarily by cargo fleet expansion.

**Profitability compression:** Operating margins declined from 12.1% (FY2023) to 8.9% (FY2025). Net income stabilized at ~$53M in both FY2024 and FY2025. The margin compression reflects fuel cost increases (22% YoY in Q1 2026) and rapid cargo expansion costs.

**Q1 2026 specifics:**
- Revenue: $338.4M (+4% YoY)
- Adjusted Operating Income: $48.3M (-19% YoY)
- EPS: $0.43 diluted
- Cargo revenue surged 64% ($46.1M) but generated a segment operating loss of $5.3M due to fleet expansion growing pains
- CASM inflated to 14.27¢ (from 11.41¢), partly from $9.8M merger-related charges

**Balance sheet:** $144.7M cash, $302.8M total liquidity, $323.3M net debt, $251.1M finance lease obligations. Debt-to-capital 0.49x. Current ratio 0.82x (typical airline structure with deferred revenue liabilities). Free cash flow was $84M in FY2025.

**Earnings beat history:** Mixed — 2 beats, 2 in-line over the last 4 quarters. The beats were modest (15-27% surprises on small absolute EPS numbers).

## Growth Analysis

Standalone growth prospects are effectively moot given the pending acquisition. For context:

The cargo segment was the primary growth engine, expanding from 12 to 22 aircraft in ~18 months. This drove 64% cargo revenue growth in Q1 2026. However, the rapid expansion created operational challenges resulting in a temporary segment loss. The Amazon relationship underpins this segment entirely — single-customer concentration that is a risk standalone but becomes a strategic asset in the merged entity.

Passenger growth was flat as capacity shifted toward cargo. Average fares rose 12%, suggesting pricing power in the leisure-focused route network, but total passenger revenue was essentially unchanged.

**Estimate revisions are sharply negative:** Current quarter consensus has swung from +$0.20 to -$0.12 (a -160% revision over 90 days). Current year estimates fell from $1.60 to $0.92 (-42%). This reflects merger-related charges, operational disruption from deal uncertainty, executive departures, and fuel cost increases rather than structural business deterioration.

## Valuation Assessment

This is a merger-arb valuation, not a fundamental one.

**Deal mechanics:** 0.1557 ALGT shares + $4.10 cash per SNCY share. At ALGT ~$83, implied value is approximately $17.02. SNCY trades at $16.86-17.05.

**Spread analysis:** The spread is effectively zero to slightly positive, depending on the precise ALGT price used. This implies:
- High deal completion probability (market consensus)
- Minimal remaining upside from the arb itself
- If ALGT appreciates toward its $22 deal-implied target scenario, SNCY would mechanically follow (0.1557 × ALGT move)

**Standalone valuation (if deal breaks):** At $17, SNCY trades at ~23x trailing EPS ($0.96) and approximately 18-19x the revised FY2026E of $0.92. Peers trade at median 8.4x P/E (though peer group is distorted — Allegiant at -42.5x, JetBlue at -2.6x, SkyWest at 8.4x). On a standalone basis without the deal premium, SNCY likely retraces to $12-14, representing 15-30% downside.

**Analyst consensus targets:** Low $19, high $22, median $20. These reflect deal-completion scenarios with varying assumptions about ALGT's stock price at close.

## Competitive Landscape

Sun Country's hybrid model (passenger + charter + cargo) provides genuine differentiation versus pure LCCs. The Amazon cargo contract supplies revenue visibility and fleet utilization that competitors lack. The leisure-focused route network from Minneapolis complements rather than overlaps Allegiant's Sunbelt-focused operations — the strategic logic of the combination is sound.

The merged entity will become the largest ultra-low-cost leisure carrier in the U.S. with $140M in targeted annual synergies, shared 737 fleet type enabling maintenance and crew pooling, and Amazon cargo diversification.

## Risk Assessment

**Deal break risk (primary):** All regulatory approvals have been secured. Shareholder vote is May 8 (days away). Merger litigation (two lawsuits alleging proxy deficiencies) was addressed with supplemental disclosures on April 28 and characterized as "wholly without merit." Deal break probability is estimated at 5-10%.

**ALGT stock price risk:** Because consideration is partially stock-based, SNCY's value at close depends on where Allegiant trades. A material decline in ALGT before close would compress the consideration. Allegiant itself trades at distressed multiples (-42.5x P/E) reflecting its own operational challenges.

**Fuel price environment:** Fuel cost per gallon rose 22% YoY in Q1 2026, compressing passenger margins. This is primarily relevant in a deal-break scenario where SNCY would face continued margin pressure as a standalone entity.

**Executive turnover:** Four senior executives departed in 2025 (President/CFO, COO, Interim CFO, CRO). This level of churn is partially explained by pre-merger uncertainty and likely post-close workforce changes (HQ relocation to Nevada), but signals organizational instability.

**Amazon concentration:** The entire cargo segment (22 aircraft, ~$180M+ annualized revenue) depends on a single customer. Amazon's warrant vesting at close and likely post-close shareholding in Allegiant aligns incentives, but concentration risk is structural.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case:** Deal closes on schedule (May 13), SNCY shareholders receive $4.10 cash + 0.1557 ALGT shares. If ALGT trades at $90+ by close, implied value exceeds $18. Amazon cargo synergies in the merged entity drive ALGT appreciation, benefiting former SNCY holders through stock consideration. Upside from current: 6-15% depending on ALGT price at close.

**Bear Case:** Deal breaks due to shareholder rejection or litigation injunction (low probability but nonzero). SNCY reverts toward standalone valuation of $12-14 given margin compression, executive turnover, and negative estimate revisions. Downside from current: 18-30%. Alternatively, ALGT stock declines materially before close, compressing the stock component of consideration below current SNCY price.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Timeframe:** Days to weeks. The shareholder vote is May 8, 2026. Closing expected as early as May 13, 2026.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):** If SNCY traded below $15.50 with deal mechanics intact (all approvals secured, vote scheduled), the widened spread would create an attractive arb entry with 10-15% upside to close. This would require ALGT weakness or temporary SNCY dislocation.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):** Evidence of shareholder opposition sufficient to block the vote, or a court injunction on the merger. Either would eliminate the deal premium and expose holders to standalone downside. Also: material ALGT stock decline reducing implied consideration below SNCY's current price.

**Reassessment timeline:** May 8 shareholder vote. If approved, hold through close. If rejected (highly unlikely given institutional alignment and proxy advisory support), exit immediately.

## Conclusion

SNCY is a merger-arb situation trading at approximately deal value with days until resolution. The risk/reward profile is asymmetric in a way that does not favor new positions: upside is limited to modest spread capture plus potential ALGT appreciation, while downside in a deal-break scenario is 18-30%. For existing holders, the correct action is to hold through the vote and close. For new capital, the spread is too narrow to justify entry given the tail risk.

Short interest at 8.2% likely represents merger-arb hedging (short SNCY as part of a long ALGT ratio trade) rather than fundamental bearishness.

HOLD. This resolves within 1-2 weeks.
