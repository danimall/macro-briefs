## Executive Summary

EQT Corporation is the largest natural gas producer in the United States, operating approximately 6.5 Bcfe/d from the Appalachian Basin's Marcellus and Utica shales. The company completed its vertical integration through the July 2024 Equitrans Midstream merger and a subsequent $3.5B midstream JV transaction, reducing gathering costs from $0.60/Mcfe to $0.08/Mcfe and generating $5.1B in operating cash flow on $23.8B of revenue in 2025. Trailing FCF reached $2.84B, a 36.7% margin, with analysts projecting ~$3.5B in 2026.

At $57.55, EQT trades at approximately 12.1x FY2026E EPS ($4.74 consensus) and 17.6x trailing EPS. The current-quarter estimate of $2.19 has been revised upward 64.3% over the past 90 days, driven by a winter nat gas price spike where first-of-month settlements reached $7.22-$7.46/MMBtu. EQT has beaten consensus EPS in each of the last four quarters by an average of 16.1%. The stock sits 16% below its 52-week high of $68.24, having pulled back from late-March highs alongside broader energy weakness. Lowest-cost producer status, a secular demand thesis (data centers, LNG, power generation), and accelerating earnings momentum support a BUY recommendation, tempered by $7.8B in total debt and near-total dependence on natural gas pricing.

## Company Overview

EQT operates three segments following the Equitrans merger: (1) **Upstream** — exploration and production in the Appalachian Basin, generating sales volumes of ~2,382 Bcfe in 2025; (2) **Gathering** — gas gathering infrastructure producing $1.3B in 2025 revenue split between firm reservation fees (~49%) and volumetric fees (~51%); and (3) **Transmission** — interstate natural gas transmission and storage via the Mountain Valley Pipeline and related infrastructure, generating $572M in 2025 revenue with ~95% under long-term negotiated rate contracts.

The company holds equity interests in the Mountain Valley Pipeline (MVP Mainline, in service June 2024) and is acquiring additional ownership from Con Edison for ~$213M. EQT has secured 4.5 mtpa of LNG offtake agreements with Sempra, NextDecade, and Commonwealth LNG beginning 2030-2031, and is pursuing data center development opportunities near its Appalachian operations.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and earnings recovery:** Revenue expanded from $5.0B in 2023 to $23.8B in 2025, driven by the Equitrans merger adding midstream revenue, higher natural gas prices (NYMEX averaged $3.42 in 2025 vs. $2.30 in 2024), and 154 Bcfe of production growth. Net income recovered from a trough of $231M in 2024 to $2.04B in 2025 ($3.31 diluted EPS). Average realized price improved 16.4% to $3.19/Mcfe.

The 2025 results were achieved without hedging tailwinds. EQT paid net cash settlements of $83M on derivatives, compared to receiving $1.22B in 2024. The underlying business produced stronger economics than the headline numbers suggest relative to 2024.

**Cash flow generation:** Operating cash flow of $5.1B against capex of $2.32B produced $2.84B in FCF. The FCF-to-net-income conversion of ~139% signals high-quality earnings. Capex guidance for 2026 is $2.65-$2.85B with production roughly flat at 2,275-2,375 Bcfe, implying maintenance-level spending with FCF remaining robust.

**Balance sheet:** Total debt is $7.8B per the 10-K, with investment-grade ratings (BBB-/Baa3, stable outlook). The debt retirement plan targets ~$4.7B in net debt, funded through asset monetizations and FCF. The midstream JV obligates EQT to distribute available midstream cash flows to Blackstone's Class B unitholders (~$3.41B remaining), a meaningful encumbrance. Stockholders' equity of $23.8B produces a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.61x and ROE of 9.8%. The Altman Z-Score of 1.93 places EQT in the grey zone, typical for capital-intensive E&P companies but requiring monitoring. Current ratio of 0.76 is below 1.0, though this is partially offset by the revolving credit facility.

**Dilution:** Shares outstanding expanded 65% from 371M (2022) to 612M (2025), primarily through Equitrans merger and Olympus acquisition equity issuance. Per-share value creation must be evaluated against this dilution; per-share FCF is approximately $4.55, supporting the current price.

## Growth Analysis

Current-quarter EPS estimates rose 64.3% over 90 days to $2.19, driven by January-February realized prices well above the strip. Full-year 2026 estimates increased 13.2% over 90 days to $4.74. EQT's four-quarter track record of beating consensus by an average of 16.1% suggests the $4.74 estimate has upside risk.

**Secular demand drivers provide multi-year volume visibility:**
- **Data centers:** ~45 GW under construction nationally, 12 GW in EQT's Appalachian footprint. Natural gas is the primary baseload/backup fuel for these facilities.
- **LNG exports:** 4.5 mtpa of contracted offtake beginning 2030-2031 provides locked-in demand growth.
- **In-basin demand:** Management estimates 6-7 Bcf/d of new in-basin demand this decade, absorbing Appalachian supply growth.
- **Power generation:** Natural gas is increasingly the fuel of choice for AI-driven electricity demand expansion.

**Production volume growth is modest near-term.** 2026 guidance of 2,275-2,375 Bcfe is roughly flat to slightly below 2025's 2,382 Bcfe. Growth will be driven primarily by price realization rather than volume expansion. Holding production flat while demand grows organically tightens the supply-demand balance over time.

**Negative signal in next-quarter estimates:** FY2027 EPS estimate of $4.69 is 2.6% below the 90-day-ago level, and Q2 2026 estimates declined 16.2% over 90 days. This likely reflects the nat gas forward curve pricing mean reversion after winter peaks. The current-year/next-year estimate crossover ($4.74 vs. $4.69) suggests the market views 2026 as a peak year, which may limit multiple expansion.

## Valuation Assessment

At $57.55, EQT trades at:
- **17.6x trailing EPS** ($3.31) — a peak-year base that understates forward earnings
- **12.1x FY2026E EPS** ($4.74) — roughly in line with peer median P/E of 14.3x
- **~10x FCF** on trailing $2.84B FCF ($4.55/share)
- **1.1% dividend yield** ($0.66/year), well below the 2.3% peer median

Against direct peers: Devon (11.0x), Expand Energy (12.9x), Coterra (14.3x), Diamondback (32.6x). EQT's 12.1x forward multiple sits at the lower end of this range, despite its position as the largest and lowest-cost natural gas producer. The discount to Diamondback reflects pure-play natural gas exposure vs. FANG's oil-weighted production, but the discount to Coterra and Expand Energy appears insufficient given EQT's scale advantages and vertical integration.

Analyst consensus target is $68.81 (median $70), implying ~20% upside from current levels. The target range spans $55-$76, with recent upward revisions from Morgan Stanley ($74), BMO ($76), and Jefferies ($76).

A simple framework: $4.74 FY2026E EPS × 14x P/E (peer median) = $66.36. At 15x (premium for scale, vertical integration, and demand optionality) = $71.10. FCF-based: $3.5B estimated 2026 FCF / 625M shares = $5.60/share × 12x = $67.20. These approaches converge on a $66-71 fair value range.

## Competitive Landscape

EQT's competitive position is among the strongest in U.S. natural gas:

1. **Cost leadership:** Gathering costs collapsed from $0.60/Mcfe to $0.08/Mcfe post-Equitrans merger. Well costs declined 13% YoY per lateral foot through combo-development drilling. This cost structure is difficult for peers to replicate without similar vertical integration.

2. **Scale:** At ~6.5 Bcfe/d, EQT produces roughly double the output of its next-largest Appalachian peer. Scale provides operating leverage, pipeline negotiating power, and the ability to curtail production during weak pricing without operational disruption (14 Bcfe curtailed in 2025 vs. 107 Bcfe in 2024).

3. **Basin dominance:** The Appalachian Basin is the lowest-cost gas basin in the U.S. EQT's multi-decade drilling inventory ensures long reserve life without requiring premium acreage acquisitions.

4. **Midstream integration:** Owning gathering and transmission infrastructure eliminates margin leakage to third-party midstream operators and provides fee-based revenue diversification.

5. **Vulnerability:** Single-basin, single-commodity concentration means EQT has no diversification against Appalachian-specific regulatory changes or prolonged nat gas price weakness. Every competitor with oil exposure (Devon, Diamondback, Coterra) has a natural hedge that EQT lacks.

## Risk Assessment

**Natural gas price dependence:** This is the dominant risk. With ~93% of reserves being natural gas and only ~25% hedge coverage, EQT is essentially a leveraged bet on Henry Hub pricing. The NYMEX spot range of $2.65-$9.86/MMBtu in 2025 (and a spike to $30.72 in January 2026) illustrates the magnitude of potential outcomes. Each $0.50/MMBtu change in realized price moves annual revenue by roughly $1.2B and FCF by a comparable amount. A sustained return to $2.50 gas would compress FCF to $1.5-2.0B and stress the debt retirement timeline.

**Debt burden:** The $7.8B in total debt combined with the $3.41B Blackstone midstream JV obligation creates combined fixed charges exceeding $11B. While manageable at current commodity prices, this structure amplifies downside risk in a weak pricing environment. The debt retirement plan depends on asset monetizations and sustained FCF generation, both of which are commodity-price-dependent.

**Securities litigation:** The $134M loss contingency recognized in 2025 (up from $18M in 2024) indicates the class action is developing unfavorably. Further reserve increases are possible.

**Tariff and geopolitical risk:** Retaliatory tariffs on U.S. LNG exports could undermine the long-term demand thesis. The current Middle East conflict creates nat gas price volatility in both directions: supply disruptions support prices, but a durable peace settlement could deflate the energy complex.

**Share dilution:** 65% dilution over three years means per-share metrics have not kept pace with aggregate cash flow growth. Further equity issuance for acquisitions would compound this.

## Conclusion

EQT represents the premier U.S. natural gas investment at a reasonable valuation. The vertical integration achieved through the Equitrans merger creates a structural cost advantage visible in the $0.08/Mcfe gathering cost, and trailing FCF of $2.84B demonstrates the model is producing real cash returns. Estimate revisions are strongly positive, the earnings beat track record is consistent, and multiple secular demand catalysts (data centers, LNG, power generation) provide medium-term support.

The risks are concentrated: $7.8B in debt, single-commodity exposure, and the Blackstone midstream obligation constrain financial flexibility. These risks are manageable at current commodity prices but would amplify downside in a sustained low-price environment. The 12.1x forward earnings multiple provides modest valuation protection given the risk profile.

At $57.55, with analyst consensus at $69 and a convergence of valuation methods pointing to $66-71 fair value, EQT offers approximately 15-20% upside with identifiable catalysts. BUY with a 9-12 month horizon, with position sizing reflecting the commodity price sensitivity inherent in a pure-play natural gas producer.
