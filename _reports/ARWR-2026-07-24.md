**Research Date: July 24, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Arrowhead completed its transition from a clinical-stage platform to a commercial-stage RNAi therapeutics company with the November 18, 2025 FDA approval of REDEMPLO (plozasiran) for Familial Chylomicronemia Syndrome (FCS). The investment case rests on three legs: a proprietary TRiM platform whose tissue breadth (liver, lung, muscle, adipose, CNS) differentiates it from more liver-centric RNAi peers; a wholly-owned cardiometabolic franchise now pushing into the two largest cardiometabolic markets, severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG) and obesity; and a prolific out-licensing engine (Sarepta, Novartis, Sanofi, Amgen, GSK, Takeda, Madrigal) that Arrowhead states carries up to ~$15.2 billion of potential future milestones. The July 22, 2026 positive Phase 3 SHASTA-3/SHASTA-4 topline in sHTG (both trials hitting all primary and secondary endpoints, with an ~18% single-day stock move to a 52-week high) is the pivotal near-term value catalyst, because sHTG is an order of magnitude larger than ultra-rare FCS.

The financial picture requires careful reading. FY2025 revenue of $829.4M (versus $3.5M in FY2024) is roughly 85% Sarepta upfront/milestone recognition, not product sales, and it produced the company's first operating income (+$98.3M) and near-breakeven net loss. Q2 FY2026 revenue fell back to $73.7M with a net loss of -$0.93 per share as R&D (+30% YoY to $173.3M) and launch spend scaled. Screening on trailing revenue growth or trailing P/E misreads this company; the real signal is the sHTG Phase 3 win plus the REDEMPLO launch ramp, neither of which is yet in trailing financials. The balance sheet is now a genuine strength: ~$1.78B total cash resources at 3/31/26 following $930M of January 2026 financing, including a $700M 0% convertible.

We rate ARWR a HOLD with a growth-hold rationale. The platform is validated, the pipeline is broad, and the cash runway de-risks the story for years. The stock has rallied ~445% over twelve months to $86.82, sitting essentially on top of its ~$87.08 convertible conversion strike, and the live Ionis patent litigation over plozasiran is a binary that could directly threaten the flagship's commercialization economics. Estimates are also drifting lower in the near term even as the 90-day trend improved. The risk/reward is not asymmetric enough to chase after the pop; a pullback toward the low-$70s or resolution of the Ionis overhang would upgrade the setup.

## Company Overview

Arrowhead is a commercial-stage RNAi (RNA interference) company built around its TRiM (Targeted RNAi Molecule) platform, which silences disease-causing genes across a broad set of tissue types. The company runs a hybrid model. It retains full economics on a wholly-owned cardiometabolic franchise: plozasiran/REDEMPLO (FCS approved; sHTG pending), zodasiran (HoFH), the dual-gene ARO-DIMER-PA targeting PCSK9 and APOC3, and an emerging RNAi obesity portfolio (ARO-INHBE, ARO-ALK7). In parallel, it monetizes the platform through out-licensing deals that generate upfronts, equity, milestones, and royalties.

REDEMPLO, approved November 18, 2025, is the first marketed drug and the evidence that the platform can traverse the full development-to-commercial cycle. The company priced it at a unified $45,000/year WAC ("One-REDEMPLO"), deliberately set to carry across FCS and, if approved, sHTG to simplify payer contracting. Ex-US approvals/positive actions have followed in China (NMPA, marketed by Sanofi), Canada, Australia, and the EU (European Commission marketing authorization June 22, 2026). Fiscal year ends September 30.

## Financial Analysis

The dominant feature of Arrowhead's financials is that revenue is driven by lumpy collaboration accounting rather than product sales. FY2025 revenue compares to $240.7M in FY2023, and was driven by ~$696.8M of Sarepta deal recognition plus $130M from the Sanofi/Visirna China agreement. FY2025 delivered a near-breakeven net loss of -$1.6M (against -$599M in FY2024), operating cash flow of +$179.6M, and FCF of +$156.9M.

Q2 FY2026 (ended 3/31/26) reverts to the underlying investment-phase reality: revenue down from $542.7M in the year-ago quarter that held the Sarepta upfront, a net loss of -$132.7M, and G&A up ~47% to $41.7M on launch scaling. The GAAP swings between these two periods are a function of deal-timing and revenue-recognition mechanics, not commercial deterioration.

Total cash resources rose from ~$919M (9/30/25) to ~$1.78B at 3/31/26 ($188.5M cash + $1,595.6M securities), funded by two January 2026 offerings totaling $930M gross: a $700M 0.00% convertible senior notes due 2032 (conversion price ~$87.08, capped call cap at $119.33) and a $230M equity raise at $64.50/share. The 0% convert funds the wholly-owned pipeline for years at low cost.

Leverage is nuanced. The company carries a Sixth Street credit facility (~$200M net after paydown, originally up to $500M with a punitive 15% PIK rate and a 2x-MOIC prepayment penalty) and a ~$384M liability tied to the sale of future olpasiran royalties to Royalty Pharma. Reported D/E (~2.97) and a grey-zone Altman Z of ~2.16 overstate distress risk for a company sitting on ~$1.8B of liquidity with a current ratio near 4.9. The covenant to maintain ≥$250M liquidity if market cap exceeds $2B and the high cost of the Sixth Street debt are worth watching but are not near-term threats given the cash position. Financial health scores a 6.5: the balance sheet is a strength on liquidity terms, tempered by structural leverage complexity and the punitive Sixth Street facility.

## Growth Analysis

The growth engine has three components, each independent of trailing revenue.

First, the REDEMPLO launch is tracking well. The Q2 FY2026 update reported >400 prescriptions received/in-process (>40% growth in the prior four weeks), ~180 patients with at least one shipment, ~30 new scripts/week, and ~85% APOC3-class-naïve patients, meaning the launch is expanding the treated population rather than switching existing patients, a healthy early-adoption signal.

Second, the SHASTA-3/SHASTA-4 Phase 3 topline supports a supplemental NDA in the vastly larger sHTG population. The dataset (83% median TG reduction, 96% of patients below the 500 mg/dL pancreatitis-risk threshold, and no adjudicated pancreatitis events in the 2-year Phase 2b OLE) is viewed by analysts as competitive-to-best-in-class against Ionis's olezarsen (Tryngolza), which won an sHTG label expansion about a month earlier. Plozasiran's edge is dosing convenience; Ionis's edge is first-mover regulatory timing.

Third, obesity optionality. ARO-INHBE combined with tirzepatide delivered -9.4% weight loss at week 16 (roughly 2x tirzepatide alone) with strong visceral/liver fat reduction, and ARO-ALK7 achieved -88% mean target knockdown, the first RNAi silencing of an adipocyte-expressed gene in humans. This is a wholly-owned call option on the GLP-1/obesity megamarket that could become the largest value driver if it advances.

The milestone engine adds contingent growth: the May 2026 Madrigal deal for ARO-PNPLA3 (MASH) added $25M upfront plus up to $975M milestones and high-single-digit to mid-teens royalties. Estimate revisions are mixed: the 90-day trend improved (current year +10.0%, current quarter +9.9%), but the last 30 days softened across every window (current year -2.7%, next year -5.2%). For a pre-profit biotech, these EPS revisions reflect spending/launch-cost timing more than commercial signal, but the near-term deceleration keeps this from being a clean, strong revision-momentum BUY. Growth prospects score 7.5: the sHTG readout and obesity optionality are genuine, durable catalysts, offset modestly by near-term estimate softness.

## Valuation Assessment

Standard multiples are meaningless here. Trailing/forward P/E of -40.1x reflects an investment-phase company whose GAAP earnings swing with deal timing. Peer P/E medians (39.8x on the profitable BMRN; negative on IONS, AXSM, MDGL, SMMT) confirm that the biotech comp set is largely valued on pipeline and platform, not earnings.

Arrowhead is best valued as a sum-of-parts: (a) the wholly-owned cardiometabolic pipeline (REDEMPLO in FCS plus a large sHTG opportunity, zodasiran, ARO-DIMER-PA, and the obesity program); (b) a large, contingent milestone/royalty stream (nominal but heavily contingent and partner-controlled); and (c) ~$1.8B of cash and liquidity. At a ~$12.2B market cap, the equity already prices meaningful sHTG success and platform optionality. Analyst price targets (mean $102.9, median $100, high $126, low $87) sit above the current price, implying ~19% upside to consensus mean, with the low target essentially at the current price. Sentiment is improving (hold ratings dropped to zero; 12 buy/strong-buy).

The key valuation tension: the current price is nearly identical to the ~$87.08 convertible conversion strike. Prior appreciation is not by itself a reason to mark down valuation; the move is grounded in real fundamental improvement (approval, Phase 3 win, balance-sheet transformation). The July catalyst has pushed the price to consensus/conversion levels, and a portion of the sHTG value is now embedded. With a live patent overhang capable of directly impairing the flagship's economics and near-term estimates drifting lower, the setup is not the asymmetric, mispriced-cheapness case that would warrant a high valuation score. We assign 5.5: fair value with modest upside to consensus, better entry available on a pullback.

## Competitive Landscape

Arrowhead sits in the RNAi/oligonucleotide set alongside Alnylam (RNAi leader) and Ionis (antisense). The most direct active rivalry is with Ionis in the APOC3/triglyceride space: plozasiran (siRNA) versus olezarsen (antisense). Arrowhead's competitive position is improving on trajectory: the strength of its SHASTA-3/-4 dataset and quarterly-ish dosing convenience position plozasiran as competitive-to-best-in-class, and Ionis shares fell on the Arrowhead readout even though Ionis holds first-mover sHTG timing.

The deeper moat is platform validation. The breadth of blue-chip partners willing to pay for the TRiM platform is a depth of external validation few clinical/commercial-stage biotechs possess. TRiM's tissue reach beyond the liver (adipose silencing demonstrated in humans via ARO-ALK7) differentiates it structurally in the cardiometabolic and obesity markets. The competitive risk that tempers the score to 7.0 rather than higher is the Ionis patent litigation, a legal rather than scientific challenge to the franchise, and the reality that obesity and ASCVD are the most crowded, capital-intensive markets in pharma.

## Risk Assessment

**Ionis patent litigation over plozasiran** is the single most acute, company-specific risk. Ionis sued Arrowhead (filed 9/11/2025, C.D. Cal.) alleging REDEMPLO/plozasiran infringes U.S. Patent 9,593,333, and Arrowhead's own Delaware declaratory-judgment action was dismissed (12/23/2025), leaving it on the defensive. An adverse outcome would directly threaten the commercialization economics of the flagship just as the sHTG expansion amplifies that value. This is a live, binary overhang with no confirmed trial/resolution date.

**Revenue lumpiness and partner control** create GAAP fragility. Most revenue is milestone/royalty-based and controlled by partners' decisions. In Q2 FY2026, Sarepta exercised a "step-in right" on certain Category-1 programs, a modification that reduced Arrowhead's remaining variable consideration, a concrete example of partner behavior swinging reported results.

**Counterparty (Sarepta) risk** is under-appreciated. Sarepta, the largest partner (deal worth up to ~$11B all-in and the single biggest milestone source), has faced well-publicized gene-therapy safety issues (Elevidys). Deterioration at Sarepta could impair the largest slice of the milestone book; the diversified-looking milestone stream is more concentrated than it appears.

**Commercial supply concentration**: REDEMPLO relies on single-source drug substance/product manufacturing and a single specialty pharmacy channel; disruption would hit the launch trajectory directly.

**Pricing/policy pressure**: IRA negotiation, most-favored-nation, and OBBBA are recurring risk-factor themes, and the premium WAC is exposed to payer/formulary pushback.

**Dilution**: between the converts (~8M+ potential shares near $87), the equity raise, pre-funded warrants, and an upsized ~$500M ATM, share count rose from 135.7M to 140.6M in two quarters.

Given the binary Ionis litigation, partner-concentration fragility, and supply single-sourcing, none of which is fully de-risked at a 52-week high, risk factors score 4.5.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is not pricing acute directional stress despite the run-up. Near-term ATM IV of 58.8% is slightly below 1-year historical volatility of 63.7% (spread -4.9%, "cheap"), and IV has collapsed 23.2pp (from 82.0% on 7/19 to 58.8% on 7/24) as the sHTG binary resolved and the stock rose 16.9%. Put/call skew is balanced (0.5%) at the near tenor, and the put/call volume ratio (0.37) leans bullish. There is mild backwardation (near-term 58.8% > 6-month 56.0%) and a modest negative skew at the 3-month tenor (-5.2%), consistent with hedging around subsequent catalysts (sNDA timing, litigation news). The implied ±12.8% one-month move reflects a still-volatile biotech, but the overall read is that post-catalyst uncertainty has deflated rather than a bearish setup building. This confirms the fundamental view: the near-term binary is behind, and the next legs (Ionis ruling, sNDA) are the sources of forward risk.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$126, matching the high analyst target and above):** The sHTG sNDA is filed and approved, unlocking a market an order of magnitude larger than FCS; REDEMPLO's script ramp compounds off a class-naïve base; the Ionis litigation resolves favorably or settles; and the wholly-owned obesity program (ARO-INHBE, ARO-ALK7) advances into a value-driving asset in the GLP-1 megamarket. The milestone book converts at a healthy rate, and the platform continues to attract blue-chip partners. In this scenario the sum-of-parts supports a materially higher equity value and the stock re-rates toward and beyond $126.

**Bear Case (~$60, ~-31% from current):** The Ionis patent ruling goes against Arrowhead, impairing plozasiran's economics just as the sHTG expansion should be scaling; Sarepta's troubles impair the largest milestone source; launch metrics plateau under payer pushback on the WAC; and continued dilution (converts, ATM, warrants) reduces per-share value. GAAP losses persist and the stock retraces toward its pre-catalyst trading range.

**Base Case (~$100, roughly the analyst median, ~+15% from current):** REDEMPLO launch continues its healthy ramp, the sHTG sNDA proceeds on track, the Ionis litigation remains an unresolved overhang that caps re-rating but does not blow up the thesis, and the obesity program stays a promising early-stage option. The equity holds its platform premium with modest appreciation to consensus over 12 months.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth-hold).** This is the fifth consecutive HOLD, and the bar for a continued unchanged call is higher, but the current data supports it. The fundamentals have improved (approval achieved, Phase 3 win, ~$1.8B liquidity), yet the stock has run to a 52-week high sitting on its conversion strike, with a live binary litigation risk that argues against chasing here.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Favorable resolution or settlement of the Ionis '333 patent litigation, removing the flagship's binary overhang.
- A pullback toward the low-$70s (below the ~$87 conversion strike), restoring asymmetric upside to the $100-126 target range on unchanged fundamentals.
- Formal sNDA acceptance for plozasiran in sHTG with a clear regulatory timeline, plus continued script acceleration above ~30/week.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Adverse Ionis ruling that materially impairs plozasiran commercialization economics.
- Material deterioration at Sarepta (further safety actions, deal restructuring) that impairs the milestone/royalty engine.
- Launch stall (scripts plateauing or payer coverage restrictions on the WAC) combined with accelerating dilution.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next natural checkpoints are the sNDA filing/acceptance news, any Ionis litigation scheduling or ruling, and the next quarterly print (fiscal Q3 FY2026), which should show a full quarter of launch trajectory and updated obesity data.

## Conclusion

Arrowhead is a validated RNAi platform that has crossed the commercialization threshold, backed by a ~$1.8B cash position and a competitive Phase 3 sHTG dataset that opens a market far larger than the ultra-rare FCS indication its first approval addressed. The breadth of blue-chip partnerships and the wholly-owned obesity optionality are durable sources of upside that the trailing financials obscure.

The reason to hold rather than buy is timing and risk symmetry. The stock sits on top of its convertible conversion strike and now embeds a meaningful portion of the sHTG success that just resolved. The Ionis patent litigation is a live binary that could directly impair the flagship's economics, near-term estimates are drifting lower, and management's insider behavior (broad selling, zero open-market buying, though largely programmatic) offers no confirming conviction signal at these prices. The base case implies roughly 15% upside to consensus, but the asymmetric entry that would justify a BUY requires either a pullback toward the low-$70s or resolution of the Ionis overhang. We rate ARWR a HOLD and would re-engage the buy case on either condition.
