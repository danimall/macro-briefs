## Executive Summary

Worthington Enterprises is a post-separation industrial company organized around consumer products (propane cylinders, tools, balloon kits) and building products (pressure vessels, HVAC components, metal roofing). Its financial architecture is distinctive: $144.8M in annual equity income from JVs (WAVE and ClarkDietrich) flows below the operating line, meaning GAAP operating income systematically understates true economic earnings. On an adjusted basis, TTM EBITDA is $297M and growing 22% YoY with net leverage of just 1.0x.

The company has legitimate secular tailwinds: ASME liquid cooling tanks for data centers are tripling, domestic manufacturing creates tariff-protected moat positions, and the LSI acquisition (Jan 2026) adds a high-switching-cost roofing component business. Q3 FY2026 showed 24% revenue growth (14% organic), representing the sixth consecutive quarter of YoY improvement.

Despite these positives, the stock trades at 24.8x trailing earnings — a 14% premium to peer median (21.8x) — precisely as analyst estimates are declining 5.5-6.5% across the forward quarters. This combination of above-peer valuation with negative estimate momentum is the specific concern preventing a BUY recommendation. The thesis requires confirmation that tariff benefits and data center demand are translating to sustained earnings power before the multiple is justified.

## Company Overview

Worthington Enterprises was created on December 1, 2023, when legacy Worthington Industries spun off its steel processing business into Worthington Steel (WS). What remains is a designer/manufacturer of consumer and building products:

**Consumer Products (~43% of sales):** Bernzomatic torches, Coleman camping cylinders (licensed), Balloon Time helium kits, Level5 drywall tools, Halo grills. Sold primarily through mass retail channels.

**Building Products (~57% of sales):** Amtrol well/expansion tanks, ASME data center cooling tanks, Ragasco composite propane cylinders, Elgen HVAC components, LSI metal roofing clips.

**Equity Method JVs (not in revenue, but massive earnings contribution):**
- WAVE (50% with Armstrong World Industries): ceiling suspension systems — $110M equity income FY2025
- ClarkDietrich (25%): light gauge metal framing — $41M equity income FY2025

The critical structural fact: JV equity income ($144.8M) exceeds the company's reported GAAP net income ($96M). These JVs generate cash with minimal capital requirements but are not consolidated and are outside management's direct operational control.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:** FY2025 consolidated revenue was $1.154B. Q3 FY2026 reached $379M (+24% YoY, +14% organic), with LSI contributing $32M in approximately 6 weeks post-close. Full-year FY2026E revenue is tracking toward approximately $1.4B.

**Profitability:** GAAP operating income is distorted by the $50.8M GTI goodwill impairment in FY2025. Adjusted EBITDA of $297M TTM (22.4% margin) is the appropriate profitability measure. Gross margin improved to 27.6% from 22.9% following the SES deconsolidation and mix improvement.

**Balance Sheet:** Conservative. Cash $250M, long-term debt $303M, net debt/EBITDA ~1.0x. $500M undrawn revolver expiring Sept 2028. Current ratio 3.48x. This is a business that can comfortably fund acquisitions without stretching its capital structure.

**Free Cash Flow:** $164M TTM (95% FCF conversion vs. adjusted net earnings). FCF margin approximately 12-13%. Post-modernization capex completion (mid-FY2027) should improve normalized FCF by ~$25M annually.

**Capital Allocation:** Dividend raised 11.8% to $0.76/year (1.4% yield), $30.9M buybacks in FY2025, and three acquisitions (Ragasco, Elgen, LSI) totaling approximately $295M deployed since June 2024. The acquisition strategy targets niche manufacturers with moats — a sensible playbook for a company generating $160M+ in annual FCF.

**EPS:** $1.92 TTM reported; consensus forward year estimate $3.44 (current year) and $3.90 (next year). The trailing P/E of 24.8x reflects the impairment-depressed denominator; forward P/E on $3.44 consensus is 16.2x, which is more relevant.

## Growth Analysis

**Organic Growth Acceleration:** From +3.5% organic in FY2025 to +14% organic in Q3 FY2026 — material acceleration driven by:
1. Data center ASME tanks "tripling" this year with continued growth next year
2. Balloon Time store count up 64% YoY to 55,000 stores
3. LSI accretive contribution ($32M in 6 weeks)
4. Tariff-driven domestic demand shifts

**Data Center Optionality:** Five separate business lines (WAVE, ClarkDietrich, Elgen, LSI, Amtrol) have data center exposure. Currently <10% of individual line revenue but growing fastest. If data center construction capex continues its current trajectory, this becomes a 15-20% revenue contributor within 2-3 years.

**Estimate Revisions — The Headwind:** Despite the strong organic growth, consensus EPS estimates are declining: current quarter -6.0% over 30 days, next quarter -6.5% over 30 days, full year -1.3%. This negative revision momentum contradicts the growth acceleration story and represents the primary analytical tension. Possible explanations: (a) tariff cost headwinds partially offsetting tariff demand benefits, (b) ClarkDietrich equity income pressure continuing, (c) construction market weakness (HMI at 34) constraining Building Products outside data center.

**Forward Growth Consensus:** $3.44 current year → $3.90 next year = 13.4% EPS growth. Reasonable but not exceptional, and the trajectory is getting revised downward.

## Valuation Assessment

At $55.67, WOR trades at:
- Forward P/E: 16.2x on $3.44 FY2026E (reasonable)
- Next-year P/E: 14.3x on $3.90 FY2027E
- EV/EBITDA: ~9.4x on $297M TTM adjusted EBITDA (attractive for 22% EBITDA growth)
- Dividend yield: 1.4%

**Peer Comparison:** Peer median P/E is 21.8x (TREX 21.8x, BCC 22.6x, ROCK 17.7x). WOR's trailing P/E of 24.8x is above peers, but its 16.2x forward P/E is actually below peers, suggesting the market is skeptical of earnings sustainability. The trailing P/E reflects the impairment-depressed $1.92 TTM earnings and is not the correct valuation anchor.

**The EBITDA Disconnect:** At 9.4x EBITDA with 22% growth and 1.0x leverage, WOR is trading below reasonable fair value for an industrial compounder. The market appears to be applying a conglomerate discount to the JV-heavy earnings stream and/or skepticism about growth sustainability.

**Fair Value Estimate:** 11-12x EBITDA on $310-320M forward EBITDA (mid-FY2027) = $3.1-3.8B EV, or $57-70 per share. Current price at the low end of this range. Analyst consensus mean target of $65.40 (median $69.00) implies 17-24% upside.

**Why not BUY:** The forward P/E on consensus estimates appears optically cheap (16.2x), but those estimates are declining. If the Q4 FY2026 report (due approximately June-July 2026) shows another miss or guidance cut — as Q2 FY2026 did (-8.6% miss) — the stock retraces toward $47-50 where it bottomed in March. The earnings trajectory must stabilize before the valuation can be reliably anchored.

## Competitive Landscape

**Domestic Manufacturing Moat:** WOR is the sole U.S. manufacturer in several product categories (low-pressure LPG cylinders, certain pressure containment products). Section 232 tariffs (aluminum raised to 50%) create structural barriers to foreign competition. CEO noted "a level-playing field is always a good thing for us" — implying WOR gains share as tariffs increase.

**Data Center Positioning:** ASME pressure vessel manufacturing requires specialized certifications and capacity. WOR has available capacity and is scaling. First-mover advantage in liquid cooling tank supply for hyperscaler data centers creates relationship-based competitive advantages.

**JV Quality:** WAVE is the largest of 4 North American ceiling suspension manufacturers. ClarkDietrich is the industry leader in light gauge steel framing. These are duopoly/oligopoly positions that generate outsized returns on capital.

**Competitive Risks:** Consumer Products face retail channel concentration (one customer = 28% of segment, 12% of consolidated). Private-label substitution risk exists in commoditized product categories.

## Risk Assessment

**JV Dependence (HIGH):** $144.8M equity income exceeds consolidated net income. WOR does not control these businesses. ClarkDietrich already showed a 32% income decline in FY2025 from steel price compression. A WAVE deterioration would be existential for the earnings story.

**Customer Concentration (MODERATE):** 12% of revenue from one retail customer. Loss would impair Consumer Products profitability materially.

**Construction Cyclicality (MODERATE):** HMI at 34, ABI below 50 for over a year. While data centers offset, broad residential/commercial construction remains weak under elevated rates.

**GTI Impairment Signal (MODERATE):** The $50.1M write-down indicates at least one acquisition that underperformed — highlighting execution risk in the M&A-heavy strategy.

**McConnell ~35% Ownership (LOW-MODERATE):** Limits takeover premium potential and governance challenge ability. Not a near-term risk but caps long-term optionality.

**Estimate Revision Momentum (MODERATE):** Current quarter estimates down 6.0% in 30 days. This directly opposes the growth narrative and creates concrete downside risk if the next earnings report confirms the market's skepticism.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($68-76):** Data center revenue triples as guided, tariff benefits fully materialize in FY2027 margins, ClarkDietrich recovers to $55-60M annual equity income, LSI integration exceeds plan, and normalized EBITDA reaches $330-350M by mid-2027. At 11-12x EBITDA, this supports $68-76. Catalyst: Q4 FY2026 earnings beat with raised guidance.

**Bear Case ($42-47):** Broad construction downturn overwhelms data center growth, ClarkDietrich equity income continues declining, another earnings miss triggers additional estimate cuts to $3.00-3.20 FY2026E, and the stock re-rates to 14-15x depressed forward EPS.

**Base Case ($60-65):** Estimates stabilize near current levels ($3.40-3.50 FY2026E), data center growth provides offset to construction weakness, and the stock trades at 17-18x forward P/E as the market prices moderate growth. This implies 8-17% upside from current levels.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Analyst estimate revisions turn positive (30-day revision >0% for current and next quarter)
2. Q4 FY2026 earnings beat consensus by >5% with forward guidance above current consensus
3. Data center revenue confirmed at >$100M annual run rate across all business lines

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Next quarter EPS miss >10% combined with guidance cut
2. WAVE equity income declines >15% YoY in any quarter
3. Loss of the concentrated retail customer or >20% volume decline in that channel

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (Q4 FY2026, likely late June 2026). If the company demonstrates estimate stabilization or an upside surprise, the negative revision trend breaks and the valuation support strengthens materially.

## Conclusion

Worthington Enterprises is a fundamentally sound industrial compounder with genuine competitive advantages (domestic manufacturing moat, tariff protection, data center optionality) and an attractive adjusted valuation (9.4x TTM EBITDA on 22% growth). The financial architecture — with JV income flowing below the operating line — creates persistent undervaluation on GAAP metrics.

The specific barrier to a BUY recommendation is the active deterioration in consensus estimates (-5.5% to -6.5% over 90 days). This is measurable, directionally inconsistent with the growth narrative, and creates concrete downside risk if the next earnings report confirms the market's skepticism.

At current prices, the risk/reward skews slightly positive (base case 8-17% upside vs. 16-24% downside to bear case), but insufficient to warrant an active BUY given the negative estimate momentum and the system's documented poor performance in Industrials sector BUY calls. The appropriate stance is HOLD with a growth orientation — own the stock if already positioned, but require estimate stabilization as confirmation before initiating new positions.
