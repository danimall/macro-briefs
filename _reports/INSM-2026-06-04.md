**Research Date: June 4, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Insmed is a commercial-stage respiratory biopharma in the middle of a genuine top-line inflection. Revenue scaled from $363.7M in FY2024 to $606.4M in FY2025, and Q1 2026 revenue of $306.0M was up 230% year-over-year, driven almost entirely by the launch of BRINSUPRI (brensocatib), the first and only approved therapy for non-cystic fibrosis bronchiectasis. BRINSUPRI did $172.7M in roughly four-and-a-half months of FY2025 and then $207.9M in Q1 2026 alone (44% sequential growth), with management reiterating guidance of at least $1B in BRINSUPRI revenue for 2026. The March 2026 ENCORE confirmatory trial was a clean win on its primary endpoint and every multiplicity-controlled secondary, de-risking ARIKAYCE's full approval and setting up a 2H 2026 sNDA to expand the label from refractory-only to all MAC lung disease patients. This is a broad-based commercial story rather than a single-data-point binary.

The reason the stock sits at ~$104 (down roughly 51% from its $212.75 high) is the gap between commercial momentum and financial structure. Insmed lost $1.28B in FY2025 and $163.6M in Q1 2026, burned ~$222.7M of operating cash in a single quarter, and carries ~$5.8B of accumulated deficit. Liquidity of ~$1.22B against that burn, combined with management's "at least 12 months" language, points to another equity raise within roughly 12-18 months. Share count already grew ~19% year-over-year. The May 8 ~23% drop was a sell-the-news reset against lofty buy-side whisper numbers rather than a fundamental break, but it crystallized the actual debate: the slope of the BRINSUPRI launch curve, not whether the drug works.

We rate INSM a HOLD with a growth bias. The franchise is high quality and the addressable markets are large and underserved, but three things keep us from a BUY at this price: near-certain dilution that caps per-share upside, a thesis concentrated in one launch trajectory the market is actively contesting, and a drug-pricing-policy overhang (IRA negotiation plus the MFN/GUARD models) that management itself flags as a direct threat to BRINSUPRI's pricing and to its international expansion strategy.

## Company Overview

Insmed (HQ Bridgewater, NJ; ~1,664 employees) operates as a single reportable segment organized across Respiratory, Immunology & Inflammation, and Neuro & Other Rare. Two commercial products, both in Respiratory, generate all revenue:

- **ARIKAYCE** (amikacin liposome inhalation suspension): the only inhaled therapy specifically indicated for *Mycobacterium avium* complex (MAC) lung disease. Approved US (2018), EU (2020), Japan (2021). Orphan plus QIDP designations provide 12 years of US exclusivity. Delivered via PARI's Lamira nebulizer. FY2025 revenue $433.8M (+19% YoY, international-led).
- **BRINSUPRI** (brensocatib): first-in-class oral, once-daily reversible DPP1 inhibitor for NCFB, a chronic lung disease with no other approved therapy anywhere. Licensed from AstraZeneca (2016). Approved US (Aug 2025), EU (Nov 2025), UK (Feb 2026); Japan submission accepted. The US diagnosed NCFB population is estimated at ~500K, with a ~14-year exclusivity runway. This is the growth engine.

The pipeline is led by **TPIP** (treprostinil palmitil inhalation powder), a once-daily inhaled prostanoid for PAH/PH-ILD with strong Phase 2b PAH data (35% placebo-adjusted PVR reduction, +35.5m 6MWD). Phase 3 PALM-ILD started Q4 2025 and PALM-PAH started April 2026. Earlier-stage assets include INS1148 (anti-SCF248 mAb for IPF/PPF, acquired Dec 2025), INS1201 (DMD gene therapy) and INS1202 (ALS gene therapy).

## Financial Analysis

The profile is a textbook high-conviction, launch-stage biotech: explosive revenue, deepening losses, persistent cash burn.

| Revenue | $305.2M | $363.7M | $606.4M | $306.0M (+230% YoY) |
| — ARIKAYCE | — | $363.7M | $433.8M | $98.1M |
| — BRINSUPRI | — | — | $172.7M | $207.9M |

| Accum. deficit | — | — | $(5.6)B | $(5.8)B |

The commercial mechanics are favorable on three fronts. First, the BRINSUPRI ramp is steep and broadening, with international EAP/named-patient sales only beginning. Second, gross margin is inflecting: cost of product revenue fell to 15.5% of sales in Q1 2026 from 22.9% a year earlier, because BRINSUPRI carries structurally lower COGS than ARIKAYCE and pre-approval BRINSUPRI inventory was already expensed through R&D, a mechanical tailwind to reported 2026-2027 gross margin as that inventory sells through. Third, operating burn is improving on an absolute basis: Q1 2026 operating cash burn of $222.7M was down from $262.1M a year earlier even as the company funds two new Phase 3 programs.

Spend remains heavy and is the source of the losses. Q1 2026 R&D was $209.5M (+37%, with TPIP external R&D up 308% to $37.3M as the Phase 3s launch); SG&A was $247.3M (+68%, professional fees +147% on the BRINSUPRI launch). Stock-based comp was $45.8M in the quarter, with $163M unrecognized option expense and $260M unrecognized RSU expense outstanding.

Liquidity is the constraint that defines the recommendation. Cash of $582.2M plus marketable securities of $641.3M totaled ~$1.22B at 3/31/26, down from ~$1.43B at year-end. Against the ~$222.7M quarterly operating burn, and with management stating funds last "at least 12 months," another capital raise within roughly 12-18 months is the base case absent faster-than-expected operating leverage. Insmed has already raised $2.2B+ since 2023, including a June 2025 offering of $823.3M net (8.98M shares at $96). Weighted shares outstanding grew from ~180.9M (Q1 2025) to ~215.5M (Q1 2026).

On the debt side, the structure is manageable near-term: $500M Pharmakon term loans (plus $46.8M capitalized PIK interest) at a fixed 9.6%, maturing Sept 2029 with amortization starting Jan 2028; and a $150M OrbiMed royalty financing (4.5% of ARIKAYCE plus 0.75% of brensocatib net sales, capped at 1.8-1.9x, with a $150M minimum guaranteed by 3/31/2028). No near-term maturities. The screen-flagged negative Altman Z-Score is structurally misleading here: with ~$1.2B liquidity, a current ratio of ~3.8x, fixed-rate non-amortizing debt until 2028, and a fast-growing high-exclusivity revenue base, the genuine financial risk is dilution and eventual refinancing, not insolvency.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis is unusually well-evidenced for a single-product launch story because it rests on two independent vectors.

**BRINSUPRI (the engine).** NCFB has ~500K US diagnosed patients and no competing approved therapy. The Q1 2026 ramp implies a strong annualized base before international launches contribute meaningfully, and the ≥$1B 2026 guidance is consistent with that trajectory and the EU/UK approvals layering on. The 14-year exclusivity runway protects the franchise from generic erosion for the duration of any reasonable investment horizon.

**ARIKAYCE (the de-risked optionality).** ENCORE (N=425) hit its primary respiratory symptom endpoint (p=0.0299) and every multiplicity-controlled secondary culture-conversion endpoint at p<0.0001, including durable culture conversion at Month 15 of 76.2% vs. 47.6% for placebo plus background. This converts ARIKAYCE's accelerated approval to a path toward full approval and supports a 2H 2026 sNDA to move from refractory-only into front-line MAC, a materially larger addressable population. It also removes a long-standing tail risk that the FDA could have withdrawn approval on a confirmatory-trial failure.

**TPIP (the second growth leg).** Phase 2b PAH data were strong and two Phase 3 programs are now enrolling, with open-label extension data expected Q3 2026. The once-daily inhaled dosing differentiates against entrenched 4-9x/day treprostinil franchises. This is multi-year optionality, not a 2026 contributor.

The estimate-revision picture is mixed. Current-quarter and current-year loss estimates have improved modestly (+6.5% and -1.7% over 90 days, both small). Next-year EPS is the relevant line: consensus has swung from $0.30 (90 days ago) to $0.40 currently but was $0.75 thirty days ago, a -46% one-month revision. That pattern reflects the market marking down its expectation for the slope of the path to profitability after the Q1 sales miss vs. whispers, even while the longer-arc revisions stayed positive. This is not the clean, accelerating +30% revision velocity that would force a higher conviction call; it is a launch story where consensus is genuinely uncertain about the pace.

The earnings-surprise record reinforces caution on near-term visibility: three consecutive larger-than-expected losses (Q2-Q4 2025: -28.9%, -28.1%, -17.0% surprises) followed by a Q1 2026 beat (+22.3%). The single beat is encouraging, but the prior streak of misses argues against treating current consensus as conservative.

## Valuation Assessment

Trailing and forward P/E are negative and uninformative for a pre-profitability biotech, so valuation has to be framed against the revenue franchise and the path to profitability. At ~$104 and ~216.8M shares, market cap is ~$22.6B. Against a FY2026 revenue base that should comfortably clear $1.4-1.6B (BRINSUPRI ≥$1B plus ARIKAYCE in the $400M+ range plus international layering), the stock trades at roughly 14-16x forward revenue, full but not extreme for a first-in-class franchise with two large underserved markets, an inflecting gross margin, and a credible second product (TPIP) in Phase 3.

The analyst distribution frames the asymmetry. Consensus is uniformly constructive: 22 buy/strong-buy ratings, zero holds or sells, with a price-target range of $140 (low) to $243 (high), mean $200, median $205. Every analyst target sits well above the current price. On inflection names with rising estimates and reasonable forward multiples, consensus targets often lag, and the entire target band being above spot is a meaningful data point. The countervailing consideration is that those targets were largely set before or are being recalibrated after the Q1 expectations reset, and the one recent rating change was a downgrade within strong-buy (4→3 strong-buys). We treat the target band as supportive of the long-run franchise value rather than as a near-term floor.

The genuine valuation discount today is the dilution drag. If the company raises ~$800M-1B of equity within 12-18 months at a price not far from current levels, that is ~7-9% incremental dilution, which directly caps per-share upside even if the franchise performs. The market is correctly pricing some of this. We score valuation moderately positive: there is a specific reason the franchise could be worth more (front-line MAC label expansion plus a TAM the market is discounting on launch-slope skepticism), but the dilution overhang and policy risk prevent a high score.

## Competitive Landscape

Insmed's positioning is strong and improving on the trajectory that matters most.

- **NCFB:** BRINSUPRI is the only approved therapy for a ~500K-patient US market with high unmet need. First-mover with a 14-year exclusivity runway. The competitive risk is future DPP1 inhibitors — Insmed's own WILLOW/ASPEN success has validated the mechanism and attracted followers — but those are clinical-stage and years from launch.
- **MAC lung disease:** ARIKAYCE is the only inhaled therapy indicated, embedded in ATS/ERS/ESCMID/IDSA guidelines. ENCORE positions it to expand from refractory-only into front-line MAC, a structural share gain rather than defense of existing share.
- **PAH/PH-ILD:** the most contested arena; TPIP enters against United Therapeutics' established treprostinil products. Once-daily dosing and apparent tolerability advantages are the differentiation thesis, still to be proven in Phase 3.

The competitive trajectory is improving (front-line label expansion, broadening international footprint, a Phase 3 second product) against a backdrop of near-zero on-label competition today. The principal medium-term threat is not a competitor but US drug-pricing policy.

## Risk Assessment

**Concentration of the thesis.** BRINSUPRI is effectively the entire equity story. The whole stock moved ~23% on a single quarter's sales print relative to expectations. Any quarter where the launch slope disappoints will be punished disproportionately.

**Dilution and refinancing.** The "at least 12 months" liquidity statement against the quarterly burn makes another raise the base case. This is the single clearest cap on per-share returns.

**Drug-pricing policy.** Management explicitly flags that BRINSUPRI, as a small molecule without orphan shielding, is "likely to be subject to IRA price negotiation in the future." The 10-K devotes unusual space to Executive Order 14297 (most-favored-nation pricing) and the proposed GUARD/GENEROUS models, and warns that if subject to GUARD, Insmed "may determine to forego commercializing… outside of the US." That is a direct threat to both pricing on the crown-jewel asset and the international expansion that underpins the bull case. This risk is live given the current policy environment.

**Single-source supply.** PARI is the sole maker of the Lamira nebulizer with no backup by design; ARIKAYCE cannot be sold without it. ARIKAYCE drug substance is Canada-made (tariff exposure); TPIP's treprostinil palmitil active prodrug depends on a single Taiwan supplier with explicit geopolitical caveats. The BIOSECURE Act could disrupt China-based contractors.

**Customer concentration.** In Q1 2026, four customers each represented ≥10% of gross revenue (38%/22%/16%/14% ≈ 90% combined). Loss of a specialty pharmacy/distributor would materially disrupt revenue recognition timing.

**Pipeline label-expansion limits.** Brensocatib failed Phase 2b CEDAR in hidradenitis suppurativa (program discontinued April 2026) and previously failed in CRSsNP, limiting optionality beyond bronchiectasis. ENCORE tolerability (dysphonia 58.7%, discontinuation 18.3% vs. 11.8% placebo) is manageable but a real-world adherence consideration.

The term structure is flat (near 57.5% ≈ far 56.7%), with uncertainty evenly distributed rather than concentrated around a near-term binary, consistent with a launch-trajectory debate playing out over several quarters rather than a single event. The options market is consistent with the HOLD: a two-sided, slow-resolving situation.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** BRINSUPRI clears ≥$1B in 2026 and the launch curve steepens as EU/UK/Japan layer on, validating a multibillion-dollar NCFB franchise. ENCORE drives a successful 2H 2026 sNDA expanding ARIKAYCE into front-line MAC. Gross margin inflects as BRINSUPRI mix grows and pre-expensed inventory flows through. TPIP Phase 3 data extend the strong Phase 2b profile, adding a second large franchise. The company reaches profitability faster than feared, minimizing or avoiding a dilutive raise. In this scenario the stock re-rates toward the analyst mean/median of $200-205 and potentially the $243 high target.

**Bear case.** The BRINSUPRI launch curve flattens on persistence/discontinuation issues, missing the ≥$1B guidance and forcing a larger, more dilutive raise. IRA negotiation and/or MFN/GUARD models compress BRINSUPRI pricing and force Insmed to scale back ex-US commercialization, shrinking the embedded TAM. A supply disruption (PARI nebulizer, Taiwan prodrug, Canada drug substance) interrupts revenue. In this scenario the stock drifts toward the $70-90 range, near its 52-week low and below the analyst $140 low target.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** This is a high-quality franchise at a full-but-defensible valuation, held back from a BUY by near-certain dilution, single-product thesis concentration, and a live drug-pricing-policy overhang. The validation concern — that a 6.0+ overall with 8.0 growth should clear a BUY on an inflection name — is answered by three concrete, falsifiable conditions: (1) management's "at least 12 months" liquidity language against the quarterly burn makes a dilutive equity raise the base case within 12-18 months, mechanically capping per-share upside; (2) the next-year EPS consensus was cut 46% in 30 days, the opposite of the accelerating upward-revision velocity that would justify a high-conviction long; and (3) management discloses BRINSUPRI is "likely to be subject to IRA price negotiation" and that under GUARD it "may determine to forego commercializing outside of the US," directly threatening the pricing and international TAM embedded in the bull case. Any one of these would be a reason for pause; together they keep the call at HOLD.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- BRINSUPRI Q2/Q3 2026 revenue tracking to or above the implied run-rate for ≥$1B full-year guidance, with management commentary on persistence/discontinuation that resolves the bear concern, confirming launch slope.
- A clear path to cash-flow breakeven that removes the need for a near-term dilutive raise (e.g., operating burn falling below ~$150M/quarter while revenue accelerates), OR a completed raise on terms that de-risk the balance sheet without excessive dilution.
- FDA acceptance and a clean path on the front-line MAC sNDA filed in 2H 2026.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- A BRINSUPRI quarter that misses the implied ≥$1B trajectory materially, or a withdrawal/reduction of the $1B guidance.
- Concrete adverse policy action (BRINSUPRI selected for IRA negotiation on punitive terms, or GUARD/MFN inclusion that forces an explicit ex-US retreat).
- A supply disruption affecting ARIKAYCE (PARI/Lamira) or a TPIP Phase 3 setback.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next natural checkpoint is Q2 2026 earnings (the first full quarter to test the launch slope against guidance), then the 2H 2026 sNDA filing and Q3 2026 TPIP open-label extension data.

## Conclusion

Insmed is a legitimately improving rare-disease respiratory franchise: BRINSUPRI is ramping fast in a large untapped market, ENCORE removed a long-standing overhang and opened a bigger ARIKAYCE label, and gross margin is inflecting. The price decline reflects an expectations reset rather than a fundamental break, and the analyst target band sits entirely above the current price. We do not, however, treat the ~51% drawdown as simply volatility: the market is pricing two specific, identifiable risks: near-certain dilution given the burn rate, and a drug-pricing-policy overhang that management itself flags against the crown-jewel asset and its ex-US strategy. With per-share upside structurally capped by dilution and next-year estimates recently cut sharply, the risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric. We rate INSM a HOLD with a growth bias and would move to a BUY on confirmation that the BRINSUPRI launch slope is holding to guidance and that the balance sheet is de-risked.
