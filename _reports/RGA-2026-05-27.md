**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Reinsurance Group of America is the world's largest life and health reinsurer by revenue, with $4.3 trillion of life reinsurance in force across 26+ countries. The company reported Q1 2026 adjusted operating EPS of $6.97, beating consensus by 13%, and full-year estimates have subsequently been revised upward (+3.8% over 30 days to $27.08). At $206.78, the stock trades at ~7.6x FY26 estimated adjusted operating earnings and approximately 1.23x book value excluding AOCI, while generating a 15–16% adjusted operating ROE on that same ex-AOCI basis.

The stock has declined from its February 2026 high of $229.21 to the current $206.78 (-10%), creating a valuation opportunity. The decline appears linked to: (1) a broad reinsurance sector derating in Q3-Q4 2025 following UK mortality assumption charges, and (2) general financial sector uncertainty during the rate volatility period. The fundamental trajectory remains positive: revenue grew approximately 23.5% YoY in Q1 2026, the Equitable Holdings transaction is accretive, and management is executing a $400M subordinated debt refinancing that optimizes the liability structure. The market is applying a discount more appropriate to a structurally impaired franchise when the evidence suggests an assumption update that has already flowed through earnings.

The primary risks are genuine: UK mortality deterioration could recur, the investment portfolio's credit quality is declining at the margin (losses up 2.4x YoY), and the rapid balance sheet expansion ($33B in new assets) introduces execution risk. These merit a lower risk score but do not justify the current valuation discount to a 15%+ ROE business.

## Company Overview

RGA operates through two primary segments — Traditional Reinsurance and Financial Solutions — across four geographic regions (U.S./Latin America, Asia Pacific, EMEA, Canada). The Traditional segment provides mortality and morbidity reinsurance on individual and group life, health, disability, and critical illness policies. Financial Solutions includes longevity reinsurance, asset-intensive (annuity) reinsurance, pension risk transfer, and capital solutions.

As of year-end 2025, total assets reached $156.6 billion with cash and invested assets of $134.6 billion. The company's scale provides a data advantage in pricing mortality and morbidity risk that smaller competitors cannot replicate.

## Competitive Landscape

RGA's competitive position is improving structurally for several reasons:

1. **Industry consolidation:** The number of credible global life reinsurers has declined over 20 years. RGA, Swiss Re, Munich Re, SCOR, and Hannover Re dominate, with RGA #1 in life and health reinsurance revenues based on 2024 data.

2. **Data advantage:** With $4.3T in force, RGA's mortality and morbidity databases are among the deepest globally, enabling superior pricing accuracy on new business.

3. **Alt-asset manager competition limited to asset-intensive blocks:** Apollo, KKR, and Blackstone compete aggressively for annuity/asset-intensive reinsurance, but they cannot replicate RGA's traditional mortality/morbidity underwriting capabilities. This protects the core franchise.

4. **AA- financial strength rating:** Essential for counterparty acceptability in major ceding company relationships.

5. **Natural hedge structure:** The combination of life reinsurance (loses if mortality worsens) and longevity reinsurance (loses if mortality improves) provides a built-in portfolio hedge, though the longevity book is substantially smaller.

Rising cession rates mean more business available to fewer qualified reinsurers.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **UK mortality deterioration (SPECIFIC, MATERIAL):** The $222M 2025 charge stemmed from NHS-driven mortality improvement degradation. RGA attributed this to "deterioration in mortality improvements and ongoing challenges within the healthcare system." If UK healthcare continues deteriorating — a plausible scenario given fiscal constraints — additional charges are possible. EMEA in-force grew to $1.1T, meaning exposure is increasing. While the charge has been recognized in 2025 results, the underlying structural drivers have not been resolved, and recurrence risk remains.

2. **Investment portfolio credit deterioration:** Level 3 assets rising to 13.5% of fixed maturities, with significant new asset deployment during 2025 heavily influenced by the Equitable transaction. A credit cycle turn would amplify losses disproportionately given the portfolio's growth rate.

3. **Equitable transaction execution risk:** A single $12B liability assumption represents concentration risk. If adverse selection manifests in the ceded block (i.e., Equitable retained the better lives), the transaction economics could disappoint over the 10-30 year horizon.

4. **Interest rate reversal:** While current rates benefit new money yields, a rapid rate decline would compress margins on the asset-intensive book, where $21.6B in annuity balances are already at minimum guaranteed rates. The floor creates asymmetric downside.

5. **Pandemic/epidemic tail risk:** As the world's largest life reinsurer, RGA has the largest absolute exposure to mass mortality events among publicly traded companies.

**Options Market Signal:**

Near-term IV (26.3%) sits modestly above 1-year historical volatility (23.2%) — a 3.1pp premium suggesting slightly elevated uncertainty but nothing extreme. IV has compressed sharply from 51.1% at the time of earnings (May 6) to 26.3% currently, indicating the market has resolved the earnings binary and returned to baseline. Put/call skew is balanced (2.8%), and the term structure is flat (26.3% near vs. 27.6% far), suggesting no significant event premium is being priced. Short interest at 1.6% is negligible. The options market is not signaling either extreme caution or opportunity.

**Score Trajectory Context:** The declining score trajectory (7.2 → 7.4 → 6.7 → 6.8) reflects the accumulation of risk factors (UK mortality, credit deterioration, executive turnover). The most recent data point — Q1 2026's 13% earnings beat with rising estimates — represents a positive fundamental development that the declining trajectory hadn't yet incorporated.

## Conclusion

RGA is a high-quality life reinsurance franchise trading at a discount to intrinsic value. The Q1 2026 beat, rising estimates, and active liability management provide near-term catalysts. The risks — UK mortality, credit quality, and balance sheet growth execution — are identifiable, quantifiable, and largely already reflected in the 10% pullback from highs. At current levels, the asymmetry favors ownership. The BUY recommendation is maintained from prior reports, now supported by Q1 confirmation of earnings power and improving estimate trajectory.

The score decline from prior reports reflected legitimate risk accumulation, but the fundamental trajectory has stabilized with Q1 results. The specific opportunity lies in the market applying a P/E appropriate for a shrinking or flat-ROE franchise to a business growing in-force 12% annually and generating mid-teens returns on equity.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The corrected valuation framework (1.23x ex-AOCI book rather than 1.0x book, refinancing rather than deleveraging, and a less precise peer comparison) narrows the re-rating gap and moderates the strength of the valuation catalyst underpinning the target price.
