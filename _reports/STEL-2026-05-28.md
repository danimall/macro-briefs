**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Stellar Bancorp is a merger-arbitrage position. Prosperity Bancshares (PB) agreed to acquire STEL on January 27, 2026, at 0.3803 PB shares plus $11.36 in cash per STEL share. All regulatory approvals cleared on April 22, 2026, and the shareholder vote was held on May 27, 2026. Expected close is approximately July 1, 2026. At $37.34 current price, the stock trades essentially at deal value, leaving negligible spread for new entrants.

The underlying franchise is strong: 4.24% net interest margin, 35.7% noninterest-bearing deposits, 15.48% total risk-based capital, and net charge-offs of only 7 basis points annualized. Q1 2026 results showed improving fundamentals with EPS of $0.53 (beating estimates by 15.3%). However, none of this matters for the stock price over the next 30-35 days. The equity is entirely deal-value constrained until close.

For existing holders, there is no reason to sell ahead of close given minimal execution risk remaining. For prospective buyers, the position offers approximately 1-2% potential upside tied to PB stock appreciation minus the opportunity cost of capital deployment elsewhere for 30+ days.

## Company Overview

Stellar Bancorp is a Houston-based bank holding company with $10.9 billion in assets, operating 52 banking centers concentrated in the Houston MSA (35 branches), Beaumont MSA (16 branches), and Dallas (1 branch). The company was formed through the October 2022 merger of equals between Allegiance Bancshares and CBTX. It focuses on small- to medium-sized business lending with particular strength in Houston's commercial real estate and C&I markets.

Loan portfolio composition as of March 31, 2026: CRE including multi-family (50.7%), C&I (20.6%), 1-4 family residential (15.4%), CRE construction & land development (10.8%), residential construction (1.3%), and consumer/other (1.2%).

## Valuation Assessment

STEL at $37.34 trades at 18.2x trailing EPS ($1.99) and approximately 17.3x FY2026E EPS ($2.16). Against peer median P/E of 13.7x, this appears expensive. However, the premium exists entirely because of the merger — the stock trades at deal value, not at a standalone fundamental multiple.

**Deal Value Math:**
- Cash component: $11.36 per share
- Stock component: 0.3803 × PB stock price
- At PB ≈ $68.30 (implied from $37.34 - $11.36 = $25.98 / 0.3803): deal value ≈ $37.34
- The stock is at parity with deal economics

**Standalone value (if deal broke):** At peer median 13.7x on $2.16 FY2026E EPS = ~$29.60. Tangible book = $21.62. Standalone fair value range approximately $28-32. This represents 16-25% downside in a deal-break scenario, which carries very low probability given all approvals secured.

**Dividend yield:** 1.6% ($0.60 annualized), well below peer median of 3.0% — reflecting the merger premium embedded in the share price.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risk: Deal Break (Very Low Probability, ~2-3%)**
No material adverse change has been disclosed. The termination fee structure and voting agreements make a voluntary walk-away extremely unlikely. Expected loss from this risk ≈ 0.5-0.7% (2-3% probability × 16-25% decline to standalone value of $28-32).

**Secondary Risk: PB Stock Decline**
Since 0.3803 shares of PB constitute the majority of consideration, STEL is effectively a leveraged bet on PB stock price until close. If PB declines 10% before close, STEL's deal value drops approximately $2.60/share (7%).

**Credit Quality Trends:**
The rising NPA trajectory (0.50% → 0.72% → 0.80%) is a Prosperity problem post-close, not a STEL shareholder problem given the fixed exchange ratio. However, if material credit deterioration triggered an MAC clause, this could theoretically delay or jeopardize close.

**CRE Concentration:**
61.5% of loans in CRE (including construction) represents above-average concentration. Office exposure of $365M (9.5% of CRE portfolio) is manageable but bears monitoring in the current environment.

**Options Market Signal:**
Short interest at 2.0% with 3.1 days to cover is minimal — consistent with a near-certain merger close.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$38.50-39.00, +3-4%):**
PB stock appreciates modestly into close, lifting the stock component of consideration. Smooth close on schedule (July 1). Limited upside given proximity to deal value.

**Bear Case (~$28-32, -14% to -25%):**
Deal breaks (extremely low probability). STEL reprices to standalone fundamental value at peer multiples. Alternatively, PB stock declines significantly before close, dragging STEL's deal value lower.

**Base Case (~$37-38, flat to +2%):**
Deal closes on schedule at approximately current value levels. PB stock trades roughly flat. Total return over 30 days equals the spread (if any) plus one dividend payment.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**

## Conclusion

Stellar Bancorp is a HOLD — the equity is a time-limited merger-arb position with minimal spread remaining. The underlying bank is a high-quality Texas community franchise with above-average NIM, strong noninterest-bearing deposits, disciplined credit culture, and adequate capital. Q1 2026 results showed genuine operational improvement, but the Prosperity acquisition subsumes all standalone fundamentals.

For existing holders: continue holding through close (July 1). The risk/reward of selling pre-close to avoid a 2-3% probability deal break versus capturing the final weeks of consideration makes holding the correct decision.

For prospective buyers: the opportunity cost of deploying capital for ~30 days at essentially 0% spread argues against initiating a position unless PB stock (and consequently STEL) pulls back materially, creating a spread.

The score trajectory improvement from 6.0 to 6.3 reflects the de-risking from regulatory approvals clearing, not fundamental improvement. The stock is rated HOLD because the position resolves mechanically in approximately one month.
