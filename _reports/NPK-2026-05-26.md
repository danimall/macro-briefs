## Executive Summary

National Presto Industries is a sole-source defense ammunition manufacturer disguised by its consumer appliance heritage. With 81% of revenue from defense (growing 43% annually) and a $1.84B backlog representing 1.9x its market cap, the company offers extraordinary revenue visibility. Q1 2026 delivered +$31.3M operating cash flow after two years of negative OCF driven by deliberate inventory buildup, the credit line is fully repaid, and the FY26 contract award year could add $400-500M to backlog.

The investment case hinges on a specific tension: the stock trades at 29x trailing EPS ($4.63), which appears expensive on face value but is significantly distorted by the housewares segment's collapse from 25% gross margins to operating losses under tariff pressure. Defense-only earnings power suggests a P/E closer to 13-15x. The 57% price appreciation over 12 months has already priced in meaningful recovery expectations. At $135, the market has partially recognized the defense story, but the backlog execution runway and potential housewares recovery provide continued upside optionality that justifies holding rather than selling.

The primary reason this is not a BUY at current levels: with no analyst coverage, 8.8% short interest (suggesting active skeptics), insider selling (CFO and director in March at $140-144), and the housewares segment actively destroying value with no clear tariff resolution timeline, the 29x multiple on depressed earnings requires confidence in near-term earnings normalization that cannot be verified with precision. The next 2-3 quarters of defense margin and cash flow data will determine whether this deserves re-rating to BUY.

## Company Overview

National Presto Industries operates across three segments with dramatically different profiles:

**Defense (81% of revenue):** Through subsidiary AMTEC Corporation, NPK is the sole prime contractor for the U.S. Army's 40mm Ammunition System — a position held continuously since 2016. The company operates five vertically integrated manufacturing facilities covering fuzes, detonators, metal parts, cartridge cases, and final assembly. FY2025 defense revenue reached $406M (up 43% YoY), with backlog growing from $564M (Dec 2023) to $1.84B (April 2026).

**Housewares (19% of revenue):** The legacy Presto® brand designs and distributes pressure cookers, electric skillets, and other kitchen products sourced from China. Gross margins collapsed from 25% in FY2024 to 8% in FY2025 and turned negative in Q1 2026 due to tariffs on Chinese imports.

**Safety (<1% of revenue):** Startup-stage smoke/CO alarms and fire extinguishers through Rely Innovations. Consistently loss-making.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue Trajectory
Revenue has grown from $322M (2022) to $504M (2025), a 56% increase driven entirely by defense. Q1 2026 revenue was $118.6M (+15% YoY), with defense contributing $99.6M (+23%).

### Profitability Under Pressure from Mix
FY2025 EPS of $4.63 declined 20% despite 30% revenue growth. The explanation is straightforward: housewares gross margins collapsed from 25% to 8%, and then to negative in Q1 2026 (a -$2.4M gross loss). Defense margins compressed modestly from 21% to 19%, likely from fixed-price contract exposure to input cost inflation.

On a defense-only basis, segment operating income was approximately $77M in FY2025 on $406M revenue (19% margin). Applying a 21% tax rate yields ~$61M net income from defense, or ~$8.50/share — implying a defense-only P/E of approximately 16x at current prices.

### Cash Flow Inflection — The Critical Signal
- FY2024 OCF: -$23.1M
- FY2025 OCF: -$9.1M
- Q1 2026 OCF: +$31.3M

The inventory buildup that consumed cash ($190M → $278M → $307M from 2023-2025) appears complete. AR dropped $36.7M in Q1 2026 as defense shipments converted to cash. The $23.6M credit line was fully repaid. Capex dropped to $2M from $27M in FY2025. Free cash flow generation should accelerate materially through the remainder of FY2026.

### Balance Sheet
- Zero long-term debt (credit line fully repaid April 2026)
- Stockholders' equity: $395M
- Current ratio: 5.9x
- Cash position recovering from $3.3M (FY2025 low) — should rebuild rapidly given OCF trajectory
- $602M in outstanding purchase orders for defense materials

## Growth Analysis

The growth story is defense backlog conversion. At $1.84B backlog and a current defense run rate of ~$400M annually, the company has approximately 4.3 years of contracted revenue visibility. The contract ceiling analysis reveals additional upside:

- 40mm system contract: $1.413B ceiling, ~$965M awarded = ~$448M remaining
- TP-DNT IDIQ: $818.9M ceiling, ~$256M awarded = ~$563M remaining
- Combined additional ordering capacity: ~$1B+

Global ammunition demand remains elevated due to Ukraine replenishment, NATO defense budget increases, and U.S. industrial base rebuilding priorities. The company explicitly identifies the Ukraine Security Assistance Initiative as a demand driver.

Revenue growth should moderate from 30% (FY2025) to 15-20% as the base grows, but the backlog provides high confidence in sustained double-digit growth through at least FY2028.

The housewares segment is optionality rather than a growth driver. If tariffs are successfully litigated (Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs Feb 2026, CIT struck Section 122 tariffs May 2026), the segment could recover from operating losses to 25% gross margins — representing $15-20M in incremental gross profit annually.

## Valuation Assessment

At $135.45 with FY2025 EPS of $4.63:
- Trailing P/E: 29.3x
- Forward P/E: 29.3x (per Yahoo Finance)
- EV/Backlog: 0.53x
- Defense backlog/market cap: 1.9x

The 29x trailing P/E is misleading because it includes a housewares segment that is temporarily value-destructive. A sum-of-parts approach:
- Defense segment at 18x defense-only earnings (~$8.50/share) = $153/share
- Housewares at zero (currently money-losing but with recovery optionality)
- Less: corporate overhead allocation

This suggests ~$140-155 fair value under current conditions, with upside to $165-175 if housewares recovers or defense margins expand.

Against peers, the 29.3x P/E matches the peer median of 29.7x (SARO). NPK's sole-source position and 4.3-year backlog visibility arguably warrant a premium over the peer group. The counter-argument: peers like AIR (24x) and PSN (24.8x) have broader diversification and less government concentration risk.

The stock has appreciated 57% from its 52-week low of $82.67. This appreciation is fundamentally justified by the backlog growth from $564M to $1.84B and the cash flow inflection. However, at $135, much of the "discovery" upside has been captured. The remaining upside requires either margin expansion on defense (from 19% toward 22-23%) or housewares recovery — both plausible but not yet confirmed.

## Competitive Landscape

NPK's competitive position in defense is exceptional. The sole-source designation on 40mm ammunition since 2016, supported by 20+ years of continuous contract history dating to 2005, creates a moat that is effectively impossible to replicate in the medium term. Barriers include:

- Specialized manufacturing of explosive/energetic materials requires years of qualification
- Vertically integrated production from fuzes through final assembly
- Multiple facilities with explosive handling licenses
- Decades of testing and quality history with the Army

The 40mm ammunition system is critical to U.S. and allied ground forces. The Army's repeated renewal of contracts (now on the 4th five-year term) and expansion of ceiling values demonstrates sustained demand and satisfaction with NPK/AMTEC as the provider.

In housewares, competitive position is deteriorating. The segment operates in a "highly competitive and extremely price sensitive environment" with larger competitors, private label encroachment, and structural sourcing challenges from tariffs. This segment has no meaningful moat.

## Risk Assessment

### 1. Government Contract Concentration (High)
Substantially all defense revenue derives from DOD contracts that can be terminated for convenience. Any shift in procurement priorities, budget sequestration, or change in Ukraine-related demand could materially impair revenue. The current geopolitical environment favors ammunition spending, but political winds shift.

### 2. Fixed-Price Contract Margin Risk (Moderate)
Escalation clauses cover only steel, aluminum, and zinc. Broader inflation in labor, energy, and other materials compresses margins without recovery. Defense gross margins have already compressed from 21% to 19%. Further compression to 16-17% is possible if inflation persists.

### 3. Tariff-Driven Housewares Losses (Active/Ongoing)
The legal situation is extraordinarily fluid. Three separate tariff authorities have been challenged; outcomes are binary and unpredictable. Each quarter of operating losses in housewares reduces consolidated EPS by $0.30-0.50.

### 4. Governance/Liquidity Concerns (Moderate)
Only ~7.17M shares outstanding creates a relatively small float. CEO holds Chair/President/CEO roles simultaneously. The Voting Trust Agreement (originally from 1997, amended 2008 and 2024) concentrates control. Average daily volume of ~82K shares limits institutional participation and creates liquidity risk in drawdowns (the July 28 drop of 8% on 91K shares illustrates this).

### 5. Insider Selling (Notable)
CFO David Peuse sold 500 shares at $140 in March 2026, leaving only 308 shares — a concerning signal given his direct visibility into the company's trajectory. Director Randy Lieble sold 2,417 shares at $144 ($348K). No insider buying observed. While small in absolute terms, the CFO reducing his position to 308 shares during the stock's high watermark deserves attention.

### 6. Cybersecurity (Tail Risk)
March 2025 cybersecurity incident on a defense contractor handling sensitive military information creates potential for contract disruption, regulatory penalty, or security clearance issues.

### Why Not BUY at 6.74 Score

The specific reason preventing a BUY recommendation: **the CFO sold down to 308 shares at $140 in March 2026 during the stock's peak, and no insider buying has occurred despite the stock's 57% run**. This is a small company where insiders have direct visibility into contract economics and margin trajectories. A CFO reducing his position to minimal levels at precisely the prices where the stock trades today is an actionable negative signal. Combined with:

1. The trailing 29x multiple requires earnings normalization that hasn't yet been confirmed beyond one quarter of positive cash flow
2. Housewares is actively losing money with no timeline for resolution (tariff legal situation described as "extraordinarily fluid")
3. The 8.8% short interest means informed participants are actively betting against the stock — unusual for a defense contractor with a growing backlog
4. No analyst coverage means no external verification of the financial trajectory

The combination of insider selling at current levels + elevated short interest + no analyst coverage + one quarter of positive data (vs. two years of negative OCF) creates insufficient confidence for a BUY at this price. If Q2 2026 confirms continued strong OCF, defense margins stabilize at 19%+, and the FY26 award year materializes, this becomes a BUY.

## Conclusion

National Presto Industries possesses a genuinely exceptional competitive asset in its sole-source defense ammunition position, supported by a $1.84B backlog that provides years of revenue visibility. The cash flow inflection is real and significant. The housewares drag is temporary in some scenarios and permanent in others — the tariff legal situation provides no clarity.

At $135, the stock prices in meaningful execution on the backlog but does not yet price in full margin recovery or housewares normalization. The 57% appreciation from May 2025 lows reflects legitimate fundamental improvement. However, the insider selling pattern, elevated short interest, and single-quarter confirmation of the cash flow inflection argue for patience rather than aggressive positioning. This is a high-quality HOLD with clear catalysts that could upgrade it to BUY within 2-3 quarters if execution confirms the trajectory.
