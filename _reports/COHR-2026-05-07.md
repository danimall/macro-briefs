## Executive Summary

Coherent Corp has transformed from a sprawling photonics conglomerate into one of the leading vertically-integrated suppliers of optical transceivers for AI datacenter infrastructure. The stock has risen ~400% in 12 months, driven by Datacenter & Communications segment revenue growing 41% YoY in Q3 FY2026 and management guiding Q4 revenue 30%+ above year-ago levels. Non-GAAP EPS of $1.41 in Q3 FY2026 is running at a $6.40+ annualized rate, with FY2027 consensus at $7.88 and revisions accelerating upward at +9.2% over 90 days.

The investment case rests on Coherent's rare vertical integration — designing and fabricating its own InP lasers, VCSELs, ICs, and passive components in-house — which provides cost advantages, supply control, and performance differentiation during the capacity-constrained AI infrastructure buildout. The 800G-to-1.6T transceiver upgrade cycle has multi-year duration, with CPO (co-packaged optics) representing incremental upside. Against this, the stock trades at ~44x FY2027 consensus EPS, options price 107% near-term IV (39pp above realized), and a BIS inquiry regarding past Huawei sales creates unquantifiable regulatory risk. The balance of evidence supports a BUY with the understanding that position sizing must reflect elevated volatility.

## Company Overview

Coherent Corp. operates as a vertically integrated photonics company with ~30,000 employees across manufacturing facilities in the U.S., Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Following its July 2022 merger with the former Coherent Inc. (previously II-VI Incorporated), the company has been restructuring toward its highest-growth opportunity: AI datacenter optical interconnects.

Effective FY2026, the company reorganized into two segments:
- **Datacenter & Communications** (75% of Q3 revenue, $1.36B): Optical transceivers (800G, 1.6T), coherent telecom modules, optical components
- **Industrial** (25%, $444M): Excimer lasers, solid-state lasers, SiC substrates, engineered materials

Datacenter & Communications grew 41% YoY in Q3 while Industrial declined 16% on a reported basis (approximately 2% pro forma, excluding divested businesses). Portfolio simplification continues through divestitures — the aerospace & defense business and Munich tools operations were sold for $437M combined.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Acceleration:**

| FY2025 | $5.81B | +23% |
| Q3 FY26 | $1.81B | +21% |
| Q4 FY26 Guide (mid) | $1.98B | ~30%+ |
| Annualized Run-Rate | ~$7.9B | — |

Revenue is re-accelerating as AI datacenter demand compounds. Q4 FY26 guidance of $1.91-2.05B implies the business is approaching a ~$8B annual revenue run-rate. Based on reported results for the first nine months ($5.07B) plus Q4 guidance midpoint ($1.98B), FY2026 full-year revenue is approximately $7.05B.

**Margin Expansion:**

GAAP gross margins improved from 31% (FY2024) to 35% (FY2025) to 37.7% (Q3 FY2026). Non-GAAP gross margins guided to 39-41% for Q4. This 800+ bps expansion over two years reflects mix shift toward higher-ASP AI transceivers, manufacturing yield improvements, and operating leverage on the fixed-cost base. Non-GAAP operating margin reached 20.3% in Q3, up from 18.6% a year ago.

**Balance Sheet:**

Debt has been reduced from $4.1B (June 2024) to $3.19B (March 2026). Two key capital-structure events occurred in FY2026: (1) the conversion of $2.48B in Series B preferred stock into approximately 30.1 million common shares, which eliminated ~$96M/year in preferred dividends and reclassified $2.5B from mezzanine equity to common stockholders' equity; and (2) a separate $2.0B equity investment by NVIDIA, which purchased approximately 7.8 million common shares at $256.80 per share under a multi-year strategic agreement that includes a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment. Cash plus short-term investments totaled $2.42B at Q3 end, driven primarily by the NVIDIA cash proceeds. Net debt is approximately $770M, down from the $3.2B net debt position 18 months ago.

**Cash Flow Concern:**

Operating cash flow was only $10M in the first 9 months of FY2026, compared to $634M in full-year FY2025. The culprit: inventory grew 48% ($1.44B to $2.13B) and receivables grew 23% ($964M to $1.19B). This is characteristic of a company building ahead of visible demand — management is investing in capacity and pre-positioning materials. Capital expenditures of $547M (9 months) are running above FY2025's full-year $441M. Free cash flow recovery depends on inventory conversion as revenue ramps in Q4 and beyond.

## Growth Analysis

**Multi-Year Demand Visibility:**

The AI datacenter infrastructure buildout is confirmed by hyperscaler capex commitments, semiconductor demand indicators, and disclosed customer agreements. The NVIDIA strategic agreement includes a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment over multiple years, providing concrete revenue visibility beyond typical backlog disclosures. Coherent is also expanding InP capacity at its Sherman, Texas facility to address what management describes as an industry-wide shortage in indium phosphide components.

**Product Cycle:**

The transition from 400G → 800G → 1.6T transceivers is in early-to-middle innings. Coherent demonstrated 1.6T, 3.2T, and 6.4T technologies at OFC 2026. Each generation step requires new laser technologies and higher-density packaging where Coherent's vertical integration provides differentiated capability.

**CPO Upside:**

Co-packaged optics represents a significant incremental market opportunity by integrating optical engines directly with switch ASICs. Coherent demonstrated multiple CPO approaches at OFC 2026 — including 6.4T socketed CPO, ELS powered by InP CW lasers, multimode socketed CPO with VCSELs, and 400G-per-lane InP modulator technology — and has partnerships with both ASIC vendors and hyperscalers. This is a 2027-2028 catalyst that is partially priced but not fully reflected in consensus estimates.

**SiC Pivot:**

The SiC substrate business has been weak due to EV/automotive softness, but Coherent's April 2026 announcement of thick epitaxy for 10kV power devices targeting AI datacenter power management could re-purpose this technology for a growing market. This is speculative but illustrates management's ability to redirect R&D toward demand.

**Estimate Revision Velocity:**

FY2027 EPS estimates have risen 9.2% in 90 days ($7.22 → $7.88). Current quarter estimates rose 6.3% in the same period. This revision pace, while positive, is below the 30%+ threshold that would constitute a strong signal. The momentum is moderate and sustained rather than explosive.

The 44x forward P/E on FY2027 estimates is premium but defensible for a company growing EPS 40-50% annually with margin expansion headroom. The PEG of ~1.0 suggests the growth rate approximately justifies the multiple. Peer comparisons: Lumentum trades at ~35x forward, Marvell at ~30x, while Nvidia trades at ~24x on higher absolute margins. Coherent's premium reflects its specific exposure to the optical interconnect bottleneck.

The consensus analyst target mean of $355.64 and median of $371 sit 3-8% above current price, suggesting limited near-term upside per sell-side models. However, on high-growth names with positive estimate revisions, analyst targets typically lag reality and get revised upward. The $455 high target implies 32% upside in an optimistic scenario.

The trailing P/E of 284x reflects GAAP losses from $160M in restructuring charges, $85M in impairments, $303M in amortization of intangibles, and ~$130M in preferred dividends (prior to conversion), none of which represent the normalized earnings power of the business.

**Valuation Score Rationale:** At 44x FY2027, the stock is priced for continued strong execution. The PEG of ~1.0 is reasonable but not cheap. The EV/Revenue of ~9.7x on FY2026 estimates is rich relative to hardware peers. Any deceleration in AI datacenter spending growth or a miss on margin targets would compress the multiple 20-30%. The stock is fairly valued assuming continued execution, not deeply mispriced.

## Competitive Landscape

Coherent's primary competitive advantage is vertical integration. Most transceiver competitors (InnoLight, Lumentum, Cisco/Acacia) source some or all of their semiconductor laser components externally. Coherent fabricates InP lasers, GaAs VCSELs, driver ICs, TIAs, and passive optical components in-house across multiple fabs. During capacity-constrained environments, this provides:

1. **Supply security** — no reliance on external component allocation
2. **Cost advantage** — captured internal margins vs. merchant pricing
3. **Performance optimization** — co-design across the full optical stack
4. **Speed to market** — faster iteration on new wavelengths and form factors

The company's customer list (two >10% customers, whose identities are not publicly disclosed) and protocol-agnostic approach (supporting Ethernet, InfiniBand, and NVLink) provide demand diversification within AI datacenter buildout. The NVIDIA strategic agreement provides concrete evidence of deep customer relationships with major AI infrastructure players.

Competitive threats: InnoLight is gaining share in 800G pluggable transceivers with aggressive pricing from Chinese manufacturing. Broadcom's integrated approach (combining switch ASICs with optical PHYs) could displace standalone transceiver vendors longer-term. However, the current supply shortage means all vendors have more demand than capacity — market share battles are a 2028+ concern.

## Risk Assessment

**1. BIS/Huawei Inquiry (Material, Unquantifiable):**
The January 2025 Bureau of Industry and Security inquiry regarding past Huawei product sales creates regulatory overhang. Coherent has ceased all Huawei shipments and is cooperating. Potential outcomes range from no action to significant fines. The company's extensive Asia-Pacific workforce (22,340 employees) amplifies exposure to any broader export control escalation.

**2. Customer Concentration:**
Two customers each >10% of revenue. If AI infrastructure capex decelerates (whether from efficiency breakthroughs like DeepSeek or macroeconomic pressure), Coherent's revenue could decelerate sharply. The 10-K explicitly identifies this risk.

**3. Free Cash Flow Consumption:**
$547M in capex (9 months) plus ~$690M inventory build are consuming cash during the ramp. If demand disappoints, the company would face inventory write-downs and stranded capex. The $2.4B in cash/investments provides a buffer, but this is a leveraged bet on continued demand growth.

**4. Valuation Compression Risk:**
At 44x FY2027 earnings, the stock is vulnerable to sharp drawdowns on any guidance disappointment. The Aug 13-14, 2025 drop from $123 to $91 (-26% in two days) demonstrates the stock's fragility to earnings disappointments. The stock also declined ~6% after Q3 FY2026 results despite beating consensus estimates, illustrating that high-multiple stocks can sell off even on modest beats if forward expectations are not exceeded sufficiently.

**5. Tariff/Geopolitical:**
Rare earth sourcing from China (germanium, yttrium), combined with manufacturing in Asia-Pacific, creates supply chain vulnerability in an escalating trade war environment.

### Options Market Signal

## Conclusion

Coherent Corp represents a high-quality, structurally advantaged position in the AI datacenter optical interconnect supply chain. The vertical integration, accelerating revenue growth, expanding margins, and improving balance sheet all support fundamental conviction. The stock warrants a BUY recommendation with the following caveats: position sizing should be moderate (2-3%) given elevated implied volatility and 44x forward multiple, the BIS inquiry is a genuine risk requiring monitoring, and the stock's 400%+ appreciation means any execution stumble will be punished severely.

The specific reason the market is likely mispricing this stock: consensus FY2027 EPS of $7.88 is probably conservative given the Q4 FY26 run-rate of ~$1.62/quarter ($6.48 annualized) with continued 20%+ sequential growth in datacenter revenue, margin expansion, and the elimination of $96M in annual preferred dividends. The revision velocity of +9.2% over 90 days should continue as the datacenter build-out intensifies through calendar 2026-2027. The NVIDIA multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment provides concrete demand visibility that further de-risks forward estimates. Analyst targets lag on stocks in this part of the growth curve.
