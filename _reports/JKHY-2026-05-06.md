## Executive Summary

Jack Henry & Associates is a financial technology company providing core banking platforms, digital banking, and payment processing to approximately 7,400 financial institutions and diverse corporate entities in the United States. The stock trades at $149.34, down approximately 23% from its 52-week high of $193.39, despite delivering four consecutive earnings beats and raising FY2026 guidance. The disconnect between price and fundamentals creates an attractive entry.

The investment case rests on three pillars: (1) a structural competitive opportunity created by a rival's core platform consolidation — Q2 FY2026 core wins doubled to 22 from 11 — (2) accelerating margin expansion from cloud migration and operating leverage, and (3) a fortress balance sheet (zero long-term debt, strong FCF generation) supporting aggressive buybacks at prices above current levels. At approximately 22-24x forward earnings with mid-single-digit revenue growth accelerating toward high single digits and 15-25% EPS growth, the risk/reward is skewed favorably at these levels.

The primary risk is that JKHY remains a steady mid-single-digit organic grower in a sector where peers have delivered faster top-line growth, keeping the stock range-bound. However, the current competitive dislocation and cloud modernization investment could alter this trajectory over 12-18 months.

## Company Overview

Jack Henry & Associates, founded in 1976 and headquartered in Monett, Missouri, operates four segments: Core (31% of revenue, $739M), Payments (37%, $873M), Complementary (28%, $675M), and Corporate & Other (4%, $87M). The company serves ~950 banks and ~715 credit unions on its core platforms, with an additional 5,710 non-core clients purchasing specialized solutions.

The business model is characterized by recurring revenue dominance — a high proportion of core clients operate in private cloud with 6-year contracts — high switching costs, and consistently top-rated service scores. JKHY's Symitar platform is the most-used credit union core system for institutions over $25M in assets per NCUA data.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:**
Revenue growth has been consistent and modestly accelerating: FY2023 +6.9%, FY2024 +6.6%, FY2025 +7.2%, and H1 FY2026 annualized ~7.6%. FY2026 non-GAAP revenue guidance of +6.4% to +7.1% was raised at Q2, reflecting pipeline strength.

**Profitability:**
Operating margins recovered from 22.1% in FY2024 (depressed by a $16.4M voluntary separation program) to 23.9% in FY2025 and are running at 25.7% in the most recent quarter (Q2 FY2026 operating income of $159.1M on revenue of $619.3M). EPS growth has significantly outpaced revenue growth: FY2025 EPS of $6.24 was +19.3% YoY, and H1 FY2026 EPS of $3.70 is +24.5% YoY. FY2026 guidance of $6.61-$6.72 implies continued 6-8% EPS growth even against FY2025's elevated base.

**Balance Sheet:**
Zero long-term debt. The company paid off a $275M term loan and revolving facility. As of December 31, 2025, only $20M was drawn on its revolving credit facility. In March 2026, the company entered into a new $1.0B five-year unsecured revolving credit agreement, replacing the prior $600M facility. Total liabilities-to-equity of 0.43 reflects minimal leverage, with interest-bearing debt/equity near zero. Altman Z-Score of 3.83 confirms fortress status. ROE of 21.4% demonstrates efficient capital deployment without leverage dependency.

**Cash Flow:**
FY2025 operating cash flow was $641.5M. Under the company's own non-GAAP free cash flow definition — which subtracts capital expenditures ($53.4M), internal-use software ($5.4M), and capitalized software ($172.4M) from operating cash flow — FY2025 free cash flow was $410.3M, representing FCF conversion of 90.0% of net income. H1 FY2026 operating cash flow is $273M (+32% YoY). Management guides FCF conversion of 90-100% of net income on a full-year basis, though H1 FY2026 FCF conversion was lower (55%) due to timing of capitalized software investments and capex. Capitalized software investment of $172M in FY2025 reflects meaningful R&D into the Jack Henry Platform.

**Capital Allocation:**
Management repurchased $125M of stock in H1 FY2026 (795K shares at ~$157 average), a 7.5x acceleration from H1 FY2025 ($17M, 99K shares). Dividends of $2.32/share provide a 1.6% yield.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Catalysts:**
The dominant growth driver is a competitor's core platform consolidation forcing community banks to evaluate alternatives. This produced 22 core wins in Q2 FY2026 (doubled from 11 in the prior-year quarter), with 68% including digital and card processing "trifecta" deals (up from 45%), according to management's earnings call commentary. These are 6-year contracts generating increasing revenue as implementations complete over 12-18 months.

**Digital Banking & Payments:**
The Banno digital banking platform continues adding users. Payments segment growth is driven by card processing volumes, faster payments adoption (Zelle, RTP, FedNow), and the Victor Technologies acquisition ($42.4M, closed Sept 2025) adding Payments-as-a-Service capabilities.

**Tap2Local:**
300 clients live with 100-150 being added monthly. This product helps community banks compete against Stripe/Square for small business deposits — a strategic necessity as fintechs pull SMB relationships away from traditional institutions.

**Jack Henry Platform:**
The multi-year public cloud-native modernization effort has 22 components developed with deposit-only core functionality in testing. This investment creates optionality for a step-function improvement in growth and client acquisition once fully deployed.

**Growth Limitations:**
Industry consolidation reduces total addressable client count by ~3% annually (US banks declined 13% from 2019-2024, credit unions 15% over the same period). JKHY is growing net core clients despite this headwind, but the trend is structural and could accelerate during credit stress periods.

JKHY has zero long-term debt (peers carry significant leverage), delivers consistent 15-25% EPS growth, and has a fortress balance sheet — the quality premium is only partially reflected in the multiple.

GPN trades at 16x (declining margins, integration issues), CPAY at 20.4x (faster growth but leveraged), FIS at 63x (recovering from impairments), and FISV at 9.8x (potential data anomaly). Block trades at 33x with lower profitability.

**Analyst Consensus:**
Consensus analyst targets suggest meaningful upside from current levels, though precise figures vary across data providers and time periods. The stock trades at a substantial discount to most published consensus targets, which is unusually wide for a stable, low-volatility fintech name.

**Buyback Signal:**
Management repurchased at ~$157 average in H1 FY2026. The stock is now approximately 5% below that level. Open-market repurchases do not necessarily prove management views shares as undervalued — the company's authorization has no price targets or timetable — but the 7.5x acceleration in buyback pace represents a meaningful positive signal about management's confidence.

## Competitive Landscape

JKHY operates in an effective oligopoly for community bank/credit union core processing alongside FIS and Fiserv. Barriers to entry are substantial: core banking migrations are 12-18 month projects with significant operational risk, creating deep client stickiness.

The current competitive environment is unusually favorable. A competitor's platform consolidation announcement is forcing clients to evaluate alternatives, and JKHY's Q2 FY2026 core wins (confirmed in the company's earnings press release) demonstrate it is capturing this dislocation. Management has described the service culture advantage as a key differentiator, with decades of relationship investment in community banking.

The company faces competitive pressure from fintechs at the SMB layer (Stripe, Square pulling deposits away from community banks) but is actively responding with Tap2Local. At the enterprise layer, cloud-native challengers like Corelation exist but lack JKHY's scale and breadth of complementary products.

JKHY's competitive position is strengthening: market share gains from competitor disruption, deepening digital banking penetration, and expanding per-client revenue create a trajectory of improving franchise value.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Secular Client Decline:** Banks and credit unions are consolidating at ~3% annually. JKHY is growing net core clients despite this, but the trend is structural and could accelerate during credit stress periods.

**2. Technology Platform Risk:** The dual-track strategy of maintaining existing private-cloud clients while building the Jack Henry Platform requires sustained R&D investment (~$172M annually in capitalized development) without guaranteed adoption timing.

**3. Vendor Concentration:** A $1.02B strategic services agreement (through January 2038) and $450M cloud services contract create meaningful dependency on single providers. Service interruptions or pricing disputes with these vendors could impact operations.

**4. Cybersecurity:** Processing sensitive data for 7,400 financial institutions creates concentrated attack surface risk. The 10-K specifically highlights AI-enabled attacks as an emerging threat.

**5. Contract Renewal Pricing:** Management acknowledges that "certain renewals have resulted in price compression." With 6-year contracts, any pricing deterioration takes years to fully manifest in financials.

**6. Stock Decline Context:** The ~23% decline from the January 2026 high of $193.39 appears driven primarily by: (a) broad market rotation out of defensive/lower-growth names, (b) higher rate expectations reducing multiple on steady growers, and (c) general market volatility. The fundamental trajectory (accelerating wins, expanding margins, raised guidance) contradicts the price action.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($185-$210, +24-41% upside):**
The competitor core consolidation accelerates market share gains over 12-24 months. Core wins sustained at 20+ per quarter convert to revenue as implementations complete (12-18 month lag). Operating margins expand toward 26-27% on volume leverage and cloud scale benefits. EPS reaches $7.50+ in FY2027 and the stock re-rates to 25-28x reflecting accelerating growth profile. Buybacks at accelerated pace reduce share count by 2-3% annually.

**Bear Case ($125-$135, -10-16% downside):**
Growth remains stuck at 6-7% revenue, the competitor consolidation benefit proves transient (one-time switching rather than sustained pipeline), and the market continues to de-rate steady growers in a higher-rate environment. Contract renewal pricing pressure intensifies. Multiple compresses to 18-19x forward EPS. Downside is bounded by the fortress balance sheet, strong FCF generation, and dividend support.

**Base case target:** $185 (approximately 25x FY2027E EPS of $7.00, supported by quality balance sheet and buyback contribution). This target represents a modest re-rating from the current ~22x as the market recognizes accelerating growth and margin expansion.

**Bull case target:** $210 (28x FY2027E EPS of $7.50, assuming sustained 20+ core wins per quarter and margin expansion to 26%+).

**Bear case target:** $130 (18.5x FY2027E EPS of $7.00, representing multiple compression without fundamental deterioration).

**Timeframe:** 12-18 months. Primary catalysts: Q3 FY2026 earnings (~May 2026), Q4 FY2026 earnings (~August 2026), and FY2027 guidance issuance.

**Upside/downside:** +24% to base, +41% to bull, -13% to bear. Approximately 2:1 reward/risk to base case vs bear case.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Core wins decelerate below 12 per quarter for two consecutive quarters (indicating competitor dislocation is fading)
2. FY2027 EPS guidance below $6.80 (indicating margin expansion has stalled)
3. Management halts or materially slows buyback pace (contradicting stated confidence)

## Conclusion

Jack Henry & Associates offers a compelling risk/reward at $149.34. The company is a high-quality, recurring-revenue fintech with zero debt, strong cash flow generation, and an accelerating competitive position, yet trades below its own management's buyback entry price. Four consecutive earnings beats demonstrate that estimates remain conservative. The competitive opportunity from a rival's platform consolidation provides a specific, identifiable catalyst for growth acceleration that the market has not yet fully valued.

The primary risk — that JKHY remains a steady 6-7% organic grower — produces limited downside given balance sheet strength, high FCF generation, and dividend support. The asymmetry of potential outcomes favors the long side at current levels.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** Corrected FCF ($410M vs. previously cited $588M) reduces FCF yield from 5.7% to ~3.9% and increases EV/FCF from ~17.5x to mid-20s, moderating the "cheap quality compounder" valuation argument. The stock is fairly valued rather than deeply undervalued on a cash-flow basis, though the earnings-based and competitive-catalyst arguments remain intact.
