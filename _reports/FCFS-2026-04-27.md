## Executive Summary

FirstCash Holdings reported an exceptionally strong Q1 2026 — revenue of $1.05B (+26% YoY), adjusted EPS of $2.69 (+30%), and same-store pawn receivables accelerating to +19% in the U.S. and +30% in Latin America. Management raised full-year guidance and described April demand as "unprecedented." The H&T U.K. acquisition is delivering a 39% pre-tax operating margin in its first full quarter, well above initial expectations. Consensus estimates have moved sharply higher: current-year EPS now at $10.90, up 8.1% over 90 days.

The investment case rests on three pillars: (1) FirstCash is the dominant global scale player in a fragmented industry where economic stress and elevated inflation directly increase demand for pawn lending; (2) gold price appreciation creates a powerful tailwind that mechanically increases loan sizes, inventory values, and scrap margins; and (3) the AFF payment solutions business is stabilizing after merchant bankruptcies, with margins expanding from 13% to 19%. The stock trades at ~20x FY2026E consensus EPS — a premium to the 13.6x consumer finance peer median, but justified by superior growth, a non-recourse lending model with no traditional credit risk, and a 4-quarter streak of earnings beats averaging 9.1%.

The principal risk is the reflexive gold correlation. With 75% of pawn collateral in gold jewelry and wholesale scrap jewelry revenue nearly doubling to $263M in 2025, a sustained decline in gold prices would compress revenue, margins, and loan values simultaneously. At current prices (~4% below the 52-week high), the stock embeds meaningful optimism about continued gold strength and macro stress. The risk/reward still favors ownership given accelerating fundamentals and rising estimates, but position sizing should reflect the gold concentration.

## Company Overview

FirstCash Holdings operates 3,330+ pawn stores across seven countries, making it the world's largest pawn retailer. The business is organized into four segments: U.S. Pawn (1,207 stores, $1.75B revenue), Latin America Pawn (1,837 stores, $890M revenue), U.K. Pawn (286 stores, $151M revenue in ~4.5 months post-acquisition), and AFF Retail POS Payment Solutions ($870M revenue). Total 2025 revenue was $3.66B.

The pawn model differs fundamentally from traditional consumer lending. Loans are non-recourse, secured by physical collateral (primarily gold jewelry) with significant over-collateralization. If a borrower defaults, collateral transfers to inventory at the loan principal amount and is resold at retail — typically at a substantial markup. The company does not record pawn loan losses. This creates a self-reinforcing cycle where loan growth feeds inventory, which feeds retail sales.

The August 2025 acquisition of H&T Group for £289M ($392M equity + $108M assumed debt) added the U.K.'s leading pawnbroker and opened a new geographic segment. The AFF business provides virtual lease-to-own and consumer finance products through ~16,400 merchant partner locations, serving credit-constrained consumers.

### Cash Flow & Profitability

Operating cash flow was $586M in 2025 (+9% YoY), with adjusted free cash flow of $307M (+17% YoY). Trailing twelve-month OCF through Q1 2026 reached $613M. The FCF-to-net-income conversion ratio exceeds 90%, indicating high earnings quality.

Segment-level margins are strong: U.S. Pawn at 26% pre-tax operating margin, Latin America at 20%, and U.K. at 35-39% (Q1 2026 run rate). AFF margins improved to 19% from 13% as cost synergies and merchant diversification took hold.

### Balance Sheet

Total debt of $2.22B yields a net debt/adjusted EBITDA ratio of ~2.6x (improving from 2.9x post-H&T). The current ratio of 4.55x is robust, reflecting the nature of pawn assets. Cash on hand is $125M with $178M in available credit lines. Interest expense of $121M annually (+15% YoY) is well-covered by OCF of $613M (5.1x coverage).

Goodwill at $2.02B (38% of total assets) reflects the serial acquisition history. This is a structural feature of the consolidation strategy and suppresses accounting-based metrics like the Altman Z-Score. The Piotroski F-Score of 5/5 (all available signals positive) is a more relevant indicator of financial health for this business model.

### Capital Allocation

In 2025, capital deployment totaled approximately $724M: $475M in acquisitions (primarily H&T), $116M in buybacks, $71M in dividends, and $62M in real estate purchases. A new $150M buyback authorization was approved in October 2025 with $100M remaining after Q1 2026 repurchases. The dividend yield of ~0.8% is modest but covered, and the buyback pace is disciplined.

Total shareholder returns (buybacks + dividends) were approximately $187M in 2025. The company returned ~50% of net income to shareholders over the trailing 12 months through dividends and buybacks combined with broader capital deployment.

## Growth Analysis

Three growth vectors support the forward trajectory:

**Organic pawn demand acceleration.** Same-store pawn receivables grew 19% in the U.S. and 30% in Latin America in Q1 2026. Management described April trends as "unprecedented." Elevated inflation and consumer credit tightening directly increase demand for short-term, non-recourse pawn lending. This is a counter-cyclical growth lever.

**Gold price tailwind.** With gold prices up 25%+ over the past year, pawn loan sizes increase mechanically as jewelry collateral is appraised at higher values. Wholesale scrap jewelry revenue nearly doubled from $132M (2024) to $263M (2025). Every 10% move in gold prices has an outsized effect on both pawn fee revenue and retail/wholesale merchandise margins.

**H&T acquisition contribution.** The U.K. segment generated $102M in revenue and $39M in pre-tax income in Q1 2026 (39% margin), significantly above initial expectations. FirstCash opened 3 new U.K. stores in Q1, signaling confidence in the platform's expansion potential. The U.K. pawn market has slightly under 1,000 stores total — with 286, FirstCash is already the dominant player.

Consensus current-year EPS estimates have risen 8.1% over 90 days to $10.90, and next-year estimates are at $12.41 (+6.1% over 90 days). The revision trend is accelerating: 30-day current-year revisions are +3.8%, indicating positive momentum is still building post-Q1 earnings.

## Valuation Assessment

At $219, FCFS trades at:
- **~27.4x trailing EPS** ($7.42 LTM GAAP EPS per Phase 1 data)
- **~20.1x FY2026E consensus** ($10.90)
- **~17.6x FY2027E consensus** ($12.41)

The peer median P/E of 13.6x reflects a group that includes Ally Financial (traditional auto lending, credit losses), SLM (student loans), and Bread Financial (credit card issuer) — businesses with fundamentally different risk profiles. All of these peers carry traditional credit risk and face potential impairment in a recession. FirstCash's non-recourse, physically collateralized lending model means it does not face traditional credit losses and actually benefits from economic stress.

EZCorp (EZPW) at 19.1x is the closest pure comp — also a pawn operator, but smaller, less diversified geographically, and without a payment solutions business. FCFS's premium to EZPW reflects scale, geographic diversification, and the AFF platform.

At 20x forward earnings with EPS growing 25-30% and estimates rising, the valuation is full but defensible. Applying 18-22x to FY2027E EPS of $12.41 yields a range of $223-$273. The stock is trading at the low end of this range, which limits near-term upside but does not suggest overvaluation given the growth trajectory.

Analyst consensus targets cluster at $241 (mean) and $250 (median), with a low of $220 and a high of $252. The current price of $219 offers ~10% upside to the mean target.

## Competitive Landscape

FirstCash's competitive position is improving across three dimensions:

**Scale.** 3,330+ stores globally, with the next largest public competitor (EZCorp) at roughly half the size. Scale advantages compound through technology investment (proprietary POS/loan management systems), procurement efficiencies, and regulatory compliance capabilities that smaller operators cannot replicate.

**Market density.** 490 stores in Texas alone, 1,732 in Mexico. Local density creates brand recognition and convenience advantages. The H&T acquisition gave FirstCash instant dominance in the U.K. market.

**Counter-cyclical demand characteristics.** Pawn lending demand increases when consumer credit tightens. FirstCash is one of the few financial services companies where deteriorating consumer conditions are a net positive for revenue growth.

AFF faces more competition from Snap Finance, Progressive Leasing, and various BNPL providers. However, AFF's margin improvement from 13% to 19% while origination volume stabilized suggests the post-bankruptcy merchant disruption has been absorbed.

## Risk Assessment

### Gold Price Reversal (High Impact, Medium Probability)
This is the single most material risk. Gold jewelry constitutes 75% of pawn collateral and 62% of inventory. A 20% decline in gold prices would compress: (a) pawn loan amounts, (b) wholesale scrap jewelry revenue, (c) inventory valuations, and (d) retail margins. The sensitivity is asymmetric — gold's recent appreciation has been a tailwind that flatters organic growth metrics. Backing out the gold effect, underlying same-store transaction count growth is likely mid-single digits rather than the reported 19-30%.

### Leverage Post-Acquisition
At 2.6x net debt/EBITDA, the balance sheet is adequate but not conservative. A deterioration in operating cash flow (e.g., from gold price decline) combined with existing debt service could constrain capital allocation flexibility.

### AFF Concentration and New Credit Products
The AFF business lost two major furniture merchants to bankruptcy in late 2024. While diversification is progressing, the segment still faces execution risk. The new off-balance-sheet bank loan product ($28.1M outstanding, $13.8M loss liability) introduces traditional credit risk. If this program scales, it could meaningfully alter the company's risk profile.

### Mexico Political & Currency Risk
55% of all stores are in Latin America. Mexico's judicial reform, U.S.-Mexico trade tensions, and currency volatility (5% unfavorable FX impact in 2025) create persistent headwinds that can temporarily mask strong local-currency operating trends.

### Regulatory Risk
The CFPB settlement ($11M in 2025) was modest, but the regulatory environment remains fluid. State attorneys general can enforce consumer finance rules regardless of the CFPB's future. The AFF business is particularly exposed to evolving lease-to-own regulation and interest rate caps.

### Options Market Signal
Near-term ATM IV of 32.9% sits 5.5 percentage points above historical volatility of 27.4%, indicating the options market is pricing elevated uncertainty. This is consistent with the post-earnings gap (stock opened at $215.75 on April 23, hit a high of $227.26, then settled back). The flat term structure (32.9% near vs. 33.2% far) suggests uncertainty is evenly distributed rather than concentrated around a single event. Put/call OI ratio of 0.09 is extremely call-heavy, indicating options positioning for further upside. Total open interest is thin (83 contracts across 3 expirations), insufficient to draw strong institutional positioning conclusions.

Short interest at 3.5% of float with 2.9 days to cover is low, suggesting bears are not crowded against this name.

## Conclusion

FirstCash Holdings is the dominant global pawn operator with accelerating organic growth, a newly accretive U.K. platform, and powerful macro tailwinds from consumer credit distress and gold price appreciation. The valuation at 20x FY2026E and 17.6x FY2027E commands a premium to consumer finance peers, but the premium is earned by a business model that benefits from economic distress, generates high-quality cash flow without traditional credit losses, and holds a dominant competitive position in a fragmented industry. Gold price concentration is the primary risk factor requiring active monitoring. At $219 with a base case target of $245, the risk/reward favors ownership at 2-3% portfolio sizing.

**Recommendation: BUY** with a 12-month base case target of $245 and thesis invalidation below $170 on gold reversal or demand deceleration.
