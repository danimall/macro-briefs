## Executive Summary

Halozyme Therapeutics operates an exceptionally rare business model in biopharma: a capital-light royalty platform generating $645M in free cash flow on $1.4B in revenue (46% FCF margin), with minimal capex requirements. The company's ENHANZE technology powers 10 commercially approved subcutaneous drug formulations across partnerships with 13 major pharmaceutical companies. Revenue grew 38% in FY2025, and management raised 2026 guidance to $1.71-1.81B (+23-30% YoY) with adjusted EBITDA margins approaching 65%.

The stock currently trades at approximately $66, below its 52-week high of $82.22 and roughly 8x FY2026 guided non-GAAP EPS (midpoint $8.00). The company spent $1B+ in Q4 2025 acquiring Hypercon ($810M total consideration) and Surf Bio ($305M + up to $100M milestones) technologies with IP extending to mid-2040s, directly addressing the patent cliff concern.

The investment case centers on three elements: (1) near-term growth acceleration from VYVGART Hytrulo (+444% YoY royalties) and RYBREVANT FASPRO approval, (2) Merck MDASE litigation providing asymmetric upside optionality on a ~$29.5B/year drug, and (3) a valuation disconnect between the stock's single-digit earnings multiple and its structural growth profile. Risks are real but quantifiable: this is a mispriced asset if the patent cliff is navigable rather than terminal.

## Company Overview

Halozyme is the developer and licensor of ENHANZE® drug delivery technology, which uses the proprietary enzyme rHuPH20 to enable subcutaneous delivery of drugs that would otherwise require lengthy IV infusions. The enzyme temporarily degrades hyaluronan in subcutaneous tissue, allowing rapid absorption of large-volume injectable biologics. This converts multi-hour hospital infusions into minutes-long subcutaneous injections.

**Revenue Mix (FY2025):**
- Royalties: $868M (62% of total) — earned on commercial sales of 10 partner products
- Product Sales: $376M (27%) — bulk rHuPH20, Hylenex, XYOSTED, device products
- Collaborative Revenue: $152M (11%) — licenses, milestones

**Key Partners:** Janssen (DARZALEX), argenx (VYVGART Hytrulo), Roche (Phesgo/Herceptin SC), Pfizer, Takeda, AbbVie, Eli Lilly, BMS, ViiV Healthcare, Chugai, Merus, Skye Bioscience.

The business model functions as a toll on the IV-to-SC conversion trend. Partners reformulate their drugs with rHuPH20, and in some cases may seek co-formulation patent filings that, if granted, could provide extended intellectual property protection and competitive differentiation. Halozyme earns royalties on each unit sold with minimal ongoing cost. However, co-formulation patent protection is not automatic — Halozyme's 10-K discloses that two Janssen co-formulation patents for DARZALEX SC were revoked by the European Patent Office Opposition Division in 2023 and 2024, demonstrating that such IP can be challenged.

6. **Merck litigation optionality:** If HALO prevails in MDASE patent dispute, royalties on SC Keytruda (~$29.5B/year drug based on Merck's 2024 reported sales) would be transformational.

### 2026 Guidance Implies Continued Acceleration

Management raised 2026 guidance by ~20% in January 2026 (revenue from $1,430-1,530M to $1,710-1,810M). Royalties expected to exceed $1B "one year earlier than previously projected." Non-GAAP EPS guidance of $7.75-$8.25.

## Valuation Assessment

At approximately $66, HALO trades at:
- ~8x FY2026 guided non-GAAP EPS (midpoint $8.00)
- ~13x trailing FCF (assuming ~$800M+ in FY2026 FCF given guidance)

Even applying a substantial discount for patent cliff risk and leverage, the current single-digit multiple implies the market is pricing ENHANZE royalties as effectively terminal within 3-5 years. This appears overly pessimistic given:
- VYVGART Hytrulo, RYBREVANT FASPRO still in early ramp (royalties growing, not declining)
- Hypercon/Surf Bio acquisitions extend relevance to mid-2040s
- $1B+ in pipeline milestone opportunities

However, the durability of existing royalty streams post-patent expiry remains uncertain. While the company's 10-K indicates it has patent applications relating to individual product candidates and co-formulations that, if granted, may be valid into the 2040s, the specific contractual terms governing whether royalties continue after core rHuPH20 patent expiration have not been publicly disclosed.

A fair multiple of 12-15x forward earnings on $8.00 FY2026 EPS yields a fair value range of $96-120. Even at 10x, fair value is $80 (~21% above current). The discount appears excessive for a 25%+ revenue growth company generating 46% FCF margins.

## Competitive Landscape

### Dominance with Emerging Challengers

Halozyme's ENHANZE platform remains the gold standard with 10 approved products, 13 partners, and 100+ market approvals globally. The regulatory track record and manufacturing reliability create meaningful switching costs for existing partners.

**Alteogen's Hybrozyme (ALT-B4)** is the most credible competitive threat. Halozyme's own 10-K identifies ALT-B4 as a modified human hyaluronidase licensed for SC formulations and as an emerging competitive approach. Reports indicate that Alteogen has secured partnerships with multiple pharma companies, including GSK and Biogen, though the specific deal terms and regulatory status of Alteogen-enabled products could not be independently verified from primary sources.

Several factors may limit near-term impact: (1) Existing ENHANZE partners face multi-year reformulation timelines to switch, (2) Halozyme has a 15+ year track record of commercial-scale manufacturing reliability that new entrants must replicate.

The Merck situation is different and more concerning for long-term IP defensibility: Merck appears to be developing SC Keytruda using a modified hyaluronidase without an ENHANZE license, relying on what Halozyme alleges is infringing MDASE™ technology.

### Competitive Position Trajectory

The competitive position is deteriorating from a monopoly toward an oligopoly. But ENHANZE's installed base (existing commercial products, manufacturing infrastructure, partner relationships) creates a large incumbency advantage. The acquisitions of Hypercon and Surf Bio represent offensive moves to maintain differentiation against next-generation competitors.

## Risk Assessment

### 1. Patent Cliff (High Impact, Medium Probability)
Core rHuPH20 U.S. patents expire 2027, with European extension to 2029. MDASE patents extend to 2032-2034. Hypercon/Surf Bio extend to mid-2040s. The central uncertainty is whether existing ENHANZE royalty streams continue post-expiry and whether competitors can replicate rHuPH20 manufacturing at commercial scale. The specific contractual terms governing royalty continuation after patent expiry have not been publicly disclosed, making this the primary source of uncertainty and the key reason for the valuation discount.

### 2. Alteogen Competition (Medium Impact, High Probability)
Alteogen is likely to win incremental deals, particularly with pharma companies that don't already have ENHANZE partnerships. Impact on existing royalty streams is lower (switching costs) but impact on new collaboration economics could be significant (pricing pressure).

### 3. Merck Litigation — Binary Outcome (High Impact, Uncertain Probability)
German preliminary injunction favors Halozyme. However, PTAB patent validity challenge could go either way. If MDASE patents are invalidated, it removes a key differentiator and signals broader IP vulnerability. If Halozyme prevails, damages + ongoing royalties on the ~$29.5B Keytruda franchise would be material.

### 4. Convertible Note Dilution
The $470M 2028 notes at $56.02 conversion price are currently in-the-money. Full conversion would add ~8.4M shares (~7% dilution). Capped calls offset dilution up to ~$137/share on the 2031/2032 notes.

### 5. Partner Concentration
DARZALEX represents approximately 56% of royalty revenue ($483M of $868M) and about 35% of total company revenue, creating meaningful single-product dependency. Any competitive displacement of daratumumab in multiple myeloma (from bispecifics, CAR-T) directly impacts HALO.

### 6. Options Market Signal

Market-based sentiment indicators suggest elevated near-term uncertainty, with implied volatility appearing significantly above historical levels. Short interest has been elevated, confirming institutional skepticism, though this also creates potential short-squeeze fuel on positive catalysts. These market-based indicators are highly time-sensitive and should be verified against current data before acting.

- RYBREVANT FASPRO exceeds expectations in first-line NSCLC

## Conclusion

Halozyme combines a platform business with 25%+ revenue growth, 46% FCF margins, and a single-digit earnings multiple. The market is pricing the ENHANZE patent cliff as near-terminal, while the company has taken concrete steps (Hypercon, Surf Bio, MDASE defense) to extend relevance beyond 2029. The royalty model generates exceptional cash that funds both buybacks and portfolio evolution.

The ~8x forward P/E is appropriate for a declining asset but inappropriate for a company guiding to 23-30% revenue growth in 2026. If growth delivery continues and the Alteogen threat remains manageable, the multiple should expand toward 10-12x. The Merck litigation provides asymmetric upside optionality that is currently assigned near-zero value by the market.

DARZALEX concentration at approximately 56% of royalty revenue remains a key risk, and the uncertainty around post-patent royalty durability, absent disclosed contractual continuation provisions, is the most important unresolved question for long-term holders.

**Recommendation: BUY at ~$66 with a 12-month base case target of $84.** The risk/reward ratio of approximately 1.3:1 (base case upside vs. bear case downside) is acceptable given the FCF generation that provides a floor under the stock, the multiple identifiable near-term growth catalysts, and the asymmetric Merck optionality.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** Post-patent royalty durability — the report's most critical thesis pillar — relies on assumed contractual continuation provisions that have not been verified from public disclosures; DARZALEX concentration is ~56% of royalty revenue (not 35% as originally framed), increasing single-product risk. These factors may warrant a more conservative target multiple.
