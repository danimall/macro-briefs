**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Kennametal is in the middle of a commodity-driven earnings supercycle. China's tightening tungsten export controls — affecting ~80% of global supply — have created a pricing windfall for KMT's vertically integrated operations, driving FY2026 guided EPS to $3.75-$4.00 versus $1.20 in FY2025. Q3 FY2026 revenue grew 22% YoY with Infrastructure segment operating margins hitting 18.1%, roughly double the prior year. The stock has appreciated ~70% over 12 months from the low $20s to ~$35.

The central investment question is whether this represents a structural re-rating or a cyclical peak. Evidence leans toward the latter: the company's $64M nine-month favorable pricing spread versus raw materials is a transient LIFO benefit that normalizes as higher-cost inventory turns; insiders sold $2.5M+ at $36-$40 in February-March 2026; and the company's 5-year TSR of -8% versus peers at +167% reflects a business that has historically struggled to compound value outside commodity spikes. At ~9x FY2026 guided EPS, the stock prices in strong near-term performance but offers limited margin of safety if tungsten spreads compress in FY2027.

## Company Overview

Kennametal is a Pittsburgh-based industrial materials company with 85+ years of expertise in tungsten carbide, ceramics, and super-hard materials. The business operates through two segments: Metal Cutting (62% of FY2025 revenue, $1.22B) serving machining applications across general engineering, transportation, and aerospace; and Infrastructure (38%, $747M) providing earthworks, energy, and defense applications including tungsten penetrators and armor solutions. The company employs 8,124 people globally, with 60% of sales generated internationally.

KMT's distinctive competitive asset is its vertically integrated tungsten processing — from ore and scrap recycling through to finished products. This vertical integration positions the company as both a supplier and competitor to hundreds of smaller fabricators who depend on cemented tungsten carbide. The company also supplies the U.S. defense industrial base, making it strategically relevant for Western critical minerals reshoring.

The driver is almost entirely the tungsten pricing spread in Infrastructure, which reported $39M of favorable timing between pricing increases and raw material costs in Q3 alone ($64M for nine months). Infrastructure operating margin hit 18.1% in Q3, up from 10.7% a year prior.

### Cash Flow Concerns

Despite tripled earnings, operating cash flow for 9M FY2026 was only $69.7M versus $129.7M in the prior period. The culprit: a $216M inventory build as rising tungsten prices inflate working capital. Inventories grew from $538M to $747M. This is mechanically logical — higher unit costs flow through inventory before being recovered in COGS — but it means the earnings quality is temporarily poor. The LIFO reserve increased $54M (from $106M to $160M), confirming inflationary accounting effects.

The balance sheet is adequate but not fortress-like. Investment-grade ratings provide flexibility, and the recently extended credit facility removes near-term refinancing risk.

### Estimate Revisions

Current quarter estimates revised +143% over 30 days and +190% over 90 days. Next quarter: +145% and +218%. Current year: +60% and +72%. These are among the most extreme upward revisions observable in an industrial name and would normally constitute a strong BUY signal per earnings momentum frameworks.

However, the critical qualifier: these revisions are driven by a commodity windfall, not by structural business improvement (market share gains, new products, secular demand shift). The durability question dominates the directional signal.

### Durability Assessment

Stripping out the tungsten pricing spread, KMT's underlying organic volume growth is likely in the 5-8% range — decent for an industrial, but not transformational. The defense/critical minerals angle provides a genuine structural argument (Western reshoring of tungsten supply), but the magnitude of the current earnings benefit (~$85M annualized favorable spreads) is overwhelmingly driven by the commodity, not by structural demand.

The valuation is not stretched, but it offers limited asymmetry. The bull case ($47.50) requires sustained tungsten spreads and potential defense premium re-rating. The bear case ($22-25) is a reversion to historical earnings power if tungsten normalizes and the cyclical downturn resumes.

## Competitive Landscape

KMT's competitive position has temporarily strengthened through the tungsten cycle:

1. **Vertical integration advantage:** Competitors dependent on Chinese tungsten supply face disruption; KMT's recycling capacity and non-Chinese sourcing relationships are differentiating.
2. **"Secure source" positioning:** Western defense primes and industrial customers increasingly value supply chain security. KMT makes tungsten penetrators and armor — direct defense relevance.
3. **Dual role as supplier/competitor:** KMT sells cemented tungsten carbide to smaller fabricators. In a supply-constrained environment, these customers become more dependent on KMT.

Against global peers (Sandvik, IMC/Berkshire, Sumitomo, Mitsubishi Materials), KMT is smaller but uniquely positioned as a Western-hemisphere integrated producer. Long-term competitive trajectory depends on whether the company can convert this cyclical advantage into permanent market share gains.

The concerning counter-signal: the market has persistently valued KMT as an underperformer, and one commodity cycle does not necessarily change that assessment.

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

1. **Tungsten spread reversion (HIGH probability, HIGH impact):** The ~$85M+ annualized windfall normalizes as inventories turn over at higher costs. Timing uncertain (2-4 quarters), but direction is near-certain. Could reduce EPS by $0.75-$1.25 from peak levels.

2. **MachiningCloud litigation ($330M claim, MEDIUM probability, HIGH impact):** A claim representing ~12% of market cap. No outcome determinable yet, but an adverse judgment would be catastrophic. Even a settlement in the $50-100M range would represent 2-4% of market cap.

3. **China supply disruption (MEDIUM probability, MIXED impact):** Further Chinese restrictions could benefit pricing but constrain KMT's own raw material access. Net positive for now, but tail risk of outright export embargo exists.

4. **Global industrial recession (MEDIUM probability, HIGH impact):** Current stagflationary environment with rising rates, negative real wages globally, and consumer spending compression could cascade to industrial demand. General Engineering and Transportation (58% of Metal Cutting revenue) are highly cyclical.

5. **Tariff policy reversal:** U.S. Supreme Court invalidated certain IEEPA tariffs in February 2026. If KMT's tariff surcharges must be reversed, this creates pricing headwinds.

Put/call skew at 6% indicates modest elevated put demand but nothing extreme. The options market is not pricing significant directional conviction in either direction.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($42-47)
- Tungsten supply constraints prove structural — China's export controls are permanent policy
- Defense/critical minerals re-rating: KMT receives premium as Western governments designate tungsten as strategic
- Restructuring benefits ($35M annualized) compound with volume recovery
- FY2027 EPS sustains at $3.50+ as pricing proves sticky even after spread normalization
- Multiple re-rates from 9x to 12-13x on perceived durability = $42-$47

### Bear Case ($22-26)
- Tungsten prices stabilize or decline as non-Chinese supply responds and demand normalizes
- LIFO reserve unwinds, creating earnings headwinds in FY2027
- Industrial recession reduces volumes 5-10%
- MachiningCloud settlement creates cash drain and management distraction
- EPS normalizes to $1.50-$2.00; stock reverts to historical 15-16x normalized P/E = $22-$32

### Base Case ($35-39)
- Tungsten spreads partially normalize but pricing remains above historical averages
- FY2027 EPS of $2.75-$3.25 on modest volume growth + retained pricing + restructuring savings
- Multiple of 11-12x normalized = $30-$39, supporting current price range
- Dividend (2.2%) provides floor

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Stock declines below $28 on unchanged estimates (creating 10x forward earnings on normalized $2.75+)
- Evidence of permanent market share gains from tungsten disruption (measured by organic volume growth >10% sustained for 3+ quarters)
- Defense contract wins explicitly tied to "secure tungsten source" positioning (providing secular demand floor)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- FY2027 guidance below $2.50 EPS (indicating spread normalization worse than expected)
- MachiningCloud settlement or adverse judgment exceeding $100M
- Insider selling accelerates at current prices (would signal those closest to the business expect reversion)

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (expected August 2026 for Q4 FY2026/full year results). This will reveal whether the company signals FY2027 earnings normalization or maintains elevated guidance.

## Conclusion

Kennametal is experiencing a genuine commodity-driven earnings supercycle, with tungsten supply constraints creating extraordinary short-term profitability. The stock's 70% appreciation from its lows reflects this, and guided EPS of $3.75-$4.00 versus trailing $1.20 is dramatic. However, the weight of evidence suggests this is primarily a transient commodity windfall rather than a permanent re-rating: insiders sold into the spike, the pricing-versus-cost spread normalizes mechanically as inventories turn, and free cash flow has deteriorated even as earnings soar.

At ~9x peak earnings or ~12x likely normalized FY2027 earnings, the valuation is fair but does not offer sufficient asymmetry to warrant a BUY — the upside to $42-47 requires sustained commodity dynamics that are inherently uncertain, while the downside to $22-26 on normalization represents meaningful risk. The stock belongs in a monitoring bucket, with a catalyst-driven upgrade possible if price compresses or if structural demand evidence (defense contracts, market share data) accumulates.

**Recommendation: HOLD** — insufficient asymmetry at current price; the commodity windfall is priced, and durability remains unproven.
