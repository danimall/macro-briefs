## Executive Summary

Autoliv is the world's largest passive automotive safety supplier with ~44% global market share, operating in an effective oligopoly alongside Joyson Safety Systems and ZF LIFETEC. The company has executed a meaningful margin recovery from 6.6% operating margin in 2023 to 10.1% in 2025, driven by structural cost reductions (8% annual productivity improvement achieved), headcount optimization (~2,000 indirect positions removed), and pricing discipline. Management guides 10.5–11.0% adjusted operating margin for 2026 with $1.2B in operating cash flow.

The Q1 2026 results (April 17) demonstrated strong execution: revenue of $2.75B beat consensus by ~$140M, adjusted EPS of $2.05 beat by $0.13–$0.28, and full-year guidance was reiterated. However, the stock subsequently pulled back from $125 to $117, reflecting broader concerns about tariff uncertainty and weakening global auto production volumes. Consensus EPS estimates for FY2026 have declined 4.8% over the past 90 days, indicating negative earnings momentum despite the Q1 beat.

At 12.6x forward earnings with a 3.0% dividend yield and a $2.5B buyback authorization representing ~27% of market cap, valuation is attractive relative to peers (median P/E 16.6x). The investment case hinges on whether margin expansion and capital returns can offset a challenging volume environment and unresolved tariff risks. We rate ALV a HOLD with a positive growth lean, acknowledging that the stock offers favorable risk/reward but fails to meet the stringent conditions required for a Consumer Discretionary BUY given declining estimate revisions and deteriorating consumer confidence.

## Company Overview

Autoliv supplies passive safety systems (airbags, seatbelts, steering wheels) to virtually every major global OEM. Revenue splits approximately 67% airbags/steering wheels and 33% seatbelt products. Geographic exposure is well-diversified: Americas (32%), Europe (29%), China (19%), and Asia ex-China (20%).

The company holds over 6,600 patents and supplies products to more than 1,400 vehicle models across ~100 car brands. Top customers include Stellantis (9.8%), Toyota (9.6%), VW (9.0%), Honda (8.3%), and Hyundai (7.0%), with the top 5 representing 44% of sales.

Content per vehicle (CPV) averages ~$268 globally, ranging from ~$350 in North America to ~$140 in India. Regulatory tailwinds (Euro NCAP upgrades in 2026, China NCAP overhaul 2027-2030, India Bharat NCAP upgrade 2027) support long-term CPV growth, particularly in emerging markets where penetration of advanced safety features remains low.

## Growth Analysis

Revenue growth is modest — management guides ~0% organic growth for FY2026 assuming a ~1% decline in global light vehicle production. The growth story is primarily margin-driven and capital-return-driven rather than top-line-driven.

**Positive structural drivers:**
- India growing from 2% to 5% of sales (2020–2025), with Autoliv as "clear market leader" and regulatory mandates driving CPV expansion
- Chinese domestic OEMs now 44% of China sales (up from 22% in 2022); sales to Chinese OEMs grew 23% in 2025
- Regulatory upgrades across all major markets (Euro NCAP 2026, China NCAP 2027-2030, India Bharat NCAP 2027) driving content growth
- 8% annual productivity improvement creating operating leverage even on flat volumes

**Growth headwinds:**
- Global LVP expected to decline ~1% in 2026
- CPV mix dilution as growth shifts to lower-CPV regions (India $140 vs. North America $350)
- BYD's FinDreams subsidiary vertically integrating passive safety, limiting content growth with the largest Chinese OEM
- OEM sourcing delays: order intake flat at $7.7B lifetime value — same depressed level as 2024

**Estimate revision trajectory is concerning:** FY2026 EPS consensus has fallen from $11.01 (90 days ago) to $10.48 today — a 4.8% decline. FY2027 estimates similarly declined 4.2%. This reflects analyst concerns about tariff pass-through sustainability and volume uncertainty rather than operational deterioration.

## Valuation Assessment

At $117.44 with consensus FY2026 EPS of $10.48, ALV trades at 11.2x forward earnings (data shows 12.6x at the $121.25 level). Either way, this represents a meaningful discount to the peer median of 16.6x. The 3.0% dividend yield exceeds the peer median of 1.3%.

**Valuation context:**
- Forward P/E of 11-13x on a company generating 28.6% ROE with 10%+ operating margins and a dominant market position is objectively cheap
- The peer group median of 16.6x includes Borgwarner (42.4x — distorted), Lear (15.2x), and Gentex (12.9x)
- Applying a modest 14x multiple to FY2026E EPS of $10.48 yields $147; at 12x (bear case) yields $126
- FCF yield of ~7.9% exceeds the 4% threshold substantially
- The $2.5B buyback program mechanically supports the stock: at current prices, the company can retire ~4-5% of shares annually

**Why is the market discounting?** Three factors: (1) tariff uncertainty creating binary risk to margins if recovery rates deteriorate; (2) near-zero organic volume growth; (3) the CFO transition (now resolved). The Q1 beat and guidance reiteration suggest factors 1 and 3 are more manageable than feared, but the market requires further confirmation.

**Analyst targets:** Low $110, median $134, high $150. Current price sits 13% below median target.

## Competitive Landscape

Autoliv's competitive position is among the strongest in the auto parts universe. The passive safety market is a mature oligopoly: Autoliv (~44% share), Joyson (~25%), ZF LIFETEC (~15%), with regional competitors filling the remainder.

**Moat characteristics:**
- Scale advantages in R&D amortization, testing infrastructure, and global manufacturing
- Quality track record: involved in only ~3% of industry recalls despite 44% market share — critical for OEM confidence
- 6,600+ patent portfolio
- Deep customer relationships across both legacy OEMs and emerging Chinese/Indian OEMs
- Regulatory complexity creates barriers to entry: NHTSA, Euro NCAP, China NCAP certification requirements

**Competitive threats:**
- BYD's FinDreams subsidiary represents genuine vertical integration risk, though limited to one OEM currently
- Chinese regional suppliers (e.g., Jinheng Automotive Safety) expanding capabilities
- Potential long-term disruption from autonomous driving reducing passive safety content (very long-dated — safety systems likely required regardless of autonomy level for decades)

**Market share trajectory:** Autoliv consistently outperformed global LVP by 4pp+ in Q1 2026, suggesting share gains or favorable mix. The company won 30% of 2025 order intake from Chinese OEMs, demonstrating successful pivot to growth markets.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Tariff Escalation (HIGH):** The 2026 guidance explicitly assumes "no material changes to tariffs or trade restrictions" vs. January 2026 levels. A scenario where recovery rates deteriorate from >80% to 60-70% could compress margins by 50-100bps.

**2. Volume Cyclicality (MEDIUM):** In a recession scenario, production declines of 5-10% are plausible, which would overwhelm margin expansion and cost reduction efforts.

**3. ARC Inflator Investigation (MEDIUM):** NHTSA investigation could result in recall orders. The 10-K states this "could have a material impact on our business." Binary risk with limited visibility on timing or magnitude.

**4. Mexican Tax Dispute (LOW-MEDIUM):** Early-stage dispute with potential for "materially negative impact." Insufficient detail to quantify.

**5. Customer Concentration (LOW-MEDIUM):** Stellantis (9.8% of sales) faces its own operational challenges. A significant Stellantis production cut would create meaningful headwinds.

## Consumer Discretionary BUY Gate Assessment

The calibration system requires four conditions for a Consumer Discretionary BUY:

**(a) Positive sequential comparable revenue growth:** Q1 2026 revenue grew 6.8% YoY and organic growth was +0.8%, outperforming LVP by >4pp. However, this is YoY, not sequential (Q4 2025 data unavailable for direct sequential comparison). Full-year 2026 guidance is ~0% organic growth. **Partially met.**

**(b) FCF yield ≥ 4%:** At $716M FCF / $9.09B market cap = 7.9%. **Met.**

**(c) Consumer confidence not in declining trend:** Consumer confidence indicators in clear declining trend given stagflationary conditions. **NOT met.** Autoliv sells to OEMs rather than directly to consumers, creating a lag, but ultimately depends on consumer demand for vehicles.

**(d) Positive estimate revisions over trailing 90 days:** FY2026 EPS consensus declined 4.8% over 90 days; FY2027 declined 4.2%. **NOT met.**

Two of four conditions fail. The falling estimate revision trend and deteriorating consumer confidence environment prevent meeting the BUY gate requirements. The stock remains attractive on fundamentals but the timing signal is not present.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. FY2026 EPS estimate revisions stabilize or turn positive (30-day revision >0%) — would indicate tariff/volume concerns receding
2. Consumer confidence readings stabilize or management raises full-year guidance, confirming demand resilience
3. Stock pulls back to $105-110 range (≤10x forward) providing sufficient margin of safety to offset macro uncertainty

**Downgrade triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Q2 2026 adjusted operating margin <8.5%, indicating tariff recovery deterioration
2. Management cuts full-year 2026 guidance below 10% operating margin
3. NHTSA issues ARC inflator recall order with quantified liability exceeding $200M

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (likely July 2026 for Q2 results). Secondary checkpoint: any tariff policy changes affecting auto parts industry.

## Conclusion

Autoliv represents a high-quality business at a reasonable valuation: 44% global market share, 28.6% ROE, 7.9% FCF yield, and aggressive capital return. The margin expansion trajectory from 6.6% (2023) to guided 10.5-11.0% (2026) is structurally supported by productivity gains and cost reductions. The Q1 2026 beat confirms execution quality.

However, the macro environment creates genuine uncertainty: declining consumer confidence, tariff escalation risk, near-zero volume growth guidance, and falling consensus estimates (-4.8% over 90 days) signal that timing is premature for a high-conviction BUY. The stock's 24% appreciation from its May 2025 low to current levels reflects much of the positive re-rating from margin improvement already.

At 11-13x forward earnings with a 3.0% yield and 7.9% FCF yield, downside appears limited (bear case -15-19%) while upside is meaningful (bull case +19-28%). We maintain HOLD with a positive growth lean, positioning for an upgrade if estimates stabilize or the stock revisits the $105-110 range. Base case fair value is $134-140 (13-14x FY2026E EPS of $10.48), representing 14-19% upside from current levels over 12 months, requiring improved macro signals to act upon.
