## Executive Summary

Cboe Global Markets has executed a strong operational transformation under new CEO Craig Donohue. Q1 2026 delivered record net revenue of $729M (+29% YoY) and adjusted EPS of $3.70 (+48% YoY), beating consensus by 10%. The May 2026 announcement of a ~20% workforce reduction (~330 employees) targeting approximately $40-50M in annualized savings from additional realignment actions cements the cost re-basing thesis.

Fundamental quality is high: Q1 2026 adjusted operating margins of 72.4%, approximately $1.15-1.68B in annual FCF (depending on clearing-related working capital movements), A-/A2 credit ratings, and a strong competitive moat in proprietary index options (SPX, VIX) backed by exclusive licenses through 2032/2033. However, at approximately 27x trailing earnings and with analyst consensus targets near the current price, the stock has largely re-rated to reflect the restructuring benefits. The specific risk that prevents upgrading to BUY: structural license renewal risk (S&P 500 exclusivity expires 2032/2033) and the potential normalization of the elevated volatility regime that currently drives record options volumes leave limited margin of safety at current levels.

## Company Overview

Cboe operates the largest U.S. options exchange and the third-largest U.S. equities exchange network. Five segments generate revenue: Options (63% of net revenue), North American Equities (17%), Europe & Asia Pacific (11%), Futures (5%), and Global FX (4%). The company holds exclusive U.S. listing rights for S&P 500 Index options through December 2032 and owns the proprietary VIX methodology — two assets that constitute a highly differentiated franchise in derivatives markets.

Under Donohue (appointed May 2025, former CME Group CEO), the company is unwinding a multi-year period of unfocused global expansion. Divestitures of Cboe Japan, Australia, Canada, CEDX, and U.S./European corporate listings are eliminating drag from value-destructive acquisitions that generated $590M+ in cumulative impairments between 2022-2025.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement Acceleration:**
FY2025 net revenue grew 17% to $2.43B while operating expenses declined 1%, producing 34% operating income growth and 45% EPS growth ($10.42 vs. $7.21). This operating leverage profile is structural: incremental exchange volume flows to the bottom line with near-zero marginal cost.

Q1 2026 extended this trajectory — net revenue of $729M implies a $2.9B+ run-rate (20% above full-year 2025). Q1 2026 adjusted operating margin reached 72.4%, up from the FY2025 level of 65.6%, with further room to expand as restructuring savings flow through in H2 2026.

**Cash Generation:**
Reported FCF of $1.68B (operating cash flow of $1.75B less $71M capex) represents a 35.7% margin on total revenue and covers the $284M dividend 5.9x. However, 2025 operating cash flow benefited from approximately $529M in positive changes from margin deposits, clearing funds, and customer bank deposits — non-recurring collateral movements that inflate the headline figure. Excluding these clearing-related flows, underlying FCF was closer to $1.15B, still robust and covering the dividend approximately 4x. Cash on hand grew to $2.22B against $1.45B in long-term debt (net cash of approximately $770M as of year-end 2025; $691M as of Q1 2026). All three debt tranches carry investment-grade coupons (1.625%-3.65%) with maturities staggered through 2032. There is no refinancing stress.

**Volume Metrics (2025 vs. 2024):**
- Total options ADV: 60.8M contracts (+25%)
- Index options ADV: 4.9M (+21%)
- U.S. equities: 1.8B shares/day (+26%)
- European equities ADNV: €12.8B (+31%)

The only soft spot is Futures ADV, which declined 5%, notable given VIX futures are a flagship product.

**Earnings Surprise Pattern:** 3 beats in 4 quarters, with the most recent beat at +10.3%. The single miss (Q2 2025, -0.4%) was trivial. Consensus has systematically underestimated this business during its restructuring phase.

## Growth Analysis

**Estimate Revisions:** Current quarter estimates have risen 20.9% over 90 days and 14.5% over 30 days. Full-year 2026 estimates moved from $11.79 to $13.65 (+15.7% in 90 days). These are meaningful upward revisions but fall below the +30% threshold that would signal a true earnings inflection demanding immediate action.

**Secular Growth Vectors:**
1. **0-DTE options expansion:** Retail and institutional adoption of same-day expiry options continues driving record volumes. Cboe pioneered this with SPX Tuesday/Thursday expirations.
2. **Data Vantage recurring revenue:** Approximately 26% of net revenue growing at ~10% annually. Segment-level margins are not separately disclosed, but the revenue stream is predominantly data licensing and connectivity fees with inherently high incremental margins.
3. **International derivatives:** Extended trading hours (planned July 2026) capture demand outside U.S. hours.
4. **Clearing expansion:** Cboe Clear Europe grew share from 37.4% to 39.4%; SFT clearing represents a new frontier.

**Growth Ceiling Concerns:**
- U.S. equities segment is commoditizing: net capture per 100 shares fell 32% in 2025, market share declined from 11.4% to 10.0%.
- Futures segment weakness (-5% ADV) suggests the VIX ecosystem faces some saturation in its current form.
- The volume boom correlates strongly with the current elevated-volatility macro regime. Normalization of geopolitical tensions or VIX contraction would compress volumes.

## Valuation Assessment

At approximately $282, CBOE trades at:
- ~27.0x trailing EPS ($10.42)
- ~20.7x FY26E EPS ($13.65)
- ~19.6x FY27E EPS ($14.36)
- ~17.5x EV/EBITDA on ~$1.65B adjusted EBITDA (based on enterprise value of approximately $28.8B)

Against peers: NDAQ at 27.9x, MCO at 31.8x, MSCI at 33.4x. CBOE's ~27x trailing P/E sits below the peer median on a trailing basis, and its forward multiple of ~20-21x represents a discount to the high-quality exchange/data cohort.

The critical valuation question is whether CBOE deserves a premium or discount to this cohort. The bull case for premium: exclusive product moats, restructuring-driven margin expansion still playing out, 15%+ EPS growth. The bear case: license renewal risk creates a terminal value discount that other exchanges don't carry, and the current volume regime may represent peak cyclical conditions.

Analyst consensus target estimates are near or slightly above the current price, suggesting limited near-term upside in consensus views. The distribution is 2 Strong Buy / 1 Buy / 10 Hold / 2 Sell / 2 Strong Sell, which is cautious for a stock that has appreciated approximately 25-26% in 12 months.

At ~21x forward earnings, the stock prices in the restructuring savings and mid-teens growth trajectory. Further upside requires: (a) volumes accelerating beyond current levels, (b) savings exceeding expectations, or (c) the company deploying its substantial cash balance into accretive M&A or aggressive buybacks.

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat Assessment:**
CBOE's competitive position is bifurcated. In proprietary products (SPX options, VIX futures), the moat is strong — no competitor can currently list these products under exclusive license arrangements extending through 2032/2033. However, the 10-K explicitly warns that index owners may not renew licenses exclusively or at all, and that loss of proprietary VIX rights could materially harm the business. In multi-listed options and equities, the business is intensely competitive with 14+ rival options exchanges and 13+ equities exchanges, and margins are compressing.

The strategic pivot under Donohue correctly focuses investment on the proprietary assets (index derivatives, data) while rationalizing commodity businesses (equities, international subscale operations). Revenue mix is shifting toward higher-margin proprietary products: derivatives grew to 55% of net revenue in 2025 vs. 53% in 2024.

**Competitive Position Trajectory:** Improving. The portfolio rationalization removes drag and concentrates resources on differentiated assets. Data Vantage pricing power is increasing. The clearing expansion in Europe diversifies beyond U.S. equities pricing pressure.

## Risk Assessment

**1. License Renewal (2032/2033) — Structural Terminal Value Risk:**
S&P Dow Jones Indices could theoretically not renew exclusivity, license to multiple exchanges, or demand substantially higher royalty rates. This is explicitly flagged in the 10-K and represents the single most material long-term risk. The risk is not imminent but is embedded in any fair value estimate beyond 7 years.

**2. Volume Normalization:**
The current geopolitical regime (oil crisis, rate uncertainty, equity vol elevated) drives record options volumes. If vol normalizes — SPY annualized HV was 12.1% as recently as early 2025 — options revenue could contract 15-25% from current run-rates.

**3. Cboe Clear Europe Credit Facility (June 26, 2026):**
The €1.2B facility expiring imminently is a near-term watchpoint. The 10-K explicitly states the company may not be able to replace it on commercially reasonable terms. Non-renewal or adverse terms could disrupt European clearing operations.

**4. Executive Transition Risk:**
Near-complete C-suite overhaul within 12 months. Donohue's pedigree is strong (CME, OCC) but execution risk on simultaneous divestitures + restructuring + culture change is real.

**5. Tick Size/Access Fee Rule (Nov 2026):**
SEC implementation could compress equities revenue further and increase compliance costs, though the equities segment is already a declining portion of the business.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$330-350, 17-24% upside):**
- Restructuring savings exceed expectations as additional cost efficiencies are identified
- Sustained elevated vol regime drives options ADV growth of 15%+ through 2027
- Cash deployment into accretive acquisition or aggressive buyback (remaining authorization of ~$569M as of Q1 2026, plus substantial balance sheet capacity)
- FY27 EPS reaches $15.50+ on combination of volume + savings + buyback; 23x multiple = $357

**Bear Case (~$220-240, 15-22% downside):**
- Volume normalization as geopolitical tensions resolve; options ADV reverts toward 2024 levels
- Equities segment deterioration accelerates with new regulatory rules
- Clearing facility renewal on adverse terms
- Multiple contracts to 16-17x on growth deceleration; 17x * $13 = $221

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Price pulls back to $240-255 range (17-18x forward FY27 EPS), creating asymmetric risk/reward
2. FY26 estimate revisions accelerate to +25%+ over 90 days, indicating market still underestimates the earnings trajectory
3. Announcement of material share repurchase acceleration or special dividend utilizing the substantial cash balance

**Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Options ADV declines for 2+ consecutive months below 50M contracts, signaling volume normalization
2. S&P licenses SPX options to a competing exchange (unlikely before 2032 but would be devastating)
3. Cboe Clear Europe credit facility renewal fails or contains materially adverse terms

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings (Q2 2026, likely August 2026). Monitor monthly volume disclosures for early signs of normalization.

## Conclusion

Cboe Global Markets is an exceptional business undergoing a well-executed transformation: proprietary products generating 72%+ margins (Q1 2026), record volumes, accelerating earnings, and a new CEO driving cost efficiencies through restructuring. The fundamental story merits a BUY at the right price.

At approximately $282, the stock trades at ~27x trailing and ~21x forward earnings, with analyst consensus targets near the current price. While valuation is not extreme relative to high-quality exchange peers, the license renewal risk (2032/2033) creates a structural overhang that warrants a discount, and the current volume regime may represent cyclically strong conditions rather than a permanent step-up. The estimate revision velocity of +15-21% over 90 days, while positive, has not reached the +30% threshold that historically justifies buying at fair-value multiples.

The position is a Growth HOLD: the thesis is positive, the catalysts are real, and the business quality supports appreciation over time. At current levels, reward:risk is approximately balanced — adequate for existing holders to maintain exposure but insufficient asymmetry for a new position without a pullback or further earnings acceleration.
