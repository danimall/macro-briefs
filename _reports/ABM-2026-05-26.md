## Executive Summary

ABM Industries is a $2.3B market cap provider of integrated facility solutions generating $8.75B in annual revenue across janitorial, engineering, parking, and technical services. The stock trades at approximately 10x forward FY2026 EPS guidance of $3.85-$4.15, a discount to the peer median and to its own historical range, following a 25% decline from its 52-week high of $52.94.

The decline is fundamentally justified by four consecutive quarterly earnings misses (cumulative miss rate of -10.3% on average), intentional margin compression from "strategic pricing" decisions, and ongoing ERP system disruption. However, the data center and semiconductor fabrication services positioning (WGNSTAR acquisition, Quality Uptime, microgrid capabilities) provides a credible re-rating catalyst if execution improves. At current levels, the stock offers modest downside protection via a 2.9% dividend yield and active buyback program ($183M remaining authorization), but the earnings trajectory must demonstrate stability before the valuation discount can close.

ABM is a low-margin (1.9% net), labor-intensive business intentionally sacrificing profitability to win accounts, with four straight earnings misses and insider selling, yet it trades cheaply relative to the secular growth opportunity in data center/chip fab services. Resolution requires 1-2 quarters of earnings delivery above consensus.

## Company Overview

ABM Industries, founded in 1909, operates five segments serving commercial, industrial, and institutional clients:

- **Business & Industry (B&I):** $4.13B (47% of revenue, 7.7% operating margin) — commercial offices, sports venues, healthcare, parking
- **Manufacturing & Distribution (M&D):** $1.62B (19%, 9.4% operating margin) — factories, warehouses, data centers
- **Aviation:** $1.12B (13%, 5.8% operating margin) — airport/airline support
- **Education:** $922M (11%, 7.3% operating margin) — K-12 and universities
- **Technical Solutions:** $961M (11%, 9.0% operating margin) — microgrid, EV charging, UPS maintenance

The company employs approximately 113,000 workers (45% unionized) and competes primarily on scale and multi-service integration in an otherwise highly fragmented market with low barriers to entry.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** ABM has grown revenue from $7.81B (FY2022) to $8.75B (FY2025), representing a 3.9% CAGR. FY2025 organic growth was 3.8% with an acceleration to 5.5% organic growth in Q1 FY2026. Technical Solutions grew 18.7% in FY2025, the fastest segment.

**Profitability Under Pressure:**
- Gross margin declined from 13.1% (FY2023) to 12.3% (FY2025) due to deliberate pricing concessions
- Operating margin: 3.6% (FY2025)
- Net margin: 1.9% (FY2025), recovering from 1.0% (FY2024, distorted by $95.7M RavenVolt contingent consideration charge) but below 3.1% (FY2023)
- ROE: 9.1%, ROA: 3.1%

**Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:**
- Operating cash flow: $234.4M (FY2025)
- Free cash flow: $155.1M (1.8% FCF margin)
- Total debt: $1.57B (D/E ratio 1.95, net leverage ~2.0x EBITDA)
- Goodwill: $2.59B (49% of total assets)
- Available revolver capacity: $577.5M (maturity extended to 2030)
- Self-insurance reserves: $558.6M

**Capital Return:** FY2025 saw $65.6M in dividends ($1.06/share, ~2.7% yield) and $121.3M in buybacks (2.56M shares at avg $47.35). Q1 FY2026 added $90M+ in repurchases. Buybacks at $47.35 average are now underwater relative to the $39.87 current price, raising questions about capital allocation discipline.

**Earnings Miss Pattern:** Four consecutive quarterly misses (-1.1%, -14.4%, -19.8%, -5.8%) represent a clear deterioration in earnings visibility and/or execution. The Q2 FY2025 miss of -19.8% was the most severe and coincided with increased volume around December 2025 in the price data.

## Growth Analysis

**Bull Case Growth Drivers:**
- Data center and semiconductor fabrication services: WGNSTAR acquisition (semiconductor fab environment services) directly positions ABM for CHIPS Act-driven U.S. manufacturing buildout
- Technical Solutions segment growth (18.7% YoY) driven by microgrid and UPS maintenance demand
- Aviation recovery (+8.3% growth) as travel volumes normalize
- Q1 FY2026 organic growth acceleration to 5.5%

**Bear Case Growth Headwinds:**
- Commercial office vacancy elevated nationally — directly pressures B&I (47% of revenue)
- EV charging station revenue declining within Technical Solutions
- Strategic pricing sacrifices margin for volume — growth is partially "bought" through lower profitability
- Estimate revisions are flat-to-slightly-negative (current quarter: -4.3% over 90 days; full year: -0.8%)

**Growth Assessment:** ABM's mid-single-digit organic revenue growth is respectable for a mature facility services provider, and the data center/chip fab positioning provides a genuine secular tailwind. However, growth quality is questionable when margins are simultaneously compressing. Revenue growth of 5.5% paired with margin compression produces EPS growth well below top-line growth, which limits the re-rating opportunity.

## Valuation Assessment

At $39.87 with FY2026 EPS guidance of $3.85-$4.15 (midpoint $4.00), ABM trades at approximately 10.0x forward earnings. Analyst consensus for the current fiscal year is $3.96, implying 10.1x on current-year earnings. Consensus for next fiscal year is $4.34, implying 9.2x on NTF+1 earnings.

**Relative Valuation:** The peer median P/E of 38.2x is skewed by CWST (795x) and CLH (38.2x), making direct comparison misleading. GEO at 11.7x is the closest comparable valuation. ABM's ~10x multiple reflects the market's assessment of a low-margin, labor-intensive business with declining earnings reliability.

**Analyst Targets:** Consensus median target is $50.00 (25% upside), with a range of $45-$68. The low target of $45 implies even bearish analysts see limited further downside.

**Absolute Valuation:** At ~10x earnings with a 2.9% dividend yield, ABM is not expensive on traditional metrics. The FCF yield (approximately 6.6% on $155M FCF / $2.33B market cap) provides support. However, cheap valuation on a stock with four consecutive earnings misses, margin compression, and insider selling may reflect warranted skepticism rather than opportunity. The market is pricing execution risk, and that pricing appears reasonable given the evidence.

**Why the market may be wrong:** If the WGNSTAR acquisition and data center positioning drive segment margin expansion (specifically in M&D and Technical Solutions) while the restructuring program delivers its $35M in annualized savings, normalized EPS power of $4.30-$4.50 at 12-13x forward earnings would imply $52-$58 — 30-45% upside. This requires execution that has been lacking for four straight quarters.

## Competitive Landscape

ABM operates in a highly fragmented market competing primarily against regional operators with lower overhead. Competitive advantages include:

- National scale enabling single-source contracts for multi-site clients
- Integrated multi-service capability (janitorial + engineering + technical)
- Industry-specific expertise (healthcare, aviation, education, data centers)
- Long-standing relationships (Vanderbilt NYC campus win in April 2026 demonstrates continued competitiveness)

Competitive position is stable but not strengthening materially. The deliberate margin sacrifice to win/retain accounts suggests pricing power is limited and competitive pressure is intensifying in core segments. Contracts are generally cancellable on 30-90 days' notice, which limits switching costs. Customer concentration is a vulnerability: one M&D client at 32% of segment revenue, one Technical Solutions client at 30%, and two Aviation clients at 27%.

The data center/semiconductor positioning is a genuine differentiator, but the market has not yet rewarded it — ABM's 5-year TSR of 136 vs. peer group 191 indicates consistent underperformance relative to facilities services peers.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Continued Earnings Deterioration:** Four consecutive misses suggest structural challenges in forecasting. If Q2/Q3 FY2026 continue missing, the stock likely retests the $37 low.

2. **Customer Concentration:** Loss of the 32% M&D client or 30% Technical Solutions client would be immediately material (~5-6% of total revenue per client).

3. **RavenVolt Contingent Consideration:** Up to $205M payable in calendar 2026 if targets are met (ABM expects only $32.5M). The delta between $32.5M expected and $205M maximum represents a potential $172M P&L surprise.

4. **Margin Compression Trajectory:** Management's explicit strategy of sacrificing margins creates an uncertain terminal margin structure. With 3.6% operating margins, the business has minimal buffer against cost inflation or execution errors.

5. **Leverage (2.0x net EBITDA, D/E 1.95):** In a rising-rate environment, interest costs ($96.4M annually, up 13.4% YoY) are a significant drag. Interest rate swap maturities in 2026 could expose unhedged rate risk.

6. **Self-Insurance Reserve Volatility:** $558.6M in reserves with prior-year development increases of $23.3M (FY2025) and $20.3M (FY2024). A 10% adverse development = $40M hit to net income.

7. **Goodwill Impairment Risk:** $2.59B in goodwill (49% of assets) from acquisition-heavy strategy. Currently passing impairment tests, but sustained margin compression could trigger writedowns.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing elevated near-term uncertainty: ATM IV of 44.2% vs. 28.9% historical volatility represents a 15.3 percentage point premium. The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 44.2% > 6-month 41.7%), consistent with the market pricing continued earnings volatility in upcoming quarters. The put/call OI ratio of 0.49 (call-heavy) suggests some speculative interest in upside, possibly related to the data center thesis. The ±9.2% implied move on the nearest expiry is consistent with the earnings-driven volatility observed in recent quarters.

Short interest at 4.5% of float with 4 days to cover is moderate — enough to provide fuel for a recovery rally if execution improves, but not extreme enough to create squeeze dynamics.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

3. Management provides FY2027 preliminary guidance implying EPS growth >10%

### Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):
1. Q2 FY2026 earnings miss exceeds -10% (fifth consecutive miss with deteriorating magnitude)
2. Full-year FY2026 guidance reduced below $3.70 (below the low end of current $3.85-$4.15 range)
3. Loss of a top-3 customer in any segment, or announcement of material goodwill impairment

### Reassessment Timeline:
- Next natural catalyst: Q2 FY2026 earnings (likely June 2026)
- Secondary: Any announced completion milestones for WGNSTAR integration
- Tertiary: RavenVolt contingent consideration finalization (expected May 2026 / calendar year 2026)

## Conclusion

ABM Industries presents a value-with-catalyst setup that has not yet earned conviction. The 10x forward multiple, 2.9% dividend yield, and data center positioning are genuinely attractive attributes. However, four consecutive earnings misses, intentional margin compression, leverage in a rising-rate environment, insider selling by the COO, and elevated options-implied volatility collectively argue for patience.

The stock is fairly valued at current levels given its demonstrated execution challenges. Upside requires proof that the strategic pricing gambit converts to volume-driven earnings growth and that the data center/chip fab positioning generates tangible margin improvement. Until at least one earnings beat materializes, the risk-reward does not support a more constructive stance.

Rating: **HOLD** — revisit at Q2 FY2026 earnings for potential upgrade if execution demonstrates recovery.
