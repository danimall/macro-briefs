**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

ScanSource is a specialty technology distributor emerging from a two-year revenue decline with Q3 FY2026 showing +8.8% YoY growth, its strongest quarter since FY2023. The company trades at 13.8x forward P/E versus a peer median of 18.0x, with a clean balance sheet (near-zero net debt), aggressive buyback program ($146M remaining authorization), and a high-margin recurring revenue platform (Intelisys) generating 27.7% operating margins on $2.79B in influenced billings.

Three specific concerns prevent a BUY recommendation despite the valuation discount: (1) CEO Mike Baur sold approximately 50% of his equity holdings over September 2025 – March 2026, a pattern consistent with either succession planning or diminished conviction in forward earnings power; (2) goodwill on the Specialty Technology Solutions segment sits within 2% of impairment — any deterioration in discount rates or projected growth could trigger a $100M+ write-down representing over 10% of the current market cap; and (3) consensus EPS estimates are declining across all periods (-0.9% current year, -3.0% next year over 90 days), indicating the analyst community is not yet confirming the revenue recovery's earnings power. The stock has appreciated 10.6% over the past year and 30% from its February 2026 lows, partially pricing in the recovery without yet delivering sustained estimate upgrades.

## Company Overview

ScanSource connects approximately 25,000 channel sales partners with over 500 technology suppliers across two segments:

**Specialty Technology Solutions** ($2.94B revenue, FY2025): Traditional wholesale distribution of hardware (barcode scanners, POS systems, networking equipment, payment terminals, physical security) and increasingly software/SaaS subscriptions. Operating margin of 2.2% reflects the inherently thin-margin nature of distribution.

**Intelisys & Advisory** ($98M reported revenue, FY2025): An agency-model technology services distribution platform with ~99% gross margins and 27.7% operating margins. Intelisys influences $2.79B in annual net billings from suppliers to end users across connectivity, UCaaS, CCaaS, and cloud services. This segment's economics more closely resemble a software business than a distributor.

The company operates primarily in the U.S. with secondary presence in Canada and Brazil, employing approximately 2,100 people.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Profitability:**

Revenue peaked at $3.79B in FY2023 before declining to $3.04B in FY2025 (-20% from peak) as channel partners destocked post-pandemic over-ordering. The recovery is now visible: Q3 FY2026 revenue of $767M represents +8.8% YoY growth (+7.6% organic).

Gross margin has expanded consistently despite revenue declines: 11.9% (FY23) → 12.2% (FY24) → 13.4% (FY25) → 14.0% (Q3 FY26). This reflects favorable mix shift toward Intelisys and higher-margin specialty products. Management's restructuring program ($20.5M annualized savings) is flowing through SG&A.

FY2026 guidance: Revenue $3.0-3.1B, Adjusted EBITDA $140-150M, FCF at least $90M.

**Balance Sheet:**

- Cash: $120M (March 2026)
- Debt: ~$102M (term loan being paid down)
- Net debt: approximately zero
- $350M revolver fully undrawn
- Current ratio: 2.01
- Altman Z-Score: 3.65 (safe zone)
- Inventory turns improving: 5.9x (Q4 FY25) vs. 5.0x prior year

**Cash Flow and Capital Returns:**

Free cash flow generation has been strong: $104M (FY25), $119M YTD through Q3 FY26. The share count has declined from 25.5M (FY22) to 20.8M (March 2026) — an 18% reduction over four years. At $45/share with $146M remaining on the buyback authorization, the company could retire another 3.2M shares (~15% of current float).

## Growth Analysis

The revenue recovery is real but modest. Q3 FY2026's +8.8% growth is encouraging after two years of decline, driven by:

1. Hardware refresh cycles resuming after extended deferral
2. Intelisys net billings growing 4.5% (to $2.79B annually)
3. Recent acquisitions (Resourcive, Advantix) adding ~$30M revenue
4. New "converged communications" business unit targeting UCaaS/CCaaS convergence

Analyst estimates tell a more cautious story. Current year EPS consensus of $3.93 is down -0.9% over 90 days; next year consensus of $4.47 is down -3.0% over the same period. This negative revision trend indicates analysts are not yet extrapolating the Q3 recovery into sustained earnings acceleration.

The realistic growth trajectory appears to be mid-single-digit revenue growth with EPS growth of 10-15% driven by operating leverage, mix improvement, and buyback-driven share count reduction. Solid but not exceptional.

## Valuation Assessment

At $45.45, SCSC trades at:
- 13.8x forward P/E (trailing and forward P/E both at 13.8x on ~$3.00 trailing EPS)
- 11.6x FY2026 consensus EPS ($3.93)
- 10.2x FY2027 consensus EPS ($4.47)
- Peer median P/E: 18.0x

The 23% discount to peer median P/E appears meaningful. If SCSC traded at even 14x FY2027 EPS ($4.47), the stock would be worth $62.60 — 38% upside. The median analyst target of $50 implies 10% upside; the high target of $71 implies 56% upside.

The discount is partially justified. SCSC's revenue base has contracted 20% from peak, goodwill impairment risk exists, the company has thinner margins than peers like CNXN or PLUS, and supplier concentration creates binary risk. The market is applying a "show me" discount until multi-quarter revenue growth is confirmed.

At 10.2x FY2027E earnings with improving ROIC and aggressive buybacks, the stock offers reasonable upside if the recovery sustains, but the market's skepticism (negative estimate revisions, single-quarter recovery) has validity.

## Competitive Landscape

ScanSource occupies a challenging competitive position. It is 50-100x smaller than broad-line distributors (Ingram Micro, TD Synnex) that can leverage scale for pricing, yet competes in many overlapping product categories. Its differentiation rests on:

1. **Deep specialization** in AIDC/POS, payment, and physical security categories where technical expertise matters
2. **Multi-modal distribution** combining hardware with cloud/connectivity via Intelisys
3. **25,000 partner relationships** creating switching costs (partners build workflow around distributor portals/tools)

Intelisys provides genuine competitive differentiation — the combination of hardware distribution with services distribution under one roof is relatively unique. But the segment is only 3% of reported revenue, and competitors like Telarus and Avant are growing aggressively in the TSD (technology services distributor) market.

The supplier concentration risk (Cisco and Zebra each >10% of revenue, terminable on 30-60 days' notice) is structural and limits competitive pricing power.

## Risk Assessment

**Quantified Risks:**

1. **Goodwill Impairment (2% Headroom):** The Specialty Technology Solutions goodwill of $159.8M exceeded carrying value by only 2% at last annual test. A 50-100bp increase in WACC assumptions could bridge the 2% gap and trigger a $100M+ non-cash impairment. This would not affect cash flow but would impact reported GAAP earnings and book value.

2. **CEO Insider Selling:** Mike Baur reduced holdings from ~330K to ~157K shares over six months. This represents a 50%+ reduction in the founder's equity stake, coinciding with a "Departure/Election of Officers" 8-K filing. While one board member bought $211K in February 2026, the net insider signal is heavily negative.

3. **Tariff-Driven Demand Destruction:** Products distributed by SCSC are overwhelmingly sourced from Asia by SCSC's suppliers. While SCSC passes through price increases (protecting margins), higher end-user prices suppress demand volumes. The magnitude is unquantifiable but real.

4. **Negative Estimate Revisions:** EPS estimates declining across all periods contradict the recovery narrative from Q3 results. Analysts appear to be incorporating tariff headwinds and Q2 weakness more than Q3 strength.

**Options Market Signal:**

Near-term ATM IV of 33.0% sits below 1-year historical volatility of 38.1%, a spread of -5.1pp. IV percentile is at 18%, suggesting the options market is pricing SCSC as relatively calm. The put/call OI ratio of 0.76 (call-heavy) and call-heavy skew (-5.8%) indicate modest bullish positioning. Term structure in contango (33.0% → 36.1%) is normal/complacent — no event premium priced. Short interest at 7.7% / 4.0 days to cover is moderate but not extreme.

The options market is not flagging imminent risk, consistent with a "show me" stock rather than a distressed situation.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($55-62, 21-36% upside):**
- Hardware recovery sustains into FY2027 with mid-to-high single digit revenue growth
- Intelisys scales past $100M revenue as cloud/UCaaS adoption accelerates
- Operating leverage + restructuring savings drive margins toward 5% EBITDA
- Buyback reduces share count another 10-15% at accretive prices
- Re-rating toward peer P/E of 16-18x on FY2027 EPS of $4.47 → $71-80

**Bear Case ($33-37, 19-27% downside):**
- Tariff-driven demand destruction stalls recovery; FY2027 revenue flat
- Goodwill impairment triggered on next annual test ($100M+ charge)
- CEO succession creates operational disruption
- Cisco or Zebra terminates distribution agreement
- Stock reverts to February 2026 lows of $33-35

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of positive EPS estimate revisions (30-day) confirming sustained recovery
2. CEO succession resolved cleanly (new CEO named, Baur transitions to Chairman-only) without operational disruption
3. Goodwill impairment test passes with >10% headroom (would require strong FY2027 outlook)

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2027 EPS estimates fall below $4.00 (currently $4.47), indicating recovery failure
2. Cisco or Zebra distribution agreement terminated or materially modified
3. Goodwill impairment charge taken in FY2026 or FY2027

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings release (Q4 FY2026, likely August 2026) will confirm whether Q3's +8.8% growth was sustainable or a one-quarter anomaly.

## Conclusion

ScanSource presents a recovery story at a reasonable valuation (10-12x forward earnings vs. 18x peer median), with a high-margin recurring revenue platform in Intelisys and aggressive capital returns via buyback. The Q3 FY2026 revenue recovery is encouraging.

The combination of (1) founder/CEO selling 50% of his holdings, exceeding the threshold for bearish signal; (2) goodwill sitting 2% from impairment in a rising-rate environment; and (3) consensus estimates declining rather than rising across all periods creates enough uncertainty to prevent a BUY recommendation at the current 6.12 overall score. The stock scores above average on financial health and valuation but below average on growth prospects, competitive position, and management quality — the weighted combination does not clear the threshold for high-conviction capital deployment.

HOLD with growth thesis intact. The recovery is plausible and the valuation provides margin of safety, but the burden of proof rests on the next 1-2 quarters of data to confirm estimate revisions inflect positive and insider selling stabilizes.
