**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

ScanSource is a specialty technology distributor trading at $45.45 with a forward P/E of ~11.6x on FY26E EPS of ~$3.93, a discount to the peer median of 18.0x. The discount is partially justified by the company's thin-margin distribution model, supplier concentration (Cisco and Zebra each >10% of revenue on short-term agreements), and a goodwill balance whose fair value cushion is only 2% above carrying value. The company generates excellent free cash flow ($104M in FY25, $119M through 9 months of FY26) relative to its ~$924M market cap, and management is deploying it aggressively into buybacks — 16% share count reduction over two years.

The bull case centers on cyclical revenue recovery (Q3 FY26 grew 8.8% YoY), continued buyback accretion, and the strategic value of Intelisys, a recurring-revenue technology services distribution business generating ~28% operating margins on $2.88B in billings. The bear case focuses on the structural vulnerability of distribution economics, the 2% goodwill impairment cushion that could produce a write-down under modest stress, and CEO insider selling that contradicts the corporate buyback signal. At current prices, the risk/reward is reasonable but not compelling enough to warrant an active BUY given the macro headwinds facing enterprise IT spending in H2 2026.

## Company Overview

ScanSource operates as a specialty technology distributor connecting ~500 suppliers with ~25,000 channel partners across the US, Canada, and Brazil. The business is organized in two segments:

**Specialty Technology Solutions (~97% of reported revenue)**: Traditional distribution of barcode/mobility (Zebra), networking (Cisco, Extreme), physical security (Axis, Hanwha), POS/payment terminals, and communications equipment. This segment operates at 11% gross margins and ~3% operating margins.

**Intelisys & Advisory (~3% of reported revenue)**: An agency-model technology services distributor (TSD) reporting net commissions rather than gross billings. Actual transaction volume flowing through Intelisys is ~$2.88B annualized. Operating margins of 28-31%. This segment distributes connectivity, cloud, and communications services and represents the company's strategic transformation vector.

The company is headquartered in Greenville, SC, has approximately 20.3M shares outstanding, and pays no dividend.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory**: Revenue peaked at $3.79B in FY2023 and declined 20% through FY2025 ($3.04B), driven by enterprise IT spending caution. Q3 FY2026 showed clear recovery at $766.8M (+8.8% YoY), the strongest sequential improvement in over a year. Growth was broad-based across most technologies in North America. Full-year FY26 guidance of $3.0-3.1B implies roughly flat performance year-over-year.

**Margin Improvement**: Gross margins expanded from 11.9% (FY23) to 13.4% (FY25) to 14.0% in Q3 FY26. This reflects favorable mix (higher Intelisys contribution), supplier program recognition, and improved pricing discipline. Operating margins have been slower to expand (2.8% in FY25, 3.0% in Q3 FY26) as SG&A crept up to 10.2% of revenue despite restructuring savings of ~$20.5M annualized.

**Free Cash Flow**: FCF of $104M in FY25 and $118.6M through 9 months of FY26, on capex of only $8-15M annually. At the current market cap of ~$924M, the trailing FCF yield is approximately 12.8%. This reflects the asset-light nature of the business model.

**Balance Sheet**: Net debt near zero ($102M debt vs. $120M cash at Q3 FY26). Altman Z-Score of 3.65. $400M undrawn revolver with maturity extended to December 2030. Current ratio of 2.01. The balance sheet is a clear strength.

**Capital Return**: 2.48M shares repurchased in FY25 for $106.5M, plus $71.4M additional in 9M FY26. Share count declined from 24.9M to 20.8M in two years. At current prices and remaining authorization of $146M, the company could retire another 3.2M shares (~15% of outstanding), providing meaningful EPS accretion.

## Growth Analysis

Revenue growth is recovering from a cyclical trough but remains early-stage. The 8.8% YoY growth in Q3 FY26 is encouraging but follows a 20% peak-to-trough decline. Consensus expects FY27 EPS of $4.47, representing ~14% growth from estimated FY26 EPS of $3.93.

Growth vectors include:
- **Cyclical recovery** in enterprise IT spending (the primary driver of near-term upside)
- **Intelisys billings growth** — the TSD model benefits from secular cloud migration and managed services adoption
- **Converged communications** — newly launched business unit combining hardware distribution with cloud/connectivity
- **Bolt-on acquisitions** — DataXoom ($18.4M + $13.4M contingent) and Advantix expand wireless enablement capabilities

Headwinds to growth include the macro environment, Brazil weakness (-5.8% organic decline in 9M FY26), and the structural challenge of growing a distribution business when suppliers can increasingly go direct.

Estimate revisions are slightly negative: current-year EPS estimates declined 0.9% over 90 days, and next-year estimates declined 3.0%. This negative revision trend, while modest, does not suggest accelerating growth expectations.

## Valuation Assessment

At $45.45, SCSC trades at:
- **Forward P/E**: ~11.6x FY26E EPS of $3.93 (or ~13.8x trailing P/E per Phase 1 data)
- **Forward P/E on FY27**: ~10.2x consensus $4.47
- **FCF yield**: ~12.8% on trailing FCF
- **EV/EBITDA**: approximately 7-8x

Against peer medians of 18.0x P/E, the discount is 36%. The discount is partly structural — SCSC is smaller ($924M market cap vs. $2-11B peers), less diversified, and more cyclically exposed. However, the magnitude of the discount seems excessive given the FCF generation profile and Intelisys margin structure.

The analyst consensus target median is $50.00 (10% upside), with a high target of $71.00 (56% upside) and low of $43.00 (5% downside). The split between 2 Buys and 2 Holds reflects the balanced risk/reward at current levels.

The stock has appreciated ~10.6% over the past year and recently broke through the $45 level on Q3 FY26 results. It trades at 97% of its 52-week high of $46.64. The valuation is reasonable but not deeply discounted relative to its own recent trading range.

## Competitive Landscape

ScanSource occupies a niche between broad-line distribution giants (TD Synnex at $60B+ revenue, Ingram Micro) and pure specialty players. Its differentiation lies in:

1. **Technical expertise** in specific verticals (AIDC, physical security, payments) where solution configuration adds value
2. **The Intelisys TSD model** — relatively unique in combining hardware distribution with an agency-model services distribution platform
3. **Multi-model approach** — converging traditional product distribution with cloud/connectivity services

Competitive threats include:
- Supplier disintermediation (manufacturers going direct to large enterprise customers)
- Broad-line distributor scale advantages in procurement and logistics
- In the TSD space, Avant and Telarus compete for the same partner base
- Margin compression from commoditization of technology products

The competitive position is stable rather than improving. Market share data is difficult to assess for specialty distribution, but revenue declines of 20% from peak suggest some combination of cyclical headwinds and structural pressure.

## Risk Assessment

**Goodwill Impairment (HIGH)**: The Specialty Technology Solutions reporting unit's fair value exceeded carrying value by only 2% at the most recent annual test. With $160M in goodwill, any combination of revenue shortfall, margin pressure, or WACC increase triggers impairment. In the current rate environment, discount rates have likely risen since the last test. This could produce a non-cash charge equal to ~17% of market cap.

**Supplier Concentration (MEDIUM-HIGH)**: Cisco and Zebra each represent >10% of net sales on agreements terminable with 30-60 days' notice. Either supplier shifting distribution strategy would be immediately material.

**Macro/Cyclical Risk (MEDIUM)**: Enterprise IT spending is sensitive to economic conditions. [unverified] macro headwinds could cause customers to defer purchases, reversing the Q3 recovery trend.

**CEO Insider Selling (MEDIUM)**: CEO Baur sold ~170,000 shares ($7.6M) between September 2025 and March 2026, including large option exercises. While Director Mathis purchased $211K in February 2026, the CEO's actions represent a larger economic signal. The juxtaposition of personal selling with corporate buyback creates a credibility gap.

**Tariff Exposure (LOW-MEDIUM)**: Products sourced from Asia face tariff uncertainty. As a pass-through distributor, SCSC can pass price increases to customers, making this somewhat asymmetric — price inflation helps margins but demand destruction hurts volume.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($55-60, +21% to +32%)
- Revenue recovery continues through FY27, reaching $3.2-3.3B with the improved cost structure generating $100M+ operating income
- EPS exceeds $4.50 on reduced share count (continued buybacks at $71M+ annual pace)
- Intelisys billings growth accelerates toward $3.5B as cloud/connectivity adoption grows
- Multiple re-rates toward 13-14x (still below peer median) as the market recognizes the dual-model strategy
- FCF deployment at current valuations remains highly accretive

### Bear Case ($33-36, -21% to -27%)
- Enterprise IT spending stalls in H2 2026 amid economic pressure, reversing Q3 recovery
- Goodwill impairment triggered as WACC rises and revenue projections disappoint (non-cash but sentiment-damaging)
- Key supplier (Cisco or Zebra) restructures distribution channel, removing exclusivity or reducing territory
- CEO selling intensifies, undermining buyback narrative
- Stock returns to February 2026 lows

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Two consecutive quarters of 8%+ organic revenue growth confirming cyclical recovery durability
- Goodwill cushion expanding above 10% at the next annual impairment test (signaling reduced risk)
- Stock pullback to $38-40 range without fundamental deterioration (would create 15x FCF entry point)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Revenue growth turning negative again (below -3% YoY) for any quarter
- Goodwill impairment charge announced
- Key supplier (Cisco or Zebra) providing notice of distribution agreement termination
- CEO Baur selling remaining shares below 100K held (would signal potential departure)

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (Q4 FY26, expected August 2026). The annual goodwill impairment test (conducted in the fiscal fourth quarter) will be particularly important given the 2% cushion.

## Conclusion

ScanSource presents a reasonable value proposition at 11.6x forward earnings with 12.8% FCF yield and aggressive capital return. The Intelisys segment is a genuine strategic asset that differentiates SCSC from pure hardware distribution peers. However, several factors prevent a BUY recommendation: (1) negative estimate revision trends, (2) the 2% goodwill cushion representing material non-cash risk, (3) CEO insider selling conflicting with the corporate buyback narrative, and (4) the stock already trading near 52-week highs after digesting Q3 results. The cyclical recovery thesis needs at least one more quarter of confirmation before warranting higher conviction. HOLD with a growth orientation — the fundamental trajectory is positive but not yet sufficiently demonstrated.
