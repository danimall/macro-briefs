## Executive Summary

Gilead Sciences trades at $139 after a 37% rally over the past year, driven by the Yeztugo (lenacapavir) PrEP launch, a Biktarvy patent settlement extending exclusivity to April 2036, and improving underlying fundamentals. The company generated $9.5B in free cash flow on $29.4B in revenue in FY2025, with HIV accounting for 71% of product sales. At 20.5x trailing and approximately 16.2x current-year consensus EPS of $8.56, Gilead trades in line with the peer median P/E of 20.0x on trailing but at a discount on forward estimates, reflecting both a maturing base business and genuine pipeline optionality.

The investment thesis rests on three pillars: (1) Biktarvy's $14.3B franchise now protected through 2036, providing a durable cash flow base; (2) Yeztugo's twice-yearly PrEP injection targeting $800M in 2026 with consensus viewing this as conservative; and (3) $9.5B in annual FCF funding a 2.4% dividend, buybacks, and an aggressive M&A pipeline ($14.8B announced in early 2026). The primary risks are Biktarvy's selection for Medicare price negotiation (effective 2028), persistent oncology setbacks, and execution risk on the M&A spree. These risks are real and quantifiable but do not fully offset the combination of a protected franchise, a blockbuster launch, and a valuation that provides adequate margin of safety relative to the $160 analyst median price target.

## Company Overview

Gilead Sciences is a biopharmaceutical company operating in 35+ countries, focused on HIV, liver disease, oncology, and inflammation. FY2025 revenue of $29.4B breaks down as: HIV $20.8B (71%), liver disease $3.2B (11%), oncology $3.2B (11%), Veklury (COVID-19) $0.9B (3%), and other $0.8B. The company's competitive position in HIV is anchored by Biktarvy, which holds 52%+ U.S. treatment market share and generated $14.3B in 2025 — nearly half of all Gilead revenue.

CEO Daniel O'Day has led an aggressive diversification push since taking the role, spending over $35B on acquisitions including the $21B Immunomedics purchase (Trodelvy) and, more recently, ~$14.8B across Arcellx, Ouro Medicines, and Tubulis in early 2026. The company employs a vertically integrated model for manufacturing key products, with facilities in the U.S., Ireland, Canada, and Alberta.

## Financial Analysis

**Profitability:** Gilead's underlying profitability is strong: 34.0% operating margin, 28.9% net margin, 14.4% ROA, and 37.5% ROE in FY2025. The FY2024 earnings collapse (net income of $0.5B, EPS of $0.38) was entirely driven by $8B in non-cash charges from acquired IPR&D and Trodelvy impairments, not operational deterioration. Stripping those charges, the business was consistently profitable.

**Cash Flow:** FY2025 operating cash flow of $10.0B with only $563M in capex produced $9.5B in FCF, a 32.1% FCF margin. This level of cash generation funds dividends ($4.0B), buybacks ($1.9B), and debt reduction ($1.8B) with $1.8B remaining before M&A. Capital intensity is low, and FCF conversion substantially exceeds net income, indicating high earnings quality.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of ~$24.9B against cash and marketable securities of ~$10.6B produces net debt of ~$14.3B. The D/E ratio of 2.6x is elevated for a healthcare company, and the Altman Z-Score of 1.96 sits in the "grey zone," reflecting leverage rather than operational distress. With $9.5B in annual FCF, the net debt/FCF ratio is approximately 1.5x, which is manageable. However, the $14.8B M&A commitment in 2026 will require additional debt issuance, temporarily increasing leverage before the acquired assets generate meaningful cash flow.

**Gross-to-Net Compression:** Gross-to-net deductions jumped to 41% of gross product sales in FY2025 from 38% in FY2024, a 250bps increase driven by Medicare Part D redesign. This structural headwind will intensify when Biktarvy's negotiated Medicare price takes effect in 2028.

**Quarterly Earnings:** The 4-quarter record shows 3 beats and 1 marginal miss (Q1 2025 at -0.6%). Q3 2025's 13.7% beat stands out as the strongest signal. The pattern suggests management guides conservatively but not dramatically so.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Drivers:**
- *Yeztugo ramp:* The twice-yearly injectable PrEP generated $150M in its first ~6 months (including $96M in Q4 2025). The 2026 target of $800M implies roughly $200M/quarter, achievable given 90% U.S. payer coverage already secured, DTC marketing launched, and a CDC strong recommendation. Mizuho and Citi both view the $800M target as conservative. The TAM expansion opportunity is significant: twice-yearly dosing reaches populations who would never take daily pills.
- *Biktarvy stability:* With generic entry pushed to April 2036, Biktarvy's $14.3B annual revenue base is protected for approximately a decade. The 2+ additional years of exclusivity (vs. December 2033 prior expectation) translates to $25-30B in incremental cumulative cash flow.
- *BIC/LEN combo:* ARTISTRY-1 and -2 trials met primary endpoints for bictegravir+lenacapavir daily oral HIV treatment switch, providing a lenacapavir platform expansion within existing treatment categories.

**Medium-Term Concerns:**
- *Estimate revisions are negative.* Current-year EPS has been cut from $8.77 (90 days ago) to $8.56 (-2.5%). Next quarter estimates fell 12.0% over 90 days. Earnings momentum is deteriorating.
- *Oncology disappointments are accumulating.* EVOKE-01 failed (NSCLC). ASCENT-07 failed (HR+/HER2- breast cancer). STAR-221 was discontinued (gastric cancer). Cell therapy revenue declined 7% YoY. The return on the $21B Immunomedics acquisition remains questionable.
- *Veklury is in secular decline.* Down 49% YoY to $911M. Further declines are near-certain.

**FY2026 Guidance:** Management guided product sales of $29.6B-$30.0B (2-4% growth), with 8% HIV growth offset partially by pricing concessions. The Street's $8.56 current-year EPS consensus suggests modest profitability growth, with FY2027 at $9.61 (+12.3%) reflecting fuller Yeztugo contribution.

## Valuation Assessment

At $139, Gilead trades at:
- 20.5x trailing EPS ($6.78)
- 16.2x FY2026E EPS ($8.56)
- 14.5x FY2027E EPS ($9.61)
- ~14.6x FCF ($9.5B on ~$174B market cap)
- 2.4% dividend yield

Against the peer median trailing P/E of 20.0x, Gilead appears fairly valued. On forward estimates, the stock is cheaper than Amgen (23x), Vertex (29.6x), and Biogen (20x). Regeneron at 18.6x and UTHR at 17.5x are the closest comparables, both of which have less revenue scale and lower dividend yields.

The analyst consensus median price target is $160 (15% upside), with a range of $123-$180. The $160 target implies approximately 18.7x FY2026E EPS, a modest re-rating from current levels that seems achievable given the Yeztugo ramp trajectory.

The valuation is not stretched, but the negative estimate revision trend (-2.5% on current-year over 90 days, -12% on next quarter) creates a risk that the denominator shrinks, inflating the multiple. The key variable is whether Yeztugo outperformance offsets the pricing and Veklury headwinds; early launch data suggests it will.

## Competitive Landscape

**HIV (71% of revenue):** Gilead's moat in HIV treatment is among the strongest in biopharma. Biktarvy's 52%+ U.S. market share, combined with the April 2036 patent protection, creates a near-impregnable position. ViiV Healthcare's cabotegravir is the primary competitive threat in long-acting formats, but Yeztugo's twice-yearly dosing is clearly differentiated from cabotegravir's monthly/bi-monthly regimen. The PrEP market is likely to expand materially rather than cannibalize existing treatment volumes.

**Oncology (11%):** The competitive position here is significantly weaker. Trodelvy faces competition from Daiichi Sankyo/AstraZeneca's Enhertu franchise in multiple indications. Cell therapy competition from BMS (Breyanzi) and J&J/Legend Biotech (Carvykti) is intensifying. The Arcellx acquisition (BCMA CAR-T) and Tubulis (next-gen ADCs) represent strategic bets to rebuild the oncology portfolio, but these are largely pre-revenue and carry execution risk.

**Liver Disease (11%):** Epclusa and Vemlidy provide stable revenue. Livdelzi (for PBC) is a modest growth driver. This segment is not a competitive concern but is unlikely to be a meaningful growth accelerator.

## Risk Assessment

**Biktarvy Medicare Negotiation (HIGH):** HHS selected Biktarvy for Medicare price negotiation in January 2026, with negotiated prices effective 2028. A 25-40% Medicare price reduction on Biktarvy could reduce total revenue by $1.0-1.5B annually, concentrated in 2028-2029. Lower Medicare prices cascade into Medicaid rebates and 340B ceiling prices. The 10-K states Gilead anticipates "substantially lower" Medicare pricing. Quantifying the impact precisely requires knowing the negotiated price.

**Oncology Execution Risk (MEDIUM-HIGH):** Multiple Phase 3 failures (EVOKE-01, ASCENT-07, STAR-221) combined with $14.8B in M&A for largely pre-revenue oncology assets creates a scenario where Gilead is doubling down on a therapeutic area where its track record has been poor.

**M&A Integration (MEDIUM):** Three acquisitions in six weeks totaling ~$14.8B will stress management bandwidth and balance sheet capacity simultaneously. The Arcellx tender offer has already been extended, suggesting the process is not frictionless.

**Pricing & Regulatory Environment (MEDIUM):** The MFN agreement, IRA implementation, Medicaid work requirements, and ACA subsidy expiration all compress realized pricing. Gross-to-net deductions rising from 38% to 41% in a single year confirms this is already materializing.

**Clinical Pipeline Risk (MEDIUM):** FDA clinical holds on GS-1720 and GS-4182 (WONDERS trials) remove potential mid-term pipeline candidates from the near-term catalysts.

**Declining Estimate Momentum:** Current-quarter estimates down 6.0% over 90 days and next-quarter down 12.0% over 90 days. The revision trend must stabilize for the stock to sustain its valuation.

### Options Market Signal

ATM IV of 29.4% is approximately in line with 28.3% historical volatility; the options market is not pricing unusual uncertainty. However, the 1-month put/call volume ratio of 3.67x and unusual activity in May $130 puts (3,006 contracts vs. 592 OI, 5.1x volume/OI) suggest institutional hedging or positioning for a pullback. The contango term structure (near-term 29.4% vs. 6-month 32.7%) is normal, but the elevated put activity at 6.5% out-of-the-money warrants monitoring. With IV at the 17th percentile of its current chain, options are relatively cheap, favorable for entry via stock or call spreads. The put activity is a cautionary signal but does not override the fundamental thesis at current valuations.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($160-170):**
- Yeztugo exceeds the $800M 2026 target, reaching $1.0-1.2B
- Biktarvy's 2036 exclusivity supports $14B+ annual revenue for another decade
- FY2027 EPS of $9.61 × 17-18x multiple = $163-173
- M&A pipeline (Arcellx, Tubulis) delivers clinical catalysts in 2026-2027
- FCF of $9.5B+ supports continued buybacks and dividend growth

**Bear Case ($115-120):**
- Biktarvy Medicare negotiation results in steeper-than-expected price cuts, reducing revenue by $1.5B+ starting 2028
- Oncology acquisitions fail clinically (Arcellx BCMA CAR-T setback, Tubulis ADC platform disappoints)
- Yeztugo launch stalls due to access/affordability barriers or competitive response from ViiV
- Estimate revisions continue deteriorating, EPS falls below $8.00
- Stock re-rates to 14x forward = $120

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Base case target: $158** — 18.5x FY2026E EPS of $8.56, reflecting modest multiple expansion as Yeztugo contribution becomes visible in reported numbers. Alternatively: 16.4x FY2027E EPS of $9.61. Both paths converge near $158.

**Bull case target: $170** — 17.7x FY2027E EPS of $9.61, reflecting full Yeztugo ramp and M&A pipeline optionality.

**Bear case target: $117** — 13.7x FY2026E EPS of $8.56, or 14.5x a downwardly revised $8.00, reflecting accelerated pricing compression and failed oncology bets.

**Upside/downside from current ($139):** Base +13.7%, Bull +22.3%, Bear -15.8%. Risk/reward ratio approximately 1.4:1 to base case.

**Timeframe:** 12 months. Key catalysts: Q1 2026 earnings (April 2026 — Yeztugo quarterly revenue), Arcellx acquisition closing (Q2 2026), ASCO/EHA data presentations (June 2026), Biktarvy negotiation progress (H2 2026).

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Yeztugo quarterly revenue falls below $150M in any Q2-Q4 2026 quarter (would indicate launch stall, not just timing)
2. FY2026 EPS consensus falls below $8.00 (would indicate structural earnings deterioration beyond current estimate trajectory)
3. Arcellx acquisition fails to close or anitocabtagene autoleucel reports clinically significant adverse events in ongoing trials

## Conclusion

Gilead Sciences at $139 offers a protected $14.3B franchise (Biktarvy through 2036), a strong launch (Yeztugo targeting $800M in 2026), and $9.5B in annual FCF at approximately 16x forward earnings. The negative estimate revision trend and the 2028 Medicare pricing risk for Biktarvy are the primary headwinds preventing a higher-conviction call. However, the stock's valuation at 14.5x FY2027 consensus provides adequate margin of safety: the market is pricing in the near-term headwinds without fully crediting the Yeztugo upside or the 2036 exclusivity extension. The stock is a BUY with a 12-month base case target of $158, representing approximately 14% upside.
