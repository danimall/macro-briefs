**Research Date: June 1, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Coherent has executed one of the cleaner turnarounds in the photonics/optical space. A messy post-merger entity (II-VI's $7B+ acquisition of Coherent Inc., July 2022) carrying three legacy segments, $3.7B of debt, a $2.5B Bain preferred overhang, and back-to-back annual net losses has, under a new semiconductor-pedigreed leadership team, become a focused AI datacenter optics play. Revenue grew 23% in FY2025 and 19% in the first nine months of FY2026; the Datacenter & Communications segment (~75% of revenue) grew 41% YoY in Q3 FY2026 to $1.36B; gross margin expanded from 31% to 38%; and diluted EPS swung from -$0.52 (FY2025) to +$2.92 (9M FY2026). The March 2026 NVIDIA $2B equity investment plus a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment through 2030, combined with the full conversion of Bain's Series B preferred to common, lifted total equity from $6.0B to $11.0B and removed the preferred-dividend drag.

The fundamental case is sound and improving. The problem is price. At $361, Coherent trades at roughly 66x current-year consensus EPS ($5.45) and 45x next-year EPS ($8.10). At those multiples, the estimate-revision tailwind that would normally constitute a strong buy signal loses its force: beats are already the base case (3 of last 4 quarters were beats, but the magnitude has narrowed and Q3 was a 1.1% miss), and any disappointment derates a stock with no valuation cushion. The options market is pricing this explicitly: near-term implied volatility of 106% against 70% realized, a backwardated term structure signaling imminent stress, and an implied ±9.2% move into the next weekly expiry.

I rate COHR a HOLD with a growth lean. Fundamentals are stable to improving, and the May pullback from a 52-week high of $413 to $361 modestly improves risk/reward. The HOLD reflects a specific, falsifiable concern: the stock has fully discounted continued flawless AI-capex growth, while carrying an unquantified BIS/Huawei export-control investigation, intensifying single-customer concentration, and a working-capital profile (9M operating cash flow of $10M against $564M net earnings) that has temporarily diverged from earnings. The path from here is more two-sided than the prior BUY implied.

## Company Overview

Coherent is a vertically integrated photonics manufacturer: lasers (GaAs VCSELs, InP DMLs/EMLs/CW lasers), optical transceivers (800G/1.6T), optoelectronic devices, ICs, detectors, passive optics, and engineered materials including silicon carbide. Headquartered in Saxonburg, PA, with ~30,000 employees (83% in manufacturing) across the US, China, Germany, Malaysia, and Vietnam. Designing and building both the transceivers and the underlying lasers and ICs in-house across multiple 6-inch GaAs and InP fabs is a genuine differentiator in a market where few competitors are this deep.

Effective FY2026 (July 1, 2025), management collapsed three legacy segments (Networking, Materials, Lasers) into two: **Datacenter & Communications** (the growth engine) and **Industrial** (being pruned via divestitures). The business is now an AI optics company with an industrial appendage that is shrinking by design.

## Financial Analysis

The two-year financial trajectory is a textbook operating-leverage turnaround:

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | 9M FY2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $5.16B | $4.71B (-9%) | $5.81B (+23%) | $5.07B (+19% YoY) |
| Gross margin | 31% | 31% | 35% | 37% (Q3: 38%) |
| Net income (to COHR) | -$259M | -$156M | +$49M | +$564M |
| Diluted EPS | -$2.93 | -$1.84 | -$0.52* | +$2.92 |

*FY2025 EPS negative after ~$97M preferred dividends despite positive net income.

The reacceleration is overwhelmingly AI datacenter transceiver demand. Datacenter & Communications carried $348M segment profit in Q3; Industrial declined 16% to $444M on a mix of genuine SiC/automotive softness and divestitures. Margin expansion came from volume leverage, yield improvements, pricing, and cost reduction.

**Balance sheet — radically de-risked in FY2026.** Cash of $1.59B plus $825M short-term investments. Debt fell from $3.69B to $3.19B via $502M of voluntary prepayments, refinanced at lower spreads in September 2025 (new $1.25B Term A, revolver up to $700M, Term B-3 at SOFR+1.75%, maturities 2029-2030). In compliance with covenants (max 4.0x net leverage, min 2.5x interest coverage). A 100bp rate move equals roughly $19M of annual interest, partly hedged via a $1.5B-notional cap at 1.92% SOFR.

A note for anyone running a naive screen: the Altman Z-Score of ~1.7 and Piotroski 5 derived from the FY2025 10-K are stale. They reflect ~$7.4B of merger goodwill and intangibles depressing the ratios and the pre-NVIDIA, pre-Bain-conversion capital structure. Post-March 2026, with equity nearly doubled and the preferred eliminated, the company is materially healthier than those figures suggest.

**The one earnings-quality caveat.** Despite a 4x jump in net earnings, 9M FY2026 operating cash flow was only $10M, driven by a deliberate ~$700M inventory build to support the demand ramp plus AP timing. This is growth-driven working capital rather than distress, but reported earnings are temporarily running ahead of cash conversion. If AI demand disappoints, that inventory becomes an obsolescence risk the 10-K explicitly flags. This is a watch item, and it tempers the Financial Health score from what the equity and debt picture alone would support.

## Growth Analysis

Growth is the strongest dimension. The thematic backdrop is durable: multiple hyperscalers have committed to large AI infrastructure capex, NVIDIA conducted a $40B+ 2026 AI equity-investment spree, and the transition from 800G to 1.6T optics and toward co-packaged optics (CPO) expands the addressable opportunity. Within the optics complex, multiple suppliers are showing 40%+ growth simultaneously — a structural demand shift rather than a single-company story.

Estimate revisions confirm momentum: current-quarter EPS revised +5.8% (30d) / +5.3% (90d), next-quarter +7.7% / +8.3%, and next-year +8.6% (30d) / +10.3% (90d). Revenue is annualizing toward ~$7.5B. The NVIDIA agreement embeds a multi-billion-dollar purchase commitment and capacity/access rights through 2030, which lengthens the visibility on the growth runway beyond the typical optics order cycle.

The honest counterweight: revision velocity here is positive but moderate (high single digits), not the +30% that signals the market is systematically underpricing the trajectory. The recovery from a trough has largely happened — EPS is already positive and scaling. From here, growth durability is the question, and CPO introduces genuine structural uncertainty over whether co-packaged optics expands the pluggable-transceiver opportunity or disintermediates it. The growth score is high (8.5) on durability and breadth of confirming signals, but the easy part of the recovery is behind, not ahead.

## Valuation Assessment

This is where the BUY case breaks down. At $361:
- Current-year (FY2026E) EPS $5.45 → ~66x
- Next-year (FY2027E) EPS $8.10 → ~45x
- Trailing GAAP EPS is essentially nil on a 12-month basis given the H1 turnaround, making trailing P/E (~151x) meaningless — correctly disregarded.

The right lens is forward and cycle-adjusted, and even there the multiple is premium. At 45x next-year EPS with consensus next-year growth around 49%, the forward PEG is roughly 0.9, which looks reasonable in isolation. But that PEG depends on (a) the +49% FY2027 growth materializing, which is itself a function of AI-capex sustaining at current intensity, and (b) the 45x multiple not compressing. At >50x current-year, analyst price targets are not independent floors — the $386 median and $462 high targets reflect the same AI-capex extrapolation embedded in the price. The consensus low of $230 (36% below current) shows the dispersion: the bear scenario is a sentiment-driven derating of a high-beta AI proxy, and the analyst community has it on the table.

Peer context reinforces caution. The AI optics complex (LITE, AAOI) has been one of 2026's most violent trades — AAOI reportedly +441% YTD — with the group correcting 6-8% on multiple May sessions on profit-taking. These are not stable compounders trading at durable multiples; they are momentum vehicles where multiple expansion has done much of the work. A correct sector theme does not protect a >50x forward stock from a large drawdown when multiple compression hits.

I score Valuation 3.5. The multiple is stretched against fundamentals that, while excellent, are already extrapolated forward; there is no absolute-yield support (no dividend) and no margin of safety.

## Competitive Landscape

Coherent is a top-tier AI datacenter optics supplier competing with Lumentum (LITE), Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI), Marvell, Innolight, and increasingly Broadcom (which leads CPO switching ASICs). Its vertical integration — in-house lasers, ICs, silicon photonics, and a broad CPO-enabling portfolio including a liquid-crystal-based optical circuit switch — positions it to participate as the industry moves to 1.6T and integrated optics.

The NVIDIA capacity agreement embeds Coherent in NVIDIA's optical roadmap through 2030, a strong validation of its competitive standing. Trajectory is improving: gross margin expanding faster than peers, share gains in 800G/1.6T transceivers, and portfolio focus sharpening under new management. The central competitive debate — whether CPO expands or disintermediates pluggable transceiver suppliers — is a group-wide structural uncertainty rather than a Coherent-specific weakness; its vertical integration arguably positions it better than pure transceiver assemblers for either outcome. Competitive Position scores 8.0 on improving trajectory and the NVIDIA embedment.

On the Industrial side, Coherent leads SiC substrates (now a ~75%-owned JV with Denso and Mitsubishi Electric) and excimer/CO₂/solid-state laser systems, but this segment is cyclically weak and being de-emphasized.

## Risk Assessment

**BIS/Huawei export-control investigation (unresolved).** The single largest unquantified overhang. Coherent received a BIS inquiry into past sales to Huawei in January 2025, stopped shipping, and per the Q3 FY2026 10-Q explicitly cannot estimate the loss or penalty range. No determination has been issued. This is a clean tail risk capable of producing a one-off negative headline or penalty not in any model.

**Customer concentration, amplified by NVIDIA.** Two customers each exceeded 10% of FY2025 revenue; the NVIDIA purchase commitment deepens concentration and ties Coherent's fortunes to NVIDIA's roadmap and hyperscaler capex cycles. This is a positive on the way up and a single point of failure on the way down.

**AI demand cyclicality / efficiency risk.** The 10-K explicitly names DeepSeek as an example of efficiency gains that could reduce datacenter transceiver demand. As a high-beta AI proxy, COHR is exposed to any deceleration in AI-capex sentiment.

**Goodwill/intangible impairment risk.** ~$4.4B goodwill + ~$3.0B intangibles from the merger; a demand reversal could trigger material non-cash charges.

**Geopolitical/supply chain.** China tariffs and rare-earth export controls (yttrium, germanium). Offsetting: a February 2026 SCOTUS ruling that IEEPA tariffs were unauthorized creates a potential refund (not booked).

**Working capital.** The inventory build supporting the ramp is appropriate if demand holds; it becomes a liability if it doesn't.

Risk Factors scores 4.0 — the BIS investigation is a genuine unquantified binary, and the valuation provides no cushion against any of these crystallizing.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing meaningful near-term stress. Near-term ATM IV is 106% against 70% one-year realized — rich, in the 86th percentile of the current chain. The term structure is in backwardation (106% near-term vs 89% at six months), which signals the market expects an imminent move. The nearest weekly expiry prices a ±9.2% move, the one-month ±16%. ATM IV has collapsed from ~200% to 106% since early May (post-earnings de-risking), but the absolute level remains elevated. Put/call volume is 1.50 and OI 1.19 — moderately put-tilted. The longer-dated skew turns negative (call-tilted at 3-6 months: -2.1% to -3.8%), suggesting positioning is hedged near-term but constructive on longer horizons. The single unusual print was a $300 June put (3.3x vol/OI). The market is bracing for a large near-term swing in either direction, consistent with a HOLD into resolution rather than a high-conviction directional entry at this level.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($460-500):** AI-capex sustains, the NVIDIA purchase commitment ramps through 2030, 1.6T and CPO expand the optics TAM, and Coherent's vertical integration captures disproportionate share. Margins continue expanding past 38%, FY2027 EPS beats $8.10, and the multiple holds at 50-55x next-year as the growth durability case is confirmed. The Bain preferred removal and balance-sheet cleanup support a re-rating. At ~57x the $8.10 estimate (revised higher on beats), the stock reaches ~$460-500.

**Bear Case ($230-250):** A broad AI-optics sentiment correction compresses the multiple from 45x to ~28-30x next-year EPS. A disappointing CPO transition, an adverse BIS/Huawei determination, hyperscaler capex digestion, or excess inventory triggers a derating. The consensus low target of $230 (-36%) reflects this scenario, which is a multiple-compression risk rather than a fundamental collapse.

The asymmetry from $361 is roughly +30% to a base case against -36% to the bear case — not the favorable skew a BUY requires.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth lean). Reassessment timeline: next earnings (Q4 FY2026, expected August 2026).**

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. A pullback toward $260-290 (near the NVIDIA $256.80 entry / price-protection floor), which would bring next-year multiple to ~32-36x and restore a margin of safety.
2. Resolution of the BIS/Huawei investigation with immaterial penalty, removing the largest unquantified overhang.
3. Operating cash flow re-converging toward net earnings (inventory build normalizing), confirming earnings quality.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. An adverse BIS determination with material financial or shipment consequences.
2. Datacenter & Communications growth decelerating below ~15% YoY, signaling AI-capex digestion.
3. Gross margin reversing below 35%, indicating pricing pressure or CPO disintermediation beginning to bite.
4. A guidance cut accompanying continued inventory build (the obsolescence scenario).

## Conclusion

Coherent is a high-quality, genuinely improving business that has transformed itself under credible new management — AI-driven revenue acceleration, margin expansion, a balance sheet repaired by the NVIDIA investment and Bain conversion, and a strategic position validated by NVIDIA's multi-year commitment. The fundamentals support a constructive view, and the trajectory of prior scores (improving valuation, growth, and competitive position) reflects real progress.

But the prior BUY at 6.9 understated the valuation risk that accumulated as the stock ran to its 52-week high before pulling back. The price fully discounts continued flawless AI-capex growth, while the company carries an unquantified BIS/Huawei investigation, deepening single-customer concentration, a temporarily strained cash-conversion profile, and membership in an AI optics complex pricing near-term stress. The specific, falsifiable concern that prevents a BUY is the valuation: at >50x current-year earnings, rising estimates are already the base case and provide no re-rating cushion, while the consensus low target sits 36% below the current price. I rate COHR HOLD with a growth lean, with a clear path to upgrade on a pullback toward the $260-290 zone or resolution of the BIS overhang.
