**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Annaly Capital Management is the largest publicly traded mortgage REIT in the United States with $135.6 billion in total assets. The company delivered a 20.2% economic return in FY2025, driven by 39 basis points of mortgage basis compression that is unlikely to repeat at current spread levels (87 bps vs. 126 bps a year ago). At $21.66, shares trade at 1.07x book value ($20.21) with a ~13% dividend yield covered at approximately 1.04x by Earnings Available for Distribution (EAD).

The investment case centers on whether the current yield compensates for the rate sensitivity embedded in a 5.6x leveraged Agency MBS portfolio. With the Fed Funds rate at 3.50-3.75%, carry remains attractive, but further rate increases create asymmetric downside to book value. A 25 bps widening in MBS spreads would reduce NAV by approximately 8.6% ($1.74/share), eliminating the current price premium to book.

The company's diversification into residential credit (Onslow Bay securitizations) and mortgage servicing rights provides partial offsets and distinguishes NLY from pure-Agency peers. At current pricing, the stock offers a yield story without meaningful capital appreciation potential, making it an income-oriented hold rather than a buy.

## Company Overview

Annaly Capital Management operates as an internally managed mortgage REIT through three segments:

- **Agency Group (62% of capital, 89% of portfolio):** Mortgage-backed securities guaranteed by Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, or Ginnie Mae. No credit risk, but highly sensitive to interest rates and spreads.
- **Residential Credit Group (19% of capital, 7% of portfolio):** Non-Agency whole loans and securitized products via Onslow Bay Financial, which priced 29 securitizations totaling $15.2 billion in 2025 (38% YoY growth).
- **Mortgage Servicing Rights Group (19% of capital, 4% of portfolio):** MSR portfolio valued at $3.8 billion with weighted average note rate of 3.28%, providing a natural hedge against rising rates.

The internal management structure eliminates the conflicts of interest inherent in externally managed peers (no external management fees, aligned incentives). NLY maintains 38 repo counterparties and operates Arcola Securities, a FINRA-member broker-dealer providing self-clearing capabilities.

**Income Statement Trajectory:**

| EAD/Share | $2.92 | $2.70 | $2.86 |
| Economic Return | 20.2% | 11.9% | 6.1% |

The improvement from 2023's loss to 2025's strong profitability was driven primarily by mortgage basis compression (spreads tightened 39 bps) rather than operational improvements. Economic net interest income rose 30% YoY to $1.94 billion on both wider spreads (1.40% vs. 1.22%) and larger portfolio balances.

**Balance Sheet & Leverage:**

- Economic leverage: 5.6x (conservative relative to 10x policy maximum)
- GAAP leverage: 7.2x (includes non-recourse securitization debt)
- Unencumbered assets: $7.8 billion
- Cash: $2.0 billion
- Repo financing: $81.9 billion at 35-day weighted average maturity with 3% average haircut

The 35-day repo duration creates meaningful rollover risk. In a market dislocation, margin calls could force asset sales at depressed prices, though the $7.8 billion unencumbered asset buffer provides substantial cushion relative to prior cycles.

**Capital Activity & Dilution:**

NLY issued 127.9 million new shares in 2025 via ATM programs ($2.6B proceeds), expanding shares outstanding from ~522 million to ~640 million (22% dilution). While accretive to book value, persistent dilution constrains EAD/share growth. A new $2.5 billion ATM was authorized in December 2025, signaling continued issuance.

**Dividend Sustainability:**

- Implied annual dividend at 13% yield: ~$2.82/share
- FY2025 EAD/share: $2.92
- Coverage ratio: ~1.04x

Coverage is adequate but thin. If NIM compresses due to rate increases or spread widening, EAD pressure would quickly threaten the dividend. Consensus estimates project current year EAD at $3.00/share, suggesting near-term coverage remains intact.

## Growth Analysis

Growth for mortgage REITs operates differently than operating companies. NLY's "growth" derives from:

1. **Portfolio expansion:** Total assets grew from ~$101B to ~$135.6B (2024-2025), though much funded by dilutive equity issuance.
2. **Platform scaling:** Onslow Bay's 38% YoY securitization growth demonstrates organic deal flow generation.
3. **MSR accumulation:** 15% YoY growth positions NLY as the 2nd largest conventional MSR buyer.

Consensus estimates project stable earnings: $3.00/share FY2026 and $2.98/share FY2027. Estimate revisions are modestly positive (+1.7% over 30 days for current year), reflecting stable conditions. The limited upside in estimates reflects that the 2025 spread compression benefit is fully priced and new investment spreads are tighter.

The strategic shift toward non-Agency credit (Q1 2026) represents appropriate capital allocation given compressed Agency spreads, but introduces credit risk that didn't previously exist in the portfolio to the same degree.

## Competitive Landscape

Annaly's competitive position within mortgage REITs remains strong:

- **Scale:** Largest mREIT with 22% weight in iShares Mortgage Real Estate ETF (REM)
- **Internal management:** Eliminates fee conflicts that plague externally managed peers
- **Diversification:** Agency + Residential Credit + MSR provides multi-vector income and natural hedging that pure-Agency competitors (AGNC) lack
- **Proprietary platform:** Onslow Bay's 29 securitizations and self-clearing via Arcola create barriers competitors cannot easily replicate
- **GIC partnership:** Sovereign wealth fund backing provides validation and co-investment capital for residential credit

The competitive position score reflects NLY's dominant market position, though the mortgage REIT industry lacks meaningful moats — the assets are commoditized, and competitive advantages derive primarily from scale (funding access, counterparty relationships) rather than proprietary products.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Interest Rate Sensitivity (HIGH):** With the Fed having already cut 75 bps in 2H 2024, future policy direction remains a key variable for leveraged MBS positions.

2. **GSE Reform (MODERATE-LOW, but catastrophic if triggered):** Any material change to Fannie/Freddie's guarantee structure would fundamentally impair 89% of NLY's portfolio. The reform window appears to be narrowing and IPOs appear stalled, but this remains an existential tail risk. Probability: <10% in next 12 months.

3. **Dividend Sustainability (MODERATE):** At 1.04x coverage, any meaningful NIM compression forces a dividend cut. NLY has cut its dividend multiple times (2013, 2020). The market typically reprices mREIT stocks 15-25% on dividend cuts.

4. **Leverage Amplification:** The 35-day repo maturity means NLY effectively refinances $81.9B of debt every 5 weeks. In a credit event or Treasury market dislocation, access to repo financing could be disrupted regardless of asset quality.

5. **Dilution:** Continued ATM issuance ($2.5B authorized) constrains per-share value creation even in favorable environments.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is consistent with a range-bound income vehicle.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($24-25, +11% to +16% price appreciation + 13% yield = 24-29% total return)

- Fed pauses or cuts rates further, reducing borrowing costs and supporting MBS valuations
- Mortgage basis remains stable or tightens further as GSE $200B purchase directive absorbs supply
- Onslow Bay and MSR platforms continue scaling, providing EAD growth even as Agency spreads compress
- Stock re-rates toward historical P/B premium (~1.10-1.15x) as macro uncertainty resolves

### Bear Case ($18-19, -12% to -17% price decline partially offset by yield)

- Rates rise unexpectedly, pushing long-end yields higher and widening MBS spreads 25-35 bps
- Book value declines to $18.50-19.00 per share, eliminating current premium
- Dividend cut becomes necessary as NIM compresses, triggering additional 10-15% repricing
- Geopolitical volatility pushes long-end yields higher
- Even the 13% yield fails to offset capital losses in this scenario

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (→ BUY):**
- Stock declines to 0.90-0.95x book value ($18.00-19.20) providing margin of safety on income thesis
- Fed clearly signals rate cuts within 6 months, reducing the primary risk to leveraged MBS
- MBS spreads widen to 110+ bps, creating reinvestment opportunity that improves forward carry

**Downgrade Triggers (→ AVOID):**
- Stock rises above 1.15x book value with no change in fundamentals (pure premium expansion)
- EAD/share declines below $2.60 annualized for two consecutive quarters (indicating dividend coverage <0.92x)
- Management announces dividend cut
- Any concrete legislative movement toward GSE privatization

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings (July 2026) is the natural checkpoint.

## Conclusion

Annaly Capital Management is a well-run, institutionally dominant mortgage REIT trading at fair value for the current environment. The 13% dividend yield provides attractive current income, but the 1.07x P/B premium and thin dividend coverage leave minimal margin for error with rates elevated and future policy direction uncertain.

The dividend is covered today, the platform advantages are real, and the diversification into credit/MSR provides better balance than pure-Agency peers. But the absence of a catalyst for capital appreciation, combined with meaningful rate sensitivity and potential for book value impairment, prevents a stronger recommendation. The risk/reward is approximately symmetric at current levels, and the income must justify the position rather than any growth expectation.

Hold for income; upgrade if the stock corrects to a meaningful discount to book or if the rate environment decisively improves.
