## Executive Summary

Golden Entertainment Inc. (GDEN) completed its previously announced go-private transaction on April 30, 2026, and has been delisted from the NASDAQ exchange. The transaction involved VICI Properties acquiring the company's real estate assets for approximately $1.16 billion, while founder and CEO Blake Sartini acquired all operating assets through a newly formed private entity. Public shareholders received 0.902 shares of VICI common stock per GDEN share plus a $2.75 per share cash dividend, implying total consideration of approximately $28-30 per share.

The current "price" of $28.55 reflects the final transaction/delisting value rather than active market trading. There is no further investment thesis for GDEN as a standalone public equity. Former shareholders now hold VICI Properties (NYSE: VICI), a gaming-focused triple-net-lease REIT, if they retained shares through the conversion.

## Company Overview

Golden Entertainment operated as a diversified gaming and hospitality company focused exclusively on Nevada following a series of divestitures in 2023-2024. The business comprised three segments: Nevada Casino Resorts (The STRAT, Aquarius, Edgewater), Nevada Locals Casinos (Arizona Charlie's properties, Pahrump), and Nevada Taverns (72 branded locations in the Las Vegas metro area). Total revenue for FY2025 was $634.9 million with Adjusted EBITDA of $140 million.

The company underwent a strategic transformation from 2022 to 2025, divesting operations in Maryland, Montana, and Nevada's distributed gaming segment for approximately $582.5 million in combined proceeds. This narrowed the business from a $1.12 billion multi-state operator to a $635 million pure Nevada-focused entity, a deliberate repositioning ahead of the management buyout.

**Earnings Track Record:** GDEN missed consensus EPS in all four reported quarters, with particularly large misses in Q3 2025 (-$0.18 actual vs. -$0.04 estimate) and Q4 2025 (-$0.33 actual vs. $0.17 estimate). ## Growth Analysis

The growth story for GDEN as a public entity ended with the delisting. Prior to the transaction:

- Revenue declined 5% organically in FY2025, with Casino Resorts experiencing the steepest drop (-6%)
- Same-store performance was pressured by declining hotel occupancy at The STRAT and Laughlin properties
- The Tavern segment showed modest contraction (-2%), though the asset-light model post-distributed gaming sale provided operational flexibility
- Locals Casinos were the one stable segment (revenue flat YoY) with 45% EBITDA margins

Forward estimates (now moot for public investors) suggested EPS of $0.86 for FY2026 and $1.21 for FY2027, implying significant recovery from the -$0.23 FY2025 result. These estimates were likely predicated on a private company context where capital structure changes (debt elimination via VICI assumption) would flow through to net income.

## Valuation Assessment

The transaction value was approximately $28-30 per share, consisting of 0.902 VICI shares (valued at the time of close) plus $2.75 cash. The single analyst target of $30.00 closely matched the implied deal consideration. At the final trading price of $28.55, the spread to deal value was minimal, consistent with a completed merger.

On the pre-announcement operating metrics, the deal implied EV/EBITDA of approximately 11.4x based on total transaction value of $1.6 billion (including debt assumption) against $140M Adjusted EBITDA. This is a reasonable multiple for a mid-tier regional gaming operator inclusive of real estate value. Among peers, Monarch Casino (MCRI) traded at approximately 8x and Red Rock (RRR) at approximately 9x on an operating basis, though those comparisons are imperfect given GDEN's transaction captured full real estate value.

## Competitive Landscape

Golden occupied a distinct but challenging position in Nevada gaming:
- The STRAT sits on the less-trafficked northern end of Las Vegas Boulevard, isolated from the main Strip corridor
- Laughlin properties (Aquarius, Edgewater) compete in a declining drive-in market dependent on Southern California retirees
- The locals casino segment faces competition from Station Casinos (Red Rock Resorts) which dominates the Las Vegas locals market
- The 72-tavern network was differentiated but margins were compressing post-distributed gaming divestiture

Under private ownership, Sartini's long-term local market knowledge and freedom from quarterly reporting may allow operational improvements that weren't feasible as a public company.
