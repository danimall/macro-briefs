**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Azenta Inc is a $1.1B market cap life sciences company trading at $21.34 per share, approximately 49% below its 52-week high of $41.73 and 63% of book value. The company holds $565M in cash with zero debt, which superficially suggests deep value. However, the operating business is losing money, the Multiomics segment just took a $149M goodwill impairment, management has disclosed three concurrent material weaknesses in internal controls, and FY2026 EPS estimates have been cut 51% in 90 days. The transformation initiated after the 2022 semiconductor divestiture has failed to produce sustainable profitability after 2+ years of execution. The stock's decline reflects genuine structural deterioration, not a temporary market dislocation.

The bear case dominates because the problems are compounding: declining organic revenue (-2% H1 FY2026), worsening segment margins (Multiomics adjusted operating loss widening from -$7.8M to -$14.4M YoY), executive turnover (7+ changes in 18 months), a stalled divestiture (B Medical buyer failed to secure financing), and systematic estimate cuts across all forward periods. The $565M cash pile, while providing a floor, has been shrinking through operating losses and failed initiatives without generating returns for shareholders.

## Company Overview

Azenta operates two segments serving life sciences customers globally. Sample Management Solutions (SMS, ~55% of revenue) provides automated ultra-cold storage systems, cryogenic storage, and sample repository services. Multiomics (~45% of revenue) offers genomic services including NGS, gene synthesis, and Sanger sequencing across 14 laboratories globally. The company serves approximately 14,000 customers across 95 countries.

The company was formerly Brooks Automation, a semiconductor equipment firm that sold its automation division in February 2022 for $2.9B and attempted to redeploy capital into life sciences. Of that $2.9B, approximately $1.3B went to share repurchases and the remainder was consumed by operating losses, acquisitions (subsequently impaired), and the B Medical venture (which has generated $80M+ in losses and whose divestiture is now in jeopardy).

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 continuing operations revenue was $594M, growing 3.6% YoY. However, H1 FY2026 revenue of $293M was essentially flat, with organic revenue declining 2%. FY2026 guidance was cut to $603-$621M (down 2% to up 1% organic) from prior guidance of 3-5% organic growth.

**Profitability:** The company reported adjusted EBITDA of $7.8M in Q2 FY2026 (5.4% margin), down from 8.5% in the prior year. Adjusted gross margin declined 240 bps in H1 FY2026 vs. H1 FY2025 (44.2% vs. 46.6%). The Multiomics segment lost $14.4M in adjusted operating income in H1 FY2026, nearly doubling from -$7.8M in H1 FY2025.

**Balance Sheet:** The company holds $565M in cash and marketable securities with zero debt. Book value per share is approximately $33.75 vs. a market price of $21.34 (0.63x P/B). Altman Z-Score is 4.81, indicating no bankruptcy risk.

**Cash Flow:** FY2025 FCF was $38M (6.5% margin). H1 FY2026 FCF was $19.4M. FY2026 FCF guidance was cut from 30% improvement to 10-15% improvement.

**Capital Allocation:** A $250M buyback was authorized in December 2025 but zero shares have been repurchased despite the stock falling 49% from its high. This inaction undermines the bull case that the stock is cheap relative to intrinsic value.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are severely impaired:

- Organic revenue is declining (-2% H1 FY2026)
- FY2026 EPS estimate has fallen from $0.77 to $0.38 (51% cut) in 90 days
- FY2027 EPS estimate has fallen from $1.04 to $0.61 (41% cut)
- Current quarter EPS estimate has been cut from $0.25 to $0.10 (60% cut in 90 days)
- The company's long-range profitability plan was extended from 2028 to 2029
- Multiomics revenue in Gene Synthesis and Sanger sequencing is declining due to commoditization and Chinese competition

The SMS segment shows some bright spots: recurring revenue from Sample Repository Services is growing, and the installed base of automated stores creates ongoing consumables and service revenue. However, capital equipment sales (Automated Stores, Cryogenic Systems) are weak due to biotech funding constraints and what appears to be product quality issues requiring rework.

The Multiomics segment has no credible path to profitability within 12 months. NGS outsourcing volumes are growing, but pricing pressure from BGI Genomics and others means revenue growth does not translate to margin expansion. Gene Synthesis is being commoditized. The $112.4M goodwill impairment confirms that management's prior fair value assumptions for this segment were materially wrong.

## Valuation Assessment

At $21.34, Azenta has an enterprise value of approximately $535M ($1.1B market cap minus $565M net cash), implying an EV/Revenue of approximately 0.9x on $600M in revenue. For a company generating low-single-digit margins with declining organic growth, this appropriately reflects the limited earnings power of the business.

Forward P/E on $0.38 FY2026 estimates is approximately 56x. On FY2027 estimates of $0.61, it's approximately 35x. These are premium multiples for a company with no demonstrated ability to sustain profitability.

The book value provides a theoretical floor: $33.75/share implies 58% upside if assets are liquidated at carrying value. However, book value includes goodwill/intangibles (post-impairment) that may face further write-downs. The real "hard" book (tangible book + cash) is closer to $26-27/share.

The market is effectively valuing the operating business at negative: take-private value of cash ($565M = $12.26/share) + tangible assets ($14-15/share) exceeds the market cap. This suggests the market believes the operating business will continue to consume cash faster than its assets warrant. Given that it consumed the vast majority of a $2.9B windfall over 4 years without achieving profitability, this skepticism is warranted.

## Competitive Landscape

In SMS, Azenta has a defensible position in automated ultra-cold storage, serving major pharmaceutical companies with proprietary systems. However, the competitive set includes Thermo Fisher (massive scale advantage), Hamilton Company (automation expertise), and other sample management providers. SMS margins are positive but the capital equipment portion is highly cyclical and exposed to biotech funding cycles.

In Multiomics, the competitive position is deteriorating. BGI Genomics provides sequencing services at significantly lower cost from China. Twist Bioscience and GenScript dominate gene synthesis. Eurofins has scale advantages in European markets. Azenta's 14-lab network provides geographic reach but lacks the cost structure or proprietary technology to command pricing power.

The company's TSR of 62% (38% loss from starting value) vs. peer group TSR of 102% over the measurement period confirms that Azenta has materially underperformed its life sciences tools and services peer group.

## Risk Assessment

**Material Weaknesses (Critical):** Three concurrent material weaknesses in internal controls — cash flow statement review, account reconciliation, and cost classification. This is among the most severe internal control failures for a publicly traded company. It increases the probability of future restatements, regulatory action, and auditor-qualification risk.

**Multiomics Strategic Failure:** The $112.4M goodwill impairment came just 6 months after the annual test showed "fair value exceeded carrying amount." This magnitude of deterioration suggests either management was overly optimistic in their prior assessment or competitive conditions worsened dramatically. Either explanation is concerning.

**B Medical Divestiture at Risk:** The buyer failed to secure financing by the March 31, 2026 deadline. If this deal collapses, Azenta faces further write-downs and ongoing management distraction.

**Executive Turnover:** Seven-plus executive changes in 18 months signals organizational instability during a critical transformation period. The new President of Multiomics (appointed April 2026) is the latest in a series of leadership changes.

**China/BIOSECURE Risk:** Three genomics labs in China with growing revenue face regulatory headwinds from the BIOSECURE Act and Bulk Transfer Rule.

### Options Market Signal

The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 79.4% > 6-month 61.6%), which typically signals imminent event risk or unresolved stress. Short interest at 21.3% of float with 4.9 days to cover is very high, reflecting broad institutional conviction in the bearish thesis. ## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($28-33):** SMS segment achieves profitability targets by end of FY2027. Management executes the $250M buyback at depressed prices, reducing share count 15-20%. A strategic acquirer recognizes the value of SMS's recurring revenue base and $565M cash. P/B reverts toward 1.0x.

**Bear Case ($12-15):** Multiomics continues hemorrhaging cash, requiring further impairments. B Medical divestiture collapses, forcing a write-off. Material weaknesses lead to restatement or auditor qualification. Management burns through cash at $50-80M/year of operating losses, steadily eroding the balance sheet floor. The stock trades toward tangible book minus present value of future losses.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Next earnings report (likely August 2026 for Q3 FY2026) represents a concrete catalyst. Current quarter EPS estimate has been cut 57% in 30 days to $0.10, suggesting analysts are still catching down to deteriorating reality. Any formal announcement of B Medical deal collapse would be an additional catalyst.

**Timing Window:** 3-6 months. Q3 FY2026 earnings in August should reveal whether the guidance cut in May was sufficient or if further deterioration is occurring. The B Medical situation should resolve within 60-90 days.

**Short Interest and Squeeze Risk:** SI at 21.3% with 4.9 days to cover is elevated. This is the primary risk to the short thesis — crowding. However, the stock has declined steadily without meaningful squeeze events, suggesting the borrow is available and institutional holders are not covering.

**Borrow and Options Signal:** IV at 79.4% (rich to historical) makes puts expensive. The options market has very thin liquidity (21 contracts across 3 expirations). The most efficient expression would be equity short rather than options.

**Downside Target and Risk/Reward:** Bear target of $12-15 represents 30-44% downside from $21.34. Upside risk: a takeout bid near book value (~$28-30) would represent 30-40% upside. This creates a risk/reward of approximately 1.2:1 to 1.5:1 — below the 2:1 threshold for an active short recommendation.

**Verdict: CROWDED SHORT.** The thesis is correct (operating fundamentals are deteriorating), but 21.3% short interest means much of the bearish view is already expressed, and the $565M cash floor creates meaningful squeeze/takeout risk. The risk/reward for initiating a new short at current levels is unfavorable.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry Criteria (for potential future BUY):**
1. Multiomics segment achieves positive adjusted operating income for two consecutive quarters
2. Material weaknesses remediated (clean SOX opinion)
3. FY2027 EPS estimates stabilize or begin rising (currently falling 41% over 90 days)
4. Management executes meaningful buyback ($100M+) at prices below $25

**Timeline:** Headwinds expected to persist at least 12-18 months. The long-range plan now targets FY2029 for full profitability milestones. Reassess after Q4 FY2026 earnings (November 2026).

## Conclusion

Azenta presents a textbook value trap. The $565M cash pile and 0.63x P/B appear cheap until you examine the trajectory: organic revenue declining, Multiomics losing money at an accelerating rate, three material weaknesses, estimate cuts exceeding 50%, executive instability, and a stalled divestiture. The semiconductor windfall that was supposed to fund a life sciences transformation has been largely consumed without producing sustainable earnings power. The company's own long-range profitability plan has already been extended from 2028 to 2029, and given execution to date, further extensions are probable. At 56x current-year earnings (which are declining) with no positive estimate momentum and severe organizational issues, the stock does not offer compelling risk/reward from either the long or short side. AVOID.
