## Executive Summary

Agilysys is a focused hospitality software provider executing a successful transition from perpetual licenses and hardware to recurring subscription/SaaS revenue. Revenue grew 16.1% in FY2025 (ended March 2025) to $275.6M, with subscription and maintenance revenue up 23.2% to $170M (61.7% of total revenue). Operating margins expanded from 6.6% to 8.5%, and free cash flow reached $52.3M, up 31% YoY. The Book4Time acquisition ($146M) in August 2024 added spa management SaaS capabilities and $104M of goodwill, funded partly by a $50M revolver draw now substantially repaid.

The fundamental trajectory is strong, but the valuation is demanding. At $73.94 (current price), AGYS trades at 111x trailing EPS ($0.82) and approximately 45x the FY2026 consensus EPS estimate of $1.65. Even applying the FY2027 estimate of $2.17, the forward multiple is 34x. Peer median P/E is 28x. The stock has declined roughly 49% from its October 2025 high of $145.25, reflecting disappointment after two earnings misses in the last four quarters (Q2 FY2026 at -10.7% and Q3 FY2026 at -9.6%). Analyst consensus targets range from $120 to $155, implying 62-110% upside; the Street remains constructive, but the stock is trading through a credibility gap. Net insider selling of ~$800K, including CFO and Controller sales near highs, adds caution. HOLD: the business quality warrants continued ownership, but the valuation requires demonstrated execution to recover.

## Company Overview

Agilysys provides end-to-end hospitality technology solutions across POS, PMS, inventory/procurement, payments, and guest experience applications. The company serves casinos, hotels, resorts, cruise lines, corporate foodservice, universities, stadiums, and healthcare facilities. Approximately 2,200 employees, with 68% in India. Revenue is reported in three categories: Products (15% of FY2025 revenue, declining), Subscription & Maintenance (61.7%, growing), and Professional Services (23.3%, growing). The company estimates a $16B TAM in annual recurring revenue. Key competitors include Oracle, Shiji, Amadeus, and Infor.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue of $275.6M represents a 16.1% increase, building on 19.9% growth in FY2024 and 21.8% in FY2023. The growth rate is decelerating modestly but remains healthy. Subscription revenue specifically grew 39.5% in FY2025 (including Book4Time contribution of $11.4M). Products revenue declined 15.8% as customers shift to subscription models — a positive mix shift even if it temporarily depresses top-line optics. International revenue reached 10% of total, up from 6% in FY2024.

**Margins:** Gross margin recovered to 62.4% in FY2025 from 60.7% in FY2024, driven by subscription mix. Subscription and maintenance gross margin is 78.0%, vs. 31.3% for professional services and 46.6% for products. Operating margin expanded to 8.5% (from 6.6%), though this includes $4.6M in other charges and $844K in legal settlements. Normalized EBITDA was $36.0M (13.1% margin), up from $26.1M.

**Quarterly trajectory:** The most recent quarters show lumpy profitability. Q2 FY2026 (Sept 2025) posted $0.41 diluted EPS on $79.3M revenue — the strongest recent quarter. Q3 FY2026 (Dec 2025) showed $0.35 on $80.4M revenue. Q1 FY2026 (Jun 2025) was the weakest at $0.17 on $76.7M, with an operating cash outflow of -$4.3M due to seasonal working capital effects.

**Cash flow:** Operating cash flow was $55.1M in FY2025 (up from $48.2M), with FCF of $52.3M after $2.8M capex. FCF/Net Income was 225%, reflecting high earnings quality. Capital intensity is low. The company generated $23.3M operating cash flow in Q3 FY2026 alone. Cash on hand was $81.5M at December 31, 2025, up from $73M at end of FY2025.

**Balance sheet:** Total debt was $24M at FY2025 end (revolving facility from Book4Time acquisition). By Q2 FY2026, the revolver was fully repaid (no long-term debt on the Q2 balance sheet). Debt/equity peaked at 0.18x and is now essentially zero. Goodwill rose to $130.6M from $32.8M due to Book4Time ($104M). Net tangible book value was $64.4M at FY2025 end, recovering to $108.5M by Q3 FY2026. Deferred tax assets of $66.5M (from NOL carryforwards) provide substantial future cash tax shields: $72.2M in federal NOLs and $111.5M in state NOLs.

## Growth Analysis

**Subscription transition:** Total subscription revenue grew 39.5% in FY2025. The core thesis rests on the shift from one-time software/hardware sales to recurring SaaS revenue, which improves predictability and lifetime customer value. Current deferred revenue of $92.5M (Q3 FY2026) is up from $70.7M at FY2025 end, suggesting strong forward visibility for subscription revenue.

**Book4Time integration:** The $146M acquisition adds spa management SaaS globally. Revenue contribution was $11.4M in the ~7 months post-acquisition. The 20-year useful life assigned to customer relationships suggests management sees durable value. Integration risk is moderate given the relatively small scale relative to Agilysys' core business.

**Product development:** R&D spending was $62.4M (22.6% of revenue), up 10% YoY. The company is investing in AI-powered features (IG PanOptic computer vision kiosk), cloud-native PMS (Stay), and an expanding ecosystem of experience enhancers. The breadth of the product suite (30+ named products) creates cross-sell opportunities.

**International expansion:** Revenue from international operations reached 10% of total, up from 6-7%. The global hospitality market provides further expansion room.

**Estimate revisions:** Consensus estimates are essentially flat over the past 90 days — current year EPS at $1.65 and next year at $2.17. The lack of upward revision momentum after the stock's 50% decline suggests the Street is in "show me" mode.

## Valuation Assessment

At $73.94:

- **Trailing P/E:** 111x (FY2025 EPS of $0.82, which was depressed by the Book4Time acquisition costs and tax normalization after FY2024's $65.5M tax benefit)
- **Forward P/E (FY2026E):** ~45x on $1.65 consensus
- **Forward P/E (FY2027E):** ~34x on $2.17 consensus
- **EV/Revenue (TTM):** ~7.5x on $276M revenue
- **EV/EBITDA:** ~57x on normalized EBITDA of ~$36M (FY2025); improving to ~35-40x on FY2026 projected EBITDA
- **Market cap:** $2.07B
- **FCF yield:** ~2.5% on FY2025 FCF of $52M

**Peer comparison:** The peer median P/E is 28x. AGYS trades at 1.2-1.6x the peer median on forward estimates. The premium is justified by the company's faster organic growth rate (16%+ vs. typical mid-single-digit for peers) and margin expansion trajectory, but a 34-45x multiple leaves no margin for execution disappointment. BL (Blackline) at 142x P/E is the only peer with a higher multiple, and it trades at a similar discount to its 52-week high (-37% vs. AGYS at -49%).

**Analyst targets:** Consensus price targets range from $120 (low) to $155 (high), with a mean of $138 and median of $140. Current analyst ratings: 6 Buy/Strong Buy, 1 Hold, 0 Sell. The target implies 62-110% upside, which is unusually wide for a $2B company and reflects conviction that the drawdown is overdone.

The valuation is the binding constraint. The business quality would support a BUY at ~25-30x forward earnings, which corresponds to roughly $41-$50 on FY2026 estimates or $54-$65 on FY2027 estimates. The current price of $74 requires assuming continued 20%+ EPS growth for multiple years.

## Competitive Landscape

Agilysys competes in a fragmented market against Oracle (much larger), Shiji, Amadeus, Infor, and smaller specialists like Maestro. Its competitive advantages include:

1. **Deep hospitality focus:** 45+ years of specialization, unlike Oracle/Amadeus which serve hospitality as one vertical among many
2. **Comprehensive product suite:** POS + PMS + I&P + payments + experience enhancers in a single ecosystem
3. **Cloud transition leadership:** Stay PMS is cloud-native; subscription growth of 39.5% suggests customers are adopting
4. **Customer stickiness:** Mission-critical software with high switching costs; maintenance/subscription renewal rates appear strong given growing deferred revenue balances

Risks to competitive position include larger competitors investing heavily in cloud hospitality solutions, potential consolidation, and the fact that Agilysys is a small player against well-resourced incumbents.

## Risk Assessment

**Hospitality cyclicality:** Agilysys is directly exposed to hospitality capex cycles. In the current stagflationary environment with $105+ oil, elevated travel costs, and consumer spending weakness, hospitality operators may defer technology investments. The 10-K explicitly flags macroeconomic conditions, changes in travel patterns, and tariff impacts as risks.

**Concentration and scale:** At $276M revenue, Agilysys is materially smaller than Oracle or Amadeus. Scale disadvantages in R&D investment, global sales coverage, and brand recognition persist.

**Acquisition integration:** Book4Time added $104M of goodwill and $61M of intangible assets to a company with $266M in equity. If the acquisition underperforms, impairment charges could be material.

**Earnings volatility:** Two misses in the last four quarters (Q2 FY2026 at -10.7% and Q3 FY2026 at -9.6%) create execution uncertainty. At 45x forward earnings, misses are punished severely — the stock dropped 20%+ after the January 2026 Q3 report.

**India concentration:** 68% of employees are in India, with 197K sqft of office space in Chennai. This creates operational risk from regulatory changes, currency fluctuation, and geopolitical events. The company already noted immigration policy risk for employees sponsored for US visas.

**Key person risk:** CEO Ramesh Srinivasan has led the transformation since 2017 and holds ~800K shares. His departure would create significant uncertainty.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case
- Subscription transition continues at 25-30% growth, driving margin expansion toward 15%+ operating margins within 2-3 years
- Book4Time integration succeeds, adding cross-sell into global spa management
- International expansion from 10% to 20%+ of revenue provides additional growth runway
- FCF continues expanding, enabling further acquisitions or buybacks
- $66.5M deferred tax asset shields cash taxes for several years
- At $74, the stock has already priced in significant skepticism; consensus targets imply 60-110% upside
- Hospitality IT spend remains resilient as operators invest in guest experience differentiation

### Bear Case
- Hospitality capex cycle turns down in stagflationary environment (oil at $105, consumer weakness)
- Revenue growth decelerates below 15% as subscription transition matures and macro weighs
- Operating leverage disappoints as SBC ($17.8M, 6.5% of revenue) and R&D investment continue rising
- Two recent earnings misses signal diminishing visibility; consensus estimates may need further cuts
- 45x forward earnings leaves no margin for error in a rising-rate environment
- Book4Time goodwill of $104M represents a binary risk if integration stumbles
- Small-cap software in a macro environment hostile to high-multiple stocks

## Conclusion

Agilysys is a high-quality, focused hospitality software company executing well on a subscription transition. Revenue growth of 16%+, expanding margins (62.4% gross, 8.5% operating), and strong FCF generation ($52M) support the long-term thesis. The Book4Time acquisition adds strategic value. However, at ~45x FY2026E earnings, the valuation requires sustained double-digit growth and margin expansion for years. The recent 50% drawdown has improved risk/reward, but two earnings misses in the last four quarters, net insider selling, and macro headwinds to hospitality spending argue against aggressive positioning. The business trajectory warrants continued ownership for current holders, but new capital should wait for either (a) a more compelling valuation entry below ~$55-60 (25-30x FY2027E), or (b) demonstrated execution with two consecutive earnings beats restoring credibility.

**Recommendation: HOLD**
