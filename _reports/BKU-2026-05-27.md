**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

BankUnited is a $35 billion-asset bank executing a multi-year strategic transformation from a diversified bank with heavy residential mortgage exposure toward a regionally focused commercial and small-business lender. The transformation is producing measurable results: net interest margin expanded from 2.56% (2023) to 2.99% (Q1 2026), non-interest bearing demand deposit accounts grew 20% in 2025, and tangible book value per share increased 10% year-over-year to $40.05. Management is simultaneously returning capital aggressively via $60M in Q1 2026 buybacks and a 6% dividend increase.

The complication is credit quality. While Q1 2026 showed improvement (NPAs down 26% sequentially), the quarter still produced the first earnings miss in four quarters (-14.5% vs. consensus) and elevated C&I net charge-offs at an annualized 1.67%. The allowance for credit losses is declining (0.87% of total loans) at a time when CRE office exposure ($1.4B) remains a secular risk and the macro environment features elevated rates and a weakening consumer. The stock's 33% appreciation from its June 2025 lows reflects the market pricing in transformation progress, leaving limited upside unless credit improvement sustains and loan growth accelerates.

## Company Overview

BankUnited operates through a single national bank subsidiary, BankUnited N.A., providing commercial and consumer banking primarily in Florida (primary market), New York Tri-State, Dallas, Atlanta, and Charlotte. The bank has 1,785 employees and manages $35.4B in assets with a loan portfolio of $24.2B (68.6% commercial). Key deposit verticals include title insurance (~$4.1B) and HOA (~$2.3B), specialized funding sources that provide non-interest bearing deposits at scale.

The strategic direction: reduce residential mortgage runoff ($7.6B declining at ~$120M/quarter), grow core commercial relationships, replace wholesale/brokered funding with relationship deposits, and exit legacy businesses (franchise finance, equipment leasing). Capital return via buybacks supplements per-share value creation from the organic mix shift.

## Financial Analysis

**Earnings Trajectory:**
- Net income: $179M (2023) → $232M (2024) → $268M (2025) → $62M Q1 2026 ($248M annualized)
- EPS: $3.08 (2024) → $3.53 (2025); consensus $4.10 FY2026, $4.57 FY2027
- Q1 2026 miss: $0.83 actual vs. $0.97 estimate (-14.5%), driven by elevated C&I charge-offs and NIM compression

**NIM Mechanics:**
NIM expanded consistently from 2.56% to 3.06% (Q4 2025), then compressed 7bps to 2.99% in Q1 2026 as floating-rate assets repriced faster than deposit costs declined. With the Fed signaling rates on hold or potentially higher, this dynamic reverses: asset yields stay elevated while deposit competition can gradually ease. BKU's rate sensitivity modeling shows NIM declines 3.6% in Year 1 of a -200bp scenario, which appears unlikely in the near term given the hawkish policy stance.

**Capital Position:**
- CET1: 12.2% (well above 10.5% well-capitalized threshold)
- TCE/TA: 8.3%
- TBV/share: $40.05 (current price = 1.16x TBV)
- Loan-to-deposit: 82.3% (conservative)

**Capital Return:**
$60M in Q1 2026 buybacks, $200M additional authorization (January 2026). At $46.64, this authorization covers ~4.3M shares (5.9% of outstanding shares). Quarterly dividend of $0.33 ($1.32 annual) yields 2.8% at current price. Combined payout is aggressive for a bank in transformation mode.

**Funding Improvement:**
- NIDDA: $9.1B (31% of deposits), up $1.5B YoY
- Brokered deposits: $4.6B, down from $5.2B year-ago
- Wholesale funding/total assets: 18-20%, well within 37.5% policy limit
- Spot deposit APY declined to 2.09% at Q1 2026 end

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are moderate. The transformation creates a natural NIM tailwind (higher-yielding commercial replacing lower-yielding residential), but absolute loan growth is limited:

- Core loans grew $786M in FY2025 and only $9M in Q1 2026 (seasonal)
- Residential runoff absorbs commercial origination — net loan growth is minimal
- Total assets have been relatively flat at $35B for multiple quarters
- Consensus estimates: $4.10 FY2026 (-2.7% revision over 90 days), $4.57 FY2027 (+1.4% revision)

The current-year estimate revision is mildly negative (-2.5% over 30 days), reflecting the Q1 miss. Next-year estimates are stable-to-rising, suggesting analysts view the credit issues as transitory. However, there is no evidence of an earnings inflection — this is a low-single-digit EPS grower with modest per-share accretion from buybacks.

The geographic growth opportunities (Texas, Georgia expansion) are real but early-stage and competing against well-entrenched incumbents. Revenue diversification through non-interest income remains limited (fee income ratio not provided but implied to be a strategic priority).

- 10.2x FY2027 consensus of $4.57

**Peer Comparison:**

- Peer median yield: 3.8% (BKU's 2.8% is below, reflecting lower payout ratio)
- Peer median 52-week discount: -3% (BKU at -10.5% from 52-week high)

BKU is not obviously cheap relative to peers. The 1.16x TBV multiple is appropriate for a bank generating ~9% ROE, roughly breakeven on cost-of-equity. For the stock to sustain a premium to peers, ROE needs to improve toward 11-12% (which the transformation should achieve if NIM holds above 3% and credit normalizes).

Analyst consensus target: $53.00 median (+13.6% upside), range $46-$60. The low target ($46) is essentially current price, suggesting limited downside in the base case but also limited conviction in material upside from the sell-side.

## Competitive Landscape

BKU occupies a challenging middle ground: too large to benefit from community bank relationship premiums, too small to compete with JPMorgan/BofA on rates and technology in Florida commercial banking. Its differentiation rests on:

1. **Specialized deposit verticals** (title insurance, HOA) — provide structurally low-cost funding but are concentrated and potentially disruption-vulnerable
2. **Entrepreneurial culture with lean staffing** (1,785 employees for a $35B bank)
3. **Florida market knowledge** — particularly in commercial real estate and small business

The competitive position is stable but not strengthening materially. Commercial loan growth of $786M against a $16.6B book represents ~5%, roughly in line with peer growth rates. The bank is improving its existing balance sheet quality rather than gaining share.

CRE office exposure ($1.4B, declining from $1.77B) is being managed down through natural amortization and selective dispositions, which is prudent but reduces earning assets. The weighted average DSCR of 1.78x and LTV of 64.2% on the office book provide reasonable buffer, though these are backward-looking metrics that may not reflect current market values.

## Risk Assessment

**Credit Quality (Primary Risk):**
The most significant near-term risk is whether Q1 2026's elevated charge-offs (0.61% annualized, 1.67% C&I NCO) represent a clearing event or the beginning of a broader deterioration cycle. Supporting the clearing thesis: NPAs fell 26%, criticized assets fell 12%, and pass-rated loans rose to 94%. Against it: ACL coverage is declining (0.87%) precisely when CRE stress may be building, and the current macro features rising real rates with potential for CRE → regional bank stress transmission.

**Uninsured Deposit Concentration:**
~$12B in adjusted uninsured/uncollateralized deposits remains a structural vulnerability in a post-SVB world. The 136-138% liquidity coverage ratio provides buffer, but commercial deposit bases can move quickly in confidence crises.

**CRE Office:**
$1.4B office exposure with 33% maturing within 12 months ($469M). In the current rate environment, refinancing these loans at higher rates creates borrower stress. The declining ACL coverage on office (1.69%, down from 2.30%) suggests management is becoming more optimistic about this portfolio — if that proves premature, reserve builds would compress earnings.

**Florida Geographic Concentration:**
Hurricane exposure, rising insurance costs, and climate-related property value uncertainty create idiosyncratic risk for a Florida-concentrated bank.

### Options Market Signal

Options IV has compressed significantly (54.3% → 35.1% over the past three weeks), suggesting post-earnings uncertainty is resolving. However, the term structure is in backwardation (near-term 35.1% > 6-month 32.4%), which typically indicates the market still prices near-term event risk above longer-term baseline uncertainty. The put/call skew is slightly call-heavy (-6.0%), consistent with the stock having already declined from its February highs and positioning being marginally constructive. The overall options picture is neutral-to-mildly-positive with no extreme signals in either direction.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($55-60, +18-29%):**
- Q1 credit issues prove transitory; NPAs continue declining, NCOs normalize to 0.25-0.35% in Q2-Q3
- NIM stabilizes at 3.0-3.1% in a higher-for-longer rate environment
- Buyback execution at current prices accelerates TBV/share growth toward 12-15% annualized
- ROE improves to 10-11% over 12-18 months as transformation matures
- Multiple re-rates to 1.3-1.4x TBV on improved profitability trajectory

**Bear Case ($37-40, -15-21%):**
- CRE office losses materialize as $469M in loans mature into a weak market
- Additional C&I problem credits emerge, requiring reserve builds that compress EPS to $3.50-3.75
- Deposit cost competition intensifies, capping NIM below 2.85%
- Florida-specific economic weakness (insurance costs, housing correction) impacts borrower quality
- Market reverts to 1.0x TBV on ROE stagnation around 8%

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Two consecutive quarters of normalized NCOs (<0.35% annualized) with stable or improving ACL coverage
- NIM expansion above 3.10% for two quarters
- Core loan growth accelerating to >8% annualized

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- NCOs exceeding 0.50% annualized for two consecutive quarters
- ACL build of >$50M in a single quarter
- Loss of a major deposit vertical relationship (title insurance, HOA)
- TBV declining sequentially

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (late July 2026). This quarter will clarify whether Q1's charge-offs were a cleaning event or a trend.

## Conclusion

BankUnited is a competently managed regional bank executing a sensible transformation strategy in a challenging environment. The NIM expansion and deposit improvement are real accomplishments. However, the Q1 2026 earnings miss, elevated charge-offs, and declining reserve coverage introduce meaningful uncertainty about near-term earnings power. At 1.16x TBV and ~11.4x FY2026 earnings, the stock is fairly valued for a bank generating 9% ROE — not expensive but not offering a margin of safety against credit deterioration. The 2.8% dividend yield provides income support while waiting for clarification on the credit cycle. HOLD with an income orientation, pending evidence that Q1 credit clearance represents a turning point rather than early-cycle normalization.
