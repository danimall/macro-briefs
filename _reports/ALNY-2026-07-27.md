**Research Date: July 27, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Alnylam is the clear leader in RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics, with six approved drugs and a validated siRNA/GalNAc delivery platform. The investment story now centers on a single event: the March 2025 U.S. approval of AMVUTTRA in ATTR cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM), a far larger indication than the drug's original polyneuropathy label. That launch drove Q1 2026 total revenue to $1,167M (+96% YoY), pushed AMVUTTRA to $889.9M in the quarter (+187%), and carried the company to its first sustained GAAP profits after roughly two decades of losses. Net income swung to $206M in Q1 2026 from a loss of $18M a year prior, and operating cash flow turned positive.

The stock has nonetheless declined 45% from its October 2025 high of $495.55 to $271.77, and the reasons for that drawdown are the crux of the analysis. The drawdown is not indiscriminate volatility on unchanged fundamentals. Next-year consensus EPS has been revised down 4.8% over 90 days and 2.4% over the last 30 days, indicating the launch trajectory that supports the multiple is decelerating relative to the extrapolation baked into the peak price. The Q4 2025 print, where AMVUTTRA ATTR revenue disappointed even as adjusted EPS beat, established that this stock trades on launch-curve slope, not headline beats. Layered on top are a new DOJ subpoena on government price reporting (October 2025), a second FDA advertising letter (April 2026), structural gross-margin erosion from escalating Sanofi royalties on AMVUTTRA, and a capital structure whose true cost is hidden in royalty-monetization liabilities carrying effective rates of 32-49%.

We rate ALNY a HOLD (growth_hold). The platform, franchise durability, and the July 2026 competitive tailwind (AstraZeneca/Ionis' Wainua failed its ATTR-CM trial) support a constructive multi-year view, and the drawdown has removed much of the froth. But at ~117x trailing and ~29x current-year consensus EPS with next-year estimates falling and a stacked idiosyncratic risk list, the setup does not offer the asymmetric entry or positive estimate momentum a BUY requires. We would upgrade on a nucresiran Phase 3 signal, an estimate-revision inflection, or a pullback into the low-$200s.

## Company Overview

Alnylam pioneered and commercialized the first RNAi medicines, using small interfering RNA to silence disease-causing genes upstream of protein production. The company operates as a single business segment and has six approved products:

- **AMVUTTRA (vutrisiran)** — the franchise anchor, approved for hATTR polyneuropathy and, since March 2025, ATTR-CM. Quarterly subcutaneous dosing.
- **ONPATTRO (patisiran)** — first-generation TTR drug in decline as patients migrate to AMVUTTRA.
- **GIVLAARI (givosiran)** — acute hepatic porphyria.
- **OXLUMO (lumasiran)** — primary hyperoxaluria type 1.
- **Leqvio (inclisiran)** — cholesterol, commercialized by Novartis; Alnylam earns royalties (partly monetized to Blackstone).
- **Qfitlia (fitusiran)** — hemophilia, commercialized by Sanofi (approved March 2025); royalty income.

The moat rests on a deep IP position around siRNA chemistry and GalNAc-conjugate delivery, which Alnylam licenses to competitors including Ionis, Arrowhead, and Takeda, generating royalty exposure to the entire field. The "Alnylam 2030" strategy targets 10 tissue types, 40+ clinical programs, at least two new transformative non-TTR medicines, 25%+ revenue CAGR, and roughly 30% non-GAAP operating margin by 2030.

## Financial Analysis

The central financial story is the inflection to profitability. After an accumulated deficit that still stands at $6.50B, Alnylam turned its first full-year GAAP profit in FY2025 (net income $313.7M) and accelerated in Q1 2026.

| Metric | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total revenue | $1,167M | $594M | +96% |
| Net product revenue | $1,036M | $469M | +121% |
| AMVUTTRA total | $889.9M | $310M | +187% |
| — AMVUTTRA US | $702.6M | $198M | +255% |
| Net income | $206M | –$18M | swing to profit |
| Diluted EPS | $1.51 | –$0.14 | — |

Nearly all growth is the U.S. ATTR-CM launch (+$505M U.S. YoY). Europe (+41%) and rest-of-world (+132%) are ramping. FY2025 combined net product revenue was approximately $3.0B (+81% YoY). The rare-disease franchise (GIVLAARI +11%, OXLUMO +22%) is steady but small at roughly $126M per quarter combined.

Three items warrant careful attention.

First, gross margin is compressing as revenue grows, which is the opposite of the usual operating-leverage story. COGS rose to 20.0% of product revenue from 15.0% because Alnylam pays Sanofi escalating royalties on AMVUTTRA, stepping from 15% up to 30% above $1.5B in annual sales. Management explicitly guides COGS percentage higher in 2026. As AMVUTTRA scales, this structurally erodes margin, and it is the reason the next-generation royalty-free asset (nucresiran) matters as much for economics as for the pipeline.

Second, the reported debt understates the true cost of capital. Beyond roughly $1B of convertibles, Alnylam carries an estimated $1.7B+ of Blackstone royalty and development-funding liabilities. The Leqvio royalty sale (2020) raised $1B upfront at an ~11% effective rate and carries a step-up trigger: if Blackstone has not collected $1B by December 31, 2029, its Leqvio share rises from 50% to 55%, permanently cutting Alnylam's retained royalty. Development-funding arrangements carry effective rates of 49% (vutrisiran) and 32% (zilebesiran), and the ATTR-CM approval triggered a $175M payment obligation. This is why interest expense of roughly $69M per quarter dwarfs the nominal coupon on the debt. A standard leverage screen would miss this.

Third, R&D ran $365M (+38%) on Phase 3 ZENITH, TRITON-CM, and TRITON-PN, and SG&A was $323M (+34%) on the AMVUTTRA launch. The reinvestment is deliberate and consistent with the 2030 strategy, but it means reported profitability, while now positive, remains modest relative to the market cap. Cash and marketable securities of $3.01B provide a comfortable liquidity cushion against the 2027 ($362.8M) and 2028 ($661.3M) convertibles.

One near-term GAAP catalyst is telegraphed: management may reverse the U.S. deferred-tax-asset valuation allowance within 12 months given sustained profitability, which would create a one-time reported income boost and signal confidence in durable earnings.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis is well-supported and broad-based within the ATTR franchise, but the market is now discounting deceleration at the margin. The estimate-revision data is the key tell:

| Period | Current Est. | 90d Ago | 90d Revision |
|--------|-------------|---------|--------------|
| Current Quarter | $2.16 | $2.08 | +3.4% |
| Next Quarter | $2.41 | $2.54 | –5.0% |
| Current Year | $9.42 | $9.21 | +2.3% |
| Next Year | $13.44 | $14.12 | –4.8% |

Near-term estimates have firmed slightly, but the forward-looking next-quarter and next-year figures are falling. This does not meet the strong-BUY inflection signal (+30% revisions with accelerating revenue). The revenue growth rate is high, but the negative direction of out-year estimate revisions is the more important forward signal, and it argues against the thesis that consensus is systematically underestimating the franchise. It is being reset lower.

The earnings-surprise record is volatile: +177% (Q3 2025), +54% (Q1 2026), but –28.5% in Q4 2025, when AMVUTTRA ATTR revenue disappointed. The Q4 miss is instructive because it shows the stock trades on the shape of the launch curve, not on aggregate beats. Enrollment in the TRITON-CM trial expanded from 1,250 to 1,750 patients on faster-than-expected demand, a positive underlying signal, and Regeneron submitted a cemdisiran NDA (myasthenia gravis) in April 2026. The pipeline is deep, but the near-term earnings power hinges almost entirely on AMVUTTRA's continued ATTR-CM penetration against oral competitors.

The durable growth optionality sits in nucresiran, the next-generation TTR silencer offering twice-yearly dosing with >90% knockdown and carrying no Sanofi royalty. A successful Phase 3 readout would both defend the franchise against oral and gene-editing threats and materially improve gross margin. Targeted launch is 2030.

## Valuation Assessment

At $271.77, ALNY trades at roughly 117x trailing GAAP EPS of $2.33 and approximately 29x current-year consensus EPS of $9.42, falling to ~20x next-year consensus of $13.44. The trailing multiple is not the right frame given the profitability inflection; on forward earnings the stock is expensive but not egregiously so for a company growing revenue near triple digits.

The relevant tension is that the forward multiple is defensible only if the growth durability holds, and the estimate-revision data shows out-year consensus being trimmed. A stock at ~20x next-year EPS on 30%+ revenue growth would ordinarily screen as reasonable, but two factors temper that. First, next-year estimates are falling, so the "20x next-year" figure is on a shrinking denominator that may fall further. Second, the earnings base carries structural margin erosion from Sanofi royalties and a hidden high-cost capital structure that limits the flow-through of revenue growth to per-share earnings.

Analyst targets span a wide range (low $285, mean $432, median $425, high $551), with the current price sitting below the entire target distribution. On an inflection stock with rising estimates and a reasonable multiple, we would treat consensus targets as floors. Here, with next-year estimates falling, the targets are stale in the other direction and should not be over-weighted as evidence of upside.

We resist scoring the stock highly on valuation simply because it has fallen from its high. Prior appreciation is not a valuation input, and neither is prior decline. The relevant question is price versus forward fundamentals today, and on that basis the stock is fairly valued to modestly expensive, with the growth genuinely present but the estimate direction working against a cheapness argument. A superficially reasonable forward multiple masks decelerating revision momentum, so we hold the valuation score in neutral territory rather than reward it.

## Competitive Landscape

The ATTR amyloidosis market is expanding rapidly on improving diagnosis and has become the most contested cardiology-rare-disease battleground in biopharma. Alnylam's positioning has both a near-term tailwind and a structural long-term threat.

The July 2026 Wainua failure is a clear positive: AstraZeneca/Ionis' eplontersen failed its late-stage ATTR-CM trial, removing a would-be direct silencer/antisense competitor from the cardiomyopathy market and protecting AMVUTTRA (and BridgeBio's Attruby) share. This is a genuine competitive-position improvement, not yet fully reflected in sentiment.

Against that, AMVUTTRA competes with oral, lower-list-price stabilizers — Pfizer's Vyndaqel/Vyndamax and BridgeBio's Attruby (acoramidis). AMVUTTRA counters with a silencing mechanism (knockdown versus stabilization) and infrequent dosing, but it must overcome a convenience and price gap against oral rivals. The longer-term structural threat is Intellia/Regeneron's nex-z, a CRISPR gene-editing therapy with potential one-time dosing, which would challenge the economics of a chronically dosed injectable. Novo Nordisk's coramitug and AstraZeneca/Alexion's cliramitug add to the Phase 3 pipeline.

Alnylam's internal answer is nucresiran, which if successful defends the franchise and improves margin. The company also benefits from the entire RNAi field's growth via GalNAc licensing royalties, which provides a hedge even as it competes on individual products. The competitive trajectory is stable-to-improving in the near term (Wainua removal) but faces a credible structural disruptor over the multi-year horizon.

## Risk Assessment

The risk list is unusually stacked for a profitable large-cap biotech, and several items are new or recently surfaced:

1. **Single-product concentration.** AMVUTTRA is ~86% of net product revenue. Any safety signal, share loss, reimbursement setback, or manufacturing disruption would be materially damaging. This is the dominant risk.

2. **DOJ pricing subpoena (added October 2025).** The U.S. Attorney (D. Mass) sought documents on government price reporting for all four marketed products and on distributor fee/discount arrangements. Management expects significant expense regardless of outcome. This is a new, material legal overhang with an undefined resolution timeline.

3. **FDA advertising scrutiny.** Two untitled letters on AMVUTTRA DTC advertising, including a new one on April 23, 2026 (consumer website), reflecting a broader FDA DTC-enforcement crackdown.

4. **Structural margin erosion.** The escalating Sanofi royalty works against the drug's own success and is a headwind to per-share earnings flow-through.

5. **Hidden capital cost.** Blackstone royalty and development-funding liabilities at 32-49% effective rates, plus the Leqvio step-up trigger that could permanently cut retained royalties if the $1B collection threshold is not met by December 31, 2029.

6. **Competitive/pipeline binary risk.** Setbacks in nucresiran or zilebesiran Phase 3 readouts carry asymmetric downside given how much of the 2030 thesis is priced.

7. **IP and pricing policy.** University of Texas ONPATTRO suit (claim construction July 2026), IRA Medicare negotiation, most-favored-nation orders, and Medicaid changes all pressure net pricing.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing near-term stress. ATM IV has risen to 58.5% from 44.6% in early June, well above the 37.6% one-year historical vol (a 20.9-point spread), sitting at the 76th percentile within the current chain. The term structure is in backwardation (58.5% near-term versus 49.4% at six months), consistent with the market pricing an imminent catalyst, most likely the next earnings print. The implied one-month move is ±12.5%. Put/call skew is balanced (2.9%) and OI ratio is 0.82, so positioning is not aggressively bearish, but the P/C OI ratio rose from 0.61 as the stock fell, and IV expanded on a declining price. Short interest is modest at 5.8% of float (3.6 days to cover), so this is not a crowded short and there is little squeeze fuel. The options market is bracing for a volatile catalyst rather than signaling a directional collapse, which argues for patience on entry rather than chasing.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($400-450+).** AMVUTTRA sustains high ATTR-CM penetration as diagnosis expands and the Wainua failure protects share; Europe and rest-of-world ramps continue; the DTA valuation-allowance reversal delivers a one-time GAAP boost and signals durable profitability; nucresiran Phase 3 succeeds, defending the franchise and improving margin by removing the Sanofi royalty; and the broader RNAi platform delivers on the 2030 blockbuster targets. In this scenario, out-year estimates re-inflect upward and the stock re-rates back toward the analyst mean.

**Bear Case ($200-230).** Oral stabilizers (Attruby, Vyndaqel) and eventual gene-editing (nex-z) compress AMVUTTRA's share and pricing; single-product concentration bites; the DOJ pricing investigation produces a material settlement or ongoing expense; Sanofi royalty escalation and the high-cost Blackstone liabilities keep per-share earnings growth well below revenue growth; and next-year estimates continue their downward revision. The forward multiple compresses toward 15x on a shrinking estimate base.

The current setup sits between these, with the estimate-revision data leaning modestly toward the cautious side and the Wainua tailwind and drawdown leaning constructive.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** The multi-year growth thesis is intact and the platform is genuinely differentiated, but the near-term risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric, and forward estimate momentum is negative.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Positive nucresiran or zilebesiran Phase 3 data, or an estimate-revision inflection where next-year consensus stops falling and turns positive for two consecutive prints.
- AMVUTTRA ATTR-CM revenue re-accelerating above consensus for two consecutive quarters, confirming the launch curve is steepening rather than flattening.
- A pullback into the low-$200s, where the forward multiple (~15-16x next-year EPS) would offer a genuine margin of safety against the risk stack.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- A material adverse development in the DOJ pricing investigation (settlement in the hundreds of millions or expanded scope).
- An AMVUTTRA safety signal, a nucresiran/zilebesiran trial failure, or accelerating share loss to oral competitors visible in sequential revenue.
- Next-year estimates falling more than 10% cumulatively while the multiple stays above 25x forward.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report, and the University of Texas claim-construction ruling expected July 2026. The DTA valuation-allowance decision within the next 12 months is a discrete positive to watch.

## Conclusion

Alnylam has crossed from a two-decade pre-profit RNAi pioneer to a profitable commercial company anchored by a fast-ramping ATTR-CM franchise, and the Wainua failure genuinely improves its competitive standing. Those are real positives, and the drawdown from the October high has removed the peak-froth risk that made the stock uninvestable at $495.

The reasons to wait rather than buy are equally concrete. Next-year consensus EPS is being revised down, which undercuts the case that the market is underestimating the franchise; the stock still trades at ~29x current-year and ~117x trailing earnings; single-product concentration is extreme; the DOJ pricing subpoena and the second FDA advertising letter are unresolved overhangs; and the true cost of capital embedded in Blackstone royalty monetizations plus escalating Sanofi royalties structurally caps the flow-through of revenue growth to per-share earnings. This is a high-quality business at a full price with decelerating forward estimates and a demanding risk list.

We rate ALNY a HOLD (growth_hold). The path to a BUY runs through either a nucresiran signal, an estimate-revision turn, or a re-entry in the low-$200s.

> **[FACT-CHECK FLAG]** The following corrections may warrant revisiting this recommendation: the trailing P/E was overstated as ~68x; using GAAP EPS of $2.33 the correct trailing multiple is ~117x, which strengthens rather than weakens the "full price" case underpinning the HOLD.
