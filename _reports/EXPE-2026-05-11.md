## Executive Summary

Expedia Group delivered a strong Q1 2026 quarter with revenue of $3.43B (+15% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA of $542M (+83%), confirming that the multi-year platform unification and B2B expansion strategy is generating meaningful operating leverage. The company simultaneously reduced debt by ~$1.7B, authorized a new $5B buyback, and raised its quarterly dividend 20%. At ~11x forward FY26 EPS of $19.67, the stock appears reasonably valued relative to its improving earnings power and 12%+ annual FCF yield.

The complication is timing. Consumer discretionary spending is under significant pressure from macroeconomic headwinds. Management itself noted "weaker than expected travel demand in the United States in the first half of 2025" and geopolitical pressures. Options markets are pricing 58% near-term IV (vs. 47% historical) with steep backwardation and a 2.06x put/call OI ratio — informed participants see near-term downside risk. The stock dropped 9% on the day following earnings (May 8), suggesting the market is skeptical that Q1 strength persists into a weakening consumer environment.

The thesis is fundamentally constructive but timing-dependent. Expedia is executing well operationally, generating exceptional FCF, and returning capital aggressively. The B2B segment provides structural growth insulation. But the stock needs summer travel season to hold — a miss on Q2/Q3 demand in the current macro would likely send shares back toward $190-200. This is a growth-oriented hold that becomes a BUY on either a pullback to $190-195 or confirmation of sustained demand in Q2 reporting.

## Company Overview

Expedia operates the world's second-largest online travel marketplace behind Booking Holdings. The platform encompasses approximately 3.6 million lodging properties (including 2.4M alternative accommodations via Vrbo), 500+ airlines, and extensive ancillary services across car rental, cruise, activities, and insurance.

**Revenue composition (FY2025):**
- B2C (64%): Consumer-facing brands — Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo. Grew 2% in 2025.
- B2B (33%): Technology, supply, and distribution via Rapid API to airlines, corporate travel, banks, and retailers. Grew 18% in 2025, accelerating to 25% in Q1 2026.
- trivago (3%): Majority-owned metasearch platform. Grew 33% from a low base.

The business model is 70% merchant (Expedia collects payment at booking, remits to supplier after stay), creating a favorable working capital cycle that effectively provides interest-free float from travelers.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue & Growth Acceleration

Revenue growth has been accelerating: +7% (2024) → +8% (2025) → +15% (Q1 2026). The acceleration is driven almost entirely by B2B, which grew revenue from $3.4B (2023) to $4.8B (2025) — a 43% increase in two years — and posted 25% growth in Q1 2026. B2C remains the larger segment but is growing low-single-digits.

Gross bookings reached $119.6B in FY2025 (+8%), with room night growth of 8% in FY25 and 6% in Q1 2026. Revenue margin (revenue/gross bookings) has held flat at 12.3% for three consecutive years despite the mix shift toward lower-take-rate B2B, indicating improving unit economics within both segments.

### Profitability Inflection

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | Q1 2026 (ann.) |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|----------------|
| Operating Margin | 8.0% | 9.6% | 12.7% | N/A |
| Adj. EBITDA Margin | 20.9% | 21.4% | 23.8% | 26.7% (est.) |
| FCF Margin | ~18% | ~20% | 21.1% | ~27% |

Cost of revenue declined from 12.3% to 9.9% of revenue over the period (payment/service efficiencies). Technology & content fell from 10.6% to 8.7% (platform optimization post-unification). G&A declined from 6.0% to 5.2% (restructuring benefits). The platform unification completed in 2023 is now generating its expected cost efficiencies, with Q1 2026 showing the most dramatic leverage yet.

### Cash Flow & Capital Return

Free cash flow of ~$3.1B in FY2025 (21% margin) places Expedia among the most cash-generative internet companies on a yield basis. At the current ~$25.3B market cap, FCF yield is approximately 12.3%.

Capital return is aggressive and accelerating:
- Shares outstanding: 157M (2022) → 114M (May 2026) — **28% reduction in 3.5 years**
- New $5B buyback authorized May 2026 (plus $870M remaining) = $5.87B total capacity
- At $221/share, this could retire ~26M additional shares (23% of current outstanding)
- Dividend raised 20% to $0.48/quarter ($1.92/year, ~0.87% yield)

### Balance Sheet — Clarifying the Apparent Distress

The Altman Z-Score of 0.74 is a false signal. The $16.7B in current liabilities includes $10.4B in deferred merchant bookings — prepayments from travelers that will be settled through travel delivery, not cash. Face value debt was reduced to ~$4.5B after Q1 2026 maturities (repaid $750M notes + settled $1.1B converts). Cash on hand is ~$5.5B plus a $0.6B investment portfolio. **Net debt is approximately zero.** Moody's rates the company Baa2 with a positive outlook (potential upgrade). The new $2.5B undrawn revolver maturing 2031 provides ample liquidity.

## Growth Analysis

### Near-Term: Q2 Guidance Signals Confidence

Management guided Q2 2026 revenue of approximately $4.16B, implying mid-teens growth continuation. The Q1 beat of $0.54 above consensus (38.4% EPS surprise) and current-year estimate revisions of +8.2% over 90 days confirm positive momentum.

### Medium-Term: B2B as Structural Growth Engine

B2B is the highest-quality growth vector in the online travel space. The Rapid API platform powers travel for airlines, banks, retailers, and corporate travel managers. Revenue margin is 13.6% (higher than B2C's 11.3%), customer switching costs are high once integrated, and the addressable market is massive — the total travel technology services market is estimated at $50B+ [unverified]. At 25% growth and approaching $6B annual revenue run-rate, B2B could become Expedia's largest segment within 3-4 years.

### Long-Term: AI Both Threat and Opportunity

AI represents Expedia's most complex strategic challenge. The company is investing in AI-powered trip planning, customer service automation, and developer productivity. However, Google's integration into travel search and emerging AI travel agents (from OpenAI, Perplexity, etc.) could bypass the OTA search funnel entirely. This is a genuine structural risk that creates a valuation ceiling — Expedia cannot command Booking Holdings-type multiples while this uncertainty persists.

The stock trades at a material discount to the peer median P/E of 17.1x, despite superior FCF generation and accelerating growth. The discount likely reflects: (1) AI disintermediation risk, (2) consumer spending headwinds, (3) dual-class governance structure, and (4) historical execution inconsistency under prior management.

At $221 on $19.67 FY26 EPS consensus, the forward P/E is 11.2x. This drops to 9.6x on FY27 consensus of $22.94. For a company growing revenue 15%, expanding margins, and buying back 10%+ of its float annually, this multiple would normally be too low. The counter-argument is that peak travel demand assumptions may be embedded in current consensus and that AI threatens the OTA business model within 3-5 years.

**Base case valuation:** 14x FY27 EPS of $22.94 = $321. This assumes moderate multiple expansion toward (but not reaching) peers, reflecting persistent AI and macro uncertainty.

## Competitive Landscape

Expedia occupies a structurally #2 position behind Booking Holdings, which enjoys significantly higher margins (40%+ EBITDA vs. 24%) and stronger international penetration. The gap is closing on B2B technology capabilities but widening on efficiency metrics.

**Competitive threats by severity:**
1. **Google Travel (HIGH):** Leverages search monopoly; Expedia spends ~50% of revenue on marketing, much flowing to Google.
2. **AI Travel Agents (MEDIUM-HIGH, RISING):** Could bypass OTA search workflows entirely within 3-5 years.
3. **Hotel Direct (MEDIUM):** Chains investing in loyalty programs and direct booking incentives.
4. **Airbnb (MEDIUM):** Vrbo trails Airbnb on alternative accommodations but benefits from niche (whole-home) focus.
5. **Booking Holdings (MEDIUM):** Scale advantages in Europe/Asia; less direct competition in B2B technology.

Expedia's B2B platform creates a differentiated competitive position that functions as infrastructure-as-a-service for travel rather than a traditional OTA. This is the segment that justifies multiple expansion potential.

## Risk Assessment

Current macro conditions create headwinds for travel. As a discretionary spending category, travel demand is cyclically sensitive. Management noted geopolitical pressures (Middle East, Mexico) impacting demand. This is the primary reason for maintaining HOLD rather than upgrading to BUY despite attractive valuation metrics.

## Consumer Discretionary BUY Gate Assessment

Addressing the required conditions for a Consumer Discretionary BUY:

**(a) Positive sequential same-store sales or comparable revenue growth:** ✅ MET. Q1 2026 revenue grew 15% YoY; room nights grew 6%; gross bookings grew 13%. Management guided Q2 revenue of ~$4.16B implying continued acceleration.

**(b) FCF yield >= 4%:** ✅ MET. FCF yield is approximately 12.3% — triple the 4% threshold.

**(c) Consumer confidence not in declining trend:** ❌ NOT MET. Consumer confidence is in a declining trend with multiple weakness signals documented across the economy.

**(d) Positive estimate revisions over trailing 90 days:** ✅ MET. Current year estimates revised +8.2% over 90 days; next year +5.5%.

**Result:** Three of four conditions are met. Condition (c) — consumer confidence — is the binding constraint. This is the appropriate reason to maintain HOLD rather than BUY. The fundamental thesis is positive but the macro timing is wrong for maximum conviction.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($300-320, +36-45%)
- B2B continues at 20-25% growth through 2027, approaching $8B revenue
- Summer 2026 travel demand holds despite macro headwinds (pent-up demand, employment still solid)
- Buyback reduces shares to ~95-100M by end of 2027 (from 114M currently)
- Multiple re-rates toward 14-15x on FY27 EPS of $22.94+ = $321-344
- Moody's upgrade to Baa1 reduces cost of capital

### Bear Case ($170-190, -14-23%)
- Consumer recession materializes H2 2026; US travel demand declines 5-10%
- Energy prices stay elevated, directly compressing leisure travel budgets
- AI travel agents accelerate disintermediation of OTA search funnel
- Revenue growth decelerates to low-single-digits; margin expansion stalls
- Stock re-rates to 8-9x trough earnings of ~$17 = $136-153 (worst case)

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock pulls back to $190-195 (providing 15%+ upside to base case with confirmed fundamentals)
2. Q2 2026 results (August) show travel demand holding — revenue ≥ $4.15B with stable room night growth
3. Consumer confidence stabilizes or improves

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Q2 room night growth turns negative or decelerates below 3%
2. B2B growth decelerates below 15% for two consecutive quarters
3. Management cuts full-year guidance or suspends buyback program
4. AI-driven booking channel shift becomes quantifiable in industry data (>5% volume displacement)

**Reassessment timeline:** August 2026 (Q2 earnings). Summer travel season data will either validate or refute the demand thesis. Secondary check: November 2026 (Q3 earnings, booking trends for holiday travel).

## Conclusion

Expedia is a fundamentally improving business. B2B growth is structural, margin expansion is real, capital return is among the most aggressive in the internet space, and the balance sheet is healthier than it appears. At 11x forward earnings with a 12% FCF yield, the stock is cheap relative to its improving fundamentals.

The constraint is macro timing. Consumer confidence is declining, and options markets are pricing elevated near-term risk. Three of four Consumer Discretionary gate conditions are met, but the consumer confidence condition is binding. A HOLD with growth conviction is appropriate — the entry point requires either a lower price or confirmation that summer travel demand holds despite macro headwinds.

Target entry for BUY: $190-195 (12% below current), or Q2 results confirming demand resilience.
