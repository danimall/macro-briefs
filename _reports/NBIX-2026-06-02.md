**Research Date: June 2, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Neurocrine Biosciences is a profitable, San Diego–based neuroscience biopharmaceutical company built around INGREZZA (valbenazine) for tardive dyskinesia and Huntington's chorea, supplemented by a fast-ramping first-in-class CAH therapy (CRENESSITY) and, as of May 2026, a newly acquired rare-disease asset (VYKAT XR for Prader-Willi Syndrome via the ~$2.9B Soleno acquisition). Q1 2026 delivered total revenue of $814.5M (+42% YoY), net income of $197.9M (versus $7.9M a year earlier on a depressed comparison base), and diluted EPS of $1.91, beating the $1.18 consensus by 62%. Revenue has compounded at roughly 27% since 2021 ($1.09B → $2.83B in 2025).

The investment case turns on a valuation that has not kept pace with the earnings trajectory. The stock trades at ~24.8x trailing EPS of $4.67 but only ~16x the FY2026 consensus of $10.27, which has been revised up 33.8% over the past 30 days and 12.4% over 90 days. Next-year consensus of $12.71 has risen 48.6% in 30 days. A mid-teens forward multiple on a franchise delivering mid-20s revenue growth, with a clean cost structure (COGS ~1.7% of revenue), is the specific mispricing here. Some of the revision velocity is mechanical, reflecting VYKAT revenue and EPS folding in after the Soleno close, but the underlying INGREZZA/CRENESSITY engine independently supports the forward number.

What caps conviction rather than killing the thesis is a tight cluster of company-specific overhangs on the asset that still drives ~80% of revenue: a DOJ civil investigative demand (August 2025) into INGREZZA sales and marketing, an FDA untitled letter (September 2025) on promotional claims, an active Zydus Para IV ANDA challenge, and the resetting of VMAT2 class pricing as Teva's competing AUSTEDO enters Medicare negotiation with an effective price year of 2027. The options market is pricing this tension directly: near-term IV of 41.9% sits well above long-term IV of 35.3% (backwardation), with elevated put skew. The recommendation is a disciplined-size BUY, with the risk cluster the reason sizing is bounded.

## Company Overview

Neurocrine (founded 1992, ~2,000 employees) operates as a single reporting segment focused on neurological, psychiatric, endocrine, and increasingly immunological disorders. The commercial portfolio:

- **INGREZZA (valbenazine):** A VMAT2 inhibitor, first FDA-approved drug for tardive dyskinesia (2017) and Huntington's chorea (2023). U.S. TD addressable population estimated at ~800,000. INGREZZA was ~88% of 2025 revenue and ~81% of Q1'26 product sales ($656.9M, +20% YoY).
- **CRENESSITY (crinecerfont):** First-in-class CRF1 receptor antagonist for classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia, launched December 2024. Q1'26 sales of $153.3M (versus $14.5M a year ago) make it the key incremental growth driver.
- **VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline ER):** Acquired via Soleno (closed May 2026). The first and only FDA-approved therapy (March 2025) for hyperphagia in Prader-Willi Syndrome, with ~$190M in launch-year 2025 sales (~$92M in Q4'25 alone).
- **AbbVie-partnered royalties** (ORILISSA/ORIAHNN via elagolix) and DYSVAL (valbenazine in Japan via Tanabe Pharma).

The company is deliberately broadening from small molecules into biologics, peptides, antibodies, conjugates, gene therapy, obesity/metabolic, and immunology, supported by collaborations with Voyager (~19.9% owned), Nxera, Xenon, and Takeda.

## Financial Analysis

The financial profile is strong on every axis that matters for a commercial-stage biopharma.

**Revenue and earnings.** Revenue scaled $1.09B (2021) → $1.44B → $1.86B → $2.33B → $2.83B (2025), a ~27% CAGR. Net income grew from $89.6M (2021) to $478.6M (2025). Q1'26 revenue was the first quarter above $800M. The prior-year Q1 was artificially depressed by a $37.5M Takeda milestone outflow and a $30.6M unrealized equity loss, so the headline net income jump overstates the operational acceleration; the cleaner read is the +20% INGREZZA growth plus the CRENESSITY ramp.

**Margins and reinvestment.** R&D ran $296M (36% of revenue) and SG&A $318M (39%) in Q1'26, reflecting heavy pipeline investment and sales-force expansion rather than margin distress. Operating cash flow was $145.8M in the quarter. A modeling nuance to track: CRENESSITY's early inventory carries no cost basis (pre-approval manufacturing was expensed as R&D), flattering gross margin until cost-bearing inventory cycles through.

**Balance sheet.** Pre-Soleno, the balance sheet carried ~$2.65B cash and marketable securities, no funded debt, $3.4B equity, and ~$1.6B working capital. The Soleno deal ($53.00/share cash) changes this. Management funded it with cash plus "a modest amount of pre-payable debt," and the May 18, 2026 8-K (Items 1.01/2.01/2.03) confirms a new credit facility was incurred at close. NBIX moves from net-cash to modestly levered, having spent the bulk of its cash position. This is the largest capital-structure change in the company's history and converts a defensive cash cushion into an operating bet on VYKAT scaling.

**Earnings surprise track record.** Three beats and one miss over four quarters: +62.3% (Q1'26), -22.9% (Q4'25), +26.1% (Q3'25), +7.0% (Q2'25). The Q4'25 miss is the one cautionary data point — visibility is imperfect, and the -22.9% magnitude shows quarter-to-quarter lumpiness in milestone timing and operating spend.

## Growth Analysis

Three growth vectors are stacking:

1. **INGREZZA durability.** Reaffirmed FY2026 guidance of $2.7B–$2.8B in INGREZZA net sales implies continued mid-to-high-teens growth off a large base. Real-world persistence data favoring INGREZZA over AUSTEDO XR supports formulary defense.

2. **CRENESSITY ramp.** The $14.5M to $153.3M YoY move is the clearest near-term acceleration. As the only first-in-class CAH therapy against an entrenched corticosteroid standard of care, the runway is multi-year.

3. **VYKAT XR optionality.** The competitive field thinned materially in NBIX's favor — Aardvark paused its Phase 3 and Acadia abandoned intranasal carbetocin after a September 2025 Phase 3 failure, leaving VYKAT as the lone near-term approved PWS therapy. Management claims exclusivity into the mid-2040s and modeled the deal on U.S.-only sales. The bull case requires the ~$190M run-rate to scale several-fold; the bear case is the Street's open skepticism (Wells Fargo) on community-setting adoption and adherence given edema/fluid-overload label warnings.

The estimate revision profile is the strongest forward signal. CY consensus moved to $10.27 from $7.67 thirty days ago and $9.13 ninety days ago; next-year moved to $12.71 from $8.55. Revision velocity of this magnitude combined with 40%+ revenue growth is among the more reliable predictors of continued re-rating as consensus catches up to delivered results. I weight this signal heavily because the stock is cheap relative to the improving estimates (~16x forward), the condition under which the revision signal is most predictive rather than already priced.

Two caveats temper the signal. First, a meaningful portion of the revision is mechanical — VYKAT revenue and EPS now consolidate post-close, so the 30-day jump partly reflects the deal closing rather than organic acceleration. Second, the current-quarter 90-day revision is -5.0%, meaning near-term estimates softened before the deal-driven jump. The durable signal sits in the full-year and next-year numbers, not the next print.

Pipeline catalysts beyond the commercial ramp are dated to ~2027: Phase 3 readouts for osavampator (AMPA PAM in MDD, Takeda-partnered) and direclidine (schizophrenia), plus a March 2026 Phase 2 start for NBI-1117570 (M1/M4 muscarinic agonist). These are 2027 events, so they do not anchor the 12-month thesis but do provide a reason the franchise retains value once INGREZZA matures.

## Valuation Assessment

The valuation case rests on the gap between trailing optics and forward reality. At $161, NBIX trades at ~24.8x trailing EPS, which screens as a premium to the biotech peer median P/E of 16.7. That comparison is misleading. On FY2026 consensus EPS, the forward multiple is ~15.7x; on next-year $12.71, ~12.7x. For a franchise growing revenue ~27% with rising estimates, a mid-teens forward multiple implies a PEG well below 1.0.

Peer context is heterogeneous and of limited use: MRNA and ARWR carry negative P/Es (loss-making), EXEL and INCY trade at 16.7x and 13.7x on slower or flat growth, and BMRN at 41.0x on a comparable rare-disease growth profile. NBIX's forward multiple sits closer to the slower-growth value names than to BMRN, despite a superior growth and cash-generation profile. The market is anchoring on the trailing 24.8x and the INGREZZA-concentration discount rather than the forward earnings power.

What the market is correctly worried about, and why the discount is not pure inefficiency: the forward EPS depends on (a) VYKAT scaling, which is unproven, and (b) INGREZZA pricing holding through the IRA/AUSTEDO-negotiation transition. The valuation score reflects genuine asymmetric upside if those resolve favorably, tempered by the fact that the discount is partly a rational hedge against the regulatory cluster rather than a clean oversight.

Analyst targets bracket the range: low $144, mean $192, median $187, high $250.65 (Bernstein initiated Outperform May 22 at ~23% upside). On a stock trading below 16x forward with rising estimates, consensus targets tend to lag and get revised up as estimates climb. The current price sits below even the mean target, leaving room before the stock reaches "fully valued" on the Street's own framework.

## Competitive Landscape

**Tardive dyskinesia / Huntington's chorea:** INGREZZA versus Teva's AUSTEDO/AUSTEDO XR is a two-player class. NBIX holds category leadership and is presenting real-world persistence data favoring its product. The wildcard is AUSTEDO's Medicare-negotiated price (effective 2027 price year, MFP below list), which could reset class pricing and formulary leverage. This competitive risk arrives on a known timeline.

**CAH:** CRENESSITY is first-in-class against high-dose corticosteroids, with several competitors in development but no approved direct rival. Trajectory is improving — share is being created, not defended.

**PWS (via Soleno):** The field thinned in NBIX's favor (Aardvark and Acadia failures), leaving VYKAT XR as the lone near-term approved therapy. Competitive position is favorable; the open question is commercial execution, not competition.

**Schizophrenia (pipeline):** NBIX's muscarinic programs will compete with Bristol Myers' COBENFY (xanomeline) and others — a 2027+ consideration.

The overall trajectory is improving: leadership in two established categories, share creation in CAH and PWS, with the main competitive threat (AUSTEDO pricing) being a pricing reset rather than a share loss.

## Risk Assessment

The risk cluster is the reason this is a disciplined-size BUY rather than a high-conviction one, and it is concentrated on the asset that matters most.

1. **INGREZZA concentration + IRA exposure.** ~80% of revenue rides on one drug. INGREZZA currently benefits from a "small biotech exception" deferring Medicare negotiation eligibility (earliest selection 2027, price 2029) plus Part D discount phase-in. These exemptions can be lost if a third party acquires NBIX — a structural anti-takeover feature that removes the long-assumed acquisition floor under the stock. AUSTEDO's selection for negotiation (2027) could pressure INGREZZA's pricing and formulary positioning.

2. **DOJ civil investigative demand (August 2025)** on INGREZZA sales and marketing. Pharma S&M and patient-assistance investigations can produce material settlements and corporate integrity agreements. Unquantified, timing unknown.

3. **FDA untitled letter (September 2025)** alleging misleading INGREZZA promotional claims, consistent with current administration scrutiny of DTC advertising.

4. **Zydus Para IV ANDA on INGREZZA SPRINKLE** (notice March 2025), litigation in Delaware. Composition patents nominally run to ~2038, but ANDA challenges introduce earlier-erosion risk.

5. **Drug-pricing policy overhang:** MFN executive orders, proposed CMS Part B/D MFN demonstrations, OBBBA Medicaid cuts, and a potential 100% pharmaceutical tariff (Commerce investigation, April 2025). NBIX relies entirely on European third-party manufacturers, amplifying tariff exposure.

6. **Customer concentration:** Four specialty-pharmacy/distributor customers account for >90% of gross product sales; CRENESSITY runs through a single specialty pharmacy.

7. **Execution/transformation risk:** Integrating the largest acquisition in company history while pivoting into biologics and gene therapy, modalities where management explicitly notes no prior commercialization experience. The Soleno integration risk was newly added to the Q1'26 10-Q risk factors.

8. **Contingent milestones:** Up to ~$15.39B in potential collaboration milestone payments (Voyager $6.13B, Nxera $2.48B, Xenon $1.66B, Takeda $742.5M) — success-contingent (largely positive optionality) but representing future cash calls.

Several of these risks are partially priced (the trailing-multiple discount embeds the concentration concern). The two least-priced, most company-specific items are the DOJ CID and FDA untitled letter, both targeting the ~80% revenue driver and both surfacing in the most recent fiscal year. They warrant monitoring as settlement/CIA risk.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing imminent stress. Near-term ATM IV of 41.9% sits well above long-term IV of 35.3% (backwardation), and above the 30.3% one-year historical vol — IV is rich, with the spread at 11.6pp and a 68th-percentile reading. Put/call skew is elevated at 4.4% (heavier put demand), and ATM IV rose 5.1pp (36.8% → 41.9%) over the past week on a roughly flat price, with P/C OI jumping from 0.07 to 0.68. The implied move on the nearest expiry is ±7.3%. This configuration signals the market expects a near-term catalyst with two-sided but downside-skewed risk, consistent with the regulatory cluster and the next earnings print. For a BUY, this argues for scaling in rather than committing full size into the event window, and the rich near-term IV makes covered-call overwriting attractive for existing holders.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case (~$200–250).** INGREZZA holds its $2.7B–$2.8B 2026 guidance and defends formulary position through the AUSTEDO-negotiation transition; CRENESSITY continues its multi-fold ramp; VYKAT scales from ~$190M toward the several-hundred-million level the $2.9B price implies; the DOJ and FDA matters resolve without material financial penalty; and consensus continues revising EPS toward the $12.71 next-year figure. At 18–20x $12.71, the stock reaches $230–250, matching the Street high and Bernstein's Outperform thesis. The estimate-revision momentum (+33.8% CY, +48.6% next-year over 30 days) is the engine.

**Bear case (~$120–130).** The DOJ CID produces a material settlement and corporate integrity agreement; AUSTEDO's negotiated price resets class economics and pressures INGREZZA net pricing; VYKAT adoption disappoints in the community setting on adherence and edema-warning concerns, impairing the $2.9B deal; and the newly added leverage compounds the disappointment. EPS estimates roll back toward the pre-revision $7.67–$8.55 range, and the multiple compresses to ~16x, taking the stock to the 52-week-low zone around $121–130, roughly 20–25% below current.

The asymmetry favors the upside given the forward multiple, but the bear case is concrete and timed, which is why sizing should be disciplined.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: BUY (disciplined size, 1.5–2.5% position).**

- **Base case target: $185** over 12 months. Reasoning: ~14.5x the next-year consensus plus a discount for the regulatory/integration overhang, landing near the analyst median of $187 and mean of $192. This sits between the bear and bull scenarios. Upside of ~15% from $161.
- **Bull case target: $230–250** (+43% to +55%), on multiple expansion to 18–20x next-year EPS as the revision cycle and VYKAT ramp validate.
- **Bear case target: $120–130** (-19% to -25%), on DOJ settlement, AUSTEDO-driven pricing reset, and VYKAT disappointment.
- **Timeframe:** 12 months, anchored to FY2026 INGREZZA guidance delivery, sequential CRENESSITY/VYKAT sales prints, and any DOJ/FDA resolution. The 2027 Phase 3 readouts (osavampator, direclidine) are beyond this window but underpin the longer-term franchise.

**Thesis invalidation triggers (exit or downgrade if any occur):**
1. INGREZZA FY2026 guidance cut below $2.7B, or a sequential INGREZZA revenue decline indicating pricing/volume erosion ahead of the AUSTEDO transition.
2. A DOJ settlement exceeding ~$300M or imposition of a corporate integrity agreement that constrains the sales-force model.
3. VYKAT XR sales failing to grow sequentially through 2H 2026 (no progress toward the several-fold scaling the $2.9B price requires), or an adverse label change tied to fluid-overload warnings.
4. CY/next-year EPS estimate revisions turning negative over a 90-day window, reversing the current momentum.

## Conclusion

Neurocrine is a profitable, cash-generative neuroscience franchise growing revenue ~27% with a forward multiple (~15.7x FY2026 EPS) that has not caught up to its rising estimates. The CRENESSITY ramp is accelerating, the VYKAT acquisition adds a near-monopoly rare-disease bridge to 2027 pipeline readouts, and the estimate revisions are a strong forward signal that the market is underestimating earnings power. The concern that prevents maximum conviction is the regulatory and concentration cluster on INGREZZA — the DOJ CID, FDA untitled letter, Zydus ANDA, and AUSTEDO Medicare-negotiation reset all target the ~80% revenue driver, and the options market is pricing this tension in near-term backwardation with elevated put skew. The appropriate response is a disciplined-size BUY, scaled in given the rich near-term IV, with hard exit triggers tied to guidance, the DOJ outcome, and VYKAT scaling.
