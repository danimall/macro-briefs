## Executive Summary

Veralto is a high-quality industrial technology company spun from Danaher in September 2023, operating in water analytics/treatment (60% of sales) and product quality/marking solutions (40%). The company generates $1B+ in annual free cash flow on just $63M of capex, maintains ~60% gross margins, and carries 61% recurring revenue. At $86.88, the stock trades at approximately 20.3–20.7x FY2026 guided EPS of $4.20-$4.28, a meaningful discount to its peer group median of 33x.

The fundamental quality profile includes four consecutive earnings beats, accelerating FCF conversion (>100% of net income), rapid deleveraging from $1.9B net debt at spin-off to $0.7B by end of FY2025, and a newly initiated $65-75M cost optimization program that should add ~75-80bps to operating margins by 2028. Management raised FY2026 guidance with Q1 results, and estimates are modestly trending upward (+1.7% on current year over 90 days).

The specific obstacle preventing a BUY recommendation today is the confluence of: (1) options market pricing imminent stress (30% near-term IV vs. 20.2% HV, backwardation term structure), (2) tariff/trade policy uncertainty with 56% international revenue exposure and no management assurance for 2026 mitigation, and (3) current-quarter estimate revisions declining 3.5% over 90 days, suggesting near-term earnings pressure not yet fully absorbed. The stock is 21% below its 52-week high. Once Q2 results (expected late July) demonstrate tariff-mitigation execution and stabilize near-term estimates, the investment case becomes compelling for upgrade.

## Company Overview

Veralto operates two segments serving essential, non-discretionary end markets:

**Water Quality (60% of revenue, $3.32B FY2025):** Hach provides water analytics instrumentation serving 149,000+ customers and claims to help ensure safe water for ~3.4 billion people daily. Trojan Technologies provides UV disinfection. ChemTreat delivers chemical treatment solutions. The segment generates 25.4% operating margins with 4.7% core growth.

**Product Quality & Innovation (40%, $2.18B FY2025):** Videojet leads in inline printing/coding with the largest installed base globally among top-25 CPG and top-20 pharma brands. Esko dominates packaging prepress workflow with 25,000+ customers in 140+ countries. Pantone is the preeminent color standard. The segment generates 25.2% operating margins with 4.8% core growth.

The business model is exceptionally capital-efficient: $5.5B revenue generated on $63M annual capex. The installed base of equipment drives consumable pull-through (reagents, inks, chemicals), creating razor/razorblade economics where 61% of revenue is recurring.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Margins:**
Revenue grew from $5.02B (FY2023) to $5.50B (FY2025), a 4.7% CAGR. Gross margins expanded 210bps to 59.9%. Operating margins held steady at ~23%. Q1 2026 revenue of $1.42B grew 6.7% YoY, suggesting acceleration.

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow rose 23% to $1.077B in FY2025. FCF of $1.014B (18.4% margin) converts at >100% of net income. This is among the highest FCF/capex ratios in the industrial sector (16:1). The asset-light model means nearly all operating cash flow converts directly to deployable capital.

**Balance Sheet:**
Cash of $2.03B against $2.7B total debt (net debt ~$0.7B). Net leverage has declined dramatically from spin-off levels. The 3.75x net leverage covenant provides ample headroom. Current ratio of 1.67x indicates comfortable liquidity.

**Capital Deployment (2026):**
- Acquisitions: ~$620M (In-Situ $427M, GlobalVision ~$195M)
- Buybacks: $300M in Q1 (~3.2M shares at ~$94/share)
- Dividends: ~$130M annualized
- Total: ~$1B deployed against $1B+ annual FCF generation

**Tax Rate Consideration:**
FY2025 effective rate of 19.9% was 340bps below FY2024's 23.3% due to discrete benefits. Normalizing to 22% would have reduced FY2025 EPS by approximately $0.12. This is a one-time tailwind that investors should not extrapolate.

## Growth Analysis

Organic growth of 4-5% represents the sustainable base case for Veralto, driven by:

1. **Municipal water infrastructure spending** (non-discretionary, counter-cyclical): PFAS regulations, lead/copper rule tightening, aging infrastructure replacement
2. **Consumable volume growth** tied to expanding installed base
3. **Software/digital layering** via TraceGains, In-Situ, and GlobalVision acquisitions (~$1B deployed, all skewing toward higher-margin recurring software)
4. **Cost optimization** ($65-75M savings by 2028 = 75-80bps margin expansion)
5. **Pricing power** (1.9% price contribution in FY2025 within 4.7% organic growth)

Growth is not accelerating materially. Full-year 2026 core growth guidance of 3-4.5% is essentially in-line with FY2025's 4.7%. The company remains a mid-single-digit organic grower supplemented by bolt-on M&A. This is adequate for a compounder but does not indicate acceleration.

Current-quarter EPS estimates declined 3.5% over 90 days ($1.04 → $1.00), which contrasts with full-year estimates rising 1.7%. This divergence likely reflects tariff-related caution for Q2 specifically rather than structural deterioration.

## Valuation Assessment

At $86.88 with FY2026 guided EPS of $4.20-$4.28:
- Forward P/E: 20.3-20.7x
- FCF yield: ~4.7% (assuming $1B+ FCF maintained)
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 15-16x

Against peers:
- Rollins (ROL): 50.7x P/E
- Republic Services (RSG): 30.4x
- Waste Management (WM): 33.3x
- Peer median: 33.3x

VLTO trades at a 39% discount to peer median P/E. The discount partially reflects its younger public company status and lower organic growth visibility vs. waste services peers. However, VLTO's 60% gross margins, 18% FCF margins, and Danaher operational culture argue for a narrower gap.

At 20x forward earnings, the market is pricing Veralto as a below-average industrial rather than a premium compounder. This creates value if: (a) organic growth sustains at 4-5%, (b) cost savings deliver on schedule, and (c) acquisitions prove accretive. The analyst consensus mean target of $108.41 (median $106) implies 25% upside from current levels, with a floor of $100 (15% above current).

The 21% drawdown from the 52-week high of $110.11 has compressed the multiple from ~26x to ~20x on fundamentals that are modestly improving. The stock is cheaper today on better fundamentals than three months ago.

## Competitive Landscape

**Water Quality:** Hach's installed base of analytics equipment in municipal and industrial settings creates high switching costs. Regulatory compliance workflows are built around specific platforms. Competitors (Xylem, Endress+Hauser) compete more in treatment/infrastructure hardware rather than the analytics/monitoring niche where Hach dominates. The addition of In-Situ extends coverage to environmental monitoring (surface water, groundwater), a complementary rather than overlapping capability.

**Product Quality & Innovation:** Videojet's moat comes from the largest installed base of marking/coding systems integrated into production lines globally. The consumable attachment rate creates a recurring revenue stream with minimal competitive switching (production line downtime cost far exceeds consumable pricing differential). Esko's packaging design workflow dominance is embedded in customer operations. GlobalVision adds AI-augmented quality inspection to this workflow.

**Trajectory:** Improving. The software acquisitions (TraceGains, GlobalVision) layer higher-margin, stickier digital solutions on top of hardware-based installed bases. This follows the Danaher playbook at company level: build installed base → drive consumable pull-through → add software/subscription layers. Competitive position is incrementally strengthening.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Tariff Exposure (Specific and Material):**
With 56% international revenue and a globally distributed supply chain, the current tariff environment presents direct cost and demand pressure. Management stated tariffs were "largely mitigated" in 2025 via VES processes but provides no assurance for 2026's broader tariff regime. China specifically showed low-single-digit core sales declines in Water Quality during 2025.

**2. Macro Sensitivity in PQI Segment:**
CPG/industrial customers can defer coding equipment upgrades during economic slowdowns. While consumable revenue (ink) is largely maintenance-driven, new equipment orders correlate with industrial confidence. In a stagflationary environment with consumer weakness, PQI new equipment growth may decelerate.

**3. Acquisition Integration Risk:**
Three acquisitions totaling ~$1B in 12 months (TraceGains, In-Situ, GlobalVision). While all are bolt-on complementary deals at reasonable multiples (15x EBITDA for GlobalVision), integration execution risk is inherent. Management's own 10-K acknowledges historical acquisition underperformance.

**4. AI Disruption (Longer-term, PQI-specific):**
Management explicitly added AI risks in the 10-K. For Esko's packaging design workflow and Pantone's color management, AI tools could either augment (positive) or displace (negative) existing solutions. GlobalVision's AI-augmented inspection is the defensive response.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing elevated near-term risk: 30% ATM IV vs. 20.2% historical volatility (9.8pp premium), with term structure in backwardation (30% near-term > 23.9% six-month). Put skew of 3.2% indicates modestly elevated hedging demand. With Q1 results already reported (April 28) and a beat, the elevated near-term vol likely reflects macro/tariff uncertainty rather than company-specific earnings risk. The backwardation structure typically resolves within 4-6 weeks post-earnings as event premiums decay.

- Cost optimization program tracking ahead of schedule, delivering $30-40M in FY2027 (partial year)

- Math: 24x × $4.24 = $102

## Conclusion

Veralto is a high-quality industrial compounder with exceptional capital efficiency, durable competitive positioning, and reasonable valuation relative to its quality characteristics. The 21% drawdown from highs has compressed the multiple from ~26x to ~20x on fundamentals that are modestly improving.

The specific factor preventing a BUY today is unresolved tariff impact on near-term earnings combined with options market pricing of imminent stress. Q2 results in late July will either confirm management's ability to mitigate tariffs (bullish) or reveal margin compression (requiring thesis reassessment). With 63% of Wall Street covering analysts at Buy/Strong Buy and a consensus target of $108, the institutional view is constructive. I share that view but wait for tariff-mitigation evidence before adding.

The stock is positioned for upgrade to BUY once near-term estimate revisions stabilize or the stock declines further to create a more asymmetric entry.
