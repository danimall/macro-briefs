**Research Date: August 10, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Dauch Corporation is American Axle & Manufacturing renamed (effective January 26, 2026) after absorbing Dowlais Group plc — parent of GKN Automotive and GKN Powder Metallurgy — on February 3, 2026. The transaction roughly doubled revenue (FY2026 guidance $10.6–10.8B versus $5.84B standalone in FY2025), issued approximately 117 million shares against a ~120 million pre-deal base (about 49% ownership transferred to former Dowlais holders), and left gross debt of $5,119.9M against $880.8M of cash. At the frozen basis of $6.72, market capitalization is $1,596M and enterprise value approximately $5,835M. Equity is 27% of the capital structure; debt is 73%. Everything about this investment case follows from that ratio.

The first full combined quarter was better than the GAAP headline suggests. Q2'26 segment adjusted EBITDA margin was 13.2%, identical to Q2'25, while gross margin fell 170bp. The difference is step-up depreciation (D&A nearly doubled to $215.4M; guided to $825M for FY2026 versus roughly $450M annualized standalone) and a Q1 inventory fair-value charge. SG&A fell from 6.6% to 5.6% of sales, worth roughly $30M per quarter on a $3.0B revenue base, which is the clearest evidence of real synergy capture. Management raised the low end of every guidance element on August 7 — sales, adjusted EBITDA, in-year synergy benefit, China JV equity income, and adjusted free cash flow — and the stock rose roughly 13%. Consensus adjusted EPS for the current year has been revised from $0.56 to $1.07 over 90 days.

The offsets are substantial and mostly structural. FY2026 GAAP free cash flow is guided negative ($105)M to ($90)M, and GAAP net loss is guided at ($200)M to ($135)M. H1'26 GAAP operating income of $66.0M did not cover $179.4M of interest expense. The guidance package requires operating cash flow of $352–367M in the second half against $43.1M in the first, into quarters that contain OEM model-year and December shutdowns. An IRS foreign base company sales income dispute with $335–385M of potential exposure — 21–24% of market capitalization — is scheduled for trial in 2026. Financial maintenance covenants on the assumed $349.0M of U.S. private placement notes exist but their thresholds and headroom are not disclosed in the filings reviewed. Roughly half of combined revenue sits inside a control environment where the acquiree had identified matters that "would have been characterized as material weaknesses," and which is excluded from 2026 ICFR assessment. On a probability-weighted basis the expected return is positive (approximately +25% to a base case of $9.50 over 12–18 months), but the bear case is a 48% loss and there is essentially no asset-value floor: tangible book is roughly $2.01 per share.

## Company Overview

Dauch is a Tier 1 driveline and metal forming supplier headquartered in Detroit, operating in 24 countries across more than 175 locations, reporting two segments.

**Driveline** produces rear and front axles, 4WD/AWD systems, power transfer units, and, post-Dowlais, sideshafts and constant-velocity joint systems. The legacy franchise is anchored in North American full-size rear-wheel-drive light trucks and SUVs, where the company supplies a significant portion of GM's rear axle and 4WD/AWD requirements. It also supplies GM's Colorado/Canyon mid-size pickups, Ford (Bronco Sport, Maverick, Escape, Lincoln Nautilus) and Stellantis heavy-duty Ram derivatives. The 10-K states the company has been awarded multiple next-generation full-size pickup front and rear axle programs expected to generate revenue beyond 2030.

**Metal Forming** is described in the 10-K as the largest automotive forging operation in the world and is vertically integrated as a key supplier to Driveline. GKN Powder Metallurgy sits here, and appears materially more profitable than legacy forging: segment adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 11.6% in Q2'26 from 8.6%, though a Q1 plant reallocation between segments and a revised adjusted EBITDA definition make that delta impure.

The company also holds a 50% interest in Shanghai GKN HUAYU Driveline Systems (SDS), carried at $848.8M at 6/30/26 and accounted for under the equity method. It contributes to adjusted EBITDA ($70–80M guided for 2026) but not to consolidated revenue. Dauch received a $49.7M dividend from SDS in Q2'26.

Geographic mix shifted materially: Europe rose from 13.4% to 24.9% of sales; North America fell from 73.7% to 60.5%.

## Financial Analysis

### The standalone base was eroding

Legacy AAM entered this transaction from weakness. Net sales fell 4.7% in FY2025 to $5,836.7M; operating income fell to $112.3M (1.9% margin) from $241.4M, though that decline is largely explained by restructuring and acquisition costs rising to $113.4M from $18.0M. Segment adjusted EBITDA was roughly flat at $743.2M versus $749.2M on 4.7% lower sales, which says the underlying operating business held up. The more damning series is the proxy's company-selected performance measure, adjusted free cash flow: $422.9M (2021), $313.0M (2022), $246.4M (2023), $230.3M (2024), $213.0M (2025) — five consecutive annual declines totalling roughly 50%. Cumulative five-year TSR was 76.86 against a peer group at 127.05.

The Dowlais acquisition is best read as a response to that erosion: buy scale and geographic breadth to reset a cost base that was shrinking with North American ICE truck content. That framing raises the stakes on synergy delivery rather than lowering them.

Two mechanical points matter for interpretation. First, adjusted EPS of $0.32 bears the full step-up D&A; the add-backs to GAAP (roughly $77M after tax in Q2) are transaction, restructuring, integration, SDS intangible amortization and FX items, not depreciation. That makes the $1.07 consensus current-year figure a more economically meaningful earnings measure than it first appears. Second, the adjusted EBITDA definition was revised in Q2'26 to exclude four additional items with prior periods recast, so the flat 13.2% margin partly reflects a widened exclusion set rather than pure operating stability. Q1'26 backs out to adjusted EBITDA of $308.5M on $2,378.9M of sales (13.0%), so the sequential margin trend is modestly positive.

Driveline adjusted EBITDA margin declined to 13.0% from 14.0%, consistent with GKN Automotive's sideshaft/CV-joint business carrying lower margins than legacy captive GM axle content. Net R&D rose in dollars ($56.8M from $36.1M) but fell to 1.9% of sales from 2.4%, reflecting either engineering scale efficiency or under-investment relative to a doubled portfolio; the filings do not resolve which.

### Balance sheet and coverage

Gross debt principal at 6/30/26 was $5,119.9M; less $880.8M cash gives net debt of $4,239.1M. Against the FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance midpoint of $1,392.5M, net leverage is 3.04x; adjusting for the ~$491M of January Dowlais revenue excluded from the consolidated year at an assumed ~12% incremental margin gives a full-year-equivalent EBITDA near $1,452M and leverage near 2.92x (this margin assumption is mine; the company does not disclose pro forma adjusted EBITDA).

Weighted-average interest rate is 7.1%, up from 6.8% at year-end, with a disclosed 100bp sensitivity of $9.4M annual pretax and $1.0B notional of variable-to-fixed swaps running through Q4 2028. Interest expense is guided at $350M for 2026, giving adjusted EBITDA/interest coverage of 3.89–4.07x. Unadjusted EBITDA (guided EBITDA of roughly $995–1,055M after backing out D&A) covers interest 2.84–3.01x. GAAP operating income covered interest only 0.37x in H1 and 1.11x in Q2; the company is servicing debt out of EBITDA while integration charges run.

The liability management has been competent. The deal was pre-funded in escrow in October 2025 ($850M secured 2032 notes at 6.375%, $1,250M unsecured 2033 notes at 7.75%), all but $349.0M of the $1,876.9M of assumed Dowlais debt was repaid, and the remaining $125M of 6.875% 2028 notes was redeemed in August 2026. The result is approximately $2.5B of liquidity and no significant maturities before 2029, which removes near-term refinancing risk and is the single strongest financial-health argument.

Against that: total Dauch equity is $1,509.7M on $11,050.0M of assets (13.7%); tangible book, after removing $684.7M goodwill and $348.6M other intangibles, is roughly $476.4M or $2.01 per share, and that figure still treats the $848.8M SDS carrying value as tangible despite an embedded intangible amortizing at ~$25M per year. Credit ratings are sub-investment grade: S&P BB− with negative outlook, Moody's B1 stable, Fitch BB− stable. Recomputing Altman Z on the 6/30/26 balance sheet with FY2026 guidance produces roughly 1.20, worse than the 1.56 on the pre-deal FY2025 data. That number correctly flags thin equity cushion and heavy fixed charges; it does not signal a liquidity wall, given $2.5B of liquidity and a clean maturity profile to 2029.

Both bridges reconcile exactly. Two conclusions follow. The adjusted FCF story depends entirely on $365–415M of 2026 cash costs being genuinely non-recurring. And the required H2 operating cash flow of $352–367M is more than 3x the Q2 rate of $107.5M — mitigated by one-time cash payments falling (H1 consumed $258.9M of the full-year $365–415M, leaving $106–156M) and a $166.3M H1 receivables build that should partially unwind, but a stretch nonetheless into seasonally shorter production quarters. Q2 adjusted FCF was $148.4M; two Q2-like quarters would clear the adjusted-FCF requirement, but not the OCF line without the one-time cost step-down.

## Growth Analysis

The revenue growth in the reported numbers is acquisition arithmetic, not demand. Pro forma H1'26 sales were $5,825.8M versus $5,749.2M in H1'25 — up 1.3%. Management is planning against a flat-to-declining volume environment: North American light vehicle production ~15.1M units (down roughly 2%), Europe ~16.9M, China cut to ~31.6M from ~32.7M in the February assumption. Growth must come from content, program launches and synergies.

Synergies are the credible part. Filing- and release-confirmed figures are $60–75M of in-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA benefit (raised from $50–75M) and greater than $100M of run rate by the end of year one. The widely circulated $300M total synergy target appears only in a secondary contributor piece and is unconfirmed against any company disclosure; it should not be modeled. SG&A at 5.6% of sales versus 6.6% is the operational corroboration.

Program pipeline: seven 2025 launches (Ford, Stellantis, Skywell, Dongfeng); 2026 launches with GM, Audi, Volkswagen, FAW and Phoebus; next-generation full-size pickup axles generating revenue beyond 2030; Scout Motors front electric drives and rear e-beam axles from 2027. The FY2025 10-K Item 1 does not disclose a quantified new-business backlog, which legacy AAM historically provided. Deferred revenue ($23.4M current, $40.4M long-term) is not a backlog proxy.

The estimate revision picture requires care. Current-year consensus moved from $0.56 to $1.07 over 90 days (+90.8%) and next quarter +58.2% — but on two contributing analysts, and largely reflecting sell-side catching up to a merger they were modeling poorly rather than a demand shift. The more informative datapoint is next year: $1.09 against $1.07, with 11 analysts, and down 0.7% over 30 days. Eleven analysts see essentially no adjusted EPS growth in 2027 despite synergies ramping toward and beyond $100M run rate. That implies they expect volume and pricing headwinds to absorb the synergy benefit. Four consecutive earnings beats (including +81% in Q2) support execution confidence but are partly a function of low, poorly-anchored estimates during a merger year.

## Valuation Assessment

At $6.72:
- **EV/FY2026E adjusted EBITDA**: $5,835M / $1,392.5M = **4.19x** (4.02x on the $1,452M full-year-equivalent estimate)
- **P/E on consensus adjusted EPS**: 6.3x current year ($1.07), 6.2x next year ($1.09)
- **Adjusted FCF yield on equity**: 16.3%–20.4% ($260–325M / $1,596M)
- **GAAP FCF yield**: negative
- **Price/tangible book**: ~3.3x ($6.72 / $2.01)

The peer table supplied (ADNT 32.3x, XPEL 25.3x, THRM 48.1x, SMP 10.4x, FOXF negative; median 32.3x) is not a usable P/E benchmark; those multiples reflect trough or distorted earnings across a heterogeneous set of aftermarket, thermal and accessory suppliers with different capital structures. The relevant benchmark for a levered, ICE-weighted driveline supplier is EV/EBITDA, where the historical band for this cohort is roughly 4.5–6.0x. DCH sits below the low end.

Two mechanisms could explain the discount. First, GAAP optics: guided net loss of ($200)M–($135)M and negative GAAP free cash flow screen badly for quantitative and quality-oriented buyers, even though roughly half the gap between adjusted EBITDA and GAAP loss is depreciation on assets marked to fair value, and about a quarter is interest. Second, and unverifiable from available data, is potential mechanical selling from UK index and mandate funds that received ~117M shares in a primarily US-listed security; the 10-K added a dual-listing risk factor. I have no fund-flow or register data and cannot establish this.

The competing explanation is that the market is pricing the situation correctly: 3x net leverage, negative GAAP FCF, sub-IG ratings with a negative outlook, a declining North American production forecast, 24.9% newly-acquired exposure to Europe, and a $335–385M tax trial. That explanation is at least as plausible, which is why I am not scoring valuation above 6.3 despite the headline cheapness. Cheapness in a 73%-debt capital structure is not margin of safety.

The deleveraging math is where the return actually comes from. Holding enterprise value constant, every $250M of net debt reduction adds roughly 16% to equity value. Conversely, at a constant 4.19x multiple, a 10% adjusted EBITDA variance (~$139M) moves enterprise value by ~$583M — 37% of the equity. The equity is a levered claim on synergy delivery against a declining volume backdrop, in both directions.

Scenario construction, on a 12–18 month horizon and normalizing to FY2027:
- **Base ($9.50)**: 2027 adjusted EBITDA $1.43B (synergies at run rate, volumes down modestly), multiple 4.4x, net debt reduced to ~$4,020M. EV $6,292M − $4,020M = $2,272M / 237.5M shares = $9.57. **+42%.**
- **Bull ($14.50)**: 2027 EBITDA $1.52B (full synergy capture, stable NA production, SDS dividends recurring), multiple 4.9x on demonstrated deleveraging, net debt $3,950M. EV $7,448M − $3,950M = $3,498M = $14.73. **+119%.**
- **Bear ($3.50)**: North American production down 5%, European weakness, 2027 EBITDA $1.28B, multiple compresses to 4.0x on covenant concern, no deleveraging (net debt $4,300M). EV $5,120M − $4,300M = $820M = $3.45. **−49%.**

At 45%/20%/35% weights the expected value is $8.42, or +25% from $6.72. Analyst consensus (mean $8.94, median $8.13, range $6.00–$17.00, with Barclays reinstated at Equal Weight/$8 in June per secondary reporting) brackets that base case closely. This stock is cheap on forward earnings, which argues consensus can drift up, but the target dispersion here ($6 to $17) reflects capital-structure leverage, not analytical conviction.

## Competitive Landscape

The 10-K's own competition language is unflattering to any moat narrative: "Technology, design, quality, delivery and cost are the primary elements of competition," with competitors including independent suppliers and the in-house operations of vertically integrated OEMs, and electrification having "increased the level of new market entrants." The Q2 press release risk list explicitly names "the ongoing proliferation of Chinese original equipment manufacturers in certain regions in which we operate," which matters more now that Europe is a quarter of sales, because Chinese share gains in Europe pressure Dauch's European customers' volumes.

What the company does have is incumbency on engineered-in, tooling-heavy, single-sourced full-size truck axles with multi-year switching costs and programs extending beyond 2030, plus vertical integration from the world's largest automotive forging operation into its own Driveline plants. Quality standing is a real if modest asset on sole-sourced safety-critical parts: four facilities won GM Supplier Quality Excellence Awards, Changshu won GM's Quality Pioneer Award, and Minerva received Ford's Q1 Quality Award.

Trajectory is roughly stable with mix dilution. The Dowlais combination adds content per vehicle (axles plus sideshafts and CV joints allow quoting a fuller driveline package) at lower blended margin in Driveline, offset by higher-margin Powder Metallurgy in Metal Forming. Customer concentration improved in percentage terms (top three from 72% of FY2025 sales to ~54% in H1'26) but GM dollars rose roughly 25% in absolute terms because GKN Automotive also supplies GM, and no fourth customer reaches 10%. Portfolio-level shock absorption improved; exposure to a GM full-size truck program cut did not.

Electrification is a demonstrated risk rather than a hypothetical one: in April 2024 a major customer terminated e-Beam axle purchase orders, producing ~$20M of asset write-offs and ~$22M written off in Q4'25 before a January 2026 settlement returned ~$28M. Forward EV exposure is concentrated in the Scout Motors award from 2027.

## Risk Assessment

**IRS foreign base company sales income dispute.** Potential exposure of $335–385M including interest for 2015–2023, with trial scheduled in 2026. That equals 21–24% of market capitalization and is the largest discrete contingent claim on the equity. Management believes it is more likely than not to prevail and has paid $10.1M recorded as a receivable. A binary event of this size against a $1.6B equity is not obviously priced.

**Covenant headroom is unverified.** The assumed $349.0M of U.S. private placement notes are unsecured and carry financial maintenance covenants. The specific thresholds and current headroom were not obtainable from the filings reviewed. This is the most consequential unresolved item, because it determines how much EBITDA decline the structure tolerates before a technical breach, and technical breach is the mechanism by which the bear case gets to a 4.0x multiple.

**Control environment at half the revenue base.** The 10-Q discloses that Dowlais had identified matters that "would have been characterized as material weaknesses," that remediation is underway, and that Dowlais is excluded from management's 2026 ICFR assessment. Purchase accounting remains preliminary; a Q2 measurement-period adjustment already increased deferred tax liabilities by ~$43M. Exclusion of a newly acquired business is a standard SEC accommodation and is not evidence of misstatement — Dauch's own disclosure controls were assessed effective at 6/30/26 — but reported combined results carry above-average measurement risk for at least four more quarters.

**Guidance assumptions embedding recoveries.** Tariffs cost ~$30M in H1'26 against full-year guidance of $10–20M net, which implies a $10–20M net benefit or recovery in H2 rather than a realized result. Guidance also explicitly assumes no changes to USMCA; the 10-K flags the Guanajuato, Mexico complex as carrying a high concentration of global business and therefore as the specific asset most exposed to renegotiation.

**Impairment risk shifts to long-lived assets.** The purchase price allocation produced only $511.0M of goodwill while consolidated PP&E rose from $1,591.5M to $4,108.0M. Low goodwill limits goodwill-impairment exposure but concentrates risk in $4.1B of fixed assets that would be tested if volumes decline.

**Earnings quality.** Roughly 5% of guided adjusted EBITDA ($70–80M) is non-consolidated SDS equity income where Dauch owns 50% and does not control dividend policy. The $49.7M dividend received in Q2'26 materially mitigates this for the current year but does not establish a repeatable stream given Chinese approval and capital-control considerations.

**Labor.** Approximately 76% of hourly associates are under collective bargaining agreements. A June 2026 work stoppage at one U.S. facility was resolved with a four-year agreement at ~$8M of one-time Q2 cost. The four-year term removes this specific risk until 2030 but demonstrated that a single sole-source axle plant can threaten a customer's truck line.

**Macro.** Higher-for-longer policy rates compress affordability at the consumer level and raise the marginal cost of the 7.1% weighted-average debt stack as tranches reprice. European exposure is 24.9% of sales.

**Positioning signal.** Short interest is 9.9% of float at 5.7 days to cover — moderate, but enough to amplify moves in a name where the float is a thin slice of enterprise value. Part of the 13% post-earnings move likely reflects short covering rather than incremental long conviction. No listed options data was available for this analysis.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The combined company delivers on synergies: greater than $100M of exit run rate in year one, building toward more in 2027 as procurement and footprint actions land. Adjusted EBITDA reaches $1.52B in 2027 with margin above 13.5%. One-time cash costs fall from $365–415M in 2026 toward $100–150M in 2027, flipping GAAP free cash flow positive and allowing $200–300M of annual debt reduction. Net leverage falls below 2.5x, the S&P negative outlook is resolved, and the multiple normalizes to 4.9x — within the auto supplier band, not above it. The IRS matter is resolved at or below the recorded position. Equity reaches $14.50, more than doubling, because a 27% equity slice of enterprise value converts modest EBITDA growth and deleveraging into large per-share gains. The SG&A improvement already visible (100bp, ~$30M per quarter) and the raised guidance in the first combined quarter are the early evidence.

**Bear case.** North American light vehicle production falls 5% rather than 2% as high mortgage rates and elevated auto financing costs bite the truck buyer; European volumes contract, hitting the 24.9% of sales just acquired; Chinese OEM share gains pressure European customer volumes. 2027 adjusted EBITDA lands at $1.28B. The H2'26 operating cash flow ramp — 3x the Q2 rate — falls short, adjusted FCF prints near the $260M low end, and GAAP free cash flow stays negative into 2027 as restructuring persists. A leverage test on the private placement notes or revolver tightens, the multiple compresses to 4.0x, and the equity falls to $3.50. The IRS trial goes against the company, adding a claim worth a fifth of the market capitalization. Tangible book of $2.01 per share provides no floor.

The bear case does not require a recession; it requires a modest volume disappointment plus one financing or contingency event, because at 27% equity/EV the operating and financial leverage compound.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD.** Existing holders own a reasonably-evidenced 12–18 month appreciation thesis; new capital should wait for a better entry.

- **Base case target: $9.50** — 4.4x EV/2027E adjusted EBITDA of $1.43B ($6,292M EV) less net debt of ~$4,020M, divided by 237.5M shares. **+42% from $6.72.**
- **Bull case target: $14.50** — 4.9x on $1.52B EBITDA less $3,950M net debt. **+119%.**
- **Bear case target: $3.50** — 4.0x on $1.28B EBITDA less $4,300M net debt. **−49%.**
- **Probability-weighted expected value: ~$8.42 (+25%)** at 45% base / 20% bull / 35% bear.
- **Timeframe:** 12–18 months, anchored on the Q3'26 print (early November), the FY2026 close confirming the H2 cash-flow ramp and exit synergy run rate, and resolution of the 2026 IRS trial.

**Why HOLD rather than ACCUMULATE.** The expected return is positive but the entry is poor: the stock has moved from $5.09 in early July to $6.72, including a 13% single-day gain on the guidance raise, and the return distribution is bimodal with a 49% downside case. At $5.50 the same scenario set produces a materially higher expected return against a smaller bear-case loss, which clears an accumulation bar. At $6.72, with covenant headroom undisclosed and a tax trial pending, it does not.

**Staged accumulation zone: $5.25–5.75.** Add there, or on the upgrade triggers below.

**Upgrade triggers (to ACCUMULATE or BUY):**
1. Q3'26 operating cash flow of $170M or more (implying the guided H2 ramp is on track) with adjusted FCF above $140M.
2. Disclosure of private placement note covenant thresholds showing at least 25% EBITDA headroom, or refinancing of those notes into the covenant-light structure.
3. Confirmed exit synergy run rate above $100M with FY2027 adjusted EBITDA guidance at or above $1.45B, plus net leverage guided below 2.75x.
4. Favorable resolution of the IRS FBCSI matter at or below the recorded position.

**Downgrade triggers (to REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. FY2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance cut, or Q3 segment adjusted EBITDA margin below 12.5%.
2. Any covenant amendment, waiver request, or S&P downgrade from BB− following the existing negative outlook.
3. North American production assumption cut below 14.7M units, or a European volume warning from GM/Ford/Stellantis/VW affecting Dauch content.
4. Adverse IRS trial outcome with an accrual above $200M.
5. Long-lived asset impairment charge on the acquired PP&E base, which would signal the volume assumptions underpinning the fair-value step-up have broken.

**Reassessment timeline:** the Q3'26 report in early November is the first clean test of both the cash-flow ramp and synergy pacing. Reassess unconditionally at that print.

## Conclusion

Dauch is a genuinely cheap asset attached to a genuinely fragile capital structure. At 4.19x EV/adjusted EBITDA and 6.3x consensus adjusted EPS with a 16–20% adjusted free cash flow yield on equity, the multiple sits below the auto supplier band, and the operating evidence from the first combined quarter — flat 13.2% adjusted EBITDA margin, SG&A down 100bp, guidance raised at the low end across every metric, four consecutive earnings beats — supports the case that purchase accounting rather than deterioration explains the ugly GAAP optics. Liability management has been competent: no significant maturities before 2029 and $2.5B of liquidity remove the near-term default question.

What prevents a positive action call is the shape of the distribution rather than its center. Equity is 27% of enterprise value, so a 10% EBITDA variance moves 37% of the equity. The company must convert roughly $1.4B of adjusted EBITDA into $350M of interest, $500M of capex and $160–170M of cash taxes before any deleveraging occurs, with negative GAAP free cash flow guided for 2026 and an H2 operating cash flow requirement more than 3x the Q2 rate. Layered on top: a tax trial worth 21–24% of market capitalization scheduled this year, financial maintenance covenants whose headroom is undisclosed, half of revenue in a control environment not yet attested, and 24.9% of sales freshly acquired in Europe. Eleven analysts model 2027 adjusted EPS at $1.09 against $1.07 — no growth despite ramping synergies — which is the sell-side's way of saying volumes and pricing will absorb the self-help.

Own it if you own it; the 12–18 month base case of $9.50 is 42% above the current price and the deleveraging mechanism is real. Do not chase it at $6.72 after a 13% post-earnings move.
