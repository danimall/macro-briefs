## Executive Summary

Revvity is a $11.2B life sciences tools and diagnostics company navigating the tail end of a sector-wide downturn. Q1 2026 results showed encouraging signs: 7% reported revenue growth, pharma/biotech customers returning to positive low double-digit growth, and the first positive U.S. academic growth since Q2 2023. The company is executing a strategic pivot toward AI-enabled software (40% ARR growth, Eli Lilly partnership) while aggressively returning capital ($821M buybacks in FY2025).

The fundamental challenge is timing and valuation. At ~47x trailing earnings with estimates declining 2-6% across all periods over the past 90 days, the market is pricing recovery that hasn't yet materialized in the numbers. The $9B goodwill balance (74% of assets) creates meaningful write-down risk if growth disappoints. FY2026 revenue guidance came in 5.2% below Street expectations on the Q1 call, forcing a reset of forward assumptions. The stock's 10% rally on the Q1 beat and China divestiture announcement may have front-run the near-term catalyst path.

The 12-month thesis rests on: (1) continued pharma/biotech spending recovery translating to accelerating organic growth in H2 2026, (2) the software segment proving durable recurring revenue that warrants a higher blended multiple, and (3) successful execution of the China immunodiagnostics divestiture improving the growth and margin profile. These are credible catalysts with identifiable timelines, making this a growth hold rather than a dead-money position.

## Company Overview

Revvity (formerly PerkinElmer, rebranded 2023-2024) operates across two segments of approximately equal size:

**Life Sciences (~50% of revenue):** Reagents (BioLegend antibodies, CRISPR tools), instruments (high-content screening, in vivo imaging, liquid handling), and enterprise software (Signals Research platform, ChemDraw, electronic lab notebooks). Customers are pharma/biotech companies, academic institutions, and CROs.

**Diagnostics (~50% of revenue):** Reproductive health (newborn screening — global leader, prenatal testing, Vanadis NIPT), immunodiagnostics (T-SPOT TB testing, EUROIMMUN autoimmune), and genomics services. Customers are hospitals, public health authorities, and clinical labs.

The company employs approximately 11,000 people across 160+ countries, with ~80% of employees outside the U.S. and majority of revenue from international operations.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue of $2,856M represented 3.7% growth after a flat FY2024 ($2,755M). Q1 2026 accelerated to 7% reported / 3% organic growth ($711M). Diagnostics grew 5% in FY2025 (immunodiagnostics +$41M, reproductive health +$27M) while Life Sciences was essentially flat (-$3.1M).

**Margins:** Gross margin declined 104 bps to 54.8% in FY2025 from tariff impacts (~$20M net) and FX headwinds. GAAP operating margin held at 12.5%. Adjusted operating margin was 23.6% in Q1 2026, beating the 23% outlook. Management guides to ~28.4% adjusted operating margin for FY2026 pro forma (excluding the divesting China business).

**Cash Flow:** FCF of $515M in FY2025 (18% FCF margin) on only $74M capex — a genuinely asset-light model. OCF declined from $665M to $589M YoY on working capital timing (China collections). Q1 2026 FCF was $115M at 97% conversion.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt $3.2B, net debt ~$2.3B, net debt/EBITDA ~2.8x. Goodwill and intangibles total $9.0B — 74% of the $12.2B asset base. The Life Sciences Solutions reporting unit ($4.5B goodwill) had fair value exceeding carrying value by only 10-20% at last impairment test. The company plans to retire €500M Eurobonds in July 2026, which will improve the capital structure.

**Capital Allocation:** Buybacks of $821M in FY2025 (7.3% of market cap) exceeded operating cash flow ($589M), funded from existing cash. This is aggressive at 2.8x leverage. An additional $86M was repurchased in Q1 2026, with $880M remaining on the $1B authorization.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis has identifiable catalysts on specific timelines:

1. **Pharma/biotech recovery (H2 2026):** Q1 showed "positive low double-digit" growth from large pharma customers and positive U.S. academic growth for the first time since Q2 2023. If this normalizes at mid-single-digit growth (typical cycle), it supports 4-5% organic growth by H2.

2. **Software/AI pivot (November 2026 Investor Day):** ARR growing ~40% YoY on Signals platform, Xynthetica (AI models as a service), BioDesign, and LabGistics. The Eli Lilly "TuneLab" partnership provides validation. Software is still a small portion of total revenue but at these growth rates becomes meaningful within 2-3 years. The November 2026 Investor Day will be the proving ground for the recurring revenue narrative.

3. **China divestiture accretion (closing by end-2027):** Removing the ~6% revenue drag from China immunodiagnostics improves organic growth by ~100 bps and margins by ~30 bps annually. The letter of intent is signed with a management-led buyer.

4. **AI validation demand thesis:** Management's argument that AI-driven computational drug discovery creates a bottleneck for physical wet-lab validation (where Revvity's reagents and instruments are essential) is intellectually coherent and differentiated. It is, however, unquantified and early-stage.

Against these catalysts: FY2026 guided organic growth of 3-4% is below the ~5-6% the Street had modeled prior to Q1. Software revenue expected to decline ~20% organically in Q2 on tough comps before rebounding. Estimate revisions are negative across all periods (-2% to -6% over 90 days).

## Valuation Assessment

At $99.83, Revvity trades at:
- 47.4x trailing P/E (on $2.06 GAAP EPS)
- ~19.0x FY2026 adjusted EPS ($5.25 midpoint)
- ~17.0x FY2027 adjusted EPS ($5.88 consensus)
- ~21.7x EV/EBITDA (estimated)

Relative to peers: The Health Care Equipment peer median P/E is 44.2x (including Insulet at 43.5x, Masimo at 44.2x, Penumbra at 71.7x). RVTY's trailing multiple is at the peer median, but these are growth companies — Insulet grows mid-teens, Penumbra grows high-teens. Revvity's 3-4% organic growth profile does not support a comparable premium multiple.

The more appropriate comparison is to life sciences tools peers (Thermo Fisher, Danaher, Agilent) which trade at 22-28x forward earnings with mid-single-digit organic growth. On this basis, RVTY at ~19x FY2026 adjusted EPS is reasonable to slightly cheap — if the recovery materializes as guided.

The problem: estimates are falling, not rising. Current quarter EPS revised down 6% in 90 days. Revenue guidance missed expectations by 5.2%. This is not the estimate revision momentum profile associated with re-rating higher. At current prices, the stock prices in recovery without yet confirming it in the fundamental data.

## Competitive Landscape

Revvity occupies defensible niches within a fragmented life sciences tools market:

**Strengths:** Global leader in newborn screening (high barriers — regulatory approval + government contracts). Strong BioLegend reagent franchise with reproducibility/quality advantages. T-SPOT TB leadership in immunodiagnostics. Growing software platform with academic/pharma customer lock-in.

**Weaknesses:** Significantly smaller than Thermo Fisher ($200B+) and Danaher ($45B+), limiting procurement leverage and R&D scale. TSR has lagged peers by nearly 37 percentage points (68.2% vs. 105.15% on indexed basis through FY2025), suggesting the market views the portfolio as inferior to better-positioned peers. The China divestiture acknowledges the company cannot compete effectively in that market against local champions.

**Trajectory:** Competitive position is stable-to-improving. The software/AI pivot is differentiated and additive. BioLegend integration appears successful. The company is pruning weaker businesses (China) and investing in higher-value software. This earns a 6/10 — maintaining positions in attractive niches with modest improvement, but not meaningfully gaining share at the expense of larger peers.

## Risk Assessment

**Goodwill impairment ($9B, 74% of assets):** A discount rate increase of 100-150 bps or sustained organic revenue declines could trigger a multi-billion dollar write-down. At current interest rates and sluggish growth, this is a real risk, not a theoretical one.

**Tariff escalation:** $20M net impact in FY2025 with manufacturing in Europe for U.S. delivery. Further tariff rounds on EU imports would compress margins. Management's FY2026 guidance already incorporates known tariffs but explicitly warns about further escalation risk.

**Government funding cuts:** New risk factor language explicitly references "recently announced and proposed changes in U.S. funding" creating cautious spending. If NIH/academic funding faces material cuts, this directly impacts the academic customer base showing nascent recovery.

**Software Q2 headwind:** Expected ~20% organic decline in software revenue in Q2 2026 on tough comps creates near-term noise that could pressure the stock despite being flagged in advance.

**Estimate deterioration:** If Q2/Q3 results don't demonstrate acceleration, further downgrades are likely.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($115-125, +15-25%):** Pharma/biotech recovery accelerates through H2 2026, driving 5%+ organic growth. Software ARR continues 30-40% trajectory, and the November Investor Day re-rates the multiple higher as the market assigns SaaS economics to a growing portion of revenue. China divestiture closes smoothly. Buybacks reduce share count by another 5-7%. The stock re-rates to 22-24x adjusted EPS on FY2027 consensus ($5.88), implying $129-141.

**Bear Case ($75-85, -15-25%):** Recovery stalls as government funding cuts and tariff escalation offset pharma recovery. Organic growth remains 2-3% or below. Goodwill impairment test fails, triggering a multi-billion dollar write-down that depresses reported book value. Software comp headwinds in Q2 create a narrative reset. Estimates continue declining. Stock trades to 14-15x adjusted EPS on compressed FY2027 estimates ($5.50), implying $77-82.

**Base Case ($108-115, +8-15%):** Recovery proceeds roughly as guided (3-4% organic), software grows into a more meaningful revenue contributor, China divestiture confirms timeline. Stock grinds toward 20x FY2027 adjusted EPS as confidence builds. Timeline: 12 months.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of 5%+ organic growth confirming the recovery thesis
2. Estimate revisions turning positive (current quarter EPS revised up by 3%+ from current level)
3. Stock pullback to $85-90 range where forward multiple compresses to <16x FY2027 adjusted EPS, providing genuine valuation cushion

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2026 organic growth guidance reduced below 2% (implying recovery stall)
2. Goodwill impairment charge announced (any amount — signals fundamental deterioration in unit economics)
3. Software ARR growth decelerates below 20% (invalidates the recurring revenue re-rating thesis)
4. Management abandons or materially reduces buyback pace while leverage remains elevated (signals cash flow deterioration)

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (late July/early August) will provide the next critical data point on organic growth trajectory, software comp headwind impact, and China divestiture progress. The November 2026 Investor Day is the key catalyst for the software narrative.

## Conclusion

Revvity has improving fundamentals: pharma recovery underway, software growing rapidly, portfolio being simplified, capital being returned aggressively. These are real, positive developments with identifiable timelines. The 12-month growth thesis (pharma recovery → software validation → multiple expansion) is credible and specific.

However, the stock at ~19x FY2026 adjusted EPS with falling estimates, 11.2% short interest, and $9B in goodwill risk is not mispriced on the upside. The risk/reward is balanced at current levels: 8-15% upside in the base case against 15-25% downside in the bear case. The negative estimate revision trend prevents a BUY recommendation until either the price compresses further or the fundamental recovery accelerates enough to reverse estimate momentum. This is a name to hold through the recovery thesis development and upgrade on confirmation, not chase at current levels.
