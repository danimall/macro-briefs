## Executive Summary

Mister Car Wash operates 548 express car wash locations across 21 states with a subscription-first model (76% of wash revenue from 2.3 million Unlimited Wash Club members). The underlying business is operationally healthy: $1.05B revenue growing mid-single digits, $345M adjusted EBITDA at 33% margins, and $286M operating cash flow. However, the stock is no longer a fundamental equity investment.

On February 17, 2026, MCW entered a definitive merger agreement with affiliates of Leonard Green & Partners (LGP) — the company's 67% controlling shareholder — at $7.00/share in cash. The stock now trades at $7.01, offering approximately zero risk-adjusted return. LGP executed written consent to approve the deal without a minority shareholder vote; the HSR waiting period and Information Statement mailing are the only remaining conditions. Competing bids are functionally precluded by LGP's controlling stake and a $31.25M breakup fee. Multiple law firms are investigating fiduciary duty breaches, but these are overwhelmingly likely to result in minor settlements rather than deal blockage or material price improvement.

The calculus is straightforward: there is no equity upside beyond a few cents of time-value-of-money on the $7.00 merger consideration. The opportunity cost of holding this position while waiting for deal closure (expected H1 2026) is the primary risk.

## Company Overview

Mister Car Wash is the largest national car wash brand in the US, operating primarily Express Exterior Locations (485 of 548 total) using a self-drive tunnel format. The company was founded in 1996, grew through acquisition, was taken public by Leonard Green & Partners in June 2021 (likely at $15-16/share), and is now being taken private again by the same sponsor at $7.00.

The Unlimited Wash Club (UWC) subscription program is the business's economic engine: 2.3 million members paying monthly fees that generate 76% of wash sales (up from 74% in 2024). Average UWC members spend approximately 7x more annually than retail customers, providing revenue predictability and high customer lifetime value. The company opened 29 greenfield locations in 2025 at approximately $7.8M per site and acquired 5 additional locations.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** $1.052B in FY2025, up 5.7% YoY. Revenue growth has been steady: $877M (2022) → $927M (2023) → $995M (2024) → $1,052M (2025). Comparable store sales grew +2.9% in 2025, supplemented by new unit contribution.

**Profitability:** Operating income of $200M (19% margin) improved from $179M (18% margin) in 2024. Net income jumped 47% to $103M, driven partly by $20.6M in interest expense savings from voluntary debt repayment. Adjusted EBITDA of $345M at 32.8% margin (vs. 32.3% prior year) demonstrates stable operating leverage.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow of $286M is strong. However, growth capex of $227M consumes most of this, leaving only $30M in reported free cash flow. Maintenance capex is modest at $29M, implying underlying FCF of $257M before growth investment — a healthy 24% margin on revenue.

**Balance Sheet:** $797M in first-lien term loan debt against $28.5M cash. Net leverage of approximately 2.3x EBITDA is manageable. Goodwill of $1.13B (36% of total assets) reflects the company's acquisition-driven history. The Altman Z-Score of 1.33 flags "distress zone," but this is a mechanical artifact: subscription businesses with minimal inventory/receivables and heavy goodwill from PE roll-ups will always score poorly on Z-Score metrics. The company is in full covenant compliance with a $300M undrawn revolver.

**Shareholder Returns:** Since IPO, MCW has generated a total shareholder return index of 36 (starting from 100) versus a peer group at 134. Investors have lost approximately 64% of their capital since LGP took the company public. LGP is now buying back the minority shares at what appears to be a trough valuation.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are functionally irrelevant for current investment decisions given the pending merger. For context on whether the $7.00 price is fair:

- UWC membership grew approximately 7% YoY to 2.3 million
- Greenfield openings moderated to 29 (from 39 in 2024)
- Comparable store sales growth of +2.9% is healthy but decelerating
- Growth capex declined from $301M to $227M, suggesting management is moderating expansion pace
- The company has runway to expand beyond 548 locations in a 60,000+ location fragmented industry

At $7.00/share (approximately $2.3B equity value, or $3.1B enterprise value), the take-private values MCW at roughly 9.0x EV/EBITDA. Comparable recurring-revenue services businesses typically trade at 12-15x EBITDA in private markets. This gap is the core of the fiduciary duty allegations.

## Valuation Assessment

The stock trades at $7.01 against a $7.00 cash merger consideration. Trailing P/E is 22.6x on $0.31 EPS; forward P/E is also 22.6x on consensus $0.31. These metrics are irrelevant given the imminent take-private.

Analyst consensus has collapsed around the deal price: mean target $7.06, median $7.00, range $7.00-$7.50. Twelve analysts currently rate the stock HOLD — the universal response to a pending acquisition with no competing bid.

Against peers: SCI trades at 21.9x, FTDR at 17.7x, ADT at 10.4x (peer median 17.7x). MCW's 22.6x appears elevated but reflects the $7.00 deal floor against depressed trailing earnings. At approximately 9.0x EV/EBITDA, MCW is being acquired below where private market multiples for subscription services businesses would typically transact.

## Competitive Landscape

MCW holds a legitimate competitive position as the largest national car wash operator with meaningful scale advantages: purchasing leverage on chemicals and equipment, brand recognition, proprietary wash chemistry (Unity Chemistry™, Titanium 360°), and a technology platform for subscription management.

The industry is highly fragmented (60,000+ locations, MCW at <1% market share) with active PE capital inflows creating overbuilding risk in certain markets. Competitors include Driven Brands (divesting its car wash operations), Zips Car Wash, and numerous regional operators. The subscription model has become industry-standard rather than proprietary, reducing MCW's early-mover advantage.

Environmental and water access regulation in western states creates operational complexity that advantages scaled operators with compliance infrastructure.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risk — Opportunity Cost:** The deal is overwhelmingly likely to close. The risk is earning near-zero return (approximately 0% annualized) while capital is locked in a collapsed merger-arb spread.

**Deal Break Risk (estimated <5%):** LGP controls 67%, has executed written consent, and conditions are routine. A regulatory challenge to a car wash rollup is essentially impossible. The only conceivable path to a deal break is a successful legal challenge that the entire transaction process was flawed — extremely unlikely under Delaware business judgment review, even for a controlled-company squeeze-out.

**Legal Investigation Risk:** Historical precedent suggests the ongoing investigations result in (a) nuisance settlements of $0.05-0.15/share added to merger consideration, or (b) dismissal. They do not typically block transactions.

**Downside if Deal Fails:** Pre-announcement stock was trading at approximately $5.50-6.00. A deal collapse would send the stock to that range — approximately 15-20% downside.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($7.15-7.50, ~10-15% probability):** Legal proceedings pressure LGP into a modest price bump of $0.15-0.50/share. Some analysts carry targets to $7.50.

**Base Case ($7.00, ~80-85% probability):** Deal closes as agreed, likely by June-July 2026. Shareholders receive cash.

**Bear Case ($5.50-6.00, <5% probability):** Deal collapses due to financing failure, material adverse change, or successful legal injunction. Stock falls 15-20% to pre-announcement levels.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** There is no catalyst to drive the stock materially below $7.00 given the pending acquisition. The deal floor functionally eliminates sustained downside below the merger price. The only short thesis — deal failure — has <5% probability and uncertain timing.

**Timing window:** Indeterminate. Deal expected to close H1 2026, but delays are possible.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** 8.0% SI with 3.5 days to cover. Not crowded, but no rational short thesis when a $7.00 cash deal is 80%+ likely to close.

**Borrow and options signal:** With the stock pinned at $7.00-7.01, options activity is minimal and uninformative. The tight range since February confirms the market views deal closure as near-certain.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Even in a deal-break scenario, downside is approximately $1.00-1.50. Against the carry cost and squeeze risk of shorting into a merger-arb with 85%+ deal completion probability, risk/reward is deeply unfavorable.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** No identifiable catalyst, near-certain deal floor at $7.00, and unfavorable risk/reward for shorts.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria:** Not applicable. Once the $7.00/share cash consideration is paid, MCW ceases to exist as a public company. For investors considering the stock today:

- If seeking merger-arb yield: the spread is <$0.01 (essentially zero). No entry is justified.
- If seeking legal settlement upside: the expected value is approximately $0.05-0.10/share against months of capital lockup. Does not clear minimum hurdle rates.

**Timeline:** Deal expected to close within 2-4 months. After closure, stock is delisted and cash is distributed.

**Headwind duration:** Permanent — this is a terminal event for the public equity.

## Conclusion

MCW is a fundamentally healthy operating business being acquired at what appears to be a below-fair-value price by its controlling shareholder. The $7.00 take-private values the company at approximately 9.0x EBITDA for a recurring-revenue services business growing mid-single digits — a price that likely reflects LGP capturing the remaining upside for itself.

For public market investors, this analysis is academic. The stock trades at $7.01 with a $7.00 cash deal that is 80-85% likely to close within months. There is no equity return available. The recommendation is AVOID: zero upside potential relative to opportunity cost, minimal probability of a price bump from legal proceedings, and no rational entry point for new capital at current levels.
