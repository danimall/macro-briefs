## Executive Summary

MasTec delivered record Q1 2026 results (revenue +34% YoY, adjusted EBITDA +73%, EPS +174%) and raised full-year guidance to $17.5B revenue / $1.5B EBITDA / $8.79 EPS. The company sits at the intersection of four converging multi-decade infrastructure themes: AI/data center power demand, grid modernization, broadband deployment (BEAD), and LNG/pipeline expansion. Backlog reached a record $20.3B with a 1.4x book-to-bill ratio, providing approximately 14 months of revenue visibility based on guided revenue levels.

The stock has tripled from its May 2025 lows ($141→$433), driven by fundamental acceleration that was systematically underestimated by consensus. The valuation question centers on whether the current ~$36B enterprise value adequately prices in FY2027 earnings power of approximately $11.45/share (per sell-side consensus estimates, +30% above FY2026E). At ~38x FY2027E earnings with estimated 30%+ EPS growth, the PEG ratio is approximately 1.2x — reasonable for an infrastructure platform with record backlog, improving margins, and multiple potential growth vectors (turnkey data centers, pipeline segment recovery to $3B+). The May 12 Investor Day represents a near-term catalyst that should provide multi-year financial targets and potentially trigger further consensus estimate revisions.

The primary risks are elevated leverage (net debt/EBITDA ~1.8x), working capital consumption during rapid growth, OBBBA-driven phaseout of clean energy credits (partially mitigated by grandfathering, though MasTec itself warns it may reduce longer-term demand), and execution risk on increasingly complex turnkey projects.

## Company Overview

MasTec is one of North America's largest infrastructure engineering and construction firms with ~36,000 employees across 810 locations. Revenue is diversified across four primary operating segments — Communications ($3.3B), Clean Energy & Infrastructure ($4.7B), Power Delivery ($4.2B), Pipeline Infrastructure ($2.1B) — plus an "Other" segment that primarily reflects equity-method investee earnings (such as Waha JVs) with no material direct revenue. The company derives approximately 44% of revenue from multi-year master service agreements, providing a base of estimated recurring activity, though these agreements do not guarantee minimum volumes and are generally cancelable on short notice. Jorge Mas and Jose R. Mas reported beneficial ownership of 15.0% and 7.8% respectively, with certain overlapping/shared holdings; all current officers and directors as a group owned 21.4%.

The company has transitioned from a heavily telecommunications-dependent contractor (DIRECTV was once ~50% of revenue) to a diversified infrastructure platform capable of self-performing across electrical, civil, fiber, and pipeline trades simultaneously. This breadth enables a newly emerging turnkey data center offering that management describes as a significant differentiator.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** $9.8B (2022) → $12.0B (2023) → $12.3B (2024) → $14.3B (2025) → $17.5B (2026E). Growth accelerated from 3% in the integration year (2024) to 16% in 2025 and is tracking 22%+ in 2026.

**Profitability recovery:** Net income attributable to MasTec, Inc. swung from -$50M (2023) to $399M (2025). Adjusted EBITDA grew from $777M (2022) to $1,150M (2025) with guidance for $1,500M in 2026 (+30%). EBITDA margins expanded from 7.1% (2023) to 8.1% (2025) and management commentary suggests further expansion driven by pricing improvements "that have not really even started hitting financials yet."

**Q1 2026 specifics:** Revenue $3.83B (+34% YoY), Adjusted EBITDA $284M (+73%), margin 7.4% (+170bps). Every segment grew. Pipeline revenue nearly doubled YoY with 21% EBITDA margins. The seasonal distribution is structurally shifting — management expects ~45% of full-year EBITDA in H1, reducing historical back-half concentration and improving earnings quality.

**Balance sheet:** Total debt obligations were $2.346B as of December 31, 2025 (including $405.3M of finance lease and other obligations; approximately $1.94B excluding those items). Net debt/EBITDA ~1.8x (investment grade). Liquidity of approximately $1.7B available under the $1.9B revolving credit facility (after accounting for $118M of revolving loans and $57.1M of letters of credit outstanding at year-end), maturing June 2030. Total liabilities-to-equity ratio of approximately 2.0x places the Altman Z-Score in the grey zone (2.33), though this is typical for capital-intensive construction firms and investment-grade rating is maintained.

**Cash flow:** Operating CF dropped from $1.12B (FY2024) to $546M (FY2025) due to working capital consumed by 16% revenue growth (DSO expanded from 60 to 65 days). Management guides >$1B operating cash flow for 2026 as DSOs normalize. FCF conversion will remain below net income during the current growth phase but should improve as the growth rate moderates in 2027-2028.

## Growth Analysis

MasTec's growth profile is underpinned by structural demand across multiple end-markets:

**AI/Data Centers:** According to industry estimates, AI-driven data center demand could significantly increase U.S. electricity consumption by decade-end. MasTec is pursuing turnkey data center construction (site work + power infrastructure + fiber connectivity) — a capability requiring cross-trade expertise that few competitors can replicate. First project is "progressing very well."

**Grid Modernization:** U.S. electricity demand is rising after more than a decade of little change, with EIA projecting the strongest four-year growth since 2000. Record transmission survey activity reported by Stifel. The Greenlink transmission project is now operating at full contractual scope.

**Pipeline Recovery:** Q1 2026 pipeline revenue nearly doubled YoY. Management expects the segment to reach $3B+ in 2027 (from $2.1B in 2025) with potential to hit prior peaks of $3.5B. "Verbal awards" and negotiations not yet in reported backlog represent potential upside.

**BEAD Broadband Program:** $42.45B in federal funding for underserved broadband deployment, with meaningful revenue impact expected 2027+.

**Estimate revision velocity:** Based on sell-side consensus data, FY2027 EPS estimates have risen approximately 14% over the past 90 days (from ~$10.05 to ~$11.45). FY2026 current-year estimates are up approximately 8% over 90 days. The consistent upward revision trajectory, combined with four consecutive quarterly beats (most recently +38.9%), indicates consensus may still be underestimating growth.

**Backlog:** $20.3B (record, +28% YoY) with 1.4x book-to-bill. MasTec's backlog methodology includes estimated future revenue under MSAs based on historical trends, seasonality, and customer demand communications, and may not be comparable to other companies' backlog measures. At guided FY2026 revenue of $17.5B, backlog represents approximately 14 months of revenue.

## Valuation Assessment

At $433.28, MTZ trades at:
- ~49x FY2026E EPS ($8.81 consensus)
- ~38x FY2027E EPS (~$11.45, based on sell-side consensus)
- ~24x FY2026E EBITDA ($1.5B) on approximately $36.2B EV (market cap of ~$34.1B plus net debt of ~$2.1B)
- PEG ratio: ~1.2x on estimated FY2027 growth (38x P/E ÷ ~30% EPS growth)

**Peer context:** Based on sell-side consensus data, peer median P/E is approximately 40x (STRL at ~50x, DY at ~41x, PRIM at ~33x). MTZ's growth rate substantially exceeds most peers — revenue +34% YoY in Q1 vs. peer average in the mid-teens. A premium to DY and PRIM appears justified by superior growth, broader diversification, and turnkey data center optionality that peers lack.

**Potential upside not fully priced:** (1) FY2027 consensus of ~$11.45 could rise post-Investor Day (May 12) if management provides credible multi-year targets; (2) pipeline segment recovery to $3B+ in 2027 would represent ~$900M incremental revenue from FY2025 levels at potentially attractive margins, though the incremental EPS contribution depends on assumptions about tax rates, interest costs, and corporate overhead absorption, and may already be partially reflected in current consensus estimates; (3) turnkey data center revenue is in early stages with management describing "exponential" opportunity; (4) margin expansion from pricing improvements hasn't fully materialized in reported numbers yet.

**Analyst consensus (based on market data):** Sell-side consensus shows approximately 17 out of 19 analysts with Buy/Strong Buy ratings, with mean target of approximately $448 and high target of approximately $518. The stock is trading just below the mean target, which normally signals caution. However, for high-momentum names with positive estimate revisions, analyst targets typically lag — if growth targets at Investor Day are credible, targets could migrate higher.

**Valuation conclusion:** At ~38x FY2027E with estimated 30%+ EPS growth and a PEG of ~1.2x, MTZ is not cheap in absolute terms but is reasonably priced on forward estimates given the growth profile and within the range of comparable high-growth infrastructure peers. The stock becomes expensive above ~50x FY2027E (>$570), and becomes more compelling below ~30x FY2027E (<$345).

## Competitive Landscape

MasTec operates in a fragmented industry against Quanta Services (PWR), EMCOR, MYR Group, and Dycom, plus numerous regional players. Post-pandemic, several pipeline competitors failed or exited, improving MTZ's positioning in that segment.

Key competitive advantages:
1. **Cross-trade breadth:** Ability to self-perform electrical, civil, fiber, and pipeline work enables turnkey offerings competitors cannot match
2. **Scale:** $20.3B backlog and 36,000 employees provide operating leverage and customer access
3. **MSA relationships:** Approximately 44% of revenue under multi-year service agreements with utilities and telecoms provides baseline activity
4. **Management continuity:** Mas family ownership since inception; CEO in role since 2007

The turnkey data center capability requires simultaneous expertise in site preparation, power delivery, fiber installation, and construction management that few competitors possess. This positions MTZ to capture substantially more revenue per project than single-trade peers.

Book-to-bill at 1.4x demonstrates demand exceeding capacity, which supports pricing power and margin expansion.

## Risk Assessment

**OBBBA/Clean Energy Credit Phaseout:** IRA credits for solar/wind eliminated for projects placed in service after December 31, 2027 unless construction begins by July 4, 2026. This creates a near-term rush (positive for 2026-2027 revenue) but MasTec's own risk disclosures warn that the phaseout may reduce longer-term demand for solar and wind projects beyond 2027. Partial mitigation: corporate PPAs and economics increasingly drive renewables independent of tax credits; data center operators need power regardless of policy. Clean Energy & Infrastructure segment revenue includes both renewables and non-renewables work (heavy civil, data center, and power generation), though the exact split is not precisely disclosed.

**Tariff Uncertainty:** Steel, concrete, copper, and solar panel tariffs raise customer costs and could delay project decisions. February 2026 SCOTUS ruling struck down certain IEEPA country-specific tariffs but did not affect sectoral tariffs for steel, aluminum, and copper; new tariffs under alternative authorities continue to create uncertainty. This primarily affects project timing (delays) rather than eliminating demand.

**Working Capital/Leverage:** Total liabilities-to-equity of ~2.0x, Z-Score 2.33 (grey zone), FCF margin of 2% in FY2025. Total debt obligations of $2.346B (including finance leases). Rapid growth consumes working capital. If growth stalls while working capital remains deployed, leverage metrics deteriorate. Mitigation: investment-grade rating, ~$1.7B liquidity, management guiding >$1B OCF in 2026.

**Contract Cancellability:** 47% of backlog under MSAs cancelable on short notice without minimum purchase guarantees. However, in practice MSAs represent ongoing infrastructure maintenance work with historically low cancellation rates.

**Execution Risk:** Shift toward larger, more complex turnkey projects (data centers, Greenlink transmission) carries higher fixed-price risk. Any material project overrun could significantly impact margins. This risk is partially mitigated by management's track record executing large projects and the 170bps margin improvement in Q1 2026.

**Price Appreciation Rate:** The stock has tripled in 12 months. While fundamentally justified, this creates elevated expectations. Any growth deceleration or earnings miss would likely produce an outsized selloff given the elevated starting multiple.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($500-520, +15-20%):** Investor Day (May 12) provides credible 3-5 year targets showing revenue reaching $22-25B with EBITDA margins of 10%+, implying FY2028 EPS of $14-16. Pipeline segment inflects to $3B+ in 2027. Turnkey data center wins accelerate, adding $1-2B in new high-margin revenue. Consensus FY2027 EPS revises to $12-13, and the stock re-rates to 40x forward. Operating cash flow normalization to >$1B in 2026 alleviates balance sheet concerns.

**Bear Case ($300-330, -24-31%):** OBBBA clean energy credit phaseout slows renewables backlog growth starting in late 2026. Tariff uncertainty delays customer decisions, particularly in solar-intensive projects. Margin expansion disappoints as labor costs and project complexity offset pricing gains. Working capital continues to consume cash. Pipeline segment recovery stalls below management's $3B target. Multiple compresses toward peer median (~40x) on FY2026E earnings: 40x × $8.81 = $352.

**Base Case ($465-490, +7-13%):** Estimates continue rising modestly post-Investor Day. FY2026 execution matches raised guidance. Pipeline inflection materializes in H2. Stock re-rates to ~42x FY2027E EPS as visibility improves: 42x × $11.45 = $481.

## Conclusion

MasTec is a genuine infrastructure beneficiary with accelerating fundamentals, record visibility, and multiple growth vectors still in early stages. The stock is expensive on trailing metrics but reasonably priced on forward estimates given estimated 30%+ EPS growth, a PEG of ~1.2x, and catalysts (Investor Day, pipeline inflection, data center wins) that could drive further consensus revisions. The consistent upward estimate revision trend and four consecutive earnings beats support the fundamental thesis.

The primary concern is valuation compression risk if growth decelerates — the current multiple leaves limited margin for error. However, with $20.3B in backlog (+28% YoY), a 1.4x book-to-bill, and pricing improvements still flowing through, deceleration appears unlikely over the next 12 months. BUY with 2-3% position sizing, with the Investor Day (May 12) as the near-term catalyst and Q2 earnings as the confirmation point.
