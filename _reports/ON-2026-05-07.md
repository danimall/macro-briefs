## Executive Summary

ON Semiconductor is executing through the most severe cyclical downturn in its recent history — revenue declined 28% from peak ($8.33B in 2022 to $6.0B in 2025) — and is now showing clear signs of recovery. Q1 2026 delivered 5% YoY revenue growth (the first positive quarter in over a year), AI data center revenue doubled, and GAAP gross margins recovered from 20.3% to 38.5%. The restructuring program ($935M in total charges) is winding down, capex has normalized from 19% to 5% of revenue, and the company is positioned across three secular growth vectors: silicon carbide for EVs, power management for AI data centers, and advanced imaging for ADAS.

The fundamental recovery is supported by multiple data points: NIO/Geely SiC partnerships, the Qorvo/Aura acquisitions for AI data center power, and management's characterization of an inflection in demand. Estimate revisions are positive across all periods (6-8% upward in 30 days). However, the stock has appreciated 175% from its May 2025 low of $37.19 to $104.78, and now trades at approximately 34x FY2026 consensus EPS ($3.09 current year, $4.27 next year). This multiple is reasonable for the growth trajectory but does not embed material upside unless revenue acceleration exceeds current expectations. With 10.5% short interest, implied volatility at 89% (vs. 51% HV), and insider selling at elevated levels, the risk/reward from current levels is balanced rather than asymmetric.

## Company Overview

ON Semiconductor is a $41B market cap semiconductor company operating through three segments: Power Solutions Group (PSG, 47% of revenue), Analog & Mixed-Signal Group (AMG, 38%), and Intelligent Sensing Group (ISG, 15%). The company serves automotive (51% of revenue), industrial (28%), and other markets including AI data centers (21%).

The strategic transformation under CEO Hassane El-Khoury since 2020 has repositioned ON from a broad-line analog/discrete supplier into a focused automotive/industrial power and sensing company, with vertically integrated SiC manufacturing as the core competitive differentiator. The 2025 Manufacturing Realignment Program accelerated this pivot by exiting lower-value product lines (evidenced by the $230M ISG inventory write-down) while concentrating capital on SiC and AI data center power products.

## Growth Analysis

**Three growth vectors at varying maturities:**

1. **Silicon carbide for EVs (near-term catalyst):** The NIO and Geely partnerships for 900V architectures are the most tangible near-term revenue driver. The shift from 400V to 900V doubles SiC content per vehicle. ON's vertically integrated manufacturing (crystal growth through packaging) provides cost and supply security advantages vs. competitors reliant on external substrates. PSG revenue grew 14% YoY in Q1 2026.

2. **AI data center power (emerging, high-growth):** Revenue doubled YoY in Q1. The Qorvo SiC JFET ($119M) and Aura Semiconductor Vcore (up to $144M) acquisitions assembled a full power tree solution covering AC-DC through DC-DC and point-of-load. This positions ON across the entire power delivery chain for AI accelerator systems.

3. **Advanced imaging for ADAS (restructuring, then growth):** ISG took a $230M write-down and is pivoting to high-value automotive ADAS sensors and away from commoditized applications. Near-term drag, longer-term margin accretion.

**Estimate revision momentum:** All periods show positive revisions — current quarter +8.2% in 30 days, next year +5.9%. This confirms fundamental improvement is being recognized by consensus. However, the revision magnitude (6-8%) is meaningful but below the 30%+ threshold that would indicate systematic underestimation.

**Q2 2026 guidance:** Revenue $1.535-$1.635B (midpoint ~$1.585B, implying ~8% YoY growth), non-GAAP EPS $0.65-$0.77. Sequential acceleration from Q1 is embedded in guidance.

If ON achieves its prior peak profitability on current revenue, EPS would be ~$5.50-6.00 (given lower share count), placing the stock at 18-19x normalized earnings.

For the stock to re-rate further, ON needs to demonstrate sustained revenue growth above current consensus (the $4.27 FY2027 estimate implies ~38% EPS growth, which is aggressive but achievable given operating leverage). The stock is not cheap, but it is not egregiously expensive for a semiconductor company in early-cycle recovery.

## Competitive Landscape

**SiC positioning — top-3 globally:** ON's vertical integration in SiC (from boule growth to device fabrication) is a genuine differentiator. The company can control substrate supply and quality while competitors (notably Wolfspeed) have struggled with manufacturing yields. The 200mm SiC transition will be critical — ON has not yet fully transitioned but is investing toward it.

**AI data center power — entering a crowded market:** The full power tree strategy is compelling but ON faces entrenched competitors. Texas Instruments, Analog Devices, and Monolithic Power Systems all have strong data center franchises. ON's differentiation lies in SiC-based AC-DC solutions (from the Qorvo acquisition) where existing players use silicon. The revenue doubling suggests traction, but market share data is not yet available.

**Automotive imaging — strong market position, narrowing focus:** ON is a leading supplier of CMOS image sensors for ADAS, competing primarily with Sony. The ISG restructuring suggests product rationalization, likely exiting consumer/industrial imaging to concentrate on high-value automotive applications where ON has a structural advantage in long product lifecycles and qualification requirements.

**Competitive trajectory:** Improving. The NIO/Geely wins, AI traction, and portfolio rationalization all suggest ON is gaining relevance in the highest-growth semiconductor sub-segments.

## Risk Assessment

**Near-term risks:**

1. **Convertible note dilution at current prices.** The 0% Notes ($805M, strike $52.97) are deeply in-the-money. Hedges cover only to $74.34 — above that price, warrants create approximately 15.2M shares of dilution. The 0.50% Notes ($1.5B, strike $103.87) just breached conversion threshold. While the new 2031 Notes (strike ~$160) aren't immediately dilutive, ON carries ~$3.6B in convertibles with real dilution implications at current prices.

2. **Chinese revenue concentration.** 27% of billings through Hong Kong (proxy for China-based customers). Tariff escalation, export controls, or geopolitical disruption could impair this revenue stream — particularly relevant for SiC products used in Chinese EVs.

3. **Cyclical recovery may stall.** Automotive production schedules remain uncertain. European OEM weakness, mixed Western EV demand, and industrial capex sensitivity to macro conditions could delay the anticipated H2 2026 acceleration.

4. **Execution on AI data center still early.** Revenue doubled from a small base. The competitive landscape (TXN, ADI, MPS) is fierce, and ON's historical strength has been automotive/industrial, not hyperscaler relationships.

**Structural risks:**

5. **EV adoption trajectory.** A sustained slowdown in EV penetration (beyond the current pause in Western markets) would impair the SiC investment thesis. However, Chinese EV adoption continues accelerating, providing geographic diversification.

6. **Technology risk.** If GaN displaces SiC in certain voltage ranges, or if OEMs shift architecture preferences, ON's heavy SiC investment could yield lower returns.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

2. SiC order cancellations from major automotive customers (NIO/Geely partnerships retracted)
3. Revenue guidance cut or flat sequential guidance in Q3/Q4 — would signal the recovery was a head-fake

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (likely late July/early August 2026). Q2 results will either confirm the recovery is accelerating (bull case builds) or reveal a slower ramp (creating entry point or suggesting structural challenges).

## Conclusion

ON Semiconductor presents a genuine cyclical recovery story supported by structural growth vectors in SiC/EVs and AI data center power. The management team has executed well through the downturn (aggressive buybacks at trough, portfolio rationalization, capex normalization) and the company is positioned for meaningful margin expansion as volumes recover.

At $104.78, after a 175% rally from 52-week lows, the stock trades above consensus analyst targets and at 25x FY2027 earnings — a multiple that embeds substantial recovery expectations. The positive estimate revision trend confirms fundamental improvement but does not indicate systematic market underestimation that would drive further re-rating from current levels.

The appropriate posture is to hold for existing owners (the growth trajectory supports continued appreciation toward $120-125 over 12 months) while acknowledging that initiating new positions at $105 does not offer favorable risk/reward. A pullback to $80-85 on broader market volatility or a quarter of revenue acceleration above consensus would shift the calculus toward an active BUY.
