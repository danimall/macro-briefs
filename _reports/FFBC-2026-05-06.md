## Executive Summary

First Financial Bancorp is an approximately $22 billion-asset regional bank headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, that has materially transformed its earnings profile through three acquisitions completed between February 2024 and January 2026. Per the company's Q1 2026 earnings release, adjusted EPS was $0.77 (+22% YoY), revenue was $271.5M (+29% YoY), and net interest margin was 3.99%. Consensus EPS estimates have reportedly risen approximately 5% over the past 30 days and 3.7% over 90 days, reflecting improving fundamental visibility as acquisition synergies begin to materialize.

The stock trades at approximately 9.3x consensus FY2026E EPS of $3.28, a substantial discount to estimated peer medians. This discount exists partly because of acquisition integration execution risk, but the BankFinancial deal generated a bargain purchase gain, Westfield system conversion is complete, and management guides to 55-56% efficiency ratio by H2 2026. The bank's 3.33% dividend yield provides income support while investors wait for the multiple gap to narrow.

The primary risks are integration execution across three deals completed over approximately 22 months, sensitivity to rate movements, and the broader macro uncertainty around credit quality (tariffs, elevated rates, CRE stress). Capital ratios remain solid (preliminary CET1 11.32% at year-end 2025), asset quality is manageable (Q4 2025 NCOs at 27 bps, ACL/nonaccrual coverage at 183%), and the specialty lending platforms provide diversification absent from most regional bank peers.

## Company Overview

First Financial Bancorp operates through approximately 134 full-service banking centers (prior to BankFinancial) across Ohio, Indiana, Kentucky, and Illinois, with a newly established Chicago presence via the BankFinancial acquisition (adding approximately 17-18 offices). Beyond traditional community banking, FFBC has built differentiated national specialty platforms: Oak Street Funding (lending to insurance agents/RIAs), First Franchise Capital (QSR/restaurant financing), Bannockburn Global Forex (middle-market FX hedging, 11 offices), Summit Funding Group (equipment leasing), and Agile Premium Finance (insurance premium financing, all 50 states). Yellow Cardinal Advisory Group manages $3.9B in wealth assets.

This multi-platform structure generates fee income diversification unusual for a bank of this size and provides organic growth avenues in specialized lending niches where pricing power is more defensible than in commodity commercial lending.

## Financial Analysis

**Profitability:** Q1 2026 NIM of 3.99% (per company release) is well above the regional bank median. Management guides NIM between 3.99%-4.04% for the balance of 2026, supported by asset-sensitive positioning that benefits from the current elevated rate environment. Net income for FY2025 was $255.6M (adjusted: $281.1M), and the Q1 2026 annualized run rate, adjusting for the reported bargain purchase gain, suggests FY2026 net income could reach the $280-310M range, though this projection depends on acquisition synergy realization and credit trends.

**Capital Position:** As of December 31, 2025, preliminary capital ratios were CET1 11.32%, Tier 1 11.60%, and Total Capital 15.46% — all well above well-capitalized minimums, though CET1 and Tier 1 declined from Q3 2025 levels (12.91% and 13.23%, respectively) due to the Westfield acquisition. Tangible book value per share was $15.74 at December 31, 2025, down 2.8% linked quarter from $16.19 at September 30, 2025, reflecting acquisition-related impacts. Per the company's Q1 2026 release, TBV reportedly recovered to $16.15. The $300M subordinated notes issued in November 2025 at 6.375% fixed support the acquisition-driven balance sheet growth while maintaining regulatory capital well above targets.

**Asset Quality:** At Q4 2025, annualized net charge-offs were 27 bps, improved from 40 bps in Q4 2024. ACL coverage at 1.39% of loans (183% of nonaccruals) provides a meaningful reserve cushion. Classified assets of $235.5M include $20.4M from acquired Westfield portfolios. Nonaccrual loans were 0.76% of total loans and nonperforming assets were 0.48% of total assets at year-end 2025. Commercial office exposure is estimated at approximately $347.5M (roughly 3% of total loans), per company filings.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow of $337.9M and free cash flow of $317.1M for FY2025 reflect strong cash generation, though for a bank, capital ratios, NII trends, and tangible book growth are more relevant valuation metrics than free cash flow. The 50-60% total payout ratio target (dividends plus buybacks) is conservative and leaves substantial retained capital for organic growth.

**Efficiency:** Currently elevated due to acquisition costs. Management guides to 55-56% by H2 2026 as Westfield saves are fully realized (target Q3 2026) and BankFinancial integration completes (target Q4 2026). This represents meaningful earnings leverage from the current cost structure.

## Growth Analysis

FFBC's growth is driven by three vectors:

1. **Acquisition Synergies (Near-Term).** Westfield added $2.1B assets/$1.7B loans/$1.8B deposits with system conversion already complete. BankFinancial added ~$1.4B assets/$1.2B deposits and 17 Chicago offices. Full run-rate cost savings from both deals should be achieved by Q4 2026. The BankFinancial transaction reportedly generated a bargain purchase gain of $8.9M per the Q1 2026 release, which may suggest favorable deal economics, though bargain purchase gains can also reflect purchase accounting marks and fair-value adjustments on the acquired balance sheet.

2. **Specialty Platform Organic Growth.** Leasing revenue grew 25.4% YoY through 9M 2025. Bannockburn FX is annualizing at ~$57M+ in revenue. Agile Premium Finance (acquired Feb 2024) is scaling across all 50 states. These platforms operate in less competitive niches where FFBC has operational expertise and established distribution.

3. **Chicago Market Entry.** The BankFinancial footprint provides physical infrastructure to build a full-service wholesale banking team in one of the Midwest's largest commercial markets. Management is actively hiring commercial bankers, wealth advisers, and private bankers for this platform.

Consensus EPS estimates of $3.28 for FY2026 and $3.43 for FY2027 represent 23% and 5% growth respectively. The reported 30-day revision of approximately +5% for FY2026 suggests analysts may still be catching up to the run-rate earnings power of the combined entity, though this estimate revision data has not been independently verified from primary sources.

## Valuation Assessment

At $30.67 current price, FFBC trades at:
- ~9.3x FY2026E consensus EPS of $3.28
- ~10.8x trailing P/E
- ~1.9x tangible book value ($16.15 per Q1 2026 company report)
- 3.33% dividend yield

The ~9.3x forward multiple represents a meaningful discount to regional bank peers, which generally trade at higher multiples. Even adjusting for FFBC's slightly higher risk profile (acquisition integration), a re-rating to 12-13x on $3.28 EPS would yield a $39-43 fair value range. The analyst consensus median target of $34.00 (mean $33.43) likely hasn't fully updated for the Q1 2026 beat and subsequent estimate revisions.

The discount to peers is specific and identifiable: investors are penalizing FFBC for three acquisitions completed over approximately 22 months (Agile in February 2024, Westfield in November 2025, BankFinancial in January 2026), with the latter two closing just two months apart. As integration milestones are achieved through H2 2026 (Westfield saves by Q3, BankFinancial by Q4), the discount should narrow. At 12x $3.28, the stock would trade at $39.36 — 28% above current levels.

## Competitive Landscape

FFBC occupies a strategic position: large enough to offer sophisticated products (FX hedging, equipment leasing, premium finance) but small enough to maintain relationship-based decision-making. Management explicitly noted competitive pressure from large regionals offering sub-200bp spreads with eliminated covenants on commercial loans — FFBC has chosen to maintain pricing discipline rather than chase volume.

The specialty platforms create durable competitive advantages: Oak Street, First Franchise Capital, and Agile Premium Finance operate in niches where borrower relationships, underwriting expertise, and operational infrastructure create switching costs. Bannockburn's 11-office national FX platform is difficult to replicate and benefits from market volatility.

CRA rating of "Outstanding" provides regulatory advantage for future M&A approvals. The bank's 163-year operating history and relationship depth in Ohio/Indiana/Kentucky create deposit franchise value that new entrants cannot easily replicate.

Key vulnerability: at $22B+ in assets post-BankFinancial, FFBC is approaching regulatory thresholds that impose additional compliance costs. Additionally, stablecoin legislation (GENIUS Act) and fintech deposit competition represent structural headwinds to deposit gathering.

## Risk Assessment

**Integration Execution (Moderate-High).** Three acquisitions over approximately 22 months represents an aggressive pace, particularly given that Westfield and BankFinancial closed just two months apart. Westfield conversion is complete, BankFinancial is in progress. The 55-56% efficiency ratio target by H2 2026 is achievable but requires disciplined execution. Any integration stumbles would delay the earnings leverage investors are counting on.

**Credit Quality in Elevated Rate Environment.** With rates held elevated, borrowers face sustained pressure. FFBC's franchise lending (QSR restaurants) is sensitive to consumer spending weakness. Management flagged tariff risk and geopolitical uncertainty (Iran) in the Q1 2026 call.

**ICRE Payoff Headwinds.** Elevated payoffs in the institutional CRE portfolio due to property sales and competitive pressure create a drag on loan growth. Management says this is moderating but the competitive environment remains fierce.

**Bannockburn Concentration.** The FX business relies on a small number of large clients and benefits from volatility. The $37M receivable from a terminated trade counterparty (declining from $45M, expected to be collected in full) highlights counterparty risk inherent in this business.

**Goodwill Risk.** $1.1B in goodwill represents 34% of market cap. While impairment testing is unlikely to be triggered at current valuations and profitability levels, a sustained market cap decline would create headline risk.

**Rate Sensitivity.** Asset-sensitive positioning (+5.6% NII in +200bp scenario, -4.4% in -100bp). In the current environment where rates are held elevated, this benefits FFBC. A shift to rate cuts would create NIM headwinds with approximately 6-9 month lag.

**Capital Consumption from Acquisitions.** CET1 declined from 12.91% (Q3 2025) to 11.32% (Q4 2025) following Westfield. While still well above minimums, further capital deployment capacity is more limited until organic earnings rebuild the ratio.

**Short Interest.** At 4.6% of float with 5.3 days to cover, short interest is modest and creates no material squeeze risk.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($37-40; +21% to +30%):** Integration proceeds on schedule. Efficiency ratio reaches 55% by Q4 2026. NIM stays above 3.95%. Chicago market ramp contributes incremental growth. Multiple re-rates from ~9.3x to 11-12x FY2026E as execution proves out. EPS of $3.30-3.40 at 12x = $39.60-40.80.

**Bear Case ($25-27; -12% to -18%):** Credit quality deteriorates as recession materializes. Integration costs exceed expectations. NIM compresses on rate cuts or deposit competition. Multiple stays depressed at 8-9x on reduced EPS of $2.80-3.00.

**Base Case ($35-37; +14% to +21%):** Consensus estimates are achieved. Partial re-rating toward peer multiples as integration milestones are met. 10.8x $3.28 = $35.42. Plus 3.3% dividend yield.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. NIM falls below 3.75% for two consecutive quarters (indicates structural competitive/rate pressure)
2. NCO ratio exceeds 50 bps (indicates credit deterioration beyond normal cyclical range)
3. Management announces delays to cost save targets beyond Q4 2026

## Conclusion

FFBC offers a compelling risk/reward at approximately 9.3x consensus FY2026 EPS with a 3.3% dividend yield and identifiable catalysts (acquisition cost saves) that should drive the stock toward a more appropriate 10.8-12x multiple over the next 9-12 months. The bank's above-peer NIM, diversified specialty platforms, and adequate capital position justify at least a partial closing of the valuation gap with peers. The primary risk is integration execution, but early evidence (reported bargain purchase gain on BankFinancial, completed Westfield conversion, Q1 beat) is encouraging. At current levels, the stock is priced for mediocre execution; if management delivers on its 55-56% efficiency target, meaningful upside exists.
