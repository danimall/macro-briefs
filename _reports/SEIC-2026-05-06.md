## Executive Summary

SEI Investments is a $11B financial technology and asset management company generating $2.3B in annual revenue with 27% operating margins, 29% ROE, and near-zero leverage. The stock trades at 15.5x forward earnings—below the peer median of 18.5x—despite accelerating revenue growth (+13% YoY in Q1 2026), a consistent earnings beat pattern, and a transformational acquisition (Stratos) that shifts the company's growth profile toward direct advisory delivery. Consensus estimates have risen 3-4% over the past 90 days, and the most recent quarter beat consensus by 7%.

The core thesis rests on three pillars: (1) SEI's integrated technology-custody-investment model creates switching costs that support 90%+ revenue retention, (2) the Stratos acquisition and alternative asset administration tailwinds provide visible 10-15% revenue growth through 2027, and (3) aggressive share buybacks ($208M in Q1 alone) compound EPS growth well beyond organic revenue gains. The stock has appreciated 14% over the past year but remains 3% below its 52-week high of $93.96, with analyst consensus targeting $105-107.

Key risks include market-sensitive fee revenue (a 20% equity market decline would reduce revenue by approximately $100-150M), Stratos integration execution, FCA regulatory constraints on the UK subsidiary, and secular decline at LSV Asset Management. These are real but quantifiable risks that do not warrant the current discount to peers.

## Company Overview

SEI Investments operates through five segments serving different client channels: Investment Managers (35% of revenue), Private Banks (26%), Investment Advisors (25%), Institutional Investors (12%), and New Businesses (2%). The company administers, advises, or manages $1.93 trillion in assets and supports $8.1 trillion on its wealth management platforms. Key clients include 8 of the top 20 US banks and 43 of the top 100 global investment managers.

The business model is distinctive: SEI integrates proprietary technology platforms (SEI Wealth Platform, SEI Scope), trust-based custody, and investment management into a single offering. This creates operational complexity for clients to replicate or switch away from—multi-year implementation cycles produce contracts with 5-7 year effective durations. Revenue splits approximately 57% technology and operations fees (more recurring) vs. 38% asset management fees (market-sensitive) vs. 5% professional services.

SEI also holds a 38.5% equity interest in LSV Asset Management, a $105B value equity specialist that contributed $132M in equity earnings in FY2025.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Earnings Trajectory**

FY2025 revenue reached $2.30B (+8% YoY) with net income of $715M (+23% YoY) and EPS of $5.63 (+28% YoY). The EPS growth outpaced revenue and net income growth due to share count reduction from buybacks. Q1 2026 continued the trend: revenue $622M (+13% YoY), operating income $190M (+21% YoY), and adjusted EPS $1.44, beating consensus of $1.34 by $0.10 (7%).

Operating margins expanded from 22.1% in FY2023 to 27.3% in FY2025. The Private Banks segment showed the most dramatic improvement—from 10% margins in 2023 to 21% in Q1 2026—indicating successful standardization of platform implementations.

**Balance Sheet**

The balance sheet carries minimal risk: $32M in long-term debt, current ratio of 3.29, Altman Z-Score of 4.8, and a $500M undrawn credit facility extending to 2030. SEI generated $585M in free cash flow in FY2025 (25.5% FCF margin) and $222M in operating cash flow in Q1 2026 alone (+51% YoY). ROA of 20.8% and ROE of 29.2% rank among the highest in financial services.

**Capital Return**

SEI returned $740M to shareholders in FY2025 ($616M buybacks + $124M dividends) and $272M in Q1 2026 ($208M buybacks + $64M dividends). Shares outstanding declined from 137.4M in 2022 to 120.4M in Q1 2026—a 12% reduction in ~3 years. At the current buyback pace (~$800M annualized), SEI is reducing share count by 4-5% per year, creating a mechanical 4-5% EPS tailwind independent of business growth.

**Cash Flow Quality**

FCF/Net Income conversion was approximately 82% in FY2025, indicating high-quality earnings. The operating cash flow surge in Q1 2026 (+51% YoY) partly reflects working capital timing, but the underlying trajectory supports continued strong cash generation.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Growth Drivers (12-18 months)**

1. **Alternative asset administration:** Investment Managers revenue grew 15% YoY in Q1 2026, driven by outsourcing of fund administration for private credit, hedge funds, and private equity. SEI administers $1.0T in alternative assets. This is a secular trend—large managers shifting from in-house operations to third-party platforms—with years of runway.

2. **Stratos contribution:** The acquisition contributed $19M in Q1 2026 revenue ($76M annualized). With 350+ advisors and $38.4B in client assets, plus ongoing EMI entity acquisitions ($130.5M deployed in Q1), Stratos could contribute $100-120M in annual revenue within 12 months. It shifts SEI from exclusively enabling advisors to also participating in advisory economics.

3. **Private Banks margin expansion:** From 10% to 21% in two years, with management guiding toward continued improvement as standardized SWP implementations replace custom work. Each additional margin point on $573M revenue adds ~$6M to operating income.

4. **Buyback-driven EPS accretion:** 4-5% share count reduction annually, funded comfortably from FCF.

**Medium-Term Growth (2-3 years)**

Consensus projects FY2026E EPS of $5.88 (+4% vs. FY2025) and FY2027E of $6.62 (+13%). The modest FY2026 growth rate reflects Stratos integration costs and intangible amortization (~$24M/year). Normalized for these items, underlying organic growth is closer to 10-12%.

**Estimate Revision Momentum**

Current quarter estimates rose 2.6% in 30 days. FY2026 estimates rose 3.7% in 30 days, from $5.67 to $5.88. FY2027 estimates rose similarly. This is positive directional momentum, though not at the 30%+ velocity that signals a major re-rating. The trend is consistent with a steady-growth compounder rather than a cyclical recovery story.

## Valuation Assessment

At $91.17, SEIC trades at:
- 15.5x forward P/E (using $5.88 FY2026E)
- ~13.8x FY2027E EPS of $6.62
- ~5.3% FCF yield (on FY2025 FCF of ~$585M / $11B market cap)
- 1.14% dividend yield

**Peer Comparison**

The peer median P/E is 18.5x. SEIC at 15.5x trades at a 16% discount despite superior margins (27% vs. peers averaging 15-20%), higher ROE (29%), near-zero leverage, and accelerating growth. The discount likely reflects: (a) SEI's historical underperformance vs. peers on a TSR basis (152 vs. 202 indexed over 5 years), (b) market-sensitivity of AUM-linked revenue, and (c) complexity of the Stratos transaction structure.

**Analyst Targets**

Consensus median target is $107 (18% upside), with a range of $69-$122. At peer-median 18.5x on FY2026E EPS of $5.88, fair value would be $109. At 18.5x on FY2027E of $6.62, fair value reaches $122 (matching the high target).

**Valuation Verdict**

The stock is reasonably valued on trailing metrics but cheap relative to forward earnings growth and peer multiples. The 5.3% FCF yield provides downside support, while multiple expansion toward peers (even partially—from 15.5x to 17x) combined with earnings growth provides a path to $100-110 within 12 months.

## Competitive Landscape

SEI occupies a defensible niche as an integrated technology-operations-investment platform that spans custody, fund administration, and investment management. Competitors typically excel in one of these areas (SS&C in technology, State Street in custody, BlackRock in investment management) but few combine all three under a single platform with the depth SEI offers.

SWP implementations take 12-18 months and involve migrating custody relationships, data feeds, reporting frameworks, and compliance infrastructure. Client contracts effectively run 5-7 years. Revenue retention rates consistently exceed 90%.

The Investment Managers segment is gaining market share in alternative fund administration—a segment growing 15-20% annually as managers outsource. The Stratos acquisition positions SEI to benefit from RIA consolidation, addressing advisor succession planning with an integrated platform-plus-services offering that standalone aggregators lack.

**Vulnerability:** Fee compression is real. SEI fund program revenue in Investment Advisors declined from $239M to $225M over two years as clients shift to lower-fee SMAs and ETFs. The platform-only model (SEI earns technology fees but not investment management fees) is growing at 31-33% but carries lower per-dollar economics. LSV's persistent client outflows and value-style underperformance represent a structural headwind to one of SEI's highest-margin revenue streams ($132M equity earnings).

## Risk Assessment

**Market Sensitivity (Moderate-High)**

Approximately 38% of revenue is directly tied to asset levels. A 20% equity market decline would reduce AUM-linked revenue by an estimated $100-150M (6-7% of total revenue), compressing margins given the largely fixed cost base. However, the technology/operations segment (57% of revenue) is more resistant—fees are often transaction-based or fixed retainers.

**Stratos Integration (Moderate)**

The $544M acquisition with complex minority interest structure (42.5% put/call at 36/54/72 months) creates: (a) $24M/year intangible amortization drag, (b) potential additional $400-500M in future payments, (c) integration execution risk across 11+ EMI entities. Early results are encouraging ($19M Q1 revenue, $3.1M operating profit) but the full structure won't be resolved until 2028-2031.

**FCA Regulatory Overhang (Moderate)**

The UK subsidiary operates under a Voluntary Requirement limiting growth until governance remediation is confirmed. Given that UK/Ireland represents a meaningful portion of international operations, this creates a real constraint on one growth vector. Timeline for resolution is uncertain.

**LSV Structural Decline (Low-Moderate)**

LSV contributed $132M in FY2025 equity earnings but faces persistent net outflows and value-style underperformance. SEI has no operational control. A sustained decline to $90B AUM (from $105B) would reduce contributions by approximately $35-40M pre-tax. The risk is gradual rather than catastrophic.

**Options Market Signal**

Near-term IV at 31.6% (vs. 22.3% historical volatility) indicates the market is pricing elevated uncertainty post-earnings. The term structure is in backwardation (31.6% near-term vs. 26.3% at 6 months), suggesting imminent event risk that dissipates over time—likely reflecting the recent large earnings move and potential for mean reversion. Put/call skew at 8.9% shows modestly elevated put demand but nothing extreme. The 9.3pp IV premium over HV suggests buying protection is expensive at current levels—consistent with post-earnings positioning rather than structural concern.

**Overall Risk Profile**

SEI's risks are real but diversified and quantifiable. No single risk threatens more than 15-20% of earnings. The near-zero leverage and massive FCF generation provide a substantial buffer against adverse scenarios. Short interest at 1.9% with 2.6 days to cover confirms minimal institutional bearish positioning.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($107-122, +18% to +34%)**

Multiple expands toward peer median (18.5x) as: (a) Stratos integration proves accretive and validates the advisory delivery model, (b) alternative fund administration growth sustains 15%+ revenue growth in Investment Managers, (c) Private Banks margins stabilize at 20%+ levels, (d) aggressive buybacks compound EPS growth to $6.60+ in FY2027. At 18.5x on $6.62 FY2027E EPS = $122.

**Bear Case ($69-75, -20% to -24%)**

Equity markets decline 15-20% reducing AUM-linked fees; Stratos integration proves costlier than expected; LSV suffers accelerated outflows; FCA regulatory issues expand to material fines or growth restrictions. At 12x on reduced FY2026E EPS of ~$5.50 = $66-75.

**Base Case ($100-107, +10% to +18%)**

Revenue grows 8-10% organically plus Stratos contribution; margins stable to slightly expanding; buybacks reduce shares 4%; FY2026 EPS lands at $5.90-6.00. Market applies 17x multiple (still below peers) = $100-102. FY2027 visibility at $6.62 EPS draws the stock toward analyst consensus of $107.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Base case target:** $102 within 12 months. Derivation: 17x FY2026E EPS of $5.88 = $100; or 15.5x FY2027E of $6.62 = $103 (applying current multiple to next-year earnings as we approach year-end). Midpoint $102.

**Bull case target:** $115-122 within 18 months. Requires partial multiple re-rating (17-18.5x) on FY2027E EPS ($6.62).

**Bear case target:** $69-75 (-20% to -24%). Requires both multiple compression and earnings decline.

**Upside/downside from current:** +12% to base, +27-34% to bull, -18-24% to bear. Risk/reward approximately 2:1 (base case upside vs. bear case downside).

**Timeframe:** 12-18 months. Key catalysts: Q2 2026 earnings (July), continued Stratos contribution reporting, FCA resolution timeline, and equity market direction as primary external variable.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Two consecutive quarters of negative net new business flows in Investment Managers segment (would signal alternative administration growth stalling)
2. Stratos contributing less than $15M/quarter in revenue for two consecutive quarters (would indicate integration failure)
3. LSV AUM declining below $85B (would signal accelerated structural deterioration requiring write-down consideration)

## Conclusion

SEI Investments is a high-quality financial services compounder trading at a meaningful discount to peers (15.5x vs. 18.5x) with positive estimate revision momentum, accelerating revenue growth, and a transformational acquisition that expands addressable market. The 5.3% FCF yield, near-zero leverage, and 4-5% annual share count reduction provide downside protection and mechanical EPS accretion. Risks are real (market sensitivity, integration, regulatory) but diversified and quantified.

The stock remains below both the 52-week high ($93.96) and consensus target ($107). At current prices, the risk/reward is favorable: 12% upside to base case, 27-34% to bull case, with 18-24% downside in the bear scenario. BUY with a 12-month target of $102.
