## Executive Summary

argenx has converted a single-molecule franchise into one of the fastest-scaling profitable businesses in biopharma. H1 2026 product net sales reached $2,813M, up 61.8% year over year, while total operating expenses grew only 37.1%, producing operating profit of $887M against $340M, a 160.9% increase, and lifting the operating margin on total operating income from 19.2% to 31.1%. Q2 alone delivered $1,516M of product sales, up 17% sequentially, on the newly launched all-serotype US label for generalized myasthenia gravis. Gross margin on product sales improved to 90.4% from 88.3%. The company carries $5.2B of cash and current financial assets, financial expense of $2M in the half against $92M of financial income, and pays no dividend. The mechanism of margin expansion is specific and identifiable: SG&A fell to 27.5% of product sales from 34.2% as incremental VYVGART revenue flowed through a commercial infrastructure already built, while R&D held roughly flat as a percentage of sales at 32.1% and grew 47.3% in absolute dollars.

Consensus is chasing this. Current-year EPS estimates rose 9.2% in the last 30 days to $28.45 and next-year estimates rose 6.4% to $39.56; the company has beaten in four consecutive quarters (16.5%, 1.9%, 28.4%, 18.6%). At $883.22 the stock trades at 22.3x FY2027 consensus against roughly 39% implied earnings growth, a PEG below 0.6 on reported numbers, and roughly 25.7x if the tax line is normalized to the current-tax rate rather than the 12.4% effective rate. That is not an expensive multiple for a 90%-gross-margin franchise with an unlevered balance sheet, and it sits 12.8% below the low end of the published analyst target range ($770-$1,350, mean $1,086).

What keeps this at ACCUMULATE rather than BUY is the concentration of risk in the next two quarters against a full-ish entry price. Effectively 100% of product revenue is efgartigimod, and the exclusivity horizon for that molecule could not be established from available disclosure. For an asset whose enterprise value of roughly $52B is a long-duration annuity on one biologic plus pipeline options, the loss-of-exclusivity year is the highest-leverage input to fair value and it is unresolved. Two registrational toplines (ALKIVIA in myositis, 3Q26; EMPASSION for empasiprubart in multifocal motor neuropathy, 4Q26) arrive inside six months, and the December 2025 discontinuation of the UplighTED thyroid eye disease studies for futility is direct evidence that FcRn blockade does not generalize across every autoantibody-associated indication. J&J (nipocalimab) and Immunovant (IMVT-1402) are actively generating evidence in gMG, CIDP and Graves' disease, which is argenx's own expansion set. Buy this on weakness and around catalyst resolution, not in size at 7.4% below the all-time high.

## Company Overview

argenx SE is a Netherlands-domiciled immunology company, ADR-listed on NASDAQ, reporting under IFRS as a foreign private issuer (20-F and 6-K filings; no 10-K, no DEF 14A, no Section 16 Form 4s). Its entire commercial business is efgartigimod, an IgG1 antibody fragment that blocks the neonatal Fc receptor and thereby accelerates clearance of circulating pathogenic IgG autoantibodies. It is marketed as VYVGART (intravenous) and VYVGART Hytrulo (subcutaneous, using Halozyme's ENHANZE recombinant hyaluronidase).

Approved footprint as of Q2 2026: generalized myasthenia gravis across all serotypes in the US (anti-AChR, anti-MuSK, anti-LRP4 and triple seronegative — argenx states it is the only approved treatment covering all serotypes); chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy globally; and primary immune thrombocytopenia in Japan only.

The strategy management calls "Vision 2030" targets 50,000 patients treated, 10 labeled indications, and five pipeline candidates in registrational development by 2030. The operating model is serial application of a pipeline-in-a-product approach: efgartigimod is the first instance, empasiprubart (anti-C2 complement) is management's stated second, and FB102 (anti-CD122, acquired via the pending Forte Biosciences tender) is the third. The company expects ten molecules in clinical development by end-2026, including next-generation FcRn assets ARGX-213 (Phase 3 ready, engineered for monthly dosing) and ARGX-124, plus adimanebart (MuSK agonist), ARGX-121 (anti-IgA), ARGX-109 (anti-IL-6), TSP-101, ARGX-118 and ARGX-125.

Three items materially affect how these numbers should be read.

**Operating momentum is understated by the pre-tax line because of an FX reversal.** Exchange results swung from a $76M gain in H1 2025 to a $19M loss in H1 2026, a $95M adverse move. Operating profit grew 160.9% while pre-tax grew 95.9%; most of that gap is non-operating currency translation for a euro-domiciled, USD-reporting, globally-selling issuer. FX is a recurring volatility source in both directions, not an item to exclude, but the underlying operating inflection is stronger than headline pre-tax or net growth implies.

**The 12.4% effective tax rate is not the cash tax rate.** Income tax expense of $119M on $958M pre-tax comprises current tax expense of $230M and a deferred tax benefit of $110M. On current tax alone the rate is 24.0%. Normalizing FY2027 consensus EPS of $39.56 to a 24% tax burden implies roughly $34.3 of EPS, and the forward multiple rises from 22.3x to about 25.7x. The deferred benefit may reflect legitimate recognition of previously unrecognized deferred tax assets as profitability becomes durable, in which case it could persist for several periods; the tax note was not accessible. argenx separately flags dependence on the Belgian innovation income deduction in its own forward-looking-statement risk list, which is a named, company-specific tax dependency.

**Cash conversion in H1 2026 was ~84%, with a prior-year comparison that is unexplained.** Operating cash flow was $0.7B against $838M of net profit — a shortfall consistent with receivable and inventory build against 62% revenue growth plus $230M of current tax. The unresolved item is H1 2025, where $415M of net profit coincided with $0.4B of cash *used* in operations. That gap requires the FY2025 cash flow statement to explain, and it means a single-period conversion ratio should not be extrapolated as a normal run-rate.

**Balance sheet.** Cash, cash equivalents and current financial assets totaled $5.2B at June 30, 2026 versus $4.4B at December 31, 2025. Composition shifted: cash and equivalents rose only ~$0.1B while current financial assets (3-12 month deposits) rose ~$0.7B. Financial expense of $1M in Q2 and $2M in H1 against $92M of H1 financial income supports a strong inference of a substantially unlevered structure; the notes confirming absence of borrowings, lease liabilities or covenants were not accessible, so treat "no debt" as inference rather than verified fact. Implied yield on liquidity is roughly 3.7% annualized, meaning approximately $190M of annualized pre-tax income — about 20% of annualized H1 operating profit — is interest income that compresses if rates fall or if cash is deployed. The pending $2.2B Forte payment consumes roughly 42% of the June 30 liquidity balance, taking pro forma liquidity to about $3.0B before H2 cash generation.

**Share count is working mildly in shareholders' favor.** Weighted basic shares rose from 61.08M to 62.31M while weighted diluted shares *fell* from 65.64M to 64.52M, narrowing the basic-to-diluted gap from 4.56M to 2.21M. Diluted EPS grew 95.7% against 92.7% net profit growth. These are reported diluted counts, not maximum contingent dilution; the outstanding award pool and unvested performance shares are not disclosed in the interim release, so no fully diluted capitalization can be stated. At $883.22, market capitalization is $55.0B on weighted basic shares and $57.0B on weighted diluted shares; enterprise value net of the $5.2B liquidity balance is approximately $51.8B, rising to roughly $54.0B pro forma for Forte.

**A data conflict worth naming:** the supplied profile reports trailing and forward P/E both at 33.0 alongside trailing EPS of $19.57, which at $883.22 implies 45.1x. The 33x figure reconciles instead to H1-annualized diluted EPS of $26.00 ($34.0x). Neither is a usable forward metric. All valuation work below uses consensus forward estimates.

**Financial health score: 8.0.** Strong operating cash generation, 90% gross margins, no meaningful debt, $5.2B liquidity, and improving unit economics. Marked below 9 for the unexplained prior-year cash flow gap, the unverifiable balance-sheet detail, and a tax line whose sustainability is not established.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on evidence rather than projection. Product sales have grown for what management describes as 18 consecutive quarters, and Q2's sequential step-up is directly traceable to the Q2 2026 launch of the expanded all-serotype US gMG label, layered on continued CIDP ramp. That was the second label-driven inflection in twelve months and it was executed cleanly: positive Phase 3 seronegative data reported in December 2025, FDA filing by year-end, approval and launch in Q2.

Forward drivers are dated and specific:
- **ALKIVIA (efgartigimod in myositis)** topline 3Q26 — registrational.
- **EMPASSION (empasiprubart in MMN)** topline 4Q26 — the first registrational readout for the second platform.
- **ADVANCE-NEXT (ITP)** 1H27; **UNITY (Sjögren's)** 2H27; Graves' disease registrational ongoing; oMG expansion following positive ADAPT OCULUS.
- **Subcutaneous autoinjector launch 2027** across approved indications.
- **ARGX-213**, monthly-dosing next-generation FcRn, Phase 3 ready.
- **FB102** Phase 2 celiac data 2H26 (pending tender close).

Consensus reflects this: current-quarter EPS estimates rose 7.7% in 30 days and 12.8% in 90 days; current-year estimates rose 9.2%/9.1%; next-year 6.4%/7.0%. The 30-day revisions exceed the 90-day revisions at the quarter level, meaning revision velocity is accelerating, not decaying. Combined with a four-for-four beat record averaging roughly 16%, confidence in the FY2026 consensus of $28.45 (implying H2 EPS of $15.45 against H1's $13.00) is reasonable. These revisions, while consistently positive, do not reach the +30%/90-day threshold that marks a violent trough-to-recovery inflection; this is a company compounding from an already-profitable base, which argues for treating the revision trend as confirmation rather than as a standalone signal.

The composition of margin expansion matters for durability. Operating leverage came almost entirely from SG&A, not R&D restraint — R&D grew 47.3% in dollars. Sequentially, SG&A grew 17.5% against 17% revenue growth, so within-2026 leverage has been neutral even as year-over-year leverage was large. With ten clinical molecules planned by end-2026, a 2027 autoinjector build, and Forte integration, the 32% operating margin should be treated as a stage rather than a steady state. Management is choosing to spend gross margin on pipeline breadth, which is defensible but caps near-term margin extrapolation.

One presentation change reduces transparency: equity-method joint venture losses are now reported inside the R&D line with comparatives restated. The JV is not identified and its contribution to the reported 47% R&D growth is no longer separable. Restating comparatives is the correct treatment and the amount may be immaterial; the point is that a data series was removed.

**Growth prospects score: 8.0.** Revenue growth above 60% with margin expansion, a dated multi-indication catalyst calendar, and rising estimates. Held below 9 because near-term revenue remains a single-molecule story and pipeline breadth has not yet reached revenue.

## Valuation Assessment

| Metric | Value at $883.22 |
|---|---|
| FY2026 consensus EPS ($28.45) | 31.0x |
| FY2027 consensus EPS ($39.56) | **22.3x** |
| FY2027 tax-normalized (~$34.3) | 25.7x |
| Implied FY2026→FY2027 EPS growth | +39% |
| PEG (FY2027 P/E ÷ growth) | ~0.57 |
| EV / annualized Q2 product sales ($6.06B) | ~8.5x |
| Analyst mean / median target | $1,086 (+23.0%) / $1,067 (+20.8%) |
| Analyst target range | $770 (-12.8%) to $1,350 (+52.9%) |

The central valuation observation is that 22.3x forward earnings on ~39% expected earnings growth, funded by a 90%-gross-margin franchise with $5.2B of liquidity and no meaningful debt, is not a demanding multiple. Large-cap pharma with mid-single-digit growth trades in the mid-teens; high-quality commercial biotech with 15-25% growth trades in the low-to-mid twenties. argenx is inside that band while growing revenue at three times the pace, which is the specific reason to think the market is discounting the durability of the earnings rather than the earnings themselves.

The discount has an identifiable cause: single-molecule concentration and an unverified exclusivity horizon. A 22x multiple on a business growing 39% is consistent with the market applying a materially shorter effective duration to the cash flow stream than a diversified pharma would receive. That is a rational discount, and it means the multiple should not be expected to expand toward diversified-pharma levels until revenue itself diversifies. As of Q2 2026, product concentration in efgartigimod is 100% and has not improved. A broadening pipeline is not a broadening revenue base.

Two adjustments cut against the headline cheapness. First, the tax normalization: at a 24% cash tax rate, FY2027 EPS is nearer $34.3 and the multiple 25.7x. Second, consensus estimates presumably reflect the reported (low-ETR) basis; if that reverses, reported EPS declines roughly 13% relative to consensus without any operational change.

Cross-checking with sales multiples: EV of roughly $51.8B against annualized Q2 product sales of $6.06B is 8.5x. Annualizing Q2 is a sensitivity, not a forecast — it overstates the base if any portion of the sequential step reflected launch-period channel stocking or one-time gross-to-net favorability, and understates it if sequential growth persists. No disaggregation by indication or geography was disclosed, so the mix cannot be decomposed.

On analyst targets: with the stock at 22.3x forward and a PEG under 1, the published mean of $1,086 is more plausibly a lagging indicator than a ceiling — estimates have moved up in each of the last two 30-day windows and targets typically follow. But there is a split in sell-side positioning (UBS Hold reiterated; J.P. Morgan Buy reiterated; Guggenheim resumed at Buy after a research restriction tied to its role as Forte's advisor), and the low target of $770 sits only 12.8% below spot. Sell-side conviction is not uniform.

**Valuation score: 6.5.** Genuinely attractive on growth-adjusted forward earnings, with a specific reason the multiple is compressed (concentration duration risk). Not scored higher because the compression is at least partly justified, the tax normalization removes a chunk of the apparent discount, and the entry price sits 7.4% below the all-time high with two binary readouts pending.

## Competitive Landscape

argenx created the FcRn category and holds the broadest label — the only all-serotype gMG approval plus global CIDP — with a subcutaneous formulation already commercial. That incumbency is real: 18 consecutive quarters of growth and 60%+ year-over-year sales expansion indicate share gain, not defense.

The mechanism is no longer proprietary in practice. Johnson & Johnson presented 12 abstracts on IMAAVY (nipocalimab) at the European Academy of Neurology congress in June 2026, positioned around sustained disease control in gMG — a direct label-overlapping competitor with commercial and payer infrastructure argenx cannot match. Immunovant confirmed on August 6, 2026 that IMVT-1402 timelines remain on track in Graves' disease, myasthenia gravis and CIDP, which overlays argenx's entire near-term expansion map; a third-party initiation put roughly $4B of peak sales on that asset, an unconfirmed estimate but indicative of how challengers are being underwritten. UCB markets agents in gMG; its current position was not verified here and should be treated as a known unknown rather than dismissed.

argenx's counter-strategy is disclosed and coherent along four axes. Convenience: the 2027 autoinjector and ARGX-213's monthly dosing directly address the differentiation vector later entrants would otherwise own. Breadth: myositis, ITP, Sjögren's, Graves', ocular MG. Mechanism diversification: empasiprubart (C2 complement), adimanebart (MuSK agonist), anti-IgA, anti-IL-6, Fn14, Galectin-10, a bispecific, and now anti-CD122. Combination: ADAPT-Forward tests empasiprubart on top of efgartigimod in gMG, which if successful converts a single-mechanism competitor entry into an incomplete offering.

The Forte acquisition changes the commercial axis. FB102 targets pathogenic T-cell and NK-cell activity via CD122 — a different immune axis from autoantibody clearance or complement — with lead indications in vitiligo, celiac disease and alopecia areata, all far more prevalent than gMG, CIDP or MMN. That implies a lower-price, higher-volume, more payer-contested commercial model than the rare-disease model argenx has mastered. The alternative reading is that argenx intends to run FB102 through a specialist playbook targeting severe refractory populations, in which case no model shift is implied. The company has not said which.

**Competitive position score: 6.8.** Trajectory is improving — share gain, expanding label, an offensive/defensive lifecycle plan already in the clinic. Constrained by direct entry from a better-capitalized incumbent (J&J) and a well-funded challenger targeting the identical expansion set, and by no evidence of improvement in product concentration.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Single-product concentration (dominant).** Effectively 100% of product net sales are efgartigimod. Every element of the roughly $52B enterprise value not attributable to pipeline options depends on one mechanism in a small number of indications. A safety signal, manufacturing interruption or reimbursement reversal in gMG would be uncushioned.

**2. Exclusivity horizon unverified.** argenx's own risk taxonomy names exclusivity duration and patent term extensions. No efgartigimod expiry date, patent-term-extension status, or BPCIA/IPR status could be verified. For a long-duration annuity on one biologic, this is the highest-leverage single input to fair value and it is unresolved. Mitigant: efgartigimod is a biologic, where biosimilar entry is slower, costlier and less erosive than small-molecule generic entry, and the 2027 autoinjector plus ARGX-213 could migrate the franchise onto later-expiring, differentiated presentations before any LOE.

**3. Binary event density in the next two quarters.** ALKIVIA (myositis, 3Q26) and EMPASSION (empasiprubart in MMN, 4Q26). EMPASSION is the first registrational test of the second platform; a miss would not touch current cash flows but would remove a meaningful share of the non-efgartigimod option value in the multiple.

**4. Demonstrated limits to mechanistic generalization.** argenx permanently discontinued the UplighTED Phase 3 studies of subcutaneous efgartigimod in moderate-to-severe thyroid eye disease in December 2025 after an independent data monitoring committee recommended stopping for futility at a pre-specified 24-week interim; safety was unremarkable and the shares fell more than 5%. Those trials enrolled TED patients with Graves' or Hashimoto's disease, and argenx now runs a separate registrational Graves' study. Counterargument: TED pathophysiology is substantially IGF-1R- and fibroblast-driven and the endpoint was proptosis response, a structural rather than serological readout, so failure there does not straightforwardly predict failure in Graves' hyperthyroidism where TSH-receptor autoantibodies are more directly causal. The two should not be conflated, but the episode does argue for discounting probability weights on the more speculative label expansions.

**5. Third-party platform dependency.** VYVGART Hytrulo depends on Halozyme's ENHANZE technology under license. Royalty rate, term, and any change-of-control or patent-challenge provisions are undisclosed and presumably embedded in cost of sales. Since the subcutaneous presentation is the growth engine and the base for the 2027 autoinjector, this is a non-trivial economic dependency.

**6. Governance visibility is structurally limited.** As a foreign private issuer, argenx files no proxy and its insiders are exempt from Section 16. There are no Form 4s. Any screen reading "no insider selling" here is reading a reporting-regime artifact, not information about insider behavior. An Extraordinary General Meeting is called for September 17, 2026 with an agenda not retrieved; an EGM arriving four months after a CEO transition and seven weeks after a $2.2B acquisition most commonly concerns board appointments, remuneration policy, or renewal of share issuance/repurchase authorizations. A renewed issuance authorization would be dilution-relevant. This is a hypothesis, and the agenda may be routine.

**7. Tax, FX and policy.** The 12.4% effective rate versus 24.0% current-tax rate; a named dependency on the Belgian innovation income deduction; a $95M adverse FX swing in a single half; and argenx's own enumeration of tariffs, export controls and sanctions among its risks. A Fujifilm partnership expansion to bring VYVGART production to the US was reported at headline level only and is consistent with tariff mitigation, but the rationale, scope and cost are unconfirmed.

**8. Reinvestment intensity.** R&D grew 47.3% year over year in Q2 with ten clinical molecules planned by end-2026 plus FB102. Current margins reflect a spending stage, not a steady state.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility of 41.1% sits 10.5 points above one-year realized volatility of 30.6% — the market is paying up for near-dated protection or leverage. But that figure sits in only the 12th percentile within the current chain, and the term structure is flat (41.1% near versus 40.1% at six months), so there is no single-date event dominating pricing. The three-month tenor at 44.3% with a ±12.0% implied move is the most informative point: that window (expiry September 18, 2026) contains the September 17 EGM and, on management's guidance, the ALKIVIA myositis topline (3Q26), and the market is pricing a genuine binary. Put/call volume of 1.25 against put/call open interest of 0.66 indicates recent hedging flow layered onto a call-tilted standing base, consistent with holders protecting gains into a catalyst rather than a directional bearish position building. The nearest expiry skew is described as balanced at 2.7%. Short interest is 2.5% of float with 4.1 days to cover — uncrowded, no squeeze dynamic either way.

Net read: options are consistent with the fundamental view. Elevated near-dated vol into a dated registrational readout argues for staged entry, and the rich IV makes selling near-dated puts a defensible way to leg in for holders who use options.

**Risk factors score: 4.8.** The concentration is extreme, the exclusivity horizon is not verifiable, two binary readouts land within six months against a documented precedent of mechanistic failure, direct competitive entry targets the exact expansion map, and the governance disclosure regime removes the most useful real-time signal. Much of this is known, but the combination is not fully priced at 22x forward earnings.

## Management Assessment

The CEO succession, announced January 5, 2026 and effective May 2026, moved COO Karen Massey to CEO with co-founder Tim Van Hauwermeiren to chair. That is close to a best-case template: internal candidate, four-month pre-announcement, founder retained on the board, and the incoming CEO is the operator who built the commercial organization. Massey signed both the July 23 results release and the July 27 Forte release.

The first observable capital allocation decision under the new CEO is the Forte Biosciences tender: $77.00 per share in cash, roughly $2.2B equity value, structured through Avena Merger Sub with a majority-tender and HSR condition, no financing condition, funded from cash on hand, expected to close Q3 2026. The asset is FB102, a first-in-class anti-CD122 antibody with positive Phase 1b results in vitiligo (July 9, 2026) and celiac disease (2025), and Phase 2 celiac data expected 2H26. argenx framed the price as an approximately 86% premium to Forte's VWAP since the vitiligo data; Forte shares moved up 39.2% pre-market on announcement, implying a last unaffected close near $55 and a post-data VWAP near $41 — both premium figures are defensible but describe different things and neither should be quoted as "the" premium. ARGX traded modestly lower on the announcement, consistent with investors viewing $2.2B for a Phase 1b/2 asset as a full price, though same-day sector drift is an equally available explanation.

argenx held a pre-existing strategic investment in Forte whose size is not disclosed, so the net incremental cash outlay is less than $2.2B by an amount no outside party can compute. Pro forma liquidity of roughly $3.0B against $0.7B of half-yearly operating cash generation leaves the balance sheet comfortable and unlevered either way.

Supporting positives: the diluted share count declined year over year, an uncommon outcome for a high-growth biotech, and gross margin improved 210 basis points. Against that: no insider transaction visibility exists, no proxy compensation or pay-versus-performance disclosure is available, the new CEO's capital allocation record is one deal long and that deal was received coolly, and an R&D line item was consolidated in a way that removes a data series.

**Management quality score: 6.0.** Clean succession and disciplined balance sheet management, offset by an unproven allocation record, a fully-priced first acquisition, and structurally unavailable alignment signals.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $1,300 (+47.2%)

Revenue compounds through 2027 on the all-serotype gMG label, CIDP ramp, and the 2027 autoinjector, with SG&A intensity continuing to decline as the commercial base scales. ALKIVIA reads out positive in 3Q26, adding myositis, and EMPASSION reads out positive in 4Q26, validating empasiprubart as a genuine second platform and converting a meaningful share of pipeline option value into probability-weighted revenue. FY2027 consensus moves above $42 as estimates continue their current trajectory, and the multiple expands toward 30x as the market extends the effective duration of the cash flow stream on evidence of mechanism diversification. FB102's Phase 2 celiac data in 2H26 validates the Forte price. ARGX-213's monthly dosing and the autoinjector blunt convenience-based competition before J&J and Immunovant can scale. $1,300 sits near the high published target of $1,350.

### Bear Case — $650 (-26.4%)

ALKIVIA misses, or EMPASSION misses, removing pipeline option value and forcing the market to value argenx primarily on the efgartigimod annuity. Nipocalimab and IMVT-1402 begin taking share in gMG and pre-empt the Graves' and CIDP expansions, decelerating sales growth from 60% toward 25-30%. The deferred tax benefit reverses, cutting reported EPS roughly 13% relative to consensus. The multiple compresses to 16-18x on tax-normalized earnings as duration concerns dominate. $650 is roughly the 52-week low of $631.47 and 16.4x reported FY2027 consensus — the level at which the market would be pricing efgartigimod as a maturing single-product franchise with limited pipeline credit.

### Base Case — $1,040 (+17.7%)

FY2026 lands at or above consensus $28.45 given the four-beat record and rising revisions; FY2027 lands near $39.56. One of the two near-term registrational readouts succeeds and one disappoints or delivers an ambiguous result, which is the modal outcome for a two-shot binary set. The multiple holds in the 26x range on reported FY2027 earnings (roughly 30x tax-normalized), reflecting continued high growth offset by unresolved concentration duration risk. $1,040 = 26.3x FY2027 consensus $39.56, and sits between the published median target of $1,067 and the mean-minus-a-margin. The mechanism supporting it is arithmetic rather than multiple expansion: earnings growing 39% at a static multiple produces most of the return.
