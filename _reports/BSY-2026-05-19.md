## Executive Summary

Bentley Systems is the dominant provider of infrastructure engineering software, serving customers in civil infrastructure, utilities, mining, and industrial sectors. The company generates $1.5B in annual revenue with 92% subscription mix, $520M in annual free cash flow, and 99% account retention. Q1 2026 revenue accelerated to 14.5% year-over-year growth, the fastest rate in recent history, driven by mining/resources demand and AI product traction.

At $33.20, BSY trades at approximately 24x forward FY2026 earnings ($1.38 consensus) and 38x trailing — a substantial discount to application software peer medians of 47.6x. The stock has declined 44% from its July 2025 high of $59.25, driven by a broader de-rating of mid-growth software and the overhang of a $575M convertible note maturing in July 2027 that will require cash settlement (conversion price of $83.23 is far above the current stock price). The business fundamentals are stable-to-improving, making this a growth hold with clear conditions under which an upgrade or downgrade becomes appropriate.

## Company Overview

Bentley Systems develops software for designing, building, and operating infrastructure — roads, bridges, railways, airports, power grids, water systems, mines, and industrial facilities. Founded in 1984 by the Bentley brothers, the company went public in September 2020 and is organized around four product pillars: Bentley Open Applications (desktop modeling), Seequent (subsurface/geoscience), Bentley Infrastructure Cloud (enterprise collaboration), and the Cesium/iTwin platform (digital twins).

The business model centers on the E365 enterprise subscription program (now 60% of ARR), which provides consumption-based access to the full portfolio. This drives net revenue retention of 109% and creates a predictable expansion dynamic. No single customer represents more than 2% of revenue, and 70%+ of revenue comes from accounts retained for over 10 years.

The Bentley family controls approximately 53.5% of voting power through a dual-class structure. Gregory Bentley transitioned from CEO to Executive Chair in July 2024, with Nicholas Cumins taking the CEO role.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Growth:** FY2025 revenue was $1.502B (+11.0% reported, +10.1% constant currency). Q1 2026 revenue of $424.2M (+14.5% YoY) represents a clear acceleration. ARR stood at $1.462B at year-end 2025, growing 11.5% on a constant-currency basis.

**Profitability:** Gross margins expanded to 81.5% (FY2025 from 78.4% in FY2022). Operating margins reached 24.1%. Adjusted operating income less stock-based compensation hit 28.6% margin. This margin trajectory demonstrates operating leverage in the model.

**Free Cash Flow:** FY2025 FCF was $520M, representing a 34.6% margin. Capital expenditure is negligible ($18M annually). Q1 2026 FCF of $188M tracks toward the $500-570M full-year guidance. FCF conversion relative to net income is well above 100%, indicating high earnings quality.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of $1.249B produces a debt/equity ratio of 1.99x. The Altman Z-Score of 1.39 reflects the SaaS balance sheet structure (high deferred revenue, goodwill from acquisitions, convertible notes) rather than actual financial distress — annual FCF covers the entire debt load in under 2.5 years. The $575M 2027 convertible note is the primary balance sheet concern: at $33.20/share versus a conversion price of $83.23, this will require cash repayment. The company has $1.3B in credit facility capacity to absorb this, but it will convert fixed/low-coupon obligations into floating-rate debt in an uncertain rate environment.

**Capital Return:** $500M share repurchase authorization through December 2028. $125M repurchased in FY2025. Quarterly dividend of $0.07/share (~0.8% yield).

## Growth Analysis

BSY's growth profile is moderate but accelerating. The 10-11% constant-currency growth rate over FY2023-2025 stepped up to 14.5% in Q1 2026. Key growth vectors:

1. **E365 Conversion:** At 60% of ARR, significant runway remains to migrate legacy perpetual/maintenance customers. Each conversion typically increases account spending through broader portfolio access.

2. **Seequent:** The geoscience/mining platform acquired in 2021 remains a growth contributor. Mining sector demand was specifically cited as strong in Q1 2026.

3. **AI/Digital Twins:** Asset Analytics exceeded $50M ARR run rate. The iTwin platform + Cesium positions Bentley as the data/context layer for AI agents operating on physical infrastructure. MCP server launch for structural engineering represents early AI monetization.

4. **SMB Penetration:** Management highlighted strength in the SMB segment, suggesting market expansion beyond traditional enterprise accounts.

Estimate revisions are modestly positive: FY2026 EPS consensus at $1.38 (up 2.4% over 90 days), FY2027 at $1.58 (up 3.3%). These are not the >30% revision velocity that constitutes a strong BUY signal, but they indicate stability with positive direction.

**Headwinds:** China revenue was essentially flat in FY2025 with subscription revenue declining. Currency exposure (59% international revenue) creates translation headwinds when the dollar strengthens. Infrastructure spending could face headwinds from trade war impacts on project economics.

## Valuation Assessment

At $33.20, BSY trades at:
- 24.1x FY2026E EPS ($1.38)
- 21.0x FY2027E EPS ($1.58)
- ~18.7x FY2025 FCF ($520M ÷ $9.7B market cap)
- EV/Revenue: approximately 7.3x FY2025 revenue

Relative to application software peers (median P/E of 47.6x), BSY's 24x forward multiple represents a ~49% discount. Peers like Manhattan Associates (38.3x) and Dynatrace (73.8x) trade at substantially higher multiples with similar or slower growth profiles. Docusign at 33.1x is the closest comparable, but BSY's 14.5% revenue growth and 99% retention likely warrant a premium to DOCU.

The discount appears driven by: (1) the 2027 note overhang creating uncertainty, (2) the dual-class/controlled company structure limiting governance appeal, (3) significant stock price decline eroding momentum-investor interest, and (4) historical underperformance vs. peer TSR (96 vs. 183 cumulative since IPO).

However, I cannot identify a specific non-consensus catalyst that makes this a clear mispricing today. The growth is steady at 11-14% — good, not exceptional. The valuation is reasonable, but "cheap relative to peers" is not in itself a mispricing when the discount has structural explanations (governance, leverage, note overhang). At 24x forward earnings for an 11-14% grower, the stock needs sustained acceleration above 14% or visible AI monetization to justify re-rating toward peer multiples.

## Competitive Landscape

Bentley occupies a defensible position as the infrastructure-specific engineering platform. Unlike Autodesk (buildings/architecture focus), Hexagon (hardware-centric), or Trimble (construction/agriculture), Bentley's specialization in civil infrastructure — roads, rail, utilities, mining — creates deep domain expertise that takes decades to replicate.

**Improving trajectory signals:**
- Net retention steady at 109% (no competitive erosion)
- 99% annual account retention (highest in sector)
- Revenue acceleration in Q1 2026 (gaining share or expanding market)
- AI/digital twin platform positioning (first-mover in infrastructure context layer)

**Structural advantages:**
- Multi-disciplinary portfolio covering the full asset lifecycle
- Deep regulatory/compliance domain knowledge (especially in utilities, water)
- Long-term data relationships (customers' project histories locked into Bentley formats)

The primary competitive risk is from AI-native entrants that could bypass traditional engineering workflows. However, infrastructure engineering requires domain-specific physics engines, regulatory compliance, and decades of accumulated design standards — making greenfield displacement unlikely in the medium term.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **2027 Convertible Note Maturity ($575M, July 2027):** At current stock prices, cash settlement is near-certain. This consumes credit facility capacity and shifts debt from fixed/low-rate to floating at a time when rates are elevated. Not existential (FCF covers it within ~1.1 years), but limits capital allocation flexibility for 12-18 months around the event.

2. **Dual-Class Governance:** The credit facility includes a covenant requiring the Bentley family to maintain at least 20% voting control — meaning any ownership reduction below that threshold triggers a default. This unusual structure limits corporate action (no takeout premium likely) and reduces institutional appeal.

3. **China Exposure:** Revenue flat, subscription declining. Geopolitical tensions and preference for domestic software create a multi-year headwind to an addressable market that should otherwise be growing.

4. **Macro Sensitivity:** Rising interest rates increase the cost of the eventual note refinancing. Tariff impacts on construction materials could delay customer projects. Oil price volatility affects resources sector spending in both directions.

5. **Short Interest:** At 10% of float with 10.2 days to cover, short interest is elevated but not at squeeze levels. It indicates meaningful institutional skepticism about the path forward.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($45-50, +35-50%):** Q1 2026 acceleration proves durable. Infrastructure spending remains resilient despite tariff noise. AI monetization (Asset Analytics, MCP) drives growth above 15%. 2027 note refinances smoothly at acceptable rates. Market re-rates toward 30-35x forward earnings, in line with the lower quartile of application software peers. Price target: 32x × $1.58 FY2027E = ~$50.

**Bear Case ($25-28, -15-25%):** Growth decelerates back to 8-9% as infrastructure spending weakens under trade war headwinds. China deteriorates further. Note refinancing occurs at unfavorable terms (higher rates + limited alternatives). Market maintains or compresses multiple. Price target: 18x × $1.38 = ~$25.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of revenue growth above 14%, confirming durable acceleration that would justify re-rating
2. Stock declines to $28-29 (20x FY2026E EPS) while fundamentals hold steady, creating more favorable risk/reward asymmetry
3. Management provides clarity on 2027 note resolution at favorable terms (fixed-rate refinancing at <5%), removing the primary balance sheet overhang

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Revenue growth decelerates below 9% for two consecutive quarters, suggesting competitive or macro impairment
2. Net retention drops below 105%, signaling customer attrition
3. Credit facility amendment containing materially restrictive covenants, or 2027 note refinancing at >7% cost

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (Q2 2026, likely August 2026). The note maturity becomes an increasingly important catalyst as July 2027 approaches; refinancing terms should become visible by early 2027.

## Conclusion

Bentley Systems is a high-quality, asset-light infrastructure software franchise with improving growth momentum, best-in-class retention, and a 5.4% FCF yield on a business that requires minimal capital reinvestment. At 24x forward earnings, the stock is meaningfully cheaper than application software peers.

The specific factor preventing a BUY is the absence of a clear re-rating catalyst. Growth is good but not exceptional enough (sub-15%) to drive multiple expansion on its own. The 2027 note overhang creates tangible near-term uncertainty that institutional investors will price. The dual-class structure eliminates any M&A premium. And estimate revisions, while positive, are running at +2-3% over 90 days — well below the velocity that historically signals continued outperformance.

This is a business worth owning at a lower price or with growth confirmation. The current configuration — decent value, decent growth, real structural overhang — warrants holding with active monitoring of the acceleration thesis and the refinancing timeline.
