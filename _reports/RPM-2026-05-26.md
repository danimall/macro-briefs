## Executive Summary

RPM International is a $12.9B specialty chemicals and coatings company trading at $100.90, approximately 22% below its 52-week high of $129.12. The stock has declined meaningfully since mid-2025, reflecting slowing organic growth in the Consumer segment, tariff-related margin headwinds, and broader materials sector weakness. The company's operational execution remains strong (MAP 2025 delivered 500+ basis points of gross margin improvement over three years), but the key question is whether a successor initiative can extract further gains against a backdrop of trade policy uncertainty and weak DIY spending.

At 18.4x forward FY2026 EPS of ~$5.47, the valuation is neither cheap nor expensive for a specialty chemicals business with mid-single-digit growth. The stock sits well below the analyst consensus target of $128, but estimates are drifting lower (next quarter cut 6.7% over 30 days, next year down 2.2% over 90 days), and the $110.8M litigation verdict creates an asymmetric risk that the market may not fully appreciate. The bull case requires renewed organic growth in CPG/PCG from infrastructure spending and successful execution of the next MAP initiative; the bear case centers on sustained Consumer weakness, tariff cost absorption, and an adverse litigation outcome.

## Company Overview

RPM International operates through three segments following a June 2025 reorganization: Construction Products Group (CPG, 38.4% of revenue), Consumer (33.7%), and Performance Coatings Group (PCG, 27.9%). The company generates approximately $7.4B in annual revenue, with 70% from the U.S. and 30% internationally. Key brands include Rust-Oleum, DAP, Zinsser, Tremco, Carboline, and Stonhard. RPM is the fifth-largest global coatings company, differentiated by its niche specialty focus rather than commodity architectural paints.

Fiscal year ends May 31. The company is incorporated in Delaware and led by CEO Frank C. Sullivan, whose family has managed RPM for multiple generations.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:** FY2025 revenue was $7.37B (+0.5% YoY), a significant deceleration from prior years. The first nine months of FY2026 show re-acceleration to $5.63B (+6.4%), decomposing into 1.7% organic, 3.6% acquisition, and 1.1% FX. The organic growth is modest and entirely driven by CPG and PCG; the Consumer segment declined -3.4% organically.

**Profitability:** Gross margin reached 41.4% in FY2025, up from 36.3% in FY2022—the direct result of MAP 2025. Nine-month FY2026 gross margin held at 41.0%, suggesting the gains are sticky but not expanding further. Net margin was 9.3% in FY2025 (boosted by a 12.9% effective tax rate versus the normalized 23-24%). On a normalized basis, sustainable net margins are closer to 7.5-8.0%.

**Balance Sheet:** Net leverage of 1.77x (well below the 3.75x covenant), $1.02B available liquidity, current ratio of 2.28x. Total long-term debt of $2.55B against equity of $3.15B (D/E 0.81x). The credit facility extends to 2031. This is a conservatively capitalized company with significant financial flexibility.

**Cash Flow:** Nine-month operating cash flow of $656.7M (+6.1%). Estimated full-year FCF of $630-660M implies a ~5% FCF yield at current market cap. Capital allocation is well-balanced: dividends ($203M), CapEx ($160M), acquisitions ($162M), and buybacks ($53M) are funded entirely from operations.

**Interest Expense:** Rose 19.4% to $84.3M over nine months due to acquisition-related borrowings—a modest headwind.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects for RPM are mixed:

**Positives:**
- CPG and PCG benefit from U.S. infrastructure spending (IIJA), re-shoring trends, and building envelope sustainability mandates
- European revenue growing strongly (+20% in Q3, partly acquisition-driven)
- Kalzip acquisition (closed March 2026, ~€75M revenue) adds to building envelope strategy
- New restructuring action targeting SG&A optimization signals further margin opportunity
- The "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" enables immediate asset expensing, favorable for RPM's capital deployment

**Negatives:**
- Consumer segment organic decline of -3.4% with no clear catalyst for reversal given elevated mortgage rates suppressing home improvement activity
- Tariff headwinds on raw materials (pigments, resins, solvents, containers) expected to persist through FY2027
- Star Brands earn-out reduction ($12.7M) signals acquisition underperformance in Consumer
- Total organic growth of just 1.7% is barely above inflation

Consensus estimates project $5.47 EPS in FY2026 (+2.2% vs. FY2025) and $5.86 in FY2027 (+7.1%). Revisions are trending negatively: next quarter estimates cut 6.7%, next year down 2.2% over 90 days. No upward revision signal is present.

## Valuation Assessment

RPM trades at 18.4x forward FY2026 EPS of $5.47. Relative to peers:
- NEU (Newmarket): 16.5x P/E, 1.7% yield
- EMN (Eastman): 21.2x P/E, 4.8% yield
- Broader specialty chemicals peer median: elevated due to cyclical trough earnings at some peers

RPM's valuation sits reasonably within the specialty chemicals peer range for a company with its margin profile and growth rate. The 2.1% dividend yield is modest but well-covered (payout ratio ~39% of normalized earnings). The ~5% FCF yield provides a valuation floor.

The analyst consensus target is $128 (median $125), representing 24-27% upside. The lowest target of $115 implies 14% upside. Every analyst has a target above current price, which reflects the post-decline positioning. However, with estimates drifting lower, these targets may compress.

The valuation is not egregiously cheap—18.4x on flat-to-low-single-digit growth is fair for a quality specialty chemicals business, but does not offer asymmetric upside unless growth re-accelerates or the next MAP initiative delivers material margin expansion.

## Competitive Landscape

RPM maintains a defensible competitive position as a niche specialty player:

**Strengths:**
- Portfolio of #1-#2 branded positions in narrow categories (Rust-Oleum in aerosol paints, Tremco in roofing/waterproofing, Stonhard in flooring)
- 500bp margin improvement demonstrates operational capability that few peers matched
- Serial acquisition model provides consistent bolt-on growth and category expansion
- Building envelope and infrastructure repair are structural growth markets

**Weaknesses:**
- Consumer segment faces intense competition from Sherwin-Williams, PPG, and private label
- No pricing power evident in Consumer (organic declines suggest volume and/or price concessions)
- International scale still limited relative to Sherwin-Williams or PPG

**Trajectory:** Competitive position is stable in CPG/PCG (gaining from infrastructure spending) but deteriorating in Consumer. The segment reorganization (eliminating SPG) may improve operational focus. Net competitive position is holding but not clearly improving.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Litigation ($110.8M verdict):** A former supplier won a jury verdict that RPM has accrued only $0.5M against, with a range extending to $152.5M (including ~$70M+ in prejudgment interest at 9% since 2018). An adverse outcome on appeal would represent $1.00-1.20/share after-tax—not existential (~1.2% of market cap) but material to a single year's earnings.

**2. Tariff/Input Cost Inflation:** RPM sources raw materials globally. Management has explicitly warned that tariff-driven cost inflation will persist through FY2027. The strategic inventory build ($1.12B vs. $1.04B) provides near-term buffer but carries working capital risk.

**3. Consumer Segment Secular Pressure:** Elevated mortgage rates suppress existing home sales and renovation activity. With the Consumer segment at -3.4% organic, this represents a meaningful drag on consolidated growth with uncertain duration.

**4. Estimate Revision Momentum:** Negative revision momentum typically precedes further estimate cuts unless a catalyst emerges.

**5. Integration Risk:** Kalzip and Star Brands integrations are concurrent with organizational restructuring. Star Brands already showing below-expectation performance (earn-out reduction).

**Options Market Signal:** Options activity is extremely thin (89 contracts across 4 expirations, zero put/call volume), reflecting RPM's status as an institutional-held specialty chemicals name with low retail participation. The 6-month implied move of 17.1% versus 28.1% historical volatility suggests the market is not pricing elevated uncertainty—potentially underpricing the litigation tail risk. Short interest at 2.7% with 2.4 days to cover is unremarkable.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$125-130, +24-29%):**
- Infrastructure spending accelerates CPG/PCG organic growth to 4-6%
- Next MAP initiative delivers additional 100-200bp of margin expansion
- Consumer segment stabilizes as housing market normalizes
- Litigation resolves favorably on appeal (RPM's confident $0.5M accrual proves correct)
- Valuation re-rates to 22-24x on improved growth trajectory
- Math: 22x × $5.86 FY2027 EPS = $129

**Bear Case (~$85-90, -11-16%):**
- Consumer segment organic decline deepens to -5-7% on further DIY weakness
- Tariff costs cannot be fully passed through, compressing margins 100-200bp
- Litigation affirmed at $150M+ including interest, creating one-time charge
- Estimates cut further, forward P/E compresses to 15-16x on materials sector de-rating
- Math: 16x × $5.30 revised FY2026 EPS = $85

**Base Case (~$112-118, +11-17%):**
- Modest organic growth (2-3%) supplemented by acquisitions
- Margins flat to slightly expanding on efficiency actions
- Litigation resolved through settlement at $40-60M
- Multiple holds at 19-20x forward earnings
- Math: 20x × $5.50 = $110

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Consumer segment organic growth turns positive for 2 consecutive quarters
- Estimate revisions turn positive (30-day revisions >+2% for current and next year)
- Litigation settled for ≤$50M or dismissed on appeal
- Stock price declines to $88-92 (providing 15x forward P/E and >6% FCF yield)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Consumer segment organic decline accelerates to -6%+ for 2 consecutive quarters
- Gross margins compress below 39% (indicating MAP 2025 gains eroding)
- Litigation affirmed at >$120M
- Net leverage exceeds 2.5x from aggressive M&A funded by debt
- Full-year FY2026 EPS comes in below $5.20

**Reassessment timeline:** Next natural checkpoint is Q4 FY2026 earnings (expected July 2026), which will provide full-year results and likely commentary on FY2027 outlook, tariff impacts, and the next MAP initiative.

## Conclusion

RPM International is a quality specialty chemicals business with demonstrated operational execution, conservative capitalization, and a ~5% FCF yield. However, it faces a challenging near-term environment: organic growth is anemic in its largest segment, estimates are being revised downward, tariff headwinds persist through FY2027, and a $110.8M litigation risk remains unresolved. At 18.4x forward earnings, the valuation is fair but offers limited margin of safety. The analyst consensus target of $125-128 provides upside but requires improvement in organic growth and/or successful execution of the next MAP initiative—neither of which is confirmed. HOLD with reassessment at Q4 FY2026 earnings.
