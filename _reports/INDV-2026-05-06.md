## Executive Summary

Indivior is executing a financial transformation from a litigation-burdened, multi-product company into a focused, high-margin single-franchise business centered on SUBLOCADE, its long-acting injectable buprenorphine treatment for opioid use disorder. Q1 2026 results confirmed the trajectory: revenue grew 19% YoY, Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled, and management raised full-year guidance by $85-90M at both revenue and EBITDA levels. The stock has appreciated ~233% from its 52-week low of $10.63, driven entirely by fundamental improvement and a 50% YoY EBITDA growth trajectory.

At approximately 10x forward P/E on FY2026 consensus ($3.93 EPS) and ~8.1x EV/EBITDA, valuation remains compelling relative to the growth rate and competitive position. The discount reflects legitimate concerns: SUBLOCADE will represent ~77% of FY2026 revenue (single-product concentration), both pipeline candidates failed in Q1 2026 (no disclosed internally advancing clinical-stage pipeline beyond lifecycle work and external BD optionality), and core patents expire in 2031. These risks are real but quantifiable, and current pricing already embeds significant pessimism on the sustainability of the franchise. With 83% of OUD patients still untreated, SUBLOCADE's addressable market expansion is far from complete.

## Company Overview

Indivior Pharmaceuticals is the global leader in long-acting injectable treatments for opioid use disorder. The company's core product, SUBLOCADE (buprenorphine extended-release monthly injection), holds approximately 75% share of the U.S. LAI OUD segment. The company demerged from Reckitt Benckiser in 2014 and completed its domestication as a U.S. Delaware corporation in January 2026, with shares now trading exclusively on Nasdaq.

The business is undergoing a strategic transformation under CEO Joseph Ciaffoni's "Indivior Action Agenda," which involves: (1) rationalizing the product portfolio (discontinuing OPVEE, PERSERIS, and non-core geographies), (2) driving SUBLOCADE penetration through label expansion and new access channels, and (3) returning capital aggressively to shareholders while seeking external business development opportunities.

**Revenue composition (FY2025):** SUBLOCADE 69% ($856M), Sublingual & other 28% ($346M), Other products 3%. By FY2026, SUBLOCADE should reach ~77% of total revenue.

## Growth Analysis

SUBLOCADE's growth is being driven by three vectors:

1. **Market penetration:** Only 17% of the 4.8M Americans with OUD receive medication. SUBLOCADE's record ~31,800 new patient starts in Q1 2026 demonstrate sustained adoption acceleration. Over 500,000 patients in the U.S. have been prescribed SUBLOCADE since launch.

2. **Label expansion (Feb 2025):** FDA approved rapid initiation (day-1 start) and alternative injection sites. The rapid initiation protocol removes the "lost patient" problem during induction — previously requiring stabilization on oral buprenorphine before transitioning. This is a material commercial unlock.

3. **Criminal justice & institutional channels:** The INSUPPORT reentry program and healthcare system partnerships expand SUBLOCADE into settings where adherence is critical and alternatives are limited.

SUBLOCADE grew 32% YoY in Q1 2026 and is guided to $950-$990M for FY2026 (+11-16% YoY). The LAI OUD category is growing high-teens, and SUBLOCADE maintains ~75% share.

**The pipeline gap is the primary growth constraint.** Both INDV-6001 (3-month LAI) and INDV-2000 (orexin-1 antagonist) failed/were discontinued in Q1 2026. The company has no disclosed internally advancing clinical-stage pipeline beyond lifecycle work and external BD optionality. Indivior retains potential U.S. commercial rights to INDV-6001 if Alar (which regained development rights) eventually obtains FDA approval. Future growth beyond SUBLOCADE depends on external acquisition or licensing. Management has signaled intent to pursue "external business development opportunities" with the cash flow and balance sheet flexibility now available.

Consensus estimates show FY2026 EPS of $3.93 and FY2027 of $3.82. The slight decline in FY2027 estimates likely reflects conservatism around SUBOXONE Film erosion and uncertainty about deployment of pipeline investment. If SUBLOCADE continues its current trajectory and the company deploys capital effectively, FY2027 estimates have upside risk.

## Competitive Landscape

SUBLOCADE's competitive position is strong and improving:

- **~75% share of U.S. LAI OUD market** — stable in recent quarters despite BRIXADI (Camurus/Braeburn) launch in 2023
- **Pharmacological advantage:** SUBLOCADE delivers sustained plasma levels that block fentanyl's effects — increasingly important as fentanyl dominates the illicit supply
- **Switching costs:** Patients stabilized on monthly SUBLOCADE have strong clinical inertia against switching
- **Safety-controlled distribution:** SUBLOCADE is subject to a REMS program requiring distribution through certified healthcare settings and specialty pharmacies for provider administration. While REMS programs cannot legally be used to block generic competition, the controlled distribution infrastructure, combined with the ATRIGEL formulation complexity, clinical adoption patterns, and prescriber/payer familiarity, creates meaningful operational barriers for new entrants.
- **Label expansion advantage:** Rapid initiation protocol and alternative sites were validated specifically for SUBLOCADE's ATRIGEL delivery system

The primary competitive threat is BRIXADI, which offers both monthly and weekly dosing options. BRIXADI has gained traction outside the U.S. (marketed as BUVIDAL) but has not materially eroded SUBLOCADE's domestic share. The market is large enough (83% untreated) that both products can grow simultaneously from expanded penetration rather than share-taking.

Generic oral buprenorphine (Suboxone generics) competes on cost but not on adherence or outcomes. The value proposition of LAI treatment — guaranteed monthly adherence, elimination of diversion risk, consistent plasma levels — positions SUBLOCADE in a qualitatively different market tier.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **Single-product concentration (HIGH).** SUBLOCADE will be ~77% of FY2026 revenue. Any manufacturing disruption (single Raleigh facility under expansion), safety signal, or competitive loss would be catastrophic. The company is building manufacturing redundancy but remains concentrated.

2. **Pipeline vacuum (HIGH).** With no internally advancing clinical programs, the company must acquire or license products to diversify. This introduces execution risk on M&A and potential overpayment risk.

3. **Patent cliff (MODERATE, 5+ years out).** Core patents expire June 2031; method patents extend to November 2035. The ATRIGEL delivery system's complexity provides additional formulation barriers, but this timeline will increasingly weigh on the multiple as 2031 approaches.

4. **Medicaid/policy risk (MODERATE).** Budget reconciliation could restrict Medicaid eligibility, reducing the insured patient pool. OUD treatment has bipartisan support but is not immune to budget pressure.

5. **Dental litigation (LOW-MODERATE).** Approximately 2,000 Dental MDL cases naming more than 25,000 plaintiffs allege SUBOXONE Film caused dental injuries. These cases are in early stages, with bellwether trials not expected before Q4 2027 at the earliest. Insurance is covering defense costs but has reserved rights against liability payments.

6. **FTC/DOJ restrictions through 2030.** Marketing and product launch restrictions limit strategic flexibility for new products.

## Conclusion

Indivior combines a dominant franchise in a structurally underpenetrated market, 50%+ EBITDA margins, double-digit growth, aggressive capital return, and a significant discount to specialty pharmaceutical peers. The discount for single-product concentration and pipeline failure is excessive at current levels. Estimate revision momentum and consecutive earnings beats signal that consensus remains behind the operational reality of the post-restructuring business.

The primary risk is the pipeline vacuum — the company must either demonstrate that SUBLOCADE alone can sustain growth through the patent cliff (via penetration of the 83% untreated population) or successfully acquire diversifying assets. Management's aggressive buyback activity (including the May 2026 $175M ASR at prices below today's level) demonstrates conviction in the core franchise.

At 10x forward earnings on a business growing EBITDA 50% YoY, the risk/reward favors buyers.
