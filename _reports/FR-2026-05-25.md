## Executive Summary

First Industrial Realty Trust is a $8.4B mid-cap industrial REIT owning 420 properties (70.9M SF) across 15 key U.S. logistics markets. The company generates consistent internal growth through exceptional rent mark-to-market (32-41% cash spreads on new/renewal leases) and targeted development in supply-constrained markets. Q1 2026 delivered same-store NOI growth of +8.7% and an occupancy rate of 94.3%, though FFO/share was flat YoY due to $5.6M in contested proxy costs.

Fitch upgraded the company to BBB+ in May 2025, the balance sheet is 92.3% fixed-rate with laddered maturities, and the $131M Phoenix land sale (3x market value) demonstrates hidden asset value in the portfolio. However, at $63.28—within 2% of its 52-week high—the stock prices in much of this quality. FFO growth is decelerating from 8-15% historically to 3-6% in 2026, occupancy is trending lower (95.3% → 94.3%), and next-year EPS of $1.86 implies a forward P/E of ~34x once this year's gain-inflated earnings normalize. With only 5.9% upside to the consensus target of $67, the risk/reward is approximately symmetric.

## Company Overview

First Industrial Realty Trust is a self-administered, fully integrated industrial REIT focused exclusively on U.S. logistics properties. The company operates 420 industrial properties totaling 70.9 million square feet across 19 states, concentrated in 15 key logistics markets selected for favorable demand characteristics, constrained land supply, and population growth. Key markets include Northern and Southern California (26.3% of consolidated NOI), Philadelphia (11.4%), Dallas, Phoenix, Chicago, Miami/South Florida, Seattle, and Houston.

The business model combines three growth levers: (1) internal growth via above-market rental rate increases on renewals, (2) external growth via development of best-in-class logistics facilities, and (3) portfolio recycling through dispositions of lower-growth assets and reinvestment in higher-return markets. The company employs only 152 people to manage nearly 71 million SF—reflecting the capital-light, net-lease operational model.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and FFO Trajectory:**

Revenue has grown from $540M (2022) to $727M (2025), a 10.5% CAGR. Q1 2026 revenue annualizes to ~$779M, representing continued 10% YoY growth. FFO/share has compounded from $1.97 (2021) to $2.96 (2025), with 2026 guidance of $3.05-$3.15 implying 3-6% growth, a meaningful deceleration from the 8-15% range delivered over the prior four years.

**Q1 2026 Operating Metrics:**

- FFO: $0.68/share (flat YoY; $0.72 excluding proxy costs)
- Same-Store NOI (cash basis): +8.7% YoY
- Cash rental rate increases: 32% on commenced leases; 41% on signed leases commencing in 2026
- Occupancy: 94.3% (vs. 95.3% prior year)
- Tenant retention: 85.7%

The 32-41% rent spread figure is the most important metric in the report. It indicates FR's in-place rents are 25-30% below market across the portfolio, providing a multi-year embedded growth runway as leases roll regardless of market rent growth.

**Balance Sheet:**

- Total debt: ~$2.58B (D/E ~1.1x)
- Weighted average interest rate: 4.21% (up from 4.03% prior quarter)
- 92.3% fixed-rate (including swaps)
- Liquidity: $724M available ($850M revolver + $37M cash)
- No debt maturities within 12 months
- Credit ratings: BBB/Baa2/BBB+ (all Stable)

The 16% debt increase from $2.22B (2024) to $2.57B (Q1 2026) reflects acquisition activity (including $245M from JV wind-down) but is manageable given the Fitch upgrade trajectory and well-staggered maturity profile.

**Cash Flow and Dividend:**

Operating cash flow: $461M (2025), up 31% from $352M (2024). AFFO of $90.6M in Q1 well covers the $68.3M quarterly dividend (payout ratio ~75%). The annualized dividend of $2.00/share yields 3.16% at the current price, below the 4.0% peer median but reflecting FR's growth premium.

## Growth Analysis

**Internal Growth (Primary Driver):**

With ~20% of leases rolling annually at 32-41% cash spreads, the portfolio mechanically generates 5-8% annual same-store revenue growth for 3-5 years even without market rent increases.

**External Growth (Development):**

FR has ~$124M under construction across 4 projects (0.7M SF), with $57M remaining to fund. Development yields typically run 150-200bps above acquisition cap rates. However, the development pipeline is modest relative to portfolio size, and higher construction costs plus tariff uncertainty may constrain new starts.

**Growth Deceleration Factors:**

1. Fewer asset sales available at elevated gains (Phoenix $109M gain is a one-time event)
2. Higher interest expense as older debt refinances at current rates
3. Occupancy headwinds (94.3% vs. 95.3% a year ago)
4. Market rent growth moderating from pandemic-era highs
5. 2026 FFO guidance of $3.05-$3.15 implies single-digit growth

**FFO Growth Outlook:** Mid-single-digit FFO/share growth (4-7%) over the next 2-3 years, driven primarily by rent mark-to-market and modest development completions, partially offset by rising interest costs and normalizing occupancy.

## Competitive Landscape

FR operates in a competitive sector dominated by Prologis (~$120B market cap). Its differentiation comes from:

1. **Supply-constrained market focus:** The 15-market strategy targets areas with natural barriers to entry (limited land, zoning restrictions), giving FR pricing power on rent renewals.
2. **Operational efficiency:** 152 employees managing 70.9M SF (466K SF/employee) demonstrates best-in-class productivity.
3. **Development capability:** Building at cost and delivering at market rents adds 150-200bp yield premium vs. acquisitions.
4. **Decentralized management:** Regional teams with 12-year average tenure provide deep local market intelligence.

The competitive position is stable. FR is neither gaining nor losing meaningful market share relative to peers. The key structural tailwind—nearshoring/onshoring demand for domestic logistics space—benefits the entire sector. The key structural headwind—new supply delivery moderating the post-pandemic tightness—also affects the entire sector.

The occupancy decline from 95.3% to 94.3% suggests FR is prioritizing rent maximization over occupancy maximization. This is rational when below-market leases are rolling at 32%+ spreads, but it creates short-term FFO headwinds.

## Risk Assessment

**Rate Sensitivity (Moderate-High):** Higher rates compress REIT valuations through higher discount rates, increase refinancing costs (FR's average rate rose from 4.03% to 4.21% in one quarter), and create competition from risk-free alternatives. The 3.16% dividend yield offers limited spread over risk-free rates.

**Tariff/Trade Risk (Moderate):** The 2025 10-K explicitly added tariff risk as a new factor. FR owns properties near ports; tenants relying on imported goods face demand disruption. However, nearshoring trends could offset this by increasing domestic distribution needs.

**Occupancy Trend (Low-Moderate):** Management guides 94.0-95.0% for 2026. If occupancy falls below 93%, the growth thesis weakens.

**Tenant Credit (Low-Moderate):** Q1 disclosure of a "3PL tenant on credit watchlist" behind on rent is a yellow flag. Industrial tenants have generally strong credit, but logistics providers face margin pressure from trade disruptions.

**Leverage Trajectory (Low-Moderate):** Debt rose 16% in 18 months to $2.58B. While covered by the Fitch upgrade and maturity profile, continued debt growth in a rising-rate environment warrants monitoring.

**Options Market Signal:**

Limited options data available (36 contracts across 3 expirations), suggesting low institutional hedging activity. Historical volatility of 19.2% and an implied move of ±5.3% (1-month) indicate the market prices benign near-term outcomes. Short interest of 3.5% (days to cover: 3.9) is unremarkable and consistent with a consensus HOLD.

## Conclusion

First Industrial Realty Trust is a well-managed industrial REIT with genuine competitive advantages: supply-constrained market positioning, exceptional rent mark-to-market potential (32-41%), investment-grade credit, and efficient operations. The fundamental quality merits a premium valuation.

At $63.28, the stock trades within 2% of its 52-week high, offers only 5.9% upside to the consensus mean target ($67), yields 3.16% vs. a 4.0% peer median, and trades at ~34x normalized next-year earnings (above the 29.5x peer median). FFO growth is decelerating from 8-15% to 3-6%, and the rate environment is a direct headwind to REIT multiples through higher discount rates and yield competition. The risk/reward is approximately symmetric: modest upside if rates moderate and rent spreads persist, material downside if rates rise further. The right entry point is $56-57, where the yield approaches 3.5-3.6% and the P/FFO multiple compresses to ~18x, providing a margin of safety.
