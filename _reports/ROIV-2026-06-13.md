**Research Date: June 13, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Roivant Sciences is a biopharmaceutical holding company that develops in-licensed clinical assets inside semi-autonomous "Vant" subsidiaries and monetizes them through divestitures and Vant IPOs. It carries no approved or commercial product today, having sold VTAMA/Dermavant to Organon in October 2024 and Telavant's RVT-3101 to Roche in 2023. The entity is best understood as a balance sheet plus a portfolio of clinical options. Standard ROE/margin/P/E screens are not meaningful, and the deeply negative trailing and forward P/E (-54.8x) reflects deliberate R&D investment against negligible revenue, not distress.

The defining feature is the balance sheet. As of December 31, 2025, Roivant held ~$4.53B in cash and U.S. Treasuries with essentially no debt and total liabilities of only $251M. A near-certain ~$950M Moderna settlement payment lands in July 2026 (reaffirmed at the May 20 update), with up to ~$1.3B more contingent on the §1498 appeal. Add Roivant's ~52-56% stake in publicly-traded Immunovant — re-rated up 27-35% on May 20 RA data and the single largest non-pipeline asset — plus ~$187M in Arbutus and ~$247M in Datavant, and a conservative sum-of-parts floor reaches roughly $9-10B before assigning any value to brepocitinib, the wholly-owned pipeline, Dermavant royalties, or contingent Moderna. Against a ~$21.3B market cap, the market is paying roughly $11-12B for brepocitinib (now under FDA Priority Review in dermatomyositis) and Roivant's share of IMVT-1402, a next-generation anti-FcRn antibody.

I maintain HOLD with a constructive (growth) bias. The thesis pivoted favorably in 2026: batoclimab's failure in thyroid eye disease was offset by strong IMVT-1402 RA data and brepocitinib's Priority Review, and 12 of 13 covering analysts rate the stock Buy/Strong Buy with a ~$39 mean target. The reason this is not a BUY is specific: forward EPS estimates have been cut sharply across every horizon (current year -29% in 30 days), the near-term value drivers are binary 2H26 clinical reads, and the asset floor, while real, sits ~45-55% below the current price, so it caps catastrophic downside but does not contain the 25-35% derating a single pipeline miss would produce. This is a name to hold for a dense, dated catalyst path, with concrete triggers to upgrade.

## Company Overview

Roivant is Bermuda-incorporated and UK tax-resident, with a fiscal year ending March 31. The "Vant" model in-licenses or acquires clinical assets (typically with low upfront cost and milestone/royalty-heavy structures) and develops them in subsidiaries with their own management and equity plans. Monetization occurs through M&A (Telavant→Roche, Dermavant→Organon) or Vant IPOs (Immunovant, ticker IMVT).

Key assets and ownership (per the 12/31/25 10-Q):

- **Priovant (73% basic / 66% FD):** brepocitinib, an oral TYK2/JAK1 inhibitor. NDA filed in dermatomyositis with **FDA Priority Review** granted; Phase 2 win in cutaneous sarcoidosis (Feb 2026) with Breakthrough Therapy designation (May 2026); Phase 3 in non-infectious uveitis reading 2H26. Pfizer originated the asset and holds a minority equity stake in Priovant; Roivant controls development. This is the nearest-term path to a first approved product.
- **Immunovant (56% basic / 52% FD; publicly traded):** IMVT-1402, a next-generation anti-FcRn antibody across Graves', myasthenia gravis, RA, Sjögren's, CIDP, and cutaneous lupus. Batoclimab is discontinued after both Phase 3 TED studies were wound down; IMVT-1402 is the franchise's future.
- **Pulmovant (98%):** mosliciguat, an inhaled sGC activator for PH-ILD, Phase 2 data 2H26.
- **Genevant/GSG (83%):** lipid nanoparticle delivery IP underpinning litigation against Moderna (largely settled) and Pfizer/BioNTech (ongoing).
- **Other:** discovery-stage Covant/PsiThera/Zest, plus equity stakes in Arbutus (~$187M) and Datavant (~$247M).

## Financial Analysis

The balance sheet is the asset. At 12/31/25, cash plus marketable securities were ~$4.53B ($1.48B cash, $3.05B Treasuries), debt was effectively nil, and total liabilities were $251M against total equity of $4.97B (including $705M noncontrolling interest). Management states it has no near-term need for additional capital.

The income statement is a pre-revenue burn profile. Revenue is negligible — $2.0M in Q3 FY26 and $5.7M for the first nine months of FY26, down from $21.5M as Dermavant revenue rolled off. The operating loss was $339M in Q3 and $930M for 9M FY26 (versus $718M the prior-year period). R&D rose 19% to $483M (9M), driven by the anti-FcRn franchise (+$27M), brepocitinib (+$13.9M), and mosliciguat (+$12.4M). G&A of $452M (9M) was heavily inflated by $229M of share-based compensation tied to the 2024 Senior Executive Compensation Program and the Priovant equity exchange. Interest income of $137M (9M) is a meaningful offset but is shrinking as cash draws down and rates fall (from $204M prior year). Net loss attributable to Roivant was $603M for 9M FY26.

Operating cash burn ran ~$587M over nine months. Cash and securities declined from $4.89B (3/31/25) to $4.53B (12/31/25), with burn partially offset by $217M of option-exercise proceeds and the Immunovant raise. Capital allocation has been aggressive on buybacks: a $1.5B program was exhausted by June 2025 (including the $648M Sumitomo block at $9.10), followed by $500M and a subsequent $1.0B authorization. In December 2025, Roivant invested $350M (16.67M shares at $21) into Immunovant's $544M raise, lifting its stake to ~56%. Repurchasing stock while burning cash signals management views the equity as trading below sum-of-parts.

The "$4.35B net income" some screens show reflects prior-year one-time gains (the Telavant/Roche sale, ~$5.2B cash in Dec 2023, and the Dermavant/Organon gain of $376M in FY25). Underlying operations are loss-making. The negative earnings surprise pattern (3 misses in the last 4 quarters, most recently -$0.58 vs. -$0.35 estimate) reflects spending decisions, not demand erosion — the right read for a company with no revenue base to disappoint against.

## Growth Analysis

Growth here means clinical and regulatory progress, not revenue trajectory. The 2026 inflection is genuine and favorable:

- **Brepocitinib** has an NDA filed in dermatomyositis with Priority Review, a Phase 2 cutaneous sarcoidosis success plus Breakthrough Therapy designation, and a Phase 3 uveitis read in 2H26. As an oral dual TYK2/JAK1 in orphan derm-rheum indications with few approved therapies, it has early-mover potential and orphan dynamics.
- **IMVT-1402** posted strong open-label Week-16 data in difficult-to-treat RA on May 20, 2026 (72.7% ACR20 / 54.5% ACR50 / 35.8% ACR70), which analysts called "highly compelling." Registrational RA reads come 2H26, with Graves'/MG in 2027. The December raise extended Immunovant's runway toward a potential Graves' launch.
- **Mosliciguat** reads Phase 2 in PH-ILD in 2H26.

The growth concern is not the science but the estimate trend. Consensus EPS has been cut across every horizon: current-quarter -22.6% (90d), current-year -29.1% (30d) and -19.5% (90d), next-year -72.4% (90d). For a pre-revenue biotech these revisions reflect higher planned R&D and G&A rather than weakening demand, but the magnitude and breadth are material and run directly against the strongest forward-looking positive signal an inflection name can offer. The franchise risk has shifted from "will the lead asset work" (batoclimab answered that, negatively) to "execution across a broad label-expansion program" — a better problem to have, but still one resolved through binary trial reads.

## Valuation Assessment

Roivant cannot be valued on multiples; the forward P/E of -54.8x is uninformative, and the peer table (INCY 15.3x, UTHR 20.2x, NBIX 25.0x, EXEL 17.7x, MRNA -6.1x) compares profitable or near-profitable biotechs to a pre-revenue holding company. The correct frame is sum-of-parts.

Building the floor explicitly, since it is the crux of the call:

- Net cash + securities (12/31/25): ~$4.53B
- Near-certain Moderna payment (July 2026): ~$0.95B
- Immunovant stake (~52-56% of a publicly-traded company that re-rated on RA data; triangulating from the $21 December raise and the May 20 move, IMVT's market cap sits in the multi-billion range, putting Roivant's economic share at roughly $3.5-5B): use ~$4B midpoint
- Arbutus stake: ~$0.19B
- Datavant stake: ~$0.25B

Conservative hard floor ≈ **$9.9B**, before any value for contingent Moderna (up to ~$1.3B), Dermavant royalties (30% on VTAMA sales above $1B plus up to $950M milestones), brepocitinib, the wholly-owned Pulmovant pipeline, or Genevant's remaining LNP cases.

Against the market cap, the residual the market pays for the pipeline (brepocitinib, Roivant's share of IMVT-1402, litigation and royalty optionality) is roughly **$11-12B**. Whether that is cheap depends entirely on the 2H26 reads. For a derm/rheum oral with Priority Review and a competitive anti-FcRn at registrational stage, $11-12B is defensible but not obviously mispriced — it embeds meaningful clinical success.

The asymmetry assessment: the floor caps catastrophic downside, but at ~$9-10B it sits roughly 45-55% below the current price. That protects against a worst-case zeroing of the pipeline; it does not protect against a 25-35% derating if a single major catalyst disappoints, which would occur well before the asset floor is tested. The analyst mean target of ~$39 (~30% upside) and TD Cowen's $41 Buy (raised May 21, from $38) are credible, and on an inflection name with a low residual valuation, consensus targets tend to lag, but the realization path runs through binary events. I score valuation 6.0: the floor is real and the residual is reasonable, but the upside is contingent on catalysts that can equally cut the other way.

## Competitive Landscape

- **Anti-FcRn (IMVT-1402):** competes against argenx's VYVGART/VYVGART Hytrulo (the category leader), J&J's IMAAVY (nipocalimab), and UCB's RYSTIGGO. IMVT-1402's differentiation is deeper IgG suppression with a cleaner analyte profile than batoclimab (less albumin/LDL effect), enabling broad autoimmune expansion. It is a late entrant into a crowded, well-capitalized class, so commercial share will be contested even if the science holds.
- **Brepocitinib in DM:** faces Pfizer's dazukibart (an IFN-β inhibitor). As an oral dual inhibitor in indications with few approved therapies, brepocitinib has early-mover and orphan advantages.
- **Mosliciguat:** competes against United Therapeutics' Tyvaso and Liquidia's Yutrepia in pulmonary hypertension.
- **Genevant LNP:** an industry-wide IP battle. The Moderna matter resolved favorably, Pfizer/BioNTech is ongoing, and the January 2026 EPO Board of Appeal revocation of the '254 European patent is a genuine negative for the European cases. This is two-sided and screens routinely miss the downside leg.

Competitive trajectory is improving on the strength of the 2026 data but remains a challenger position in the anti-FcRn class. Score 6.0.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Binary clinical/regulatory concentration.** Near-term value hinges on brepocitinib DM approval and uveitis Phase 3 (2H26) plus IMVT-1402 registrational reads (RA 2H26; Graves'/MG 2027). The 10-K itself flags that a few Vants may comprise a large share of value.
2. **Batoclimab failure already realized,** with a ~$39M minimum manufacturing obligation through FY30 and a potential rights-return dispute with HanAll.
3. **HanAll dependency.** All of Immunovant's core IP is in-licensed from HanAll with territorial limits; termination or dispute would be highly damaging.
4. **Vant governance friction.** Immunovant's public status requires an independent special committee Roivant does not control for related-party transactions, limiting operational control; the decentralized model duplicates costs.
5. **Genevant litigation (two-sided).** The EPO '254 revocation (Jan 2026) offsets favorable U.S. Moderna and Pfizer rulings.
6. **Cash deployment risk.** Management has broad discretion over $4.5B+; the strategy depends on repeatedly in-licensing winners.
7. **Structural/tax risks:** Bermuda incorporation (weaker shareholder remedies), UK tax residency, PFIC/CFC considerations for 10%+ U.S. holders.
8. **Heavy equity-comp dilution:** ~48.8M PSUs and ~97M options outstanding; the largest driver of GAAP G&A and a material per-share consideration.

On insider activity: the data show systematic executive and 10%-holder selling, including a fresh Torti 10b5-1 plan for up to 3.0M shares running into mid-2026, and no open-market buying. I treat this as low-signal rather than bearish — it is mechanically explained by PSU hurdle tranches that just vested at $15/$17.50/$20 and by pre-scheduled 10b5-1 plans, which is typical for a cash-rich pre-revenue biotech with hurdle-based equity now vesting. It is not evidence of deteriorating conviction, and I do not weight it as a negative. The absence of conviction buying at ~$30 does not change the call.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV is 68.1% against 40.1% one-year historical vol — rich, with the term structure in backwardation (68.1% near-term vs. 46.7% six-month). The one-week put/call skew is elevated at 57.3%, and IV jumped 26.9 points (41.3% → 68.1%) in a single session. This is front-end event hedging around an imminent scheduled catalyst rather than a structural bearish signal: the one-month skew flips call-heavy (-15.7%), and the three- and six-month skews are roughly flat. The implied one-week move of ±5.8% confirms the market is pricing a near-dated binary. For a fundamental holder, this argues against initiating fresh exposure into elevated event premium; it does not undermine the longer-term thesis.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The sum-of-parts floor means downside is structurally capped, and the market pays only the residual for a pipeline that just produced strong IMVT-1402 RA data and a brepocitinib Priority Review. If the 2H26 reads (uveitis Phase 3, mosliciguat Phase 2, IMVT-1402 RA registrational) land and brepocitinib is approved in DM, the stock re-rates toward the ~$39-45 analyst range and beyond, with multiple uncorrelated shots on goal funded entirely by the balance sheet. Contingent Moderna and Dermavant royalties provide free optionality. Bull target ~$45 (+50%).

**Bear case.** The near-term drivers are binary. A uveitis Phase 3 miss, an IMVT-1402 registrational disappointment, or a brepocitinib regulatory setback would each take the stock down 25-35% before the asset floor is approached. Estimates are already falling sharply, the anti-FcRn class is crowded and well-capitalized, Genevant's European IP is weakening, and the cash is being burned at ~$780M annualized while management exercises broad and unproven reinvestment discretion. In a scenario where the pipeline broadly disappoints, the stock converges toward the floor, roughly $13-14 per share. Bear target ~$15-18 (-40% to -50%).

The base case is that the catalysts deliver mixed-to-positive results and the stock holds in the high-$20s to low-$30s range as consensus digests each read, with upside skewed by the analyst target cluster but gated by event risk.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** The franchise has improving asymmetry and a hard floor that caps catastrophic downside, but the formal conditions for a high-conviction long are not met: forward EPS estimates are falling across every horizon, the bear-case downside to the asset floor is ~45-55% (well beyond the ≤15% threshold a BUY requires), and the value drivers are binary 2H26 reads rather than a contained, near-dated event.

**Reassessment timeline:** the July 2026 Moderna payment confirmation, then the cluster of 2H26 clinical reads (uveitis Phase 3, mosliciguat Phase 2, IMVT-1402 RA registrational), and any announced brepocitinib PDUFA date.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Brepocitinib DM approval, or a confirmed PDUFA date within 60 days paired with a stabilizing estimate trend (revisions no longer negative).
2. A positive IMVT-1402 RA registrational read or positive uveitis Phase 3 data in 2H26 that de-risks the largest pipeline contributors.
3. A pullback toward the high-teens/low-$20s on no fundamental deterioration, which would compress the residual the market pays for the pipeline toward the asset floor and restore contained downside.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. A uveitis Phase 3 or IMVT-1402 registrational failure, or a brepocitinib Complete Response Letter.
2. A material adverse turn in the HanAll relationship or a Genevant litigation reversal that impairs U.S. LNP recoveries.
3. Acceleration of cash burn beyond ~$1B annualized via dilutive in-licensing without a corresponding clinical de-risking.

## Conclusion

Roivant is a well-capitalized clinical-stage holding company whose 2026 thesis pivot — IMVT-1402's strong RA data and brepocitinib's Priority Review replacing the failed batoclimab program — is genuine and constructive. Quantifying the sum-of-parts confirms the audit's central point: a conservative floor caps catastrophic downside, and the market pays the residual for a pipeline with several near-term shots on goal. The insider selling and options backwardation cited as bearish are correctly treated as noise — mechanical PSU vesting and front-end event hedging, respectively — and I do not lean on them.

What keeps this at HOLD rather than BUY is that the floor sits ~45-55% below the current price, EPS estimates are being cut sharply across every horizon, and the value drivers are binary clinical/regulatory reads. The asset floor protects against a worst-case, not against the 25-35% derating a single 2H26 miss would produce. That is a hold-for-the-catalysts profile, with concrete, near-dated upgrade triggers, rather than the contained-downside setup that justifies underwriting a high-conviction long. Existing holders should retain the position for the catalyst path; new capital has clearly defined entry points on either de-risking events or a pullback toward the floor.
