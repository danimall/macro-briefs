## Executive Summary

National Fuel Gas trades at $81.51 (as of early April 2026), representing approximately 10.8x forward earnings against a gas utility peer median of 19.6x P/E. This discount reflects the market's treatment of NFG as a commodity-exposed E&P company rather than a diversified, vertically integrated utility with growing regulated earnings. The CenterPoint Ohio acquisition ($2.62B, expected close Q4 2026) will approximately double the utility rate base and shift the earnings mix toward higher-quality regulated cash flows, which should compress the valuation discount over time.

The company's pipeline segment is directly positioned for the emerging data center power generation theme via its Line N system, with the Shippingport Lateral specifically serving a power plant for a co-located data center and the Line N System Upgrade adding 94,000 Dth/day of incremental capacity targeting the same corridor. The Shippingport Lateral and Tioga Pathway are under construction with targeted late 2026 in-service dates, while the Line N System Upgrade is supported by a precedent agreement signed April 2026, targeting late 2028 completion. The company's hedging program, with significant volumes locked at prices well above current spot, provides meaningful earnings protection through 2028-2029.

The primary risk preventing a higher conviction level is acquisition execution: the $1.2B seller note at 6.5% requires permanent refinancing within a year of closing, creating a capital markets dependency. Additionally, FY2026 guidance was reduced from $7.60-$8.10 to $7.45-$7.75, primarily due to lower NYMEX assumptions. However, at approximately 11x earnings with identifiable catalysts for re-rating, the risk/reward favors accumulation.

## Company Overview

National Fuel Gas is a diversified natural gas company operating across four business functions in the Appalachian Basin: E&P production (Seneca Resources, 427 Bcfe FY2025), midstream gathering, interstate pipeline/storage (Supply Corporation and Empire Pipeline, FERC-regulated), and retail utility distribution (756,000 customers in western NY and northwestern PA). The geographic concentration of all segments in the same basin creates operational efficiencies and captive internal demand — approximately 35% of Pipeline & Storage revenue comes from intercompany transactions.

FY2025 segment earnings mix: Integrated Upstream/Gathering ~62%, Pipeline & Storage ~23%, Utility ~16%. Post-CenterPoint Ohio close, the utility segment should approach ~25-30% of earnings, materially improving the regulated earnings percentage.

## Valuation Assessment

NFG trades at approximately 10.8x forward earnings based on the $81.51 price (early April 2026) versus FY2026E EPS midpoint of ~$7.60. Note: The company's April 27, 2026 investor materials imply a market cap of approximately $8.4B on 95.0M shares outstanding, suggesting the share price may have appreciated since the $81.51 reference date. At an approximately $88 price, the forward multiple would be closer to 11.6x — still a significant discount to peers. This compares to:

| Metric | NFG | Peer Median |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| P/E (forward) | ~10.8-11.6x | 19.6x |
| Dividend Yield | ~2.4-2.6% | 3.4% |

The 40-45% discount to the peer median P/E is the central valuation argument. The discount is partially justified by commodity exposure in the E&P segment — pure utilities typically command higher multiples due to earnings stability. However, several factors argue the discount is excessive:

1. **Post-CenterPoint earnings mix shift**: Regulated utility earnings rising from ~16% to ~25-30% should warrant 2-3 multiple turns of expansion.
2. **Hedging program**: Significant volumes hedged at prices well above current spot effectively transforms much of the near-term E&P earnings into quasi-fixed cash flows through 2029.
3. **Data center pipeline growth**: High-visibility, contracted, FERC-regulated returns on pipeline investments tied to the strongest demand theme in energy.

At 13x FY2027E EPS of $7.97, NFG would trade at ~$104. At 15x (still a 24% discount to peers), it would trade at ~$120. The analyst consensus target range of $93-$110 (median $102) supports this re-rating thesis.

**Downside valuation floor**: At 9x trough-scenario earnings of $6.50 (gas price decline + acquisition dilution), downside target would be ~$58-60, representing approximately 28-34% downside from current levels depending on the reference price used.

## Competitive Landscape

NFG's vertical integration is genuinely differentiated among publicly traded gas companies. The combination of Appalachian Basin upstream production, owned gathering systems, FERC-regulated interstate pipelines, and retail distribution creates a self-contained value chain where production growth directly feeds pipeline revenue, and the utility provides weather-normalized counter-cyclical stability.

The data center pipeline positioning through Line N is an emerging competitive advantage that the market has not yet fully priced. Natural gas infrastructure hubs are becoming focal points for data center siting decisions due to the reliability and cost advantages of on-site gas generation. NFG's position in southwestern Pennsylvania — proximate to both in-basin production and potential power generation sites — creates a natural competitive moat for these projects.

Competitive threats include NY CLCPA legislation (restricting new gas hookups in buildings effective Dec 2025, currently suspended pending litigation), which represents a long-term structural headwind to the NY utility franchise. The CenterPoint Ohio acquisition partially mitigates this by diversifying into a more gas-friendly regulatory jurisdiction.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **CenterPoint Ohio execution and financing (MODERATE-HIGH)**: The $1.2B seller note at 6.5% requires permanent refinancing within 364 days of closing. In the current elevated rate environment, capital markets access for investment-grade utilities remains functional but more expensive than historical norms. A credit downgrade during the process would amplify costs. The HSR waiting period has expired and is deemed satisfied; PUCO notice review remains the primary regulatory condition.

2. **Natural gas price exposure (MODERATE)**: Despite robust hedging, the Q2 FY2026 guidance reduction demonstrates residual commodity sensitivity. If NYMEX declines below $2.50 for an extended period, ceiling test impairments return and E&P earnings compress beyond hedge coverage.

3. **NY CLCPA regulatory risk (LOW-MODERATE near-term, HIGH long-term)**: Currently suspended via litigation, but the structural direction of NY policy is toward electrification. The utility serves 756,000 customers; any material decline in new connections or forced asset stranding would impair the NY franchise value.

4. **Dilution (MODERATE)**: Approximately 4.4M shares issued in a private placement ($338M net proceeds) with potential for additional equity to permanently finance the acquisition. At current price levels, each additional $100M in equity issuance dilutes EPS by approximately 1.5%.

5. **Negative estimate revision momentum**: FY2026 guidance reduction signals analysts may still be adjusting models downward. This creates near-term headwind to multiple expansion.

## Conclusion

NFG presents an attractive risk/reward at approximately 11x forward earnings for a vertically integrated gas company with identifiable re-rating catalysts. The 40-45% P/E discount to gas utility peers is partially justified by commodity exposure but is excessive given the hedging program's earnings protection, the CenterPoint acquisition's shift toward regulated earnings, and the data center pipeline optionality along the Line N corridor. The FY2026 guidance reduction is a legitimate near-term headwind, but the magnitude of the valuation discount provides margin of safety: even at the guided midpoint of $7.60, the stock trades at just 10.7-11.6x current-year earnings depending on the reference date.

The specific catalyst for re-rating is the CenterPoint Ohio close and first full quarter of combined operations (likely Q1-Q2 FY2027), which will demonstrate the improved earnings mix and accretion. KeyBanc's recent initiation at Overweight with a $110 target validates the thesis from the sell-side. The risk/reward of +20% to base case versus -20% to bear case, with identifiable near-term catalysts, supports a BUY recommendation with 2-3% position sizing.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The hedge book's "embedded value" was previously overstated using a simplistic calculation (~$270M); actual net derivative fair value at March 31, 2026 was approximately $116.6M, which reduces one supporting element of the downside protection argument. Additionally, the $81.51 reference price may be stale relative to an implied ~$88 price as of late April 2026, which would modestly narrow the upside to target while still supporting the thesis.
