## Executive Summary

CONMED Corporation trades at $36.76, down 36% from its 52-week high of $61.08, reflecting the market's assessment of three compounding issues: (1) an $800M convertible note maturity in June 2027 that represents ~73% of market cap, (2) CEO/CFO turnover creating governance uncertainty, and (3) portfolio restructuring costs that collapsed GAAP profitability in FY2025. Underlying operating performance is inflecting positively: Q1 2026 organic growth was 2.1% (raised to 5.0-6.5% for the year), adjusted gross margins expanded 100bps to 57.4%, AirSeal capital unit placements grew 50%+, and the company beat Q1 estimates by 6.5%.

The central investment question is whether the refinancing of the $800M convert (planned for Q2 2026 via bank debt) is manageable or structurally impairs the equity. At ~6% bank debt rates, annual interest expense rises from ~$18M to ~$50M, representing a $0.70-$0.80 EPS headwind partially offset by operational improvements. Management chose bank debt over new converts explicitly because they believe the stock is at a "historic trough in med tech multiples." The 8.4x forward P/E on FY2026 adjusted EPS of $4.37 is reasonable given mid-single-digit growth, but the debt overhang, absence of insider buying, and CFO vacancy create material uncertainty that prevents a high-conviction call in either direction.

## Company Overview

CONMED manufactures surgical devices for orthopedic and general surgery procedures, generating $1.375B in FY2025 revenue. The business splits between Orthopedic Surgery (42% of sales, 78% recurring) and General Surgery (58%, 92% recurring). Key growth platforms include AirSeal (clinical insufflation for minimally invasive/robotic surgery, 6-7% U.S. penetration of 3M+ procedures), Buffalo Filter (smoke evacuation with legislative tailwinds in 20+ states), and BioBrace (soft tissue repair, enrolling 268-patient RCT). The company employs ~3,900 people and sells in 100+ countries, with 56% U.S. revenue.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue of $1.375B grew 5.2% YoY. Q1 2026 organic constant-currency growth was 2.1% (impacted by GI exit timing), with full-year guidance raised to 5.0-6.5%. The GI divestiture removes ~$14.5-17.5M in 2026 revenue but eliminates a low-margin, non-strategic business.

**Profitability:** GAAP operating margin collapsed to 7.5% in FY2025 from 15.3% in FY2024, driven by $64M in contingent consideration swings, $12.2M in former CEO advisory fees, $22.2M in inventory write-offs, and $12.5M in consulting costs. Adjusted EPS grew from $4.17 to $4.59 YoY. Q1 2026 adjusted gross margin of 57.4% (+100bps) indicates underlying operational improvement.

**Cash Flow:** FY2025 operating cash flow was $170.7M with FCF of $150.9M (11% margin). FY2026 FCF guidance is ~$125M due to elevated capex ($20-30M) and working capital investment. The $150M FCF run-rate against a $1.1B market cap produces a 13.6% FCF yield on trailing, or ~11.4% on forward estimates.

**Balance Sheet — The Core Concern:** Total debt is $860M (Q1 2026), consisting primarily of $800M in 2.250% convertible notes due June 15, 2027. Leverage stands at 3.1x. The company holds only $40.8M cash (mostly held overseas) with $648.5M undrawn on the revolver. Goodwill and intangibles comprise $1.39B (60% of assets). The Altman Z-Score of 2.02 sits in the grey zone.

**Capital Return:** Dividend was suspended October 2025. Share repurchases of $37.4M occurred in Q1 2026 at ~$43.60/share average (above current price). Management targets at least $25M annually in buybacks.

## Growth Analysis

CONMED's growth thesis rests on three platforms with multi-year runways:

**AirSeal** is the strongest near-term driver. With 10,000+ installed systems globally but only 6-7% penetration of U.S. laparoscopic procedures, the addressable opportunity is large. Capital placements up 50% YoY in Q1 2026 are a leading indicator of future consumable revenue (each placement generates recurring single-use revenue for years). Robotic surgery expansion (particularly da Vinci system proliferation into ASCs) is a structural tailwind — AirSeal attaches to robotic procedures at a 10-20% rate with room to grow.

**Buffalo Filter** benefits from secular legislative momentum. Twenty states now mandate smoke-free OR environments, with 13 more pending. Each new mandate creates a step-function demand increase that is predictable and non-cyclical.

**BioBrace** has the longest development timeline. The 268-patient RCT is enrolling now with publication expected in 2027. If successful, this creates a differentiated augmentation product with strong clinical evidence, but clinical trial risk is real and the competitive landscape in orthobiologics is crowded.

Consensus estimates show FY2026 EPS of $4.37 and FY2027 of $4.85 (+11%), reflecting expected normalization once the GI exit drag subsides and gross margins continue expanding. The 90-day revision trend is essentially flat (+0.5% on current year, -2.6% on next year), indicating no analyst conviction on an upside inflection.

## Valuation Assessment

At $36.76, CNMD trades at:
- 8.4x forward P/E on FY2026E adjusted EPS of $4.37
- ~7.6x FY2027E EPS of $4.85
- 24.3x trailing P/E on $1.51 GAAP EPS (distorted by one-time charges)
- ~11% FCF yield on trailing; ~9% on forward
- Analyst consensus mean target of $42.00 (14% upside), with a range of $39-$52

Compared to peers, the sector median P/E of 10.2 (from the comparables provided) is misleading because most peers are unprofitable (TNDM, IART, STAA, ENOV all have negative P/Es). CNMD is one of the few profitable mid-cap medical device companies, which should command a premium to loss-making peers. Against historical med-tech multiples (typically 25-35x for profitable growers), CNMD at 8.4x adjusted earnings appears deeply discounted.

The discount is explained by: (1) the $800M convert overhang, (2) management transition, (3) GAAP earnings distortion. If the refinancing resolves cleanly and operational momentum continues, re-rating to 12-15x adjusted earnings ($52-$73) is plausible over 18 months. The refinancing execution risk is real enough to prevent assigning high confidence to this outcome today.

## Competitive Landscape

CONMED competes against companies 10-50x its size (Stryker, Medtronic, J&J, Arthrex, Smith & Nephew). This creates a structural disadvantage in R&D spending and sales force coverage, offset by product-specific differentiation:

- **AirSeal** has no direct clinical equivalent in insufflation management for MIS/robotic surgery. Clinical evidence supporting its use creates physician preference that resists displacement.
- **Buffalo Filter** has the broadest smoke evacuation portfolio, providing first-mover advantage as legislative mandates proliferate.
- **BioBrace** is the only FDA-cleared implant combining structural reinforcement with biologic healing, a unique positioning that requires RCT data validation.

Scale disadvantages manifest in GPO/IDN negotiations where hospital systems prefer broader portfolios from a single vendor. China volume-based procurement has already compressed margins in that market. The mid-size positioning makes CONMED a logical acquisition target for larger players seeking to bolt on AirSeal or BioBrace technology.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Convertible Note Refinancing (Critical, Binary):** $800M due June 2027 against $1.1B market cap. Management plans bank debt refinancing in Q2 2026. If executed at 6%, annual interest rises ~$32M ($0.70+ EPS impact). If credit markets tighten or CNMD's operational results disappoint, refinancing terms could be worse, or in a stress scenario, the company's equity could face severe dilution. This risk is partially priced (stock down 36%) but not fully resolved until the deal closes.

**2. Management Vacuum:** CEO newly installed, CFO departed March 2026 with search ongoing, former CEO received $12.2M in advisory fees. Multiple Item 5.02 filings in 2025. This level of turnover in a company facing a major refinancing is concerning. No insider buying despite a 36% stock decline.

**3. Inventory Risk:** 246-day inventory (up from 207) with $355M tied up. Prior $22.2M write-off demonstrates obsolescence risk is real. Management positions this as intentional service improvement, but the capital consumption constrains FCF during the refinancing window.

**4. Tariff Exposure:** Manufacturing in U.S./Mexico with global sales creates exposure to both import tariffs on components and retaliatory tariffs on exports. The current elevated-tariff macro environment adds cost uncertainty.

**5. GI Exit Execution:** Transition services extending to 2027 add complexity during an already complex period. Revenue shortfall ($14.5-17.5M vs prior $21-25M guide) shows execution isn't frictionless.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($52-60, +42% to +63%)
- Convertible notes refinanced cleanly at manageable rates (5-6%) in Q2 2026, removing the primary overhang
- AirSeal installations continue at 40-50% growth, driving accelerating consumable revenue into FY2027
- Gross margin expansion continues (+100-200bps annually) as manufacturing optimization takes hold and GI exit completes
- New CFO hire restores governance confidence
- Re-rating from 8x to 12x adjusted earnings on reduced uncertainty ($52-$58)
- Potential acquisition by a larger strategic player seeking AirSeal/BioBrace platforms at a discount

### Bear Case ($25-30, -18% to -32%)
- Refinancing executes at punitive terms (7%+) due to credit market stress or operational miss, adding $40M+ annual interest
- AirSeal growth decelerates as robotic surgery adoption plateaus
- BioBrace RCT fails to demonstrate superiority, eliminating a key growth driver
- Tariff impacts compress gross margins, offsetting manufacturing efficiency gains
- No CFO hire for extended period; governance deteriorates further
- Stock trades toward 5-6x forward EPS reflecting permanent value trap status

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Convertible note refinancing closes at ≤6% interest rate with covenant terms that preserve operational flexibility
2. New CFO hired with credible capital markets background, demonstrating governance stabilization
3. Stock declines below $33 (7.5x FY2026 adjusted EPS), creating sufficient margin of safety against refinancing scenario analysis

**Downgrade triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Refinancing terms reveal >7% rate or require equity issuance/dilution
2. Organic revenue growth falls below 3% for two consecutive quarters, indicating AirSeal momentum stalling
3. Insider selling accelerates or additional executive departures emerge

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026, when management indicated refinancing would occur. Secondary reassessment at Q2 2026 earnings (late July) for operational confirmation.

## Conclusion

CONMED's underlying operational trajectory is improving: AirSeal installations up 50%, organic guidance raised, gross margins expanding, and strategic portfolio cleanup underway. These improvements support a 12-month growth thesis centered on consumable revenue acceleration from the expanded installed base. At 8.4x adjusted earnings with an 11% FCF yield, the stock prices significant distress, and if the refinancing resolves cleanly, material upside exists toward $50+.

The $800M convertible note refinancing (planned for Q2 2026 execution) represents a binary event whose terms will define EPS power for years. The absence of insider buying, CFO vacancy, and 9.1% short interest indicate sophisticated investors share this concern. The options market's 73% near-term vol (vs 39% realized) confirms extreme uncertainty around the refinancing timeline.

The appropriate posture is HOLD: the growth thesis (AirSeal penetration runway, Buffalo Filter legislative tailwinds, margin expansion) is specific and measurable on a 12-month horizon, but execution on the refinancing must be demonstrated before committing capital. The stock becomes a BUY either on successful refinancing at reasonable terms or on price decline to $33 (where the margin of safety absorbs adverse refinancing scenarios). It becomes an AVOID if the refinancing reveals structural impairment to earnings power or governance deteriorates further.
