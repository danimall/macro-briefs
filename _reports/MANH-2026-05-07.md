## Executive Summary

Manhattan Associates is a pure-play supply chain and omnichannel commerce software company trading at $138, representing a 44% drawdown from its July 2025 high of $247. The sell-off has been driven by broader software sector multiple compression, a CEO transition, headline revenue growth deceleration to 4% in FY2025, and securities class action lawsuits. The fundamental picture diverges from the price action: cloud revenue growing 21%+, remaining performance obligations (RPO) growing 25% to $2.2B, a pristine zero-debt balance sheet generating $374M in annual free cash flow (35% FCF margin), and Q1 2026 results that beat estimates and prompted a guidance raise.

The core investment thesis centers on a "hidden growth" dynamic. The 4% total revenue growth in FY2025 reflects the arithmetic of a declining legacy maintenance stream (-6%) and temporarily soft services revenue (-4%) offsetting robust 21% cloud subscription growth. As cloud becomes a structurally larger share of revenue (38% in FY2025 → estimated ~43% in FY2026), headline growth should improve, though the pace depends on the trajectory of services and maintenance revenue streams. Management guides 6-7% total growth for FY2026, with cloud at ~21%. The $2.2B RPO backlog, 98% of which represents non-cancelable multi-year cloud commitments, provides multi-year forward visibility unusual in enterprise software.

At 26x forward FY2026 adjusted (non-GAAP) EPS ($5.36 consensus) and approximately 22x EV/FCF on run-rate free cash flow, the stock offers an attractive entry point for a high-quality compounder. On a GAAP basis, the forward P/E is approximately 38x (based on GAAP EPS guidance of $3.55–$3.63), reflecting the impact of approximately $111M in annual stock-based compensation. The 4-quarter consecutive beat track record (average surprise 10.5%) suggests estimates remain beatable.

## Company Overview

Manhattan Associates develops mission-critical supply chain execution, omnichannel commerce, and supply chain planning software for retailers, wholesalers, manufacturers, and logistics providers. Founded in 1990, the company is headquartered in Atlanta and employs approximately 4,370 people across Americas (75% revenue), EMEA (20%), and APAC (5%).

The company's competitive differentiation rests on its versionless cloud-native platform (Manhattan Active®), which runs exclusively on Google Cloud Platform and delivers quarterly innovation updates to all customers simultaneously on a single codebase with zero-downtime updates. This architecture eliminates the upgrade cycles that plague legacy ERP-embedded supply chain modules from Oracle and SAP, creating significant switching costs and enabling rapid AI feature deployment. The company also delivers its SCALE solution on Microsoft Azure and via on-premise perpetual licenses, though Manhattan Active represents the strategic growth platform.

Revenue mix as of FY2025: Cloud subscriptions 38% ($408M), Professional Services 47% ($503M), Maintenance 12% ($130M), Hardware 2%, License 1%.

## Growth Analysis

**Cloud Revenue — The Primary Value Driver:**
Cloud subscription revenue grew 21% in FY2025 and 24% in Q1 2026. RPO reached $2.2B at FY2025-end, up 25% YoY, with over 98% representing non-cancelable multi-year cloud commitments (typically 5+ year terms). RPO reached $2.35B as of Q1 2026-end, up 24% YoY.

**Revenue Mix Shift Math:**
As cloud becomes a larger revenue share, its growth rate increasingly influences total revenue growth:
- FY2025: Cloud 38% of revenue, growing 21% → contributes ~8pts to total growth, offset by declines in maintenance/services
- FY2026E: Cloud ~43% of revenue, growing 21% → contributes ~9pts; services recovering (+3%), maintenance declining (-17%) → total growth 6-7% per company guidance
- FY2027E (analyst estimate, not company guidance): Cloud >48% of revenue → total growth could reach 8-10% as legacy decline becomes a smaller drag, though this depends on services recovery and the pace of maintenance runoff

However, the path to re-acceleration is not purely mechanical. Services revenue remains large (47% of FY2025 revenue) and is sensitive to customer budgetary constraints and economic conditions. Maintenance will continue declining as cloud migration proceeds.

**AI Optionality:**
Manhattan Active® Agents and Agent Foundry represent meaningful upside not embedded in current estimates. The company has begun 90-day paid AI pilot programs with initial conversion conversations underway. Pricing targets subscription uplift at margins consistent with existing SaaS. Given the company's real-time operational data flowing through its platform, deep domain expertise in supply chain optimization, and unified architecture enabling simultaneous deployment, this represents potential incremental revenue requiring no new customer acquisition.

**Q1 2026 Highlights:**

- RPO: $2.35B, up 24% YoY

**Historical Context:**
MANH has historically traded at 35-55x forward adjusted earnings during its cloud transition period. At 25.7x forward adjusted, the stock is trading at a significant discount to its own history, reflecting macro-driven multiple compression rather than fundamental deterioration.

**EV/FCF as a Cross-Check:**
Because stock-based compensation is a non-cash expense, EV/FCF provides a useful alternative valuation lens. At ~22x EV/FCF, MANH is attractively valued for a business with its growth profile, margin structure, and balance sheet quality, regardless of the GAAP vs. non-GAAP EPS debate.

**Valuation conclusion:** The most compelling valuation argument rests on EV/FCF (~22x) rather than P/E, given the significance of stock-based compensation. On a GAAP P/E basis (~38x forward), the stock is not obviously cheap. However, the combination of zero debt, 35% FCF margins, 21% cloud growth, 25% RPO growth, and AI monetization optionality provides a quality premium that supports the current level with upside as growth re-accelerates.

## Competitive Landscape

Manhattan Associates operates in a market with well-funded competitors (Oracle, SAP, Blue Yonder/Panasonic, Infor) but maintains meaningful competitive advantages:

1. **Cloud-native architecture:** Unlike ERP vendors offering bolt-on supply chain modules migrated from on-premise, Manhattan Active® was built cloud-native. The versionless architecture delivers quarterly updates simultaneously to all customers — a substantial advantage over competitors requiring expensive upgrade projects.

2. **Domain specialization:** While Oracle/SAP offer broad ERP functionality, Manhattan specializes exclusively in supply chain execution, omnichannel commerce, and planning. This specialization translates to >70% win rates per management commentary and Gartner Magic Quadrant leadership.

3. **Switching costs:** Multi-year contracts (5+ year terms), deep system integration with customer operations, and implementation complexity create substantial switching barriers.

4. **ERP modernization tailwind:** When enterprises upgrade their SAP S/4HANA or Oracle Cloud ERP, they frequently evaluate best-of-breed supply chain solutions rather than generic ERP modules. This creates a persistent pipeline of opportunities.

5. **Google Cloud partnership:** Exclusive GCP deployment for Manhattan Active allows customers to retire committed GCP spend through the Marketplace, simplifying procurement and expanding Manhattan's addressable market.

**Competitive Position Trajectory:** Improving. Strong net-new logo acquisition, record OMS deal activity per management commentary, and AI-first product strategy all indicate strengthening competitive position. No evidence of share erosion.

## Risk Assessment

**Securities Class Action Lawsuits (Medium Risk):**
Two putative securities class actions (February/April 2025) and a shareholder derivative suit (September 2025). The lawsuits allege violations of Sections 10(b) and 20(a) of the Exchange Act and Rule 10b-5, based on purported materially false and misleading statements and omissions during specified class periods. The derivative action asserts claims including alleged federal securities-law violations, breach of fiduciary duty, waste, and unjust enrichment. The company denies all allegations and states it is unable to predict the ultimate timing or outcome, or reasonably estimate possible losses. While such lawsuits frequently follow periods of significant stock underperformance and often resolve without material impact, the allegations are substantive and warrant monitoring.

**CEO Transition (Diminishing Risk):**
Eric Clark became President & CEO effective February 12, 2025. Eddie Capel transitioned to Executive Vice-Chairman, then Executive Chairman (May 2025), and subsequently to non-executive Chairman / non-employee status effective January 1, 2026. Clark's first full quarter produced record results and a guidance raise, suggesting confidence building. CFO transition (Dennis Story retired effective March 31, 2026; Linda Pinne appointed CFO, CAO & Treasurer effective April 1, 2026) was an orderly internal succession.

**Services Revenue Cyclicality (Medium Risk):**
Services represent 47% of revenue and declined in FY2025 due to "customer budgetary constraints." In a macro environment with elevated rates, stagflationary pressures, and enterprise IT budget scrutiny, implementation services remain vulnerable to deferrals. However, management guides 3% recovery for FY2026, and strong cloud bookings imply future services demand.

**Macro/Spending Environment (Elevated Risk):**
Enterprise software spending is under pressure from budget constraint and economic uncertainty. Extended sales cycles could slow cloud bookings growth. However, supply chain software is relatively resilient because it directly drives operational efficiency and cost reduction — "must-have" rather than "nice-to-have."

**Single Cloud Vendor Dependency (Manhattan Active Platform):**
The Manhattan Active Platform runs exclusively on Google Cloud Platform, with $202.7M in contractual cloud infrastructure obligations over five years. Any service disruption on GCP could impact all Manhattan Active customers simultaneously. The SCALE product is delivered on Microsoft Azure and on-premise, providing some diversification at the company level though not for the strategic growth platform.

**Stock-Based Compensation (Structural Consideration):**
FY2025 stock-based compensation of $111.3M represents approximately 51% of GAAP net income. While non-cash, this is a real economic cost that dilutes shareholders and creates a persistent gap between GAAP and adjusted earnings. Investors relying on adjusted EPS for valuation should recognize this as a recurring expense, not a one-time adjustment.

**Options Market Signal:**
The options market is pricing elevated near-term risk: near-term ATM IV of approximately 50.8% vs. 36.5% historical volatility, with term structure in backwardation. Elevated put demand at this price level, combined with the stock already being 44% off highs, may reflect hedging by holders rather than directional positioning.

## Conclusion

Manhattan Associates represents a high-quality compounder caught in a broad software drawdown, trading at a significant discount to its historical multiple. The fundamental picture — zero debt, 35% FCF margins, 21% cloud growth, 25% RPO growth, consecutive earnings beats, and AI monetization optionality — contradicts the 44% price decline. The revenue mix shift thesis (headline deceleration masking accelerating cloud momentum) creates an identifiable gap between the market's perception and the underlying business trajectory, though the pace of total revenue re-acceleration depends on services recovery and is not purely mechanical. At ~22x EV/FCF with multiple catalysts ahead and a demonstrated beat-and-raise pattern, the risk/reward favors accumulation.

The key risk is macro-driven deceleration in enterprise software spending that extends sales cycles and slows cloud bookings. The options market confirms elevated near-term uncertainty. Position sizing should reflect this: size at 2-3% given the macro sensitivity of enterprise software spending, with a willingness to add on evidence of sustained bookings strength in subsequent quarters.

**Recommendation: BUY at $138, targeting $165-175 over 12-15 months.**
