## Executive Summary

USA Rare Earth is a 17-month-old public company assembling a non-China rare earth value chain through acquisition. It generated its first revenue in history in 2025 ($1.643M), reported $11.519M in H1 2026, and carries a $4.67B equity value. The gap between those numbers is filled entirely by forward projections attached to assets the company either does not yet own (Serra Verde's Pela Ema mine, pending an 8/28/2026 shareholder vote), has not yet quantified (Round Top, which the FY2025 10-K states has **no declared mineral resources under Item 1300 of Regulation S-K**), or has not yet sold anything from (Stillwater magnets, where the 10-K disclosed "no definitive off-take or sales agreements with customers in place" and subsequent releases reference only a "growing pipeline").

The central number in the thesis is management's projection of **$550-650M of annualized run-rate EBITDA from Serra Verde by end-2027**. The footnotes to the 4/20/2026 announcement disclose that this assumes ~6,400 t TREO nameplate achieved by end-2027 (not current production), ~4,400 t after lanthanum removal (31% of nameplate stripped out), an illustrative ~$190/kg basket, BRL/USD of 5.91, and "**on the basis of 100% separated oxide sales**." Serra Verde produces mixed rare earth carbonate. The same release lists a "dedicated third-party MREC separation line" and access to Carester's separation IP as *future* opportunities. The headline 4.7x purchase-price-to-2027E-EBITDA multiple therefore prices a separation capability that does not yet exist inside the combined group, at a basket price set by Argus projections from December 2025.

The only operating business deteriorated. Derived from the Q2 10-Q, LCM's gross margin went from **+1.9% in Q1'26 to -27.2% in Q2'26** ($5,821K revenue against $7,404K cost of product revenue), unexplained in the filings, against a newly disclosed risk that USAR's "designation on an export control list by China... **has had** and is expected to continue to have an adverse impact on our ability to source key raw materials." H1 operating expense of $78.8M ran 6.8x revenue. Q2 operating cash outflow accelerated to $(56.9)M from $(18.4)M in Q1. The Q2 10-Q discloses tightened covenants requiring roughly **$1.567B of new equity by 12/31/2027** ($375M plus Serra Verde cash costs by 3/31/2027, plus $875M additional), a contractual obligation regardless of market conditions. The stock's 57% decline from the October 2025 high of $43.98 and 32% decline from the 6/3/2026 award-date close of $27.98 reflect identifiable developments: the tightened covenants, the imminent 34.1% ownership dilution, the Q2 revenue and margin miss, the China designation, and the CEO change, rather than sentiment drift.

## Company Overview

USAR went public via reverse merger with Inflection Point Acquisition Corp. II in March 2025. Four asset pillars:

**Less Common Metals (Cheshire, UK)** — acquired 11/18/2025; ~2,500 MTPA nameplate for rare earth metals, alloys and strip-cast material; the source of 100% of reported revenue. On 4/15/2026 LCM completed a first commercial pour of 2N-2N5 yttrium metal, placing it among few non-China commercial yttrium producers. Hooton Park land and buildings were purchased 7/2/2026 for £7.0M (~$9.3M), with management stating certain environmental liabilities are expected to be recognized, amount and timing "subject to further analysis."

**Stillwater, Oklahoma** — Phase 1a commissioned Q1 2026 at ~600 MTPA. The 2026 objective is to "reach 600 MTPA of run-rate magnet manufacturing *capacity*" in Q4 2026. That is a capacity milestone. No magnet revenue appears in the Q2 revenue disaggregation, which attributes all revenue to LCM casting and strip-casting.

**Round Top, Texas** — heavy rare earth deposit, 100% owned following the 8/7/2026 TMRC acquisition (~3.8M shares, ~$73.9M). Carried at $17.339M of mineral interests, unchanged since 12/31/2025, with no depletion. No declared resource; DFS targeted for Q4 2026 completion and Q1 2027 publication against a stated >$1.4B expected capital requirement.

**Serra Verde / Pela Ema, Brazil (pending)** — $300M cash plus 126.849M shares. At the 4/17/2026 reference price of $19.95 that valued the deal at ~$2.83B; at $19.13 the share leg is worth $2.43B, so total consideration is now ~$2.73B plus ~$16.6M of employer payroll taxes. Only USAR's shareholder vote on the issuance (8/28/2026) and regulatory approvals remain outstanding; Serra Verde holders approved by written consent at signing.

Supporting assets: a Wheat Ridge, Colorado hydrometallurgical demonstration facility (operations commenced 6/15/2026; produced commercial-grade Dy oxide and NdPr oxide from Stillwater magnet swarf on 7/14/2026); an announced Blacksburg, South Carolina plant with commissioning targeted to *begin* in 2028; and a pending 13.6% stake in French separator Carester SAS for €40.0M.

**Gross margin.** -27.2% in Q2 versus +1.9% in Q1, derived by subtraction. The filings do not explain the swing. Candidate causes: first-pour yttrium campaign yields, absorption of pre-production cost, raw-material cost pass-through timing, or restricted Chinese feedstock access following the export-control designation. At $5.8M of quarterly revenue a single low-margin contract or inventory charge could produce this, so it is not yet established as a durable trend. It is the wrong direction in the one business that sells product.

**GAAP earnings are not usable.** Q2's headline net loss of $(12.0)M includes a net fair-value *gain* of +$22.4M (a +$107.4M gain on the Government Grant Warrant less $(70.7)M of earnout loss and $(14.2)M of Series A warrant loss) plus $14.6M of money-market dividend income. FY2025's $(298.5)M loss included ~$244.5M of non-cash remeasurement against a $59.5M operating loss. On a like-for-like non-GAAP basis, Q2 adjusted EPS of $(0.15) missed the $(0.13) consensus and revenue of $5.82M missed ~$6.45M (both consensus figures from third-party aggregators, not independently verified). Three of the last four quarters were misses.

**Balance sheet composition matters more than its size.** At 6/30/2026: cash $1,530.1M plus restricted cash $64.2M; inventories $50.1M; PP&E net $146.8M; mineral interests $17.3M; goodwill and other intangibles $200.7M; and **deferred arrangement costs of $912.1M**. Total assets $2,998.8M, equity $2,537.0M. Cash is 53.2% of assets, deferred financing costs 30.4%, and **PP&E plus mineral interests only 5.5%**. Book equity of $2.54B supports a $4.67B market capitalization (1.84x book) with almost no productive-asset backing behind it.

Drawn debt is effectively zero. The $364.2M "warrant and earnout liabilities" line is fair value on equity-linked instruments (Government Grant Warrant $323.5M, Series A Warrant $40.7M). The Government Grant Warrant is a liability specifically because Commerce can elect cash redemption on a business combination, a conditional cash claim ahead of common in any change of control and a permanent source of quarterly earnings noise.

**Burn and liquidity.** H1 operating outflow $(75.3)M; capex and equipment deposits $(108.4)M; combined $(183.7)M. Simple annualization gives ~$367M/year, presented as a sensitivity rather than a forecast, and more likely to understate than overstate: capex is ramping into Blacksburg, Stillwater Phase 1b and Round Top; ~$316.6M of Serra Verde cash costs and ~$32.4M for Carester are committed but unpaid; and a 2% annual ticking fee on $1.3B of undrawn loan commitment accrues at ~$26M/year. Adjusting total cash of $1,594.4M for those commitments leaves ~$1,245M of uncommitted liquidity, $64.2M of which is escrowed for Blacksburg. Twelve months of runway is credible. The problem is dated: the 10-Q states plainly that "we will be required to raise a significant amount of capital during 2027."

**Financial Health score: 4.2.** A $1.59B cash pile with no drawn debt is genuine resilience, and access to 15-year Treasury+150bps money is a structural advantage no private-market borrower with these cash flows could obtain. Against that: negative and deteriorating gross margin, accelerating operating burn, opex at 6.8x revenue, 30.4% of assets consisting of capitalized financing cost, prime money-market credit exposure ($728.5M in floating-NAV prime funds), and a contractual $1.567B equity requirement into an unknown 2027 market.

## Growth Analysis

The growth optionality is real and large. Revenue went from zero to $11.5M in a half-year; Serra Verde would convert USAR into an operating producer of all four magnetic rare earths plus yttrium; the Wheat Ridge recycling flowsheet could, per management, support up to 30% of future magnetic REO feedstock; and the sponsorship stack is unusually concrete — $277M of CHIPS Act direct funding, a $1.30B Federal Financing Bank loan guarantee, Serra Verde's $565M DFC package, a 15-year offtake in which Serra Verde **supplies** 100% of Phase 1 Nd/Pr/Dy/Tb to a USG-capitalized SPV **buyer** with guaranteed minimum floor prices, a $14.2M Texas state grant, a $19.3M DOE selection (not yet a binding award), and French state co-investment alongside Carester.

What has not converted: no magnet revenue, no binding magnet offtake, no declared Round Top resource, no positive gross margin, and no revenue or EBITDA guidance for 2026 — the 2026 outlook is entirely milestone-based (DFS completion, 600 MTPA capacity, LCM capacity evaluation).

Consensus is moving against the growth case at the point that matters. Current-year EPS improved to $(0.38) from $(0.49) ninety days ago, but **next-year consensus went from +$0.03 to $(0.51)** — the sell side abandoned 2027 profitability during the same window in which Serra Verde was announced. Next-quarter estimates fell 38.9% over 90 days. The estimate-revision pattern that historically precedes re-rating (rising forward EPS on cheap forward multiples) is not present here; the out-year revision is a swing from profit to loss.

Contract liabilities should not be mistaken for demand: $10.930M at 6/30/2026 versus $10.500M at 12/31/2025, with only $798K of the opening balance recognized in H1 and the balance now almost entirely non-current. The company discloses no backlog or remaining performance obligations.

**Growth Prospects score: 6.0.** Credit for scale of opportunity, a real structural dislocation, and government-funded optionality; discount for zero conversion to profitable volume, negative out-year revisions, and the fact that essentially all near-term growth arrives via an acquisition that has not closed.

## Valuation Assessment

Conventional multiples are uninformative: EV of ~$3.07B ($4.67B market cap less $1.594B cash) against H1 revenue of $11.5M is ~133x EV/sales on a doubled-H1 basis, with negative gross margin. The question is what the price implies about Serra Verde and the 2030 aspiration.

**Pro forma arithmetic.** Post-vote share count: 244.671M + 3.8M (TMRC, closed) + 126.849M = **375.3M shares**, or $7.18B of market value at $19.13. Uncommitted cash of ~$1,245M less assumed Serra Verde debt (unquantified; the "Retained Finance Agreement" is plausibly the $565M DFC facility, unconfirmed) puts pro forma EV in a **$6.3-6.6B** range. Against the $600M midpoint of the 2027 target, that is roughly **10.5-11x 2027E EBITDA** — before the required $1.567B raise, which at $19.13 would add ~81.9M shares and take the count to ~457M and market value to ~$8.75B.

Working the other direction: to justify $19.13 fully diluted for the mandated raise, and assuming $1.5-2.0B of net debt by 2028 (DFC plus drawn FFB), you need roughly **17-18x** on $600M of Serra Verde EBITDA, or you need meaningful credit for the **~$1.8B combined 2030 EBITDA** aspiration at ~6x. The 2030 figure depends on Round Top (no declared resource), Blacksburg (commissioning begins 2028), a full Stillwater ramp, and a ~$4.1B capex program, a stack of individually uncertain conditions.

**The 2027 target's own sensitivity.** 4,400,000 kg × $190/kg = $836M implied revenue against $550-650M EBITDA, a 65.8-77.8% margin and an implied all-in cash cost of ~$53.6/kg. Holding that ~$236M cost base fixed: at $150/kg, EBITDA falls to ~$424M (-29%); at $250/kg it rises to ~$864M. The undisclosed offtake floors mitigate the downside by an unknown amount. The target is stated on 100% separated-oxide realization, which the company's own release frames as a future capability.

**Sell-side targets are not independent confirmation here.** Eight analysts, all Buy or Strong Buy, mean target $37.50 (low $30, high $45) — roughly 2x the current price on a company with negative earnings, negative gross margin, and no published resource or DFS. Those targets capitalize the same 2030 projection the stock is capitalizing, on assumptions about dilution and multiple that I cannot reproduce from the filings. On a pre-profit asset story, target proximity carries no information about mispricing.

The stock also sits **11% below the $21.50 gross PIPE price** (January 2026, $1.50B / 69.767M shares) and 32% below the $27.98 price at which the government's 16.13M shares were valued on 6/3/2026.

**Valuation score: 3.2.** No identifiable margin of safety. The price requires either a premium multiple on a conditional 2027 target or substantial credit for a 2030 aspiration resting on unquantified assets, while a mandated $1.567B raise sits between here and there.

## Competitive Landscape

The strategic dislocation is real: China dominates separation, metallization and magnet-making, and Western governments are paying to build alternatives. USAR's verified differentiators are LCM's operating non-China metal and alloy capability (including commercial yttrium metal), the demonstrated recycling flowsheet at Wheat Ridge, and the breadth of governmental sponsorship, which is genuinely difficult for competitors to replicate.

Three offsets are specific and current:

**China export-control designation.** The 8/10/2026 Q2 release risk list states USAR's "designation on an export control list by China... **has had** and is expected to continue to have an adverse impact on our ability to source key raw materials and supplies from China." For a business that converts purchased feedstock into metals and alloys, that is a direct operational constraint and a candidate explanation for the Q2 margin reversal. The designation's date, scope and quantified impact are established by nothing beyond the company's own risk list.

**MP Materials litigation.** On 5/22/2026 MP Materials, MP Magnetics and MP Mine Operations sued USAR, its Director of Magnet Operations, and FOM Technologies in the Business Court of Texas (Case No. 26-BC08A-0018) for trade-secret misappropriation, breach of contract, tortious interference and unjust enrichment, seeking **temporary and permanent injunctive relief**. No loss accrual is disclosed. The exposure is not damages; an injunction restricting magnet-production technology or personnel at Stillwater would directly threaten the milestones on which government disbursements are conditioned. An allegation is not a finding, and litigation against a fast-scaling rival is a recognized competitive tactic; both readings remain live.

**Customer concentration has not improved.** Q2'26: four customers at 26%, 29%, 23% and 18% = **96% of revenue**; H1: 95%; FY2025 stub: two customers at 91%. The number of >10% customers rose from two to four while top-tier dependence was unchanged. Roughly 76% of revenue is non-U.S. (Europe $3,982K, U.S. $1,742K, Asia $97K in Q2), notwithstanding the domestic-supply-chain framing.

Both risk lists cite "dumping, predatory pricing and other tactics by the Company's competitors **or state actors**," the mechanism by which Western rare earth entrants have historically failed, and the reason the undisclosed offtake floor levels carry so much weight.

**Competitive Position score: 5.0.** Improving via acquisition and government sponsorship; impaired concurrently by feedstock restriction, injunction risk, and unchanged concentration.

## Risk Assessment

Ranked by decision relevance:

1. **8/28/2026 shareholder vote on the Serra Verde share issuance.** Approval brings 34.1% ownership dilution (existing holders retain 244.671/371.520 = 65.9%; the share count rises 51.8%). Failure removes the entire near-term EBITDA source and, per the company's own disclosure, leaves USAR without a designated CEO from 10/1/2026 because the Moraitis succession "is contingent on the timely closing of the Serra Verde acquisition."
2. **The 2027 equity covenant.** ~$1.567B by 12/31/2027 ($375M plus Serra Verde cash costs by 3/31/2027; $875M additional), plus a ≤$250M revolver by 6/30/2027 with cash collateral to be equity-funded. Up to $300M may be substituted with convertible notes at the company's option. Dilution magnitude is a function of the share price at issuance, while the obligation is fixed.
3. **Government funding is conditional and undisbursed.** USAR issued 16,132,790 shares ($451.4M at the $27.98 award-date close) plus a 10-year warrant on 17,600,584 shares at a $17.17 strike ($430.9M initial fair value on 79.4% assumed volatility), totaling $882.3M, described by the 10-Q as the "total cost incurred to obtain access." Add a $26.0M upfront fee to reach the $912.1M deferred asset. **As of 6/30/2026 there had been no direct funding disbursements, no formal approval of any disbursement request, and no loan advances.** The FY2025 10-K discloses the government retains the securities whether or not funding is provided. The cost of access equals 55.9% of the $1,577M maximum facility and 1.63x the $277M grant. In fairness to the other side: full warrant exercise would deliver $302.2M of cash to USAR, the shares are locked up a year with voting rights restricted, and Treasury+150bps for 15 years is unobtainable privately for this credit. Attached restrictions include no dividends or repurchases for five years, minimum liquidity, additional-indebtedness limits, clawback on milestone failure, and cross-default risk across financing arrangements.
4. **Round Top has no declared mineral resource** under Item 1300, carried at $17.3M against >$1.4B of expected capital investment. Any value ascribed to Round Top today is value ascribed to an unquantified orebody. DFS publication targeted Q1 2027.
5. **Serra Verde execution and the separation gap**, plus Brazil political/tax/currency exposure (projection assumes BRL 5.91) and assumption of Retained Finance Agreement debt with restrictive covenants. **Serra Verde's audited historical financials, current production tonnage and realized pricing were not located in any source — the largest unverified input in the thesis.**
6. **No committed magnet offtake.** Both April and August 2026 risk lists retain the need to convert MOUs "into definitive orders."
7. **Governance and control.** Six 8-K Item 5.02 officer/director change filings between 7/9/2025 and 7/20/2026; two auditor changes (4/25/2025, 11/6/2025) whose underlying disclosures were not read; smaller-reporting and emerging-growth status exempting the company from SOX 404(b) auditor attestation of internal control. An unquantified Hooton Park environmental liability is expected to be recognized.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 102.2% sits below 113.9% one-year realized volatility — options are modestly cheap versus realized, unusual for a name with a dated binary two weeks out. The term structure is in backwardation (102.2% near versus 94.1% at six months), and the 16-day tenor expiring 8/28/2026 prices a ±16.1% move, bracketing the shareholder vote. Put/call skew is balanced (-1.4% near-term; the 3m and 6m tenors read -4.2% and -5.9%), and put/call volume is call-tilted at 0.43. The most informative change is structural: put/call open interest rose from 0.50 to 0.92 while the stock fell 31.9% from $27.98 between 6/3 and 8/11, so protection was accumulated into the decline rather than after it. Short interest is 15.1% of float at 2.4 days to cover — high in absolute terms but easily covered, which limits squeeze mechanics from float scarcity while leaving policy-headline gap risk fully intact. The market prices a large, near-dated, two-sided event, with no directional conviction that would either confirm or contradict the fundamental case.

**Risk Factors score: 2.8.** Multiple dated binaries, an injunction claim with no accrual, a contractual capital requirement, and a core acquisition whose historical financials are unverifiable from public sources.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $34 (+78%)

The 8/28 vote passes; Serra Verde ramps to nameplate on schedule; separation access via Carester or third-party tolling materializes so that realization approaches the separated-oxide basket; the USG-SPV floors hold realized pricing near or above the basket; Commerce milestones are met and the grant plus FFB draws fund Stillwater Phase 1b and Blacksburg without punitive equity; the Q1 2027 Round Top DFS declares a resource with credible economics; Xstrata alumni Moraitis and Davis convert a project company into an operating miner; and the 2027 equity requirement is met at higher prices. On roughly $700M of 2028 combined EBITDA at 15x against ~460M shares and ~$1.8B of net debt, the equity supports the mid-$30s. Chairman Blitzer's 100,000-share open-market purchase at $21.44 on 1/29/2026 ($2.14M) is the one insider transaction consistent with this case.

### Bear Case — $8.00 (-58%)

Either the vote fails — removing the near-term EBITDA source and the designated CEO — or the ramp disappoints: separation access lags, MREC realization runs materially below the separated basket, the basket price falls toward $150/kg, and 2028 EBITDA lands near $300-400M. The 2027 covenant then forces $1.567B of issuance into a falling market, and the MP Materials injunction or continued Chinese feedstock restriction delays the magnet milestones that gate government disbursement, triggering clawback and cross-default risk. LCM's negative gross margin persists. In that state the equity is a $3B+ EV claim on a single Brazilian mine, an undeclared Texas orebody, and $912.1M of sunk financing cost, and it trades below the $11.45 52-week low on a per-share basis after dilution.

### Base Case — $14.50 (-24%)

The vote passes, Serra Verde closes, and the ramp lands late and below target — call it $400-450M of EBITDA by 2028 on partial separated-oxide realization. The 2027 requirement is met at prices near current levels, taking the count to ~457-480M shares. At 12-13x $425M less ~$1.8B of net debt, per-share value sits in the mid-teens.

Probability-weighting bull 25% / base 45% / bear 30% gives ~$17.40, roughly -9% against $19.13, with a 30% branch losing more than half. That distribution does not support ownership.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst.** Two dated events: the **8/28/2026 special meeting** on the Serra Verde share issuance, and the **Q1 2027 Round Top DFS publication**. A third, undated but live, is any ruling on MP Materials' request for temporary injunctive relief. The 8/28 vote is the only near-dated catalyst, and its directional outcome for the share price is genuinely ambiguous: approval delivers the 34.1% dilution, which the market may read as either dilutive or de-risking, since it also secures the only near-term EBITDA source and the CEO succession.

**Timing window.** 17 days to the vote; ~5-7 months to the DFS. The 2027 equity raise — the most reliably negative event — is 8-16 months out, well beyond a reasonable carry window.

**Short interest and squeeze risk.** 15.1% of float short, 28.9M shares, 2.4 days to cover. Not crowded on days-to-cover, but the price history is disqualifying for a short: +47% in six sessions in October 2025, +$4.01 (17%) on 1/26/2026 on 122.6M shares, +32% in the eight sessions to 8/7/2026. Rare earth policy headlines and government-support announcements repeatedly gap this name double digits. Institutional ownership includes Artisan Small Cap.

**Borrow and options signal.** Options do not corroborate a bearish thesis. Near-term skew is balanced at -1.4%, put/call volume is 0.43 (call-tilted), and IV at 102.2% is *below* 113.9% realized. Longer tenors show slight negative skew readings whose directional interpretation is not established by the data convention. Elevated absolute IV across the curve makes put-based expression expensive relative to the move required.

**Downside target and risk/reward.** Bear case $8.00 (-58%) against a bull case $34 (+78%) on a name that has twice moved 30%+ in weeks on policy news. Reward:risk is worse than 1:1 on any position sized to survive a headline gap.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The bearish valuation case is sound, but the near-dated catalyst has ambiguous sign, the durable negative catalyst (the 2027 raise) is too distant to carry at 102% IV, and the gap risk from government announcements is severe. Avoid owning; do not short.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria** — any two of the following would move this to HOLD, and three or more with a price below $12 would justify ACCUMULATE:

1. **Verified Serra Verde economics.** Audited historical revenue, EBITDA, current production tonnage and realized pricing in the merger proxy, plus disclosure of contracted separation capacity (owned or tolled) sufficient to support separated-oxide realization on Phase 1 volumes. The offtake floor levels for Nd, Pr, Dy and Tb are the single most valuable undisclosed data point.
2. **Positive gross margin at LCM for two consecutive quarters**, with disclosure of why Q2'26 ran -27.2%, and quantification of the China export-control designation's scope and impact.
3. **First government disbursement received** — an approved CHIPS direct-funding drawdown or an FFB advance, converting the $912.1M of deferred arrangement cost from optionality into cash.
4. **A binding magnet offtake or definitive sales agreement** at Stillwater, replacing the "growing pipeline" language.
5. **Round Top DFS published (Q1 2027) with a declared Item 1300 resource** and stated NPV/IRR at disclosed price decks.
6. **Resolution of MP Materials' injunction request** without restriction on Stillwater technology or personnel.
7. **The 2027 equity requirement funded** at or above $19, or substantially reduced through the $300M convertible substitution plus FFB draws.

**Escalation to a harder AVOID / short reassessment** would follow: an injunction restricting magnet operations; disclosure that the offtake agreement's conditions precedent were not satisfied or that it was terminated; a milestone failure triggering clawback or cross-default; equity issued below $12; or a third consecutive quarter of negative LCM gross margin.

**Expected duration of headwinds.** Through at least Q1 2027. The two events capable of materially changing the analytical picture — the merger proxy's audited Serra Verde financials and the Round Top DFS — fall between late August 2026 and Q1 2027. **Next natural reassessment: immediately after the 8/28/2026 vote and the associated proxy disclosure.**

## Conclusion

USAR is a credible attempt at a genuinely important industrial objective, backed by the most concrete government sponsorship stack in the sector, and it holds $1.59B of cash with no drawn debt. None of that resolves the valuation problem. At $19.13 the market pays $4.67B for a business that produced $11.5M of revenue at negative gross margin in H1 2026, whose 5.5%-of-assets productive base sits behind 53% cash and 30% capitalized financing cost, and whose forward case rests on a $550-650M EBITDA projection explicitly conditioned on separated-oxide sales the combined company cannot yet deliver, drawn from an asset whose audited historicals are not publicly verifiable.

Fully diluted for the 126.849M Serra Verde shares and the ~82M shares implied by the mandated $1.567B raise, the current price requires roughly 17-18x the 2027 Serra Verde target or material credit for a 2030 aspiration built on an undeclared orebody and a plant that begins commissioning in 2028. Probability-weighted return is modestly negative with a 30% branch losing more than half. The correct action is to wait for the proxy's audited target financials, the first actual government disbursement, and evidence that LCM can sell product at a positive margin. **AVOID**, and not shortable at 102% implied volatility with policy-headline gap risk in both directions.
