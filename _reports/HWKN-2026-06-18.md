**Research Date: June 18, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Hawkins is a Roseville, MN specialty-chemicals consolidator that crossed $1.0B in revenue for the first time in FY2026 and has paid a dividend for 41 consecutive years. The business has shifted its mix toward higher-margin Water Treatment (now 50% of sales and 56% of operating income at a 27% gross margin), funded by an aggressive M&A program: 17 acquisitions in five years, six in FY2026 alone, headlined by the $149.9M WaterSurplus deal. The Water Treatment segment is structurally attractive: non-cyclical municipal demand, sticky high-touch route-based service, and recurring chemical consumption.

The central tension is that GAAP EPS fell 3% to $3.91 in FY2026 despite 11% revenue growth and record gross profit and operating income, and the stock trades at roughly 41x trailing / ~33x forward GAAP earnings. The EPS decline is fully attributable to the acquisition spree: about $16.5M of incremental amortization, interest, and earnout accretion. Management leans on pro forma EPS of $3.95 (+9%) and Adjusted EBITDA of $179M (+6%) to carry the bull case, while the headline GAAP number went backward. Hawkins has also missed consensus three of the last four quarters (Q4 FY2026 EPS $0.74 vs. $0.78; Q3 $0.69 vs. $0.76; Q2 $1.08 vs. $1.24).

I maintain a HOLD with a growth-hold rationale. The thesis that justifies holding over 12 months is specific: consensus EPS estimates have been revised up roughly +15% across all forward periods over the last 90 days (current year $4.17 → $4.82, next year $4.70 → $5.39), and management has guided FY2027 to grow operating income in all three segments while deleveraging to ~1x EBITDA from 1.37x. That combination of rising estimates plus a deleveraging year that mechanically reduces the interest/amortization drag supports modest appreciation. The premium multiple on a Materials-sector commodity-chemicals name, the unproven WaterSurplus integration ($1.8M operating loss in its first nine months), and a regulatory fluoride tail keep this short of a BUY.

## Company Overview

Founded in 1938, Hawkins formulates, manufactures, distributes, and blends chemicals across three recast segments:

- **Water Treatment** — FY2026 sales $543.3M (+22% YoY), 27% gross margin, 56% of operating income. Delivers chemicals, filtration media/systems, equipment, and services for potable water, municipal/industrial wastewater, and non-residential pools through ~53 warehouses across 28 states and a fleet of ~400 commercial vehicles whose route drivers double as salespeople and technicians. This is the growth engine and the focus of nearly all M&A.
- **Food & Health Sciences** — FY2026 sales $320.7M (-1% YoY), 21% gross margin. Ingredient distribution and formulation for nutrition, food, pharma, and agriculture. This segment is the laggard, facing competitive pricing pressure and soft volumes; segment operating income fell from $41.2M to $34.4M.
- **Industrial Solutions** — FY2026 sales $219.7M (+7% YoY), 15% gross margin, the lowest of the three. Bulk acids and alkalis to manufacturing, electronics, energy, and plating; ~21% of sales are pure commodity passthroughs.

No customer exceeds 10% of sales, a genuinely low concentration. The competitive edge is local service density, multi-segment supplier buying power, and value-added formulation in fragmented regional markets where no single competitor dominates.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and profitability (fiscal years ending late March):**

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $935M | $919M | $974M | $1,084M |
| Gross profit | $165M | $194M | $226M | $245M |
| Operating income | $88M | $104M | $119M | $121M |
| Net income | $60M | $75M | $84M | $81.5M |
| Diluted EPS | — | — | $4.03 | $3.91 |

EPS declined 3% in FY2026 on 11% revenue growth and record operating income because of the cost structure of the acquisition program: ~$16.5M of incremental amortization, interest, and earnout fair-value accretion from six FY2026 deals. Operating margin compressed from 12.2% to 11.2% and net margin from 8.7% to 7.5%, driven by amortization and interest rather than operational deterioration; gross margin held near 23%. Hawkins uses LIFO, and a $1.5M FY2026 reserve build on rising raw-material costs was a modest gross-profit headwind versus a prior-year tailwind.

Cash generation is the strongest part of the story. Operating cash flow was $144.3M (+30% YoY), comfortably above net income, a high-quality-earnings signal. FCF was ~$86M (7.9% margin) after $58.2M capex elevated by facility expansion; FY2027 capex is guided to ~$55M, which should lift free cash flow.

On the balance sheet, long-term debt rose from $138.9M to $244M to fund deals, and the revolver was upsized to $400M. Net leverage rose from 0.86x to 1.37x pro forma Adjusted EBITDA, with an effective interest rate of ~4.4-4.8% and a $60M notional swap hedging part of the variable exposure (maturing May 2027). Interest expense more than doubled to $13.5M. Covenants (max 3.5x leverage, 4.0x post-acquisition election; min 1.15x fixed-charge coverage) leave ample headroom. Altman Z is 3.13 (safe zone), current ratio 2.21, D/E 0.85, ROE ~16%, ROA 8.3%. Management is prioritizing M&A and debt paydown over buybacks (no repurchases in FY2026 versus $20.7M in FY2025) and guides to ~1x leverage by FY2027-end, implying a slower deal pace.

## Growth Analysis

Estimate revisions are the most important forward signal here, and they are clearly positive. Over the last 90 days, consensus has moved up ~+15% across every forward period: current quarter $1.33 → $1.53, next quarter $1.20 → $1.36, current year $4.17 → $4.82, next year $4.70 → $5.39. The 30-day and 90-day revisions are identical, meaning the upgrade came as a single step rather than a steady acceleration, but the direction is unambiguous.

I weight this signal but temper it against two facts. First, this is not a classic inflection setup: revenue is growing in the low-teens, not >40%, and GAAP EPS fell. The strongest interpretation of the estimate-revision rule applies to depressed-earnings inflection stocks at sub-30x forward multiples; HWKN is a steady compounder at ~33x forward. Second, the underlying organic growth is far softer than the headline. Of the $96.8M Water Treatment revenue increase, $83.3M came from acquisitions and only ~$13.5M was organic, while Food & Health Sciences is shrinking. The growth machine is the roll-up, not the base business.

The forward case rests on: (1) the FY2027 guide for operating-income growth in all three segments plus EPS growth, (2) the mechanical relief as deleveraging trims the interest/amortization drag that suppressed FY2026 GAAP EPS, and (3) continued tuck-in M&A in a fragmented water-treatment market. WaterSurplus completes a "full product portfolio" (chemicals + media/filtration + equipment) for water customers, expanding cross-sell potential. The durability of the growth depends on integration converting to organic momentum rather than serial dealmaking masking flat underlying volumes.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$159.93 against $3.91 trailing GAAP EPS, HWKN trades at ~41x trailing and ~33x the $4.82 current-year consensus. Against Materials peers (Avient 21.3x, H.B. Fuller 22.0x, Scotts Miracle-Gro 17.7x, Graphic Packaging 11.6x, peer median 21.3x) Hawkins commands a ~55-90% premium. The dividend yield of ~0.47% offers no valuation support; peer median yield is 3.1%.

The premium is partly justified by the higher-quality mix shift toward recurring water-treatment revenue, low customer concentration, strong cash conversion, and a 41-year dividend record. A ~33x forward multiple on a commodity-chemicals consolidator growing GAAP EPS in the low-single-digits is hard to defend as offering asymmetric upside today. The earnings-quality issues compound the concern: earnout fair-value remeasurement (~$50.8M of liabilities) swings results both ways through SG&A, and the bull case relies on pro forma/Adjusted EBITDA framing while GAAP EPS declined.

Analyst targets (low $175, mean $189, median $193, high $200) sit 10-25% above the current price, which would normally argue the stock is cheap to consensus. I discount this on a premium-multiple name: at ~33x forward, the targets and the price likely reflect the same roll-up extrapolation, so they are not independent confirmation. A low valuation score is warranted not because the business is impaired but because the multiple is stretched relative to the GAAP growth it delivers, with the deleveraging EPS recovery as the only near-term catalyst to grow into it.

## Competitive Landscape

Hawkins is a consolidator in fragmented, regional chemical distribution and formulation markets. Its moat is local route density, multi-segment supplier buying power, and high-touch service where drivers act as technicians: sticky relationships that produce recurring consumption. The strategy of rolling up small, family-owned regional water-treatment distributors (most deals under $10M, concentrated in the Southeast, Texas, and Midwest) buys route density and cross-sell capacity. Water Treatment's structural attractiveness supports a competitive position that is improving in mix and durability.

The trajectory is mixed across segments. Water Treatment is gaining share through acquisition and is the clear winner; Food & Health Sciences is exposed to sector-wide ingredient deflation and soft demand, which is a relative-laggard rather than a Hawkins-specific share-loss story; Industrial Solutions is the lowest-margin, most commodity-exposed line. Net, the company is widening its position in its best market while carrying a soft mid-segment.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Acquisition integration and overpayment.** Goodwill ($223M) plus intangibles ($233M) total ~46% of assets and exceed shareholders' equity ($534M). Any impairment would hit earnings materially. WaterSurplus is the single most important integration to watch, with $76M of customer-relationship intangibles and $82.6M goodwill attached, and an earnout that grows as a cash liability if WaterSurplus performs.
2. **Earnings-quality noise.** Earnout remeasurement runs through SG&A and swings both ways (a $6.2M favorable adjustment boosted FY2026; WaterSurplus accretion dragged), making GAAP results harder to read.
3. **Regulatory fluoride tail.** The 10-K explicitly added language on regulators attempting to prohibit fluoride in drinking water and certain food ingredients. Given the shift in U.S. health-agency posture, any federal or state move to remove fluoride would directly reduce demand in the flagship Water Treatment segment. This is a genuine, idiosyncratic, not-fully-priced overhang.
4. **Unhedged commodity inputs.** Hawkins does not hedge caustic soda, chlorine, or acids; margins lag spot prices. Tariffs are a named risk on imported nutrition ingredients.
5. **Logistics and environmental.** Chlorine ships only by rail amid tightening toxic-inhalation restrictions; a $7.7M PCB remediation reserve remains at Rosemount; three terminals and a key plant sit on leased land (2029-2044 expirations).
6. **Management turnover.** Three Item 5.02 officer-change 8-Ks in ~18 months, an interim General Counsel since December 2025, and a February 2025 auditor change. Individually routine, collectively worth monitoring. CFO continuity since 2017 and family CEO continuity since 2011 offset.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 87.1% sits richly above 1-year HV of 36.8% (91st percentile within the chain), with a term structure in steep backwardation (87.1% 1-week vs. 37.0% 3-month) and a 5.3% put/call skew indicating elevated near-term put demand. This likely reflects a thin-options artifact rather than a fundamental tell given the post-earnings calendar. IV has fallen from 92.9% to 70.9% since mid-May and the P/C OI ratio collapsed from 0.82 to 0.11 (heavily call-skewed open interest), which leans modestly constructive on a one-month horizon. Short interest is moderate at 8.0% of float with 9.3 days to cover. The options picture neither confirms nor strongly contradicts the fundamental HOLD; the rich near-term IV argues against chasing the stock into any near-dated catalyst.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case (~$190-200):** The water-treatment roll-up compounds, WaterSurplus turns profitable and the full chemicals-plus-equipment portfolio drives cross-sell, FY2027 deleveraging removes the interest/amortization drag and GAAP EPS re-accelerates toward the $5.39 next-year consensus, and the premium multiple holds on the higher-quality recurring mix. Insiders bought near the lows (director Faulconbridge ~$388K at ~$129 in Feb 2026; VP Grahek at ~$126 in Nov 2025), and a respected quality fund (Giverny) built a stake at ~$124, supporting the compounder narrative. 33x on $5.39 = ~$178; 37x = ~$200.

**Bear case (~$120-130):** The multiple compresses toward peer norms (~21-25x) as the market refocuses on declining GAAP EPS, the three-of-four-quarter misses, and the fact that nearly all Water Treatment growth is bought rather than organic. A WaterSurplus impairment or a fluoride regulatory action accelerates the de-rating. 25x on $4.82 = ~$120, roughly -25% from current.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

This is a HOLD with a growth-hold rationale over a 12-month horizon. The thesis is appreciation toward the high-$170s as FY2027 deleveraging restores GAAP EPS growth and rising estimates pull the price up, capped by a premium multiple that limits the asymmetry and keeps this short of a BUY.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- WaterSurplus turns to a positive operating contribution and organic Water Treatment growth (ex-acquisition) accelerates above ~6%, confirming the roll-up converts to durable organic momentum.
- A pullback to the low-$130s or below (≈25x forward) without fundamental deterioration, restoring asymmetric upside.
- Two consecutive quarters of consensus beats reversing the three-of-four-miss pattern, validating the upgraded estimates.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- A goodwill/intangible impairment charge, or earnout remeasurement reveals a deal materially underperforming.
- A concrete federal or state fluoride-removal action affecting Water Treatment demand.
- Leverage rising above ~2x (versus the ~1x FY2027 target) on a fresh large deal while organic growth stays soft.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (FQ1 FY2027, expected ~late July/August 2026), with attention to WaterSurplus operating results, organic Water Treatment growth, and the deleveraging trajectory.

## Conclusion

Hawkins is a well-run, cash-generative water-treatment consolidator with a defensible service model, low customer concentration, and a long dividend record. The forward growth case is real but modest: rising estimates and an FY2027 guide for segment-wide operating-income growth and deleveraging that should restore GAAP EPS growth as the acquisition drag rolls off. Against that, the stock trades at a premium to Materials peers for a business whose GAAP EPS fell in FY2026, that has missed consensus in three of four quarters, and whose growth is overwhelmingly bought rather than organic. The integration of WaterSurplus, the earnings-quality noise from earnouts, and the fluoride regulatory tail are genuine overhangs. The risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric: enough quality and forward momentum to hold, but a multiple too rich and an organic story too soft to add at current levels. **HOLD.**
