## Executive Summary

Service Corporation International is the dominant player in North American deathcare, operating 1,485 funeral homes and 500 cemeteries with approximately 18% market share. The company possesses an extraordinary competitive moat: a $17 billion preneed backlog of locked-in future revenue, unmatched scale advantages, and a Baby Boomer demographic tailwind that will drive elevated mortality volumes for the next 15-20 years. At ~19x forward earnings with $554M in annual FCF and a 1.7% dividend yield, the stock offers reasonable value for a high-quality defensive franchise.

The core obstacle to a stronger recommendation is deteriorating near-term fundamentals. Consensus EPS estimates have been revised downward across all periods (current quarter -9.3% over 90 days, current year -3.0%), the company has missed earnings estimates in two of the last three quarters, and Q1 2026 showed a 6% decline in comparable funeral volumes with 300bp margin compression. Additionally, the trust portfolio (60% equities, ~$5.8B) posted -0.7% returns in Q1 2026 after delivering 15.1% in 2025, creating earnings headwind in the current volatile-equity environment. These are concrete issues that explain the market's skepticism (stock down 12% from 52-week high).

The stock warrants holding for its income characteristics, demographic optionality, and competitive positioning, but requires either estimate stabilization or a lower entry point to justify initiating new positions.

## Company Overview

SCI operates a dual-segment model: Funeral (~56% of revenue) and Cemetery (~44%). The funeral segment provides at-need and preneed funeral services, cremation, merchandise, and related products across 1,485 locations. The cemetery segment sells burial plots, mausoleums, niches, markers, and related merchandise through 500 locations. The company's cluster operating model groups proximate locations to share resources (fleet, preparation facilities, personnel), creating cost advantages unavailable to independent operators.

The preneed business model is the company's defining structural advantage. SCI sells funeral and cemetery services in advance — currently holding $17.0 billion in unfulfilled, price-guaranteed contracts. This backlog grows approximately $1B annually and provides decades of revenue visibility. Contracts are funded through trust investments and third-party insurance policies, with revenue recognized when services are ultimately performed.

Key brands include Dignity Memorial (primary), National Cremation Society, Neptune Society, and Funeraria del Angel. The company maintains acquired local brand names, a strategy that preserves community relationships while layering corporate resources on top.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Profitability:**
- 2025 revenue: $4.31B (+2.9% YoY)
- Q1 2026 revenue: $1.097B (+2.1% YoY)
- Operating margin: 22.7% (2025), compressed to ~21.4% in Q1 2026 on volume decline
- Net margin: 12.6%
- ROE: 33.1% (elevated by leverage structure)
- FCF: $554M (2025), representing ~12.9% of revenue

**Cash Flow Quality:** Operating cash flow of $943M in 2025 is highly stable and predictable. Management guides $1,005-$1,065M in adjusted operating cash flow for 2026. The conversion from GAAP net income to FCF is strong — preneed contract maturation creates a natural cash generation mechanism.

**Balance Sheet Context:** The D/E ratio of 11.4x and Altman Z-Score of 0.56 are structurally misleading. SCI's "liabilities" include billions in deferred revenue from prepaid contracts — these are not obligations requiring cash outflows but rather future revenue waiting to be recognized. Actual funded debt is $5.08B at a leverage ratio of 3.67x (vs. 5.0x covenant limit and 3.5-4.0x target). The company refinanced in November 2025, extending its revolver/term loan maturity to 2030 at favorable pricing. Available revolver capacity is $1.45B.

**Capital Return:** SCI repurchased $464M in shares at $79.15 average during 2025 (5.86M shares), plus $44M post-year-end at $80.48. The dividend of $1.36/share annually yields 1.7% at current price. Share count has declined from 157.7M (2022) to ~138M (current), a 12.4% reduction in 3.5 years. Management targets a 30-40% dividend payout ratio.

**Trust Portfolio Risk:** The $5.8B trust portfolio (60% equities) generated 15.1% returns in 2025 but -0.7% in Q1 2026. In the current environment of elevated equity volatility, trust returns represent a meaningful headwind. A sustained equity correction would require SCI to reduce recognized trust revenue, directly compressing reported earnings. This is the primary near-term earnings risk.

## Growth Analysis

SCI's growth profile is modest but durable:
- Revenue CAGR 2022-2025: ~1.6%
- 2026 EPS guidance midpoint: $4.20, implying 8-12% growth from 2025
- Long-term demographic tailwind: Baby Boomers (born 1946-1964) entering peak mortality over next 15-20 years

The demographic case is structurally sound. The oldest Boomers are now 80, and elevated death counts are an actuarial certainty. However, timing is uncertain — the tailwind has not yet translated into sustained above-trend volume growth. Q1 2026 showed a 6% comparable funeral volume decline (tough flu-season comp), illustrating that near-term volume remains unpredictable despite the favorable long-term setup.

Cemetery preneed production grew 9.7% in Q1 2026, and the preneed backlog grew $1B (6.2%) in 2025 to $17B. This signals that Boomers are actively pre-planning, building the revenue pipeline that will be recognized over coming decades.

The cremation trend (now 64.5% of services) remains a structural headwind. Cremation average revenue ($1,899 non-funeral-home) is roughly one-third of traditional burial ($6,522 at-need core). While SCI is investing in cremation memorialization, this mix shift will continue pressuring revenue per case absent ongoing pricing power.

Estimate revisions are negative: current quarter EPS revised down 9.3% over 90 days, current year down 3.0%, next year down 2.7%. This is not a stock experiencing positive estimate momentum.

## Valuation Assessment

At $78.76, SCI trades at:
- 20.5x trailing P/E ($3.80 EPS)
- ~19.1x forward P/E ($4.13 consensus FY2026)
- ~17.3x FY2027 P/E ($4.55 consensus)
- ~5.1% FCF yield on 2025 FCF ($554M / $10.9B market cap)
- 1.7% dividend yield

Versus peers: the sector median P/E is 18.0x. SCI's premium to peers is modest and justified by its scale, market share, backlog visibility, and demographic positioning. Among direct peers, Matthews International (closest deathcare competitor) trades at 89.6x — reflecting a lower quality, more cyclical business — while ADT (9.4x) and H&R Block (6.5x) are structurally different businesses.

Analyst consensus targets range from $90 (low) to $100 (high), with a mean of $96.33 — implying 22% upside from current price. All 6 covering analysts rate the stock Buy or Strong Buy with no Holds or Sells. This unanimous bullishness at prices 10-22% below targets suggests the sellside sees the post-earnings selloff as opportunity.

The valuation is not stretched — 19x forward for a predictable, defensively-positioned franchise with 8-12% EPS growth potential is reasonable. But the specific catalyst for re-rating is unclear: estimates are declining, earnings have disappointed, and the demographic acceleration hasn't yet manifested in volume numbers. At 19x, the market is appropriately discounting modest growth with execution risk — neither cheap enough to be compelling nor expensive enough to avoid.

## Competitive Landscape

SCI's competitive position is exceptional and arguably improving:
- 18% market share in a fragmented industry with thousands of independents
- $17B preneed backlog locks in future market share regardless of competitive dynamics when contracts mature
- Scale enables lower cost per service, better trust management, and superior digital/marketing capabilities
- Permitting and zoning barriers to new cemetery development are nearly insurmountable
- 60-year acquisition track record with disciplined integration

The company added 22 funeral locations and 2 cemeteries through acquisition in 2025 for $101M — maintaining steady consolidation. The industry's independent operators face succession challenges as family-owned businesses lack successors, creating an ongoing pipeline of acquisition targets.

Cremation is the primary structural challenge: it represents a consumer preference shift that reduces average revenue per service rather than a competitive threat from rivals. SCI is addressing this through cremation memorialization products and expanded celebration-of-life offerings, but this is mitigation rather than solution.

The competitive position score trajectory shows a decline from 8.5 to 8.0 — this reflects the cremation headwind and volume volatility, not actual competitive deterioration. No meaningful competitor has emerged, and the preneed backlog continues growing.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Trust Portfolio Drawdown (High Impact, Moderate Probability):** A 20% equity market correction would create ~$700M in unrealized losses on the trust portfolio, directly reducing recognized revenue when contracts mature during the correction period.

2. **Persistent Volume Weakness (Moderate Impact, Moderate Probability):** The Q1 decline was attributed to flu-season comps, but consecutive volume misses would signal that the demographic tailwind is further away than assumed. The high-fixed-cost model means operating leverage works against SCI on volume declines.

3. **Estimate Revision Trajectory (Active):** The track record shows 1 beat in 3 reported quarters (excluding stale data). If this pattern continues, the stock faces ongoing multiple pressure.

4. **Interest Rate Sensitivity:** $5.08B in debt creates ~$255M in annual interest expense. While the November 2025 refinancing extended maturities to 2030, floating-rate portions are exposed to the current elevated rate environment. Rising long-term yields also increase the discount rate applied to SCI's long-duration preneed earnings stream.

5. **Cremation Mix Shift:** At 64.5% and rising ~50bp/year, cremation will reach 70%+ within a decade. Each percentage point shift represents structural pressure on revenue per case unless offset by pricing or memorialization product innovation.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($90-100, +14-27%):**
Baby Boomer mortality acceleration begins manifesting in 2H 2026/2027, driving comparable funeral volume growth of 2-4% annually. Combined with 3%+ pricing power and continued share repurchase (~$400-500M annually), EPS reaches $4.50-5.00 by 2027. At 20-22x forward (warranted for the improved visibility), the stock reaches $90-110. Trust portfolio recovers as equity markets stabilize. Cemetery preneed acceleration (+10% Q1) proves to be the leading indicator. Consensus targets of $90-100 prove achievable within 12 months.

**Bear Case ($65-70, -10-17%):**
Trust portfolio suffers sustained losses as equity markets decline 15-20%. Volume growth remains elusive through 2026, with funeral services flat-to-declining. Cremation mix shift accelerates, compressing revenue per case. Earnings come in below the low end of guidance ($4.05), and the stock de-rates to 16-17x forward on continued misses. Rising rates increase borrowing costs on the $5.08B debt stack at refinancing.

**Base Case ($85-88, +8-12%):**
Estimates stabilize near current consensus ($4.13 FY2026, $4.55 FY2027). Volume normalizes to flat-to-slightly-positive in remaining quarters. Trust returns modestly positive (~5-8% annually). Continued buyback reduces share count 3-4% annually. Market awards 19-20x forward multiple as the defensive characteristics attract buyers during macro uncertainty. Stock recovers from post-earnings selloff toward analyst consensus.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (likely late July/early August 2026 for Q2). Estimate revision trajectory over next 30 days will indicate whether the post-Q1 reset is complete or continuing.

## Conclusion

SCI is a high-quality defensive franchise trading at a reasonable valuation with a visible multi-decade growth driver. The $17B preneed backlog, 18% market share, and Baby Boomer demographic tailwind create an attractive long-term profile. However, the negative estimate revision trajectory (current quarter estimates down 9.3%, current year down 3.0%) and recent earnings misses represent concrete near-term fundamental softness sufficient to prevent deploying fresh capital at 19x forward without positive revision inflection as confirmation.

The stock is appropriate to hold for income (1.7% yield growing mid-single-digits annually, supported by 30-40% payout ratio against stable FCF) and for exposure to the demographic catalyst when it materializes. Position sizing should reflect the trust portfolio's equity market sensitivity — in the current environment, SCI has more macro beta than its "defensive" classification implies.
