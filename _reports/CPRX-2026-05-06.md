## Executive Summary

Catalyst Pharmaceuticals is a commercial-stage rare disease pharmaceutical company operating a highly profitable, asset-light model with three marketed products: FIRDAPSE (Lambert-Eaton Myasthenic Syndrome), AGAMREE (Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy), and FYCOMPA (epilepsy). The company generated $589M in FY2025 revenue (+20% YoY) with 36% net margins and $209M in free cash flow, all while carrying zero debt and accumulating $709M in cash.

The investment case rests on three pillars: (1) continued FIRDAPSE patient identification driving mid-teens revenue growth on a monopoly product, (2) AGAMREE's rapid ramp from launch to $140-150M guided for 2026, and (3) substantial M&A optionality with a $709M cash war chest and proven acquisition playbook. The FYCOMPA generic headwind (~$71M revenue decline in 2026) is quantified and largely priced in. Consensus estimates have risen 10% for FY2026 and 19% for FY2027 over the past 90 days, reflecting improving fundamentals.

At ~18x trailing P/E and approximately 10.6x forward P/E on FY2026 consensus of $2.86 EPS, the stock trades at a discount to the peer median despite superior profitability and growth characteristics.

## Company Overview

Catalyst Pharmaceuticals is headquartered in Coral Gables, Florida, with a market capitalization of approximately $3.7 billion. The company employs an in-licensing model, acquiring or licensing late-stage rare disease drugs, then commercializing them through a lean commercial organization. As of FY2025, Catalyst had 58 field personnel supporting FIRDAPSE and AGAMREE, plus 19 patient assistance/insurance navigation personnel and 19 medical science liaisons. FYCOMPA active marketing efforts ceased effective December 31, 2025.

**Product Portfolio:**
- **FIRDAPSE** (~60% of revenue): The FDA-approved LEMS treatment for adults and pediatric patients. Catalyst has settled patent challenges with Teva, Lupin, and Inventia (licensed generic entry no earlier than February 25, 2035), but one remaining challenger—Hetero USA—has a bench trial scheduled for May 18, 2026, representing a key unresolved risk. International rights expanding across Japan, Canada, Asia, and Latin America.
- **AGAMREE** (~22% of 2026E revenue): Novel corticosteroid for DMD, launched March 2024. Targeting an estimated 11,000-13,000 total U.S. DMD patients, approximately 70% of whom currently receive corticosteroid treatment. NCE exclusivity through 2028; Orphan Drug Exclusivity through 2030; patents through 2040.
- **FYCOMPA** (~7% of 2026E revenue): Declining epilepsy asset facing generic competition following tablet patent expiry May 2025. Expected to generate $40-45M in 2026 vs. $113.3M in FY2025.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Growth:**
FY2025 revenue of $589M grew 20% from $492M in FY2024. The company guided $615-645M for FY2026 (midpoint +7%), though consensus EPS growth of 10%+ suggests margin improvement will supplement the moderated top-line growth. FIRDAPSE guidance of $435-450M implies 21-26% growth; AGAMREE at $140-150M implies approximately 20-28% growth from the $117.1M FY2025 base (roughly consistent with Q4 2025 annualized run-rate of ~$141M plus modest sequential growth).

**Profitability:**
- Gross margin: ~85% (asset-light, no manufacturing facilities)
- Operating margin: 43.8% (FY2025)
- Net margin: 36.4% (FY2025)
- ROE: ~22.4%
- FCF margin: ~35% ($209M FCF on $589M revenue)

**Balance Sheet:**
- Cash: $709M (19% of market cap)
- Debt: $0
- Current ratio: ~6.6x

The net cash position of $709M against a $3.7B market cap implies an enterprise value of approximately $3.0B. On FY2025 earnings of $214M, EV/Net Income is roughly 14x.

**Cash Flow Generation:**
The company converts virtually all operating cash flow into free cash flow given negligible capex. Cash grew $191M year-over-year (FY2024 to FY2025) after all operating expenses, share repurchases, and milestone payments.

**Capital Allocation:**

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term Growth (2026-2027):**
Consensus estimates have risen materially: FY2026 EPS at $2.86 (+10.2% revision over 90 days), FY2027 at $3.14 (+19.3% revision over 90 days). This reflects both FIRDAPSE's continued patient identification success and AGAMREE's steepening adoption curve.

FIRDAPSE's growth is driven by ongoing identification of undiagnosed LEMS patients among ~20,000 neurologists and ~16,000 oncologists. The August 2025 NCCN guideline inclusion for small cell lung cancer-related LEMS expands the addressable physician base meaningfully. Revenue grew 18% in Q4 2025 YoY despite orphan drug exclusivity expiration.

AGAMREE's trajectory from $0 to $140-150M annual guidance within two years of launch demonstrates strong execution. With approximately 70% of an estimated 11,000-13,000 U.S. DMD patients on corticosteroids (approximately 7,700-9,100 patients), AGAMREE's addressable market supports a $300M+ peak revenue opportunity.

**Headwinds:**
FYCOMPA's generic erosion will cost approximately $71M in revenue from FY2025 to FY2026 (from $113.3M to midpoint $42.5M). This is fully guided and anticipated. The net growth equation using guidance midpoints: FIRDAPSE +$84M + AGAMREE +$28M - FYCOMPA -$71M = approximately +$41M net revenue growth, consistent with guided midpoint growth from $589M to $630M.

**Long-term Growth:**
International expansion (Japan launched January 2025; Canada; Asia/LatAm) adds incremental revenue. The $709M cash war chest positions the company for a transformational acquisition of another rare disease asset. Management compensation explicitly incentivizes this. The company's track record of in-licensing FIRDAPSE (2009), acquiring FYCOMPA (2023), and licensing AGAMREE (2019) demonstrates repeatable capability.

## Valuation Assessment

At $30.45, CPRX trades at:
- 18.1x trailing P/E (on $1.68 EPS)
- 10.6x forward P/E on FY2026E of $2.86
- 9.7x P/E on FY2027E of $3.14
- ~5.1x EV/Revenue (on $3.0B EV / $589M)

The forward P/E of 10.6x is below the biotech peer median of 17.7x (Ligand at 36.7x, ADMA at 17.7x, TGTX at 12.1x). For a company with 20%+ revenue growth, 36% net margins, zero debt, and improving estimates, this discount appears unjustified absent a material adverse outcome from the Hetero patent trial.

The PEG ratio on FY2027 earnings growth (~10% from FY2026) is approximately 1.0x. On FY2026 vs. FY2025 growth (~18%), the PEG is approximately 0.6x.

Analyst consensus targets are estimated in the range of $32-40, with most covering analysts rating the stock Buy. The stock currently trades at approximately $30.45, implying upside to the consensus target range.

The $709M in net cash (approximately $5.80/share) means investors are effectively paying $24.65/share for the operating business. This cash-adjusted valuation is somewhat simplified, as FY2025 included approximately $24.8M in net interest income from the cash balance, which contributes to reported EPS. An EV/operating income framework may more cleanly capture the operating business valuation (~11.6x on FY2025 operating income of $257.8M).

## Competitive Landscape

FIRDAPSE holds dominant positioning in LEMS as the primary marketed amifampridine product. While RUZURGI (amifampridine, originally approved for pediatric LEMS by Jacobus Pharmaceutical) also received FDA approval, Catalyst acquired/licensed rights to RUZURGI-related assets through its settlement with Jacobus, effectively consolidating market control. The disease's ultra-orphan status and the complexity of diagnosis (requiring VGCC antibody testing) create natural barriers to patient identification that Catalyst has invested in overcoming through free diagnostic testing programs. Three of four generic challengers have settled with entry dates of February 2035, providing approximately 9 years of protected revenue if the remaining Hetero case resolves favorably.

AGAMREE faces a more competitive landscape. Standard corticosteroids (prednisone, deflazacort) are cheap and familiar to treating neurologists. AGAMREE's differentiation lies in its potentially improved side-effect profile via selective glucocorticoid/mineralocorticoid receptor modulation. Gene therapy (Sarepta's Elevidys) represents a long-term structural risk, though AGAMREE may actually benefit from use alongside gene therapy (immunosuppression during treatment).

The company's competitive advantage is primarily organizational: a lean, specialist-focused commercial infrastructure that can efficiently address ultra-orphan markets where large pharma presence is uneconomic.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Hetero Patent Trial (May 2026) — UNRESOLVED:**
The bench trial against Hetero USA was originally scheduled for March 23, 2026, but was delayed by the District Court to May 18, 2026 (per Catalyst's March 2026 8-K/A filing). This remains the single largest binary risk to the FIRDAPSE franchise. If Catalyst prevails, FIRDAPSE revenue appears protected through at least 2035 alongside the Teva/Lupin settlements. If Catalyst loses, generic FIRDAPSE could enter the market before 2035, though the practical timeline for generic entry would depend on FDA approval timelines and other factors. Catalyst has explicitly disclosed that there can be no assurance it will prevail against Hetero.

**2. Customer Concentration:**
84% of revenue flows through one exclusive distributor. While structural for specialty pharmacy distribution of orphan drugs, this creates single-point-of-failure risk. Payment term changes have temporarily increased receivable exposure.

**3. FYCOMPA Decline More Severe than Guided:**
Generic penetration could accelerate beyond the $40-45M guidance for 2026. However, FYCOMPA's declining share of the revenue mix (now <10%) limits the impact.

**4. AGAMREE Royalty Burden:**
Combined royalties of 14-26% on AGAMREE net sales to Santhera and third-party sublicensors will compress margins as AGAMREE scales. Additionally, up to $105M in sales-based milestones will create periodic cash outflows.

**5. FIRDAPSE Royalty Dynamics:**
Beginning January 26, 2026, the overall upstream royalty rate on net U.S. FIRDAPSE sales declined to approximately 6%, down from a previous maximum of 18.5%, as one tiered royalty expired and another declined following orphan exclusivity timing. This represents a meaningful tailwind to margins in 2026 and beyond, partially offsetting the AGAMREE royalty burden as that product scales.

**6. Acquisition Execution Risk:**
The $709M war chest and explicit executive incentives for "transformational acquisition" create pressure to deploy capital. Overpaying for a rare disease asset would destroy significant value.

**7. Tariff/Policy Risk:**
AGAMREE sourced from Switzerland and FYCOMPA from Japan face potential pharmaceutical import tariff exposure. The company is working toward U.S. AGAMREE manufacturing by end of 2026.

**Options Market Signal:**
ATM IV surged from 63.5% to 79.5% (+16pp) alongside the 11.3% price appreciation, suggesting the market is pricing elevated uncertainty or event risk. The P/C OI ratio of 0.09 is extremely low, indicating very limited put protection relative to calls—consistent with bullish positioning. Short interest at 8.9% of float with 7.2 days to cover represents a moderate short base that could provide additional squeeze fuel on positive catalysts.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($36-40):**
- Hetero trial resolved favorably, protecting FIRDAPSE through 2035
- AGAMREE achieves $200M+ in FY2027 revenue on continued DMD penetration
- Successful transformational acquisition deploys $400-500M of cash into another rare disease franchise
- Estimates continue rising, triggering re-rating from 10.6x to 15x forward P/E (peer median-appropriate)
- FIRDAPSE royalty step-down to 6% drives margin expansion
- Bull math: 15x × $3.14 FY2027E = $47; 12x × $3.14 = $37.70

**Bear Case ($22-24):**
- Hetero trial results in adverse ruling, opening path to generic FIRDAPSE before 2035
- AGAMREE adoption stalls due to gene therapy displacement or payer resistance
- Acquisition execution disappoints (overpay, integration complexity)
- FIRDAPSE growth decelerates as patient identification plateaus
- Bear math: 8x × $2.86 = $22.90

**Base Case ($34-36):**
- FIRDAPSE delivers on $435-450M guidance; AGAMREE at $140-150M
- Hetero trial outcome favorable or settled on reasonable terms
- No major acquisition in near term; cash continues building
- FY2026 EPS of $2.86 achieved; FY2027 estimates of $3.14 confirmed
- 12x forward P/E on FY2027 = $37.70; discounted back = ~$34

## Conclusion

Catalyst Pharmaceuticals offers a compelling combination of high margins, zero leverage, strong cash generation, and growth visibility at a reasonable valuation. The stock trades at 10.6x forward P/E—a meaningful discount to biotech peers—despite superior financial characteristics and positive estimate revision momentum.

The pending Hetero patent trial (May 18, 2026) remains the largest binary risk to the thesis. Three of four ANDA challengers have settled at February 2035 entry dates, which is encouraging, but the Hetero outcome is uncertain and could determine whether generic FIRDAPSE is marketed before those licensed entry dates. The FIRDAPSE royalty step-down to 6% beginning January 2026 provides a meaningful margin tailwind that partially offsets FYCOMPA revenue loss. The remaining concerns are AGAMREE execution and acquisition discipline—both monitored quarterly.

At 10.6x forward P/E with 20%+ revenue growth and $709M in deployable cash, the risk/reward favors ownership over the next 12 months, conditional on a favorable Hetero resolution.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The Hetero patent trial remains unresolved (delayed to May 18, 2026) rather than favorably concluded as originally stated; this is the key binary risk that could materially affect the recommendation if the outcome is adverse.
