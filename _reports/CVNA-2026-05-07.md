## Executive Summary

Carvana has executed one of the most remarkable corporate turnarounds in recent consumer retail history, moving from near-bankruptcy in late 2022 to S&P 500 inclusion, $2.2B annual Adjusted EBITDA, and a $56B market cap. Q1 2026 results confirmed the growth trajectory remains intact: 187K retail units (+40% YoY), $6.4B revenue (+52% YoY), and $672M Adj. EBITDA at 10.4% margins. The operational leverage thesis — 1.5M unit IRC capacity operating at ~40% utilization — provides genuine runway for continued expansion without proportional capex.

The investment case remains clouded by unresolved governance and regulatory risk. The SEC subpoena (received June 2025) regarding related-party transactions with DriveTime — a Garcia-family-controlled entity responsible for loan servicing, VSC administration, and other functions — has neither been resolved nor dismissed. Gotham City Research's allegations that DriveTime subsidies account for >73% of Adjusted EBITDA are contested by management and JPMorgan, but the regulatory investigation remains open. At 46x trailing earnings and 51x forward P/E (FY2026E $7.66), the stock prices in substantial growth execution while offering limited margin of safety against an adverse SEC outcome.

The improving fundamental trajectory warrants upgrading from prior AVOID ratings, but the combination of regulatory uncertainty, governance concentration, and premium valuation against a macro backdrop of rising subprime delinquencies and consumer stress argues for waiting rather than buying.

## Company Overview

Carvana operates as the leading e-commerce platform for used vehicle transactions in the United States. The company's vertically integrated model spans vehicle acquisition (trade-ins, auctions, wholesale marketplace), inspection and reconditioning (at IRC facilities with 1.5M annual capacity), direct-to-consumer digital sales, captive financing origination, ancillary product sales (VSCs, GAP waiver, insurance), and last-mile logistics through a proprietary delivery network.

Revenue composition for FY2025: retail vehicle sales $14.5B (71%), wholesale sales $4.1B (20%), and other revenues $1.7B (9%) — the last category being primarily loan sale gains and product commissions carrying near-100% gross margins and contributing 41% of total gross profit despite single-digit revenue share.

The company acquired five franchise dealerships in FY2025 for $160M, signaling potential expansion into new vehicle sales as a customer acquisition and trade-in funnel strategy. It was added to the S&P 500 in December 2025 and announced a 5-for-1 stock split in early 2026.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Growth:** Revenue inflected from the $10.8B trough in FY2023 to $20.3B in FY2025 (+88% over two years) and is running at ~$25.7B annualized based on Q1 2026's $6.4B quarter. Retail unit sales grew from 313K (2023) to 597K (2025) to 187K in Q1 alone (749K annualized). This represents approximately 1.5% of the ~40M annual U.S. used vehicle market.

**Profitability Inflection:** Adjusted EBITDA moved from -$1.0B (FY2022) to $339M (2023) to $1.4B (2024) to $2.2B (2025) to $672M in Q1 2026 alone ($2.7B annualized). GAAP net income reached $1.4B in FY2025, though this was boosted by a $2.8B tax benefit partially offset by a $2.2B TRA expense — normalized ongoing EPS is approximately $8.45 trailing.

**Unit Economics:** Total GPU stabilized at ~$7,000-$7,200 while SG&A per unit dropped from $4,900 (2023) to ~$3,400 (Q1 2026 implied). The spread between GPU and SG&A per unit has expanded significantly over this period. With each incremental unit sold through existing infrastructure, this spread captures essentially pure incremental EBITDA.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of $5.0B (down from ~$6.6B peak) against $2.3B cash and $4.4B committed liquidity. D/E ratio of 2.61 is still elevated but declining rapidly. The Altman Z-Score of 2.59 approaches the 2.99 "safe" threshold. The company elected to pay cash interest rather than PIK on its 2028/2030 secured notes — an important confidence signal. Retained earnings have nearly turned positive (-$9M), eliminating the negative equity concern.

**Cash Flow:** FCF of $889M in FY2025 (4.4% margin). Operating cash flow of $1.04B. The company repurchased $611M of debt while also issuing $539M in equity through its ATM program at ~$365/share average.

**Key Concern — TRA Liability:** The Tax Receivable Agreement liability surged from $82M to $2.3B in 2025 following the valuation allowance release. Of this, $1.7B is payable to Garcia family entities (85% of tax benefits from LLC Unit exchanges). This represents a material ongoing cash obligation and wealth transfer mechanism to the controlling family.

## Growth Analysis

The growth trajectory is supported by multiple vectors:

1. **Volume headroom:** Operating at ~40% of 1.5M unit IRC capacity. Path to 1M+ units requires no new facilities.
2. **Market share opportunity:** 1.5% of a 40M unit market. Even modest share gains translate to significant volume growth.
3. **Operating leverage:** Each incremental unit generates meaningful contribution above current run-rate SG&A per unit.
4. **Loan sale infrastructure:** $18B+ committed loan purchase capacity (Ally $6B MPSA + three $4B agreements) enables continued monetization of originations.
5. **Franchise dealership expansion:** Five acquisitions in 2025 open new vehicle sales and trade-in pipeline.

Management guided for sequential increases in both units and Adj. EBITDA in Q2 2026, targeting all-time records. Consensus estimates for FY2026 are $7.66 EPS and FY2027 $10.19 — implying 33% earnings growth.

Estimate revisions are positive: current quarter estimates revised up 12.6% over 30 days and 7.1% for the full year. This is a positive momentum signal, though the magnitude (+7.5% over 90 days for FY2026) falls short of the >30% threshold that would constitute a strong inflection BUY signal.

**Growth Risks:** Subprime auto delinquencies at 6.74% (highest since early 1990s) threaten loan sale economics. Consumer stress indicators could suppress discretionary vehicle purchases. Tariff uncertainty creates affordability headwinds. The 2/4 earnings beat record over the last year suggests execution is not as consistent as the headline growth numbers imply.

## Valuation Assessment

At $389.38 per share:
- Trailing P/E: 46x (on $8.45 EPS)
- Forward P/E (FY2026E): 51x ($7.66 consensus)
- Forward P/E (FY2027E): 38x ($10.19 consensus)
- EV/EBITDA (FY2025 Adj.): ~26x ($2.2B)
- EV/EBITDA (Q1 ann.): ~23x ($2.7B)
- P/FCF: ~63x ($889M)

Against peer medians of 21.3x P/E, Carvana commands a significant premium. The growth differential justifies some of this: peers like AutoZone (24.7x) and O'Reilly (30.9x) are growing revenue at single-digit rates, while Carvana is growing 40-50%. However, those peers carry minimal governance risk, proven long-term profitability track records, and no SEC investigations.

The bull case valuation path: at 1M units and $3.5B Adj. EBITDA (achievable within 18-24 months on current trajectory), the stock trades at ~17x forward EV/EBITDA — reasonable for a scaled, high-growth retailer. On FY2027 consensus of $10.19 EPS at 35x (growth-adjusted), fair value would be ~$357 — essentially current price. At 40x (reflecting sustained 30%+ growth), fair value reaches ~$408.

The analyst consensus target of $465 (median) implies 19% upside and assumes continued execution without regulatory disruption. The $335-$600 target range reflects the exceptional uncertainty surrounding this name.

Current valuation incorporates the turnaround but leaves limited margin of safety. The stock is not expensive enough to short, but not cheap enough to buy with conviction given the open risks.

## Competitive Landscape

Carvana's competitive position is strengthening. The company's scale advantages — 1.5M unit IRC capacity, national logistics network, 1M+ unit wholesale marketplace providing pricing intelligence, and established consumer brand — create barriers to entry that did not exist three years ago.

CarMax (KMX) remains the primary direct competitor at ~800K annual units, but its omnichannel model carries higher per-unit real estate and personnel costs. Traditional dealer groups (AutoNation, Penske, Lithia) are expanding used vehicle operations but lack Carvana's digital-native infrastructure. Potential tech entrants (Amazon) have not materialized as competitive threats.

The wholesale marketplace (1M+ units transacted) creates a data and liquidity moat — Carvana sees a disproportionate share of market transactions, providing superior pricing intelligence and acquisition flexibility. The operational efficiency trajectory (SG&A per unit declining 30%+ over two years) suggests the company is still early in its scale-curve optimization.

The primary competitive risk is demand destruction from macro conditions (rising rates reducing affordability, consumer credit tightening) or regulatory action disrupting the business model.

## Risk Assessment

**SEC Investigation (HIGH):** The June 2025 subpoena regarding related-party transactions with DriveTime remains unresolved. Potential outcomes range from no action (bull case) to enforcement action requiring restatement or structural separation of DriveTime relationship (bear case). The short-seller allegations — that DriveTime subsidies account for >73% of Adj. EBITDA — remain unproven but also unrefuted by independent audit. JPMorgan's defense and the company's assertion that allegations are "inaccurate" are reassuring but not dispositive.

**Governance Concentration (HIGH):** Garcia family's ~83% voting control through Class B shares eliminates any external governance check. The TRA liability ($2.3B, with $1.7B to Garcia entities) is a legal wealth transfer mechanism. Ernest Garcia II's $272M covered call monetization in April/May 2026 represents sophisticated value extraction by the controlling shareholder. No insider open-market purchases provide bullish counterweight.

**Subprime Exposure (MODERATE):** 26% of gross profit from loan sales, with subprime delinquencies at cycle highs (6.74%). Further deterioration would compress loan sale premiums. The $18B+ committed purchase capacity provides distribution stability, but pricing is market-dependent.

**Dilution (MODERATE):** Diluted share count increased 70% from 132M (2024) to 224M (2025) due to LLC Unit exchanges. Additional ATM capacity of ~$461M remains available. The 5-for-1 split increases retail participation but doesn't change economics.

**Macro Consumer Stress (MODERATE):** Rising interest rates directly impact affordability. However, tariffs on new vehicles could paradoxically increase used vehicle demand.

- Related-party accounting concerns prove partially substantiated

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. SEC investigation formally closed with no enforcement action
2. Stock pulls back below $320 (35x FY2026E) providing margin of safety at current growth rates
3. Two consecutive quarters of earnings beats combined with upward estimate revision momentum exceeding +15% over 90 days

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. SEC enforcement action announced (Wells notice, formal charges)
2. Quarterly unit growth decelerates below 15% YoY without a macro explanation
3. Loan sale economics deteriorate materially (GPU from "Other" segment declining >20%)

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July 2026) is the next natural catalyst. SEC investigation developments could occur at any time and would warrant immediate reassessment. Monitor subprime delinquency data monthly.

## Conclusion

Carvana's operational execution has been strong — the company has built genuine competitive advantages, achieved positive operating leverage, and demonstrated a credible path to sustained profitability at scale. The improvement from prior AVOID ratings reflects this reality: financial health is better, growth is confirmed, and the turnaround is no longer speculative.

However, the SEC investigation into related-party transactions represents a genuinely unquantifiable risk at this price level. At 46x trailing earnings with 13.5% short interest, elevated options-implied volatility in backwardation, and Garcia family monetization through covered calls, the risk/reward profile does not favor initiating a position. The stock is pricing in execution without pricing in regulatory risk.

The correct posture is watchful patience: the business may continue to perform exceptionally, but the governance structure and regulatory overhang demand either a resolution of the SEC matter or a meaningfully lower entry price before committing capital.
