## Executive Summary

AMETEK delivered an exceptional Q1 2026: revenue of $1.93B (+11.3% YoY), adjusted EPS of $1.97 (beating consensus by 2.5%), record orders (+23% YoY), and record backlog. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance to $7.94–$8.14 adjusted EPS, implying approximately 7–10% growth over the comparable adjusted 2025 base of $7.43. The orders acceleration is the most important data point — at 23% growth vs. 11% revenue growth, book-to-bill has expanded materially, providing 2-3 quarters of elevated revenue visibility. EMG segment margins have expanded 570 bps in just 15 months (from 20.0% to 25.7%), a trajectory that consensus models have likely not fully incorporated.

The stock trades at $234.54 (pulled back from the $242 post-earnings spike), or approximately 29x the midpoint of 2026 guidance. This is a premium industrial multiple, but AMETEK earns it through 22%+ FCF margins, a 1.8% capex-to-sales ratio, and a self-funding acquisition compounding model that has added $1.8B in annualized revenue since 2021. The 23% order growth, if even partially sustained, suggests upside to current estimates. The combination of a proven beat track record (4 consecutive quarters) and post-Q1 guidance raise still being absorbed supports a BUY at current levels with a 12-month base case of $260-270.

## Company Overview

AMETEK is an S&P 500 component operating as a diversified manufacturer of electronic instruments and electromechanical devices across two segments:

**Electronic Instruments Group (EIG, 66% of revenue):** Advanced analytical, test, measurement, and monitoring instruments serving process/industrial, aerospace, power, medical, and semiconductor markets. Key recent additions include FARO Technologies (3D metrology, acquired July 2025) and Kern Microtechnik (precision machining). Operating margins: ~29-31%.

**Electromechanical Group (EMG, 34% of revenue):** Precision motion control, medical device components, thermal management, specialty metals, and electrical interconnects. Operating margins have expanded from 20.0% (2024) to 25.7% (Q1 2026).

The business model emphasizes niche market leadership across hundreds of product categories where no single customer exceeds 5% of sales, technological differentiation (27% of sales from products introduced in the last 3 years), and disciplined acquisition integration. AMETEK has manufacturing operations in 22 countries outside the United States with approximately $7.4B in annual revenue.

## Financial Analysis

**Profitability Metrics:**
- Operating margin: 26.7% (Q1 2026 GAAP); core operating margin of 27.9% after adjusting for acquisition-related costs, acquisition dilution, and foreign exchange impacts
- Net margin: 20.0% (FY2025)
- FCF margin: 22.6% ($1.67B on $7.4B revenue)
- Capital expenditure: 1.8% of sales
- FCF conversion: ~113% of net income

At 1.8% capex-to-sales, nearly all operating cash flow converts to distributable cash. The $1.67B in FY2025 FCF represents a 3.0% yield on the approximately $54B market cap — modest in absolute terms but extraordinary relative to reinvestment requirements.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Net debt: $1.83B (net debt/EBITDA roughly 0.9x)
- Available credit: $1.49B facility + $700M accordion
- Debt/equity: approximately 0.21x (total debt of $2.28B vs. stockholders' equity of $10.63B)
- Goodwill + intangibles: $11.3B (70% of assets)

The balance sheet is conservatively managed with substantial acquisition capacity. The high intangible asset ratio is structural to the serial acquirer model, and management's most recent annual impairment testing (Q4 2025) found no impairment — though this remains a latent risk in a severe downturn.

**Orders and Revenue Visibility:**
- FY2025 orders: $7.58B (+11.3% YoY), book-to-bill 1.02x
- Q1 2026 orders: +23% YoY (record)
- Backlog: Record $3.58B at year-end 2025 (now likely higher)

Even discounting for some tariff-related pre-buying, the magnitude of order growth suggests genuine demand acceleration across defense, automation, and semiconductor end markets.

**Capital Allocation:**
- Acquisitions: $933M deployed in FY2025; LKC Technologies closed in January 2026, and a definitive agreement to acquire First Aviation Services was announced April 30, 2026 (subject to customary closing conditions including regulatory approvals)
- Buybacks: $443M in FY2025 (doubled from 2024); $807M authorization remaining
- Dividends: $285M in FY2025; 10% increase announced February 2026

Total shareholder returns (buybacks + dividends) in FY2025: $728M.

## Risk Assessment

**Macro/Cyclical Risk (Medium):**
Elevated recession risk poses a real threat to industrial capex. However, AMETEK's exposure to aerospace/defense, medical, and other less-cyclical end markets provides partial insulation. The record backlog provides approximately 5.8 months of revenue coverage based on FY2025 run-rate, offering meaningful visibility even if new orders slow.

**Tariff Exposure (Medium-Low):**
48% international revenue creates exposure, but management raised guidance in April 2026 during peak tariff uncertainty. Manufacturing in 22 countries enables production localization. The risk is more to customer demand (if tariffs slow broader industrial activity) than to AMETEK's direct cost structure.

**Acquisition Integration (Medium):**
Multiple acquisitions are being integrated simultaneously (FARO, Kern, LKC, Paragon). While AMETEK's track record is excellent (15 acquisitions since 2021 without impairment), execution risk scales with deal activity. Recent acquisitions were dilutive to segment operating margins by approximately 60 bps in FY2025, with an additional 30 bps drag from integration costs.

**Goodwill Risk (Low but Present):**
$11.3B in goodwill and intangibles (70% of assets) creates latent write-down risk. A severe industrial recession affecting multiple reporting units simultaneously could trigger impairment charges. While the most recent testing (Q4 2025) confirmed no impairment, this remains the structural vulnerability of the serial acquirer model.

**Options Market Signal:**
Near-term IV of 27.6% sits 6.1pp above 21.5% historical vol. The OI put/call ratio of 1.47 shows moderate put demand, but the put/call volume ratio of just 0.12 suggests no active near-term hedging flow. The elevated IV/HV spread indicates the market is pricing somewhat more uncertainty than historical norms, but not at extreme levels. Term structure is flat, indicating uncertainty is distributed evenly rather than concentrated at a specific event.

## Conclusion

AMETEK is a high-quality industrial compounder delivering its best quarter in recent history while the market prices elevated uncertainty across the industrial sector. The 23% order growth, record backlog, and EMG margin expansion collectively represent a fundamental acceleration that current consensus has not fully discounted. At 29x forward earnings — a valuation broadly in line with quality industrial peers — the risk/reward favors accumulation. The primary risk is that order strength proves transient (tariff pre-buying) rather than demand-driven; this will become clearer with Q2 2026 results. Position sizing should reflect the premium valuation and cyclical sensitivity: 2-3% of portfolio with a 12-month hold.
