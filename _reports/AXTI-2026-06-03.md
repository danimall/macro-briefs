**Research Date: June 3, 2026**

## Executive Summary

AXT manufactures compound and single-element semiconductor substrate wafers (indium phosphide (InP), gallium arsenide (GaAs), and germanium (Ge)) used where silicon cannot meet performance requirements. The InP franchise is the entire investment case: InP is an enabling material for the high-speed lasers and optical transceivers used in AI data-center connectivity, and AXT is one of only a few merchant suppliers globally alongside Japan's Sumitomo and JX. Q1 2026 revenue rose 39% YoY to $26.9M, substrates rose 74%, InP is now >50% of revenue at ~$13.6M, the InP backlog exceeds $100M (up from ~$60M one quarter earlier), and gross margin recovered from -6.4% a year ago to +29.6%.

The problem is price. AXT has been re-rated from $1.47 to a high of $143.16 over twelve months (roughly a 7,000% move) and currently trades near $111 against a business that posted a Q1 net loss of $1.6M and burned $11.7M of operating cash. Management itself flags a forward price/sales multiple near 52x against a five-year median of 1.48x. The demand thesis is credible and broad-based, but the equity has already priced in flawless execution of a capacity build that doubles InP output in 2026 and doubles again in 2027, plus conversion of a backlog that is itself hostage to a foreign government's discretionary export licensing.

We rate AXTI an AVOID at current levels. The decisive signal is the combination of extreme valuation with aggressive, systematic insider selling: the CEO sold at $112-113 in early June, the CFO sold ~$16.8M in a single day in March, and two directors sold repeatedly from $10 to $111, with no open-market buying anywhere in the chain. When insiders monetize a spike across a $36-to-$113 range while the stock trades at 52x forward sales, their behavior is materially incongruent with the bull narrative. We would revisit on a meaningful multiple compression toward double-digit forward sales, U.S. export-permit clarity, and a sustained shift to GAAP profitability with positive operating cash flow.

## Company Overview

AXT is a materials-science company headquartered in Fremont, California, that develops and produces specialty semiconductor substrate wafers. It does not fabricate chips; it makes the wafers on which optoelectronic and RF devices are built. The business operates in a single segment, split roughly 67% substrates / 33% raw materials. The core proprietary technology is the vertical gradient freeze (VGF) crystal-growth process.

All manufacturing occurs in China through majority-owned subsidiary Beijing Tongmei Xtal Technology ("Tongmei") and a network of consolidated and equity-method raw-material joint ventures (purified gallium, pBN crucibles, boron oxide, arsenic, germanium). Approximately 99% of revenue is international (China, Europe, Japan, Taiwan), with only ~2% from North America in 2025. Vertical integration in raw materials is a genuine differentiator for supply security and cost, made more valuable by Chinese export controls on gallium and germanium themselves.

InP enables high-speed optical data transmission in laser and transceiver applications for AI data centers, 5G, and passive optical networks. Direct InP competitors are Sumitomo and JX; broader peers include Freiberger, Umicore, Vital, and CCTC. Per the Q1 2026 commentary, China InP-laser revenue is doubling sequentially and U.S. hyperscaler-ecosystem adoption is building, though U.S. shipments are constrained by the export-permit issue discussed below.

## Financial Analysis

AXT is a loss-making, cash-burning small-cap that is genuinely inflecting toward profitability on InP mix, but is not yet there.

**Revenue and margins.** Substrates grew 74% to $19.3M while raw materials declined 7.6% to $7.6M. The standout trend is gross margin recovery: Q1'25 -6.4% → Q2'25 +8.0% → Q3'25 +22.3% → Q1'26 +29.6%, driven by volume absorbing fixed costs plus favorable InP mix. The mechanism is real: InP carries higher margins than legacy GaAs/Ge and the substrate business has high operating leverage as volumes ramp.

**Profitability.** The company is still unprofitable. Q1'26 operating loss was -$1.6M and net loss attributable to AXT was -$1.6M (-$0.03/share), a large improvement from -$8.8M (-$0.20) a year earlier. The estimate-revision data shows consensus now expects modest profitability: current-year EPS revised to $0.30 from roughly breakeven 90 days ago, and next-year EPS to $0.75, which would represent a genuine swing to GAAP profit if delivered. The earnings surprise record is favorable (three beats of four quarters), though the absolute numbers remain near zero.

**Cash flow.** Q1'26 operating cash flow was -$11.7M, driven by inventory (+$7.4M) and receivables (+$4.9M) as the business ramps. Capex was modest at $1.4M, but the planned capacity build is large. Cash burn at the operating line is the figure that does not yet support the valuation.

**Annual context.** Revenue collapsed from a ~$141M peak in 2022 through the semiconductor inventory correction and tariff/export shocks to the ~$75-88M range in 2023-2025; FY2025 revenue still declined ~11% YoY. This is a business with a demonstrated cyclical history, which matters when judging whether today's "structural AI demand" is durable.

**Balance sheet.** At 3/31/26: cash $41.8M, restricted cash $16.1M, short-term investments $65.4M; total assets $444.6M; total liabilities only $107.6M (mostly $68.9M of short-term PRC bank loans at 2.0-3.8%); equity $298.5M. The April 2026 equity raise then added ~$600M net cash, leaving the company over-capitalized relative to its operating base. Inventory of $90.2M (roughly 3.3x quarterly revenue) carries a $29.8M obsolescence reserve, partly a deliberate pre-build for the ramp, but also a historical source of write-downs and a yellow flag.

**Dilution.** Two equity raises in four months (December 2025, $93.9M net at $12.25, and April 2026, $600.2M net at $64.25) lifted the share count from ~43.5M (Q1'25 weighted average) to ~65M. Management used the spike to fortify the balance sheet and fund the capacity build, which materially de-risks execution funding, but at meaningful dilution. The company sold its own stock at $12.25 in December and $64.25 in April, well below where the shares now trade and below where insiders have been selling personally.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis is real and is the strongest part of the case. InP demand for AI optical interconnect is inflecting, the backlog has grown from ~$60M to over $100M in a single quarter, and management guided Q2 2026 to be the largest InP quarter in company history. This aligns with broader confirmation across the optical/photonics supply chain, where multiple participants are reporting accelerating demand for high-speed data-center interconnect.

The estimate-revision velocity is high: current-quarter EPS revised +562.5% over 30 days to $0.07, next-year EPS revised +110.8% over 90 days to $0.75, on revenue growth near 40%. In isolation, revisions of this magnitude combined with accelerating revenue are among the strongest forward indicators and would ordinarily support a constructive view. The guardrail that matters: this signal is most reliable when the stock is cheap relative to the improving estimates. At ~52x forward sales and a forward P/E that only becomes meaningful if the company hits the optimistic $0.75 next-year figure (which would still leave the stock above 140x forward earnings), the multiple already prices in continued beats. The asymmetry is unfavorable: beats are expected, and any permit delay or backlog conversion miss produces outsized downside.

Capacity expansion is aggressive: management plans to roughly double InP capacity in 2026 versus Q4'25 and double again in 2027 via a new adjacent facility, implying quarterly InP capacity rising from a historical peak near $17M toward ~$35M by end-2026 and ~$65-70M by late 2027/early 2028. Management emphasizes that InP capacity is technically hard to add (crystal-growth yields, clean-room scaling, permit timing), which supports pricing if executed but raises execution risk if not. The growth runway is credible; the question is whether it is already in the price.

## Valuation Assessment

This is where the case breaks. AXT trades at roughly 52x forward sales against a five-year median of 1.48x and a Zacks Value Score of "F." Even crediting the optimistic consensus path ($0.30 current-year and $0.75 next-year EPS), the stock trades above 140x next-year earnings at ~$111. There is no peer cohort, even within the AI-optics complex, that supports this. Peers riding the same wave (Lattice, Diodes) are profitable, far less China-exposed, and trade at small fractions of this sales multiple.

We apply cycle-adjusted and forward-looking logic deliberately rather than reflexively penalizing a high trailing multiple. On a normalized basis, even if AXT executes the full capacity doubling and reaches, say, $250-300M of annual revenue by 2028 at a 30% gross margin and a generous 15% net margin, that would imply roughly $40-45M of net income. At a premium 30x multiple appropriate for a high-growth, high-quality, low-risk franchise, that would be a ~$1.2-1.4B equity value, versus a current market capitalization near $6.6B. The gap requires either revenue substantially above $300M or a multiple far above any sustainable level for a China-domiciled, permit-constrained materials company. The market is extrapolating a best-case 2028-2029 outcome and discounting none of the binary risks.

Analyst price targets here are unusual and informative: the mean is $87.75 and the median $91.50, with a high target of $95 and a low of $73; the entire distribution sits below the current ~$111 price. The sell side is signaling the stock has run ahead of even optimistic fundamentals. We do not treat targets as floors on a stock this far above its own analysts' high estimate. The valuation score is 2.5: the multiple is stretched against fundamentals with binary downside risks unpriced.

## Competitive Landscape

AXT's competitive position is genuinely improving on a trajectory basis, which is the one structural positive that keeps this from being a pure value trap. It is one of a small handful of merchant InP suppliers globally at the precise moment AI data-center optical interconnect demand inflects. Its vertically integrated Chinese raw-material supply chain (purified gallium, germanium, pBN crucibles) is a real moat that became more valuable as those materials themselves came under Chinese export control. InP capacity is technically difficult to scale, which protects incumbents.

The same China footprint is the company's greatest liability. AXT depends on discretionary Chinese export permits to serve Western customers while facing U.S. tariffs to ship into the U.S.; it sits squarely on the US-China tech-decoupling fault line. The commodity-like history (5-10% annual ASP erosion, brutal cyclicality, 2022→2023 revenue halving) cautions that today's structural demand can still prove cyclical. Net assessment: improving share and a widening moat in InP, offset by a geographic structure that converts geopolitical risk into a recurring operational constraint. Competitive position scores 6.5.

## Risk Assessment

The idiosyncratic risk profile is severe and largely unpriced at current levels.

1. **China InP export-permit risk (the #1 issue).** On Feb 4, 2025, China added InP to its export-control list; all three substrate families now require per-shipment Ministry of Commerce permits. AXT received initial InP permits for Europe and Japan on June 11, 2025 and continues to receive some, but holds a backlog of orders awaiting permits and cannot predict timing. No InP or GaAs permits to the U.S. have been granted (U.S. GaAs customers are deemed dual-use/military). The bull thesis, particularly U.S. hyperscaler adoption, is hostage to a foreign government's discretionary licensing.

2. **100% China manufacturing in the tariff crossfire.** U.S. tariffs on AXT substrates escalated to 70% in 2025. The Feb 2026 Supreme Court invalidation of IEEPA tariffs helps, but Section 232/301 remain, and China's new Foreign Trade Law (effective Mar 1, 2026) expands its export-restriction powers. The company is also exposed to mandatory air-pollution factory shutdowns (precedent: a 10-day shutdown in Q1 2018).

3. **Tongmei STAR Market IPO redemption overhang.** AXT raised ~$49M from 10 PRC private-equity funds (2020-21) toward a Shanghai STAR Market listing of Tongmei, now carried as a redeemable noncontrolling interest at $38.5M. If the IPO fails CSRC approval or is cancelled, investors can force redemption at ~$49M. The IPO has been pending with the CSRC since August 2022, stalled nearly four years.

4. **Securities litigation.** A putative securities class action (filed May 2024) names the company, CEO Morris Young, and CFO Gary Fischer, class period March 2021-April 2024; motion to dismiss pending. A dismissed derivative suit is on appeal to the 9th Circuit.

5. **Valuation/volatility/squeeze dynamics.** The company itself warns of stock-price volatility and short-squeeze risk. Customer concentration (top 5 = 32% of revenue), silicon/SOI substitution risk, gallium commodity swings, and structural ASP erosion are recurring.

6. **Governance.** Combined CEO/Chairman roles, a classified board, an adjourned 2026 Annual Meeting (May 15, worth monitoring as a possible proxy/quorum issue), and the death of the audit-committee chair in July 2025 that briefly put AXT out of Nasdaq compliance.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing imminent stress. Near-term ATM IV is 190.5% against 1-year historical volatility of 132.2%, rich by roughly 58 points, in the 86th percentile of the current chain. The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 190.5% > 6-month 147.1%), and the near-term put/call skew is elevated at +15.3%, indicating meaningful put demand into the front-week expiry with an implied move of ±11%. This is consistent with a market hedging a binary outcome rather than expressing directional confidence. Short interest is 13.2% of float with only 0.7 days to cover, elevated but not crowded, and the low days-to-cover means squeeze fuel is limited. The options configuration supports caution rather than a high-conviction short.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case

AXT is a scarce, technically differentiated InP supplier at the start of a multi-year AI optical-interconnect demand cycle. The backlog converts, the capacity doubling executes, China grants broadening export permits including eventually to the U.S., and the company swings durably to profitability. Gross margins, already at 29.6% and rising, expand further on mix and scale. If AXT reaches $250-300M+ of revenue at mid-teens net margins by 2028 and the market sustains a premium multiple, the equity could grow into and beyond today's valuation. The over-capitalized balance sheet (~$600M+ cash) fully funds the ramp. Bull case: ~$143 (recent high), roughly +29% from current.

### Bear Case

The valuation already prices a flawless 2028 outcome, leaving no margin for the binary risks. A China export-permit delay or a U.S.-permit denial strands a meaningful slice of the backlog; the Tongmei redemption right is triggered for ~$49M; tariffs compress the economics of any U.S.-bound volume; or the AI optical cycle proves cyclical as the 2022→2023 halving did. Insiders (CEO, CFO, two directors) have already monetized the spike across $36-113 with no buying. Multiple compression from 52x toward even 15x forward sales, on revenue near the consensus path, implies a price in the $30-45 range. Bear case: ~$40, roughly -64% from current.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria.** We would reconsider a constructive stance on: (1) forward price/sales compressing into the low double digits or below; (2) a confirmed shift to sustained GAAP profitability with positive operating cash flow over at least two consecutive quarters; (3) granting of U.S. InP export permits, removing the single largest constraint on the backlog conversion thesis; and (4) resolution of the Tongmei IPO/redemption overhang and the securities class action. A clear signal of insider alignment (open-market purchases rather than systematic selling) would also materially change the picture.

**Timeline.** The permit, tariff, and litigation overhangs are likely to persist through at least 2026 and possibly into 2027 given the CSRC IPO has been stalled since 2022. The valuation gap is unlikely to close on fundamentals alone within a 12-month horizon; it would more plausibly close through multiple compression. We do not expect the headwinds that justify the AVOID to resolve within the next 6-12 months.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst.** The clearest near-term catalysts are quarterly earnings (next print following the 4/30/26 report), where any backlog-conversion shortfall or permit-driven revenue mismatch would disappoint a market priced for perfection, and any China export-permit news flow. There is no single dated binary event that reliably resolves the thesis; the overhangs (Tongmei redemption, litigation, U.S. permits) are open-ended. The strongest bearish marker is the insider selling, which is informative but not a dated catalyst.

**Timing window.** Diffuse. The valuation is extreme today, but extreme valuations can persist for quarters in a momentum-driven AI name. Without a hard dated trigger, the carry on a short is open-ended.

**Short interest and squeeze risk.** The elevated short interest confirms institutional skepticism, but the very low days-to-cover means a squeeze, while possible on positive permit or backlog news, would be brief. The stock's realized volatility (132% annualized) and near-term implied vol (190%) make adverse moves of ±20-30% in days entirely plausible.

**Borrow and options signal.** The put-heavy skew confirms the bearish lean is partly priced. High near-term IV implies elevated borrow/carry cost. Buying puts is expensive; the options market is already discounting downside.

**Downside target and risk/reward.** Bear target ~$40 (-64%) on multiple compression; bull/squeeze risk to ~$143 (+29%) or higher on a permit catalyst. That is roughly a 2:1 reward-to-risk on the numbers, but the absence of a dated catalyst, the open-ended carry, the 190% implied vol, and the demonstrated capacity for violent upside spikes make the practical risk far higher than the static ratio suggests.

**Verdict.** **NOT SHORTABLE.** The valuation is indefensible and the insider behavior is a genuine warning, but there is no dated catalyst to force the repricing, the stock can spike 20-30% on any export-permit headline, and 190% near-term implied vol makes the carry and gap risk punishing. This is a stock to avoid owning, not to actively short.

## Conclusion

AXT presents a genuine fundamental improvement: InP demand for AI optical interconnect is real, the backlog has more than doubled in a quarter, gross margins have swung positive over the year, and the balance sheet is now over-capitalized to fund a credible capacity build. The growth and competitive trajectory are the strongest parts of the case.

The valuation has run far ahead of that reality. At ~52x forward sales and well over 100x even the optimistic forward EPS, the equity prices in a flawless 2028-2029 outcome while discounting none of the binary China export-permit, tariff, Tongmei-redemption, and litigation risks. The analyst target distribution sits entirely below the current price. Most decisively, the CEO, CFO, and two directors have sold systematically into the rally across a $36-113 range with no open-market buying, behavior that contradicts the bull narrative far more than it confirms it. We rate AXTI an **AVOID**. At current levels the risk/reward is sharply skewed to the downside, though we do not recommend shorting given the absence of a dated catalyst and the violent two-way volatility.
