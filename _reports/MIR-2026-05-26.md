## Executive Summary

Mirion Technologies is a global radiation safety and measurement company benefiting from the nuclear power renaissance driven by AI/data center energy demand and aging reactor fleet replacement cycles. The company achieved its first full-year profitability in FY2025 (+$30M net income vs. -$37M prior year) and delivered Q1 2026 orders growth of 42% (19% organic). Management's 30% Adjusted EBITDA margin target by 2028 (vs. 24.6% in FY2025) is credible given amortization tailwinds and operating leverage.

However, the stock sits 39% below its 52-week high of $30.28, analyst consensus targets cluster around $27-29 (implying 45-60% upside from current $18.35), and the sell-side maintains a 9/10 Buy or Strong Buy consensus. The specific impediment to a BUY recommendation is the combination of: (1) declining near-term EPS estimate revisions (-16.4% for the current quarter over 30 days), (2) 15.6% short interest reflecting institutional skepticism about earnings quality/sustainability, and (3) $1.87B in goodwill from an acquisition-driven strategy where the two most recent deals ($670M combined, closed H2 2025) have not yet demonstrated integration success. The 30-day estimate revision trend is actively negative, meaning the market is adjusting downward on Mirion's near-term earnings power precisely as the stock approaches convertible note conversion prices. This creates a falsifiable test: if Q2 2026 earnings (likely August 2026) deliver organic revenue growth above 8% and management reaffirms the 2028 margin trajectory, the estimate revision trend should reverse, and the thesis upgrades to BUY.

## Company Overview

Mirion Technologies provides radiation detection, measurement, and monitoring solutions across two segments: Nuclear & Safety (66% of revenue) and Medical (34%). Products are installed in 98%+ of global nuclear power plants and 80%+ of cancer treatment centers. The company serves 23 of 32 NATO militaries and major U.S. government agencies.

The business model features high recurring revenue from an entrenched installed base, long-cycle contracts (12-60 months in Nuclear), and regulatory qualification barriers that limit competitive entry. Mirion was formed through a 2021 SPAC merger and has completed 18 acquisitions since 2016, most recently Certrec ($83M, July 2025) and Paragon Energy Solutions ($588M, December 2025).

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:**
Revenue grew from $717.8M (FY2022) to $925.4M (FY2025), a 29% increase over three years (~9% CAGR). Q1 2026 revenue of $257.6M (+27.5% YoY) includes Paragon/Certrec contributions. Organic growth has been running ~7-8% annually, now supplemented by acquired growth.

**Profitability Inflection:**
The profitability improvement is genuine and structural:
- Gross margin: 43.2% → 46.4% → 47.4% (FY2023 → FY2025)
- Operating income: -$22M → +$25M → +$52M
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 22.6% → 25.5% → 24.6%
- Net income: -$97M → -$37M → +$30M

A meaningful contributor is amortization roll-off: purchase accounting amortization declined from $131M (FY2023) to $102M (FY2025), and this decline will continue as legacy intangibles from the 2021 SPAC transaction fully amortize. This delivers essentially zero-effort earnings growth of $15-20M annually.

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow reached $143M in FY2025 (up from $99M), with free cash flow of $107M (11.6% FCF margin). Capex is modest at $36M. The FCF/net income ratio is healthy given the GAAP EPS is suppressed by amortization.

**Balance Sheet:**
Cash of $412M provides substantial liquidity. Management executed a sophisticated capital structure transformation: $775M in convertible notes at blended ~0.1% interest rates funded two acquisitions while paying down $245M of floating-rate debt. Net interest expense dropped 41% ($51M → $30M). The term loan ($450M) matures in 2032, and convertibles mature in 2030-2031.

The concerns: goodwill of $1.87B represents 52% of total assets, and the Altman Z-Score of 1.93 sits in the grey zone. The retained deficit of -$513M reflects cumulative legacy losses. Current ratio of 2.83x is strong for near-term obligations.

**Backlog:**
Remaining performance obligations surged 36% to $1.1B, with 49% expected to convert to revenue in 2026 (~$541M). This provides meaningful forward visibility and supports the full-year guidance.

## Growth Analysis

**Secular Tailwinds:**
The nuclear renaissance is real and multi-vector:
- 440 NPPs operating globally, 70 under construction, 115 more planned within 15 years
- AI/data center demand driving recommissioning (Palisades, Three Mile Island, Duane Arnold)
- IAEA raised nuclear capacity projections for the 5th consecutive year
- SMR market emerging as a potential step-function demand increase

**Organic Growth Drivers:**
- Aging reactor fleet requiring replacement/upgrade instrumentation
- Dosimetry privatization in Europe
- Nuclear medicine/radiopharmaceutical expansion
- Digital platform migration (SunCHECK, Vital Platform)
- SMR market (longer-term, contingent on developer success)

**Growth Targets:**
Management targets 30% Adjusted EBITDA margin by 2028. On an estimated revenue base of ~$1.2B+ (assuming ~8% organic growth plus Paragon annualization), this implies ~$360M+ EBITDA. Q1 2026 orders growth of 42% total / 19% organic provides early validation.

**Estimate Revision Concern:**
Current quarter EPS estimate has declined from $0.12 to $0.10 over 30 days (-16.4%). Full-year estimates have drifted from $0.55 to $0.54 (-1.9%). While modest in absolute terms, falling estimates during an ostensible growth acceleration warrant caution. The revisions suggest analysts are adjusting for integration costs or delayed Paragon revenue conversion.

## Valuation Assessment

At $18.35 with ~245M shares outstanding, Mirion's market cap is approximately $4.5B. Enterprise value is approximately $4.5B + $450M term loan + $775M convertibles - $412M cash ≈ $5.3B.

**Current multiples:**
- EV/FY2025 EBITDA: ~23x ($228M adjusted EBITDA)
- P/E trailing: ~167x (GAAP EPS $0.11, heavily depressed by amortization)
- EV/Revenue: ~5.7x
- Price/FCF: ~42x ($107M FY2025 FCF)

**Forward view:**
- If 2028 EBITDA reaches $360M at 30% margins on $1.2B revenue: EV/EBITDA = ~14.7x (two years out)
- FY2026 consensus EPS of $0.54 implies ~34x forward P/E
- FY2027 consensus of $0.65 implies ~28x forward P/E

**Peer comparison:**
Peer median P/E is 23.8x. MIR at ~34x FY2026E is at a premium, but peers (VNT at 10x, ITRI at 13x) lack comparable nuclear exposure or growth profiles. The more relevant comp is BWXT (BWX Technologies), which trades at similar forward multiples given shared nuclear tailwinds.

**Assessment:** The valuation is neither cheap nor stretched when evaluated on forward earnings. The 30% EBITDA margin target by 2028 provides a credible path to ~$360M EBITDA, at which point the current EV implies a reasonable ~15x multiple. The risk is that this path has execution dependencies (integration, SMR timing, organic growth sustainability) that the multiple already partially reflects. The analyst consensus target of $27-29 implies the market assumes successful execution, which is the base case but far from certain given declining near-term estimates.

## Competitive Landscape

Mirion's competitive position is strong and improving:

1. **Breadth advantage:** No single competitor spans nuclear safety + medical radiation + dosimetry + nuclear power plant lifecycle services. Thermo Fisher, Curtiss-Wright, and Fortive compete in individual segments but lack the integrated offering.

2. **Installed base entrenchment:** 98%+ nuclear plant penetration creates switching costs due to regulatory qualification requirements and 50+ year customer relationships.

3. **Paragon/Certrec strategic logic:** The acquisition strategy creates a lifecycle value chain (licensing → regulatory → monitoring → parts/engineering) that simplifies nuclear utility supply chains. This integration thesis is strategically sound but execution-dependent.

4. **SMR positioning:** If SMRs commercialize on schedule, Mirion's radiation monitoring/control systems would be required for every unit. First-mover advantage in a new reactor type creates potential long-duration growth.

5. **Medical segment trajectory:** Margins expanding from 4.6% to 15.1% in two years suggests operational improvement is real, though the 10-K notes "softer demand in cancer care end-market in certain geographies."

## Risk Assessment

**High-Impact Risks:**

1. **Integration execution (probability: 30-40% of meaningful stumble).** Two major acquisitions ($670M) closed in H2 2025, less than a year ago. The 10-K's decentralized "entrepreneurial" operating model is a strength for retention but creates coordination challenges. Paragon adds ~150 employees to a 3,281-person organization.

2. **SMR timeline dependency.** The 10-K explicitly warns SMR developers "face multiple commercialization obstacles" and "may fail operationally." If the SMR thesis delays 3-5 years beyond current expectations, Paragon's strategic premium diminishes.

3. **Convertible dilution.** The $400M convertible at $23.11 conversion price is only 26% above current price. If the stock approaches $23, the 0.25% note creates ~17M shares of potential dilution. The $375M note at $28.82 adds another ~13M shares. Capped calls partially hedge this, but the overhang creates a valuation ceiling effect.

4. **Short interest at 15.6%** with 7.1 days to cover signals meaningful institutional bearishness. The short thesis likely centers on: (a) earnings quality concerns (heavy adjustments to GAAP), (b) integration risk from rapid M&A, and/or (c) the view that nuclear enthusiasm will fade before earnings materialize.

5. **Russian exposure.** $103M in Russian-related backlog (~9% of total). Ongoing sanctions complications and a prior $21M damages claim settlement create persistent headline risk.

**Moderate Risks:**
- Fixed-price contract overruns on long-cycle nuclear projects
- Tariff exposure on 37% non-North American revenue
- Customer concentration in nuclear utilities (any regulatory freeze could impact order intake)
- Officer departure flagged in April 2026 8-K

## Conclusion

Mirion Technologies possesses genuine competitive advantages, structural tailwinds from the nuclear renaissance, and a credible path to materially higher profitability by 2028. The capital structure engineering has been excellent, and the $1.1B backlog provides forward visibility. Analyst consensus overwhelmingly favors BUY with targets of $26-29.

The impediment to upgrading at current levels is specific and measurable: near-term EPS estimates are declining, short interest at 15.6% reflects informed institutional skepticism, and two major recent acquisitions have not yet demonstrated integration success in reported financials. The trailing ~167x P/E, while misleading given amortization distortion, highlights that the stock requires sustained growth execution to justify any premium. The market appears to be overestimating near-term earnings while the integration plays out, which is the opposite of what a BUY thesis requires.

The stock should be held for the nuclear sector tailwind and the approaching profitability inflection. The Q2 2026 earnings report is the key decision point. If organic growth and margin trajectory confirm management's vision, the estimate revision trend should turn positive, and the stock becomes a BUY with $24+ as the base case target.
