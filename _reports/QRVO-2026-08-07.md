## Executive Summary

Qorvo has ceased to be an equity research problem in the conventional sense. Since October 27, 2025 the company has been under a signed merger agreement with Skyworks Solutions, under which each Qorvo share converts into 0.960 Skyworks shares plus $32.50 in cash. Both shareholder bases approved the transaction on February 11, 2026, which extinguished Qorvo's ability to accept a competing bid. The realizable value of a Qorvo share is therefore a mechanical function of one variable: Skyworks' share price. At Skyworks' August 7 quote of $69.62, consideration is worth $99.34 (0.960 × $69.62 + $32.50), a gross spread of 1.16% against Qorvo's $98.20. At Skyworks' own 23-analyst consensus target of $68.25, a target that sits below spot after eight of nine post-earnings revisions were cuts, consideration is worth $98.02, or 0.2% below today's price.

That arithmetic is the entire recommendation. A 1.16% gross spread annualizes to roughly 2.8% on a December 31, 2026 close and 1.8% on a March 31, 2027 close, both below the short end of the Treasury curve. Against that sub-cash carry sits an estimated 10-20% loss if the transaction fails, a $100M reverse termination fee (about $1.13 per share) that provides negligible protection, and a merger-agreement covenant capping Skyworks' divestiture obligation at $100M of aggregate annual revenue, a clause that permits a regulator demanding more to end the deal without Skyworks breaching. Probability-weighting a ~92% close (implied by the spread) against a $85 break price produces expected return of roughly zero to slightly negative over the next five months. Approximately 67% of the consideration is unhedged Skyworks equity, so an unhedged Qorvo holder is running a leveraged, cash-cushioned long position in a company its own analyst community rates Hold.

The operating business has improved materially and verifiably since announcement. Non-GAAP gross margin expanded 880 basis points year over year to 52.8% in the June quarter on declining revenue, traceable to five documented footprint actions rather than cyclical leverage. FY2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance was raised from "approaching $7.00" to "above $7.00," consensus sits at $7.68, and the High Performance Analog defense franchise grew 50% at a 33.9% segment margin. None of this is monetizable at a price pinned to the exchange ratio, and it is why the correct action is to trim rather than avoid: the standalone asset is worth more than it was in October 2025, which raises the floor in a break scenario. This report moves off three prior HOLD calls (4.5 in March, 5.6 in May, 5.5 in July) because the stock rose 9.7% in four sessions on apparent deal-certainty news and now trades at or above the value the exchange ratio can deliver, not because fundamentals deteriorated.

## Company Overview

Qorvo designs and manufactures radio-frequency, analog mixed-signal, connectivity and power semiconductors across three reportable segments (FY2026, fiscal year ended March 28, 2026):

| Segment | FY2026 revenue | % of total | YoY | Segment op. margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Advanced Cellular (ACG) | $2,551.2M | 69.4% | — | 26.2% (from 23.1%) |
| High Performance Analog (HPA) | $705.7M | 19.2% | +10.7% | 26.8% (from 17.1%) |
| Connectivity & Sensors (CSG) | $421.7M | 11.5% | −10.8% | −10.0% |

ACG supplies RF front-end modules, envelope-tracking power management, antenna tuners and filters for smartphones and wearables. HPA covers defense and aerospace RF, cellular and broadband infrastructure, and industrial power management; its Richardson, Texas facility holds U.S. DoD Category 1A "Trusted Source" accreditation for GaN/GaAs design, fabrication, packaging and test, which is a slow-to-replicate qualification advantage. CSG addresses ultra-wideband, Matter, Bluetooth LE, Zigbee, Thread and automotive V2X, and has been loss-making.

Customer concentration is the defining structural feature: Apple, served indirectly through contract manufacturers, was 50% of FY2026 revenue, up from 47% in FY2025. Samsung was 10% in both years. Combined top-two exposure was 59%, 57% and 58% across FY2026, FY2025 and FY2024. Headcount was approximately 5,200 across 23 countries, 68% in engineering and technician roles, with roughly 2,700 patents expiring between 2026 and 2044 and no material dependence on any single patent per the 10-K.

Remaining internal fabs after the restructuring program are Oregon and Texas; internal assembly and test is Germany and the U.S.

## The Skyworks Transaction: Mechanics and Arithmetic

**Consideration.** 0.960 Skyworks shares plus $32.50 cash per Qorvo share. Skyworks is the acquirer; Qorvo survives as a wholly-owned subsidiary before a second-step merger into an LLC.

**Consideration sensitivity to Skyworks' price** (0.960 × SWKS + $32.50, versus the $98.20 basis):

| SWKS price | QRVO consideration | Return from $98.20 |
|---|---|---|
| $55.00 | $85.30 | −13.1% |
| $60.00 | $90.10 | −8.2% |
| $65.00 | $94.90 | −3.4% |
| **$68.25** (SWKS consensus PT) | **$98.02** | **−0.2%** |
| **$69.62** (Aug 7 spot) | **$99.34** | **+1.2%** |
| $75.00 | $104.50 | +6.4% |
| $80.00 | $109.30 | +11.3% |
| $90.90 (SWKS 52-wk high) | $119.76 | +22.0% |

**Dilution.** New Skyworks shares issued = 0.960 × 88.22M = 84.69M, equal to 56.3% of Skyworks' 150.47M pre-deal count. Post-issuance count ≈ 235.16M; former Qorvo holders own 84.69/235.16 = 36.0% of the combined company, and legacy Skyworks holders are diluted from 100% to 64.0%. These are the same transaction described two ways and should not be conflated.

**Cash requirement.** $32.50 × 88.22M = $2.867B. Skyworks has been reported preparing roughly $2B of debt, has suspended its dividend, authorized a $2B repurchase program as the combined company's capital framework, and filed an automatic mixed securities shelf around August 3, 2026.

**Regulatory and deal protection.** FTC Second Requests were issued to both parties on February 5, 2026. Two Skyworks Item 8.01 8-Ks were filed August 3, 2026, and secondary reporting states the HSR waiting period has expired; the primary filing text was not retrievable, so treat U.S. antitrust clearance as reported but unverified. The outside date is April 27, 2027, extendable twice to October 27, 2027. Termination fees are $298.7M each way (~$3.39/share), with only a $100M reverse fee (~$1.13/share) if the deal fails on antitrust or foreign-investment grounds. The efforts covenant caps Skyworks' divestiture obligation at $100M of aggregate annual revenue and excludes behavioral remedies material to the combined company. Because shareholders have already adopted the agreement, no Superior Proposal is available. Section 368(a) reorganization treatment is intended but is not a closing condition and no IRS ruling was sought; the $32.50 is likely taxable boot regardless.

Management moved from "early calendar 2027" (10-K) to "increasingly hopeful that the transaction will close within the calendar year" (Q1 FY2027 10-Q, filed July 28, 2026).

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue has been flat for four years.** FY2023 $3,569.4M; FY2024 $3,769.5M (+5.6%); FY2025 $3,719.0M (−1.3%); FY2026 $3,678.5M (−1.1%). Q1 FY2027 revenue was $784.8M, down 4.2%. That quarter must not be annualized: the September quarter is the Apple ramp (FY2026 Q2 was approximately $1,058M), and FY2027 is a 53-week year with a 14-week second quarter.

**The margin story is real and structural.**

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2026 | Q1 FY26 | Q1 FY27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAAP gross margin | 41.3% | 45.9% | 40.5% | 51.1% |
| Non-GAAP gross margin | — | — | 44.0% | **52.8%** |
| GAAP operating margin | 2.6% | 11.2% | 3.7% | 12.3% |
| Non-GAAP operating margin | — | — | 13.2% | **22.6%** |
| GAAP diluted EPS | $0.58 | $3.62 | $0.27 | $0.96 |
| Non-GAAP diluted EPS | — | — | $0.92 | **$1.64** |

The 880bp non-GAAP gross-margin expansion occurred on declining revenue, which rules out volume leverage as the explanation. It traces to five documented actions: SiC power devices sold January 2025 ($117.5M), China assembly and test divested in FY2025 ($55.6M), MEMS sensing sold October 2025 ($21.5M), the North Carolina fab sold in Q4 FY2026 ($36.8M proceeds, $7.9M gain), and the Costa Rica assembly and test facility closed in Q3 FY2026. Accrued restructuring fell from $29.3M to $11.5M and management states no further material charges are expected. A second contributor is mix: HPA rose from 16.8% to 26.3% of revenue at 33.9% segment margin, while ACG revenue fell 16.6% year over year but its segment margin rose from 17.1% to 22.8%, a deliberate revenue-for-margin trade.

**Segment detail, Q1 FY2027 versus Q1 FY2026:** HPA revenue $206.3M (+50.1%), operating margin 33.9% versus 15.7%; CSG $101.9M (−7.5%), margin 2.9% versus −6.8% (first profit in the disclosed periods); ACG $476.6M (−16.6%), margin 22.8% versus 17.1%; unallocated −$84.7M.

**Cash flow and balance sheet.** FY2026 operating cash flow $808.6M, capex $129.1M, free cash flow $679.6M (18.5% of revenue). Q1 FY2027 OCF was $139.5M versus $182.9M, but this is working-capital timing: net income rose $60.2M while inventories consumed $39.0M of cash versus releasing $4.7M a year earlier (attributed to customer launch timing), and receivables released $2.9M versus $58.2M. Capex intensity fell to 3.1% of quarterly revenue.

At June 27, 2026: cash $1,328.9M; long-term debt $1,549.1M ($850M 4.375% notes due October 2029, $700M 3.375% due April 2031); **net debt $220.2M**; current ratio 3.54; undrawn $325M revolver; total equity $3,471.9M; goodwill $2,353.2M (67.8% of equity). Of the cash, $1,133.8M sits in foreign subsidiaries with $914.3M in Singapore.

The screened Altman Z-Score of 2.81 (grey zone) is misleading here. Retained earnings were $38.8M against $5,825.6M of assets (0.7%) at FY2026 year-end because a decade of buybacks and the FY2024 loss depleted the account, and retained earnings/assets carries a 1.4 weight in the original model. Asset turnover of 0.63 is depressed by the goodwill balance. Against $1.33B cash, $220M net debt, no maturity before October 2029 and $679.6M of trailing FCF, near-term solvency risk is not a live concern. The screen's flags on capital efficiency (0.63× turnover, goodwill at 67.8% of equity, $82.4M of FY2026 impairments and $192.6M in FY2025) are legitimate on their own terms.

**Capital allocation.** FY2024 $403.0M repurchased, FY2025 $358.8M, FY2026 $536.7M (of which $400M in Q4 alone), Q1 FY2027 zero with $416.2M of authorization unused, consistent with merger interim operating restrictions. Diluted shares fell from 93.8M to 89.4M year over year. No dividend.

Earnings growth is running well ahead of revenue growth, and both the beat record and revision trend are constructive:

Four consecutive beats with widening magnitude. Estimate revisions: current quarter $2.11 (+9.8% in 30 days, +11.0% in 90 days), current year $7.68 (+11.3% / +9.8%), next year $7.96 (+1.7% / +0.5%). Consensus of $7.68 for FY2027 sits above the company's own "above $7.00" guide, which the beat record makes reasonable.

Two qualifications. First, revisions of +9.8% over 90 days fall well short of the threshold at which revision velocity becomes a standalone directional signal, and next-year revisions are essentially flat at +0.5%. Second, and more important, revisions do not transmit to the share price here. The price is a function of the exchange ratio, which is why the stock declined on a 49% non-GAAP EPS beat on July 28. Fundamental momentum matters in this name only through its effect on the break-scenario floor and on the earnings the combined entity inherits.

Where growth genuinely exists: HPA defense and aerospace, driven by Golden Dome, radar modernization, migration to higher frequency bands, the shift toward large numbers of smaller networked platforms, allied defense funding and LEO satellite communications. HPA at 26.3% of revenue and 33.9% margin is the highest-quality revenue in the company. Power management for enterprise SSDs also benefits from AI data-center build-out.

Where it does not: revenue has not grown in four years, CSG is shrinking against Broadcom, Qualcomm, NXP, Murata, Nordic and Silicon Labs, ACG is being deliberately shrunk, and China-headquartered revenue fell 50.6% year over year to $77.0M. Whether that China decline reflects Android weakness, share loss to Maxscend, Vanchip and RichWave, or export-control effects is not disclosed; all three readings are structural rather than cyclical.

Guidance quality has degraded. The July 28 release states Qorvo "has discontinued conducting conference calls and providing forward-looking guidance" while simultaneously raising the full-year non-GAAP outlook. The practical effect is an annual EPS number with no quarterly revenue guidance, no analyst Q&A, and no management commentary beyond prepared quotes until closing.

## Valuation Assessment

**Standalone metrics** (88.22M shares at $98.20 = $8.664B market cap; EV = $8.884B including $220.2M net debt):

- EV / FY2026 FCF ($679.6M) = **13.1×**
- EV / FY2026 non-GAAP operating income ($797.0M) = **11.1×**
- P/E on FY2027 guided non-GAAP EPS above $7.00 = **14.0× or less**
- P/E on FY2027 consensus $7.68 = **12.8×**; on FY2028 consensus $7.96 = **12.3×**
- Trailing P/E on FY2026 GAAP EPS of $3.62 = **27.1×**

Note a data conflict: the supplied profile reports both trailing and forward P/E at 22.08, which is not reproducible from $98.20 and $3.62 (27.1×) and appears to reflect a stale price near $80. I use the reproducible figures above.

On those standalone numbers the business is not expensive. The problem is that standalone cheapness is unmonetizable. Realizable value is capped at 0.960 × SWKS + $32.50, and only a deal break, an outcome that would itself likely compress the multiple, converts standalone value into market value.

**Peer comparison is largely uninformative.** Of five same-size semiconductor comparables, three carry negative trailing P/Es (ALGM −595.7, SLAB −142.8, MXL −53.2), producing a median of 68.8 that reflects the group's loss-making state rather than any usable multiple. Skyworks at 30.2× trailing GAAP is the only relevant comparable, and its listed 4.0% dividend yield is stale, since the dividend was suspended July 28, 2026. The cleaner read is that both Qorvo and Skyworks trade at low-double-digit multiples of forward non-GAAP earnings because RF front-end is a mature, customer-concentrated business inside an industry where value has migrated to AI compute.

**The decisive valuation point.** At $98.20 the stock trades 0.2% above the consideration implied by Skyworks' own consensus target and 1.2% below the consideration implied by Skyworks' spot price. Probability-weighted expected return over roughly five months to a December close:

- Close with SWKS flat/at consensus (50% of close scenarios): ≈ +0.5%
- Close with SWKS −15% (25%): ≈ −9%
- Close with SWKS +15% (25%): ≈ +11%
- Weighted within-close outcome: **+0.75%**
- Break at an estimated $85 (mid of an $80-$90 range): **−13.4%**
- At 92% close / 8% break: 0.92 × 0.75% + 0.08 × (−13.4%) ≈ **−0.4%**

Roughly zero expected return against approximately 1.7% risk-free over the same period. The valuation score of 4.0 reflects that asymmetry, not any view that the underlying earnings stream is overpriced.

A fair counterpoint: the break floor is probably higher than the $8B pre-announcement reference implies, because FY2027 guided EPS above $7.00 and a restored $416.2M buyback authorization did not exist in October 2025. Twelve to fourteen times $7.00 is $84-98. That argues the bear case is nearer $85 than $78, which is how I have modeled it. It does not create upside.

## Competitive Landscape

The merger is an admission that neither party could independently resist pricing pressure from a customer representing about half of each company's revenue. Qorvo's own 10-K lists Skyworks as a direct competitor in both ACG and CSG, and observes that "in some cases, our competitors are also our customers or suppliers," a reference to Qualcomm, Broadcom and Apple's in-house RF ambitions. Combined revenue of roughly $7.7B ($3.68B Qorvo FY2026 plus $4.01B Skyworks trailing) remains smaller than the relevant Broadcom and Qualcomm franchises.

The five-year record is the industry's verdict on the segment. Proxy pay-versus-performance TSR indexed to $100 at FY2021 year-end: Qorvo 40.10 versus a semiconductor peer group at 470.02, approximately −60% against +370%. Net income over the span went $1,033.3M → $103.2M → −$70.3M → $55.6M → $339.0M.

Position by segment: HPA is improving on a genuine moat (DoD Trusted Source accreditation, in-house GaN/GaAs, direct government engagement, multi-year program visibility). ACG is trading share for margin, which is the right response to a concentrated customer but leaves the socket-loss risk intact. CSG is losing against better-capitalized incumbents and already triggered $82.4M of FY2026 impairments ($36.5M goodwill, $45.9M intangibles) within the segment.

Sell-side positioning: 1 strong buy, 2 buy, 16 hold, 1 sell, stable across four months. Target range $66 to $105, mean $90.77, median $95.00. The mean sits 7.6% below the current price, and TD Cowen's recent raise to $95 is also below spot. On Skyworks, consensus is Hold at $68.25 with eight of nine post-earnings revisions being cuts (TD Cowen $75→$65, RBC $80→$70, BofA $70→$65, JPMorgan $70→$65, Morgan Stanley $76→$72, Mizuho $55→$52, Stifel $75→$70, Citi $77→$64; UBS $65→$70 the lone raise). Because two-thirds of Qorvo's payoff is Skyworks equity, that cluster of cuts is directly material.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Deal break with a capped remedy obligation.** Skyworks need not divest more than $100M of aggregate annual revenue nor accept material behavioral remedies. Given direct overlap in both ACG and CSG, a regulator demanding more can end the transaction without Skyworks breaching, and Qorvo holders would receive only the $100M reverse fee (~$1.13/share) against a 10-20% price gap.

**2. Acquirer equity risk, the dominant marked-to-market exposure.** 67% of consideration is Skyworks stock trading above its own consensus target. Skyworks' broad-markets growth decelerated from +30% to +15% year over year between its Q2 and Q3, and it reported $935M revenue with $1.08 non-GAAP EPS on July 28. A 20% Skyworks decline takes Qorvo consideration to about $86.

**3. Unverified regulatory status.** HSR expiration is reported in secondary sources and circumstantially corroborated by two Skyworks Item 8.01 8-Ks on August 3, but the primary text was not retrievable. Which non-U.S. jurisdictions remain outstanding, China SAMR in particular given Qorvo's China revenue and the export-control environment, is not identified. This is the largest single open question.

**4. Apple concentration, worsening.** 50% of FY2026 revenue, up from 47%, with the 10-K explicitly noting customers provide no guarantee of inclusion in next-generation designs. The geographic mix shift (U.S. revenue to $509.0M from $425.3M, China to $77.0M from $155.9M) reflects HPA growth and China Android weakness; it is not evidence of customer diversification, since the directly disclosed concentration metric moved adversely.

**5. Goodwill and CSG.** $2,353.2M of goodwill equals 67.8% of equity, and CSG already triggered impairment in FY2026. A standalone plan reset following a break would be the most likely trigger for further write-downs.

**6. Manufacturing transition.** SAW filter production is transferring from the sold North Carolina fab to Texas under a short-term supply agreement with the buyer, with $15.0M of FY2026 and $3.7M of Q1 FY2027 start-up costs already incurred. A yield or timing problem lands directly on gross margin, and the interim third-party dependence on a critical filter technology is a time-boxed vulnerability.

**7. Defense concentration is now two-sided.** HPA at 26% of revenue and the highest margin means a Golden Dome or radar program disruption would damage both the growth and the margin narrative simultaneously.

**8. Tax treatment.** Section 368(a) qualification is intended, is not a closing condition, and no IRS ruling was sought. Taxable holders face certain tax on the $32.50 cash and uncertainty on the stock portion.

**9. Disclosure quality.** No conference calls, no quarterly guidance, no analyst Q&A until closing. The July 28 release and the 10-Q constitute the full information set.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility is 48.2% against 36.7% trailing one-year realized, an 11.5-point premium at the 90th percentile of the current chain, with a backwardated term structure (48.2% at 13 days versus 42.5% at 104 days) and a ±7.4% implied move on the nearest expiry. For a security trading 1.2% from its deal value, that is a large priced move. Put/call skew is 5.5% with the data source characterizing it as elevated put demand, though volume and open-interest ratios are strikingly call-tilted (0.12 volume, 0.05 open interest), so the positioning read is mixed rather than clearly bearish. ATM IV has compressed from 60.7% to 48.2% since July 29 as the price rose 6.9%, consistent with partial resolution of an event, plausibly the reported HSR expiration.

My reading: the residual near-term volatility premium is not a Qorvo-fundamental signal but a transmission of Skyworks-linked and closing-timing uncertainty into a security whose payoff is 67% acquirer equity. It corroborates the view that the remaining risk in this position is binary and exogenous. Short interest of 9.0% of float with 5.4 days to cover is consistent with routine merger-arb hedging rather than a directional bear case.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $110 (+12.0%)

Skyworks re-rates toward $80 on AI data-center traction (its own commentary described that demand as running above a prior +50% expectation and supply-constrained), the $2B buyback replacing the dividend supports the shares, and cost synergies from combining two subscale RF suppliers, unquantified in any Qorvo filing, are credited by the market. At SWKS $80, Qorvo consideration is $109.30. The deal closes by December 31, 2026, adding the 1.2% spread. Qorvo's own contribution to the combined entity is better than Skyworks agreed to buy: 52.8% non-GAAP gross margin, HPA growing 50% at 33.9% margin, $679.6M of FCF and $220.2M of net debt.

### Bear Case — $85 (−13.4%), with a tail to $78 (−20.6%)

An outstanding foreign clearance demands remedies exceeding the $100M divestiture cap, or a foreign-investment review stalls past the outside date. Qorvo returns to standalone status and re-rates to 12× guided non-GAAP EPS above $7.00, or roughly $85, cushioned by $1.33B cash, a restored $416.2M buyback and no maturity before October 2029. The deeper tail applies if the break coincides with Skyworks-driven sector derating or an Apple socket loss, in which case Qorvo's own multiple compresses simultaneously and the $8B pre-announcement reference (~$83 on the then-outstanding share count) becomes the anchor rather than the floor. Holders receive $1.13/share from the reverse fee in the antitrust scenario only.

### Base Case — $98 (−0.2%)

The deal closes in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027 with Skyworks near its $68.25 consensus target, delivering $98.02 of consideration. The holder earns nothing over five months, forgoing approximately 1.7% of risk-free return, and ends up owning 36% of a combined company rated Hold by its own analyst community.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: REDUCE.** Trim existing exposure into the current strength; prospective investors should not initiate.

**Base case target: $98** — 0.960 × Skyworks' $68.25 consensus target + $32.50 = $98.02, or −0.2% from the $98.20 basis.
**Bull case target: $110** — 0.960 × $80.00 + $32.50 = $109.30, or +11.3%.
**Bear case target: $85** — 12× FY2027 guided non-GAAP EPS above $7.00 in a standalone break scenario, or −13.4%; tail to $78 (−20.6%) if a break coincides with sector derating.

**De-risking plan.** Reduce materially now, while the stock sits within 1% of the exchange-ratio ceiling and has captured a 9.7% four-session move on apparent deal-certainty news. Holders who wish to retain exposure to the combined entity's synergy potential should retain a residual position sized to their view on Skyworks specifically, and should recognize that view as the entire remaining thesis. Holders unwilling to underwrite Skyworks at $69.62 against a $68.25 consensus target should exit fully. Taxable holders should weight the unresolved Section 368(a) treatment, which is not a closing condition, in their timing.

**Upgrade triggers back to HOLD or better.** (1) Qorvo trades below $92, restoring 6-8% of spread to spot-implied consideration and roughly 15% annualized to a December close. (2) Skyworks consensus targets are revised upward through $75, which would place consideration near $104 and re-establish genuine spread. (3) Primary-filing confirmation of both HSR expiration and the specific remaining foreign clearances, which would compress break probability below current pricing and make even a thin spread defensible.

**Avoid triggers.** (1) Any disclosure that a regulator has demanded remedies above the $100M divestiture cap, or that Skyworks has invoked the antitrust efforts limitation. (2) An outside-date extension past April 27, 2027 without a stated path to closing. (3) Skyworks below $60, which takes consideration to $90 or less and makes the residual stub the dominant loss driver. (4) Evidence of an Apple socket loss for the next design cycle, which would impair both the break floor and the combined company's earnings base.

**Reassessment timeline.** The next natural decision points are any 8-K disclosing satisfaction of remaining regulatory conditions or a definitive closing date; Skyworks' next quarterly report; and closing itself, currently guided by management as "within the calendar year." Absent a break, this position resolves within six months and the analysis then transfers wholly to Skyworks.

## Conclusion

The score improves nowhere and declines in the dimension that matters: valuation. Financial health remains strong at 6.8 — $220.2M of net debt against $1.33B of cash, a 3.54 current ratio, no maturity before October 2029 and $679.6M of trailing free cash flow, tempered by goodwill at 67.8% of equity and 0.63× asset turnover. Growth prospects at 5.2 reflect genuine earnings expansion (FY2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance raised to above $7.00, HPA +50% at 33.9% margin) against four consecutive years of flat-to-declining revenue and a 50.6% collapse in China revenue. Competitive position at 5.0 balances an improving defense franchise with a loss-making connectivity segment and Apple concentration that rose to 50%. Management quality at 5.5 credits a verifiable 880bp margin turnaround and a rational decision to sell after a five-year TSR of −60% against a peer group at +370%, against $13.55M of CEO compensation over that record, net insider selling including approximately $192.3M by the activist director who forced the sale, and no open-market purchases. Risk factors at 4.0 reflect a binary deal outcome with a capped remedy obligation, unverified regulatory status, and 67% of the payoff in unhedged acquirer equity.

The three prior HOLD calls were correct when the spread compensated for the risk. It no longer does.
