## Executive Summary

Boyd Gaming is a geographically diversified gaming operator that recently underwent a meaningful balance sheet transformation via the $1.76B FanDuel stake sale. The proceeds funded aggressive deleveraging (net debt from $3.2B to $2.1B) and $778M in share repurchases in 2025 alone. The share count has declined 25% since 2022, and the stock has outperformed gaming peers by roughly 125 percentage points on a total shareholder return basis.

The current challenge is that underlying operating performance is decelerating. Q1 2026 delivered an 8.5% EPS miss, online segment EBITDAR permanently declined from $108M to a guided $30-35M run-rate, and analyst estimates have been revised downward by 8-9% over 90 days. The options market is pricing significant near-term stress (47.3% ATM IV vs. 26.5% realized, steep backwardation). At ~11.4x forward normalized earnings ($7.20 CY2026E), the stock is cheap relative to the 17x peer median, but that discount appears partially justified by the negative earnings revision cycle and execution risk on the $750M Norfolk development.

The investment merits monitoring for an upgrade if: (1) Las Vegas locals demand stabilizes, (2) Norfolk execution stays on budget/timeline, and (3) estimate revisions bottom. Until those conditions are met, the declining fundamental momentum and elevated options-implied uncertainty argue for patience.

## Company Overview

Boyd Gaming operates 27 gaming entertainment properties across 11 states, organized into four reportable segments: Las Vegas Locals (8 properties, $890M revenue), Downtown Las Vegas (3 properties, $229M), Midwest & South (16 properties, $2,117M), and Online/Boyd Interactive ($708M). The company also manages the Sky River Casino in California under a management agreement generating ~$99M annually in fee income.

Founded in 1975 and still family-controlled (~30% ownership by the Boyd family), the business model is predominantly cash-based gaming operations with 64% of revenue from gaming (primarily slots). The Las Vegas Locals segment specifically targets resident gamblers rather than tourists, creating recurring revenue characteristics unusual for the gaming sector.

## Financial Analysis

**Balance Sheet Transformation Post-FanDuel:**
The July 2025 FanDuel sale was genuinely transformative. Total debt declined from $3.2B to $2.1B, debt-to-equity improved from ~2.0x to ~0.8x (net), and 92% of remaining debt is fixed-rate. The January 2026 credit agreement ($1,450M revolver + $1,200M delayed draw term loan) provides ample liquidity. Boyd has $1,277M in available credit facility capacity.

**Operating Performance — Decelerating:**
- Revenue: $3.56B (2022) → $3.74B (2023) → $3.93B (2024) → $4.09B (2025) → $997M Q1 2026 (+0.6% YoY)
- Adjusted EBITDAR: $1,391M (2024) → $1,354M (2025, -2.6%) → $317M Q1 2026 (-6.0% YoY)
- Normalized operating income declined 19.3% in 2025 (excluding FanDuel gain)
- Online EBITDAR collapsed 41% ($108M → $63M) due to FanDuel access agreement renegotiation

**Cash Flow:**
- Operating cash flow: $977M (2025), strong conversion
- CapEx: $588M (2025), guided $650-700M (2026) — heavy cycle
- Free cash flow: ~$389M (2025), likely ~$275-325M (2026) given elevated capex

**Capital Return:**
- Share repurchases: $778M (2025) + $686M (2024) = $1.46B in 2 years
- Shares outstanding: 109M (2022) → ~77M (2025) — 29% reduction
- Dividend: $0.80/year ($0.20/quarter, raised from $0.72), ~1.0% yield
- Remaining buyback authorization: $362M

The buyback program partially obscures underlying business softness. Q1 2026 EPS of $1.60 was actually higher than Q1 2025's $1.31 despite lower absolute net income ($105.5M vs. $111.4M) purely because of the reduced share count.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term headwinds (2026):**
1. Online EBITDAR permanently declined from $108M to ~$30-35M (guided) — annual ~$70-75M headwind
2. Las Vegas Locals revenue declined 0.5% in 2025; hotel ADR fell 7.8%
3. Suncoast renovation disruption through 2026
4. Consumer spending pressure from inflation
5. Midwest & South segment facing competitive pressure and prior impairments ($72.3M in 2025)

**Growth pipeline (2027+):**
1. Norfolk permanent resort ($750M investment, late 2027 opening): 1,500 slots, 50 tables, 200-room hotel
2. Cadence Crossing (Henderson, NV): Just opened March 2026, "enthusiastic" early response
3. Par-A-Dice new facility (pending regulatory approval)
4. 100% bonus depreciation (OBBBA) provides cash tax benefit during heavy capex cycle

**Estimate trajectory:** Analyst EPS estimates have declined consistently — CY2026 from $7.85 (90 days ago) to $7.20 currently (-8.3%), CY2027 from $8.61 to $7.83 (-9.1%). This negative earnings momentum has not yet stabilized.

Revenue growth is likely to be low-single-digits (2-3%) through 2027 when Norfolk opens, after which the contribution could add 3-5 percentage points if execution is successful. The Norfolk project is both the largest growth opportunity and the largest execution risk in the portfolio.

## Valuation Assessment

The reported trailing P/E of 3.6x is misleading — it includes the one-time $1.75B FanDuel gain that inflated 2025 EPS to $22.56. On normalized forward earnings:
- CY2026E EPS: $7.20 → Forward P/E: 11.4x
- CY2027E EPS: $7.83 → Forward P/E: 10.5x

Against the peer median of 17x (RRR at 17.0x, CHDN at 15.8x, MCRI at 19.6x), Boyd trades at a 33% discount. Part of this discount is justified:
- Negative estimate revision cycle (-8-9% over 90 days)
- Q1 2026 miss (-8.5%)
- Lower growth profile than CHDN (horse racing, iGaming optionality) and RRR (Station expansion in Las Vegas)

However, the discount appears excessive given:
- Best balance sheet in its history (0.8x net debt/equity)
- Consistent buyback reducing shares 8-10% annually
- ~$275-325M FCF supporting continued capital return
- Consensus analyst target: $94 median (14% upside from current $82.21)

An EV/EBITDA framework: At ~$6B market cap + $2.1B net debt = ~$8.1B EV on ~$1,300M forward EBITDAR = ~6.2x. Gaming peers typically trade 7-9x on this basis. This suggests 13-30% enterprise value upside to fair range, though the negative revision cycle warrants patience.

## Competitive Landscape

**Las Vegas Locals:** Boyd's primary competitor is Station Casinos (Red Rock Resorts). Station is aggressively expanding with new tavern concepts (5th "Seventy Six" location) and benefits from similar local population growth tailwinds. Boyd's renovations at Suncoast and Orleans are defensive investments to maintain relevance. The Cadence Crossing opening represents Boyd's first new development in the Las Vegas market in over 20 years — a positive signal of reinvestment.

**Virginia Market:** The Norfolk development enters a crowded landscape. Rivers Casino in Portsmouth (10 miles away) already generates $309M in annual revenue. Live! Casino Petersburg ($1.4B project) opens on a similar 2027 timeline. Academic research (ODU Dragas Center) suggests only ~10% of Norfolk visitors will come from out-of-state, and city tax projections have been halved. The risk of building a $750M casino into a market that may already be saturated is the single largest idiosyncratic concern.

**Competitive position trajectory:** Stable with defensive investment required. Boyd's loyalty program (Boyd Rewards) provides cross-property retention. The management contract model (Sky River) generates high-margin fees without capital risk. Market share in Las Vegas locals is likely stable but not growing ahead of renovation completion.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Norfolk Execution (High Impact, Medium Probability):** $750M investment into uncertain competitive environment. Even if on time/budget, returns may disappoint given market saturation. A $50-60M EBITDAR contribution would be adequate; anything below $40M creates value destruction relative to the capital deployed.

**2. Consumer Spending Deterioration (High Impact, Medium-High Probability):** Boyd's customer base (Las Vegas locals, Midwest/South regional gamblers) skews middle-income and is particularly sensitive to discretionary spending pressure. Q1 2026 softness may be the leading edge.

**3. Online Segment Permanent Impairment (Confirmed, Ongoing):** The $70-75M annual EBITDAR decline from FanDuel renegotiation is permanent. New agreements are at structurally lower rates. Boyd Interactive's own platform must grow to offset this, but margins have already compressed from 69.6% to 48.4%.

**4. 2027 Debt Maturity ($1.16B):** $1B in 4.75% senior notes due 2027 will need refinancing. In a persistent high-rate environment, replacement debt will likely cost 6-7%, adding $20-25M in annual interest expense.

**5. Midwest & South Goodwill/Impairment:** Management explicitly warned that fair values in this segment "did not significantly exceed" carrying values. With $958M goodwill + $1.47B intangibles and a weakening consumer, further write-downs are probable.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 47.3% vs. 26.5% historical volatility represents a 78% premium. The term structure is in steep backwardation (47.3% near-term vs. 27.5% at 6 months), indicating the market believes a resolution event is imminent rather than structural elevated risk. The OI put/call ratio of 2.75x confirms heavy put positioning. This likely reflects (a) post-earnings uncertainty digestion, (b) macro consumer spending concerns, and/or (c) positioning ahead of potential consumer data deterioration. The backwardation suggests this stress is expected to resolve within weeks rather than months, consistent with a "soft patch" interpretation rather than structural breakdown.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of flat-to-positive estimate revisions (currently declining 3-4% per 30 days)
2. Las Vegas Locals segment revenue returns to positive YoY growth for two consecutive quarters
3. Stock pulls back to $72-74 (10x forward, near 52-week low support) providing sufficient margin of safety

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. CY2026 EPS estimates fall below $6.50 (additional -10% revision from current $7.20)
2. Midwest & South segment reports additional goodwill impairment >$150M in a single quarter
3. Consumer spending data deteriorates further AND stock rallies above $90 (reduces risk/reward)

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (late July 2026) is the natural reassessment point. By then, two full quarters post-FanDuel transition will be visible, Cadence Crossing will have measurable contribution, and summer Las Vegas season strength/weakness will be quantifiable.

## Conclusion

Boyd Gaming is a well-capitalized, well-managed gaming operator trading at a meaningful discount to peers (11.4x vs. 17x median). The FanDuel-funded deleveraging and buyback program have created substantial value. However, the stock is in a negative estimate revision cycle, just delivered an earnings miss, faces permanent online segment EBITDAR loss, and operates in a consumer discretionary environment showing weakness signals. The options market confirms elevated near-term uncertainty.

The 33% peer discount is partially justified by lower growth (2-3% revenue vs. high-single-digits for CHDN) and Norfolk execution risk. At current prices (~11.4x forward), the risk/reward is roughly balanced — not cheap enough to compensate for negative momentum, but too cheap to ignore given the balance sheet strength and buyback support. Wait for estimate stabilization or a pullback to $72-74 for a more compelling entry.
