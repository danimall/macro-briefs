**Research Date: June 2, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Revvity is the post-PerkinElmer life-sciences tools and diagnostics company, split roughly evenly between Life Sciences (reagents, instruments, software, anchored by BioLegend) and Diagnostics (reproductive health screening, EUROIMMUN autoimmune testing). The investment question is narrow: is 2026 the trough of an organic-growth deceleration that ran from 9.1% (2023) to 4.1% (2024) to 1.9% (2025), or is this a structurally sub-scale competitor losing ground to larger consolidators? The data does not resolve this, which is the core of our HOLD.

Reported growth overstates underlying demand. Q1 FY2026 revenue rose 7% reported, but stripping out ~3pp of FX, ~1pp from the ACD/Labs acquisition, and an extra fiscal week (FY2026 is a 53-week year) leaves organic growth near 3%. EPS gains rest on a share count that fell from 120.6M to 111.6M YoY on $820.8M of FY2025 buybacks, while operating income stayed roughly flat and gross margin compressed another 200bps in Q1 to 54.5%. Estimates are falling across every horizon (current-year EPS revised down 2.9% over 30 days, current-quarter down 5.2%), the inverse of an earnings-inflection profile.

We rate the stock HOLD with an "uncertain" rationale rather than a confident growth or income call. The case for not selling is specific: expectations have been demonstrably reset (the stock rose ~7% on May 5 despite FY2026 revenue guidance landing ~5.2% below consensus, because the EPS guide of $5.20-$5.30 and margin held), the stock trades near the low end of the analyst range (~$101 vs. $90-$145), and ~$515M of FCF backstops continued buybacks and a clean investment-grade balance sheet. The case against owning it is equally specific: a ~48x trailing multiple on flat-to-low-single-digit organic growth, falling estimates, and a $4.5B goodwill pool with only 10-20% fair-value headroom. We cannot articulate a credible path to material appreciation on a 12-month horizon, which is why this is not a BUY; we cannot identify a dated catalyst for material downside, which is why it is not an active short.

## Company Overview

Revvity (Waltham, MA; ~11,000 employees; ~80% of staff outside the U.S.; sells in 160+ countries) was created from the 2023 rebranding of PerkinElmer after that company sold its legacy Applied, Food and Enterprise Services business. It operates two roughly equal segments:

- **Life Sciences (~$1,431M FY2025, ~50% of revenue):** Reagents, instruments, software and licensing for drug discovery and development. The 2021 BioLegend acquisition (antibodies, flow-cytometry reagents, 35,000+ SKUs) is the anchor, alongside Dharmacon, HTRF/AlphaLISA detection chemistries, high-content imaging (Opera Phenix, Operetta), and the Signals software suite (ELN, ChemDraw, image analysis).
- **Diagnostics (~$1,425M FY2025, ~50%):** Reproductive Health (newborn/prenatal screening — DELFIA, NeoBase, EONIS, Vanadis NIPT, a global leadership position) and Immunodiagnostics (EUROIMMUN autoimmune/allergy testing, Oxford Immunotec T-SPOT.TB, plus China infectious-disease/blood screening), plus the Revvity Omics clinical lab network.

The business is consumables- and reagents-weighted, giving it recurring-revenue characteristics, but it is tied to the same pharma/biotech/academic funding cycle as the broader tools sector. It competes against far larger, better-capitalized players (Thermo Fisher, Danaher, Bio-Rad, Roche, bioMérieux), and management explicitly expects the proportion of large competitors to increase as the industry consolidates.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue is stalling underneath optical growth.** FY2023 $2,750.6M → FY2024 $2,755.0M → FY2025 $2,856.1M. The proxy's non-COVID organic growth metric tells the story plainly: 9.1% (2023), 4.1% (2024), 1.9% (2025).

**Margins are compressing, and the drivers are not all transient.** Gross margin fell 104bps to 54.8% in FY2025. The mechanism: tariffs (~$25M cost / ~$20M gross-margin hit in FY2025, primarily European-made product sold into the U.S.), unfavorable FX, and adverse product mix tied to China diagnostics policy changes. Diagnostics segment operating margin fell 194bps in FY2025 (to 24.2%); Life Sciences fell 139bps (to 32.0%).

**Net income volatility is largely tax- and one-time-driven.** Net income: $693M (2023) → $270M (2024) → $241M (2025). The 2023 figure benefited from a 0.5% effective tax rate; FY2025 carried $55.9M of restructuring (~5% of the workforce). Operating income was relatively stable in the ~$300-357M range. The earnings line is not collapsing, but it is not growing either.

**Cash generation is strong.** FY2025 operating cash flow was $589M against just $73.5M of capex, producing ~$515M FCF (~18% FCF margin), consistent with an asset-light reagents/software model. Total debt is ~$3.2B (senior unsecured notes plus an undrawn $1.5B revolver); net debt is modest against ~$860-920M cash; the company is investment-grade. The €500M 1.875% notes mature July 2026 and are repayable from cash/revolver.

**The balance sheet is intangible-heavy.** Roughly $8.9-9.0B of net intangibles sit against ~$12.0B total assets (~74%), a direct legacy of BioLegend (~$5.3B) and EUROIMMUN. The Altman Z of 2.14 (grey zone) is mechanically a function of that goodwill base and modest EBIT/assets ratio, not liquidity distress; the current ratio is 1.68 and Piotroski signals are positive. This is acquisition-accounting drag, not financial fragility.

**Capital allocation runs through buybacks.** FY2025 repurchases of $820.8M (vs. $369.6M in 2024) cut the share count ~7%, the primary support for EPS. The dividend is a token $0.07/quarter (~$33M/year, ~0.27% yield). $800M remains on the $1.0B authorization. This is sustainable given the FCF, but it is not a substitute for organic reacceleration, and at ~48x earnings the buyback is not being executed at an obviously attractive price.

## Growth Analysis

The growth profile is the weakest part of the story and the reason the elevated-revision BUY signal that drives this system's best calls is absent here; the opposite is present. Consensus EPS is falling across every horizon: current quarter -5.2% (30d), current year -2.9% (30d) / -3.1% (90d), next year -3.2% (30d). Revenue growth is low-single-digit organic and decelerating on a three-year trend.

The strategic response is reasonable but incremental: tilt toward higher-margin, stickier software and consumables (ACD/Labs bolt-on for ~$72M in Q1), prune lower-return geographies (the formal decision to divest China Immunodiagnostics), and continue restructuring (~5% of the workforce in FY2025, another ~2% in Q1 FY2026). None of these is a growth accelerant in 2026; they are margin and portfolio-quality moves.

The end-market backdrop is unhelpful. The 10-K risk factors were sharpened to call out that "recently announced and proposed changes in U.S. funding and regulations have created a more cautious spending environment" — a reference to NIH/academic funding uncertainty and biopharma capex discipline. Revvity's ~3% organic growth is broadly in line with a soft sector but trails higher-quality compounders.

The one defensible forward thesis is a trough call: if 2026 marks the bottom of the organic deceleration, the high incremental margins on recurring reagents/software revenue and the reduced share count could produce EPS leverage in 2027. The market reaction to Q1 (a +7% pop on below-consensus revenue guidance) is consistent with expectations having been washed out. This is plausible but not yet evidenced — there is no acceleration in the data, only stabilization at a low level, and estimates are still being cut.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$101, RVTY trades at ~48x trailing EPS of $2.06 and roughly 19x the $5.24 current-year and ~17x the $5.81 next-year adjusted EPS consensus. The forward multiple is more relevant given the gap between GAAP and adjusted figures, and on that basis the stock is not egregiously expensive for a recurring-revenue tools/diagnostics business, but it is not cheap relative to ~3% organic growth either, implying a forward PEG well above 2 on organic terms.

Peer context cuts both ways. The Health Care Equipment peer median P/E is 44.2 (PODD 33.8, PEN 73.4, MASI 44.2, GMED 19.1; BAX negative). On trailing P/E, RVTY at ~48x sits in line with that median, so it is not an outlier. The trouble is that several of these peers carry higher growth or clearer catalysts, and RVTY's TSR has materially lagged its life-sciences-tools peer set (indexed 68.2 vs. peer group 105.15 from a 2021 base per the proxy), reflecting the growth deceleration the market is pricing.

I am deliberately not applying the cycle-adjusted/inflection framework here. That framework requires earnings recovering from a trough with estimate revisions rising — RVTY shows the inverse, with estimates falling and trailing earnings depressed mainly by one-time restructuring and tax effects rather than a cyclical earnings collapse poised to snap back. There is no evidence the current multiple understates normalized earnings power; if anything, ~48x trailing on flat operating income prices in a recovery that has not begun.

Analyst targets span $90 (low) to $145 (high), mean ~$114, median $110. The stock at ~$101 sits near the low end, which provides modest valuation support and is consistent with washed-out sentiment, but the median target implies only ~9% upside — not the asymmetry a BUY requires. The valuation score of 4.0 reflects a multiple that is full relative to the growth it is delivering, with no specific identified mispricing in either direction.

## Competitive Landscape

Revvity holds genuinely strong positions in newborn screening (global leadership) and autoimmune diagnostics (EUROIMMUN), plus a respected reagents/antibodies franchise (BioLegend). Trajectory, not level, is what matters for scoring, and the trajectory is mixed-to-soft: the company is sub-scale versus Thermo and Danaher, more geographically and product-concentrated, and operating in a sector where management explicitly expects large-competitor share to rise through consolidation.

The China Immunodiagnostics divestiture is the clearest competitive-position signal. Volume-based procurement and price reform in China have turned a long-standing diagnostics growth story into a margin drag, and the decision to exit is an admission that the regional thesis deteriorated structurally rather than cyclically. Pruning it improves portfolio quality but shrinks the addressable footprint. The software tilt (Signals suite, ACD/Labs) is the most promising competitive vector — higher margin, stickier — but it is too small to move the consolidated growth rate yet. Net, the competitive position is stable-to-slightly-eroding, which supports a 5.0.

## Risk Assessment

**Life Sciences Solutions goodwill impairment is the single most specific risk.** As of the November 3, 2025 annual test, all reporting units' fair value substantially exceeded carrying value except Life Sciences Solutions, where the cushion was only 10-20% against a $4.5B goodwill balance. This is the segment most exposed to soft pharma/biotech instrumentation demand. Continued organic softness, a higher discount rate, or weak FY2026 demand could trigger a material non-cash impairment at the next annual test (early November 2026) or on an intra-year triggering event.

**China divestiture execution.** Newly disclosed as a risk factor — transaction costs, operational disruption, and the possibility of failing to achieve strategic objectives.

**End-market funding.** NIH/academic and biopharma capex caution, with a high fixed-cost base that amplifies the margin impact of small revenue declines.

**Tariffs, with optionality.** The Feb 20, 2026 Supreme Court IEEPA ruling that the imposed tariffs were unauthorized could generate refunds, but Revvity has not booked a receivable given timing uncertainty — modest unquantified upside.

**FX.** ~80% of staff and a majority of business outside the U.S.; FX swings both revenue and margin, and translation losses flow through AOCI.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV is 41.8% against 38.5% one-year historical volatility — slightly rich (3.3pp spread), with an implied move of ±7.1% on the nearest expiry. IV percentile within the current chain is low (26th), and the term structure is flat (1m 41.8% ≈ 6m 41.9%), so uncertainty is evenly distributed rather than concentrated around a near-term event. The options market is not pricing an imminent binary catalyst; it confirms a "show-me" posture rather than directional conviction either way.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (to ~$130-145)

2026 marks the organic-growth trough. As pharma/biotech R&D budgets normalize and academic funding uncertainty resolves, organic growth reaccelerates toward mid-single digits in 2027. High incremental margins on recurring reagents and software flow through, the smaller share base amplifies EPS, the China divestiture and software tilt lift portfolio quality and margins, and the IEEPA tariff refund provides a one-time tailwind. The stock re-rates as estimates inflect from falling to rising, carrying the multiple toward the high end of the analyst range. This requires demonstrated organic reacceleration, which is not yet present.

### Bear Case (to ~$80-85)

Organic growth stays stuck at ~1-3% as competitive consolidation and funding caution persist. Margins keep compressing on mix and tariffs. The Life Sciences Solutions goodwill cushion erodes and a material impairment is taken at the November 2026 test, hitting GAAP earnings and sentiment. With EPS leaning on buybacks rather than demand, the multiple compresses toward the GMED-style ~19x peer floor on the realization that there is no growth premium to justify ~48x trailing. This implies roughly 15-20% downside to the 52-week low area near $81-85.

The asymmetry is roughly balanced. The base case (~$110, the analyst median) is ~9% above current, the bull is ~30-45% upside, the bear is ~15-20% downside — but the bull requires a reacceleration with no current evidence, while the bear has live, quantified triggers (falling estimates, thin goodwill cushion). That balance, with the bull case unsupported by current data, is a HOLD.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (Q2 FY2026, early August) and the FY2026 annual goodwill test in early November are the two natural checkpoints. The August print is the first clean read on whether organic growth is troughing.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Organic revenue growth (ex-FX, ex-acquisition, ex-extra-week) reaccelerates to ≥4-5% for two consecutive quarters, and consensus EPS revisions turn positive (current-year revision flips from -2.9% to net positive over 90 days).
- Gross margin stabilizes or expands YoY for two consecutive quarters, confirming the tariff/mix/FX drags are abating rather than structural.
- Clustered open-market insider buying, which would convert the currently routine insider activity into a real signal.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- A material Life Sciences Solutions goodwill impairment is taken (or the FY2026 annual test discloses a cushion below ~10%), confirming the largest goodwill pool is impaired.
- Organic growth turns negative, or FY2026 revenue guidance is cut again, signaling the deceleration is not a trough.
- Estimate revisions accelerate downward (current-year cut widening beyond ~5% over 90 days), which would invalidate the washed-out-expectations premise underpinning the hold.

The reason to hold rather than sell today is that expectations are demonstrably reset, the stock sits near the low end of the target range, and FCF on an investment-grade balance sheet limits downside velocity. The reason not to buy is that there is no dated catalyst or estimate inflection to drive appreciation over the next 12 months.

## Conclusion

**Recommendation: HOLD (uncertain rationale). Overall score 4.83.**

Revvity is a reasonable-quality, sub-scale life-sciences tools and diagnostics business whose problem is growth, not solvency. Organic growth has decelerated, margins are compressing, and EPS is being sustained by the share-count reduction rather than demand. Estimates are falling across every horizon, which removes the inflection signal that would justify a BUY, and the ~48x trailing multiple (in line with the peer median but unsupported by RVTY's growth) leaves no asymmetric upside. The Life Sciences Solutions goodwill pool is a quantified, dated downside risk to monitor at the November 2026 test.

We hold rather than avoid because the offsetting factors are specific and current: sentiment is washed out, the stock trades near the low end of analyst targets, FCF and an investment-grade balance sheet cap downside velocity, and the China divestiture plus software tilt are sensible portfolio-quality moves. The entire 12-month case turns on whether 2026 is the organic-growth trough, a question the data leaves open. Until organic growth reaccelerates and estimate revisions inflect positive, there is no reason to add; until a goodwill impairment or renewed guidance cut materializes, there is no specific catalyst to short. This is a name to hold and monitor against the August print and November goodwill test, not to commit fresh capital to.
