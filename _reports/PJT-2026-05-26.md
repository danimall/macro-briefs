## Executive Summary

PJT Partners is one of the highest-quality pure-play advisory franchises in investment banking — zero debt, 28% FCF margins, 100%+ ROE, and a structural counter-cyclical hedge through its #1-ranked global restructuring practice. Revenue has grown 67% over three years to $1.71B in FY2025, with Q1 2026 posting +29% YoY growth to a record $418M. The $800M new buyback authorization (20%+ of market cap) demonstrates management conviction in intrinsic value.

The core issue preventing a BUY at current levels is a specific, quantifiable near-term headwind: management disclosed on the Q1 2026 earnings call that announced-pending-close deal backlog is below year-ago levels. Combined with a 2-for-4 recent earnings beat record (including a -6% miss in the most recent reported quarter), the stock's forward multiple of ~22x carries execution risk in the current environment of elevated trade policy uncertainty and M&A volume fragility. The stock sits 22% below its 52-week high of $195.62, and while fundamentals remain strong, the declining backlog is a concrete signal that growth may decelerate in H2 2026 — a risk the current multiple does not adequately discount.

## Company Overview

PJT Partners is an independent, pure-fee advisory investment bank spun off from Blackstone in October 2015. The firm operates across three segments: Strategic Advisory (M&A, activist defense, capital markets advisory — 87.6% of FY2025 revenue), Restructuring & Special Situations (#1 global ranking by LSEG Refinitiv), and PJT Park Hill (alternative asset fundraising/placement). With 1,224 employees and 133 partners across 7 global offices, PJT distinguishes itself through conflict-free advice (no lending, trading, or principal positions) and an integrated platform enabling cross-referral across its business lines.

## Competitive Landscape

PJT occupies a defensible niche: conflict-free independent advice across M&A, restructuring, and alternatives placement. Key competitive advantages:

1. **Restructuring #1 global ranking** — provides counter-cyclical ballast that pure M&A advisors lack
2. **Integrated platform** — cross-referral between strategic advisory, restructuring, and Park Hill creates relationship deepening impossible at segment-focused firms
3. **Conflict-free positioning** — as bank lending increasingly creates conflicts for bulge-bracket competitors, PJT's independence is a structural tailwind
4. **Park Hill breadth** — the only major peer with dedicated top-tier teams across all alternative strategies

Competitive threats include intensifying competition in independent advisory (Evercore, Lazard restructuring, Moelis, Centerview, PWP) and increasing difficulty enforcing non-compete agreements, which the 10-K explicitly flags as a retention risk.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Near-Term Revenue Deceleration (HIGH probability):**
Declining announced-pending-close backlog is the most concerning disclosure from Q1. Combined with elevated geopolitical/trade policy uncertainty suppressing M&A confidence, FY2026 growth will likely moderate materially from Q1's 29% pace. The 90-day negative revision trend (-3.5% for FY2026, -8.2% for next quarter) reflects this expectation.

**2. Key Person Risk:**
Paul Taubman's personal relationships and reputation are the franchise's foundation. No visible succession plan in public filings. His ~$820M partnership stake provides alignment but creates concentration risk. If his health or availability changes, the firm's competitive position could deteriorate rapidly.

**3. Earnings Volatility:**
The 2-for-4 beat record over the last 4 quarters is concerning for a high-multiple stock. The -6.0% most recent quarter miss demonstrates the lumpiness of advisory revenue. A stock at 22x P/E has limited margin for error on execution.

**4. Macro Sensitivity:**
Despite restructuring's counter-cyclicality, the overall revenue mix remains dominated by strategic advisory (87.6%). A sharp recession or sustained M&A freeze — plausible in the current environment of elevated uncertainty — would pressure the dominant revenue driver.

**5. Partnership Dilution:**
14.97M Partnership Units convertible to Class A shares represent potential 58% dilution of the public float. While economically neutral (income comes with the shares), it creates supply overhang.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Backlog disclosure reverses — if management indicates announced-pending-close backlog returns to or exceeds year-ago levels in Q2 2026 commentary
2. Stock declines to $135-140 range on unchanged fundamentals — would create 18-19x on FY2026 EPS and substantially improve risk/reward
3. Two consecutive quarters of earnings beats (>5% surprise) demonstrating improved visibility and execution

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2026 consensus EPS estimates decline below $7.00 — would indicate structural deceleration rather than temporary backlog normalization
2. Comp ratio rises above 70% for two consecutive quarters — would signal loss of operating leverage and potential talent bidding war
3. Key person departure or health event involving CEO Taubman

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (likely late July 2026 for Q2). The specific data point to monitor is the backlog commentary and whether mandates continue growing at the 15% pace disclosed for Q1.

## Conclusion

PJT Partners is a genuinely exceptional franchise — zero debt, 28% FCF margins, market-leading restructuring business, accelerating operating leverage, and an owner-operator CEO aligned through $820M+ of equity. These qualities justify a premium to peers and make PJT a core holding for investors with long time horizons.

The specific impediment to a BUY recommendation at $152.66 is the combination of: (1) declining announced-pending-close deal backlog explicitly disclosed by management, (2) a 2-for-4 recent earnings beat record including two material misses, (3) 90-day negative estimate revisions across all forward periods, and (4) the current macro regime's elevated uncertainty for M&A volumes (trade policy, rate path). At 22x trailing and ~20x forward — right at the peer median — the stock needs clearer evidence that growth re-accelerates before the risk/reward favors initiation. The $800M buyback provides a floor, and the stock's 22% discount to its January highs creates a more attractive entry than 3 months ago, but the specific headwinds identified require resolution before upgrading.

HOLD with a growth orientation. Monitor Q2 backlog disclosure and estimate revision trajectory.
