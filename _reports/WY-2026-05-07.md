## Executive Summary

Weyerhaeuser is the world's largest private timberland owner with 10M+ acres, structured as a Timber REIT operating across Timberlands, Wood Products, and the newly-segmented Strategic Land Solutions businesses. The company sits at a cyclical trough: lumber composite prices reached multi-decade inflation-adjusted lows in Q4 2025 ($378/MBF), Wood Products EBITDA collapsed 62% in 2025, and net earnings fell to $0.20/share from $2.55 at the 2022 peak. The stock has declined from $27.86 (52-week high) to $24.04 — a 14% discount — reflecting this earnings compression.

Three specific catalysts support a 12-month growth thesis: (1) lumber prices have already begun recovering (+22% from Q4 2025 to Q1 2026 at $470/MBF) as industry supply rationalization (mill closures, curtailments) meets lean dealer inventories; (2) the Climate Solutions business generated $111M in Q1 2026 revenue versus $13M in Q1 2025, demonstrating WY's ability to monetize its land portfolio through conservation easements, carbon credits, and renewable energy leases — management targets $425M in Strategic Land Solutions EBITDA for 2026; and (3) housing demographics remain structurally favorable (decade of underbuilding, strong home-buying cohort growth) and any normalization in mortgage rates from current 6.4% levels would directly accelerate wood products demand. These catalysts are partially reflected in consensus estimates: FY2026 EPS of $0.33 rising to $0.71 in FY2027, representing a 115% YoY increase.

The primary concerns are elevated leverage ($5.57B debt, rising from $4.07B in 2022), a dividend that exceeded 2025 free cash flow by over 4x ($606M paid vs. $139M FCF), and imminent labor risk from the May 31, 2026 CBA expiration in Western operations. These are real constraints but represent the typical profile of a cyclical business at earnings trough rather than structural impairment.

## Company Overview

Weyerhaeuser operates three segments:

**Timberlands** (~$1.5B revenue): Manages standing timber inventory across the U.S. South and Pacific Northwest, selling sawlogs and pulpwood to domestic mills and export markets (Japan, China). This segment provides stable, low-volatility cash flows with biological asset regeneration.

**Wood Products** (~$5.0B revenue): Manufacturing structural lumber, OSB, engineered wood products (TimberStrand, I-joists), plywood, and MDF. This is the high-beta segment — EBITDA swung from over $2B at peak to $250M in 2025.

**Strategic Land Solutions** (~$454M revenue, renamed Q1 2026): Real estate sales, natural resources (mineral rights/royalties), and the emerging Climate Solutions business (conservation easements, forest carbon credits, renewable energy, carbon capture). Climate Solutions generated $111M in Q1 2026 alone.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term recovery signals:**

1. **Lumber price recovery:** Framing Lumber Composite moved from $385/MBF (Q4 2025 average) to $470/MBF during Q1 2026. Industry supply rationalization (permanent mill closures in Canada and temporary curtailments) has tightened the market. Management noted dealer inventories are lean, driving restocking demand.

2. **Climate Solutions breakout:** The $111M Q1 2026 revenue came primarily from a $94M conservation easement sale. Management's $425M 2026 Strategic Land Solutions EBITDA guidance implies this business is scaling rapidly. Conservation easements, carbon credits, and renewable energy leases monetize 10M+ acres in ways that were not available a few years ago. This is structurally new revenue, not cyclical recovery.

3. **Monticello TimberStrand facility:** $500M investment in 10M cubic feet of additional capacity, expected operational in 2027. Engineered wood products carry higher margins than commodity lumber and face less import competition.

4. **China export resumption:** After a March-December 2025 suspension, China lifted restrictions on U.S. log imports. WY is re-establishing export relationships.

**Housing structural case:** U.S. single-family starts are running at ~957K annualized in Q1 2026 versus normalized levels of 1.2M+. A decade of underbuilding has created a structural deficit. The median housing stock age is 46 years, driving replacement demand. Any mortgage rate normalization would accelerate recovery.

**Estimate revisions confirm recovery trajectory:** Current quarter EPS revised +91.5% (30-day) and +79.4% (90-day). Full-year FY2026 revised +15.7% (30-day) and +29.0% (90-day). FY2027 consensus at $0.71 represents 115% growth over FY2026's $0.33.

## Competitive Landscape

Weyerhaeuser's competitive position is strong and improving:

1. **Unmatched scale:** 10M+ acres of timberlands is the largest private holding in the U.S. This scale provides: (a) harvest flexibility to shift volumes between regions, (b) log cost advantages from proximity to mills, (c) unique positioning for Climate Solutions (conservation easements and carbon credits require large contiguous tracts).

2. **Vertical integration:** Owning timber supply and manufacturing creates margin stability versus pure converters who must buy logs at market prices.

3. **REIT tax advantage:** No corporate-level tax on timber income — a structural cost advantage versus C-corp competitors.

4. **Climate Solutions moat:** Few companies can replicate WY's ability to offer large-scale conservation easements and forest carbon programs. The 10M-acre portfolio is essentially irreplaceable.

5. **Supply rationalization beneficiary:** Canadian mill closures and U.S. curtailments reduce competitor supply. WY's Southern operations are positioned to capture increased demand as new processing capacity opens in the U.S. South.

Climate Solutions creates a new revenue stream unique to WY's scale, and industry supply rationalization enhances pricing power for surviving producers.

## Risk Assessment

**High-severity risks:**

1. **Prolonged housing weakness:** If mortgage rates remain above 6% and housing starts stay below 1M through 2027, Wood Products recovery delays significantly. Consensus FY2027 EPS of $0.71 likely embeds assumptions of starts recovering to 1.1M+.

2. **Dividend sustainability:** At current FCF levels, the dividend requires either: (a) earnings recovery, (b) continued large asset sales, or (c) additional borrowing. A dividend cut would likely pressure the stock 10-15%.

3. **CBA expiration May 31, 2026:** A strike at Western operations during building season would reduce Q2/Q3 production and earnings. The 2022 precedent lasted approximately 5 weeks.

4. **Tariff escalation:** Section 232 lumber tariffs (effective Oct 2025) and potential Chinese retaliatory measures could disrupt both cost structure (Canadian operations) and export demand.

5. **Rising debt in rising-rate environment:** $1,050M of variable-rate term loans maturing in 2028 create refinancing risk. Higher-for-longer rates increase costs on the $5.57B debt stack.

**Moderate risks:**
- Monticello execution risk ($500M capex, completion 2027)
- Wildfire/climate risk on uninsured timberlands
- Southern pulpwood price weakness from mill closures

## Conclusion

Weyerhaeuser is a high-quality cyclical business trading at cyclical trough earnings with identifiable recovery catalysts. The 12-month thesis rests on three pillars: (1) lumber prices have already inflected +22% from trough, (2) Climate Solutions represents genuinely new, high-margin revenue that could re-rate WY beyond a pure commodity REIT multiple, and (3) housing demographics support medium-term demand recovery. Insider buying at $25-26 by board members and the 4-quarter earnings beat streak (including a 124% Q1 beat) provide supporting evidence.

The concerns are real — dividend coverage is inadequate at trough, leverage is elevated, the CBA expiration creates near-term risk, and mortgage rates above 6% continue to suppress housing activity. These factors prevent a BUY recommendation at current levels. The stock at $24.04 with 34x FY2027E and a 3.5% yield is neither compellingly cheap nor expensive for its quality — it requires either a price pullback for better entry or confirmation that the lumber recovery is sustained for conviction to strengthen.

HOLD with a growth thesis: the cycle appears to be turning, management is positioning for value creation beyond commodity lumber, and insiders are buying. Collect the 3.5% yield while waiting for catalysts to confirm.
