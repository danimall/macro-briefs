## Executive Summary

STAG Industrial is a well-managed, diversified industrial REIT owning 601 single-tenant properties (120M sq ft) across 41 states. The company has delivered consistent mid-single-digit FFO growth ($2.29 in 2023 → $2.55 in 2025, with Q1 2026 annualizing at ~$2.60), supported by 96.4% occupancy and 24% cash rent spreads on re-leasing activity. The portfolio's granular diversification (no tenant exceeding 2.8% of revenue, no industry exceeding 11.4%) provides genuine recession resilience.

At $38.15, the stock trades at approximately 14.7x annualized FFO ($2.60) and 29.6x trailing GAAP earnings, near the peer median P/E of 28x. The 4.1% dividend yield is attractive relative to peers (median 3.2%), but analyst consensus suggests only 7-8% upside to the median target of $41. The interest rate environment remains the primary headwind: new debt issuances at 5.5-6.0% versus legacy rates of 2-4% will continue compressing FFO growth for several years. The stock is best held for its income characteristics and modest organic growth, with limited near-term catalyst for material re-rating.

## Company Overview

STAG Industrial acquires, owns, and operates single-tenant industrial properties throughout the United States, focusing on mid-market buildings (predominantly 150,000-250,000 sq ft) with triple-net lease structures. This positions STAG in a distinct niche from larger peers like Prologis (mega-logistics), Rexford (infill SoCal), and EastGroup (Sun Belt). The company employs only 93 people to manage a $7.3B enterprise, reflecting the capital-light nature of triple-net industrial ownership.

The portfolio composition includes warehousing/distribution (the majority), light manufacturing, and flex/office-showroom space. Tenants are diversified across industries including automotive aftermarket, building materials, e-commerce fulfillment, food/beverage distribution, and increasingly data center-related logistics operations.

## Growth Analysis

**Organic Growth (Internal):**
The 24% cash rent spread on leasing activity is the most important growth driver. With 8.2% of annualized base rent expiring in 2026 and 21.8% by end of 2027, the mark-to-market opportunity provides 2-3 years of predictable organic growth. National vacancy is expected to peak in coming months, with management guiding toward a leasing recovery in H2 2026 as speculative supply slows (~40% of new supply is now build-to-suit).

**External Growth (Acquisitions):**
The acquisition pace has decelerated from peak levels ($1.4B+ in 2021-22 to $457M in 2025) due to seller-buyer price gaps and macro uncertainty. STAG has the capacity to accelerate ($750M in ATM authorization and $735M in undrawn credit) but external growth is no longer a reliable driver at the current pace.

**Emerging Tailwind — Data Center Logistics:**
STAG has signed 8 leases totaling 1.6M sq ft to data center-related tenants since early 2025, primarily third-party logistics providers supporting data center construction. This new demand source diversifies growth beyond traditional e-commerce and manufacturing, and STAG's broad geographic footprint (including Ohio, South Carolina) positions it well for this channel.

**Estimate Revisions:**
The revision picture is mixed. Full-year 2026 EPS estimates have risen ~9.5% over 90 days, but current and next quarter estimates have declined 17-20% over 30 days. This likely reflects timing differences in property dispositions or one-time items rather than fundamental deterioration — Q1 2026 already delivered a 40% EPS beat. The 4-quarter track record of 100% beats with large surprises (14-92%) supports confidence in forward estimates.

## Competitive Landscape

STAG's competitive position is differentiated and defensible:

1. **Mid-market niche:** Focuses on 150,000-250,000 sq ft buildings across all Tier 1 markets, avoiding direct competition with Prologis for mega-logistics facilities and Rexford for infill coastal assets.

2. **Granular acquisition approach:** Buying properties individually or in small portfolios creates a larger opportunity set and reduces auction pressure from large platform buyers.

3. **Triple-net simplicity:** 93 employees managing 601 buildings and 120M sq ft represents extreme operational efficiency. Tenants bear operating costs, reducing STAG's capital intensity.

4. **Diversification advantage:** No single tenant >2.8%, no industry >11.4%, across 41 states. This provides genuine recession resilience that more concentrated peers lack.

5. **Monthly dividend:** A marketing advantage for income-focused retail investors, supporting a stable shareholder base.

The primary competitive risk is that larger, better-capitalized industrial REITs (Prologis, $125B+ market cap) can offer lower yields to sellers, potentially pricing STAG out of certain acquisitions as institutional capital concentrates in fewer platforms.

## Risk Assessment

**Rate Sensitivity (Moderate-High):**
The 30-year Treasury above 5.1% directly compresses REIT valuations through the income alternative channel (why buy a REIT at 4.1% yield when Treasuries offer 5%+?) and through higher refinancing costs. Each 100bp increase in refinancing cost on the full debt stack would reduce FFO by ~$33M or ~$0.17/share (6.5%).

**Recession/Tenant Credit:**
With unemployment at 4.4% and economists flagging elevated recession probability, tenant credit is a real concern. STAG's diversification provides a buffer — no single tenant failure is catastrophic — but a broad-based recession would pressure occupancy below 96.4% and narrow rent spreads.

**Trade Policy / Tariff Risk:**
Newly flagged in the 2025 10-K. Tariffs can weaken tenants in import-dependent industries, but also accelerate reshoring (driving demand for domestic industrial space). Net effect is likely modestly positive for STAG's portfolio given its domestic manufacturing/logistics tenant mix.

**Acquisition Pace:**
If acquisition volume remains below $500M annually, STAG's external growth rate will slow materially from historical levels, making it more reliant on organic rent growth (mid-single digits at best).

**Interest Rate Environment:**
[unverified] The rate backdrop for REITs remains challenging. If inflation moderates and rates stabilize or decline, that would represent upside for STAG.

**Options Market Signal:**
ATM implied volatility collapsed from 28.3% to 18.5% over the past month (down 9.7pp), suggesting resolved uncertainty following the Q1 earnings beat. Put/call OI ratio increased from effectively zero to 0.40 — modest put activity appearing but far from alarming. The options market is pricing STAG as a low-vol income vehicle with limited binary risk, consistent with its fundamental profile.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($43-45, +13-18%):**
- Rate stabilization → Treasury yields fall 50-75bp → REIT multiple expansion
- Cash rent spreads sustain at 20%+ for 2-3 years as embedded below-market leases roll
- Data center logistics demand accelerates, driving leasing volumes above plan
- Acquisition opportunities re-emerge as seller expectations adjust, deploying STAG's $750M+ in capacity
- 14.7x FFO re-rates to 16-17x on stabilizing rates = $42-44

**Bear Case ($33-35, -8-13%):**
- Rates hike further → 30Y sustains above 5.2%
- REIT multiples compress to 12-13x FFO as income alternatives become more attractive
- Recession materializes → occupancy drops to 93-94%, rent spreads narrow to 10-15%
- Acquisition market freezes entirely, limiting growth to organic-only
- Interest expense headwind accelerates as more legacy debt matures

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock declines below $35 (approaching 13.5x FFO) while occupancy and rent spreads remain intact
2. Clear evidence of rate stabilization (10Y below 4.0%, or Fed explicitly signals easing)
3. Acquisition volume re-accelerates above $700M/year at accretive yields (>7%)

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Occupancy drops below 94% for two consecutive quarters
2. Cash rent spreads compress below 15% (indicating market rent stagnation)
3. Rates rise further and 30Y sustains above 5.5%, making the 4.1% REIT yield structurally uncompetitive

**Reassessment Timeline:**
- Next quarterly earnings (July 2026) — verify same-store trends, acquisition pipeline
- Post-FOMC rate path clarity
- Macro resolution outcomes — significant for rate expectations and REIT multiples

## Conclusion

STAG Industrial is a competently managed, well-diversified industrial REIT offering stable income (4.1% yield) with modest organic growth (5-7% FFO CAGR). The 24% embedded rent spreads and emerging data center logistics demand provide reasonable visibility on forward cash flows. At 14.7x FFO in a rising-rate environment, the risk/reward is approximately symmetric around current prices.

The stock is appropriate for income-oriented portfolios that value monthly dividend payments, diversified industrial exposure, and recession resilience. For total-return investors seeking material capital appreciation, the upside requires either a rate environment shift or acquisition re-acceleration — neither of which is the base case over the next 6-12 months. The 7.5% upside to analyst median target, combined with 4.1% current yield, produces an expected ~11-12% total return under favorable conditions, adequate but not compelling enough to warrant aggressive accumulation.

HOLD for income.
