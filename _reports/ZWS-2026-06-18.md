**Research Date: June 18, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Zurn Elkay Water Solutions is a pure-play water management company that emerged from the 2021 Reverse Morris Trust spin-off of Rexnord's process-and-motion business and the 2022 merger with Elkay Manufacturing. The business sells specification-driven products (backflow preventers, valves, drains, flush valves, drinking-water stations) that represent a small fraction of total project cost but are project-critical and must pass stringent code certifications. Once an engineer or contractor specs a Zurn or Elkay product, re-specification on future projects creates durable switching costs and recurring demand. The standout story is margin expansion: operating margin has climbed from 8.4% in 2022 to 16.4% in FY2025 to 19.0% in Q1 2026, with the company attributing the gains to price realization plus continuous-improvement productivity under its operating system.

The Q1 2026 print (April 21) was the cleanest evidence yet that the Elkay integration is delivering. Revenue rose 11.4% to $433M (11% organic), operating margin expanded 270 basis points, and net income grew 35%, with EPS growth materially outpacing the top line. This was the fourth consecutive beat, with the trailing surprise averaging ~10%. Consensus EPS estimates have been revised up 5.9% over the past 90 days for the current year and 5.5% for next year, a constructive forward signal. The balance sheet is exceptionally healthy: net first-lien leverage of 0.57x against a 4.0x covenant, ~$273M cash, and the February 2026 revolver upsize from $200M to $550M (maturity extended to 2031) materially expands M&A capacity for a company whose growth strategy is explicitly acquisition-led.

The constraint is price. At ~$50 the stock trades at roughly 29x the current-year EPS estimate of $1.76 and 26x next year's $1.92, full multiples for a business growing low-double-digit revenue. We rate ZWS a BUY because the combination of durable margin expansion, recurring specification revenue, accelerating EPS, rising estimates, and M&A optionality justifies the premium, and the stock sits ~6% below the $56 analyst median with the low target ($52) already above the current price. The case rests on continued execution against the FY26 guide (+8–9% Q2 core sales, ~$335M FCF), not on multiple expansion.

## Company Overview

Zurn Elkay operates as a single reportable segment organized around four product families: water safety and control (backflow preventers, pressure-reducing and mixing valves under the Zurn and Wilkins brands); flow systems (drains, interceptors, fixture carriers under Zurn, Wade, Green Turtle); hygienic and environmental products (flush valves, faucets, sinks, partitions under Elkay, JUST, Hadrian, World Dryer); and filtered drinking water (bottle-filling stations and fountains under Elkay and Halsey Taylor). Roughly 92% of sales are US, 5% Canada. The Q1'26 revenue mix was 49% institutional, 29% commercial, 21% other.

The competitive model centers on specification. Products are highly engineered and must pass third-party code certifications (NSF, IAPMO, UL, USC). They are a low percentage of total project cost but are project-critical, so the cost of switching away from a proven spec is high relative to the savings. Distribution runs through roughly 1,000 independent sales reps across about 120 agencies, with local inventory supporting fast lead times. Management cites the Zurn Elkay Business System (ZEBS), a Danaher/Rexnord-lineage continuous-improvement framework, as the engine behind the recent margin expansion.

Secular demand drivers are credible and largely non-cyclical: water safety and conservation regulation, PFAS and lead remediation (Elkay's NSF/ANSI 53-certified Pro Filtration launched in 2025), touchless hygienic fixtures, and the displacement of single-use plastic bottles by filtered bottle-filling stations. Institutional end markets (education, healthcare, government) are historically more stable through construction downturns than residential, partially insulating the cycle.

## Financial Analysis

The multi-year trajectory shows consistent growth and steady margin gains:

| Metric | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | Q1'26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue ($M) | 1,281.8 | 1,530.5 | 1,566.5 | 1,695.9 | 433.0 (+11.4% YoY) |
| Operating income ($M) | 107.1 | 191.4 | 244.6 | 278.9 | 82.1 |
| Operating margin | 8.4% | 12.5% | 15.6% | 16.4% | 19.0% |
| Net income ($M) | 61.7 | 112.7 | 160.2 | 198.0 | 58.9 |

FY2025 revenue rose 8.3% (core sales +8%, growth across all categories), operating margin expanded 80 bps, and net income grew 24%. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $0.41 versus consensus near $0.37, an ~12% beat. Adjusted EBITDA margin reached roughly 26.8% on the call.

Cash flow quality is a genuine strength. FY2025 operating cash flow of $346.5M against net income of $198M is a 1.75x conversion. Capex is light at ~$30M/year (~1.8% of revenue), reflecting an asset-light assembly model, yielding free cash flow near $316M in FY25; management guides to ~$335M for 2026, an FCF yield of roughly 4% on the current $8.4B market cap. The balance sheet is strong: net first-lien leverage of 0.53x at year-end and 0.57x in Q1'26, ~$273.5M cash, ~$499M total debt in a single term loan maturing October 2028 at ~5.78%, and a current ratio of 3.13x.

Capital allocation has been disciplined and shareholder-friendly. FY2025 returned $160M in buybacks and $64M in dividends; Q1'26 added $50M of repurchases (at ~$47.71) and $18.4M in dividends, with the quarterly dividend raised to $0.11 from $0.09. The board increased buyback authorization to $500M in October 2025 (~$432M remaining at Q1'26). Diluted share count is steadily declining, from ~174.7M in 2024 to ~169.7M in Q1'26.

Two accounting features warrant flags but do not signal operating weakness. Goodwill plus intangibles of $1.61B exceeds total equity of $1.60B, and retained earnings is a deficit of ~$1.13B, both artifacts of the Elkay merger and Rexnord/LBO heritage, with equity sustained by paid-in capital. The Altman Z-Score of 2.96 (grey zone) is depressed by the heavy intangible base relative to assets, not by operating distress; ROE is 12.3%, ROA 7.4%, net margin 11.7%. Reported 2025 net income also benefited from a $10.0M pension settlement gain and a favorable tax presentation, so adjusted figures are the more reliable run-rate guide.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on three pillars. First, organic momentum: Q1'26 delivered 11% core growth with management guiding Q2 core sales to +8–9%, suggesting the top line is accelerating rather than rolling over. Second, margin-driven earnings leverage: with EPS growing 35% on 11% revenue in Q1, ZEBS productivity and price realization are converting modest volume growth into outsized profit growth. The operating margin progression from 8.4% to 19.0% over four years is the most concrete evidence the integration thesis is real.

Third, M&A optionality. The revolver upsize, combined with 0.57x leverage and cash on hand, gives the company substantial dry powder to resume its acquisition-led strategy in a fragmented market with no single competitor matching its product breadth. M&A is a stated growth pillar, and the lender confidence implied by the upsize (with JPMorgan replacing UBS/Credit Suisse as agent) supports it.

Estimate revisions corroborate the trajectory, running positive and steady rather than explosive; they fall short of the velocity that flags an inflection trade, so the BUY case here is "durable compounder with rising estimates," not "underestimated turnaround." The secular tailwinds (filtration, hygienic, conservation, plastic displacement) add a layer of demand durability that should hold even if non-residential construction softens, given institutional weighting.

## Valuation Assessment

ZWS trades at ~29x the current-year EPS estimate and ~26x next year's. On trailing EPS of ~$1.12 the multiple is ~45x, but the forward figures are the relevant anchor given the earnings ramp. This is a full multiple for ~10–12% revenue growth, even allowing for margin-driven EPS growth in the 15–20% range.

Peer comparison is noisy in this group. The reported peer-median P/E of 62.2 is distorted by names with depressed or negative trailing earnings (ENPRO at 163x, Chart at negative). The cleaner comps — Timken at 31.2x and Middleby at 21.5x — bracket ZWS. Against Timken, ZWS commands a justified premium for its higher margins, lighter capital intensity, and recurring specification revenue; against Middleby it screens expensive, but Middleby carries more cyclical kitchen-equipment exposure and a weaker margin trajectory. On a growth-adjusted basis, a PEG near 1.5–1.8 on forward EPS growth is not cheap but is defensible for a low-cyclicality compounder with this cash conversion.

The asymmetric case rests on a few specifics rather than on the headline multiple being low (it is not). The analyst median target is $56, the low is $52 (already above the current ~$50), and the high is $65, a target band that sits entirely above the current price, which on names with rising estimates tends to lag rather than lead. There is also a potential IEEPA tariff-refund asset: the Q1'26 10-Q discloses that the February 2026 Supreme Court ruling against IEEPA tariffs could entitle ZWS, as importer of record, to refunds on previously paid duties, but management has booked nothing. This is unquantified optionality that a screen would miss. The valuation score of 5.0 reflects an honest read: the price is full and offers no margin-of-safety discount, so the upside depends on continued execution and estimate revisions, not on multiple re-rating from a depressed base.

## Competitive Landscape

ZWS competes in a fragmented market with no single peer spanning its full product breadth; Watts Water Technologies is the closest comparable, with the rest occupying valve, drainage, or fixture niches. The specification model is the moat: code-certified, project-critical products with high re-specification loyalty and a broad distribution network. The trajectory is favorable, with core-sales growth above end-market growth alongside margin expansion, the signature of share gains plus pricing power rather than volume-chasing.

Customer concentration cuts both ways competitively. The largest customer fell from 20% of sales in 2023 to 18% in 2025, and the largest distributor is also 18%. The decline could reflect deliberate de-risking and broadening, or modest share erosion at a key account; worth monitoring, but the parallel margin and core-growth gains argue for the benign interpretation. The secular drivers (PFAS/lead filtration, touchless hygienic fixtures, conservation retrofit) reinforce a structurally widening addressable market that the company is positioned to capture given its breadth.

## Risk Assessment

**Customer and distributor concentration** is the principal company-specific risk. The top customer and top distributor each represent 18% of sales; top 5 customers ~38%, top 3 distributors ~32%. Loss of a key relationship would be highly material, and this is not fully priced given the stock's premium multiple.

**Construction cyclicality and rates.** Demand is tied to institutional and commercial non-residential construction and retrofit. The higher-for-longer rate environment pressures construction starts, though institutional weighting (education, healthcare, government) provides relative stability versus residential-exposed peers.

**Tariffs and raw materials.** Bronze, brass, iron, steel, zinc, and plastics are ~30% of sales, and the company does not hedge commodities. Tariff actions are a live input cost variable; the IEEPA Supreme Court ruling cuts the other way as potential refund upside, but the net direction of trade policy remains a swing factor.

**Variable-rate debt and intangibles.** The term loan floats at SOFR + 2.00%; a 100bp move is ~$4.9M of annual interest, mitigated by low absolute leverage. The $1.61B goodwill/intangibles base carries impairment risk if discount rates rise or end markets deteriorate, though none has been taken since 2023.

**Governance / CEO succession.** The May 2026 internal promotions (CFO Dave Pauli to COO, Daniel Klun named CFO) were read by sell-side as possible succession setup for CEO Todd Adams, who has led since the Rexnord days. The transition appears orderly and reflects internal development, but key-person dependence is a watch item.

### Options Market Signal

Options positioning is benign and supports the constructive thesis. Near-term ATM IV of 25.4% sits below the one-year historical volatility of 28.9% (cheap by ~3.5 points), with IV in the 3rd percentile of the current chain, pricing complacency rather than stress. Put/call skew is balanced at 2.4%, the put/call OI ratio is a low 0.09, and the term structure is in normal contango. There is no elevated put demand or unusual hedging activity that would flag informed bearish positioning ahead of a catalyst. The cheap near-term IV is mildly favorable for entry timing.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The Elkay integration continues to deliver, with operating margin pushing toward 20%+ and EPS compounding 15–20% on high-single-digit to low-double-digit revenue. The company deploys its expanded revolver and cash into accretive bolt-on M&A in a fragmented market, accelerating growth. The IEEPA tariff refund materializes as an earnings tailwind. Estimates continue rising and the multiple holds, taking the stock toward the $65 high target. Bull case ~$62–65.

**Bear case.** Non-residential construction weakens under sustained high rates, organic growth decelerates to low single digits, and the forward multiple compresses as the market re-rates a slowing compounder. Tariff-driven input inflation outpaces price realization, stalling margin expansion. Customer concentration bites if the 18% account erodes further. Bear case ~$40–42, roughly 15–20% below current.

The balance of evidence favors the bull case at this stage: four consecutive beats, accelerating Q1 organic growth, rising estimates, low leverage, and an explicit M&A pivot, against a full but not extreme valuation. The primary risk is that the premium multiple offers no cushion if execution stumbles.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Base case target: $58.** Applying ~30x to the next-year EPS estimate yields ~$58, roughly in line with the analyst median and consistent with the multiple holding as estimates grind higher. This represents ~15% upside from ~$50.

**Bull case target: $65** (~30%+ upside): accretive M&A plus tariff refund plus continued margin gains, matching the analyst high.

**Bear case target: $41** (~18% downside): construction deceleration and multiple compression to ~21–22x on stalled estimates.

**Timeframe:** 12–18 months, anchored to quarterly prints (next: Q2 FY26) testing the +8–9% core-sales guide and the ~$335M FCF target, plus any announced M&A deployment of the upsized revolver.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:** (1) core organic revenue growth decelerating below 5% YoY in any quarter; (2) operating margin contracting sequentially for two consecutive quarters, signaling the ZEBS/pricing engine has stalled; (3) the 18% customer or distributor relationship visibly eroding (concentration disclosure falling materially or a contract loss); (4) a leveraging M&A deal closing above ~3x revenue that strains the balance-sheet discipline that underpins the thesis.

## Conclusion

Zurn Elkay is a high-quality, low-cyclicality water compounder with a genuine specification moat, exceptional cash conversion, a strong balance sheet, and a margin-expansion story that is delivering in real time, with EPS growing more than three times the rate of revenue. Four straight beats, rising 90-day estimate revisions, and the revolver upsize signaling an M&A pivot constitute a coherent set of positive catalysts. The valuation is full at ~29x forward and offers no margin-of-safety discount, which is the chief reason to size the position with discipline rather than aggression. With the stock ~6% below the analyst median, the entire target band above the current price, benign options positioning, and a credible path to ~$58 over 12–18 months, the risk/reward favors a BUY. The thesis depends on continued execution against guidance, and the invalidation triggers above provide concrete checkpoints to reassess.
