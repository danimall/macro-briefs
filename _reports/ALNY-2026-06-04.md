**Research Date: June 4, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Alnylam has completed the transition that defines its investment case: the company turned its first annual profit in FY2025 (net income ~$314M on ~$3.71B revenue, +65% YoY) and accelerated into 2026, with Q1 total revenue of $1,167M (+96% YoY) and net product revenue of $1,036M (+121%). The engine is AMVUTTRA (vutrisiran), which won FDA approval for ATTR amyloidosis with cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM) in March 2025 and grew 187% YoY in Q1 to $889.9M, led by US sales of $702.6M (+255%). Management has reaffirmed 2026 guidance at roughly $5.1B total net product revenue, implying ~71% growth, and frames the ATTR-CM opportunity as still early; it raised its US patient estimate to ~200,000 (from 125,000), with ~80% untreated.

The case against paying up here rests on three concrete items. First, gross margin is structurally constrained: COGS rose to 20.0% of net product revenue in Q1 2026 from 15.0% a year earlier, driven by an escalating Sanofi royalty on AMVUTTRA that steps up by sales tier (15% → 17.5% → 20% → 25%, and 30% above $1.5B in global annual sales). The more AMVUTTRA sells, the higher the blended royalty, mechanically compressing margins. Management's path to its ~30% operating-margin 2030 target depends on nucresiran, the royalty-free next-generation TTR silencer, displacing AMVUTTRA later this decade, with a 2030 launch at earliest if approved. Second, the October 2025 DOJ subpoena from the US Attorney in Massachusetts covers government price reporting and distributor discount arrangements across all four marketed products. This is the territory where pharma False Claims Act settlements originate, it has no resolution date, and no loss has been accrued. Third, the stock trades at ~32x current-year consensus EPS ($9.51) and ~74x trailing, leaving limited margin for error given those two overhangs.

The result is a growth HOLD. The revenue trajectory is genuine and durable, estimate revisions are modestly positive (current-year EPS +2.6% over 30 days), and the franchise is a category leader. But with two unquantified, unresolved overhangs (one regulatory, one structural) and a multiple that already discounts continued share gains, the risk/reward at ~$293 is balanced rather than asymmetric. Upgrade triggers and downgrade triggers are specified below.

## Company Overview

Alnylam is the pioneer and clear leader in RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics: small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) that silence disease-causing genes upstream of where conventional drugs act. It is the only company to translate RNAi into a sustained commercial franchise, with six approved first-in-class medicines. The company operates as a single segment from Cambridge, MA.

The commercial portfolio splits into self-marketed and partnered assets:

- **Self-marketed (4):** AMVUTTRA (vutrisiran) and ONPATTRO (patisiran) form the TTR franchise; GIVLAARI (givosiran, acute hepatic porphyria) and OXLUMO (lumasiran, primary hyperoxaluria type 1) form the "Rare" franchise.
- **Partner-marketed (2):** Leqvio (inclisiran, LDL cholesterol) via Novartis; Qfitlia (fitusiran, hemophilia) via Sanofi.

AMVUTTRA is the lead asset. Its March 2025 ATTR-CM approval opened a market multiples larger than its original polyneuropathy (ATTR-PN) indication and converted Alnylam from a chronic money-loser into a scaling, profitable enterprise. The platform advantage is a patent-protected GalNAc liver-delivery system plus emerging CNS, eye, muscle, and adipose delivery; proprietary chemistry; twice-yearly subcutaneous dosing convenience; and a built-out global commercial infrastructure across the US, EU, and Japan. Foundational IP is licensed in part from Ionis, Arbutus, and Dicerna.

## Financial Analysis

**Profitability inflection, confirmed and accelerating.** FY2025 delivered the first profitable year: total revenue ~$3.71B (+65% YoY), net product revenue $2.99B (+81%), and net income ~$314M against a ~$278M loss in 2024. The company also reached free-cash-flow breakeven.

Q1 2026 extended the trend:
- Total revenue $1,167M (+96% YoY); net product revenue $1,036M (+121%)
- AMVUTTRA $889.9M (+187%), with US $702.6M (+255%)
- ONPATTRO $20.5M (−59%), reflecting intentional cannibalization as patients migrate to AMVUTTRA
- GIVLAARI $74.4M (+11%); OXLUMO $51.3M (+22%)
- Royalty revenue $49M (+85%, mainly Leqvio); collaboration revenue $82M (−17%)
- Income from operations $269M; net income $206M; GAAP diluted EPS $1.51; adjusted EPS $1.99 vs. $1.43 consensus
- Operating cash flow +$70.5M

R&D rose 38% to $364.9M and SG&A rose 34% to $322.6M, both growing slower than the 121% product-revenue line, which is the operating-leverage signature that supports the profitability story.

**The central financial tension — gross margin.** COGS climbed to 20.0% of net product revenue in Q1 2026 from 15.0% a year earlier, and management explicitly guides it higher. The mechanism is structural rather than operational: the Sanofi royalty on AMVUTTRA escalates by tier (15% / 17.5% / 20% / 25%, then 30% on global annual sales above $1.5B). As AMVUTTRA scales, the blended royalty rate rises, mechanically compressing gross margin. Revenue growth and margin trajectory are pulling in opposite directions, and the resolution is not a cost program; it is nucresiran, the royalty-free next-generation TTR silencer targeted for a 2030 launch if approved.

**Balance sheet.** As of 3/31/26: ~$3.01B in cash and marketable securities; total assets $5.13B; total liabilities $4.05B; equity $1.08B; accumulated deficit of $6.50B (the legacy of decades of pre-commercial R&D). Debt comprises convertible notes carrying ~$1.01B (2027 Notes $362.8M at 1.00%; 2028 Notes $661.3M at 0.00%) plus ~$1.70B of liabilities related to the sale of future royalties and development funding to Blackstone. A $500M revolver is undrawn, and the company is in compliance with its covenants (leverage ≤3.75x, interest coverage ≥3.0x). The Blackstone arrangements carry punishing implied economics: the vutrisiran development funding accretes at a ~49% effective rate, and Blackstone's Leqvio royalty share steps up to 55% in 2030 if a $1B threshold is not met by end-2029. These erode future royalty cash without showing up in headline leverage.

**Earnings quality and surprise pattern.** The beat/miss record is volatile: +56.5% (Q1 2026), −28.5% (Q4 2025), +177.2% (Q3 2025), +30.6% (Q2 2025). The swings reflect milestone and settlement timing plus convertible-debt accounting rather than underlying commercial performance. Product-revenue trends are the reliable signal; single-quarter GAAP EPS is not.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis is strong and broad-based on the top line. ATTR-CM is the driver: AMVUTTRA captured 35% new-to-brand share within roughly three quarters of launch, and Alnylam holds ~70% of new starts and ~80% total share in the older ATTR-PN indication, a precedent management argues is transferable to cardiomyopathy. With the US ATTR-CM addressable population revised up to ~200,000 patients, ~80% untreated, and ~15,000 new starts annually, the runway for the franchise extends well beyond 2026. Management targets being the TTR revenue leader by 2030 and treating >75,000 US ATTR-CM patients.

Estimate revisions corroborate near-term momentum without signaling a sharp acceleration. Current-quarter EPS estimates rose +4.7% over 30 days and current-year +2.6%, while next-year drifted −0.5% (30d) and −4.4% (90d). Revenue growth is well above the 30% threshold that typically marks a genuine inflection, and current-year revisions are positive, but the next-year EPS revisions are slightly negative, consistent with the margin drag rather than a revenue problem. This is steady upward drift on the current year with a soft patch on the out-year, not the velocity that would force a BUY on its own.

Beyond TTR, the pipeline provides optionality without near-term revenue dependence: zilebesiran (hypertension, Roche-partnered, Phase 3 ZENITH cardiovascular outcomes; $300M milestone received Sept 2025, up to $2.15B further contingent), mivelsiran (cerebral amyloid angiopathy / Alzheimer's, currently under a partial clinical hold at certain doses), and cemdisiran (Regeneron-partnered, NDA submitted for generalized myasthenia gravis in April 2026). The Alnylam 2030 strategy targets 10 tissue types, >40 clinical programs, ≥2 new non-TTR blockbusters, a 25%+ revenue CAGR, and ~30% non-GAAP operating margin.

The durability of the revenue line is high; the durability of margin expansion is the open question, and it is gated on a single asset (nucresiran) with a 2030 horizon.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$293, Alnylam trades at ~32x current-year consensus EPS ($9.51) and ~21x next-year consensus EPS ($13.90), against trailing P/E of ~74x. For a company growing product revenue >70% with category leadership, the forward multiple is not stretched on a PEG basis: a ~21x next-year multiple against a 25%+ revenue CAGR and continued operating leverage is defensible. Trailing P/E overstates the multiple because FY2025 captured only a partial year of ATTR-CM contribution, so forward and normalized metrics are the right lens.

The reason the valuation score is mid-range rather than high is specific. First, the margin path is capped by the Sanofi royalty escalator, so revenue growth does not convert to proportional earnings growth; the next-year EPS revision turning slightly negative (−4.4% over 90 days) even as revenue accelerates is direct evidence of this. Second, the analyst target range is unusually wide: low $310, median $444, mean $446, high $566. The current price sits below even the low target, which on a clean inflection stock would argue the targets are stale floors. But the dispersion (a $256 spread between low and high on a ~$293 stock) signals genuine disagreement about how the DOJ inquiry and margin trajectory resolve, rather than uniform under-pricing. When a target range is this wide, the consensus midpoint is the average of two materially different outcomes rather than a reliable floor.

Against biopharma peers with category-leading franchises and similar growth, ~21x next-year EPS is a fair-to-slightly-rich price for the visible growth, not an obvious mispricing. The stock has round-tripped from a $495.55 52-week high to ~$293 (−41%). That drawdown is not, by itself, evidence the market is wrong; at a decline of this magnitude, the burden is on identifying what sellers are missing, and here the sellers appear to be pricing the two overhangs rather than mistaking volatility for impairment. Net product revenue is unchanged-to-rising, so the decline is multiple compression on those specific concerns, not deteriorating fundamentals.

## Competitive Landscape

Alnylam's competitive position is improving in the indication that matters most. AMVUTTRA's 35% new-to-brand share in ATTR-CM within three quarters, layered on ~80% total share in ATTR-PN, indicates the company is taking the new market quickly. The trajectory, more than the absolute level, supports an above-average competitive score.

The competitive field is crowded and intensifying, however:
- **Oral, cheaper incumbents:** Pfizer Vyndaqel/Vyndamax (tafamidis; loss of exclusivity expected late 2028) and BridgeBio Attruby (acoramidis). AMVUTTRA is priced higher and is subcutaneous against pills.
- **Pipeline silencers and editors:** Ionis/AstraZeneca Wainua (eplontersen; Cardio-TTRansform Phase 3 data due 2026), Intellia/Regeneron nex-z (one-time gene editing, Phase 3), Novo Nordisk coramitug, AstraZeneca/Alexion cliramitug.

Management reframes the two biggest threats as tailwinds: a second silencer (Wainua) would validate a "class effect," and the 2028 tafamidis patent cliff could make cheap generic stabilizers attractive combination partners for AMVUTTRA, supported by HELIOS-B data showing benefit with or without background tafamidis. This is optimistic but not implausible. The genuine threats to the franchise are the gene-editing one-shot competitor (nex-z), AMVUTTRA's higher price and injectable route, and payer step-edit policies once cheaper stabilizers go generic.

## Risk Assessment

**1. DOJ / US Attorney subpoena (October 2025) — the highest-priority new thread.** No loss has been accrued, but management concedes it will be expensive regardless of outcome. There is no disclosed resolution timeline, which makes it an unquantified, open-ended overhang.

**2. Single-product concentration.** AMVUTTRA is now the overwhelming majority of product revenue. Any safety, reimbursement, or competitive setback hits the entire company disproportionately.

**3. Structural margin drag.** The escalating Sanofi royalty caps gross margin until nucresiran (royalty-free) potentially displaces AMVUTTRA late this decade. This is a known, mechanical headwind, not a surprise.

**4. FDA advertising scrutiny.** Two untitled letters within roughly seven months (AMVUTTRA TV ad, Sept 2025, closed out Nov 2025; AMVUTTRA consumer website, April 23, 2026) suggest aggressive DTC marketing is drawing regulatory friction precisely as the company leans on DTC to expand the ATTR-CM market.

**5. Patent/legal.** University of Texas suit on ONPATTRO (claim construction July 2026); Cipla ANDA seeking generic Leqvio (suit filed March 2026), which threatens the retained Leqvio royalty.

**6. Convertible/dilution risk.** The 2027 Notes conversion price of $286.20 sits near the current price; conversion was triggered in Q4 2025. Capped calls ($837.61) and the 2028 refinancing mitigate but do not eliminate dilution or cash-settlement risk.

**7. Governance/technical overhangs.** No open-market insider buying; the eight largest holders own >50% of shares (block-sale overhang); a classified board with full anti-takeover machinery (blank-check preferred, supermajority provisions, Delaware §203) entrenches management and caps takeover optionality.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is benign and does not corroborate an imminent negative catalyst. Near-term ATM IV is 44.6% against 36.2% historical vol — modestly rich (spread 8.4%) but at only the 18th percentile within the current chain. Put/call skew is balanced (2.4%), the put/call volume ratio is low at 0.21, and the put/call OI ratio is 0.61, indicating no concentrated put demand. The term structure is in contango (44.6% near vs. 49.4% at six months), a normal, non-stressed shape; the rising long-dated IV reflects the cluster of 2026 catalysts (DOJ developments, Wainua Phase 3 data, FDA advertising follow-up) rather than acute near-term fear. The nearest-expiry implied move is ±7.0%. This pricing is consistent with a market that views the next month as low-event but the back half of 2026 as catalyst-heavy, supportive of a HOLD rather than flagging a divergence against the fundamental view.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case (~$444–566, matching the median-to-high analyst range).** AMVUTTRA continues to take ATTR-CM share toward management's >75,000 US patient target; full-year 2026 revenue meets or exceeds the guide; the DOJ inquiry resolves without a material settlement; and the market begins to credit nucresiran's royalty-free margin expansion as TRITON-CM (now enrolling ~1,750 patients) progresses toward a 2030 launch. In this path, the gross-margin drag is recognized as transitory and the stock re-rates as operating leverage compounds. Pipeline optionality (zilebesiran in hypertension, cemdisiran in gMG) provides additional upside.

**Bear case (~$220–250, −15% to −25% from current).** The DOJ price-reporting inquiry escalates to a False Claims Act settlement with a quantified financial and reputational cost; the Sanofi royalty escalator compresses gross margin faster than revenue can offset, holding EPS growth well below revenue growth and keeping next-year estimates drifting lower; oral and gene-editing competitors (Attruby, nex-z) erode AMVUTTRA's new-start share; and payer step-edits post-tafamidis-genericization pressure net price. In this path, the forward multiple compresses on flat-to-down EPS revisions.

The base case sits between these: revenue continues to grow at the guided ~71% in 2026, margin compression continues but is offset by operating leverage on opex, the DOJ inquiry remains unresolved through 2026 as a persistent overhang, and the stock trades in a range as the bull and bear arguments offset.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold). Reassessment timeline: next two earnings reports (Q2 2026, ~late July/early August; Q3 2026, ~late October) and any DOJ disclosure.**

**Why this is a HOLD and not a BUY — the specific, falsifiable obstacles:**

1. **The DOJ subpoena is an unquantified, open-ended False Claims Act overhang.** It covers the exact pattern that produces nine-figure pharma settlements. There is no resolution date and no accrued loss, meaning the downside is undefined. A BUY requires either resolution or evidence the scope is narrow; neither exists today. If the inquiry closes without material penalty, the obstacle is removed and the rating moves up.

2. **The margin trajectory caps EPS growth below revenue growth, and the data already shows it.** Next-year EPS estimates have drifted −4.4% over 90 days even as revenue accelerated >100%. The path to the ~30% operating-margin 2030 target depends entirely on nucresiran, which will not launch before 2030 if approved. Until gross margin stabilizes despite the escalator, revenue growth does not translate into the proportional earnings re-rating a BUY would require.

These are two specific, monitorable conditions, each of which, if it resolves favorably, would move the rating to BUY.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- DOJ price-reporting inquiry resolved or formally narrowed without a material settlement.
- Gross margin stabilizes (COGS as a percentage of net product revenue flat or improving for two consecutive quarters despite revenue growth), or next-year EPS estimate revisions turn positive on a sustained basis.
- TRITON-CM (nucresiran) Phase 3 readout supporting a credible royalty-free margin-expansion path on or ahead of the 2030 timeline.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- DOJ inquiry results in a quantified False Claims Act settlement or expands materially in scope.
- AMVUTTRA new-to-brand ATTR-CM share declines for two consecutive quarters as Attruby/Wainua/nex-z gain traction.
- Full-year 2026 product revenue guidance cut, or current-year EPS estimates fall >10% over 90 days.

**Current-price context:** ~$293, down ~41% from the $495.55 52-week high, above the $283.10 52-week low. Analyst targets span $310 (low) to $566 (high), median $444.

## Conclusion

Alnylam is a genuine commercial-stage RNAi leader that has crossed into sustained profitability, with AMVUTTRA driving >100% product-revenue growth and a category-leading position in a large, under-penetrated ATTR-CM market. The revenue thesis is durable and the franchise quality is high. The obstacles to a higher rating are equally concrete: a DOJ price-reporting subpoena with undefined downside and no resolution date, and a structural Sanofi royalty escalator that compresses gross margin as the franchise scales, capping EPS growth below revenue growth until royalty-free nucresiran potentially arrives in 2030. At ~21x next-year EPS, the multiple already credits much of the visible growth, and the wide analyst target dispersion ($310–$566) reflects real disagreement about how these two items resolve rather than uniform under-pricing. The risk/reward at ~$293 is balanced, not asymmetric. HOLD, with clearly defined upgrade triggers (DOJ resolution, margin stabilization) that would convert this into a BUY.
