**Research Date: June 9, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Domino's is the world's largest pizza company and the #1 U.S. QSR pizza brand, running a nearly fully franchised, asset-light model that throws off substantial free cash flow ($671.5M in FY2025) and returns nearly all of it to shareholders through buybacks and a growing dividend. The business quality is not in dispute; the question is whether the growth engine has stalled. Q1 2026 marked the third quarterly EPS miss in four quarters, U.S. same-store sales decelerated to +0.9% (from +3.0% for FY2025), and international comps turned negative (−0.4%) for the first time in years. Management responded by cutting 2026 guidance to low-single-digit same-store sales growth. The stock has fallen roughly 33% from its 52-week high of $496 to ~$314.

The investment debate reduces to a single question: is this a temporary cyclical air pocket in a structurally advantaged compounder, or the early innings of a durable demand slowdown that de-rates a stock priced for steady mid-single-digit growth? The bull case rests on share gains (U.S. share rose to 23.3% from 22.5%), expanding gross margins, a record FCF yield, and a fresh $1.0B buyback authorization at depressed prices. The bear case rests on a stagnant U.S. pizza category (+1.4% in 2025), a value-stressed consumer, GLP-1 demand erosion flagged in the company's own 10-K, and a $1.34B 2027 refinancing wall that will be termed out at ~5% versus the legacy ~3.8% average.

We rate DPZ a HOLD with an uncertain rationale. At ~18x trailing earnings, the stock is no longer priced for perfection, and the value-buyer thesis has merit. But the evidence currently in hand — three misses, falling estimates across every horizon, a guidance cut, negative international comps, and no insider conviction buying — does not support upgrading to BUY, and the cash generation, share gains, and balance-sheet engineering do not support an AVOID. We will monitor the name through the next two quarters of comp data before committing.

## Company Overview

Domino's operates 22,322 stores across 90+ markets as of Q1 2026, roughly 99% franchised. The model generates three revenue streams:

- **Supply Chain ($2.99B, 60.5% of FY2025 revenue):** Vertically integrated dough manufacturing and distribution to ~7,800 U.S. stores via 22 centers, sold to franchisees at formula-based markups, with 50% supply-chain profit-sharing arrangements that bind franchisees to the system.
- **U.S. Stores ($1.61B, 32.6%):** 6,943 franchise plus 262 company-owned stores generating 5.5% royalties, fees, and advertising contributions.
- **International Franchise ($338.7M, 6.9%):** 15,117 stores, mostly master franchisees at ~3.0% average royalty — very high incremental margin given negligible COGS.
