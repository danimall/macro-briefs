## Executive Summary

East West Bancorp delivered Q1 2026 EPS of $2.57, beating consensus of $2.46 by approximately 4.5%, with record quarterly loans ($58.1B), deposits ($68.9B), and fee income. The bank guided to 6-8% NII growth in 2026 on a flat-rate assumption; based on our estimates, this implies full-year EPS of $10.50-$10.80, consistent with the current consensus of $10.65. At $123.71, the stock trades at 11.6x forward earnings and approximately 1.99x tangible book value, a discount to the regional bank peer median P/E of 12.3x and well-justified by ROA of 1.79% (vs. industry average 1.0-1.2%), an efficiency ratio of 35.7% (vs. 55-60% for peers), and CET1 capital of 15.1%.

The primary investment risk remains geopolitical — EWBC's unique China banking subsidiary creates a perceptual overhang that exceeds actual economic exposure (~4% of revenue, ~6% of assets). In the current environment of elevated US-China trade tensions and tariff escalation, this discount may persist near-term. However, the underlying franchise continues to compound book value at 14-16% annually with credit quality among the best in the industry (net charge-offs of 0.11%, CRE LTVs averaging 50%). The combination of premium profitability, conservative underwriting, and a unique competitive moat at a reasonable multiple supports continued ownership.

## Company Overview

East West Bancorp is the parent of East West Bank, the largest independent commercial bank headquartered in Southern California with $82.9 billion in total assets. The bank occupies a unique niche as the largest independent U.S. bank focused on cross-border financial services between the U.S. and Asia, operating 110+ locations across the U.S. and maintaining a commercial banking license in China — a differentiator no other U.S. regional bank can replicate. Net interest income comprises approximately 87% of revenue, with the remainder from deposit fees, foreign exchange, wealth management, and customer derivatives.

The bank serves three segments: Consumer & Business Banking, Commercial Banking, and Treasury & Other. The loan book ($58.1B) is diversified across CRE ($21.4B, 37%), C&I ($19.6B, 34%), residential mortgage ($17.1B, 29%), and other categories. Deposits ($68.9B) fund 85% of loans.

## Financial Analysis

**Earnings Power:** Full-year 2025 net income was $1.325B ($9.52 diluted EPS), up 14% YoY. Q1 2026 continued the trajectory at $358M net income ($2.57 EPS), representing 23% YoY growth. The Treasury segment's swing from -$79M (2024) to +$331M (2025) contributed significantly, but core banking operations also expanded with record fee income.

**Net Interest Margin:** NIM recovered from 3.27% (2024) to 3.41% (2025) as deposit costs fell 42 bps to 2.46%. The 58% variable-rate loan portfolio creates asset sensitivity, partially offset by $4.25B in receive-fixed hedges. NII sensitivity is approximately ±3.2% for each 100 bps rate move. Management's 6-8% NII growth guidance for 2026 assumes flat rates — in the current environment where the Fed is likely to hold at 3.50-3.75% through at least June, this appears achievable.

**Efficiency:** The 35.69% efficiency ratio is exceptional. Most regional banks operate at 55-60%. This structural advantage, maintained consistently over multiple years, translates into approximately $500M+ in annual operating leverage relative to a peer running at 55%.

**Capital Position:** CET1 at 15.1% provides 600+ bps of buffer above well-capitalized minimums. Total available liquidity of $40.2B (50% of total assets) provides substantial coverage against deposit flight scenarios. The bank holds approximately $36M in long-term junior subordinated debt, though total funding obligations also include $3.0B in FHLB advances, $494M in repurchase agreements, and $68.9B in deposits — a typical funding structure for a bank of this size, but one where long-term senior/sub debt is minimal.

**Credit Quality:** Net charge-offs of 0.11% (2025) improved from 0.26% (2024). Nonaccrual loans at 0.29% of total loans are minimal. Allowance coverage of 488% of nonaccrual loans (up from 441%) indicates management is building reserves proactively. CRE weighted-average LTV of 50% provides substantial collateral protection.

**Capital Allocation:** The 33% dividend increase in January 2026 (to $3.20/year annualized) signals management confidence. With a 25% payout ratio, there is ample room for continued increases. The original $300M share repurchase authorization (through December 2026) had approximately $117M remaining after Q1 2026 repurchases of $98M. Book value per share is growing at 14-16% annually.

## Growth Analysis

EWBC's growth is driven by three channels:

1. **Organic loan growth:** Loans grew from $51.1B (2023) to $56.9B (2025) to $58.1B (Q1 2026) — approximately 6-7% annual organic growth, supported by cross-border trade flows and the bank's unique positioning in Asian-American commercial lending.

2. **Fee income diversification:** Wealth management fees grew 29% in 2025 and continued strong in Q1 2026. Fee income grew 12% YoY in Q1 to $99M. This is a higher-multiple revenue stream that improves overall franchise value.

3. **NIM expansion:** The shift from rate-compression (2024: 3.27%) to recovery (2025: 3.41%) provides a tailwind that should persist as long as rates remain at or above current levels. The 6-8% NII growth guidance for 2026 is achievable through volume growth alone even without further margin expansion.

Estimate revisions are modestly positive: current-year EPS estimates rose 3.9% over 90 days to $10.65, and next-year estimates rose 3.0% to $11.30. Growth is steady and compounding rather than explosive.

Non-depository financial institution (NDFI) lending ($8.3B, up approximately 24% YoY from $6.7B) is a fast-growing segment that warrants monitoring for concentration risk. Approximately 30% of NDFI loans are underwritten as capital call lines, with the remainder comprising other lending to financial institutions. While capital call lines carry structurally low credit risk, the broader NDFI portfolio has a more varied risk profile.

## Valuation Assessment

At $123.71, EWBC trades at:
- 11.6x FY2026E EPS ($10.65)
- 10.9x FY2027E EPS ($11.30)
- ~1.99x tangible book value
- ~1.88x book value

Relative to peers (median P/E 12.3x, median yield 2.8%), EWBC's forward P/E of 11.6x represents a slight discount despite superior profitability metrics (ROA 1.79% vs. ~1.0-1.2%, efficiency ratio 35.7% vs. 55-60%). The stock trades at a premium on P/TBV (~1.99x vs. peer median ~1.0-1.5x), which is warranted by the ROE differential. The 2.6% forward yield compares to a 2.8% peer median — the slightly lower yield reflects the higher TBV premium.

The stock has appreciated 40% over the past year, from $88 to $124, and now sits 3% below its 52-week high of $127.52. Much of the fundamental improvement has been recognized by the market. However, at 11.6x forward earnings with a 16%+ ROE bank compounding book value at 14-16% annually, the stock offers reasonable upside to a $135-140 fair value (13x 2026E EPS) without requiring multiple expansion — purely from earnings growth.

The consensus analyst target of $137 (median $139, high $155) implies 11-25% upside. The low target of $123 is essentially the current price, suggesting limited downside in consensus views.

The stock's score trajectory shows valuation declining from 7.5 to 6.5 as the price appreciated. This is appropriate — the risk/reward has narrowed from the $103-106 levels seen in March, but the forward multiple remains undemanding for this quality of franchise.

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat durability:** The China banking license and cross-border expertise are essentially unreplicable. No new U.S. bank will receive a Chinese commercial banking license in the current geopolitical environment. This creates structural franchise value that persists regardless of near-term tensions.

**Performance and growth trajectory:** The bank ranked as the top-performing bank in the $50B+ category by Bank Director (2025) and top-3 by American Banker. Total assets have grown from $64.1B (2022) to $82.9B (Q1 2026) — 29% in under four years. This is organic growth plus compounding, not acquisition-driven.

**Efficiency advantage:** The 35.7% efficiency ratio is structural, driven by technology investment and the high-value commercial customer base that generates larger average balances per account. This reflects decades of franchise development and is not easily replicable by peers.

**Competitive threats:** Stablecoin legislation (GENIUS Act) is flagged as an emerging risk to the deposit franchise, though impact is speculative and years away from materiality. The more immediate competitive landscape remains favorable — EWBC's niche is underserved by both money-center banks (which lack the cultural expertise) and smaller community banks (which lack the scale for cross-border services).

## Risk Assessment

**China/Geopolitical (Primary):** With $4.7B in combined China/Hong Kong assets (approximately 5.7% of total assets), the economic exposure is bounded. A complete write-off of the China subsidiary alone would represent approximately 2.5% of assets, though combined China/Hong Kong exposure is larger. International branch revenue represents approximately 4% of consolidated revenue. The risk is primarily perceptual — tariff escalation, data privacy restrictions (Executive Order 14117), and headlines create sentiment swings that affect the multiple.

**California CRE Concentration:** 68% of CRE loans in California creates geographic concentration. However, weighted-average LTV of 50% across the CRE portfolio and only 11% in office (with 53% LTV) means the actual loss content in even a severe CRE downturn is manageable.

**Rate Sensitivity:** The 58% variable-rate loan book means rate cuts would compress NIM. The $4.25B hedge book and ability to lower deposit costs provide partial offsets.

**Uninsured Deposits (55%):** The $37.2B in uninsured deposits (adjusted to $28.8B or 45% of domestic deposits excluding collateralized/affiliate deposits) creates theoretical run risk. Total available liquidity of $40.2B provides substantial coverage, though management acknowledges that maintaining adequate liquidity requires ongoing access to borrowing capacity and collateral. Post-SVB, this is a monitored metric but manageable given the liquidity position.

**Rising Tax Rate:** Effective rate rose from 20.5% (2023) to 23.2% (2025) due to California tax law changes. This is a modest, quantified headwind already embedded in forward estimates.

Fee income diversification continues at 15-20% growth. US-China tensions moderate (potentially aided by trade deal progress), allowing the stock to re-rate toward 14-15x earnings as the geopolitical discount narrows. Book value compounds at 15%+. ## Conclusion

East West Bancorp remains a BUY at $123.71. The stock has appreciated meaningfully over the past year, narrowing the risk/reward from prior coverage, but the fundamental franchise quality at 11.6x forward earnings continues to offer attractive total return potential. The China geopolitical discount creates a structural mispricing that exceeds actual economic exposure by a meaningful margin. Positive estimate revision momentum, record operational metrics in Q1 2026, and the 33% dividend increase signal management confidence in sustained earnings power. The primary risk is further US-China escalation that compresses the multiple below fundamental value — a scenario where the stock could decline 15-20% but where the underlying franchise remains intact. Size position at 2-3% given the geopolitical binary risk.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The NDFI portfolio composition (only ~30% capital call lines vs. "primarily" as originally characterized) and the narrower-than-presented risk offsets on uninsured deposits and China exposure modestly reduce the margin of safety supporting the recommendation.
