## Executive Summary

LivaNova is a medical technology company with approximately $3.2B market capitalization operating across three high-value clinical markets: cardiopulmonary bypass, vagus nerve stimulation for epilepsy, and — as of March 2026 — hypoglossal nerve stimulation for obstructive sleep apnea. The stock trades at ~13.7x FY2026 adjusted EPS ($4.21) against 10-11% organic revenue growth, improving operating margins, and a major pipeline event in the aura6000 FDA approval. This valuation represents a meaningful discount to the 29x peer median P/E, which is justified partly by litigation/environmental tail risks but is excessive given the operational trajectory.

The prior BUY recommendation issued April 22 at $65.18 has lost approximately 11.6% (current price $57.59). The fundamental thesis has strengthened since then: Q1 2026 results beat expectations by 7.1% on EPS, management raised full-year guidance, the OSPREY trial results were published in the Annals of Internal Medicine, and the balance sheet improved further (debt reduced to $288M from $377M at year-end). The price decline appears driven by broader market risk-off behavior in medtech (peer ITGR -35% from 52-week high, TMDX -36%, BRKR -38%) rather than company-specific deterioration. With the stock now 20% below its February 2026 high of $71.92 on unchanged-to-improving fundamentals, the risk/reward has improved.

## Company Overview

LivaNova operates through two segments generating $1.39B in 2025 revenue:

**Cardiopulmonary (57% of revenue, $785M):** Heart-lung machines, oxygenators, autotransfusion systems, and perfusion tubing. The flagship Essenz Perfusion System is driving a multi-year upgrade cycle. LivaNova has been gaining oxygenator market share, with a competitor actively exiting.

**Neuromodulation (43% of revenue, $593M):** Vagus Nerve Stimulation (VNS) therapy for drug-resistant epilepsy, with the aura6000 hypoglossal nerve stimulation system for obstructive sleep apnea approved in March 2026 (commercial launch anticipated 2027).

The company is UK-incorporated, Nasdaq-listed, and employs approximately 3,300 people across 34 countries.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** LivaNova has delivered consistent acceleration. Revenue grew from $1,022M (2022) to $1,388M (2025), a 35.8% cumulative increase over three years (approximately 10.7% CAGR). Q1 2026 showed 11% constant-currency growth, with Cardiopulmonary at +14% and Epilepsy at +8%. Management raised 2026 guidance to 7-8% constant-currency growth (from 6-7%).

**Profitability:** Gross margins expanded to 67.7% in 2025 (from 65.5% in 2023). Segment income reached $323.8M combined in 2025 (+19% YoY). The GAAP net loss of $(242.5M) is primarily attributable to the $365.6M SNIA environmental charge. Adjusted operating margin was 19.6% in Q1 2026, and adjusted diluted EPS reached $0.98 in Q1 2026 (vs. $0.88 prior year). Neuromodulation segment income declined 15.7% YoY in Q1 2026 (from $52.4M to $44.2M), reflecting higher ImThera contingent consideration expense and OSA development spending, an investment burden that will persist through launch.

**Balance Sheet:** Debt has been reduced from $628M (end 2024) to $288M (Q1 2026). Cash stands at $540M as of March 31, 2026. Available liquidity is approximately $765M (cash plus $225M undrawn revolver). The remaining debt is primarily the 2029 convertible notes with a conversion strike at ~$90.22. The balance sheet also carries a **$389.5M current SNIA environmental liability** (as of Q1 2026), which the company's liquidity discussion explicitly identifies among potential uses of cash. The Altman Z-Score of 1.82 is artificially depressed by the SNIA charge; excluding that item, the underlying business generates robust positive operating income and FCF.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow grew 39% to $254M in 2025. Adjusted free cash flow was $183M in 2025. Management guides adjusted FCF of $160-180M for 2026, with the temporary reduction reflecting aura6000 launch investments.

**Earnings Track Record:** Four consecutive quarterly beats with a strong average surprise of +12.3%. The most recent quarter (Q1 2026) beat by 7.1% on $0.98 vs. $0.91 expected.

## Growth Analysis

LivaNova has four distinct growth vectors, each supported by specific catalysts:

1. **Cardiopulmonary market share gains (near-term, high visibility):** Essenz penetration targeting 80% of HLM placements in 2026 (up from 55% in 2025). A competitor is actively exiting. Oxygenator capacity expansion of 60% targeted by 2030, with a new manufacturing line coming online H2 2026. Every incremental unit of capacity converts directly to revenue given persistent backorders.

2. **Epilepsy VNS reimbursement tailwind (near-term, quantifiable):** The 48% Medicare hospital outpatient payment increase effective January 2026 removes a structural adoption barrier. Management reports reduced volume discounting and expects new center openings in H2 2026. VNS penetration remains below 10% of the ~1M U.S. drug-resistant epilepsy patients.

3. **aura6000 for OSA (medium-term, large addressable market):** FDA PMA received March 2026. Management has indicated a limited market release in H1 2027, with full commercial launch in H2 2027. The addressable market is large: up to 1M patients abandon CPAP annually, and HGNS penetration is below 5%. Differentiators vs. Inspire Medical: no CCC exclusion (broader eligibility), no pre-implant DISE requirement (simpler pathway), proximal nerve stimulation approach, rechargeable battery, PolySync algorithm (>80% cumulative response rate), and the only device with RCT evidence published in a top-tier journal.

4. **DTD coverage expansion (speculative):** CMS coverage decision for treatment-resistant depression remains pending. If approved, this would expand the VNS addressable market materially. Low probability as a near-term catalyst.

Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS consensus stands at $4.21, implying 13.7x forward P/E at the current $57.59 price. FY2027 consensus of $4.57 implies 12.6x. These estimates do not incorporate meaningful aura6000 revenue (launch H2 2027), suggesting upside to estimates beginning in FY2027-2028.

## Valuation Assessment

At $57.59, LIVN trades at 13.7x FY2026E adjusted EPS ($4.21) and 12.6x FY2027E ($4.57). This represents a 53% discount to the peer median P/E of 29.0x.

The discount is partially justified by: (a) SNIA environmental liability uncertainty ($389.5M current liability that may require substantial cash outlays), (b) ongoing 3T heater-cooler litigation, and (c) the absence of OSA revenue until 2027-2028. However, the magnitude of the discount appears excessive given:

- Double-digit organic growth (11% cc in Q1 2026)
- Expanding margins (approaching 20% adjusted op margin)
- Significant deleveraging ($288M debt, though offset by the $389.5M SNIA current liability)
- 67.7% gross margins characteristic of high-quality medtech
- Specific aura6000 launch timeline (limited H1 2027, full H2 2027)

Analyst consensus target is $79.18 (median $80.00), implying 38% upside. The range is $67-$90. All 11 covering analysts have Buy or Hold ratings with zero Sells.

At 20x FY2026E EPS (still a 31% discount to peer median), the stock would be $84. At 16x FY2027E (reflecting current earnings trajectory with modest re-rating), the target is $73. The compressed valuation relative to growth rate implies a PEG ratio well below 1.0x on the current growth trajectory.

**Base case fair value:** $75-80 (17-19x FY2026E). **Bull case:** $90+ if aura6000 launch exceeds expectations and/or epilepsy acceleration sustained. **Bear case:** $45-50 if SNIA liability materially increases and/or OSA launch disappoints.

## Competitive Landscape

**Cardiopulmonary:** LivaNova holds a dominant and strengthening position. Cardiopulmonary revenue grew 14.9% in 2025, with a competitor actively exiting (revenue declining from $27M to ~$5M). Management sees no capacity expansion or innovation investment from other competitors. The Essenz platform creates switching costs through embedded monitoring technology and clinical familiarity. A next-generation oxygenator launching 2028 should extend the competitive gap.

**Epilepsy VNS:** LivaNova's VNS Therapy is the established leader in vagus nerve stimulation for drug-resistant epilepsy, with decades of clinical evidence and deep physician relationships. The broader epilepsy neuromodulation market includes other FDA-approved approaches: NeuroPace's RNS System (approximately $100M revenue in 2025) treats drug-resistant focal epilepsy, and Medtronic's DBS system received FDA approval in 2018 for focal epilepsy. LivaNova's competitive advantages include broader indication coverage, the longest clinical evidence base, and a simpler surgical procedure. A potential competitor for generalized seizures is expected H2 2026, but management assesses limited impact (generalized seizures represent only ~1/3 of epilepsy, and fewer than 50% of those are GTC-type).

**OSA (future):** Inspire Medical ($912M revenue, established sales force) dominates current HGNS but faces its own reimbursement challenges and lacks RCT evidence. LivaNova's clinical differentiation is meaningful (broader eligibility, simpler pathway, superior algorithm response rates). The challenge is execution: building a commercial infrastructure against an established competitor.

## Risk Assessment

**High-impact risks:**

1. **SNIA environmental liability escalation.** The $389.5M current liability (as of Q1 2026) reflects current estimates. The Italian Supreme Court has held LivaNova can be jointly and severally liable for SNIA environmental costs, and discussions on a possible out-of-court resolution remain ongoing. The company's Q1 2026 liquidity discussion explicitly includes "commitments and contingencies, including the SNIA environmental liability" among uses of liquidity. An additional charge or accelerated payment schedule would meaningfully impair the balance sheet and could necessitate capital raises. The 10-K explicitly states future exposure "could further adversely affect financial condition and liquidity."

2. **3T Heater-Cooler litigation.** Provisions decreased $15.3M in 2025 (positive trend), but risk factor language about "raising substantial doubt about ability to continue as a going concern" from future claims persists. Low-probability but high-severity tail risk.

3. **OSA commercial execution.** The aura6000 must compete against Inspire's established infrastructure while the broader HGNS reimbursement environment is uncertain. If reimbursement coding/coverage deteriorates before launch, the market opportunity could be delayed.

4. **Tariff exposure.** Cardiopulmonary products manufactured in Italy/Germany, sold into the U.S. Management estimates <$5M 2026 impact but acknowledges dynamic trade policy environment.

**Options Market Signal:**

Near-term IV (44.3%) trades 4.2pp above historical vol (40.1%), with the term structure in backwardation, pricing elevated near-term uncertainty. Put skew at 7.5% indicates moderate put demand. ATM IV has declined 9.2pp since April 22 (from 53.5%) while price rose modestly, suggesting uncertainty has dissipated somewhat since the prior analysis. The P/C OI ratio collapsing from 0.38 to 0.00 is consistent with put protection rolling off.

Short interest at 6.7% of float with 4.3 days to cover is moderate — sufficient to provide short-covering fuel on positive catalysts but not elevated enough to suggest significant informed bearish positioning.

## Conclusion

LivaNova is executing at a high level across its existing businesses while approaching a large product launch, yet trading at approximately half the peer-group multiple. The SNIA environmental liability is the primary source of discount — a $389.5M current liability with uncertain resolution. Against ~$765M of available liquidity, continued strong cash generation ($160-180M adjusted FCF guided for 2026), and minimal remaining debt ($288M), the company retains meaningful financial flexibility even in an adverse SNIA scenario. At 13.7x earnings with 11% organic growth, improving margins, and unpriced OSA optionality, the stock offers asymmetric upside with identifiable catalysts over the next 12-18 months. The decline from the prior recommendation reflects sector-wide medtech pressure rather than thesis deterioration, and the improved entry point strengthens the risk/reward.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The SNIA environmental liability ($389.5M, classified as current) represents a potential near-term cash obligation that partially offsets the balance-sheet strength cited in the thesis; the net liquidity position after accounting for this liability is approximately $375M rather than the $765M gross liquidity figure.
