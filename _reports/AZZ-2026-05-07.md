## Executive Summary

AZZ Inc. is North America's leading independent hot-dip galvanizer with a 42-plant network that positions it as the primary beneficiary of the multi-year infrastructure buildout cycle — data centers, electrical T&D, industrial reshoring, and grid modernization. The company delivered record FY2026 results with Metal Coatings growing 14.1% (including a small contribution from the Canton, Ohio acquisition), partially offset by Precoat Metals declining 2.3%. Adjusted EBITDA reached $367.6M, up from $347.9M in FY2025. The company reduced debt by $385M in a single year and guided FY2027 adjusted EPS of $6.50–$7.00, implying 5–13% earnings growth. At $144 per share, the stock trades at ~21.3x the midpoint of FY2027 guidance and approximately 12.3–12.4x EV/EBITDA — modestly below industrial peers at 22.8x P/E.

The investment case rests on three pillars: (1) Metal Coatings is a high-quality franchise with structural demand tailwinds that should persist for 3-5 years as data center capex and T&D investment accelerate; (2) the balance sheet transformation from 3.5x leverage to a projected sub-1.0x within 18 months creates optionality for accretive M&A and shareholder returns; and (3) the Precoat Metals headwinds (residential construction weakness, substrate availability challenges) are understood by management and reflected in flat segment guidance for FY2027, though the duration of these headwinds remains uncertain. The stock has appreciated 61% over the past year, driven by earnings improvement and deleveraging — the forward valuation remains reasonable relative to growth and capital structure trajectory.

## Company Overview

AZZ operates two core segments from Fort Worth, Texas:

**Metal Coatings (46% of FY2026 sales, $759M):** 42 galvanizing plants and 4 surface technologies plants across the U.S. and Canada providing corrosion protection for fabricated steel. Serves construction, electrical T&D, industrial, and transportation end markets. This segment delivered 31.0% adjusted EBITDA margins in FY2026.

**Precoat Metals (54% of FY2026 sales, $891M):** 14 coil coating plants applying protective and decorative coatings to steel and aluminum coils. Construction represents 75% of end-market exposure (including ~25% residential). The new Washington, Missouri aluminum coil coating facility became operational in FY2026.

The company also holds a 40% non-controlling interest in the AVAIL JV, which has largely wound down through asset sales (EPG to nVent, WSI to Pelican Energy). Remaining carrying value: $20M.

## Growth Analysis

**Structural Tailwinds (Metal Coatings):**

The primary growth engine operates at the intersection of multiple secular trends:

1. **Data center construction:** Management has cited substantial U.S. hyperscale spending plans as a key demand driver. Data centers require galvanized steel structural components, and the associated power generation, transmission, and substation infrastructure multiplies the demand effect.

2. **Electrical T&D investment:** Grid modernization spending is accelerating to serve new load centers (data centers, manufacturing reshoring). Transmission towers, substation structures, and distribution infrastructure all require galvanizing.

3. **Industrial reshoring:** New domestic manufacturing facilities (semiconductor fabs, EV battery plants, clean energy) drive fabricated steel demand.

4. **Federal infrastructure programs:** Bridge and highway investment continues flowing from prior authorization.

Management guided Metal Coatings to "mid-to-upper single-digit" sales growth for FY2027, representing a deceleration from FY2026's 14.1% but still reflecting healthy demand across end markets.

**Precoat Metals — Near-Term Headwind, Long-Term Stable:**

Flat FY2027 guidance reflects residential construction weakness and substrate availability challenges. However, the Washington, MO aluminum facility ramp (approximately 75% covered by take-or-pay contracts) provides a baseline growth offset. The plastic-to-aluminum shift in beverage containers is a multi-year secular driver for aluminum coil coating.

**Bolt-On M&A Pipeline:**

Management identified 3-4 Metal Coatings targets and 1 Precoat target, each approximately $15M revenue / $4–6M EBITDA. At sub-1x leverage, the company has significant capacity for these acquisitions without stretching the balance sheet.

**Earnings Growth Path:**

FY2027 adjusted EPS guidance of $6.50–$7.00 represents 5–13% growth over FY2026's $6.19. Consensus for FY2028 is approximately $7.63, representing 12% growth. The compounding of organic growth, deleveraging-driven interest savings (~$15–20M/year in reduced interest expense = ~$0.35–0.50/share after tax), and share repurchases creates a credible path to $8+ EPS by FY2029.

## Competitive Landscape

**Metal Coatings Moat:**

AZZ's 42-plant galvanizing network creates a meaningful competitive advantage rooted in proximity. Galvanizing is a local business — fabricated steel is heavy and expensive to transport, so customers require plants within economical trucking distance. This geographic density allows AZZ to serve multi-state projects (large data centers, transmission corridors) that smaller competitors cannot match.

Additional moat sources:
- Proprietary digital galvanizing and CooloZone technology enhances throughput and quality
- Turnaround speed ("Monday to Friday") builds switching costs into customer workflows
- Scale enables investment in larger kettles that accommodate utility-scale structures

Competition comes from other independent galvanizers (typically single-plant operators), captive facilities within steel fabricators, and alternative corrosion protection methods (painting, powder coating, weathering steel). None of these alternatives match the combination of longevity, cost-effectiveness, and structural integrity of hot-dip galvanizing for outdoor steel infrastructure.

**Precoat Metals Position:**

Less differentiated than Metal Coatings — coil coating is a more commoditized process where integrated mills (ArcelorMittal, Nucor) compete. However, AZZ's 14-plant network and the new aluminum capability provide breadth that pure-play competitors lack. Take-or-pay contracts on the Washington facility reduce demand risk.

**Trajectory:** Metal Coatings' competitive position is strengthening as data center/T&D spending accelerates and the network effect of 42 plants becomes more valuable for large-scale projects. Precoat's position is stable but faces more competitive pressure.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Construction cycle sensitivity in Precoat Metals (75% construction exposure).** Residential weakness at 30-year rates above 6% directly impacts ~25% of Precoat's revenue. If non-residential construction also slows (rising rates, geopolitical uncertainty), Precoat could underperform flat guidance. The 10-K describes these end markets as cyclical, and the duration of current weakness is uncertain.

2. **Substrate availability challenges.** Tariffs on steel imports combined with slowly-ramping domestic supply may create substrate availability challenges for Precoat Metals customers. This could cause unpredictable demand patterns and difficulty predicting volumes, though the company's 10-K notes there are currently no concerns regarding availability of customer-owned bare substrate for coil coating.

3. **Commodity cost inflation (zinc, natural gas, paint).** Zinc is Metal Coatings' largest variable cost. While the company uses value pricing and contractual pass-throughs, there is a lag that can compress margins in inflationary environments.

4. **CEO succession (medium-term).** Tom Ferguson at 69 creates event risk if an unplanned transition occurs. The COO transition (Bryan Stovall retiring June 8, 2026, succeeded by Todd Bella effective March 1, 2026) was orderly, but the CEO role is more impactful.

5. **Data center spending sensitivity.** If hyperscale capex moderates or delays (higher rates, geopolitical disruption), Metal Coatings' strongest growth driver could decelerate. However, the breadth of end-market exposure (T&D, bridges, industrial) provides diversification.

**Mitigants:**

- No single customer concentration reduces idiosyncratic demand risk
- Leverage declining below 1x provides ample financial cushion
- 39 consecutive years of profitability from continuing operations demonstrates through-cycle management discipline
- Contractual coverage (take-or-pay) on new Washington facility
- Diversified end-market mix limits single-sector dependence

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($175–200, +22% to +39%):**

Metal Coatings sustains 10%+ organic growth through FY2028 as data center and T&D spending accelerates beyond current expectations. Precoat recovers in H2 FY2027 as residential construction stabilizes. Leverage reaches 0.5x, enabling meaningful M&A (2–3 bolt-ons at $15M revenue each) and accelerated buybacks. Earnings reach $8.00+ by FY2029. Multiple expands to 25x on quality re-rating as the market recognizes AZZ as an infrastructure compounder. At 25x $7.63 FY2028E = $190.

**Bear Case ($110–120, -17% to -24%):**

Construction recession impacts both segments simultaneously. Metal Coatings growth decelerates to low-single-digits as project starts slow. Precoat volumes decline 5–10% on residential weakness. Commodity inflation compresses margins by 200bps. Stock derated to 16–17x reduced earnings estimate of ~$6.50. Unplanned CEO transition creates uncertainty.

**Base Case ($170–175, +18% to +22%):**

Metal Coatings delivers mid-to-upper single digit growth as guided. Precoat is flat. Leverage falls below 1x. Buybacks accelerate. Market gradually re-rates the stock toward peer-median P/E of 22.8x on FY2028 consensus of approximately $7.63 = $174.

## Conclusion

AZZ represents a well-positioned industrial compounder at a reasonable valuation with clear catalysts for continued appreciation. The Metal Coatings franchise is high-quality (31% margins, structural demand, network moat) and the balance sheet transformation from 3.5x to sub-1.0x leverage within 18 months unlocks significant capital return and M&A optionality. The stock's 61% one-year appreciation is largely explained by fundamentals — earnings grew 19%, debt declined by $385M, and the business demonstrated sustained execution. At 21.3x FY2027E and approximately 18.9x FY2028E with ~10% EPS growth and declining leverage, AZZ offers estimated 15–22% upside to fair value with identifiable catalysts over the next 12–18 months. Precoat weakness is a known headwind reflected in flat segment guidance, though its duration remains uncertain. Recommend BUY with 2–3% portfolio sizing.
