**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

ADMA Biologics has experienced a 60% price correction over the past year from ~$20 to ~$8 following a confluence of events: a short-seller report alleging channel stuffing (March 2026), an abrupt withdrawal of all long-term guidance (May 2026), and a fundamental shift in competitive dynamics within the U.S. immunoglobulin market. The stock now trades at ~6.5x FY2026E Adjusted EBITDA ($265-300M guided) and ~10x adjusted net income on a business generating 71% gross margins and $58M of operating cash flow in Q1 2026 alone.

The core analytical question is whether the market is correctly pricing structural impairment or overreacting to a temporary competitive reset. The yield enhancement technology creates genuine economic value (20% more product from the same plasma), ASCENIV continues growing at 28% YoY with pediatric label expansion, and insider buying at current levels provides some confidence signal. However, the withdrawal of $775M+ revenue guidance for FY2027, the 54% collapse in BIVIGAM revenue, and limited analyst coverage create meaningful uncertainty about forward trajectory. The stock is cheap if ASCENIV growth sustains, but the competitive environment for plasma-derived therapies is deteriorating in ways that could compress addressable market faster than anticipated.

## Company Overview

ADMA Biologics is one of only four U.S.-based plasma fractionators (alongside CSL Behring, Grifols, and Takeda), manufacturing plasma-derived immunoglobulin products for immunodeficient patients. The company operates vertically from plasma collection (7-8 centers plus third-party supply agreements) through manufacturing at its 600,000 liter/year Boca Raton facility to finished drug distribution.

The product portfolio consists of: **ASCENIV** (71% of FY2025 revenue, $362.5M), a differentiated IVIG using proprietary high-titer RSV antibody plasma; **BIVIGAM** (24% of revenue, $122M), a standard IVIG facing severe competitive pressure; and **Nabi-HB** (hepatitis B immune globulin, de minimis). Two customers — BioCare and CuraScript — represent approximately 73% of revenue and 87% of accounts receivable.

## Financial Analysis

After growing 68% (2023) and 65% (2024), revenue growth slowed to 20% in FY2025 and was essentially flat in Q1 2026 at $114.5M. The FY2026 guidance of $530-560M implies only 4-10% growth, down from previous expectations of $635M+. The driver is BIVIGAM's collapse (-54% YoY in Q1 2026) offsetting ASCENIV's continued strength (+28% YoY to $97.5M).

**Profitability metrics are strong and improving.** Q1 2026 gross margin reached 71%, up from 53% a year earlier, driven by the yield enhancement process commercialized in April 2025. Net income was $45.3M (+68% YoY) and Adjusted EBITDA was $59.7M (+24%). FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance of $265-300M implies margins continuing to expand even on lower-than-expected revenue.

**Balance sheet is solid.** Current ratio of 6.71x, debt/equity of 0.31x, $138M cash against $194M total debt (net leverage <0.5x). The JPMorgan credit facility (SOFR+250-300bps) replaced a higher-rate Ares facility in August 2025, saving significant annual interest expense. Altman Z-Score of 5.61 indicates zero near-term credit risk.

**Cash flow is recovering.** FY2025 OCF dropped to $50.4M from $118.7M in FY2024, primarily due to the $108M AR buildup that fueled short-seller allegations. Q1 2026 OCF of $58M already exceeds full FY2025, suggesting normalization. AR declined from $158M to $136M quarter-over-quarter.

## Growth Analysis

ASCENIV remains the growth engine but faces a higher bar. The FDA pediatric label expansion (May 2026, age 2+) opens additional market, and the company targets 500+ leading specialty immunology programs. The U.S. IG market is projected to grow from ~$13B to $30B+ by 2033. However, several factors limit near-term visibility:

1. **BIVIGAM is in structural decline** — standard IG is experiencing a supply glut from elevated post-pandemic plasma collections and aggressive competitor discounting. Management has essentially conceded this segment to competitors.

2. **ASCENIV growth rate has a natural ceiling** — the product requires high-titer RSV plasma where <10% of donor samples qualify. Supply constraints limit production scaling regardless of demand.

3. **Long-term guidance withdrawal is unprecedented** — moving from "$775M+ FY2027 revenue" to "we cannot provide long-term guidance" within three months signals management lost visibility into competitive dynamics.

4. **SG-001 pipeline** — the S. pneumoniae hyperimmune globulin targets $300-500M peak revenue potential, with pre-IND submission anticipated FY2026. This is early-stage and would not contribute revenue before FY2028-2029 at earliest.

Estimate revisions confirm deteriorating momentum: current quarter EPS estimates fell 28% in the last 30 days (from $0.25 to $0.18), and full-year 2026 estimates dropped 20.2% (from $1.04 to $0.83).

## Valuation Assessment

At the current price of ~$8.02 and market cap of ~$1.86B:
- P/E (trailing): 11.7x on $0.60 EPS (includes one-time DTA benefits in prior periods)
- P/E (forward FY2026): ~9.7x on $0.83 consensus
- P/E (forward FY2027): ~7.6x on $1.06 consensus
- EV/EBITDA (FY2026E): ~6.5x on midpoint guidance of $283M
- Price/Revenue (FY2026E): ~3.4x

Against peers: the biotech peer median P/E is 26.3x, making ADMA appear significantly cheap at 11.7x. However, comparisons to SRPT, VCEL, or HRMY are imperfect — those are pure pharma/biotech with distinct risk profiles. ADMA is more analogous to a specialty manufacturer with commodity-like input costs and customer concentration.

The valuation is genuinely compressed if FY2026 EBITDA guidance of $265-300M is achievable. At 10x EV/EBITDA (modest for a business with 71% gross margins and clear market positioning), fair value would be $12-13, or 50-60% upside. At 8x (reflecting competitive uncertainty), $10-11 is reasonable.

The $7.21 52-week low provides a technical floor, and analyst consensus targets range from $12 (low) to $20 (high), with a $16.75 mean — implying 50-150% upside if consensus is correct.

**Key question:** Is the market pricing in a permanent impairment to growth (justified if ASCENIV eventually faces the same competitive pressure as BIVIGAM), or is this a temporary dislocation driven by short-seller narrative and guidance confusion? The answer depends on ASCENIV's ability to maintain differentiation pricing, which in turn depends on whether its RSV-high-titer clinical profile creates genuine switching costs for prescribers.

## Competitive Landscape

ADMA's competitive position is bifurcated:

**ASCENIV (improving):** The product's patented manufacturing process (RSV microneutralization assay, proprietary plasma pooling) creates genuine barriers. Competitors would need to replicate both the donor screening methodology and the plasma sourcing infrastructure. Real-world outcomes data showing statistically significant infection rate reductions versus standard IG creates clinical differentiation. The pediatric expansion broadens the addressable base.

**BIVIGAM (deteriorating rapidly):** Standard IG is becoming a commodity as post-pandemic plasma supply catches up to demand. CSL, Grifols, and Takeda have significantly larger manufacturing scale and can absorb pricing pressure. ADMA has effectively lost competitive viability in this segment.

**Structural threats:** Anti-FcRn inhibitors (e.g., nipocalimab, efgartigimod) could displace IVIG for autoimmune indications, though these primarily compete with standard IG for conditions like MG, CIDP, and ITP — not ASCENIV's primary PI/PIDD indication. This limits near-term direct displacement risk.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Channel-stuffing allegations remain unresolved in market perception.** While the Audit Committee cleared ADMA with independent forensic accountants, Cantor downgraded citing insufficient specificity in the rebuttal. Law firms have opened investigations. The AR buildup ($50M → $158M on 20% revenue growth) provides circumstantial basis for continued skepticism. Resolution requires additional clean quarters of normal working capital dynamics.

**2. Customer concentration creates binary risk.** Two customers at 73% of revenue means loss of either relationship would be catastrophic. At this concentration level, contract renegotiation represents ongoing margin pressure.

**3. Competitive dynamics may spread to ASCENIV.** The standard IG supply glut could eventually pressure differentiated products if payers push back on premium pricing or if competitors develop similar high-titer products. Management withdrew long-term guidance specifically citing "uncertainty in the competitive landscape."

**4. CFO departure (Feb 2026)** during a period of accounting scrutiny is a governance concern, though no formal allegations connect the departure to financial irregularities.

**5. Short interest at 10.6% of float** reflects institutional conviction on the bear case. Days to cover of 2.6 limits immediate squeeze potential.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($12-15, 50-87% upside):**
- ASCENIV continues 20-30% growth, reaching $450-500M within 2 years
- Q1 2026's 71% gross margin represents the new sustainable level from yield enhancement
- FY2026 EBITDA meets or exceeds $300M high-end guidance
- AR normalizes fully over 2-3 quarters, disproving channel-stuffing thesis
- SG-001 advances to IND with pipeline catalyst
- Re-rating to 10-12x EBITDA from current 6.5x

**Bear Case ($5-6, 25-35% downside):**
- ASCENIV growth decelerates as competitive pressure migrates from standard IG to differentiated products
- Channel-stuffing allegations gain regulatory traction (SEC investigation opens)
- Revenue comes in below $530M low-end guidance as customer concentration creates pricing power asymmetry
- Market assigns a permanent "accounting risk" discount (5-6x EBITDA)
- Estimates continue declining through FY2026

3. Loss of either major customer (BioCare or CuraScript)
4. ASCENIV quarterly growth decelerating below 15% YoY

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected August 2026) is the next critical data point. Two clean quarters of working capital + maintained ASCENIV growth would substantially de-risk the bull case.

## Conclusion

ADMA is cheap on the numbers (~6.5x EBITDA, ~10x earnings on a 71% gross margin business with net leverage <0.5x) but carries sufficient uncertainty — unresolved accounting allegations, competitive headwinds potentially spreading from standard to differentiated IG, severe guidance reduction, and limited analyst coverage — to prevent a high-conviction call in either direction.

Insider buying at current levels ($8.01 by Jerrold Grossman) and the genuine economic value of yield enhancement technology tilt slightly bullish, but the 60% drawdown from $20 represents informed selling by participants who assessed the Q1 2026 results and guidance withdrawal as evidence of structural impairment rather than temporary disruption. Limited analyst coverage of a $1.9B market cap stock indicates institutional interest has thinned considerably.

If ASCENIV sustains 25%+ growth and working capital normalizes over the next 2-3 quarters, the stock is meaningfully undervalued at current levels. If competitive dynamics worsen or accounting concerns resurface, the bear case targets $5-6. HOLD with reassessment at Q2 2026 earnings.
