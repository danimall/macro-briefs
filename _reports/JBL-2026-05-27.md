## Executive Summary

Jabil has transformed itself from a diversified contract manufacturer with heavy Apple/mobile exposure into a primary beneficiary of AI infrastructure buildout, with its Intelligent Infrastructure segment growing 53% YoY in H1 FY2026 and now representing 49% of total revenue. The Amazon warrant, the Hanley acquisition ($748M in critical power), and the $500M U.S. manufacturing expansion confirm a deepening strategic position in the hyperscaler supply chain.

However, the stock has appreciated 125% from its May 2025 lows, now trades at ~51x trailing earnings, and sits near the analyst consensus high price target of $384. At $380, the market is pricing in both the current AI infrastructure ramp and substantial future growth. Options markets are pricing 79% near-term IV (vs. 41% historical) with backwardation — indicating event risk near-term. With consensus FY2026 EPS at $12.36, the stock trades at ~31x forward earnings, which is reasonable for the growth profile but leaves limited margin of safety if AI capex cycles moderate or execution stumbles.

The core tension: Jabil's fundamental transformation is real and underappreciated by those anchored to its EMS history, but the stock price has caught up aggressively. The risk/reward at current levels favors patience over new capital deployment.

## Company Overview

Jabil operates as a global manufacturing solutions provider across approximately 100 facilities in 30 countries, employing ~135,000 people. Following the December 2023 divestiture of its Mobility Business (primarily Apple iPhone manufacturing) to BYD Electronic for ~$2.2B, the company restructured into three segments:

- **Intelligent Infrastructure (49% of Q2 FY2026 revenue):** AI server racks, cloud/data center infrastructure, liquid cooling, critical power, networking, optical transceivers
- **Regulated Industries (36%):** Automotive, healthcare, renewable energy
- **Connected Living & Digital Commerce (15%):** Warehouse automation, robotics, consumer devices

The Amazon warrant (1.16M shares at $137.77, vesting on purchase volumes over 7 years) signals a partnership-level relationship with the world's largest cloud infrastructure builder.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:**
- FY2025: $29.8B (+3.2% YoY, but +34% in Intelligent Infrastructure)
- H1 FY2026 annualized: ~$33.2B (+20.9% vs. prior period)
- Q2 FY2026: $8.28B (+23.1% YoY)
- Intelligent Infrastructure specifically: $4.03B in Q2, implying ~$16B+ annualized run rate vs. ~$11B two years ago

**Margin Expansion:**
- Gross margin: 9.0% in H1 FY2026 vs. 8.6% prior year
- Core operating margin: 5.3% (Q2 FY2026) vs. 5.0% prior year
- Segment income margins improving across all three segments
- Mix shift toward higher-margin AI infrastructure is the primary driver

**Cash Flow:**
- Operating cash flow: $1.64B (FY2025), $734M (H1 FY2026)
- Adjusted FCF: $1.32B (FY2025), $632M (H1 FY2026)
- CapEx guided to ~1.0% of revenue FY2026, rising to 1.5-2.0% in FY2027 with U.S. expansion

**Capital Return:**
- Buybacks: $6.6B since 2016, $1.0B in FY2025, $600M in H1 FY2026
- Shares outstanding reduced 25% over 4 years (141M → ~106M)
- Dividend negligible ($0.32 annualized, <0.1% yield)

**Balance Sheet:**
The 11.25x D/E ratio appears alarming but requires context. Total debt stands at $3.88B (up from $2.89B after January 2026 $1B issuance for refinancing). Stockholders' equity of $1.35B is depressed by $8.5B in treasury stock from buybacks. Actual liquidity is robust: $1.8B cash + $4.2B unused credit facility + $700M securitization + ~$4B in receivable sale programs. Interest coverage is comfortable at ~8x ($1.2B operating income vs. $147M interest expense). The Altman Z-Score of 1.99 reflects the engineered equity base, not operational distress.

Working capital management has improved materially — the sales cycle compressed from 34 days (Q4 FY2024) to 18-21 days, reflecting faster-turning AI infrastructure products.

## Growth Analysis

**AI Infrastructure is the Growth Engine:**
The Intelligent Infrastructure segment grew from 32% of revenue in FY2023 to 49% in Q2 FY2026, at 53% in H1 FY2026. This is driven by:

1. AI server rack manufacturing for hyperscalers (Amazon confirmed via warrant)
2. Liquid cooling systems (Mikros acquisition)
3. Critical power management (Hanley acquisition, $748M)
4. Optical transceiver development (Sivers partnership for 1.6T modules)

**Estimate Revisions:**
- Current year EPS: $12.36 (up 6.5% over 90 days)
- Next year EPS: $14.59 (up 8.2% over 90 days)
- 4/4 quarterly beats with average surprise of 7.9%

The estimate revision velocity is positive but modest (+6.5% over 90 days for current year), suggesting consensus is tracking the fundamental improvement rather than lagging significantly behind it.

**Growth Concerns:**
- Connected Living segment declining 8-10% YoY — organic weakness in consumer-facing markets
- Regulated Industries growing at low single digits, not contributing to the growth story
- AI infrastructure growth is concentrated in a single large customer (Amazon ~16% of total revenue, likely higher % of Intelligent Infrastructure)

## Valuation Assessment

At $380, JBL trades at:
- ~64x trailing EPS ($5.92 — though this appears to reflect a TTM including transition quarters)
- ~31x FY2026E EPS ($12.36)
- ~26x FY2027E EPS ($14.59)
- Peer median P/E: 56.9x (FLEX at 56.9x, FN at 60.5x, SANM at 52.2x)

On forward estimates, JBL's 31x multiple is below the EMS peer median, which supports a relative case. The PEG ratio on FY2027 growth (~18% EPS growth implied) is roughly 1.4x — reasonable but not cheap.

The critical valuation concern: **the stock is trading near the analyst consensus high target of $384.** The mean target is $316, and median is $300. When a stock trades at its consensus high, there is limited near-term upside from traditional re-rating. Analysts will eventually raise targets, but buying here assumes the next round of estimates and targets will be significantly higher — which requires continued acceleration or beat-and-raise execution.

At $170, this was a mispriced EMS company with an emerging AI story. At $380, it is a fully-priced AI infrastructure play that needs to continue executing flawlessly.

## Competitive Landscape

Jabil's competitive position within AI infrastructure manufacturing is strengthening:

1. **Vertical integration:** Liquid cooling (Mikros), critical power (Hanley), advanced optics (Sivers partnership), and mechanical/thermal engineering capabilities create a full-stack offering that pure contract manufacturers cannot replicate
2. **U.S. manufacturing footprint:** 30+ domestic sites with the $500M expansion addressing reshoring demand; competitive advantage vs. Foxconn/Chinese EMS providers
3. **Strategic customer relationships:** The Amazon warrant represents a partnership-level commitment unusual in EMS
4. **Diversification beyond AI:** Automotive, healthcare, renewable energy provide countercyclical ballast

**Vulnerabilities:**
- AI infrastructure revenue is concentrated in hyperscaler capex cycles that could decelerate
- Flex (FLEX) at $53B market cap is a direct competitor also pivoting toward AI infrastructure
- Thin operating margins (5.3%) mean competitive pricing pressure is existential, not incremental
- Foxconn remains the scale leader globally

The company is moving up the value chain from commodity EMS into engineering-intensive manufacturing with higher switching costs.

## Risk Assessment

**Customer Concentration:**
Amazon appears to be ~16% of total revenue and likely 25-30%+ of Intelligent Infrastructure revenue. This single-customer dependency on AI capex decisions represents the primary fundamental risk. If Amazon slows or diversifies its manufacturing partners, the growth narrative breaks.

**Cyclicality:**
AI infrastructure capex has been sustained over 2+ years, but hardware spending cycles eventually mature. A 30% reduction in hyperscaler capex (which occurred in prior cycles — 2019, 2022) would represent a $4-5B revenue hit to Jabil's highest-margin segment.

**Execution Risk:**
Multiple concurrent integrations (Hanley, Pii, Mikros) plus a new $500M facility build while managing 23% revenue growth creates operational complexity. The restructuring history ($500M in charges over FY2024-FY2025) suggests management is still optimizing the post-divestiture business.

**Balance Sheet Sensitivity:**
At $3.88B in debt with 4-5% coupons, a deterioration in cash flow from AI capex slowdown would quickly stress coverage ratios given the thin equity base. The aggressive buyback posture amplifies this — returning $1B+/year while carrying $3.9B in debt assumes continued cash generation.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV of 79.3% vs. 41% historical volatility represents an extreme premium (38.3pp spread). The term structure is in backwardation (79.3% near-term vs. 55.7% 6-month), indicating the market is pricing specific near-term event risk. The put/call volume ratio of 0.66 (call-heavy) and call-heavy skew (-6.8%) suggest participants are positioned for continued upside, but the rich IV signals they're paying significantly for that exposure. The combination of rich IV + call skew + backwardation is consistent with a momentum stock where participants expect continued movement but recognize the risk of a sharp reversal.

**Interpretation:** The options market is pricing ~5% weekly moves and 18.5% 3-month moves. At $380, an 18.5% downside move reaches $310 — near the analyst mean target. The market is pricing a wide distribution of outcomes, appropriate given customer concentration and AI cycle uncertainty.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($450-480, +18-26%)
- Intelligent Infrastructure growth sustains at 40%+ through FY2027
- Margin expansion continues as mix shifts further toward AI (operating margin → 6-7%)
- Amazon relationship deepens; additional hyperscaler contracts won
- FY2027 EPS exceeds $16, stock re-rates to 28-30x = $450-480
- Buybacks continue reducing share count 8-10% annually

### Bear Case ($250-280, -26-34%)
- AI capex cycle matures; hyperscaler spend growth decelerates to 10-15%
- Amazon diversifies manufacturing partners; Jabil's growth premium evaporates
- Operating margins compress from pricing pressure or integration costs
- FY2027 EPS comes in at $12-13 (flat vs. FY2026); stock re-rates to 20-22x
- Market cap contracts to $28-30B range

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock pulls back to $300-320 range (24-26x FY2026E EPS), creating sufficient margin of safety
2. FY2026 full-year results demonstrate sustained 50%+ Intelligent Infrastructure growth with margin expansion to >6%
3. Second major hyperscaler partnership confirmed (reducing Amazon concentration)

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Intelligent Infrastructure growth decelerates below 20% for two consecutive quarters
2. Core operating margin contracts below 4.5% for two consecutive quarters
3. Amazon capex guidance indicates meaningful deceleration (>20% cut)

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (likely mid-June for Q3 FY2026). The upcoming quarter will confirm whether 50%+ Intelligent Infrastructure growth is sustainable or peaking.

## Conclusion

Jabil's transformation into an AI infrastructure manufacturing leader is genuine and substantiated by revenue growth, customer relationships, and strategic acquisitions. The fundamental story is compelling over a multi-year horizon. However, at $380, the stock trades near all analyst targets, at ~31x forward earnings with execution risk concentrated in a single-customer AI capex cycle. The prior score trajectory (6.5 → 6.0) reflected deteriorating risk/reward as the stock appreciated; at current levels this concern is amplified.

The appropriate posture is HOLD with a growth orientation — the business deserves ownership but current entry prices leave insufficient margin of safety for new capital. A pullback to $300-320 or continued earnings acceleration above current consensus would shift the risk/reward toward a BUY.
