## Executive Summary

BGC Group is a global brokerage, marketplace, and financial technology company that has undergone significant transformation over the past 18 months, completing the OTC Global acquisition ($325M, April 2025), achieving 30% revenue growth in FY2025, and launching FMX as a direct competitor to CME in U.S. interest rate futures and Treasuries. The stock trades at $11.10, near its 52-week high of $11.91, following a 34% rally from its November 2025 lows.

The market is pricing BGC at 36x trailing GAAP EPS of $0.31, which makes the stock appear expensive against peers trading at 21-24x. Trailing GAAP earnings are depressed by OTC Global integration charges, elevated interest costs from $700M in new notes, and non-cash partnership amortization. On consensus FY2026 estimates of approximately $1.43 EPS, the forward P/E is approximately 7.8x — a deep discount to peers. Free cash flow of approximately $328M in FY2025 (operating cash flow of $394M less $66M in capital expenditures) against a fully-diluted equity value of approximately $5.3B (based on ~480M total Class A and Class B shares) yields an FCF yield of approximately 6.3%.

The primary risks are governance-related (Lutnick family dual-class control, extensive related-party transactions with Cantor Fitzgerald, untested three-CEO structure) and balance sheet stress (leverage up to $1.78B debt as of year-end 2025). These risks are real but specific and monitorable, and are partially offset by investment-grade credit ratings and strong liquidity ($979M at year-end 2025).

## Company Overview

BGC Group operates as a global intermediary and technology provider across two primary verticals: (1) Energy, Commodities & Shipping (ECS), which became the largest segment at approximately 34-37% of brokerage revenue following the OTC Global acquisition, and (2) Financial Markets including rates, FX, credit, and equities. The company serves institutional clients — banks, hedge funds, corporates, and sovereign entities — through a hybrid execution model combining voice brokerage, electronic trading (Fenics platform), and its disruptive FMX exchange venture.

Key strategic assets include:
- **Fenics**: Technology platform generating $160M quarterly revenue (+12.7% YoY), encompassing fully electronic execution, market data, network connectivity, and post-trade services
- **FMX**: A separately-valued ($667M post-money) challenge to CME's position in U.S. Treasuries and rates futures, backed by 10 major bank equity partners including Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan, and Citadel Securities
- **OTC Global**: The April 2025 acquisition that made BGC the world's largest ECS broker, contributing $341.7M in revenue and $32.9M in pre-tax income from April 1 to December 31, 2025

The company employs 2,513 front-office personnel globally and is headquartered in New York.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:**
Revenue has grown 64% from $1.80B (2022) to $2.94B (2025), with growth accelerating from 12-13% organic to 30% inclusive of OTC Global. Organic growth (excluding OTC Global) was approximately 12% in Q3 2025 and 16% for the first nine months.

**Profitability:**
- Pre-tax margin: 4.5% (Q3 2025) vs. 3.5% (Q3 2024), showing improvement despite integration costs
- Nine-month pre-tax income: $189M (+29% YoY)
- Net margin suppressed at ~5.3% due to elevated interest expense ($125M+ run-rate) and integration-related charges
- Compensation ratio: 64.4% of revenue (Q3 2025), slightly elevated due to OTC Global's commission-heavy model

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow was $394M in FY2025 with free cash flow of approximately $328M (after $66M in capital expenditures). BGC's cash flow from operations includes large non-cash add-backs — particularly $330M in equity-based compensation and allocations of net income to limited partnership units/FPUs, and $140M in employee loan amortization/reserves — while $109M in payments for tax obligations related to equity awards is classified as a financing outflow. These structural features of BGC's compensation model mean the high OCF/net income conversion ratio reflects the company's unique partnership-legacy economics rather than straightforward earnings quality.

**Balance Sheet:**
As of December 31, 2025, total notes payable and other borrowings stood at $1.776B against equity of $972M, producing leverage of approximately 1.8x. The company carries a retained deficit of -$910M (legacy from partnership-era distributions). Liquidity of $979M at year-end 2025 is strong. Credit ratings are investment-grade: BBB- (Fitch, S&P stable), BBB+ (JCR stable), BBB (KBRA Positive). The $300M 4.375% notes that matured in December 2025 were repaid during 2025, removing near-term maturity risk.

**Capital Allocation:**
The company repurchased 32M shares ($282M) through November 2025, representing roughly 8% of the float at an average price of ~$8.81 — well below the current $11.10. A $400M buyback authorization was renewed in November 2025. The quarterly dividend of $0.02/share is nominal.

**Share Count & Market Capitalization:**
BGC has both Class A shares (~370.5M as of April 2026) and Class B shares (~109.5M), for a total of approximately 480M economically-participating common shares. At $11.10 per share, the total equity value is approximately $5.3B. Class B shares, held primarily by Cantor/CFGM, are convertible into Class A and participate fully in economic returns.

## Growth Analysis

**Estimate Revisions — Strongest Forward Signal:**
Based on available consensus data, current quarter EPS estimates have risen approximately 15.7% over 90 days and FY2026 consensus is estimated at approximately $1.43, with FY2027 consensus estimated at approximately $1.60, implying 12% earnings growth. This positive revision momentum is meaningful given BGC's consistent track record of meeting or beating (2 beats, 2 in-line over last 4 quarters, avg surprise +2%).

**Growth Drivers:**
1. **OTC Global Revenue Synergy**: $341.7M revenue contribution from April 1 to December 31, 2025, on a $325M purchase price (~0.75x revenue acquisition multiple). Cross-selling OTC Global's energy client base onto BGC's electronic platforms represents multi-year revenue runway.
2. **FMX Ramp**: UST platform ADV hit $59.4B with 37% CLOB market share. FX ADV grew 44% to $13.1B. SOFR futures open interest grew 3x sequentially in Q3 2025.
3. **Macro Environment**: Elevated interest rates, expanded sovereign debt issuance, and geopolitical volatility all drive secondary market trading volumes. The current environment is structurally favorable for BGC's transaction-based revenue model.
4. **Fenics Electronification**: 12.7% organic growth in Fenics revenue; transition from voice to electronic raises margins. Fenics contributed approximately 24% of total brokerage revenues in FY2025.
5. **$25M Cost Reduction Program**: Completed in Q4 2025, expected to enhance margins in 2026.

## Valuation Assessment

The surface-level 36x trailing P/E is misleading and explains why the stock may be mispriced:

| Metric | BGC | Peer Median | Premium/Discount |
|--------|-----|-------------|------------------|
| Trailing P/E | 36x | 21.8x | +65% premium |
| Forward P/E (FY2026 ~$1.43) | ~7.8x | 21.8x | -64% discount |
| P/FCF (total shares) | ~16x | — | Moderate |
| Price/Sales (total shares) | ~1.8x | — | Modest |

The divergence between trailing and forward P/E arises because: (a) OTC Global closed April 2025 and its $450M+ annualized revenue is only partially reflected in TTM earnings; (b) interest costs from the $700M April 2025 debt issuance hit GAAP earnings fully while OTC Global's contribution ramps; (c) integration charges and non-cash amortization depress GAAP near-term.

If BGC achieves approximately $1.43 EPS in FY2026 (which rising estimates support), the stock trades at approximately 7.8x forward earnings — roughly one-third the peer group's multiple. Even applying a governance discount of 30% to the peer median of 21.8x yields a warranted multiple of ~15x, implying a target price near $21.50. A more conservative 12-14x on $1.43 yields $17-20.

**FMX Embedded Option**: BGC's ~64% stake in FMX (valued at $667M post-money in the 2024 financing round) is worth approximately $425M or approximately $0.89/share (based on total share count) — representing meaningful optionality not fully reflected in the current valuation.

Analyst consensus targets of $14.00-$15.00 represent 26-35% upside from current levels, with both covering analysts at Buy/Strong Buy.

## Competitive Landscape

1. **ECS Market Leadership**: The OTC Global acquisition made BGC the world's largest ECS broker by revenue. In a market where scale begets liquidity, which begets more flow, this is a structural advantage. ECS revenue grew 114% YoY in Q3 2025.

2. **FMX vs. CME**: FMX has achieved 37% CLOB market share in U.S. Treasuries and is gaining traction in rates futures. The 10-bank equity partner consortium provides structural volume commitments and represents a significant competitive effort against CME's rates franchise. FMX remains early-stage in futures but the strategic backing is substantial.

3. **Technology Differentiation vs. TP ICAP/Tradition**: BGC's Fenics platform and electronic execution capabilities differentiate it from pure voice-brokerage peers. TSR of 163.7% vs. peer group's 121.7% reflects the market recognizing this differentiation.

4. **Benchmark Administration**: The January 2026 FCA license as a U.K. registered benchmark administrator opens recurring, high-margin revenue streams.

BGC is gaining share in ECS, growing faster organically than peers, and investing in technology platforms (Fenics, FMX) that should command higher multiples over time.

## Risk Assessment

**Governance & Related-Party Risk (Primary):**
The Lutnick family maintains approximately 75% voting control through dual-class shares despite only ~23% economic ownership. Cantor Fitzgerald provides administrative services and employee leasing arrangements (BGC was charged $141.9M by Cantor and affiliates for the full year 2025, including $103.6M to cover compensation to leased employees). This creates extraction risk that cannot be fully monitored by outside shareholders. The untested three-CEO structure adds execution uncertainty.

**Leverage:**
Total debt at $1.78B as of year-end 2025, down from $1.84B at Q3 following the repayment of the $300M December 2025 notes. At current investment-grade rates (~6-6.5%), remaining maturities (May 2028, June 2029, April 2030) provide adequate runway. The company's $979M liquidity and $700M revolver provide strong cushion.

**Integration Execution:**
OTC Global's personnel, technology, and operations are still being integrated. Management excluded OTC Global from internal controls assessment as of Q3 2025 — a standard but non-trivial disclosure for a business contributing over $340M in revenue.

**Volume Cyclicality:**
Transaction-based revenue is inherently cyclical. A sharp decline in volatility or return to near-zero rates would compress volumes. Current macro conditions (elevated rates, geopolitical stress, expanded debt issuance) are highly favorable, but the business model has demonstrated vulnerability in QE environments.

**FMX Execution Risk:**
CME's network effect moat in rates futures is formidable. FMX's success requires sustained bank support and gradual liquidity migration — a multi-year effort with uncertain outcome.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($14-16 | +26% to +44%):**
BGC achieves consensus FY2026 EPS of ~$1.43+ as OTC Global synergies ramp, Fenics grows 15%+, FMX captures meaningful rates futures market share (demonstrated through ADV milestones), and the favorable macro environment sustains above-trend trading volumes. The market re-rates BGC from "brokerage" to "technology + exchange" valuation framework, narrowing the discount to peers. Buybacks continue to reduce float. FMX approaches an IPO or spin-off event, unlocking embedded value.

**Bear Case ($8.00-8.50 | -24% to -28%):**
Macro normalization (diplomatic resolution of geopolitical conflicts, QE resumption) compresses trading volumes. OTC Global integration struggles emerge (key personnel departures, client attrition). FMX fails to gain futures traction and bank partners reduce support. Interest expense burden on $1.78B debt constrains earnings growth. Governance risks materialize as Brandon Lutnick, a relatively untested operator, exercises control over strategic direction.

## Conclusion

BGC offers approximately 6.3% free cash flow yield, 30% revenue growth, rising estimate revisions, and an embedded option on FMX, trading at a significant discount to peer multiples. The governance structure and leverage are genuine risks that justify some discount, but the current market appears to be anchoring on misleading trailing GAAP P/E rather than normalized forward earnings power. At $11.10, the stock prices in minimal value for OTC Global's earnings contribution and limited value for FMX's exchange optionality. With both covering analysts at Buy/Strong Buy and consensus targets of $14-15, the setup favors patient capital willing to accept governance complexity in exchange for fundamental undervaluation. BUY with a 2% position sizing and 9-12 month holding period.
