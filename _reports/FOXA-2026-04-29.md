## Executive Summary

Fox Corporation delivered FY2025 (ended June 2025) revenue of $16.3B (+17% YoY), $3.62B in adjusted EBITDA (+26%), and $2.99B in free cash flow on only $331M of capex. The 18.4% FCF margin reflects a capital-light business model. These results were materially inflated by two non-recurring events: Super Bowl LIX (contributing the bulk of an $870M sports advertising increase) and the 2024 presidential election cycle (~$550M in incremental political advertising at owned stations). FY2026 will face a pronounced revenue cliff from the absence of both.

The stock trades at approximately 12.9x trailing EPS ($4.91) and ~13.7x FY2026E consensus of $4.63 — a significant discount to the peer median P/E of 65.8x, though the peer set is heterogeneous and not directly comparable. The discount partly reflects real structural headwinds: affiliate subscriber counts fell 9% in a single year (67M to 61M for FOX News), a trend management expects to "continue and possibly accelerate." FOX One, the company's forthcoming DTC streaming service, is strategically sound but introduces meaningful execution and channel conflict risk.

The investment case is complicated by governance, insider behavior, and market positioning. The Murdoch Family Trust controls ~44% of voting power with negligible Class A economic exposure. Both the CEO and COO exercised and sold substantially all of their Class A shares in March 2026. Short interest stands at 18.4% of float with 8.4 days to cover — among the highest readings in the broadcasting sector. Options markets are pricing near-term stress (39.7% near-term IV vs. 27.6% HV, steep backwardation). These signals collectively argue for caution despite a cheap headline multiple.

## Company Overview

Fox Corporation was spun off from 21st Century Fox in March 2019, retaining the live, appointment-based content businesses while Disney acquired the entertainment assets. The company operates through two segments:

**Cable Network Programming (43% of FY2025 revenue, $6.93B):** FOX News (the #1 cable network in the U.S. for 10 consecutive years), FOX Business, FS1, FS2, Big Ten Network, and FOX Deportes. This segment generates a 43.7% EBITDA margin — exceptionally high for media — driven primarily by FOX News's pricing power and loyal audience.

**Television (57% of revenue, $9.33B):** The FOX broadcast network, Tubi (AVOD streaming with ~2.2% of all U.S. TV viewing and 11B hours streamed in FY2025), 29 owned-and-operated stations in 14 of the top 15 DMAs, and FOX Entertainment Studios.

Revenue splits roughly 47% affiliate fees ($7.7B), 42% advertising ($6.9B), and 11% other (primarily sports sublicensing). The dual-class share structure gives the Murdoch Family Trust controlling voting power (~44%) with minimal Class A economic exposure.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue and Profitability

FY2025 revenue of $16.3B represented 17% growth, but the underlying organic trajectory is far more modest once cyclical items are stripped out:

| Metric | FY2022 | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Revenue | $13.97B | $14.91B | $13.98B | $16.30B |
| Net Income | $1.21B | $1.24B | $1.55B | $2.29B |
| Adj. EBITDA | ~$2.96B | $3.19B | $2.88B | $3.62B |
| Adj. EBITDA Margin | ~21% | ~21% | ~21% | ~22% |

The revenue pattern is clearly cyclical: FY2023 (Super Bowl + midterms) was strong, FY2024 (no major events) pulled back, and FY2025 (Super Bowl + presidential election) surged. FY2026 will follow the same pattern downward. Consensus FY2026 EPS of $4.63 implies a ~6% decline from FY2025's $4.91 — but this likely understates the headwind given the magnitude of the non-recurring items.

Current quarter estimates have been revised down 16.5% over the last 90 days ($1.16 to $0.97), suggesting the market is adjusting to the post-cyclical reality. Full-year FY2026 estimates are more stable at $4.63 (flat over 30 days), and FY2027 at $5.16 (+1.8% over 90 days), implying a return to growth.

### Balance Sheet and Cash Flow

The balance sheet is in strong condition:

- **Cash:** $5.4B
- **Long-term debt:** $6.6B (net debt ~$1.2B)
- **Current ratio:** 2.91x
- **FCF:** $2.99B (18.4% margin) on $331M capex
- **Credit ratings:** Baa2/BBB (both stable)

The $5.4B cash balance and only $1.2B net debt provide substantial financial flexibility. FOX repaid $600M of maturing notes in April 2025 and has a $1.0B undrawn revolver maturing June 2028. The Altman Z-Score of 1.97 falls in the "grey zone," but this metric is misleading for asset-light media companies with large intangibles; the underlying cash generation is robust.

Capital allocation has been shareholder-friendly: ~$1.25B returned via buybacks and dividends in FY2025, with a $12B cumulative repurchase authorization. The $0.54/share annual dividend yields ~0.9%.

### Earnings Quality

Four consecutive quarterly earnings beats averaging 35.7% surprise partly reflects conservative sell-side modeling of election/Super Bowl revenue. FCF of $2.99B exceeded net income of $2.29B (131% conversion), indicating high earnings quality. Net income benefited from $438M in non-operating gains (primarily Flutter/equity investment fair value changes), which are non-recurring and non-core.

## Growth Analysis

### Secular Headwinds

The most important growth variable for FOX is the trajectory of its affiliate subscriber base. FOX News dropped from 67M to 61M subscribers (-9%) in a single year. FS1 mirrored this decline. Big Ten Network fell from 45M to 42M (-7%). Management's own 10-K language states these declines "are expected to continue and possibly accelerate."

FOX has partially offset subscriber losses through higher per-subscriber rates: approximately $790M in rate increases versus ~$460M in subscriber loss impact in FY2025. But this math becomes progressively harder as the base shrinks — each rate increase required to offset a given subscriber loss must be proportionally larger, eventually testing MVPD willingness to pay.

### Growth Vectors

**Tubi:** The AVOD platform grew hours streamed 13% YoY to 11B, reaching ~2.2% of all U.S. TV viewing. Tubi serves a younger, cord-cutting audience that FOX News and linear sports cannot reach. Advertising revenue growth here is real, but Tubi operates in an increasingly competitive AVOD landscape (Pluto TV, Roku Channel, Samsung TV+, Amazon Freevee).

**FOX One:** The wholly-owned DTC streaming service expected to launch Fall 2025 (likely in market by the time of this report) is the most strategically significant initiative. By bundling FOX News, FOX Sports, Big Ten Network, and the broadcast network into one subscription, FOX One addresses cord-cutting directly. However, it risks cannibalizing existing MVPD relationships. If MVPDs respond by reducing carriage fees or dropping FOX channels, the near-term revenue impact could be negative. Management acknowledged this risk explicitly in the 10-K.

**Sports Rights:** The NFL contract (through 2033), MLB World Series rights, Big Ten, NASCAR, FIFA Men's World Cup, INDYCAR, and LIV Golf provide premium live content that is highly resistant to time-shifting. These rights are expensive (operating expenses rose $1.4B in FY2025 partly from higher sports costs) but structurally important as linear TV's remaining competitive advantage.

**Flutter/FanDuel Option:** FOX holds a ~2.4% equity stake in Flutter Entertainment plus a 10-year call option (expiring December 2030) to acquire 18.6% of FanDuel at ~$4.5B. This option has material embedded value given FanDuel's market position, but it is not reflected in operating earnings.

### Net Assessment

Stripping out cyclical events, FOX's underlying revenue growth trajectory is low-single-digits at best, constrained by accelerating cord-cutting partially offset by Tubi growth and rate increases. The FY2027 EPS estimate of $5.16 implies ~11% growth over FY2026 — achievable if FOX One gains traction and Tubi continues scaling, but not a high-growth story.

## Valuation Assessment

FOXA trades at ~12.9x trailing EPS ($4.91) and ~13.7x FY2026E ($4.63). Against the peer set:

| Metric | FOXA | FOX (Class B) | NXST | TGNA | WBD | Peer Median |
|--------|------|---------------|------|------|-----|-------------|
| P/E | ~12.9x | 13.9x | 65.8x | 16.3x | 94.4x | 65.8x |
| Div Yield | 0.9% | 1.0% | 3.8% | 2.5% | — | 2.5% |

The massive discount to peer median (65.8x) is misleading — Nexstar and WBD trade at inflated P/Es due to depressed or distorted earnings, not because the market values them more highly. Versus TGNA at 16.3x, FOX's ~13x multiple is modestly discounted but not dramatically so, and TGNA lacks FOX's sports rights and national platform.

On an EV/EBITDA basis, FOX trades at roughly $12.7B market cap + $6.6B debt - $5.4B cash = ~$13.9B EV, or ~3.8x FY2025 EBITDA ($3.62B). Adjusting for a normalized EBITDA of ~$3.0-3.2B (stripping Super Bowl and election), the multiple expands to ~4.3-4.6x — reasonable for media but not cheap.

FCF yield is attractive: $2.99B trailing FCF on a $12.7B market cap = 23.6%, though this overstates the normalized yield given the cyclical peak. Normalizing FCF to ~$2.0-2.3B (removing election/Super Bowl contribution) yields 16-18% — still strong and supportive of the current price.

The valuation is fair given the structural headwinds. A cheap headline multiple on a structurally challenged business with declining subscribers is consistent with value-trap risk. The market is not obviously mispricing FOX — the discount reflects real concerns about cord-cutting acceleration and cyclical earnings normalization. A score of 6 reflects that the stock is not expensive but lacks a clear catalyst for re-rating.

## Competitive Landscape

FOX occupies a defensible niche as the only major U.S. media company focused exclusively on live, appointment-based programming without legacy studio/entertainment operations. This strategic focus provides capital efficiency and clear identity but also limits diversification into content licensing or international distribution.

**Strengths:**
- FOX News: 10 consecutive years as #1 cable network, deeply entrenched audience loyalty. The franchise is structurally durable because its audience identity is tied to political/cultural alignment, creating high switching costs.
- Sports rights: NFL, MLB World Series, Big Ten, and FIFA World Cup give FOX must-have content for MVPDs and advertisers. Sports remain the single strongest argument against cord-cutting.
- Tubi: Positioned in the growing AVOD segment, reaching audiences that traditional FOX cannot.
- 29 O&O stations in top DMAs provide local market dominance and political advertising capture.

**Weaknesses:**
- Accelerating subscriber losses with management acknowledging likely continuation.
- Sports rights cost escalation outpacing revenue growth — the NFL contract has escalating payments through 2033.
- No scripted content library for licensing revenue (unlike Disney, NBCUniversal, or Warner Bros. Discovery).
- FOX One launch risks cannibalizing MVPD relationships that currently generate $7.7B in affiliate fees.

The competitive position is stable but the trajectory is negative. FOX News's dominance is secure near-term, but the platform through which viewers access it (linear cable) is structurally shrinking. FOX One is the strategic response, but its success is unproven.

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

**1. Cord-Cutting Acceleration (High Impact, High Probability):** If FOX News subscribers fall to 50M within 3 years, rate increases alone may not offset the volume loss. This risk is partially priced at ~13x earnings but could warrant an even lower multiple if the decline accelerates beyond current trajectory.

**2. FY2026 Revenue Cliff (High Impact, Near-Certainty):** Current quarter estimates already reflect the post-cycle normalization (-16.5% revision), but full-year estimates may still be optimistic if advertising markets weaken due to macro headwinds (consumer weakness, CIO budget freezes affecting ad spending).

**3. FOX One Execution / Channel Conflict (High Impact, Moderate Probability):** A failed or underwhelming FOX One launch would eliminate FOX's primary strategic response to cord-cutting. Conversely, a successful launch that aggressively cannibalizes MVPD revenue before DTC subscriptions scale could create a transition gap.

**4. Smartmatic Litigation (Moderate Impact, Uncertain Probability):** The Dominion settlement ($800M) established that FOX faces material financial exposure from defamation claims. Smartmatic and other pending cases could result in additional settlements or judgments.

**5. C-Band Spectrum Loss (Moderate Impact, Moderate Probability):** The July 2025 legislation directing FCC to auction 100 MHz of C-Band spectrum within two years could disrupt FOX's satellite distribution infrastructure.

**6. Governance / Controlled Company Risk (Ongoing):** Class A shareholders have negligible voting power. The Murdoch Family Trust's interests may diverge from minority shareholders — including potential News Corp-related transactions.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 39.7% is 12.1 percentage points above 1-year historical volatility of 27.6%. The term structure is in steep backwardation (39.7% at 1-month collapsing to 26.0% at 6-month), indicating the market is pricing a specific near-term event rather than sustained elevated risk. The put/call volume ratio of 5.0 and OI ratio of 3.63 confirm heavy put demand.

Combined with 18.4% short interest and 8.4 days to cover, the derivatives and lending markets are both positioned for downside. The fundamental case (cheap on normalized earnings, strong FCF, low net debt) argues for at least a HOLD, while positioning indicates informed participants see near-term risks the headline numbers don't capture. The options backwardation suggests the catalyst is expected within weeks — possibly related to FOX One launch reception, advertising market data, or upcoming earnings.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($75-80, +19-27%)
- FOX One launches successfully and gains 5-10M subscribers within 12 months, creating a new revenue stream that more than offsets MVPD subscriber attrition
- Tubi continues scaling (15%+ hours growth) and advertising monetization improves
- FanDuel option value crystallizes as sports betting continues expanding
- Aggressive buyback program at depressed valuations concentrates earnings per share
- Normalized FY2027 EPS of $5.16 at 15x = $77

### Bear Case ($45-48, -29-24%)
- Cord-cutting accelerates beyond 9% annually as FOX One cannibalizes MVPD relationships faster than it acquires DTC subscribers
- Advertising recession hits FY2026 harder than expected, particularly with consumer weakness and $100+ oil acting as headwinds
- Smartmatic settlement adds another $500M+ liability
- Short thesis plays out as FOX One launch disappoints
- FY2026 EPS of $4.00 at 11x = $44

### Base Case ($67-72, +6-14%)
- FOX One launches with modest initial traction, neither transformative nor disruptive
- FY2026 represents a cyclical trough at ~$4.50-4.63 EPS
- Cord-cutting continues at ~8-10% annually, offset by rate increases and Tubi growth
- FY2027 earnings recovery to $5.00+ supports gradual re-rating
- 14x FY2027E EPS of $5.16 = $72

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

### Upgrade Triggers (to BUY)
1. FOX One subscriber count exceeds 5M within 6 months of launch with MVPD affiliate fee renewals showing no deterioration — confirming the DTC transition is additive rather than cannibalistic
2. Stock declines to $52-55 range (11-12x FY2026E) with no fundamental deterioration beyond expected cyclical normalization — creating more compelling risk/reward
3. Two consecutive quarters of affiliate subscriber declines slowing to <5% annually, demonstrating cord-cutting deceleration

### Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID)
1. FOX News affiliate subscribers fall below 55M (indicating acceleration beyond the current 9% decline rate)
2. MVPD partners publicly reduce carriage commitments or renegotiate terms materially downward in response to FOX One launch
3. Smartmatic settlement or judgment exceeding $1B

### Reassessment Timeline
- **Next earnings (May 2026):** Q3 FY2026 results will be the first full quarter reflecting post-Super Bowl, post-election normalization and potentially early FOX One subscriber data
- **Fall 2026:** FOX One will have been in market for approximately one year, providing meaningful subscriber and retention data

## Conclusion

Fox Corporation has a strong balance sheet, an exceptionally capital-efficient business model, and a dominant franchise in FOX News. At ~13x trailing earnings and 13.7x FY2026 consensus, the stock is not expensive in absolute terms.

However, the valuation discount reflects real structural challenges: accelerating affiliate subscriber declines, a cyclical revenue cliff, unproven FOX One execution, and controlled-company governance. Options and short-selling markets are both positioned for near-term downside, and both the CEO and COO sold substantially all of their Class A shares in March 2026.

The risk/reward is balanced: ~6-14% upside to base case versus 24-29% downside in the bear case. FOX One is the swing variable — a successful launch path would validate a BUY thesis, while a disappointing one would confirm the structural concerns driving the short interest. Until execution evidence materializes, the appropriate posture is to wait.

**Rating: HOLD (Uncertain)**
