## Executive Summary

Ormat Technologies is the world's leading vertically integrated geothermal energy company, operating 1,340MW of capacity across 35 plants globally with an 84% capacity factor—three to four times that of wind or solar. The company recently secured a 150MW PPA with Google for data center baseload power, validating a structural demand thesis for geothermal that could drive meaningful revenue growth in 2028-2030.

The stock currently trades at 56x trailing earnings on three consecutive years of flat net income ($124M), negative free cash flow (-$285M in FY2025), and a deteriorating core electricity segment margin (from 36.6% to 28.5% over two years). Net debt/EBITDA of 4.36x with $780M+ in maturities due in 2027 adds financing risk. The stock has rallied 56% from its 52-week low, pricing in substantial forward optimism, while consensus estimates are declining (-9.6% current year over the past 90 days).

Ormat's competitive moat in geothermal is genuine and the data center demand catalyst is specific and dated (Nevada PUC approval expected H2 2026, energy deliveries 2028-2030). At current valuation, the margin of safety is thin and the risk/reward asymmetry is unfavorable for new capital deployment. Rating: Growth Hold.

## Company Overview

Ormat operates three business segments:

**Electricity (70.1% of FY2025 revenue):** Owns/operates geothermal, solar PV, and recovered energy plants. Revenue is contracted under long-term PPAs with a weighted average remaining term of ~14 years. The segment generates the bulk of profitability, with international operations (primarily Kenya at 150MW/Olkaria III) contributing 49% of segment net income.

**Products (21.9%):** Designs and manufactures Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC) power units, providing EPC services globally. Backlog was $352M as of February 2026, concentrated in New Zealand (68%). Revenue surged 55% in FY2025 on large project deliveries.

**Energy Storage (8.0%):** Owns 19 BESS facilities (415MW/1,038MWh) with 410MW/1,540MWh under construction. Management targets 950-1,050MW by year-end 2028. Revenue doubled YoY to $79M.

The company holds 182 active patents related to ORC binary geothermal technology, which reinjects all fluids (near-zero emissions), operates without cooling water in most configurations, and achieves high availability. Manufacturing is primarily in Israel (~26 miles from Gaza).

### The Flat Earnings Problem

| Metric | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Net Income ($M) | $124 | $124 | $124 |
| Gross Margin | 31.8% | 31.0% | 27.6% |
| Operating Margin | 20.1% | 19.6% | 17.1% |
| Interest Expense ($M) | $99 | $118 | $142 |
| Depreciation ($M) | $263 | $275 | $292 |

Net income has been flat for three years despite 19% cumulative revenue growth. The cause is quantifiable: depreciation increased $29M and interest expense increased $43M over two years—a combined $72M headwind against ~$161M in revenue growth. The Electricity segment gross margin deteriorated 810bps, from 36.6% to 28.5%, driven by resource cooling at multiple plants, higher property taxes, and curtailment losses.

Adjusted EBITDA grew to $582M (+5.7% YoY, +21% over two years), which better reflects underlying operational performance. But GAAP earnings drive the P/E multiple investors actually pay.

### Balance Sheet & Cash Flow

Total debt is approximately $2.66B against $6.25B in total assets. Net debt/EBITDA is 4.36x (covenant limit 6.0x). The current ratio is 0.81, indicating reliance on ongoing capital market access. Free cash flow was -$285M in FY2025 on $620M in capex against $335M in operating cash flow. FY2026 capex is budgeted at $675M.

The $476M in convertible notes due July 2027 is the most significant near-term financing event. Total 2027 maturities exceed $780M. Refinancing these obligations in an elevated rate environment will either increase interest expense further or dilute shareholders.

### Tax Benefit Monetization

Ormat generated $67M from sale of tax benefits in FY2025. This is a structurally recurring revenue source given the OBBBA legislation extending geothermal eligibility for 100% PTC/ITC through end of 2033, providing a long runway.

## Growth Analysis

### The Data Center Demand Catalyst (Specific and Dated)

The Google 150MW PPA is the most important growth catalyst. Signed in February 2026, it involves building new geothermal capacity in Nevada for delivery through NV Energy's Clean Transition Tariff. Key parameters:
- **Size**: Up to 150MW—equal to ~11% of current installed capacity
- **Pricing**: Likely >$100/MWh vs. historical $60-80/MWh (management commentary)
- **Timeline**: Nevada PUC approval expected H2 2026; energy deliveries 2028-2030
- **Contract duration**: 15 years from last project COD

Additional demand signals: Switch PPA (13MW, 20-year term from 2030), Indonesia expansion (~40MW potential), Israel storage awards (300MW/1,200MWh JV).

### Growth Pipeline Quantification

Management has guided to adding 70MW of geothermal capacity by year-end 2026, with the Google project representing a larger step-change in 2028-2030. Energy Storage targets 950-1,050MW by 2028 (from 415MW today—a potential 2.3-2.5x increase).

If the Google PPA adds 150MW at $100/MWh with 84% capacity factor, that's ~$110M in incremental annual revenue at higher margins than the current blended rate, representing ~11% revenue uplift.

### Estimate Revision Concern

Consensus estimates have declined meaningfully:
- Current year EPS: -9.6% over 90 days ($2.50 → $2.26)
- Next year EPS: -6.8% over 90 days ($2.77 → $2.58)

This negative revision trend conflicts with the bullish growth narrative. The 30-day current-quarter revision was +4.4%, possibly reflecting Q1 seasonal strength, but the broader trajectory is clearly downward. Analysts appear to be incorporating the margin compression and interest expense headwinds faster than the Google PPA upside.

### Earnings Beat Track Record

Four consecutive quarters of beats (5.5%, 8.9%, 25.2%, 17.4%) indicate management tends to be conservative in guidance. This provides some offset to falling estimates.

## Competitive Landscape

Ormat's competitive position is strong and improving along one dimension while facing emerging threats along another:

**Strengths (Moat Intact):**
- Only vertically integrated geothermal company globally (exploration → manufacturing → operations)
- 182 patents on ORC binary technology
- 84% capacity factor provides 24/7 baseload power—uniquely valuable for data centers
- $352M product backlog + multi-decade operating history
- Long-term PPAs (~14-year weighted average remaining term) provide revenue visibility
- OBBBA legislation extending tax credits through end of 2033

**Emerging Threats:**
- EGS companies (Fervo Energy, others) could expand the geothermal market dramatically but also challenge Ormat's conventional resource advantage
- Battery storage market is highly competitive with many well-funded entrants
- Product segment facing increasing competition from Mitsubishi/Turboden, Kaishan
- Resource cooling at key plants (McGinness Hills declining ~5°F/year) is a slow degradation of competitive assets

The Google PPA validates that large hyperscalers are willing to pay premium pricing for geothermal baseload, positioning Ormat as a preferred counterparty for similar deals. The constraint is not demand but Ormat's ability to develop new geothermal resources fast enough.

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

1. **Refinancing Risk (Near-Term):** With negative FCF and elevated rates, the 2027 refinancing will either increase interest burden or dilute equity.

2. **Electricity Margin Erosion (Ongoing):** Resource cooling, curtailments, and cost inflation continue compressing margins. McGinness Hills (largest complex, 141MW) declining 5°F/year is particularly concerning.

3. **Collection Risk (Chronic):** KPLC (Kenya) $29.5M overdue; ENEE (Honduras) $20.3M overdue with minimal collections. Kenya contributes disproportionate profit—any PPA renegotiation at lower rates would have outsized earnings impact.

4. **Capital Intensity:** $675M FY2026 capex budget with no FCF generation. Company is dependent on continuous capital market access.

5. **Tariff/Supply Chain (Storage):** BESS growth relies on Chinese battery imports facing FEOC requirements and tariff volatility.

6. **Israel Operations:** Manufacturing and management concentrated near conflict zone. Three years of disruption history.

## Investment Thesis

- Rising geothermal PPA prices (>$100/MWh) flow through on contract renewals, reversing Electricity segment margin compression
- Energy Storage reaches 800-1,000MW by 2028, becoming meaningful revenue contributor

## Conclusion

Ormat Technologies occupies a genuinely unique competitive position as the only vertically integrated geothermal company with proprietary ORC technology and 84% capacity factors, offering 24/7 baseload power with minimal emissions. The Google 150MW PPA validates that hyperscale data centers will pay premium prices for this attribute.

The specific growth catalyst justifying a HOLD over AVOID is the Google PPA and its likely precedent effect for additional hyperscaler contracts, with Nevada PUC approval in H2 2026 as the dated catalyst.

At 56x trailing earnings with flat net income, negative FCF, 4.4x leverage, falling consensus estimates, and deteriorating core margins, the current price discounts optimistic scenario outcomes without adequate margin of safety. The stock has rallied 56% from lows and sits 14% below its 52-week high. New capital deployment here requires near-perfect execution on growth projects that won't contribute to earnings until 2028-2030.

Rating: HOLD for existing positions with a watch for valuation correction (BUY trigger at $95-100) or Google PPA regulatory de-risking.
