## Executive Summary

Ecolab is a high-quality, service-embedded franchise that has just made a large, levered, timing-dependent bet on AI infrastructure. In seven months the company deployed roughly $6.35bn — $1.60bn for Ovivo Electronics (semiconductor ultrapure water, closed 16 December 2025) and $4.75bn for CoolIT Systems (direct liquid cooling, closed 2 July 2026) — funded with $5.0bn of May 2026 senior notes at a weighted-average coupon of 5.011%. Pro forma net debt/EBITDA moved from 2.0x to approximately 3.1x in a single quarter. Management has committed to returning to 2.0x by roughly mid-2028 and disclosed that CoolIT is accretive to adjusted EPS only in 2028, and then only excluding non-cash amortization.

The underlying operating business is performing respectably but narrowly. Q2 2026 organic sales grew 5% — volume +1%, pricing +4% — with adjusted diluted EPS of $2.09, up 11%. High-Tech organic growth was +29% and Life Sciences +15%, while Heavy Water declined 1% and Paper was flat. Organic gross margin rose 10bp against energy-driven raw material, manufacturing and logistics inflation, which is genuine evidence of pricing power. Adjusted operating margin was flat year over year, Global Water's organic operating margin *declined* 50bp as a 2.4pp pricing benefit was more than offset by 3.2pp of cost and investment, and part of the pricing is a disclosed energy surcharge that management describes as adjustable "as market conditions evolve." Disinflation is a bear case for this earnings model, not a bull case.

Valuation is the binding constraint. At $285.17 the stock trades at ~39x trailing GAAP EPS of $7.28, ~35x the FY2026 adjusted guidance midpoint of $8.15, ~30x FY2027 consensus of $9.42, and ~22.8x pro forma EV/EBITDA, against a specialty-chemicals peer median P/E of 30.4x and 5% organic growth. Consensus EPS has been cut across every measured period (current quarter −5.4%, next quarter −6.7%, FY2026 −3.2%, FY2027 −2.3% over 90 days), and the company has now missed consensus in four consecutive quarters, albeit by one to two cents each time. The counterweight, which is real and underappreciated, is that Ecolab has de-rated for five straight years: adjusted EPS compounded ~12.6% annually from 2021 to 2025 while total shareholder return compounded ~5.1%, trailing its own proxy peer group in every measured year. On its own history, 35x forward is not a peak multiple. That keeps the name a HOLD rather than a REDUCE, but it does not make it a purchase at the current price.

## Company Overview

Ecolab describes itself as a global leader in water, hygiene and infection prevention, with $16bn of annual sales, ~48,000 associates and customers in more than 170 countries across 40 industries. The economics are service economics, not commodity chemistry: more than 25,000 of the ~48,000 employees are sales and service personnel, supported by ~3,000 R&D, engineering and digital staff. Customer-premise dispensing equipment, the 3D TRASAR monitoring platform and the field technician relationship are the switching costs.

FY2025 reportable segments and margins (10-K, with margins derived):

| Segment | FY2025 sales | Operating income | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global Water | $7,679.9M | $1,263.9M | 16.5% |
| Global Institutional & Specialty | $5,962.0M | $1,357.8M | 22.8% |
| Global Pest Elimination | $1,219.2M | $237.1M | 19.4% |
| Global Life Sciences | $706.1M | $120.7M | 17.1% |
| Corporate | — | $(353.2)M | — |
| **Consolidated (GAAP)** | **$16,081.2M** | **$2,737.6M** | **17.0%** |

Concentration at the customer level is genuinely diffuse: no customer or distributor exceeded 10% of consolidated revenue in 2023, 2024 or 2025; the largest single raw material is ~5% of raw material purchases; only warewashing exceeds 10% of sales (~13%). Effective 1 January 2026 the former Light & Heavy segment was split into Heavy Water, Light Water and High-Tech, the organizational marker of the pivot.

## Financial Analysis

**Q2 2026 (10-Q filed 6 August 2026).** Net sales $4,415.4M, +10%; organic sales +5%; GAAP operating income $757.9M (+7%); adjusted operating income $809.0M (+10%); GAAP diluted EPS $1.90 (+3%); adjusted diluted EPS $2.09 (+11%). Reported gross margin 44.1% versus 44.8%, but organic gross margin +10bp; adjusted operating margin flat at 18.3%, organic operating margin +40bp to 18.8%. SG&A/sales improved to 25.9% from 26.5%.

The reported gross margin decline is mix, not pricing failure: Ovivo Electronics and CoolIT are equipment and engineering businesses that structurally carry lower gross margin than chemistry. Reported gross margin should keep drifting lower as High-Tech hardware grows. The composition of growth matters more: of the 10% reported increase, volume contributed 1pp, price 4pp, acquisitions 3pp and FX 2pp. Roughly 80% of organic growth is price, and half of reported growth is inorganic or currency.

**Cash conversion is the item a screen would miss.** Derived from the 10-Q cash flow statement: H1 2026 free cash flow was $1,175.4M operating cash flow less $588.6M capex = $586.8M, versus $616.6M in H1 2025 — a 4.8% decline while adjusted diluted EPS rose 12%. Two identifiable reconciling items partly close the gap: $60M of one-time equity incentive payments to Ovivo Electronics employees (adding this back gives $646.8M, +4.9%), and capex up 29% with construction in progress of $945.0M at 30 June. Receivables built $197.0M and inventory $131.4M against 10% sales growth, which is mechanically normal for an accelerating business. I am deliberately not annualizing a single half-year: second-half working-capital release, cash tax timing and discretionary capex could produce full-year FCF growth. As a labeled sensitivity only, holding the H1 rate would imply ~$1.17bn of FY2026 FCF, a ~1.5% yield on $80.3bn of market value, and the ~$250M of incremental annual interest begins landing in H2.

**Leverage.** The 10-Q's reported 2.0x net debt/EBITDA at 30 June is a balance-sheet-date artifact. Total debt was $13,176.2M against $5,135.3M of cash that existed, per the filing's own language, to fund a deal that closed two days after quarter-end. Deriving pro forma: $13,176.2M debt less ~$385M residual cash = ~$12,791M net debt; against standalone TTM EBITDA of $3,925.7M plus CoolIT's implied NTM adjusted EBITDA of ~$164M ($4,750M ÷ 29x) gives **~3.13x**. That reconciles with management's March guidance of "approximately 3x at close, returning to 2x by the end of the second year." This is disclosure timing, not concealment, but any database pulling the June balance sheet understates leverage by more than a full turn.

Liquidity is adequate: $2.0bn revolver undrawn to March 2030, $2.0bn of commercial paper capacity with nothing outstanding, CP rated A-2/P-2/F-1, full covenant compliance. Interest coverage on H1 adjusted operating income was ~10.7x ($1,488.7M ÷ $139.2M). Long-term debt carries at $13,170.8M against fair value of $12,416.7M, reflecting legacy low coupons. Goodwill plus net intangibles of $12,868.4M already exceeds total equity of $10,084.4M; post-CoolIT that gap widens materially and tangible book is deeply negative.

**Capital allocation.** H1 2026 returned $1,093.4M ($424.1M dividends, $669.3M buybacks) against $586.8M of FCF — ~186% of FCF, funded from the balance sheet. Q2 repurchases averaged $252.41, with the bulk executed in May at $252.31, below the current price. Only 3,454,001 shares remain under the November 2022 authorization — about 1.23% of shares outstanding. With leverage at ~3.1x and a stated de-levering commitment, the H1 repurchase pace should not be extrapolated; a per-share growth lever is effectively switching off for the next 18-24 months. The dividend was raised 12.3% to $0.73/quarter, an annualized $2.92 (~1.04% yield, ~36% of guidance midpoint) — comfortably covered.

**Delivery record.** Four consecutive small consensus misses (−0.3% to −1.1%). Individually trivial; collectively they indicate the company is guiding to numbers it lands slightly short of, which argues against assuming upside to FY2027 consensus.

## Growth Analysis

Organic growth by operating segment in Q2: High-Tech +29% (H1 +27%) on microelectronics and data-center wins; Life Sciences +15% with organic operating margin of 26.5% versus 21.0% on a bioprocessing spike; Pest Elimination +7%; Food & Beverage +7%; Specialty +6%; Light Water +3%; Institutional +3%; Heavy Water −1%; Paper flat. Global Water sales into Greater China rose from $202.2M in H1 2025 to $298.9M in H1 2026 (+47.8%), consistent with fab and data-center exposure rather than broad Chinese industrial recovery.

The strategic architecture is coherent: fabs (Ovivo ultrapure water), power generation (the legacy Nalco franchise, serving by Ecolab's account a quarter of generated power), and the data centers themselves (CoolIT hardware plus existing water treatment across >1,000 sites). Management has articulated a Global High-Tech target of $4bn of revenue by 2030 at roughly 25% margins — reported through the 28 July call and multiple outlets, but not verified in a filing, and High-Tech's current revenue base is not disclosed in the 10-Q. That target is the load-bearing assumption in the bull case and it is not yet a filing fact.

The near-term arithmetic is less flattering. FY2026 guidance moved from $8.43-$8.63 excluding CoolIT (March 8-K) to $8.05-$8.25 including it (July call) — roughly $0.38 lower at the midpoints, cutting guided FY2026 growth from +12-15% to ~+8.2% on FY2025's $7.53. That reconciles with the interest math: $250.6M of annual gross interest at seven months' weight is ~$146M pre-tax, or ~$0.40/share after tax, against a CoolIT half-year contribution near break-even after its own depreciation.

FY2027 consensus of $9.42 requires ~+15% growth. Bridging from $8.15: the residual One Ecolab savings ($325M target less $180M delivered = $145M pre-tax, ~$0.40/share after tax) plus roughly $0.87 from operations, with buybacks largely unavailable and incremental CoolIT interest roughly offsetting incremental CoolIT earnings. That implies meaningful operating margin expansion, which Q2's flat adjusted operating margin does not yet evidence. This is the mechanism behind the negative revision trend, and it is why I haircut consensus modestly in the base case.

**On the estimate-revision signal:** large positive 90-day revisions with accelerating revenue are absent here and inverted. Estimates are falling 2-7% across all periods while the stock has risen ~5% since 28 July. That combination is a caution, not a catalyst.

The bull-side valuation argument is the five-year de-rating. From the proxy pay-versus-performance table, $100 invested at 12/31/2020 became $128.15 by 12/31/2025 (a ~5.1% annual TSR) while adjusted EPS went from $4.69 to $7.53 (a ~12.6% CAGR). Roughly 7.5 points of annual multiple compression have already occurred over five years. If the multiple merely holds at 35x on FY2027 consensus, the stock is worth ~$330, precisely the analyst median. Consensus targets are $295 low / $324.67 mean / $330 median / $360 high, and the low target sits above spot, with sentiment classified as improving (5 strong buy, 15 buy, 6 hold).

I do not treat that consensus as independent confirmation. At 35x forward, analyst targets and the share price are extrapolating the same 2030 High-Tech assumption, and estimates behind those targets are being cut. The less charitable reading of the de-rating is also available: the market has been correctly discounting a +1% volume business all along, and further compression is warranted rather than exhausted.

My base case: 33x on a modestly haircut FY2027 EPS of $9.30 ≈ $307, rounded to **$305 (+7.0%)**. That is a positive but unremarkable 12-month return against a 10-year Treasury and a ~20% downside scenario. There is no margin of safety at $285.17, and the specific mispricing required for a high valuation score — a catalyst the market has not seen, or a multiple demonstrably below quality-adjusted peers — is not present.

## Competitive Landscape

The legacy franchise is defensible. The 10-K characterizes Global Water as "led by a few large companies," and Ecolab's structural advantage is the >25,000-person direct sales and service organization plus installed dispensing, dosing and monitoring equipment — expensive and slow to replicate, and being made cheaper to operate by One Ecolab's digital centralization. Q2 evidence supports pricing power: Global Institutional & Specialty expanded organic operating margin 50bp on 3.0pp of pricing against 2.2pp of cost.

The trajectory within that franchise is mixed. Heavy Water fell 1% on softer basic industries, Paper was flat on soft-but-stabilizing customer production, and Institutional's 3% reflected hospitality strength offsetting weaker hospital sales. Global Water — the largest reportable segment — saw organic operating margin *decline* 50bp because pricing did not cover cost and investment. The segment carrying growth (via High-Tech) is where unit economics are currently weakest.

CoolIT changes the competitive character of the incremental business. It is a 25-year-old pure-play supplying coolant distribution units, cold plates, liquid loops and rack manifolds, with custom design work for NVIDIA and AMD — design partners and commercial counterparties, not customers of Ecolab, and neither holds any disclosed ownership interest. CoolIT's customers are hyperscale and colocation operators: a small number of extremely sophisticated buyers with high bargaining power and demonstrated interest in supplier diversification and insourcing. Listed comparables indicated by secondary coverage are Vertiv and nVent, both large and well-capitalized; my broader view of the DLC competitive set is general industry knowledge rather than verified research and should be discounted.

The unresolved question is whether Ecolab converts engineered-hardware sales into a recurring "Cooling-as-a-Service" relationship wrapped in chemistry, monitoring and field service. If it does, the acquisition multiple (29x NTM adjusted EBITDA, 8.6x NTM sales) is defensible as buying a durable annuity. If it does not, Ecolab has paid a chemistry multiple for a hardware business exposed to a capex cycle. There is no disclosed CoolIT backlog, contract structure, customer concentration or margin history. The $204.9M contract liability at 30 June is not backlog; the 10-Q states it is recognized primarily in the subsequent quarter.

One measurement caution: Ecolab's *end-market* diversification is improving (data centers added to 40 industries) while *customer* concentration within the fastest-growing portfolio segment is almost certainly worsening. Those are different metrics and improvement in the first does not establish improvement in the second. The FY2025 10-K was filed four weeks before CoolIT was announced and contains no data-center concentration or hardware supply-chain risk factor; the Q2 10-Q states risk factors are unchanged. The FY2026 10-K is the document that will settle this.

## Risk Assessment

**1. AI capex duration is now the marginal valuation driver.** For $4.75bn to earn Ecolab's cost of capital, CoolIT EBITDA must roughly double from ~$164M toward $330M+, requiring sustained multi-year double-digit liquid-cooling growth. The demand hypothesis rests on sustained hyperscaler capex; the risk is that Ecolab bought at 29x NTM EBITDA into a hardware layer exposed to a capex cycle and to buyers who pursue dual-sourcing.

**2. Impairment exposure.** The FY2025 10-K's impairment risk factor was modified to add Ovivo Electronics alongside Nalco and Purolite, against $9.2bn of goodwill at year-end. Post-CoolIT, goodwill plus intangibles likely approaches or exceeds $17bn against ~$10.1bn of equity. Nine of ten reporting units cleared the Q2 test by "a significant margin," but Ovivo Electronics was tested only qualitatively and CoolIT's purchase accounting has not begun. Ovivo's allocation has already moved once (customer relationships −$116.6M, goodwill +$108.4M in H1). Freshly acquired, cycle-exposed, goodwill-heavy reporting units are where impairment concentrates.

**3. Pricing dependence into possible disinflation.** Volume has been +1% for consecutive reported periods. Management explicitly ties input inflation to Middle East energy volatility and began implementing an energy surcharge in Q2 that "might be adjusted as market conditions evolve." The 10-K warns that Ecolab may find it difficult to hold price if inflation eases with slowing growth, and that multi-year contracts contain terms limiting pricing flexibility. Energy-cost normalization would be a headwind to Ecolab's earnings model, not a tailwind.

**4. Leverage and the de-levering commitment.** ~3.1x pro forma with a stated return to 2.0x by roughly mid-2028. Not distressed for an investment-grade industrial with 10.7x coverage, but it removes the buyback lever (1.23% of shares remain authorized) at a time when higher rates raise the cost of the floating portion (~$1.5bn swapped, 100bp = ~$15M) and of any refinancing. Long-term issuer ratings and any outlook change following the $5.0bn issuance were not verified.

**5. Execution load.** Simultaneous phased ERP upgrade, One Ecolab restructuring through 2027, Ovivo integration, CoolIT integration and a nine-operating-segment reorganization effective 1 January 2026.

**6. Credit-loss direction.** H1 bad debt expense $36.1M versus $25.6M (+41%), allowance up from $74.1M to $100.7M (of which +$14.7M is primarily currency). Small against $3.45bn of net receivables, but worth tracking given hospitality, restaurant and heavy-industry exposure.

**7. Litigation, largely resolving.** In July 2026 Nalco reached a settlement in principle with substantially all remaining TPC Group plaintiffs (>5,000 claimants from the November 2019 Port Neches explosion), following a partial July 2025 settlement. Ecolab states any loss "should be covered by insurance subject to deductibles." A settlement in principle is not executed or court-approved and the amount is undisclosed; the Q3 10-Q should be checked for a charge. Separately, 16 US CERCLA sites and a $21.5M remediation accrual.

**8. FX.** FX added 2pp to Q2 sales and ~$0.04 to EPS. With $2.6bn of forwards and $4.9bn of cross-currency net investment hedges, a dollar reversal removes a current tailwind.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 24.5% against 21.8% one-year realized is fair (2.7pp spread), and ATM IV compressed from 30.5% to 24.5% between 28 July and 8 August — routine post-earnings vol crush, not a signal. The term structure is in contango (24.5% at one month, 27.3% at six months) with the nearest-expiry implied move at ±4.0% and the December tenor at ±12.0%, and the December expiry shows the largest absolute skew reading in the chain. The market prices no near-term dislocation but assigns more uncertainty to the longer-dated window, which spans the FY2026 print and the FY2026 10-K, where CoolIT purchase accounting, the revised concentration risk factors and the FY2027 guide all land. The put/call open-interest ratio jumped from 0.36 to 5.06, but the chain totals only 171 contracts across five expirations — too thin to carry inference. Short interest of 1.3% of float and 2.1 days to cover confirms no meaningful bearish positioning. Nothing in the options data contradicts the fundamental read; nothing supports adding at this price either.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $355 (+24.5%)

The five-year de-rating stops. High-Tech compounds toward the $4bn 2030 target at ~25% margins, and Ecolab demonstrates that CoolIT hardware pulls through recurring chemistry, monitoring and service revenue, converting a hardware sale into an Ecolab-style annuity. Ovivo and CoolIT together give Ecolab a position across fabs, power and data centers that no water-treatment peer can assemble, justifying a re-rating rather than continued compression. One Ecolab delivers the remaining $145M of savings, price/cost stays positive, and Life Sciences bioprocessing strength persists. FY2027 adjusted EPS reaches $9.75-$10.00 and the multiple holds at 35-36x. Board and management alignment supports this: a director bought $2.0M of stock at $258 on 10 June and an EVP bought $264K at $263.83 on 11 June, and the company repurchased ~1.15M shares in May at $252.31.

### Bear Case — $225 (−21.1%)

Energy prices normalize, the surcharge rolls off and value-based pricing decelerates while organic volume stays at +1%, so organic growth falls toward 2-3%. AI capex digestion or hyperscaler dual-sourcing slows CoolIT below the ~21% EBITDA growth implied by the 29x/24x purchase multiples, pushing accretion past 2028 and raising impairment risk on a freshly recognized, cycle-exposed reporting unit. The ~$250M of annual interest bites while the buyback authorization exhausts and de-levering absorbs FCF, so per-share growth stalls near 6-8%. Consensus FY2027 of $9.42 proves too high, consistent with four straight misses and 90-day cuts of 2-7%, and the multiple compresses to 24-25x on $8.90-$9.10.

### Base Case — $305 (+7.0%)

Organic growth of 4-5% continues with High-Tech and Life Sciences offsetting flat Heavy Water and Paper. FY2026 lands within $8.05-$8.25; FY2027 comes in modestly below consensus at ~$9.30 as CoolIT interest and slower buybacks offset One Ecolab savings. The multiple stabilizes near 33x — below the ~35x exit-2025 level, above the peer median, reflecting a franchise with genuine pricing power but 1% volume and 3.1x leverage. $9.30 × 33x ≈ $307.

Probability-weighted at 25% bull / 50% base / 25% bear: (0.25 × 24.5%) + (0.50 × 7.0%) + (0.25 × −21.1%) ≈ **+4.4%** over twelve months. That is approximately fair, with negatively skewed dispersion.

### Addressing the trajectory

Scores have declined 6.9 → 6.5 → 6.0 → 5.58 across four reports, all HOLD. This report confirms rather than reverses that direction, and it does so on specific fundamental developments rather than price action — the stock is up ~5% since 28 July and up ~16% from its 19 May low of $243.15. The confirmations: pro forma leverage moved from a guided ~3x to a realized ~3.1x with $5.0bn of notes at 5.011% now on the balance sheet; buyback authorization is down to 1.23% of shares; H1 FCF fell 4.8% while adjusted EPS rose 12%; consensus was cut 2-7% across all periods; and a fourth consecutive small miss was recorded. Financial health steps down from 6.0 to 5.5 on the leverage-plus-FCF-plus-buyback-runway combination, and competitive position from 7.0 to 6.5 because the incremental business Ecolab is buying has weaker margin durability characteristics than the franchise it augments. A fourth consecutive HOLD is warranted only because the five-year de-rating genuinely caps how expensive 35x is on this company's own history and because the insider cluster and May buyback at $252 mark where informed parties saw value. A repeat at a higher price on further estimate cuts would become a REDUCE.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Rating: HOLD** (hold_rationale: uncertain — the 2028-2030 High-Tech outcome that justifies the multiple is not yet evidenced in filings, and near-term earnings momentum is negative)

**Reassessment timeline:** Q3 2026 results (late October 2026), then the FY2026 10-K (expected February 2027), which will disclose CoolIT purchase accounting, revised risk factors on data-center customer concentration, and the FY2027 guide.

**Upgrade triggers (to ACCUMULATE or BUY):**
1. **Price:** entry at $250-262 — the zone where the company repurchased in May ($252.31 average) and where a director and an EVP bought in June ($258.00 and $263.83). At $255 the base case of $305 offers ~20% upside against ~12% downside to the bear case, which clears the bar.
2. **Cash conversion:** FY2026 free cash flow of $2.0bn+ with FCF/adjusted net income above 90%, confirming that H1's shortfall was working capital and growth capex rather than earnings quality.
3. **CoolIT disclosure:** a filing that quantifies CoolIT revenue, margin and customer concentration, plus either evidence of Cooling-as-a-Service conversion (recurring revenue attach) or a guide for accretion pulled forward from 2028.
4. **Volume:** organic volume growth reaching +3% or better for two consecutive quarters, breaking the price-only dependence.

**Downgrade triggers (to REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. **Estimates:** a further 5%+ cut to FY2027 consensus (from $9.42) without a corresponding price decline, or an outright guidance cut for FY2026 below $8.05.
2. **Pricing reversal:** withdrawal or reduction of the energy surcharge, or organic pricing decelerating below +2% while volume stays at or below +1%.
3. **Leverage:** net debt/EBITDA above 3.3x at any quarter-end, a suspension of the dividend growth trajectory, or an incremental debt-funded acquisition before leverage returns below 2.5x.
4. **Impairment:** any goodwill or intangible impairment charge in the Ovivo Electronics, data-centers-and-microelectronics or CoolIT reporting units.
5. **Deal economics:** disclosure that CoolIT accretion has slipped beyond 2028 even on the adjusted, ex-amortization basis.

**Upside/downside from $285.17:** base $305 = +7.0%; bull $355 = +24.5%; bear $225 = −21.1%. Analyst range $295-$360 (mean $324.67, median $330); the consensus low sits 3.4% above spot, but consensus targets on a 35x forward multiple with falling estimates are not independent confirmation.

## Conclusion

Ecolab remains a genuinely differentiated business: 25,000 sales and service personnel, installed dispensing and monitoring equipment, and pricing power demonstrated by a 10bp organic gross margin gain in a quarter of energy-driven input inflation. Management's decision to buy into the AI physical stack is strategically coherent and was made against its own incentive metric, since the CoolIT deal dilutes the adjusted EPS that anchors executive compensation.

The equity is priced for the 2030 outcome, not the 2026 one. At $285.17 the stock trades at ~35x FY2026 guidance and ~30x FY2027 consensus against 5% organic growth of which 80% is price, flat adjusted operating margin, +1% volume for three consecutive periods, pro forma leverage of ~3.1x, a buyback authorization with 1.23% of shares remaining, H1 free cash flow down 4.8% while adjusted EPS rose 12%, four consecutive small consensus misses, and estimates cut 2-7% across every measured period. The offsetting fact — five years of persistent de-rating, 12.6% EPS compounding against 5.1% TSR — is why this is not a REDUCE. It caps the downside argument without creating an entry.

Existing holders should stay. New capital should wait for the low-$260s or below, or for a filing that turns the High-Tech story from a management target into disclosed revenue, margin and recurring-revenue attach. **HOLD.**

### Valuation and Price Discipline

- Current price: $285.17
- Fair value: $305.00
- Margin of safety: +7.0%
- Initiate/add below: $262.00
- Reduce/exit above: $330.00
- Rationale: 33x a modestly haircut FY2027 EPS of $9.30. A premium to the 30.4x specialty-chemicals median is defensible on service-embedded pricing power, but not on +1% volume and ~3.1x pro forma leverage.

**Thesis:** Ecolab is a genuinely differentiated water/hygiene service franchise with demonstrated pricing power, now carrying a $6.35bn levered bet on AI infrastructure (Ovivo, CoolIT) that is accretive only in 2028 and only ex-amortization. At $285.17 the equity prices the 2030 High-Tech outcome, not the 2026 one: +1% volume, flat adjusted operating margin, ~3.1x pro forma leverage, H1 FCF down 4.8% while adjusted EPS rose 12%, and four consecutive small misses.

**Variant view:** Analyst targets ($324.67 mean, $330 median, low $295 above spot) and the share price extrapolate the same unfiled 2030 High-Tech target while estimates are cut 2-7%; that is one correlated assumption, not independent confirmation. I also read energy-cost disinflation as a bear case for a price-led earnings model, not relief.
