## Executive Summary

GameStop now contains two businesses that require separate valuation. The first is a US-centric specialty retailer that has been genuinely repaired: consolidated gross margin has moved from 23.2% (FY2022) to 33.0% (FY2025) to 40.7% (Q1 FY2026), SG&A fell $220.2M (−19.5%) in FY2025 to 25.1% of sales and 24.1% in Q1, and the company produced its first full-year GAAP operating profit since FY2021 at $232.1M. US segment sales grew 3.6% in FY2025 *while closing 727 US stores, 31% of the domestic fleet*, then accelerated to +21.1% in Q1 FY2026. The engine is collectibles, which grew 47.7% in FY2025 and 65.0% in Q1 to become the largest category at 41.8% of sales. Capex is 0.48% of revenue and net PP&E is $51.9M against $10.97B of total assets.

The second business is a discretionary capital-allocation vehicle run by the Investment Committee (CEO Ryan Cohen plus two independent directors) with delegated authority over the entire balance sheet. It holds $8.37B of cash and marketable securities, $983.3M of collateral pledged against put/call pairs referencing roughly 39.0M eBay shares, a $369.6M digital-asset receivable representing 4,709 Bitcoin now roughly 26% below cost with covered calls sold against it, and $4.2B of zero-coupon convertible notes — $1.4B of which is being exchanged for common stock at a 35-day VWAP with an undisclosed floor and ratio, closing around September 23, 2026. Q1 FY2026 reported net income of $389.6M included a $268.4M net unrealized gain on the eBay derivative; ex-marks, we estimate roughly $182M of net income, about $0.41 basic EPS versus the $0.87 reported.

At $18.66 the arithmetic is defensible but not compelling. Post-exchange net financial assets are approximately $13.69/share on a central 526.5M share count, leaving about $2.62B — roughly 6.8x LTM operating income of $386.2M, or 8.8x after a 23% tax — for the operating business plus whatever the eBay pursuit is worth. That is a reasonable multiple. What it does not compensate for is the denominator risk: shareholders approved raising authorized shares from ~450M to 2.5B on July 8 with only about two-thirds in favor, the Jefferies ATM remains live, and management states its strategy is to use "cash, flexible capital structure, **and stock**" to acquire assets, having bid $56B for a company roughly seven times its market capitalization. We rate the shares HOLD with a $20.50 base case, $26.00 bull and $14.00 bear.

## Company Overview

The FY2025 10-K restructures the company around two stated pillars: "Capital Allocation" (deploying the balance sheet into "acquisitions and control transactions") and "Operational Excellence" (maximizing cash flow from what management itself calls the "legacy retail business"). The store fleet is 2,206 (1,598 US, 308 France, 300 Australia), down from 3,203 a year earlier. Canada was divested in Q2 FY2025, New Zealand closed in Q4, and an agreement is signed for a potential sale of France expected to close within twelve months of May 2, 2026. Ireland, Switzerland, Austria, Germany and Italy exited over the prior three years.

Product mix (FY2025): hardware and accessories $1,840.4M (50.7% of sales, −12.3%), software $729.3M (20.1%, −27.5%), collectibles $1,060.2M (29.2%, +47.7%). By Q1 FY2026 collectibles was $348.9M, or 41.8% of sales. Largest vendors are Nintendo, Sony and Pokemon, collectively a majority of new product purchases; a standalone risk factor names Pokemon trading cards specifically as generating a significant portion of collectibles revenue and profit. Graded trading-card submission services were rolled out to all US stores in FY2025, alongside graded-collectibles trade-ins and the Power Packs platform, developed in collaboration with PSA.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and margin.** FY2022 through FY2025 revenue fell from $5,927.2M to $3,629.9M, a −15.1% CAGR. The FY2025 decline of 5.1% was almost entirely divestiture arithmetic (Canada −81.3%, Europe −32.7%) against US +3.6% and Australia +22.2%. Q1 FY2026 revenue was $835.3M, +14.0%.

The margin progression is the most important operating datapoint and it is mechanically explainable: 23.2% → 24.6% → 29.1% → 33.0% → 40.7%. MD&A attributes it in both the annual and quarterly periods to mix shift into collectibles. Console hardware is sold at or near cost by industry convention; collectibles are not. Q1 operating margin of 17.2% ($143.3M on $835.3M) is unusually high for any retailer and is not seasonally representative — Q4 was 30% of FY2025 sales.

**Earnings quality.** FY2025 net income of $418.4M decomposes into operating income $232.1M (itself net of $53.8M of impairments, largely Canada and France disposal groups), net interest income $271.5M (including a $42.2M non-cash warrant charge), a $131.6M loss on digital assets, $12.0M of other income, and a $34.4M income tax *benefit* at a −9.0% effective rate driven by a $141.1M valuation-allowance release. Roughly $306M of the $418.4M was interest income and tax benefit.

That tailwind is over. The Q1 FY2026 effective rate was 23.1% versus 7.2% a year earlier, and the 10-Q states prior-year benefits were "substantially all utilized in fiscal 2025." A nuance worth naming: while the US valuation allowance was released, total valuation allowances *increased* a net $111.9M to $403.2M, meaning releases in one jurisdiction were offset by additions elsewhere. FY2026 reported EPS faces a full statutory-rate headwind against FY2025.

**Cash flow.** FY2025 operating cash flow was $614.8M against $17.5M of capex, giving $597.3M of FCF at a 16.5% margin. Stripping the roughly $314–319M of interest income on the cash pile leaves approximately $280–330M of operating-only FCF (approximate — accrual interest income differs from cash received and no separate cash-interest disclosure exists). Q1 FY2026 OCF of $337.4M should not be annualized: accounts payable and accrued liabilities contributed +$153.5M and income taxes payable +$39.5M, roughly $193M of timing. A normalized alternative build — net income $389.6M less the $285.3M derivative gain and $1.1M digital gain, plus $4.5M D&A, $8.5M SBC and $61.4M deferred taxes — gives approximately $177M before working-capital swings, consistent with the ex-marks earnings estimate.

**Balance sheet (May 2, 2026).** Cash $7,397.6M, marketable securities $970.5M, pledged derivative collateral $983.3M, derivative asset $285.3M, digital assets and receivables $369.6M, inventories $423.3M, total assets $10,974.3M, long-term debt carrying value $4,166.1M, equity $5,842.1M. Financial assets total $10,006.3M; against $4,200M of note principal that is $5,806.3M, or $12.94/share pre-exchange.

Three qualifications matter. The $983.3M of collateral sits in a restricted account controlled by the derivative counterparty and is economically committed. Physically settling the full 39.0M-share eBay position would require roughly $3.6–3.9B of cash on an assumed $91–100 average strike (the only disclosed pricing is $91.20 on the initial 22,176,000-share tranche; strikes on later tranches are undisclosed). And the Bitcoin collateral can be rehypothecated, commingled or unilaterally sold by Coinbase Credit, with the 10-K warning this "may obscure legal title" and that recovery in a custodian insolvency "may be subject to competing claims from other creditors."

Standard screens are uninformative here. An Altman Z of 2.90 is dominated by working-capital-to-assets of 0.901, which restates that roughly 87% of assets are financial. Current ratio of 15.3x and asset turnover of 0.35 are artifacts of the cash pile, not statements about operating health.

**Capital structure.** $4.2B of 0.00% converts — $1.5B due 2030 and $2.7B due 2032 — with holder put dates of April 3, 2028 and December 15, 2028 that constitute the binding obligation. The derived weighted-average conversion price is approximately $29.25 ($4,200M ÷ 143.6M if-converted shares from the Q1 EPS reconciliation), leaving the notes roughly 37% out of the money. Separately, 59,153,963 warrants struck at $32.00 cash expire October 30, 2026; they are classified anti-dilutive and their $1.89B of theoretical proceeds should not be modeled as liquidity. Their expiry removes a stated ~13% overhang.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on three verifiable facts and one large unknown.

Verifiable: collectibles compounding at +47.7% (FY2025) and +65.0% (Q1); US segment sales +21.1% in Q1 on a fleet cut by nearly a third, which is direct evidence that store density has transactional value rather than being pure cost; and gross margin expanding more than 600bps a year, with the mechanism disclosed rather than inferred. Consensus estimates are rising — current quarter +42.1% over 30 days to $0.27, current year +19.3% to $1.30, next year +13.2% to $1.37 — and the company has beaten in four consecutive quarters by 18.8% to 85.6%.

Two important discounts on those estimate revisions. Coverage is one analyst; the recommendation buckets are entirely empty. And the 90-day columns read $0.00, meaning there is no genuine 90-day revision baseline. This is not the broad, multi-analyst +30% revision pattern that reliably precedes re-rating; it is one contributor marking to a beat. It also almost certainly does not reflect the pending share issuance: $1.30 on a diluted base of roughly 526.5M shares becomes approximately $1.11.

The unknown is durability. Collectibles supply is allocated by a licensor GameStop does not control, in a category the 10-K describes as "similarly fragmented and highly competitive, with significant price and allocation pressure," where popularity depends on "perceived scarcity or lack of scarcity." Trading-card cycles have historically been cyclical. Because margin expansion is mix-driven rather than efficiency-driven, a cooling card cycle compresses gross margin mechanically and immediately.

Against that, 70.8% of FY2025 revenue is in structural decline: hardware −12.3% and software −27.5% in FY2025, −3.4% and −13.0% in Q1. The Nintendo Switch 2 launched in June 2025 and hardware still declined — a console launch failing to lift the hardware line indicates digital distribution now overwhelms cycle effects. A widely circulated report that Sony will stop manufacturing physical PlayStation discs from January 2028 could not be verified in any filing or primary source and should not be treated as established, though it would be material if accurate. The pending France sale removes further revenue (Europe generated −$1.3M of Q1 segment operating income, so earnings impact is negligible).

## Valuation Assessment

**Sum of parts, post-exchange.** The August 3 exchange retires $1.4B of note principal (~$400M of 2030s, ~$1.0B of 2032s) for stock, leaving $2.8B outstanding, with no cash proceeds. Share count is set by a 35-trading-day VWAP from August 3 subject to an undisclosed floor. Sensitivity: $1.4B at an $18–20 VWAP implies 70.0–77.8M new shares; if exchanged near the notes' ~111%-of-par May marks, up to 86.3M. That is +15.6% to +19.2% on the existing count, or 13.5% to 16.1% of ownership transferred. Central case 77.8M shares → 526.5M total.

| Item | Pre-exchange | Post-exchange (central) |
|---|---|---|
| Financial assets | $10,006.3M | $10,006.3M |
| Note principal | $(4,200)M | $(2,800)M |
| Net financial assets | $5,806.3M | $7,206.3M |
| Shares | 448.7M | 526.5M |
| **Net financial per share** | **$12.94** | **$13.69** |

The range across the dilution sensitivity is $13.47–$13.89. Note that the exchange is modestly *accretive* to net financial assets per share (issuing equity above net-cash-per-share to retire a par obligation) while diluting the claim on the operating business. At $18.66, post-exchange market capitalization of $9,824M less $7,206M leaves **$2,618M** for the operating stub — **6.8x LTM EBIT of $386.2M** (FY2025 $232.1M, less Q1 FY2025 −$10.8M, plus Q1 FY2026 $143.3M), or **8.8x** after a 23% tax. The pre-exchange calculation gives 6.6x, so the transaction is roughly stub-neutral at these prices; the −8% announcement reaction reflects share supply and convertible-arb hedging through the reference period rather than a change in intrinsic value.

**Reported multiples are unreliable.** The data feed shows both trailing and forward P/E at 10.86 alongside an EPS field of $0.77, which do not reconcile ($18.66/$0.77 = 24.2x). GAAP EPS will swing with eBay's share price — roughly $390M of pretax mark per $10 move on 39.0M shares of exposure — so any GAAP P/E is uninformative. On the single-analyst consensus of $1.30, the shares are 14.4x; adjusted for the pending dilution, roughly 16.8x. Peer median P/E across the $8–9B Consumer Discretionary comparables (CAVA 126.6, KMX 39.2, NCLH 11.7, LAD 11.7, ALV 14.2) is 14.2. So the headline discount to peers disappears once the share count and the marks are handled properly.

**Scenarios (12 months, 526.5M shares central).**

- **Base $20.50 (+9.9%):** $13.69 net financial plus the operating business at 9x normalized EBIT of $400M ($3,600M, $6.84/share). Assumes collectibles growth decelerates from +65% toward the teens, gross margin holds in the high 30s, France exits, and the eBay position is neither materially marked up nor down.
- **Bull $26.00 (+39.3%):** net financial rises toward $14.44 on favorable eBay marks; operating business at 13x EBIT of $450M ($5,850M, $11.11/share). Requires collectibles compounding, a monetizable eBay partnership using the ~1,600-store network for authentication and intake, and at least partial execution of the $2.0B buyback below intrinsic value.
- **Bear $14.00 (−25.0%):** trading-card cycle cools, mix reverses and gross margin compresses; roughly $700M of combined eBay and Bitcoin marks against; share count drifts higher via ATM or further issuance to ~545M; operating business at 5x depressed EBIT of $250M.

Probability-weighted at 50/25/25: approximately +8.5%. Positive, but a large share of the base-case value is cash that management has demonstrated it will redeploy into positions with asymmetric loss profiles.

## Competitive Landscape

The 10-K names Walmart, Target, Best Buy and Amazon in the US, FNAC-Darty and Leclerc in Europe, JB HiFi and Big W in Australia — and separately names Sony, Nintendo and Microsoft as competitors for direct-to-consumer digital and physical offerings. Its three largest suppliers are also its structural disintermediators. Management's own characterization of physical game sales as "totally irrelevant" (Bloomberg TV, per secondary reporting, July 16, 2026) is consistent with the filings even if the rhetoric overstates it, given hardware and software were 70.8% of FY2025 revenue.

The collectibles position is different in kind. GameStop has no merchandising edge — Walmart, Target and Costco sell identical sealed Pokemon product and the 10-K concedes allocation pressure. The edge, if it exists, is transactional infrastructure: 1,598 US locations that take graded-card trade-ins for immediate cash or credit, submit raw cards to PSA for grading, and provide instant sell-back of vaulted cards. Q1's +21.1% US growth on a fleet reduced 31% is the strongest available evidence that this network has value beyond shelf space.

The eBay logic is intelligible even if the $56B bid was not. eBay's structural weakness in collectibles is trust and physical handling — authentication, condition disputes, returns. GameStop has physical intake points and a PSA relationship. A partnership giving eBay store access for authentication and fulfillment (the concept reported August 10 and unconfirmed by any filing) is a far more defensible proposition than an $8.4B acquirer buying a company seven times its size. eBay rejected the $125.00/share cash-and-stock proposal on May 12.

One governance feature deserves weight: Director Nat Turner is Chairman and CEO of Collectors Holdings, parent of PSA, which grades cards in GameStop stores and co-developed Power Packs. The 10-Q states transactions with Collectors/PSA were not material in Q1 FY2026 or Q1 FY2025 — which simultaneously indicates Power Packs is not yet financially significant and that a director-affiliated counterparty sits inside the flagship growth initiative.

## Risk Assessment

**Reported earnings are a function of asset prices GameStop does not control.** The FY2025 10-K's new lead risk section, placed ahead of all retail risks, states plainly that changes in market prices of held securities "can have a significant impact on the Company's reported results for a particular period, even though those changes do not bear on the performance of the Company's operating businesses." Q1 demonstrated this positively; the mechanism runs both ways. Q2 FY2026 (quarter ended roughly August 1) is unreported.

**Written puts on ~39M eBay shares create unhedged downside.** Each pair combines a purchased call and a written put with the same counterparty, accounted for as a single forward, so GameStop bears full downside on the underlying. The Master Agreement also requires collateral exchange when fair value crosses thresholds, so adverse moves consume liquidity. eBay fell roughly 4% on August 10 on the report that GameStop might withdraw its bid, meaning part of the Q1 gain was already at risk before Q2 closed.

**Dilution capacity is now effectively unbounded.** Roughly 2.05B authorized-but-unissued shares (~4.6x current count), an open Jefferies ATM that has already raised $3.47B, a pending stock-settled exchange with undisclosed terms, and a stated strategy of using stock to acquire assets. The July 8 authorization passed with only about two-thirds support, unusually thin for a routine charter item.

**Bitcoin: capped upside, retained downside, single-counterparty exposure.** 4,709 BTC bought for $500M (~$106,157/BTC) sit as a $369.6M receivable — an implied ~$78,488/BTC, roughly 26% below cost — with covered calls written at an $80,000 strike as of May 2 and rolled since on undisclosed terms. Whether this reflects yield harvesting on an unwanted position or something else cannot be determined from the filings; either way the economics are capped upside with full downside plus rehypothecation risk.

**Other material items:** the 2028 note puts on $2.8B remaining principal; Investment Company Act 40%-test classification risk, which the 10-K addresses at length and which tightens as the balance sheet shifts toward equity derivatives; formal key-person dependence on Cohen, who receives no salary, cash bonus or time-vested equity; a "Principal Financial and Accounting Officer" rather than a titled CFO for a company running a multi-billion-dollar OTC derivatives book; disclosed Investment Committee co-investment conflicts; and US fulfillment concentrated largely in a single Grapevine, Texas facility. A December 2024 auditor change (8-K Item 4.01) is flagged as uninvestigated.

**Options Market Signal.** Near-term ATM IV of 34.9% sits slightly below one-year realized volatility of 37.8% and at the 3rd percentile of the current chain — near-term optionality is fair to modestly cheap. Term structure is in contango (34.9% at one week, 40.1% at one month, 46.9% at the September 25 tenor, 41.7% at December), pricing ±11.9% by late September and ±20.4% by December. That shape lines up with the exchange reference period ending late September and the unreported Q2 print. Directional positioning is not bearish: put/call volume is 0.21, the data source characterizes skew as call-heavy at −10.6%, and unusual activity is concentrated in August 21 $22 calls at 5.7x open interest with 80.6% implied volatility. Put/call open interest has risen from 0.45 to 0.70 since July 23 as the stock fell 13.8%, so some hedging has been added, but the flow remains call-tilted. Short interest is 13.1% of float with 16.2 days to cover — a high cover ratio that cuts both ways: it constrains the downside path on any positive catalyst, and it means informed bearish capital is already committed. The options market is pricing event magnitude, not direction, which is consistent with our two-sided base case.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The operating turnaround is real, traceable, and being obscured by the balance sheet. Strip out cash, crypto and derivatives and what remains is a US-centric, negative-working-capital, 0.5%-of-revenue-capex business earning roughly $386M of LTM operating income, growing US sales 21%, expanding gross margin 600+bps a year, for which the market pays about $2.6B. The 1,598-store network is a differentiated physical layer for authentication, grading intake and instant liquidity in a collectibles market where trust is the binding constraint. Cohen bought 1,000,000 shares on the open market in January at $21.12–$21.60 (~$21.4M, lifting him to 38.35M shares, ~8.5%), joined by three directors — a cluster of open-market buying above today's price. He withdrew his own performance award, worth up to a reported ~$35B if all hurdles were met, removing a very large contingent-dilution scenario. A $2.0B buyback authorization exists, warrant overhang expires October 30, and an eBay partnership rather than an acquisition would be a capital-light way to monetize the store network. Post-exchange net financial assets of ~$13.69/share provide a hard floor roughly 27% below spot before any credit for the operating business.

**Bear case.** Margin expansion is mix arithmetic, not efficiency, and reverses mechanically if the trading-card cycle cools — a cycle whose supply is allocated by a licensor GameStop does not control and whose demand depends on perceived scarcity. Roughly 71% of revenue is in secular decline, and a new console launch failed to lift hardware. The tax tailwind is spent: the effective rate goes from −9.0% to ~23%. Reported EPS is now a leveraged bet on eBay's share price with written puts providing no downside protection and collateral calls draining liquidity on adverse moves. The company authorized $2.0B of buybacks on June 2 and agreed to issue roughly $1.4B of stock on August 3, with zero shares repurchased in FY2023, FY2024 or FY2025 — a seven-year record that puts the burden of proof on management. Authorized shares were raised roughly 5.5x with a third of votes opposed. Capital allocation to date has produced a 26% loss on Bitcoin, a rejected bid, and a stock-for-debt exchange at an undisclosed premium. Finance leadership is thin relative to the instruments being used.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Horizon: 12 months, with two hard checkpoints — the exchange reference period ending on or about September 23, 2026, and the Q2 FY2026 report.**

- **Base case $20.50 (+9.9% from $18.66):** $13.69 post-exchange net financial assets per share plus the operating business at 9x normalized EBIT of $400M ($6.84/share).
- **Bull case $26.00 (+39.3%):** net financial $14.44 plus operating business at 13x EBIT of $450M ($11.11/share).
- **Bear case $14.00 (−25.0%):** net financial reduced by ~$700M of eBay and Bitcoin marks and a share count near 545M, plus operating business at 5x EBIT of $250M.
- **Probability-weighted expected return (50/25/25): approximately +8.5%.** Positive but with wide two-sided variance and undisclosed terms in the largest near-term variable, which is why this is a HOLD rather than an ACCUMULATE.

**Upgrade triggers (toward ACCUMULATE or BUY):**
1. The exchange settles at or below roughly 78M new shares (i.e., an effective price at or above $18, no meaningful VWAP discount), confirmed on or about September 23, *and* the company actually repurchases stock under the $2.0B authorization at a price below the then-current net financial assets per share.
2. Q2 and Q3 FY2026 show collectibles growth above 25% y/y with consolidated gross margin sustained above 37% — establishing the mix as a durable earnings level rather than a card-cycle peak.
3. A definitive, filed eBay agreement (partnership, JV or commercial arrangement) that monetizes the store network without requiring multi-billion-dollar equity issuance, combined with an orderly reduction of the written-put exposure.

**Downgrade triggers (toward REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. Any ATM usage or new equity issuance beyond the announced exchange, or a filed transaction requiring share issuance above roughly 20% of the post-exchange count.
2. Collectibles revenue growth decelerating below 15% y/y, or consolidated gross margin falling below 35%, in either of the next two quarters — the mechanical margin-reversal scenario.
3. A quarterly loss on the eBay position exceeding roughly $400M, or a disclosed collateral call requiring additional cash posting, or any disclosure of impairment/loss on the Coinbase Credit digital-asset receivable.
4. Physical settlement of the full eBay call position, which would commit an estimated $3.6–3.9B of cash to a single non-controlled equity stake.

**Next reassessment: the Q2 FY2026 release and the exchange close, both expected in September 2026.** Note that GameStop does not hold traditional earnings calls, so no management Q&A will be available to interpret either event.

## Conclusion

The score trajectory has improved steadily (3.7 in March to 4.6 in July), and this review continues that direction to 4.95 on genuine new evidence rather than price action: Q1 confirmed collectibles as the largest category at +65% growth with 40.7% gross margin and +21.1% US sales, the CEO performance award was withdrawn (removing a very large contingent-dilution scenario), the warrant overhang expires in October, and the August exchange — while dilutive to the operating-business claim — is roughly neutral to slightly accretive on net financial assets per share and removes $1.4B of a 2028 cash obligation. The 12.6% price decline since the July review is broadly proportional to the 15–19% share increase being issued, so risk/reward has improved only marginally, not materially.

What keeps this a HOLD rather than an ACCUMULATE is that the cheapest-looking part of the story is the part management controls least on shareholders' behalf. The operating stub at 6.8x LTM EBIT is defensible. The $13.69/share of net financial assets is a real floor. But 2.05B shares of authorized headroom, an open ATM, an undisclosed exchange ratio, written puts on 39M shares of a company that has rejected GameStop's advances, and a Bitcoin structure with capped upside and full downside mean the per-share denominator and the mark-to-market numerator are both outside the reach of fundamental analysis. Existing holders have adequate downside support and a plausible path to $20.50. New capital has no urgent reason to commit before the share count is known on or about September 23 and Q2 discloses where the eBay position stands.

> **[FACT-CHECK FLAG]** The following corrections may warrant revisiting this recommendation: the report's claim that reported P/E of 10.86 implies "LTM GAAP EPS of roughly $1.72" was unverifiable and internally inconsistent with the feed's own $0.77 EPS field, and has been removed; the secular-decline revenue share was corrected from "58%" back to the ~71% (70.8%) figure the sources support — neither change alters the sum-of-parts valuation that drives the HOLD, but the P/E discussion should not be relied upon.
