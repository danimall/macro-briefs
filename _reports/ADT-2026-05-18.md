## Executive Summary

ADT is a highly cash-generative home security monitoring business trading at a significant discount to its cash flow generation capacity. At $6.83/share, the stock sits at ~9.4x forward earnings, well below its specialized consumer services peers (median 20.5x), and generates $1.71B in annual free cash flow against a $5.1B market cap — a 33% FCF yield. Apollo Global Management's complete exit in May 2026 (102M shares at $7.25) removes a multi-year overhang, and four insiders made open-market purchases at $6.75-$6.91 immediately afterward.

The bull case centers on an absurdly cheap cash flow stream with a new $1.5B buyback authorization (~30% of market cap), improving capital structure post-refinancing, and potential multiple re-rating as the PE sponsor overhang clears. The bear case centers on $7.8B of debt (2.9x net leverage), rising attrition (13.1% gross, trending higher), flat revenue guidance for 2026, and intensifying competition from Amazon Ring, SimpliSafe, and cable bundlers that could structurally compress the monitoring fee pool. Management is explicitly prioritizing cash return over growth, which may limit upside while capping downside.

The stock is appropriately held for its income characteristics (3.2% yield + buyback support) while monitoring whether the ADT+ platform can arrest attrition trends and restore subscriber growth by 2027.

## Company Overview

ADT provides residential and small business security monitoring services to approximately 6.1 million subscribers in the U.S. The company operates a subscription model generating $359M/month in recurring monitoring revenue, with average customer lifespans of ~15 years and revenue break-even on new customers at ~2.3 years.

Following divestitures of Commercial (Oct 2023), Solar (2024), and Multifamily (Oct 2025) segments, ADT is now a pure-play consumer/SMB security company. The company has partnered with Google (exclusive Nest device supply for professional installation customers through 2030, $200M cloud commitment) and acquired Origin AI ($170M, Feb 2026) for AI-powered ambient sensing technology.

Distribution is dual-channel: direct (call centers, field advisors, e-commerce, retail) and indirect (~140 authorized dealers). The company employs a national field force of 12,200 and operates 6 redundant UL-listed monitoring centers.

## Growth Analysis

Growth is the weakest dimension of the ADT thesis. Management is explicitly guiding for flat revenue and flat Adjusted EPS in 2026, prioritizing cash flow optimization over subscriber expansion.

**Growth Drivers:**
- ADT+ platform adoption (~30% of new adds in Q1 2026, up from zero in 2023)
- ARPU expansion via interactive services, premium packages
- Origin AI integration (improved alarm verification, new use cases like aging-in-place)
- Installation/product revenue surging (+28% FY2025, +7% Q1 2026) as business transitions to outright equipment sales model

**Growth Headwinds:**
- Monitoring & services revenue growing only +1.4% (FY2025) as volume declines offset pricing gains
- Attrition rising to 13.1% (from 12.7%), with legacy subscriber base at elevated risk
- State Farm partnership wound down (Oct 2025), removing a customer acquisition channel
- Net subscriber count likely flat-to-declining given attrition trajectory

Estimate revisions show modest deterioration: current quarter EPS estimates cut 8.6% over 90 days, current year cut 3.4%. The 30-day revisions have stabilized (current year +2.0%, next year +0.9%), but the longer-term trend is negative. This is consistent with a business in harvest mode rather than growth mode.

The ADT+ platform and Origin AI acquisition are genuine long-term opportunities that could restore organic growth by 2027-2028, but near-term evidence of their revenue impact is limited.

## Competitive Landscape

ADT holds the strongest brand in professional home security (brand heritage since 1874) and the largest subscriber base (6.1M). Its competitive position is stable but under structural pressure from three vectors:

1. **DIY/self-monitor disruption:** Ring (Amazon), SimpliSafe, Wyze, and Roku offer $0/month self-monitoring options. These directly compete with ADT's $40-60+/month monitoring fees. The category continues growing rapidly among younger homeowners.

2. **Cable/telco bundling:** Comcast Xfinity Home and similar offerings leverage existing billing relationships and can subsidize security. ADT cannot match the bundling economics.

3. **Platform ecosystem risk:** Google (partner), Amazon (competitor), and Apple all control device ecosystems. The Matter protocol reduces switching costs. Google could eventually vertically integrate monitoring, converting ADT from partner to competitor.

**ADT's defensible advantages:**
- Professional monitoring infrastructure (6 redundant centers, UL certification) remains a genuine moat for insurance-discount-seeking and liability-conscious customers
- National installation/service network (12,200 field employees) is expensive to replicate
- Multi-year contracts with ETFs create switching friction
- Brand trust matters for security purchases (psychologically high-stakes)

**Trajectory assessment:** Stable-to-slightly-deteriorating. The professional monitoring segment is defensible against DIY alternatives (different customer psychographic), but the addressable market ceiling is being compressed. ADT's pivot toward ADT+ and smart home integration is the right strategic response but faces execution risk.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **Leverage in a rising-rate environment:** Each 100bp rate increase costs ~$25-35M annually on variable portions (SOFR+2.00%). Debt is serviceable at current rates but leaves minimal margin for revenue decline. Maturities are laddered 2027-2042 with no near-term cliff, but the absolute quantum is large relative to equity value.

2. **Legacy subscriber attrition acceleration:** The majority of ADT's 6.1M subscribers use legacy systems incompatible with ADT+ without costly upgrades. Rising attrition (trending up) could accelerate as these systems reach end-of-life and competitors offer cheaper alternatives. Each 1% of additional attrition = ~$40M revenue impact. A scenario where attrition hits 15% would reduce annual monitoring revenue by ~$80M, directly impacting FCF.

3. **Google concentration risk:** Exclusive DIFM device commitment through 2030 + $200M cloud commitment creates vendor dependency. Google's strategic priorities may not align with ADT's over time. Google entering monitoring directly (low probability but non-zero) would be catastrophic.

4. **Macro sensitivity:** Consumer home security is partially discretionary. In a recession with negative real wages, new subscriber additions could decline while cancellations from non-payment rise. ADT specifically cited "increased non-payment" as a driver of higher attrition.

5. **Tariff exposure:** Equipment sourced from China faces ongoing tariff risk. The Feb 2026 IEEPA ruling + new 10% global tariff creates uncertainty. ADT expects to mitigate through vendor arrangements and price increases, but margin compression is possible.

- Rising rates increase debt servicing costs on variable portions
- Consumer spending weakness reduces new subscriber additions and increases non-payment disconnects
- Google partnership underperforms or is not renewed post-2030, creating strategic uncertainty
- Competitive pricing pressure compresses ARPU as DIY alternatives normalize $0/month monitoring

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**For HOLD:**

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Gross attrition declines below 12.5% for two consecutive quarters, indicating ADT+ platform is successfully retaining subscribers
2. Stock declines below $6.00 on no fundamental deterioration (mechanical selling from index rebalancing or secondary-related flow), creating >40% FCF yield
3. Net subscriber additions turn positive for the first time in multiple quarters

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Gross attrition exceeds 14.0%, indicating legacy base deterioration is accelerating beyond management's ability to offset
2. Management cuts dividend or suspends buyback due to leverage concerns
3. Google announces competing monitoring service or partnership terms deteriorate materially at renewal

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings (likely late July/early August 2026 for Q2). Key metrics to watch: attrition rate, ADT+ penetration of new adds (currently ~30%), RMR trajectory, and FCF conversion.

## Conclusion

ADT is a high-quality cash flow business with a leveraged balance sheet and limited near-term growth visibility. At 9.4x forward earnings and a 33% FCF yield, the stock is clearly cheap on absolute metrics, but the discount reflects real structural risks: rising attrition, competitive encroachment, and a legacy technology base that requires significant investment to modernize.

The Apollo exit, clustered insider buying, buyback authorization, and S&P 600 inclusion are all constructive developments that should provide a floor under the stock. However, without evidence that ADT+ can bend the attrition curve and restore subscriber growth, the stock is likely to remain range-bound ($6.50-$8.50) as buyback accretion offsets fundamental stagnation.

The appropriate posture is HOLD for income: the 3.2% dividend yield plus buyback-driven EPS accretion delivers mid-to-high single-digit total return potential annually, with limited downside given cash flow coverage. A more aggressive stance requires proof of the growth thesis (attrition improvement, ADT+ adoption acceleration) that remains 2-3 quarters away from verifiable.
