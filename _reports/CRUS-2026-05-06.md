## Executive Summary

Cirrus Logic delivered record FY2026 revenue of ~$2B, with operating margins expanding to 24% and a structural ~10pp effective tax rate reduction (from OBBBA legislation) adding $0.80-$1.00/share to annual EPS. The stock has appreciated 74% over the past year from $98 to $171, and now trades at 22.6x trailing EPS of $6.00 (or ~18.9x estimated FY2026 EPS of ~$9.07). Four consecutive earnings beats averaging 20% upside surprise demonstrate strong execution.

The fundamental challenge preventing a higher-conviction rating is that Apple revenue concentration has *increased* from 89% in FY2025 to 94% in Q3 FY2026. At $171, the stock trades above the analyst consensus mean target of $162, and the options market is pricing an 11% implied move on the nearest expiry with IV at 88.3% vs. 34.2% historical vol. While PC content growth (SDCA standard) and automotive haptics offer legitimate long-term diversification pathways, neither has yet reached a scale that would alter the single-customer risk profile in a measurable timeframe.

The specific concern preventing a BUY at this price: if Apple insources even one product category (e.g., camera controllers or haptic drivers — areas where Apple has demonstrated silicon design capability), the revenue impact would be $200-400M on a $2B base, with no immediate replacement customer available. Apple's semiconductor design team has grown to thousands of engineers, and the 10-K risk factors explicitly acknowledge the threat.

## Company Overview

Cirrus Logic is a fabless semiconductor company headquartered in Austin, Texas, specializing in low-power mixed-signal processing ICs. The company employs ~1,660 people (71% in R&D) and outsources fabrication to TSMC and GlobalFoundries.

**Revenue Mix (FY2025):**
- Audio Products: 60% — amplifiers, codecs, smart codecs, ADCs, DACs, DSPs
- High-Performance Mixed-Signal (HPMS): 40% — camera controllers, haptic/sensing solutions, battery/power ICs

**Customer Concentration:** Apple accounts for 89% (FY2025) to 94% (Q3 FY2026) of total revenue. Top 10 customers represent 96-97% of sales.

**Strategic Priorities:**
1. Maintain smartphone audio IC leadership (primarily iPhone)
2. Increase HPMS content per smartphone
3. Expand into laptops/PCs and automotive (diversification imperative)

## Competitive Landscape

**Strengths:**
- Near-monopoly position in iPhone audio/mixed-signal ICs
- Deep engineering integration with Apple creating substantial switching costs
- Years of co-development on custom, system-specific components
- Leading-edge low-power mixed-signal performance
- Apple Manufacturing Program inclusion (March 2026) signals continued partnership investment

**Threats:**
- Apple in-sourcing: Apple's semiconductor design team has expanded significantly. The 10-K explicitly acknowledges customers may "develop competing technologies and integrated circuits."
- Qualcomm: Integrating more audio functionality into application processors
- Chinese competitors (Awinic, Goodix): Offering lower-cost alternatives for non-Apple smartphones
- Architecture risk: system-level changes that reduce discrete audio IC content

**Key Competitive Dynamic:** The Apple relationship is symbiotic — Apple benefits from Cirrus's specialized mixed-signal expertise, and Cirrus benefits from Apple's volumes. The March 2026 Apple Manufacturing Program inclusion suggests Apple views this partnership as strategic. However, Apple's history (dropping Intel modems, designing custom GPUs, developing M-series chips) demonstrates willingness to insource when it achieves competency.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Apple Concentration (Critical — Quantified):**
At 94% revenue concentration, a single design-out of one product category (e.g., haptic drivers, ~$200M revenue) would reduce total revenue by ~10% with no replacement customer of comparable scale available within 12-18 months. A broader insourcing move (camera controllers + haptics) could remove 15-20% of revenue. The market applies a persistent valuation discount for this risk.

**2. IRS Transfer Pricing Dispute ($232M Exposure):**
$168.3M additional tax + $63.7M penalties related to FY2017-2019. Resolution could take years; adverse outcome would consume ~22% of current cash position. Company is in IRS Appeals and "prepared to pursue judicial remedies."

**3. Earnings Flatline Risk:**
FY2027 consensus EPS ($9.01) is essentially flat vs. FY2026 ($9.07). If the company delivers flat earnings at current valuations, re-rating drivers are absent. Management guided higher R&D spending in FY2027, suggesting near-term margin pressure.

**4. Geopolitical/Tariff Risk:**
99% international revenue; 55% ship-to China. Apple product tariffs could reduce iPhone demand, flowing directly to Cirrus revenues. Manufacturing shift from China to India/Vietnam is visible but creates transition risk.

**5. GlobalFoundries Capacity Agreement:**
Committed wafer purchases through CY2026. If demand weakens, CRUS faces excess inventory or shortfall payments. Currently well-managed (inventory drawdown from $299M to $189M).

## Conclusion

Cirrus Logic is a well-run, highly profitable fabless semiconductor company executing on margin expansion, capital returns, and content growth within its primary customer relationship. Financial health is excellent with $1.08B in liquidity and zero debt.

The specific, falsifiable concern preventing a BUY at $171: the stock trades above the analyst consensus mean target ($162), near its 52-week high ($175.78), with FY2027 estimates flat ($9.01 vs. $9.07 FY2026E), while Apple concentration has *increased* to 94%. For the stock to deliver meaningful returns from here, either PC/auto diversification must show measurable traction (non-Apple revenue inflecting above 10-15% of total) or the market must eliminate the long-standing concentration discount. Neither appears imminent based on available evidence. The 30-day estimate revision has flattened to +0.7%, indicating the catch-up cycle is complete. The risk/reward at $171 is approximately balanced: ~15% upside to bull case vs. ~27% downside to bear case, with no near-term catalyst favoring the upside scenario.

At $145-150, the risk/reward would become compelling enough for a BUY upgrade. At current levels, this is a quality HOLD for existing shareholders capturing further content growth and buyback compounding.
