## Executive Summary

LegalZoom is a leading online platform for legal services focused on small business formation and compliance, generating $756M in 2025 revenue (up 11% YoY). The company has a strong free cash flow profile ($148M FCF in 2025) and nearly zero debt. GAAP profitability is severely distorted by $113.7M in stock-based compensation (15% of revenue), which caused reported net income to decline 49% YoY to $15.4M ($0.08 diluted EPS) even as revenue grew. The stock trades at 124x trailing earnings, a valuation that requires sustained acceleration in both revenue and profit margins that current trends do not support.

The company faces several compounding headwinds: Barclays downgraded to Underweight on March 24, 2026, citing concerns about limited addressable market size; insiders are net sellers with no open-market purchases; short interest sits at 12.6% of float; Q4 2025 EPS missed consensus by 6.7%; and current-quarter estimates are being revised down 18-20%. The stock is down 35% over the past year and 52% from its 52-week high. The company generates real operating cash flow, but the gap between cash economics and GAAP profitability, combined with deteriorating near-term estimates and negative analyst sentiment shifts, makes the risk-reward unattractive.

## Company Overview

LegalZoom provides online legal services to individuals and small businesses across all 50 U.S. states. Core products include LLC and corporation formations, registered agent services, compliance subscriptions, legal advisory plans, virtual mail, and intellectual property services. The company operates one reportable segment. In February 2025, LZ acquired Formation Nation (Inc Authority, Nevada Corporate Headquarters) for $83.2M to expand its customer acquisition funnel with both lower-cost DIY and higher-value DIFM offerings.

As of December 31, 2025, the company had 1.94M subscription units (up 10% YoY), with subscriptions generating approximately 65% of total revenue. The company employed 1,196 people plus ~700 contractors.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** $756M in FY2025, up 10.9% YoY. Subscription revenue grew 13% to $492.5M; transaction revenue grew 7% to $263.6M. Formation Nation contributed approximately $51.4M in total revenue (~$33.2M transaction, ~$18.2M subscription) since the February 2025 acquisition date. Organic revenue growth was therefore approximately 4-5%, a meaningful deceleration from the headline figure.

**Profitability:** GAAP operating income declined to $25.0M from $35.6M, and net income fell to $15.4M from $30.0M. The primary driver is SBC increasing $42.2M to $113.7M (15.0% of revenue vs. 10.5% in 2024), largely from PSU grants to senior leadership. Adjusted EBITDA was $172.2M (22.8% margin), up from $148.1M (21.7%). The divergence between Adjusted EBITDA margin improvement and GAAP operating margin compression ($25M operating income on $756M revenue = 3.3% GAAP operating margin) reflects heavy reliance on SBC add-backs.

**Quarterly trajectory:** Q4 2025 revenue was $190.3M (broadly flat QoQ), with operating income of $12.6M improving sequentially from $6.5M in Q3 and -$3.1M in Q2. The Q2 operating loss coincided with peak SBC expense ($30.6M) and elevated marketing spend. Q4 EPS of $0.03 missed consensus of $0.18 (when comparing to Wall Street's adjusted EPS expectations of $0.18 per Nasdaq article, the GAAP figure was $0.03).

**Cash flow:** Operating cash flow was $178.2M (up 31% YoY), and FCF was $147.9M (up 48% YoY). FCF/net income ratio of 959% reflects the massive SBC add-back. Capex declined to $30.3M from $35.7M. Cash on hand was $203.1M with no debt outstanding on the $100M revolving facility.

**Balance sheet:** Total debt is $14.4M (entirely capital lease obligations). Stockholders' equity of $171.9M includes $1.13B in accumulated deficit. Working capital is negative at -$76.6M, driven by $216.3M in current deferred revenue. Goodwill increased to $140.7M from $63.3M due to Formation Nation. The balance sheet is fundamentally sound given the cash position and minimal debt.

**Share count dynamics:** Share count increased from 173.6M to 177.6M despite $80.5M in buybacks, because 9.4M RSU vesting shares and 4.7M option exercises more than offset the 8.3M shares repurchased. This dilution pattern is structural: the company is buying back stock while simultaneously diluting through SBC, making buybacks largely an offset to dilution rather than genuine capital return.

## Growth Analysis

Organic growth is decelerating. Excluding Formation Nation's ~$51.4M contribution, organic FY2025 revenue growth was approximately 4-5%, down from the headline 10.9%. Business formations grew only 4% (500K vs. 482K), and total transactions actually declined 1%. The FinCEN ruling eliminating beneficial ownership reporting removed a revenue contributor.

**Subscription unit growth:** 1.94M units as of Q4 2025, up 10% YoY but down 1% sequentially from Q3's 1.96M. ARPU was $266, up only 1% YoY. The company acknowledged that bundling lower-value subscriptions (forms, eSignature, legal advisory) into formation offerings is diluting ARPU while inflating unit counts. Annual small business retention rate was approximately 58%, impacted by the anniversary of bundled subscriptions.

**Formation Nation integration risks:** The acquired business contributed $51.4M revenue but a $4.9M net loss, suggesting it is dilutive to margins during the integration period. Goodwill of $77.4M from this acquisition is subject to impairment risk if integration targets are not met.

**AI and DIFM initiatives:** LZ is investing in AI-powered tools and concierge services. These are early-stage with uncertain returns. The AI Grant Finder launched in March 2026 is a free tool, indicating it's a traffic/engagement play rather than a direct revenue driver.

**Estimate revisions:** Current-quarter (Q1 2026) EPS estimates have been revised down 18-20% over the past 30 days, from $0.16 to $0.13. Annual estimates for FY2026 show modest upward revisions (+6.6% over 30 days to $0.73), but this follows a pattern of consistent slight misses (4 consecutive quarters of in-line or missed EPS). The forward P/E of 124x on trailing earnings versus roughly 8.1x on FY2026 consensus EPS of $0.73 captures the full gap between GAAP and adjusted metrics.

## Valuation Assessment

**Trailing P/E of 124x** is unsustainable and reflects the SBC-depressed GAAP earnings. Using FY2026 consensus EPS of $0.73 (which presumably uses adjusted metrics), the forward P/E would be approximately 8.2x. On an EV/EBITDA basis using Adjusted EBITDA of $172M and an enterprise value of approximately $822M ($1.025B market cap - $203M cash), the EV/Adjusted EBITDA is 4.8x.

**Valuation disconnect:** The low EV/EBITDA suggests the market already discounts the GAAP profitability issue. The gap between Adjusted EBITDA ($172M) and GAAP net income ($15.4M) — a factor of 11x — means investors must believe that SBC is truly non-economic. With shares outstanding increasing despite buybacks, SBC represents genuine dilution cost. At $5.98/share and $172M Adjusted EBITDA, the stock appears optically cheap, but normalized for real dilution costs (say 50% of SBC as economic cost), earnings power is roughly $60-70M pre-tax, yielding a P/E of 15-17x.

**Peer comparison:** Peer median P/E is 33.9x (EXPO, FCN, BAH, TRU). LZ's 124x trailing P/E is far above peers. Even on adjusted metrics, LZ's growth rate (organic 4-5%) is below what peers with lower multiples deliver (BAH at 12.5x P/E with defense exposure, FCN at 20.7x with consulting margins).

**Analyst targets:** Consensus median is $8.50, low $6.00, high $11.00. At $5.98, the stock trades below the low-end target, which ordinarily suggests value — but the recent Barclays downgrade to Underweight with reduced estimates may pull consensus targets lower.

## Competitive Landscape

LegalZoom holds a strong position in online business formation with 500K formations in 2025 and over 5M cumulative formations. Brand recognition drives meaningful organic traffic. The registered agent business across all 50 states creates switching costs for existing subscribers.

Competitive threats are growing: (1) state agencies are improving their own free online formation portals, (2) AI-powered legal tools from new entrants and large platform companies could commoditize document preparation, (3) offline law firms are developing online offerings, and (4) the company's own 10-K acknowledges that AI search platforms may reduce the effectiveness of their search engine marketing, which is their largest customer acquisition channel.

The Formation Nation acquisition expands the price spectrum (Inc Authority for low-cost DIY, LegalZoom for premium), but also introduces multi-brand complexity and integration risk. The 58% annual retention rate signals that a meaningful portion of the subscriber base churns each year, requiring continuous reinvestment in customer acquisition.

## Risk Assessment

**Structural SBC dilution:** $113.7M in SBC on $756M revenue (15%) is among the highest ratios in the sector. Share count is growing despite $80.5M in buybacks. Unless SBC moderates significantly, GAAP earnings will remain depressed relative to cash flow.

**Insider selling:** The CLO sold 23,325 shares at $6.63 in February 2026; the COO/CFO sold 15,000 shares at $6.96. No open-market purchases by any insider. Grant/vest-sell patterns are consistent with executives monetizing equity compensation rather than expressing confidence.

**Short interest at 12.6%** with 4.7 days to cover reflects institutional bearish positioning. Combined with the Barclays downgrade, this creates overhang.

**Macro sensitivity:** The company's 10-K explicitly warns that tariffs, inflation, and economic uncertainty are reducing small business formation rates. In the current stagflationary macro environment (per the world model: $119 oil, NFP -92K, consumer spending weakening), small business formation is a cyclically vulnerable activity.

**Earnings quality:** FCF/NI ratio of 959% is a red flag for earnings quality analysis. The cash generation is real, but the extreme divergence means GAAP profitability provides very little signal about the company's economic value creation.

**Recent analyst downgrade:** Barclays moved to Underweight on March 24, 2026, citing limited addressable market — a fundamental concern about the company's TAM ceiling that cannot be resolved through execution improvements.

**Executive turnover:** Multiple 5.02 (departure of directors/officers) 8-K filings in 2024-2025 suggest leadership instability during a period of strategic transition.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case:** LZ generates $148M in FCF on a $1B market cap (14.8% FCF yield). If SBC normalizes toward 8-10% of revenue, GAAP margins would expand materially. The Formation Nation integration could accelerate subscriber growth and improve unit economics. At 4.8x EV/Adjusted EBITDA, significant upside exists if the company can demonstrate organic growth re-acceleration above 8-10% and margin expansion.

**Bear Case:** Organic revenue growth has decelerated to 4-5%. SBC is structural rather than transitory — the company needs equity compensation to retain talent in a competitive labor market, meaning the current dilution rate persists. The 58% retention rate requires continuous marketing spend ($175.6M in 2025). AI disruption could commoditize core formation services. Macro headwinds (stagflation, reduced small business formation) compress the addressable market. At 124x trailing GAAP P/E, even moderate deterioration in fundamentals could drive further multiple compression.

## Conclusion

The fundamental case for LegalZoom requires believing that: (1) organic revenue growth will re-accelerate from 4-5% to a higher trajectory, (2) SBC will normalize as a percentage of revenue, and (3) the macro environment will remain supportive of small business formation. None of these conditions hold today, and none appear likely in the near term.

Current-quarter estimates are being revised down 18-20%. The company missed EPS consensus in 2 of the last 4 quarters and met it in the other 2 — no beats. Insiders are net sellers. Short interest is elevated. Barclays just downgraded to Underweight on a structural TAM concern. The stock has declined 35% over the past year with no clear catalyst to reverse the trend within 12 months.

The FCF profile is strong and the balance sheet is clean, but these positives are already reflected in the stock's 4.8x EV/Adjusted EBITDA. The risk of further downside from estimate cuts, macro deterioration in small business formation, and continued GAAP earnings dilution from SBC outweighs the value opportunity. **AVOID.**
