## Executive Summary

Waystar is a cloud-based healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM) platform serving 30,000+ clients and processing 7.5 billion transactions annually across ~$2.4 trillion in gross claims volume. The company reported Q1 2026 results on April 29 that beat on both revenue ($313.9M vs. $311.7M estimate, +22.4% YoY) and EPS ($0.42 vs. $0.39 estimate), while achieving a record 43.1% adjusted EBITDA margin. Full-year 2026 guidance of $1.274B–$1.294B implies ~16-18% growth, and consensus estimates of $1.63 in current-year EPS place the stock at ~13x forward earnings, a meaningful discount to the healthcare IT peer median of ~35x.

The stock has declined ~50% from its 52-week high of $42.55 to $21.38, falling below its June 2024 IPO price of $21.50. This drawdown reflects three identifiable pressures: (1) PE sponsors (EQT, CPPIB, Bain) sold 55.4 million shares across three secondary offerings in 2025, creating persistent supply; (2) the fading tailwind from the 2024 Change Healthcare cyberattack, which contributed $34M in FY2024 and $11M in FY2025 revenue through elevated competitive win rates; and (3) broader macro-driven rotation away from mid-cap growth stocks with leverage. The fundamental business is strengthening: revenue growth accelerated, margins expanded, FCF generation nearly doubled, and the Iodine acquisition ($1.26B, closed October 2025) extends the addressable market into clinical documentation and mid-cycle workflows. At 13x forward earnings with 22%+ revenue growth, 43%+ EBITDA margins, and ~26% FCF margins, the current price embeds an excessively pessimistic view of a business that produces durable, recurring cash flows from a critical healthcare infrastructure function.

## Company Overview

Waystar provides AI-powered software that automates the payment process between healthcare providers and insurance companies. The platform handles front-end (eligibility, prior authorization), mid-cycle (clinical documentation integrity, coding), and back-end (claims submission, denial management, patient billing) revenue cycle workflows. Over 99% of revenue is recurring — split approximately 55% subscription (provider-count-based) and 44% volume-based (transaction fees), with the remainder from implementation. The company serves 16 of the top 20 U.S. News Best Hospitals and covers approximately 60% of U.S. patients.

The October 2025 acquisition of Iodine ($1.26B, 50/50 cash/stock) added clinical intelligence capabilities — specifically clinical documentation integrity (CDI) and utilization management — filling a gap between care delivery and claim submission where approximately 60 million claims are denied annually. William Chan, Iodine's co-founder, became Chief Product & AI Officer, signaling the strategic priority of AI-driven product development.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue growth is accelerating.** FY2025 revenue of $1.099B grew 16.5% YoY from $943.5M. Q1 2026 revenue of $313.9M grew 22.4% YoY, boosted by the Iodine contribution and continued organic expansion. Subscription revenue grew 37.7% YoY in Q1, reflecting the mix shift toward more predictable, higher-margin streams. The 2026 guidance midpoint of $1.284B implies ~16.8% full-year growth, suggesting Q1's 22.4% pace moderates as the Iodine contribution anniversaries in Q4.

**Margin expansion is structural, not cyclical.** Operating margin improved from 13.1% (FY2024) to 22.7% (FY2025) to 25.6% (Q1 2026). Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded from 40.6% to 42.0% to 43.1% over the same periods. The drivers are operating leverage on a largely fixed cost base and the reduction in interest expense from $146.3M (FY2024) to $77.5M (FY2025) following IPO-funded debt paydown. Gross margin is approximately 69%.

**Free cash flow generation is strong and capital-light.** FY2025 FCF of $283.2M on $26.5M capex represents a 25.8% FCF margin. Q1 2026 FCF of $69.6M on $15.3M capex (22.2% margin) is slightly compressed by elevated capitalized software development ($7.9M vs. $4.5M prior year), reflecting AI investment. Operating cash flow nearly doubled from $169.8M (FY2024) to $309.7M (FY2025).

**Balance sheet is adequate but leveraged.** Total debt of $1.48B against $283M annual FCF implies roughly 5.2x debt/FCF or approximately 3.0x net debt/EBITDA (trailing adjusted). The $1.4B term loan at SOFR + 2.0% matures October 2029, providing adequate runway. Cash and short-term investments total $158.9M (Q1 2026). The Altman Z-Score of 2.51 sits in the grey zone, but this is mechanically driven by the massive goodwill base ($4.0B) suppressing asset turnover, not by operational distress.

**Goodwill dominates the asset base.** Goodwill and intangible assets of $5.31B constitute 91.7% of total assets ($5.79B). This reflects Waystar's acquisition-driven history (10 acquisitions since 2018, most recently Iodine adding ~$1B to goodwill). While manageable given current growth rates, this creates binary impairment risk if the business trajectory weakens.

**Net revenue retention rate of 110.5%** (trailing 12-month as of Q1 2026) remains healthy but declined from 112.0% in prior periods as the Change Healthcare cyberattack tailwind fades. Management attributes approximately $11M in Q1 2025 revenue to elevated win rates from that event.

## Growth Analysis

Waystar's growth thesis rests on four pillars:

**1. Secular demand for RCM automation.** U.S. healthcare administrative costs run approximately $440B annually, with ~$100B in labor services for revenue cycle functions. Chronic staffing shortages, rising claim denial rates, and increasing payer complexity create structural demand for software automation. Waystar's goal of an "autonomous revenue cycle platform" targets this labor pool directly.

**2. AI as a competitive differentiator.** Approximately 50% of Waystar's solutions are AI-powered, 40% of revenue flows through AI-embedded workflows, and 40% of new bookings in Q1 2026 were driven by AI capabilities. The company was rated #1 in Black Book's Q1 2026 Agentic and Generative AI RCM Benchmark. The Google Cloud partnership (March 2026) provides hyperscale infrastructure and access to Gemini LLMs. New product launches — AI-powered payer "take-back" recovery and agentic workflows for denials — represent tangible monetization of AI investment.

**3. Iodine-driven TAM expansion.** The Iodine acquisition extends Waystar into mid-cycle clinical intelligence — CDI, utilization management, and prebill revenue leakage. Management estimates this expands the TAM by 15%+. Early cross-sell traction is reported, integration is "running ahead of plan," and $15M+ in run-rate cost synergies are expected within 18-24 months.

**4. Large-client land-and-expand.** $100K+ clients grew from 1,244 (Q1 2025) to 1,433 (Q1 2026), a 15.2% increase. The client base of 30,000+ providers with 110.5% NRR demonstrates the ability to expand wallet share within existing accounts.

Consensus estimates project $1.63 EPS for FY2026 and $1.85 for FY2027, implying ~13.5% EPS growth. Estimate revisions are modestly positive over 30 days (+0.1% to +0.7% across periods), though next-year estimates have declined 1.7% over 90 days, which warrants monitoring.

## Valuation Assessment

The stock trades at $21.38 against consensus EPS of $1.63 (FY2026) and $1.85 (FY2027), yielding forward P/E ratios of approximately 13.1x and 11.6x respectively. Given trailing P/E is reported at ~31.8x (reflecting partial-year GAAP EPS of $0.61 TTM, which understates run-rate profitability), the forward multiples are the relevant anchor.

Against peer benchmarks, Waystar's 13.1x forward P/E is a substantial discount to the peer median of 34.8x. Among healthcare technology peers specifically, Doximity trades at 20.3x, HealthStream at 34.8x, and Omnicell at 97.9x. Even applying a discount for leverage and sponsor overhang, the gap is difficult to justify given the growth and margin profile described above.

On an EV/EBITDA basis, enterprise value of approximately $5.6B ($4.1B market cap + $1.48B debt - $0.16B cash) against FY2026E adjusted EBITDA of approximately $550-560M (43% of $1.284B midpoint) implies ~10x forward EV/EBITDA. For a SaaS-like business with 99%+ recurring revenue, 43% EBITDA margins, and mid-to-high-teens growth, this multiple screens cheap relative to both public comps and recent private market transactions in healthcare IT (typically 12-18x).

FCF yield is approximately 6.9% ($283M FY2025 FCF / $4.1B market cap), well above the typical growth software range of 2-4%. Even adjusting for FY2026 modestly higher capex, the FCF yield should remain above 5.5%.

The analyst consensus target of $36.00 (median) implies 68% upside. The low target of $27.00 implies 26% upside — even the most bearish covering analyst sees significant appreciation from current levels. Twenty-three of 24 analysts rate the stock Buy or Strong Buy.

The market is mispricing this stock because: (1) PE sponsor selling created sustained technical pressure that depressed the price well below fundamental value; (2) the stock's lack of a dividend and relatively recent IPO limits the natural buyer base among income-oriented institutional investors; and (3) the sharp drawdown from $42+ to $21 likely triggered systematic stop-losses and momentum-driven selling that overshot.

## Competitive Landscape

Waystar operates in a fragmented market against several categories of competitors: (1) pure-play RCM software (Athenahealth, R1 RCM, Inovalon); (2) EHR/PM vendors expanding into RCM (Epic, Oracle Health); (3) outsourced RCM services (Optum, Conifer); and (4) emerging AI-native startups.

- The 7.5 billion annual transactions create a data flywheel for AI training that smaller competitors cannot replicate
- The Iodine acquisition filled the mid-cycle gap, creating an end-to-end clinical-financial intelligence platform
- 99%+ recurring revenue and deep EHR integrations create high switching costs
- Black Book #1 ranking in Agentic AI RCM validates the AI competitive positioning

The primary competitive threat comes from EHR vendors (particularly Epic) integrating AI-powered RCM directly into their clinical workflows, which would reduce demand for standalone RCM platforms. The 10-K explicitly identifies this risk. However, the complexity and specialization of RCM workflows, combined with the multi-payer, multi-system reality of most provider organizations, has historically limited EHR vendors' success in this space.

The NRR decline from 112.0% to 110.5% reflects the Change Healthcare tailwind fading rather than competitive share loss. The 15.2% growth in $100K+ clients supports continued competitive strength.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Goodwill impairment (moderate-high probability of being a concern, low probability of near-term writedown).** The $5.31B goodwill/$5.79B asset ratio means any significant business deterioration could trigger material charges. The qualitative test passed in FY2025, and current growth rates support carrying values, but the risk is structural and permanent.

**2. PE sponsor overhang (high probability, moderate impact).** Board nomination rights persist at >5% ownership thresholds. Additional secondaries are likely. However, this is a known, visible pressure. As sponsors reduce their holdings, the overhang diminishes. The stock's decline to IPO price may moderate the pace of future sales.

**3. Change Healthcare tailwind exhaustion (high probability, quantified impact).** The $11M Q1 2025 contribution is now being lapped. NRR should normalize to the 108-110% range absent new catalysts. This is a 1-2% organic growth headwind for FY2026, embedded in guidance.

**4. AI disruption from large tech.** Google, Microsoft, and Amazon all have healthcare AI initiatives. If AI commoditizes basic RCM functions (eligibility checks, clean claim submission), Waystar's lower-complexity products face pricing pressure. The company's response — moving up the value chain to agentic AI and clinical intelligence — is the correct strategic response but carries execution risk.

**5. Healthcare volume sensitivity.** ACA subsidy expiration could reduce insured patient volumes, directly impacting Waystar's volume-based revenue (~44% of total). Management flagged weaker patient volume trends on the Q1 call. In a recession scenario, elective procedure volumes decline further.

**6. Iodine integration.** While reported "ahead of plan," the $1.26B acquisition at substantial premium carries integration risk. The $15M+ synergy target is modest relative to the price paid; the thesis depends on cross-sell revenue acceleration, which remains unproven at scale.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 55.8% sits 12.7 percentage points above 1-year historical volatility of 43.2%, indicating the options market is pricing elevated uncertainty following the Q1 earnings report and the sharp post-earnings decline (stock dropped from ~$25 to $21 on April 30). The 21.6% put skew at the 1-month tenor confirms elevated near-term downside hedging demand. The put/call OI ratio of 1.23 is modestly bearish. The flat term structure (near 55.8% ≈ far 56.1%) suggests the market views current uncertainty as persistent rather than event-driven, consistent with the ongoing sponsor overhang creating steady-state IV elevation rather than a single binary catalyst. For a fundamental buyer, the elevated IV makes selling puts or covered calls more attractive as entry strategies.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($32–36, 50–68% upside)
- Iodine cross-sell drives NRR back above 112% by H2 2026
- AI-driven product launches accelerate new bookings, sustaining 18-20% revenue growth
- EBITDA margins expand to 44-45% as Iodine synergies materialize and operating leverage continues
- PE sponsor selling pace slows as remaining positions diminish, removing technical overhang
- Re-rating to 18-20x forward EPS ($29-37) as the market recognizes the SaaS quality of earnings
- FY2027 EPS of $1.85 at 18x = $33.30 base; at 20x = $37.00

### Bear Case ($16–18, 16–25% downside)
- Healthcare volumes deteriorate on ACA expiry and recession; volume-based revenue declines
- EHR vendors (Epic) successfully integrate AI-powered RCM, eroding Waystar's competitive position
- NRR declines below 108% as Change Healthcare tailwind fully laps and organic expansion slows
- PE sponsors accelerate selling, overwhelming thin float
- Revenue growth decelerates to 10-12%, EBITDA margins compress to 38-40%
- Stock de-rates to 10x forward EPS on growth deceleration: $1.63 × 10x = $16.30

1. NRR declines below 107% for two consecutive quarters, indicating competitive share loss or pricing pressure

## Conclusion

Waystar is a high-quality healthcare technology business — 99%+ recurring revenue, 43% EBITDA margins, 26% FCF margins, mid-to-high-teens growth — trading at 13x forward earnings. The drawdown from $42 to $21 was driven primarily by PE sponsor selling and macro rotation, not by deterioration in the core business. Q1 2026 results demonstrated accelerating growth, expanding margins, and credible AI monetization. The Iodine acquisition extends the TAM and creates cross-sell optionality not reflected in the current price. At 10x forward EV/EBITDA and ~6.9% FCF yield, the risk/reward is asymmetric: the base case offers 40% upside, the bull case 68%, against 20% downside in a recession/competitive impairment scenario. BUY recommendation with a 12-18 month horizon.
