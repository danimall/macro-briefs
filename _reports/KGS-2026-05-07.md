## Executive Summary

Kodiak Gas Services is the dominant provider of large horsepower contract compression in the Permian Basin, operating ~4.5 million fleet HP at 97.7% utilization under long-term take-or-pay contracts. The business model generates high-visibility recurring revenue with inflation escalators, and FY2025 demonstrated strong execution: Adjusted EBITDA of $715M (54.7% margin), operating cash flow of $600M (+83% YoY), and Contract Services gross margin expansion of 270bps to 68.4%. The April 2026 completion of the DPS acquisition (now Kodiak Power Solutions) adds 395 MW of distributed generation capacity serving data centers, creating meaningful optionality in the behind-the-meter power market.

The fundamental story is compelling, but the market has fully recognized it. KGS has appreciated ~109% in the past 12 months and now trades above the analyst consensus median price target. At 80x trailing P/E and approximately 26x FY2026 consensus EPS of $2.71, the stock prices in substantial growth that must actually materialize. Estimate revisions are accelerating (+31.9% for current year over the past 30 days), which typically signals continued momentum, but the combination of leverage ($3.5B+ post-DPS), a DOJ/OFAC investigation, geographic concentration, and near-term IV of 60.4% (nearly 2x historical vol) indicates the market itself is pricing elevated uncertainty. The risk/reward at $71 favors patience over new capital deployment.

## Company Overview

Kodiak Gas Services operates two segments: Contract Services (90.3% of revenue), which provides compression, gas treating, and cooling infrastructure under fixed-fee contracts; and Other Services (9.7%), which includes station construction, maintenance, and parts sales. The company IPO'd in July 2023 after being assembled through private equity (EQT AB) acquisitions.

The fleet consists of ~4,736 compression units, with 80% classified as large horsepower (>1,000 HP). Approximately 82.8% of assets are deployed in the Permian Basin and Eagle Ford Shale. Contracts are typically 3-5 year primary terms with inflation adjusters, and only 9% of revenue-generating HP is on month-to-month terms post-primary expiration.

In April 2026, KGS completed the acquisition of Distributed Power Solutions for ~$587M cash plus 2.4M shares. Rebranded as Kodiak Power Solutions, this adds 395 MW of generation capacity serving data centers, microgrids, and industrial customers — a strategic diversification beyond traditional oilfield compression.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Growth:**
Revenue grew from $850M (2023) to $1,159M (2024, +36.4% including CSI acquisition) to $1,308M (2025, +12.8%). Contract Services revenue grew 14.2% in 2025, driven almost entirely by pricing — fleet HP grew only 1.2%, demonstrating genuine rate increases in a supply-constrained market.

**Profitability:**
Adjusted EBITDA reached $715M in FY2025 (54.7% margin, up from 52.6%). Contract Services adjusted gross margin expanded to 68.4% from 65.7%. Adjusted diluted EPS grew 47% to $1.54. GAAP net income was $81.6M (6.2% margin), depressed by $198M in interest expense and one-time charges including the Mexico disposition loss ($33.3M) and sales tax settlements ($28M).

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow nearly doubled to $600M from $328M in 2024. Free cash flow was $284M (21.7% margin). The improvement reflects both underlying business performance and working capital normalization following the CSI integration.

**Balance Sheet:**
Total debt stands at approximately $2.6B pre-DPS, rising to >$3.5B post-acquisition and the $1B March 2026 notes offering. Net leverage is approximately 3.6x EBITDA — aggressive but within industry norms for midstream infrastructure. The debt maturity ladder is well-structured: $750M 2029 notes (targeted for redemption with new issuance), $465M (2030), $1B (2031), $770M (2033), $630M (2035). Interest expense was $198M in 2025 (~15.2% of revenue), though the March refinancing saves approximately 140bps on $750M of debt.

Cash on hand is minimal ($3.2M); liquidity is maintained through a $2.0B ABL facility with $1.5B available. Current ratio of 0.84 is below 1.0 but acceptable for a contracted infrastructure business. The Altman Z-Score of 1.17 reflects the capital-intensive structure rather than operational distress — covenant compliance is confirmed and cash flow generation comfortably covers debt service.

**Capital Allocation:**
Dividends of $1.96/share annualized (2.7% yield). Share repurchases of $104M in 2025 (2.7M shares from former PE sponsor EQT AB). Growth capex of $200M. The DPS acquisition at $587M cash plus stock represents the most significant capital deployment decision since CSI. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act's full expensing provisions materially reduce current tax obligations for the capital-heavy business.

## Growth Analysis

KGS has multiple growth vectors:

**1. Pricing Power (Near-term, High Confidence):** With 97.7% utilization, extended equipment lead times, and customers booking capacity into 2027-2028 (per RBC Capital), KGS can continue achieving rate increases on existing contracts as they roll and on new deployments. Contract Services revenue grew 14.2% on 1.2% HP growth in 2025 — almost pure pricing.

**2. Fleet Expansion (Medium-term):** The company deployed $200M in growth capex in 2025. New build compression units take 12-18 months to deliver, and the tight supply/demand balance supports high returns on incremental HP additions.

**3. Kodiak Power Solutions (Medium-term, Higher Uncertainty):** The DPS acquisition adds 395 MW of distributed generation with data center exposure. If behind-the-meter power demand materializes as expected — driven by AI infrastructure creating structural shortages of grid-connected power — this could become a material growth driver. However, this is a new end market for KGS with limited track record.

**4. Estimate Revisions Signal Acceleration:** Current year EPS estimates revised up 31.9% in 30 days (from $2.05 to $2.71). Next quarter estimates revised up 54.9% in 30 days. This velocity typically signals continued fundamental improvement, likely reflecting initial DPS accretion being modeled in. FY2027 consensus of $3.31 represents 22% growth over FY2026.

The estimate revision velocity exceeds the +30% threshold that historically signals continued outperformance. However, with the stock at approximately 26x FY2026 estimates, a substantial portion of this growth is already priced.

## Competitive Landscape

KGS holds a dominant competitive position in U.S. large HP contract compression:

**Market Position:** Largest large-HP fleet in the Permian Basin. 97.7% utilization indicates pricing power rather than market share competition. The compression market is an oligopoly — KGS, Archrock, and USA Compression control the majority of the large HP market.

**Barriers to Entry:** New compression unit lead times of 12-18+ months. Equipment costs of $5M+ per large HP unit. Existing customer relationships with multi-year contracts. Specialized operational expertise (KGS operates a dedicated training facility — BEARS Academy). Scale advantages in maintenance and parts procurement.

**Competitive Dynamics:** RBC Capital confirms "tight equipment availability and extended lead times," with customers securing capacity "well into 2027 and 2028." This supports sustained pricing power absent a demand collapse. The industry is shifting from cyclical to quasi-utility in character, as outsourced compression becomes permanent infrastructure.

**DPS/Data Center Positioning:** The Kodiak Power Solutions pivot places KGS among early movers in distributed behind-the-meter power for data centers. Competition here includes Aggreko, rental power companies, and vertically integrated solutions from hyperscalers. KGS's differentiation is its operational expertise in rotating equipment and its customer relationships in energy infrastructure.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Leverage Risk (Moderate-High):** Post-DPS, total debt exceeds $3.5B against ~$800M+ pro-forma EBITDA (assuming DPS contribution). Net leverage of ~4x+ is manageable but creates vulnerability if utilization drops below 90% or if refinancing costs rise. The March 2026 refinancing was positive (140bp savings), but the debt quantum is substantial.

**2. DOJ/OFAC Investigation (Low probability, High severity tail risk):** The Mexico cartel-linked payments investigation is ongoing. Self-reporting typically results in more lenient treatment, and the underlying amounts were "not material." However, OFAC/counter-terrorism frameworks can impose penalties disproportionate to underlying conduct. This is an unquantifiable tail risk.

**3. Geographic Concentration (Moderate):** 82.8% Permian/Eagle Ford exposure. A sustained production decline in these basins (unlikely in the medium term given LNG export buildout) or adverse regulation would disproportionately impact KGS.

**4. Texas Sales Tax Liability:** $102.3M accrued; potential for additional exposure as audits continue.

**5. DPS Integration Execution:** KGS is entering the data center power market — a new end market with different customer relationships and competitive dynamics. Integration risk exists despite management's rotating equipment expertise.

**6. Valuation Risk:** At 26x FY2026 and 22x FY2027, any growth disappointment (DPS underperformance, utilization decline, delayed fleet expansion) could trigger multiple compression. The stock has minimal margin of safety at current levels.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV of 60.4% versus 32.9% historical volatility represents a 27.5pp premium — the options market is pricing significant near-term uncertainty. The term structure is in steep backwardation (60.4% near-term vs. 41.7% six-month), suggesting the market expects an imminent catalyst or event resolution. IV rose 8.9pp in the past week (from 51.6% to 60.4%) alongside a 4.6% price increase — rising vol on a rising price is unusual and may signal informed positioning ahead of the next earnings release or DPS integration updates.

The put/call OI ratio of 0.07 indicates minimal put hedging, suggesting existing holders remain confident. Combined with the 8.9% short interest and 5.3 days to cover, there is meaningful short positioning that could fuel continued momentum if a positive catalyst emerges.

## Investment Thesis

- DPS acquisition delivers $100-150M+ incremental EBITDA within 18 months as data center power demand accelerates
- Compression pricing power continues with 3-5% annual rate increases on a 97%+ utilized fleet
- FY2027 EPS reaches $3.50-4.00 as DPS accretion fully materializes

- DOJ/OFAC investigation results in material penalties or management distraction
- Rising rates increase refinancing costs on $3.5B+ debt, compressing equity value
- Multiple compresses to 15-17x forward as growth disappointment reclassifies KGS as a leveraged cyclical rather than infrastructure growth

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**

## Conclusion

Kodiak Gas Services operates an exceptional franchise: dominant market position, 97.7% utilization, contracted inflation-adjusted cash flows, expanding margins, and a compelling strategic diversification into data center power. The estimate revision velocity confirms accelerating fundamentals. Management has executed well since IPO (271% TSR vs. 174% peers), and the removal of the EQT AB overhang is structurally positive.

However, the stock at $71.40 has fully discounted most near-term positive catalysts. Trading above the analyst consensus median target, at 26x FY2026 consensus, and after a ~109% 12-month move, the risk/reward is unfavorable for new capital. The combination of leverage, DOJ investigation, geographic concentration, and a new-market acquisition creates meaningful downside scenarios not adequately compensated at current prices. Options markets confirm elevated uncertainty (steep IV backwardation).

The appropriate posture is to hold existing positions and wait for either a pullback to $55-60 (providing 20%+ margin of safety) or confirmation that DPS accretion exceeds expectations. The fundamental trajectory is positive, but the stock price has front-run the fundamentals.

**Recommendation: HOLD (Growth Hold)**
