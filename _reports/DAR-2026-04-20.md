## Executive Summary

Darling Ingredients presents a bifurcated investment case. The core rendering and food ingredients business is performing well: segment-level EBITDA grew 17% YoY to $923M in FY2025, driven by fat price recovery (+21% BFT) and margin expansion in both Feed (23.2%) and Food (27.7%) segments. Free cash flow of $679M (11.1% margin) significantly exceeds reported net income of $63M, a divergence explained by $509M in D&A, non-cash DGD equity losses, and the cash-generative nature of the collection model.

The problem is the Diamond Green Diesel joint venture. DGD's EBITDA contribution to Darling collapsed from $290M in FY2024 to $104M in FY2025 (with a full-year standalone net loss of $68M) due to the BTC-to-CFPC policy transition, tariffs on imported feedstocks, and operational disruptions from catalyst turnarounds. The Q4 sequential improvement ($58M of the $104M annual total came in Q4 alone) offers a recovery signal, but final 45Z regulations remain unpublished, SAF credit reductions under OBBBA cap upside at $1.00/gallon (down from $1.75), and feedstock sourcing restrictions add compliance complexity.

The stock has rallied 88% from its April 2025 lows on surging estimate revisions (+59% on FY2026E EPS over 90 days) and the Nexida collagen JV announcement. At $59, it trades at ~14x trailing FCF and ~16x forward P/E on FY2026 consensus ($3.75), which is reasonable given the recovery trajectory. However, 4 consecutive earnings misses, elevated leverage at 3.8x, and unresolved DGD policy risk argue for waiting until the next 1-2 quarters confirm the recovery before adding aggressively.

## Company Overview

Darling Ingredients operates the world's largest rendering network, processing approximately 15% of global animal agricultural by-products across 260+ facilities in 15+ countries. The business converts waste streams from meatpackers, restaurants, and food processors into usable products: animal fats, protein meals, collagen, renewable diesel feedstock, and biogas.

**Segment Structure:**
- **Feed Ingredients** (~65% of revenue, ~$4.0B): Core rendering — collects and processes animal by-products and used cooking oil into non-food-grade fats, protein meals, pet food ingredients, and organic fertilizers.
- **Food Ingredients** (~25%, ~$1.5B): Collagen (gelatin and hydrolyzed peptides via Rousselot brand, ~30% global share), natural casings, crude heparin, food-grade fats.
- **Fuel Ingredients** (~10%, ~$600M): 50% ownership in DGD (1.2B gallon renewable diesel/SAF capacity with Valero), European biogas, fallen stock processing.

The business model rests on a network effect: the collection infrastructure creates high barriers to entry, and the scale allows processing optimization across multiple end-markets depending on relative prices.

Altman Z-Score at 1.91 (grey zone) reflects this leverage concern.

Management guided Q1 2026 core EBITDA at $240-250M, implying a ~$1B annualized run rate (vs. $923M in FY2025). Feed segment margins expanded from 21.5% to 23.2% on pricing power and volume growth (+1.7%). Food segment margins expanded from 25.1% to 27.7% with 7.0% volume growth driven by collagen demand.

**Estimate Revisions — Strongly Positive:**
FY2026E EPS has risen from $2.36 (90 days ago) to $3.75 currently (+59%). FY2027E has risen from $2.97 to $4.30 (+45%). Next quarter estimates jumped +75.6% over 90 days. This is among the sharpest upward revision profiles in Consumer Staples.

**Growth Catalysts:**
1. **DGD Recovery**: Q4 showed DGD approaching breakeven/positive margins as CFPC credit transfer markets develop and catalyst turnarounds complete. DGD2/DGD3 operational efficiency improving.
2. **Nexida JV**: Combining Rousselot with Tessenderlo's PB Leiner creates a ~30% global collagen entity at no cash cost. Potential for premium valuation, eventual IPO/spin.
3. **Fat Price Tailwinds**: BFT prices +21% in FY2025 with energy price volatility supporting biofuel feedstock demand. Elevated oil prices support sustained fat/UCO pricing.
4. **UPI Bovinos Acquisition**: ~$114M for Brazilian rendering assets expands South American collection network.

**Headwinds:**
- Protein meal prices declining (Pet Food PM -26.6%, Feather Meal -25.1%)
- DGD SAF credit reduction ($1.75 → $1.00/gallon under OBBBA)
- Potential new renewable diesel capacity adding feedstock competition
- Enviroflight (black soldier fly) writeoff signals capital allocation misstep

## Competitive Landscape

Darling holds a dominant position in global rendering with genuine barriers to entry:

1. **Collection Network Moat**: Building a rendering collection route requires years of relationship building, permits, and infrastructure investment. DAR's 260+ facilities and established collection relationships represent decades of accumulated positioning that new entrants cannot replicate economically.

2. **Vertical Integration via DGD**: The rendering-to-fuel integration provides a guaranteed buyer for lower-grade fats (DGD purchased ~$1.2B from DAR in FY2025), creating a pricing floor that smaller renderers lack.

3. **Collagen Scale**: 30% global market share through Rousselot. The Nexida JV will further consolidate this position.

4. **Threats**: Captive rendering by integrated meat processors (JBS, Tyson, Cargill) limits raw material access. Increasing UCO competition from multiple biofuel producers competing for restaurant grease. Plant-based collagen alternatives remain a long-term substitution risk.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**
1. **DGD Policy/Regulatory Risk**: Final 45Z regulations still pending from Treasury. OBBBA reduced SAF credits to $1.00/gallon. Feedstock sourcing restrictions complicate operations. If CFPC is repealed or further reduced, DGD's asset value ($2.1B on DAR's books) faces impairment risk.

2. **Leverage in a Cyclical Business**: The $500M 2027 maturity requires refinancing into what may be a higher-rate environment. Limited cushion if commodity prices decline and DGD remains challenged simultaneously.

3. **Earnings Visibility**: 4 consecutive quarterly misses demonstrate that consensus models struggle with DAR's multiple commodity exposures and DGD accounting. The sharp estimate revisions could reverse if DGD recovery stalls.

4. **Commodity Cyclicality**: Protein meal prices declining 16-27% in key categories. Fat prices elevated but potentially peaking. A global recession would reduce meat consumption and restaurant activity, constraining raw material availability and pricing.

5. **Goodwill**: One reporting unit ($563M goodwill) has fair value only 20-30% above carrying value. Already recorded $19M goodwill impairment + $30M+ long-lived asset impairments in FY2025.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($70-78, +19-32%):**
DGD achieves full CFPC compliance and margins normalize in H2 2026. Core business sustains $1B+ annual EBITDA run rate. Fat prices remain elevated due to energy disruption. Nexida JV closes and market assigns independent value to collagen franchise. FY2026 EPS reaches $4.00+ as both segments contribute. Stock re-rates to 17-19x forward P/E on confirmed recovery. FCF supports continued deleveraging.

**Bear Case ($38-42, -28-36%):**
DGD recovery stalls due to regulatory delays or further credit reductions. Protein meal prices continue declining. A recession reduces restaurant activity and raw material availability. The company misses estimates for a 5th consecutive quarter, destroying credibility with investors. Leverage becomes constraining, forcing equity raise or asset sales at disadvantageous terms. Stock returns to $38-42 range where it traded for much of 2025.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. First earnings beat after 4 consecutive misses — specifically if Q1 FY2026 (reporting ~May 2026) delivers core EBITDA above $250M guidance AND DGD contribution improves sequentially
2. DGD policy clarity: final 45Z Treasury regulations published with favorable terms OR DGD achieves $50M+ quarterly EBITDA contribution consistently (2+ quarters)
3. Stock pullback to $48-50 range (~14x FY2026E EPS) providing wider margin of safety

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. 5th consecutive earnings miss combined with estimate revisions turning negative (30-day cuts >10%)
2. Net leverage exceeding 4.5x with no clear path to reduction
3. DGD records additional impairment or announces capacity reduction beyond DGD1 idling
4. Nexida JV falls apart or faces regulatory block

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings (expected May 2026). The Q1 2026 report is the critical data point — management guided $240-250M core EBITDA. Delivery at or above that level combined with DGD sequential progress would justify upgrading.

## Conclusion

Darling Ingredients is a high-quality franchise business (30% global collagen, 15% animal by-product processing) going through a cyclical and policy-driven trough. The core rendering operation is strengthening, and estimate revisions are sharply positive. The Nexida collagen JV and DGD Q4 improvement provide legitimate upside catalysts.

However, the 88% rally from April 2025 lows has repriced much of the recovery. Rich options IV and term structure backwardation confirm near-term uncertainty. Elevated leverage in a cyclical commodity business with an unreliable DGD contribution adds fragility.

The appropriate posture is to hold for investors already positioned, monitoring Q1 2026 results for confirmation of the earnings recovery. New money should wait for either a pullback to $48-50 or evidence of consecutive earnings delivery before building a position. Analyst consensus ($58-78 range, $70 median) suggests limited downside from current levels but meaningful upside if the recovery thesis executes.

**Recommendation: HOLD** — growth thesis intact with specific catalysts (DGD recovery, Nexida JV, rising estimates) but unverified after 4 consecutive misses. Wait for execution evidence.
