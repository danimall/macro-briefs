## Executive Summary

EQT Corporation is the largest natural gas producer in the United States, operating a vertically integrated upstream-midstream model concentrated in the Appalachian Basin. The 2024 Equitrans Midstream merger restructured EQT's cost profile, collapsing third-party gathering expense from $0.35/Mcfe to $0.08/Mcfe and adding $1.2B in midstream operating income in FY2025. The company generated record free cash flow of $1.8B in Q1 2026 while actively deleveraging from $7.8B toward a $5.0B debt target.

The investment case rests on three pillars: (1) a structural cost advantage from vertical integration that persists regardless of commodity price, (2) multi-year demand tailwinds from data center power consumption (6-7 Bcf/day of new in-basin demand expected this decade) and contracted LNG offtake (4.5 MTPA + 2.0 MTPA tolling beginning 2030-2031), and (3) a valuation at ~11.4x forward earnings versus a peer median of 15.9x. The primary risks are EQT's ~93% natural gas concentration, the $7.8B debt load, and geographic concentration in a single basin. At $60.08, the stock trades at a meaningful discount to the $70.00 consensus median target, with a credible path to $68-72 over 12 months as deleveraging proceeds and data center demand materializes in production contracts.

## Company Overview

EQT operates through three segments: Upstream (exploration and production of natural gas), Gathering (1,995 miles of pipeline, 650,000 HP compression), and Transmission (950 miles of pipeline, 5.0 Bcf/day throughput, 44 Bcf storage). The company holds 28.0 Tcfe of proved reserves across ~2.3 million gross acres, with 93% located in the Marcellus Shale. EQT has approximately 4,000 gross undeveloped drilling locations representing 30+ years of inventory at current drilling pace.

The Equitrans Midstream merger, completed in mid-2024, brought gathering and transmission assets in-house, eliminating EQT's largest cost center and creating a self-sustaining midstream annuity with weighted average contract terms of 10-13 years. The Mountain Valley Pipeline (MVP Mainline), now operational with 1.29 Bcf/day of capacity, provides premium market access. Expansion projects — MVP Boost (0.6 Bcf/day compression, target mid-2028) and MVP Southgate (0.55 Bcf/day extension to North Carolina, target mid-2028) — are in regulatory process.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Earnings:** FY2025 revenue was $23.8B with net income of $2.04B, operating margin of 42.1%, and net margin of 30.1%. The revenue figure includes significant derivative settlement impacts — the underlying upstream revenue was approximately $7.6B, up ~24% from FY2024's $6.1B, driven by a 16% increase in average realized price ($3.19/Mcfe vs. $2.74) and 7% volume growth (2,382 Bcfe vs. 2,228 Bcfe).

Q1 2026 was exceptional: sales volume of 618 Bcfe beat the high end of guidance, non-GAAP EPS of $2.33 exceeded the $2.10 consensus by 11.0%, and the company generated record quarterly free cash flow. The January 2026 Henry Hub spike to $30.72/MMBtu, during which EQT captured ~98% of production at first-of-month pricing ($7.22-$7.46/MMBtu), was a significant contributor. This was a one-time event, and the quarterly run-rate should normalize.

**Cash Flow:** FY2025 operating cash flow was $5.13B with free cash flow of $2.84B, translating to a 36.7% FCF margin. FCF conversion well above net income signals high-quality earnings. The 2026 capex budget of $2.65-2.85B includes $580-640M allocated to growth projects (MVP expansions, additional MVP equity).

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt stands at $7.8B against stockholders' equity of $23.75B (debt-to-equity of approximately 0.33x). The current ratio of 0.76x is below 1.0, reflecting reliance on the revolving credit facility for near-term liquidity — standard for the industry but worth monitoring. Credit ratings are investment-grade (Baa3/BBB-/BBB-), all stable. In FY2025, EQT retired $1.4B of senior notes. The Midstream Joint Venture structure creates a fixed obligation to distribute cash to the Class B Unitholder ($355M in 2025, with $3.41B remaining to achieve Base Return).

**Earnings Trajectory:** EQT has beaten EPS estimates in 4 consecutive quarters: +9.7% (Q2 2025), +19.8% (Q3 2025), +20.0% (Q4 2025), +11.0% (Q1 2026). This consistent execution supports confidence in near-term estimates. However, the estimate revision picture is mixed: FY2026 current-year estimates have risen 18% over 90 days (to $4.85) and 3.1% over 30 days, but current-quarter ($0.57, down 21.7% over 90 days) and next-quarter ($0.71, down 26.3% over 90 days) estimates have fallen materially. This divergence reflects the Q1 2026 windfall pulling forward full-year estimates while subsequent quarters face tougher comps and lower spot prices. FY2027 estimates of $5.00 are down 11% over 90 days, suggesting the market is tempering longer-term enthusiasm.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Volume:** 2026 guidance of 2,275-2,375 Bcfe implies flat-to-slightly-lower production versus FY2025's 2,382 Bcfe. EQT prioritizes disciplined capital allocation over volume growth — management will curtail production when prices are unfavorable (14 Bcfe curtailed in 2025, 107 Bcfe in 2024).

**Data Center Demand:** This is EQT's most differentiated growth driver. Management and industry sources estimate 6-7 Bcf/day of new in-basin natural gas demand this decade, anchored by ~45 GW of data center capacity under construction, with ~12 GW in EQT's core Appalachian footprint. EQT's integrated pipeline network positions it as the natural supplier. While the demand estimates are credible given AI infrastructure buildout trajectories, actual contracted volumes to data center operators have not been disclosed in detail.

**LNG Offtake:** EQT has 4.5 MTPA of LNG offtake agreements (Sempra, NextDecade, Commonwealth LNG) plus 2.0 MTPA of tolling, beginning 2030-2031. Of the capacity commencing in 2030, 1.0 MTPA offtake and the entire 2.0 MTPA tolling commitment relate to projects that have not yet reached final investment decision (FID). These are long-duration commitments providing demand visibility, but the execution timeline extends years into the future with construction and regulatory risk.

**MVP Expansions:** MVP Boost and MVP Southgate, each targeting mid-2028 completion, would add 1.15 Bcf/day of combined capacity. The original MVP Mainline suffered years of delays and cost overruns from regulatory challenges — the same opposition remains active for these projects.

**Structural Growth Assessment:** EQT's growth story is real but back-loaded. Meaningful volume uplift from data centers and LNG is a 2028-2031 story. Near-term growth depends on natural gas prices, which the company does not control. The consistent earnings beats suggest operational execution is strong, but the declining estimate revisions for Q2-Q3 2026 indicate the market recognizes the Q1 windfall was partially non-recurring.

## Valuation Assessment

At $60.08, EQT trades at approximately 11.4x forward earnings ($4.85 FY2026E) and 18.1x trailing EPS ($3.31 FY2025). This represents a 28% discount to the peer median P/E of 15.9x on a forward basis.

The discount is partially justified: EQT is a pure-play natural gas producer in an environment where oil prices are driving peer outperformance, while Henry Hub has pulled back from its January 2026 spike. The single-commodity, single-basin concentration warrants some discount. However, the magnitude of discount seems excessive given: (1) EQT's integrated midstream infrastructure provides margin stability that pure E&P peers lack, (2) the 30+ year drilling inventory eliminates reserve replacement risk, and (3) the data center/LNG demand pipeline is more visible than for most gas peers.

Consensus targets range from $57 (low) to $83 (high), with a median of $70, implying 16.5% upside. 19 of 25 analysts rate EQT Buy or Strong Buy, with zero Sell ratings in the current period (the single Strong Sell from last month was removed). The $57 low target is essentially current price, suggesting limited perceived downside even among skeptics.

Using normalized FCF: $2.84B FY2025 FCF on a $36.7B market cap implies a 7.7% FCF yield. At the peer median valuation (~15.9x), the stock would trade at approximately $77, consistent with the consensus target range.

The dividend yield of 1.1% is below the peer median of 2.1%, reflecting management's preference for debt retirement and buybacks over dividend growth. The $2B share repurchase authorization provides additional return capacity.

## Competitive Landscape

**Cost Structure:** LOE of $0.09/Mcfe is among the lowest in the industry. The Equitrans integration reduced gathering costs by 77%. The combo-development drilling approach, where multi-well pads are synchronized with midstream infrastructure, drives efficiencies that smaller operators cannot replicate.

**Scale and Integration:** EQT is the only large-scale, vertically integrated natural gas producer in the US. The 2,945-mile pipeline network, 44 Bcf of storage, and 5.0 Bcf/day throughput capacity create a moat. Competitors accessing the same Marcellus gas must pay third-party gathering fees that EQT has internalized.

**Market Access:** 4.3 Bcf/day of firm pipeline takeaway capacity, including 1.29 Bcf/day on MVP Mainline, provides access to premium markets (Southeast US, Gulf Coast LNG). The planned MVP expansions would widen this advantage.

**Inventory Depth:** ~4,000 gross undeveloped locations representing 30+ years at current pace. This differentiates EQT from peers facing inventory exhaustion, a concern across the broader shale industry where Tier 1 acreage is depleting.

**Relative to peers:** DVN (12.2x P/E) is comparable on valuation but is oil-weighted. CTRA (15.9x) is a smaller gas/oil hybrid. EXE (7.6x) trades at a steep discount reflecting its own integration complexity. FANG (35.8x) is primarily oil-weighted at a premium multiple. EQT's integrated model and gas-focused positioning are differentiated.

## Risk Assessment

**Commodity Price Sensitivity:** This is the dominant risk. EQT is ~93% natural gas by reserves. With only ~25% of 2026 production hedged (floors $3.50-$4.25/Dth, ceilings $4.94-$6.29/Dth), the company has substantial unhedged exposure. A sustained decline in Henry Hub below $3.00/MMBtu would compress cash flows sharply and slow deleveraging. The Q1 2026 windfall from the $30.72 Henry Hub spike is not replicable on a routine basis.

**Leverage:** The $7.8B debt load creates meaningful fixed charges. While the deleveraging trajectory is credible ($1.4B retired in FY2025), it requires sustained commodity prices. The Midstream JV obligation ($3.41B remaining Base Return to Class B Unitholder) is an additional fixed claim.

**Geographic Concentration:** All assets in the Appalachian Basin. Seismicity, water availability, coal mine interference, and regulatory changes (Pennsylvania, West Virginia) would impact the entire portfolio simultaneously.

**Securities Class Action:** $134M recognized in FY2025, up from $18M — a significant and growing legal exposure.

**LNG Execution Risk:** Projects tied to uncommenced FID represent billions in assumed demand that may not materialize on schedule.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($72-80):** Natural gas prices remain elevated (Henry Hub sustained above $4.00/MMBtu) through 2026-2027, driven by data center demand acceleration, LNG export growth, and reduced drilling activity across the basin. EQT's integrated model captures outsized margins. Debt retirement proceeds ahead of schedule, triggering credit upgrades. MVP expansions complete on time. Applying 15x forward earnings on $5.00+ EPS yields $75+. Additional upside from announced data center supply contracts.

**Bear Case ($45-50):** Henry Hub declines below $3.00/MMBtu on warm weather, demand disappointment, or increased associated gas production from oil-weighted operators. Deleveraging stalls. LNG project FIDs are delayed. Securities class action settlement exceeds provisions. Applying 10x on a compressed $4.00 EPS yields $40-45.

**Base Case ($68-72):** Henry Hub averages $3.50-4.00/MMBtu through 2026. EQT delivers on FY2026 consensus of $4.85 EPS. Deleveraging continues, with total debt reaching $6.5-7.0B by year-end 2026. Data center demand contracts begin to be announced. Market re-rates toward peer median as integration benefits become fully visible. 13-14x on $5.00 FY2027E EPS = $65-70; midstream asset value provides additional support.

**Upside/downside:** +17% to base case, +32% to bull case, -22% to bear case. Risk/reward ratio of approximately 1.5:1 base case, improving to ~1.5:1 when probability-weighting outcomes.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Henry Hub sustained below $2.75/MMBtu for 2+ consecutive months, eliminating FCF capacity for deleveraging
2. Total debt increases above $8.0B (reversal of deleveraging trajectory)
3. Material negative regulatory action on MVP Southgate or MVP Boost that delays projects beyond 2029

## Conclusion

EQT offers a differentiated natural gas investment with structural competitive advantages from vertical integration, a credible multi-year growth pathway from data center and LNG demand, and a valuation discount to peers. The 4-for-4 earnings beat track record, record Q1 2026 FCF, and active deleveraging support the thesis. The primary risk — natural gas price sensitivity — is genuine and unhedgeable at scale, but EQT's cost structure provides more downside resilience than most pure-play gas producers. At $60.08 with a consensus median target of $70, the entry point offers adequate compensation for commodity risk. BUY with a 12-month base case target of $68-72 and a 2-3% position sizing given the single-commodity concentration.
