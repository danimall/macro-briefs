## Executive Summary

Fox Corporation trades at $63.98 with a forward P/E of approximately 12.8x on consensus FY2026 EPS of $4.99, supported by robust free cash flow generation ($3.0B in FY2025), a dominant cable news franchise, and an aggressive capital return program that has retired 36% of outstanding shares since the 2019 spin-off. The company delivered record Q3 FY2026 EBITDA of $954M (+11% YoY) despite lapping a Super Bowl comparison year, with Tubi reaching profitability at scale and FOX One demonstrating early DTC traction.

The investment case is complicated by three specific concerns that prevent an upgrade to BUY: (1) 16.9% short interest with 8.8 days to cover signals meaningful institutional skepticism beyond what is visible in headline metrics; (2) the $2.7B Smartmatic litigation has entered trial proceedings with the $787.5M Dominion settlement as precedent — a similar or larger adverse outcome would represent 6-10% of market cap; and (3) concentrated C-suite insider selling in March 2026 (CEO exercised all remaining options and sold ~$65M, COO sold ~$26M) while technically driven by option expirations, removes any insider alignment signal that would support conviction in the bull case. These factors, combined with structural cord-cutting that reduces the cable subscriber base ~6-7% annually, create a risk profile that warrants holding for existing investors rather than initiating new positions.

## Company Overview

Fox Corporation operates as a U.S.-focused news, sports, and entertainment company through two segments: Cable Network Programming (43% of FY2025 revenue, 84% of EBITDA) and Television (57% of revenue, 16% of EBITDA). Key assets include FOX News (#1 cable network for 10 consecutive years), FOX Sports (NFL, MLB, Big Ten, FIFA World Cup rights), Tubi (~100M MAUs, breakeven), and the recently launched FOX One DTC service ($20/month).

The company holds a 2.4% equity stake in Flutter Entertainment and a 10-year call option (expiring December 2030) to acquire 18.6% of FanDuel at an exercise price of approximately $4.5B. Revenue mix is 47% affiliate fees, 42% advertising, and 11% other.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement:**
FY2025 revenue of $16.3B grew 16.6% YoY, driven by Super Bowl LIX (~$870M sports advertising impact), presidential elections, and secular growth in Tubi and affiliate pricing. Adjusted EBITDA reached $3.62B (+26% YoY) with net income of $2.26B (+51%). The Cable segment generated 43.7% EBITDA margins — among the highest in media — reflecting FOX News's operating leverage.

Q3 FY2026 (March quarter): Revenue of $4.0B declined 8.6% YoY due entirely to the Super Bowl comp, but adjusted EBITDA of $954M set a Q3 record (+11%) as underlying growth ex-Super Bowl accelerated. Advertising revenue ex-Super Bowl grew double-digits. Distribution revenue rose 3% (Cable +5%). EPS of $1.32 beat consensus by 34%.

**Cash Flow & Capital Returns:**
Free cash flow of $3.0B (18.4% margin) in FY2025, with $1.77B generated in Q3 FY2026 alone. Cumulative buybacks since 2019 total $8.5B, reducing the share count by 36%. The annual dividend of ~$0.56/share ($250M total) represents minimal payout relative to cash generation. This leaves substantial capacity for continued buybacks — $1.95B deployed in FY2026 through Q3 with $3.6B in remaining cash.

**Balance Sheet:**
Cash of $3.6B against $6.6B long-term debt implies net debt of ~$3.0B, or roughly 0.8x EBITDA. Current ratio of 2.91x provides ample liquidity. Investment-grade credit ratings (Baa2/BBB, stable) support access to capital markets. The $1.0B undrawn revolver adds additional buffer.

**Earnings Consistency:**
Four consecutive quarters of beats averaging 39.7% above consensus. This sustained outperformance suggests estimates may still be too conservative, though the magnitude of beats is partially explained by cyclical event timing (Super Bowl, elections) that consensus struggled to model precisely.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):**
- FIFA Men's World Cup (summer 2026): 104 matches on broadcast TV, revenue split between Q4 FY2026 and Q1 FY2027
- 2026 midterm elections: political ad market trending to record $11B
- FOX One scaling: early churn data "well below expectations," 50%+ news engagement creating sticky cross-genre usage
- FOX News CPM expansion: up 45% with 500+ new advertisers, management notes "long way to go" vs. broadcast pricing

**Structural Growth Drivers:**
- Tubi: revenue +23% in Q3 FY2026, 100M MAUs, now profitable — represents a scaled AVOD platform at essentially zero incremental investment
- Affiliate rate escalators: Cable distribution revenue growing +5% despite 6-7% subscriber decline, implying 11-12% rate increases
- Digital advertising: programmatic and addressable TV capabilities expanding
- FanDuel option: embedded optionality with 4+ years remaining

**Growth Headwinds:**
- Cable subscriber base declining 6-7% annually (67M → 61M in one year)
- FY2025 was a "triple crown" year (Super Bowl + elections + World Cup qualifiers); FY2027 lacks Super Bowl and presidential elections
- Sports rights costs escalating — NFL contract costs growing faster than advertising revenue
- Post-election political advertising cycle creates natural revenue troughs in odd years

Consensus estimates reflect these dynamics: FY2026E EPS of $4.99 and FY2027E of $5.68 (+14%). The 90-day revision trend is positive (+7.5% current year, +9.7% next year), indicating analysts are still adjusting upward.

## Valuation Assessment

At $63.98, FOXA trades at:
- 12.8x FY2026E EPS ($4.99)
- 11.3x FY2027E EPS ($5.68)
- ~3.5x EV/EBITDA (trailing)
- 0.78x EV/Revenue

Against peers: FOX Class B trades at 15.1x, TGNA at 16.3x, Nexstar at 39.8x. FOXA's discount to its own Class B shares reflects the voting rights differential (Murdoch family controls ~44% of votes via Class B). Relative to TGNA (the most comparable traditional broadcaster), FOXA trades at a slight discount despite superior scale, growth assets (Tubi, FOX One, FanDuel option), and cash generation.

The median analyst target of $73.00 implies 14% upside; the mean of $73.88 implies 15% upside. The high target of $97 assumes full valuation of embedded assets (FanDuel option, Tubi platform value).

**Why the stock isn't clearly mispriced:** The 16.9% short interest tells a different story than the fundamental metrics. The short thesis likely centers on: (a) accelerating cord-cutting overwhelming rate escalators; (b) Smartmatic settlement risk; (c) sports rights cost inflation compressing margins; and/or (d) the lumpiness of revenue making forward earnings less predictable than consensus implies. At 12.8x earnings, the stock is not expensive, but the short interest suggests informed capital believes the earnings power is overstated or temporary.

## Competitive Landscape

FOX News maintains an unassailable #1 position in cable news, finishing as the top-rated cable network for the 10th consecutive year. The franchise's competitive moat derives from viewer loyalty and habit formation that have survived multiple host departures and controversies. CPMs are expanding from a historically low base relative to broadcast network levels, providing pricing runway even as reach moderates.

In sports, FOX holds premium rights (NFL through 2029, MLB World Series, Big Ten) that provide appointment viewing resistant to DVR and time-shifting. The addition of INDYCAR and LIV Golf diversifies the sports portfolio. Competition for rights is intensifying (Amazon, Netflix, YouTube entering), but FOX's broadcast reach gives it structural advantages in advertiser appeal.

Tubi at 100M MAUs has achieved scale in the FAST/AVOD space, competing effectively against Pluto TV and The Roku Channel. Its path to profitability demonstrates the viability of the ad-supported model at scale without premium content investment.

FOX One creates a DTC hedge against cable bundle economics, effectively recreating bundling economics for FOX's own portfolio. Early data showing cross-genre discovery (sports subscribers consuming news) validates the strategic thesis, though the $20/month price point will face ongoing competition from lower-cost alternatives.

## Risk Assessment

**Smartmatic Litigation ($2.7B claim):** The most quantifiable near-term risk. The Dominion settlement of $787.5M establishes precedent. FOX has gained some leverage (discovery into Smartmatic's criminal case), but the trial is proceeding. A settlement in the $500M-$1B range would reduce cash available for buybacks by 2-3 quarters. A worst-case adverse judgment at trial could exceed the Dominion figure. Probability-weighted impact: 15-25% chance of a >$500M payout within 12 months.

**Cord-Cutting Acceleration:** If the pace accelerates to 10-12% annually while rate increases remain 5-7%, the Cable segment's revenue growth turns negative. FOX One partially offsets but is not yet at scale to fully compensate.

**Insider Selling:** The complete disposition of option positions means senior management no longer has leveraged upside exposure through equity compensation (until new grants). No insider purchases observed.

**Short Interest (16.9% of float, 8.8 days to cover):** At 8.8 days to cover, any positive catalyst (Smartmatic resolution, FOX One subscriber disclosure, earnings beat) creates meaningful squeeze potential — but the persistence of short interest at these levels over multiple quarters suggests bears have conviction.

### Options Market Signal

Put/call volume ratio of 1.67 indicates moderately elevated put demand. The 6-month implied move of 15.2% reflects uncertainty around litigation resolution and cyclical event timing. Options positioning shifted from a 3.63 P/C OI ratio in late April to essentially neutral (0.00), suggesting institutional put positions may have been rolled or closed ahead of the May earnings beat.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($75-80, 17-25% upside):**
- FOX One scales to 3-5M subscribers by FY2027, adding $600M-$1B in high-margin DTC revenue
- Smartmatic settles for <$400M (below Dominion), removing the overhang
- FIFA World Cup drives upfront advertiser commitments and Tubi engagement
- CPM expansion continues (still 30-40% below broadcast network levels)
- Buybacks reduce share count another 8-10%, driving EPS to $6.50+ in FY2027
- Forward P/E re-rates to 13-14x on improved visibility

**Bear Case ($48-52, 20-25% downside):**
- Smartmatic judgment/settlement exceeds $1B
- Cord-cutting accelerates to 12%+ annually, overwhelming rate increases
- FOX One fails to achieve scale (<1M subs), burning $200M+ annually
- NFL rights renewal (beyond 2029) requires materially higher payments
- Political environment shifts adversely (advertiser boycotts, regulatory challenges)
- FanDuel option expires worthless or Flutter acquisition structure changes

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Smartmatic resolution for <$500M, removing the litigation overhang and freeing cash for accelerated buybacks
2. FOX One subscriber disclosure showing >2M paid subscribers with <8% monthly churn
3. Short interest declining below 10% of float, indicating institutional bears covering
4. Stock pullback below $55 on market weakness (rather than fundamental deterioration), creating >15% upside to consensus targets

**Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Smartmatic adverse judgment or settlement exceeding $1B
2. Cable subscriber loss accelerating to >10% sequential rate (annualized >15%)
3. FOX One shut down or pricing materially reduced (indicating DTC strategy failure)
4. Lachlan Murdoch departure or Murdoch family exploring asset sale below intrinsic value

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q4 FY2026 earnings (August 2026) will provide: (a) FIFA World Cup early revenue data, (b) full-year EBITDA guidance confirmation, (c) FOX One post-launch anniversary metrics, and (d) potential Smartmatic trial developments.

## Conclusion

Fox Corporation is a fundamentally sound media company trading at a reasonable valuation with demonstrated ability to generate significant free cash flow and return capital aggressively to shareholders. The business is well-positioned in live content categories (news, sports) that retain relevance in a fragmenting media landscape.

The specific concerns preventing a BUY recommendation are falsifiable: (1) FY2027 (no Super Bowl, no presidential election) will test whether earnings power is sustainable in non-event years; (2) the Smartmatic litigation represents a quantifiable binary risk that could consume 2-3 years of buyback capacity if resolved adversely; and (3) the absence of insider alignment removes a signal that would provide conviction for adding to positions. Existing holders should maintain exposure given the attractive FCF yield (~23% of market cap), capital return program, and upcoming World Cup catalyst, while monitoring the three upgrade triggers identified above.
