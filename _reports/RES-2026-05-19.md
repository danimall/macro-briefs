## Executive Summary

RPC Inc is an oilfield services company trading at 76x trailing earnings ($0.15 EPS) after a 50% price rally from its 52-week low of $4.175. The stock now sits at $7.12, above the consensus analyst target of $6.54 and the median target of $6.20. The fundamental picture is deteriorating: EPS estimates are being revised downward across all time horizons (current year -5.7% over 90 days, next year -18.3%), pressure pumping revenue has fallen 37% cumulatively over two years, and Q1 2026 produced negative free cash flow.

The company's balance sheet remains strong ($210M cash, minimal debt), and the Pintail wireline acquisition provides some diversification away from the commoditized pressure pumping segment. However, RPC lacks electric frac fleet capability in a market increasingly demanding it, has 15% customer concentration in a single private E&P, and operates in an industry characterized by structural oversupply and E&P consolidation that extracts pricing power from service providers. The 26.9% short interest reflects widespread institutional skepticism about the sustainability of the recent rally.

## Company Overview

RPC, Inc. provides completion, production, and maintenance services to U.S. E&P companies, primarily in the Permian Basin. Revenue is split between Technical Services (94.4%) and Support Services (5.6%). The April 2025 acquisition of Pintail Completions for $245M transformed the revenue mix, making wireline perforating the third-largest revenue contributor (19.4%) behind pressure pumping (29.8%) and downhole tools (24.2%).

The company is controlled by the Rollins family (~61% voting power) and operates under the NYSE Controlled Corporation exemption, which eliminates requirements for majority-independent board composition, independent compensation committee, or independent nominating committee. The company employs approximately 2,893 people.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Margins:**
FY2025 revenue was $1.63B, up 15% from $1.41B in FY2024. This growth was entirely acquisition-driven; excluding Pintail's $296M contribution, organic revenue declined approximately 6%. The underlying pressure pumping business fell from $587M to $485M (-17% YoY).

Operating margin collapsed from 6.9% (2024) to 2.8% (2025), continuing a severe multi-year compression from 18.0% in 2022. Net income fell 65% to $32M ($0.15/share). Adjusted EBITDA was flat at $233M despite adding Pintail, suggesting either Pintail is margin-dilutive or core business deterioration offset its contribution.

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow declined 42% from $349M to $201M. Free cash flow fell to $53M from $129M. Q1 2026 produced negative FCF (-$1M) due to working capital pressure.

**Balance Sheet:**
The balance sheet is the sole unambiguous positive: $210M cash, $50M debt (Pintail seller note), no revolver draws, 3.24x current ratio, Altman Z-Score of 4.44. This provides substantial survival cushion through the cycle.

**Q1 2026 Results:**
Revenue of $455M (+7% sequential, +37% YoY on Pintail comp), Adjusted EBITDA of $53.5M (11.8% margin), Adjusted EPS of $0.03. Pressure pumping saw a 20% sequential revenue increase but management explicitly stated they "would not reactivate a fleet at current pricing levels."

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects are poor across measurable dimensions:

1. **Estimate revisions are uniformly negative.** Current quarter EPS estimates fell 24% over 90 days. Next year estimates fell 18.3%.

2. **Core business is shrinking.** Organic revenue declined 6% in FY2025. Pressure pumping revenue has fallen 37% cumulatively since 2023.

3. **Industry headwinds are structural.** U.S. rig count declined 6.3% in 2025 (average 562 vs. 723 in 2022). Efficiency improvements mean fewer service hours produce the same output. E&P consolidation creates larger, more powerful buyers who extract pricing concessions.

4. **Technology positioning is weak.** RPC's stacked pressure pumping fleets are Tier 2 diesel — the least desirable equipment class. Management's CFO explicitly acknowledged these "aren't what customers are more focused on." Without electric frac capability, RPC is locked out of the most attractive pressure pumping demand from public E&Ps.

5. **Potential positives:** Metal Max technology (15% of power section utilization, growing), On-Plug adoption by multiple operators, and AI-driven energy demand growth could benefit the broader sector. Natural gas takeaway capacity additions could unlock New Mexico activity. However, these are multi-quarter or multi-year stories without near-term earnings impact.

## Valuation Assessment

At $7.12, RPC trades at:
- 76x trailing P/E (on $0.15 EPS)
- ~36x forward P/E on FY2026 consensus of $0.20
- ~26x forward P/E on FY2027 consensus of $0.27
- EV/EBITDA approximately 6.5-7x on $233M adjusted EBITDA

The stock trades above the consensus analyst target ($6.54 mean, $6.20 median) and near the high target of $8.00. The peer median P/E is 40.3x, which is inflated by Helix Energy's 107x. The more relevant comp is Core Labs at 22x, where RES trades at a significant premium.

The dividend yield of 2.3% ($0.16/year) provides modest income support but insufficient total return without earnings recovery. Forward earnings would need to reach approximately $0.35-0.40 (70-100% above current consensus) to justify the current price at a reasonable 18-20x multiple. No visible catalyst supports that level of earnings expansion.

The stock has appreciated 50% from its 52-week low on oil geopolitical tailwinds, but this price appreciation has not been accompanied by positive estimate revisions — estimates have moved in the opposite direction. This divergence between price and fundamentals is a warning sign.

## Competitive Landscape

RPC competes against Halliburton, Baker Hughes, SLB, Liberty Energy, Patterson-UTI, ProFrac, and ProPetro. Its competitive position is deteriorating on multiple fronts:

**Weaknesses:**
- No electric frac fleet capability (Liberty, ProFrac have converted)
- Stacked assets are Tier 2 diesel (potentially stranded)
- Smaller scale than integrated OFS majors
- Customer base weighted toward private operators (more price-sensitive)
- 15% single-customer concentration (counterparty risk)

**Strengths:**
- Leading Permian wireline position (via Pintail)
- Proprietary downhole tools (Metal Max, On-Plug)
- Strongest balance sheet among mid-cap OFS peers
- Conservative management enables survival through deep cycles

The Pintail acquisition provides meaningful differentiation in wireline perforating, and the downhole tools segment is less commoditized than pressure pumping. However, these segments represent diversification, not dominance. The core revenue engine (pressure pumping) is in structural decline with no obvious path to competitive parity without significant capital investment in fleet upgrades.

## Risk Assessment

**Structural oversupply (HIGH):** Industry efficiency improvements mean fewer assets produce the same output. Management explicitly refuses to reactivate fleets at current pricing. This is structural overcapacity, not cyclical.

**Technology obsolescence (HIGH):** Tier 2 diesel fleets may be permanently stranded. Without electric frac capability, RPC cannot compete for premium pressure pumping contracts from public E&Ps.

**Customer concentration (MODERATE):** 15% from a single private E&P. Private operator financial stability is lower than public E&P. Loss of this customer would create a material revenue gap.

**Controlled company governance (MODERATE):** Rollins family control eliminates standard governance protections for minority shareholders. CEO compensation increased 72% while net income fell 65%.

**Estimate deterioration (HIGH):** Negative revisions across all time horizons with no identified catalyst for reversal.

**Short interest (ELEVATED):** At 26.9% of float with 4.6 days to cover, this reflects significant informed institutional bearishness. While this creates squeeze potential, it signals that sophisticated capital sees sustained downside.

**Options Market Signal:**
ATM IV declined from 79.1% to 53.5% (-25.6pp) over the prior two weeks, coinciding with an 8.2% price decline. The IV compression suggests the market views post-earnings uncertainty as largely resolved — and resolved negatively (stock fell on/after earnings). Current 53.5% IV remains elevated relative to typical OFS names, reflecting ongoing uncertainty about the fundamental trajectory.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($8.00-9.00):**
Oil remains elevated through H2 2026 driven by geopolitical supply disruption, U.S. drilling activity stabilizes or modestly increases, Pintail integration succeeds with margin expansion, Metal Max technology gains share, and E&P capital discipline eventually loosens. At $0.35+ EPS and 22x forward P/E = $7.70-8.50. Short squeeze on 26.9% SI provides additional upside torque if fundamentals confirm.

**Bear Case ($4.00-5.00):**
Oil drops on diplomatic resolution or demand destruction, U.S. rig count continues declining, pressure pumping pricing deteriorates further, Pintail fails to deliver margin improvement, and estimates continue falling. At $0.12-0.15 EPS (trough) and 30x depressed P/E = $3.60-4.50. Customer loss of 15%-of-revenue private E&P would accelerate this.

**Base Case ($5.50-6.50):**
Oil remains elevated but E&P capital discipline holds, RPC's organic revenue stabilizes but doesn't grow, Pintail contributes modestly to diversification, margins remain compressed at 11-13% EBITDA level. At $0.20-0.25 EPS and 25-28x P/E = $5.00-7.00. This aligns with the consensus analyst target ($6.54 mean).

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Continued estimate deterioration visible in Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August 2026). If pressure pumping pricing continues declining and Pintail margins disappoint, a miss on $0.04 current quarter estimate is plausible. Secondary catalyst: oil price decline on any geopolitical diplomatic resolution would immediately compress the premium embedded in the stock.

**Timing window:** 60-90 days. Next earnings report is the primary catalyst. Oil price direction provides the exogenous variable.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** SI at 26.9% of float with 4.6 days to cover is extremely elevated. This is the primary risk for shorts. Any positive oil headline or earnings beat would trigger violent covering. HIGH-RISK short mechanically despite the fundamental case.

**Borrow and options signal:** IV at 53.5% suggests moderate-to-elevated borrowing cost environment. The options market is not pricing extreme directional conviction in either direction post-earnings compression.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case target $5.00-5.50 represents 23-30% downside. Upside risk to $8.50 (squeeze + oil spike) represents 19% upside from current. Risk/reward is approximately 1.3:1 to 1.6:1 favorable but below the 2:1 threshold given squeeze risk.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE — The 26.9% short interest creates unacceptable squeeze risk relative to the 1.3-1.6:1 reward/risk ratio. The fundamental bear case is valid, but the trade is too crowded to initiate new short positions. Avoid owning; do not actively short.**

## Conclusion

RPC Inc is an oilfield services company with a strong balance sheet trading at an extreme earnings multiple during a period of negative estimate revisions, structural industry headwinds, and competitive positioning gaps. The stock has rallied 50% from its lows on oil geopolitical tailwinds, but this price appreciation is disconnected from deteriorating fundamentals. The company trades above its consensus analyst target. Management's refusal to deploy the $210M cash balance on buybacks at these levels — despite a 12.8M share authorization — signals internal uncertainty about the forward trajectory.

The Pintail acquisition provides useful diversification, and the balance sheet ensures survival through deep cycles. However, survival is already priced at $7.12 for a $0.20 EPS business. Without a specific catalyst for earnings recovery — which would require either material oil price increase, pressure pumping pricing recovery (management says pricing inadequate to reactivate fleets), or dramatic technology deployment — the current valuation offers asymmetric downside risk. AVOID.
