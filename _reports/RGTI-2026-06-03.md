**Research Date: June 3, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Rigetti Computing is a full-stack superconducting quantum computing company valued at roughly $7.7 billion against trailing-twelve-month revenue of about $7 million. That implies a price-to-sales multiple near 1,000x. The valuation is a forward bet on technology breakthroughs that management itself says may not arrive "as quickly as hoped, or even at all." Revenue declined for three consecutive years (~$13.1M in 2022 to $7.1M in 2025) before a Q1 2026 uptick to $4.4M that came almost entirely from one-time hardware sales (Novera units and an India C-DAC order), not recurring or scalable revenue. The operating loss was $26.0M in Q1 2026, and the company carries an accumulated deficit of $737.8M.

The reported Q1 2026 GAAP net income of $33.1M is misleading. It reflects a $53.7M non-cash gain from remeasuring derivative warrant liabilities, which occurs mechanically when the stock falls. The same accounting drove most of FY2025's $216.2M net loss, when the stock rose. GAAP net income for Rigetti functions as an inverse proxy for its own share price rather than a measure of operating performance. The genuine financial story is a roughly $569M equity-funded cash and Treasuries cushion against an annualized operating cash burn near $65M, providing two to three years of runway before the company's own $250M Quanta commitment and fab capex are layered in.

The stock has appreciated about 119% over the past year and surged roughly 50% in late May 2026 on a Letter of Intent with the U.S. Department of Commerce for up to $100M over three years. That LOI is non-binding and represents contingent, not committed, funding, a material attribution point given how much of the rally rests on it. Combined with uniform insider selling (zero open-market buys), embedded structural dilution, a roadmap reset four times since 2018, and falling forward earnings estimates, the risk/reward is unfavorable. We rate RGTI AVOID.

## Company Overview

Rigetti designs and builds superconducting quantum processing units (QPUs) and complete quantum computers, sold under the Novera (9-qubit) and Cepheus (up to 108-qubit) brands, plus cloud access via Rigetti Quantum Cloud Services and third-party distribution through AWS Braket, Microsoft Azure Quantum, and qBraid. The company went public via a March 2022 SPAC merger and is headquartered in Berkeley, California, with operations across the US, UK, Australia, Canada, and India.

A genuine structural differentiator is vertical integration: Rigetti owns Fab-1, an in-house wafer fab, and pursues a modular multi-chip (chiplet) architecture, with its Cepheus-1-108Q comprising twelve interconnected 9-qubit chiplets. Management argues this is the fastest, lowest-risk path to scaling versus monolithic designs. Owning a fab is also capital-intensive and a recurring claim on cash.

The business model is explicitly aspirational. Management states the company is "still in the technology development phase" and that "our scalable business model has not been formed as of yet." Revenue today is dominated by government and a handful of large development contracts. This is a research company with a public market quotation, not a commercial enterprise with an emerging earnings stream.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue.** Q1 2026 revenue was $4.4M versus $1.47M in Q1 2025 (+199%), but $3.0M of that was point-in-time hardware sales of 9-qubit Novera systems. Management cautions that revenue will remain highly lumpy and variable. Remaining performance obligation backlog is roughly $2.8M, which is tiny relative to the market capitalization.

**Profitability.** The underlying operating loss of $26.0M widened from $21.6M in Q1 2025. Q1 operating expenses were R&D $20.0M plus SG&A $7.4M (total $27.3M) against $4.4M of revenue; gross profit was just $1.4M. The reported GAAP net income is entirely a warrant remeasurement gain (the stock fell from $22.15 to $14.04 during the quarter). The directional relationship is mechanical: warrant gains in down-stock quarters, warrant losses in up-stock quarters. Treat the headline net income figure as noise.

**Balance sheet.** Liquidity is the real strength: $48.1M cash plus $520.8M in US Treasuries against minimal debt. This is why distress screens read "safe" — it reflects an equity-raised war chest, not operating health. Total equity of $583.6M is almost entirely funded by stock issuance (additional paid-in capital $1.32B).

**Cash burn and runway.** Operating cash outflow was about $16.2M in Q1 2026 (~$65M annualized). The company has separately committed at least $250M over five years under the Quanta collaboration, plus planned fab capex, both of which compress the effective runway. The market may be under-appreciating that the $250M Quanta figure is Rigetti's own future cash obligation.

**Dilution.** Shares outstanding grew from ~184M (2022) to 332.4M (May 2026). A $350M ATM offering ran in 2025, and a new ~$337M shelf was filed in May 2026 (the stock fell ~8% on the news). The 2022 equity plan auto-increases by 5% of fully diluted shares annually, adding 17.8M shares on January 1, 2026 alone. An additional ~25.9M shares are reserved for warrants, RSUs, and options. Public warrants ($11.50 strike) expire March 2, 2027 and are redeemable above $18, adding further share supply. Management has stated that future funding will likely come through more dilutive equity. Dilution here is structural, not incidental.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case is entirely forward-looking and contingent. Near-term catalysts include the Cepheus-1-108Q general availability (April 2026; 108 qubits, 99.1% median two-qubit gate fidelity), the $8.4M C-DAC India order (delivery expected 2H 2026), the Quanta manufacturing collaboration, and the contingent Commerce LOI. Each is real, but none translates into recurring, scalable revenue today.

Estimate revisions are negative across every horizon: current-year EPS estimates have been revised down 22.5% over 90 days, and next-year estimates down 30.3% over 90 days. This is the opposite of the rising-estimate trajectory that would justify a forward-earnings re-rating. The consensus still models persistent losses ($-0.18 current year, $-0.20 next year). The Q1 revenue spike, while large in percentage terms, is off a tiny base and explicitly one-time in nature.

The structural quantum-computing opportunity is genuine and well-capitalized across the industry, but the timeline to commercial quantum advantage is uncertain and the company has reset its technology roadmap in 2018, 2022, 2023, and 2025, with another update promised "later in 2026." A serial pattern of milestone resets undercuts confidence in any specific growth trajectory.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$26.88 and roughly 332.4M shares, the market capitalization is approximately $7.7B against ~$7M trailing revenue — about 1,000x sales. There is no earnings to anchor a P/E, and forward EPS is negative ($-0.18 to $-0.20 consensus). The trailing/forward P/E of roughly -31x is a function of warrant accounting and is not interpretable.

The cycle-adjusted valuation framework that rescues many high-multiple stocks does not apply here. Cycle-adjustment is appropriate when earnings are recovering from a trough and estimates are rising; Rigetti's estimates are falling, revenue declined for three years before a one-time bump, and there is no normalized earnings power to discount because the business model "has not been formed." This is a pre-revenue research enterprise, not a trough-earnings story.

Peers offer little comfort. The pure-play public quantum cohort (IonQ, D-Wave) all trade at extreme multiples on steep losses; IonQ leads on revenue and backlog while Rigetti and D-Wave chase. Benchmarking one richly-priced pre-commercial name against another establishes only that the entire group is priced on sentiment and optionality rather than fundamentals.

Analyst price targets (low $15.91, mean $29.24, median $30.00, high $40.00) bracket the current price, but these targets should not be read as floors. The "targets are floors" principle applies to cheap, rising-estimate stocks; here estimates are falling and the multiple is far beyond any growth-adjusted anchor. The bear analyst's $15.91 target implies roughly 41% downside from current levels. We assign a low valuation score (2.0). The price embeds a successful technology outcome that is years away and uncertain.

## Competitive Landscape

Rigetti is one of three US pure-play public quantum names alongside IonQ and D-Wave. Its superconducting modality (shared by IBM and Google) competes on gate speed and a modular chiplet architecture for scaling, against IonQ's trapped ions and D-Wave's annealing. Vertical integration through Fab-1 is a legitimate structural differentiator, though capital-intensive.

The competitive trajectory is stable-to-uncertain rather than clearly improving. The 108-qubit milestone and Quanta partnership are credible technical progress, and Quanta's $35M equity stake (3.02M shares at ~$11.59) with a three-year lockup signals partner conviction. Against this, IBM, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and well-funded private players such as PsiQuantum operate with vastly larger R&D budgets. The risk that a larger competitor reaches practical quantum advantage first is existential to Rigetti's thesis. Customer concentration is severe — Q1 2026 saw Customer A at 54%, Customer B at 15%, and Customer D at 12% of revenue, with 69% of revenue from "Asia and other" (the India order). Loss of any single contract materially swings results. We score competitive position at a neutral 5.0: genuine differentiation, but a precarious position against far larger rivals and concentrated demand.

## Risk Assessment

The risk profile is heavily weighted to the downside and is not fully priced given the elevated multiple.

- **No proven commercial model.** Management states success "ultimately relies upon fundamental research and development breakthroughs in the coming years" with "no certainty these…will be achieved as quickly as hoped, or even at all."
- **Serial roadmap resets.** The Q1 2026 10-Q modified its risk factor to disclose roadmap changes in 2018, 2022, 2023, and 2025, with another update promised in 2026 — a material credibility risk.
- **Funding dependence.** Profitability is years out; the company must repeatedly raise capital, and failure to do so on attractive terms would force it to "delay, reduce or eliminate" R&D.
- **Structural dilution** via ATM, the new shelf, the 5%-auto-increasing plan, and warrant supply.
- **Customer and geographic concentration** as noted above.
- **Non-cash earnings volatility** from warrant remeasurement, which disconnects GAAP results from operations and can mislead screens.
- **The $100M Commerce "grant" is a non-binding Letter of Intent**, not funded cash. A roughly 50% rally was built partly on a contingent commitment.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing imminent, sizable movement. Near-term ATM implied volatility is 160.6% against one-year historical volatility of 104.2% — rich by 56.4 points and in the 91st percentile of the current chain. Term structure is in backwardation (1-week 160.6% vs. 6-month 110.2%), which signals the market expects a near-term catalyst or stress event. Positioning is call-heavy: put/call skew is -9.9%, put/call volume ratio is 0.14, and unusual activity is concentrated in out-of-the-money June calls ($30, $27, $26 strikes at 3–4x volume/OI). This is consistent with speculative upside chasing rather than informed bearish hedging, indicating the tape is momentum-driven and can squeeze violently in either direction. The implied ±9.3% near-term move on a 1-week tenor underscores how unstable the price is.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** Quantum computing is a genuine, multi-decade structural opportunity. If Rigetti's chiplet architecture and 108-qubit progress translate into demonstrable quantum advantage, the Quanta manufacturing partnership and government funding (including the $100M LOI if it converts) could fund a path to commercialization. With ~$569M of liquidity, the company can sustain R&D for years. In a sentiment-driven sector, headline catalysts can drive the stock far above fundamentals, as the 2025 range to $58.15 demonstrated. Bull target ~$40 (the high analyst target, +49%).

**Bear case.** Revenue declined for three years and the Q1 bump is one-time. Operating losses are widening, estimates are falling, the roadmap has reset four times, dilution is structural, insiders are uniformly selling, and the $100M "grant" is non-binding. At ~1,000x sales with no business model formed, the multiple embeds a successful outcome that is years away and uncertain. Bear target ~$15.91 (the low analyst target, -41%), with further downside on any roadmap disappointment, failed funding round, or sector sentiment reversal.

The asymmetry favors the bear case at the current price because the bull case requires fundamental breakthroughs on an uncertain timeline, while the bear case rests on present, observable facts.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst.** There is no single dated, high-probability negative catalyst. The roadmap update promised "later in 2026" could disappoint, and any further dilutive raise off the ~$337M shelf would pressure the stock, but neither is precisely dated. The next earnings report and any conversion (or non-conversion) of the Commerce LOI are the most likely catalysts, both with uncertain timing.

**Timing window.** Open-ended. The fundamental overvaluation could persist for quarters in a momentum-driven group. Carry costs and squeeze risk are meaningful relative to any near-term resolution.

**Short interest and squeeze risk.** Short interest is 14.9% of float with only 1.5 days to cover — elevated but not extreme, and the low days-to-cover means a sharp upward move could be amplified quickly. The stock has demonstrated repeated 40–120% swings; the May surge on the LOI is a recent example. Squeeze risk is high.

**Borrow and options signal.** Call-heavy positioning with unusual OTM call buying contradicts a clean bearish setup — the market is leaning long via cheap upside calls, exactly the configuration that punishes shorts during sentiment rallies. Near-term IV makes options-based short expressions expensive.

**Downside target and risk/reward.** Fundamental downside toward the $15.91 low target implies roughly 41% downside. But upside risk in a squeeze or headline rally is comparably large (the stock rose ~119% over the year and ~50% in days on the LOI). Risk/reward is roughly symmetric, below the 2:1 threshold needed for an active short.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The fundamental thesis is correct, but the absence of a dated catalyst, low days-to-cover, call-heavy positioning, expensive borrow/IV, and demonstrated squeeze history make this a name to avoid owning rather than to short. The bearish view is best expressed by staying out.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

This is an AVOID. We are not buyers at current levels and do not recommend an active short.

**Re-entry criteria (what would change our view):**
- Conversion of the Commerce LOI into a signed, funded award, alongside additional binding multi-year contracts that establish a recurring revenue base rather than lumpy one-time hardware sales.
- A roadmap update that is met and sustained (no further reset within 12–18 months), demonstrating credible milestone discipline after four prior resets.
- Evidence of a forming commercial model: positive estimate revisions, backlog growth materially above the current ~$2.8M, and a path to narrowing operating losses.
- A valuation reset to a level where price reflects achievable near-term fundamentals rather than ~1,000x sales — or insider open-market buying that signals management views the stock as undervalued.

**Timeline.** The headwinds — pre-commercial model, structural dilution, uncertain breakthrough timeline — are likely to persist for multiple years. We would revisit at the next earnings report and on any concrete conversion of the government funding or a new binding commercial contract.

## Conclusion

Rigetti is a high-quality research effort in a real long-term opportunity, wrapped in a valuation that prices a successful outcome that has not yet materialized and may not for years. Revenue fell for three straight years before a one-time Q1 bump, operating losses are widening, forward estimates are falling, the technology roadmap has been reset four times, dilution is structurally embedded, and insiders are selling uniformly with no open-market buying. The reported GAAP "profit" is a warrant-accounting artifact, and the much-publicized $100M government funding is a non-binding Letter of Intent. At roughly 1,000x sales, the price offers no margin of safety against execution, funding, or sentiment risk.

We rate RGTI **AVOID**. The stock is too sentiment-driven and squeeze-prone to short, and too speculative and overvalued to own. Investors seeking quantum exposure should wait for evidence of a forming commercial model, a met-and-sustained roadmap, and a valuation that reflects achievable near-term fundamentals.
