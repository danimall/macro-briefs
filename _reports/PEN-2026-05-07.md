## Executive Summary

Penumbra Inc is a $12.8B medical device company focused on thrombectomy and embolization products, currently subject to a definitive acquisition agreement with Boston Scientific at $374/share (~73% cash, ~27% BSX stock). At $325.99, the stock trades at a 14.7% discount to the cash-election deal price. A special meeting for shareholder approval was scheduled for May 6, 2026; investors should confirm vote results via the company's 8-K filing before treating this condition as satisfied. Regulatory approval — specifically antitrust clearance given BSX's acknowledged competitive overlap and an outstanding FTC Second Request issued on March 16, 2026 — remains the key remaining uncertainty, with the deal expected to close by year-end 2026.

The investment thesis is primarily a merger arbitrage opportunity with asymmetric downside protection. If the deal closes as expected, the annualized return on the spread is approximately 20-25% depending on timing. If the deal breaks, Penumbra's standalone fundamentals — 15-17% revenue growth, 67%+ gross margins, accelerating operating leverage, $545M cash, minimal debt — suggest downside is limited to the mid-$250s to $280 range, implying 14-20% downside versus 14.7% upside to the cash-election price. The actual value of the stock component (~27% of consideration) will fluctuate with BSX's share price, meaning the effective deal value is not fixed at $374 for all shareholders. The risk/reward favors ownership for investors comfortable with the regulatory uncertainty.

The underlying business is performing at its best level in company history: Q1 2026 revenue of $374.8M (+15.6% per the company's May 6 earnings release), FY2025 operating margins of 13.5% (up from an adjusted ~10% in FY2024 after excluding the $115M immersive healthcare write-off), and $175M in free cash flow. Four consecutive earnings beats (per consensus tracking services) reinforce execution quality.

## Company Overview

Penumbra develops and markets medical devices for mechanical thrombectomy (removal of blood clots) and vascular embolization. Founded in 2004 and headquartered in Alameda, California, the company reports as a single operating segment with two principal product categories:

- **Thrombectomy** (67.5% of FY2025 revenue, $948M): Computer Assisted Vacuum Thrombectomy (CAVT) systems including the Indigo System (peripheral) and Penumbra System (neuro). Recent product launches include Lightning Flash 3.0 and Lightning Bolt 12.
- **Embolization & Access** (32.5%, $456M): Ruby Embolization Platform, coil systems, and access catheters. Growing faster than the core thrombectomy category.

The company maintains a direct salesforce covering 90% of revenue, manufactures primarily in California (with a Costa Rica facility under construction), and holds CAVT patents extending through 2039-2044. Revenue is 77.8% U.S.-based.

## Growth Analysis

Penumbra's growth is underpinned by low procedure penetration rates in a large addressable market. The company estimates ~2.15 million annual U.S. clot incidences where mechanical thrombectomy is clinically appropriate — versus current treatment volumes that leave the vast majority untreated. At ~$948M in thrombectomy revenue, there is substantial headroom for continued mid-teens growth even without new product categories.

Growth drivers include:
1. **New product launches** (Lightning Bolt 12, TraX technology) expanding the treatable anatomy
2. **Embolization & Access acceleration** (+23.8% in Q1 2026) from Ruby XL and SwiftSET launches
3. **THUNDER IDE study** completion (enrolled September 2024) — if positive, opens neurovascular PMA pathway
4. **Costa Rica manufacturing** — once operational, reduces cost structure and diversifies geographic risk

The estimate revision trend is modestly negative (current year EPS down -0.9% over 90 days, from $5.05 to $5.00), but this is meaningless in the context of a pending acquisition where the company is not providing guidance or hosting conference calls. Consensus estimates reflect deal economics, not standalone operating expectations.

## Competitive Landscape

Penumbra holds the market-leading position in vacuum-based mechanical thrombectomy, differentiated by its proprietary CAVT technology platform. The competitive landscape is consolidating rapidly:

- **Stryker/Inari Medical** ($4.9B acquisition, January 2025): The most direct competitor in peripheral thrombectomy (PE, DVT). Inari's FlowTriever and ClotTriever compete directly with Penumbra's Indigo System. Stryker's distribution scale significantly enhances Inari's competitive position.
- **Medtronic**: Competes across neuro and peripheral vascular, with aspiration and stent retriever technologies.
- **Terumo**: Smaller competitor primarily in embolization.

Boston Scientific is paying 10x revenue for the thrombectomy market leader. Penumbra's advantages include: (a) broadest product portfolio spanning both peripheral and neuro thrombectomy, (b) CAVT software-driven approach that enables faster product iteration, (c) direct salesforce with deep physician relationships, (d) patents through 2039-2044.

The competitive risk is real but already capitalized into the deal price. Post-acquisition under BSX, Penumbra's products would benefit from BSX's global distribution infrastructure and hospital relationships.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Regulatory/Antitrust Risk (Primary — Elevated by FTC Second Request):** Boston Scientific is explicitly named as a competitor in Penumbra's 10-K, particularly in embolization products and certain peripheral intervention categories. On March 16, 2026, both BSX and Penumbra received an FTC Second Request for additional information, indicating the agency is conducting an in-depth review of the transaction. Both companies are in the process of responding. A Second Request extends the regulatory timeline and increases the probability of either (a) required divestitures of overlapping product lines, or (b) in a worst case, an FTC challenge to block the transaction.

**2. Deal Break Downside:** If the acquisition fails, the stock likely trades to $250-$280 based on standalone fundamentals. However, the business would retain its growth trajectory, and alternative acquirers (J&J, Abbott, Medtronic) would likely emerge given the Stryker/Inari precedent.

**3. Timeline Extension:** The FTC Second Request process could extend regulatory review into 2027, reducing the annualized return on the spread. Each additional quarter adds opportunity cost.

**4. BSX Stock Component:** Approximately 27% of the consideration is BSX stock (3.8721 shares per PEN share for the stock election, subject to proration). BSX price depreciation between now and close directly reduces deal value for shareholders receiving stock consideration. BSX currently trades at ~$100/share; a 15% decline would reduce the effective blended deal price by approximately $15/share.

**5. Termination Fee Structure:** The $525M termination fee is payable by **Penumbra to BSX** if the deal terminates under certain specified circumstances (e.g., Penumbra accepts a superior proposal). This fee discourages Penumbra from walking away or accepting a competing bid, but it does **not** function as a reverse break fee that would penalize BSX for failure to obtain regulatory clearance. If the FTC blocks the deal, BSX may not owe Penumbra a comparable fee — investors should review the specific termination provisions in the merger agreement.

**6. Estimate Revisions Modestly Negative:** Current quarter EPS revised down -5.7% over 30 days and -6.5% over 90 days. This reflects the absence of guidance during the merger period rather than fundamental deterioration.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($374, +14.7% to cash-election price):** Deal closes on schedule in H2 2026. FTC clears the transaction with manageable divestitures (if any). BSX stock remains stable or appreciates, delivering full blended value or better. Annualized return of 22-30%.

**Bear Case ($250-280, -14% to -23%):** Deal is blocked by the FTC or abandoned. Penumbra reverts to standalone valuation at peer-appropriate multiples. 15%+ revenue growth, 67%+ gross margins, and $545M cash provide a fundamental floor well above pre-announcement trading levels (~$260-$315 in late 2025).

**Base Case ($374, +14.7% to cash election):** Assessed as the most probable outcome, though the FTC Second Request introduces meaningful uncertainty. BSX has strong strategic rationale. The Penumbra termination fee discourages alternative paths, and both parties appear committed to closing. Probability-weighted expected value: ~0.70 × $374 + 0.30 × $265 = ~$341 (4.7% above current), before accounting for BSX stock-component variability, transaction costs, and borrow costs.

## Conclusion

Penumbra represents a merger arbitrage opportunity with favorable but qualified risk/reward characteristics. The 14.7% spread to the $374 cash-election deal price provides attractive compensation for regulatory uncertainty. Shareholder approval was scheduled for May 6, 2026 (investors should confirm passage via 8-K). The outstanding FTC Second Request (issued March 16, 2026) indicates heightened regulatory scrutiny and introduces meaningful uncertainty around both timing and close probability.

**Rationale for BUY over HOLD:** The risk/reward is asymmetric and time-bounded. In the base case (estimated 70% probability), the stock reaches $374 (cash election) within 6-8 months — a 14.7% return with a defined endpoint. In the bear case (30% probability), downside to ~$265 represents the mid-point of standalone valuation supported by exceptional fundamentals. The probability-weighted expected value of ~$341 exceeds current price by ~5%, though this modest margin is before transaction costs, borrow costs, and stock-consideration variability. The combination of (a) scheduled shareholder approval, (b) strong strategic rationale for BSX, (c) Penumbra termination fee discouraging alternative paths, and (d) continued strong operating execution supports owning the spread for investors with appropriate risk tolerance for regulatory uncertainty.

This is a time-limited position that resolves upon deal close or regulatory denial, expected within 6-8 months.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The FTC Second Request (omitted from initial report), unverified shareholder vote passage, stock-consideration variability, corrected termination fee direction (Penumbra pays BSX, not reverse), and modest probability-weighted expected value (~4.7% before costs) collectively suggest the BUY recommendation should be treated as speculative/qualified, contingent on confirmed shareholder approval and explicit modeling of FTC remedy/timing risk.
