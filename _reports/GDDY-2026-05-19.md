## Executive Summary

GoDaddy trades at $90.71, down 52% from its 52-week high of $190.50, following a deliberate promotional pricing strategy that compressed near-term bookings and revenue growth guidance to 5-6% for FY2026. The stock now trades at approximately 14.5x forward earnings against $1.6B in annual free cash flow (31.8% FCF margin), a sharp discount to the Information Technology peer median P/E of 73.8x and even absolute value benchmarks for recurring-revenue businesses.

The core tension: GoDaddy possesses an exceptionally cash-generative subscription model (85% retention, $242 ARPU growing double-digits, 10.5x interest coverage), yet management allocated $767M in accelerated share repurchases at $176/share — nearly double the current price — while simultaneously implementing a promotional pricing shift that telegraphed the very deceleration that cratered the stock. Multiple securities fraud investigations add governance overhang.

The stock offers asymmetric upside if the promotional .com strategy drives customer LTV expansion, but the burden of proof lies with management to demonstrate this outcome over the next 2-3 quarters. With estimates modestly rising (+2.2% current year over 30 days) but the AI disruption thesis unresolved and the capital allocation track record impaired, the appropriate stance is to hold existing positions while monitoring for clearer evidence of either fundamental stabilization or further deterioration.

## Company Overview

GoDaddy is the world's largest domain registrar and a comprehensive small-business online presence platform serving approximately 20.4 million customers with ~80.8 million domains under management. Revenue is split between Core Platform (61.8% of FY2025 revenue, domains/hosting/security, growing 4.9%) and Applications & Commerce (38.2%, website builders/commerce/payments, growing 14.3%).

The company is in a strategic transition from a domain registration utility to an AI-powered small business platform. GoDaddy Airo, the company's agentic AI product, handles domain search, logo creation, and website building through natural language prompts. Recent expansion to WordPress represents the latest iteration. Simultaneously, GoDaddy is migrating from owned data centers to AWS, reducing capital intensity and depreciation.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue & Profitability

FY2025 revenue reached $4.95B (+8.3% YoY), accelerating from 7.5% in FY2024 and 4.0% in FY2023. The growth acceleration was driven by A&C segment momentum (+14.3%) and ARPU expansion from $220 to $242. However, FY2026 guidance of $5.195B–$5.275B implies a deceleration to 5-6%, directly attributable to the promotional .com pricing strategy reducing upfront bookings recognition.

Operating margins expanded dramatically: 12.9% (FY2023) → 19.5% (FY2024) → 22.8% (FY2025). NEBITDA margins reached 32.0%. This margin expansion reflects the AWS migration reducing infrastructure costs, operating leverage on customer care (-140bps), technology/development (-270bps over two years), and restructuring normalization.

### Cash Flow Quality

Free cash flow of $1.576B on $4.95B revenue (31.8% margin) with only $24M in capex represents best-in-class capital efficiency. Operating cash flow grew 24% YoY despite the bookings pressure. The subscription model's prepaid collection dynamics create a natural cash buffer — revenue may decelerate but cash generation remains insulated over near-term horizons.

Interest coverage stands at 10.5x ($1.6B OCF / $151M interest expense). While gross debt of $3.77B against $215M equity appears alarming (37.4x D/E), this reflects intentional financial engineering: aggressive buybacks have consumed cumulative retained earnings (now -$2.79B). Net leverage of ~2.4x NEBITDA is moderate for this quality of cash flow.

### Capital Allocation — The Critical Failure

Management executed $767M in accelerated share repurchases at a weighted-average $176/share in early 2025. The stock trades at $90.71. This represents approximately $335M in realized value destruction — the company paid roughly 2x current market price for its own shares just 12-15 months ago. Total FY2025 buybacks reached $835M.

Shares outstanding declined from 159M (2022) to 138M (2025), a 13% reduction. The buyback program has been aggressive and effective in reducing share count, but the timing of the 2025 ASR — executed before or concurrently with the decision to implement promotional pricing that would predictably compress growth — raises serious questions about capital allocation discipline and information flow within the organization.

## Growth Analysis

### Near-Term Deceleration (FY2026)

Guided revenue of $5.195B–$5.275B represents 5-6% growth, down from 8.3%. The promotional .com pricing is the proximate cause: offering 1-year domain terms at promotional rates shifts revenue recognition and compresses average bookings per customer in the near term. Management frames this as an investment in customer acquisition with expected LTV payoff over 2-3 years as promotional customers renew at full price and adopt additional products.

### Medium-Term Catalysts

- **ARPU Expansion:** $203 → $242 in two years (19% growth) demonstrates pricing power over the existing base. If promotional customers follow historical attach patterns, the strategy could prove accretive by FY2027-2028.
- **A&C Mix Shift:** From 33.6% to 38.2% of revenue in two years. Higher-margin, stickier revenue. If this mix reaches 45%+ by FY2028, blended growth could reaccelerate.
- **Airo AI Platform:** The WordPress expansion and agentic AI capabilities could deepen engagement. However, competitive validation is absent — Wix, Squarespace, and new AI-native builders are pursuing identical strategies.

### Structural Headwinds

- Customer count declining (21.0M → 20.4M), partially offset by ARPU but signaling saturation in the addressable market.
- Core Platform growth of 4.9% is near GDP-level, and domain registration is a mature category.
- AI assistants could reduce demand for traditional web presence over a 3-5 year horizon — if businesses are discovered through AI rather than web search, domain ownership becomes less critical.

Estimate revisions are modestly positive: current year EPS moved from $6.96 to $7.12 over 30 days (+2.2%), and next year from $8.83 to $8.98 (+1.8%). These do not constitute the strong revision velocity that would indicate an earnings inflection. Growth is stabilizing, not accelerating.

## Valuation Assessment

At $90.71 with $7.12 in estimated FY2026 EPS, GDDY trades at 12.7x current-year earnings and approximately 10.1x next-year earnings ($8.98). The forward P/E reported at 14.5x appears to use trailing EPS of $6.22 rather than forward estimates.

**Peer comparison:** The peer median P/E is 73.8x. Even the lowest-valued IT peer in the comparable set (Zebra at 31.5x) trades at more than double GDDY's multiple. This discount is extreme and reflects the market pricing either (a) meaningful growth deceleration beyond current guidance, (b) structural AI disruption risk, or (c) governance/capital allocation concerns.

**FCF yield:** At $12B market cap and $1.576B FCF, the stock offers a 13.1% FCF yield. For a subscription business with 85% retention and growing ARPU, this is objectively cheap if cash flows are sustainable.

**Buyback math:** The $2.165B remaining repurchase authorization represents 18% of current market cap. At current prices, this would retire approximately 24M shares (18% of current outstanding), versus the same authorization at $176 would have retired only 12M shares.

**Why might the market be right to discount?** The 52% drawdown from the high demands an explanation beyond "promotional pricing." The market may be pricing: (1) a permanent growth rate reset to 5-6% rather than a temporary dip; (2) increasing probability that AI disrupts domain/website demand over 3-5 years; (3) management credibility impairment after the ASR timing failure; (4) the securities investigations creating potential settlement liability.

**Why the market could be wrong:** $1.6B in durable FCF on a recurring-revenue base with demonstrated pricing power suggests a floor valuation of ~$12B ($9B enterprise value at 6x EV/EBITDA is conservative for this quality). Current EV of ~$15.8B ($12B market cap + $3.8B debt) implies 10x NEBITDA — not distressed, but below SaaS/internet averages of 15-25x. If growth reaccelerates to 8%+ by FY2027 as promotional customers renew, the stock re-rates.

## Competitive Landscape

GoDaddy's moat rests on three pillars: (1) brand recognition and trust among small businesses, (2) an 80.8M domain portfolio creating switching costs, and (3) operational scale enabling low-cost domain registration paired with cross-sell into higher-value products.

**Threats are intensifying:**
- Wix and Squarespace have deployed competitive AI website builders with arguably superior design capabilities.
- Shopify dominates commerce for growth-stage businesses, limiting GoDaddy's addressable commerce TAM.
- New AI-native builders (from startups to foundation model companies) could bypass the traditional domain → hosting → website funnel entirely.
- VeriSign's ongoing .com price increases ($10.26/year and rising) compress margins on the domain registration product.

**GoDaddy's counter:** The 20.4M existing customer base provides distribution for new products. The 89% revenue from returning customers creates predictability. The ARPU trajectory demonstrates successful cross-sell execution. Whether this existing-customer monetization can offset potential new-customer acquisition headwinds is the central strategic question.

Competitive position is stable but not improving. The company is defending share rather than gaining it, with customer count declining and growth dependent on extracting more value per customer rather than expanding the base.

## Risk Assessment

### Quantified Risks

1. **Securities fraud investigations (near-term overhang):** Multiple law firms investigating potential claims related to the Q4 2025 disclosure. Settlement probability is moderate; typical resolution involves $10-50M payment and consent decree for a company of this size. The stock likely won't re-rate until these resolve or are dismissed.

2. **AWS single-vendor dependency:** GoDaddy's migration from owned infrastructure to AWS creates operational concentration risk. AWS can terminate upon notice and upon GoDaddy's failure to cure a breach within 45 days. While AWS termination is a low-probability event, the company has deliberately eliminated its backup infrastructure.

3. **Growth deceleration permanence:** If the promotional .com strategy fails to generate LTV payoff (customers churn at promotional renewal, don't cross-sell), the 5-6% growth guide could represent the new normal rather than a temporary dip.

4. **AI-driven demand erosion:** If 20% of current domain demand shifts to AI platforms by 2030, that represents approximately $600M in revenue at risk. This is unquantifiable with precision but real.

5. **Interest rate exposure:** With floating-rate debt and rates elevated, refinancing costs on future maturities will be higher than origination rates.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 54.2% vs. 36.3% historical volatility indicates the options market prices 17.9pp of excess uncertainty. The backwardation structure (54.2% near-term vs. 44.0% 3-month) suggests positioning for imminent resolution of uncertainty, potentially the securities investigations or an upcoming catalyst.

The put/call OI ratio rose from 0.31 to 0.79 over the past month while the stock rallied 10.7% — increasing put demand during a rally indicates informed hedging, not broad capitulation. Near-term put/call volume ratio of 2.28 at the 1-week expiry confirms elevated near-term concern despite the recent bounce.

Short interest at 6.0% with 2.9 days to cover is moderate — not crowded enough to create squeeze dynamics, but above average for a large-cap IT name, confirming skepticism among sophisticated participants.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (~$130-140, +43-54%)

- Promotional .com pricing drives customer acquisition that converts to full-price renewals by Q3 2026, revenue growth reaccelerates to 8%+
- Airo AI platform achieves meaningful product differentiation, improving A&C attach rates
- Buybacks at current prices ($90) are dramatically more accretive than at $176; remaining $2.2B authorization retires 15-20% of shares by end of 2027
- Securities investigations are dismissed without material liability
- FCF grows to $1.8-2.0B by FY2027, stock re-rates to 15-18x earnings ($135-160)

### Bear Case (~$65-70, -24-29%)

- Promotional customers churn at renewal, validating market's permanent growth deceleration pricing
- AI disruption accelerates — domain relevance declines measurably within 24 months
- Securities settlements drain $50M+ and distract management for 12+ months
- Interest rates remain elevated, limiting multiple expansion and increasing refinancing costs
- Growth decelerates further to 3-4%, market assigns utility-like multiple of 10-12x forward earnings ($71-108)

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Q2 or Q3 2026 bookings growth demonstrates measurable improvement from the promotional pricing investments — specifically, new customer cohort attach rates exceeding 30% within 12 months of acquisition
2. Stock trades below $80 on no new fundamental deterioration (would represent <9x forward earnings on $8.98 NTM EPS)
3. Securities investigations formally dismissed or settled at immaterial amounts (<$20M)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2026 revenue guidance reduced below $5.1B (would signal promotional strategy failure)
2. Customer count declines below 19.5M without offsetting ARPU acceleration (above $260)
3. FCF margin contracts below 25% due to competitive investment requirements or settlement costs

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August 2026) will be the first quarter to show whether promotional pricing is generating acquisition volume that converts. The May 2026 Airo WordPress launch needs 6+ months to demonstrate impact.

## Conclusion

GoDaddy's financial fundamentals — 31.8% FCF margin, 85% retention, growing ARPU, manageable leverage — support a business worth meaningfully more than the current $12B market cap. At 12.7x current-year earnings, the stock is cheap relative to both its own quality metrics and its peer group.

However, management credibility is impaired by the $767M ASR at nearly double the current price, executed proximate to a strategic decision that predictably compressed growth. The promotional pricing strategy's outcome is genuinely uncertain — it could prove either far-sighted or value-destructive, and the 2-3 quarter lag before evidence emerges makes a BUY recommendation premature. The securities fraud investigations, while likely resolving without material liability, prevent institutional capital from sizing positions aggressively.

The correct framework: hold existing positions, size any new entry conservatively, and reassess after Q2 2026 earnings provide the first clean read on whether the customer acquisition strategy is working. The 13.1% FCF yield provides margin of safety, but insufficient evidence exists today to determine whether the 52% drawdown represents opportunity or appropriate repricing of a structurally challenged business.
