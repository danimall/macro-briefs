## Executive Summary

Fox Corporation is a structurally sound media business with dominant positions in cable news and live sports broadcasting, trading at 13.4x forward earnings with an extraordinary cash position ($5.4B, representing 43% of market capitalization). The company delivered a record FY2025 driven by the Super Bowl, political advertising, and Tubi growth, but faces a well-telegraphed revenue cliff in FY2026 as both the Super Bowl and political cycle rotate away — a pattern that historically strips $1-1.5B from top-line revenue.

The investment case rests on three factors: (1) whether the FOX One DTC launch can structurally offset accelerating cord-cutting (9% annual MVPD subscriber erosion), (2) whether Tubi's growth trajectory can sustain advertising revenue growth in an off-cycle year, and (3) the embedded optionality of the FanDuel call option (expiring Dec 2030), which represents potentially billions in hidden value against a $4.5B strike price. Against these positives, the stock faces near-term options market stress (90.8% near-term IV vs. 26.9% HV), ongoing Smartmatic litigation risk, and governance concerns around concentrated Murdoch family control that eliminates any takeout premium.

The prior BUY recommendation at higher scores reflected a more optimistic growth outlook. With FY2026 representing a cyclical trough year and the DTC launch introducing binary execution risk, the risk/reward is balanced rather than compelling at current levels.

## Company Overview

Fox Corporation operates two segments: Cable Network Programming (43% of FY2025 revenue, including FOX News, FOX Business, FS1/FS2, and Big Ten Network) and Television (57%, including the FOX broadcast network, 29 owned-and-operated stations, Tubi AVOD platform, and FOX Entertainment Studios). Revenue splits approximately 47% affiliate fees, 42% advertising, and 11% other (sublicensing, content sales).

The company is strategically differentiated from peers like Warner Bros. Discovery and Disney by its deliberate avoidance of expensive scripted entertainment production. The business model is built on live/appointment content — news and sports — that resists time-shifting and maintains advertising pricing power. Tubi (11 billion hours streamed, 2.2% of all U.S. TV viewing) provides digital growth exposure without subscriber acquisition costs.

Strategic assets include: a 10-year call option on 18.6% of FanDuel Group at ~$4.5B exercise price (expiring Dec 2030), a ~2.4% stake in Flutter Entertainment (~4.3M shares), the 50+ acre FOX Studio Lot in Los Angeles, and ~42% ownership of the United Football League.

## Financial Analysis

**FY2025 Performance (Year ending June 30, 2025):**
- Revenue: $16.3B (+17% YoY)
- Adjusted EBITDA: $3.62B (+26% YoY)
- Net Income: $2.3B (+53% YoY)
- Operating Cash Flow: $3.3B
- Free Cash Flow: ~$3.0B (18.4% FCF margin)

Revenue growth was driven by advertising (+26%, with the Super Bowl contributing ~$800M), affiliate fee rate increases (+5% net of subscriber losses), and sports sublicensing (+47%). Cable Network Programming delivered $3.03B segment EBITDA at 43.7% margins; Television produced $945M at 10.1% margins (+87% YoY, driven by Super Bowl).

**Balance Sheet:**
- Cash: $5.4B
- Long-term debt: $6.6B
- Net debt: ~$1.2B
- Debt/Equity: 1.94x
- Current ratio: 2.91
- Credit ratings: BBB/Baa2 (stable)

The balance sheet is a clear strength. With $5.4B in cash representing 43% of the $12.6B market capitalization, the company has extraordinary flexibility for buybacks, debt reduction, or strategic investments. The $12B share repurchase authorization represents nearly the entire float at current prices.

**FY2026 Revenue Cliff — The Core Near-Term Risk:**
FY2023 (Super Bowl year): $14.9B → FY2024 (off-year): $14.0B (-6%). Applying similar cyclicality to FY2025's $16.3B base implies FY2026 revenue of approximately $14.5-15.0B. The loss of Super Bowl revenue (~$800M) and political advertising (~$300-500M) creates a $1.1-1.3B combined headwind. This is fully expected by the market but will still produce headline declines in revenue and EBITDA.

**Earnings trajectory:** The trailing EPS of $4.91 reflects the peak year. Forward consensus (implied by 13.4x forward P/E at $56.22) suggests ~$4.20 in forward EPS — a 15% decline consistent with the cyclical off-year pattern.

## Growth Analysis

**Positive growth vectors:**
1. **Tubi:** 11B hours streamed, 13% view time growth YoY, 2.2% of all TV viewing. Reaches cord-cutters/cord-nevers and is purely advertising-supported. Digital advertising revenue is growing faster than linear declines.
2. **FOX One DTC:** Launching Fall 2025, this bundles the full FOX portfolio (news + sports + entertainment) into a subscription streaming product. FIFA World Cup 2026 (hosted in U.S.) provides an ideal launch catalyst. Delta partnership confirmed.
3. **Affiliate rate increases:** ~$790M in rate benefit in FY2025 offset $460M in subscriber loss. The spread remains positive, but the arithmetic tightens annually.
4. **Content licensing:** FOX Entertainment Studios sells to Netflix, Amazon, Hulu, and Apple TV+ — diversifying revenue while maintaining programming.

**Structural headwinds:**
1. **MVPD subscriber erosion:** ~9% annual decline (67M → 61M in one year for FOX News). This is accelerating, not decelerating.
2. **Revenue cyclicality:** Every other year lacks Super Bowl/political tailwinds, creating pronounced revenue volatility.
3. **Sports rights inflation:** The 11-year NFL deal (through 2029+) and new Big Ten rights lock in escalating costs that must be offset by advertising growth.
4. **Competition for live sports:** ESPN's standalone streaming, Netflix live sports, Amazon TNF all bid up rights costs and fragment audiences.

Growth Prospects score of 5.0 reflects the reality that FY2026 will be a down year, Tubi growth is real but insufficient to offset linear declines alone, and FOX One is unproven. The long-term growth trajectory depends heavily on DTC execution.

## Valuation Assessment

At $56.22 with trailing EPS of $4.91 (13.4x) and forward EPS of ~$4.20 (13.4x forward), FOX trades at a significant discount to the peer median P/E of 67.6x. However, peer comparisons require context:
- WBD at 93.3x is distorted by near-zero earnings and massive debt
- NXST at 67.6x reflects peak political cycle expectations
- TGNA at 16.3x is the most comparable pure-play broadcaster

Against TGNA (16.3x) and FOXA (15.1x, the Class A equivalent), FOX's 13.4x appears modestly cheap. The discount to FOXA likely reflects the dual-class voting structure penalty on the Class B shares.

**Hidden asset value:**
- FanDuel option: If Flutter's market cap (~$30B+) values FanDuel at $40-50B, FOX's 18.6% call at $4.5B exercise represents $3-5B in embedded optionality. Against a $12.6B market cap, this is material (24-40% of current valuation).
- Cash: $5.4B (43% of market cap). Even backing out the full exercise price on FanDuel, net cash provides substantial downside protection.

**Fair value assessment:** On trough FY2026 earnings of ~$4.20, a 14-15x multiple (reflecting the off-cycle and structural headwinds but acknowledging cash/FanDuel optionality) suggests fair value of $59-63. At $56.22, the stock offers modest upside to fair value but limited margin of safety against further estimate reductions. The valuation score of 6.5 reflects cheap-to-fair on current earnings with meaningful hidden asset value partially offsetting cyclical headwinds.

## Competitive Landscape

FOX's competitive position is bifurcated:

**Strengths (improving):**
- FOX News maintains #1 cable news position for 20+ consecutive years
- Sports rights portfolio (NFL, MLB World Series exclusivity, Big Ten, FIFA WC 2026) creates appointment-viewing moat
- Tubi growing share (2.2% of all TV) in an otherwise fragmented AVOD market
- No scripted studio cost burden — asset-light relative to Disney/WBD

**Weaknesses (deteriorating):**
- 9% annual subscriber erosion accelerating — the worst rate in company history
- ESPN's standalone streaming launch directly challenges sports dominance
- Netflix/Amazon entering live sports bidding puts upward pressure on rights costs
- The broadcast network model itself is structurally challenged (FCC license impairments already occurring — $70M in FY2025)

The trajectory is mixed: digital properties growing, linear properties declining. FOX One is an attempt to merge these trajectories, but execution is unproven. Competitive Position score of 6.5 reflects strong brand assets with unclear long-term trajectory.

## Risk Assessment

**Litigation risk (medium-high):**
- Smartmatic defamation lawsuit ongoing. Dominion settled for $787.5M in 2023. A similar outcome here would represent ~6% of market cap, though FOX has sufficient cash to absorb it.
- Newsom $787M lawsuit advancing past initial motions. Unlikely to result in similar-magnitude damages given different facts, but adds uncertainty.

**Cyclical risk (high):**
FY2026 revenue decline of 10-12% is near-certain. The market likely prices this in, but estimate revisions could still pressure the stock.

**Strategic execution risk (medium-high):**
FOX One launch could (a) cannibalize MVPD affiliate relationships (47% of revenue), (b) underperform subscriber targets, or (c) require more investment than expected. The DTC market is brutal — Disney+, Peacock, and Paramount+ all struggled with profitability.

**Governance risk (structural):**
Murdoch Family Trust controls ~43.9% of Class B voting stock. Class A holders (the common traded stock) have virtually no influence on strategic decisions. This eliminates any M&A premium and concentrates key decisions.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is flashing significant near-term stress. Near-term ATM IV of 90.8% vs. 26.9% historical volatility represents a 63.9pp spread. The term structure is in steep backwardation (90.8% near-term vs. 30.5% 6-month), indicating the market prices a specific imminent event. IV jumped from 49.5% to 87.8% between April 13 and May 6 while the stock was essentially flat (-0.1%). This likely reflects anticipation of the next quarterly earnings report or a material corporate event (possibly FOX One launch details, litigation developments, or a rights deal announcement). The call-heavy skew (-5.6%) suggests some participants are positioning for upside, not just hedging downside.

This divergence between very high near-term IV and stable stock price warrants caution — the market is pricing a wider range of outcomes than the stock's stability suggests. Any position should account for the implied ±9.5% move in the nearest expiry.

**Short interest:** 8.0% of float with 5.7 days to cover — moderate, not extreme in either direction.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($65-70, +16-25%):**
- FOX One launches successfully with World Cup 2026 catalyst, establishing a DTC growth pathway that structurally offsets cord-cutting
- FanDuel option value crystallizes through exercise or monetization ($3-5B in potential value)
- FY2026 trough earnings prove better than feared as Tubi advertising growth and rate increases partially offset cyclical headwinds
- $12B buyback authorization at current depressed levels meaningfully reduces share count (10-15% over 2 years)

**Bear Case ($44-48, -15-22%):**
- FY2026 earnings disappoint below ~$4.00 as cord-cutting accelerates beyond rate-increase offset
- FOX One launch fails to gain traction, alienating MVPD partners while burning cash
- Smartmatic settlement at or above Dominion levels ($787.5M+) depletes cash
- Advertising recession in a stagflationary environment hits both linear and digital revenue simultaneously
- FanDuel option expires worthless or is exercised at unfavorable terms

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock falls below $50 (12x trough EPS), providing genuine margin of safety against the bear case
2. FOX One subscriber numbers exceed 5M within first 6 months, demonstrating DTC viability
3. FanDuel option monetization or exercise announcement crystallizing value

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. MVPD subscriber erosion accelerates beyond 12% annually, breaking the rate-increase offset model
2. Smartmatic settlement exceeds $1B AND advertising revenue declines >15% YoY in FY2026
3. Management abandons share repurchases despite depressed valuation (signals internal concern about cash needs)

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (likely May/August 2026 for FQ4/FY2026), which will provide the first clean look at trough-year economics and early FOX One metrics. The World Cup (June-July 2026) is a critical catalyst for evaluating the DTC thesis.

## Conclusion

Fox Corporation is a fundamentally sound business trading at a reasonable valuation with significant hidden asset value (FanDuel option, cash). However, the stock faces a predictable cyclical trough in FY2026, meaningful structural headwinds from cord-cutting, unproven DTC execution, and near-term options market stress suggesting elevated uncertainty around an imminent catalyst.

The prior BUY recommendations at higher scores reflected a growth outlook that has since deteriorated (Growth Prospects declined from 7.0 to 5.0 in recent assessments). At $56.22, the stock is neither compellingly cheap on trough earnings nor expensive given asset value. The correct posture is patience: wait for either (a) price weakness creating genuine margin of safety, or (b) evidence that FOX One and Tubi can structurally reverse the subscriber erosion math.

**Rating: HOLD.** The risk/reward is balanced at current levels, and the binary near-term catalysts (earnings, DTC launch, litigation) argue against initiating new positions until clarity improves.
