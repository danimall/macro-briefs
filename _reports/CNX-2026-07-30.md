**Research Date: July 29, 2026**

## Executive Summary

CNX is a 9.66 Tcfe-reserve Appalachian dry-gas producer with an integrated gathering system, roughly 390 employees running ~630 Bcfe of annual production, and a capital-allocation framework built explicitly around free cash flow per share. The business is financially sound. FY2025 EBIT of ~$973M covered $170M of interest 5.7x; net debt of ~$2.42B against a derived "cleaned" EBITDA of ~$1.17B implies ~2.1x leverage; $1,295.7M of the revolver was undrawn at 3/31/2026; the borrowing base was redetermined *upward* to $2,400M in May 2025; and in February 2026 the company placed $500M of 2034 notes at 5.875% to retire $500M of 6.00% 2029 paper. Screening tools flagging an Altman Z of ~1.27 and a 0.44 current ratio are mis-specified here: current liabilities include $377.9M of derivative liabilities settled against production revenue and $208.1M of convertible notes that CNX elected to settle in shares, and the revolver's own current-ratio covenant excludes both while adding revolver availability. CNX certified full covenant compliance at 3/31/2026.

Two things prevent that solidity from translating into an actionable long. First, the earnings engine is being throttled by the hedge book. Q1 2026 pre-hedge gas realizations were $4.74/Mcf, but 106.1 Bcf of Shale hedges settled at an average loss of $1.97/Mcf, producing a $221.6M cash hedge loss (−$1.59/Mcf) and cutting the portfolio realization to $3.28/Mcfe. Remaining 2026 fixed-price volume is 346.0 Bcf at $2.74/Mcf. The strike rolls to $3.31 on 402.2 Bcf in 2027, which mechanically adds roughly $0.57/Mcf on ~400 Bcf (~$230M pre-tax) without any operational change. Second, and cutting directly against that, consensus estimates are falling in every window: current quarter -15.5% over 30 days and -35.6% over 90 days, next year -4.0% and -8.5%, to $4.06. The most parsimonious reading is that the 2027 forward gas curve has softened faster than the hedge roll improves realizations, so the mechanical uplift is being partly absorbed before it arrives.

The third issue is measurement. The quoted $4.83B market capitalization implies ~141.5M shares and predates the May 1, 2026 settlement of the 2.25% converts. At a $12.84 conversion price, $208.6M of notes implies ~16.2M shares, which the Q1 2026 diluted count of 160,288,606 already reflects against 142,202,197 basic. On a post-settlement basis, equity value at $34.12 is closer to $5.4B and enterprise value to ~$7.55B (net debt falls to ~$2.16B as the converts become equity). Per-share multiples computed off $4.8B understate the valuation by roughly 12%. Capped calls (strike $12.84, cap $18.19) return shares to CNX and reduce net dilution, but the covered notional is unresolved. HOLD, with the honest classification that the near-term evidence is genuinely two-sided.

## Company Overview

CNX operates three reporting units. **Shale** (Marcellus/Utica plus consolidated CNX Midstream Partners) is the earnings engine: Q1 2026 realized $4.62/Mcfe pre-hedge against $1.55/Mcfe of total production cost, a $1.62/Mcfe margin. **Coalbed Methane** produced 9.1 Bcf at a $6.46/Mcf realization, a $1.84 premium to Shale reflecting local/industrial market access and environmental-attribute economics, but at $4.25/Mcf of cost the margin is only $0.76/Mcf and the segment contributed $6.9M of $428.4M of Q1 pre-tax income on volumes down 2.2% y/y. **Other** holds purchased gas, unrealized derivative marks, environmental attributes, and unallocated corporate costs.

The production mix is 91% gas (Q1 2026: $658.6M of $722.0M of hydrocarbon revenue), with $60.1M of NGLs and $3.4M of oil. Proved reserves of ~9.66 Tcfe against 2026 guided volumes of 605-620 Bcfe imply a ~15.8-year reserve life, long relative to shale peers. PV-10 is ~$6.83B on a $3.387/MMBtu SEC deck.

Two structural features matter. CNXM carries its own $400M 4.75% 2030 notes and a $600M revolver that **CNX does not guarantee**, which flatters consolidated leverage analysis if treated as fully recourse. And CNX holds $1,373.8M of gas firm transportation and processing obligations, $255.7M of which comes due within a year. In a tight, high-basis market that fixed cost converts into a marketing asset: Q1 2026 excess firm transportation income tripled to $14M and unutilized FT fees flipped to a $9M credit. In a loose market it is stranded cost. The position is a leveraged bet on Appalachian takeaway scarcity.

## Financial Analysis

Headline revenue is close to uninterpretable without adjustment. The Q1 2025 → Q1 2026 swing from $82.4M to $786.7M is roughly 75% explained by the derivative line moving from a $528.2M loss to a $4.0M gain, of which the unrealized mark alone accounted for $644M. The same mechanic runs in reverse: Q2 2025 GAAP diluted EPS of $2.53 was hedge-mark-driven as prices fell, which is why the externally reported Q2 2026 GAAP EPS of $1.32 is a mechanical, not operational, decline.

The underlying trend, on CNX's own cash-inclusive sales measure, is better and far less dramatic:

| Metric | Q1 2026 | Q1 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales incl. cash settlements | $500M | $441M | +13.4% |
| Per unit realization | $3.28/Mcfe | $2.99/Mcfe | +9.7% |
| Production margin | $1.56/Mcfe | $1.31/Mcfe | +19.1% |
| Volumes | 152.4 Bcfe | 147.8 Bcfe | +3.1% |
| Operating cash flow | $277.5M | $215.7M | +28.7% |
| Capex | $169.9M | $131.5M | +29.2% |

Q1 FCF of $107.6M should not be annualized. It benefited from a $63.0M receivable release and seasonal gas strength, and carried $288.4M of cash derivative settlements plus $25.5M of accrued-interest paydown. A normalized 2026 frame starts from guidance: capex $556-586M against volumes 605-620 Bcfe. Q1 tracked ~25% of guided volume on ~30% of guided capex, so spend is front-loaded. On a reasonable OCF band of $950M-$1,000M less $571M midpoint capex, 2026 simple FCF lands near $380-430M, below FY2025's $534M.

Cash taxes remain de minimis ($1.1M in Q1 2026 against $80.3M of book tax expense) against ~$0.7B federal and ~$1.2B state NOLs. Deferred taxes grew $78.7M in the quarter. This is a real near-term cash tailwind and a real long-term reversal.

On management's non-GAAP "Adjusted Free Cash Flow per Share," the proxy discloses $4.53 for FY2025. A GAAP-anchored derivation ($534M ÷ ~146.6M weighted basic shares) gives $3.66. The ~$128M gap is plausibly asset-sale proceeds and working-capital normalization, but the definition is not verified. At $34.12 those imply 7.5x and 9.3x respectively; the conservative figure is the one to underwrite.

Debt at 3/31/2026 was $2,378.8M principal: $600M 7.25% 2032, $500M 5.875% 2034, $500M 7.375% 2031, $400M CNXM 4.75% 2030 (non-guaranteed), $208.6M converts (share-settled), $105M CNXM revolver (non-guaranteed), $76.3M CNX revolver. Long-term debt fair value of $2,797.0M against carrying value evidences favorable credit perception. The February refinancing extends the wall five years at a lower coupon; the ~$2.6M/yr of coupon savings against ~$20.1M of extinguishment and issuance cost means the benefit is maturity extension, not interest expense.

## Growth Analysis

This is the weakest dimension and the reason the score has drifted lower across successive reviews. Three facts:

**Volumes are guided down while capex rises.** 605-620 Bcfe versus FY2025's 629.0 Bcfe, on capex of $556-586M versus $495M. That is a 12-18% capex increase against a 1.4-3.8% volume decline. Management frames this as maintenance-plus-Apex-integration spending in an inflating service market, with the delta routed to per-share returns. The alternative reading, that capital intensity per unit is rising because base decline is accelerating, is not confirmed by Q1 data (Shale depletion rose only to $0.68/Mcfe from $0.66, and Shale volumes grew 3.5%), but the 10-Q attributes part of the volume growth to Apex wells rather than organic performance, so a single quarter does not settle it.

**The environmental-attribute leg is contracting.** Sales fell to $14.9M in Q1 2026 from $22.9M (−34.8%) on both lower volumes and lower prices; FY2025 was ~$78M against ~$95M in FY2024. This was historically the differentiating piece of the CNX story versus pure shale peers, and it is shrinking on both price and volume. Attribute contracts are short-term with revenue recognized on transfer, so quarterly figures are lumpy, but the annual direction matches.

**Estimate revisions are negative in every window.** Current quarter $0.46 (from $0.72 ninety days ago), next quarter $0.62 (from $0.79), current year $2.88 (from $3.07), next year $4.06 (from $4.44). Revisions of this direction and magnitude are a warning, and the fact that the stock rose ~2.5% over the same interval that the near-term estimate fell 15.5% is the specific combination that historically precedes disappointment.

Against that, the beat record is unbroken: four consecutive adjusted beats, most recently Q2 2026 at $0.72 against a $0.60 supplied estimate (+20.3%; Zacks cited a $0.57 consensus and a ~26% beat). CNX keeps clearing a bar that keeps being lowered. The cuts therefore look curve-driven rather than execution-driven, which is meaningful context but does not change the direction of forward earnings power.

The one genuine forward driver is the hedge roll. If spot holds near $4.74/Mcf, 2026's remaining ~346 Bcf of fixed-price volume at $2.74 (plus 117.2 Bcf already settled) implies a further cash hedge drag in the hundreds of millions for the balance of 2026; 2027's 402.2 Bcf at $3.31 implies a meaningfully smaller drag at the same spot. That swing is fully disclosed and requires no management execution. It is also capped: 2027 remains ~65% hedged, and the 8.5% cut to next-year consensus indicates the offsetting curve move is already partly in the numbers.

## Valuation Assessment

The supplied profile lists forward P/E identical to trailing P/E at 4.78x, which is internally inconsistent with consensus estimates and should be disregarded. Working from the numbers:

| Basis | EPS | On quoted 141.5M sh ($4.83B) | On diluted ~158M sh ($5.39B) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FY2025 GAAP (mark-inflated) | $3.98 | 8.6x | 8.6x |
| 2026 consensus | $2.88 | 11.8x | 11.8x |
| 2027 consensus | $4.06 | 8.4x | 8.4x |

Consensus EPS is presumably struck on diluted shares, so the multiple table is unaffected by the conversion; the distortion sits in *asset-based and cash-flow-based* metrics. On post-settlement equity of ~$5.39B and EV of ~$7.55B, 2026E FCF of $380-430M is a 7.1-8.0% equity FCF yield and a 5.0-5.7% FCF/EV yield. That is respectable for a gas E&P but not a mispricing.

Peer context is mixed and the median is not the right anchor. The supplied peer set medians at 15.8x P/E, but that median is drawn from CRC (-10.6x), MGY (15.8x), MUR (65.8x), CRK (6.2x), and CRGY (-11.1x). The only genuine dry-gas comparable, Comstock, trades at 6.2x. Against that reference, CNX at 8.4x on 2027 and 11.8x on 2026 is not cheap, and Comstock's -53% discount to its 52-week high versus CNX's -22% shows CNX is the better-treated of the two.

Sell-side posture is unusually unenthusiastic: 1 buy, 9 hold, 1 sell, 1 strong sell, with the only recent change a sell-to-hold upgrade. Mean target $37.64 (+10.3% from $34.12), median $36.00 (+5.5%), range $32-$48. On a stock where estimates are falling, the guidance that analyst targets lag reality does not apply; targets and price are both drifting with the same gas curve, and there is no independent confirmation here.

The clearest support is the buyback. $2,374.0M of remaining authorization equals ~44% of diluted market capitalization, and the deployment pattern is genuinely price-sensitive: 16.9M shares at a $31.00 average in FY2025 versus 1.45M shares at $37.36 in Q1 2026. That behavior, paired with a proxy whose company-selected performance measure is Adjusted FCF per Share, implies a soft bid in the low $30s. It also implies the reverse: at $38-40 management has historically stood aside. With the stock at $34.12-$34.70, that puts the price roughly in the middle of management's own demonstrated buy zone, which is consistent with a fair-value read rather than an asymmetric one. Valuation scores 5.0.

## Competitive Landscape

CNX's stated edge is per-share compounding through free cash flow and share-count reduction, plus monetization of non-traditional assets (surface acreage, rights-of-way, environmental attributes, excess firm transportation). Nothing in the filings claims differentiated resource quality, and none is evident. The durable advantages are cost structure (~390 employees against ~630 Bcfe; Q1 SG&A $32.2M, down 17.9% y/y, with only $8M of salaries), reserve life (~15.8 years), and integrated gathering.

Basis conditions improved sharply in early 2026. A $4.74/Mcf pre-hedge realization, up 29.5% y/y, implies little or no punitive in-basin discount, corroborated by excess FT income tripling and unutilized FT fees flipping to a credit on cold-weather capacity optimization. Q1 is the seasonal peak for both basis and capacity value, so durability is unproven.

Three trajectory items are negative. Customer concentration worsened: Q1 2026 disclosed NRG Business Marketing at $156.0M (21.6% of hydrocarbon revenue) and DTE Energy Trading at $83.3M (11.5%), 33.1% across two names, versus Citadel Energy Marketing alone at 11.8% a year earlier, with complete counterparty turnover. These are purchasers of CNX's gas; nothing in the filings characterizes them as partners or offtake counterparties, and remaining performance obligations on fixed-price contracts longer than one year total only $15.6M. A broadening revenue *mix* does not constitute customer diversification when the direct concentration disclosure is deteriorating. Credit risk appears well controlled (trade credit loss allowance of $84 thousand, unchanged), but rotation this rapid is more consistent with opportunistic netback optimization than contracted offtake.

Second, the environmental-attribute franchise is contracting, removing the piece of the story that supported a premium to pure shale peers. Third, relative TSR has lagged badly: the proxy's cumulative indexed measure shows CNX at 340.8 against a peer group at 531.8 at FY2025 (roughly +241% versus +432%), with the gap widening every year in the table. The most consistent explanation, aligned with the hedge analysis, is that low-strike hedges capped participation in gas upside that less-hedged peers captured. Longleaf Partners' Q2 2026 letter independently describes CNX as more conservatively financed than peers that "got a fuller runup," which triangulates with this reading while coming from a long holder.

On the dominant Appalachian narrative, in-basin power and data-center demand: no supply agreement, generation joint venture, or behind-the-meter arrangement is disclosed in anything reviewed. NRG's marketing entity buys gas and pays for it. No data-center linkage is asserted here.

## Risk Assessment

**Hedge opportunity cost through 2026** is the largest quantified drag: $221.6M of realized loss in Q1 alone, 346.0 Bcf of remaining 2026 volume at $2.74/Mcf. Disclosed sensitivity: a 10% rise in forward gas reduces derivative fair value by $367M. The book is symmetric protection if gas falls, so this is a timing and participation issue rather than pure value destruction.

**Share count and quoted market cap.** ~16.2M shares implied by the share-settlement election on $208.6M of converts at $12.84. Framed correctly, that is ~11.4% new shares relative to the pre-conversion count and takes existing holders to ~89.7% ownership (roughly a 10.3-percentage-point ownership dilution). Capped calls at a $12.84 strike and $18.19 cap return roughly 2.5M shares at a $34 price if they cover only the residual notional, materially more if the original ~26.9M-share notional remains. Direction is certain; magnitude is not. No post-May-2026 filing was available to confirm actual shares outstanding.

**Legacy coal liabilities.** The UMWA 1992 Benefit Plan suit seeks to hold CNX jointly and severally liable for Coal Act retiree health benefits for ~2,159 retirees; motions to dismiss were denied in March 2022 and **no accrual** has been recorded (loss possible, not probable). The parallel 1974 Pension Plan matter settled at $75M over five years, and CNX won partial summary judgment against Core Natural Resources in November 2024 with all payments reimbursed plus interest through 3/31/2026. The precedent on indemnity rights is favorable, but the 1992 matter is unresolved and the indemnity is only as good as Core's credit. This is the cleanest example of an unpriced binary.

**Reserve and impairment sensitivity.** PV-10 of ~$6.83B rests on a $3.387/MMBtu deck. No impairments were taken in 2024 or 2025, and $473.0M of goodwill has already been written off, leaving $323.3M in Midstream. A sustained gas decline would compress EBITDA and the borrowing base simultaneously, which is where the low Z-Score does carry information even though it mis-reads the near term.

**Firm transportation obligations** of $1,373.8M and **uncertain tax positions** of $131.3M round out the disclosed exposures.

**Verification gaps that materially limit confidence:** no Q2 2026 10-Q was available, so Q2 volumes, capex, cash flow, hedge settlements, buyback activity, and any updated guidance are unknown; the Adjusted FCF/share definition is unverified; post-conversion share count is unconfirmed; and the circumstances of four Item 5.02 officer-change filings between June 2025 and January 2026 were not read.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 42.0% against 28.5% one-year historical vol is rich (78th percentile within the chain), with backwardation into the 3-month tenor at 32.7% and 6-month at 30.0%, and an implied ±8.9% move at the 21-day expiry. IV rose 7.2 points (34.8% → 42.0%) between July 19 and July 30 as the price rose 2.5%, consistent with hedging into the Q2 print rather than a directional view. Signals conflict: the put/call volume ratio is 2.22 and the near-tenor put/call skew reads 4.6%, but put/call open interest fell from 0.89 to 0.56 and the aggregate skew is described as call-heavy at -4.0%. The sign convention is not defined in the supplied data, so no directional conclusion is drawn. The actionable read is that near-term vol is expensive and should compress once the print is digested, which argues against paying up for near-dated optionality either way.

Separately, short interest of 15.2% of float at 6.7 days to cover is elevated. That cuts both directions for a HOLD: it confirms institutional skepticism consistent with the falling estimates, and it creates squeeze mechanics if the gas curve firms or the buyback re-accelerates at these levels.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $45-48

Gas realizations hold near Q1's $4.74/Mcf while the hedge strike rolls from $2.74 to $3.31 on 402.2 Bcf, materially reducing the cash hedge drag into 2027 with zero operational dependency. Apex integration continues delivering (Shale volumes +3.5%, central Pennsylvania FT utilization up), and the staged $49.5M Utica option beneath existing acreage converts to low-cost stacked-pay inventory. Appalachian basis stays tight, keeping excess firm transportation an income line rather than a cost. Management redeploys the $2,374.0M authorization at low-$30s prices, retiring shares as it did in FY2025 at a $31.00 average. On 2027 EPS above consensus at 10x, that supports the low-to-mid $40s; the $48 high target requires both a firm gas tape and multiple expansion toward the non-gassy peer median.

### Bear Case — $26-28 (−19% to −24%)

The forward gas curve continues to soften, which is what the estimate revisions are already telling us: 2027 consensus cut 8.5% in 90 days. The hedge roll then arrives into lower spot, muting the uplift. Capex stays elevated at $556-586M against declining volumes, so per-unit capital intensity rises and 2026 FCF falls to $380M or below. Environmental-attribute revenue keeps contracting, removing the narrative premium. Customer concentration at 33.1% across two rotating marketers becomes a netback problem if one exits. If the SEC deck falls materially below $3.387/MMBtu, the borrowing base and PV-10 compress together and impairment risk returns. On 2027 EPS of $3.20-3.40 at 8x, that is $26-27, near the $27.72 52-week low. The UMWA 1992 matter resolving against CNX with an unfunded indemnity would be additive downside not currently reflected anywhere.

### Which side the evidence favors

Neither decisively, which is why this is a HOLD classified as uncertain rather than a growth hold. The balance sheet, the four-for-four adjusted beat record, the disclosed 2027 hedge roll, and a demonstrated price-sensitive buyer of its own stock in the low $30s all cap downside meaningfully above the bear case. Falling estimates in every window, declining guided volumes on rising capex, worsening customer concentration, a contracting attribute business, and a 1-buy/9-hold/2-sell sell-side posture all cap upside meaningfully below the bull case. At $34.12-$34.70 the stock sits roughly at the midpoint of that range and at the upper edge of where management itself has been willing to repurchase.

On trajectory: the score has declined from 6.7 in May to 5.3 in July, driven by valuation (7.0 → 5.0), growth (5.8 → 4.3), and competitive position (6.5 → 5.2). New data confirms rather than reverses that direction. The 30-day estimate cuts (-15.5% current quarter, -6.1% current year) landed *after* the prior review, and they arrived while the stock rose ~2.5%. The offsetting positives — the Q2 adjusted beat, the completed refinancing at a lower coupon, and clarity on the conversion mechanics — are enough to keep this from becoming an AVOID but not enough to arrest the drift. Score 5.1.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Reassessment timeline:** the Q2 2026 10-Q (expected within weeks of the July 30 8-K) is the near-term checkpoint, since it will supply the first verified post-conversion share count, Q2 hedge settlements, updated 2026 guidance, and any change in the >10% customer list. The Q3 print in late October is the second, and 2027 is where the hedge-roll thesis either shows up or does not.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Next-year consensus EPS stops falling and turns positive over a 30-day window, confirming the 2027 hedge roll is flowing through faster than the curve is eroding. Threshold: 2027 consensus back above $4.25.
2. Buyback re-accelerates to $125M+ in a quarter at prices below $33, which would reset the effective floor and demonstrate the authorization is being used rather than warehoused.
3. A verified post-conversion share count materially below ~158M (i.e., capped calls covering the original ~26.9M-share notional), which would cut the diluted equity value and improve per-share metrics by more than the ~12% currently assumed.
4. A pullback into the $29-31 band, where management historically bought aggressively, would improve risk/reward independent of any fundamental change.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. 2026 capex guidance raised above $586M without a corresponding volume increase, which would confirm rising capital intensity rather than transitional Apex spending.
2. 2027 hedged volume added at strikes below $3.20, which would extend the participation cap into the one year where the thesis depends on it.
3. Environmental-attribute revenue below $12M in a quarter, or FY2026 tracking below $60M, confirming structural rather than lumpy deterioration.
4. Any accrual taken on the UMWA 1992 matter, or evidence of Core Natural Resources credit stress that would impair the indemnity.
5. Combined >10% customer concentration above 40% of hydrocarbon revenue.

**What would not change the view:** further GAAP EPS volatility from derivative marks in either direction. The Q2 2026 GAAP decline from $2.53 to $1.32 is a mark-to-market artifact and carries no operating information.

## Conclusion

**HOLD.** CNX is a well-financed, low-cost, long-lived gas asset run by a management team whose incentives (Adjusted FCF per share as the company-selected measure) and behavior (16.9M shares at $31.00 in FY2025, near-abstention at $37.36 in Q1 2026) are properly aligned with per-share value. The distress signals thrown by automated screens are artifacts of derivative liabilities and share-settled converts sitting in current liabilities, and should be disregarded in favor of 5.7x interest coverage, ~2.1x leverage, $1.30B of undrawn revolver, an upward borrowing-base redetermination, and notes trading $418M above carrying value.

What is missing is a reason to expect the equity to outperform over the next twelve months. Guided volumes are lower than 2025 on 12-18% more capex. The differentiating environmental-attribute business is shrinking on both price and volume. Customer concentration has increased to 33.1% across two names that turned over entirely in a year, against $15.6M of long-dated contracted revenue. Consensus EPS has been cut in every window, including 35.6% for the current quarter, while the stock rose. Adjusting for the ~16.2M shares that settle in May, the equity is nearer $5.4B than the quoted $4.8B, so the 4.8x headline P/E overstates cheapness on every asset- and cash-flow-based measure. The one clean forward driver, the hedge roll from $2.74 to $3.31 on 402.2 Bcf in 2027, is real and fully disclosed, but the 8.5% cut to 2027 consensus suggests the gas curve is eating into it before it arrives.

Base case $36-37 (roughly 9x 2027 consensus EPS of $4.06, cross-checked against a 7.5% target FCF yield on ~$5.4B of diluted equity), or +5% to +8% from $34.12. Bull $45-48, bear $26-28. That distribution is close to symmetric with a modest positive skew, which is a hold. Constructive interest returns in the $29-31 band where management's own bid has historically appeared; conviction returns when next-year estimates stop falling.
