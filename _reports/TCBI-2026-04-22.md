## Executive Summary

Texas Capital Bancshares has completed a multi-year strategic transformation from a traditional Texas commercial bank into a diversified financial services firm. Full-year 2025 EPS of $6.79 represents a 430% increase from the 2024 trough of $1.28 (depressed by a deliberate $179.6M securities portfolio repositioning loss). The three most recent quarters delivered an average 19.6% earnings surprise, NIM expanded 32bp to 3.35%, and the efficiency ratio improved from 81.3% to 61.2%. Analyst estimates for FY2026 ($7.63) and FY2027 ($8.49) are both rising, with 30-day revisions of +1.5% and +1.8% respectively.

At $103.09, the stock trades at 13.5x FY2026E consensus EPS of $7.63, a modest premium to the regional bank peer median of 12.8x. That premium is warranted by a $105M investment banking revenue stream, an accelerating NIM trajectory, a clean CET1 ratio of 12.1%, and a management team with a demonstrated track record of value creation (152.2% five-year TSR vs. 129.4% for the peer group). Active insider buying by directors in the $91-95 range during early 2026 provides additional conviction.

The key risks are Texas geographic concentration (~50% of loans), non-interest bearing deposit runoff ($9.8B → $8.2B over two years increasing structural funding costs), and elevated near-term implied volatility (37.8% vs. 29.0% HV) suggesting the options market is pricing a potential catalyst or stress event. These are manageable risks for a bank with this capital position and earnings trajectory.

## Company Overview

Texas Capital Bancshares is a $31.5B-asset financial holding company headquartered in Dallas, operating primarily through Texas Capital Bank and broker-dealer subsidiary Texas Capital Securities. The company runs a branch-lite model with offices in Dallas, Austin, Fort Worth, Houston, and San Antonio.

The loan portfolio breaks down as: commercial banking (51%, $12.3B), mortgage finance (25%, $6.1B), commercial real estate (22%, $5.4B), and consumer (2%). Revenue diversification comes from four distinct fee income streams: investment banking and advisory ($104.6M in 2025), service charges on deposits, trading income, and trust/wealth management fees. The Bask Bank digital platform, which offers American Airlines AAdvantage miles in lieu of interest, is a differentiated deposit-gathering innovation.

In September 2025, the Bank became a Federal Reserve System member, shifting its primary federal regulator from the FDIC to the Federal Reserve — a change that may affect future regulatory interactions and supervisory resources.

### NIM Expansion Mechanics

NIM expanded 32bp in 2025 (3.03% → 3.35%) driven by asymmetric repricing: deposit costs fell 33bp (2.93% → 2.60%) while asset yields declined only 6bp (5.82% → 5.76%). This favorable funding-cost-led NIM expansion has further room to run if rates remain elevated — the $12.3B in floating-rate loans repricing above one year provides asset yield support, while competitive deposit pricing pressure moderates.

Q4 2025 NIM reached 3.38%, suggesting further sequential improvement.

### Balance Sheet Strength

Capital ratios are well above well-capitalized thresholds: CET1 at 12.1%, total capital at 16.1%, leverage ratio at 11.7%. Total deposits of $26.4B are 97.2% customer deposits with only 2.8% brokered. Estimated uninsured deposits at 42% are elevated but within the range of institutional-focused banks.

Free cash flow was $348M in 2025, providing ample capacity for the $200M share repurchase program authorized in December 2025. The 2025 buyback of $185.8M at a weighted average of $82.01/share was well-timed relative to the current price.

## Competitive Landscape

TCBI competes against national, super-regional, and community banks across Texas and increasingly across the broader US. Its competitive positioning rests on three structural advantages:

1. **Local headquarters with institutional capabilities.** TCBI is the largest Texas-headquartered bank, meaning it can offer investment banking, treasury management, and institutional services that community banks cannot, while maintaining local decision-making speed that JPM/BAC branches cannot.

2. **Investment banking platform.** The investment banking business is rare among sub-$50B asset banks. This provides fee income countercyclicality and attracts corporate relationships that deepen the deposit and lending franchise.

3. **Bask Bank digital innovation.** The AAdvantage miles deposit product is a creative differentiator in the commodity deposit market, particularly effective at attracting and retaining consumer deposits without competing purely on rate.

The 152.2% five-year TSR vs. 129.4% for the peer group confirms market recognition of TCBI's differentiated trajectory, though the majority of that outperformance has materialized in the past 12 months.

## Risk Assessment

### Credit Risk

The loan portfolio is 97% collateralized. The CRE portfolio (22% of loans) includes $588M in interest reserve loans, primarily construction/development exposures that carry elevated risk if economic conditions deteriorate. Office exposure at 4.8% of CRE is modest. Criticized loans declined from $714M to $635M, indicating improving credit trends. The provision expense fell from $67M to $55M.

The $1.8B oil and gas portfolio (7.5% of loans) creates direct energy sector sensitivity. With oil near $100, this is a tailwind. A sharp oil price decline would stress these credits, though TCBI has historically managed energy lending conservatively.

### Deposit Composition Risk

Non-interest bearing deposits have declined from $9.8B (2023) to $8.2B (2025) — a 16% structural decline. Each dollar of NIB runoff that reprices into interest-bearing deposits costs approximately 2.6% in funding (the average IB deposit rate). This ongoing mix shift is a structural headwind to NIM that partially offsets the cyclical benefits of rate stability.

Estimated uninsured deposits at 42% remain a potential vulnerability in a bank stress scenario, though the 2023 regional banking crisis demonstrated that institutional depositors are stickier at well-capitalized, well-managed banks.

### Geographic Concentration

Despite diversification efforts (>50% of loans and deposits now outside Texas), the Texas concentration remains material. The CRE portfolio is 52% Texas-located, and the bank's identity, brand, and relationship network are deeply rooted in Texas markets. A Texas-specific economic downturn (energy, housing, corporate relocation reversal) would disproportionately impact TCBI.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility of 37.8% is rich relative to 29.0% historical volatility — an 8.8pp spread. The term structure is in backwardation (37.8% near-term vs. 30.2% at 6 months), suggesting the market expects a specific near-term catalyst or stress event. Put/call skew of 3.6% shows modestly elevated put demand. Short interest at 9.7% of float with 6.7 days to cover is notable but not extreme.

The near-term IV richness likely reflects positioning ahead of Q1 2026 earnings (late April/early May) or broader concerns about the credit environment. The backwardation structure is consistent with uncertainty around an upcoming earnings report rather than structural deterioration. Given the strong recent beat record, elevated IV may create a favorable setup if Q1 2026 results meet or beat expectations.

### Macro Sensitivity

Regional banks are directly exposed to credit cycle dynamics. TCBI's high floating-rate loan book (93% of >1yr loans are floating) provides income protection but creates borrower stress risk if the economy contracts. The $6.1B mortgage finance book is sensitive to housing transaction volumes, which decline in both recession and high-rate environments. Current recession probability estimates of 50-55% make this a material consideration.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($120-125, ~16x FY2026E)

NIM continues expanding toward 3.50%+ as deposit costs reprice lower while asset yields hold. Investment banking revenue grows 15-20% as Texas-based M&A activity accelerates. ROE expands to 11-12%, justifying a premium multiple among regional peers. The $200M buyback reduces share count by ~4-5% at current prices. Estimate revisions continue higher, pulling the stock toward a 16x FY2026E multiple ($122). Oil at $100 supports the Texas energy economy and the energy lending book.

### Bear Case ($80-85, ~10.5x FY2026E)

The credit cycle turns: rising defaults in CRE (particularly the $588M interest reserve book) and commercial lending force provision expense to double from $55M to $110M+. NIB deposit runoff accelerates, compressing NIM back toward 3.10%. Recession reduces mortgage finance volumes and investment banking fees simultaneously. The stock re-rates to peer median or below at 10-11x earnings on lower EPS. A 10.5x multiple on reduced FY2026 EPS of $6.50 yields ~$68.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Base case target: $115** — 15.1x FY2026E EPS of $7.63. This reflects a sustainable premium to peer median given TCBI's superior growth rate and fee income diversification, while acknowledging the stock is no longer cheap after its 62% rally. The $115 target implies 11.5% upside from $103.09.

**Bull case target: $122-125** — 16x FY2026E if NIM expands further and investment banking scales. 18-21% upside.

**Bear case target: $80-85** — 10.5x reduced FY2026E EPS. 18-22% downside.

**Upside/downside ratio:** ~1.3:1 base case, ~2:1 inclusive of dividend-free buyback yield.

**Timeframe:** 9-12 months, keyed to: Q1 2026 earnings (late April/early May 2026), Q2 2026 earnings (July 2026), and the trajectory of NIM/fee income through mid-year.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Q1 2026 EPS misses consensus by >10%, reversing the three-quarter beat streak and suggesting the transformation is stalling.
2. NIM contracts below 3.20% in any reported quarter, indicating the favorable repricing dynamic has reversed.
3. Criticized loans rise above $800M or provision expense exceeds $30M in any single quarter, indicating a credit cycle turn.

## Conclusion

TCBI earns a BUY recommendation at $103.09 based on: (1) accelerating earnings with positive estimate revisions and a strong beat record, (2) reasonable valuation at 13.5x forward with a premium to peers justified by growth and diversification, and (3) management credibility supported by insider buying, disciplined capital allocation, and a demonstrated transformation track record. The elevated near-term IV creates a caution flag, but the backwardation structure is more consistent with event-positioning around upcoming earnings than structural deterioration. The 9.7% short interest adds potential short-covering fuel if Q1 results continue the beat streak. The stock has run significantly from its 2025 lows, limiting absolute upside, but the earnings trajectory supports continued appreciation toward $115 over the next 9-12 months.
