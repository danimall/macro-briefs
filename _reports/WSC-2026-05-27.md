## Executive Summary

WillScot Holdings is North America's largest modular space and portable storage leasing company, operating ~304,000 units across 260 branch locations. The business generates exceptional EBITDA margins (42.6% in FY2025) and counter-cyclical free cash flow ($489M adjusted FCF in FY2025 despite declining revenue). At $25.72 per share, the stock trades at approximately 8.9x EV/EBITDA on FY2026 guidance — cheap relative to the margin profile and asset durability — but this discount exists for identifiable structural reasons.

The company faces its third consecutive year of declining units on rent (down 11.3% in FY2025), is executing an unprecedented network optimization that will abandon 53,000 units and close 108 locations, carries 3.7x net debt/EBITDA, and has missed earnings expectations in each of the last four quarters. Q1 2026 results showed the first genuine improvement — revenue beat, raised guidance, and management commentary suggesting large-project demand inflection in H2 2026. However, the 22.3% short interest (10.1 days to cover) signals that sophisticated investors remain skeptical of the recovery thesis. The absence of any insider buying during the stock's 50%+ decline from 2022 highs reinforces this caution.

The risk/reward profile is balanced at current levels: cheap enough that significant downside is limited by asset value and FCF generation, but insufficiently de-risked to warrant a directional call until volume inflection is confirmed over multiple quarters.

## Company Overview

WillScot operates a specialty leasing business model where long-lived physical assets (modular offices lasting 20+ years, storage containers lasting 30+ years) generate recurring monthly rental revenue. Over 90% of revenue derives from leasing and related services. The company serves 85,000+ customers across 15 end markets, with no single customer exceeding 2% of revenue.

Revenue composition (FY2025): Leasing $1.75B (77%), Value-Added Products $398M (17%), Delivery/Installation $389M, Unit Sales $144M. Total revenue: $2.28B, down 4.8% from FY2024's $2.40B.

The business model's structural advantage is counter-cyclical capex flexibility — when demand declines, WSC reduces fleet investment, and the long asset lives mean depreciation continues regardless. This creates a natural cash flow cushion during downturns that sustains debt service and capital return.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** Three consecutive years of decline. FY2023 peaked near $2.4B; FY2024 was $2.40B; FY2025 was $2.28B (-4.8%); FY2026 guidance is $2.25B (roughly flat). The decline is entirely volume-driven — units on rent fell 11.3% in FY2025 as elevated interest rates suppressed non-residential construction starts. Offsetting: modular rental rates grew 4.9% to $1,243/month; storage rates grew 7.5% to $286/month. Pricing power remains intact.

**Margins:** Adjusted EBITDA was $971M in FY2025 at 42.6% margin, down from $1.06B and 44.4% in FY2024. Q1 2026 showed $211M at 38.5% — temporarily compressed by volume ramp costs and seasonality. FY2026 guidance of $915M implies further modest margin compression but excludes potential upside from the network optimization (expected $25-30M annual cost savings).

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was $762M in FY2025, up from $562M in FY2024 — the counter-cyclical mechanism working as designed. Adjusted FCF was $489M. Even at trough conditions, the business generates ~$25M+ in monthly FCF.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of $3.51B against equity of ~$605M (5.8x D/E). Net Debt/EBITDA of 3.7x. No maturities until August 2028. Weighted average interest rate approximately 5.7% (90% fixed). Annual cash interest of ~$202M is well covered by operating cash flow (3.8x coverage). The ABL facility provides $1.4-1.5B of additional liquidity.

**Earnings Quality Concern:** FY2025 included $63.5M in additional accounts receivable write-offs recorded as revenue reductions, plus $302M in restructuring charges. Net loss was $53M. The trailing P/E of -68.6x reflects these charges — not operational reality. On an adjusted basis, EPS was approximately $1.00-1.10 in FY2025, with FY2026 consensus at $1.11 and FY2027 at $1.36.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term (FY2026):** Revenue guided approximately flat at $2.25B. Management sees a "credible path to inflect leasing revenues to year-over-year growth in H2 2026" driven by large project demand in data centers, power generation, and manufacturing. Q1 2026 order and activation trends strengthened through April.

**Medium-term (FY2027+):** Consensus FY2027 EPS of $1.36 implies 23% growth vs. FY2026's $1.11. This recovery depends on: (1) non-residential construction cycle turning with rate relief, (2) large project demand materializing at scale (data centers, reshoring), (3) successful execution of the network optimization plan creating margin expansion.

**Structural Growth Drivers:**
- Value-Added Products penetration increasing revenue per unit
- Modular space gaining share vs. permanent construction (speed/flexibility advantage)
- Data center and power generation buildouts requiring temporary space
- Long-term infrastructure spending (IIJA, CHIPS Act) creating multi-year demand pipeline

**Growth Constraints:**
- Storage utilization at 51.5% (from 60% prior year) needs meaningful volume recovery
- The 53,000-unit fleet abandonment may limit upside capacity when cycle turns
- Rate growth of 5-7% annually cannot fully offset double-digit volume declines indefinitely
- Three years of declining Dodge Starts data in non-residential construction

The growth thesis requires an exogenous catalyst (rate cuts stimulating construction) or continued scaling of large project demand. Neither is certain within 12 months given the current macro environment.

## Valuation Assessment

At $25.72 with ~181M shares outstanding, market cap is $4.65B. Adding $3.5B net debt yields an enterprise value of approximately $8.15B.

**EV/EBITDA:** $8.15B / $915M (FY2026 guided) = 8.9x. Against FY2025 actual of $971M, this is 8.4x. Using a normalized mid-cycle EBITDA assumption of $1.0-1.1B (achievable if volume stabilizes and rate growth continues), the stock trades at 7.4-8.2x.

**P/E on Forward Estimates:** At $1.11 FY2026 consensus, forward P/E = 23.2x. At $1.36 FY2027 consensus = 18.9x. These multiples are modest for a business with structural recurring revenue and high margins.

**FCF Yield:** $489M adjusted FCF on $4.65B market cap = 10.5% FCF yield. Even assuming FCF compresses to $400M in FY2026, the yield is 8.6%. This is genuinely cheap for the quality of cash generation.

**Why the discount exists (and whether it's warranted):** The market is pricing (1) 3.7x leverage in a rising-rate environment, (2) three years of declining volumes with no confirmed inflection, (3) the failed McGrath acquisition ($226M wasted), (4) 22.3% short interest reflecting informed bearish positioning, (5) 4 consecutive earnings misses. The low Altman Z-Score (0.49) — while structurally distorted by the leasing business model — scares quantitative screens.

**Is the market wrong?** Partially. The 10.5% FCF yield is too cheap for a business with no near-term maturity risk, diversified customers, and long-lived assets. However, the recovery timeline is uncertain. Valuation provides a floor but does not create urgency for immediate appreciation without volume confirmation.

## Competitive Landscape

WSC is the clear market leader with advantages in scale (260 locations), fleet diversity (modular + storage + clearspan), VAPS penetration, and technology investment. The $20B North American TAM is highly fragmented, giving WSC pricing power and consolidation optionality.

**Improving factors:** Large project demand (data centers, power generation) favors WSC's scale and ability to deploy hundreds of units to single sites. The VAPS strategy (furniture, power, connectivity) creates switching costs and revenue lift per unit that smaller competitors cannot match. Target IRRs exceeding 25% including VAPS demonstrate the value creation model remains intact even at lower utilization.

**Competitive risks:** The failed McGrath acquisition means WSC missed its best consolidation opportunity. Local competitors can underprice on specific regional bids. The network optimization (closing 108 locations) may temporarily reduce geographic coverage advantages.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical risks not fully priced:**

1. **Volume recovery fails to materialize in H2 2026:** If Dodge Starts remain weak and large project demand disappoints, FY2026 guidance gets cut, triggering another leg lower. A guidance cut would likely test the November 2025 low ($14.91).

2. **Restructuring execution disruption:** Closing 108 locations while trying to inflect revenue growth creates operational tension. Customer defections during branch closures are a real risk. If restructuring costs exceed the guided ~$60M, cash flow guidance gets revised.

3. **Leverage in a rising-rate environment:** The 10% of floating debt reprices if rates rise further, and any refinancing of the August 2028 maturities would occur at higher rates.

4. **Credit deterioration:** The $63.5M incremental AR write-offs may signal broader customer stress, particularly among smaller construction/industrial customers most exposed to rate-sensitive sectors.

5. **Short interest at 22.3%:** While this creates squeeze potential, the weight of informed bearish positioning suggests the consensus recovery thesis may be too optimistic.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 49.6% is approximately in line with 1-year historical volatility of 51.4% — the market is not pricing unusual near-term stress. The term structure is in mild backwardation (49.6% near vs. 47.5% far), which could reflect residual post-earnings positioning rather than imminent stress. Put/call OI ratio of 0.58 and volume ratio of 0.03 suggest the options market is not confirming the bearish thesis implied by the short interest. This divergence (high SI, low options put demand) may indicate the short thesis is structural rather than catalyst-driven.

- H2 2026 volume inflection confirmed (management's "credible path" materializes)
- Large project demand from data centers/power/manufacturing drives multi-quarter order growth
- Network optimization delivers $25-30M in annual savings with minimal revenue leakage
- Stock re-rates from 8.9x to 10-11x EV/EBITDA as growth returns
- Short squeeze potential with 10.1 days to cover

- Restructuring causes customer losses and service disruptions
- Rising rates compress valuation multiples and increase refinancing risk

**Base Case ($26-30; flat to +16%):**
- Revenue approximately flat in FY2026 as guided
- Modest volume recovery offset by continued rate growth
- FCF remains strong ($400-450M) supporting debt reduction and moderate buybacks
- Stock trades in a range until multi-quarter volume growth is confirmed

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarters of YoY volume growth (units on rent inflecting positive)
2. Storage utilization recovering above 55% (from current 51.5%)
3. Any insider open-market purchase by C-suite executives
4. Short interest declining below 15% (signal: bears capitulating)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2026 guidance cut (revenue below $2.2B or EBITDA below $880M)
2. Net Debt/EBITDA rising above 4.0x
3. Q2 2026 earnings miss combined with continued volume declines
4. Any liquidity concern (ABL facility drawdown exceeding 50%)

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected early August 2026) is the next critical data point. Management's claim of H2 2026 revenue inflection must show evidence in Q2 order/activation data.

## Conclusion

WillScot is a high-quality specialty leasing business in a cyclical downturn with execution uncertainty layered on top. The 10.5% FCF yield and 8.9x EV/EBITDA provide valuation support, and the Q1 2026 beat represents the first positive datapoint after four consecutive misses. However, one quarter does not overcome three years of volume decline, 22.3% short interest, no insider buying, $226M wasted on the failed McGrath deal, and an aggressive restructuring plan that reduces future capacity.

The correct posture is patience. Wait for volume confirmation (H2 2026 data) before committing capital. If the inflection materializes, the stock has meaningful upside from compressed multiples and potential short covering. If it does not, the leverage and restructuring risks create further downside. At $25.72, the stock is fairly valued for current uncertainty — neither cheap enough to own against the shorts nor expensive enough to fade.
