**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Flex Ltd has transformed from a commodity EMS provider into an AI infrastructure beneficiary through its Cloud and Power Infrastructure (CPI) segment, which grew 38% in FY2026 to $6.6B and is guided for 65-75% growth in FY2027. The planned spin-off of CPI into an independent company (targeted Q1 CY2027) represents a legitimate value-unlocking event, as the high-growth data center business is currently obscured within a diversified manufacturing conglomerate trading at blended multiples.

The fundamental strength is validated by four consecutive earnings beats, estimate revisions of +67.9% for next-year EPS over 90 days, and structural commercial relationships with Amazon (warrant-backed) and Google ("foundational driver"). CPI revenue is on track to nearly triple from FY2026 to FY2028, and the segment's integrated power-cooling-compute offering creates competitive differentiation relative to single-product incumbents like Vertiv.

The critical question is whether the stock's 237% appreciation in 12 months — culminating in the post-earnings/spin-off announcement gap from $96 to $135 — has already captured the sum-of-parts thesis. At $145, FLEX trades at 32x FY2027E EPS ($4.50) and 21x FY2028E ($6.79). The spin-off introduces substantial execution risk: regulatory approvals, debt allocation between entities, CEO departure to SpinCo, and the possibility that RemainCo (ITS + RMS) derates once separated from the growth engine. The stock deserves a growth premium but requires near-flawless delivery to justify current levels.

## Company Overview

Flex Ltd is one of the world's largest electronic manufacturing services providers with $27.9B in FY2026 revenue, approximately 150,000 employees across 100+ facilities in 30 countries. The company recently reorganized into three segments:

- **Cloud and Power Infrastructure (CPI):** 24% of revenue ($6.6B), serving hyperscaler data centers with integrated compute, liquid cooling, and power solutions. Being spun off as "SpinCo" in Q1 CY2027.
- **Regulated Manufacturing Solutions (RMS):** 36% of revenue ($10.2B), covering industrial automation, automotive, and healthcare manufacturing.
- **Integrated Technology Solutions (ITS):** 40% of revenue ($11.1B), including communications/networking equipment and consumer products.

Geographic mix: Americas 50% (Mexico 25%, US 19%), Asia 30% (China 16%, Malaysia 11%), Europe 20%. No single customer exceeds 10% of consolidated revenue, though hyperscaler concentration within CPI is pronounced.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Recovery and CPI Acceleration:**

Revenue declined from $30.3B (FY2023) to $25.8B (FY2025) following Nextracker deconsolidation and market softness, then recovered to $27.9B in FY2026 (+8% YoY). The recovery was driven entirely by CPI, which grew from $3.2B (FY2024) to $6.6B (FY2026) — a 106% increase in two years. RMS grew 5%; ITS declined 2%.

**Margin Expansion:**

Consolidated gross margins expanded from 7.1% (FY2024) to 9.2% (FY2026), a 210bps improvement. Operating margins went from 3.2% to 4.9%. CPI margins actually compressed 100bps in FY2026 due to ramp costs, meaning the improvement came from both mix shift toward higher-margin products and operational efficiency in RMS/ITS (+80bps and +60bps respectively).

**Cash Flow and Capital Structure:**

Operating cash flow was $1.69B in FY2026; free cash flow $1.05B. Capex is guided to nearly triple from $633M to $1.4-1.6B in FY2027 to support hyperscaler contracts, which will temporarily compress FCF. Total debt stands at $3.8B plus a $1.45B term loan drawn in April 2026 = $5.25B against $2.4B cash and $5.1B equity. D/E of 3.29x is elevated for the sector.

**Capital Return:**

Flex repurchased $900M in FY2026 at an average $49.08/share — highly accretive given the subsequent move to $145. Shares outstanding declined 18% from 454M (FY2023) to 371M (FY2026). No dividend.

**Earnings Consistency:**

Four consecutive quarterly beats: +12.7%, +2.7%, +8.6%, +4.3%. The beat cadence suggests management is guiding conservatively relative to operational visibility, which supports credibility of forward guidance.

## Growth Analysis

The growth story is concentrated in CPI:

- CPI FY2026 revenue: $6.6B (+38% YoY)
- Management guidance: 65-75% growth in FY2027 (implying $10.9-11.6B)
- FY2028 guidance: 80%+ growth (implying ~$19.6-20.9B)
- The segment would grow from $6.6B to roughly $20B in two years

This is validated by the Amazon warrant (vesting tied to purchasing commitments), Google described as "foundational driver" on the Q4 call, and management's statement that CPI capacity is "booked out for the next couple of years."

Estimate revisions confirm the acceleration: next-year EPS revised +67.9% in 90 days (from $4.04 to $6.79). Current-year EPS revised +23.4% (from $3.65 to $4.50). This velocity is among the strongest observable in the EMS/IT hardware universe.

The structural demand drivers — hyperscaler capex acceleration ($80-100B+ annually per major cloud provider), power density increases from 10kW to 100kW+ per rack, and liquid cooling adoption — are multi-year and confirmed across multiple independent companies.

**RemainCo Growth:** ITS + RMS businesses are roughly flat (ITS -2%, RMS +5%). Post-spin, these will need their own growth narrative. Automotive compute and healthcare device manufacturing have structural tailwinds but lack the explosive growth profile of CPI.

## Valuation Assessment

Current valuation metrics:
- Market cap: $53.2B
- Trailing P/E: 61.5x ($2.33 LTM EPS)
- Forward P/E on FY2027E ($4.50): 32.3x
- Forward P/E on FY2028E ($6.79): 21.4x
- EV/Revenue (FY2026 $27.9B): ~2.0x

**Peer comparison:** The peer median P/E is 52.2x. FLEX at 61.5x trailing appears premium, but on forward FY2028 earnings ($6.79), the stock is at 21.4x, which is cheaper than TE Connectivity (20.7x trailing on flat growth) while growing dramatically faster. The trailing multiple reflects trough earnings, not normalized earnings power.

**Sum-of-parts framework:**

SpinCo (CPI): At $10.9-11.6B FY2027 revenue with 9-10% operating margins and infrastructure-comparable valuations (Vertiv trades at ~4x EV/Revenue), SpinCo could be worth $40-50B. Conservative at 3x FY2027 revenue = $33-35B.

RemainCo (ITS + RMS): ~$21B revenue with 5-6% operating margins. Traditional EMS valuations of 0.5-0.8x revenue or 10-14x earnings suggest $12-20B.

Combined sum-of-parts: $45-70B, with $55-60B as the central case. The current $53.2B market cap sits at the lower bound of this range, suggesting moderate upside if the spin executes successfully.

**Why this does not merit a BUY despite the estimate revision velocity:**

The +67.9% estimate revision in 90 days is a strong BUY signal in the framework, but it must be weighed against three specific factors:

1. **The stock has already repriced** from $42 to $145 (237% in 12 months), with the last $50 of appreciation occurring on the spin-off announcement. The estimate revision and the price move occurred simultaneously — unlike typical inflection situations where estimates rise while the stock lags, here the market recognized the value instantaneously. The gap between estimates and price that typically generates alpha has already closed.

2. **At 32x FY2027E, the stock requires CPI to deliver 65-75% growth AND the spin-off to execute without value destruction.** If CPI delivers 50% (still exceptional) instead of 70%, FY2027 EPS would be closer to $3.80-4.00, putting the stock at 36-38x — premium territory requiring further acceleration.

3. **The spin-off is a binary event with material execution risk** (Singapore court approval, SEC registration, debt allocation, CEO departure, IT/manufacturing separation). History of EMS/industrial spin-offs suggests 30-40% probability of meaningful value leakage during separation. The $145 price already incorporates substantial spin-off success.

The stock is not expensive on FY2028 estimates, but those estimates embed aggressive growth assumptions for a segment that was $3.2B just two years ago. At 21x FY2028E, if guidance is met, the stock could reach $170-180. If guidance is missed by 15-20%, the stock is fairly valued at current levels. The asymmetry is roughly balanced, not skewed upward.

## Competitive Landscape

Flex/SpinCo's competitive position in AI infrastructure is genuinely differentiated:

**vs. Traditional EMS (Jabil, Celestica, Foxconn):** Flex has moved up the value chain from board/system assembly into proprietary products (power conversion, liquid cooling). Competitors remain primarily build-to-print. This creates margin expansion opportunity that pure EMS providers cannot replicate.

**vs. Infrastructure Incumbents (Vertiv, Eaton, Schneider Electric):** SpinCo's differentiator is "grid-to-chip" integration — delivering complete rack/pod-level solutions combining power, thermal management, and compute housing rather than individual subsystems. At scale (150K employees, 100+ factories), this is difficult to replicate.

**Key risk to competitive position:** Hyperscaler vertical integration. If Google or Amazon decide to bring power/cooling design in-house (as they have with custom silicon), the value of Flex's integration role diminishes. The Amazon warrant suggests multi-year commitment, but hyperscalers have historically commoditized their supply chain partners once relationships mature.

**Market share trajectory:** CPI is clearly gaining share within the AI infrastructure buildout, evidenced by 38% organic growth against an industry growing perhaps 20-25%. The booked-out capacity statement suggests continued share gains in the near term.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Spin-off execution (HIGH):** Requires Singapore court approval, SEC registration, IRS tax opinion, shareholder vote, physical separation of manufacturing, IT systems split, and debt allocation. CEO departure to SpinCo introduces succession risk for RemainCo. Target Q1 CY2027 — roughly 7 months away.

2. **CPI growth deceleration (MODERATE):** If AI capex plateaus or hyperscalers pause (budget constraints, energy availability), CPI's 65-75% growth guidance becomes unachievable. The stock at 32x FY2027E has minimal margin for error.

3. **Capex peak compression (MODERATE):** FY2027 capex of $1.4-1.6B (up from $633M) will pressure FCF. If revenue ramp is delayed, the company will be spending heavily without proportional returns.

4. **Customer concentration within CPI (MODERATE):** Google and Amazon appear to constitute a majority of CPI revenue. Contract structures are not disclosed. Loss or reduction of either relationship would be material.

5. **Leverage at separation (MODERATE):** $5.25B total debt must be allocated between entities. An unfavorable split could constrain one or both companies.

6. **Tariff/trade policy (LOW-MODERATE):** 25% of revenue from Mexico, 16% from China. USMCA tariff changes could require rapid supply chain reconfiguration.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($170-180, +17-24%):**
- CPI delivers 70%+ growth in FY2027 with margin expansion
- Spin-off executes on schedule (Q1 CY2027) with clean separation
- Market values SpinCo at infrastructure multiples (3-4x revenue)
- RemainCo re-rates as operational improvements become visible
- FY2028 EPS of $6.79+ achieved, stock trades at 25x = $170

**Bear Case ($95-105, -28-35%):**
- Spin-off delayed or structurally impaired (regulatory, tax, or operational issues)
- CPI growth disappoints (45-50% vs. 65-75% guided) due to hyperscaler capex pause
- RemainCo derates to traditional EMS multiples (12-14x EPS)
- Capex peak erodes FCF without corresponding revenue acceleration
- FY2027 EPS of $3.50-4.00, stock trades at 25x = $88-100

**Central Case ($140-160):**
- CPI delivers 55-65% growth (slightly below guidance)
- Spin-off proceeds on timeline but with modest value leakage
- Market maintains current valuation framework through FY2027 reporting cycle
- FY2027 EPS of $4.20-4.50, stock trades at 32-35x

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Stock pulls back to $110-120 (25x FY2027E) without fundamental deterioration — this would create meaningful asymmetry
- Spin-off receives Singapore court approval and SEC "effectiveness" — removing the largest binary risk
- CPI Q1 FY2027 results confirm 65%+ growth trajectory with stable/expanding margins

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- CPI quarterly growth decelerates below 40% without identified temporary cause
- Spin-off delayed beyond Q2 CY2027 or material adverse conditions attached
- Hyperscaler commentary suggesting capex retrenchment affecting Flex order flow
- Debt allocation reveals unfavorable structure for one or both entities

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (likely August 2026 for Q1 FY2027) will provide the first post-announcement operational data point. Spin-off proxy filing (expected Q3 2026) will reveal financial structure details.

## Conclusion

Flex's transformation into an AI infrastructure play through CPI is real, validated, and structurally advantaged. The estimate revision velocity (+67.9% in 90 days) and 4-for-4 earnings beat record confirm operational momentum. The planned spin-off should unlock sum-of-parts value by allowing CPI to trade at infrastructure multiples rather than EMS multiples.

However, the stock's 237% appreciation in 12 months — including a 50% move in three weeks post-announcement — has substantially compressed the risk/reward from current levels. At $145, the stock prices in CPI growth execution, successful spin-off completion, and continued re-rating. The near-term binary risk of the spin-off (regulatory, operational, tax) combined with the lack of valuation margin of safety argues for holding rather than initiating or adding at these levels. Position entry should wait for either a pullback or elimination of the spin-off's execution risks.
