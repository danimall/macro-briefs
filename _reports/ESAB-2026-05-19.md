## Executive Summary

ESAB Corporation is a $5.1B market cap global welding/cutting equipment and consumables manufacturer operating under a Danaher-inspired industrial compounder model. The stock has declined 35% from its February 2026 high of $137.42 to $84.55 (currently near its 52-week low of $84.42), driven by concerns around the $1.45B Eddyfi acquisition, consecutive EPS misses (3 of the last 4 quarters), and broader macro headwinds facing industrials.

The fundamental tension in ESAB is straightforward: the business model works — 252% total shareholder return since the 2022 spin-off, adjusted EBITDA margins expanding to 20%, and Q1 2026 showing 10% core sales growth acceleration. But the Eddyfi deal materially alters the risk profile, pushing pro forma leverage to an estimated 3.5-4x net debt/EBITDA at a 5.625% coupon rate. Combined with tariff headwinds, gross margin compression, and a CFO transition mid-integration, the near-term execution risk is substantial enough to warrant patience despite a valuation that is now clearly below intrinsic value on success-case scenarios.

At $84.55, the stock trades at approximately 14.6x forward EPS ($5.79 FY2026E) — a significant discount to peer medians (~25x) and well below the lowest analyst target of $117. The market is pricing a meaningful probability of integration failure or sustained leverage-driven derating. If the Eddyfi deal closes successfully and margin recovery materializes in H2 2026 as guided, the stock offers 40-65% upside to analyst targets. The uncertainty around that "if" is what keeps this a HOLD rather than a BUY.

## Company Overview

ESAB was founded in 1904 and became independently traded in April 2022 following its spin-off from Enovis Corporation. The company operates in two segments: Americas (40% of revenue, $1.13B) and EMEA & APAC (60%, $1.71B). The product mix is approximately 66% consumables (higher-recurring, razor-and-blade model) and 34% equipment.

The company positions itself as an "industrial compounder" — a platform that acquires businesses and systematically improves them through its EBXai business system (analogous to the Danaher Business System). Mitchell Rales, co-founder of Danaher, sits on the board and maintains registration rights, providing direct oversight of the playbook execution.

ESAB competes in a ~$45B addressable market (by 2028) against Lincoln Electric and Illinois Tool Works' welding division, plus numerous regional specialists. A key differentiator is geographic diversity: approximately 52% of revenue comes from "high-growth" markets (South America, Eastern Europe, India, Asia Pacific, Middle East), providing structural growth exposure above developed-market welding demand.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Growth:**
- FY2025 revenue: $2.84B (+3.7% YoY), but organic growth was -1.1% (acquisition contribution of +4.2%)
- Q1 2026 revenue: $745.6M (+9.9% YoY) with core sales growth of 10% — a meaningful acceleration
- Revenue consensus: $5.79 EPS for FY2026, $6.50 for FY2027

**Profitability:**
- Gross margin contracted from 37.9% (2024) to 36.9% (2025) — 100bps compression from tariff-related cost inflation
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded slightly to 19.7% (20.0% ex-Russia)
- Q1 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin compressed to 18.3% due to acquisition integration costs
- Net margin: 8.2% (2025), down from 10.7% (2024)

**Cash Flow:**
- FY2025 FCF: $213M (7.5% margin), down from implied ~$300M+ in 2024
- Operating cash flow: $261M (2025) vs. $355M (2024) — significant decline from deal-related spending
- At current market cap of $5.1B, FCF yield on trailing basis is approximately 4.2%

**Balance Sheet — The Critical Issue:**
- Year-end 2025 long-term debt: $1.24B (net debt/EBITDA ~2.2x)
- Post-Eddyfi pro forma: ~$2.2-2.4B debt ($1B new senior notes at 5.625% + existing) → estimated net debt/EBITDA of 3.5-4x
- Altman Z-Score: 2.18 (Grey Zone) — pre-Eddyfi
- Goodwill already $1.95B (41% of assets); will increase substantially post-Eddyfi
- Available liquidity: $865M revolving facility + cash

The debt profile is manageable if EBITDA continues expanding, but at 5.625% coupon rates, the carrying cost is material. Roughly $125-135M in annual interest expense post-Eddyfi on a business generating ~$560M adjusted EBITDA = interest coverage of ~4.2x. This leaves limited room for operational disappointment.

**Earnings Surprise Pattern:**
The recent track record is concerning: 3 misses in 4 quarters, with the most recent (Q1 2026) missing by 4.3%. This pattern suggests either deteriorating visibility, overly aggressive consensus, or execution challenges. Management has historically beaten estimates — the recent miss pattern coincides with the Eddyfi integration period and tariff disruptions.

- Estimate revisions are mixed: current quarter down -2.8% (30d) and -3.1% (90d), next quarter up +2.7% (30d), FY2026 down -0.6% (90d), FY2027 down -0.4% (90d)

**Medium-term (1-3 years):**

**Growth Vulnerabilities:**
- Organic growth in the core welding business was essentially flat in 2025 — the company relies heavily on acquisitions for top-line growth
- This model requires continued access to attractively-priced acquisitions and financing; with rates elevated, the financing leg is now significantly more expensive
- Tariff environment creates both headwinds (steel/aluminum cost inflation compressing margins) and potential tailwinds (reshoring activity)

## Valuation Assessment

At $84.55, ESAB trades at:
- 14.6x FY2026E EPS ($5.79)
- 13.0x FY2027E EPS ($6.50)
- ~9.1x EV/adjusted EBITDA (on $560M FY2025 EBITDA; lower on post-Eddyfi pro forma EBITDA)
- ~4.2% trailing FCF yield

**Relative to Peers:**
The peer median P/E is 25.4x. ESAB at 14.6x forward represents a 42% discount. This is unusually wide for a company with superior growth (10% core Q1 vs. peers generally in low single digits) and a proven operational improvement playbook. The discount reflects the market's assessment of leverage risk and integration uncertainty.

**Relative to Analyst Targets:**
Every covering analyst rates ESAB Buy or Strong Buy (9 of 9). The lowest target ($117) is 38% above current price. Median target ($140) implies 66% upside. Mean target is $136. JPMorgan had a $153 target as recently as February 2026.

**What the Market is Pricing:**
At $84.55, the market appears to be pricing either: (a) a significant probability of Eddyfi integration failure, (b) sustained margin compression from tariffs and cost inflation that prevents recovery to 20% EBITDA margins, or (c) a broader industrial recession that compresses earnings below current consensus. The valuation is compelling if any one of these fears proves overstated, but not so cheap that you're being paid adequately for all three risks materializing simultaneously.

**Valuation Verdict:**
The stock offers asymmetric upside on a 12-18 month view if execution proceeds as planned. However, the consecutive EPS misses, mixed estimate revisions, and rising leverage prevent this from being a clear mispricing. The market is applying a large uncertainty discount — whether that discount is "too large" depends entirely on Eddyfi integration trajectory, which won't be visible until H2 2026/early 2027.

## Competitive Landscape

ESAB is one of three global leaders in welding/cutting alongside Lincoln Electric (LECO) and ITW's welding division. The market is fragmented enough that no single player dominates all niches, giving ESAB room to grow through both organic share gains and tuck-in acquisitions in specific geographies/product segments.

The EBXai operating system is a genuine competitive advantage in the acquisition integration process — evidenced by the 252% TSR since spin-off outperforming peer group by 86 percentage points. However, this advantage has not translated into organic growth outperformance: core organic growth was essentially flat in 2025, suggesting the operational improvement methodology drives margin expansion more than top-line acceleration.

The Eddyfi acquisition, if successful, would give ESAB a differentiated position in the inspection/monitoring segment of the fabrication workflow — creating a more complete offering than pure-play welding competitors. Eddyfi's 65% gross margins also suggest strong competitive positioning in its own market (NDT/integrity monitoring).

Competitive trajectory is stable to slightly improving: the additive manufacturing opportunity and defense spending tailwind provide emerging vectors, while the core welding market in developed economies remains mature and cyclical.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **Eddyfi Integration & Leverage (HIGH):** If Eddyfi's 65% gross margins don't translate to promised accretion, or if the integration absorbs excessive management bandwidth during the CFO transition, ESAB will be overleveraged with limited cushion. Timeline: next 12-18 months.

2. **Tariff & Input Cost Escalation (MODERATE-HIGH):** 50% tariffs on steel/aluminum imports directly impact raw material costs. ESAB's pricing power has partially offset this (per Q1 commentary on surcharges), but further tariff escalation remains possible.

3. **Russia Exposure (MODERATE):** $142M revenue, $50M trapped cash, $110M translation loss on exit. Ongoing compliance and reputational risk. Gradual wind-down appears underway but no commitment to full exit.

4. **Organic Growth Dependence on Acquisitions (MODERATE):** The compounder model requires sustained access to acquisitions at reasonable prices. With credit tightening and rising rates, the deal pipeline economics deteriorate. If organic growth remains below 2-3%, the growth thesis weakens considerably.

5. **CFO Transition (MODERATE):** R. Brent Jones replacing Kevin Johnson effective May 2026 — during the most complex integration period in company history.

6. **Asbestos Legacy (LOW-MODERATE):** ~$15M/year outflow with uncertain long-term trajectory. Inherited from former parent.

## Investment Thesis

- Eddyfi closes mid-2026 and integration proceeds on schedule; Eddyfi's 65% gross margins begin lifting blended margins within 3-4 quarters
- Margin recovery materializes in H2 2026 as guided (back toward 20% EBITDA margins)

**Bear Case ($65-70, -23% to -17%):**

**Base Case ($100-115, +18% to +36%):**
- Eddyfi closes on schedule; early integration is messy but ultimately succeeds over 18 months
- Margins recover partially but don't reach full 20% until mid-2027
- FY2026 EPS approximately in-line with consensus ($5.79) with slight upward revision to FY2027 as Eddyfi begins contributing
- Stock re-rates modestly from extreme discount as execution evidence accumulates

## Conclusion

ESAB presents a "good business, stressed balance sheet" situation. The industrial compounder model has a proven track record, the geographic mix tilts toward higher-growth markets, and the Eddyfi acquisition — if executed successfully — would be highly accretive to the margin and growth profile. At 14.6x forward earnings and a 42% discount to peer medians, the valuation clearly discounts significant execution risk.

The constraint on a more positive recommendation: three EPS misses in four quarters, mixed estimate revisions, leverage about to spike to 3.5-4x at 5.625% rates, a CFO transition during peak integration complexity, and a macro environment (tariff escalation, potential industrial slowdown) that is actively hostile to leveraged industrials. The stock is near its 52-week low for identifiable reasons. The next 2-3 quarters will determine whether ESAB successfully executes the most important integration in its history or whether the balance sheet becomes a binding constraint on value creation. HOLD until that visibility improves.
