**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Fox Corporation delivered exceptional FY2025 results — $16.3B revenue (+16.6%), $2.26B net income (+51%), and $2.99B in FCF — driven by cyclical events: Super Bowl LIX hosting (~$870M incremental sports ad revenue) and 2024 presidential election political advertising (~$550M). The company sits on $5.4B in cash against $6.6B gross debt, yielding net debt of just ~$1.2B. However, FY2026 lacks both cyclical tailwinds, and normalized EBITDA is likely $2.8-3.2B versus the $3.62B reported. At $58.46 (15x forward P/E), the stock is priced for a mature media company — neither cheap enough to be compelling nor expensive enough to avoid.

The structural challenge: FOX News lost 6 million cable subscribers in one year (-9%), affiliate fee growth of 5% came entirely from rate increases offsetting ~$460M in subscriber losses, and this dynamic has a mathematical ceiling. The FOX One DTC launch ($20/month, August 2025) represents management's strategic response, but it risks cannibalizing the very affiliate revenue it's meant to replace. The FanDuel call option (18.6% at ~$4.5B, expiring December 2030) is a genuine hidden asset potentially worth $1-2B in intrinsic value, providing optionality the market may underappreciate.

The stock has declined ~14% from its January 2026 high of $68.17, reflecting the market's correct recognition that FY2025 earnings were cyclically inflated. The current price represents a reasonable entry for investors willing to collect ~1% dividend yield plus substantial buyback-driven EPS accretion while waiting for the next cyclical upturn (FY2027 midterm elections, future Super Bowl rotations). The FIFA 2026 World Cup (hosted in the U.S.) provides a meaningful near-term offset.

## Company Overview

Fox Corporation operates three principal businesses: Cable Network Programming (43% of FY2025 revenue), Television (57%), and a small Corporate/Other segment. Revenue sources split approximately 47% affiliate fees, 42% advertising, and 11% other (sublicensing, content sales).

**Key assets:**
- FOX News: #1 cable network in primetime for 10+ consecutive years, dominant in conservative news with no credible competitor
- Sports rights: NFL (11-year deal from 2023), MLB World Series exclusive, Big Ten Conference, NASCAR, FIFA World Cup 2026, INDYCAR, LIV Golf
- Tubi: Leading AVOD streaming service with ~2.2% of all U.S. TV viewing and ~11 billion hours streamed in FY2025
- FOX One: DTC streaming service launched August 2025 at $20/month
- FanDuel option: 10-year call (expiring Dec 2030) to acquire 18.6% at ~$4.5B exercise price
- 29 owned-and-operated TV stations covering 14 of top 15 DMAs

The business model is structurally differentiated from peers (Disney, Warner Bros. Discovery, Paramount) by its focus on live, unscripted, and news content — avoiding the massive scripted content spending that burdens competitors.

## Growth Analysis

**Organic growth drivers:**
- Tubi: Growing viewership and monetization (CPMs rising as scale improves ad targeting)
- FOX One: New revenue stream, though cannibalization risk is real
- FIFA 2026 World Cup: Hosted in the U.S., with FOX holding rights — meaningful one-time revenue boost
- Affiliate rate increases: 5% growth overcoming subscriber losses (for now)

**Structural headwinds:**
- Cable subscriber losses of ~8-10% annually with no sign of deceleration (FOX News: 67M → 61M in one year)
- Affiliate fee rate increases have a mathematical ceiling — MVPDs will eventually resist or collapse
- The advertising market continues shifting to digital; live content provides only partial protection
- Sports rights cost escalation (NFL deal significantly more expensive under the new 11-year contract)

**Growth quantification:** Stripping out cyclical events, organic revenue growth is likely 2-4% annually, driven by rate increases and digital/streaming growth, partially offset by subscriber losses. EPS growth is higher (6-8%) due to aggressive buybacks reducing share count. This is a single-digit grower.

The score trajectory shows Growth Prospects declining from 7.0 to 5.0 over recent assessments. This decline is warranted: the FY2025 results masked structural challenges, and FY2026 faces a $1.4B+ revenue cliff from lost cyclical tailwinds. The 2026 World Cup provides partial offset but not full replacement.

## Valuation Assessment

At $58.46 with a market cap of $12.9B:
- Trailing P/E: 11.9x (on cyclically inflated $4.91 EPS)
- Forward P/E: 15.2x (on normalized ~$3.85 EPS)
- EV/EBITDA (trailing): ~3.9x ($12.9B + $1.2B net debt / $3.62B)
- EV/EBITDA (normalized): ~4.4-5.0x (on $2.8-3.2B)
- FCF yield: ~23% (on FY2025) / ~15% (normalized ~$2.0B)
- Dividend yield: ~1%

**Peer comparison:**
- FOXA (Class A, same company): 16.7x P/E — FOX trades at a slight discount reflecting lower voting rights
- TGNA: 16.3x P/E
- NXST: 39.6x P/E (distorted by one-time charges)
- Peer median: 16.5x P/E

FOX trades roughly in-line with broadcasting peers on a forward basis. The discount to normalized earnings is modest but not compelling enough to constitute a clear mispricing.

**Hidden asset value:**
- FanDuel option: If FanDuel is worth $25-35B (implied by Flutter's market cap), FOX's 18.6% option represents $4.7-6.5B in gross value minus $4.5B exercise price = $200M to $2B in net option value. At midpoint (~$1B), this represents approximately 8% of market cap not reflected in operating earnings.
- Flutter equity stake (~2.4%, 4.3M shares): Worth ~$700-800M at current Flutter prices.

Combined, these represent ~$1.7-2.8B in off-balance-sheet value (13-22% of market cap). This is meaningful but the market likely partially prices it given it's well-disclosed.

The valuation is fair. The score trajectory shows valuation declining from 7.5 to 6.5: at $58 the stock has recovered from its February lows without fundamental improvement in the structural growth picture.

## Competitive Landscape

**Dominant positions:**
- FOX News: Unassailable in conservative news. 10+ years at #1 in primetime with no credible challenger. The competitive moat here is cultural/brand loyalty, not merely content quality.
- Sports rights: Among the most valuable portfolios in broadcasting. The NFL alone provides a structural floor for advertising revenue.

**Improving positions:**
- Tubi: Growing within AVOD, competing effectively against Pluto TV, Roku Channel, and Amazon Freevee. The 2.2% of total U.S. TV viewing is meaningful scale.
- FOX One: Too early to assess competitively; the $20/month price point faces pressure from cheaper alternatives.

**Deteriorating positions:**
- Linear cable distribution: Structural decline accelerating. The 9% subscriber loss rate shows no sign of stabilization.
- Local TV stations: $70M FCC license impairment in FY2025 reflects declining broadcast economics.

**Structural advantage vs. peers:** FOX's "asset-light" model (live/unscripted content, no massive scripted spending) means it avoids the losses plaguing Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount in streaming. FOX generates $3B+ in FCF rather than losing billions annually on content. This positions it to outlast competitors in the transition.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Smartmatic litigation ($2.7B claim):** Following the $800M Dominion settlement, this remains material. However, Smartmatic's own federal criminal indictment (bribery, money laundering) significantly strengthens FOX's defense. Settlement value likely $200-400M vs. the $800M Dominion precedent. Probability-weighted cost: $100-200M.

**2. Cord-cutting acceleration:** The most important structural risk. If subscriber losses accelerate from 9% to 12-15% annually, rate increases cannot compensate. The tipping point where affiliate revenue begins declining absolutely may arrive in 2-3 years.

**3. FOX One cannibalization:** Success cannibalizes affiliate fees; failure wastes investment. This is a no-win scenario in the near term, though long-term DTC positioning is necessary.

**4. Revenue cyclicality:** FY2026 faces ~$1.4B headwind from loss of Super Bowl and election revenue. The FIFA World Cup provides partial offset.

**5. Sports rights cost inflation:** NFL and other rights packages are escalating faster than advertising revenue growth. Television segment margins (10%) may compress further.

**6. Murdoch family governance:** Dual-class structure with ~44% voting control limits minority shareholder influence. Lachlan Murdoch's $33M compensation (with $66M "actually paid") is above median for a company this size.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($70-75, +20-28%):**
- FIFA 2026 World Cup drives significant incremental revenue in FY2026-2027
- FOX One gains traction at scale, creating a new recurring revenue stream that offsets cord-cutting
- FanDuel option exercised or monetized, unlocking $1-2B in shareholder value
- Aggressive buyback program ($12B authorized) materially reduces share count, driving EPS growth of 10-15% annually
- Smartmatic settles at modest cost ($200-300M)
- Normalized EPS grows to $4.50+ by FY2028 on buybacks + modest organic growth; 16x multiple = $72

**Bear Case ($42-48, -18-28%):**
- FY2026 revenue decline exceeds expectations as cyclical headwinds compound with accelerated cord-cutting
- FOX One cannibalization begins without offsetting subscriber revenue
- Smartmatic trial results in adverse verdict or large settlement ($500M+)
- Sports rights costs escalate beyond advertising revenue growth, compressing margins
- Macro advertising recession (H2 2026 consumer cliff) hits FOX's advertising-dependent model
- Normalized EPS compresses to $3.00-3.25; 14x trough multiple = $42-46

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock declines to $50 or below (forward P/E <13x normalized), providing sufficient margin of safety for the structural risks
2. FOX One reports subscriber count exceeding 5M in first 12 months, demonstrating DTC model viability
3. FanDuel option exercise or monetization announcement providing concrete value realization

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Cable subscriber losses accelerate to >12% annually for two consecutive quarters
2. Smartmatic verdict exceeds $1B
3. FCF declines below $2B on a normalized basis (excluding cyclical events)

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (August 2026 for Q4 FY2026) will provide the first full-year read on FOX One performance and the FIFA World Cup revenue impact. This is the natural reassessment point.

## Conclusion

Fox Corporation is a well-managed media company with genuine competitive advantages (FOX News dominance, premium sports rights, Tubi's AVOD leadership) and a fortress balance sheet. The FanDuel option provides legitimate hidden value. However, the stock at $58 is fairly priced for a mature media business facing structural cord-cutting headwinds, cyclical earnings normalization, and uncertain DTC transition economics. The 15x forward P/E on normalized earnings, in-line with broadcasting peers, does not offer asymmetric upside for a high-conviction position.

The appropriate posture is to hold for the ~1% dividend yield, benefit from aggressive buyback-driven EPS accretion (5-8% annually), and reassess after the FIFA World Cup revenue impact and FOX One traction become visible in FY2026-2027 results. The score trajectory (6.9 → 6.3 → 6.0) reflects diminishing growth optionality as the stock recovered from its February lows without fundamental improvement in the structural picture.
