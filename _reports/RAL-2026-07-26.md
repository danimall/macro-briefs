**Research Date: July 26, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Ralliant is the former Precision Technologies segment of Fortive, spun off as an independent NYSE-listed company on June 28, 2025. It comprises two businesses of very different quality: Sensors and Safety Systems (S&SS, 61% of FY2025 revenue, ~27% operating margin), a defense/space/grid-monitoring franchise with genuine secular tailwinds, and Test and Measurement (T&M, 39%, structurally lower margin), anchored by Tektronix and the poorly-timed 2024 EA Elektro-Automatik EV-test acquisition that has since been written down. The FY2025 GAAP results look catastrophic (-$1.22B net loss, -57% operating margin, Altman Z of 0.32) but are almost entirely an artifact of a $1.44B non-cash goodwill impairment. Underlying, the company generated ~$358M free cash flow (17.3% FCF margin) and mid-teens operating margins ex-impairment.

The investable story is a turn from a demand trough to broad-based recovery. FY2025 was a trough (organic revenue -4.1%, T&M organic -15.3%), while Q1 FY2026 showed a sharp turn to +8.8% organic with both segments accelerating (S&SS +8.8%, T&M +8.7% as communications and diversified-electronics demand recovered). Consensus EPS estimates have risen 13-14% over the past 90 days across current-year and next-year windows, and the company has beaten in all four reported quarters. Management de-risked the balance sheet in March 2026 (refinanced the near-term maturity wall to 2029), increased the buyback authorization from $200M to $500M, launched a $100M ASR, and initiated a $50-60M cost program.

The problem for a fresh buyer is price. The stock has roughly doubled from its ~$42 early-2026 trough to a ~$69 vicinity (52-week high $75.41), and now trades at roughly 26x current-year and 22x next-year consensus EPS, a full multiple that already embeds the recovery, the guidance raise, and the coming cost savings. Estimate revisions are positive but decelerating (30-day revisions near flat versus the +13% 90-day move), and a residual impairment tail remains at T&M (Tektronix trade-name cushion only ~20%, $901M of goodwill still on the books). The score trajectory has improved steadily (4.3 → 5.5 → 5.9), and this analysis confirms the improvement is fundamentally justified, but the risk/reward at $69 is roughly balanced. HOLD with a growth-hold rationale, adding on a pullback toward the mid-$50s.

## Company Overview

Ralliant is a precision-instruments and highly-engineered-products company headquartered in Raleigh, NC, with ~7,000 employees, ~90,000 customers across 90+ countries, ~2,200 active patents, and ~1,300 engineers. Its operating backbone is the Ralliant Business System (RBS), the Danaher/Fortive-lineage continuous-improvement methodology that carried over at the spin. Fortive distributed 1 RAL share for every 3 Fortive shares; regular-way trading began June 30, 2025.

The two segments (FY2025 revenue $2,068.8M):

- **Sensors and Safety Systems (S&SS) — $1,267.3M, ~27% operating margin.** Brands include Qualitrol (power-grid/transformer monitoring), Gems Sensors, Setra, Hengstler Dynapar, Anderson-Negele, Dover Motion, and Pacific Scientific Energetic Materials (PacSci EMC — energetics and ignition-safety systems for satellites, rocket propulsion, and defense). End markets: utilities (grid modernization), defense and space, and industrial sensing. This is the higher-quality, secular-growth, defensively-positioned franchise.

- **Test and Measurement (T&M) — $801.5M, structurally lower margin.** Brands include Tektronix (oscilloscopes, the flagship), Keithley, Sonix, and EA Elektro-Automatik (high-power EV/energy-storage test, the 2024 acquisition that missed on EV-adoption timing). End markets: semiconductor, communications, diversified electronics, EV.

The strategic tension is straightforward: one segment is a defense/grid/sensing compounder, the other is a cyclical, lower-margin portfolio still running at a small operating loss even in a recovering quarter. A two-year Tax Matters Agreement restricts M&A and strategic flexibility (including any T&M divestiture) through roughly mid-2027, after which sum-of-the-parts optionality becomes more actionable.

## Financial Analysis

The reported numbers require decomposition. FY2025 revenue fell 4.0% to $2,068.8M (organic -4.1%: volume -7.3%, price +3.2%), with weakness concentrated in T&M (organic -15.3%, driven by oscilloscopes and EA/high-power). The reported operating loss of -$1,183M and net loss reflect the goodwill impairment tied to EA Elektro-Automatik. Ex-impairment, operating profit was ~$259M (~12.5% margin), gross margin was 50.3%, S&SS ran at 26.9% operating margin, and the business generated $397.6M of operating cash flow and ~$358M of free cash flow. The Altman Z and negative operating margin are non-cash accounting artifacts, not signs of operating distress.

Q1 FY2026 (quarter ended April 3, 2026) is the turn. Revenue rose 11.0% to $534.6M (organic +8.8%: volume +6.9%, price +1.9%), a swing from -4% organic to +9% in two quarters. Both segments turned positive (S&SS +8.8% on defense/space/sensors; T&M +8.7% as its operating loss narrowed from -6.3% to -1.5% margin). Net income was $44.2M, diluted EPS $0.39, operating margin 12.7%. The YoY net income decline (Q1'26 $44.2M vs. carve-out Q1'25 $63.9M) is distorted: the 2025 carve-out period carried zero standalone interest expense and understated public-company overhead, while Q1'26 now bears $14.7M interest and full standalone costs.

On capital structure, total debt is $1.15B (all variable-rate term loans) against $268M cash as of Q1'26. D/E of 2.34x is elevated but appropriate for an industrial with mid-teens margins and 17% FCF conversion, and net leverage sits comfortably below the 3.5x covenant. The debt was created by the spin (RAL borrowed $1.15B and paid it to Fortive as consideration). The current ratio of 0.84 was skewed by the 18-month term loan sitting in current liabilities at year-end; the March 2026 refinancing pushed that maturity to 2029, removing the near-term wall. The earnings-surprise record is clean (four beats in four quarters: Q1'26 +15.3%, Q4'25 +5.2%, Q3'25 +3.7%, Q2'25 +18.1%), which lends confidence to forward consensus.

Financial health scores 6.3: strong FCF generation and a de-risked maturity profile, tempered by 100% floating-rate debt with no hedges (a 100bp move is ~$6M of annual interest, effective rates ~4.83-4.95%) and leverage that is real, if manageable.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis rests on the transition from a demand trough to a broad-based recovery, layered on genuine secular tailwinds. The Q1'26 organic acceleration is corroborated by three converging tailwinds visible in the S&SS end markets: grid modernization/electrification driving Qualitrol demand, a defense-and-space cycle supporting PacSci energetics, and AI-datacenter-adjacent power and communications testing. The recovery in communications and diversified electronics inside T&M suggests the semiconductor/comms test cycle has bottomed.

Estimate revisions confirm positive momentum: current-quarter consensus rose to $0.62 (+18.1% over 90 days), next-quarter to $0.69 (+10.7%), current-year to $2.66 (+13.3%), and next-year to $3.09 (+13.9%). The +30%-threshold BUY signal is not fully triggered here: the 90-day revisions are strong but below the 30% bar, and 30-day revisions have gone nearly flat (+0.3% to +0.7%). The initial re-rating momentum has been priced; consensus is no longer scrambling upward. Combined with revenue growth in the high single digits (rather than >40%), this is a solid inflection, not the extreme, sustained-underestimation pattern that warrants overriding valuation concerns.

Two incremental levers support the forward story: the Enterprise Productivity Program targeting $50-60M net annualized savings by end-2028, and a possible one-time tailwind from IEEPA tariff refund claims (filed, nothing booked). Growth prospects score 6.9: the recovery is real and durable given the S&SS secular exposure, but the pace of positive surprise is normalizing.

## Valuation Assessment

At $69.43 and ~112M shares, Ralliant carries a ~$7.8B market cap on ~$2.1B revenue and ~$260M ex-impairment operating profit. On the correct forward basis, the stock trades at ~26x current-year ($2.66) and ~22x next-year ($3.09) consensus EPS. The trailing GAAP EPS of -$10.84 and reported P/E of zero are meaningless artifacts of the impairment; trailing metrics must be discarded here in favor of forward/normalized earnings.

The peer set is heterogeneous and unhelpful as a median: Novanta at 115x, Cognex 73x, Mirion 168x, Impinj negative, and Vontier at 10x (itself a Fortive spin). The peer median P/E of 115x is distorted by growth-stock outliers; RAL at ~22-26x forward sits well below the median but above the value-oriented Vontier comparable. This is roughly fair for a company with a high-quality 27%-margin segment (S&SS bulls cite ~28% EBITDA there), a recovering cyclical segment, and mid-teens blended margins.

The core issue is that after doubling from ~$42, the current multiple already reflects the good news. Analyst targets bracket the price: low $50, mean $71.18, median $70, high $85, with the mean ~2.5% above spot. On a recovery stock with a reasonable forward multiple, targets can act as floors, but this stock is no longer cheap relative to its growth: PEG is roughly 1.8-2.0x (22x forward on ~11-13% forward EPS growth), above the 1.5x threshold that would justify treating consensus as a stale floor. The re-rating from trough-multiple to a full mid-cycle multiple has largely occurred.

I score valuation 4.8. The prior-appreciation caveat matters, and the doubling is fundamentally justified rather than a reason in itself to mark down, but the absolute forward multiple is full, PEG is above 1.5x, and there is no identified catalyst for a further re-rating from here. The value dimension has been reliably anti-predictive across this system's history, which cuts both ways: it argues against over-rewarding a "cheap" tag (there isn't one here) and against penalizing on trailing optics (correctly avoided). What remains is a stock priced for continued execution, with modest asymmetry.

## Competitive Landscape

Ralliant's competitive trajectory is stable-to-improving, with the mix skewing toward its stronger businesses. In T&M, Tektronix is a strong #2 in oscilloscopes but faces Keysight, the larger and better-capitalized scale leader, alongside Rohde & Schwarz, Ametek, and Teledyne. The EA/EV-test bet within T&M has been the clear competitive misstep, and the impairment is an honest indictment of that timing. Offsetting this, the S&SS franchise holds leadership positions in fragmented markets — grid-transformer monitoring (Qualitrol) and defense energetics (PacSci) — where the competitive set (Esco, Ensign-Bickford, Endress+Hauser, ifm, Brooks Instrument) is diffuse and the tailwinds (grid, defense/space) are structural.

The gross-margin trajectory (50.3% in FY2025, down modestly from 51.6%) and the segment-margin gap (S&SS ~27% vs. T&M near breakeven) suggest the portfolio's value accrues disproportionately to S&SS, with T&M as an option on cyclical recovery or eventual optimization/divestiture post-2027. The RBS operating model provides a credible, repeatable margin-improvement mechanism. Within the Information Technology sector — the one area where security selection has historically added durable alpha — RAL's mix of a defensible sensing/safety franchise and a recovering instrumentation business is a reasonable, if not exceptional, competitive setup. Competitive position scores 6.5.

## Risk Assessment

**EA Elektro-Automatik residual impairment.** The single most important company-specific risk. The write-down reflected a fundamental miss on the January-2024 EA acquisition ($1.72B price, $1.18B goodwill). Management explicitly warns that further impairment could follow if T&M misses revised projections, and the Tektronix trade name's fair value exceeds carrying value by only ~20%, a thin cushion. Remaining T&M goodwill is $901M. This is a binary tail that would be non-cash but sentiment-damaging.

**Floating-rate debt, unhedged.** 100% of the debt is variable-rate with no interest-rate derivatives, a persistent if modest earnings headwind (~$6M per 100bp) in a higher-for-longer rate environment.

**Spin dis-synergies and the first 404 audit.** Standalone costs compressed margins ~170bp in FY2025 and ~260bp in Q1'26. The two-year TMA limits strategic flexibility through mid-2027, indemnities with Fortive are uncapped, and the first full Section 404 internal-controls audit is upcoming, a common stumbling point for newly separated companies.

**Tariff/trade exposure.** ~46-49% of sales are ex-U.S., with recurring management commentary on tariff-driven input-cost pressure. The IEEPA refund claim is a potential modest offset, unbooked.

**Cyclicality and government dependence.** T&M rides semiconductor/EV/comms cycles; S&SS defense/space depends on U.S. budgets.

**Governance.** Typical spin-off entrenchment: staggered board, supermajority provisions, no written-consent or special-meeting rights, all in place until the fourth annual meeting, reducing takeover pressure for ~4 years.

Risk factors score 5.2. The impairment tail and the unhedged floating-rate structure are specific, identifiable downside catalysts not fully priced, offset by a de-risked maturity profile and the fact that the "distress" screen is a false alarm.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing near-term stress. Near-term ATM IV of 60.3% is rich versus 54.2% trailing historical vol, the term structure is in backwardation (60.3% near vs. 54.0% long-dated), and the nearest expiry implies a ±13.8% move, consistent with an imminent earnings catalyst. Put/call skew of 6.0% signals elevated near-term put demand, and the put/call OI ratio has spiked (from ~0.02 to 115.27 in the delta window), indicating heavy defensive positioning ahead of the next print. For a HOLD, this reinforces patience: the market is bracing for a binary move, and the rich near-term IV argues against chasing at $69 before the event resolves. Short interest is moderate at 8.9% of float (3.9 days to cover), not a squeeze setup in either direction.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case (~$85, roughly +22%).** Both segments sustain high-single-digit organic growth as the T&M cycle recovers and S&SS rides the defense/space and grid super-cycles. The cost program lands, standalone dis-synergies fade, and blended operating margins climb back toward mid-teens and higher, driving EPS toward and beyond the $3.09 next-year consensus. The buyback (with the ASR) compounds per-share value at attractive prices, the IEEPA refund provides a one-time tailwind, and post-2027 the market begins to price sum-of-the-parts optionality (a cleaner, higher-multiple S&SS pure-play). This case supports the analyst high of $85.

**Bear case (~$50, roughly -28%).** T&M's recovery stalls, EA misses its revised projections, and a further non-cash impairment reignites concern about capital-allocation quality inherited from the Fortive era. Standalone costs prove stickier than modeled, floating-rate interest bites in a higher-for-longer environment, and the multiple compresses back toward the low-$50s as the recovery premium unwinds. This aligns with the analyst low of $50.

**Base case (~$71).** Organic growth normalizes to mid-single digits, margins improve modestly on the cost program, and EPS tracks the $2.66/$3.09 consensus path. The stock holds a full ~22-24x forward multiple, delivering returns roughly in line with earnings growth plus the ~0.3% dividend. This maps to the ~$71 consensus mean, implying limited upside from $69.

The asymmetry is roughly balanced: ~+22% to the bull case, ~+2-3% to the base case, ~-28% to the bear case. That is a HOLD, not a BUY, at current price.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth-hold).** The 12-month growth thesis is intact and improving, but the re-rating from a ~$42 trough to ~$69 has captured most of the near-term upside. Own on weakness rather than chase at the high.

- **Upgrade triggers (to BUY):** (1) a pullback toward the mid-$50s (~$54-56), which would restore a PEG below 1.5x and meaningful asymmetry to the $85 bull case; (2) a resumption of positive estimate-revision velocity (>+5% over 30 days) alongside a T&M margin turn to sustained positive operating margin; (3) confirmation that further EA/Tektronix impairment risk has cleared (e.g., a clean annual goodwill test with a widening trade-name cushion).
- **Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):** (1) a further material goodwill impairment at T&M or a downward guidance revision; (2) organic revenue growth decelerating back below low-single digits with T&M relapsing to a widening operating loss; (3) a failed Section 404 audit or a material-weakness disclosure; (4) leverage drifting toward the 3.5x covenant.
- **Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (the options market implies a ±13.8% move into the near-term print, ~late August). The mid-2027 TMA-restriction expiry is the key longer-dated re-rating catalyst for sum-of-the-parts optionality.

## Conclusion

Ralliant is a fundamentally improving business whose headline GAAP loss masks ~$358M of free cash flow and a genuine organic-growth turn from -4% to +8.8%. The high-quality S&SS franchise carries real defense/space/grid tailwinds, management has de-risked the balance sheet and is returning capital counter-cyclically, and the earnings-surprise record supports confidence in rising consensus. The score trajectory (4.3 → 5.5 → 5.9 → 6.0) reflects this steadily-confirming thesis, and the new data validates rather than reverses the trend.

The constraint is price. After doubling to ~$69 at a full ~22-26x forward multiple and a PEG near 1.8-2.0x, the stock prices in the recovery, the guidance raise, and the coming cost savings, with 30-day estimate revisions now flat. Consensus targets bracket the price (mean $71, ~2-3% above spot), the EA/Tektronix impairment tail remains open, and the options market is bracing for a binary near-term move. The risk/reward at $69 is roughly balanced: limited base-case upside against a meaningful bear-case drawdown to the low-$50s. This is a fourth consecutive HOLD, and the bar for continuing it is met: the business quality justifies ownership on weakness, but the current price does not offer the asymmetry required for a fresh purchase. Accumulate toward the mid-$50s; hold existing positions.
