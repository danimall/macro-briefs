## Executive Summary

Protagonist Therapeutics has, over the past five months, become a different company than the one most models were built for. FDA approved ICOTYDE (icotrokinra) on March 18, 2026, and Johnson & Johnson — which owns, manufactures, promotes and books the product — began launching it immediately. In April 2026 Protagonist exercised its U.S. opt-out on rusfertide, handing Takeda exclusive worldwide rights in exchange for $200M received, a further $200M plus a $75M milestone contingent on FDA approval, up to $775M of sales milestones, and stepped-up worldwide royalties of 14%–29%. The company now owns two royalty streams it does not control, a wholly-owned Phase 1 pipeline, $849.5M of cash and marketable securities, and no debt.

The Q2 2026 print reads spectacularly and means very little. Revenue of $213.5M produced $162.8M of net income and $2.29 of diluted EPS, but $192.4M of that revenue — roughly 90% — was proportional recognition of the non-refundable $200M Takeda opt-out payment. Strip it out and the quarter's underlying pre-tax result was approximately negative $27M ($21.1M residual revenue less $54.7M of operating expense plus $6.4M of interest income). The residual $21.1M is itself described as primarily development services, post-opt-out wind-down services and clinical supplies — revenue that terminates by contract. In ICOTYDE's first full commercial quarter neither Protagonist nor J&J disclosed a royalty or product-level revenue figure, even as J&J quantified Tremfya at +71% growth on the same call and reported that more than 10,000 patients had initiated ICOTYDE therapy since March. Clinical uptake appears genuinely strong; the financial translation has not started to scale.

That gap is the crux of the recommendation. At $135.81 the enterprise value is roughly $8.79B ($8.52B pro forma for the $275M of approval-contingent rusfertide payments). Using the company's own royalty reference points — ICOTYDE at $4B of annual net sales at a ~7.25% weighted rate, rusfertide at $1.5B at a ~21% weighted rate — combined peak royalty is about $605M per year, so the shares trade near 14–15x undiscounted peak royalty before corporate expense, before a normalized cash tax rate, and before discounting the eight-to-twelve-year gap to those levels. On a present-value basis the two partnered franchises support roughly $65–75 per share; the remaining $60–70 of the price rests on the wholly-owned pipeline and on peak sales above the anchors. With a binary rusfertide PDUFA decision due within weeks, near-term implied volatility at 83.4% versus 49.8% realized, and the stock already trading above the $133.31 consensus mean target, we move to HOLD with a lower score. This is the second consecutive HOLD; the reduction from 6.3 to 5.75 reflects new information from the August 5 print rather than the 2.8% price decline since the last review.

## Company Overview

Protagonist is a peptide discovery company operating with 132 full-time employees at year-end 2025 (103 in R&D). It has never recorded product sales. Its economics are entirely indirect and rest on four buckets:

**ICOTYDE (icotrokinra)** — oral IL-23 receptor antagonist peptide, jointly discovered with J&J scientists, with J&J assuming all development and commercialization after Phase 1. Approved March 18, 2026 for moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in adults and patients ≥12 years weighing ≥40 kg. Protagonist receives tiered royalties of 6%–10% on global net sales (approximately 7.25% weighted at $4B of annual net sales, with a 10% tier above $4B) plus up to $580M of remaining regulatory and sales milestones. A $50M approval milestone was earned in Q1 2026. Phase 3 programs are running in psoriatic arthritis, ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease.

**Rusfertide** — first-in-class subcutaneous hepcidin mimetic for polycythemia vera, discovered by Protagonist and developed through the Phase 3 VERIFY trial, now wholly Takeda's. NDA under Priority Review with an August 2026 PDUFA date; Breakthrough Therapy, Orphan Drug and Fast Track designations. Approval triggers $275M of cash to Protagonist and starts the 14%–29% royalty.

**Wholly-owned pipeline** — PN-881 (oral IL-17 antagonist, Phase 2/2b psoriasis program to initiate early Q1 2027), PN-477 (triple GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist, subcutaneous Phase 1 begun, oral Phase 1 expected 1H 2027), PN-458o (oral dual GLP-1/GIP, Phase 1 2H 2027), PN-8047 (oral hepcidin mimetic, Phase 1 Q1 2027), plus discovery-stage oral IL-4Rα and amylin programs.

**The platform** — de novo peptide discovery, now validated by two first-in-class molecules that have reached approval or the approval doorstep. The 10-K discloses that the platform is protected primarily by trade secrets and confidentiality agreements rather than patents, which is a materially weaker form of protection than a patent estate.

Both license agreements are terminable for convenience. J&J holds sole decision authority over ICOTYDE development and commercialization; Protagonist has no sales force and, per the 10-K, "generally will not have discretion over marketing decisions."

## Financial Analysis

Revenue is lumpy collaboration revenue, so year-over-year comparisons require decomposition. Net income by year: $(125.6)M in 2021, $(127.4)M in 2022, $(79.0)M in 2023, +$275.2M in 2024 on roughly $434M of revenue (Takeda upfront plus J&J milestones), and $(130.1)M in 2025 on roughly $46M of revenue. Accumulated deficit was $(470.7)M at 12/31/2025, improving to $(304.0)M at 6/30/2026 on the Q1 milestone and Q2 opt-out recognition.

**Q2 2026 (GAAP):** revenue $213.5M; operating expense $54.7M (R&D $42.1M, G&A $12.6M); operating income $158.8M; interest income $6.4M; tax expense $2.3M; net income $162.8M; EPS $2.47 basic / $2.29 diluted. Revenue composition: $192.4M of opt-out recognition plus $21.1M of development services, wind-down services and clinical supplies "and other revenues." Annualizing $2.29 to $9.16 would be an error of category. The correct read of underlying quarterly economics is approximately a $27M pre-tax loss.

**Balance sheet.** Cash and marketable securities $849.5M at 6/30/2026 versus $646.0M at 12/31/2025 — a balance movement of +$203.4M, not a cash-flow-statement figure, broadly reconciled by the $200M Takeda receipt, $12.3M of first-half interest income and equity-issuance proceeds against roughly $86M of cash operating expense (1H opex $114.7M less $28.6M of stock compensation). Working capital $770.0M; total assets $885.5M; stockholders' equity $841.5M; deferred revenue only $9.7M; no debt. Screening metrics on the FY2025 basis show an Altman Z near 8.4 and a current ratio near 12.7. Those establish solvency, not value: for a debt-free entity whose principal economic assets are contingent royalty rights held off balance sheet, leverage-based distress models carry no information about price.

**Burn and runway.** Q2 cash operating expense was roughly $40.7M ($54.7M less $14.0M of stock compensation), or about $163M annualized, against roughly $26M per year of interest income at the current rate — approximately $137M of net cash burn before milestones. Management has guided that R&D will "increase significantly in the second half of 2026" on the PN-881 Phase 2 program, at-risk clinical manufacturing for PN-477sc, PN-458 and PN-8047, additional discovery programs and higher headcount. At a 2027 burn of $180M–$220M against $849.5M of current liquidity, plus the $275M contingent on rusfertide approval, pro forma liquidity of roughly $1.12B implies about five years of runway before any material royalty contribution. This is a sensitivity, not a forecast: it does not reconcile working-capital timing, cash taxes or milestone receipts, and the R&D step-up is guided but unquantified. The practical conclusion holds regardless — Protagonist is under no pressure to issue equity, which is a real strategic advantage at this share price.

**The tax shield is finite and is a valuation input.** Q2 tax expense of $2.265M on $165.1M of pre-tax income is a 1.37% effective rate; the first half was 0.46%. The 10-K discloses that the company has already experienced ownership changes producing annual §382/§383 limitations on NOL and credit use. Long-run royalty cash flows should be modeled at a materially higher cash tax rate. The book accumulated deficit is not a proxy for the usable NOL pool; the income-tax footnote detail is not in hand, so the shield's magnitude is unquantified.

**Data-basis notes.** Supplied trailing and forward P/E of -75.81 are identical and stale relative to the Q2 result; they carry no information. Three price observations appear in the data — official close $135.81, daily-history close $137.32 (+1.11%), and an extended-hours last trade of $139.65 (+2.83%, which produces the supplied ~$8.98B market cap). All arithmetic here uses the $135.81 official close.

**Earnings-surprise history has low predictive content for this issuer.** The Q1 2026 "beat" of 109.2% ($0.05 versus $(0.54) estimated) was the $50M ICOTYDE approval milestone landing in the quarter; the Q4 2025 miss of 38.5% was milestone timing in the other direction. A three-of-four beat record on a P&L driven by third-party regulatory events should not raise confidence in forward consensus.

## Growth Analysis

**The estimate-revision signal is present numerically and does not apply.** Current-year consensus EPS has risen 61.1% in 90 days to $4.47 and the current quarter is up 36.4%; on the surface this is the revision velocity that normally precedes further re-rating. But next-year consensus is $(0.19). The revisions are analysts marking to the recognition schedule of the $200M opt-out payment and the $50M ICOTYDE milestone, not to an operating inflection, and the reversal to a loss in 2027 confirms it. This is precisely the case where the revision signal must be discounted: there is no cheap forward multiple against improving estimates, because the improved estimates are non-recurring and the forward multiple is negative.

The genuine growth thesis operates on a longer clock and runs through three channels.

**ICOTYDE royalty ramp.** J&J reported more than 10,000 patients initiating therapy since the March launch, with uptake "accelerating" and "outpacing competitors at comparable points in their launches." As a calibration exercise: 10,000 patients on paid, persistent therapy at an illustrative $45,000 U.S. annualized net realized price (an assumption — neither company has disclosed net price) would be $450M of annualized net sales, generating roughly $27M per year to Protagonist at the bottom 6% tier. Assume half of initiated patients convert to persistent reimbursed therapy and the figure is nearer $13M. Both numbers are trivial against an $8.8B market capitalization, and both are consistent with the absence of a disclosed royalty line. The direction is right; the magnitude in year one is small.

**Indication expansion.** J&J guided to a Phase 3 psoriatic arthritis readout for ICOTYDE in 2H 2026, with ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease also in Phase 3. Each successful indication broadens the royalty base without cost to Protagonist and is the cleanest identifiable driver of upward revision to the peak-sales anchor.

**Rusfertide launch.** Contingent on the August PDUFA. Approval starts a 14%–29% royalty on a first-in-class mechanism in polycythemia vera and unlocks up to $775M of sales milestones.

Against those, reported financials deteriorate before they improve. Opt-out recognition completes, wind-down service revenue terminates by design, and R&D rises significantly. Investors should expect 2027 to look like a loss-making Phase 2 biotech with a growing royalty line, not like a profitable royalty company.

## Valuation Assessment

Peer multiples are close to useless here. Of the five nearest-market-cap biotechnology comparables, three (NUVL, KYMR, APGE) have negative earnings, and the two with positive P/Es — KRYS at 43.1 and HALO at 24.9 — own and sell their own products. The relevant relative observation is structural: PTGX sits at the top of the $8–10B cohort as the only member with an approved product and a second at the PDUFA stage, but it captures 6%–10% and 14%–29% of net sales rather than the full commercial margin. Equal enterprise value therefore requires a multiple of the underlying commercial opportunity that peers need.

**The royalty arithmetic.** On the issuer's own anchors, combined peak royalty is $290M (ICOTYDE, $4B × 7.25%) plus $315M (rusfertide, $1.5B × 21%) ≈ $605M per year at near-100% gross margin. Against a diluted enterprise value of roughly $8.79B, that is about 14.5x undiscounted peak royalty, or 14.1x on the $8.52B pro forma for the $275M of approval-contingent payments. Pair more conservative peaks — ICOTYDE $1.5B, rusfertide $600M — and interpolating toward the low end of the disclosed tiers gives roughly $185M per year, or about 47x. The disclosed schedules provide only two anchor points, so the interpolation is inference rather than a disclosed rate.

**A present-value cross-check.** Take the optimistic anchors at face value: $605M of peak royalty, taxed at 21% (above the current 1.4% rate, given §382 limitations already incurred), less corporate operating expense at a mature run rate, leaves roughly $330M–$400M of attributable free cash in the peak year — about $4.65–$5.63 per diluted share. Capitalize at a generous 20x for a royalty stream with rusfertide's U.S. patent expiring in 2034 (extendable up to five years) and discount eight years at 10%, and the peak-year value discounts back to roughly $43–$53 per share. Add pro forma net cash of about $15.80 per diluted share and a risk-adjusted, discounted credit for the $1.63B of contingent milestone pools of roughly $4–6 per share, and the two partnered franchises support approximately **$65–$75 per share of present value** on the company's own peak assumptions.

The stock is $135.81. Roughly $60–$70 per share, or about half the market value, therefore rests on the wholly-owned pipeline plus peak sales above the anchors. The wholly-owned pipeline consists of a Phase 1-complete oral IL-17 peptide whose Phase 2b starts in Q1 2027 (first efficacy data plausibly 2028) and obesity incretin programs entering Phase 1 in 2026–2027 against multiple large-cap Phase 3 competitors. PN-881's advance to Phase 2b rests on pharmacokinetic evidence — 24-hour Ctrough above prospectively targeted IC90 values — which de-risks oral delivery but is not clinical efficacy in patients.

**Consensus context.** Analyst price targets span $110 to $172 with a mean of $133.31 and a median of $130.00; the current price is 1.9% above the mean and 4.5% above the median. Ratings are 3 strong buy, 9 buy, 1 hold, unchanged for three months. The guardrail on treating targets as floors applies to stocks that look cheap against rising estimates; this stock trades above consensus on a negative forward EPS with the value case resting on option premia. Targets here are a ceiling-ish reference, not confirmation.

The $1.63B of headline contingent payments (up to $580M ICOTYDE, $775M rusfertide sales milestones, $275M approval-contingent) is not backlog, not deferred revenue and not a remaining performance obligation. Deferred revenue is $9.7M. Every dollar is conditioned on approvals and net-sales thresholds achieved by third parties Protagonist does not control.

Valuation score: 4.5. The stock is 2.8% cheaper than at the July review, which helps at the margin, but the Q2 disclosure that royalty translation is not yet visible offsets it.

## Competitive Landscape

**Psoriasis.** ICOTYDE is the first and only FDA-approved targeted oral peptide blocking the IL-23 receptor, supported by four Phase 3 studies meeting all primary endpoints, and it is being launched by the strongest immunology commercial organization in the industry. J&J's framing bears on the addressable pool: "With the dual powerhouse of Tremfya and icotide in psoriasis, we now have a complementary portfolio of both first-line biologic and first-line systemic treatments." That positions ICOTYDE upstream of biologics, into the first-line oral systemic slot contested by apremilast and the oral TYK2 class. The patient pool there is far larger, which supports both the >10,000-patient start figure and the $4B peak anchor. It also historically realizes lower net price per patient than biologic therapy, so volume may convert into less revenue per prescription than a biologic-displacement strategy would. An alternative reading is that real-world prescribing skews to biologic-experienced patients at higher net price and the call language was simply non-cannibalization framing for investors. Neither reading is established.

A latent misalignment worth monitoring: J&J retains 100% of Tremfya economics and pays a 6%–10% royalty on ICOTYDE. J&J's disclosed conduct — heavy launch investment, 60bp of SM&A deleverage attributed to new product launches, "uptake accelerating" — is revealed-preference evidence of genuine prioritization, so this is a latent rather than an observed conflict.

**Polycythemia vera.** Rusfertide would be first-in-class against an incumbent branded option (PharmaEssentia's Besremi, approved November 2021) and a volume standard of care of phlebotomy and hydroxyurea. The 10-K is unusually candid that phlebotomy and chelation "are inexpensive and may present pricing challenges." Orphan exclusivity helps but, as the filing notes, does not bar different active moieties approved for the same condition.

**Platform.** Two first-in-class validations from a 132-person company is rare and real. The offsets are that the platform is protected primarily by trade secrets rather than patents, and that the wholly-owned programs target the two most crowded arenas in biopharma. Protagonist enters obesity with a subcutaneous Phase 1 triple agonist in 2026 and an oral Phase 1 not expected until 1H 2027 — late, against multiple Phase 3 programs from far better-capitalized sponsors.

Trajectory assessment: improving at the asset level (an approval secured, a second pending), stable-to-weaker at the platform-economics level (fractional capture, late entry in the wholly-owned fields). Competitive position 6.0, down from 6.5.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Binary PDUFA within weeks.** Approval triggers $275M of cash ($200M second opt-out fee plus $75M milestone), unlocks up to $775M of sales milestones and starts the royalty. As of the latest filing (8-K dated 2026-08-05) no approval or CRL 8-K had been filed, implying the decision was still pending. Priority Review, Breakthrough designation and a Phase 3 that met its primary endpoint raise the probability but do not assure it; the 10-K enumerates CRL, advisory committee and facility-inspection paths.

**2. Asset-specific safety overhang.** In September 2021 FDA placed rusfertide studies on a brief full clinical hold following a rasH2 transgenic mouse finding of benign and malignant subcutaneous skin tumors. For a lifelong therapy in a non-fatal chronic disease, carcinogenicity language in the label is a direct commercial variable that can constrain uptake even on approval.

**3. Total dependence on two licensees with no operational control.** Both agreements are terminable for convenience. J&J holds sole development and commercialization authority. Protagonist has no sales force and explicitly flags risk of disagreements over milestones and royalty payments. Protagonist is currently a service provider to Takeda on wind-down work that terminates by design.

**4. Drug pricing policy hits royalties one-for-one.** Royalties compute on net sales with no cost of goods to absorb pressure. Disclosed exposures include the May 2025 most-favored-nation pricing executive order, IRA negotiation and inflation-rebate mechanics, and the September 2025 announcement of tariffs of up to 100% on imported branded pharmaceuticals. J&J separately referenced a pricing agreement with the U.S. government on its Q2 call, indicating its net-price environment is being actively reset.

**5. Finite royalty life and an unquantified exclusivity input.** Rusfertide's U.S. patent expires in 2034, extendable up to five years. No equivalent icotrokinra expiry was located; that is a first-order valuation input that remains unquantified.

**6. Rising cash tax rate.** The 1.4% Q2 effective rate is unsustainable, and §382 limitations on the NOL pool are already in place.

**7. Disclosure staleness.** The FY2025 10-K was filed February 25, 2026 — three weeks before ICOTYDE's approval — and states in the present tense that the company has no approved products. The 10-Q updated only two risk areas (Takeda dependence post-opt-out, macro/tariffs). No filing yet contains a full commercial-stage risk framework covering royalty audit rights, gross-to-net dynamics or payer step-therapy coverage for an oral.

**8. Governance and operational items.** Classified board, blank-check preferred, Delaware exclusive forum and DGCL §203 coverage reduce takeover optionality for an asset base that would otherwise attract royalty aggregators or acquirers. Contract manufacturers are described as sole sources of clinical supply; the company has no internal audit group and does not carry earthquake insurance on Bay Area facilities.

**9. Valuation risk itself.** Because roughly half the price rests on option value, a disappointment in either the royalty ramp or the pipeline can compress the multiple without any change in cash.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing an imminent event and pricing it richly. Near-term ATM implied volatility is 83.4% against 49.8% one-year realized — a 33.6-point spread at the 80th percentile of the current chain — with a term structure in clear backwardation (83.4% at 14 days, 56.4% at 42 days, 42.4% at 161 days). The August 21 tenor implies a ±13.9% move. ATM IV has risen from 56.6% on July 19 to 83.4% while the stock fell 2.5%, which is consistent with hedging into the PDUFA rather than directional repositioning. Put/call skew is characterized as balanced at 0.9%, so no directional inference is available from skew. Put/call volume is 23.50 while put/call open interest is 0.04, meaning heavy same-session put activity has not accumulated into standing put positioning; we would not read a bearish conclusion from the volume figure alone.

Short interest is 15.0% of float at 8.7 days to cover. That cuts both ways: it corroborates a real institutional bear case, and it creates squeeze mechanics on a clean approval. The practical takeaway for a fundamental holder is that near-term optionality is expensive, the event is priced, and the asymmetry available to new capital ahead of the decision is poor.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $185 (+36%)

Rusfertide is approved in August with a label that does not restrict chronic use, delivering $275M of cash and starting a 14%–29% royalty on a first-in-class PV therapy with orphan exclusivity. The ICOTYDE psoriatic arthritis Phase 3 reads out positively in 2H 2026, and J&J's UC and Crohn's programs keep the $4B peak anchor credible or push it higher. The first quantified royalty disclosures in the Q3 or Q4 reports show gross-to-net drag unwinding faster than expected, converting the >10,000 initiated patients into a visible, compounding revenue line. PN-881's Phase 2b starts on schedule in Q1 2027, and the market continues to capitalize the oral IL-17 and oral incretin programs as credible option value on a platform with two first-in-class validations. The $172 high target becomes the anchor. Financing risk is nil throughout — $849.5M of cash means no equity issuance into strength.

### Bear Case — $95 (-30%)

The PDUFA produces a complete response letter, or an approval with carcinogenicity labeling that materially constrains uptake in a chronic, non-fatal disease where cheap phlebotomy is the volume standard of care. The $275M does not arrive, the $775M sales-milestone pool becomes remote, and the rusfertide half of the royalty arithmetic — roughly $315M of the $605M peak anchor — is impaired. Simultaneously, ICOTYDE royalties are disclosed in the Q3 print at a level that annualizes below $50M, confirming that first-line oral systemic positioning trades volume for net price, and MFN or IRA mechanics compress the net-sales base on which royalties are computed. Reported 2027 financials revert to a loss-making Phase 2 company with R&D guided sharply higher, and the market re-prices toward the present value of the partnered franchises plus cash, which our arithmetic puts nearer $65–75 per share before any pipeline credit. A move to $95 would still leave meaningful pipeline value embedded, and the lowest published target is $110.

### Base Case — $148 (+9%)

Rusfertide is approved, $275M lands, and the royalty begins from a small base. The PsA readout is positive but does not change 2026 or 2027 cash flow. The first quantified ICOTYDE royalty disclosures land in the low tens of millions annualized — good for a launch, immaterial to a $9B capitalization — and consensus targets drift up modestly from $133.31 as analysts add the approval milestone and the PsA indication. The stock holds a mid-teens multiple on peak royalty and gains roughly the $3.90 per diluted share of contingent cash plus a modest upward revision to peak assumptions. Reported earnings deteriorate through 2027 as opt-out recognition completes and R&D steps up; the shares track catalyst news rather than financials.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD.** Existing holders should carry the position through the August PDUFA; the probability-weighted outcome is positive and hedging at 83.4% implied volatility is expensive. New capital has no asymmetry at $135.81, above the consensus mean of $133.31 and days from a binary.

**Reassessment timeline:** the rusfertide PDUFA decision (days to weeks); J&J's Q3 call in late October for the first potential ICOTYDE quantification; Protagonist's Q3 report in early November for the first royalty line and 2027 R&D framing; the ICOTYDE psoriatic arthritis Phase 3 readout in 2H 2026.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. Rusfertide approval with a label free of carcinogenicity restrictions on chronic use, confirmed by 8-K, plus receipt of the $275M.
2. A separately disclosed ICOTYDE royalty of $25M or more in any single quarter (annualizing above $100M), which would demonstrate that gross-to-net conversion is running well ahead of our calibration and would materially raise the credible peak anchor.
3. Price toward $105–110 — below the lowest published target — with the royalty and pipeline theses intact, which would restore a margin of safety against the $65–75 present value of the partnered franchises plus a defensible pipeline credit.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. A complete response letter, or an approval carrying a boxed or carcinogenicity warning that constrains chronic dosing.
2. Any termination, regional termination or de-prioritization language from J&J or Takeda, or a disclosed royalty dispute.
3. 2027 cash operating expense guided above $250M without a royalty line of at least $75M annualized, or a delay of PN-881's Phase 2b initiation beyond Q1 2027, which would push first efficacy data past 2028 and extend the period during which the pipeline half of the valuation has no evidence behind it.

## Conclusion

Protagonist has executed well. It discovered two first-in-class peptides with 132 employees, monetized one for $475M of cash consideration plus stepped-up royalties rather than building a commercial organization it could not afford, reached an FDA approval on the other with the best immunology partner available, and sits on $849.5M of cash with no debt and roughly five years of runway. The opt-out decision in particular looks like sound capital allocation for a company of this size.

The problem is price. The Q2 report's $2.29 of diluted EPS was ~90% non-recurring, the underlying quarter lost about $27M pre-tax, and ICOTYDE's first full commercial quarter produced no separately disclosed royalty from either party. Using the company's own peak anchors — $4B of ICOTYDE net sales at 7.25% and $1.5B of rusfertide net sales at 21% — the two partnered franchises support roughly $65–75 per share of present value after tax, discounting and corporate expense. At $135.81 the remainder is a bet on a Phase 1 oral IL-17 program with no efficacy data until approximately 2028 and on obesity incretins entering the clinic several years behind the field. The consensus mean target sits below the current price, near-term implied volatility is nearly 34 points above realized, and 15% of the float is short into a binary regulatory decision.

The score declines from 6.3 to 5.75 on new information rather than on price: the revenue decomposition, the absence of a quantified royalty in the launch quarter, the guided R&D step-up, and the compression of a major binary from months to weeks. We would become buyers on demonstrated royalty conversion — a quarter with a disclosed ICOTYDE royalty above $25M — or on a price in the $105–110 range. Until one of those arrives, HOLD.
