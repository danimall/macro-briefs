## Executive Summary

TD SYNNEX delivered a strong Q1 FY2026 quarter: revenue of $17.2B (+18% YoY), GAAP EPS of $4.04 (vs. $1.98 prior year), and the formal breakout of Hyve Solutions as a separate reportable segment generating $2.15B in revenue at 7.2% operating margins. The Hyve segment designs and manufactures custom AI/accelerated compute infrastructure for hyperscale cloud customers. Its actual economic activity is growing far faster than reported revenue due to a shift toward net revenue recognition (customer-owned procurement models), which the 10-Q states negatively impacted Hyve's revenue growth compared to the prior period by approximately 71%.

The stock has appreciated approximately 75-78% over the past year, moving from the low-$90s range to approximately $187. This appreciation is fundamentally supported: consensus EPS for the current year has been revised upward 14.5% over 90 days (from $14.63 to $16.76), and the Q1 beat of 41.6% ($4.73 vs. $3.34 expected) demonstrates the market was materially underestimating Hyve's contribution. At approximately 11-13x current-year non-GAAP EPS of $16.76, the stock trades at a discount to distribution peers (median 16.8x) and a significant discount to AI infrastructure plays (Celestica, Jabil at 20-30x). The risk that prevents a higher-conviction call is concentrated customer exposure in Hyve, tariff uncertainty on Asian-sourced inputs, and proximity to the 52-week high of approximately $192, meaning any negative surprise has meaningful downside from current levels.

## Company Overview

TD SYNNEX is a leading global IT distributor and solutions aggregator, formed from the 2021 merger of SYNNEX Corporation and Tech Data Corporation. The company operates across four segments: Americas Distribution (45% of Q1 revenue), Europe Distribution (36%), APJ Distribution (6%), and the newly separated Hyve Solutions (13%). The distribution business connects ~2,500 OEM vendors with 150,000+ resellers globally, while Hyve designs and manufactures custom compute infrastructure for hyperscale cloud providers.

Key suppliers include Apple (12% of revenue) and HP Inc. (10%). One customer represents 11% of FY2025 revenue — the company does not identify this customer, though the 10-K notes that the systems design and integration business has significant customer concentration. The company employs a capital-light distribution model augmented by Hyve's higher-margin manufacturing capabilities.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue & Profitability Inflection:**

Q1 FY2026 demonstrated acceleration across all metrics. Revenue grew 18.1% YoY (13.2% constant currency), with Hyve contributing disproportionately to margin expansion. Gross margin improved 43bps to 7.30%, and operating margin expanded 75bps to 2.85%. Non-GAAP EPS of $4.73 represented 69% YoY growth.

The accounting distortion in Hyve is critical to understand: as customers shift to procurement models where TD SYNNEX doesn't take ownership risk, revenue is recognized on a net basis (margin only). This mechanically reduces reported revenue while improving gross margins. Based on the 10-Q's disclosure that net revenue recognition negatively impacted Hyve's revenue growth by approximately 71%, estimated underlying gross billings growth in Hyve was potentially in the range of 90-95% YoY. The company's operational momentum significantly exceeds what headline numbers suggest.

**Balance Sheet:**

Total debt of approximately $4.7B ($1.13B current borrowings + $3.59B long-term borrowings) with stockholders' equity of $8.78B yields a debt-to-equity ratio of approximately 0.54x. The $3.5B undrawn revolver, $1.5B undrawn AR securitization, and $1.56B cash provide ample liquidity. The $700M notes due August 2026 are easily manageable. Cash conversion cycle of 23 days and $3.4-3.7B in supplier finance programs (vendor-funded extended payment terms) provide structural working capital advantages.

**Capital Return:**

Share count has declined 13% over 2.5 years (92.6M to 80.4M) through aggressive buybacks ($596M in FY2025). Combined with the $1.92/share annual dividend (~1.0% yield), total shareholder returns are substantial. The $1.1B remaining buyback authorization provides continued EPS support.

**FCF Generation:**

FY2025 FCF of $1.39B on $62.5B revenue (2.2% margin) translates to approximately $17.30/share in FCF. At ~$187, FCF yield is approximately 9.3%. However, Q1 FY2026 saw negative free cash flow of approximately $929M, reflecting significant working capital absorption as sales volumes surged — a pattern management has noted is inherent to the business model when revenues are growing rapidly. With Hyve's margin expansion trajectory and normalization of working capital over the balance of the year, full-year FCF conversion should remain healthy.

## Competitive Landscape

TD SYNNEX holds a leading position globally in IT distribution by revenue, providing scale advantages in procurement, logistics, and vendor relationships. The 2021 Tech Data merger created operational synergies estimated at $200M+ annually.

**Hyve Solutions' competitive differentiation** comes from combining distribution scale (access to components, vendor relationships) with ODM manufacturing capabilities. Pure ODMs (Quanta, Wiwynn) lack the distribution relationships; pure distributors (Ingram Micro, Arrow) lack the manufacturing capability. This hybrid model creates a structural advantage for hyperscale customers seeking a single partner for both sourcing and assembly.

The competitive position is improving: Hyve's margin expansion from 5.4% to 7.2% operating margins YoY suggests pricing power or efficiency gains. The segment restructuring signals management confidence that the business has reached critical mass.

**Risk to competitive position:** Hyperscale customers have the engineering capability to design their own hardware and source directly. The shift toward customer-owned procurement (driving net revenue recognition) could eventually lead to full disintermediation if customers determine they don't need TD SYNNEX's manufacturing services.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Customer Concentration in Hyve (HIGH IMPACT):** With one customer at 11% of total company revenue and the 10-K disclosing significant customer concentration in systems design and integration, Hyve likely has a small number of customers representing a substantial majority of its run-rate. Loss of any single hyperscale customer would be highly impactful to the growth thesis. There are no volume guarantees in customer contracts.

**2. Tariff Exposure (MODERATE):** While distribution typically passes tariff costs through to customers, Hyve's manufacturing operations source components from Asia. Extended tariff uncertainty could delay hyperscale procurement decisions, creating quarterly volatility. The company flagged tariff risk prominently in both the 10-K and 10-Q.

**3. Valuation Vulnerability Near 52-Week High:** At approximately $187 (near the ~$192 52-week high), the stock has limited cushion. Any negative surprise — a Hyve customer loss, tariff-driven volume deceleration, or broader AI infrastructure spending pause — would trigger a correction from an elevated base.

**4. Working Capital Intensity:** The business is highly working-capital intensive. Q1 FY2026's approximately $929M in negative free cash flow demonstrates this dynamic when sales volumes surge. While expected to normalize over the fiscal year, it creates quarterly cash flow volatility.

**5. Cybersecurity Risk:** Four breaches in four years (2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) suggests systemic vulnerability. While management claims no material impact, this pattern is concerning.

**6. Insider Selling:** All insider activity is selling — CEO ($2.4M), CFO ($611K), former CEO/director (exercised all options at $107 and sold at $155-160). No open-market purchases. While some transactions occur under pre-established Rule 10b5-1 plans (such as Richard Hume's sales) and may be routine, the complete absence of any buying alongside strong stock appreciation warrants attention.

## Conclusion

TD SYNNEX merits a BUY recommendation driven primarily by the Hyve Solutions thesis: a high-growth, higher-margin AI infrastructure business now separately reported and growing at rates well above what headline revenue figures suggest due to net revenue accounting. The +14.5% estimate revision over 90 days, 41.6% Q1 earnings beat, and broad semiconductor demand confirmations support continued momentum.

The conviction level is moderate rather than high because: (1) the stock is trading near its 52-week high with analyst consensus targets only modestly above current levels, (2) Hyve customer concentration creates binary risk, (3) insider selling is universal with no offsetting purchases (though some sales occur under 10b5-1 plans), and (4) Q1's significant working capital cash absorption demonstrates the capital-intensive nature of rapid growth. The position is appropriate at 2% portfolio weight rather than a full 3-4% allocation, with willingness to add on a pullback toward $160-170 where the risk/reward improves materially.
