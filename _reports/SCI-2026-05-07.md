## Executive Summary

Service Corporation International is North America's dominant deathcare provider with 1,487 funeral locations and 503 cemeteries, commanding approximately 18% market share in a $21B+ industry. The company's core asset is a $17.0 billion preneed backlog — expected future revenue from pre-planned funeral and cemetery services — that is now entering its demographic sweet spot as the first Baby Boomers turn 80 in 2026.

The stock declined roughly 12% from its late-April high of $88.67 to the current $78.44, driven by a Q1 2026 EPS miss ($0.97 actual vs. approximately $1.01 consensus) caused by an unfavorable flu-season volume comparison. This selloff creates an entry point at ~19x forward earnings for a business generating $554M in annual free cash flow, buying back 3-4% of its float annually at these prices, and positioned to benefit from a multi-decade demographic tailwind that begins in earnest now. Analyst targets range from $90 to $100, implying 15-27% upside.

The key question is whether the Q1 volume decline signals something structural or is genuinely temporary. The evidence supports the latter: cemetery preneed production grew 10%, operating cash flow rose 6% YoY, funeral average revenue per service grew 3.5%, and management confirmed full-year guidance of $4.05-$4.35 adjusted EPS. The selloff provides an asymmetric risk/reward entry into a business with few public comparables and structural competitive advantages.

## Company Overview

SCI operates a dual-segment deathcare platform:

**Funeral Operations ($2.41B revenue FY2025):** Services include arranging and conducting funeral ceremonies, cremations, and providing caskets, urns, and related merchandise. The company operates under the Dignity Memorial brand (premium positioning) and Neptune Society/National Cremation Society (value segment via SCI Direct). Comparable average revenue per funeral service was $5,823 in FY2025, up from $5,658 in FY2024.

**Cemetery Operations ($1.90B revenue FY2025):** Includes sales of burial lots, mausoleum crypts, cremation niches, cemetery memorialization products, and perpetual care services. Cemetery margins (32-34%) significantly exceed funeral margins (20-24%) due to real estate economics and lower variable costs.

**Preneed Business Model:** SCI sells approximately $2.6B annually in preneed contracts (future funeral/cemetery arrangements funded via insurance policies or trust deposits). These create a $17.0B backlog of expected future revenue. The insurance-funded portion ($8.73B) pays out at face value upon death, reducing trust-market exposure but introducing counterparty/insurer credit risk. The trust-funded portion ($5.78B at fair value) carries investment risk but returned 15.1% in 2025. Insurance-funded funeral preneed maturities are already accelerating: growing from 62,138 to 65,452 contracts in 2025 (+5.3%), directly reflecting the aging Boomer cohort.

## Growth Analysis

**Demographic Tailwind — The Core Growth Engine:**
U.S. deaths are projected to rise from ~3.1M (2025) to ~3.7M (2040) as Baby Boomers (born 1946-1964) enter peak mortality years. The first Boomers turned 80 in 2026. SCI's preneed backlog was built specifically to capture this wave. Insurance-funded preneed maturities are already accelerating at over 5% annually, and this rate should compound through the 2030s.

**Pricing Power:**
Core average revenue per funeral service grew 3.5% in Q1 2026 and 2.9% in FY2025, consistently exceeding general inflation historically. The Dignity Memorial brand commands premium pricing, and SCI is successfully upselling cremation customers into memorialization packages, celebrations of life, and bundled services that increase average revenue per cremation toward $3,000+ from $1,500 historically.

**Cemetery Underdevelopment:**
SCI owns approximately 36,000 acres across its cemetery portfolio, with only about 66% developed. This represents decades of inventory for property sales without acquisition spend, and cemetery pre-need production is growing at 10%+ with higher margins (32-34%) than funeral operations.

**Insurance Provider Switch:**
The July 2024 switch to a new preferred preneed insurance provider lifted general agency revenue from $231M to $292M (+27%) in FY2025 and drove insurance-funded preneed production up 20% to $817M. This transition is substantially complete and represents a durable revenue enhancement.

**Growth Constraints:**
Revenue growth is structurally limited to 2-4% organically given the industry's correlation to death rates and the ongoing cremation mix shift (which reduces revenue per case even as volume grows). EPS growth of 8-12% relies on buybacks contributing 3-4% annually plus margin discipline. This is a compounder, not a hyper-growth story.

**Why the Market May Be Underpricing SCI:**
The Q1 miss created an opportunity because the market is anchoring to a single quarter's flu-season volume comparison while underweighting: (1) 10% cemetery preneed growth, (2) confirmed full-year guidance, (3) $144M in buybacks executed in Q1 alone at ~$80.69 average, and (4) the accelerating demographic tailwind. Management is buying stock at these levels, signaling confidence in the trajectory.

## Competitive Landscape

SCI's competitive position is improving along several vectors:

**Scale Advantages:** With 1,487 funeral locations and 503 cemeteries, SCI has purchasing power, brand investment capacity, and technology development budgets that no competitor can match. The second-largest public competitor (Carriage Services) was recently taken private at a fraction of SCI's scale.

**Preneed Lock-In:** The $17B preneed backlog creates significant switching costs — customers who have pre-arranged services rarely switch providers. While preneed contracts include cancellation provisions, historical cancellation rates have been low, and the backlog represents large expected future revenue growing as maturities accelerate annually.

**Industry Consolidation:** SCI acquired 22 funeral locations and 2 cemeteries in FY2025 for $101M, continuing to roll up a fragmented industry. Many independent operators face succession challenges (aging owners, no family successor), creating a multi-decade acquisition pipeline. SCI's proprietary ranking model has identified 12,000+ strategic acquisition targets.

**Cremation Adaptation:** Rather than being disrupted by the cremation trend, SCI has built a dedicated value channel (Neptune Society/National Cremation) and developed premium cremation memorialization products that maintain or grow revenue per case.

**Moat Trajectory:** Improving. Cemetery real estate is unreplicable (zoning laws make new cemetery development nearly impossible in most jurisdictions). The preneed backlog grows annually. Brand investment compounds. Scale advantages widen with each acquisition.

## Risk Assessment

**Near-Term Risks:**

1. **Volume Recovery Uncertainty:** The -6% Q1 funeral volume decline needs to normalize over Q2-Q4 to hit guidance. If deaths run below trend for demographic/seasonal reasons, the $4.05-$4.35 EPS range could require revision. Management has historically hit guidance (Q3 2025 beat), but the bottom end ($4.05) implies limited room for further misses.

2. **Trust Fund Market Exposure:** $5.78B in trust assets had -0.7% returns in Q1 2026. A significant equity market correction (10-20%) would reduce recognized trust income by $50-100M annually and potentially require trust replenishment in certain states (though exposure was only $0.6M at year-end 2025).

3. **Interest Rate Sensitivity:** With $5.08B in total debt and floating-rate exposure on portions of the credit facility, sustained higher rates increase interest expense. However, the November 2025 refinancing locked in favorable terms through 2030.

**Structural Risks:**

4. **Cremation Mix Shift:** Each 100bp increase in cremation rate creates approximately $10-15M in revenue headwind (lower average revenue per case). The rate is rising ~50bps/year, though decelerating from 150bps historically.

5. **Operating Leverage on Volume Declines:** Fixed costs (facilities, staff, vehicles) mean volume declines compress margins disproportionately — Q1 showed 300bps of funeral margin compression on only 6% volume decline. In a pandemic-aftermath period of reduced deaths, this creates earnings volatility.

6. **Regulatory/Legal:** Preneed contract regulation varies by state. Changes in trust funding requirements, FTC funeral rule modifications, or unclaimed property enforcement could create one-time charges or ongoing compliance costs.

7. **Preneed Backlog Risks:** The $17B backlog includes cancelable preneed contracts with allowances for cancellation. Additionally, the insurance-funded portion introduces counterparty risk related to the financial condition of third-party insurance companies. While historically reliable, these are not zero-risk future cash flows.

## Conclusion

SCI at $78.44 offers a compelling risk/reward for a quality compounder entering its demographic sweet spot. The Q1 miss was driven by a temporary flu-season comparison against funeral operations, while the higher-margin cemetery business grew 7% and operating cash flow rose 6% YoY. Management confirmed full-year guidance and continued buying back stock at ~$80.69 average in Q1, signaling confidence in the trajectory.

The stock trades at ~19x forward earnings for a business with a $17B expected future revenue backlog, 18% market share in a consolidating industry, $554M annual FCF, and a 15-year demographic tailwind. Analyst targets range from $90-$100. The combination of predictable cash generation, aggressive capital return, and secular growth positioning justifies a premium multiple that the post-earnings selloff has temporarily compressed.

The primary risk — continued funeral volume weakness — is quantifiable and time-bounded. Q2 earnings in late July will either validate the recovery thesis (driving re-rating toward $88-92) or signal a more protracted issue (potentially pushing toward $70-72). At current levels, the probability-weighted expected return is favorable: even assigning 30% weight to the bear case, the expected value is approximately +9-11% over 12 months with a 1.7% dividend yield providing income while waiting.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The corrected equity FCF multiple (~19.6x, not 14.2x as originally stated) reduces the "cheap entry point" argument; the stock is fairly valued rather than deeply discounted on a cash flow basis, making the thesis more dependent on forward earnings growth and multiple re-rating than on current valuation compression.
