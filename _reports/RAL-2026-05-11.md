## Executive Summary

Ralliant Corporation is a June 2025 spinoff from Fortive, operating precision instruments (Test & Measurement) and engineered safety/sensing products (Sensors & Safety Systems). The stock trades at $47.23, roughly 17% below its 52-week high of $57.02 set immediately before a catastrophic February 2026 earnings report that included a $1.44B goodwill impairment on the EA Elektro-Automatik acquisition.

The core investment tension: the Sensors & Safety Systems segment (61% of revenue) generates 26.9% operating margins with +4.6% organic growth, benefiting from grid modernization and defense spending. A conservative 15x operating profit multiple on this segment alone implies ~$5.1B in value, essentially the entire current market cap. The T&M segment owns the iconic Tektronix brand but is experiencing a deep cyclical downturn (-15.3% organic revenue decline) across EV, diversified electronics, and communications end markets.

Downward estimate revisions of -14% over 90 days, a $531M term loan maturing December 2026, options pricing implying 128% near-term IV (vs. 53% realized), and remaining goodwill impairment risk in T&M ($901M still on the books) create material near-term uncertainty. The stock lacks a clear catalyst for re-rating until T&M revenue stabilizes, which semiconductor end market strength (+13.5% in Q3) suggests could arrive in H2 2026.

## Company Overview

Ralliant Corporation designs, manufactures, and services precision instruments and engineered products for over 90,000 customers across 90+ countries. Spun off from Fortive on June 28, 2025, the company employs approximately 7,000 people and is headquartered in Raleigh, NC.

**Test & Measurement** ($802M FY2025 revenue): Oscilloscopes, probes, source measuring units, semiconductor test systems, and high-power bi-directional power supplies. Core brands include Tektronix, Keithley Instruments, and EA Elektro-Automatik. End markets: semiconductor, diversified electronics, communications, and EV/energy storage.

**Sensors & Safety Systems** ($1.27B FY2025 revenue): Power grid monitoring instruments, energetic materials and ignition systems for defense/space, and liquid/flow/pressure sensors. Core brands include Qualitrol, Pacific Scientific Energetic Materials, Gems Sensors, and Setra Systems. End markets: utilities, defense/space, industrial manufacturing.

The company operates the "Ralliant Business System" — a lean operations framework derived from the Danaher/Fortive operating model — as a structural competitive advantage in continuous improvement and margin expansion.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term headwinds are substantial.** Consensus EPS estimates have been revised downward meaningfully: FY2026E from $2.73 to $2.35 (-14.1% over 90 days), FY2027E from $3.10 to $2.71 (-12.4%). These revisions have stabilized over the past 30 days (essentially flat), but the direction is concerning.

**T&M Recovery Timing:** The semiconductor end market grew +13.5% in Q3 2025 even as the broader T&M segment declined 14%. Semiconductor demand is typically a leading indicator for broader electronics test equipment demand, suggesting a potential recovery in H2 2026. However, the EV/energy storage market (the core thesis for the $1.72B EA acquisition) shows no near-term recovery signal. Industry EV adoption forecasts have been "materially reduced" per the 10-K.

**S&SS Growth Runway:** The Sensors & Safety segment benefits from secular tailwinds aligned with major multi-year investment trends: AI datacenter power demand driving grid modernization, continued defense spending acceleration (defense revenue +18% in Q3), and renewable energy integration requiring advanced monitoring equipment. These tailwinds are structural and independent of the economic cycle.

**Revenue CAGR Scenario Analysis:**
- Bear case: Revenue flat at ~$2.0B through FY2027 if T&M doesn't recover and S&SS growth decelerates
- Base case: Revenue recovers to $2.15-2.20B by FY2027 as T&M stabilizes and S&SS grows mid-single-digits
- Bull case: Revenue reaches $2.3-2.4B if T&M enters recovery cycle and S&SS accelerates on defense/utilities tailwinds

## Valuation Assessment

At $47.23/share ($5.4B market cap, ~$6.2B enterprise value):
- P/FCF: 15.0x ($358M trailing FCF)
- P/Adj. EPS: 17.4x ($2.71 FY2025 adj. EPS)
- Forward P/E: 20.1x ($2.35 FY2026E consensus)
- EV/Revenue: 3.0x
- FCF Yield: 6.6%

**Peer Context:** The peer median P/E of 92.7x is distorted by high-growth/pre-earnings names (PI at -162x, MIR at 201x). The most relevant comp is Vontier (VNT) at 11.1x P/E — also a Fortive spinoff, also experiencing headwinds. Against VNT, RAL commands a justified premium for higher margins (50% gross vs. VNT's ~45%) and FCF quality. Against OSIS (27.6x P/E), RAL trades at a discount that partially reflects the T&M cyclical trough.

**Sum-of-Parts Analysis:**
- S&SS: $341M operating profit × 15x = $5.1B
- T&M (at cyclical trough): Book value of tangible assets + remaining goodwill after further potential write-downs → conservatively $0.5-1.5B
- Less net debt: -$831M
- Implied range: $4.8B-5.8B ($43-52/share)

The current price of $47.23 sits near the midpoint of this range. The stock is not obviously mispriced — it trades approximately at fair value given current uncertainty about T&M recovery timing and additional impairment risk. The 6.6% FCF yield provides a reasonable margin of safety, but the forward P/E of 20x on declining estimates is not compelling.

**Analyst Consensus:** Mean target $48.45, median $49, range $42-55. The stock is within 4% of the median target — essentially at consensus fair value.

## Competitive Landscape

**S&SS segment (improving).** Defense/space revenue grew +18% in Q3, utility monitoring benefits from grid investment cycle, and the company's energetic materials business has significant barriers to entry (safety certifications, decades of qualification data). Market share appears stable to expanding.

**T&M core brands (stable).** Tektronix remains a market leader in oscilloscopes. Keithley's semiconductor test position is defended by switching costs and precision requirements. Competitors Keysight and Rohde & Schwarz are experiencing similar cyclical weakness — this is industry-wide, not share loss.

**EA Elektro-Automatik competitive position (deteriorating).** The high-power test market for EV has fewer structural buyers than forecast. Competition is intensifying from Asian manufacturers while demand has contracted. The remaining $901M in T&M goodwill reflects continued optimism about this market that may not materialize.

**Ralliant Business System:** The operational improvement framework is a genuine differentiator that supports margin expansion as volumes recover. However, it cannot offset structural demand declines — it amplifies operating leverage on both upside and downside.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Additional Goodwill Impairment (HIGH).** $901M in T&M goodwill remains after the $1.44B write-down. The 10-K explicitly warns that further impairment is possible if "financial projections including recovery from the current policy and macroeconomic environment" aren't achieved. Given EV market trajectory and tariff headwinds, this is a meaningful probability. Additional impairment wouldn't affect cash flow but would further erode book value and shareholder confidence.

**2. Debt Maturity Concentration (MODERATE-HIGH).** The $531M term loan due December 2026 requires refinancing within 7 months. The March 2026 8-K filing of a new material agreement likely represents this refinancing, but terms are not publicly disclosed. In the current rate environment (5%+ short-term rates), refinancing costs may increase. Total floating-rate exposure of $1.15B creates earnings sensitivity to rate changes.

**3. T&M Cyclical Trough Duration (MODERATE).** If the T&M cycle extends through FY2027, the segment could require restructuring beyond the current $9-11M annualized savings program. Each quarter of delay reduces the credibility of the EA investment thesis.

**4. Tariff and Trade Escalation (MODERATE).** With 49% international sales and ~$300M in China revenue, tariff escalation directly impacts both demand and margins. Management already expects continued negative impact.

**5. Spinoff Dis-synergies (LOW-MODERATE).** Estimated at -315bps operating margin headwind, with cost savings program targeting partial offset by December 2026. Execution risk exists.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing extreme near-term uncertainty: 128.2% ATM IV for the nearest expiry (3 days) versus 53.4% historical volatility — a 74.8pp spread. The term structure is in steep backwardation (128.2% near-term vs. 53.7% 6-month), indicating the market expects an imminent binary event or resolution of uncertainty. Put/call skew of 17.0% shows elevated put demand.

This pricing likely reflects: (a) uncertainty around the next earnings report, (b) potential news on the term loan refinancing, or (c) broader geopolitical risk transmission given the company's international exposure. The backwardation suggests whatever is driving near-term stress is expected to resolve, after which longer-term volatility normalizes to ~54%.

For potential buyers, the rich near-term IV suggests selling puts could generate significant premium, but the elevated skew warns that institutional investors are actively hedging downside. Short interest of 6.8% (4.3 days to cover) is moderate — not crowded but reflects meaningful bearish positioning.

- S&SS continues +5-8% organic growth on defense and utility tailwinds
- Term loan refinancing executes cleanly at favorable terms

- Tariff escalation pressures China revenue and margins

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. T&M segment organic revenue decline narrows to <-5% for two consecutive quarters, signaling cycle bottoming
2. FY2026 EPS estimates begin revising upward (currently $2.35; watch for stabilization above $2.40 with upward momentum)
3. Term loan refinancing completes with disclosed terms at or below current rates, removing the December 2026 maturity overhang

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Additional goodwill impairment announced (any amount), confirming EA thesis is permanently impaired
2. FY2026 EPS estimates revised below $2.00, indicating T&M weakness spreading to S&SS
3. Term loan refinancing fails or requires equity dilution

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (likely May/June 2026 for Q1 FY2026 results). This will provide the first quarter of standalone operating data with comparative context and visibility into T&M trajectory. The term loan maturity in December 2026 creates a secondary hard reassessment point.

## Conclusion

Ralliant presents a value proposition built on a high-quality Sensors & Safety segment, strong free cash flow generation, and potential cyclical recovery in Test & Measurement. The sum-of-parts math is compelling on a 12-24 month horizon. However, the current moment is wrong for taking a position: estimate revisions are still falling, the options market is pricing extreme near-term uncertainty, the T&M trough hasn't demonstrably bottomed, and $901M in remaining goodwill faces further impairment risk.

The stock trades approximately at fair value given current fundamentals ($47.23 vs. median analyst target of $49.00 and sum-of-parts midpoint of ~$48). Upside from here requires conviction that T&M recovery timing is imminent — and the data doesn't yet support that conviction. The consistent earnings beats and semiconductor end market strength are encouraging but insufficient to offset still-declining estimates and near-term binary risks.

HOLD with monitoring. The right entry point is likely lower (on any additional impairment news or broader market stress) or later (when T&M revenue inflects positive), not at current prices where the risk-reward is approximately symmetrical.
