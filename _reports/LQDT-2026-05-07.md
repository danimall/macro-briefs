## Executive Summary

Liquidity Services operates the largest online marketplace network for surplus, returned, and excess commercial and government assets in the US, processing $1.57B in GMV across approximately 6.0 million registered buyers in FY2025 (growing to 6.2 million by December 2025). The company is experiencing an earnings inflection: after two years of margin compression driven by expansion of its purchase-model RSCG segment (direct profit margin fell from 28.7% to 22.6% at the annual level, with quarterly lows near 20%), Q1 FY2026 showed a recovery to 26% RSCG margins with segment profit up 29% YoY. This operating leverage, combined with a fortress balance sheet (approximately $181M in cash and short-term investments as of December 2025, zero debt), positions the company to compound earnings materially over the next 12-18 months.

The investment case hinges on three pillars: (1) the margin recovery in RSCG is durable and has further room to improve, (2) the high-margin GovDeals segment (93% direct profit margins, $903M GMV) continues growing as government digitization of surplus asset disposition accelerates, and (3) macro conditions — including potential tariff-driven surplus inventory growth and consumer price sensitivity boosting secondary market demand — may structurally benefit the reverse supply chain, though the net impact remains uncertain. At ~25x forward FY2026 earnings ($1.49 consensus) with strong EBITDA growth, the valuation is demanding but justified by the quality of the business model and the inflection dynamics.

The primary risk is Amazon vendor concentration — a dependency that, if disrupted, would materially impair the RSCG segment. The company's shift back toward consignment models may reduce this dependency over time, but it remains the key thesis vulnerability.

## Company Overview

Liquidity Services was founded in 1999 and has built a network of online auction marketplaces connecting sellers of surplus, returned, and excess assets with a global buyer base. The company operates four segments:

- **RSCG (Retail Supply Chain Group):** 69% of revenue, handles returns and overstock from major retailers including Amazon. Operates under both purchase (principal) and consignment (agent) models.
- **GovDeals:** 18% of revenue, facilitates surplus asset sales for government agencies. 93% direct profit margins with recurring government relationships.
- **CAG (Capital Assets Group):** 8% of revenue, manages disposition of heavy industrial equipment and capital assets. 89% direct profit margins.
- **Machinio & Software Solutions:** 4% of revenue, SaaS-based auction platform and advertising marketplace for heavy equipment. 93% combined segment margins.

The company's marketplace model benefits from classic network effects: more buyers drive higher recovery rates for sellers, which attracts additional sellers, which attracts more buyers. This flywheel has generated a 20.4% GMV CAGR over five years.

The operating margin compression from FY2022 to FY2024 was primarily driven by the revenue mix shift toward purchase-model transactions in RSCG, where full sale price is recognized as revenue but gross margins are structurally lower. Purchase revenue grew from 54.7% of total revenue in FY2023 to 65.1% in FY2025. This created optically poor margins while the underlying marketplace economics (GMV-based) remained strong. The Q1 FY2026 RSCG margin recovery from 21% to 26% is the most important recent development, with Q2 FY2026 guidance (Adj. EBITDA $14-17M) suggesting continued momentum.

**Balance Sheet Strength:**

The balance sheet is exceptional for a company of this size. The capital-light model requires only $7.8M in annual capex (1.6% of revenue). Free cash flow of $59M in FY2025 represents a 12.4% FCF margin and ~5% FCF yield at current market cap.

**Q1 FY2026 Operating Leverage:**

Management guided Q2 FY2026 to GMV of $375-415M, net income of $6.5-9.5M, and Adj. EBITDA of $14-17M. Q3 FY2026 (seasonally strongest for GovDeals) should provide further visibility on the full-year trajectory.

**Stock-Based Compensation:** $14.5M in FY2025 represents ~24% of Adj. EBITDA. Buybacks ($16M in FY2025) roughly offset dilution, keeping share count approximately flat.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):**

1. **RSCG margin recovery:** Management deliberately reduced purchase volumes starting Q4 FY2025, shifting mix back toward higher-margin consignment. If RSCG margins continue recovering toward 30% (halfway to prior 34%), consolidated Adj. EBITDA could reach $80M+ in FY2027.

2. **Retail Rush:** The new direct-to-consumer "bin store" format was launched during Q4 FY2025. While immaterial today, if scalable this represents a new, potentially higher-margin sales channel for purchased inventory that currently flows through wholesale.

3. **Potential tariff impact:** Increased tariffs on imports could create two effects — retailers building domestic surplus inventory that needs liquidation (supply), and price-sensitive consumers turning to secondary markets (demand). However, company filings also note that tariffs and trade barriers could adversely affect international buyers, seller activity, and cross-border commerce. The net impact remains uncertain and management has not endorsed tariffs as a quantified positive tailwind.

4. **GovDeals secular growth:** Government digitization of surplus asset sales is a multi-decade trend. The segment is growing GMV steadily with no inventory risk.

**Medium-term growth drivers (12-36 months):**

- E-commerce return rates (~20% vs. ~8-10% in-store) continue generating massive reverse logistics volume as online penetration grows
- The Machinio & Software Solutions segment (acquired/formed Jan 2025) provides combined recurring revenue at 93% direct profit margins
- Potential M&A using cash pile plus $35M undrawn credit facility

**Estimate momentum:** Consensus FY2026 EPS estimates are reportedly around $1.49 with FY2027 at approximately $1.53, implying continued growth. The company has maintained a multi-quarter earnings beat streak, suggesting consensus may remain conservative.

## Valuation Assessment

At recent prices near $37, LQDT trades at approximately:
- 40-43x trailing EPS ($0.87 FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS)
- ~25x forward FY2026E EPS (~$1.49 consensus)
- ~17x forward Adj. EBITDA (~$70M FY2026E)
- ~20x FCF (using FY2025 $59M; likely growing)

The trailing P/E is misleading because trailing EPS reflects the trough of the margin compression period. On forward estimates — which reflect the margin inflection now evident in Q1 FY2026 results — the stock is priced at a moderate premium for a capital-light marketplace business with 15-20% revenue growth, zero debt, and expanding margins.

Comparable marketplace/auction businesses (eBay, Copart) typically trade at 20-30x forward earnings. LQDT at ~25x forward is within this range, though smaller scale and Amazon concentration warrant some discount versus Copart's premium multiple.

The consensus analyst targets (approximately $42-44) represent 13-18% upside. Given the recent beat streak and rising estimates, these targets appear conservative rather than aspirational.

**Net cash adjustment:** Backing out approximately $181M in net cash (likely growing given strong FCF generation), the enterprise value implies ~22x forward earnings for the operating business — reasonable for the growth profile.

## Competitive Landscape

LQDT's competitive position is strengthening. The company's moat rests on:

1. **Buyer liquidity network:** Approximately 6.2 million registered buyers creating one of the deepest demand pools in the industry. Sellers choose platforms that maximize recovery rates; more buyers = higher prices = more sellers.

2. **Data advantage:** 25 years of transaction data across millions of auctions enables AI-powered pricing recommendations that improve seller outcomes. This data asset compounds over time and is not replicable by new entrants.

3. **Government relationships:** GovDeals serves government agencies with high switching friction — municipalities rarely change platforms once implemented.

4. **Multi-marketplace strategy:** Rather than a single platform, LQDT operates specialized marketplaces (AllSurplus, GovDeals, Liquidation.com, Bid4Assets, Machinio) each optimized for specific asset classes. This creates specialization advantages in pricing and buyer matching.

**Trajectory:** The competitive position is improving as the company adds SaaS capabilities (Auction Software acquisition), new retail channels (Retail Rush), and continues to grow its buyer base. The relevant threat is not from other liquidators but from large retailers (principally Amazon) internalizing the function.

## Risk Assessment

**Amazon Concentration (Severity: HIGH):** The single most important risk. Amazon supplies significant purchased inventory to RSCG. The 10-K states that loss of this relationship would be difficult or impossible to replace: "we likely could not procure alternative inventory from other vendors in a timely and efficient manner and on acceptable terms, or at all." Amazon has the capability to internalize liquidation (it already runs Amazon Warehouse Deals). However, the economic logic may favor outsourcing — Amazon's core competency is forward logistics and marketplace technology, not surplus recovery. LQDT provides Amazon a lower-friction solution than building internal reverse auction capability. While management's shift toward consignment models may reduce purchase-model Amazon dependency over time, SEC filings do not characterize this shift as materially mitigating the Amazon concentration risk.

**Purchase Model Inventory Risk (Severity: MODERATE):** Under mandatory supply agreements, LQDT must accept delivery regardless of demand. This created margin pressure in FY2024-2025 when purchase volumes exceeded absorption capacity. The Q1 FY2026 improvement suggests this risk is actively being managed, but it could reactivate during demand downturns.

**Tariff/Trade Uncertainty (Severity: MODERATE):** Company filings frame tariffs primarily as a risk factor, noting potential adverse effects on buyers, sellers, asset availability, and international commerce. Q3 FY2025 commentary noted that some CAG categories/regions saw tempered activity due to tariff-related supply-chain uncertainty. The net effect is uncertain.

**Key Person Risk (Severity: MODERATE):** Founder/CEO Angrick has led the company for 25 years. While alignment is excellent (~17% ownership), succession planning is not publicly detailed beyond the Board's oversight role.

**Valuation Risk (Severity: MODERATE):** At ~25x forward earnings, any miss or guidance disappointment could trigger meaningful derating. The small-cap nature (~$1.2B market cap) and thin trading volume amplify price moves around catalysts.

## Conclusion

Liquidity Services warrants a BUY recommendation based on the margin inflection evident in Q1 FY2026, balance sheet strength, structural tailwinds from e-commerce returns growth, and a valuation that — while not cheap — is appropriate for a capital-light marketplace with network effects, expanding margins, and strong EBITDA growth. The recent earnings beat streak and rising estimates suggest consensus remains conservative.

The key risk preventing maximum conviction is Amazon concentration — a dependency that, while economically rational for both parties, creates binary downside if disrupted. Position sizing should reflect this single-vendor vulnerability. A 2% portfolio position is appropriate, with willingness to add on any pullback toward $32-34 if fundamentals remain intact.
