## Executive Summary

Cboe Global Markets operates the dominant franchise in US index options — proprietary, exclusive-listing products (SPX and VIX) that carry no direct fee competition and generate the bulk of the company's high-margin transaction revenue. The structural tailwind is the explosion of zero-days-to-expiry (0DTE) options, which is compounding into estimate revisions of roughly +10% to +15% across every consensus window over the past 90 days. The company has beaten consensus in three of the last four quarters, most recently by 10.3% in the March quarter, and trades at roughly 21x forward earnings against a peer median near 26x. The forward setup for the core business is favorable.

The stock action is more complicated. Most of that drawdown was systematic (listed exchanges and financial-data names derated together on rate and regulatory fears), but the magnitude requires a specific explanation of what sellers are pricing. A difficult volume comparison against a record 2025, layered on top of a complete C-suite reset under a new CEO, are among the candidate explanations. Its proprietary SPX (S&P 500 index options) and VIX (volatility index) products are exclusively listed on Cboe exchanges, so they carry no direct fee competition — a structural advantage that distinguishes Cboe from cash-equity and multiply-listed options venues where order-flow competition compresses economics. ## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis rests on the durability of the 0DTE options boom. Short-dated index options have shifted from a niche tactical product to a structural component of both institutional and retail hedging and speculation, and Cboe's exclusive SPX/VIX listings capture that flow with no fee competition. [unverified — specific ~25% ADV growth figure not grounded in provided sources]

The critical question for 2026 is comparison difficulty. 2025 was a record volume year, so the year-over-year comps get harder; the market's concern is that revised-up estimates already assume sustained record activity, leaving little room for disappointment. The counter is that 0DTE adoption is still expanding and volatility regimes keep hedging demand elevated. Cboe benefits from volatility directly.

The recurring-revenue growth layer, market data and analytics, provides a secular growth vector less tied to volume cyclicality. [unverified — Cboe Predicts prediction-markets optionality and IBKR/Schwab distribution not grounded in provided sources]

The estimate-revision magnitude is a meaningful positive signal. It does not clear the strongest BUY-signal threshold, and the revisions have decelerated at the 30-day mark (current-quarter +4.5% 30d vs +14.5% 90d), suggesting consensus has largely caught up to the volume reality. We score Growth Prospects at 6.5 — real, durable, but with the easy re-rating on revisions already largely captured and a hard comp ahead.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$289 the stock trades at roughly 21x the $13.98 current-year consensus and ~19.2x the $15.02 next-year figure. That compares favorably to the peer median P/E of 26.1x (NDAQ 26.1x, MSCI 34.0x, COIN 56.0x). Cheaper peers exist (FDS 15.2x, MORN 18.1x), but those are pure data/analytics businesses without the exclusive-listing options monopoly. Cboe's core franchise deserves a premium to Nasdaq given the no-fee-competition dynamic in SPX/VIX; instead it trades at a discount, reflecting the regulatory overhang and volume-comp risk rather than a franchise deficiency.

On the earnings-inflection framework, we evaluate on forward and rising estimates rather than trailing. The stock has derated roughly 20-24% from its multiple peak, and with estimates still climbing, the forward multiple compression is favorable. Analyst targets frame the range: consensus mean $311.64, median $307, high $375, low $258. Because the forward multiple is below 30x with positive revisions, the standard interpretation is that consensus targets lag reality on inflection names, but we temper that here because the volume-comp risk makes the upside less clean than a typical momentum inflection.

The valuation is fair-to-attractive but not cheap. The 52-week high near $371 embedded a peak-volume, peak-multiple scenario that the market repudiated; the current ~$289 sits between a $240-floor derating case and a mid-$300s bull case. We score Valuation at 6.0 — the discount to peers on rising estimates supports a constructive lean, but the regulatory binary and hard comp cap the conviction, and calibration warns against over-rewarding cheapness absent a clean independent catalyst.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$340-375).** The 0DTE boom proves structural rather than cyclical, sustaining record volumes through the 2026 comp. Estimate revisions continue their +10-15% trajectory, next-year EPS approaches or exceeds the $15.02 consensus, and the stock re-rates back toward its peer-appropriate multiple (24-25x on $15 = $360-375). This maps to the analyst high target of $375.

**Base Case (~$310-320).** Volumes moderate off the record but 0DTE adoption keeps activity elevated; the company delivers current-year EPS near $14 and continues beating modestly. The multiple holds around 21-22x on rising next-year estimates, and the stock works back toward the consensus mean of ~$312 as the derating unwinds. This is roughly 7-11% upside plus the ~1% dividend.

**Bear Case (~$240).** The 2026 volume comp disappoints, float income compresses on Fed easing, and the C-suite transition produces an operational stumble. The stock retests its June low near $227-240 as the multiple compresses toward 16x on flat-to-negative revisions. This is roughly 15-21% downside from current levels.

The distribution is roughly symmetric with a slight positive skew given the franchise quality and the discount to peers, but the near-term event risk and the hard comp prevent the risk/reward from being asymmetric enough to warrant a BUY at the current price.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Timeframe:** 6-12 months, anchored to the upcoming earnings print and subsequent volume data through the difficult 2026 comps.

**Reassessment timeline:** The immediate reassessment is the next earnings report and management's commentary on volume durability. The next natural checkpoint is the subsequent quarter's volume trend.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- A pullback toward the low-$240s, restoring clearly asymmetric risk/reward at ~17x forward on rising estimates.
- Confirmation that 0DTE / index-options ADV is sustaining or growing against the record 2025 comp (i.e., the volume boom proves structural), with continued positive estimate revisions.
- Concrete evidence that any pending regulatory impact is contained (management quantification well below feared levels), removing a primary overhang.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Two consecutive quarters of declining year-over-year index-options volume signaling the 0DTE boom has plateaued and reverting estimates.
- A materially adverse regulatory development that management confirms will cut Market Services revenue meaningfully.
- Accelerating share erosion in cash equities/options combined with a management stumble during any C-suite transition (guidance cut or unexpected departure).

## Conclusion

Cboe owns one of the best moats in the exchange industry — an exclusive, no-fee-competition franchise in SPX and VIX index options, riding a 0DTE volume boom that has driven a four-quarter beat streak and double-digit estimate revisions. At ~21x forward on rising estimates, well below the peer median, the valuation is fair-to-attractive after a multiple derating and a round trip from the $371 high.

What keeps this a HOLD rather than a BUY is the confluence of a difficult 2026 volume comparison against a record year that already sits embedded in revised-up estimates, a governance transition, and near-term options pricing acute event stress into the upcoming print. The declining score trajectory — management, risk, and competitive position all resetting lower — reflects genuine incremental concerns; the stock's decline from its high is largely a fundamentally-neutral derating.

We recommend holding for the growth thesis with a clear plan: let the upcoming print and post-earnings volatility crush resolve the near-term event, and use a pullback toward the low-$240s or confirmation of sustained volumes as the entry to upgrade. The franchise quality warrants ownership at the right price; the current price does not yet offer the asymmetry a BUY requires.
