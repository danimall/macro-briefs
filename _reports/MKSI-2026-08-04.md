## Executive Summary

MKS Inc sells the layer beneath the semiconductor equipment OEMs — vacuum and gas delivery subsystems, RF power, lasers and precision optics — and since the 2022 Atotech acquisition also sells plating chemistry and equipment into printed circuit boards, package substrates and wafer-level packaging. The second business is what has re-rated the stock. Electronics & Packaging revenue grew 27% year over year in Q1 2026 to $321M, reaching 30% of total revenue from 25% in FY2023, driven by flexible-PCB laser via-drilling systems and plating chemistry. Higher accelerator and HBM content means more substrate layers, more vias per package and more plating steps, and each of those is a MKS unit driver. That is a mechanical content-per-unit relationship. Q1 2026 MKS semiconductor revenue growth of 13% was attributed partly to NAND memory production upgrades, the most depressed part of wafer-fab spending until recently. [unverified — TSMC and Amazon capex figures do not appear in the research brief]

Consensus has moved a long way to catch up. FY2026 non-GAAP EPS of $11.78 is up 18.1% over 90 days; FY2027 of $15.45 is up 26.4%. At $323.48 that is 27.5x this year and 20.9x next, with implied FY2026-to-FY2027 earnings growth of 31.2% — a PEG of 0.88. Against a peer set trading at a median 83.2x trailing earnings (Entegris 83.2x, Onto 141.2x, FormFactor 149.7x, Advanced Energy 70.4x), MKS at 74.0x trailing GAAP is the cheapest of the group on a like-for-like basis. The stock is 27.7% below its June 30 high of $447.62; Entegris is down 33%, FormFactor 35%, Advanced Energy 31%, Onto 29%. This was a cohort-wide multiple reassessment, not a MKS-specific event, and MKS de-rated less than its peers while its estimates rose.

Three things keep this a HOLD rather than an upgrade. First, the balance sheet: net debt of roughly $3,566M is 3.69x FY2025 company-defined Adjusted EBITDA of $966M, and the $1,400M 1.25% convertible — conversion price $154.30, principal that **must** be settled in cash — became convertible at holder option during Q2 2026 and was reclassified to current liabilities. At the current price the economically relevant incremental share count is about 4.75M (7.0% of shares outstanding, 6.6% post-issuance ownership dilution), of which roughly $781M of excess value sits above the $237.40 capped-call ceiling and is unhedged. Second, the earnings path consensus requires is steep and unverifiable: quarterly EPS of $2.30 actual, then $2.96, $3.18 and roughly $3.35, on a company that discloses no remaining performance obligations, states most orders ship within 90 days, and says backlog "is not necessarily indicative of actual sales." Third, Q2 results land August 5 with near-term implied volatility of 101.8% against 60.9% realized and a ±17.7% implied move. Own it through the print; add materially lower.

## Company Overview

MKS (renamed from MKS Instruments; founded 1961, Massachusetts) reports three segments serving semiconductor wafer fabrication, electronics & packaging, and specialty industrial markets.

| Segment | FY2025 revenue | Q1 2026 revenue | Q1 2026 segment GM | Q1 YoY GM change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vacuum Solutions (VSD) | $1,579M | $425M | 42.9% | −240 bp |
| Photonics Solutions (PSD) | $1,029M | $303M | 47.2% | +320 bp |
| Materials Solutions (MSD) | $1,323M | $350M | 52.2% | −230 bp |
| **Total** | **$3,931M** | **$1,078M** | **47.1%** | −40 bp |

End-market mix has shifted from 41% semiconductor / 25% electronics & packaging / 34% specialty industrial in FY2023 to 43% / 30% / 27% in Q1 2026. Approximately 10,300 employees in 37 countries, 585 U.S. and 2,217 foreign patents, 15 global MSD technology centers. International revenue was 82% of Q1 2026; China at $252M (23.4%) is the largest single geography, above the U.S. at $195M.

The durable competitive feature is "copy exact" qualification: MKS components are designed into OEM tool platforms and are difficult to displace mid-generation. The MSD chemistry model — consumables into installed plating lines — carries 52-55% gross margin and is more recurring than VSD's build-to-order subsystems.

## Financial Analysis

**Q1 2026 (10-Q).** Revenue $1,078M, +15.2% year over year and +4.4% sequentially. Operating income $149M (13.8% of revenue) versus $111M (11.8%). Net income $84M versus $52M, +61.5%. GAAP diluted EPS $1.18 on 71.1M diluted shares; reported non-GAAP EPS was $2.30 against a $2.08 consensus, a 10.3% beat.

The earnings leverage decomposes into three pieces with different durability. Volume and factory utilization are genuine — PSD gross margin expanded 320 bp on higher volumes, favorable utilization and lower excess-and-obsolete charges. SG&A fell from 19.8% to 17.6% of revenue following the Q1 2025 restructuring. But $8M of the $43M year-over-year pre-tax improvement came from lower interest expense ($53M → $45M), a financing action that does not repeat once annualized. Term Loan contractual interest fell from $49M to $27M, partially offset by $8M of new 2034 Notes interest.

Two of the three segments are absorbing cost pressure that the 40 bp consolidated gross margin decline conceals. VSD fell 240 bp on unfavorable mix plus duty and tariff costs; MSD fell 230 bp year over year and 140 bp sequentially, attributed specifically to palladium prices. MSD is simultaneously the highest-margin and fastest-growing segment (+22% year over year), so the input-cost squeeze lands precisely on the highest-quality part of the growth. Palladium's move is a sector-wide precious-metals phenomenon that named competitors (Element Solutions, Qnity, Uyemura) also face, and MKS may recover it through pricing, but the filings do not describe pass-through terms.

**Cash flow requires care.** Q1 2026 operating cash flow was $53M against $141M a year earlier, in a quarter when net income rose 62%. The reconciliation accounts for it: receivables +$129M on higher sales, inventories +$49M, accrued compensation −$68M on payment of prior-year variable compensation. Capex $25M, so Q1 free cash flow was roughly $28M. That figure must not be annualized — Q1 is structurally the weak cash quarter because annual bonuses are paid then, and MD&A states explicitly that growing sales increase receivables and inventories and may reduce cash from operations. As a labeled sensitivity: adding back the $68M variable-comp payment gives roughly $121M of operating cash flow and $96M of free cash flow, or about $385M annualized, against FY2025 actual free cash flow of $497M (operating cash flow $645M less capex $148M, a 12.6% FCF margin). Whether FY2026 conversion lands nearer $385M or $497M depends on whether the receivable and inventory build reverses in the second half. At $21,864M market capitalization (67.54M shares × $323.48), FY2025 free cash flow is a 2.3% yield.

**Balance sheet at March 31, 2026.** Total assets $8,728M, of which goodwill $2,565M plus net intangibles $2,065M equals $4,630M — 53.0% of assets. Stockholders' equity $2,811M, implying tangible book equity of approximately negative $1,819M. Total debt carrying value $4,135M: Term Loan $1,588M (USD Tranche B $914M, Euro Tranche B €587M), Convertible Notes $1,400M, 2034 Notes $1,147M. Cash $569M ($126M domestic, $443M offshore). Net debt approximately $3,566M, or 3.69x FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA. Interest coverage on Q1 figures is 3.3x ($149M / $45M); on derived pro forma annual cash interest of roughly $138M against TTM EBIT of $566M, coverage improves to about 4.1x. Cumulative goodwill impairment is $1,833M — MSD $1,302M, PSD $390M, VSD $141M — the audited marker of Atotech underperforming its original underwriting.

**The February 2026 refinancing was well executed.** The Sixth Amendment plus €1,000M of 4.250% senior unsecured 2034 Notes funded a $1,274M prepayment of USD Tranche B (from roughly $2,188M to $914M), with a further $100M prepaid May 6. Margins were cut 25-75 bp across all tranches, the revolver was upsized to $1,000M and extended to February 2031, and the term loan extended to February 2033. Maturities are now $14M remaining 2026, $19M each 2027-2029, $1,419M in 2030 (the convertible), $2,645M thereafter. There are no financial maintenance covenants on either term loan tranche. Two caveats: the euro debt creates translation exposure (net-investment-hedge designation drove a +$49M OCI swing in Q1 but does not protect euro cash interest in dollars), and the interest-rate swap notional was cut from $1,900M to $500M when swaps were de-designated on the USD prepayment, so the remaining floating Euro Tranche B (€587M ≈ $673M) is largely unhedged. Each 100 bp on that tranche is roughly $6.7M pre-tax, about $0.07 per share.

**The convertible is the item standard screens get wrong.** $1,400M of 1.25% notes due June 2030, conversion rate adjusted to 6.4807 shares per $1,000 effective April 1, 2026 (conversion price $154.30). Principal must be paid in cash; only the excess may be settled in cash, stock, or a combination at MKS's election. The Sale Price Condition was met in Q1 2026, the notes became convertible at holder option for the quarter ending June 30, 2026, and they were reclassified to short-term debt at March 31. Current liabilities rose from $927M to $2,291M on that reclassification alone; the current ratio fell from 2.71 to 1.11 and working capital from $1,583M to $254M. That is a share-price-driven accounting reclassification, not operating deterioration, and any screen reading it as a liquidity collapse is misreading it. The substantive facts: $1,400M is coverable from $569M of cash plus a $1,000M undrawn revolver, but doing so would consume essentially all balance-sheet cash and a meaningful revolver draw; MKS gains a par cash-call right after June 5, 2027 that lets it control timing; and the notes were marked at $2,323M fair value against $1,400M carrying, meaning holders have economic reason to wait.

On dilution, at $323.48: maximum shares issuable are 9.073M, conversion value $2,935M, excess over principal $1,535M, which if stock-settled equals 4.746M shares — 7.0% of the 67.54M shares outstanding and 6.6% post-issuance ownership dilution. Reported Q1 diluted shares of 71.1M embed only 2.9M convertible shares, computed on an implied average price near $227, so roughly 1.85M shares of incremental dilution are not yet in the last reported count. The capped calls offset appreciation only from $154.30 to $237.40 — $83.10 × 9.073M = $754M of the $1,535M excess — leaving approximately $781M of unhedged economic exposure, about 3.6% of market capitalization, that appears nowhere on the income statement.

One data note: the supplied profile shows forward P/E equal to trailing P/E at 64.86, which is internally inconsistent with a $11.78 consensus and back-solves to a price near $283 against GAAP EPS of $4.37. Treat it as a stale field; the forward multiples below are computed directly.

## Growth Analysis

Q1 2026 growth by end market: semiconductor +13% year over year and +7% sequentially on deposition and etch at VSD, NAND memory production upgrades, and lithography/metrology/inspection at PSD; Electronics & Packaging +27% to $321M on MSD chemistry and PSD flexible-PCB via-drilling; Specialty Industrial +8% with growth from datacom applications at PSD. Two of the three drivers are AI-adjacent, including inside the segment historically treated as slow-growth ballast.

The estimate revision pattern is a genuine positive signal but stops short of the extreme profile. Current-quarter consensus rose 24.6% over 90 days to $2.96, next quarter 20.1% to $3.18, FY2026 18.1% to $11.78, FY2027 26.4% to $15.45, across 12-13 analysts. Thirty-day revisions are small and positive (+0.3% to +2.3%), so the bulk of the move happened earlier in the quarter and has since flattened. Revenue growth of 15.2% is solid but not the 30%-plus acceleration that would mark a deep-trough recovery. FY2026 consensus of $11.78 against a FY2025 non-GAAP base of roughly $7.7-7.8 (Q2-Q4 2025 reported at $1.77, $1.93, $2.47) implies roughly 51% growth, then 31% in FY2027.

The reason to hold rather than press: the consensus quarterly path requires sequential acceleration in every remaining quarter of 2026 — $2.30, $2.96, $3.18, ~$3.35 — which implies revenue rising materially through the year. MKS provides no forward-visibility metric that can validate this. Lead times are short, most orders ship within 90 days, orders "may be subject to cancellation or rescheduling by the customer with limited or no penalty," and MKS elects the practical expedient and does not disclose remaining performance obligations. Deferred revenue and customer advances of $106M is not backlog and should not be read as such; the $59M of additions in the roll-forward is a quarterly flow, not a level.

The five-year record frames how much confidence to extend. Company-selected Adjusted EBITDA ran $868M (2021), $854M, $863M, $927M, $966M (2025) — a 2.7% CAGR across the period in which Atotech was acquired and now contributes essentially all of MSD's $1,323M of revenue. The 2021 comparison is a cyclical peak and 2022-2024 spanned a wafer-fab downturn plus China export controls, so the flat line conflates deal performance with cycle. The current price requires EBITDA to break decisively above the $1.1-1.2B range. My derived TTM adjusted EBITDA of roughly $1,023M (TTM EBIT $566M + D&A ~$340M + stock comp ~$76M + restructuring/legal/debt fees ~$41M) says that break is beginning. Consensus FY2026 EPS implies roughly $1.35-1.40B and FY2027 roughly $1.65-1.70B — a 73% increase off the 2025 base in two years.

## Competitive Landscape

The bundle thesis — "Optimize the Interconnect," pairing PSD laser via-drilling with MSD plating chemistry — is the reason for the re-rating and is supported by observable results: Electronics & Packaging +27% year over year, PSD gross margin +320 bp, and management identifying "growth in units and via counts in the high density interconnect PCB drilling market" as an R&D driver. Copy-exact qualification, 2,802 total patents, 15 MSD technology centers, and 6% voluntary turnover against average tenure over ten years support incumbency.

The counterweights are specific. MKS discloses that "no single competitor competes with us across all product lines," which means each line faces a focused specialist: Advanced Energy, Inficon, VAT, Horiba and Brooks Instrument in VSD; Trumpf, Lumentum, IPG, Coherent, Excelitas, Jenoptik and Thorlabs in photonics, with Via Mechanics, EO Technics and Mitsubishi Electric specifically in PCB laser drilling; Element Solutions, Qnity Electronics, Uyemura, JCU and Okuno in chemistry, plus Schmid and Manz Asia in plating equipment. The Japanese and Korean drilling incumbents compete directly in the highest-growth product line, and Qnity is now an independent, capital-markets-accountable competitor in the highest-margin segment. MKS asserts the bundle is differentiating; the filings provide no win rates or share data to demonstrate it.

Two disclosures cut against the diversification narrative. Top-ten customers were 30% of net revenues in 2023, 32% in 2024 and 35% in 2025, with no single customer above 10%. End-market mix diversified over the same period while customer concentration worsened by 500 bp — these establish different things, and only the latter measures counterparty exposure. Rising top-ten share during an upcycle is partly arithmetic, since the fastest-growing customers mechanically gain share of a growing base, but it is the only direct concentration metric disclosed and it is moving the wrong way. Non-U.S. long-lived assets jumped from 59% to 70% of the total in a single year, with new plants in Thailand and Malaysia (Malaysia revenue +37% to $70M) while China remains the largest revenue geography and is growing. MKS is building on both sides of the supply-chain split, which raises duplication and fixed-cost risk if volumes fragment.

Competitive trajectory is improving, which is what the score reflects. I trim it from 7.5 to 7.0 because the operating leverage is concentrated in PSD while VSD and MSD — together 72% of Q1 revenue — absorbed 240 bp and 230 bp of margin compression, and because Qnity's emergence changes the structure of the highest-margin segment.

## Risk Assessment

**Convertible settlement.** Now a crystallized rather than theoretical risk: reclassification occurred March 31, convertibility began in Q2. Up to $1,400M of cash may be demanded against $569M of cash and a $1,000M revolver. Manageable as a financing event, not a solvency event, but not free, and $781M of the excess value above $237.40 is unhedged.

**Leverage against a cyclical base.** 3.69x net debt to Adjusted EBITDA in a business whose semiconductor revenue fell 28% in 2023, rose 1% in 2024 and rose 13% in 2025 — a 40-point swing in growth rate inside three years. MD&A frames the present as a period in which "we are currently in a semiconductor ramp." The swap notional protecting the floating Euro Tranche B was cut from $1,900M to $500M, so the remaining floating-rate exposure carries less rate protection than before.

**Palladium in MSD.** The specifically identified cause of 230 bp of compression in the highest-margin, fastest-growing segment, in a precious-metals environment where gold, silver and platinum set records in January 2026 and palladium remains below its 2022 peak, leaving room for further pressure.

**Tariffs, two-sided.** MKS cites duty and tariff costs as a gross margin headwind in both FY2025 and Q1 2026, with 81% of FY2025 revenue international and manufacturing in 18 countries. The February 20, 2026 Supreme Court ruling invalidated IEEPA tariffs and roughly $81B has been refunded industry-wide per press reporting, but no MKS filing quantifies a refund receivable, other current assets actually declined from $263M to $252M, and the administration has pivoted to alternative statutory authorities. Unquantified in both directions.

**Goodwill.** $2,565M goodwill plus $2,065M net intangibles on an $8,728M balance sheet after $1,833M of prior impairment, with goodwill reassigned among PSD reporting units effective February 1, 2026 following a reorganization. A cycle disappointment reopens this question.

**China.** 23.4% of Q1 revenue and growing, in an industry MKS itself describes as subject to significant trade restrictions that have already "impacted our sales." A reported $25M Guangzhou plating-equipment expansion around June 25, 2026 appears only in secondary aggregators and is unverified in any filing.

**Visibility.** No remaining performance obligations disclosed, short lead times, cancellable orders. The consensus second-half ramp is unauditable from outside.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case ($470, +45%).** Q2 delivers on or above the $2.96 consensus and management points to continued sequential acceleration. Electronics & Packaging keeps compounding above 25% as HBM stack heights and accelerator substrate layer counts rise, VSD tariff pressure eases as IEEPA refunds and alternative-authority costs net out favorably, and MSD reprices palladium into contracts, restoring 54%-plus gross margin. FY2027 EPS of $15.45 proves conservative as the NAND upgrade cycle broadens. Free cash flow normalizes toward $500M-plus as the receivable build stabilizes, net leverage falls below 3.0x, and MKS calls the convertible at par after June 5, 2027, capping dilution. At 30x FY2027 EPS the stock reaches roughly $464; call it $470 with early FY2028 visibility.

**Bear case ($205, −37%).** The 2026 second-half ramp does not materialize. Advanced-packaging orders are pulled forward rather than incremental, cancellable short-lead-time orders reschedule, and a levered supplier with 35% top-ten customer concentration takes the adjustment first. Palladium stays elevated, so MSD margin drifts below 50% while VSD absorbs replacement tariffs. Consensus reverts toward $11-12 for both 2026 and 2027, and the multiple compresses to 17.5x — giving $206. Even at $205 the convertible remains far above its $154.30 conversion price, so the cash obligation and residual dilution persist into a weaker cash-flow environment, and goodwill testing becomes live.

**Base case ($370, +14%).** 24x FY2027 consensus EPS of $15.45 equals $371; adjusting for full convertible dilution at that price takes it to roughly $365. The 24x multiple is a discount to the 27.5x current-year multiple, which is the appropriate haircut for 3.7x leverage, a cyclical revenue base with no backlog visibility, and a business whose five-year Adjusted EBITDA CAGR through 2025 was 2.7%. This sits 10% below the $410.77 consensus mean, which I regard as the right side of consensus to be on ahead of a print carrying a ±17.7% implied move.

## Conclusion

Four prior reports produced four HOLDs with a rising score, and the fifth stays a HOLD, but the composition has changed. Valuation improves — the stock is 11.4% cheaper than at the July review while FY2027 estimates rose 26.4% over 90 days, and MKS de-rated less than every close peer in a cohort-wide reassessment. Competitive position trims modestly, because only PSD is converting volume into margin while VSD and MSD, 72% of revenue, absorbed 240 bp and 230 bp of compression from tariffs and palladium. Financial health remains the binding constraint: 3.69x net leverage, negative tangible book equity, a $1.4B cash-settled convertible that is now convertible with $781M of unhedged excess value above the capped-call ceiling, and a first quarter that generated $28M of free cash flow while earnings rose 62%.

At 27.5x this year and 20.9x next with a 0.88 PEG, MKS is not expensive for the growth consensus expects. The question is whether consensus is right, and MKS discloses no metric that can answer it — no remaining performance obligations, sub-90-day lead times, cancellable orders, and a five-year Adjusted EBITDA record that grew 2.7% annually through 2025 while the model now needs 73% growth in two years. Management earns credit for the February refinancing, which converted a floating, secured, near-dated capital structure into a fixed, longer-dated one and cut pro forma cash interest from roughly $198M to roughly $138M, and for putting Adjusted Net Debt in the incentive plan and delivering on it. It does not earn a high management score: twelve insiders sold roughly 67,700 shares for about $21.9M between March and June with zero open-market purchases (all under 10b5-1 plans adopted in the February-March open window, which softens but does not erase the breadth), five-year indexed TSR of 110.4 trailed the peer group's 205.1, and granted CEO compensation rose from $8.76M to $12.96M (+48%) in a year Adjusted EBITDA rose 4.2%.

Own the position through the August 5 print. Do not add into 101.8% implied volatility and a ±17.7% implied move in a name at 3.7x leverage. Add in the $255-275 zone, or upgrade on a Q2 print that shows MSD margin stabilizing and second-half cash conversion arriving.
