## Executive Summary

Onto Innovation is at the center of the AI-driven semiconductor equipment upcycle, with differentiated positioning in advanced packaging inspection and lithography — the specific bottleneck area where AI chip manufacturers are investing most aggressively. After a transitory margin compression year in FY2025 (driven by quantifiable one-time costs from the Semilab acquisition, ERP transition, and restructuring), the company posted record Q1 2026 revenue of $291.9M and guided Q2 to $320-330M, implying annualized run-rate approaching $1.4B. The company has indicated Dragonfly platforms are expected to grow more than 50% in 2026, and industry analysts expect AI-packaging demand to grow approximately 30% annually, supporting estimates of full-year revenue growth above 30%.

The $240M+ Dragonfly volume purchase agreement disclosed in February 2026 with a leading HBM manufacturer provides multi-year revenue visibility and validates ONTO's competitive positioning in the highest-growth subsegment of semiconductor equipment. Estimate revisions are accelerating: next-year EPS consensus has risen 22.1% over 90 days, from $7.69 to $9.39. The stock trades at approximately 44x FY2026E earnings and 36x FY2027E — elevated but defensible given the growth trajectory and structural demand tailwinds from AI infrastructure buildout. However, a recently announced $710M strategic investment in Rigaku Holdings (with an associated $500M bridge loan facility) materially changes the previously pristine balance-sheet picture and introduces execution and financing risk.

Key risks include customer concentration (top 3 = 49% of FY2025 revenue), China export control headwinds that benefit non-U.S. competitors, execution risk from simultaneous organizational transformation, the Rigaku transaction's financing requirements, and the cyclical nature of semiconductor equipment spending. The growth inflection is well-documented and supported by both organic demand trends and the Semilab acquisition contribution.

## Company Overview

Onto Innovation designs and manufactures process control equipment for semiconductor fabrication, specializing in optical metrology, defect inspection, and advanced packaging lithography. The company formed in 2019 from the merger of Rudolph Technologies and Nanometrics. Key product families include:

- **Atlas** — optical metrology for advanced node CD measurement
- **Dragonfly G3** — inspection systems for advanced packaging (HBM stacking, chiplets)
- **JetStep X500** — projection lithography for panel-level advanced packaging
- **Firefly/NSX** — macro defect inspection for wafer and panel substrates
- **Semilab products** (acquired Q4 2025) — wafer contamination monitoring, SiC characterization

Revenue is geographically concentrated in Asia: Taiwan (32%), South Korea (28%), with the U.S. at approximately 15% and China declining to 7%. The company's SEC filings identify significant customer concentration using anonymous labels (Customer A, B, C), with industry sources widely associating major foundry and memory customers in Taiwan and South Korea as key accounts.

**Margin recovery is underway but more gradual than initially guided:**

FY2025 gross margin compressed to 49.7% from 52.2% due to Semilab integration, inventory write-downs, and restructuring. Q1 2026 GAAP gross margin recovered to 50.1% (from 46.4% in Q4 2025), though this remained below initial Q1 guidance of 54.6-55.6% due to $6.1M of Semilab inventory step-up amortization and other items. Management targets Q4 2026 operating margins above 30%, compared to 13.2% in FY2025. Q1 2026 GAAP operating margin was approximately 11.5%, indicating substantial improvement is still needed to reach the Q4 target.

**Balance sheet — strong but evolving:**

As of March 31, 2026:
- Cash, equivalents, and marketable securities: $654M
- Debt: zero at quarter-end

However, on April 20, 2026, Onto entered into a definitive agreement to acquire approximately 27% of Rigaku Holdings Corporation from a Carlyle affiliate for approximately $710M, and concurrently arranged a $500M senior secured 364-day bridge term loan facility to help fund the transaction. This commitment — which exceeds the company's quarter-end cash balance — materially changes the forward balance-sheet profile. While Onto retains significant financial resources, investors should no longer characterize the balance sheet as "zero debt" on a pro-forma basis.

- FY2025 FCF: approximately $300M (29.8% margin), though Q1 2026 operating cash flow fell to $26.3M (from $92.0M in Q1 2025) due to receivables timing and inventory build supporting revenue growth

- Altman Z-Score: 7.1 (as of FY2025)

The asset-light model (capex ~$29M annually) historically provided substantial financial flexibility. Capital allocation has been active: buybacks accelerated from $3.2M (FY2023) to $75M (FY2025), the Semilab acquisition (approximately $432M cash plus 641,771 shares of Onto common stock) added complementary product lines, and the Rigaku investment signals a strategic pivot toward broader process control capabilities.

## Growth Analysis

**Three structural growth drivers:**

1. **AI advanced packaging** (>50% Dragonfly platform growth expected in 2026): HBM stacking, 2.5D/3D chiplet integration, and fan-out wafer-level packaging all require defect inspection before expensive die bonding. The $240M+ Dragonfly volume purchase agreement validates this directly.

2. **Panel-level manufacturing transition**: The industry is moving from 300mm wafers to 650mm panels for advanced packaging substrates. ONTO's JetStep X500 and Firefly tools are purpose-built for this transition, creating first-mover positioning in a new market segment.

3. **Front-end node shrinks**: Continued EUV adoption at 3nm/2nm drives demand for ONTO's Atlas OCD metrology systems.

## Competitive Landscape

ONTO operates below KLA Corporation (~$200B market cap, 14x ONTO's size) in the broader process control space but has carved out differentiated positions:

**Strengths:**
- Advanced packaging inspection: Dragonfly G3 has limited direct competition for HBM stack inspection
- Panel-level lithography: JetStep X500 addresses an emerging market where traditional wafer-scale tools don't compete
- Software integration: Full yield-management software stack from design to production
- Semilab adds compound semiconductor (SiC) capabilities in an underserved niche

**Weaknesses:**
- Scale disadvantage vs. KLA in R&D spending and service infrastructure
- China export controls create an asymmetric competitive disadvantage vs. non-U.S. competitors (Camtek, specifically)
- Customer concentration creates dependency

**Trajectory**: Improving. The $240M+ HBM deal and >50% expected Dragonfly platform growth indicate market share gains in the highest-growth segment. China revenue decline (17% → 7%) has been more than offset by gains elsewhere, demonstrating competitive resilience. Q1 2026 year-over-year revenue growth was partly driven by the Semilab acquisition contribution ($24.0M), and sales to advanced-node customers actually declined year-over-year, partially offsetting gains in advanced packaging.

## Risk Assessment

**Customer concentration (HIGH)**: Three customers accounted for 49% of FY2025 revenue (20%, 15%, and 14% respectively). In Q1 2026, the top three were 14.4%, 13.4%, and 13.3% (approximately 41%). SEC filings identify these customers by anonymous labels only. Given the geographic concentration (Taiwan 32%, South Korea 28%), geopolitical tensions present risk to the supply chain and customer base.

**Rigaku transaction and balance-sheet leverage (HIGH — NEW)**: The $710M Rigaku investment, funded partly through a $500M bridge facility, represents a material shift in ONTO's capital structure. If the transaction closes as expected, the company will carry meaningful debt for the first time, reducing financial flexibility and introducing interest expense. The strategic rationale and return profile of a 27% minority stake require further disclosure and investor scrutiny.

**China export controls (MEDIUM-HIGH)**: Revenue already de-risked to 7% from 17%, but the competitive disadvantage vs. non-U.S. peers persists. The "50% Rule" expansion (suspended until Nov 2026) creates ongoing regulatory uncertainty. Foreign competitors capturing ONTO's lost China customers gain scale and R&D funding.

**Execution risk during transformation (MEDIUM)**: Simultaneous Semilab integration, ERP transition, contract manufacturing transition, Rigaku investment, and management turnover (multiple exec change filings in 2025) creates above-normal operational risk. Q1 2026 GAAP margins (50.1% gross, ~11.5% operating) show improvement from Q4 2025 but remain below targets.

**Cyclicality (MEDIUM)**: Semiconductor equipment spending declined 19% in the FY2023 downcycle. While AI provides structural demand support, the industry remains susceptible to overcapacity corrections. Current capacity expansion rates in HBM/advanced packaging could create excess supply in 2027-2028.

**Earnings predictability (LOW-MEDIUM)**: Yahoo Finance earnings-surprise data shows a mixed pattern over the last four FY2025 quarters (two beats, one miss, one essentially in-line), suggesting forecasting precision is adequate but not exceptional.

## Conclusion

Onto Innovation meets the criteria for a BUY recommendation: Growth Prospects score of 8.5 with Valuation at 5.5 (forward P/E ~44x on rising estimates, supported by 30%+ revenue growth trajectory), historically strong cash generation, and structural demand tailwinds from AI infrastructure buildout. The high-growth inflection threshold applies (Growth ≥ 8.0, Valuation ≥ 5.0, estimate revisions +22% over 90 days), bringing the effective BUY minimum to 6.0 — comfortably cleared at 6.95.

The specific risks that give pause are: (1) the $710M Rigaku investment which materially changes the balance-sheet profile from "pristine" to "leveraged," (2) Q1 2026 GAAP margins that are improving but remain far below management's Q4 2026 targets, (3) weak Q1 operating cash flow ($26.3M vs. $92.0M year-ago), and (4) management turnover during organizational transformation. The Q1 2026 record revenue, Q2 guidance of $320-330M, the $240M+ Dragonfly deal, and >50% expected Dragonfly platform growth in 2026 provide external validation of product competitiveness. The stock is reasonably valued for a company growing revenue 25-30%+ with improving margins and structural AI demand tailwinds. Position sizing should reflect the elevated volatility (72% IV, beta likely >1.5) and the new balance-sheet risk from Rigaku — 1.5-2% of portfolio is appropriate.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** Multiple corrections affect the recommendation: (1) the $710M Rigaku investment with $500M bridge facility materially changes the "zero-debt pristine balance sheet" pillar; (2) actual Q1 GAAP gross margin of 50.1% (not guided 54.6-55.6%) slows the margin-recovery timeline; (3) forward P/E is closer to 44x (not 41x) using Yahoo consensus EPS of ~$6.52 (not $7.11); (4) FY2026 +30% revenue growth is an analyst/model estimate, not confirmed explicit full-year management guidance. Collectively these reduce confidence in the valuation support and financial-flexibility arguments.
