## Executive Summary

Haemonetics is a $2.7B medtech company undergoing a portfolio transition from plasma-collection dominance toward hospital/interventional technologies. The transition is producing real results in gross margin improvement (55% → 59% YoY) and strong FCF ($260M, 19.5% margin), but the execution has been marred by a catastrophic M&A failure — the $150M Attune Medical acquisition written down to $12M within one year — and revenue is declining while estimates continue to fall. The stock trades at 27.7x trailing EPS of $2.05, which includes the impairment charges, and approximately 10.9x forward FY2027 adjusted EPS of $5.22.

The core problem: management destroyed ~$138M in shareholder value with the Attune acquisition (esophageal protection device rendered obsolete by Pulse Field Ablation technology that was already known), the Interventional Technologies unit has only 6% goodwill headroom before additional impairment, and analyst estimates are declining across all forward periods. The company is simultaneously leveraging up the balance sheet ($979M net debt, D/E 3.01) to buy back shares while carrying imminent refinancing risk on $700M convertible notes due 2029. There is no specific near-term catalyst to reverse the negative estimate revision trend, and the stock has underperformed its medtech peer group by ~58 percentage points over recent years (indexed TSR of 62.6 vs. peer group 120.9).

## Company Overview

Haemonetics operates across three segments: **Plasma** (39% of FY2026 revenue, $524M) providing automated collection devices and software to biopharmaceutical fractionators; **Hospital** (44%, $588M) encompassing vascular closure, hemostasis management (TEG 6s), cell salvage, and sensor-guided technologies; and **Blood Center** (17%, $221M), a declining legacy business following the Whole Blood divestiture in January 2025.

The company holds dominant positions in automated source plasma collection (NexSys PCS platform) and viscoelastic hemostasis testing (TEG 6s global market leader). It operates in ~90 countries with approximately 3,009 employees. The strategic direction is shifting the revenue center of gravity toward Hospital, where growth opportunities in TAVR-adjacent technologies (SavvyWire, PerQseal Elite from Vivasure) and expanded vascular closure indications offer higher-margin, higher-growth potential.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** $1.33B in FY2026, down 2% YoY from $1.36B. The decline is attributed to the CSL Plasma customer transition (~$100M+ headwind) and the Whole Blood divestiture (~$48M). Excluding these items, organic growth was positive but modest.

**Margins:** Gross margin expanded 400bps to 59.0%, reflecting portfolio mix shift toward higher-margin hospital products. Operating margin compressed to 11.7% (from 16.3%) due to the $86.5M Attune Medical impairment.

**Earnings:** GAAP EPS of $2.05 (diluted) is depressed by the impairment. Adjusted EPS for FY2026 was approximately $5.0 based on the Q4 beat of $1.29 (consensus $1.28). Forward adjusted estimates: $5.22 for FY2027 and $5.77 for FY2028.

**FCF:** $260M in FY2026 = 19.5% FCF margin, representing strong cash conversion. At a $2.7B market cap, the stock trades at approximately 10.5x FCF.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Total debt: ~$1.24B ($700M convertible 2029 notes + $239M term loan + $300M revolver draw)
- Net debt: $979M (net debt/EBITDA likely ~4-5x)
- D/E: 3.01x
- Altman Z-Score: 1.71 (below the 1.81 distress threshold)
- Current ratio: 3.3x (adequate near-term liquidity)

The Z-Score in the distress zone warrants attention. While the company is generating strong operating cash flow and has adequate liquidity, the combination of high leverage and declining earnings creates refinancing risk as the 2029 maturity approaches.

**Capital Allocation:** $175M in share repurchases during FY2026 with $325M remaining authorization. Shares declined from 50.3M to 47.2M (6% reduction). Buybacks at an average estimated price of $52-$69 range could prove value-accretive if the transition succeeds, but leveraging up during a period of declining earnings and executive uncertainty is aggressive.

## Growth Analysis

**Negative Estimate Revision Trend:** Current quarter estimates have fallen 5.5% over 30 days; next quarter down 4.2%; FY2027 down 1.6% over 30 days (down 1.9% over 90 days); FY2028 down 2.3% over 30 days (down 2.7% over 90 days). This is a uniformly negative trend with no signs of stabilization.

**Growth Drivers (Potential):**
1. **Persona PLUS:** FDA-cleared next-gen plasma collection enabling mid-single-digit increases in plasma volume per donation. Competitive counter to Fresenius Kabi Aurora and Terumo Rika.
2. **PerQseal Elite (Vivasure):** CE Marked, PMA submitted to FDA. Targets large-bore vascular closure for TAVR/EVAR/MCS. TAM expansion into structural heart procedures.
3. **VASCADE MVP XL:** Label expansion to larger sheaths (10-14F ID, up to 17F OD) creates opportunity in PFA and LAAC procedures — PFA's growth creates demand for vascular closure of larger access sites.
4. **TEG 6s:** Global expansion with new indications and cartridges. Market leader in viscoelastic testing.

**Growth Headwinds:**
1. **CSL cost restructuring** ($500-550M savings target by FY28) will continue pressuring pricing in Plasma.
2. **Blood Center decline** — structural volume loss with no apparent floor.
3. **FcRn-targeted therapies** — medium-term existential threat to plasma-derived immunoglobulin demand, HAE's largest customer base.
4. **Interventional Technologies revenue declined 8.2%** in FY2026, raising questions about whether Vivasure/VASCADE growth can offset the ensoETM collapse.

The mathematical reality: FY2027 consensus EPS of $5.22 implies only ~4% growth over FY2026 adjusted earnings. FY2028 at $5.77 implies ~10.5% growth. These are moderate growth rates for a company trading at ~11x forward earnings, but the negative revision direction means these estimates may continue declining.

## Competitive Landscape

**Plasma (Strengths):** HAE holds the dominant share in automated source plasma collection in the US. The NexSys PCS platform with Express Plus and Persona technologies offers best-in-class yield and collection speed. Persona PLUS further extends this lead. However, the customer base is an oligopsony — only 3-4 major fractionators exist globally — giving buyers significant pricing power. CSL's explicit cost-cutting targets signal continued margin pressure.

**Hospital/Interventional (Mixed):** TEG 6s is genuinely differentiated with strong clinical evidence. Vascular closure faces intense competition (Terumo, Abbott, Cordis, Teleflex) but HAE's portfolio spanning VASCADE and PerQseal Elite covers multiple sheath sizes and access points. The SavvyWire sensor-guided technology for TAVR is early-stage but targets a growing procedural market.

**Trajectory:** The competitive position is evolving favorably in hospital (market expansion through Vivasure, label expansions) but facing structural headwinds in plasma (pricing pressure, FcRn therapy threat) and secular decline in blood center.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **Imminent Goodwill Impairment:** Interventional Technologies has only 6% headroom ($46M buffer over $822M carrying value). A 50bp discount rate increase OR 1% revenue growth reduction eliminates the cushion. Given the 8.2% revenue decline in FY2026, this is a near-term probability, not a theoretical risk.

2. **Customer Concentration:** One plasma customer = 13% of total revenue. Top 10 = 44%. The CSL transition demonstrated the impact of a single customer change. Loss or renegotiation by another major fractionator could produce a similar revenue shock.

3. **Balance Sheet Leverage:** D/E 3.01x, Altman Z-Score 1.71 (distress zone), with $700M convertible notes due 2029. In the current rate environment, refinancing at maturity could be materially more expensive.

4. **FcRn Therapy Threat:** If subcutaneous FcRn-targeted alternatives reduce IVIG demand, the entire plasma collection industry faces volume decline. Timeline is 3-5 years but represents secular risk to HAE's largest business.

5. **Management Credibility:** The Attune Medical acquisition ($150M → $12M value in 12 months) when PFA was already "available in Europe for several years" raises fundamental questions about acquisition due diligence and strategic judgment.

### Options Market Signal

The market is pricing HAE as less volatile going forward than it has been historically, suggesting either resolved uncertainty post-earnings or complacency about the remaining risks. ## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Goodwill impairment on Interventional Technologies could trigger on next quarterly assessment, likely at Q1 FY2027 earnings (~August 2026). Continued negative estimate revisions provide ongoing fundamental pressure.

**Timing window:** 3-6 months. The impairment assessment is quarterly; if Interventional Technologies revenue continues declining, the next 1-2 quarterly reports represent the window.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** SI at 5.7% with 3.3 days to cover — manageable. This is not a crowded short. Modest institutional ownership reduces squeeze risk. The low SI also means informed shorts may not yet see this as actionable.

**Borrow and options signal:** Options IV at 6th percentile and below historical vol suggests cheap protection available. Near-term put skew of 4.5% confirms mild institutional hedging. Borrow should be readily available at this SI level.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case $42-$47 vs. current $56.85. Upside risk to $65-$70 on analyst upgrade or estimate stabilization. Reward:risk roughly 1.5:1 ($10-15 downside vs. $8-$13 upside risk). Insufficient for active shorting.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE** — The thesis is directionally bearish but the 1.5:1 reward/risk ratio is inadequate, the company generates strong FCF creating a valuation floor, and there is no high-confidence dated catalyst. The goodwill impairment is probable but not certain in timing. Better expressed as avoidance rather than active short.

## Conclusion

Haemonetics presents a challenging investment case. The underlying business franchises (plasma collection dominance, TEG hemostasis leadership, growing vascular closure portfolio) have genuine competitive value. The valuation at 10.9x forward earnings appears optically cheap relative to medtech peers.

However, the combination of: (1) uniformly negative estimate revisions with no stabilization signal, (2) a management team that destroyed $138M in shareholder value via the Attune acquisition while leveraging the balance sheet, (3) imminent goodwill impairment risk requiring only a 50bp discount rate increase to trigger, (4) high leverage with Altman Z-Score in the distress zone, and (5) no identifiable near-term catalyst to reverse the negative trajectory makes this an AVOID at current levels. The stock needs to demonstrate that its strategic transformation can produce positive earnings momentum before it warrants capital allocation. Until estimate revisions stabilize or a specific catalyst (PerQseal Elite approval, visible Persona PLUS revenue contribution) materializes, the risk/reward is unfavorable relative to alternatives in the Health Care sector.
