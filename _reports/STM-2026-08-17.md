## Executive Summary

STMicroelectronics is emerging from the sharpest earnings collapse in its recent history. Revenue fell 31.7% from the 2023 peak of $17,286M to $11,800M in 2025 while operating income fell 96.2%, from $4,611M to $175M, roughly three units of earnings decline for every unit of revenue decline, the signature of an integrated device manufacturer carrying ~$11.1B of net PP&E and ~$1.9B of annual depreciation against a largely fixed cost base. That same leverage is now working in reverse. H1 2026 revenue reached $6,582M, up 24.6% year over year; Q2 revenue was $3,487M with GAAP operating income of $187M; unused-capacity charges have fallen from $123M in Q1 2025 to $37M in Q2 2026; days sales of inventory improved to 126 from 166 with absolute inventory roughly flat, meaning the improvement came from volume rather than destocking. Management guided Q3 to $3.70B at 37.0% gross margin and Q4 to "above $4 billion." Adding H1 actuals to the Q3 midpoint and the Q4 floor produces a FY2026 revenue floor of ≥$14,282M, or ≥+21.0% on FY2025.

The question is what the volume recovery is worth. At $54.29 the stock trades at roughly 40x the FY2026 consensus of $1.35 and 21.5x the FY2027 consensus of $2.53, against approximately 108x trailing GAAP EPS of $0.50 and ~73x underlying trailing free cash flow of about $634M (reported TTM FCF of −$261M is distorted by the $895M NXP MEMS cash-out; adding it back gives a 4.8% FCF margin). Every cheap metric in this stock is an FY2027 metric, and FY2027 is the year management has explicitly reserved for margin recovery. CFO Lorenzo Grandi tied gross-margin normalization to "completing manufacturing transformations rather than revenue scale alone." That program has consumed $376M in FY2025 and $129M in H1 2026, and its announced savings target, affected sites, headcount impact, and completion date are not verifiable from the material available.

Underneath the consolidated recovery, the segment dispersion is widening. Embedded Processing (19.7% operating margin, +35.5% y/y) and RF & Optical (21.2%, +32%) are carrying the company. Analog/MEMS/Sensors margin has fallen for two consecutive quarters, from 16.2% in Q4 2025 to 10.1% in Q2 2026, and the NXP purchase-price amortization is booked in "Others," so it is not the explanation. Power & Discrete lost $99M on $464M of revenue in Q2, a wider loss on 19.2% more sequential revenue, which is what negative incremental contribution looks like. Annualizing H1 2026's −$183M gives roughly −$366M, about 2.6 points of consolidated operating margin. We rate the shares HOLD: the growth thesis is real and estimate revisions are positive, but the valuation only works on the least reliable line in the model, and the risk composition is unusually adverse for a name already trading at 3.5x EV/sales.

## Company Overview

ST is a European IDM that designs and manufactures its own silicon, with 49,000 employees (48,000 as disclosed in January 2026; the increase most plausibly reflects the NXP MEMS employee transfer rather than hiring) and "more than 200,000 customers." Effective 1 January 2025 the company reorganized into two product groups and four reportable segments, which makes pre-2025 segment history non-comparable:

| Segment | Q2 2026 revenue | Q2 2026 op. margin | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| AM&S (Analog, MEMS & Sensors) | $1,426M | 10.1% | Analog incl. VIPower, MEMS, optical sensing |
| P&D (Power & Discrete) | $464M | −21.4% | Discretes, power MOSFETs, silicon carbide |
| EMP (Embedded Processing) | $1,147M | 19.7% | General-purpose and automotive MCUs, ADAS |
| RFOC (RF & Optical) | $445M | 21.2% | Space/ranging/connectivity, optical & RF |

Channel mix is 70% OEM / 30% distribution, versus 72/28 a year earlier. The newest strategic vector is AI data-center infrastructure delivered through optical connectivity: silicon photonics, drivers/TIAs, and the MCUs inside 800G and 1.6Tbps pluggable modules. ST is a component supplier into the optical-module supply chain, not an accelerator vendor, and it does not own the systems its parts go into. Management targets above $1B of data-center revenue in 2026 and well above $2B in 2027. A second vector is LEO satellite communications within RFOC.

Morningstar assigns a Narrow moat with Very High uncertainty and a $72 fair value estimate. The segment margin dispersion supports that characterization: MCU toolchain lock-in and specialized RF/optical/space silicon look defensible; commodity power discretes show no evidence of pricing power in this data.

## Financial Analysis

**The cycle in five years** (SEC XBRL company facts):

| $M | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 12,761 | 16,128 | 17,286 | 13,269 | 11,800 |
| Gross margin | 41.7% | 47.3% | 47.9% | 39.3% | 33.9% |
| Operating income | 2,419 | 4,439 | 4,611 | 1,676 | 175 |
| Operating margin | 19.0% | 27.5% | 26.7% | 12.6% | 1.5% |
| Diluted EPS ($) | 2.16 | 4.19 | 4.46 | 1.66 | 0.18 |
| R&D | 1,723 | 1,901 | 2,100 | 2,077 | 2,044 |

Three observations materially affect how FY2025 should be read.

First, **FY2025 GAAP operating income was arithmetically smaller than a single non-core credit**. The income statement reconciles as $3,999M gross profit − $1,632M SG&A − $2,044M R&D + $228M "other income and expenses, net" − $376M restructuring = $175M. Zero out the $228M and operating income becomes −$53M. ST's own risk language ties this line to "research and development and manufacturing programs, which benefit from public funding," and grant proceeds were $258M in FY2025 with $306M of grant advances still unspent at 27 June 2026. The arithmetic is exact; the attribution is not, because the line can also contain royalties, asset-sale gains, and grant amortization, and the 20-F note decomposing it was not available. Either way, subsidy sensitivity sits inside reported operating profit at trough volumes, which few consensus models isolate.

Second, **the tax line, not operations, drove the Q4 2025 headline**. FY2025 tax expense of $220M on $400M of pre-tax income is a 55.0% effective rate, including $163M of one-time non-cash tax expense booked in Q4. ST did not exclude that from non-GAAP net income and disclosed it as a $0.18/share drag on Q4 non-GAAP EPS of $0.11. That is a conservative convention and deserves credit; it also means the widely reported "−54.9% Q4 EPS miss" was substantially a tax event, while Q4 non-GAAP operating income of $266M (8.0% margin) rose 22.8% sequentially.

Third, **free cash flow needs a correction in the opposite direction of the headline**. Reported TTM FCF at Q2 2026 was −$261M ($130M + $257M − $723M + $75M), but Q1 2026 explicitly includes the $895M NXP MEMS cash-out. Underlying TTM FCF is approximately +$634M on TTM revenue of $13,098M, a 4.8% FCF margin. That correction improves the diagnosis without improving the valuation: $634M against a market capitalization of roughly $48.4B (892.5M shares outstanding × $54.29) is a 1.3% yield. We would not annualize Q2's $75M: it absorbed ~$44M of restructuring cash out, capex is lumpy ($409M net in Q2 against a $2.0–2.2B annual plan), and receivables rose $247M sequentially.

**Capital intensity is the structural constraint.** FY2025 net capex of $1.79B was 15.2% of revenue; the FY2026 plan of $2.0–2.2B is 14.0–15.4% of the ≥$14.28B floor case. Q2 D&A was $492M (~$1.97B annualized). Even at ≥$14.3B of revenue, ST must clear roughly $2.0B of capex and $2.0B of depreciation before free cash flow compounds meaningfully.

**Balance sheet.** At 27 June 2026: total assets $27,032M, liabilities $8,982M, equity $18,050M. Net financial position +$2,007M (liquidity $6,032M less total financial debt $4,025M), or +$1,701M adjusted for $306M of unspent capital-grant advances. Net cash fell $782M from 31 December 2025, consistent with the $895M acquisition outflow. Total financial debt nearly doubled from $2,133M after a $1.5B dual-tranche convertible issuance ($750M due 2031, $750M due 2033) alongside early redemption of the $750M 2027 convert; undrawn committed facilities rose to $1,191M from $640M. Long-term debt "does not impose minimum financial ratios." Independently computed Altman Z is 4.94 on the Q2 2026 balance sheet and 6.21 on FY2025, but the market-value-of-equity term supplies 3.36 and 4.59 of those respectively while EBIT/assets contributes 0.007–0.021; the score reflects low leverage and a large market cap, not earnings power. Solvency is not the investment question here. Piotroski F-score is 6/9, with all three failures (ΔROA, gross margin, asset turnover) being trough artifacts that reversed in H1 2026.

**Dilution.** Diluted weighted-average shares rose from 893.9M (Q2 2025) to 928.0M (Q2 2026), +3.8% of the prior base; a pre-existing holder's proportionate stake fell to 96.3% of its prior level. Shares outstanding were 892.5M. Conversion prices, settlement mechanics, and any call-spread hedging on the 2031/2033 tranches are unverified, so maximum contingent dilution cannot be stated. Treasury stock declined from −$637M to −$506M in H1, meaning employee share deliveries exceeded repurchases; the active buyback is not currently shrinking the share count net of compensation.

**Earnings surprise record** is genuinely mixed: +11.0% (Q2 2026), −27.7% (Q1 2026), −54.9% (Q4 2025), +23.6% (Q3 2025). Two beats and two misses in four quarters argue for moderate rather than high confidence in point estimates.

## Growth Analysis

The demand inflection is corroborated by four independent operating markers rather than one: strong bookings in all end markets, book-to-bill close to 2, distribution inventory below ST's own target, and DSI down 40 days on flat absolute inventory. End-market growth in Q2 2026 was broad: CECP +50%, Industrial +34%, Personal Electronics +20%, Automotive +16% (and +14% sequentially, after being described as "below expectations" as recently as Q4 2025).

Guidance is specific: Q3 revenue $3.70B ±350bps (+16.2% y/y) at 37.0% ±200bps gross margin including ~70bps of unused-capacity charges, at an assumed €/$ of 1.14; Q4 "above $4 billion"; H2-over-H1 growth "above our normal 15% seasonality." The derived FY2026 revenue floor of ≥$14,282M (+21.0%) is reproducible from H1 actuals plus the Q3 midpoint plus the Q4 floor, and should be read as a sensitivity, not a forecast, because Q3 carries a ±350bps range and Q4 is open-ended.

Estimate revisions confirm the direction without reaching the threshold that would flip this into a growth-BUY. FY2026 consensus rose 13.1% in 90 days to $1.35; FY2027 rose 15.4% to $2.53; next-quarter estimates rose 29.4% in 90 days to $0.56. Against that, the current-quarter estimate was cut 7.1% in the last 30 days to $0.41, consistent with the Q3 guide of $3.70B landing below the FactSet consensus of $3.79B. The 90-day annual revisions are meaningfully positive but below the +30% velocity that historically precedes sustained re-rating, and the near-term revision is negative.

We can sanity-check the FY2026 consensus. Q2 non-GAAP EPS was $0.31, H1 $0.44. Q3 at $3.70B and 37.0% gross margin implies roughly $1,369M of gross profit; holding Q2's ~$1,026M of net operating expense gives about $339M of operating income and roughly $0.38–0.42 of non-GAAP EPS. Q4 above $4.0B at a further-improved margin plausibly delivers $0.43–0.47. That path sums to approximately $1.25–1.33, close enough to $1.35 that the current-year number is achievable if guidance holds, with no cushion.

The FY2027 number is a different order of assumption. $2.53 on roughly 928M diluted shares requires about $2.35B of non-GAAP net income, which on a mid-teens net margin implies revenue near $16–17B and operating margins back toward 17–18%. That requires all of: AI data-center revenue above $2B, gross margin recovering toward the low 40s, the manufacturing reprogram completing on schedule, and P&D losses at minimum stabilizing. Each is plausible; the conjunction is not conservative.

**Growth prospects: 7.5.** Volume recovery is confirmed across four markers and four end markets, guidance is explicit through Q4, and the AI-optical vector is a real content story with narrower competition than general-purpose analog. The score is held below 8 because management has itself decoupled margin recovery from volume, and because the highest-margin growth line may be supply-constrained ("signs of tight supply in several product categories," plus management's acknowledgment of legacy analog capacity challenges).

The bull argument is the PEG: 21.5x FY2027 against implied FY2026→FY2027 EPS growth of 87% gives a PEG near 0.25, and the mean analyst target of $71.52 (median $72.00, matching Morningstar's FVE) implies 31.7% upside. Three considerations temper that. First, the growth denominator is a recovery from a trough, not a durable growth rate; the correct comparison is against normalized mid-cycle earnings power, and a $3.00 mid-cycle EPS puts the stock at roughly 18x, in the middle of the analog/MCU peer band (NXP mid-to-high teens, Infineon ~20x, onsemi low 20s, TI and ADI near 30x). ST at 21.5x FY2027 is priced approximately in line with peers, not at a discount, once you accept the out-year number. Second, the out-year number is the least reliable line in the model precisely because management has conditioned it on an unverified program. Third, current cash generation supports essentially none of the price: 1.3% FCF yield on underlying trailing figures, and even a successful FY2026 (OCF near $2.9B less $2.1B capex) produces roughly a 1.6% yield.

The stock is 33.3% below its 3 June 2026 high of $81.42 and 157% above its 20 November 2025 low of $21.11. At a drawdown of that magnitude the burden is to explain what the sellers see, and three things are identifiable. (a) The June peak was positioning-driven: the shares gapped from $69.02 to $79.51 on 2 June, a 15% single-session move on the AI-optical re-rating, and Morningstar's own read was that "investors were likely seeing a stronger forecast after a massive run-up in many semis stocks." (b) The 23 July print delivered a beat on Q2 ($0.31 non-GAAP vs $0.26 consensus; $3.49B revenue vs $3.45B) with a Q3 revenue guide below consensus and a gross-margin guide below expectations, attributed to "stubborn manufacturing reshaping costs," the second instance of beat-and-guide-below, which establishes a pattern. (c) Segment quality deteriorated at the same time consolidated revenue improved. We read the drawdown as a rational repricing of the timing and quality of the margin recovery, which leaves the stock closer to fair than to cheap.

**Valuation: 5.5.** Modest positive asymmetry versus consensus, but the only cheap multiple is the one contingent on an undisclosed restructuring program executing on schedule, and the trailing and current-year multiples offer no support.

## Competitive Landscape

ST sits at the intersection of two cycles moving in opposite directions, plus one cross-current.

**The AI-infrastructure cycle is a tailwind, through optics.** RFOC grew 32% y/y at a 21.2% margin and EMP 35.5% at 19.7%, with CECP up 50%. The content is optical-module silicon and the MCUs that manage those modules, where the competitive set (silicon photonics, high-speed analog) is narrower than in general-purpose semis. Management claims high forward visibility: more than half of Q2 bookings were for next year, and backlog equal to "about 4.5 to 5 quarters" of average Q2 revenue, mechanically $15.7–17.4B. That figure is a management statement relayed through secondary coverage, not a filing-defined disclosure; ST does not present it as remaining performance obligations, has published no cancellation policy alongside it, and the 2022–23 cycle demonstrated industry-wide that bookings of this character can evaporate. Treat as directional evidence of order momentum only.

**The industrial/automotive power cycle is the opposite story.** P&D revenue is down roughly 23% from Q4 2024's $602M and losing money at a −21.4% margin, with the loss widening $15M sequentially on 19.2% more revenue. That pattern of negative incremental contribution points to price realization below cash cost and/or heavy fixed-cost absorption in under-loaded dedicated capacity. The benign alternative is that ST is deliberately absorbing ramp and qualification costs for a new SiC or 300mm power platform inside the segment, in which case the losses are investment. The underlying driver (Chinese SiC and MOSFET capacity, European EV program slippage, ST's own ramp costs, or allocation effects) is not established from the available disclosure, and it is the single highest-value unresolved question in this file. Either way, a segment running at roughly −$366M annualized inside a business guiding to ~$14.3B of revenue is a persistent 2.6-point drag on consolidated margin.

**The memory cycle cuts both ways.** Morningstar attributes the softer near-term Personal Electronics outlook to high memory prices, with DRAM/NAND cost inflation absorbing device BOM budget that would otherwise fund sensors and analog. That is consistent with what memory tightness has done elsewhere in the device chain this cycle, where assemblers rather than memory suppliers have absorbed the cost. The same AI capex boom that lifts ST's optical franchise compresses its consumer franchise, an internal offset that a segment-level screen would not surface.

**Competitive position: 5.5.** Two segments (EMP, RFOC) are clearly improving with defensible franchises; AM&S margin has fallen for two consecutive quarters on growing revenue with the acquisition PPA booked elsewhere; P&D is deteriorating on both revenue and margin. The trajectory is genuinely mixed, and the AI vector places ST as a component supplier into someone else's system rather than as an owner of the architecture.

## Risk Assessment

**Extreme operating leverage in both directions.** The 2023→2025 path (revenue −31.7%, operating income −96.2%) sets the downside case quantitatively. One quarter of demand disappointment mechanically removes most of the earnings, which is why the FY2027 estimate distribution is far wider than the point estimate suggests.

**Margin recovery is gated on an unverifiable program.** Management explicitly conditions gross-margin normalization on "completing manufacturing transformations rather than revenue scale alone." Cumulative charges are $505M ($376M FY2025, $129M H1 2026), but the announced savings target, affected sites, headcount impact, and completion date are all unknown from the available material. This is the largest single evidence gap, and it sits directly under the valuation case.

**P&D structural loss.** Widening losses on rising revenue point toward needing structural action (exit, consolidation, or a JV) rather than cyclical patience. Any such action in a company with a large (commonly cited but here unverified) French/Italian state shareholding carries political constraints that a purely commercial operator would not face.

**Public-funding dependence.** $258M of FY2025 grant proceeds, $306M of unspent grant advances, and a $228M "other income" line exceeding FY2025 operating income together make EU/national subsidy policy an earnings variable rather than a footnote.

**FX asymmetry.** Dollar revenue against a euro cost base; the effective rate moved from 1.06 (Q1 2025) to 1.16, and Q3 guidance assumes 1.14. Further dollar weakness is a direct gross-margin headwind, only partly hedged.

**Tariffs explicitly excluded.** Both releases state the outlook "does not include any impact of potential further changes to global trade tariffs," and trade policy is the first-listed risk factor.

**Macro exposure.** Roughly half of revenue sits in automotive and industrial end markets concentrated in Europe. The automotive recovery is one quarter old. [unverified — the research brief does not establish an automotive/industrial revenue share, a European demand-deterioration characterization, or ECB policy stance]

**Governance information deficit.** As a foreign private issuer ST files 20-F and 6-K; there is no DEF 14A and no Form 4 record (`insiderTransactionForIssuerExists: 0`). The empty insider record should not be read as "no insider selling," because Section 16 does not apply. For a company whose equity story is a management-guided 2027 margin recovery, the absence of verifiable pay-for-performance linkage and of any insider transaction signal is a genuine deficit. The FY2025 20-F itself was not read; risk-factor commentary rests on the condensed forward-looking-statement enumeration in the 6-K releases, which appears substantially identical between January and July 2026.

**Risk factors: 4.2.** Several of these (P&D's negative incremental contribution, the undisclosed program economics, the subsidy sensitivity inside operating income) are specific, quantifiable, and not obviously reflected in a 3.5x EV/sales multiple.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** Volume recovery is confirmed by four independent markers, not by guidance alone. FY2026 revenue reaches at least $14.3B (+21%) with Q4 above $4B, and unused-capacity charges (already down from $123M to $37M per quarter) approach zero, mechanically adding gross margin. The manufacturing reprogram completes through 2027, gross margin recovers toward the low 40s, and AI data-center revenue exceeds $2B at RFOC/EMP-class margins near 20%, improving mix quality as well as volume. P&D either stabilizes or is addressed structurally, removing 2.6 points of consolidated margin drag. FY2027 EPS reaches or exceeds $2.53 and estimates continue rising; at 28x on $3.00 the shares reach approximately **$84** (+55%), near the June high of $81.42 and below the $98 street high. Balance-sheet strength (+$2.0B net cash, no financial-ratio covenants, $6.0B liquidity) means the company can fund the recovery without equity.

**Bear case.** The margin recovery slips again, as it already has once. P&D losses keep widening on rising revenue, indicating a pricing rather than a volume problem. European industrial and automotive demand rolls over while memory-cost inflation continues to squeeze Personal Electronics. The €/$ moves against the 1.14 guidance assumption. Tariff changes, explicitly excluded from guidance, land on a business that ships globally from European and Asian fabs. Subsidy support normalizes and the "other income" credit shrinks. FY2027 EPS is cut toward $1.80, and at 15x (a multiple ST has traded at in prior cycles) the shares reach approximately **$38** (−30%), between the $32.80 street low and the current price. The book-to-bill of 2 and 4.5–5 quarters of "backlog" prove cancellable, as the 2022–23 cycle demonstrated they can be.

**Base case.** Guidance holds through Q4, FY2026 non-GAAP EPS lands near $1.35, and FY2027 progresses toward but does not fully deliver $2.53; the plausible path is $2.20–2.50 with gross margin in the high 30s rather than the low 40s. At 25x $2.53, the shares are worth approximately **$63** (+16%). Probability-weighting 45% base / 20% bull / 35% bear gives roughly +8% expected return, which does not clear the bar for new capital at a 4.2 risk score and a 4.8 management score.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Rating: HOLD** (growth hold — there is a positive 12-month path with dated catalysts, but insufficient margin of safety for initiation)

- **Base case target: $63** — 25x FY2027 consensus EPS of $2.53, a multiple consistent with the analog/MCU peer band once AI-optical mix improves earnings quality. **+16% from $54.29.**
- **Bull case target: $84** — 28x on $3.00 FY2027 EPS. **+55%.**
- **Bear case target: $38** — 15x on FY2027 EPS cut to $1.80. **−30%.**
- **Timeframe:** 12–18 months, anchored on the Q3 2026 print (late October), the Q4 print and FY2027 guidance (late January 2027), and the FY2025/FY2026 20-F filings that would disclose the reprogram economics.

**Upgrade triggers (to ACCUMULATE or BUY):**
1. Q3 delivers gross margin at or above the 37.0% guide midpoint *and* Q4 guidance implies gross margin of 39%+, evidence the reprogram is converting to margin rather than deferring again.
2. P&D operating loss narrows for two consecutive quarters, or management announces a structural remedy (exit, JV, consolidation) with quantified P&L impact. This alone is worth roughly 2.6 points of consolidated margin.
3. A price in the low-to-mid $40s with FY2027 consensus intact at or above $2.50, which would put the stock under 18x the out-year and restore genuine asymmetry.
4. Disclosure of the manufacturing-reprogram parameters (annual savings target, total charge, completion date) with a credible bridge to a low-40s gross margin.

**Downgrade triggers (to REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. A third consecutive quarter of guiding below consensus, or any deferral of the margin-recovery timeline beyond 2027.
2. FY2027 consensus cut more than 10% from $2.53, which would remove the only supportive valuation metric.
3. P&D operating loss exceeding $110M in any quarter, or AM&S operating margin falling below 10% for a third consecutive quarter.
4. Book-to-bill falling below 1.0, or distribution inventory rising back above ST's target, the two markers that would indicate the order book was cancellable.
5. Any indication that European public funding or the "other income" credit is normalizing downward by a material amount.

**Reassessment point:** the Q3 2026 earnings release in late October 2026, which tests the 37.0% gross-margin guide and the "above $4 billion" Q4 framing simultaneously. Existing holders should carry the position through that print; new capital should wait for it.

## Conclusion

ST offers a real cyclical recovery attached to a real secular content story, priced as though both are already delivered. The operating evidence for the recovery is strong and multi-sourced: 24.6% H1 revenue growth, book-to-bill near 2, DSI down 40 days on flat inventory, unused-capacity charges down two-thirds, and guidance implying a FY2026 revenue floor 21% above FY2025. Estimate revisions of +13.1% (FY2026) and +15.4% (FY2027) over 90 days confirm that consensus is catching up rather than backing away.

What the recovery has not yet produced is earnings or cash. Trailing GAAP P/E is roughly 108x, underlying trailing FCF is 1.3% of market cap against 15% capex intensity, and the only defensible multiple, 21.5x FY2027, depends on a manufacturing reprogram whose economics are undisclosed and whose timeline has already been pushed once. The segment mix is deteriorating where it matters: Power & Discrete lost $99M on $464M of revenue while growing 19% sequentially, and AM&S margin has compressed for two consecutive quarters for reasons the acquisition accounting does not explain.
