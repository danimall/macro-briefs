## Executive Summary

United Therapeutics has crossed a line that changes how the business should be valued. For the first time in the modern history of the treprostinil franchise, revenue declined: $1,564.8M in the first half of 2026 versus $1,593.0M, down 1.8%, with Q2 down 1.9% to $783.3M. Operating income fell 12.1% for the half to $656.6M and the operating margin compressed 470 basis points to 42.0%. The product detail identifies the source precisely — Nebulized Tyvaso fell 20% in the half with U.S. volumes down $70.9M, Remodulin fell 7% with U.S. volumes down $23.4M, and Tyvaso DPI's +6% growth was roughly half price. Management's own language is unambiguous: "We believe the availability of competitive therapies negatively impacted sales of Nebulized Tyvaso, Tyvaso DPI, and Remodulin."

Reported earnings mask this. Q2 net income rose 7.6% to $333.0M while operating income fell 9.3%, because the 1H26 effective tax rate was 12% against 24% a year earlier — a function of $561.4M of intrinsic value on 1,390,672 options exercised, versus $52.8M on 270,000 options in 1H25. Applying the prior-year rate to 1H26 pretax income of $691.0M produces net income near $525M rather than $607.9M, roughly $1.78 of half-year diluted EPS. This is a genuine cash-tax saving (cash taxes paid fell to $102.5M from $253.9M), but it scales with the share price and is the weakest available input for extrapolating earnings power. Consensus has noticed: current-year EPS estimates have been cut 5.2% over 90 days to $26.92, below FY2025's actual $27.86, and next-year estimates are down 3.6% to $31.51.

The offsetting case is substantial and unusually well-defined. TETON-1 (+130.1 mL absolute FVC, p<0.0001) and TETON-2 (+95.6 mL, p<0.0001) both succeeded in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, with an sNDA submitted in June 2026 against a market UTHR sizes at ~100,000 U.S. patients. ADVANCE OUTCOMES delivered a 55% reduction in clinical-worsening risk (HR 0.45, p<0.0001) for oral ralinepag, NDA submitted the same month, aimed at J&J's Uptravi franchise that management sizes above $1.5B in U.S. sales. The balance sheet carries $3,803.4M of cash and investments against zero debt and a $2.5B undrawn revolver. At $519.08 the stock trades at 18.6x FY2025 diluted EPS of $27.86, 16.5x next-year consensus, and roughly 12.4x FY2025 operating income on an enterprise value near $18.5B.

The reason this resolves to HOLD rather than BUY — after four consecutive BUY ratings and a score trajectory of 7.3 → 6.6 — is a timing problem disclosed in the Q2 filing. Before Nebulized Tyvaso can generate meaningful IPF revenue, the Durable Medical Equipment Medicare Administrative Contractors must revise the local coverage determination governing Part B coverage. Management states the DME MACs have "significant discretion with respect to the overall timing" and that once initiated the process "could take well over a year to complete," and that until then IPF revenue "could be limited, since we believe that most U.S. IPF patients are Medicare beneficiaries." Meanwhile Watson has been contractually permitted to launch generic Nebulized Tyvaso since January 2026 pending FDA approval, Orenitram ($261.3M of 1H26 revenue at ~95% gross margin) faces generics from June 2027, and the Adcirca license from Lilly expires December 31, 2026. There is a plausible and non-trivial window in which revenue declines further before the pipeline arrives.

## Company Overview

United Therapeutics is a Silver Spring, Maryland biotechnology company and, since 2021, a Delaware public benefit corporation with a charter-level dual purpose: developing novel pharmaceutical therapies and developing technologies that expand the availability of transplantable organs. Six FDA-approved products generate revenue, and four are formulations of a single active ingredient, treprostinil, delivered as dry powder (Tyvaso DPI), nebulized solution (Nebulized Tyvaso), oral extended-release tablet (Orenitram), and infusion (Remodulin). Treprostinil products were $1,424.3M of $1,564.8M of 1H26 revenue, or 91%. Unituxin (anti-GD2 monoclonal antibody in neuroblastoma) contributed $118.8M and Adcirca $9.6M.

The moat is not patent exclusivity — the filings explicitly concede that "our patents have not prevented competitors from obtaining regulatory approval to market products that compete with our treprostinil-based products." What the company actually owns is a full escalation ladder within one molecule, in-house API manufacture, and deep specialty-distribution and reimbursement infrastructure with Accredo and CVS Specialty. That infrastructure carries a cost: two U.S. distributors were 48% and 37% of Q2 2026 revenue (85% combined), essentially unchanged from 51%/35% a year earlier.

The second business — organ manufacturing — is pre-revenue and currently the only segment losing money at the gross line, with an $11.0M gross loss on $12.1M of EVLP-service revenue in 1H26. It comprises xenotransplantation (UKidney in the EXPAND study with a first transplant in Q4 2025; UThymoKidney and UHeart INDs cleared), regenerative medicine (miroliverELAP met its Phase 1 primary endpoint in January 2026, RMAT designation granted), and a commercial ex vivo lung perfusion service whose LungFX system received PMA approval in June 2026 with launch expected 2027.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory.** FY2023 $2,327.5M (+20.2%), FY2024 $2,877.4M (+23.6%), FY2025 $3,182.7M (+10.6%), 1H26 $1,564.8M (−1.8%). Two consecutive years of deceleration followed by contraction is a pattern, not a quarter.

**Margin bridge.** The $90.7M year-over-year decline in 1H operating income on $28.2M less revenue decomposes as follows. Cost of sales rose $52.8M (+29%), of which inventory reserve expense rose to $64.1M from $15.5M; Tyvaso DPI alone accounted for $47.1M of the 1H26 reserve, including $34.3M of "estimated losses related to a commercial supply agreement." SG&A rose $8.2M, but that nets a $21.7M PP&E impairment recorded in 1H25 that did not recur — excluding it, SG&A rose approximately $29.9M (+8.3%), with sales and marketing up 15% on the ILD field-force build. R&D was roughly flat (+1%), notable given two Phase 3 readouts and two regulatory submissions in the period.

The supply-agreement loss warrants attention. UTHR relies "entirely on MannKind to manufacture Tyvaso DPI finished drug product and inhalers... with no plans to develop an alternate or backup supply arrangement." Recognizing estimated losses on a purchase commitment is most consistent with contracted minimum volumes exceeding realistic demand. The filing does not disclose contract terms, so alternative explanations remain live — cold-chain shelf-life or expiry losses (Tyvaso DPI requires 2–8°C transport), manufacturing yield or specification issues, or a deliberate inventory build ahead of an IPF launch that has not arrived. What is established is that the charge is large ($47.1M, 5.2% of 1H Tyvaso DPI revenue), growing, and concentrated in the one product management relies on for growth.

**Gross-to-net erosion.** Current-period rebate and chargeback provisions were $276.1M in 1H26 versus $252.3M in 1H25 (+9.4%) on lower revenue, taking the provision from 15.8% to 17.6% of implied gross revenue. The period-end rebate/chargeback liability rose 45% to $251.3M, though a balance movement also reflects payment timing and overstates run-rate deterioration relative to the 9.4% provision increase. The driver is the IRA Part D manufacturer discount program now applying to Orenitram and Tyvaso DPI, phased in because CMS deemed UTHR a "specified small manufacturer" in April 2024 — a meaningful but finite cushion.

**Cash flow and balance sheet.** 1H26 operating cash flow was $776.9M, up 19%, but the drivers do not repeat: a $39.9M receivable release (versus a $100.0M build in 1H25) and $151.4M lower cash taxes. Cash capex was $208.8M, so half-year free cash flow was $568.1M. Applying a normalized 22% cash-tax rate and removing the receivable tailwind pulls half-year FCF toward roughly $450M. FY2025 provides the cleaner anchor: OCF $1,561.2M less capex $520.5M = $1,040.7M. Q3 2026 will absorb approximately $140M of Thymmune IPR&D expense and continuing construction spend; buildings under construction stood at $794.0M at June 30 versus $592.7M at year-end.

The balance sheet is a genuine strength: $1,803.2M cash plus $859.1M current and $1,141.1M non-current marketable investments equals $3,803.4M, against zero debt and total liabilities of only $819.8M on $7,220.1M of assets. Altman Z near 8.2. Net cash is approximately $88.68 per share, or 17% of the market price.

One capex disclosure invites misreading. The 10-Q budgets "approximately $180 million for capital expenditures for the period July 1, 2026 through the end of 2028," but that figure is scoped to specific projects (the RTP ralinepag DPI plant and clinical-scale designated pathogen-free facilities in Minnesota and Texas). It is not total company capex, which ran $520.5M in FY2025 alone, and management separately warns that commercial-scale DPF facilities "will be very capital-intensive" without attaching a number.

**Capital returns.** $1.0B via accelerated share repurchase in August 2025 (2,642,498 shares) plus $1.5B under a March 2026 authorization (2,759,343 shares), totaling 5,401,841 shares at roughly $463 average — accretive against the current $519.08. Weighted diluted shares fell from 48.3M to 45.8M year over year, a 5.2% reduction, and $500M remains authorized through March 9, 2027. The cost is visible: interest income fell to $31.5M from $51.3M in Q2 "primarily due to a decrease in marketable investments due to the sale of securities to fund" the ASRs, and liquidity fell $894M heading into a period requiring facility construction, the Thymmune payment, a potential $250.0M Arena/Pfizer milestone on ralinepag DPI approval, and a $75.7M accrued Sandoz judgment. No dividend, and the company states it does not intend to pay one.

**Earnings quality note.** Other expense of $33.0M for the half was driven principally by a $48.2M net unrealized loss on publicly traded equity securities, partially offset by a $12.8M mark-up on a privately held investment following an observable financing round. These marks are non-operating and volatile; they affect net income and ROE inputs but not operating income or cash flow.

**A data conflict worth flagging.** The supplied profile lists trailing and forward P/E as identical at 19.16, which cannot both be correct given current-year consensus of $26.92 against FY2025 actual EPS of $27.86. Our arithmetic: $519.08 / $27.86 = 18.6x trailing; $519.08 / $26.92 = 19.3x current-year; $519.08 / $31.51 = 16.5x next-year.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case rests on two submitted filings and is unusually well-evidenced clinically.

**IPF.** TETON-2 (597 patients ex-U.S./Canada) delivered +95.6 mL absolute FVC at week 52 (p<0.0001) with significance on most secondaries including time to first clinical worsening, K-BILD quality of life and DLCO. TETON-1 (598 U.S./Canada patients) delivered a larger +130.1 mL (p<0.0001) plus significance on reduced risk of clinical worsening. Integrated effect: +111.8 mL. Benefit was consistent across background therapy (nintedanib, pirfenidone, or none), smoking status and oxygen use, with no new safety signal. Overall survival trended favorably but did not reach significance in either trial. UTHR sizes the U.S. IPF population at ~100,000 patients, with TETON-PPF ongoing against a further ~200,000 progressive-pulmonary-fibrosis patients.

**Ralinepag.** ADVANCE OUTCOMES was event-driven and hit its primary endpoint with a 55% reduction in clinical-worsening risk (HR 0.45, p<0.0001) in a population where 80% were on dual background therapy and 70% were Functional Class II. Secondary endpoints (6MWD, NT-proBNP) were significant, and odds of clinical improvement at week 28 rose 47% (p=0.015). Data published in *The Lancet*. Differentiation versus Uptravi is once-daily rather than twice-daily dosing. Note the economics: ralinepag DPI approval triggers a $250.0M payment to Arena Pharmaceuticals (now Pfizer) plus low-double-digit tiered royalties, and MannKind receives up to $35M in development milestones plus a 10% royalty on ralinepag DPI net sales.

**Why the growth score sits at 5.5 despite this.** Three constraints.

First, the reimbursement gate. IPF approval and IPF revenue are separated by the DME MAC LCD revision process, which management characterizes as potentially "well over a year." UTHR has navigated this before — it obtained PH-ILD coverage after the 2021 approval — and commercial (non-Medicare) patients could seed an earlier ramp. Extending the IPF label to Tyvaso DPI would bypass the LCD entirely under Part D, but the company discloses that FDA feedback suggests bridging studies "may include pivotal clinical trials," a multi-year path.

Second, IPF is not greenfield. The filing notes three branded and two generic IPF therapies already approved, including Boehringer Ingelheim's Jascayd (nerandomilast), approved October 2025 for IPF and subsequently for PPF. UTHR's own litigation response describes Boehringer as controlling two of the three branded U.S. IPF drugs.

Third, and most immediately, the base business is shrinking faster than the pipeline can arrive. Total Tyvaso across both formulations was $910.1M in 1H26 versus $935.9M, down 2.8%. Neither PDUFA date nor FDA acceptance status is disclosed for either June 2026 submission, and the 10-Q itself flags that HHS reductions in force and FDA layoffs "have resulted in slower FDA response times and longer review periods."

Estimate revisions confirm the direction: current quarter −1.8% over 30 days, next quarter −4.8% over 90 days, current year −5.2%, next year −3.6%. Every period is negative. The strong-buy revision signal (+30% over 90 days) is not merely absent — it is inverted.

## Valuation Assessment

At $519.08 against $27.86 of FY2025 diluted EPS, UTHR trades at 18.6x trailing. Enterprise value of approximately $18.5B (using 42.89M basic shares less $3.80B of cash and investments, zero debt) equals 5.8x FY2025 revenue and 12.4x FY2025 operating income of $1,492.5M. Excluding net cash of $88.68 per share, the stock trades at 16.0x current-year consensus of $26.92.

Peer context is limited by composition. Of the five market-cap-proximate biotechnology comparables, three carry negative trailing P/E (MRNA −6.1, INSM −17.0, BNTX −15.2) and are not usable as multiple anchors. The two profitable comparables bracket UTHR: INCY at 15.3x and RPRX at 29.0x. UTHR at 18.6x sits between them and below the stated peer median of 29.0x, but that median is not meaningful when it is drawn from a two-name profitable subset. A better frame: UTHR trades at a mid-teens EV/EBIT with declining revenue, which is roughly where the market prices pharmaceutical franchises approaching genericization.

The bull valuation argument is that the multiple embeds essentially nothing for the pipeline. Against 12.4x operating income, the market is granting no separable credit to a filed sNDA addressing 100,000 U.S. patients, a filed NDA targeting a $1.5B U.S. competitor franchise, TETON-PPF, the SMI programs, or an organ platform with three cleared INDs, a completed Phase 1 and ~$794M of construction in progress. Analyst consensus reflects this: mean target $663.23 (+27.8%), median $687, high $750, low $516, with 12 of 15 ratings at buy or strong buy and sentiment stable.

We are not treating those targets as a floor. The convention that consensus targets lag reality applies to stocks with rising estimates and reasonable multiples. Here the multiple is reasonable but estimates are falling across every horizon, which is the configuration in which targets are stale on the downside — they reflect a growth path that consensus is actively marking down.

The bear valuation argument is that 18.6x is correct rather than cheap. FY2025 EPS flatters the company because it captures a full year before the 2026 declines; current-year consensus of $26.92 is already below it; Q3 will absorb ~$140M of Thymmune IPR&D; and the 12% effective tax rate propping reported EPS is reflexive to the share price. Layer in Watson's permitted-but-unapproved generic on Nebulized Tyvaso, Orenitram generics from June 2027, Adcirca expiry in December 2026, and the GLOBE/GUARD most-favored-nation rebate proposals against 94.4% U.S. revenue concentration, and multi-year revenue decline is a coherent scenario.

We score valuation 6.0 — modestly supportive, not asymmetric. The compressed multiple is not a value trap, because two genuine recovery catalysts exist and are already at FDA. But cheapness alone, with estimates falling and the catalyst timeline reimbursement-gated, does not carry a BUY.

## Competitive Landscape

The competitive deterioration is now measurable rather than prospective, which is why this dimension has fallen from 7.0 to 5.0 across five reports.

**Yutrepia is the proximate cause of the nebulized decline.** Liquidia's dry-powder treprostinil received final FDA approval in May 2025 for both PAH and PH-ILD, filed under 505(b)(2) with Nebulized Tyvaso as reference listed drug, and launched June 2025. Nebulized Tyvaso U.S. volumes fell $70.9M in 1H26, the first full half in which Yutrepia was in market for both sides of the comparison. The substitution direction is instructive — the loss concentrates in the nebulized formulation rather than DPI, consistent with convenience-driven switching. UTHR's response is a treprostinil soft mist inhaler in pivotal PK studies: 14-day multi-dose cartridge, room temperature, pocket-sized. That is the right answer, but it is not yet filed.

**Winrevair attacks the escalation trigger.** Merck's sotatercept (launched March 2024) works through activin signaling as an add-on in earlier-line patients, which compresses the point at which patients historically escalated to inhaled or infused prostacyclins. Management confirms it "competes with our treprostinil-based products." Ralinepag is the counter-move, but it competes against Uptravi rather than recovering share from Winrevair.

**The generic calendar is dense.** Watson may launch generic Nebulized Tyvaso in the U.S. as of January 2026 and lacks only FDA approval, which could arrive at any time. Actavis may launch generic Orenitram from June 2027 and ANI from December 2027, with earlier entry possible under certain circumstances. Orenitram was $261.3M of 1H26 revenue at ~95% gross margin — proportionally the most damaging line to lose.

**Insmed's TPIP** (treprostinil palmitil inhalation powder) is named in the filings as the next wave in late-stage trials for PAH and PH-ILD.

The offsetting franchise strengths are real: no competitor offers the full delivery ladder, in-house treprostinil API supports all four products, and UTHR pioneered the PH-ILD indication. But UTHR competes against "over fifteen branded and generic drugs" in PAH by its own count, and the trajectory across every product line except Tyvaso DPI is negative — with DPI's growth roughly half price-driven in a policy environment hostile to price.

## Risk Assessment

**Reimbursement timing.** The LCD gate is the single most consequential disclosure in the file and is not visible in any standard data pull. It creates a window in which the base business declines further before IPF revenue arrives.

**Policy tail.** The December 2025 GLOBE and GUARD proposed regulations would create mandatory payment models requiring manufacturers to pay federal rebates based on most-favored-nation international pricing. Management's own characterization: "a drastic and unprecedented shift in the U.S. pharmaceutical market." With 94.4% of 1H26 revenue in the U.S., there is no geographic buffer.

**Single-source manufacturing.** MannKind is the sole manufacturer of Tyvaso DPI drug product and inhalers with no backup planned. Orenitram and Unituxin are manufactured entirely in-house without an FDA-approved backup site; Unituxin already experienced 2025 supply limitations that caused UTHR's Japanese distributor to delay new patients.

**Customer concentration.** 85% of Q2 revenue through two distributors, flat to marginally worse year over year. Management explicitly warns order timing "can be impacted in a way that does not reflect patient demand."

**Litigation, both directions.** Six active matters. The Sandoz/RareGen judgment is accrued at $75.7M and on appeal to the Third Circuit; management does not expect material excess loss but concedes an adverse appellate outcome "could result in substantial monetary damages in excess of the liability we have accrued to-date." The pending Liquidia '327 decision (trial concluded June 2025, still undecided as of the August 5 filing) is materially two-sided — a UTHR win would force removal of the PH-ILD indication from Yutrepia's label, and the '327 patent expires 2042. The North Carolina trade secret trial set for January 2027 is a genuine two-way binary: an adverse outcome removes UTHR's co-ownership defense in the Liquidia '494 case and un-stays a damages claim against Tyvaso DPI. Boehringer's July 17, 2026 Chancery complaint alleging sales-force raiding matters less for damages than for injunctive relief that could restrict how recently hired reps work the IPF launch.

**Capital sink with binary regulatory risk.** The Virginia designated pathogen-free facility is "first of its kind"; management notes disease outbreak in the herd could impair timelines and that failure would leave facilities "difficult to repurpose."

**Governance and structure.** The PBC charter requires directors to balance shareholder financial interests against stakeholder interests and the public benefit purpose, with the filing stating there is "no guarantee that our Board will resolve conflicts... in favor of our shareholders' financial interests" and that in a control transaction the board "might choose to accept an offer that does not maximize shareholder value." Combined with MannKind's right to terminate the Tyvaso DPI and ralinepag DPI licenses upon certain change-of-control transactions, any takeover-premium thesis is structurally capped. Founder/Chairperson/CEO Rothblatt is named as a specific key-person risk, and the company maintains no key person life insurance.

We score risk factors 4.3. The $3.8B net cash position absorbs essentially every quantified legal outcome, which is why this is not a 3. But the number of unpriced binaries — Watson approval timing, '327 ruling, January 2027 trial, GLOBE/GUARD, LCD duration, Boehringer injunctive exposure — is unusually high for a company whose earnings are simultaneously contracting.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM implied volatility of 33.7% sits 9.2 points below trailing one-year realized volatility of 42.9%, in the 7th percentile of the current chain, with a flat term structure (33.7% near versus 34.2% at six months). The market is not pricing an imminent large event, consistent with the absence of disclosed PDUFA dates.

Positioning tells a different story. Put/call open-interest ratio jumped from 0.02 to 4.66 between July 19 and August 5, put/call volume ratio is 24.79, and the August 21 tenor shows 3.6% put skew indicating elevated put demand. Longer tenors show negative skew readings without a defined sign convention, so we draw no directional inference from those. The most parsimonious reading of heavy put open interest alongside cheap implied volatility is protective hedging of held long positions ahead of undated regulatory decisions rather than a directional bearish bet — protection is being bought precisely because it is inexpensive. Short interest of 5.8% of float at 3.8 days to cover is uncrowded and does not corroborate an organized bear case. For an owner, the configuration argues that downside protection is currently cheap relative to realized volatility.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case — $720

Both June 2026 submissions are approved within twelve to fifteen months. The DME MAC LCD process moves faster than management's cautious framing, or commercial IPF patients seed a 2027 ramp ahead of Medicare coverage. Ralinepag secures Part D formulary placement and begins converting the >$1.5B U.S. Uptravi franchise on a once-daily dosing advantage backed by a 55% clinical-worsening risk reduction. The pending '327 decision goes UTHR's way, removing Yutrepia's PH-ILD indication and reversing the nebulized volume decline. Watson's generic remains unapproved. FY2027 EPS reaches $33 and the multiple re-rates to 22x on restored growth: $726, call it $720. This is close to the $750 analyst high.

### Bear Case — $380

Watson receives FDA approval for generic Nebulized Tyvaso, compounding Yutrepia's share capture on a product already down 20%. Orenitram generics arrive on schedule in June 2027, removing a ~$261M half-year line at ~95% gross margin. The LCD process consumes eighteen months and IPF contributes negligibly through 2027. GLOBE/GUARD advance toward finalization, applying MFN-based rebates to 94.4%-U.S. revenue. The option-driven tax benefit normalizes toward 22–24% as the share price stalls, removing roughly $3.50 of annualized EPS optics. Supply-agreement loss accruals expand. FY2027 EPS falls to $24 and the multiple compresses to ~14.5x with net cash providing the floor: $348 plus support from $88/share of net cash and the remaining $500M buyback authorization, call it $380 — down 26.8%.

### Base Case — $570

Revenue troughs in 2026 and returns to modest growth in 2027 as Tyvaso DPI, ralinepag's early launch and international expansion offset nebulized and Remodulin erosion. IPF is approved but ramps slowly through the LCD process, contributing little before 2028. Orenitram genericizes on schedule. The tax rate normalizes partially. Buybacks continue to shrink the share count 3–5% annually. We haircut next-year consensus of $31.51 to approximately $30 for LCD delay and Orenitram timing, and apply 19x — the current trailing multiple, held flat on the view that pipeline validation and franchise erosion roughly offset: $570, up 9.8%.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (uncertain).** The classification is deliberate. We are not holding for yield — there is no dividend. We are not holding on a confident appreciation thesis — base-case upside of 9.8% against bear-case downside of 26.8% is negatively skewed. We are holding because the decisive variables are genuinely unresolved and several are not disclosed: PDUFA dates for two submissions, LCD initiation and duration, the '327 ruling that has been pending fourteen months, and Watson's approval timing. Existing holders own a debt-free business with $88.68/share of net cash, a validated pipeline at FDA, and $500M of authorized repurchase. New capital has no reason to commit at $519 when the same optionality is available cheaper if the Q3 print confirms further erosion.

**Reassessment point:** the Q3 2026 report (early November), which will carry the ~$140M Thymmune IPR&D charge and provide the first read on whether Nebulized Tyvaso's decline is stabilizing or steepening. Also monitor the FDA acceptance/filing announcements for both June submissions and any 8-K disclosing the '327 decision.

**Upgrade to BUY on any of:**
1. Two consecutive quarters of total revenue growth at or above 0% year over year with operating margin recovering to 45%+ — this is the cleanest evidence that the competitive erosion has been absorbed.
2. Disclosure that the DME MAC LCD revision has been initiated with a defined timeline, or evidence of commercial (non-Medicare) IPF uptake exceeding $25M in a quarter.
3. A favorable ruling in the Liquidia '327 case removing Yutrepia's PH-ILD indication.
4. Price toward $430–450 with the pipeline intact, which would put the stock near 14x next-year consensus and roughly 11x ex-net-cash.

**Downgrade to AVOID on any of:**
1. FDA approval of Watson's generic Nebulized Tyvaso.
2. An adverse verdict in the January 2027 North Carolina trade secret trial, which would un-stay Liquidia's '494 damages claim against Tyvaso DPI.
3. GLOBE and/or GUARD finalized in substantially their proposed form.
4. Current-year consensus EPS falling below $25, or supply-agreement loss accruals expanding beyond the 1H26 $34.3M run rate.

## Management Quality Note

Capital allocation over the past twelve months has been rational and, ex post, accretive: $2.5B of repurchases at roughly $463 average against a $519 price, a 5.2% reduction in weighted diluted shares, and disciplined M&A structure on Thymmune ($140M upfront against up to $160M of milestone-contingent earnouts tied to dosing by December 2028 and BLA acceptance by December 2031). The accretion is partly circumstantial — the August 2025 and March 2026 ASRs preceded or coincided with the TETON-1 and ADVANCE OUTCOMES readouts, and identical capital deployed ahead of trial failures would have looked very different.

Insider activity is structurally readable and directionally unhelpful. From July 16 through August 3, 2026, the CEO exercised exactly 9,500 options at $135.42 on essentially every trading day and sold the same 9,500 shares at $514–542, with ending common holdings constant at 324,443 and the option position stepping down from 331,910 to 217,910. Derived: roughly 110,000 shares monetized for approximately $58M gross, cutting the option overhang by about a third in three weeks. The mechanical uniformity is the signature of a formulaic program rather than discretionary selling, and the Q2 filing's Item 5(c) disclosure window ends June 30, so whether a 10b5-1 plan governs the July–August sales is unverified. We do not treat this as a discretionary bearish signal. We also decline to dismiss it: the standard mitigation — that insider selling is noise when contradicted by rapidly rising estimates — does not apply here, because estimates are falling in every period. There are no open-market purchases in the recent record; director activity is entirely annual grants dated July 22, 2026.

Two governance items reduce the score. The PBC charter's explicit statement that the board may not resolve conflicts in favor of shareholder financial interests, and may accept a control offer that does not maximize shareholder value, is an unusual disclosed limitation. ## Conclusion

United Therapeutics is a debt-free, cash-generative specialty pharmaceutical business whose core franchise entered decline in the first half of 2026 and whose two most valuable growth assets are sitting at FDA with undisclosed review timelines and a Medicare coverage process management describes as potentially exceeding a year. At 18.6x trailing and 16.5x next-year consensus with $88.68 of net cash per share, the stock is not expensive. After four consecutive BUY ratings and a score path of 7.3 → 6.6 driven by exactly the deterioration now visible in product-level revenue, we move to HOLD and would require either evidence that the franchise decline has stopped or a price near $430–450 before adding capital.
