**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

SBA Communications is a leading U.S. telecom tower REIT generating ~$2.8B in annual revenue from leasing antenna space on 46,358 towers across the Americas and Africa. The company is traversing a trough period where organic domestic growth (~75% of operating profit) is being fully offset by Sprint/EchoStar churn, resulting in a 2.3% YoY domestic revenue decline in Q1 2026. Simultaneously, $13B of debt originally issued at 1.6-2.6% rates during 2020-2021 is beginning to refinance at 5%+, driving a 23% increase in interest expense and compressing AFFO per share from $13.37 (FY2024) to $12.85 (FY2025).

The investment case rests on two near-term catalysts: (1) credible PE take-private speculation at $250/share (~24% above current), with CEO Cavanagh delivering a carefully worded non-denial on the Q1 call, and (2) management's expectation that 2026 represents "peak churn" with organic growth re-accelerating thereafter as Sprint non-renewals normalize and new 5G densification activity increases. The company raised guidance for FY2026 and is pursuing an inaugural investment-grade bond issuance that would meaningfully reduce refinancing costs.

However, four consecutive EPS misses, negative estimate revisions over 90 days (-10.2% for FY2026), and structural leverage of 6.6x net debt/EBITDA in a rising-rate environment create material uncertainty. The options market is pricing near-term stress (IV in backwardation at 33% vs. 26.8% longer-term), though call-heavy positioning suggests the market is not outright bearish. At 21x forward P/E — a discount to peer medians of 31x — the stock prices in the headwinds, but re-rating requires demonstrated domestic organic growth recovery and successful execution of the IG financing strategy.

## Company Overview

SBA Communications operates as a REIT focused exclusively on macro wireless towers. The business model is straightforward: own vertical steel structures and lease space to wireless carriers under long-term contracts (5-15 year initial terms) with built-in annual escalators of 3-5%. Each incremental tenant added to an existing tower generates near-100% margin revenue given the fixed-cost nature of tower operations.

**Segment Breakdown (Q1 2026):**
- Site Leasing: 98.5% of segment operating profit ($450.3M domestic + $205.8M international)
- Site Development: 1.5% (end-to-end network buildout services, U.S. only)

**Customer Concentration:** T-Mobile (31.1% of revenue), AT&T (20.3%), Verizon (15.1%) comprise 66.5% of total revenue. Domestically, these three represent 88% of site leasing. This concentration is structural to the industry — there are only three national wireless carriers in the U.S.

**Geographic Mix:** ~65% U.S./territories, ~21% Brazil, ~10% Central America (Guatemala, Costa Rica, Panama, El Salvador, Nicaragua), ~4% Africa (South Africa, Tanzania). The 2025 Millicom acquisition added 7,110 towers in Central America.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:**
- FY2023: $2.444B
- FY2024: $2.668B (+9.2%)
- FY2025: $2.815B (+5.5%)
- Q1 2026: $703.4M (+5.9% YoY, aided by Millicom acquisition; organic domestic -2.3%)

The revenue growth rate is decelerating as Sprint churn offsets new leasing activity. International revenue grew 24.8% in Q1 2026 on a constant currency basis, but this largely reflects the Millicom acquisition rather than organic momentum.

**Profitability & Cash Flow:**
- Adjusted EBITDA (FY2025): $1.912B (+1.4% YoY; +$25.9M constant currency)
- Tower cash flow margin: ~80% (industry-leading)
- Operating cash flow (FY2025): $1.291B (down from $1.335B)
- Free cash flow (FY2025): $1.067B
- AFFO/share: $12.85 (FY2025), down 3.9% from $13.37 (FY2024)

The AFFO per share decline is the critical metric. For a REIT, AFFO represents the core recurring earnings power. Interest expense growth (+23.4% YoY in Q1 2026) is consuming the organic revenue gains.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Total debt: ~$13.0B
- Net debt/Adjusted EBITDA: ~6.6x (within company's 6-7x target)
- Shareholders' equity: -$4.75B (structural for tower REITs — cumulative buybacks/dividends exceed retained earnings)
- Current ratio: 0.29 (reliant on revolving credit and asset-backed securities market for liquidity)

**Debt Maturity Wall:**
- November 2026: $1.165B (currently at 1.631%, refinancing at ~5.25% = ~$42M additional annual interest)
- February 2027: $1.5B (currently 3.875%)
- 2027 (various): $1.515B in additional tower securities

Over the next 18 months, approximately $4.2B of debt must be refinanced at significantly higher rates. Even achieving investment-grade status (targeting ~5% vs. 5.25%+ high-yield) would only partially mitigate the step-up.

**Capital Allocation:**
- 2025 dividends: $479M ($4.44/share)
- 2025 buybacks: $498M (2.5M shares at avg $200.73)
- 2026 dividend: $5.00/share annualized (13% increase)
- Remaining buyback authorization: $1.1B

The dividend payout ratio is ~41% of AFFO, providing substantial flexibility. The buyback at $200.73 average was executed near current levels, suggesting management views this range as fair-to-attractive.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term Growth Drivers:**
1. **Sprint churn normalization:** Management indicates ~$75M remaining Sprint non-renewals will largely conclude by late 2026/early 2027, removing the principal domestic drag.
2. **New leasing activity:** U.S. carriers are increasing 5G densification, with new build activity accelerating. Management cited growing backlog and preference of carriers to work with large public tower companies.
3. **Millicom integration:** 7,110 Central American towers purchased as single-tenant are now open for colocation with "pent-up demand exceeding projections."
4. **Escalators:** Built-in 3-5% annual rent increases across the portfolio provide a minimum organic growth floor once churn normalizes.

**Medium-Term Growth Catalysts:**
- Upper C-band spectrum auction expected mid-2027, requiring new antenna installations
- Fixed wireless access expansion adding network strain
- Mobile edge computing/AI inference workloads leveraging macro tower compounds
- 6G early planning (higher-capacity radios requiring denser antenna configurations)

**Growth Inhibitors:**
- Interest expense growth consuming organic revenue gains
- FX headwinds on international revenue (Brazilian Real particularly volatile)
- EchoStar default removing ~$56M in 2026 revenue
- International carrier consolidation creating churn in LatAm markets

**Guidance (FY2026, raised post-Q1):**
Management raised all key metrics for FY2026, signaling confidence in the trajectory despite the domestic headwinds. Specific targets were not provided in available data, but the direction is constructive.

## Valuation Assessment

**Current Metrics:**
- Price: $201.73
- Forward P/E: 21.2x (per Phase 1 data)
- Price/AFFO: ~15.7x (on $12.85 FY2025 AFFO/share)
- Dividend yield: 2.5% ($5.00 annualized)
- EV/EBITDA: ~17.9x (implied on ~$13B debt + $21.3B market cap / $1.912B EBITDA)

**Peer Comparison:**
- Peer median P/E: 31.1x
- Peer median dividend yield: 4.1%

SBAC trades at a meaningful discount to real estate peers on P/E (21x vs. 31x) and offers a lower dividend yield (2.5% vs. 4.1%). The P/E discount reflects: (1) declining AFFO/share trajectory, (2) elevated leverage, (3) four consecutive earnings misses. The lower yield reflects SBAC's preference for buybacks over dividends.

**Private Market Value:**
The PE take-private speculation at $250/share implies approximately 19.4x AFFO ($12.85) or ~17.9x EBITDA. Infrastructure assets have historically transacted at 15-20x EBITDA in private markets, suggesting $250 is within the plausible range if the buyer can finance at rates below SBAC's current blended cost.

**Valuation Assessment:** At 21x forward earnings with domestic organic growth temporarily negative and interest costs rising, the market is pricing SBAC as an income-impaired REIT rather than a best-in-class infrastructure monopoly. The discount is partially justified by near-term fundamentals, but overstates the risk if churn normalizes in 2027 and IG issuance succeeds. The PE speculation provides a floor scenario at $250 (24% upside) with uncertain probability.

## Competitive Landscape

SBA is the smallest of the "Big 3" U.S. tower companies (behind American Tower and Crown Castle) but has historically led on operational efficiency metrics:

**Competitive Advantages:**
- Industry-leading ~80% tower cash flow margins
- Pure-play macro tower focus (unlike AMT's data center diversification or CCI's fiber/small cell exposure)
- 71% of towers on owned land or 20+ year leaseholds, reducing ground rent escalation risk
- Strategic Latin American/Central American positioning with USD-denominated contracts in newer markets

**Competitive Position Trajectory:**
- Gaining share in new builds as carriers prefer large public companies
- Millicom acquisition extends LatAm coverage without the Brazil-heavy FX risk
- Mobile edge computing positioning provides optionality for tower compound monetization
- Less diversified into alternative infrastructure (small cells, fiber, data centers) which could be either a strength (focus) or weakness (missing adjacencies)

**Industry Structure:** The U.S. tower market is a mature oligopoly. New macro tower builds are limited by zoning restrictions and community opposition. The existing installed base has significant barriers to competitive displacement. Small cells and DAS are complementary rather than substitutive for macro towers — carriers need both for coverage and capacity.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Interest Rate Refinancing (HIGH):** Each 100bp increase in refinancing rates on the $4.2B maturing in the next 18 months adds ~$42M in annual interest expense, directly reducing AFFO/share by ~$0.40. If the IG issuance fails or is delayed, the cost impact compounds.

**2. Customer Concentration / Carrier Capex (MODERATE-HIGH):** 88% of domestic revenue from three carriers. A carrier capex reduction (possible in a recessionary environment) would directly slow new leasing.

**3. EchoStar/Churn Uncertainty (MODERATE):** While management labels 2026 as "peak churn," the EchoStar default was not anticipated 18 months ago. Additional carrier distress (smaller MVNOs, international operators) cannot be ruled out.

**4. Brazil Tax Liability (MODERATE):** ~$300M potential exposure (tax deficiencies of $114.1M plus $184.4M in penalties/interest). Timeline uncertain but adverse ruling would impact cash flows materially.

**5. Rising Rate Environment (MODERATE):** Higher long-term rates directly impact tower REIT valuations through discount rate and cap rate expansion. SBAC's 6.6x leverage amplifies rate sensitivity vs. lower-levered REITs.

**6. Technology Disruption (LOW-MODERATE):** LEO satellite (Starlink), network sharing, and carrier-owned small cells could theoretically reduce macro tower demand long-term, but no evidence of material impact within a 3-5 year horizon.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV at 33.0% exceeds historical volatility of 30.5% and is in backwardation vs. 6-month IV of 26.8%. This typically signals the market is pricing a near-term catalyst or elevated uncertainty, consistent with the PE take-private speculation timeline. The put/call volume ratio of 0.39 and OI ratio of 0.54 are both call-heavy, suggesting informed participants are positioned for upside rather than breakdown. The 15.6pp IV compression since late April (48.6% → 33.0%) alongside a 7% price decline indicates the market has de-risked the binary take-private catalyst somewhat — pricing it as possible but not imminent.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($240-250):**
- PE take-private materializes at $250/share (24% upside)
- Even absent PE bid, IG issuance succeeds at 5.0%, reducing refinancing headwind
- Domestic organic growth inflects positive in H2 2026 as Sprint churn normalizes
- 2027 C-band auction drives new leasing activity acceleration
- At $250: ~19.4x AFFO, reasonable for contracted infrastructure

**Bear Case ($165-175):**
- PE interest evaporates, removing floor/catalyst
- IG rating not achieved; refinancing occurs at 5.5%+ adding $60M+ in annual interest
- Domestic churn extends beyond 2026 (new carrier issues or T-Mobile renegotiates)
- Rising rates compress REIT multiples further
- Brazil tax ruling adverse ($300M cash outflow)
- At $170: ~13.2x AFFO, reflecting distressed REIT territory

**Base Case ($215-225):**
- PE rumors fade without transaction but create soft floor
- IG issuance partially succeeds; refinancing at 5.0-5.25%
- Domestic organic growth reaches flat-to-slightly-positive by Q4 2026
- AFFO/share stabilizes at $12.50-13.00 for FY2026
- Multiple re-rates modestly as churn visibility improves
- At $220: ~17x AFFO

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. PE bid confirmed with specific price and financing — would immediately make this a risk-arbitrage situation with definable spread
2. Domestic organic site leasing growth turns positive for two consecutive quarters (currently -2.3%)
3. Investment-grade rating achieved AND first IG bond priced at ≤5.0%, confirming refinancing headwind is manageable

**Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Long-term rates sustained at elevated levels for 4+ weeks, compressing REIT multiples and increasing refinancing costs beyond manageable levels
2. T-Mobile announces lease renegotiation program or additional carrier default beyond EchoStar
3. FY2026 AFFO/share guidance cut below $12.00 (implying interest costs overwhelming organic growth)

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August) is the next natural checkpoint — domestic organic growth trajectory, IG issuance progress, and any PE bid developments should all be visible by then. The November 2026 debt maturity ($1.165B) provides a hard deadline for refinancing execution.

## Addressing Score Trajectory

The prior report (April 2026) rated SBAC as AVOID at 4.6/10, down from 6.5/10 (BUY) in February 2026. The decline reflected the sharp selloff from $230 to $165 (52-week low hit March 25, 2026) driven by rate fears and EchoStar churn. Since then, three developments warrant re-evaluation:

1. **Price recovery:** SBAC rebounded from $165 to $201 (+22%), partially reflecting PE speculation and Q1 FFO beat ($3.01 vs. $2.86 consensus)
2. **Guidance raise:** Management raised all FY2026 targets post-Q1, suggesting operational trajectory improving
3. **IG pursuit confirmed:** Concrete timeline for cost-of-capital reduction

The prior AVOID recommendation at $165-175 was directionally correct to avoid at $230 but the stock has re-rated. At $201 with a potential PE floor at $250 and improving operational trajectory, the risk/reward has shifted from asymmetrically negative to more balanced. The four consecutive EPS misses and negative 90-day estimate revisions (-10.2%) prevent a BUY upgrade, but the AVOID thesis (collapsing fundamentals) is no longer supported by the data.

## Conclusion

SBAC presents a balanced risk/reward at $201 with identifiable upside catalysts (PE bid, IG issuance, churn normalization) offset by structural headwinds (rising interest costs, flat domestic growth, elevated leverage in a rising-rate environment). The stock trades at a meaningful discount to real estate peers (21x vs. 31x median P/E), which is partially justified by near-term AFFO compression but may overstate medium-term risk if 2026 proves to be the trough year management expects.

The PE take-private speculation provides asymmetric optionality — if a bid materializes at $250, it represents 24% upside from current levels. If it doesn't, the stock's fundamentals must stand on their own, and those fundamentals (declining domestic revenue, rising interest costs, four consecutive EPS misses) currently don't support aggressive positioning. HOLD with reassessment at Q2 earnings.
