## Executive Summary

Krystal Biotech is a commercial-stage gene therapy company that has achieved one of the fastest financial transformations in biotech history: from pre-revenue in 2022 to $389M in FY2025 revenue with 94% gross margins, 52% net margins, and $1B+ in cash, all from a single product (VYJUVEK for dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa). The company now funds its entire 7-program pipeline — including an inhaled gene therapy for NSCLC — from operating cash flow without requiring external financing.

At ~$289 and a $8.7B market cap, the stock trades at approximately 42x trailing earnings ($6.84) and ~37.6x forward consensus ($7.69 FY2026E, $10.21 FY2027E). VYJUVEK revenue for DEB alone likely supports $3-5B in market cap. The remaining $4-5B must come from pipeline programs, principally KB407 (cystic fibrosis) and KB707 (NSCLC, where 36% ORR in heavily pretreated patients earned RMAT designation). Multiple clinical data readouts in 2026 — including interim results, safety studies, and registrational planning milestones — create a catalyst-rich calendar. The stock has more than doubled from its May 2025 low of $122.80, but the fundamental story has strengthened commensurately — Q1 2026 revenue grew 32% YoY with further margin expansion, and the recent earnings beat was 26.3%.

The primary risks are: (1) revenue concentration in a rare disease with a finite patient pool, (2) substantial pipeline execution risk given that >50% of market cap rests on unproven programs, (3) elevated short interest at 16.3% of float, and (4) negative estimate revision momentum over the past 90 days. The bear case requires pipeline failures and DEB revenue deceleration; the bull case, which requires KB707 to advance toward registration, could be worth $400+.

## Company Overview

Krystal Biotech develops gene therapies using a proprietary engineered HSV-1 vector platform. The platform's key differentiators: vectors are redosable (evade immune response), non-integrating (remain episomal), carry large payloads (35+ KB vs. 10 KB for AAV), achieve high transduction efficiency (up to 95% in epithelial cells), and enable non-invasive delivery (topical, inhaled, eye drops). The company received FDA Platform Technology Designation in October 2025 for the engineered HSV-1 viral vector used in KB801, followed by an additional designation in April 2026 for the vectors used in KB407 and KB111. These designations offer potential development and manufacturing efficiencies, including possible shortening of preclinical and CMC review cycles for follow-on products using the same vector platform.

The sole commercial product is VYJUVEK, approved in the US (May 2023), EU (April 2025), and Japan (July 2025) for dystrophic epidermolysis bullosa. The pipeline spans DEB extensions (corneal, KB803), respiratory diseases (cystic fibrosis KB407, alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency KB408), oncology (NSCLC KB707), ophthalmology (neurotrophic keratitis KB801), dermatology (Hailey-Hailey KB111), and aesthetics (KB304 for wrinkles).

The company is founder-led (CEO Krish Krishnan and President Suma Krishnan), employs 295 people, and operates two CGMP manufacturing facilities in Pittsburgh.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term (VYJUVEK):** DEB revenue growth should continue at 20-30% through 2026-2027, driven by:
- International expansion (Germany pricing expected H2 2026, Italy launch potential H2 2026, France launched October 2025, Japan launched October 2025)
- Label expansion to patients from birth (approved September 2025)
- Self-administration option increasing compliance and convenience
- Manufacturing scale-up enabling supply for international demand

However, DEB is a rare disease (~3,000 US patients, ~9,000 worldwide). Revenue growth will naturally decelerate as the company penetrates its addressable market, likely by FY2027-2028 in the US.

**Medium-term pipeline catalysts (2026-2027):**
1. KB803 (corneal DEB): Phase 3 top-line results expected 4Q 2026 — enrollment completed
2. KB801 (neurotrophic keratitis): data expected before year-end 2026 — ~68,000 US patients (larger than DEB)
3. KB407 (cystic fibrosis): repeat-dose safety study completing enrollment 2Q 2026; registrational study design expected after FDA alignment in 2H 2026, with registrational study initiation expected in 2027 — ~4,000-6,000 addressable US patients
4. KB408 (AATD lung disease): interim repeat dose data expected 2026
5. KB707 (NSCLC): combination cohort interim efficacy data + registrational plans expected later in 2026 — enormous market
6. KB111 (Hailey-Hailey): HALITE-1 open-label repeat-dose study results expected 2H 2026, with potential registrational study start in 2027

**Long-term optionality:** KB707 in NSCLC represents the highest-value pipeline program. The 36% objective response rate as monotherapy in heavily pretreated patients (4 median prior lines, all post-immunotherapy) is striking for a lung cancer treatment, though based on only 11 evaluable patients. FDA granted RMAT designation, and based on FDA feedback, the company expects that a single Phase 3 study (inhaled KB707 + chemo vs. chemo alone in 2L NSCLC) could support potential registration. If successful, the addressable market exceeds $5B annually.

**Estimate Revision Context:** Consensus estimates have declined over 90 days (current year -8.4%, next year -17.7%). This appears to reflect conservative modeling around international launch timing and pricing uncertainty rather than fundamental deterioration. The near-term revision decline is a headwind but likely reflects analyst conservatism ahead of international pricing negotiations rather than weakening product trajectory.

## Competitive Landscape

**VYJUVEK in DEB:** Clear first-mover and competitive advantage. Abeona's Zevaskyn (autologous gene therapy, approved 2025) requires hospitalization, skin biopsies, and patient-specific manufacturing. VYJUVEK is off-the-shelf, topical, self-administered at home, and redosable. The competitive threat from autologous approaches is limited by their practical burdens. BridgeBio's protein-replacement approach is earlier stage.

**KB407 in CF:** No approved gene therapy for cystic fibrosis exists. Multiple competitors (Vertex mRNA, ReCode, Spirovant, 4D Molecular) are all early stage. KB407's demonstrated 29-42% airway transduction in Phase 1 is a strong pharmacodynamic signal. The CF Foundation's TDN sanctioning provides validation. KRYS is working toward FDA alignment on a registrational design expected in 2H 2026, with a registrational study start anticipated in 2027.

**KB707 in NSCLC:** KB707 is a differentiated inhaled gene therapy for lung cancer. No competitor with the same inhaled HSV-1 delivery mechanism has been identified in SEC filings. The enormous 2L NSCLC market has multiple entrants (ADCs, bispecifics, novel immunotherapies) but none using topical gene delivery to lungs. The clinical evidence is still early (11 evaluable patients) and combination data will determine registration viability.

**Platform:** FDA Platform Technology Designations for the HSV-1 vectors used in KB801, KB407, and KB111 provide potential regulatory advantages for these specific programs. The two in-house manufacturing facilities and proven 4x batch scale-up provide operational advantages.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Revenue Concentration (HIGH):** 100% of revenue from one product in a rare disease. The US DEB population (~3,000) sets a ceiling on domestic growth. International pricing outcomes are uncertain and typically 40-60% below US levels.

**2. Pipeline Execution Risk (HIGH):** >50% of market cap is attributable to unproven pipeline programs. KB707's 36% ORR was in only 11 patients. Phase 3 combination studies could fail. KB407's registrational study is now expected to start in 2027, not 2026. Multiple data readouts in 2026 create a catalyst-rich but binary-outcome environment.

**3. Elevated Short Interest (MODERATE):** 16.3% of float short with 10 days to cover indicates meaningful institutional bearish positioning. While short squeezes are possible on positive catalysts, this level signals informed skepticism about the pipeline value embedded in the stock.

**4. Negative Estimate Revisions (MODERATE):** 90-day EPS revisions of -8.4% (current year) and -17.7% (next year) create a headwind. This tension with the beat history suggests analysts are waiting for more pipeline clarity before re-rating estimates upward.

**5. Competition Emerging in DEB:** Abeona's Zevaskyn approval in 2025, while competitively inferior on practical grounds, demonstrates that alternative approaches exist and patients have options.

**6. Insider Selling Pattern:** Both founders have been systematic sellers throughout 2025-2026 across price levels from $150-$266. Director Janney liquidated his entire directly held position in February 2026, though he retained indirect beneficial ownership through Alta Bioequities, L.P. (approximately 73,000-85,000 shares). While founder positions remain large ($400M+ each) and selling likely reflects diversification under 10b5-1 plans, no insider buying has occurred at current levels.

## Conclusion

Krystal Biotech has proven commercial execution while maintaining a deep, high-quality pipeline funded entirely from product cash flows. The financial profile (94% gross margins, 48% FCF margins, $1B cash, no financial debt) provides exceptional downside protection for a company still in its early growth phase. At ~28x FY2027 forward earnings with 30%+ revenue growth, the valuation is reasonable relative to both sector peers and the company's own growth trajectory.

The recommendation is BUY, with the primary catalyst being 2026-2027 pipeline readouts that should progressively de-risk or enhance the value embedded in the pipeline. The 10/11 analyst Buy ratings and $318 mean target (10% above current price) support the thesis. The key monitoring points are KB707 combination data and the trajectory of international VYJUVEK revenue. Position sizing should account for the elevated short interest and binary nature of pipeline catalysts — a 2-3% allocation is appropriate.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** KB407 and KB111 registrational study starts have slipped from 2026 to expected 2027 per latest company guidance, reducing near-term catalyst density and modestly weakening the 2026 de-risking timeline central to the thesis.
