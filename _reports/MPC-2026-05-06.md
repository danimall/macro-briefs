## Executive Summary

Marathon Petroleum is the largest independent petroleum refiner in the United States (2,986 mbpcd capacity), operating in a historically favorable environment created by the Iran-Hormuz disruption that has removed approximately 6 million bpd of global refining capacity. Q1 2026 adjusted EPS of $1.65 beat consensus by 119%, the company pulled forward 40% of annual turnaround into Q1 to maximize Q2-Q4 utilization at 94%, and management secured 10 million barrels of advantaged SPR crude for processing. Estimate revisions have been extraordinary: current quarter EPS estimates rose 98% over 90 days and next quarter 100%.

The fundamental setup remains strong. However, the stock at $260.51 has appreciated 82% from its 52-week low and now trades above the analyst consensus mean target of $249.29. The critical risk is binary: a US-Iran peace deal framework (assigned 20-30% probability of holding) could normalize crack spreads from current elevated levels toward 2024-era margins within weeks, removing $3-5/bbl from blended crack spreads and compressing annual earnings from $22+ to $12-14. At current levels, the upside/downside asymmetry has shifted unfavorably: base case fair value approximates $270-285 (4-9% upside) while bear case lands at $185-195 (25-29% downside).

The stock transitions from BUY to HOLD. The thesis is intact but the entry price no longer offers adequate compensation for the geopolitical binary embedded in forward earnings.

## Company Overview

Marathon Petroleum operates three segments: Refining & Marketing (13 U.S. refineries, $6.1B 2025 adjusted EBITDA), Midstream via majority-owned MPLX LP ($6.8B 2025 adjusted EBITDA, fee-based), and Renewable Diesel (small, now marginally positive). The company's competitive advantages include the largest U.S. refining footprint with geographic diversification across Gulf Coast, Mid-Continent, and West Coast; heavy/sour crude processing capability that accesses discounted feedstocks; growing export infrastructure (401 mbpd in 2025); and MPLX ownership providing ~$2.6B in annual distributions that insulate against refining cycle troughs.

The capital return program has been transformative: $55.7B in total buybacks since 2012 have reduced shares from 512M (2022) to 295M (Q1 2026) — a 42% reduction in approximately three years. A new $5B authorization brings the total available to $8.6B.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement Trajectory:**
Revenue has declined from $177.5B (2022) to $132.7B (2025), entirely price-driven as throughput volumes actually increased. The critical metric is per-barrel margin: blended crack spread averaged $14.89/bbl in 2025, recovering from the 2024 trough but well below 2022-2023 supercycle levels. Q1 2026 R&M margin of $17.74/bbl represents meaningful expansion driven by geopolitical supply disruption.

**Earnings Power:**
- 2025 diluted EPS: $13.22
- 2026E consensus: $22.28 (implying $285 in annualized refining margins if sustained)
- Forward P/E on current 2026E: 11.7x
- Sensitivity: $1/bbl crack spread change = $1.125B annual R&M EBITDA impact = ~$2.60/share EPS

**Free Cash Flow:**
2025 FCF of $4.8B on $72.4B market cap = 6.6% FCF yield. Q1 2026 returned $1.0B to shareholders. At sustained 2026 earnings, FCF could reach $6-7B (8-10% yield), but this requires elevated crack spreads to persist.

**Balance Sheet:**
Total consolidated debt of $33.3B ($7.2B MPC, $26.1B MPLX non-recourse). Altman Z-Score of 2.14 sits in the grey zone. MPC standalone liquidity is comfortable at $6.6B ($5.0B revolver + $1.5B cash). Credit ratings are investment-grade (BBB/Baa2 stable). The MPLX debt is ring-fenced and does not represent a direct claim on MPC's refining cash flows.

**Q1 2026 Execution:**
The 119% beat was driven by: (1) elevated crack spreads from Hormuz disruption, (2) strategic turnaround acceleration leaving Q2-Q4 at full capacity, (3) lowest unplanned downtime "this decade," and (4) 10M barrels of advantaged SPR crude procurement.

The 98% 90-day estimate revision velocity is exceptional and would normally trigger a strong BUY signal. However, the growth driver (Iran conflict-elevated margins) is binary, not organic. If the peace deal framework holds (20-30% probability), estimates would need to be revised downward by 40-50%.

**Capital return amplifies earnings growth.** At current pace, MPC could retire another 10-15% of shares over 18-24 months, adding ~2-3 points of annual EPS growth through share count reduction alone.

**Renewable diesel turning positive:** Q1 2026 adj. EBITDA of +$38M after years of losses. The 45Z clean fuel production tax credit provides visibility for this segment's continued improvement.

## Valuation Assessment

At $260.51 with 2026E EPS of $22.28, MPC trades at 11.7x forward earnings — optically cheap relative to the S&P 500 but typical for a refiner in an elevated-margin environment. The peer group median P/E is 21.5x, but direct refining peers (VLO at 33.2x, PSX at 16.1x) provide better comparisons. VLO's higher multiple reflects its trough positioning while MPC has moved closer to peak.

The valuation challenge is that $22.28 in EPS requires crack spreads approximately $3-5/bbl above normalized levels throughout H2 2026. If spreads revert to 2024 levels ($14-15/bbl blended), earnings normalize to $12-14 range, implying 19-22x current price-to-normalized-earnings — a fair-to-full valuation for a cyclical refiner.

The stock trades at $260.51 against an analyst consensus mean target of $249.29 and a median of $243.00. The highest target is $331, the lowest is $186. Trading above consensus mean after an 82% rally from 52-week lows suggests the market has already priced in the near-term earnings uplift.

**Valuation under scenarios:**
- Bull case (peace deal fails, cracks sustain): $22-25 EPS × 12-13x = $264-325. At $260, limited remaining upside even in the bull scenario.
- Base case (partial normalization H2): $18-20 EPS × 12-13x = $216-260. Current price sits at the top of this range.
- Bear case (deal confirmed, rapid spread compression): $12-14 EPS × 13-14x = $156-196.

The risk/reward from $260 is asymmetric to the downside. Upside in the bull case is approximately 10-25%; downside in the bear case is 25-40%.

## Competitive Landscape

MPC's position as the largest U.S. refiner by capacity provides genuine competitive advantages in scale, feedstock flexibility, and export logistics. The company's ability to process discounted heavy/sour crudes (WCS discounts, inland Bakken discounts) and export growing volumes creates structural margin uplift versus smaller competitors.

MPLX constitutes a durable competitive moat. With 647M units worth approximately $34.6B (48% of MPC's market cap), the midstream segment generates predictable, growing distributions that fund buybacks and dividends regardless of refining conditions. The Permian-to-Gulf Coast natural gas/NGL buildout positions MPLX in the highest-growth corridor of U.S. energy infrastructure.

Relative to VLO (primarily refining) and PSX (chemicals and midstream), MPC's combination of the largest refining system plus a majority-owned midstream MLP with 12.5% growth targets creates a differentiated cash flow profile. The operational execution in Q1 2026 (turnaround acceleration, SPR procurement, record uptime) demonstrates management capability that smaller competitors cannot replicate at scale.

Global refining capacity rationalization continues — net closures are expected to outpace additions through decade-end, structurally supporting crack spreads above pre-2020 levels. U.S. refiners benefit disproportionately from lower natural gas costs and domestic crude access.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Geopolitical Binary (PRIMARY RISK — elevated):**
The US-Iran peace deal framework has a 20-30% probability of holding by May 8. If confirmed, oil falls to $85-95 and crack spreads compress rapidly. MPC's $1.125B/bbl sensitivity means a $3-5/bbl spread compression would remove $3.4-5.6B from annual R&M EBITDA, roughly $8-13 in EPS. This would invalidate the current estimate trajectory within weeks.

**2. California Regulatory Risk:**
SB X1-2 (maximum margin penalties deferred 5 years by CEC but not eliminated) and AB X2-1 (minimum inventory requirements) threaten West Coast margins, which are MPC's highest ($25.71/bbl Q1 2026). The 5-year deferral provides breathing room, but the regulatory direction is clear.

**3. Earnings Cyclicality at Peak-Adjacent Levels:**
Current crack spreads are elevated by geopolitical disruption. Even without a peace deal, normalization as the industry adjusts (exports reroute, inventories rebuild, new capacity comes online) will gradually compress margins. The direction is toward normalization; the uncertainty is timing.

**4. Long-term Demand Destruction:**
EV adoption, fuel efficiency mandates, and state-level regulations (ACC II) create secular headwinds to refined product demand growth. MPC's mitigation strategies (exports, NGL expansion through MPLX, renewable diesel) partially offset but don't eliminate this risk.

**5. Leverage Considerations:**
$33.3B consolidated debt creates refinancing risk in an elevated rate environment. MPLX issued 2025 notes at 5-6% vs. maturing obligations at 1.75-4.875%. While non-recourse to MPC, distribution stability requires MPLX maintaining investment-grade coverage ratios.

### Options Market Signal

IV at 44.1% vs. 30.8% historical volatility (13.3pp premium) confirms the market is pricing significant near-term uncertainty. The term structure is in backwardation (near-term 44.1% > 3-month 40.7%), indicating the market expects resolution of the binary event to either compress or expand vol sharply. The put/call skew is balanced (0.3%) and OI P/C ratio is 0.21, suggesting modest call-side positioning. Elevated uncertainty is priced in, and the near-term path matters more than the longer-term outlook, consistent with a binary event window.

**Base Case ($250-270):**
Deal collapses but geopolitical premium gradually fades over H2 as supply chains adapt. 2026 EPS comes in at $18-20 as crack spreads moderate from current peaks but remain above 2024. Stock consolidates near current levels with buyback providing support.

## Conclusion

Marathon Petroleum is a superbly managed, best-in-class refiner executing at the highest level during a period of geopolitically elevated margins. The Q1 2026 earnings beat, strategic turnaround positioning, and extraordinary estimate revisions all confirm the operational thesis. MPLX provides structural growth and income insulation that differentiates MPC from pure-play refining peers.

The issue is price, not quality. At $260.51, the stock has moved through consensus fair value and offers approximately 4-9% upside to base case against 25-29% downside if the geopolitical tailwind resolves. The 20-30% probability of a confirmed US-Iran deal — qualitatively different from 11 prior failed signals given Pakistani third-party verification, Chinese diplomatic pressure, and a one-page framework — creates a specific, near-term catalyst for margin compression that the current valuation does not adequately discount.

The recommendation transitions from BUY to HOLD. The earnings momentum is real and the company is executing well, but the risk/reward at current levels no longer compensates for the binary geopolitical exposure embedded in forward estimates. A pullback to $220-230 or confirmation that the peace deal has failed would re-establish favorable entry conditions.
