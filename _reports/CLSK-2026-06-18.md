**Research Date: June 18, 2026**

## Executive Summary

CleanSpark is a vertically integrated U.S. Bitcoin miner that, despite its "Internet Services & Infrastructure" sector label, functions as a leveraged Bitcoin holding company bolted onto a cash-consuming operating base. The FY2025 headline numbers look strong (revenue more than doubled to $766.3M, net income swung to $364.5M, diluted EPS reached $1.12), but the profit is a mark-to-market mirage. Roughly $425.6M of net income came from fair-value gains on Bitcoin under ASC 350-60, plus a $92.2M collateral gain, while operating cash flow was negative $461M against a thin $43M cash balance. The same accounting mechanism that produced a $364M gain in a Bitcoin-up year produces large losses when Bitcoin falls, and the FY2026 quarterly print confirms that reversal is underway: Q1 (Dec-25) EPS was -$1.34, Q2 (Mar-26) was -$1.52, both missing already-negative estimates by wide margins.

The investment case rests on three variables: Bitcoin's price direction, execution of an as-yet-uncontracted AI/HPC pivot, and tolerance for heavy ongoing dilution alongside a ~$185M unreserved CBP tariff tail. None of these is resolved. The bear case is well-evidenced: structural cash burn, ~$1.8B of convertibles, single-pool/single-vendor/single-custodian concentration, and an AI narrative that remains contract-poor. But the situation is genuinely two-sided. Short interest sits at 45.7% of float, the entire covering sell-side panel (12 buy/strong-buy, zero hold or sell) is constructive, the consensus low price target ($16) sits essentially at the $16.78 spot, and consensus models a steep loss-narrowing trajectory from current-year -$3.27 to next-year -$0.72 EPS. A single positive catalyst, a signed hyperscaler MOU or a Bitcoin rally, could trigger a violent re-rate against a heavily-shorted float.

We rate CLSK a HOLD with an "uncertain" rationale, reflecting genuine two-sided risk rather than a confident view in either direction. The fundamental fragility argues against owning the equity, but the crowded short positioning, unanimous constructive panel, and at-spot consensus floor argue equally strongly against a directional bearish call. This is a no-position situation pending resolution of the binary AI-contract and Bitcoin-direction catalysts.

## Company Overview

CleanSpark owns and operates 33 Bitcoin mining data centers across Georgia (15), Tennessee (11), Mississippi (5), and Wyoming (2). It is a single-segment business, with 100% of revenue from Bitcoin mining, having exited third-party co-location hosting in January 2025 and divested its legacy energy/microgrid operations (now discontinued operations). The company contributes hashrate to the Foundry Digital mining pool under a Full-Pay-Per-Share arrangement, earning Bitcoin daily. Its differentiation versus asset-light peers is self-built infrastructure on owned/leased land with directly procured, low-cost power, concentrated in the Southeast (TVA territory) and Wyoming.

The most consequential recent development is the strategic pivot. In October 2025 CleanSpark acquired land and long-term power in Austin County, Texas (~$66M) to build a next-generation data-center campus for HPC/AI workloads. By May 2026, management was describing an ambition to become a "multi-gigawatt AI and digital infrastructure platform," and Needham reported the company was in "advanced discussions" with a direct hyperscaler investment. This remains an aspiration. There is no signed hyperscaler agreement and zero AI/HPC revenue as of the latest filings. The Texas campus is, in effect, a Bitcoin-backed call option on a still-unproven business model.

A separate but related transformation is CleanSpark's emergence as a Bitcoin treasury company. In March 2025 it changed treasury policy to hold Bitcoin long-term, bought $160M of BTC outright, and runs a "Digital Asset Management" derivatives book (covered calls generating $134M of proceeds). By May 2026 it held ~13,470 BTC, up from 10,428 at fiscal year-end. The model is increasingly MicroStrategy-like: using convertible notes to fund both infrastructure and Bitcoin accumulation.

## Financial Analysis

The audited FY2025 statements (fiscal year ended September 30, 2025) require careful interpretation:

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 | FY2023 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue (all BTC mining) | $766.3M | $379.0M | $168.4M |
| Cost of revenue (ex-D&A) | $343.1M | $165.5M | $93.6M |
| D&A | $348.3M | $154.6M | $120.7M |
| Gain on FV of Bitcoin, net | ($425.6M) | ($113.4M) | — |
| Income (loss) from operations | $318.9M | ($149.0M) | ($131.0M) |
| Net income (loss) | $364.5M | ($145.8M) | ($138.1M) |
| Diluted EPS | $1.12 | ($0.69) | ($1.30) |
| Operating cash flow | ($461.0M) | ($233.7M) | ($17.2M) |

The central fact: GAAP profitability is a direct function of Bitcoin's price during the fiscal year. Mining revenue is received in Bitcoin (non-cash), the company reinvests heavily in rapidly depreciating miners, and fair-value gains on the BTC stack flow straight to net income. Operating cash flow has been negative and worsening for three consecutive years (-$17.2M → -$233.7M → -$461.0M). The chain runs: BTC-denominated revenue → no cash conversion → heavy capex on miners depreciated over a shortened 3-year life → continuous reliance on capital markets to fund the gap.

The balance sheet (9/30/25) confirms the "leveraged Bitcoin proxy" framing. Bitcoin holdings of 10,428 BTC at $1.19B fair value (cost basis ~$105K/BTC) dominate the $3.18B asset base. Property and equipment net of depreciation is $1.36B. Cash is only $43M, extraordinarily thin for a company of this scale, reflecting that wealth is stored in Bitcoin, not currency. Total liabilities are $1.0B, equity $2.18B. Goodwill of $131.7M reflects the GRIID acquisition.

The capital structure is the most concerning element. Shares outstanding grew from ~160M (FY2023) to ~284M (FY2025) through massive ATM equity issuance ($1.22B raised in FY2024 alone, $187M in FY2025). On top of that sit two zero-coupon convertible structures: $650M due 2030 (conversion ~$14.80, capped call cap $24.66) issued December 2024, and a subsequent-event $1.15B due 2032 (conversion ~$19.16) issued November 13, 2025, roughly $1.8B of convertibles combined. The 0% coupons limit cash interest, and ~$460M of the 2032 proceeds funded share buybacks from note holders, but these create large potential future dilution above the conversion prices. The November notes were used to fully repay the Coinbase ($174.5M drawn) and Two Prime credit lines, leaving ~$400M undrawn capacity.

The FY2026 quarterly trajectory has turned sharply negative as Bitcoin softened: Q4 FY25 (Sep-25) -$0.01 versus a +$0.33 estimate, Q1 FY26 (Dec-25) -$1.34 versus -$0.23, Q2 FY26 (Mar-26) -$1.52 versus -$0.58. The one beat in the trailing four quarters (Jun-25, +$0.77 versus +$0.28) was itself a Bitcoin-up-quarter artifact. The earnings model has essentially no visibility because it is a Bitcoin price bet.

## Growth Analysis

On reported revenue, growth has been steep ($168.4M → $379.0M → $766.3M over three years), but this is the product of fleet expansion (funded by dilution) layered on Bitcoin price. The April 2024 halving cut block rewards 50%, compressing mining economics industry-wide, which is precisely why CleanSpark and its peers are pivoting toward AI/HPC monetization of their power and land.

The forward growth thesis is bifurcated. The mining business faces structural headwinds (post-halving reward compression, rising network difficulty, energy cost inflation), and consensus has been marking down estimates aggressively: current-quarter EPS revised -41.7% in 30 days and -200% in 90 days, current-year -30% / -48.7%. The revision velocity is firmly negative.

The audit correctly flags that the bear "estimates only falling" framing is incomplete. Next-year consensus EPS is -$0.72 versus current-year -$3.27, implying the panel models a steep loss-narrowing recovery. The credible drivers of that recovery would be: (a) a Bitcoin price stabilization or rally reversing the fair-value losses, (b) the AI/HPC campus generating contracted, cash-yielding revenue with a hyperscaler anchor tenant, and (c) the absence of the one-time stock-comp and severance charges that burdened FY2025-26. None of these is contracted or confirmed. The recovery is plausible but rests on a Bitcoin price assumption plus an AI deal that does not yet exist. We treat the implied recovery as a genuine reason not to be short, but insufficient to underwrite a bull case: it is a model output contingent on unresolved binaries, not a visible operating trajectory.

The AI pivot itself is narrative-rich and contract-poor. CleanSpark is a follower, not a leader, in the miner-to-AI transition; Core Scientific and Iren have signed hyperscaler/AI deals while CleanSpark has land, power, and "advanced discussions." Its distributed Southeast footprint, optimized for mining, is poorly suited to the large, contiguous, high-reliability campuses hyperscalers require, which is why the single large Texas campus matters and why an anchor tenant is the entire thesis. The 18-month, capital-intensive buildout requires power, immersion cooling (Submer partnership), and a tenant the company does not yet have.

## Valuation Assessment

Standard multiples are not meaningful here. Trailing and forward P/E are both negative (-8.7x on the data feed), reflecting losses on the FY2026 run-rate. The sector-peer comparison is structurally misleading because CleanSpark's "Information Technology" classification groups it with semiconductor and software names (POWI at 261x, BDC 18.9x, DBX 14.8x, OLED 20x, EPAM 13.3x; peer median P/E 18.9x) that bear no resemblance to a leveraged Bitcoin miner.

The only coherent valuation anchor is sum-of-parts. At 13,470 BTC (May 2026) and Bitcoin near recent levels, the BTC stack is worth roughly $1.2-1.4B, against a ~$4.4B market cap and ~$1.8B of convertibles. The residual implied value of the mining business plus the AI option is therefore on the order of $3B+, a substantial premium that bakes in a successful AI pivot that has not been contracted. On a strict price-versus-current-fundamentals basis, the equity is not cheap; the BTC-adjusted enterprise value embeds significant AI optionality.

We score valuation 3.5. The low score reflects that the multiple compression here is not a value opportunity: it reflects a business whose cash economics are structurally negative and whose equity value depends on a Bitcoin price bet plus an uncontracted AI deal. This is consistent with the broader pattern that compressed or distressed multiples without an independent, identifiable catalyst are value-trap warnings, not buy signals. We deliberately resist treating the recent rally off the $8.00 low as evidence of either over- or under-valuation; prior price action is not a valuation input. The relevant fact is that current price embeds optimism about a deal that does not yet exist.

The analyst panel's price targets (low $16.00, mean $20.6, median $21.25, high $26.00) deserve specific engagement given the audit. For an earnings-inflection name with cheap-relative-to-growth metrics, consensus targets function as floors. That guardrail does not apply here: CLSK is not an earnings-inflection stock at a reasonable multiple, but a loss-making, Bitcoin-levered name whose forward EPS is negative. The targets reflect the same Bitcoin-up plus AI-optionality narrative embedded in the stock price; they are not independent confirmation. That said, the consensus floor sitting essentially at spot, with a unanimous 12-0 constructive panel, is a meaningful signal that the view "this is going materially lower" is contested by the entire covering sell-side. We weight this as a reason not to be directionally bearish, even as we decline to treat the targets as a reason to be bullish.

## Competitive Landscape

CleanSpark's relative edge in mining is genuine: owned, low-cost power and self-built infrastructure, versus asset-light hosting peers. Within the public Bitcoin-mining group (Riot, MARA, Core Scientific, Cipher, Iren, Canaan), this is a structural cost advantage in the post-halving environment where energy economics determine survival.

The competitive trajectory in the AI/HPC transition is less favorable. CleanSpark is a follower. Core Scientific and Iren have already converted power and land into signed hyperscaler/AI agreements; CleanSpark has the footprint but no anchor tenant. Its distributed, mining-optimized site portfolio is structurally disadvantaged for AI workloads relative to contiguous campus operators, which is the strategic rationale for the single large Texas build. The competitive position is improving at the margin (the Texas land/power acquisition and the stated multi-gigawatt ambition), consistent with the prior report's +0.5 uptick on this dimension, but the improvement is potential, not realized. We hold competitive position at 4.5: a credible low-cost mining position offset by laggard status in the transition that matters most for the equity's re-rating.

## Risk Assessment

The idiosyncratic risk profile is heavy and largely binary:

1. **Bitcoin price is the entire P&L.** Under fair-value accounting, BTC swings flow straight to net income. The FY2025 profit was a BTC-up artifact; the FY2026 losses are the same mechanism in reverse. This is a high-beta, leveraged Bitcoin vehicle despite its IT label.

2. **CBP tariff contingency up to ~$185M, unreserved.** CBP is asserting Chinese-origin import tariffs on miners imported April–June 2024, tied directly to CleanSpark's 96% reliance on Bitmain. The company recorded no reserve (deemed "not probable"). A loss would equal more than 4x current cash. This is a binary, off-balance-sheet exposure a standard screen misses entirely.

3. **Indirect tax (sales/use) contingency: $64.5M accrued** at 9/30/25, up from $2.8M, with $11.1M expensed in FY2025, a growing, real liability.

4. **Extreme concentration:** 100% of revenue via a single mining pool (Foundry), 96% of miner purchases from a single Chinese vendor (Bitmain), and all Bitcoin held with one custodian.

5. **Liquidity dependence:** the company is structurally dependent on continuous capital markets access and on monetizing its BTC stack. Any closure of capital markets during a crypto downturn is dangerous.

6. **Dilution overhang:** ~$1.8B of converts plus ongoing equity grants; if Bitcoin and the stock rise above conversion prices ($14.80 and $19.16), share count expands materially, capping per-share BTC upside even in bullish scenarios.

7. **Litigation:** A securities class action (*Hasthantra v. CleanSpark*) was class-certified September 24, 2025, alleging 2020–21 misstatements; consolidated Nevada derivative actions are pending. No loss reserve recorded.

8. **AI execution risk:** No signed contract, no AI revenue, no HPC operating track record. The 18-month buildout requires an anchor tenant the company does not have.

Several of these (Bitcoin volatility, dilution, the CBP tail) are not fully priced and could move the stock against current holders. The risk score of 3.0 reflects the magnitude of binary, asymmetric downside that the equity does not fully discount.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing imminent stress. Near-term ATM IV is 105.8% versus 87.7% historical volatility (rich, +18.1pp spread), with the term structure in backwardation (105.8% near-term versus 73.2% three-month), indicating the market expects a near-term catalyst. The skew is call-heavy (-8.2% one-week, put/call OI ratio just 0.30), not put-heavy. For a name with 45.7% short interest, this matters: positioning is not bearishly tilted in the options market despite the weak fundamentals. The combination of very high short interest and call-heavy options skew is the classic squeeze-vulnerable configuration. This directly undermines any case for shorting the stock and reinforces the two-sided HOLD framing.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case.** Bitcoin stabilizes or rallies, reversing the fair-value losses and restoring GAAP profitability. CleanSpark signs a hyperscaler anchor for the Texas campus, validating the "multi-gigawatt AI platform" ambition and triggering a re-rate of the AI optionality currently embedded but unproven. The short float fuels a squeeze on any positive catalyst, with the call-heavy options skew confirming positioning is set for upside. The unanimous sell-side panel revises targets toward the $26 high. In this scenario the stock could re-rate toward $24-26, the capped-call cap on the 2030 notes.

**Bear Case.** Bitcoin weakens further, driving continued large GAAP losses and forcing the cash-burning operation back to dilutive capital markets in deteriorating conditions. The CBP tariff resolves against the company, the AI pivot stalls without a signed tenant, and the AI optionality premium embedded in the market cap compresses toward the BTC-stack-plus-mining value. In this scenario the stock retraces toward the $8-10 range last seen in early 2026, a 40-50% decline.

The base case is that neither resolves cleanly in the near term: Bitcoin chops, the AI discussions remain "advanced" without a signature, dilution continues, and the stock trades in a wide $12-20 band driven by Bitcoin price and headline flow. We cannot assign confident probability weighting to the binary catalysts, which is the core reason this is an uncertain hold rather than a directional call.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

This is a HOLD with an "uncertain" rationale: neither a growth nor income thesis, but an acknowledgment of irreducible two-sided risk. We are not recommending a position.

**Upgrade triggers (toward BUY):**
- A signed, definitive hyperscaler/AI anchor agreement for the Texas campus with disclosed contract economics (revenue, term, ramp). This converts the AI option from narrative to cash flow.
- Demonstrated path to positive operating cash flow, or at minimum a materially narrowing burn that reduces capital-markets dependence.
- Favorable resolution or quantified de-risking of the CBP tariff contingency.

**Downgrade triggers (toward AVOID/short consideration):**
- A sustained Bitcoin breakdown that drives further large GAAP losses and forces emergency dilutive issuance.
- Evidence the AI discussions have collapsed (e.g., management walks back the "multi-gigawatt" language, or the Texas buildout is paused).
- An adverse CBP ruling or class-action development materially above current expectations, though even a confirmed bear thesis would not be shortable at 45.7% SI without a squeeze first clearing.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next fiscal Q3 earnings (early August 2026) and any 8-K disclosing a Texas anchor tenant. Given the options-implied near-term stress and backwardation, the next catalyst window is weeks, not quarters.

## Conclusion

CleanSpark is a genuinely fragile business carrying a misleading IT-sector label: a leveraged Bitcoin vehicle whose FY2025 GAAP profit is an accounting mirage against deeply negative operating cash flow and minimal cash, with heavy convertible debt, an unreserved CBP tariff tail, extreme counterparty concentration, and an AI pivot that remains contract-poor. On fundamentals alone, this is not a stock we would own.

The reason we land on HOLD rather than AVOID is that the bear thesis, however well-evidenced, is already crowded into a 45.7%-short float and contradicted by the entire covering sell-side panel, with the consensus low target sitting at spot and a modeled loss-narrowing trajectory to next-year -$0.72 EPS. The options market confirms the asymmetry: call-heavy skew on a heavily-shorted name is squeeze-vulnerable, not crash-positioned. An AVOID that cannot be expressed as a short (because squeeze risk is acute and the downside is already in the float) and that sits against a unanimous constructive panel and an at-spot consensus floor is functionally a no-position. We do not own it; we would not short it; we wait for the contract or the breakdown.
