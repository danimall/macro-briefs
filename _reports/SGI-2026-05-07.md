## Executive Summary

Somnigroup International is the world's largest bedding company following its February 2025 acquisition of Mattress Firm for $5.1B, which created a vertically integrated entity spanning manufacturing through retail with 2,100+ U.S. stores. The company now generates $7.5B in annual revenue and $1.35B in trailing adjusted EBITDA, with management guiding to $3.00-$3.40 in adjusted EPS for FY2026. The stock has declined approximately 20% from its 52-week high of $98.56 and currently trades at approximately 22-24x FY2026 guided earnings.

The investment case hinges on whether Somnigroup can simultaneously execute on Mattress Firm integration (synergies still ramping), the proposed all-stock acquisition of Leggett & Platt (~$2.5B, announced April 2026), and organic growth recovery in a challenged bedding market — all while deleveraging from 3.07x net debt/EBITDA. The analyst consensus is overwhelmingly bullish (8 Buy/1 Hold) with a $101.50 mean price target, implying approximately 43% upside from the current $71.25 price. However, negative estimate revision momentum (current year EPS estimates down 3.2% in 90 days), Mattress Firm margin deterioration (operating margin fell from 7.2% to 4.9% YoY in Q1 2026), and consumer macro headwinds create meaningful near-term uncertainty that warrants caution at current multiples.

## Company Overview

Somnigroup International (formerly Tempur Sealy International) operates through three segments: Tempur Sealy North America (manufacturing/wholesale of Tempur-Pedic, Sealy, and Stearns & Foster brands), Tempur Sealy International (Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America including UK Dreams retail), and Mattress Firm (2,100+ specialty mattress retail stores in the U.S.). The company sells in 100+ countries, employs 19,000+ workers, and now derives over 60% of global sales through direct-to-consumer channels.

The April 2026 announcement of an all-stock acquisition of Leggett & Platt (LEG) — a key component supplier of innersprings and adjustable bases — would create a $11.2B revenue entity controlling the full bedding value chain from raw components through retail. If completed, the combined entity would have approximately $1.7B in adjusted EBITDA and $1.1B in operating cash flow.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:** FY2025 revenue of $7.48B represented +51.6% growth versus 2024, driven almost entirely by the Mattress Firm acquisition contribution ($3.51B stub-period revenue). Q1 2026 revenue of $1.80B grew 12.3% YoY, reflecting the first full quarter with Mattress Firm. International organic growth was strong at 11.2% reported (+8.3% constant currency).

**Profitability:** The Mattress Firm acquisition structurally lowered margins. FY2025 adjusted operating margin was 13.6% (down from ~18% pre-acquisition), reflecting the retail segment's lower-margin profile (5.4-7.6% range). More concerning, Mattress Firm's adjusted operating margin deteriorated from 7.2% in Q1 2025 to 4.9% in Q1 2026, a 230bp decline attributed to promotional pressure, product mix, and fixed cost deleverage. The core Tempur Sealy North America segment maintains exceptional 24.3% adjusted operating margins.

**Cash Flow:** FY2025 operating cash flow of $800.1M and free cash flow of $633.2M (8.5% FCF margin on revenue) demonstrate strong conversion. Q1 2026 set a record at $246M in operating cash flow. At current market cap of ~$15B, the trailing FCF yield is approximately 4.2%.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of $4.58B at Q1 2026 with net leverage at 3.07x adjusted EBITDA. The company's Altman Z-Score of 1.43 places it technically in the "distress zone," though this is mechanistically driven by high leverage and structural negative working capital (retail lease obligations), not operational liquidity pressure. Available liquidity exceeds $773M, and the company completed $250M in debt prepayments during late 2025. Interest expense of $268M in 2025 consumes approximately 20% of adjusted EBITDA — manageable but limiting.

**Dilution:** Shares outstanding expanded 18% (from 178M to 209M diluted) on the Mattress Firm stock consideration. The Leggett & Platt deal would add approximately 9% additional dilution. Total dilution of ~28-30% since pre-deal levels means organic EPS growth must offset substantial share count expansion.

## Growth Analysis

**Guided Growth:** Management's FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $3.00-$3.40 implies approximately 11-26% growth versus FY2025 adjusted EPS of $2.70. At midpoint ($3.20), this represents 19% growth driven by: Mattress Firm full-year contribution and synergy capture, continued international growth, and lower interest expense from deleveraging.

**Synergy Execution:** Mattress Firm synergies are expected to ramp over 3 years toward $100M+ in annual run-rate savings. The Leggett & Platt deal targets $50M in synergies over 3 years ($10M in year 1). These are modest relative to the combined revenue base but additive to margins over time.

**Industry Cycle:** The bedding industry has been in cyclical downturn since 2022-2023. Mattress Firm same-store sales were flat in Q1 2026 but outperformed the broader market. Recovery in housing activity, consumer confidence, or a moderation in mortgage rates would provide meaningful operating leverage given the fixed-cost retail network.

**Headwinds to Growth:** Estimate revisions are negative across all periods. Current quarter EPS has been revised down 6.1% over 90 days; full-year 2026 down 3.2%. This negative momentum contradicts the bullish analyst stance and suggests the market is pricing in near-term challenges that could persist given the consumer macro environment.

## Valuation Assessment

At $71.25 (reported current price per Phase 1 data), SGI trades at approximately:
- 22-24x FY2026 guided midpoint EPS of $3.20
- ~11x trailing adjusted EBITDA of $1.35B (enterprise value ~$19.5B including debt)
- ~4.2% trailing FCF yield

Versus peer medians of 15.6x P/E, SGI commands a significant premium. This premium is partially justified by superior brand strength, scale advantages, and the only fully vertically integrated model in the industry. However, 22-24x forward earnings for a consumer discretionary company carrying 3x leverage in a challenged consumer environment and with falling estimate revisions leaves limited margin of safety.

The analyst consensus target of $101.50 (median $104.50) implies approximately 42-47% upside from $71.25, but these targets predate the recent sell-off and negative estimate revision trend. If FY2026 EPS comes in at the low end of guidance ($3.00), the stock at $71.25 trades at ~24x — expensive for home furnishings regardless of quality.

The Leggett & Platt acquisition, if completed, would be EPS accretive on a forward basis but adds integration complexity and regulatory risk. The synergies ($50M over 3 years) are modest relative to the $2.5B deal value.

## Competitive Landscape

Somnigroup's competitive position is strong and improving. Post-Mattress Firm and potentially post-Leggett & Platt, the company would control the entire U.S. bedding value chain: innerspring/foam components → mattress manufacturing → specialty retail. No competitor has this breadth.

**Advantages:**
- Proprietary Tempur material (trade secret, not patent-dependent)
- Brand portfolio spanning all price points (Tempur-Pedic premium, Sealy mid-market, Sleepy's value)
- 2,100+ retail locations providing direct consumer access and data
- Manufacturing largely domestic (reducing tariff exposure)
- Scale economics in procurement, logistics, and advertising

**Constraints:**
- The FTC-mandated requirement to maintain 43% of horizontal premium ($1,500+) floor slots for third-party mattresses at Mattress Firm limits vertical integration synergies
- The "bed-in-a-box" channel has matured but still exerts pricing pressure at entry-level
- Furniture/home goods peers like Purple, Sleep Number, and Casper continue to compete for consumer attention
- Potential regulatory scrutiny of the Leggett & Platt deal given existing market dominance

Somnigroup is consolidating a fragmented industry and gaining structural advantages. Whether the current valuation already reflects this positioning is the central question for new capital.

## Risk Assessment

**Integration Execution (HIGH):** Pursuing a $2.5B acquisition of Leggett & Platt barely 14 months after closing the Mattress Firm deal creates substantial execution risk. Management bandwidth, systems integration, culture alignment, and regulatory navigation across two simultaneous integrations is exceptionally demanding. The Q1 2026 margin deterioration at Mattress Firm may indicate early integration friction.

**Leverage Constraint (MODERATE-HIGH):** At 3.07x net leverage, the company has limited flexibility for operational stumbles. Share buybacks remain suspended until leverage falls below 3.50-3.75x. Any downside earnings surprise would increase the leverage ratio and potentially trigger credit concern.

**Consumer Macro (HIGH in current environment):** Bedding is a durable consumer purchase highly correlated with housing activity and consumer confidence. The demand environment remains challenged, and high interest rates continue to pressure housing — a key bedding demand driver.

**Regulatory Risk (MODERATE):** The Leggett & Platt deal faces likely antitrust scrutiny. SGI already dominates U.S. mattress manufacturing; adding its key component supplier could raise vertical foreclosure concerns. Failure to close would remove integration complexity but leave LEG shares (exchangeable for SGI stock) in an uncertain state.

**Tariff Exposure (LOW-MODERATE):** Management claims minimal direct tariff exposure due to domestic manufacturing. However, raw materials (steel for innersprings, petroleum-based foams) face indirect cost inflation from tariffs. Pricing actions have been implemented but duration/extent remain uncertain.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($95-105, 21-34% upside from recent close of ~$78.62):**
- Mattress Firm margins stabilize and improve toward 7%+ as integration synergies materialize in H2 2026
- Bedding industry reaches cyclical trough with early signs of recovery in housing/consumer metrics
- Leggett & Platt deal closes smoothly, providing EPS accretion and supply chain control
- Leverage drops below 3.0x by year-end, enabling share buyback resumption
- FY2026 EPS meets or exceeds guidance midpoint of $3.20

**Bear Case ($55-62, 21-30% downside):**
- Consumer recession deepens, pressuring Mattress Firm same-store sales into negative territory
- Integration costs exceed expectations; Mattress Firm margins stay below 5%
- Leggett & Platt deal faces regulatory challenge or LEG shareholders reject
- Leverage remains elevated, limiting capital return and creating credit anxiety
- FY2026 EPS comes in at or below low end of guidance ($3.00 or lower)
- Multiple compresses toward peer median (15-16x) on declining estimates

2. Net leverage fails to decline below 3.0x by Q3 2026
3. Leggett & Platt deal faces formal FTC challenge AND consumer metrics deteriorate further

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (likely early August 2026 for Q2 results). Key data points: Mattress Firm margin trajectory, same-store sales, deleveraging progress, and any Leggett & Platt regulatory updates.

## Conclusion

Somnigroup International is a well-positioned company executing an ambitious vertical integration strategy in a cyclically depressed industry. The dominant competitive position, strong cash flow generation ($633M+ FCF), and capable management team provide long-term appeal. However, the current risk-reward at 22-24x forward earnings is unfavorable given: (1) negative estimate revision momentum, (2) consumer macro headwinds with no clear near-term catalyst for improvement, (3) simultaneous dual-integration execution risk, (4) elevated leverage constraining capital return flexibility, and (5) Mattress Firm margin deterioration suggesting promotional pressure is intensifying.

The stock's decline from its 52-week high reflects legitimate fundamental concerns rather than an irrational selloff. At $71.25, the valuation premium versus peers (15.6x median P/E) requires continued execution on synergies and an industry recovery that may not materialize in H1 2026. The analyst consensus is strongly bullish (mean target $101.50), which provides a reference point for upside if execution improves, but the negative estimate trend suggests those targets may need revision.

HOLD with a bias toward upgrading to BUY on either: (a) a pullback to $60-65 that compresses valuation to ~20x guided EPS, or (b) positive evidence of Mattress Firm margin stabilization and industry recovery in Q2/Q3 2026 results.
