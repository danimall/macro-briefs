## Executive Summary

AppLovin has completed its transformation into a pure-play AI advertising platform following the June 2025 divestiture of its gaming apps business. The company generated $1.84B in Q1 2026 revenue (+59% YoY) at approximately 85% adjusted EBITDA margin. With $1.29B in quarterly FCF, minimal capex ($473K in FY2025), and 898 employees generating $6.1M in revenue per headcount, this is among the most capital-efficient businesses in technology.

The investment case centers on the ongoing expansion of Axon into web-based e-commerce advertising — an initiative launched in 2024 with the self-serve Axon Ads Manager platform publicly launched in 2025. This expansion either validates a 10x TAM expansion or proves the AI engine's effectiveness is confined to mobile gaming. At ~30x forward P/E on FY2026E consensus EPS of $15.67, with 8.4% upward revisions over 90 days for FY2027, the valuation requires execution on the e-commerce vertical to sustain. The near-term risk constellation — an SEC data-collection probe (reported by Bloomberg as active and ongoing), short-seller allegations regarding fingerprinting, concentrated insider selling, and a CTO transition — creates genuine binary risk that demands conviction sizing below typical high-growth allocations.

The recommendation is BUY with 2% position sizing, acknowledging that the risk-adjusted return profile is attractive given the probability-weighted scenarios but that idiosyncratic tail risks are elevated relative to typical Information Technology investments.

## Company Overview

AppLovin operates an AI-powered advertising technology platform through its core product, Axon Ads Manager, which processes real-time auctions at microsecond speeds to match advertiser demand with publisher supply. The platform serves mobile app developers, e-commerce businesses, and connected TV advertisers. The company's suite includes MAX (in-app bidding for publishers), Adjust (measurement analytics), and Wurl (CTV distribution).

Following the June 2025 sale of its first-party gaming studios to Tripledot for total consideration of approximately $715.6M (consisting of $430.6M in cash and Tripledot shares valued at $285.0M, representing approximately 20% of fully diluted equity), AppLovin operates as a single-segment pure-play platform. This eliminated a structural conflict of interest where the company was simultaneously competing with and serving its advertiser customers.

The company employs 898 people, with 42% in R&D. Market cap stands at approximately $148B on ~338.7M diluted weighted-average shares (comprising approximately 306M Class A shares and 30M Class B shares outstanding as of March 31, 2026).

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat Widening Indicators:**
- Revenue per installation grew 72% in FY2025 without volume growth — the AI engine is becoming more effective
- Operating margins expanded from 42% (FY2023) to 76% (FY2025) — reflecting a combination of pricing power and cost structure optimization, including a 40% reduction in R&D expense and 19% reduction in sales & marketing expense (largely driven by lower stock-based-compensation-related costs and headcount changes)
- The Apps divestiture removed the conflict-of-interest perception, potentially unlocking advertiser categories that previously avoided the platform

**Competitive Threats:**
- Meta and Google control the platforms AppLovin operates on AND compete in advertising
- Unity Software (direct mobile gaming ad tech competitor) has been losing share but remains present
- Amazon's advertising business is growing rapidly in e-commerce — the exact space AppLovin is entering

**Network Effect Evidence:** The revenue-per-installation trajectory is consistent with a network-effect dynamic: more advertiser spend → more auction data → better AI optimization → higher ROAS → more advertiser spend. While the 72% increase in net revenue per installation alongside only 3% volume growth in FY2025 strongly suggests improving platform effectiveness, it remains an analytical inference rather than a proven structural moat.

**Vulnerability:** The business operates entirely within Apple's and Google's ecosystems. The 2021 incident where Apple temporarily blocked Adjust SDK updates demonstrates this risk is concrete. If either platform restricted AppLovin's data access (the core of short-seller allegations), the business model faces existential risk.

## Risk Assessment

**1. SEC Data-Collection Probe / Short-Seller Allegations (HIGH):**
Multiple short-sellers (Muddy Waters Research, Culper Research, Fuzzy Panda) allege AppLovin engages in "fingerprinting" (tracking users without consent), "clickjacking," backdoor installations, and attribution fraud. Bloomberg reported in October 2025 that the SEC had been probing AppLovin's data-collection practices and alleged platform-agreement violations, and subsequently reported in February 2026 that the SEC described the investigation as "still active and ongoing." Reuters reported on the Bloomberg update but could not independently verify it; neither AppLovin nor any of its officials have been charged with wrongdoing. Bloomberg also reported in October 2025 that AppLovin shut down its "Array" product, which had been linked to unwanted app-download allegations; the company stated Array was a test product. The CLO is retiring in August 2026, and her replacement specializes in privacy/litigation — circumstantial evidence the company is preparing for regulatory engagement. If Apple or Google acted to restrict data access, the impact would be severe and rapid.

**2. Platform Dependency (HIGH):**
Substantially all revenue flows through Apple App Store and Google Play Store ecosystems. These platforms can change policies unilaterally with minimal notice. Apple's ATT framework already demonstrated willingness to disrupt mobile advertising; further restrictions remain possible.

**3. Insider Selling (MODERATE):**
CEO Foroughi sold ~$30M in March 2026. CTO Shikin (transitioning out of CTO role) sold ~$60M+. Eduardo Vivas sold ~$70M. No open-market purchases observed. While these occurred at $450-515 (near current levels), the volume is notable and concentrated in a single month.

**4. CTO Transition (MODERATE):**
Basil Shikin stepping down as CTO effective July 1, 2026. Giovanni Ge (internal promotion) takes over. Execution continuity during an active product expansion phase creates operational risk.

**5. Growth Deceleration (MODERATE):**
Revenue growth: 75% → 70% → 59%. Each quarter of deceleration at ~30x forward P/E creates multiple compression pressure. The stock needs to sustain 30%+ growth to justify current multiples.

**6. E-Commerce Execution (MODERATE):**
The $24.5M increase in bad debt expense in FY2025 "primarily related to new initiatives" suggests early e-commerce advertisers may have credit quality issues. The company's 10-Q notes there is "no assurance" broader e-commerce adoption will materialize. AppLovin expects to continue investing in sales and marketing to increase Axon brand awareness and drive new client acquisition, indicating the self-serve model complements rather than fully replaces direct sales efforts. Proving Axon works outside mobile gaming remains unproven at scale.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($650-750, +39% to +60%):**
E-commerce expansion shows meaningful traction with measurable ROAS metrics through 2026. Advertisers adopt the self-serve platform, driving revenue acceleration in H2 2026. FY2027 EPS consensus revises to $23-25+. SEC probe concerns prove manageable (no platform restrictions enacted). At 30x $23 FY2027E = $690.

**Bear Case ($300-350, -36% to -25%):**
SEC probe or platform-level response results in Apple or Google restricting data access, or the short-seller allegations prove partially valid, triggering advertiser pullback. E-commerce expansion underperforms, revealing the AI engine's effectiveness is gaming-specific. Multiple compresses to 20x on decelerating growth. At 20x $15.67 FY2026E = $313.

**Base Case ($580-620, +24% to +32%):**
E-commerce expansion shows adequate early traction without breakthrough metrics. Growth decelerates modestly to 40-50% in H2 2026. SEC probe continues without resolution but no platform restrictions enacted. The stock re-rates toward consensus target as the multiple holds at 28-30x forward on sustained growth. At 28x $20.76 FY2027E = $581.

## Conclusion

AppLovin presents an attractive opportunity in a capital-light, AI-driven advertising platform with approximately 85% EBITDA margins growing revenue 59% YoY. The PEG of ~0.9x against peers at 1.4-2.9x represents a concrete valuation discount that the market assigns due to legitimate binary risks (SEC probe, platform dependency) rather than fundamental weakness.

The BUY recommendation reflects probability-weighted expected value: the bull case (+49%) with reasonable probability (30-35%) plus the base case (+28%) with moderate probability (40-45%) outweigh the bear case (-32%) at lower but non-trivial probability (20-25%). The SEC probe risk is real and unquantifiable — it cannot be modeled, only sized for through position discipline.

Recommended position: 2% of portfolio, reflecting conviction in the business model offset by the unresolvable binary nature of the platform dependency risk.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The central catalyst was corrected from a discrete "June 2026 launch" to an ongoing e-commerce expansion (self-serve platform launched in 2025, e-commerce efforts since 2024); EV/Revenue was corrected from 14.5x to ~20x; trailing P/E was corrected from 46.7x to ~40.8x; and the base-case upside/downside ratio of 0.88:1 does not demonstrate clear asymmetry, which weakens the "asymmetric opportunity" framing of the BUY recommendation.
