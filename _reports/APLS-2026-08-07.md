**Research Date: August 7, 2026**

## Executive Summary

APLS is no longer an investable security. Biogen completed its acquisition of Apellis Pharmaceuticals on May 14, 2026, following a tender offer and second-step merger at **$41.00 per share in cash** (approximately $5.6bn of upfront equity consideration) plus one **non-transferable contingent value right** per share worth up to **$4.00**. The company's EDGAR 8-K trail terminates on the closing date, the last periodic filing is a Part III-only 10-K/A dated April 28, 2026, and no 10-Q has been filed for any period after FY2025 — the standard signature of deregistration. The authoritative price snapshot for this report returns no price, no currency, no market date and a security status of "unknown," which is consistent with a delisted issuer rather than a data outage.

The rating is therefore AVOID on the absence of an instrument, not on a fresh deterioration in the operating business. That distinction matters for how the score should be read: fundamentals are not declining, because there is no stock. The only live economic exposure for former holders is the CVR, and it is out of the money on any straightforward extrapolation of the disclosed trajectory. Tranche one requires SYFOVRE **global** net sales of $1.5bn in any calendar year 2027–2030; tranche two requires $2.0bn in the same window; a $4.00 fallback pays only if nothing has paid earlier and SYFOVRE reaches $2.0bn in calendar 2031. SYFOVRE did $587M in 2025 and that number was **down** year over year. Reaching $1.5bn by 2030 requires a ~21% five-year CAGR; reaching $2.0bn requires ~27.8%. Biogen guided the *combined* EMPAVELI plus SYFOVRE portfolio to mid-to-high-teens growth through 2028, and EMPAVELI — the newer, rare-kidney-disease asset — is structurally the faster grower. For the CVR to pay, the slower asset must outgrow the portfolio.

The second conclusion, relevant to anyone reconstructing whether $41 was a fair clearing price, is that Apellis's FY2025 profitability was an accounting artifact. Reported FY2025 revenue of $1,003.8M, operating income of +$55.4M and net income of +$22.4M all rest on a **$275M one-time upfront payment from the Sobi royalty repurchase**. Stripping it out leaves roughly a **−$220M operating loss** and a **−$253M net loss** on ~$689M of combined product revenue that was itself down ~3% from ~$710M in 2024. The quarterly earnings-surprise sequence supplied with this file corroborates the direction of the timing: Q3 2025 EPS came in at $1.67 against a $1.21 estimate, a positive surprise that stands out against loss-making quarters elsewhere in the year, consistent with a large one-time item landing in Q3. ## Company Overview

Apellis was a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company built on inhibition of **complement component 3 (C3)**, the central node of the complement cascade and upstream of the C5 inhibitors that define the Alexion/AstraZeneca franchise. The entire commercial base was a single molecule, **pegcetacoplan**, delivered by two routes:

- **SYFOVRE** (intravitreal) — the first approved therapy for **geographic atrophy** secondary to age-related macular degeneration, approved in the U.S. and Australia only. Registrational support came from the Phase 3 OAKS and DERBY studies at 24 months, with a November 2025 **post-hoc** analysis of the five-year GALE extension indicating roughly a 1.5-year delay in nonsubfoveal GA progression versus sham/projected sham.
- **EMPAVELI / Aspaveli** (systemic) — approved in **paroxysmal nocturnal hemoglobinuria**, and more strategically in **C3 glomerulopathy and primary IC-MPGN** for patients aged 12+. The VALIANT study showed a 68% reduction in proteinuria with stabilization of kidney function. The label is unusually broad: the only FDA-approved option for pediatric (12+) C3G patients and the only one covering post-transplant C3G recurrence.

Apellis commercialized both products in the U.S. directly, with a dual-specialty retina and nephrology field organization. **Sobi** holds ex-U.S. commercial rights to systemic pegcetacoplan and is a licensee/commercialization partner that pays Apellis, not a customer of Apellis product. Biogen has said it will continue working with Sobi on that basis.

The structural weakness was severe and unhedged: both products are the same molecule. A pegcetacoplan-class safety signal, a manufacturing interruption (net PP&E was $1.7M — full reliance on contract manufacturers), or an IP challenge would have impaired essentially 100% of product revenue simultaneously.

## Growth Analysis

The decisive fact is that **SYFOVRE unit demand and SYFOVRE revenue moved in opposite directions in 2025, and management guided the gap to widen.** Total injections grew roughly 17% year over year while net product revenue was modestly down. Two mechanisms: **free goods running at 12–14% of volume** and **gross-to-net deductions just above the mid-20% range in Q4 2025, guided into the high-20% range for 2026**, with net price otherwise described as broadly stable.

The proxy provides an independent anchor. The company-selected performance measure for FY2024 was **$709.95M**, and the disclosed performance metric is "Product Revenue, Net" (it is unverified that the company-selected measure is defined identically to reported net product revenue). Against $689M in FY2025, combined product revenue fell about 3% — and that decline occurred while EMPAVELI added an entire new indication and grew to $102M. The only way those reconcile is a material SYFOVRE decline; the implied 2024 SYFOVRE figure is roughly $610–615M falling to $587M, a 4–5% decrease. The FY2024 split itself is derived rather than disclosed, so treat the magnitude as approximate and the direction as sound.

The growth that did exist was in rare kidney disease. EMPAVELI's C3G/IC-MPGN launch metrics were strong for an ultra-rare indication: greater than 5% market penetration in the first full quarter, $35M of Q4 U.S. net revenue, **267 cumulative patient start forms** at year-end, and approximately 95% of published payer policies reimbursing to label or with minimal restrictions (a company figure). At 267 patients, a handful of starts moves a quarter, and management flagged Medicare reverification seasonality and lumpy patient flow.

The pipeline optionality has since been largely written off by the acquirer. Reuters and FierceBiotech both reported on June 26, 2026 that Biogen cut research roles and planned to halt work on most former-Apellis research programs about a month after closing, redirecting resources toward the two marketed products. That is journalist characterization rather than a filing enumeration, and post-merger R&D rationalization is routine, but it is directly relevant to the CVR: it is unconfirmed whether the SYFOVRE plus APL-3007 Phase 2 combination with 2027 topline — one of the few plausible step-change paths to the sales thresholds — survived.

Growth prospects score **3.0**. The score reflects two confirmed items rather than price action: the lead product's net revenue declined in 2025 with guided further gross-to-net compression, and the discovery pipeline was substantially discontinued by the owner.

There is no valuation to perform. What can be stated:

- **Realized consideration was $41.00 per share in cash**, paid at closing, on approximately 130M diluted shares plus in-the-money instruments, for ~$5.6bn of upfront equity value.
- **The premium framing points in the opposite direction from how it was marketed.** The parties disclosed an 86% premium to the 90-day VWAP and a 35% premium to the 52-week high; press reports put the premium to the prior close at roughly 137–140%. A bid 140% above the last close but only 35% above the 52-week high tells you the equity had roughly halved inside a year. The proxy corroborates: FY2024 indexed TSR of 104.21 against a peer group at 118.19 was the first underperformance year in five, after 195.49 versus 118.87 in 2023.
- **Biogen's shares fell about 5% on announcement**, indicating the acquirer's shareholders initially judged the price full, though that reaction could equally reflect the cash-plus-borrowings financing and leverage, with Biogen stating it expected to fully de-lever by end-2027.
- **Sell-side targets converged to the deal price.** The only APLS-specific target identified is Morgan Stanley at $41. The contractual maximum consideration including both CVR tranches is $45.00.

**CVR valuation.** This is the only residual instrument, and it is non-transferable, so holders cannot monetize the optionality or hedge it. From a $587M 2025 base, $1.5bn by 2030 requires ~21% compounding ($587M × 1.21⁵ ≈ $1,525M) and $2.0bn requires ~27.8% ($587M × 1.278⁵ ≈ $2,000M). At the midpoint of Biogen's *combined* mid-to-high-teens guidance — call it 16% — SYFOVRE alone reaches roughly $1,233M by 2030, short of tranche one. Because EMPAVELI is the faster-growing component of that combined guidance, SYFOVRE's own implied rate is below 16%, widening the shortfall. Biogen's own risk language contemplates that "the net sales thresholds for the CVR payments are never met."

The counterargument is that threshold structures are cleared by discontinuities, not by smooth compounding. SYFOVRE was approved in only the U.S. and Australia; the thresholds are on **global** net sales, so an EU or Japan approval under Biogen's infrastructure, the prefilled syringe (FDA submission planned 1H 2026, a genuine workflow catalyst in retina), or the APL-3007 combination could produce a step change. The CAGR framing is a sensitivity and systematically understates discontinuous outcomes. The CVR agreement was not read, so the contractual definition of net sales, any commercially-reasonable-efforts covenant binding Biogen, and any acceleration provisions are unknown, and those provisions can move a CVR's expected value substantially. Weighing both sides, the CVR is close to out of the money and the realistic consideration is $41.00.

Valuation scores **3.0**. There is no price at which to buy, no asymmetry to capture, and the one residual claim is an unhedgeable binary with the disclosed trend running against it.

## Competitive Landscape

**Geographic atrophy is a two-player market competing on net price.** SYFOVRE's principal competitor is Astellas's IZERVAY, a C5 inhibitor approved after SYFOVRE. Management claimed roughly 60% GA share at end-2025, supported by real-world evidence, the five-year GALE data, preferred payer status and workflow initiatives. That is a company estimate, not third-party verified. The revealing combination is 60% share plus 17% volume growth plus declining net revenue: the category is being contested on gross-to-net and access rather than on volume, and roughly 20 percentage points of annual volume growth were being consumed by price and access concessions.

Two readings of the free-goods and gross-to-net dynamics are live and the data cannot separate them. The defensive reading is first-mover share protection through net-price concession. The constructive reading is deliberate access expansion — management noted third-party co-pay assistance programs reopening to new patients late in the period with "limited visibility" — which mechanically inflates free goods and gross-to-net for pro-growth reasons and could partially reverse as patients convert to paid.

Structurally, GA is a difficult commercial market: a non-fatal condition treated with chronic intravitreal injections, a label carrying first-dose severe-vision-loss warnings, a 4× increase in conversion to wet AMD at monthly dosing, and a predominantly Medicare payer base that makes gross-to-net and buy-and-bill economics decisive.

**Rare kidney disease was the strategic prize.** Biogen was explicit that Apellis "brings an established U.S. sales infrastructure and capabilities, including in nephrology," which Biogen believes will "accelerate and strengthen" commercial readiness for **felzartamab**, its own Phase 3 asset in three kidney diseases with first readout in 1H 2027. Reuters framed the deal on announcement day around the kidney franchise. The reasonable inference is that Biogen paid for in-place revenue to bridge a near-term growth gap plus a ready-built nephrology channel to de-risk felzartamab, and that SYFOVRE was the component Biogen was least willing to fund with cash, which is why SYFOVRE alone sits inside the CVR. An alternative reading is that the CVR simply covers the asset with the widest forecast dispersion; both are consistent with the structure.

EMPAVELI's C3G/IC-MPGN differentiation is specific and genuine: the only FDA-approved option for pediatric (12+) patients, the only approved option for post-transplant C3G recurrence, and the first approved option in primary IC-MPGN for adults and adolescents. The offsetting friction is a boxed warning for serious infections from encapsulated bacteria and distribution restricted to a REMS program, in a specialty with little history of prescribing complement inhibitors. The C3G/IC-MPGN competitive field was not verified — including whether oral complement inhibitors from other sponsors were approved or in late-stage development — and that is a real gap in assessing the durability of the asset Biogen appears to have valued most.

Competitive position scores **4.5**: level was defensible in both franchises, but trajectory in the larger franchise was deteriorating on realized economics, and trajectory is what matters.

## Risk Assessment

**No investable instrument.** The primary risk is definitional: there is no APLS common equity to own, no authoritative price, and confirmation of deregistration mechanics is incomplete. Tender participation percentages, whether a Section 251(h) short-form merger was used, and CVR record-date mechanics are all unverified.

**CVR binary.** The residual claim is non-transferable, unhedgeable, and requires SYFOVRE to roughly triple global net sales from a base that declined in 2025. Biogen's own risk language allows that thresholds may never be met.

**SYFOVRE label safety burden.** The U.S. label carries a Warnings and Precautions entry stating that retinal vasculitis and/or retinal vascular occlusion, typically with intraocular inflammation, have been reported; "cases may occur with the first dose" and "may result in severe vision loss," with instruction to discontinue. First-dose, irreversible-harm risk in an elective chronic therapy for a non-fatal condition is the profile most damaging to prescriber adoption. Separately, neovascular AMD or choroidal neovascularization occurred in 12% with monthly dosing and 7% every-other-month versus 3% in control by Month 24 — a 4× relative increase at monthly dosing that a competitor can use directly.

**Net-price erosion, guided worse.** Free goods 12–14% of volume; gross-to-net from just above mid-20% to high-20%; co-pay assistance dynamics described by management as a "wild card" with limited visibility.

**Single-molecule, single-mechanism, single-geography concentration.** Both products are pegcetacoplan. SYFOVRE was approved in only the U.S. and Australia. Ex-U.S. systemic economics ran through Sobi and — on the most natural reading of the royalty repurchase — that stream was partly or wholly monetized away in 2025, removing a growing revenue line precisely as the EU C3G/IC-MPGN launch was starting.

**Information gaps that should temper any confident view.** Items 1, 1A, 7 and 8 of the FY2025 10-K were never accessible (direct EDGAR document fetches returned HTTP 403); no risk-factor additions between the FY2024 and FY2025 filings have been identified. The Sobi transaction's structure and direction are inferred from the revenue-recognition direction, not verified against the contract. The ~$361.7M long-term debt instrument is unnamed, with unknown covenants and change-of-control treatment. No Form 4 data was obtainable (CIK unresolvable post-deregistration), so no statement can be made about insider activity ahead of the March 31 announcement. The Schedule 14D-9 was not reviewed, so the background of the transaction, whether other bidders were solicited, Evercore's fairness opinion, management's long-range projections and change-of-control payments are all unexamined. Evercore was the sole financial advisor to Apellis on a $5.6bn take-private; that is a fact from the press release, and no process inference is drawn from it.

**Stale-data risk.** The earnings-surprise table supplied here, and at least one news source describing an EPS miss against a −$0.39 consensus with no reported figure populated, reflect a pre-acquisition data environment. A Q1 2026 print of +$0.15 against a −$0.36 estimate appears in the supplied feed but cannot be reconciled against any filed 10-Q, since none exists for that period. Any quantitative screen still ingesting APLS is producing output for a security that stopped trading on or about May 14, 2026.

Risk factors score **3.5**. It reflects an unhedgeable, non-transferable contingent claim on a single asset with a deteriorating net-revenue trend.

**Management quality** scores **5.5**. The governance record is better than the operating record. CEO Cedric Francois was co-founder and led through the full commercialization arc; summary-table compensation was remarkably flat across five years ($9.28M in 2020 to $10.96M in 2024) with no evidence of opportunistic ratcheting, and the CEO-to-NEO pay ratio of 2.7× is modest. FY2024 "compensation actually paid" was **−$1,089,135** against $10.96M of summary-table pay, with NEO average CAP collapsing to $194,303 from $5,339,504 — a mark-to-market construct, but one that transmitted the 2024 drawdown to management with unusual force. The primary financial metric was **Product Revenue, Net**, which, if applied consistently, would exclude the $275M Sobi receipt from 2025 incentive compensation; the FY2025 definitions and payouts were not verified. The board delivered a cash exit at an 86% premium to 90-day VWAP for a business that was loss-making on underlying economics with a September 2026 convertible maturity unresolved. Offsetting: no insider data, no 14D-9 review, and the sale itself is an acknowledgment that self-funded development was not achievable.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case (why the CVR could pay, and why $41 might prove cheap in hindsight):** the thresholds are on global net sales while SYFOVRE was approved in only two countries. Biogen has global regulatory and commercial infrastructure Apellis lacked, and an EU or Japan approval would be a step change rather than incremental compounding. The prefilled syringe (FDA submission planned 1H 2026) removes real workflow friction in retina practices and could lift injection volumes and, by simplifying buy-and-bill, net economics. The five-year GALE data indicating a ~1.5-year delay in nonsubfoveal GA progression strengthens the durability argument with retina specialists, and the SYFOVRE plus APL-3007 combination with 2027 topline offers a further discontinuity. If the elevated free goods and gross-to-net were access investment rather than defensive discounting, a portion mechanically reverses as patients convert to paid, and net revenue could inflect on the same volume base. Biogen guided combined portfolio growth in the mid-to-high teens at least through 2028 and expects the deal to be increasingly accretive to non-GAAP diluted EPS from 2027.

**Bear case (base case):** the CVR expires worthless. SYFOVRE net revenue fell in 2025 despite 17% volume growth, management guided gross-to-net compression to continue in 2026, and the required 21% CAGR to clear even tranche one exceeds the growth rate implied for SYFOVRE within Biogen's own combined guidance. The label's first-dose vision-loss warning and 4× increase in conversion to wet AMD are permanent adoption constraints in an elective therapy, and IZERVAY is a direct, well-capitalized competitor in a two-player Medicare market where price is the contested variable. Biogen halted most of the acquired research programs within a month of closing, which removes optionality and casts doubt on whether the APL-3007 combination — one of the few plausible step-change paths — proceeds. The CVR is non-transferable, so there is no way to monetize even a rising probability. For prospective investors, there is no security to buy at any price.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria**: none exist for APLS common equity. The security has been extinguished by merger and the issuer has ceased to be a reporting registrant. There is no valuation, catalyst, or price level at which coverage of the common stock becomes actionable. **Remove APLS from the coverage universe** pending confirmation of deregistration mechanics.

**For former holders monitoring the CVR** (the only live exposure), the specific, checkable conditions that would raise the probability of a payment are:

1. **A new-geography approval for SYFOVRE** — an EU or Japan filing acceptance, followed by approval, under Biogen. This is the single largest lever on the global net-sales definition, given approval was limited to the U.S. and Australia at closing.
2. **FDA action on the prefilled syringe** — submission was planned for 1H 2026; approval and launch would be the clearest near-term volume and workflow catalyst.
3. **Reported SYFOVRE net sales growth above roughly 21% annually** in Biogen's disclosures. Anything at or below the mid-teens keeps tranche one out of reach by 2030 on a smooth path, and requires an unmodeled step change thereafter.
4. **Confirmation that the SYFOVRE plus APL-3007 Phase 2 combination survived** the June 2026 research rationalization, with 2027 topline intact.
5. **Retrieval of the CVR agreement itself** — the contractual definition of "SYFOVRE global net sales," any commercially-reasonable-efforts or diligence covenant binding Biogen, and any acceleration provisions. Absent that document, expected-value estimates on the CVR carry wide error bars.

Conversely, the conditions that would confirm a zero: Biogen disclosing SYFOVRE net sales flat or declining in 2026–2027; further gross-to-net compression beyond the guided high-20% range; IZERVAY taking measurable share; or discontinuation of the APL-3007 combination.

**Reassessment timeline**: Biogen's quarterly disclosures through 2027, with the first meaningful full-year data point on consolidated SYFOVRE performance arriving with Biogen's FY2026 results. The CVR's first measurement year is calendar 2027, so no payment determination is possible before early 2028.

## Conclusion

**AVOID.** The recommendation reflects the absence of an investable instrument rather than a fresh operating deterioration. The prior sequence was issued against a live equity that ceased to exist on May 14, 2026 when Biogen closed its $41.00-per-share cash acquisition, plus a non-transferable CVR worth up to $4.00. There is no price, no float, and no forward participation for new capital.

Two findings inform how the standalone record should be remembered. First, FY2025 GAAP profitability was produced by a $275M one-time Sobi royalty receipt; ex-that item, the business lost roughly $220M at the operating line and roughly $253M at the net line by direct subtraction. Any screen still flagging Apellis as a newly profitable biotech is reading an artifact. Second, SYFOVRE's injection volume grew about 17% in 2025 while its net revenue declined, with free goods at 12–14% of volume and gross-to-net guided from mid-20% to high-20% in 2026 — a franchise defending roughly 60% share by conceding realized economics.

Those two facts also frame the CVR. Tranche one needs roughly 21% five-year compounding from a base that fell in 2025, and tranche two roughly 27.8%, while the acquirer guided the *combined* portfolio to mid-to-high teens with the other product growing faster. The central case is that neither tranche pays, tempered by the genuine possibility of a step change from new geographies, the prefilled syringe, or the APL-3007 combination — none of which can be underwritten from the available evidence, and the last of which may not have survived Biogen's June 2026 research cuts. The realized consideration for former holders was $41.00, with the CVR a claim that cannot be sold and is unlikely to pay.
