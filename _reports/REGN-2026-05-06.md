## Executive Summary

Regeneron Pharmaceuticals is transitioning from a company defined by its retinal franchise (EYLEA) to one increasingly driven by Dupixent collaboration economics and a deep proprietary pipeline. The market has partly penalized this transition, with REGN trading at approximately 15.1x forward earnings versus a biotech peer median, despite generating $4.1B in free cash flow annually, holding $18.9B in cash and securities, and delivering consecutive earnings beats (including an approximately 11% Q1 2026 surprise).

Q1 2026 re-accelerated to +19% YoY revenue growth as Dupixent's 33% global growth rate overwhelmed EYLEA's continued erosion. Consensus EPS for the next quarter is rising at +4.6% over 30 days and +10.6% over 90 days, indicating positive fundamental momentum. The stock's current position — approximately 12-14% below its 52-week high of $821 — reflects legitimate concern about EYLEA biosimilar competition and gross margin compression, but the magnitude of the discount appears excessive given the improving earnings trajectory and multiple pipeline catalysts in H2 2026.

Key risks are concentrated in three areas: continued EYLEA franchise decline (26% of revenue as of Q1 2026 and falling), Dupixent concentration (45% of revenue in Q1 2026 and rising) with litigation against partner Sanofi, and regulatory/pricing risks including the April 2026 U.S. Government Agreements on MFN pricing and potential IRA negotiations. These are meaningful but quantifiable, and the valuation already embeds significant pessimism relative to peers.

## Company Overview

Regeneron is a biotechnology company founded in 1988, distinguished by its vertically integrated model from drug discovery through commercialization. The company's proprietary VelocImmune and VelociSuite platforms enable rapid generation and development of fully human monoclonal antibodies, giving it one of the deepest pipelines in the industry (nearly 50 candidates in clinical development as of Q1 2026).

**Revenue composition (FY 2025 — $14.34B):**
- Sanofi collaboration revenue: $5.88B (41% of total revenue), of which Regeneron's share of collaboration profits was $5.24B
- EYLEA/EYLEA HD combined U.S. net product sales: $4.38B (31%, declining)
- Libtayo: $1.45B (10%, +19% YoY)
- Other products and royalties: $930M+
- Technology licensing/other: balance

The company employs ~15,261 people with principal manufacturing in Tarrytown, NY and Limerick, Ireland. It initiated a dividend in 2025 ($0.88/quarter, raised to $0.94 in 2026) and has been aggressively repurchasing shares.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement:**
FY 2025 revenue of $14.34B grew only 1% headline, but this masks a dramatic mix shift. EYLEA U.S. sales fell 42% (a $2.02B decline from $4.77B to $2.75B) while Dupixent collaboration profits grew 26% and Libtayo grew 19%. The combined EYLEA HD + EYLEA U.S. franchise declined by $1.58B year-over-year (from $5.97B to $4.38B), as EYLEA HD growth of $436M partially offset legacy EYLEA erosion. Q1 2026 showed the turning point: total revenue of $3.61B grew +19% YoY as Dupixent's contribution exceeded EYLEA's drag.

Operating income declined to $3.58B from $3.99B (-10.3%), driven by a deliberate $718M (14%) increase in R&D spending to $5.85B. However, net income still rose to $4.50B (vs. $4.41B prior year) due to $1.65B in other income from the investment portfolio. Diluted EPS was $41.48, +8.2% YoY.

GAAP gross margin on net product sales compressed from 86% (2024) to 82% (2025), guided to 77-78% for 2026, reflecting EYLEA pricing pressure, manufacturing investments, and Libtayo intangible amortization. This compression is real but finite — as EYLEA shrinks further as a percentage, the mix shift toward high-margin collaboration revenue should stabilize blended margins.

**Balance Sheet:**
Cash and marketable securities of $18.87B against long-term debt of $1.99B (senior unsecured notes) plus approximately $720M in finance lease obligations creates a net financial assets position of approximately $16.2B. The current ratio is 4.13x; Altman Z-Score is 3.85 (safe zone). This is among the strongest balance sheets in large-cap biotech.

**Cash Flow:**
Operating cash flow was $4.98B; free cash flow was $4.08B (28.4% FCF margin). Capex will increase to $1.1-1.3B in 2026 for manufacturing expansion.

**Capital Return:**
The company has deployed approximately $1.5B in buybacks over 10 months (share count fell from 115.1M to 108.6M diluted in FY 2025; Q1 2026 weighted-average diluted shares were 107.7M). In April 2026, the board authorized an additional $3.0B share repurchase program. The new dividend ($3.76/share annualized) yields approximately 0.5%. Combined capital return represents a mid-single-digit yield on market cap.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term drivers (6-12 months):**

1. **Dupixent expansion:** Global sales grew 33% in Q1 2026 per Sanofi. CSU approval (April 2025) adds an incremental indication. Bullous pemphigoid approved in Japan. Additional indications in development. Consensus expects continued 20%+ growth.

2. **EYLEA HD pre-filled syringe:** FDA decision anticipated in Q2 2026, though the agency did not act by the April 2026 PDUFA date for one contract manufacturer's application; the application remains pending. If approved, the convenience advantage (no reconstitution) should help differentiate EYLEA HD from biosimilars and slow franchise erosion. The prior CRL was a third-party manufacturing issue, not a drug problem.

3. **Libtayo growth:** +19% YoY to $1.45B in 2025, with new combination approaches in development.

**Medium-term catalysts (12-24 months):**

4. **Fianlimab (LAG-3 antibody):** Phase 3 first-line metastatic melanoma data readout anticipated in Q2 2026; adjuvant melanoma interim analysis expected in H2 2026. Positive results would create a potentially blockbuster immuno-oncology combination (fianlimab + Libtayo).

5. **Linvoseltamab (BCMAxCD3):** Already approved in some markets for relapsed myeloma, with potential to grow into a significant franchise (management has noted blockbuster potential).

6. **Trevogrumab (anti-myostatin):** Spending accelerated sharply ($73.7M vs. $33M prior year). If successful in obesity/muscle wasting, this could represent a significant commercial opportunity addressing the GLP-1 market from a differentiated angle.

7. **Gene therapy platform:** Otarmeni approval (April 2026) validates the platform. Regeneron is the first company to receive FDA approval for a gene therapy for inherited hearing loss, offered free to patients in the United States as part of its U.S. Government Agreements.

**Estimate revisions confirm positive momentum:** Current quarter EPS estimates rose +2.9% over 30 days; next quarter estimates rose +4.6% over 30 days and +10.6% over 90 days. Full-year FY2026 consensus is $46.37, up 4.7% over 90 days. This is steady upward momentum consistent with a company transitioning from one growth engine to the next.

## Competitive Landscape

**Improving trajectory in key markets:**

Dupixent remains the best-in-class anti-IL-4/IL-13 therapy across Type 2 inflammatory diseases. Based on current approval data, no single competitor matches Dupixent's breadth across atopic dermatitis, asthma, CRSwNP, EoE, COPD, CSU, prurigo nodularis, bullous pemphigoid, and AFRS simultaneously. JAK inhibitors compete in individual indications but carry safety boxed warnings. Anti-TSLP (Tezspire) competes only in severe asthma. The breadth moat is widening as each new approval adds to the franchise's economic value.

**Deteriorating trajectory in retinal:**

EYLEA faces structural decline from biosimilars (Amgen's Pavblu launched Q4 2024, additional entrants expected H2 2026). EYLEA HD was approved in April 2026 as the first and only injectable anti-VEGF with dosing intervals up to 5 months for wet AMD and DME, providing significant differentiation versus monthly biosimilars. However, affordability pressures from compounded bevacizumab ($50/injection) are pulling price-sensitive patients away. Roche's Vabysmo also competes directly. The retinal market is fragmenting, and EYLEA's premium pricing power is eroding.

**Platform advantages persistent:**

VelocImmune and VelociSuite technologies continue generating proprietary drug candidates at a rate unmatched in the industry. The gene therapy platform validation (Otarmeni) expands Regeneron's modality toolkit. The combination of antibody engineering, bispecific development, and gene therapy positions the company across multiple therapeutic approaches.

## Risk Assessment

**1. EYLEA franchise cliff (HIGH):** Combined EYLEA/EYLEA HD revenue fell from $5.97B (2024) to $4.38B (2025). As of Q1 2026, the franchise represented only 26% of total revenue, down from 31% for full-year 2025. Additional biosimilar entries in H2 2026 will accelerate erosion. If EYLEA HD pre-filled syringe receives another CRL, erosion could intensify further. This is the primary reason the stock trades at a discount.

**2. Dupixent concentration risk (MODERATE-HIGH):** Sanofi collaboration revenue reached 45% of total revenue in Q1 2026 and is rising. Regeneron's fortunes are increasingly tied to a single drug commercialized by a partner it is suing. Any disruption to the Sanofi collaboration, unexpected competitive entry, or safety signal would be material.

**3. Gross margin compression (MODERATE):** Guided to 77-78% for 2026 from 82% in 2025. This compression reduces operating leverage and limits earnings growth relative to revenue growth in the near term.

**4. Regulatory/pricing risk (MODERATE):** In April 2026, Regeneron entered U.S. Government Agreements, committing to provide certain wholly owned U.S. products to Medicaid at or below MFN-benchmarked prices, price certain future medicines at or below MFN pricing, participate in TrumpRx.gov for Praluent, and provide Otarmeni free in the United States. In exchange, Regeneron's products will not face Section 232 pharmaceutical tariffs through January 2029, subject to continued commitments to U.S. manufacturing and R&D investment. While the tariff exemption reduces one risk, the MFN pricing commitments add concrete reimbursement pressure on current and future products. Additionally, the IRA Medicare Drug Price Negotiation program could select Dupixent or EYLEA HD for future price negotiations, and state-level Prescription Drug Affordability Boards pose additional risk.

**5. Third-party manufacturing vulnerability (MODERATE):** Three CRLs all stemmed from FDA inspection issues at contract manufacturers. This is an operational risk that has delayed key approvals and is partially outside management's control.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing elevated near-term uncertainty. Based on available point-in-time data (not independently timestamped by the reviewer), near-term implied volatility appears meaningfully above historical realized volatility, with the term structure in backwardation — consistent with the market expecting resolution of near-term catalysts (EYLEA HD pre-filled syringe decision, pipeline data readouts) to reduce uncertainty. Institutional hedging activity is evident in put demand, though open interest patterns suggest this is tactical rather than structural. Short interest at approximately 2.9% is unremarkable.

The options pricing supports a view that the market sees event-driven risk concentrated in the near term, with longer-term fundamentals viewed more favorably. For investors with a 12-month horizon, the current elevated near-term vol may create moderately favorable entry timing.

## Conclusion

Regeneron offers a compelling risk/reward at current levels. The stock trades at a meaningful discount to biotech peers despite accelerating revenue growth, one of the strongest balance sheets in pharmaceuticals, rising consensus estimates, and multiple pipeline catalysts in the next 12 months. The EYLEA headwind is real but quantifiable and already reflected in the valuation — the franchise represented only 26% of Q1 2026 revenue and is declining, while Dupixent's 33% growth rate is mathematically overwhelming the erosion.

The approximately 15x forward P/E with ~$16B+ in net financial assets, 28% FCF margins, and a pipeline that could produce multiple new blockbusters in 2026-2028 represents a mispricing driven by backward-looking concern about EYLEA. The transition to a Dupixent-led growth company with pipeline optionality justifies gradual multiple expansion as the market gains confidence. Buy with a 12-month base case target of $870.
