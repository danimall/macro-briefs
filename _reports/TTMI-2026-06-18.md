**Research Date: June 18, 2026**

## Executive Summary

TTM Technologies has re-rated 457% over the past twelve months, from $36 in June 2025 to $202.70 as of June 17, 2026, on a confirmed earnings inflection driven by AI data-center demand. The company has transformed from a commodity-cyclical PCB maker into a dual-engine platform spanning aerospace & defense (mission systems, RF/microwave, cleared U.S. manufacturing) and commercial interconnect (PCBs and IC substrates for data center, networking, automotive, and medical). FY2025 revenue grew 19.0% to $2.91B, then accelerated to +30.4% YoY in Q1 FY2026 ($846M record quarter), with the Commercial segment up 48.8%. Net income went from a $18.7M loss in 2023 to $177.4M in 2025, and gross margin expanded from 18.5% to 21.4% over the same window.

The valuation looks alarming on trailing metrics (a stated ~110x trailing P/E) because the trailing denominator is depressed by 2024 goodwill impairments. On forward consensus, the picture is materially different: at $4.07 current-year EPS (revised up 26.6% in 90 days) the stock trades at ~49.8x, and at $5.40 next-year EPS (up 20.3% in 90 days) it trades at ~37.5x, below the EMS peer median of 50.5x for a company growing 30%. The estimate-revision velocity (current quarter +25.4%, next quarter +31.9% over 90 days) combined with 30%+ revenue growth is the kind of forward signal that has reliably preceded continued outperformance, and it sits in the one sector where security selection has produced durable alpha.

The thesis carries genuine tension. Free cash flow was essentially zero in 2025 ($291.9M operating cash flow consumed by $292.6M capex), and 2026 capex guidance was raised mid-year to $300-320M, so investors are funding a multi-year capacity build rather than harvesting cash. Customer concentration spiked from 42% to 55% of revenue (top-10) in one year, with two customers at ~23-26%; the same hyperscaler programs driving the upside are the concentration risk. A founder-CEO transition involves the outgoing CEO liquidating his entire reported equity position, and an unconfirmed June 2026 financing carries a securityholder-rights-modification flag consistent with convertible/dilution risk. On balance, the confirmed acceleration and the below-peer forward multiple outweigh these durability questions, supporting a BUY with disciplined sizing.

## Company Overview

TTM Technologies is one of the largest North American manufacturers of advanced interconnect products, with roughly 18,200 employees across 24 facilities in North America and Asia. As of Q1 FY2026, the business reports in two segments after folding the small RF&S Components unit into A&D:

- **Aerospace & Defense (A&D):** FY2025 sales $1.29B at 14.2% operating margin. Includes radar, surveillance, IFF, and communications mission systems, RF/microwave assemblies, microelectronics, and high-reliability PCBs. TTM is the sole provider of the U.S. Navy's AN/APS-153 multi-mode radar on the MH-60R helicopter and holds NISPOM/ITAR facility clearances. Built largely through the Anaren and Telephonics acquisitions.
- **Commercial:** FY2025 sales $1.59B at 15.1% operating margin. PCBs for data center computing, networking, automotive, and medical/industrial/instrumentation. Data Center & Networking reached 36% of Q1 2026 sales (up from 28%), now the single largest end market, surpassing A&D.

The competitive moat rests on manufacturing scale, a regionally diversified footprint enabling "China + 1" supply-chain diversification, one-stop design-to-volume capability, and U.S.-cleared defense manufacturing that Chinese and Taiwanese competitors structurally cannot replicate. The company cites >10-year relationships with its ten largest customers.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue acceleration is the headline.** Revenue progressed $2.23B (2023) → $2.44B (2024, +9.4%) → $2.91B (2025, +19.0%), then jumped to a record $846.0M in Q1 FY2026 (+30.4% YoY), with Commercial up 48.8%. The pattern is sustained acceleration, not a one-quarter pop.

**Margins are expanding across the stack.** Gross margin moved 18.5% (2023) → 19.5% (2024) → 20.7% (2025) → 21.4% (Q1 2026). Operating margin went 1.9% → 4.8% → 9.1%, holding at 8.6% in Q1 2026, with segment-level operating margin at 16.1%. Part of the operating-margin improvement reflects the absence of prior-year goodwill impairments ($32.6M in 2024, $44.1M in 2023), but the gross-margin expansion is genuine operating leverage on higher volume and favorable mix.

**Net income inflected hard:** ($18.7M) loss in 2023 → $56.3M in 2024 → $177.4M in 2025, then $50.0M in Q1 2026 (vs. $32.2M prior year), diluted EPS $0.47.

**Earnings surprise track record supports forward confidence:** three beats and one in-line over the last four quarters (Q1 2026 +9.9%, Q3 2025 +8.9%, Q2 2025 +8.8%). Consistent mid-single-digit-plus beats argue current consensus is, if anything, conservative.

**The cash-flow tension is the central weakness.** Operating cash flow was $291.9M in 2025, but $292.6M of capex left FCF at roughly -$0.7M. The 2026 capex guidance was raised from $240-260M (in the 10-K) to $300-320M (in the 10-Q), so FCF will stay pressured through the build-out. A holder paying ~50x current-year earnings is funding capacity, not collecting cash returns.

**Balance sheet is adequate, not pristine.** Gross debt is ~$916-923M (4.00% Senior Notes due 2029 at $500M, 5.91% Term Loan due 2030 at ~$341M, $80M Asia ABL), against cash of ~$410-501M and a current ratio near 1.9. A $250M swap at 3.49% through April 2027 hedges part of the variable-rate exposure. The Altman Z-Score of 2.18 (grey zone) is driven by capital intensity (heavy PP&E, low EBIT/assets) rather than imminent distress; OCF covers interest several times over and the company is covenant-compliant. Working capital is consuming cash as AR, contract assets, and inventory rise with revenue.

**Capital allocation:** a modest $100M buyback authorization (May 2025) with $0 repurchased through Q1 2026; capital is going to growth capex. No dividend.

## Growth Analysis

The growth case has multiple confirming legs. The primary driver is the generative-AI data-center build-out, visible in Commercial up 48.8% YoY and Data Center & Networking jumping to 36% of Q1 2026 sales. TTM's advanced interconnect (ultra-HDI, high-layer-count >70 layers, SLPs, IC substrates) is a direct picks-and-shovels beneficiary of hyperscaler capex.

The estimate-revision data is the strongest forward indicator here. Current-year EPS has been revised from $3.21 to $4.07 over 90 days (+26.6%); next-year from $4.49 to $5.40 (+20.3%); and the next-quarter estimate from $0.84 to $1.11 (+31.9%). Revisions are accelerating at the front of the curve (current quarter +7.9% in 30 days alone). Revision velocity of this magnitude paired with 30%+ revenue growth typically precedes further re-rating as consensus catches up, and these are still only four analysts, so coverage expansion is itself a forward catalyst.

A second leg is defense modernization toward electronics: radar/AESA, missiles/munitions, and the sole-source AN/APS-153 program. A third is supply-chain regionalization, with TTM ramping Syracuse, NY (ultra-HDI for national security, volume production targeted 2H 2026), Penang, Malaysia (the "China + 1" destination), and newly acquired Eau Claire, WI. The raised capex guidance is management signaling confidence in sustained demand rather than a defensive build.

The durability question: Data Center & Networking now exceeds A&D, so the revenue base is shifting from "stable defense + cyclical commercial" toward a more cyclical, hyperscaler-capex-sensitive mix. If AI capex digests, the growth rate compresses. This is the central variable to monitor.

## Valuation Assessment

The trailing P/E of ~110x is not the right lens; the trailing denominator is suppressed by 2024 impairment charges and a low operating-margin base now being left behind. For an inflection name with 90-day estimate revisions above +20% and revenue growth above 30%, forward and growth-adjusted metrics are the appropriate measure.

On consensus, $202.70 against $4.07 current-year EPS is ~49.8x; against $5.40 next-year EPS it is ~37.5x. The EMS peer set trades at a median P/E of 50.5x: Jabil 50.5x, Sanmina 52.3x, Fabrinet 50.0x, Plexus 42.1x, IPG Photonics 163.6x. TTMI sits roughly at the peer median on current-year earnings and below it on next-year, while delivering faster revenue growth (30% vs. the EMS group's high-single to low-double digits) and rising margins. On a PEG basis (~37.5x forward against a 20%+ next-year EPS growth rate and 30% revenue growth) the multiple is reasonable rather than stretched.

The analyst price-target cluster ($205 low, $215 high, $209 mean) sits just above the current price. Analysts typically revise targets upward within weeks of estimate moves of this magnitude, and the +26.6% current-year revision over 90 days has not yet been fully reflected in target prices, so these targets are likely stale floors.

The valuation score of 6.0 reflects asymmetric upside from a below-peer forward multiple on a confirmed accelerator, discounted by the FCF problem: the stock is not "cheap" in any absolute sense, and the multiple leaves no room for an AI-capex digestion or a concentration-driven order air-pocket. This is a growth-at-a-reasonable-price call contingent on the revision trajectory holding, not a margin-of-safety value call.

## Competitive Landscape

TTM's commercial PCB competitors are predominantly Asian (AT&S, Unimicron, WUS, Kinwong, Isu Petasys, Gold Circuit, Victory Giant), and TTM differentiates on advanced technology (ultra-HDI, >70-layer counts, SLPs, IC substrates) plus Western and diversified manufacturing. In A&D, it competes with BAE, Curtiss-Wright, Leonardo DRS, Mercury Systems, and Sanmina, where cleared U.S. facilities are a structural advantage that overseas PCB players cannot match.

The competitive trajectory is improving. TTM is gaining share in AI interconnect (Commercial +48.8%), is winning the "China + 1" reshoring shift with Penang and the national-security ultra-HDI build at Syracuse, and holds sole-source defense positions. The principal qualifier is that the share gains are concentrated in a small number of accounts, so the trajectory is genuine but its breadth is narrow. I score competitive position 7.5, marking down a strengthening moat in advanced interconnect and cleared defense for the concentration of the wins.

## Risk Assessment

**Customer concentration (the most important under-the-radar item).** Top-10 customers leapt from 42% to 55% of revenue in a single year; top-5 at 44%; two customers at ~23-26%; one customer is ~13-14% of receivables. These are almost certainly AI/hyperscaler programs. The upside and the downside are the same accounts; an order reduction or program shift by one or two customers would be highly material and is not fully priced at ~37.5x forward.

**AI demand durability.** With Data Center & Networking at 36% of sales, the revenue base is increasingly exposed to hyperscaler capex cycles and potential inventory digestion.

**Free cash flow and capital intensity.** FCF was ~zero in 2025 with capex raised to $300-320M for 2026. Simultaneous ramps at Penang (cited as an ongoing margin drag), Syracuse, and Eau Claire create execution and under-utilization risk on new fixed-cost capacity.

**China/tariff/FX.** ~50% of sales from non-U.S. operations, significant China manufacturing, PCBs on both U.S. and Chinese tariff lists, and a $20.4M FX loss in 2025 from RMB/MYR strength.

**Unconfirmed June 2026 financing.** A June 3, 2026 8-K combines Items 1.01, 1.02, 2.03, and 3.03; the 3.03 securityholder-rights-modification flag is consistent with a convertible-notes offering or credit refinancing. The instrument, size, and dilution terms are unverified and need direct confirmation; potential convertible dilution is a real but unsized overhang.

**Management/insider signal.** Outgoing CEO Thomas Edman liquidated his entire reported equity position via recurring 10b5-1 sales, and all twelve named officers/directors adopted sell-to-cover plans in Feb 2026. Most dispositions are programmatic Code F tax-withholding, and rising estimates are the dominant signal during a record-results period, but the complete absence of any open-market insider buying removes the strongest bullish confirmation.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV is 95.0% against 72.1% one-year historical vol, a 22.9pp rich spread; the options market is pricing elevated uncertainty, consistent with the binary nature of the AI-concentration and financing questions. Put/call skew is balanced (-2.8%) and the P/C volume ratio is very low (0.05), so there is no concentrated bearish positioning despite the run. Term structure is in contango (near-term 95.0% < long-term 102.7%), a normal/complacent shape. Short interest is light at 5.6% of float with 1.4 days to cover. The rich IV argues against buying options for entry; the balanced skew and low put demand mean the options market is not flagging a near-term collapse. (Note: the +433.9pp ATM IV move in the options-delta snapshot is a data artifact and is disregarded.)

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($260-300).** AI data-center demand sustains 25-30% Commercial growth; the Syracuse ultra-HDI ramp and Penang reach scale, lifting consolidated margins past 22%; capex peaks in 2026 and FCF inflects positive in 2027 as the build-out completes. Next-year EPS estimates ($5.40) prove conservative given the beat track record and continue rising; the multiple holds near 50x current-year as growth durability is confirmed. At ~50x a 2027 EPS approaching $6, the stock reaches the high-$200s to ~$300.

**Bear Case ($120-140, ~35-40% downside).** AI capex digests and one or two of the ~26%-concentration customers cut orders, snapping the revenue acceleration; the capacity ramp creates under-utilized fixed cost as volumes soften, compressing margins; the unconfirmed June financing proves dilutive convertible debt. Estimates reverse, the multiple compresses toward the low end of the EMS range (~30x) on a reduced EPS base near $4, and the stock retraces to the low-$120s, roughly where it traded in April 2026.

The asymmetry favors the bull case because the revision trajectory and revenue acceleration are confirmed and current, while the bear case requires a deceleration that the current order trajectory does not yet support. The forward multiple below next-year peer median provides the margin that the trailing multiple obscures.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Base case target: $250.** Reasoning: ~46x current-year consensus EPS of $4.07 plus the expected upward revision as consensus catches the +26.6% 90-day trajectory, blended toward ~38x a next-year EPS of $5.40 that the beat record suggests is conservative. This sits between the bull and bear scenarios and implies ~23% upside from $202.70.

**Bull case target: $280** (+38%) — durable 25-30% Commercial growth, margins past 22%, FCF inflection confirmed.

**Bear case target: $130** (-36%) — AI digestion plus concentration-driven order cut and dilutive financing.

**Timeframe: 12 months**, anchored to the next two earnings releases (late July / late October 2026), the Syracuse volume-production milestone (2H 2026), and verification of the June 2026 financing terms.

**Upside/downside from current ($202.70):** +23% to base, +38% to bull, -36% to bear.

**Thesis invalidation triggers:**
1. Quarterly revenue growth decelerates below 10% YoY, or either of the two ~13%+ customers is disclosed as reducing orders.
2. The June 2026 financing is confirmed as a dilutive convertible adding >8% to fully diluted share count, or 2026 capex is raised again without a corresponding raise to forward revenue/EPS guidance.
3. EPS estimate revisions turn negative for two consecutive months while the stock holds above ~45x forward — the signal that drove the BUY would have reversed.

## Conclusion

TTMI is a confirmed earnings inflection in Information Technology, the one sector where security selection has historically delivered alpha, with revenue accelerating to +30%, gross margins expanding 290bps over two years, and EPS estimates rising +20-32% over 90 days. The forward multiple (~37.5x next-year EPS) sits below the EMS peer median of 50.5x for a faster-growing, margin-expanding business, and the estimate-revision velocity paired with 30%+ revenue growth is the strongest forward signal available. The trailing P/E is a trough artifact that should not anchor the valuation conclusion.

The reasons for disciplined rather than aggressive conviction are concrete: free cash flow is roughly zero with capex raised to $300-320M, customer concentration leapt to 55% of revenue with two accounts at ~26%, the founder-CEO liquidated his entire stake with no offsetting insider buying, and an unconfirmed June financing carries dilution risk I cannot yet size. These are durability and quality-of-earnings questions on an otherwise confirmed acceleration. The combination of accelerating revenue, rising estimates, a sub-peer forward multiple, and IT-sector selection edge supports a **BUY** with position sizing scaled to the elevated implied volatility and the binary concentration/financing overhangs. Verify the June 2026 financing terms before adding aggressively.
