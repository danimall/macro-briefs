## Executive Summary

Chubb Limited is the world's largest publicly traded P&C insurer by market cap ($125B), operating across 54 countries with a uniquely diversified product and geographic portfolio. The company reported record net income of $10.3B in FY2025, driven by a combined ratio of 85.7% (improving from 86.6% in 2024), record investment income of $6.5B, and 6.6% net premium growth. Q1 2026 confirmed the trajectory with core operating EPS of $6.82 (+85% YoY), beating consensus.

At $321.51 (approximately 11.9x forward P/E), Chubb trades at a roughly 32% premium to the P&C peer median of 9.0x. This premium is warranted by three factors: (1) the compounding investment income engine ($169B portfolio, $12.8B annual operating cash flow feeding it), (2) consistently superior underwriting results that generate profit on float rather than merely managing it, and (3) the structural growth runway in Asia where life insurance premiums grew 15–17% in constant dollars. Berkshire Hathaway's 8.8% ownership stake ($11.2B), accumulated since 2023 and grown through at least December 31, 2025, serves as independent validation of these qualities.

The primary risks are catastrophe exposure ($5.8B worldwide 1-in-100 PML as of Q1 2026), casualty reserve uncertainty ($70B net reserves, 76% long-tail), and the structural Bermuda tax headwind (ETR risen from 5% to 19%). These are material but manageable relative to the earnings base, and largely priced given the stock trades 7% below its 52-week high.

## Company Overview

Chubb operates through six segments: North America Commercial P&C (38% of NPE), Overseas General (27%), Life Insurance (14%), North America Personal P&C (13%), Agriculture (5%), and Global Reinsurance (3%). The company insures large corporate risks, middle-market businesses, high-net-worth individuals, and international life policyholders across developed and emerging markets.

Key structural advantages include: unmatched scale enabling capacity provision that smaller competitors cannot replicate, 87.2% ownership of Huatai Group (largest foreign-owned financial services stake in China), proprietary claims handling that drives client retention, and $88B in gross reserves functioning as low-cost permanent capital for the investment portfolio.

With $12.8B in annual operating cash flow, a substantial portion is reinvested into the portfolio each year (after claims payments, dividends, buybacks, and taxes), generating estimated incremental annual investment income in the range of $500–600M at current market yields. The portfolio carries A/A average credit quality with 5.0-year duration, positioned to benefit from reinvestment at still-elevated yields without excessive credit risk.

**Balance Sheet:**

**Capital Allocation:**

## Growth Analysis

Premium growth of 6.6% (7.0% constant dollar) in 2025 reflects pricing power across most lines. Rate increases continue: casualty +5.7%, personal auto +19.6%. The current accident year combined ratio improvement (81.9% from 83.1%) demonstrates that rate actions are outrunning loss trends.

Growth vectors:
1. **Asia Life Insurance**: 15–17% constant dollar growth (Life Insurance net premiums written up 15.1%, or 17.3% in constant dollars). Hong Kong, Taiwan, and Korea driving penetration gains. This is the highest-growth segment with significant runway.
2. **Middle Market/Small Commercial**: E&S market expansion and digital distribution improvements.
3. **Investment income compounding**: Driven by strong operating cash flows reinvested at still-elevated yields.
4. **Huatai Group (China)**: $155B+ AUM platform; largely untapped cross-selling potential.

Consensus expects FY2027 EPS of approximately $28.95, representing 13% growth from trailing $25.68. Estimate revisions are modestly positive (approximately +2.2% over 90 days for FY2026, +1.5% for FY2027) — consistent and unidirectional, though not a high-velocity signal.

The growth profile is mid-to-high single-digit premium growth plus investment income compounding: highly predictable and protected by competitive positioning. This merits a score reflecting steady above-peer growth potential rather than high-beta acceleration.

- **Approximately 32% premium to peer median P/E** (9.0x for TRV, HIG, ALL, ACGL, PGR).
- **1.19% dividend yield** vs. peer median 1.6%.
- **Discount to 52-week high**: 7% below $345.67.

The premium is justified by: (1) consistently superior combined ratios (peers operate 3-8pp wider), (2) the investment income compounding advantage (larger portfolio relative to premiums), (3) geographic and product diversification reducing earnings volatility, (4) Buffett's ownership functioning as a quality certificate to institutional investors.

At 11.1x FY2027 EPS, the stock offers reasonable upside: consensus mean target approximately $338 (roughly 5% upside), high target $385 (19.7% upside). If the market pays 12x FY2027 EPS — still below where Chubb has traded during favorable underwriting cycles — the stock reaches $347. Under a bull case where pricing power persists, cats remain moderate, and EPS reaches $31+ in 2027, the stock at 12x exceeds $370.

The valuation represents fair value for the highest-quality P&C compounder with an improving fundamental trajectory. The current price ($321) sits below the analyst consensus mean (~$338) by approximately 5%, providing a modest margin of safety.

## Competitive Landscape

Chubb holds the #1 position in global commercial P&C insurance. Key competitive dynamics:

**Moat sources:**
- Scale enables capacity that mid-market competitors cannot match for large corporate risks.
- 54-country network provides clients with seamless global programs — few competitors replicate this.
- Claims excellence drives retention rates that reduce acquisition costs over time.
- Balance sheet strength (AA-rated) provides contractual confidence to corporate treasurers.

**Competitive trajectory:** Improving. Chubb is gaining share in middle-market commercial (digital distribution), expanding in Asia faster than peers, and benefiting from market firming while smaller competitors face reinsurance cost pressure. The current accident year combined ratio at 81.9% indicates pricing discipline that widens the gap with less disciplined competitors during hard markets.

**Vulnerability:** High-net-worth personal lines face new competition from PURE and other specialty players. Financial lines (D&O, cyber) seeing rate declines in large accounts. Workers' compensation flat (0.6% growth) as this mature line becomes commoditized.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Catastrophe Exposure (Material):**
Worldwide 1-in-100 PML of $5.8B (as of March 31, 2026). US Hurricane 1-in-250 PML of $6.4B. The 2025 California wildfires cost $1.47B. While Chubb is actively reducing wildfire exposure and has $4.0B in cat reinsurance above a $1.75B retention, a single severe hurricane or earthquake could eliminate one to two quarters of earnings. Probability of >$3B cat loss in any given year: approximately 15-20%.

**2. Casualty Reserve Development:**
$70B in net reserves (76% casualty/long-tail). Workers' comp sensitivity: ±1pp tail factor = ±$1.1B. Social inflation (nuclear verdicts, litigation funding) is accelerating loss development factors for 2016-2021 accident years. Adverse development in excess/umbrella and auto liability lines partially offset favorable short-tail. This is the single largest source of earnings surprise risk — both positive and negative.

**3. Bermuda Tax Structural Headwind:**
ETR rose from ~5% to ~19%, representing a permanent headwind of approximately $1.3–1.5B annually vs. the old regime. OECD guidance could further impair the 2023 DTA benefit after 2026, creating a one-time charge risk. Largely priced but creates lingering uncertainty.

**4. China Concentration:**
As of March 31, 2026, Chubb's disclosed China-related non-U.S. fixed-income exposure (government and corporate securities) totals approximately $11.8B, plus Huatai operations. Geopolitical deterioration (US-China relations, Taiwan tensions) could impair both the investment portfolio and the growth thesis simultaneously. Asia represents 36% of Overseas General and 95% of Life Insurance premiums.

**5. Legacy Asbestos/Molestation:**
$306M adverse development in 2025 Corporate segment. Asbestos survival ratio of 2.9 years suggests potential reserve strengthening needed. "Reviver" legislation expanding molestation claim eligibility. Slow bleed of $200-400M/year — manageable but persistent.

Chubb represents the highest-quality franchise in global P&C insurance, trading at a premium to peers that is justified by superior underwriting, a compounding investment income engine, and diversified growth. The current price offers 5-10% upside to base/consensus targets with identifiable catalysts (benign cat season, continued buyback execution, Asia growth). Risks are real (catastrophes, reserve development, China) but manageable and largely reflected in the valuation.

BUY with 2-3% portfolio allocation. The position benefits from structural P&C pricing tailwinds, reinvestment at still-elevated yields feeding investment income, and Berkshire validation of long-term compounding quality.
