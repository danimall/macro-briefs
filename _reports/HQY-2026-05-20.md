## Executive Summary

HealthEquity is the largest independent HSA custodian in the U.S. with 10.6 million accounts and $36.5 billion in HSA Assets. The company completed a profitability transformation in FY2026, with net income up 123% to $215M on 9% revenue growth, driven by Enhanced Rates adoption (49% → 58% of HSA cash) and AI-enabled service cost reductions. A structural tailwind exists: $4.5B of HSA cash currently earning 1.9% will reprice in FY2027, potentially adding $90M+ in annualized custodial revenue if rates remain elevated.

The stock has declined 25% from its June 2025 high of $116.65 to $84.65, creating a more attractive entry point. Consensus estimates project $4.63 in EPS for FY2027 and $5.34 for FY2028, implying 18.3x and 15.9x forward P/E respectively. Analyst targets range from $86 to $128 with a median of $110 (30% upside). The bull case rests on legislative expansion via the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, continued Enhanced Rates migration, and a new Marketplace revenue stream. The specific risk preventing an upgrade is the stock's direct sensitivity to rate cuts in a macro regime where the Fed path is deeply uncertain, combined with options market signals pricing imminent stress (47.8% near-term IV vs. 35.9% HV, backwardation structure). The risk/reward is attractive but not asymmetric enough absent a clearer rate outlook or a specific mispricing that consensus hasn't already identified.

## Company Overview

HealthEquity operates as an IRS-approved non-bank custodian managing health savings accounts and complementary consumer-directed benefits (FSAs, HRAs, COBRA, commuter). Revenue comes from three sources: Custodial (48%, $637M) — yield spread on $18B of HSA cash placed with insurance company and depository partners; Service (37%, $485M) — administration fees and investment management; and Interchange (15%, $192M) — merchant transaction fees on payment cards.

The company distributes through 200+ Network Partners including health plans, benefits administrators, and retirement recordkeepers. It grew market share from 4% (2010) to 20% (2025) by HSA Assets, benefiting from acquisitions (WageWorks, Further, BenefitWallet) and organic growth driven by structural healthcare cost increases and employer HDHP adoption.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** $1.31B in FY2026, up 9% from $1.20B. Growth decelerated from 20% (FY2025) as the BenefitWallet acquisition anniversary passed. Organic growth remains healthy at mid-single-digits with the legislative expansion providing a multi-year tailwind.

**Profitability:** Net income reached $215M (16.4% margin) versus a net loss of $26M three years prior. Adjusted EBITDA margins expanded from 39% to 43%. Service costs declined 7% despite account growth, demonstrating genuine operating leverage from technology investments.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow of $457M (+35% YoY) with FCF of $455M represents a 34.7% FCF margin. At the current ~$7.4B market cap, this implies a 6.2% FCF yield — reasonable but not screaming cheap.

**Balance Sheet:** Total debt of $957M ($362M revolver + $600M Senior Notes at 4.50% due 2029) against $319M cash. Net debt of $638M against $566M adjusted EBITDA implies net debt/EBITDA of approximately 1.1x. Debt/equity at 0.6x is conservative. Current ratio of 3.27 provides ample liquidity. The concern is $2.75B in goodwill/intangibles (81% of total assets), a legacy of acquisition-driven growth.

**Capital Allocation:** Aggressive buybacks ($299M in FY2026, ~4% of market cap) signal management confidence at these price levels. $100M in debt prepayment demonstrates financial discipline. No dividend.

**Earnings Quality:** 4-for-4 beat record over the last year with a modest average surprise of ~11.5%. This is a consistent operator, not a volatile earnings story.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term (FY2027-FY2028):** Consensus projects $4.63 and $5.34 EPS, representing 16% and 15% growth respectively. The $4.5B HSA cash repricing from 1.9% to current market rates (~4%+) is the primary driver, providing ~$90M in annualized revenue tailwind with minimal incremental cost. Enhanced Rates migration (58% → potentially 70%+) provides additional custodial yield lift.

**Medium-term catalysts:**
- One Big Beautiful Bill Act expanded HSA eligibility to Bronze/Catastrophic plan holders — incremental addressable market not yet in guidance
- Marketplace offering launched as new revenue stream with "little contribution contemplated in current guidance" per BMO
- AI-driven service cost reduction showing 7% cost decline on growing account base — structural margin expansion

**Growth risks:**
- Revenue growth decelerated to 9% in FY2026; organic growth excluding acquisitions is mid-single-digits
- Custodial revenue (48% of total, highest margin) is directly sensitive to interest rate levels — a 100bps decline in rates would compress the most profitable segment materially
- Estimate revisions are stable but flat (90-day revisions of +1.3% for current and next year) — no acceleration signal

## Valuation Assessment

At $84.65, HQY trades at 34.4x trailing EPS ($2.46), 18.3x forward FY2027 EPS ($4.63), and 15.9x FY2028 EPS ($5.34). On an EV/EBITDA basis, approximately 14-15x forward adjusted EBITDA.

**Peer comparison:** The managed healthcare peer median P/E is 32.7x (trailing), but this comparison is imperfect — HQY is a financial services/custody business with healthcare distribution, not a managed care company. More relevant comparables might be financial services platforms or benefits administrators. At 18.3x forward P/E with ~15% EPS growth, the PEG ratio is approximately 1.2 — reasonable but not cheap.

**Analyst consensus:** Median target $110 (30% upside), low $86, high $128. The consensus is bullish (14 Buy/Strong Buy vs. 1 Sell), but the Goldman Sell and recent RBC price target reduction from $110 to $100 introduce uncertainty. The low target of $86 is essentially current price, suggesting limited downside according to even the most conservative analyst.

**The valuation challenge:** The 18.3x forward multiple assumes rates stay elevated. If rates decline 150bps, custodial revenue could fall $100M+, compressing EPS toward $3.80-4.00 range, which would imply 21-22x forward P/E — expensive for a mid-single-digit organic grower in a declining rate scenario. The rate sensitivity prevents calling this definitively cheap.

## Competitive Landscape

HealthEquity's competitive position is strong and improving in most dimensions:

**Strengths:** Market share grew from 4% to 20% over 15 years. 200+ Network Partner integrations create meaningful switching costs. The non-bank custodian structure allows flexible partnership models. Enhanced Rates program offers higher yields than bank competitors, improving member retention. Scale enables AI investment that smaller competitors cannot match.

**Threats:** Fidelity Investments has "gained significant market share" per the 10-K, leveraging brand recognition and zero-fee strategies. Optum/UNH can offer integrated HSA+health plan solutions. Some Network Partners are beginning to offer HSAs directly, disintermediating HealthEquity. The legislative expansion of HSAs could attract new competitive investment from major financial institutions.

**Trajectory:** Improving via Enhanced Rates differentiation and AI service quality, but facing intensifying competition from well-capitalized entrants (Fidelity). Net competitive position stable-to-slightly-improving.

## Risk Assessment

**Interest Rate Sensitivity (PRIMARY):** Custodial revenue is directly linked to rate levels. Goldman's Sell rating specifically targets this exposure. While the $4.5B repricing tailwind provides near-term support, a resolution of geopolitical conflicts or economic slowdown could trigger rate cuts that would compress HQY's most profitable segment by 10-15%.

**Fraud/Cybersecurity:** Ongoing class action from 2024 data breach. Company admits "significant" costs and reputational damage. FY2025 was elevated; FY2026 showed improvement. Recurrence risk exists.

**Goodwill Impairment:** $2.75B in intangibles (81% of assets) creates potential write-down risk if acquisition economics deteriorate or growth disappoints.

**Legislative Risk:** The business exists because of tax code provisions. While recent policy was favorable, any reversal of HSA tax benefits would be existential.

**Enhanced Rates Counterparty:** 58% of HSA cash is now in non-FDIC-insured insurance company contracts. A counterparty failure could trigger member withdrawals and reputational crisis.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is pricing elevated near-term uncertainty: 47.8% near-term IV versus 35.9% historical volatility, with term structure in backwardation (near-term > long-term). Put/call skew of 3.4% indicates moderately elevated put demand. The implied move of ±10.8% on nearest expiry suggests the market expects a meaningful catalyst within 28 days. This pricing is consistent with institutional uncertainty rather than complacency — the options market does not share the equity consensus bullishness.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($110-128, 30-51% upside):**
- $4.5B repricing tailwind converts to $90M+ custodial revenue in FY2027 without management action
- Enhanced Rates migration continues to 70%+ of HSA cash, structurally lifting yields above historical averages
- Legislative expansion drives new HSA enrollment acceleration starting FY2028
- Marketplace offering adds new high-margin revenue stream not in current estimates
- AI cost efficiencies sustain margin expansion, potentially reaching 50%+ adjusted EBITDA margins
- Aggressive buybacks at current prices compound per-share value

**Bear Case ($65-72, 15-23% downside):**
- Fed cuts rates 200bps+ over 18 months as recession materializes, compressing custodial revenue by $150M+
- Fidelity wins material Network Partner contracts through zero-fee undercutting
- Fraud recurrence triggers member attrition and additional reimbursement costs
- Goodwill impairment from prior acquisitions (WageWorks integration never fully delivered promised synergies)
- Legislative reversal of HSA expansion under different political regime

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Price decline to $75 or below (~16x forward FY2027 EPS), providing adequate margin of safety against rate cut scenario
2. Evidence of rate-cut probability declining below 10% through end of FY2028 (would validate the custodial revenue tailwind with high confidence)
3. Next quarterly earnings showing HSA account growth acceleration above 8% YoY (would signal legislative expansion converting to revenue)

**Downgrade triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Fed signals rate cuts of 150bps+ within 12 months
2. Network Partner attrition exceeds 2% of total accounts in any quarter
3. Fraud-related costs spike again above $30M in a single quarter

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (expected June 2026 for Q1 FY2027). Key data points: HSA cash repricing progress, Enhanced Rates migration percentage, new account growth post-legislative expansion.

## Conclusion

HealthEquity is a high-quality business with structural tailwinds and improving profitability, trading at a reasonable forward multiple with 30%+ upside to consensus targets. The specific reason this does not reach a BUY recommendation: the company's most profitable revenue segment (48% of revenue) is directly correlated to interest rate levels, and the current macro regime features maximum uncertainty around the Fed's path. This creates a bimodal outcome distribution where the stock could see $110+ (rates hold → repricing tailwind realized) or $65-72 (rates cut → margin compression). The options market confirms this uncertainty with near-term IV 12pp above HV and backwardation structure. Until the rate path clarifies or the stock reaches a valuation ($75 or below) where even the bear case offers acceptable risk/reward, the appropriate position is HOLD with a bullish lean and monitoring for the specific upgrade triggers identified above.
