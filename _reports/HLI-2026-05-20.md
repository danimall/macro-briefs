## Executive Summary

Houlihan Lokey is a premier independent investment bank with dominant positioning in mid-cap M&A advisory and financial restructuring. The firm operates a zero-debt, high-FCF model with a natural countercyclical hedge (restructuring revenue rises when M&A declines). FY2025 was a record year with $2.39B in revenue and 33.9% FCF margins.

The investment case faces three concrete headwinds that prevent conviction at current levels: (1) the most recent quarter (Q4 FY2026, March 2026) missed EPS estimates by 11.7% — the first miss in five quarters; (2) current-year EPS estimates have declined 7.1% over 90 days, indicating negative earnings momentum; and (3) the stock trades at 24x trailing earnings versus a peer median of 20x, a premium that requires accelerating growth to sustain — but growth is decelerating. The macro environment (elevated rates, geopolitical uncertainty, tariff-driven deal hesitancy) creates genuine ambiguity about whether the Q4 miss represents timing noise or the beginning of a more sustained M&A activity slowdown.

The specific condition preventing a BUY: at $146.81, HLI's forward P/E of ~17.9x on $8.21 current-year consensus EPS offers moderate upside relative to consensus mean target of $172.50 (~17% upside), while downside risk from further estimate revisions is material. If current-year estimates continue falling at the observed pace, price support erodes toward $140-145.

## Company Overview

Houlihan Lokey operates three advisory segments from 35+ global offices with 339 Managing Directors:

- **Corporate Finance (64% of FY2025 revenue):** M&A advisory focused on transactions under $1B, where HLI is the #1 advisor by deal volume. Also includes Capital Solutions (financing advisory).
- **Financial Restructuring (23%):** Advises debtors, creditors, and other parties in distressed situations. One of the largest restructuring practices globally.
- **Financial and Valuation Advisory (13%):** Revenue is less transaction-contingent, providing stability.

The firm is structured as a controlled company — the HL Voting Trust holds 74.9% of voting power with only 23.7% economic interest. CEO Scott Adelson leads approximately 1,893 financial professionals.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** FY2025 revenue of $2.39B (+25% YoY) represented a record, driven by a 38% CF revenue surge (564 closed transactions vs. 450 prior year). The 9-month period through December 2025 showed +15% growth YoY. The March 2026 quarter missed estimates, and news reports indicate Q1 FY2027 also saw a year-over-year revenue decline.

**Profitability metrics:**
- Operating margin: 21.0% (FY2025)
- Net margin: 16.7%
- FCF margin: 33.9% ($809M on $2.39B revenue)
- ROE: 18.4%
- ROIC: 13.1% (vs. industry average 5.6%)

**Balance sheet:** Zero debt outstanding. $150M revolving facility undrawn. $1.18B cash/investments (though ~$881M reserved for bonus accruals). Goodwill/intangibles of $1.49B (38% of assets) from acquisitions.

**Cash flow quality:** Operating cash flow of $849M in FY2025, though this included favorable working capital timing. The 9-month FY2026 figure of $411M (vs. $490M prior year) reflects bonus payment timing, not deterioration.

**Capital allocation:** $2.40/share annual dividend (1.6% yield). $125M in buybacks at ~$185/share average in the first 9 months of FY2026 — these repurchases are now underwater relative to average purchase price. $280M remains on the $500M authorization.

**UWIP leading indicator:** Unbilled work in progress grew 47% from March to December 2025 ($158M to $233M), historically a positive leading indicator for near-term revenue realization.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term deceleration is confirmed.** Revenue growth went from +25% (FY2025 full year) to +15% (9-month pace) to a revenue miss in Q4 FY2026. EPS estimates tell the story clearly:

- Current year: $8.21 (down from $8.84 ninety days ago, -7.1%)
- Next year: $9.44 (down from $9.82 ninety days ago, -3.9%)
- Current quarter: $1.83 (down from $2.09 ninety days ago, -12.3%)

The negative revision momentum is broad-based. Management attributes the slowdown to "uncertainty tied to geopolitical events and sector-specific pressures affecting transaction timing." This is plausible given the current tariff regime and elevated rates environment, but it is also the standard explanation firms provide when activity slows. Distinguishing temporary timing from structural demand reduction requires visibility the data does not currently provide.

**Medium-term growth drivers remain intact:**
- European expansion (Audere Partners acquisition adds ~80 professionals in France)
- Restructuring demand elevated by high interest rates and corporate leverage
- International mix accelerating (32% of 9-month revenue vs. 29% prior year)
- Secular shift toward independent advisory over conflicted bulge-bracket firms

**Key uncertainty:** Current elevated interest rates simultaneously help (FR demand) and hurt (CF demand by chilling leveraged M&A). The net effect is unclear. The natural hedge has historically dampened — not eliminated — revenue cyclicality. FY2023's 20% revenue decline demonstrates the hedge is imperfect.

## Valuation Assessment

At $146.81, HLI trades at:
- 23.6x trailing EPS ($6.22)
- 17.9x current-year consensus ($8.21)
- 15.6x next-year consensus ($9.44)

**Peer comparison:**
| Metric | HLI | Peer Median | Premium/(Discount) |
|--------|-----|-------------|-------------------|
| P/E (trailing) | 23.6x | 20.0x | +18% |
| Div Yield | 1.6% | 1.0% | Higher |
| ROIC | 13.1% | 5.6% | 2.3x |

HLI's P/E premium to peers is partially justified by superior ROIC, zero debt, the restructuring hedge, and market-leading positions. The premium has historically compressed during earnings deceleration periods. Stifel at 14.4x and Piper Sandler at 20.0x offer relevant comparison points.

**Consensus targets:** Low $155, median $169, mean $172.50, high $200. Current price sits below the low target, suggesting analysts view the stock as undervalued. Targets were likely set before the most recent earnings miss and may be revised lower.

**The valuation is not demanding on forward earnings** — 17.9x current year and 15.6x next year are reasonable for a high-quality financial advisory franchise. The problem is that estimates are falling. If current-year estimates revise another 5% lower (to ~$7.80), the forward P/E expands to ~18.8x — still not expensive, but not cheap enough to compensate for uncertainty about the estimate trajectory.

## Competitive Landscape

HLI holds genuine structural advantages:
1. **Scale in mid-cap advisory:** #1 by deal volume under $1B. This segment is too small for Goldman/Morgan Stanley to focus on, but too complex for small boutiques.
2. **Independence:** No lending or trading conflicts, increasingly valued by boards.
3. **Restructuring franchise:** Top-3 globally, providing countercyclical revenue.
4. **International growth:** 32% and rising, particularly strong in European mid-cap where the firm is under-penetrated.

Competitive position is stable to improving. The European expansion (Audere Partners, Mellum Capital) adds density in underserved markets. Average FR fees increasing (+19% revenue on flat transaction count) suggests the firm is winning larger, more complex mandates — a positive market share signal.

The primary competitive risk is talent poaching. California's non-compete restrictions limit HLI's ability to prevent MD departures. The broad employee ownership structure (~24% by ~1,100 employees) partially mitigates this, creating retention through equity alignment.

## Risk Assessment

**1. M&A Activity Slowdown (HIGH — ACTIVE):** The most proximate risk. Trade policy uncertainty and elevated rates are creating deal hesitancy. If deal delays convert to cancellations (rather than deferrals), estimates could fall further.

**2. Revenue Cyclicality:** FY2022 → FY2023 showed a 20% revenue decline. A similar contraction from FY2025's $2.39B record would imply ~$1.9B, which at current margins would produce EPS of approximately $5.50 — implying >27x P/E at current price. Probability of this extreme scenario: 25-30% given macro headwinds.

**3. Controlled Company Structure:** HL Voting Trust eliminates takeout premium optionality. Class A shareholders cannot influence governance.

**4. Goodwill Risk:** $1.49B (38% of assets). A sustained downturn could trigger impairment charges, though this would not affect cash flow.

**5. Insider Selling:** Paul Siegert sold $8M at $199.95 (Aug 2025); Robert Schriesheim sold at $170.86 (Feb 2026). No open-market purchases by any insider. At a company with broad employee ownership, the absence of any buying during a 30%+ drawdown is not bearish per se, but not confirming either.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 33.2% versus 25.7% historical volatility represents a 7.5pp richness — the options market is pricing elevated uncertainty. The flat term structure (33.2% near vs. 31.8% far) indicates uncertainty is evenly distributed across timeframes rather than concentrated around a specific catalyst. Put/call skew is balanced (-0.8%), meaning the options market is not expressing directional conviction. The options market confirms what the fundamental picture suggests: uncertainty, not conviction in either direction.

## Investment Thesis

- In severe downturn, stock revisits FY2023 valuation trough levels
- Insider selling continues without any offsetting buying

## Conclusion

Houlihan Lokey remains a high-quality franchise with genuine competitive advantages: zero debt, 33.9% FCF margins, natural countercyclical hedging, and market leadership in mid-cap advisory.

The specific reason preventing a BUY at current levels: **the earnings trajectory is negative, the most recent quarter missed by 11.7%, estimates are falling across all time horizons (-7.1% current year over 90 days), and the stock trades at a premium to peers while offering no specific identifiable catalyst for re-acceleration within the next 1-2 quarters.** The $172.50 consensus mean target implies ~17% upside, which is moderate risk-reward when set against the realistic possibility of further estimate cuts in a deteriorating M&A environment (tariff uncertainty, geopolitical stress delaying deals).

Business quality is high but timing and price are misaligned. The stock needs either (a) lower price for sufficient margin of safety, or (b) positive estimate revisions confirming the slowdown is temporary. Neither condition is currently met. HOLD with preference to revisit at $135-140 or upon estimate stabilization.
