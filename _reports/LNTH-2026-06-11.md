**Research Date: June 11, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Lantheus is the leading U.S. radiopharmaceutical-focused company, with #1 share in PSMA-PET prostate imaging (PYLARIFY) and ultrasound echocardiography enhancement (DEFINITY, estimated ≥80% U.S. share). The franchise quality is genuine; the investment question is whether a company guiding revenue down in 2026 — because its single largest product is hitting a reimbursement disadvantage and a patent cliff at the same time — can transition to a more diversified PET radiodiagnostics platform fast enough to justify a stock that has more than doubled off its August 2025 low and now trades near its 52-week high.

The near-term picture is challenging. PYLARIFY, ~64% of revenue, fell 6.5% year-over-year in Q1 2026 on net price compression after losing CMS transitional pass-through (TPT) status on December 31, 2024 — competing PSMA agents from Telix and Novartis/Curium still hold TPT and are reimbursed at ASP+6% versus PYLARIFY's lower mean-unit-cost basis. New chemical entity exclusivity expired in May 2026. Management guides FY2026 revenue to $1.40–1.45B, an explicit decline from $1,542M in FY2025. Q1's headline EPS of $1.80 was flattered by one-time items (a $59.3M SPECT divestiture gain and a $14.9M equity mark); underlying operating income fell about 20% and gross margin eroded to 61.2% from 63.8%.

The reason to hold rather than avoid is a specific, dated set of catalysts inside the 12-month window, paired with a reasonable forward valuation. The PYLARIFY TruVu reformulation (FDA-approved March 6, 2026; commercial Q4 2026) is the defensive play to restore pass-through economics. The most important growth catalyst is MK-6240, a tau-PET agent for Alzheimer's with a PDUFA date of August 13, 2026, that taps an expanding neurodiagnostics market alongside the recently acquired Neuraceq amyloid agent. LNTH-2501 carries a near-term PDUFA on June 29, 2026. The forward multiple of roughly 16x next-year EPS ($6.40, +20% over the $5.33 current-year consensus) is below sector medians, free cash flow is strong (~$354M in FY2025), and the balance sheet is clean. That combination supports a growth-oriented hold; binary catalyst risk, a 64%-concentrated declining core product, governance turnover, and a stock already near consensus keep it short of a BUY.

## Company Overview

Founded in 1956 and based in Bedford, Massachusetts, Lantheus develops, manufactures, and commercializes diagnostic and therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals. As of January 1, 2026, after a year of portfolio reshaping, it reports across four categories:

- **Oncology — PYLARIFY (piflufolastat F-18):** PSMA-targeted PET imaging for prostate cancer, approved 2021. ~64% of revenue ($240.9M of $377.3M in Q1 2026; ~$989M of $1,542M in FY2025). The largest product and the central risk.
- **Cardiology — DEFINITY:** Ultrasound enhancing agent for echocardiography; ≥80% estimated U.S. share, protected by method-of-use patents to ~2037. Q1 2026 revenue $84.6M (+6.8% YoY).
- **Neurology — Neuraceq (florbetaben F-18):** Alzheimer's amyloid PET agent acquired via Life Molecular Imaging (July 2025). Q1 2026 revenue $35.4M (no prior-year comparable).
- **Strategic Partnerships & Other:** CDMO via Evergreen, AI tools (aPROMISE, PYLARIFY AI), and international out-licensing (GE HealthCare in Japan, Curium in Europe). Q1 2026 $16.3M (+52% YoY).

The durable competitive advantage is logistics. Radiopharmaceuticals have half-lives measured in hours to days and require just-in-time manufacture and delivery; Lantheus's PET manufacturing facility (PMF) distribution network and entrenched commercial relationships across urology, oncology, cardiology, and neurology form a barrier to entry that is hard to replicate.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue has plateaued and is now guided lower.** The trajectory: FY2022 $935M → FY2023 $1,296M → FY2024 $1,534M → FY2025 $1,542M (+0.5%). The PYLARIFY launch ramp that drove hypergrowth is over. FY2026 guidance implies a 6–9% decline, reflecting PYLARIFY net-price compression, the SPECT divestiture, and the PYLARIFY-to-TruVu transition. Q1 2026 revenue was up 1.2%, but PYLARIFY itself fell in dollars even as volume grew — the core franchise is shrinking on price.

**Margins compressed; Q1 net income quality was poor.** FY2025 operating income fell ~32% to ~$311M and net income ~25% to ~$234M, driven by ~$65M of combined Evergreen/LMI acquisition and integration costs, IP litigation, SPECT divestiture costs, and gross-margin erosion (61.1% versus 64.4%). Q1 2026 net income of $118.4M included the SPECT gain and the Perspective equity mark; normalized operating income fell about 20% to $81.3M as gross margin slipped on PYLARIFY pricing plus higher intangible amortization ($16.7M versus $8.0M). The earnings beat track record (2 beats, 2 misses over four quarters; +15.9% and +40.2% beats interspersed with -8.0% and -1.5% misses) should be read with this in mind — the headline surprises overstate the underlying trend.

**Cash flow and balance sheet are genuine strengths.** Q1 2026 operating cash flow was $125.1M against only $3.2M of capex; FY2025 FCF was roughly $354M, a ~5% FCF yield on the current ~$6.7B market cap. Cash rebuilt to $498.6M (from $359M at 12/31/25), partly from SPECT proceeds. Debt is $575M of 2.625% convertible senior notes due December 2027 (conversion price $79.81, now in-the-money at ~$103) plus an undrawn $750M revolver maturing 2029. Net leverage sits well within the 3.5x covenant; near-term liquidity is not a concern. Goodwill ($239M) and intangibles ($706M, including $249M of indefinite-lived IPR&D) are now ~40% of $2.35B total assets, raising impairment risk if pipeline assets disappoint — a risk made concrete by the prior $250M PNT2002 write-off. The $400M buyback authorization runs through 2027; $200M was executed in 2025 at an average $56.72 (alongside $100M at $79.37 under a prior program), but no shares were repurchased in Q1 2026 as the company preserves cash through the transition.

## Growth Analysis

The growth story is bifurcated by time horizon. Near-term, 2026 is a managed decline: PYLARIFY price erosion outweighs volume growth and the diversification engines (Neuraceq, CDMO/partnerships) are not yet large enough to offset it. The medium-term path back to growth depends on catalysts, several of which are dated and inside the 12-month horizon:

- **MK-6240 (tau PET for Alzheimer's), PDUFA August 13, 2026** — the single most important growth catalyst. The NDA has been accepted and the agent met its co-primary endpoints. Approval would open a second neurodiagnostics vector alongside Neuraceq's amyloid agent in an expanding Alzheimer's imaging market.
- **PYLARIFY TruVu, commercial Q4 2026** — a higher-concentration reformulation intended to restore manufacturing leverage and to seek a fresh three years of CMS pass-through status. Successful adoption would stabilize the core franchise's economics.
- **LNTH-2501 (Ga-68 edotreotide, neuroendocrine tumor imaging), PDUFA June 29, 2026** — slipped from March on manufacturing information rather than efficacy/safety.
- **PNT2003 (Lu-177 Lutathera radioequivalent)** — tentative ANDA approval March 2, 2026; launch awaits final approval, expiry of the 30-month Hatch-Waxman stay, and resolution of Novartis patent litigation (trial held December 2025, decision pending).

Estimate revisions are modestly positive: current-year EPS $5.33 (+3.2% over 30 days, +2.8% over 90 days) and next-year $6.40 (+1.6% over 30 days, +1.9% over 90 days). The next-year figure implies ~20% EPS growth off a base that has been cleaned of the SPECT gain. These are supportive but well short of the +30% revision velocity that would signal a systematic mispricing; the modesty is consistent with a transition year, not an earnings trough about to spring higher.

## Valuation Assessment

At $102.93, LNTH trades at ~16.1x next-year EPS, ~19.3x current-year EPS, and 24.6x trailing. The forward multiple is below specialty/diagnostic pharma sector medians, and on the next-year basis a 16x multiple against ~20% expected EPS growth yields a PEG below 1.0 — inexpensive in isolation.

The qualifier is the conditionality and the entry point. The 2027 growth that justifies a re-rating depends on MK-6240 approval and TruVu adoption, neither of which is certain. The stock sits essentially at the consensus mean target of $105.23 (median $106), having recovered from the $47.25 low of August 6, 2025 (a sharp gap-down day on ~14M shares that priced in the TPT shock). Upside to the mean target is ~2%; upside to the $117 high target is ~14%; downside to the $76 low target is ~26%. That asymmetry is unfavorable for a fresh purchase. The peer set provided is thin (BridgeBio at a negative P/E reflects its pre-profit profile and is not a clean comparable), so the sector-relative argument leans on the absolute forward multiple rather than a direct peer read.

The multiple is reasonable-to-cheap on a successful-transition forward basis, but the stock has already moved to consensus, and the discount to sector reflects real revenue-decline and earnings-quality concerns rather than a clean mispricing. This supports holding existing positions but does not offer the asymmetric upside that would justify adding at the high.

## Competitive Landscape

Lantheus's franchise positions are strong in level but mixed in trajectory. In PSMA-PET, it holds leading share with PYLARIFY but is structurally reimbursement-disadvantaged: it lost TPT status on 12/31/2024 and is now paid on a mean-unit-cost basis while Telix's Illuccix and Novartis/Curium products retain ASP+6% TPT reimbursement in the Medicare hospital outpatient setting. CMS reaffirmed the MUC basis for 2026. This is share/margin erosion in progress, and the TruVu reformulation plus CMS advocacy are the company's attempt to reverse it. In Alzheimer's amyloid PET, Neuraceq competes with Lilly and GE HealthCare and faces the same MUC-reimbursement disadvantage. DEFINITY remains a durable ≥80%-share franchise protected by method-of-use patents to ~2037, though a CREATES Act generic-sample request is a watch item. The PMF logistics moat and pure-play radiopharma focus position Lantheus well for the structural theranostics tailwind, but the reimbursement dynamic in its largest product is the dominant competitive fact and it is currently working against the company.

## Risk Assessment

- **PYLARIFY reimbursement disadvantage (the #1 issue).** MUC-based reimbursement below ASP+6%, while TPT-holding competitors are paid more, directly drives the net-price compression depressing revenue.
- **Patent/exclusivity cliff and TruVu execution.** NCE exclusivity expired May 2026, opening the door to generics/505(b)(2) entrants. TruVu adoption is unproven; management explicitly warns that if customers don't adopt it, "we may not be able to reintroduce PYLARIFY and we would then lose customers to competitors." The technology transfer (a PMF supplying TruVu stops supplying legacy PYLARIFY) carries real execution risk.
- **Securities and derivative litigation.** *In re Lantheus Holdings Securities Litigation* (SDNY), consolidated from suits filed late 2025, alleges misleading statements tied to PYLARIFY reimbursement/competition disclosures. Amended complaint filed 3/13/2026; the motion-to-dismiss deadline is 5/11/2026, a discrete event to monitor.
- **Concentration.** ~64% of revenue is one declining product; diversification is real but not yet sufficient to offset PYLARIFY.
- **Impairment and contingent-payment risk.** Up to ~$727.5M (Evergreen) plus ~$400M (LMI) and other milestones in contingent consideration, with $249M of IPR&D on the books. The $250M PNT2002 write-off after the confounded SPLASH trial is a precedent.
- **Convertible notes.** $575M due December 2027, now in-the-money at ~$103 versus the $79.81 conversion price — manageable given liquidity, but a dilution/refinancing item to model.
- **Governance and leadership instability.** CEO Brian Markison retired 12/31/2025; Chair Mary Anne Heino is interim CEO in a combined Chair/CEO role pending a permanent search; President Paul Blanchfield left for a competitor. The board's "strategic streamlining" (deprioritizing radiotherapeutics) reads as a reassertion of control over an aggressive M&A strategy.

**Positioning signal.** Short interest is elevated at 11.2% of float (5.48M shares, 4.8 days to cover), indicating meaningful institutional bearishness consistent with the revenue-decline and litigation overhangs. Insider activity reinforces caution: no open-market purchases, and multiple new Rule 10b5-1 sell plans disclosed in Q1 2026 (CEO Heino up to $8M of sales June 2026–March 2027, plus directors and the GC). These are routine in form but represent a net-selling posture during the transition rather than the open-market buying that would signal conviction. The stock's beta of roughly -0.04 confirms it trades on idiosyncratic FDA/reimbursement catalysts rather than the market, which makes the binary events the dominant price drivers in both directions.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (toward $117)
MK-6240 is approved on August 13, 2026, establishing a meaningful Alzheimer's tau-PET vector alongside Neuraceq. TruVu transitions smoothly through Q4 2026 and secures fresh TPT status, stabilizing PYLARIFY's economics. LNTH-2501 is approved June 29 and PNT2003 litigation resolves favorably, adding launch optionality. The company returns to growth in 2027 (EPS ~$6.40, +20%), the ~16x forward multiple holds or expands, analysts revise targets up, and free cash flow funds buybacks under the $400M authorization. The stock re-rates toward the $117 high target.

### Bear Case (toward $76)
PYLARIFY net-price erosion continues, TruVu adoption falls short of plan and customers defect to TPT-advantaged competitors, and 2026 revenue lands at the low end or below the guide. The securities litigation survives the motion to dismiss, extending the governance overhang, and an IPR&D or goodwill impairment surfaces from the $1B+ in M&A obligations. MK-6240 is delayed or its launch underwhelms. Earnings quality remains poor once one-time gains lapse, and the multiple compresses toward the low target of $76, roughly 26% below current levels.

The wide gap between these outcomes, and the fact that several catalysts resolve within months, is why this is a hold of existing positions rather than a fresh buy at the high.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** The 12-month thesis is concrete: the August 13, 2026 MK-6240 PDUFA and June 29, 2026 LNTH-2501 PDUFA are dated catalysts, the TruVu transition unfolds through Q4 2026, and the path to ~20% 2027 EPS growth is available at a below-sector ~16x forward multiple backed by free cash flow and a clean balance sheet. Existing holders are paid to wait for these resolutions; the unfavorable near-term asymmetry (stock at consensus, ~2% to mean target versus ~26% to the low) and binary catalyst risk prevent a higher rating.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. MK-6240 approval on or around August 13, 2026 **and** early evidence of TruVu uptake securing fresh TPT status, restoring a credible return-to-growth path.
2. A pullback toward the low-$80s that restores asymmetric upside (>20% to the consensus mean) with FY2027 estimates holding above $6.30.
3. Favorable resolution of the securities litigation (motion to dismiss granted) removing the governance overhang.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. FY2026 revenue tracking below the $1.40B guidance floor, or PYLARIFY net-price declines accelerating beyond the Q1 -6.5% run-rate.
2. MK-6240 delay or complete-response letter, or clear evidence TruVu adoption is failing (customer defections to competitors).
3. A material IPR&D/goodwill impairment, or the securities suit surviving the motion to dismiss alongside an adverse PNT2003 patent ruling.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next natural checkpoints are the securities litigation motion-to-dismiss deadline (5/11/2026), the LNTH-2501 PDUFA (June 29, 2026), the Q2 2026 earnings release (early August, where normalized operating trends without the SPECT gain become clearer), and the MK-6240 PDUFA (August 13, 2026).

## Conclusion

Lantheus is a financially solid franchise leader in a structurally growing field, but it is in the middle of a managed revenue decline driven by a reimbursement disadvantage and a patent cliff converging on its 64%-of-revenue core product. The Q1 2026 headline masked an underlying ~20% operating-income decline. Against that, the company offers strong free cash flow, a clean balance sheet, a reasonable ~16x forward multiple, and a slate of dated catalysts — MK-6240's Alzheimer's PDUFA on August 13, 2026 chief among them — that could re-establish growth in 2027. With the stock near its 52-week high and essentially at the consensus mean target, the risk/reward does not support adding, and the governance turnover, litigation, and concentration risks are too live to ignore. The appropriate posture is to hold existing positions through the catalyst window, with clearly defined upgrade and downgrade triggers.

**Overall recommendation: HOLD.** Overall score 5.6/10.
