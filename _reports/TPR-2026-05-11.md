## Executive Summary

Tapestry presents a genuine tension: the Coach brand is delivering growth rates (25-31% quarterly revenue growth) and margins (36%+ operating) that would command a premium multiple in any context, while the corporate structure carries meaningful baggage in the form of Kate Spade's continued deterioration, elevated tariff exposure from Vietnam/Cambodia/Philippines sourcing, and a macro environment hostile to consumer discretionary spending.

The stock has appreciated 72% from its 52-week low of $75.48, driven by three consecutive guidance raises, a massive capital return program ($3.2B+ since November 2024), and Coach's genuine brand renaissance among Gen Z consumers. At $133.48 and ~19.2x FY2026 estimated EPS of $6.97, the valuation is not stretched given the growth profile, but the consumer discretionary sector faces compound headwinds from elevated gas prices, compressed savings rates, and deteriorating confidence metrics. The 12% post-earnings selloff on May 7, despite a 26% EPS beat, demonstrates that the market is beginning to price in tariff risk and Kate Spade weakness more aggressively.

We maintain a HOLD rating with a growth thesis. Coach's trajectory justifies patience, and the ongoing buyback ($2.19B remaining authorization) provides downside support through EPS accretion. The path to BUY requires either: (a) a pullback to $115-120 that re-establishes asymmetric upside, or (b) evidence that Kate Spade has stabilized, removing the primary margin drag.

## Company Overview

Tapestry operates two brands following the August 2025 divestiture of Stuart Weitzman:

**Coach** (~86% of revenue, virtually all operating profit): A global accessible luxury brand generating ~$5.6B in FY2025 revenue with 33.5% GAAP operating margins. The brand has successfully repositioned toward Gen Z consumers through newness-driven strategies, digital engagement, and consistent AUR growth. Coach targets $10B in long-term revenue.

**Kate Spade** (~14% of revenue, near-zero profit): A challenged lifestyle brand generating ~$1.2B in FY2025 revenue with declining trends (-10.3% in FY2025, -11% in FY2026 through 9 months). An $855M impairment was recognized in Q4 FY2025, and further impairment risk is explicitly disclosed.

The company operates primarily through direct-to-consumer channels (87% of revenue), with manufacturing sourced from Vietnam, Cambodia, Philippines, India, and China. Geographic revenue split is approximately 60% North America, 25% Asia, and 15% Europe.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth Accelerating:**
- FY2025 total revenue: $7.01B (+5.1% YoY)
- FY2026 nine-month revenue: $6.13B (+15.9% YoY)
- Coach Q3 FY2026: $1.7B (+31.5% reported, +29% CC)
- Kate Spade Q3 FY2026: $220M (-10.3%)

The divergence between brands is stark: Coach's acceleration masks Kate Spade's continued deterioration, creating consolidated growth rates that understate Coach's quality and overstate Kate Spade's relevance.

**Margin Expansion:**
- Gross margin: 69% (FY2022) → 75.4% (FY2025) → 76.1% (FY2026 9mo)
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 18.7% (FY2024) → 20.0% (FY2025) → 22.4% (Q3 FY2026)
- Coach operating margin: 36%+ (Q3 FY2026)

This gross margin trajectory — 600+ basis points of expansion over four years — reflects reduced promotional activity, consistent net pricing improvements ("inflation plus a point"), and sourcing optimization. The 75%+ gross margin provides meaningful absorption capacity for tariff headwinds (currently estimated at ~230bps operating margin impact).

**Free Cash Flow:**
- FY2025 FCF: $1.09B (15.6% margin)
- FY2026 nine-month operating cash flow: $1.46B (vs. $770M prior year)
- Implied full-year FCF for FY2026: ~$1.4-1.6B

At the current market cap of $27.3B, this implies a FCF yield of approximately 5.1-5.9%, which meets the 4% threshold for consumer discretionary BUY consideration.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Total debt: $2.40B (well-laddered; next major maturity $397M in 2027)
- Cash: $1.05B
- Net debt: ~$1.35B
- Net debt/EBITDA: approximately 0.7-0.8x (comfortable)
- Undrawn revolver: $2.0B
- Negative stockholders' equity (-$3.17B retained earnings) — entirely from cumulative buybacks, not operational losses

The Altman Z-Score of 1.79 is misleading. With $1.5B+ annual OCF, sub-1x net leverage, and no near-term debt maturities, there is no genuine financial distress.

**Capital Return:**
- $2.0B ASR completed late 2024 (28.4M shares at avg. $77.64)
- $1.05B additional repurchases through Q3 FY2026
- $2.19B remaining authorization
- Share count: 264M (FY2022) → 202M (current) — 23% reduction
- Dividend: $1.60/share annualized (1.2% yield)

## Valuation Assessment

At $133.48 with consensus FY2026 EPS of $6.97:
- Forward P/E: 19.1x
- FY2027 P/E (on $7.64 consensus): 17.5x
- FCF yield: ~5.1-5.9%
- EV/EBITDA: approximately 13-14x

**Peer comparison:**
- Ralph Lauren (RL): 24.1x P/E, 1.0% yield — mature luxury positioning
- Lululemon (LULU): 10.1x P/E — distressed/declining (misleading comp)
- Nike (NKE): 29.3x P/E — brand turnaround in progress

Against the peer median P/E of 24.1x, TPR at 19.1x appears discounted, particularly given Coach's superior growth rate (25-31% vs. RL's mid-single-digit). However, the discount reflects legitimate concerns: Kate Spade drag, tariff concentration risk, and consumer macro headwinds.

The analyst consensus target median of $169 implies 27% upside, with a range of $88-$200. The wide spread reflects uncertainty around tariff outcomes and Coach's growth durability.

The valuation is reasonable for the growth profile but does not offer compelling asymmetric upside at current levels. The stock has re-rated from ~12x forward at its merger-era lows to 19x — most of the multiple expansion has occurred. Further upside requires either sustained 25%+ Coach growth (possible) or Kate Spade stabilization/divestiture (uncertain).

## Competitive Landscape

Coach's competitive position is strengthening. The brand has successfully navigated from "discount outlet brand" to genuine aspirational luxury — a repositioning that typically fails. Key evidence:

1. **AUR growth:** Consistent average unit retail increases indicate pricing power, not volume-driven growth
2. **Gen Z penetration:** Younger customer acquisition expanding TAM and extending brand lifecycle
3. **78%+ gross margins:** Approaching true luxury economics (LVMH leather goods ~70%, Hermès ~70%)
4. **DTC dominance (87%):** Control over brand presentation, pricing, and customer data

Coach's combination of brand heat, digital sophistication (Mira AI platform), and accessible price points creates a position difficult to replicate. The nearest competitors — Michael Kors (still dealing with Capri's problems), Tory Burch (private), and lower-end offerings from European luxury houses — lack Coach's growth momentum.

Kate Spade's competitive position is deteriorating. The brand has lost relevance among its target demographic, faces identity confusion, and is being squeezed between Coach's premium positioning and fast-fashion alternatives. The 10-K explicitly states the company may "seek to sell one or more lines of our business" — Kate Spade is the obvious candidate.

## Risk Assessment

**Tariff Exposure (High Impact, Moderate Probability of Worsening):** Vietnam (largest sourcing country) faces potential additional tariff actions. The ~230bps estimated margin headwind could increase if Section 301 or other mechanisms are applied. Management's mitigation (pricing, diversification, inventory pull-forward) provides partial offset, but 75%+ gross margins give significant absorption capacity relative to competitors.

**Kate Spade Further Impairment:** After the $855M write-down in Q4 FY2025, additional impairment risk is explicitly disclosed. If management concludes the brand cannot be fixed, a write-down to scrap value or a below-book-value divestiture is likely. This would be a GAAP hit but potentially liberating for the operating structure.

**Consumer Macro Deterioration:** With savings rates compressed and multiple consumer weakness signals across the economy, discretionary spending is under pressure. Coach's aspirational positioning provides some insulation (luxury consumers are less rate-sensitive), but the brand's "accessible" price points ($300-700 handbags) still expose it to middle-income spending pressure.

**Single-Brand Concentration:** Coach generating 86%+ of revenue and virtually all profit means any disruption to Coach (trend shift, Gen Z fatigue, competitive entry) would have outsized impact on consolidated results.

**Post-Earnings Volatility:** The 12% decline on May 7, despite beating estimates, demonstrates elevated sensitivity to tariff rhetoric and Kate Spade data. The market is discounting future tariff escalation possibilities.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV at 49.2% vs. 41.7% historical volatility indicates the market is pricing elevated uncertainty. The backwardation structure (49.2% near-term vs. 38.6% at 6-month) and elevated put skew (12.0%) confirm post-earnings stress pricing. The put/call OI ratio rose from 0.35 to 0.65 since mid-April, indicating meaningful protective demand buildup. This is consistent with a stock where holders are hedging tariff risk rather than selling outright — a HOLD signal rather than a panic signal.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($165-175, +24-31%):**
Coach sustains 20%+ growth through FY2027, driven by continued Gen Z acquisition, international expansion (particularly China), and AUR growth. Kate Spade is divested or restructured, removing the earnings drag. Tariff headwinds moderate through sourcing diversification or policy changes. The potential IEEPA refund (~$0.45-0.50/share) materializes. Buyback continues reducing share count by 5-7% annually. Multiple re-rates to 22-23x on $7.64 FY2027 EPS.

**Bear Case ($100-110, -18-25%):**
Tariff escalation adds 400-500bps of margin pressure beyond current estimates. Consumer spending deterioration accelerates, hitting Coach's aspirational customer. Kate Spade losses widen, requiring cash investment rather than generating divestiture proceeds. Multiple compresses to 14-15x on reduced earnings power (~$6.00).

**Base Case ($145-155, +9-16%):**
Coach growth moderates to 15-20% as comps normalize. Kate Spade stabilizes at current levels without improvement. Tariff headwinds are managed within the ~230bps estimate. Buyback provides 4-5% EPS accretion annually. Stock trades at 19-20x $7.64 FY2027 EPS.

## Consumer Discretionary BUY Gate Assessment

Evaluating the four required conditions for a Consumer Discretionary BUY:

1. **Positive sequential same-store sales or comparable revenue growth:** YES — Coach delivered +31.5% growth in Q3 FY2026, accelerating from +26% in Q2 and +22% in Q1. Consolidated revenue grew 15.9% through nine months.

2. **FCF yield ≥ 4%:** YES — FY2026 implied FCF of $1.4-1.6B on $27.3B market cap = 5.1-5.9% FCF yield.

3. **Consumer confidence not in declining trend:** NO — Multiple macro signals indicate consumer confidence is in a declining trend. The world model documents 20 consumer weakness signals, Whirlpool "recession-level" industry decline, BofA "unexplained" spending slump, and savings rate at 3.6%. Consumer confidence is deteriorating.

4. **Positive estimate revisions over trailing 90 days:** YES — Current year estimates up 10.3%, next year up 10.0% over 90 days.

**Result: 3 of 4 conditions met.** The failing condition (consumer confidence declining) is a legitimate macro headwind. While Coach's aspirational positioning provides relative insulation, the broader consumer environment creates a ceiling on conviction. This is why the recommendation remains HOLD rather than BUY — the company is executing exceptionally, but the macro environment for consumer discretionary is actively hostile.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Stock pulls back to $115-120, creating 20%+ upside to base case while maintaining the same growth profile
2. Kate Spade divestiture or strategic review announcement, removing the earnings drag and simplifying the story
3. Consumer confidence stabilizes or improves (would satisfy the 4th gate condition)

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Coach growth decelerates below 10% for two consecutive quarters, suggesting trend exhaustion
2. Tariff headwinds expand beyond 350bps of operating margin impact with no offset path
3. Management signals Kate Spade requires significant new investment rather than stabilization/divestiture

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings release (likely early August 2026 for Q4 FY2026). Key data points: Coach growth sustainability, full-year FY2026 actual EPS vs. $6.95 guide, FY2027 initial guidance, Kate Spade strategic update, tariff policy evolution.

## Conclusion

Tapestry's Coach brand is one of the best growth stories in accessible luxury — 25-31% quarterly growth, 36%+ operating margins, and expanding Gen Z customer base. The stock's 72% appreciation from its low reflects deserved re-rating. However, at 19x forward earnings with consumer confidence deteriorating, tariff exposure unresolved, and Kate Spade continuing to weigh on the portfolio, the risk/reward at $133 does not justify initiating a new position. The base case offers 9-16% upside against 18-25% downside in the bear scenario — reasonable but not asymmetric. Hold for the Coach growth trajectory, with a clear path to upgrade if price action or strategic actions improve the setup.
