**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Neogen Corporation is a food and animal safety diagnostics company that completed a Reverse Morris Trust merger with 3M's Food Safety Division in September 2022. The deal roughly doubled revenue but loaded the company with ~$900M in debt and created severe operational challenges. Management has since taken a $1.06B goodwill impairment acknowledging the overpayment, and a completely new executive team is executing a turnaround strategy centered on portfolio simplification (divestitures of C&D and Genomics businesses), debt reduction, and manufacturing consolidation.

The core question is whether NEOG at $9.33/share ($2.0B market cap, ~$2.6B EV) is a genuine turnaround opportunity or a structurally impaired business with excessive leverage. On the positive side, free cash flow is inflecting as capex normalizes, the divestiture program is generating proceeds for deleveraging, and the underlying food safety business has defensible market positions. On the negative side, revenue continues declining, consensus estimates are being revised downward, the Petrifilm manufacturing transition remains troubled, material weaknesses persist, and active litigation creates material financial uncertainty. At 15-19x EV/EBITDA on current earnings power, the stock prices in substantial improvement that remains undemonstrated.

## Company Overview

Neogen operates in two segments: Food Safety (71.3% of FY2025 revenue) and Animal Safety (28.7%). Food Safety includes Petrifilm indicator plates, pathogen detection systems, allergen testing, and culture media. Animal Safety includes veterinary instruments, pharmaceuticals, genomics services (being divested), and rodent/insect control products. The company operates in 28 countries with approximately 50% international revenue and no single customer exceeding 10% of sales.

The 2022 3M merger was structured so that 3M shareholders received 50.1% of the combined entity, effectively diluting legacy Neogen shareholders into a leveraged, operationally complex company. The integration has been deeply problematic: ERP implementation failures caused shipment delays and lost market share, the sample collection product relocation created production constraints, and Petrifilm manufacturing transfer continues to incur duplicative costs.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** FY2025 revenue was $895M, down 3.2% from $924M in FY2024. Through the first nine months of FY2026, revenue is $645M vs. $669M prior year (-3.6%). Q3 FY2026 revenue was $211.2M, down 4.4% reported. While divestitures explain some decline, organic growth remains minimal.

**Profitability:** Gross margin has compressed from 50.2% (FY2024) to 47.1% (FY2025) to 46.6% (9-month FY2026). The compression reflects inventory write-offs, Petrifilm transition duplication costs, and production inefficiencies. Nine-month operating loss was ($24.7M) on a GAAP basis (excluding the prior-year impairment). Segment operating income totaled $58.9M ($39.9M Food Safety + $19.0M Animal Safety), offset by $83.6M corporate costs.

**Debt and Leverage:** Total debt stands at $800M (down from $902M at May 2025), consisting of a $405M term loan (maturity 2030), $346.5M senior notes at 8.625% (due 2030), and $48.5M drawn on the revolver. Interest expense runs ~$58M annualized. Net debt is approximately $640M. With adjusted EBITDA in the $140-170M range, net leverage sits at approximately 4-5x — elevated for a company with flat/declining revenue.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was $53M for nine months of FY2026. Capital expenditures have been declining sharply ($111M FY2024 → $105M FY2025 → ~$50M FY2026 guidance) as the Lansing Petrifilm facility nears completion. FCF is approaching breakeven/positive, a meaningful improvement from negative FCF in prior years.

**Balance Sheet Quality:** Altman Z-Score of 1.13 places the company in the distress zone. While this is heavily influenced by the non-cash impairment, the underlying reality is that Neogen carries $2.1B in goodwill/intangibles on a business generating sub-$200M EBITDA. Tangible book value is deeply negative.

## Growth Analysis

Revenue growth is absent. The company guided FY2026 revenue of approximately $858.5M, below both FY2024 and FY2025. Organic growth, when stripped of divestitures and FX, shows only modest sequential improvement in Food Safety (indicators, pathogens, sample collection) and near-cyclical-lows in Animal Safety.

Estimate revisions are negative: current quarter EPS estimates fell 23.8% in the past 30 days, next quarter fell 18.2%, and next-year estimates declined 8.2%. Only the current fiscal year shows a modest +3.7% revision (likely reflecting stronger-than-expected Q3 results). Falling forward estimates contradict the turnaround thesis timeline.

The three most recent earnings beats (53%, 78%, 96% surprises) reflect the new team underpromising and overdelivering relative to depressed expectations. However, absolute EPS levels remain tiny ($0.04-$0.10/quarter), and the beats reflect low expectations rather than strong underlying growth.

The bull case growth thesis requires: (1) Petrifilm manufacturing fully internalized with margin expansion to 25-30% EBITDA margins, (2) food safety end-market recovery as food production volumes normalize, (3) cost savings from 10% headcount reduction and divestitures flowing through. None of these have been confirmed by actual results yet.

## Valuation Assessment

At $9.33/share, NEOG has a market cap of $2.03B and enterprise value of approximately $2.6B. Against trailing EBITDA of ~$140-170M (adjusted for one-time items), EV/EBITDA is 15-19x. Forward P/E on FY2027 consensus of $0.37 EPS is approximately 25x — expensive for a declining-revenue company with negative earnings momentum.

The peer group is heterogeneous: HAE trades at 32.8x P/E (the only profitable peer), while XRAY, BLFS, AVNS, and QDEL are all loss-making with negative P/E ratios. This makes direct comparison difficult, but NEOG's multiple is above the single profitable comp.

Analyst targets range from $11-$13 (mean $12), implying 29% upside. However, these come from only 4 analysts (1 Strong Buy, 1 Buy, 2 Hold), and the targets assume successful turnaround execution that remains unproven.

The turnaround bull case: if NEOG achieves $800M revenue with 27.5% EBITDA margins, that implies ~$220M EBITDA. At 12x EV/EBITDA (reasonable for a food safety business), EV would be $2.64B, or roughly current levels. The stock at today's price already discounts meaningful margin improvement. For material upside, you need both margin expansion AND revenue growth — a combination that current data does not support.

## Competitive Landscape

Neogen's competitive position is its relative strength. No single competitor spans its full food + animal safety portfolio. Petrifilm has a dominant installed base in ready-to-use plating systems, with high switching costs (regulatory validation required to change methods). The food safety diagnostics market has secular growth drivers (regulatory tightening, global food trade expansion, pathogen awareness).

However, the ERP implementation failures and production disruptions during integration cost market share that may be difficult to recapture. Competitors (bioMérieux, Hygiena, Romer Labs) have been gaining during NEOG's operational stumbles. The Petrifilm brand remains strong, but execution risk around the manufacturing transition could further erode customer confidence.

## Risk Assessment

**Integration Risk (Critical):** The Petrifilm manufacturing transition is the single most important catalyst/risk. Management described "significant production inefficiencies" in the most recent filing. Failure to execute this transition smoothly would perpetuate margin compression and validate the bear case.

**Material Weaknesses:** Continuing material weaknesses in internal controls related to COSO framework control activities create uncertainty about reported financial results and may limit the company's ability to execute M&A or capital markets transactions.

**Litigation:** Class action, derivative, and Securities Act lawsuits allege misleading statements about the 3M integration. Financial exposure is uncertain but potentially material. Discovery and legal costs will persist for 12-24+ months.

**Debt Burden:** $800M in debt with $58M annual interest consumes a significant portion of cash flow. The 8.625% senior notes are particularly expensive. Covenant compliance requires continued EBITDA maintenance.

**Tariff Exposure:** 50% international revenue manufactured primarily in the U.S. creates vulnerability to retaliatory tariffs. Additionally, U.S. tariffs raise input costs for imported components.

**Negative Estimate Revisions:** Current quarter and next quarter EPS estimates declining 18-24% in the past 30 days suggests the Street is losing confidence in near-term execution.

### Options Market Signal

ATM implied volatility has declined from 79.4% to 50.5% over the past two months, coinciding with a 6.1% price increase. The put/call OI ratio rose from 0.05 to 0.99, moving from extreme call dominance toward neutral. The IV compression suggests reduced uncertainty around near-term events (the most recent earnings), but the rising P/C ratio indicates growing hedging demand at these elevated prices. The current IV (50.5%) remains high in absolute terms, reflecting continued uncertainty about execution.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (25-30% probability):** New management executes the turnaround: Petrifilm manufacturing transfers successfully, margins expand to 25%+ EBITDA, genomics sale closes and proceeds reduce debt below $650M, food safety end-markets recover, and the company re-rates as a stable consumable diagnostics business. Target: $11-$13.

**Bear Case (35-40% probability):** Petrifilm manufacturing issues persist, revenue continues declining, litigation produces adverse outcome, material weaknesses require restatement, or credit markets tighten forcing refinancing at higher rates. The company trades to 10-12x normalized EBITDA on current (not improved) earnings, implying $5-6/share equity value.

**Base Case (30-35% probability):** Slow, grinding improvement with continued operational hiccups, modest margin expansion offset by revenue headwinds, debt reduction at a pace slower than hoped. The stock oscillates in the $7-10 range for 12+ months without catalyst for meaningful re-rating.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Negative earnings revisions are accelerating. The next earnings report (July/August timeframe for Q4 FY2026) could disappoint if Petrifilm transition costs persist and Animal Safety remains at cyclical lows. Material weakness remediation failures could surface at the annual audit.

**Timing window:** 2-4 months until next earnings report. Relatively short timeline reduces carrying costs.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** SI at 6.6% of float with 5.0 days to cover is manageable — not crowded. Institutional ownership provides reasonable borrow availability.

**Borrow and options signal:** IV at 50.5% (still elevated) increases options premium for put buyers, but P/C ratio at 0.99 is neutral — the market is not heavily positioned bearish despite weak fundamentals.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case target of $6-7 represents 25-35% downside. Upside risk is limited to $11-12 (analyst targets, representing 18-29% adverse move for a short). Reward:risk ratio is approximately 1.2:1 to 1.5:1 — below the 2:1 threshold for active shorting.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The reward:risk ratio is insufficient, the 3 consecutive earnings beats create squeeze potential on positive results, and the new management team's divestiture program provides periodic positive catalysts (genomics sale closure). The stock is a poor investment but not an attractive short.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry Criteria:** (1) Two consecutive quarters of organic revenue growth >3%, confirming food safety demand recovery; (2) Petrifilm manufacturing transition declared complete with gross margin expansion to >50%; (3) Net debt/EBITDA below 3.0x. Meeting 2 of 3 conditions would warrant re-evaluation for a potential upgrade.

**Timeline:** The turnaround thesis, if it works, requires 18-24 months to demonstrate measurable progress. Until Petrifilm manufacturing is fully transferred (likely mid-2027 at earliest) and genomics sale closes (expected by November 2026), fundamental clarity will remain insufficient.

## Conclusion

Neogen presents a classic turnaround story where the narrative has outrun the numbers. The stock has rallied approximately 50% from its June 2025 lows, largely pricing in the new management team's divestiture/deleveraging strategy and consecutive earnings beats relative to depressed expectations. However, revenue remains in decline, consensus estimates are being revised downward, the Petrifilm manufacturing transition continues to create operational challenges, and the balance sheet carries excessive leverage for current earnings power.

At current multiples, the stock prices in substantial operational improvement not yet confirmed by fundamental results. The material weaknesses in internal controls, active litigation, and negative estimate revisions create asymmetric downside risk. The prior AVOID recommendation from the score trajectory is appropriate and maintained. The company has not yet demonstrated the improvement needed to justify its current price.
