## Company Overview

Tri Pointe Homes was a regionally-focused national homebuilder operating across 17 markets in 12 states and the District of Columbia. The company built single-family homes at an average selling price of approximately $680,000 in FY2025, positioning itself in the premium segment of the market. Operations were organized into three segments: West (Arizona, California, Nevada, Washington), Central (Colorado, Texas, Utah), and East (DC, Florida, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia).

As of the final reporting period (December 31, 2025), TPH had 156 active selling communities, controlled 32,219 lots, and employed 1,579 people. The company also operated a financial services segment (Tri Pointe Solutions) providing mortgage lending, title/escrow, and insurance services.

On February 13, 2026, TPH entered into a definitive merger agreement with Sumitomo Forestry. Shareholders approved the transaction on April 16, 2026 with 99.99% of votes in favor. The merger closed on May 14, 2026. TPH was replaced in the S&P 600 by Bright Horizons.

## Growth Analysis

Growth analysis is moot given the completed acquisition. However, for context on why Sumitomo acquired the business:

The company was experiencing significant cyclical contraction at the time of sale. Net new orders declined 24% in FY2025 (West region down 32%), monthly absorption rates fell to 2.4 per community from 3.1, and backlog collapsed 43% to just 862 units. Completed unsold inventory rose 47% to 681 homes.

Longer-term, the structural housing supply deficit in the United States, millennial/Gen Z household formation, and TPH's premium brand positioning represented growth optionality that Sumitomo is acquiring at cyclical-trough valuations.
