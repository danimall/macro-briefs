**Research Date: July 24, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Fulton Financial Corporation is a $32B-asset Mid-Atlantic regional bank executing a consolidation-driven growth strategy with genuine operating momentum. Q2 2026 produced record operating earnings: revenue of $367.9M (+12% YoY), EPS of $0.60 (versus $0.55 a year prior), and net income of $102.4M, beating consensus. The bank is expanding net interest margin (3.51% FY2025 → 3.58% Q1 2026) while integrating acquisitions on schedule, which most regional peers cannot do in the current environment. The Blue Foundry deal closed April 2026 and the systems conversion/bank merger completed July 11, 2026, immediately accretive to Q2 results. Capital is comfortable (CET1 ~11.9–12.6%, Total RBC ~15.2%), asset quality is improving (NPAs ~0.55%, ACL coverage of NPLs ~209%), and the CRE concentration that would normally alarm (~44.8% of loans) is de-risked because non-owner-occupied office is only ~3% of loans at ~63% LTV.

The complication is price. FULT has re-rated roughly 26% over the trailing twelve months to ~$24.26, sitting within a few percent of its 52-week high of $25.23. At ~11.1x forward EPS ($2.19 current-year consensus) the stock is no longer conspicuously cheap relative to its regional-bank peer group (median P/E ~13.5), but the discount reflects the CRE concentration, the serial-acquirer profile, and the AOCI/rate sensitivity embedded in a spread-dependent business rather than a mispricing. Consensus price targets bracket the current price tightly (low $23, mean/median $25, high $27), implying roughly 3% to base and 11% to the high case. The estimate-revision picture is flat-to-slightly-negative across near-term windows, which argues against treating consensus targets as stale floors.

We rate FULT a HOLD (growth_hold). This is a well-run bank compounding book value and earnings at a respectable clip with an aligned management team and a favorable M&A regulatory backdrop, and it deserves to be owned by holders seeking a quality regional at a reasonable multiple. The re-rating that drove the stock from the mid-$16s to the mid-$24s has largely happened, the risk/reward from here is balanced rather than asymmetric, and near-term earnings momentum in the estimate data has flattened. We would upgrade on a pullback that restores asymmetry or on a decisive turn in estimate-revision velocity.

## Company Overview

Fulton Financial Corporation, incorporated in Pennsylvania in 1982 and headquartered in Lancaster, is a bank holding company operating through a single national bank subsidiary, Fulton Bank, N.A., plus five small non-bank subsidiaries. As of year-end 2025 it operated 204 financial centers across a five-state footprint (Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey, and Virginia) with roughly 3,400 employees and ~$32B in assets.

The business runs across three lines. Commercial Banking is the core, serving small and medium businesses (generally under $500M revenue) with commercial real estate, C&I, and construction lending; commercial and commercial-mortgage lending dominates the book. Consumer Banking covers deposits, residential mortgages, home equity, auto, and personal loans. Wealth Management delivers investment management, trust, brokerage, insurance, and advisory through Fulton Financial Advisors and Fulton Private Bank.

Fulton's identity over the past four years has been that of a disciplined acquirer. It absorbed Prudential Bancorp (2022), executed the FDIC-assisted Republic First acquisition (April 2024), and most recently closed the Blue Foundry Bancorp deal (April 2026), pushing deeper into Northern New Jersey. The differentiation is a community-oriented, relationship-banking model backed by an "Outstanding" CRA rating as of 12/31/2025. It competes against community banks, regional and national banks, credit unions, and increasingly fintechs, private credit, and marketplace lenders.

## Financial Analysis

The standardized Altman Z-Score of ~0.23 that a generic screen would flag as "distress" is not meaningful here. That model penalizes banks for their inherently high leverage and low asset turnover, and it is not the right lens for a well-capitalized deposit franchise. On bank-appropriate metrics, Fulton is financially strong.

**Profitability.** FY2025 net income was ~$392M, a record, up sharply from $289M in 2024, with adjusted EPS of $2.16 versus $1.68 the prior year. FY2025 ROA of ~1.23%, ROE of ~12.1%, NIM of ~3.51%, and an efficiency ratio of ~57.6% are all solid for a regional. Q1 2026 continued the trend: net income $94.8M, EPS $0.51, NIM expanding to 3.58% (+15bps YoY), efficiency 56.7%, ROA 1.20%, ROTCE 14.76%.

The earnings-surprise record supports confidence in the forward numbers: four consecutive beats over the trailing four quarters, with the most recent print (Q2 2026, $0.60 versus $0.48 estimate) a 24.8% beat, and the three prior quarters beating by 3–5%. The mechanism behind the margin expansion matters. Where most regional banks are worried about deposit-cost pressure compressing spreads, Fulton has expanded NIM by 15bps YoY, reflecting disciplined deposit pricing and a favorable asset repricing dynamic. That is a genuine differentiator.

**Capital.** FY2025 CET1 was ~12.6% and Total risk-based ~15.2%. Q1 2026 CET1 dipped to ~11.9% on the Blue Foundry share issuance and deployment, with Total RBC still ~15.2% and Tier 1 leverage ~9.9%, all comfortably above regulatory minimums including the conservation buffer. The bank issued $300M of subordinated notes at 5.95% in May 2026 to support the deal and general corporate purposes, a modest incremental leverage at a manageable cost.

**Asset quality.** NPAs are ~0.55% of assets and declining, with ACL coverage of NPLs at ~209%. CRE concentration is ~44.8% of loans, the key risk area, but the non-owner-occupied office bucket that is stressing troubled peers is only ~$730M (~3% of loans) at ~63% LTV. This is the nuance a standardized screen misses: the headline concentration looks worse than the underlying risk.

## Growth Analysis

Fulton's growth has two engines: organic NII/fee expansion and M&A. On the organic side, NIM expansion plus loan growth is producing double-digit revenue growth, and management's 2026 NII guidance of $1.12B–$1.135B frames a solid full-year trajectory. On the inorganic side, the Blue Foundry acquisition adds density in Northern New Jersey and is already contributing to record Q2 results.

The regulatory backdrop favors continued consolidation. The 2024 heightened-scrutiny merger rules were largely rescinded and reverted to prior standards in 2025, a more favorable environment for an acquisitive bank. If Fulton continues to source disciplined, accretive deals, the compounding of earnings and book value can persist beyond what organic growth alone would deliver.

The caution is in the estimate-revision data, which is flat-to-slightly-negative across near-term windows. Current-quarter consensus is $0.55 (down 0.7% over 30 days, up 0.6% over 90 days); next-quarter $0.56 (down 1.0% over 30 days, down 1.2% over 90 days); current-year $2.19 (down 0.2% over 30 days, up 1.1% over 90 days); next-year $2.36 (down 0.5% over 30 days, up 1.5% over 90 days). This is not the >30% revision velocity that signals a genuine earnings acceleration worthy of a BUY override; it is a stable, mid-single-digit-growth bank whose estimates have plateaued after the 2025 record year. The four-quarter beat streak suggests consensus may prove conservative, but the revision trend itself is not a forward tailwind. Because this system's calibration treats growth_prospects as directionally useful but with a thin recent edge, we weight it modestly and lean on the more reliable financial-health signal.

## Valuation Assessment

At ~$24.26 the stock trades at ~11.1x current-year consensus EPS ($2.19) and ~10.3x next-year ($2.36). Against the regional-bank peer set, this is a modest discount to the peer median P/E of 13.5 (IBOC 11.3, FFIN 18.1, TCBI 13.5, SFBS 15.0, CATY 12.3). The dividend yield of ~3.1% is above the peer median of ~1.9%.

The discount is defensible but not obviously a mispricing. It reflects three things a buyer must weigh: the ~45% CRE concentration (even with limited office exposure); the serial-acquirer profile, which carries integration and dilution risk; and the spread-dependent business model's exposure to a rate-cut cycle that could compress the NIM tailwind. A bank generating ~1.2% ROA and ~12% ROE with expanding NIM arguably deserves to trade closer to the middle of its peer range; that gap represents the residual upside. But the estimate data does not support treating current consensus targets ($25 mean/median, $27 high) as stale floors; with revisions flat-to-negative, consensus is not lagging a fundamental acceleration. The high case implies ~11% upside; the mean implies ~3%.

We assign a valuation score of 5.0. This is not a value trap, since the fundamentals are sound and the multiple is reasonable, but neither is it a compelling asymmetric setup. The system's calibration is emphatic that a low multiple alone is not a buy signal; cheapness has historically sorted the wrong way, and rewarding a stock simply for being "cheaper than peers" without an identified catalyst is a recognized error. Here the multiple is fair-to-slightly-cheap for the quality, but the specific mispricing catalyst is absent, and the stock's 26% run over the past year has already closed most of the gap. A neutral valuation score reflects a fairly priced quality bank rather than a bargain.

## Competitive Landscape

Fulton operates in the highly competitive, fragmented Mid-Atlantic regional banking market against banks of all sizes, credit unions, and non-bank/fintech lenders. Its competitive trajectory is stable-to-improving rather than deteriorating. The consolidation strategy is building density and entering adjacent markets, and the bank screens well on the two sector-defining worries: it has limited office CRE exposure with stable-to-improving asset quality, and it is expanding NIM while peers worry about compression.

The differentiators of relationship banking, an "Outstanding" CRA rating, and demonstrated M&A execution capability including a successful FDIC-assisted deal are real but modest moats. This is a competent operator gaining scale in its region, not a franchise with a structural, widening moat the market has failed to price. The competitive position score of 6.0 reflects a stable-to-slightly-improving trajectory. Consistent with the system's calibration, we do not lean heavily on competitive-position scoring given its weak recent predictive value.

## Risk Assessment

**CRE concentration (~44.8% of loans)** is the single most important credit risk and is explicitly flagged in the 10-K. Regulatory CRE-concentration guidance (300%-of-capital thresholds) applies. The mitigant is the small non-owner-occupied office bucket at conservative LTVs.

**Interest-rate / NIM sensitivity.** The bank's earnings depend on the rate environment and deposit-pricing discipline. NIM expansion has been a tailwind, but a rapid Fed rate-cut cycle or renewed deposit competition could compress it. This is the most probable swing factor for the forward earnings trajectory.

**M&A integration risk.** As a serial acquirer, Fulton faces conversion, culture, and deposit-runoff risks with each deal. The positive update is that Blue Foundry converted and merged on schedule, and the Republic First FDIC-assisted deal (2024) was absorbed successfully; the track record is favorable.

**Regulatory intensity.** As a >$10B-asset bank, Fulton is under CFPB supervision, faces fair-lending scrutiny, BSA/AML obligations, and evolving Basel IV / capital-rule changes, losing many community-bank exemptions. **Deposit / liquidity risk** remains a sector-sensitive theme post-2023, including the FDIC special-assessment overhang. **Cybersecurity** is flagged as a high-level ongoing risk.

None of these are binary catalysts poised to move the stock materially in the near term, and most are already priced into the peer-relative discount. The AOCI-into-capital sensitivity common to regional banks ties reported capital to rate volatility, a background risk rather than an acute one. We assign a risk_factors score of 6.0: moderate idiosyncratic risk, largely reflected in valuation, with no imminent asymmetric downside catalyst identified.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (~$27, ~+11%).** Fulton continues to expand NIM through disciplined deposit pricing, Blue Foundry cost saves and deposit retention exceed plan, and the four-quarter beat streak extends as consensus proves conservative. The stock re-rates toward the middle of its peer P/E range (~13.5x) on ~$2.36 next-year EPS, and a favorable M&A regulatory backdrop enables another accretive deal. Continued book-value compounding and a ~3.1% dividend add to total return.

**Bear Case (~$18–19, ~-22% to -26%).** A rapid Fed rate-cut cycle compresses the NIM tailwind that has driven the earnings beats. CRE stress broadens beyond the small office bucket into the wider ~45% concentration. A future acquisition proves dilutive or poorly integrated, or deposit runoff from Blue Foundry disappoints. The multiple compresses back toward the low end of the peer range on a mid-cycle EPS that no longer grows, returning the stock toward the low-$18 area where it traded in mid-2025.

**Base Case (~$25, ~+3%).** Fulton delivers mid-single-digit EPS growth on stable-to-modestly-expanding NIM, Blue Foundry integrates cleanly, and the stock holds its current ~11x multiple as earnings grow into the price. Total return is driven primarily by the ~3.1% dividend plus modest earnings growth, with limited multiple re-rating.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold). 12-month horizon.**

The stock is a quality regional bank fairly priced after a 26% run, with a durable and expanding earnings base but limited near-term asymmetry. Own it for the compounding and the ~3.1% yield; the bar for a fresh BUY is a better entry or a confirmed acceleration.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- A pullback toward the low-$21s / high-$20s, restoring asymmetric upside to the peer-median multiple.
- A decisive turn in estimate-revision velocity — current-year consensus revised up >5% over 90 days on sustained NIM expansion and Blue Foundry accretion.
- Evidence of another disciplined, clearly accretive acquisition that extends the compounding runway.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- NIM compression below ~3.40% in a rate-cut cycle, breaking the earnings-momentum thesis.
- Deterioration in CRE credit — NPAs rising materially above ~0.75% of assets or office-related charge-offs surfacing.
- A dilutive or poorly integrated acquisition, or Blue Foundry deposit runoff visible in reported results.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (Q3 2026, late October), with the Fed rate path and Blue Foundry cost-save realization the key intervening variables to monitor.

## Conclusion

Fulton Financial is a well-run, well-capitalized regional bank executing a disciplined consolidation strategy, expanding NIM while peers worry about compression, and integrating acquisitions on schedule. The trajectory data confirms an improving fundamental picture — financial health has climbed from 6.0 to 7.2 across recent reports and risk factors from 5.0 to 6.0 — but the valuation score has appropriately declined from 7.0 to the low-5s as the stock re-rated ~26% and closed most of the gap to peer multiples. The Q2 2026 record print, the four-quarter beat streak, and the strong capital position all support the quality case; the flat-to-negative estimate revisions, the near-52-week-high price, and consensus targets only ~3% above spot cap the upside.

This is a HOLD, and specifically a growth_hold: the compounding thesis is intact and the ~3.1% dividend provides support, but the risk/reward at ~$24.26 is balanced rather than asymmetric. We would become more constructive on a pullback that restores upside to the peer-median multiple, or on a confirmed acceleration in earnings estimates. The system's own experience with Financials BUYs, which have delivered negative alpha versus the market as a group, reinforces the discipline of requiring a distinct company-specific catalyst before rewarding a positive raw return here, and no such catalyst is present at the current price.
