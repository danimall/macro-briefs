## Executive Summary

Neurocrine has become a three-product commercial company in eighteen months, and the Q2 2026 report is the first quarter in which that shows up cleanly in the revenue line: total revenue of $959.0M grew 39.5% year over year, with INGREZZA re-accelerating to +14.8%, CRENESSITY up 245% to $183.5M, and a $54.3M stub contribution from the newly acquired VYKAT XR. Non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.85 beat the ~$2.26 non-GAAP consensus by roughly 26%, and management raised FY2026 INGREZZA guidance to $2,825–2,875M from $2,700–2,800M. The stock fell 5.1% on the print and has since drifted to $163.41, 12.2% below the July 30 high of $186.12.

The market's reaction is defensible on the details. The GAAP effective tax rate was 11.0% in the quarter versus 32.6% a year earlier, aided by a discrete release of a valuation allowance on certain state deferred tax assets, and the $144.4M GAAP result was bridged to $296.7M non-GAAP by $152.3M of net add-backs. The cost base stepped up beyond deal charges: FY2026 **non-GAAP** SG&A guidance rose $85M and non-GAAP R&D $30M, against a $125M midpoint raise to INGREZZA sales. Consensus responded by cutting current-quarter EPS 15.7% in 30 days ($3.24 → $2.73) and FY2027 by 3.4% in 30 days and 7.2% in 90 days ($13.12 → $12.17). Falling forward estimates on a revenue beat is the signature of an earnings-quality problem rather than a demand problem, and it is the single most important new fact since the prior review.

Layered on that are three unresolved, largely unquantifiable items: an August 2025 DOJ civil investigative demand into the sales and marketing of INGREZZA, which is ~72% of H1 product sales, carried with no reserve and no disclosed range; the May 18 Soleno acquisition, for which Neurocrine paid $2,829.9M gross ($2,358.5M net of acquired cash) for an asset whose revenue was roughly flat sequentially (~$94.6M Q1 pro forma to ~$93.7M Q2 pro forma) in the quarter the deal closed; and a policy regime — December 2025 CMS proposals for MFN-level pricing demonstrations in Parts B and D, plus a permanent Medicare negotiation framework — bearing on a company whose filings state a majority of revenue comes from federal healthcare payors. At 16.1x FY2026 and 13.4x FY2027 consensus versus a 23.5x biotech peer median, the discount is real but explicable. Base case $185 (+13%) against a $125 bear (−24%) argues for holding existing exposure and waiting for a better entry or better evidence.

## Company Overview

Neurocrine is a single-segment San Diego biopharmaceutical company in neurology, psychiatry and endocrinology, now extending into immunology and biologics. It markets three wholly owned U.S. products and collects royalties on AbbVie's elagolix franchise and Tanabe's Japanese valbenazine.

| Product | Indication | Q2 2026 net sales | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| INGREZZA (valbenazine) | Tardive dyskinesia; Huntington's chorea | $716.5M | +14.8% |
| CRENESSITY (crinecerfont) | Classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia | $183.5M | +245% |
| VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline) | Hyperphagia in Prader-Willi syndrome | $54.3M (stub) | n/a |
| Collaboration/royalty | elagolix, DYSVAL | $4.7M | — |

Three of four franchises are first-in-class monopolies in small, under-diagnosed populations distributed through specialty pharmacy. INGREZZA is the exception, competing directly with Teva's AUSTEDO/AUSTEDO XR in a two-branded-player market. The company owns no manufacturing; third-party manufacturers sit in Europe, with some suppliers and R&D collaborators in China. The moat is patents, regulatory exclusivity, field-force reach and payer contracting.

R&D is licensing-heavy: osavampator and other psychiatry assets from Takeda, the muscarinic franchise from Nxera, sodium-channel epilepsy assets from Xenon, gene therapy from Voyager (in which Neurocrine holds ~19.9% of voting stock and a board seat), and crinecerfont originally from Sanofi. Aggregate contingent milestone obligations across these agreements total up to $15.35B — a theoretical maximum contingent on full development and commercial success, not a balance-sheet liability, and not an expected outflow. Royalty economics matter for future mix: osavampator carries mid-to-upper-teens percentage U.S. royalties to Takeda, structurally lower-margin than INGREZZA or CRENESSITY.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue and the composition of growth

FY2025 total revenue was $2,860.5M, +21.4% over FY2024, with net income of $478.6M. Within that, INGREZZA grew only 8.65% ($2,313.5M → $2,513.7M), down from roughly 26% the prior year, with MD&A attributing net-price pressure to market-access investments. Practically all of FY2025's growth came from the CRENESSITY launch.

H1 2026 reversed the deceleration, from three sources:

| Total revenue | 959.0 | 687.5 | 1,773.5 | 1,260.1 |
| INGREZZA | 716.5 | 624.4 | 1,373.3 | 1,169.6 |
| CRENESSITY | 183.5 | 53.2 | 336.8 | 67.7 |

Two derived points constrain the enthusiasm. Q1 2026 INGREZZA was $1,373.3M − $716.5M = $656.8M, so Q2 grew 9.1% sequentially; but the raised FY2026 guidance of $2,825–2,875M implies H2 INGREZZA of $1,451.7–1,501.7M, or roughly $726–751M per quarter against $716.5M in Q2 — average sequential growth of only about 1–5%. The guidance midpoint of $2,850M is +13.4% on FY2025. CRENESSITY is the cleaner story: Q1 was $153.3M and Q2 $183.5M, +19.7% sequentially, with H1 alone ($336.8M) already exceeding all of FY2025 ($301.2M).

### Margins

Q2 GAAP operating income was $151.5M on $959.0M of revenue, a 15.8% margin against 21.2% a year earlier. Revenue grew 39.5% while operating income grew 4.0%. Roughly $130M of Q2 acquisition-related costs explain most of the gap ($60.1M of Soleno equity-award acceleration, $33.1M of advisory and legal fees, $21.9M of acquisition compensation and severance). Excluding $96.8M of acquisition items inside SG&A, underlying SG&A was ~$342.9M, or 35.8% of revenue, still elevated, reflecting the expansion of both the INGREZZA and CRENESSITY field forces in Q1 2026. (GAAP SG&A of $439.7M was 45.8% of Q2 revenue, up from 41.6% a year earlier.)

The guidance pattern matters more than the quarter. GAAP SG&A guidance rose $200M to $1,575–1,600M; non-GAAP SG&A rose $85M to $1,325–1,350M. The non-GAAP portion is incremental commercial spend, not add-backs. Non-GAAP R&D rose only $30M. FY2026 SG&A guidance now exceeds R&D guidance, an inversion for a company that has historically presented itself as R&D-led. H1 GAAP SG&A of $758.2M against full-year guidance implies H2 of $817–842M.

### Cash flow

H1 operating cash flow was $282.2M versus $166.8M, with $16.9M of capex, so H1 free cash flow was ~$265.3M, or 15.0% of revenue — thin against a 19.3% GAAP net margin. Two identified drags: ~$125.6M of acquisition and divestiture cash costs, and a $165.7M accounts-receivable build (AR rose from $686.8M to $887.0M, +29.1%, against a 40.7% H1 revenue increase and the addition of a new channel). Normalizing for deal costs puts underlying H1 cash generation nearer $400M. Neither figure should be annualized: H1 also contains a $28.6M non-cash divestiture gain, $60.2M of favorable tax timing, and an abnormally low tax rate. The research record leaves unreconciled whether the $60.1M of cash-settled Soleno awards is inside the $122.2M H1 stock-compensation add-back; that ambiguity is worth a sensitivity, not a conclusion.

### Capital structure

On May 14 Neurocrine entered a $1.0B senior secured revolver maturing May 2031, drew $600M for tender-offer settlement, and repaid it in full in June, paying $2.7M of interest at an all-in 4.90%. Nothing was outstanding at June 30. The facility is secured by liens on substantially all assets, carries a maximum total net leverage ratio and a minimum interest coverage ratio tested quarterly (thresholds undisclosed), and generated the company's first debt-covenant risk factor. With zero drawn and ~$400–500M of annualized underlying cash generation, covenant risk is remote; the change is in flexibility, since indebtedness, liens, asset sales and restricted payments including buybacks now run through negative covenants.

Share count rose from 100.1M to 101.6M in H1 (2.0M issued under benefit plans, 0.5M repurchased); diluted weighted-average shares were 104.2M in Q2 versus 101.0M. The 4.8M antidilutive awards excluded from Q2 diluted EPS equal 4.7% of shares outstanding and would represent 4.5% of a resulting 106.4M-share base; these are ordinary out-of-the-money awards, not contingent consideration. FY2026 stock compensation guidance is ~$285M (~$227M ex-Soleno acceleration), roughly 5.7% of estimated revenue, and all of it is excluded from non-GAAP.

Buybacks: $66.0M (0.5M shares) in H1, all executed in April at an average $131.53, with none in May or June — consistent with preserving cash for the acquisition. $266.3M remains authorized.

## Growth Analysis

The FY2026 revenue picture is the strongest in the company's history: INGREZZA at the $2,850M guidance midpoint, CRENESSITY extrapolating from H1's $336.8M with continued sequential growth toward roughly $780M, VYKAT XR contributing perhaps $250M as a stub year, plus ~$18M of collaboration revenue, gives roughly $3.9B — about 36% growth on FY2025's $2,860.5M. Neurocrine guides only INGREZZA and expense lines, so total revenue is an estimate.

Underneath, the three franchises are on different trajectories.

**CRENESSITY is the strongest asset in the portfolio.** Management reported that roughly 15% of the estimated *diagnosed* classic-CAH population has been prescribed the drug, that the prescriber base nearly tripled year over year, and that ~80% of dispensed prescriptions were reimbursed in Q2. Two-year CAHtalyst data presented at ENDO 2026 emphasized cardiometabolic outcomes, bone health and pediatric growth on sustained glucocorticoid reduction — the pharmacoeconomic argument needed to displace pennies-per-dose steroid monotherapy. The 15% figure is a share of diagnosed patients, not of the "at least 20,000" U.S. prevalence the filings cite, so the runway is longer than the headline implies. Management's own description of the market as "an inch deep and a mile wide" is the constraint: many practices treat only a handful of patients, which caps field-force productivity.

**INGREZZA growth depends on diagnosis expansion under a deflationary price structure.** The 8.65% FY2025 growth on double-digit volume growth establishes that gross-to-net erosion is already occurring. Management said roughly 70% of Medicare lives are covered under contracting completed in 2025 and that pricing should stay relatively consistent through H2 2026, but 2027 access discussions are explicitly still open. Guidance embedding only 1–5% average sequential growth in H2 is the company's own signal on the near-term ceiling.

**VYKAT XR has not yet demonstrated it can grow.** Derived from the 10-Q's pro forma disclosure: pro forma H1 revenue of $1,907.5M less reported $1,773.5M gives $134.0M of Soleno standalone contribution; the Q2 delta alone was $39.4M, implying Soleno Q1 2026 revenue of ~$94.6M against ~$93.7M on a full-quarter Q2 basis. Management corroborated this, describing new patient demand as "relatively consistent with the first quarter," guiding to sequential growth only "as we exit 2026" and "from the fourth quarter and beyond," and expecting discontinuation rates to settle at 25–30%. One quarter is not a trend, and Q1-to-Q2 comparisons in specialty pharma are distorted by January benefit resets and re-verification; a 100-person commercial organization also tells you little about the ceiling under a 2,500-person company with an overlapping endocrinology field force. Q3 is the confirming or disconfirming data point.

**Pipeline value is concentrated in H2 2027.** Osavampator Phase 3 in MDD and the first direclidine Phase 3 in schizophrenia both read out in the second half of 2027, with the second direclidine study in 2028. FY2026 GAAP R&D guidance of $1,275–1,325M compares with $1,015.7M actual in FY2025, itself +38.9% over FY2024. Late-stage R&D spend nearly doubled in H1 ($137.3M vs $74.1M). The spending is happening now; the value inflection is eighteen months out and binary.

**Estimate momentum is the counterweight.** Current-quarter consensus fell 15.7% in 30 days and 21.6% in 90 days; FY2027 fell 3.4% in 30 days and 7.2% in 90 days. FY2026 is nearly unchanged (−0.1% / −2.3%). The pattern says analysts accepted the revenue raise and cut H2 and FY2027 earnings power for the higher SG&A base and a normalizing tax rate. Revenue growth is intact and accelerating; per-share earnings growth is being consumed. Growth Prospects at 6.9 reflects both halves.

On consensus non-GAAP, the shares trade at a meaningful discount to the peer median (ASND 23.5x, EXEL 17.7x; BBIO, AXSM and ARWR are loss-making, so the median is thin). PEG below 0.7 and 4.1x EV/sales on a business growing revenue ~36% look inexpensive.

Three adjustments temper that. First, non-GAAP excludes ~$285M of FY2026 stock compensation (5.7% of revenue) and, going forward, ~$140M/year of VYKAT XR amortization plus the $168M inventory step-up releasing over four to six years. Those non-cash charges represent the amortization of $2.36B of cash the company actually paid, so permanently excluding them makes non-GAAP EPS a poor measure of the acquisition's return on invested capital. On GAAP, H1 doubled implies ~24.8x, and that GAAP figure is itself flattered by a $28.6M divestiture gain, $26.5M of equity-investment marks, and a 16.3% H1 tax rate against 33.8% a year earlier. A normalized tax rate materially above 16% should be assumed, even allowing that R&D credits and excess stock-compensation benefits are partly recurring.

Second, the discount to peers is not unexplained. It compensates for an unreserved DOJ investigation into the marketing of 72% of product sales, an open 2027 payer negotiation on the same franchise, an MFN/IRA policy regime whose litigation escape route effectively closed when the Supreme Court declined the manufacturers' challenges in May 2026, and a $2.24B single-asset intangible whose recoverability rests on one plateaued rare-disease product. A cheap multiple accompanied by falling forward estimates and unquantifiable legal exposure is the configuration that most often produces a value trap rather than a re-rating.

Third, consensus targets of $190–249 sit 16–52% above spot with a stable rating distribution (4 strong buy, 19 buy, 4 hold). At 13.4x forward those targets are not obviously extrapolative, and they would normally be treated as lagging indicators. But the same analysts cut next-year EPS 7.2% over 90 days while leaving targets in place, so the target set is stale in a direction that flatters the upside. I weight it as corroboration of direction, not as evidence of magnitude.

**Scenario framework:**

- **Base $185** — 15.2x FY2027 consensus $12.17. Assumes INGREZZA delivers guidance and holds net price broadly flat through 2027 contracting, CRENESSITY compounds toward $780M in FY2026 and beyond $1B in FY2027, VYKAT XR resumes sequential growth from Q4, DOJ resolves without a corporate integrity agreement, and the tax rate normalizes into the low-20s. +13.2% from $163.41.
- **Bull $245** — 20x FY2027 $12.17 plus early credit for the 2027 pipeline. Requires VYKAT XR re-accelerating under Neurocrine's field force, 2027 INGREZZA access concluding without a net-price step-down, and CRENESSITY penetration moving from 15% of diagnosed patients toward 25%+. +49.9%.
- **Bear $125** — roughly 11x a reduced ~$11 FY2027 figure. Requires a 2027 INGREZZA net-price reset, VYKAT XR settling near a ~$375M run rate with an intangible impairment against $967M of tangible equity, and a DOJ resolution carrying a corporate integrity agreement that constrains commercial practices. −23.5%.

Probability-weighting at 45/30/25 gives roughly +15%; at 50/20/30 it gives roughly +10%. The expected return is positive, but the spread between +13% base upside and −24% bear downside is not the asymmetry a BUY requires, and the two largest bear drivers are unresolvable from current disclosure.

## Competitive Landscape

**Tardive dyskinesia.** INGREZZA competes with AUSTEDO/AUSTEDO XR alongside off-label generic tetrabenazine and supportive agents. Neurocrine is contesting share on persistence, presenting real-world evidence at AMCP 2026 that adults on INGREZZA capsules showed higher treatment persistence than AUSTEDO XR, and it published the first expert consensus on TD screening in long-term care — a market-development play into an under-diagnosed setting. With an estimated ~800,000 U.S. TD patients and two substitutable branded agents, growth increasingly requires diagnosis expansion while net price is set by PBM and Medicare contracting between the two. Elevated telehealth use is a structural drag on screening, since observing involuntary movements requires a physical exam. Trajectory: volume-positive, price-negative.

**Congenital adrenal hyperplasia.** CRENESSITY faces no competing brand, only 60 years of cheap steroid monotherapy and more than two dozen steroid manufacturers. The barrier is physician inertia and payer willingness to fund a branded adjunct, and the evidence suggests the argument is landing (80% reimbursement, prescriber base nearly tripled, ~35,000 patient-weeks of exposure, 70% of two-year patients on physiological glucocorticoid doses). A Phase 2 in children from 3 months extends the label downward. Trajectory: improving.

**Prader-Willi hyperphagia.** VYKAT XR is the only FDA-approved therapy for hyperphagia in PWS, with no ex-U.S. approval yet. The filings concede competition from development-stage PWS candidates "as well as with therapies approved for other indications that may be used to manage appetite, weight, or other symptoms" — in an era of widely available incretin agonists, off-label appetite management is an unquantified vector in a ~10,000-patient indication. Certain VYKAT XR supply arrangements are sole- or limited-source. Trajectory: flat, pending Q3.

**Schizophrenia.** Bristol Myers' COBENFY is approved and on market. Neurocrine's stated differentiation is that its selective M4 approach does not require an add-back agent to blunt peripheral cholinergic effects, in contrast with the xanomeline-trospium architecture. Whether M4 selectivity delivers comparable efficacy without the add-back is what the H2 2027 direclidine Phase 3 answers. Hedging across four muscarinic programs is a rational response to mechanism risk but concentrates dependence on one licensor, Nxera, with up to $2.48B of contingent milestones and layered royalties.

**Channel position is deteriorating even as product mix diversifies.** Top three customers moved from 85% of gross product sales in H1 2025 to 88% in H1 2026, with Customer C rising from 15% to 28%; four customers exceed 90%. CRENESSITY and VYKAT XR are each distributed by a single specialty pharmacy. Product concentration improved (INGREZZA fell from 92.9% to 77.8% of net product sales) while channel concentration worsened. Single-pharmacy distribution is industry norm for ultra-rare products and delivers real titration and persistence-management benefits, so this is a design choice rather than a defect, but it raises single-point-of-failure and compliance exposure, and the filings now say so explicitly.

## Risk Assessment

**1. DOJ civil investigative demand (highest-impact, entirely unquantified).** Received August 2025, requesting documents related to the sales and marketing of INGREZZA. No reserve, no estimated range, no timeline. The surrounding risk factor volunteers that patient assistance programs have drawn industry-wide enforcement actions and settlements, that Neurocrine operates such a program, and that speaker programs are among the arrangements structured for Anti-Kickback compliance. Enumerated remedies include civil and criminal penalties, disgorgement, exclusion from federal programs and a corporate integrity agreement. A CID is investigative, frequently closes without action, and nothing states Neurocrine has been accused of wrongdoing, but the exposure sits on the asset generating ~72% of product sales, and a CIA would constrain commercial practices for years.

**2. INGREZZA net price and IRA/MFN exposure.** FY2025 established that gross-to-net erosion is already occurring. 2027 access discussions are open. The Q2 filing's policy paragraph is unusually dense: April 2025 CMMI executive order, May 2025 MFN executive order, December 2025 CMS proposed rules creating CMMI demonstrations for MFN-level pricing in Parts D and B, the January 2026 "Great Healthcare Plan" seeking congressional codification, OBBBA Medicaid contraction, and international reference pricing — against a company that states a majority of revenue comes from federal healthcare payors. **A first-order input is unverified in this work: INGREZZA's initial price applicability year for Medicare negotiation, when its small-biotech exception lapses, and whether AUSTEDO's negotiated maximum fair price takes effect in 2027.** That gap is a reason for a lower risk score, not a reason to assume a benign outcome.

**3. Soleno purchase-price risk.** $2,829.9M gross ($2,358.5M net of acquired cash) for a ~$375M annualized run rate is ~7.5x gross / ~6.3x net of sales, on an asset that was flat sequentially. Against ~$140M/year of amortization and $85M of incremental non-GAAP SG&A, the GAAP economics are marginal for years even before the forgone interest income on $2.36B. The 24.0% discount rate in the purchase-price allocation is a disclosed valuation input; high rates are common in rare-disease PPAs and I draw no inference about management's intent from it. What is separately established is the flat sequential revenue. If VYKAT XR stabilizes rather than growing, an impairment is the mechanical consequence against $967M of tangible equity. Impairment would not affect cash flow or covenant compliance.

**4. VYKAT XR safety and assumed litigation.** A new risk factor discloses that serious adverse events in patients taking VYKAT XR have been reported to FDA's adverse event reporting system since commercialization, noting such reports do not establish causation. Separately, Neurocrine assumed the March 6, 2026 putative securities class action against Soleno (N.D. Cal., class period March 26–November 4, 2025; consolidated complaint due August 28, 2026, motion to dismiss October 30, 2026). The complaint's substantive allegations are not described in the 10-Q and were not read.

**5. Generic exposure.** March 2025 Paragraph IV notice from Zydus on a generic INGREZZA SPRINKLE; suits filed April 2025 in D. Del. Active and unresolved. Whether any prior settled generic-entry date covers all formulations and all filers is not confirmed from the reviewed material.

**6. Balance-sheet and structural.** Cash fell from $2,543.4M to $481.7M; current ratio 1.87x; first all-asset lien in the company's recent history with undisclosed covenant thresholds. With $266.3M of buyback authorization and ~$482M of cash, capacity for another large cash acquisition without equity or term debt is limited.

**7. Manufacturing and supply.** No owned manufacturing; third-party manufacturers in Europe; some suppliers and R&D collaborators in China. The administration has indicated it may impose a 100% tariff on branded or patented pharmaceutical products absent U.S. manufacturing investment, and the filings state the ability to pass increased costs to customers is limited. The company also discloses it has experienced a ransomware attack and business email compromise, with no incident to date deemed material.

**8. 2027 pipeline concentration.** A modified risk factor names osavampator and direclidine explicitly: delays, failures or safety concerns in those Phase 3 programs could materially affect the pipeline and the share price.

The signal is consistent with the fundamental read: near-term quiet, hedged tail risk around dated catalysts.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($245, +50%)

Neurocrine is a three-product commercial company trading at 13.4x next-year consensus against a 23.5x peer median, with revenue growing ~36% in 2026 and roughly $400–500M of underlying annual cash generation. ## Conclusion

The score on this name has declined from 7.0 in early June to 6.2 in late July, and this review takes it to 6.0. The decline is not a response to the 7.5% price decline since the last review — a lower price on unchanged fundamentals improves risk/reward. It reflects specific new information from the Q2 filing and print: VYKAT XR revenue was roughly flat sequentially in the quarter Neurocrine closed a $2.83B acquisition for it; the earnings beat rested materially on an 11.0% tax rate and $152.3M of add-backs; FY2026 non-GAAP SG&A guidance rose $85M so that SG&A now exceeds R&D guidance; and consensus cut current-quarter EPS 15.7% in 30 days and FY2027 7.2% in 90 days. The dimension that improved in prior reviews — growth — remains genuinely strong on the revenue line, with CRENESSITY compounding 19.7% sequentially and total revenue tracking toward roughly $3.9B against $2,860.5M in FY2025. The divergence between accelerating revenue and falling per-share earnings estimates is the crux of the current setup.

The valuation discount to peers is real (13.4x FY2027 versus a 23.5x median, PEG ~0.68, 4.1x EV/sales) and the consensus target range of $190–249 sits well above spot. But the discount is doing identifiable work, and three of the items it prices cannot be resolved from present disclosure: the scope and outcome of the DOJ investigation into the marketing of 72% of product sales, INGREZZA's Medicare negotiation eligibility timing, and whether VYKAT XR's plateau was a one-quarter artifact. Management's capital allocation compounds the uncertainty: $2.36B of net cash converted into a single rare-disease intangible, a first all-asset lien, buybacks paused after April, incentive metrics keyed to the two measures an acquisition mechanically improves, and no open-market insider buying while executives monetized deep-in-the-money options into the July highs.

**HOLD.** Existing owners have a modestly positive 12-month expected return with a defined checkpoint at the Q3 print. Prospective buyers have no urgent entry signal at $163.41; the low $140s would put the shares near 12x FY2027 consensus and restore the asymmetry, and Q3 confirmation of VYKAT XR re-acceleration alongside continued CRENESSITY momentum would justify adding without waiting for a lower price. With near-term implied volatility at the 6th percentile and cheap to realized, patient buyers can be paid to wait via put sales at the $140–145 strikes rather than paying up here.
