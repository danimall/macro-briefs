**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Lindsay Corporation is a global leader in center pivot irrigation systems (Zimmatic brand) and road infrastructure safety products (Road Zipper System). The company is experiencing a confirmed cyclical downturn in its core North America irrigation business, compounded by the wind-down of a large MENA project that boosted FY2025 revenue by approximately $88M. The most recent quarter (Q2 FY2026, reported April 2) missed estimates by 32%, with EPS of $1.15 vs. $1.70 consensus, and the stock has declined ~20% over the past year.

The investment case rests on two competing dynamics: a balance sheet that is among the strongest in agricultural machinery (net cash of $136M, Z-Score 3.63) providing downside protection and buyback capacity, versus a deteriorating near-term earnings trajectory with current-year estimates cut 20.6% over 90 days. The specific recovery thesis centers on three dated catalysts: Brazil's expected rate reduction in July 2026 crop plan financing, Project Fortify galvanizing facility completion in early 2027, and normalized North America demand benefiting from restored 100% bonus depreciation. These create a credible path to earnings recovery over 12-18 months, but the timing remains uncertain and the MENA revenue cliff has not yet been fully replaced.

## Company Overview

Lindsay operates in two segments: Irrigation (84% of FY2025 revenue, $568M) and Infrastructure (16%, $108M). The irrigation business sells center pivot systems through 200+ independent U.S. dealers and an international network, with manufacturing facilities in the U.S., Brazil, Turkey, France, China, and South Africa. International irrigation represented 52% of segment revenue in FY2025, up from 41% in FY2024, reflecting both MENA project contributions and deliberate geographic diversification.

The infrastructure segment produces the proprietary Road Zipper System (moveable concrete barriers with limited direct competition), crash cushions, and road safety products. This segment benefits from IIJA funding ($110B for roads/bridges through September 2026) but is inherently lumpy due to large project timing.

## Financial Analysis

**Balance Sheet Strength (Score: 7.5/10):** Lindsay's financial position is exceptional for a cyclical industrial company. Cash of $251M against total debt of $115M (Senior Notes at 3.82% fixed, due 2030) produces a net cash position of $136M. Current ratio of 3.71x and an undrawn $50M revolver extended to August 2030 provide substantial liquidity runway. The Altman Z-Score of 3.63 places the company firmly in the "safe zone." Free cash flow of $90M in FY2025 (13.3% FCF margin) demonstrates the earnings quality of the business model even at moderate revenue levels.

**Earnings Trajectory (Deteriorating):** The Q2 FY2026 miss was significant. Revenue of $157.7M declined 16% YoY, with infrastructure particularly weak (-58% due to Road Zipper project timing). Operating margin compressed to 8.3% from 17.2% in the year-ago period. Full-year FY2026 estimates have been cut from $6.40 to $5.08 over 90 days (-20.6%). The current quarter estimate is $1.21, already implying continued weakness.

**Capital Allocation:** Management accelerated buybacks to $25M in Q2 FY2026 alone (vs. $11.5M for all of FY2025), signaling confidence at current levels but also potentially depleting the cash buffer that provides downside comfort. Capex of $50-55M annually for Project Fortify represents the largest investment cycle in company history.

## Growth Analysis

Near-term growth is impaired on multiple fronts:

1. **North America irrigation demand declining:** Revenue fell 8% in Q2 FY2026 on weak farmer sentiment, low crop commodity prices, and capital purchase deferrals. Management characterized this as a "trough" environment without clear visibility on recovery timing.

2. **MENA project wind-down:** The single customer representing 13% of FY2025 revenue is completing deliveries through Q3-Q4 FY2026. Order backlog fell 39% to $110.7M as of August 2025. No replacement project of similar scale has been announced, though management expressed optimism about MENA as "a strong source of growth."

3. **Brazil constrained:** High domestic interest rates (Selic at ~12.5%) are causing customers to defer purchases ahead of the July 2026 crop plan, which is expected to include lower financing rates.

4. **Infrastructure lumpiness:** The -58% Q2 infrastructure decline reflects Road Zipper project timing rather than structural demand erosion, but creates difficult near-term comparisons.

**Recovery catalysts with specific timelines:**
- July 2026: Brazil crop plan with expected lower financing rates → release of deferred demand
- Early 2027: Project Fortify galvanizing facility startup → 200-300bp margin improvement potential at normalized volumes
- Ongoing: 100% bonus depreciation restoration and EPA biofuel volume increases (8-10% for 2026-2027) support medium-term demand

Current-year EPS estimate of $5.08 and next-year of $6.34 imply a recovery trajectory, but the 90-day revision trend is negative (-20.6% current year, -6.7% next year) and has not yet stabilized.

## Valuation Assessment

- 21.9x current-year consensus ($5.08 — trough earnings)
- 17.5x next-year consensus ($6.34 — recovery scenario)

The valuation is not obviously cheap relative to the declining earnings trajectory. At 17.5x next-year earnings, the stock is pricing in modest recovery but not a deep discount to fair value. The peer comparison (AGCO at 11x, CNH at 34x, TTC at 27.4x) shows wide dispersion in the group, with Lindsay sitting roughly in the middle on a growth-adjusted basis.

The net cash position ($136M, representing ~12% of market cap) provides a meaningful valuation floor. Enterprise value of ~$1.02B against $90M normalized FCF produces an ~11x EV/FCF multiple — reasonable but not compelling given the earnings decline in progress.

Analyst consensus target of $124 (median) implies 11.5% upside from current levels, with a range of $113-$135. The stock at $111 is already below the low analyst target, which could signal either analyst lag or that the recent earnings miss has not been fully incorporated into targets.

## Competitive Landscape

Lindsay operates in a concentrated U.S. center pivot market with three other major manufacturers (Valmont/Valley, Reinke, T-L Industries). The company holds a "substantial" share but faces pricing pressure in downturns as "smaller privately held family businesses" increase competitive intensity. Management has maintained price discipline (positive average selling prices despite volume declines) but acknowledged costs exceeding pricing gains are compressing margins.

The Road Zipper System has limited direct competition, proprietary technology, and high switching costs for highway authorities. However, this business is small (16% of revenue) and lumpy.

FieldNET/FieldWise technology solutions provide differentiation and recurring revenue through subscription-based irrigation management, though the company does not separately disclose technology revenue, making it difficult to assess penetration rates or growth.

International expansion (52% of irrigation revenue in FY2025) diversifies geographic risk but introduces new concentrations — as demonstrated by the single MENA customer reaching 13% of consolidated revenue.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**
1. **Agricultural downturn duration:** If North America demand remains depressed through FY2027, the earnings recovery to $6.34 will not materialize on consensus timeline. Current crop economics do not support large capital purchases.
2. **MENA revenue cliff:** Without a replacement project, FY2027 revenue faces a ~$60-80M headwind from MENA project completion. Geopolitical conditions in the region compound this risk.
3. **Project Fortify operating leverage requires volume:** CEO explicitly stated efficiency gains "are going to be eaten up by incremental depreciation" at current volumes. This investment becomes a margin drag rather than a margin enhancer in a prolonged trough.
4. **Estimate revision momentum is negative:** 90-day revisions have not stabilized.

**Options Market Signal:** ATM IV of 34.0% vs. historical volatility of 28.6% shows the options market pricing elevated uncertainty (5.4pp rich). Put/call skew is balanced (-0.3%), suggesting no directional consensus among options participants. The flat term structure (34.0% near-term vs. 33.5% 6-month) indicates uncertainty is evenly distributed rather than concentrated around a specific event. With only 63 contracts across 3 expirations, liquidity is extremely thin — institutional options positioning is essentially absent.

**Short interest at 7.0%** with 2.7 days to cover is moderate — enough to confirm some informed skepticism but not crowded enough to create squeeze risk or suggest the bear case is fully priced.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($130-140, 17-26% upside):**
- Agricultural cycle recovers in H2 FY2027 driven by improved crop economics and restored bonus depreciation
- Brazil crop plan (July 2026) unlocks deferred demand
- Project Fortify galvanizing facility (early 2027) delivers 200-300bp operating margin improvement
- New MENA project wins replace current contract
- Normalized EPS of $7-8 at 18-20x = $126-160

**Bear Case ($85-95, 15-24% downside):**
- North America trough deepens or extends through 2027
- MENA customer reduces remaining orders amid regional conflict
- No replacement project materializes; backlog continues declining
- Project Fortify costs compress margins without offsetting volume
- Trough EPS of $4-4.50 at 20-22x = $80-99

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
1. North America irrigation orders show sequential improvement for 2 consecutive quarters
2. Announcement of a new MENA or international project exceeding $50M total value
3. Stock declines below $95 (creating >15x next-year P/E on recovery estimates with net cash representing >15% of market cap)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Next quarter estimate revised below $1.00, indicating further deterioration beyond current consensus
2. Management draws on revolver or reduces buyback pace, signaling cash preservation mode
3. MENA customer cancels or materially reduces remaining deliveries

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings report (expected early July 2026 for Q3 FY2026), which will reveal spring order trends and MENA delivery progress. Brazil crop plan announcement (July 2026) is the second key date.

## Conclusion

Lindsay Corporation is a well-managed, conservatively financed company navigating a cyclical downturn in its primary market. The balance sheet provides genuine downside protection and the capacity to act opportunistically (buybacks, potential acquisitions). However, the growth trajectory is clearly negative in the near term — estimates are falling, the MENA project is winding down, and North America demand shows no signs of recovery.

The growth thesis for holding is dated: Brazil rate relief (July 2026), Project Fortify completion (early 2027), and agricultural cycle normalization (H2 FY2027) create a credible 12-18 month recovery path. At 17.5x next-year earnings with a net cash balance sheet, the stock is not expensive on recovery estimates but is not cheap enough to compensate for the execution risk and timing uncertainty. HOLD with growth thesis intact but requiring catalyst confirmation over the next 2-3 quarters.
