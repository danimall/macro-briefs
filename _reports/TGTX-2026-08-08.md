## Executive Summary

TG Therapeutics is a single-product commercial biotech built around BRIUMVI (ublituximab-xiiy), a glycoengineered anti-CD20 antibody for relapsing multiple sclerosis. The commercial execution has been strong: Q2 2026 total revenue of $240.3M grew 70.3% year over year, U.S. BRIUMVI revenue of $227.7M grew 16.9% sequentially, and management raised FY2026 guidance to $890-905M U.S. / ~$950M global while reiterating a ~$1B annualized U.S. exit run rate. A 399-person organization producing a ~$950M revenue year against Roche's Ocrevus is a real commercial accomplishment.

The problem is what happens between the revenue line and the cash line. Accounts receivable have gone from ~$129M (Dec 2024) to $305.6M (Dec 2025) to $401.6M (June 2026) — 152 days of sales outstanding, versus a 40-70 day norm for specialty pharma selling to a handful of distributors. Management's own MD&A attributes this to "extended payment terms." FY2025 delivered $616.3M of revenue, $107.4M of pretax income, and roughly negative $25M of operating cash flow, ending the year with only $199.5M of liquidity. In March 2026 the company upsized its term loan from $250M to $750M with Blue Owl-managed funds at SOFR + 4.75%, taking annualized interest expense to roughly $66M against Q2 operating income of $21.7M — 1.31x coverage. Reported FY2025 net income of $447.2M is dominated by a $339.8M non-cash tax benefit from releasing a $371.7M valuation allowance; the operating result was a 17.4% pretax margin, not a 73% net margin.

At $49.76 the diluted equity value is $7.83B (157.28M diluted shares) and enterprise value approximately $8.0B — 8.4x guided 2026 revenue and 16.1x the $3.10 next-year consensus EPS, which itself is flattered by a deferred-tax-asset shield that produced a 9.9% H1 effective rate. Consensus has missed four consecutive quarters, most recently by 85%, and current-year estimates have been cut 42.8% in 90 days even as next-year estimates rose 17.4%. The subcutaneous Phase 3 readout at year-end 2026 or Q1 2027 is the dominant valuation variable and carries a PK exposure endpoint rather than a clinical one, which materially improves its odds. Base case $56 (+12.5%), bull $82 (+65%), bear $30 (-40%). That distribution supports maintaining exposure without adding capital.

## Company Overview

BRIUMVI was approved by FDA in December 2022 for relapsing forms of MS and launched in January 2023. Its differentiation is mechanical: glycoengineering removes certain sugar residues from the antibody, enabling efficient B-cell depletion at low doses, which translates commercially into a one-hour infusion every 24 weeks after initiation. Durability data support the franchise: six-year ULTIMATE I & II extension data presented at ECTRIMS 2025 showed 89.9% of patients free from 24-week confirmed disability progression with a year-six annualized relapse rate of 0.012, and five-year data was published in *JAMA Neurology* in February 2026.

The business model is asset-light and externally sourced. The company owns no manufacturing (Samsung Biologics primary, FUJIFILM Diosynth being qualified as secondary). Ublituximab is licensed in from LFB Biotechnologies, with ~$31.0M of milestones paid to date and a high-single-digit royalty on net sales. Ex-U.S., Neuraxpharm is the licensee: $140M upfront received, up to $492.5M of further milestones, tiered double-digit royalties to 30%, and it also purchases product from TGTX ($8.1M of transfer sales in Q2). azer-cel, the allogeneic CD19 CAR-T in progressive MS, is a field-limited license from Precision BioSciences; the ARCUS platform and underlying IP belong to Precision.

Pipeline items that matter to the equity, in order:
1. **Subcutaneous ublituximab** — Phase 3 fully enrolled, topline YE2026/Q1 2027. Primary endpoint is non-inferior AUC exposure at week 24 versus IV BRIUMVI. Phase 1 showed >60% mean bioavailability relative to IV, with modeling supporting quarterly dosing.
2. **ENHANCE Phase 3** — met its primary endpoint (May 2026), showing bioequivalent exposure for a single consolidated 600mg Day 1 infusion versus the approved two-step initiation, removing the Day 15 visit.
3. **BRIUMVI in myasthenia gravis** — positive SC Phase 1; a "potentially registration-directed" randomized Phase 2 initiated for IV BRIUMVI as maintenance after efgartigimod induction.
4. **BRIUMVI in treatment-resistant schizophrenia** — Phase 2 initiated. Pure optionality; the near-term effect is R&D cash consumption.

U.S. BRIUMVI was 94.7% of Q2 total revenue. This is a one-product, one-geography company with pipeline extensions of that product.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue and the margin compression

| Net income | $7.781M | $28.187M | $27.558M | $33.247M |
| Diluted EPS | $0.05 | $0.17 | $0.17 | $0.20 |

Operating margin fell from 24.7% to 9.0% on 70% revenue growth — 15.7 points of compression. Three mechanisms, with different implications:

**Subcutaneous manufacturing spend inside R&D.** The company disclosed $54.6M of Q2 (and $58.8M of H1) expense related to subcutaneous manufacturing and secondary-manufacturer qualification. Excluding it, Q2 R&D was $40.7M versus $31.8M — 28% growth, not 200%. Adding it back to Q2 pretax income of $10.2M gives ~$64.8M versus $30.9M a year earlier. Pre-approval biologic production must generally be expensed; if SC is approved, comparable production shifts into inventory and COGS. This is a real cash outflow, is guided to continue (roughly $41M more in H2 by the implied guidance math), and is entirely contingent on SC approval for any payoff. The adjusted figure is a construct, not a company non-GAAP measure, and should not be treated as normalized earnings power.

**Cost of revenue outgrowing revenue.** 17.5% of product revenue in Q2 2026 versus 13.6% a year earlier. Cost of revenue consists primarily of third-party royalties plus product cost, so the likely contributors are tiered royalty escalation as volume grows and margin dilution from low-margin transfer sales to Neuraxpharm. The precise royalty split within cost of revenue was not verified.

**Interest expense.** $16.572M versus $6.716M, following the March refinancing. Annualized at ~$66.3M, an all-in ~8.8% cost on $750M. Q2 coverage on operating income is 1.31x; H1 coverage is 2.33x.

Stock-based compensation of $47.8M in H1 equals 10.7% of revenue, annualizing near $96M. That is real dilution and a reason GAAP and adjusted presentations diverge sharply here.

### The FY2025 net income figure is not operating performance

FY2025 pretax income was $107.4M. Reported net income of $447.2M reflects a ~$339.8M net tax benefit from releasing a $371.7M valuation allowance in Q3 2025 ($107.4 + $339.8 = $447.2). The release is a normal, audited consequence of achieving sustained profitability and is economically real — it shields cash taxes, visible in the 9.9% H1 2026 effective rate ($3.039M on $30.597M pretax). But it inflates every net-income-based ratio for trailing FY2025. The defensible FY2025 profitability figure is a 17.4% pretax margin. It also means next-year consensus EPS of $3.10 carries a low-tax tailwind that reverses once the ~$343M deferred tax asset is consumed; on a normalized ~22% rate that figure would be closer to $2.55.

### Cash conversion is the central issue

Receivables: ~$129M (Dec 2024) → $305.6M (Dec 2025) → $401.6M (June 2026). DSO at June 30 is 152 days on total revenue (155 on product revenue). At December 31, 2025 receivables equaled 49.6% of full-year revenue. Management attributes this to extended payment terms. Corroborating evidence that FY2025 cash generation was weak: year-end cash and investments of only $199.5M after $616.3M of revenue and $100M of buybacks, followed by a $500M incremental debt raise in March.

H1 2026 continued the pattern: receivables +$96.0M and inventory +$82.2M ($141.3M → $223.5M) absorbed roughly $178M, against $27.6M of net income and $47.8M of stock compensation. A crude bridge implies materially negative H1 operating cash flow, plausibly in the negative $40M to negative $100M range once growing payables and gross-to-net rebate accruals are netted. The exact operating cash flow lines for FY2025 and H1 2026 were not directly confirmed against the filed statements; the direction is well corroborated, the magnitude is not.

Three readings coexist and I cannot adjudicate between them from disclosure. Extended terms are a legitimate competitive lever in buy-and-bill distribution — they lower infusion-site working capital and can win site-of-care preference against a larger incumbent, which means TGTX is buying share with its balance sheet and could dial it back. Alternatively, extended terms can pull demand forward, in which case a normalization reverses working capital and decelerates reported growth independent of prescription trends; no channel-inventory disclosure exists to rule this out. Mechanically, receivables outgrow revenue in any steep ramp, and pharma gross-to-net accruals sit in liabilities rather than netted against AR, which structurally inflates the ratio. What is established: reported profitability has not converted to operating cash, and the gap sits with four counterparties.

### Balance sheet

At June 30, 2026: cash and investments $612.253M against $750M of principal — net debt of ~$138M, maturity 2031, no near-term wall. Total equity fell from $648.020M to $604.083M despite $27.558M of net income; the bridge ($648.020 + $27.558 + $47.825 − $604.083) implies ~$119.3M of equity reduction from treasury purchases plus withholding on vesting.

Inventory of $223.5M includes ~$75.5M classified non-current — a direct statement that part of it will not be consumed within twelve months. Against Q2 cost of revenue of $41.2M (much of which is royalty rather than product cost), that is many quarters of supply. Plausible explanations are SC pre-build, FUJIFILM qualification batches, and tariff/supply-chain mitigation given a Korea-based primary CMO and tariffs named in the company's own risk language. Large biologic inventories carry write-down risk if a formulation transition changes mix.

The financial maintenance covenant package on the Blue Owl facility — liquidity or revenue tests, amortization, restricted-payment permissions — was not verified. That is the most consequential open item for downside scenarios and for whether the residual $200M buyback authorization and stated business-development ambitions are contractually available.

**Financial health score: 4.8.** GAAP profitable with modest net debt and no near-term maturity, offset by negative operating cash conversion, 152-day DSO, 1.31x quarterly interest coverage on floating-rate debt, an unusually large inventory position, and unverified covenants.

## Growth Analysis

The growth is real and broad enough to survive skepticism about accounting. Q2 U.S. BRIUMVI of $227.7M implies Q1 of $194.8M ($422.5M H1 less Q2), so sequential growth was 16.9%. FY2026 guidance of $890-905M U.S. was raised from $885-900M and global from ~$925M to ~$950M.

The near-term shape is the caution. Implied H2 U.S. revenue is $467.5-482.5M. If the CEO's "approximately $1 billion annualized U.S. exit run rate" implies Q4 near $250M, Q3 lands at $217.5-232.5M — midpoint ~$225M versus Q2 actual of $227.7M, flat to slightly down after a 16.9% sequential gain. That translation is an assumption, not company guidance, and 24-week dosing intervals plus summer seasonality genuinely produce lumpiness. But it is a coherent explanation for why a quarter that beat on revenue and raised guidance still fell 10-11%.

Consensus revisions carry the same split. Current-quarter estimates are down 46.5% in 30 days and 45.5% in 90; current-year estimates are down 42.8% to $0.94; next-year estimates are up 17.4% to $3.10 on six analysts. The strong estimate-revision buy signal (>+30% over 90 days combined with accelerating revenue) is **not** present here in aggregate — near-term estimates are collapsing while the out-year rises, which is what happens when analysts push the SC manufacturing charge and interest step-up through 2026 and model the payoff in 2027. That is a legitimate structure, but it means the cheap-looking 16.1x next-year multiple depends on a consensus that has been wrong on this company four quarters in a row.

Pipeline growth vectors, ranked by expected value: SC ublituximab (quarterly dosing, TAM expansion, fully enrolled, PK endpoint); ENHANCE-enabled single-visit initiation (approved-path label improvement, reduces conversion friction at infusion centers); myasthenia gravis Phase 2 (registration-directed, new indication); azer-cel in progressive MS (Phase 1 data 2H 2026, licensed asset); schizophrenia Phase 2 (low probability).

**Growth prospects score: 7.0**, down modestly from prior work. Growth remains high but the implied Q3 flattening and the 43% current-year estimate cut are new information, and the label-expansion path now runs through a binary readout rather than in-market execution alone.

## Valuation Assessment

**Share count matters here.** Q2 weighted-average basic shares were 141,770,283 and diluted 157,281,850. The commonly quoted ~$7.08B market cap is built on the basic count. On diluted shares at $49.76, equity value is $7,826M; enterprise value is $7,826M + $750M − $612.3M = approximately **$7.96B**. The 15.5M basic-to-diluted gap (11.0% of basic) is in-the-money equity awards already reflected in diluted EPS, not additional contingent dilution to layer on.

Resulting multiples:
- EV / FY2026 guided revenue ($950M) = **8.4x**
- EV / estimated 2027 revenue (~$1.20-1.28B) = **6.2-6.6x**
- P/E on next-year consensus $3.10 = **16.1x**; on a normalized ~22% tax rate (~$2.55) = **19.5x**
- P/E on current-year consensus $0.94 = 52.9x — not decision-useful, since 2026 absorbs $100M of SC manufacturing spend and a $9.2M debt extinguishment loss

Peer comparison is unhelpful in the conventional sense: the five closest-market-cap biotech peers (SYRE, IMVT, PCVX, PRAX, IBRX) all carry negative P/Es because none is profitable. TGTX is the only one in the cohort with a ~$950M revenue franchise and GAAP profitability, which is a genuine quality premium. The relevant comparison set is commercial-stage single-product specialty biotech, where 6-8x forward revenue is a fair range for a 25-35% grower with a decade of exclusivity, and TGTX sits at the upper half of that range with negative free cash flow.

The bull argument on valuation is straightforward: 16x next-year earnings for a company growing revenue 30%+ with an approved differentiated product, a 2034 BPCIA shield, and a label-expansion catalyst is not demanding. Sell-side targets sit at $65 low and $86 high, mean $76.67 — 31% to 73% above spot, with sentiment characterized as improving and a 1 strong-buy / 6 buy / 1 strong-sell distribution.

The argument against paying up: at 8.4x guided current-year revenue the market is already crediting SC approval and indication expansion. Reported earnings are not converting to cash, so the earnings multiple overstates the yield on capital. And the analyst-target-as-floor logic applies to stocks that are cheap relative to *rising* estimates. Here, the near-term estimates that drive the next four quarters are falling hard, and the out-year estimate that makes the stock look cheap has an 85% miss and three other misses standing behind it. I treat the $65-86 range as evidence of a credible bull case rather than as an independent floor.

**Valuation score: 5.5.** There is a plausible case that the market is under-crediting 2027 earnings power, but the cheapness is contingent on a binary readout, a tax shield that expires, and cash conversion that has not yet occurred. That is not the margin of safety that earns a high valuation score.

## Competitive Landscape

| Agent | Owner | Route | Position vs BRIUMVI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ocrelizumab (IV) | Roche | IV | The only other approved IV anti-CD20; direct head-to-head |
| Ocrelizumab (SC) | Roche | HCP-administered SC | Competes with IV BRIUMVI on convenience today |
| Ofatumumab | Novartis | Patient self-administered SC, monthly | Named in the 10-K as direct competition for a future SC ublituximab |
| BTK inhibitors | Multiple | Oral | 10-K: could "alter treatment paradigms, including treatment sequencing" |
| CD40L agents | Multiple | — | Early stage |

The competitive logic is a convenience-and-value ladder against a much larger incumbent. Low-dose depletion permits a one-hour infusion every 24 weeks; ENHANCE removes the Day 15 initiation visit; a quarterly SC formulation would sit between Roche's HCP-administered SC and Novartis's monthly self-injection. Four home administrations a year versus twelve is a defensible claim, and 70% revenue growth against a multi-billion-dollar incumbent is evidence the differentiation is being converted into share.

Two structural qualifications. First, convenience differentiation is replicable by better-capitalized competitors, and the 10-K concedes competitors have "significantly greater capital resources." Novartis's ofatumumab already occupies the self-administered SC niche. Second, the class-level risk is price, not efficacy: BPCIA data exclusivity protects BRIUMVI from its own biosimilars to December 2034, but ocrelizumab biosimilars — whenever they arrive, plausibly toward the end of this decade based on the statutory 12-year framework — would reset IV anti-CD20 pricing for the whole class. The earliest ublituximab composition-of-matter family expires in the U.S. in 2029 (patent term extension applied for) and expired in 2025 in Europe and other non-U.S. jurisdictions, with extensions into 2030 in a list of European countries; a later family runs to 2042 but includes two pending applications, and the 10-K is candid that claims are often narrowed before issuance.

**Competitive position score: 6.5.** Trajectory is improving through share gains and label simplification; the moat depends on a convenience advantage that competitors can attack and on patent claims whose long tail is not yet secured.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Subcutaneous Phase 3 (YE2026/Q1 2027).** The largest single driver of the multiple. A PK non-inferiority endpoint on AUC exposure at week 24, in a fully enrolled trial, with >60% Phase 1 bioavailability and modeling supporting quarterly dosing, is a substantially better-odds setup than a clinical endpoint. But the company itself flags the risk that the program fails, or succeeds and is not approved, or is approved and does not expand the addressable market. Roughly $100M of 2026 spend and a meaningful share of the current valuation ride on it.

**2. Cash conversion and receivable quality.** 152-day DSO, $401.6M of receivables, negative operating cash flow at 70% revenue growth. This is the risk least likely to be picked up by standard screens, because revenue, operating income and net income all read fine.

**3. Customer concentration.** Four specialty distributors account for essentially all gross product revenue, with the concentration profile flat year over year. This is structurally normal for buy-and-bill infused products and is not evidence of fragility on its own, but it compounds risk 2, because the $401.6M receivable sits with those four counterparties. Growing Neuraxpharm and royalty revenue does not diversify customer concentration; the direct concentration disclosure has not improved.

**4. Leverage.** $750M floating-rate term loan (SOFR + 4.75%, 1% floor) to 2031, ~$66M annual interest, 1.31x Q2 operating income coverage, covenants unverified. The near-term issue is strategic flexibility rather than solvency: liquidity of $612M plus projected revenue is stated as sufficient under the current plan. Every 100bp move in the SOFR leg adds roughly $7.5M of annual interest on the $750M principal.

**5. Negative carry on undeployed cash.** Roughly $390M of the raise sits uninvested at a ~4.5-point spread below the ~8.8% borrowing cost, roughly $17-20M annually. The constructive read is deliberate dry powder for in-licensing (consistent with a company that sources all assets externally) and non-dilutive capital raised when the stock was near $30. The cautionary read is that leverage was added while operating cash flow was negative, partly to repurchase stock.

**6. Safety label.** Infusion reactions (48% with premedication, 0.6% serious), serious infections (5% vs 3% for teriflunomide, with three infection-related deaths in MS trials), HBV reactivation, PML, hypogammaglobulinemia, and post-marketing clinically significant liver injury. Largely class effects, but a live constraint for a product whose growth depends on first-line and switch prescribing.

**7. Governance and incentives.** CEO FY2025 total compensation of $25.57M against a five-year indexed TSR of 57 versus a peer group at 120 through December 2025 (approximately -43% versus +20%). The company-selected performance measure is revenue, which does not penalize cash-conversion deterioration in a company that has disclosed extending payment terms. I draw no inference about motive — the terms and receivable growth predate the current period, extended terms are a recognized practice in this channel, and compensation committees apply metrics and discretion not visible in the summary disclosure. It is a structural incentive question worth naming. Separately, the CEO transferred ~4.67M shares in November 2025 in transactions coded as bona fide gifts (no cash proceeds; recipient unknown), reducing directly reported holdings by roughly a third to ~9.78M shares — still ~$487M of alignment at the current price. A shared office/services arrangement with Fortress Biotech is disclosed and unverified as to current terms.

**8. IRA.** BRIUMVI is an infused Part B biologic licensed December 2022; under the statutory framework negotiation eligibility for biologics generally arrives around eleven years post-licensure, placing it well into the 2030s. Part B inflation rebates apply sooner. Near-term negotiation risk appears low, though this reading of the statute is not a company statement.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 44.2% sits essentially on top of 44.7% one-year realized volatility (spread -0.4%), at the 8th percentile of the current chain, with balanced skew (-0.2%), a 0.22 put/call volume ratio, and a ±7.1% implied move on the August 21 expiry. The market is pricing no near-term event. Term structure is in contango, with 3-month IV at 56.3% and 6-month at 54.3%, and the September 18 tenor carries a -16.7% skew reading with a 15.0% implied move and a 0.61 put/call volume ratio — the one place in the chain where positioning concentrates. Since July 24, ATM IV fell from 67.1% to 44.2% (post-earnings vol crush) while the put/call OI ratio rose from 0.38 to 0.72 and the price fell 9.3%, so hedging demand increased even as near-term premium cheapened.

Two implications. First, near-term options are not expensive relative to realized volatility, so protection or long-dated call exposure into the SC readout is reasonably priced; the longer-dated 54% IV is where the binary is being expressed. Second, short interest of 29.0% of float (30.7M shares, 10.8 days to cover) with no convertible bond in the capital structure means this is genuine directional short positioning, most plausibly built on the cash-conversion and class-pricing theses. That cuts both ways: it confirms the bear case is widely held, and it means a clean SC exposure result would meet a thin borrow and forced covering. Short interest of this magnitude also makes the name a crowded and potentially volatile short.

**Risk factors score: 4.0.** A binary readout, 100% distributor concentration overlapping a $401.6M receivable, floating-rate leverage with unverified covenants, single-product dependence, and a 29% short position constitute a concentrated, largely unpriced risk stack.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($82)

Subcutaneous ublituximab hits its exposure endpoint (PK endpoints on a well-characterized biologic with >60% Phase 1 bioavailability generally do), is approved, and converts the franchise from an infusion-center product into a quarterly at-home injection — four administrations a year against Novartis's twelve and Roche's HCP-administered SC. ENHANCE removes the Day 15 visit, improving initiation conversion. The $100M of 2026 SC manufacturing expense stops appearing in R&D and reappears in inventory and COGS, and the $223.5M inventory position becomes launch supply rather than a working-capital question. Revenue reaches $1.25-1.30B in 2027 and $1.7-1.9B by 2029 as MG adds an indication. The deferred tax asset shields cash taxes through the ramp. Receivables normalize toward 90-100 days, releasing $120-150M of cash and turning the operating cash line decisively positive, which retires the accounting concern. 29% short interest covers into that. At 7.5x 2028 revenue of ~$1.5B, or 30x $2.75 of 2027 EPS, the stock reaches the low-$80s, consistent with the upper end of sell-side targets.

### Bear Case ($30)

The SC Phase 3 misses on exposure or shows an unacceptable injection-site-reaction or immunogenicity profile. Roughly $100M of 2026 spend is written off, the inventory build partially impairs, and the entire TAM-expansion leg of the thesis disappears. Growth decelerates toward the implied Q3 flattening rather than reaccelerating in Q4, and the market concludes that part of reported revenue was financed by extended terms rather than underlying demand — a conclusion that becomes self-reinforcing if receivables stop growing and reported revenue decelerates simultaneously. Interest of $66M on floating-rate debt, at 1.31x coverage, converts from a nuisance to a constraint; covenants (unverified) limit buybacks and business development. Multiple compresses to 4-5x on ~$1.05B of revenue, giving $4.7-5.3B of enterprise value and roughly $30 per share, a 40% loss from here. The 52-week low of $25.28 is the tail.

### Base Case ($56)

SC succeeds on exposure but approval and launch land in late 2027, so the revenue benefit sits outside the twelve-month window. 2027 revenue reaches $1.20-1.25B with EPS of $2.60-2.80 (below the $3.10 consensus, reflecting the four-quarter miss record and gradual tax normalization). Receivables stabilize near 140-150 days rather than reversing, so operating cash flow turns modestly positive but not impressively. At 6.5x forward revenue or 21x $2.70, the equity is worth roughly $56, about 12.5% above the current price.

Probability-weighting 55% base / 20% bull / 25% bear gives approximately $55, or +10% expected return over twelve months against a 40% downside leg. That is a fair-to-modestly-positive risk-adjusted return, not an asymmetric one.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** Since the July report the stock fell from $54.87 to $49.76 (-9.3%), which by itself improves risk/reward. But Q2 also introduced genuinely new negative information: a fourth consecutive miss (85% below consensus), operating margin compression to 9.0%, interest expense that more than doubled to 1.31x coverage, DSO extending from ~150 to 152 days on a $96M receivable build, current-year estimates cut 42.8%, and implied Q3 revenue roughly flat sequentially. Those are fundamental data points, not price action, and they justify the overall score drifting to 5.6 from 5.8. What prevents a downgrade to REDUCE is equally concrete: revenue grew 70%, guidance was raised, ENHANCE hit, SC Phase 1 confirmed >60% bioavailability, the Phase 3 is fully enrolled on a PK endpoint, next-year estimates rose 17.4%, and the entire sell-side target range ($65-86) sits above spot.

**Base case target: $56** (+12.5%). Derived as 21x $2.70 of 2027 EPS — the $3.10 consensus haircut ~13% for the four-quarter miss record and partial tax normalization — cross-checked at 6.5x estimated 2027 revenue of $1.22B ($7.93B EV, less $138M net debt, over 157.3M diluted shares ≈ $50; the blend of the two methods supports the mid-$50s).

**Bull case target: $82** (+65%). SC approval plus MG progression, 2028 revenue near $1.5B at 7.5x.

**Bear case target: $30** (-40%). SC failure, deceleration to ~$1.05B, 4.5x revenue.

**Timeframe:** twelve months, with the decisive event the SC Phase 3 topline at year-end 2026 or Q1 2027. Intermediate checkpoints are the Q3 2026 print (early November) and azer-cel progressive-MS Phase 1 preliminary data in 2H 2026.

**Upgrade to ACCUMULATE or BUY if:**
- Q3 or Q4 2026 operating cash flow turns positive with DSO falling below 130 days — the single change that would most improve the quality of reported earnings and lift financial health above 6.
- SC Phase 3 meets its non-inferior AUC endpoint with a clean injection-site-reaction and immunogenicity profile, which would remove the dominant binary and validate the $100M manufacturing pre-build.
- Q4 U.S. revenue of $245M or better confirming the ~$1B exit run rate, alongside consensus stabilization (no further cuts to the current-year line).

## Conclusion

TG Therapeutics has built a genuinely differentiated anti-CD20 franchise and is taking share from a far larger incumbent with 399 employees. The clinical pipeline is progressing on schedule, the ENHANCE result is a low-risk commercial improvement, and the subcutaneous Phase 3 carries a PK endpoint that is more likely to succeed than a clinical one. If it succeeds, the quarterly-dosing profile is a defensible position between Roche's HCP-administered SC and Novartis's monthly self-injection, and the out-year earnings power that consensus is modeling at $3.10 becomes credible.

Set against that, the company converts reported profits into cash poorly, and the mechanism is concentrated: $401.6M of receivables at 152 days owed by four distributors, on terms management describes as extended. Operating cash flow was negative in FY2025 despite $616.3M of revenue and, on a working-capital bridge, was likely negative again in H1 2026. The response was $500M of incremental floating-rate debt at ~8.8%, which now consumes interest of roughly $66M against Q2 operating income of $21.7M. Consensus has missed four straight quarters, current-year estimates are down 43% in 90 days, and the implied Q3 revenue shape is flat sequentially. At approximately $8.0B enterprise value — 8.4x guided 2026 revenue — the market is already paying for the subcutaneous outcome.

The probability-weighted return of roughly 10% over twelve months, against a 40% bear leg tied to a single readout and an unresolved cash-conversion question, is a hold. Existing owners should carry the position into the SC data with the exit triggers above monitored quarterly. New capital should wait either for cash conversion to improve or for a price near $40-42.
