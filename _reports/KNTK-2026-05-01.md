## Executive Summary

Kinetik Holdings is a Delaware Basin-focused midstream operator trading at $49.41, up 22% over the trailing period and within 3% of its 52-week high of $51.11. The stock's re-rating has been driven primarily by a reported sale process initiated in February 2026 after Western Midstream Partners (WES) approached the company about a potential merger. This M&A catalyst provides a specific path to further upside, with enterprise values in the $7-8B range implying equity value of $50-55/share depending on deal structure and premium.

The underlying business has material structural concerns: $3.6B in debt against negative stockholders' equity, a dividend payout that exceeded free cash flow in 2025 (requiring asset sale proceeds to fund), EPS estimates declining 30%+ over 90 days, and foreseeable Waha gas spread compression as new Permian pipelines come online in 2027-2028. The 12.7% short interest with 6 days to cover reflects institutional skepticism about either the deal consummating or the standalone valuation.

The recommendation is HOLD based on the M&A catalyst providing a 12-month growth thesis. A deal announcement would likely push shares to $52-55. If no deal materializes by Q3 2026, the standalone valuation at ~19x trailing earnings (with estimates declining) is difficult to sustain given the leverage profile and structural margin headwinds.

## Company Overview

Kinetik Holdings was formed in February 2022 through the merger of Altus Midstream and BCP Raptor (backed by Blackstone and I Squared Capital). The company operates exclusively in the Delaware Basin — the most prolific sub-basin of the Permian — across two segments:

**Midstream Logistics (~64% of Adjusted EBITDA):** 4,200+ miles of gas gathering pipeline, 2.4 Bcf/d cryogenic processing capacity across 8 complexes, 280 miles of crude oil gathering, and 580,000 bbl/d produced water disposal capacity.

**Pipeline Transportation (~38% of Adjusted EBITDA):** Equity interests in major long-haul pipelines including ~55.5% of Permian Highway Pipeline (PHP) and 33% of Shin Oak, plus the wholly-owned Kinetik NGL Pipeline System and Delaware Link Pipeline. The ECCC Pipeline is under construction with expected in-service Q2 2026.

Key customers include Occidental, Mewbourne Oil, APA Corp., EOG Resources, and ConocoPhillips. Blackstone and I Squared Capital retain majority voting control, with I Squared actively selling down its position.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue & EBITDA Trajectory

Revenue grew 19% YoY to $1.76B in 2025, driven by a 41% increase in NGL/condensate volumes and 33% increase in natural gas prices. Adjusted EBITDA grew only 1.7% to $988M — a critical divergence signaling margin compression. Operating expenses jumped 38% ($75M increase), split between acquired asset costs ($54M from Durango + Barilla Draw) and $21M in higher utility costs. Litigation costs tripled from $6M to $20M.

### Balance Sheet

The capital structure is aggressive:
- Total debt: $3.63B
- Stockholders' equity: -$565M
- Cash: $4.0M
- Net Debt / EBITDA: ~3.7x (within management's 3.5-4.0x target)
- Current ratio: 0.69x
- Available liquidity: $1.22B

Variable-rate exposure is $1.77B, with $500M hedged through year-end 2026. A 1% rate increase adds ~$17.7M in annual interest expense. The company refinanced in May 2025, extending maturities to 2028-2030.

### Cash Flow & Capital Allocation Sustainability

The 2025 capital allocation was unsustainable on a standalone basis:
- Operating cash flow: $604M
- Capex: $492M
- Free cash flow: $112M
- Dividends paid: $502M
- Share repurchases: $176M
- Total shareholder returns (dividends + buybacks): $678M
- Shortfall funded by: EPIC sale ($504M) + net debt increase (~$264M)

The annual dividend of ~$3.24/share ($227M total based on ~70M shares/units outstanding) alone exceeds FCF. Management targets annual dividend increases of 3-5% until coverage reaches 1.6x, implying current coverage is materially below that. With 2026 capex guided at $450-510M and EBITDA guided at $950M-$1.05B, FCF improvement is possible but dividend coverage remains thin.

### Earnings Quality

The Q4 2025 EPS of $2.16 (vs. $0.29 estimate, a 644% beat) was inflated by the $415M EPIC sale gain. Normalizing for this, the trailing EPS of $2.63 overstates recurring earnings power. The forward consensus of $1.28 for the current year and $2.01 for next year reflects the loss of EPIC contribution and normalization. These forward estimates have been revised down 30%+ over 90 days.

## Growth Analysis

Growth catalysts on a 12-month horizon:

1. **M&A Sale Process (Primary Catalyst):** Western Midstream's approach in February 2026 triggered a formal sale process. ONEOK, Targa Resources, and Energy Transfer have been flagged as potential acquirers. The FT valued Kinetik at ~$7.2B EV. With ~$3.6B net debt, this implies ~$3.6B equity value, or ~$52/share — modest premium to current price. A competitive process could push valuations higher.

2. **ECCC Pipeline In-Service (Q2 2026):** Adds takeaway capacity and new fee-based revenue. Approximately 70% of 2026 capex directed to New Mexico expansion.

3. **Kings Landing Acid Gas Injection (Year-End 2026):** Removes a processing bottleneck and adds capacity.

4. **Volume Growth:** Delaware Basin remains the highest-growth area of the Permian. Gathered gas volumes grew 17% and crude volumes grew 55% in 2025. Continued producer activity on dedicated acreage supports organic growth.

Offsetting headwinds:
- Waha spread compression as Matterhorn, Blackcomb, and PHP expansion come online (2027-2028) will directly compress gas marketing margins
- Loss of EPIC equity income following divestiture
- Guided 2026 EBITDA midpoint ($1.0B) was reported as 20% below prior market expectations

## Valuation Assessment

At $49.41, KNTK trades at:
- 19.2x trailing P/E (inflated by EPIC gain)
- ~38.6x current year consensus EPS ($1.28)
- ~24.6x next year EPS ($2.01)
- ~7.3x EV/EBITDA on 2025 Adjusted EBITDA (using $7.2B EV / $988M) or ~7.2x on guided midpoint $1.0B 2026
- 6.6% dividend yield

Versus peer medians: the midstream peer group trades at 25.4x P/E with 4.1% yield. On a P/E basis, KNTK appears cheap on trailing (19.2x) but expensive on forward (38.6x current year). The EV/EBITDA of ~7.2x on 2026 guidance is reasonable for midstream — AM trades at 25.4x P/E and DTM at 32.3x, both at lower yields.

M&A premium is supporting the valuation. Without the sale process, the standalone valuation at current prices — with declining estimates and structural margin headwinds — would be difficult to justify. The stock is pricing in either a deal or continued estimate upgrades; one needs to materialize.

## Competitive Landscape

Kinetik occupies a strong strategic position in the Delaware Basin with early-mover advantage in Eddy County, New Mexico. The integrated multi-stream offering (gas, oil, water) creates stickiness with producers who value single-provider simplicity.

Competition is intensifying. Targa Resources (TRGP) is the largest Delaware Basin processor with superior scale. ONEOK has been acquiring aggressively ($18.8B Magellan, $4.3B EnLink, $2.6B Medallion). Western Midstream benefits from Occidental's anchor production. Energy Transfer has massive infrastructure optionality.

The key structural headwind is Waha spread compression. East Daley Analytics identified that a large component of Kinetik's Midstream Logistics earnings comes from its marketing arm, which profits from the wide Waha-to-Houston Ship Channel gas price spread. As 3-4 major new gas pipelines come online by 2028, this spread tightens mechanically, compressing Kinetik's gross margin without requiring any operational deterioration. This is foreseeable and not fully reflected in sell-side models that extend current margins.

## Risk Assessment

### Primary Risks

**1. Sale Process Failure:** If no deal is announced, the stock likely reverts to standalone valuation of $40-44, representing 10-20% downside. The 12.7% short interest suggests meaningful institutional capital positioned for this outcome.

**2. Waha Spread Compression (2027-2028):** As Permian gas takeaway capacity increases, gas marketing margins compress. Management has not publicly quantified the sensitivity.

**3. Leverage Vulnerability:** At 3.7x Net Debt/EBITDA with negative equity, any EBITDA shortfall or rate increase rapidly degrades credit metrics. The dividend exceeds FCF, requiring continued balance sheet deterioration absent asset sales.

**4. Commodity Exposure:** 74% of revenue ($1.31B) comes from product sales with direct NGL, condensate, and natural gas price exposure. Structural supply additions in the Permian gas market create localized price risk.

**5. Sponsor Selling:** I Squared Capital sold ~$179M of stock in February 2026 and continues to liquidate — 4M+ units converted and sold in Q1 2026 alone.

**6. Steel Tariffs:** The 10-K explicitly flags that tariffs on steel (effective June 2025) and ongoing trade policy uncertainty could materially increase construction costs for the company's pipeline-intensive growth projects.

### Options Market Signal

Options activity is thin (24 contracts across 3 expirations), limiting signal quality. ATM IV of 36.3% is essentially fair relative to 36.7% historical vol. The collapse from 98.5% IV on April 16 to 30.4% on May 1 reflects resolution of a binary event (likely M&A-related news or earnings). Put/call skew of 4.4% shows mildly elevated put demand but nothing extreme. The options market is not currently pricing in significant near-term event risk. Term structure in contango (near 36.3% vs. 6-month 38.8%) suggests the market sees slightly higher uncertainty at longer horizons, potentially reflecting deal timeline risk.

- ECCC pipeline in-service provides near-term EBITDA uplift above guidance

3. Short interest declines below 8% of float, signaling reduced institutional skepticism

### Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):
1. Sale process formally terminated without a deal
2. Net Debt/EBITDA exceeds 4.2x (implies EBITDA miss or additional debt)
3. Dividend cut or reduction in coverage guidance below 1.0x

### Reassessment Timeline:
- **Primary:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August) — provides update on sale process, ECCC pipeline contribution, and 2H guidance
- **Secondary:** If no M&A announcement by September 2026, the thesis weakens materially as standalone valuation is stretched at current multiples

## Conclusion

Kinetik Holdings presents a specific, identifiable growth catalyst in its reported sale process, which justifies holding the position on a 12-month horizon despite the stock trading near its 52-week high. The Delaware Basin positioning is strategically valuable, and a competitive auction process could produce an exit at $52-55. The aggressive capital structure, declining forward estimates, foreseeable Waha spread compression, and sponsor selling prevent a BUY recommendation at current levels. The risk/reward is roughly balanced: +5-11% upside to the bull case vs. -15-23% downside if the deal process fails. HOLD with close monitoring of M&A developments and the Q2 2026 earnings report.
