## Executive Summary

Knowles Corporation has completed a compelling strategic transformation, divesting its commoditizing consumer MEMS microphone business and repositioning as a focused industrial technology company serving defense, medtech, and electrification markets. The results are evident: non-GAAP EPS grew from $0.69 (FY2023) to $1.11 (FY2025), adjusted FCF margin expanded to 19.3%, and book-to-bill in the Precision Devices segment has exceeded 1.0x for six consecutive quarters (1.19x in Q1 2026).

The stock has appreciated 110% over the past 12 months, reflecting this transformation and a Q1 2026 earnings beat ($0.27 vs. $0.24 consensus). At $33.96, KN trades at approximately 26x FY2026 consensus EPS of $1.29 and 22x FY2027 estimates of $1.56. While estimate revisions are modestly positive (+5.7% over 90 days for current year), the magnitude is insufficient to qualify as an inflection signal, and the current multiple leaves limited margin of safety given execution risks around the $75M+ specialty film energy order ramp and concentrated customer exposure.

The stock is a growth HOLD: the fundamental trajectory is positive, defense and electrification tailwinds are real and structural, and the balance sheet provides optionality. The specific concern preventing a BUY rating is that the valuation fully prices the current growth trajectory at 26x forward earnings for a company growing EPS at 16-21% — a PEG ratio of approximately 1.4-1.6x, which is fair but not mispriced. The H2 2026 specialty film margin inflection is the next identifiable catalyst that could warrant an upgrade if the stock consolidates.

## Company Overview

Knowles Corporation, headquartered in Itasca, Illinois, is a specialty electronic components manufacturer with approximately 5,200 employees across 11 countries. Following the December 2024 sale of its Consumer MEMS Microphone business, the company operates through two segments:

**Precision Devices (PD) — 55% of FY2025 revenue ($328.9M):** High-performance capacitors (ceramic, specialty film) and RF filtering solutions serving defense, industrial, medtech, and electrification/energy end markets. The November 2023 acquisition of Cornell Dubilier (~$247M) added specialty film capacitors targeting the energy sector.

**MedTech & Specialty Audio (MSA) — 45% of FY2025 revenue ($264.3M):** Balanced armature speakers and microphones for hearing aids and specialty audio applications. Operates in an effective duopoly with Sonion, serving hearing aid OEMs with custom-engineered components.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** Continuing operations revenue grew from $456.8M (FY2023) to $593.2M (FY2025, +7.2% organic) to $153.1M in Q1 2026 (+15.8% YoY). Both segments accelerated in Q1: PD +17.4%, MSA +13.9%.

**Margin Expansion:** Non-GAAP gross margins improved from 44.5% (FY2025) to 45.5% (Q1 2026). Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded from 20.2% (Q1 2025) to 23.1% (Q1 2026). Management guides for an additional 200-250 bps of PD gross margin improvement in H2 2026 as the specialty film ramp normalizes.

**Cash Flow:** Adjusted free cash flow reached $114.4M in FY2025 (19.3% FCF margin), up from $66.9M in FY2023. Q1 was seasonally weak (operating cash flow -$0.7M due to working capital timing), consistent with historical patterns.

**Balance Sheet:** Net debt of $90M represents only 0.6x trailing adjusted EBITDA. Liquidity exceeds $310M. The company has been aggressively repurchasing shares — $65M in FY2025 (reducing diluted shares from 91.6M to 87.3M), with $279M in remaining authorization. This leverage level provides significant M&A optionality.

**Key Financial Ratios:** Current ratio 2.75, Piotroski F-Score 5/5. Altman Z-Score of 2.47 (grey zone) is somewhat misleading for an asset-light manufacturer with significant acquisition-related goodwill ($270M).

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term growth drivers are well-identified and quantifiable:**

1. **$75M+ Specialty Film Energy Order:** This single contract is expected to generate $50-60M in revenue in FY2026, ramping to full production by end of Q2 2026. It addresses electrification/grid infrastructure demand using Cornell Dubilier's specialty film capacitor capabilities.

2. **Defense Long-Term Agreements:** PD defense customers are signing 3-5 year LTAs (vs. typical 1-year cycles), providing unusual revenue visibility. Book-to-bill of 1.19x in Q1 with 97% of orders shipping within 12 months.

3. **MSA Volume Recovery:** Hearing aid OEM customers are in a product upgrade cycle, driving Q1 MSA growth of 13.9%. The aging demographic trend and OTC hearing aid regulation expand the addressable market.

4. **Pricing Power:** Management expects 2-4% annual price increases in PD, supported by custom-engineering differentiation and defense customer willingness to pay for domestic supply.

**Longer-term (3-5 year):** Ceramic inductors represent a new product line. Management targets revenue growth above the high end of its 4-6% organic CAGR framework, with EBITDA growth exceeding the 10-14% CAGR target.

**Estimate revision velocity:** Current year EPS revisions of +5.7% over 90 days and +4.2% over 30 days represent positive momentum but are below the >30% threshold that signals a strong inflection. The revisions reflect incremental improvement, not fundamental re-acceleration.

## Valuation Assessment

At $33.96, KN trades at:
- 47x trailing GAAP EPS ($0.58 — depressed by CMM-related charges and amortization)
- 26.3x FY2026 consensus non-GAAP EPS ($1.29)
- 21.8x FY2027 consensus non-GAAP EPS ($1.56)
- Forward PEG of approximately 1.4x (using 21% EPS growth FY2025-FY2027 CAGR)

**Peer context:** The electronic components peer set shows wide valuation dispersion: Belden at 21.6x (closest relevant comp), Vishay and Rogers with negative P/Es (cyclical trough), Corning at 73.1x (large-cap premium for AI/optical exposure). KN's 26x forward earnings is a premium to Belden (the most operationally comparable peer), justified partly by higher organic growth and better margin trajectory.

**Analyst consensus:** Mean target $36.25, median $36.50, range $33-39. At $33.96, the stock sits at the low end of the target range, offering 7% upside to mean. This represents modest upside for a BUY thesis — though the guardrail on analyst targets as ceilings does not apply here because KN trades above 25x forward P/E, where analyst targets are not reliably "floors."

**The bull case for valuation:** If FY2027 EPS achieves $1.60-1.70 (above consensus via energy ramp success + continued defense acceleration) and the market applies 25x, fair value reaches $40-42.50 (17-25% upside).

**The bear case for valuation:** If the specialty film ramp disappoints and organic growth reverts to 4-6%, FY2027 EPS could come in at $1.40-1.45. At 22x (reflecting lower growth premium), fair value would be $31-32 (6-9% downside).

The stock is fairly valued at current levels — not cheap enough to provide meaningful margin of safety, not expensive enough to warrant avoidance.

## Competitive Landscape

**PD Segment:** Knowles competes against Kyocera AVX, KEMET/Yageo, Vishay, and others in capacitors. Its differentiation is custom engineering for demanding applications (high voltage, high temperature, high reliability) combined with domestic manufacturing footprint. This is critical for ITAR-compliant defense contracts and increasingly valued given reshoring trends. The competitive position is improving as defense customers seek domestic, trusted supply chains and sign multi-year LTAs.

**MSA Segment:** The balanced armature speaker market operates as an effective duopoly (Knowles + Sonion). Barriers to entry are extremely high given custom ASIC design, precision manufacturing requirements, and multi-year qualification cycles with hearing aid OEMs. The ~38-40% adjusted EBIT margins in this segment confirm pricing power consistent with duopoly economics.

**Trajectory:** Both segments show improving competitive positioning — PD via defense domestication tailwinds and the electrification growth vector; MSA via stable duopoly economics with expanding addressable market from OTC hearing aids.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Customer Concentration (Material):** WS Audiology represents 24% of MSA revenue (11% of total). TTI Inc. (distributor) accounts for 19% of PD revenue (10% of total). The top 5 MSA customers represent 76% of segment revenue. Loss or significant reduction from any major customer would create a meaningful earnings gap.

**2. Specialty Film Ramp Execution:** Below-expectation yields have already caused temporary margin headwinds. Management guides to improvement by H2 2026. If yields don't normalize, PD margins could disappoint for 2-3 quarters and the Cornell Dubilier acquisition thesis would face scrutiny.

**3. Syntiant Counterparty Risk:** The $77.2M equity stake in Syntiant (private AI chip startup) plus seller financing represents a concentrated risk that could result in a material write-off if Syntiant's business deteriorates.

**4. Trade/Tariff Exposure:** Majority of manufacturing remains in China and Southeast Asia. Management noted PD revenues have already been "adversely impacted" by China's self-sufficiency policies. A 10% USD weakening would reduce pre-tax earnings by ~$14.8M.

**5. General Counsel Departure:** Robert Perna (SVP, General Counsel) appears to have departed in February 2026 based on complete share disposal and an 8-K Item 5.02 filing. While not necessarily alarming, C-suite departures during transformation periods warrant monitoring.

### Options Market Signal

Short interest at 4.8% of float with 5.4 days to cover represents a modest short position — not concerning but not supportive of a squeeze thesis either. The relatively elevated days-to-cover for the float size suggests some bearish positioning remains, likely from investors skeptical of the valuation re-rating.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (~$40-42, +18-24%)
- Specialty film ramp succeeds on schedule, delivering 200-250 bps PD margin improvement in H2 2026
- Defense LTAs convert to sustained revenue growth above 10% in PD
- FY2027 EPS reaches $1.60-1.70 vs. consensus $1.56, driving further estimate revisions
- M&A deployment of $310M+ liquidity at attractive terms adds an accretive growth vector
- Market fully re-rates KN as an industrial compounder at 25-27x forward earnings

### Bear Case (~$26-28, -18-23%)
- Specialty film yields do not normalize, PD margins stagnate, energy order profitability disappoints
- Hearing aid OEM cycle peaks, MSA growth reverts to low single digits
- Syntiant write-off creates a one-time $50-77M charge, eroding book value
- Trade tensions escalate, impacting Asian manufacturing operations
- Multiple compresses to 18-20x as growth decelerates toward 4-6% organic range

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Stock consolidates to $28-30 range (20-22x FY2027 EPS) without fundamental deterioration, creating better risk/reward asymmetry
2. Q2 2026 earnings confirm specialty film margin inflection with PD gross margins improving >100 bps sequentially
3. FY2026 EPS consensus revisions exceed +15% over a 90-day window, signaling acceleration beyond current trajectory

**Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. PD book-to-bill falls below 1.0x for two consecutive quarters
2. WS Audiology announces supplier diversification or partnership with alternative speaker manufacturer
3. Specialty film yields show no material improvement through Q3 2026, with management pushing timeline further
4. Syntiant announces distress or Knowles takes >$30M impairment on the investment

**Reassessment Timeline:** Next earnings report (expected late July 2026 for Q2). The Q2 report should provide clarity on specialty film ramp success — this is the specific, dated catalyst that determines the H2 margin trajectory.

## Conclusion

Knowles Corporation has executed a well-structured transformation from a cyclical consumer components company to a higher-quality industrial technology platform with defense, medtech, and electrification exposure. The operational improvements are genuine: FCF margins approaching 20%, expanding EBITDA margins, and sustained book-to-bill strength provide confidence in the growth trajectory.

The specific concern preventing a BUY recommendation at $33.96 is quantifiable: at 26x FY2026 EPS with 16-21% EPS growth, the PEG ratio of 1.4-1.6x is fair value for the company's quality and growth profile. The analyst consensus mean target of $36.25 offers only 7% upside — insufficient risk-adjusted return given the execution uncertainty around the specialty film ramp and customer concentration risks. A stock that has appreciated 110% in 12 months on a 5.7% estimate revision is priced for the known catalysts.

The path to re-engagement is clear: either the stock consolidates to the $28-30 range creating better risk/reward, or Q2 2026 earnings demonstrate the specialty film margin inflection is real and sustainable, validating a higher growth rate that justifies premium valuation. Until one of these conditions materializes, HOLD with a growth thesis intact and positive 12-month outlook conditional on execution.
