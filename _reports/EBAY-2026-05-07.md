## Executive Summary

eBay is experiencing a legitimate revenue acceleration after three years of stagnation. Revenue grew from ~2% in 2023-2024 to 8% in 2025, with Q1 2026 delivering 18% YoY GMV growth (14% FX-neutral). The drivers are structural and multi-pronged: advertising revenue growing 22% YoY (now $2B annualized at 2.6% GMV penetration), AI-powered listing and discovery tools, C2C marketplace expansion, and Focus Category execution in collectibles and luxury recommerce. The company simultaneously returned $3B+ annually to shareholders through buybacks (18% share reduction since 2022) and dividends.

The complication is valuation and timing. The stock has appreciated 54% over the past 12 months to $108, now trades near the analyst consensus median target of $110, and sits at ~25x forward P/E on FY2026E EPS of $6.12. While estimate revisions are positive (+4.7% over 90 days for FY2026, +4.9% for FY2027), they do not meet the 30%+ velocity threshold that would signal an uncaptured inflection. Tariff policy remains a material binary risk for cross-border GMV, and the $1.2B Depop acquisition is dilutive until 2028.

The stock is appropriately held for its growth trajectory, but the current price largely reflects the improved fundamentals. Asymmetric upside requires either advertising penetration accelerating beyond expectations, tariff resolution benefiting cross-border trade, or Depop performing ahead of guidance.

## Company Overview

eBay operates the world's largest marketplace for pre-owned, unique, and collectible goods, connecting approximately 135 million buyers across 190+ markets. Revenue splits 82% marketplace ($9.1B in 2025) and 18% advertising ($2.0B), with the advertising component growing at 22% annually and approaching meaningful contribution. The company takes no inventory risk and operates at ~72% gross margins with an asset-light model that generates $2B+ in annual operating cash flow.

The platform's differentiation centers on recommerce (>40% of GMV), collectibles, luxury authentication, and Motors Parts & Accessories — categories where Amazon's new-goods model has limited ability to compete. The February 2026 acquisition of Depop ($1.2B, closing Q2 2026) targets Gen Z demographics where 90% of the 7M active buyers are under 34.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory confirms acceleration.** After revenue growth of 3.2% (2023) and 1.7% (2024), 2025 delivered 8.0% growth to $11.1B. Q1 2026 was the strongest quarter in years: $22.2B GMV (+18% reported), with full-year 2026 GMV guidance raised to 7-7.5% growth. Take rate expanded modestly to 13.94% in Q4 2025, indicating pricing power.

**Profitability faces temporary headwinds.** Operating margin compressed 200bps to 20.5% in 2025, driven by $91M in restructuring costs, $61M legal accruals, and $108M in UK shipping program expansion. These are largely one-time items. Underlying margins remain strong for an asset-light marketplace: ROE of 44%, FCF margin of 12.9%.

**Capital return is exceptional.** Shares outstanding fell from 558M (2022) to 459M (2025) — an 18% reduction. The $2.5B in 2025 buybacks plus $531M in dividends totals $3.0B returned, representing ~6.3% of current market cap annually. An additional $2B authorization was approved February 2026.

**Balance sheet is deliberately leveraged.** Total debt of $6.75B against $4.8B cash + investments. Debt/equity of 2.82x reflects an optimized capital structure, not operational stress. Interest coverage at ~9.3x is comfortable, and all debt is fixed-rate with staggered maturities. The Altman Z-Score of 1.67 is a model artifact — Altman was designed for manufacturing firms with tangible assets, not asset-light platforms with $15B+ in goodwill/intangibles.

**Free cash flow generation is solid but modestly declining.** 2025 FCF of $1.43B declined from $2.4B OCF on $685M higher cash taxes (one-time catch-up from Adevinta/Adyen stake sales). Normalized FCF run-rate is ~$2.0-2.2B, supporting continued aggressive buybacks.

## Growth Analysis

The growth acceleration is supported by four distinct engines:

1. **Advertising (~22% CAGR):** At 2.6% GMV penetration in Q4 2025, there is meaningful runway versus Amazon's 8%+ penetration. Each incremental ad dollar carries near-zero marginal cost. A path from $2.0B to $3.0B+ over 3 years is credible.

2. **C2C expansion:** UK seller fee elimination (shifting to buyer fees), Depop acquisition, and Australia C2C revamp (May 2026) unlock new seller supply. The recommerce market is growing at 15-20% annually, and eBay is the dominant platform for unique/used goods.

3. **AI tools:** "Magical Listing" reduces friction for sellers (AI-generated descriptions from photos). AI-powered search and recommendation engines improve buyer conversion. These are early-stage implementations with compounding benefits.

4. **Focus Categories:** Collectibles was the strongest-performing category in 2025. Authentication programs (Authenticity Guarantee) create trust moats in luxury, sneakers, and trading cards where counterfeiting risk otherwise depresses willingness-to-transact.

**Estimate revision momentum is positive but moderate.** FY2026 EPS estimates rose 4.7% over 90 days ($5.85 → $6.12); FY2027 rose 4.9% ($6.44 → $6.75). This signals improving fundamentals but does not reach the 30%+ velocity threshold associated with uncaptured inflections. The market is pricing the improvement relatively efficiently.

## Valuation Assessment

At $108, eBay trades at approximately 25x trailing EPS ($4.26) and ~17.6x FY2026E EPS ($6.12). Against the peer median P/E of 21.5x (which includes distressed brick-and-mortar retailers like Macy's at 8.5x and Kohl's at 6.1x), eBay's premium is justified by its asset-light model, high margins, and growth acceleration. Against Amazon's 32.9x, eBay's discount is appropriate given the scale and growth rate differential.

The stock trades near the analyst consensus median target of $110 and above the mean of $105.94. The high target of $130 implies ~20% upside, but only 10 of 31 analysts rate the stock Buy/Strong Buy versus 19 HOLDs and 2 SELLs. This distribution signals that the bullish case is somewhat consensus but the majority view is that current pricing is fair.

On a FCF yield basis: $1.43B FCF / $47.7B market cap = 3.0%. Including normalized FCF of ~$2.0B, the yield improves to ~4.2%. Combined with the ~1.2% dividend yield, total cash return is approximately 5.4% — reasonable but not compelling for a 25x P/E stock.

The 54% appreciation over 12 months ($70 → $108) has compressed the margin of safety. While the business fundamentals justify a higher valuation than a year ago, the risk/reward at current prices is balanced rather than asymmetric.

## Competitive Landscape

eBay occupies a defensible niche as the dominant global marketplace for pre-owned, unique, and collectible goods. Its moat components include:

- **Network effects:** 135M buyers and millions of sellers in 190 markets create self-reinforcing liquidity
- **Authentication infrastructure:** Partnerships and in-house programs for luxury goods, sneakers, and trading cards that competitors cannot easily replicate
- **Recommerce positioning:** >40% of GMV in pre-owned goods; structural tailwinds from sustainability trends and inflation-driven value-seeking
- **Advertising platform scale:** $2B in ad revenue creates a self-funding growth engine

Competitive threats are real but manageable: Amazon's renewed/refurbished programs take share at the margin; TikTok Shop captures impulse fashion buying from younger demographics; Google/AI chatbots could disintermediate search-driven discovery. The Depop acquisition directly addresses the demographic vulnerability with younger consumers.

The competitive position is improving rather than deteriorating — Focus Categories are growing faster than the platform average, authentication expands trust, and C2C initiatives unlock supply that doesn't exist on Amazon.

## Risk Assessment

**Tariff and trade policy (HIGH — binary and uncontrollable).** Cross-border trade is foundational to eBay's model. The elimination of de minimis exemptions in the US, and planned elimination in the EU (July 2026), directly increases costs for cross-border purchases. Greater China exports to Western markets are a significant GMV contributor. While the February 2026 SCOTUS ruling struck down IEEPA tariffs, the administration is pursuing alternatives. If trade normalizes, cross-border GMV benefits; if tariff escalation resumes, order cancellations increase.

**Depop dilution and integration risk (MODERATE).** Integrating a youth-focused cultural brand into eBay's ecosystem without destroying the brand identity is non-trivial. The 800-job restructuring announced one week after the deal suggests aggressive cost management but creates execution risk.

**Consumer discretionary sensitivity (MODERATE).** No subscription/recurring revenue buffer. eBay's value/recommerce positioning partially insulates it (consumers trade down to used goods during stress), but overall GMV remains correlated with discretionary spending levels.

**AI disruption to discovery model (EMERGING).** eBay explicitly flags in its 10-K that AI chatbots and shopping agents threaten its traffic acquisition model. If Google or AI assistants bypass marketplace search in favor of direct product recommendations, eBay's organic traffic — currently its primary acquisition channel — could deteriorate.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($120-130, +11-20%):** Advertising penetration accelerates toward 3.5%+ of GMV, driving margins higher while revenue grows 8-10%. Depop integration succeeds, unlocking a Gen Z growth vector. Tariff environment normalizes, benefiting cross-border GMV. FY2027 EPS reaches $7.00+ with continued aggressive buybacks reducing share count below 430M. Multiple expands to 18-19x forward.

**Bear Case ($80-90, -17-26%):** Tariff escalation resumes, de minimis elimination causes material GMV decline in cross-border categories. Consumer spending deteriorates amid stagflation, hitting discretionary spend on collectibles and non-essential goods. Depop acquisition struggles with integration, becoming a multi-year drag. AI disruption to search-based discovery begins manifesting in traffic declines. Multiple compresses to 13-14x forward.

**Base Case ($108-115, 0-7%):** Revenue growth moderates to 5-7% as Q1 2026's 18% GMV growth normalizes. Advertising continues at 15-20% growth. Buybacks remove another 5-7% of shares. EPS reaches $6.10-6.25. Stock trades at 17-18x FY2026 earnings, roughly in line with current levels.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Stock pulls back below $95 (15.5x FY2026E, creating >15% upside to base case)
- Q2 2026 GMV growth sustains above 12% FX-neutral (confirming acceleration is durable, not a one-quarter phenomenon)
- Advertising penetration reaches 3.0%+ of GMV with visible margin expansion

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Two consecutive quarters of FX-neutral GMV decline
- Cross-border GMV declines >10% on tariff implementation without offsetting domestic C2C growth
- Management cuts FY2026 guidance below original targets

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July 2026). Depop closing (Q2 2026) and initial integration commentary will provide critical data on acquisition trajectory. EU de minimis elimination (July 2026) is a concrete catalyst that will quantify cross-border risk.

## Conclusion

eBay's transformation under Jamie Iannone is producing measurable results: advertising is scaling toward a $3B+ business, recommerce positioning aligns with secular consumer trends, and AI tools are reducing marketplace friction. The Q1 2026 results confirmed the growth acceleration is broadening across categories and geographies.

The specific reason this stock does not warrant a BUY at $108 is the convergence of three factors: (1) the stock trades near the analyst consensus median target ($110) after a 54% run, meaning the improved fundamentals are largely reflected in price; (2) tariff policy represents a material binary risk to cross-border GMV with a defined catalyst (EU de minimis elimination July 2026) that could subtract 3-5% from total GMV growth; and (3) Depop dilution creates a drag that offsets some of the organic EPS improvement from buybacks. The combination means the stock needs to execute flawlessly to justify current pricing, while a miss on any vector creates 15-25% downside. At $95 or below, the asymmetry shifts in favor of a BUY.
