## Executive Summary

EZCORP is the second-largest pawn operator in the United States and one of the largest in Latin America, operating 1,506 stores across 16 countries. The company is experiencing a rare convergence of organic growth (consumer credit stress driving pawn demand), commodity tailwinds (surging gold prices boosting scrap margins), and inorganic growth (SMG acquisition adding stores across new geographies). Q2 FY2026 results demonstrated the power of this confluence: revenue grew 46% YoY, net income surged 93%, and adjusted EPS of $0.58 beat consensus of $0.42 by 38.7%.

At $33.12, the stock trades at approximately 18.5x trailing twelve-month EPS (estimated ~$1.79) and 17.9x FY2026E EPS of $1.85 and 16.2x FY2027E of $2.04. Relative to the peer median P/E of 11.7x, this appears elevated — but the comparison is misleading because peers include distressed operators (NAVI, PRAA with negative earnings). Against FirstCash (the industry leader trading at a premium), EZCORP's growth rate substantially exceeds its multiple premium. Recent estimate revisions have been meaningfully positive, signaling the market is still underestimating the fundamental trajectory. The setup favors continued appreciation toward the $38-40 range over the next 6-12 months.

The primary risk is gold price dependency: based on our scenario analysis, a significant portion of annualized pre-tax earnings derives directly from elevated gold prices. A sustained 20%+ decline in gold would meaningfully compress EPS, fundamentally altering the thesis. The dual-class share structure with Phillip Cohen controlling all voting stock permanently caps governance quality and likely places a structural discount on the multiple.

## Company Overview

EZCORP generates revenue through three streams: pawn service charges (37% of revenue, 64% of gross profit), merchandise sales of forfeited collateral (55%), and jewelry scrap/gold recycling (8% of revenue but growing rapidly). The business operates as a counter-cyclical consumer finance platform — when banks tighten credit and consumer balance sheets deteriorate, pawn loan demand increases.

The company operates in two primary segments plus the newly consolidated SMG:
- **U.S. Pawn**: 559 stores (EZPAWN, Value Pawn & Jewelry), concentrated 45% in Texas and 17% in Florida
- **Latin America**: 840 stores across Mexico (622), Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras
- **SMG**: 107 stores across U.S., Caribbean, and Central America (consolidated January 2, 2026; acquired with 105 stores, with two de novo stores added during Q2)

Strategic positioning includes a 43.7% stake in Cash Converters International (ASX: CCV, 659 stores across 15 countries) and a 6.9 million-member EZ+ Rewards loyalty platform that provides digital pawn management capabilities.

## Growth Analysis

EZCORP's growth is driven by four distinct engines operating simultaneously:

**1. Organic PLO Growth (+16% same-store in Q2):** Consumer credit stress is the primary demand driver. Credit card delinquency metrics have risen to levels not seen since 2011 (with some measures of serious delinquency reaching elevated levels), alongside an elevated cost of living and restrictive bank lending that push consumers toward pawn financing. With average U.S. loans of $200-220 at 12-25% monthly rates, pawn fills a genuine short-term liquidity need for subprime consumers.

**2. Gold Price Tailwind:** Jewelry scrap gross profit surged 288% in Q2 FY2026. At current gold prices, scrap margins of 38% translate to significant annualized scrap gross profit. Additionally, elevated gold values increase the collateral base for jewelry-backed pawn loans (68% of U.S. pawn collateral is jewelry), supporting higher average loan sizes.

**3. Inorganic Growth (SMG + Guatemala):** The SMG acquisition adds stores across new geographies. In April 2026, EZCORP acquired 32 stores in Guatemala. Store count reached 1,506 — up from 1,360 at FY2025 end, representing 11% unit growth in 6 months.

**4. Latin America Runway:** With 840 stores in Latin America (a less penetrated market than the U.S.), EZCORP has substantial organic growth ahead. Mexican same-store PLO growth consistently outpaces U.S. performance.

**Estimate Revision Momentum:** Recent EPS estimate revisions for FY2026 and FY2027 have been meaningfully positive, confirming that the market is still catching up to fundamental improvements. The company's multi-quarter earnings beat streak underscores systematic analyst underestimation.

Based on available analyst coverage (approximately 4 analysts providing current-year estimates), consensus targets suggest meaningful upside, though analyst coverage is thin and targets are time-sensitive.

The stock has appreciated 127% over the past year from $14.74 to $33.44. This appreciation is entirely justified by fundamental improvement: EPS has roughly doubled, and the PLO growth trajectory has accelerated. The market is paying a modestly higher multiple for significantly better earnings — the re-rating reflects fundamental reality, not sentiment excess.

**Valuation risk:** At 18x forward earnings, the stock is no longer deeply cheap. It requires continued execution and sustained gold prices. A reversion to 14-15x (reflecting gold uncertainty and governance discount) on current estimates would imply $26-28. This establishes the downside range.

## Competitive Landscape

EZCORP is the #2 U.S. pawn operator behind FirstCash Holdings. The industry is highly fragmented with thousands of independent operators, providing a long consolidation runway.

**Competitive advantages:**
- Scale economics in procurement, technology, and regulatory compliance across 1,500+ stores
- 6.9M-member EZ+ Rewards platform creating digital engagement and repeat borrowing
- "Circular economy" model with minimal supply chain disruption risk (collateral is literally handed to you by the customer)
- Counter-cyclical demand profile — stress periods drive growth rather than compression
- International diversification across 16 countries (post-SMG)

**Competitive position trajectory:** Improving. Same-store PLO growth of +16% indicates strong demand and increasing average loan sizes, driven by higher average loan size, continued strong pawn demand, and improved operational performance. The SMG acquisition brings new countries and establishes EZCORP as a genuinely multinational operator. Cash Converters stake provides option value on additional international expansion.

**Structural industry tailwind:** The macro environment (elevated interest rates, credit card delinquencies at cycle highs, persistent inflation) is the most favorable demand backdrop for pawn in over a decade. This positions EZCORP to grow organically even independent of store additions, though the company's own risk factors note that changes in gold prices, customer behavior, and regulatory conditions can materially affect results.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Gold Price Dependency (HIGH — underappreciated by the market):**
Jewelry scrap gross profit contributed approximately $31M in Q2 FY2026 alone (vs. approximately $4.6M in prior year Q2). If gold declines 25% from current levels, quarterly scrap contribution could fall meaningfully, potentially erasing a significant portion of annualized EPS. The company itself notes in its 10-K that the precise impact of gold price changes cannot be reasonably estimated because market and customer responses are unknown. This is the single largest risk to the earnings trajectory and is partially outside management's control.

**2. Controlled Company Governance:**
Phillip Cohen controls ALL voting shares. Public Class A shareholders have no governance rights. This permanently caps the multiple relative to well-governed peers and eliminates activist engagement as a value catalyst. No indication exists that this structure will change.

**3. Geographic Concentration:**
62% of U.S. stores in Texas (45%) and Florida (17%). Regulatory risk is concentrated, and hurricane exposure is real.

**4. Integration Complexity:**
Operating across 16 countries with a newly acquired store platform introduces meaningful operational risk. SMG's contribution margin ($8.8M on $51.3M revenue = 17%) needs to improve toward corporate averages.

**5. Short Interest:**
Short interest as a percentage of float appears elevated, creating a dual dynamic: (a) squeeze potential on further good news, and (b) signal that sophisticated investors see vulnerabilities. The most likely bear thesis behind the short interest is gold dependency and peak-cycle earnings quality.

**6. Convertible Dilution:**
$230M of convertible notes due 2029 could dilute shares outstanding upon conversion, depending on price levels at maturity.

## Conclusion

EZCORP represents a counter-cyclical business hitting on all cylinders at a moment when the macro environment is maximally favorable for pawn demand. The multi-quarter beat streak, positive estimate revisions, and 93% net income growth demonstrate genuine fundamental momentum. At approximately 18.5x trailing earnings and 18x forward earnings with a PEG of 0.75, the stock offers favorable asymmetry for continued appreciation.

The recommendation is BUY with the critical caveat that gold dependency makes position sizing discipline essential. The correct allocation is 2-3% given the gold-linked binary risk and controlled company governance discount. The stock should be sized smaller than a comparably valued business without these structural overhangs.

The primary differentiated view vs. consensus: the market is treating EZCORP's earnings quality as lower than it actually is (hence the governance/gold discount), while underweighting the structural demand tailwind from consumer stress that persists regardless of gold movements. Organic same-store PLO growth of 16% is a demand signal independent of gold — and this alone justifies a premium multiple in a consumer finance context.
