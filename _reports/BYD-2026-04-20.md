## Executive Summary

Boyd Gaming is a well-run, geographically diversified regional gaming operator generating ~$1B in annual operating cash flow across 27 properties in 11 states. The company executed a FanDuel equity sale in July 2025 for $1.76B, used proceeds to cut debt by 36%, and reduced its share count by 25% over two years through aggressive buybacks. At $87.63, the stock trades at ~11.8x consensus FY2026 EPS of $7.42, a 34% discount to the 18x peer median P/E.

The discount is partially justified. The FanDuel transaction, while financially astute at ~23x the traded EBITDAR stream, structurally removes ~$75M in annualized high-margin Online segment EBITDAR (declining from $108M in 2024 to a guided $30-35M in 2026). Capex is rising sharply to $650-700M in 2026 as the $750M Norfolk resort absorbs capital, pushing guided leverage back to 2.5x from under 2x. Management flagged softening destination travel demand and Fremont Street foot traffic declining ~10% in Q4 2025.

Given active contraction in consumer discretionary spending, negative estimate revision trends (-5.2% over 90 days on current-year EPS), and rising energy costs pressuring the middle-market demographics Boyd serves, the stock warrants a HOLD despite its valuation discount. Consumer confidence is at record lows and declining, which directly challenges a requirement for upgrading to BUY.

## Company Overview

Boyd Gaming operates 27 gaming entertainment properties across four reporting segments: **Midwest & South** (17 properties, $2.12B revenue, 52% of total), **Las Vegas Locals** (7 casinos, $890M, 22%), **Online** ($708M, 17%), and **Downtown Las Vegas** (3 properties, $229M, 6%). The company also manages Sky River Casino in Northern California under a tribal management agreement and operates Lattner Entertainment's distributed gaming network (973 units in Illinois).

Boyd's strategic identity is centered on the **Las Vegas locals market** — recurring, resident-based gaming revenue less exposed to tourism volatility than Strip-focused peers. Its nationwide Boyd Rewards loyalty program creates cross-property stickiness, and its Downtown Las Vegas properties capture a unique Hawaiian traveler niche (~75% of room nights). The Boyd family retains ~30% ownership, providing strategic stability but also governance concentration risk.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue** grew at a ~4.8% 4-year CAGR from $3.56B (2022) to $4.09B (2025), with FY2025 up 4.1% year-over-year. Q4 2025 revenue of $1.1B was a quarterly record.

**Operating income** declined from $928M (2024) to $748M (2025) on a reported basis, a 19.3% drop, though this includes $128M in long-lived asset impairment charges. Adjusted EBITDAR fell a more modest 2.6% to $1.354B. Property-level margins held near 40%, and gaming margins were essentially flat at 61.1%.

**The FanDuel transaction** dominates the reported financials. Boyd sold its 5% FanDuel equity stake for $1.758B in cash, generating a ~$1.748B pre-tax gain. This creates a trailing EPS of $22.56, producing the misleading 3.9x trailing P/E in the profile data. Stripping out this gain, normalized trailing EPS is approximately $7.20-7.50.

**Balance sheet transformation:** Total debt declined from $3.2B to $2.06B (-35.6%). Remaining debt consists of $1B of 4.75% notes due December 2027, $900M of 4.75% notes due June 2031, and $161M drawn on the credit facility. The new January 2026 credit agreement provides a $1.45B revolver and $1.2B delayed-draw term loan.

**Cash flow:** Operating cash flow of $977M was consistent with the prior year ($957M). However, capex rose to $588M from $400M, compressing FCF to $389M from $557M. With 2026 capex guided at $650-700M (Norfolk absorbing $250-300M), FCF will be further constrained. At the current ~$6.6B market cap, FCF yield on guided 2026 capex is approximately 4-5%.

**Capital allocation:** BYD returned $836M to shareholders in 2025 ($778M buybacks + $58M dividends), reducing share count from ~92M to ~82M. The quarterly dividend was raised to $0.20 from $0.16 in February 2026. $362M remains under the current buyback authorization.

**Key concern — the Online EBITDAR cliff:** Online segment EBITDAR declined from $108M (2024) to $63M (2025) and is guided to $30-35M in 2026. This represents a ~$75M annualized loss in high-margin recurring income, a direct trade-off from monetizing the FanDuel stake. At ~23x the traded income stream, the transaction was favorable on a lump-sum basis, but ongoing earnings power is structurally lower.

## Growth Analysis

Boyd's organic growth story has three components:

1. **Norfolk, Virginia resort** ($750M investment, late 2027 opening): Boyd's largest-ever greenfield development featuring 65,000 sq ft of casino space, a 200-room hotel, and 8 dining outlets. A modest 132-slot transitional casino opened in November 2025. This is a new market for Boyd with uncertain demand profiles, and the capital commitment is substantial relative to the company's ~$1.35B EBITDAR.

2. **Sky River Casino expansion**: Phase 1 (400 additional slots + parking garage) completing in early 2026; Phase 2 (300-room hotel, spa, food/beverage) due mid-2027. Management fee income guided at $110-114M for 2026, up from ~$99M. This is high-margin, capital-light growth.

3. **Cadence Crossing Casino**: Replacing Jokers Wild in Henderson, NV — a new locals casino in a high-growth Las Vegas suburb. Low-cost, low-risk expansion within Boyd's core competency.

Consensus estimates project FY2026 EPS of $7.42 and FY2027 EPS of $8.07, implying ~9% EPS growth year-over-year. However, **estimate revisions are negative across all periods**: current-year EPS estimates have been cut 5.2% over 90 days and 0.6% over 30 days.

Same-property revenue trends are modestly positive in Las Vegas Locals (+2-3% organic) but mixed in Midwest & South, where the Sam's Town Tunica closure and impairment charges reflect market deterioration. Management explicitly flagged softening destination demand and a ~10% decline in Fremont Street pedestrian traffic in Q4 2025.

BYD trades at a 34% discount to the peer median P/E. Part of this discount is structural: Boyd's geographic diversification across mature Midwest/South markets naturally commands a lower multiple than pure-play Las Vegas locals peers like Red Rock Resorts (18.1x) or premium operators. Part is cyclical: the Online EBITDAR cliff and rising capex reduce near-term earnings visibility.

Consensus analyst targets range from $83 (low) to $110 (high), with a median of $93 and mean of $94.73. At $87.63, the stock sits above the low target but 6% below the median, suggesting limited near-term upside in the sell-side consensus view.

The stock has rallied 35% from its April 2025 low of $63.33 to the current $87.63, now trading within 3% of its 52-week high of $89.96. This reduces the margin of safety.

**FCF yield assessment**: With guided 2026 capex of $650-700M against ~$975M operating cash flow, FCF approximates $275-325M, yielding approximately 4.2-4.9% on the current market cap. This falls short of the 5% threshold that would support a more constructive stance in the current environment.

## Competitive Landscape

Boyd's closest public comparable is **Red Rock Resorts (RRR, 18.1x P/E)**, the other publicly traded Las Vegas locals-focused operator. Churchill Downs (CHDN, 17.5x) is a differentiated comp with horse racing and historical racing machines. Caesars (CZR) and Penn (PENN) both trade at negative P/Es, reflecting their own operational challenges.

Boyd's competitive advantages include: (1) nationwide loyalty program enabling cross-selling, (2) geographic diversification reducing single-market risk, (3) the Sky River management agreement providing capital-light California exposure, and (4) a demonstrated track record of disciplined operations (5-year TSR of 207% vs. 82% for peers).

Risks to competitive position include: increasing Native American casino competition in several Midwest/South states, potential iGaming legalization benefiting pure-play digital operators, and the transition from high-margin FanDuel market access fees to lower-margin Boyd Interactive operations.

## Risk Assessment

**Consumer spending deterioration (HIGH):** Consumer confidence sits at record lows (Michigan sentiment 47.6) and is declining on a 3-month basis. Energy costs above $4/gallon directly pressure the middle-market demographics Boyd serves. Regional gaming revenue is historically correlated with consumer disposable income — a recession scenario (50-55% probability) would reduce visitation and spend-per-visit across Boyd's portfolio.

**Online EBITDAR structural decline (MODERATE-HIGH):** The ~$75M annualized decline from 2024 to 2026 guidance represents approximately 5.5% of total EBITDAR. This is fully embedded in estimates but reduces the company's earnings quality profile.

**Norfolk execution risk (MODERATE):** At $750M, Norfolk represents ~55% of trailing EBITDAR committed to a single greenfield project in an unproven market. Construction delays, cost overruns, or weak initial demand could destroy significant value.

**Midwest & South impairment risk (MODERATE):** The 10-K explicitly warns that ~$2.4B in goodwill and intangibles "did not significantly exceed carrying value." A consumer downturn could trigger hundreds of millions in non-cash impairment charges. While non-cash, these would reduce reported earnings and signal deteriorating franchise value.

**$1.16B 2027 debt maturity (LOW-MODERATE):** Manageable given the new $1.2B delayed-draw term loan, but refinancing in a higher-rate environment will increase interest expense.

**Energy cost passthrough (MODERATE):** Boyd's properties are electricity-intensive, and customers' willingness to visit casinos correlates inversely with fuel costs. Oil above $95 compresses both the cost structure and the customer base.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV of 36.0% trades rich to 25.8% historical volatility — a 10.1pp spread indicating the market is pricing elevated near-term uncertainty. The term structure is in **backwardation** (36% near-term vs. 26% at 6 months), consistent with stress pricing around an imminent catalyst or broader market anxiety. Put/call OI ratio of 1.27 indicates modest institutional put accumulation, though the volume ratio of 0.45 suggests recent flow is call-heavy. The backwardated structure and rich IV support waiting for a better entry point rather than chasing the current price.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (~$100-110, +14% to +26%)

Boyd's normalized P/E discount narrows toward 14-15x as Norfolk construction progresses on schedule, Sky River Phase 2 opens successfully, and Las Vegas locals revenue growth accelerates on continued population inflows. The $362M remaining buyback authorization plus ongoing cash generation reduce shares outstanding by another 5-7% annually, driving EPS accretion toward $8.50+ in FY2027. Tax legislation tailwinds (OBBBA permanent bonus depreciation) support both cash taxes and consumer spending. At 13x $8.50 FY2027E EPS, the stock reaches $110.

### Bear Case (~$65-70, -20% to -26%)

A recession compresses gaming revenue 5-8% across the portfolio, triggering Midwest & South goodwill impairments of $200-400M and reducing EBITDAR to $1.15-1.20B. Norfolk construction costs escalate from $750M to $850M+, leverage rises above 3x, and the stock de-rates to 8x EPS. Consumer spending contraction deepens as oil stays above $95 and unemployment rises. Estimate revisions accelerate downward from the current -5% pace. At 9x a reduced $7.00 FY2026E EPS, the stock trades to $63.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

### Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY)
1. **Same-store revenue growth turns positive on a sequential quarterly basis** (specifically, Midwest & South and Downtown Las Vegas segments stabilizing or growing QoQ), confirming the consumer weakness is contained.
2. **FCF yield rises above 5%** through a combination of price decline (to ~$78-80) or capex reduction once Norfolk spending normalizes post-2027.
3. **Consumer confidence reverses its declining 3-month trend**, indicating the macro headwind is easing.

### Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID)
1. **Midwest & South goodwill impairment charges exceeding $200M**, confirming structural deterioration of franchise value.
2. **FY2026 EBITDAR coming in below $1.25B** (vs. implied ~$1.30B from consensus), indicating consumer softness is deepening beyond management expectations.
3. **Leverage exceeding 3.0x** due to Norfolk cost overruns or accelerating consumer weakness.

### Reassessment Timeline
Q1 2026 earnings (expected early May 2026) is the natural checkpoint. Norfolk construction updates and Cadence Crossing opening performance will provide critical data on growth execution. Consumer spending trends through summer 2026 will determine whether the macro headwind intensifies or stabilizes.

## Conclusion

Boyd Gaming is a competently managed regional gaming operator trading at a meaningful discount to peers on normalized earnings. The FanDuel transaction was value-accretive, the balance sheet is in good shape, and the buyback program is aggressive. The 4-quarter earnings beat streak and 207% 5-year TSR demonstrate management quality.

However, three conditions required for a constructive stance in consumer discretionary are not met: (1) same-store revenue growth is mixed-to-negative in key segments, (2) FCF yield of approximately 4.2-4.9% falls short of the 5% threshold given 2026 capex guidance, and (3) consumer confidence is at record lows and actively declining. Negative estimate revisions of -5.2% over 90 days confirm the earnings trajectory is deteriorating. The options market's backwardated term structure and rich IV corroborate near-term uncertainty.

At ~12x forward earnings with declining estimates and a difficult consumer backdrop, the valuation is reasonable but does not compensate for the identifiable risks. HOLD, with re-evaluation at Q1 2026 earnings when same-store trends and Norfolk/Cadence data become available.
