## Executive Summary

Sarepta Therapeutics is a commercial-stage rare disease company whose investment case has deteriorated across multiple vectors simultaneously. Two patient deaths from acute liver failure on ELEVIDYS forced the removal of the non-ambulatory population from the label, collapsing the gene therapy's addressable market. The ESSENCE confirmatory trial for VYONDYS 53 and AMONDYS 45 failed its primary endpoint, creating real risk of accelerated approval withdrawal under FDORA's expanded procedures. Management disclosed in the 10-K that the business "does not expect to generate sufficient cash flow from operations in the foreseeable future to service debt and make necessary capital expenditures."

The company's 2026 revenue guidance of $1.2-1.4B represents a 25-33% decline from FY2025's $1.86B in product revenue. Cash declined 37% in FY2025 to $954M, with $158.6M in convertible debt due 2027 and $893.4M due 2030. The Altman Z-Score sits at 1.17 (distress zone). While Q1 2026 showed an encouraging operating profit and the siRNA pipeline produced strong early data, these positives are insufficient to offset the structural impairment of the core franchise and the binary regulatory risks facing 2 of 4 approved products.

At $23/share ($2.4B market cap), the stock trades at ~1.7x FY2026 guided revenue — optically cheap for biotech, but appropriate given the probability of further franchise erosion, regulatory action, or refinancing distress.

## Company Overview

Sarepta commercializes four FDA-approved treatments for Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD): three exon-skipping PMO therapies (EXONDYS 51, VYONDYS 53, AMONDYS 45) and one gene therapy (ELEVIDYS). The company has a collaboration with Roche for ex-US ELEVIDYS commercialization and a strategic partnership with Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals covering seven siRNA programs targeting neuromuscular diseases including FSHD and DM1.

The company employs approximately 1,100 people after a 36% workforce reduction in July 2025 and is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

## Growth Analysis

**Core franchise (declining):** Revenue guidance of $1.2-1.4B in FY2026 confirms significant contraction. Non-ambulatory ELEVIDYS may never return commercially; Cohort 8 data (year-end 2026) represents the next decision point. PMO products face FDORA withdrawal risk if FDA determines ESSENCE data do not support efficacy.

**Pipeline optionality (early stage, 3-5 years from revenue):**
- SRP-1001 (FSHD): Phase 1 data showed 28.4 nM muscle concentration at Day 42 vs. Avidity's 4.5 nM — a potentially differentiated delivery profile. FSHD US patient population ~13,000.
- SRP-1003 (DM1): 50% placebo-adjusted DMPK reduction, exceeding preclinical predictions. DM1 US patient population ~30,000.
- These programs are genuinely promising but Phase 1/Phase 2 stage. Revenue generation is unlikely before 2029-2030.

**Bull case valuation:** If ELEVIDYS ambulatory franchise stabilizes at $400-500M, PMOs maintain $900M, and the market assigns 2.5x revenue + pipeline optionality, fair value approaches $30-35/share.

**Bear case valuation:** If PMO products face withdrawal proceedings and ELEVIDYS remains restricted to ambulatory-only with further payors restricting coverage, product revenue could decline to $600-800M with the current debt load consuming most equity value. Bear case: $8-12/share.

The forward P/E of ~5.9x appears cheap but relies on the CY2026 estimate of $3.92, which itself may be inflated by one-time collaboration items recognized in Q1. The Q2 estimate is already being cut by 52% from 90 days ago.

## Competitive Landscape

Sarepta retains first-mover advantage in DMD with four approved products. However, competitive position is eroding:

1. **Exon-skipping:** NS Pharma's Viltepso (exon 53) competes directly. Wave Life Sciences, Dyne Therapeutics, and Avidity Biosciences are developing next-generation approaches.

2. **Gene therapy:** The safety deaths have significantly damaged ELEVIDYS's competitive moat. Genethon (full-length dystrophin gene therapy) and Solid Biosciences are advancing alternatives. The boxed warning and restricted label create physician hesitancy.

3. **siRNA (emerging):** Sarepta's Arrowhead-derived programs show strong early muscle delivery data. However, Avidity and Dyne are in later-stage trials for DM1 and FSHD. The competitive window is narrowing.

A Guggenheim analyst characterized Sarepta as "in essence an early clinical-stage company" — a statement that accurately reflects the existential threats facing the commercial franchise and the early stage of the pipeline meant to replace it.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks (any one could materially impair equity value):**

1. **FDORA-enabled withdrawal of PMO products.** The ESSENCE trial failed. Under new expedited withdrawal procedures, FDA could move to revoke accelerated approvals for VYONDYS 53 and AMONDYS 45. These products generated ~$500M in FY2025. Timeline: FDA action possible within 6-12 months.

2. **Further ELEVIDYS safety events.** Cohort 8 (non-ambulatory with sirolimus) enrolling ~25 patients. Any additional death would likely terminate the non-ambulatory program permanently and could trigger broader restrictions on ambulatory use.

3. **Refinancing wall.** $159M due 2027, $893M due 2030. The $600M revolver has financial covenants (3.5x leverage, 2.5x coverage). Any further revenue decline could trigger covenant issues.

4. **Inventory write-down risk.** $914.7M inventory on balance sheet against $408M annualized ELEVIDYS run-rate. Significant further write-downs possible if ambulatory uptake doesn't accelerate.

5. **Securities litigation.** Class action filed June 2025 with derivative suits — potential settlement liability and management distraction.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($30-35):** ELEVIDYS ambulatory franchise stabilizes/grows with new patient starts. PMO products avoid withdrawal through supplemental NDA pathway. Cohort 8 data support non-ambulatory reintroduction. siRNA pipeline Phase 2 data validates the Arrowhead bet. Company refinances 2027 notes without dilution. Short interest unwinds on sequential positive data points, amplifying upside.

**Bear Case ($5-12):** FDA initiates withdrawal proceedings for VYONDYS 53/AMONDYS 45. ELEVIDYS revenue remains at depressed run-rate. Cohort 8 shows additional safety signals. The company cannot refinance 2027 notes at acceptable terms and is forced into dilutive equity issuance or restructuring. Inventory write-downs accelerate. Total product revenue falls below $800M.

**Base Case ($18-22):** Current franchise muddles through with ambulatory ELEVIDYS stabilizing at $400-500M/year, PMOs maintaining $800-900M pending regulatory resolution, and the siRNA pipeline advancing in early clinical stages. Refinancing occurs but at dilutive terms. The stock remains range-bound until either the pipeline produces Phase 2 data or regulatory risks resolve.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Multiple identifiable catalysts with specific timelines: (1) FDA action on PMO accelerated approvals — possible within 6-12 months based on FDORA procedures; (2) Cohort 8 safety data expected year-end 2026; (3) Q2/Q3 2026 earnings confirming continued ELEVIDYS revenue decline; (4) 2027 convertible note maturity creating refinancing uncertainty by mid-2026.

**Timing window:** 6-12 months. The FDORA withdrawal proceedings and Cohort 8 data represent the most defined catalysts, both in H2 2026.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** 28.8% SI with 7.7 days to cover represents VERY HIGH squeeze risk. This is the primary mechanical obstacle to shorting. Institutional ownership creates concentrated positioning. Any positive data readout (Cohort 8 interim, FDA meeting, siRNA update) could trigger a violent squeeze given the borrowed share base.

**Borrow and options signal:** Elevated near-term IV (96.5%) in backwardation confirms market pricing of stress. Put skew at 5.2% and P/C volume ratio of 1.51 support the bearish thesis directionally. However, the cost of carry on the borrow is likely elevated given SI levels.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Bear case $8-12 represents 48-65% downside. Bull case on squeeze/positive catalyst could reach $30-35 (30-52% upside from $23). Risk/reward: approximately 1.3:1 to 1.5:1 — below the 2:1 threshold when adjusted for squeeze risk.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** Despite the correct directional thesis, 28.8% short interest with 7.7 days to cover creates unacceptable squeeze risk. The trade is too crowded. The risk/reward adjusted for squeeze probability is insufficient. Avoid owning but do not actively short at these levels.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria (what would change the AVOID to a HOLD or BUY):**
1. FDA provides written guidance confirming it will NOT pursue withdrawal proceedings for VYONDYS 53/AMONDYS 45 — removes single largest overhang.
2. Cohort 8 data demonstrate safety and efficacy in non-ambulatory patients sufficient for label expansion — restores ELEVIDYS revenue trajectory.
3. Stock declines to $10-12 where the siRNA pipeline optionality alone justifies the valuation (discount to Arrowhead payments made).

**Timeline for headwinds:** 12-18 months minimum. FDORA proceedings, Cohort 8 data, and 2027 refinancing all resolve in this window. Until at least 2 of 3 resolve favorably, the risk profile remains asymmetrically negative.

## Conclusion

Sarepta faces compounding binary risks: product safety failures, confirmatory trial failure, regulatory withdrawal risk, and financial distress indicators — all occurring simultaneously. The Q1 2026 earnings beat and early siRNA data provide hope but cannot offset the structural deterioration of the core franchise and the company's own admission that cash flows are insufficient to service debt obligations.

The 28.8% short interest suggests the market broadly agrees with this negative assessment, which limits remaining downside somewhat and creates squeeze risk. However, owning the stock requires conviction that multiple binary risks resolve favorably — and the base rate for that outcome is low. The analyst consensus (8 Buy, 12 Hold, 5 Sell/Strong Sell) reflects appropriate skepticism, with the median target of $20 below the current $23 price.

This is a stock to avoid until the fundamental picture clarifies. The siRNA pipeline offers genuine long-term optionality, but paying for it through an equity instrument burdened with $1B+ in convertible debt, $250M in committed Arrowhead payments, and existential regulatory risk to the core franchise is an unfavorable risk/reward proposition.
