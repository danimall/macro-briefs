## Executive Summary

Enviri Corporation is in the final stages of a transformative transaction: the sale of its highest-quality asset, Clean Earth, to Veolia for $3.04 billion. Shareholders will receive $14.50-$16.50 per share in cash plus approximately 0.33 shares of "New Enviri," consisting of the troubled Harsco Environmental and persistently loss-making Rail segments. At $19.26, the stock prices in the cash component and assigns $2.76-$4.76/share (~$70-126M market cap) to the New Enviri stub — a valuation that, on a $140M EBITDA target at 2x leverage, implies the market assigns very limited value to the remaining operations.

The underlying businesses are structurally impaired. Enviri has generated net losses every year since FY2021, with consolidated operating margins at 2.4% for the first nine months of 2025. Rail has been catastrophic: fixed-price European contracts (Network Rail, Deutsche Bahn) produced repeated forward loss provisions, and the segment ran -10.9% operating margins. Harsco Environmental lost contracts worth $44M in revenue during 9M 2025 and saw margins compress from 8.4% to 3.6%. The company required two covenant amendments within nine months (February and November 2025) to avoid breaching its 5.00x net leverage limit, ultimately sitting at 4.82x with only $10.8M of EBITDA cushion.

This is a deal-arbitrage situation with limited upside and meaningful post-separation equity risk. The spread to the cash component is narrow, the New Enviri stub has no demonstrated path to profitability, and the options market prices imminent stress (63.3% IV in steep backwardation). We recommend avoiding this name.

## Company Overview

Enviri Corporation (formerly Harsco Corporation) operates through three segments: Harsco Environmental (steel mill services, $762M revenue for 9M 2025), Clean Earth (specialty waste processing, $732M), and Harsco Rail (track maintenance equipment, $192M). The company announced in November 2025 that it would sell Clean Earth to Veolia for $3.04B in cash and distribute $14.50-$16.50 per share to shareholders while spinning off the remaining HE and Rail segments as publicly traded "New Enviri."

Shareholders approved the transaction on May 4, 2026 with 99.54% support. HSR early termination was granted in March 2026. Expected close: mid-2026.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue:** Consolidated revenue declined 5.5% YoY for 9M 2025 ($1,685M vs. $1,784M). Clean Earth grew 4.7% on hazardous waste pricing/volume. HE declined 12.5% due to divestitures ($60M impact) and net contract losses ($44M). Rail declined 10.4%.

**Profitability:** The company has not generated a net profit since FY2020. Losses: FY2022 ($180M), FY2023 ($86M), FY2024 ($128M), 9M 2025 ($80M). Trailing EPS is -$2.03. Forward consensus expects losses of -$0.49 for FY2026 and breakeven ($0.01) for FY2027 — but with only 2 analysts covering, precision is low.

**Leverage:** Total debt of $1.54B against $63M operating cash flow (9M 2025) and negative free cash flow ($-40M for FY2025). Net leverage at 4.82x required two emergency covenant amendments. The credit agreement was modified in November 2025 specifically to accommodate the Clean Earth distribution, raising the covenant to 5.50x through most of 2026. Post-separation, New Enviri targets ~2.0x leverage on ~$140M EBITDA.

**Cash Flow Quality:** Enviri relies on accounts receivable securitization ($160M sold) and factoring ($15M) to maintain liquidity. Capex of $92M for 9M 2025 exceeded operating cash flow when annualized. The revolving credit facility was drawn $489M of $675M capacity.

**Balance Sheet:** Stockholders' equity has eroded to $361M. Goodwill of $758M and intangibles of $280M represent potential impairment risk. Accumulated other comprehensive losses of $(520M) are primarily pension-related. The 10-Q contains going-concern language noting liquidity is sufficient only factoring in "expected ability to meet required financial covenants."

## Growth Analysis

There is no organic growth story here. The sole high-quality, growing asset (Clean Earth, +4.7% YoY) is being sold. What remains:

- **Harsco Environmental:** Contracting revenue, lost contracts, divested sub-businesses. Management targets $170-180M EBITDA post-separation, but the trajectory shows 3.6% operating margins, down from 8.4% a year prior. Steel industry cyclicality and EU trade policy (anti-dumping duties, import quotas) create structural headwinds for the customer base.

- **Rail:** Persistently loss-making at -10.9% operating margins. The $19-26M annual EBITDA loss drags the combined entity. The company acknowledges it may seek to exit the Network Rail contract entirely, which "could result in a material loss."

New Enviri's $140M EBITDA target requires HE margin recovery from 3.6% back toward 8-9% and Rail losses not worsening — neither is supported by recent trends. The current quarter consensus estimate is -$0.29 (revised down 45% in the past 30 days), and full-year FY2026 estimates declined 42.8% over 90 days.

## Valuation Assessment

The stock at $19.26 should be decomposed:

- **Cash component:** $14.50-$16.50/share (midpoint $15.50)
- **New Enviri stub:** $2.76-$4.76/share implied (midpoint ~$3.76)
- **New Enviri implied EV:** At 82.7M shares × $3.76 + ~$280M net debt (2.0x × $140M EBITDA) = ~$591M EV
- **EV/EBITDA on $140M target:** ~4.2x

A 4.2x EV/EBITDA looks optically cheap versus the peer median P/E of 51.6x, but this comparison is misleading. New Enviri is loss-making on a net income basis, carries pension liabilities ($319M unrecognized actuarial losses), faces potential material losses from Rail contract exits, and has a management team in transition (CFO retiring, CEO departing). The appropriate comparable for this type of entity is distressed/turnaround valuation, not healthy industrial multiples.

Analyst targets range $19.00-$25.00 with a median of $21.00, implying ~9% upside to median. The $25 high target likely assumes some operating recovery at New Enviri. The $19 low target essentially values the stock at just the deal economics.

The current price offers minimal spread to the cash distribution (3-5 months away) with the risk that New Enviri equity trades down post-separation as forced selling from index removal and small-cap disinterest pressures the stub.

## Competitive Landscape

**Clean Earth** holds a strong position in U.S. hazardous waste processing — Veolia's $3.04B acquisition price (~10x segment EBITDA) validates this. However, this asset is leaving the company.

**Harsco Environmental** is the global market leader in steel mill services, operating in ~30 countries under long-term contracts. However, contract losses ($44M revenue impact) and business divestitures suggest the competitive moat is narrowing. EU trade policy could further pressure steel production volumes.

**Rail** operates in a niche market for highly-engineered track maintenance equipment. The fixed-price contract model has proven catastrophic in an inflationary environment, with vendor bankruptcies, design changes, and supply chain disruptions creating repeated forward loss provisions. The company lacks pricing power to offset these costs given the fixed-price nature of contracts.

The competitive trajectory is negative: the company is divesting its best asset, losing contracts in its second-best segment, and stuck with unprofitable fixed-price obligations in its weakest.

## Risk Assessment

**Deal-Break Risk (LOW):** HSR early termination granted, 99.54% shareholder approval, no identified regulatory hurdles. Probability: <5%.

**New Enviri Equity Destruction (MODERATE-HIGH):** Post-separation, the stub company inherits: (1) Rail losses of $19-26M/year EBITDA, (2) $319M unrecognized pension losses, (3) potential material loss from Network Rail contract exit, (4) management transition (CEO/CFO turnover), (5) Brazil criminal proceedings ($81M), (6) Bahrain environmental reserve ($28.8M), (7) asbestos claims (~17,000 pending). At 2x leverage on $140M EBITDA, any EBITDA miss creates immediate covenant pressure, replicating the pattern that drove this forced sale.

**Forced Selling Post-Separation (MODERATE):** Index removal, institutional mandate constraints on sub-$1B market cap entities, and low liquidity will likely pressure the New Enviri stub.

**Estimate Deterioration (ACTIVE):** Current quarter estimates down 45% in 30 days. Full-year down 42.8% in 90 days. Only 2 analysts cover the name.

### Options Market Signal

ATM IV of 63.3% is 22.9pp above historical volatility (40.4%), and the term structure is in steep backwardation (63.3% near-term vs. 37.5% at 6 months). The 6-month IV drop to 37.5% likely reflects the expected deal close eliminating uncertainty in that timeframe. Put/call skew is call-heavy (-30.8%), suggesting speculative interest in upside beyond the deal price rather than protective positioning. ## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($21-25):** Deal closes on schedule at the high end of the cash range ($16.50/share). New Enviri achieves its $140M EBITDA target, Rail losses moderate through successful Deutsche Bahn/SBB completions, and the market assigns a 4-5x EV/EBITDA to a recovering industrial services company. Total value: $16.50 + ($5-8 stub) = $21.50-$24.50.

**Bear Case ($15-17):** Deal closes at the low end ($14.50/share). New Enviri immediately faces pressure: HE margin recovery stalls, Rail generates material loss on Network Rail exit, forced selling depresses the stub to $1-2/share. Total value: $14.50 + $1-2 = $15.50-$16.50.

**Base Case ($18-20):** Deal closes at midpoint ($15.50). New Enviri stub trades at $2-4 reflecting skepticism about turnaround execution. Total value: $17.50-$19.50. At $19.26, the stock is fairly valued for this outcome with limited upside.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** Post-separation forced selling as index funds and institutions with market-cap/profitability screens dump the New Enviri stub (expected mid-2026). Additionally, Q4 2025 earnings (reported February 24, 2026) showed the stock dropped from $19.11 to $17.93 on the report day — subsequent quarters for New Enviri may produce similar negative reactions given deteriorating estimates.

**Timing window:** 3-5 months to deal close, with stub pressure materializing immediately post-separation. Carry cost is moderate given the timeline.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** SI at 16.2% with 8.1 days to cover is elevated but not extreme. The deal mechanics create a natural ceiling (why pay significantly above deal value?), limiting squeeze potential. The $19.985 52-week high provides technical resistance.

**Borrow and options signal:** Options IV in steep backwardation suggests near-term uncertainty already priced. Cost of borrowing may be elevated given SI levels. The call-heavy skew (-30.8%) suggests speculative rather than informed institutional positioning.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** At $19.26, downside to bear case ($15.50-$16.50) represents 14-20% decline. Upside risk to $21-22 represents 9-14%. Risk/reward: approximately 1.5:1 favoring the short. However, the deal close introduces a definitive catalyst resolution that could trigger short-covering.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The deal mechanics create a near-term floor ($14.50 cash minimum), the timing is uncertain (exact close date unknown), and the 1.5:1 reward/risk is below the 2:1 threshold. The correct positioning is to avoid rather than actively short. Post-separation, the New Enviri stub may become a short candidate if it trades above 5x EV/EBITDA on the $140M target.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria:**
1. New Enviri stub trades below 3x EV/EBITDA ($140M target × 3 = $420M EV; subtract ~$280M net debt = $140M equity / 82.7M shares = ~$1.70/share for stub) — at that level, downside is minimal even if Rail generates further losses
2. Rail segment achieves breakeven for two consecutive quarters post-separation, demonstrating cost management under new structure
3. HE operating margins recover to >6% for two consecutive quarters

**Timeline:** The strategic uncertainty resolves mid-2026 with deal close. New Enviri's operational trajectory should become clearer by Q4 2026 earnings (reported ~February 2027). Rail contract headwinds persist until Network Rail (67% complete as of Sept 2025) and Deutsche Bahn (56% complete) finish, likely 2027-2028.

## Conclusion

Enviri at $19.26 offers limited risk-adjusted return. The deal close provides a floor ($14.50-$16.50 cash), but the current price already captures most of that value plus a modest premium for the New Enviri stub. The remaining entity inherits chronic operating losses, pension liabilities, legal exposures, and a management team in transition. Estimate revisions are sharply negative. The options market's steep backwardation and elevated short interest reflect informed skepticism about post-separation value creation.

There is no identifiable growth catalyst on a 12-month horizon. HE margins are contracting. Rail is loss-making with potential material charges. The $140M EBITDA target requires a reversal in trajectory that has not yet materialized. At the current price, investors are paying roughly fair value for deal economics and getting a structurally challenged industrial company for free — which may be exactly what it's worth.

**Recommendation: AVOID.**
