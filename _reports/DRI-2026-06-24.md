## Executive Summary

Darden is the largest full-service restaurant operator in the U.S., running 2,196 company-owned units across 11 brands anchored by Olive Garden (944 units, ~43% of sales) and LongHorn Steakhouse (608 units). The operating story has improved meaningfully over the past three quarters: blended same-restaurant sales accelerated from a modest +2.0% in FY2025 to +4.4% nine-month FY2026, with management raising full-year SRS guidance to ~4.5%.
