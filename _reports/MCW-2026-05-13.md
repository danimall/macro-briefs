## Executive Summary

Mister Car Wash is the largest car wash operator in the United States with 549 locations and ~2.5 million Unlimited Wash Club subscription members. The underlying business is operationally healthy, generating $1.05B in revenue with expanding EBITDA margins (34.8% in Q1 2026) and consistent mid-single-digit growth.

MCW is no longer a viable equity investment. On February 17, 2026, the company entered a definitive merger agreement to be taken private by controlling shareholder Leonard Green & Partners at $7.00 per share in cash. LGP owns ~66.7% of outstanding shares, has binding voting support agreements, and the deal is expected to close in H1 2026. The stock currently trades at $7.07, offering approximately -1% return to the deal price, with material downside risk (~25-35%) if the deal fails for any reason.

All 12 covering analysts have moved to HOLD ratings, reflecting the merger-arb nature of the situation. Upside is capped at deal price, and the controlling shareholder has the votes to push the transaction through. Minority shareholders frustrated by the $7.00 price (vs. $15 IPO) can pursue appraisal rights, but that is a legal process, not an investment thesis.

## Company Overview

Mister Car Wash operates 549 car wash locations across 21 states, with 487 Express Exterior locations and 62 Interior Cleaning locations. The company's competitive advantage centers on its Unlimited Wash Club (UWC), a monthly subscription that now represents 76% of total wash sales (up from 73% a year ago), providing predictable recurring revenue.

Key operating metrics as of Q1 2026:
- 2.5 million UWC members (+9% from year-end 2025)
- Comparable store sales growth: +3.9% (decelerating from +6.0% in Q1 2025)
- 549 locations (up from 518 a year ago, +6% unit growth)
- Revenue: $277.9M (+6.2% YoY)

The company was acquired by Leonard Green & Partners in 2014, taken public in June 2021 at $15/share (with LGP retaining 67%), and is now being re-privatized at $7.00/share.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue & Profitability:**
- FY 2025 revenue: $1.052B (+5.7% YoY)
- Q1 2026 revenue: $277.9M (+6.2% YoY)
- Adjusted EBITDA: $96.7M in Q1 2026 (34.8% margin, up from 32.7%)
- Net income: $34.2M in Q1 2026 (+26.6% YoY)
- Operating income margin: 19.0% (FY 2025)

**Cash Flow:**
- Operating cash flow: $285.7M (FY 2025)
- Capital expenditures: $255.4M (primarily greenfield development)
- Reported FCF: $30.3M (2.9% margin — misleadingly low)
- FCF excluding growth capex: ~$296M annualized ($74.1M/quarter in Q1 2026)

The distinction between reported FCF and maintenance FCF matters. The business generates strong cash flow from existing operations; the capex-heavy profile reflects voluntary growth investment in new locations that take ~3 years to mature.

**Balance Sheet:**
- Cash: $28.5M
- Long-term debt: $796.9M (First Lien Term Loan, SOFR + ~2.75%, maturing March 2031)
- Goodwill: $1.13B (36% of total assets)
- Net debt/Adjusted EBITDA: ~2.0x
- Operating lease obligations: $1.62B undiscounted
- Revolver: $300M fully available

The balance sheet improved materially during the public period — approximately $128M of debt was repaid in FY2025, and interest expense dropped 23% YoY in Q1 2026 following repricing. Net leverage at ~2x is manageable for a subscription business with 35% EBITDA margins.

## Growth Analysis

Organic growth drivers are intact but decelerating:
- UWC membership growing ~9% annually
- Same-store sales growth decelerating: 6.0% → 3.9% (Q1 2025 → Q1 2026)
- Unit growth of ~6% annually via greenfield development
- Revenue CAGR (2022-2025): ~6.3%

The growth profile is real but low-single-digit at the per-location level. The primary growth mechanism is new unit openings ($165M+ annualized greenfield investment) combined with gradual membership penetration increases. This is a steady-state compounder with mid-single-digit topline growth.

Estimate revisions are slightly negative: current quarter estimates revised down 3.9% (30-day) and 4.2% (90-day). Next quarter revised down 6.3% over 90 days. Full-year estimates are approximately flat (+0.7%). Fundamentals are stable but not accelerating.

## Valuation Assessment

The valuation question is moot given the pending merger. At $7.07, the stock trades at:
- 21.4x trailing EPS
- ~14.7x forward P/E on FY26 consensus of $0.48
- ~8.0x EV/EBITDA on ~$387M annual adjusted EBITDA
- The $7.00 deal price implies an enterprise value of ~$3.1B

Relative to the underlying business quality (76% recurring revenue, 35% EBITDA margins, market leadership, low churn), 8x EBITDA is arguably cheap — which is precisely the complaint of minority shareholders and the basis for ongoing legal investigations. Comparable subscription-based consumer businesses (Planet Fitness, etc.) trade at 12-18x EBITDA.

The stock is trading at deal price. There is no valuation-based equity thesis. The market has priced this as a merger-arb at $7.00 +/- a few cents.

## Competitive Landscape

MCW is the largest branded car wash operator in the U.S. by location count. Its competitive advantages include:
- Scale benefits in procurement, labor management, and brand awareness
- First-mover advantage in the subscription car wash model at national scale
- 76% recurring revenue provides visibility and reduces weather sensitivity
- Real estate portfolio of 549 locations represents substantial barriers to replication

Industry headwinds include significant PE capital inflows creating competitive build-out pressure. Operators like Whistle Express, Take 5, and Zips have grown aggressively, potentially saturating some metro markets. MCW acquired 5 Whistle Express locations in Texas in October 2025, suggesting consolidation opportunities exist.

The competitive position is strong and stable — MCW has the scale to absorb new entrants over time — but the public equity thesis is irrelevant given the merger.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risk: Asymmetric Merger Outcome**
- If deal closes (base case, ~90-95% probability): shareholders receive $7.00. Return from $7.07 is approximately -1%.
- If deal fails (5-10% probability): stock likely falls to $4.50-$5.50. The stock was at $6.01 on the trading day before the merger announcement. Removing the deal premium could mean 20-35% downside.
- Expected value calculation: ~(-1% × 92.5%) + (-30% × 7.5%) = -3.2% expected return. Unfavorable risk/reward.

**Secondary Risks:**
- Litigation investigations are unlikely to block the deal given LGP's 67% voting control
- Regulatory (antitrust) risk is minimal for a controlling shareholder going-private transaction
- Appraisal proceedings could theoretically yield more than $7.00, but Delaware courts have recently been skeptical of appraisal claims above deal price in arm's-length transactions

**Why the Deal Almost Certainly Closes:**
- LGP controls 67% of shares — binding Support Agreement ensures deal passes shareholder vote
- Special committee approved with independent financial advisor opinion
- No financing contingency (LGP has committed equity)
- Regulatory complexity is low (single industry, single country, buyer already controls)

**Short Interest:** 8.9% of float, 3.9 days to cover. Modest short interest likely reflects traders shorting the ~$0.07 premium to deal price (merger-arb position), not a fundamental bearish thesis.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case (Deal Breaks + Appraisal Premium):**
If the merger fails or if appraisal proceedings yield above $7.00, shareholders could see modest upside. The underlying business at 8x EBITDA with 35% margins and mid-single-digit growth is arguably worth 10-12x EBITDA ($8.50-$10.00/share). However, the probability of deal failure is very low (~5-10%), and appraisal proceedings are uncertain, costly, and multi-year.

**Bear Case (Deal Closes or Fails):**
If the deal closes, you receive $7.00 against a $7.07 entry — a loss. If it fails, you face 20-35% downside as the deal premium evaporates and the stock reverts to pre-announcement levels. Both outcomes are negative from current price.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** The merger spread is only ~$0.07 ($7.07 vs. $7.00 deal price). There is no significant short catalyst because the stock is already at deal price. The only short thesis would be a belief that the deal fails (extremely unlikely given 67% sponsor control) or trading the $0.07 premium decay, which offers negligible reward.

**Timing window:** Deal expected to close H1 2026. Spread is too thin to warrant the carrying costs of a short position.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** 8.9% SI with 3.9 days to cover — modest. No squeeze concern given tight trading range around $7.00.

**Borrow and options signal:** Options P/C OI ratio of 0.06 indicates negligible put positioning. The market views this as a done deal.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Even if the deal price were somehow revised upward (highly unlikely given LGP's control), the upside on a short is capped at maybe 5-7%. Not worth the risk.

**Verdict: NOT SHORTABLE.** The stock trades within $0.07 of deal price with >90% close probability. The risk/reward for either longs or shorts is deeply unfavorable. This is dead money.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Re-entry criteria:** MCW returns to consideration only if (a) the merger agreement is terminated, allowing the stock to trade on fundamentals again, at which point the recurring revenue model + market leadership would warrant fresh analysis at $4.50-$5.50, or (b) a competing bid emerges above $7.00 (extremely unlikely given LGP's controlling stake prevents a hostile approach).

**Timeline:** The merger is expected to close within 1-3 months. Once closed, the stock will be delisted. There is no investment horizon here.

## Conclusion

MCW's underlying business is well-run: subscription-based recurring revenue (76% of sales), expanding margins, market leadership in a fragmented industry, and consistent growth. Under different circumstances — particularly at a price below $5.50 without a pending buyout — this would warrant serious consideration as a quality consumer franchise at a reasonable valuation.

However, the definitive merger agreement at $7.00/share by the 67% controlling shareholder eliminates any equity thesis. The stock offers approximately -1% return to deal price with meaningful (if low-probability) downside if something goes wrong. All 12 analysts rate it HOLD, consensus price target clusters at $7.00-$7.06, and the price has traded in a $6.96-$7.16 range for three months.

There is no investment here — only merger-arb with unfavorable risk/reward from current price. AVOID.
