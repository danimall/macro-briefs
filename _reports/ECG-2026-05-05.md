## Executive Summary

Everus Construction Group is a newly independent U.S. specialty contractor (spun off from MDU Resources in October 2024) positioned squarely in the data center construction boom. FY2025 revenue grew 31.5% to $3.75 billion, operating margins expanded to 7.1% from 6.7%, and backlog reached a record $3.23 billion. The company has beaten EPS estimates by 39-78% in each of its four reported quarters, demonstrating consistent operational execution.

The investment challenge is entirely valuation and concentration risk. At $168.32, ECG trades at ~36x forward earnings and ~42.5x FY2025 EPS — a level that embeds continued high-teens growth and flawless execution. The stock has appreciated 271% from its May 2025 low and now trades materially above every published analyst price target (consensus mean $137, high $160). A single customer represented 17% of FY2025 revenue (up from <10% in FY2024), creating binary risk that would be more tolerable at a lower valuation.

The growth thesis remains intact — AI infrastructure spending is structural, backlog provides 12-month visibility, and the skilled labor scarcity creates a durable moat. But the stock has moved ahead of fundamentals. A pullback toward the $115-130 range would offer a risk/reward profile more consistent with the underlying business quality.

## Company Overview

Everus Construction Group provides specialty contracting services across two segments: Electrical & Mechanical (E&M, ~78% of revenue) and Transmission & Distribution (T&D, ~23%). The company operates through 15 wholly owned subsidiaries using 19 local brands, serving utilities, data centers, commercial/industrial, and institutional customers nationwide.

The workforce of ~9,400 employees is approximately 85% unionized under IBEW and other trade unions, providing reliable access to skilled electricians in a severely constrained labor market. Everus ranks 12th on Engineering News-Record's 2025 Top 600 Specialty Contractors and 5th on EC&M's 2025 Top 50 Electrical Contractors.

The company became independent on October 31, 2024, when MDU Resources completed its spinoff. It trades on the NYSE and is still establishing its institutional investor base — a dynamic that has contributed to re-rating velocity as dedicated small/mid-cap investors discover the story.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue & Profitability:**
- FY2025 revenue: $3.75 billion (+31.5% YoY)
- E&M segment: $2.92B (+43.8%), driven overwhelmingly by data center construction
- T&D segment: $864M (+1.4%), stable infrastructure maintenance
- Gross margin: 12.1% (vs. 11.9% prior year)
- Operating income: $264.8M (+39.4%), operating margin 7.1%
- Net income: $201.8M (+40.7%), net margin 5.4%
- EPS: $3.95 diluted

Margin expansion during 31.5% revenue growth is unusual for construction businesses and reflects strong project selection, negotiating power, and a shift toward cost-reimbursable contracts (43% of revenue vs. 34% in FY2024).

**Cash Flow & Balance Sheet:**
- Operating cash flow: $156.8M (OCF/Net Income conversion: 78%)
- Free cash flow: $100M (FCF yield on market cap: ~1.2%)
- CapEx: $66.8M (1.8% of revenue — capital-light model)
- Cash: $170.5M
- Long-term debt: $285M (Term Loan due 2029)
- Net debt/EBITDA: ~0.4x (well below 3.0x covenant)
- Current ratio: 1.76x
- Altman Z-Score: 3.92 (safe zone)

The balance sheet is conservatively levered with ample liquidity. The $225M revolving credit facility is essentially untapped ($228.2M available capacity). Working capital increased with revenue growth, which compressed OCF conversion modestly — expected for a rapidly growing contractor.

**Backlog:**
- Total backlog: $3.23B (E&M: $2.84B, T&D: $385M)
- Up 16% from $2.78B year-end 2024
- $2.59B expected recognized within 12 months (80% of backlog)

Backlog-to-revenue ratio of 0.86x provides solid visibility, though it's lower than multi-year utility infrastructure peers because E&C data center projects cycle faster.

**Earnings Quality Note:**
Favorable cumulative estimate changes contributed $113.7M to FY2025 revenue (3.0%), with a $83.7M pre-tax income impact. This is standard for percentage-of-completion accounting but represents roughly 42% of net income from prior-period estimate true-ups rather than purely current-period activity.

## Growth Analysis

**Secular Drivers:**
1. **Data Center Construction:** The dominant growth engine. Hyperscaler AI/cloud CapEx flows directly into electrical infrastructure installation. ECG's E&M commercial revenue surged $876.8M in FY2025 from data center work.
2. **Grid Modernization:** T&D benefits from utility hardening, undergrounding, and electrification investments — lower growth but recurring.
3. **M&A Optionality:** 25+ historical acquisitions demonstrate capability. Tax matters agreement restricts activity until October 2026; post-restriction, M&A re-engagement becomes a catalyst.
4. **Labor Moat:** 85% union workforce provides scalable access to electricians that competitors struggle to match.

**Estimate Trajectory:**
- Current year EPS consensus: $4.17 (up 5.2% over 90 days, up 0.5% over 30 days)
- Next year EPS consensus: $4.60 (up 6.2% over 90 days, -0.3% over 30 days)
- Four-quarter track record: beats of 41%, 39%, 78%, and 73% — suggesting consensus is systematically too low

The revision trajectory is positive but decelerating, with 30-day moves smaller than 90-day moves for next year. This suggests the beat-and-raise cycle may be moderating as analysts catch up to actual performance.

## Valuation Assessment

At $168.32:
- Trailing P/E: 42.5x (on $3.95 EPS)
- Forward P/E: 36.6x (on $4.60 NTM consensus)
- Current-year P/E: 40.4x (on $4.17 FY2026E)
- Price/Sales: ~2.3x (on $3.75B revenue)
- FCF Yield: ~1.2%
- EV/EBITDA: ~27x (on ~$320M EBITDA)

**Peer Context:**
Peer median P/E is 41.4x, but this is heavily skewed by VICR at 86x. The most comparable E&C peers (TTEK at 24x, Acuity at 21x) trade at substantial discounts. Specialty contractor comps like EMCOR and Comfort Systems typically trade at 18-25x forward earnings. ECG's 36.6x forward P/E commands a 45-100% premium to direct peers — justified only if current growth rates sustain.

**Price vs. Targets:**
ECG trades at $168.32 against a consensus mean target of $137.20 and a high target of $160.00. The stock is 5% above the highest published analyst target. When a stock exceeds every analyst's bullish case, either all analysts are behind or the market is ahead of itself.

Given the 40%+ earnings beat pattern, analysts are likely still catching up. But even applying a generous 25-30x multiple to $5.00-5.50 in potential FY2027 EPS (assuming continued 10-15% growth), fair value ranges from $125-165. The current price implies ~$5.50+ in earnings at 30x or $4.60 at 36x — both requiring sustained premium multiples on a construction business.

## Competitive Landscape

**Strengths:**
- Data center electrical expertise positions ECG as a preferred vendor for hyperscalers
- Union labor access provides reliable scaling in a tight skilled-trade market
- Capital-light model (1.8% CapEx/revenue) generates high returns on equity (32%)
- Record backlog provides revenue visibility
- Shift to cost-reimbursable contracts reduces fixed-price execution risk

**Trajectory:**
Market share is clearly expanding. The 31.5% organic growth rate far exceeds industry growth, indicating share gains. The 16% backlog growth on top of 31.5% revenue growth suggests the pipeline is still accelerating relative to delivery capacity.

**Risks to Position:**
- Hyperscaler data center spending is inherently lumpy; customers may internalize construction capabilities or rotate vendors
- Quanta Services, EMCOR, and MasTec are larger, better-capitalized competitors pursuing the same opportunities
- Customer concentration (17% single customer) means competitive displacement at one account creates outsized impact

## Risk Assessment

**Critical: Customer Concentration**
One customer grew from <10% to 17% of revenue in a single year ($637M). Two E&M customers individually represent 21% and 10% of segment revenue. This concentration is among the most extreme in the specialty contracting space and creates a genuine binary outcome — if the primary customer pauses data center construction in ECG's geographies, the revenue impact would be immediate and material.

**Elevated: Data Center Cyclicality**
Management explicitly acknowledges "uncertainties surrounding the longevity of this type of work." While AI infrastructure spending appears structural for the next 2-3 years, individual project timelines are 12-24 months. A deceleration in hyperscaler CapEx announcements (which have not occurred but remain possible) would immediately affect forward backlog.

**Moderate: Valuation Risk**
At 36x forward earnings and 271% appreciation in under a year, any deceleration in growth or earnings surprises gets severely punished. The December 12, 2025 price action ($101 to $88 in one session, -13%) demonstrates this sensitivity.

**Moderate: Tariff Exposure**
Copper, aluminum, steel, and electrical components face tariff uncertainty. Not all contracts include escalation provisions. Input cost pressure could compress margins.

**Lower: Balance Sheet/Liquidity**
Net debt/EBITDA of 0.4x with ample revolver capacity. Not a concern at present growth rates. The Altman Z-Score of 3.92 confirms minimal financial distress risk.

**$31.3M Payment Dispute:**
Unresolved for 2+ years. Represents ~15% of annual net income. Outcome uncertain despite management's "probable collection" assertion.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($190-210, 13-25% upside):**
- Data center construction remains at current intensity through 2027
- Customer concentration reflects preferred vendor status rather than dependency
- Consensus EPS reaches $5.50+ by FY2027 as analysts continue catching up
- M&A re-engagement post-October 2026 adds inorganic growth optionality
- Multiple sustains at 35-38x as growth premium remains justified
- Spinoff re-rating continues as institutional discovery broadens

**Bear Case ($95-115, 32-44% downside):**
- Primary customer pauses or reduces data center construction activity
- Hyperscaler CapEx cycle decelerates in 2027 as initial AI build-out completes
- Input cost inflation (tariffs + elevated commodity prices) compresses margins
- Stock re-rates to peer-appropriate 20-25x forward earnings on $4.50 EPS = $90-112
- December 2025 type selloff (-13% in one session) recurs as momentum unwinds

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- Stock pulls back to $115-130 range (20-25x forward earnings on current consensus), providing sufficient margin of safety
- Customer diversification: top customer falls below 12% of revenue with no revenue decline (proving growth isn't single-customer dependent)
- FY2026 backlog growth exceeds 20% and consensus FY2027 EPS reaches $5.50+

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- Top customer revenue declines >20% sequentially without offsetting new awards
- Operating margins contract below 6.0% for two consecutive quarters
- Backlog declines sequentially for two consecutive quarters
- Stock exceeds $200 without corresponding earnings acceleration (implying >40x on current-year)

**Reassessment timeline:**
- Next earnings release (Q2 FY2026, likely August 2026): evaluate backlog trajectory and customer concentration trends
- October 2026: Tax matters agreement expires, opening M&A and buyback optionality
- Any hyperscaler CapEx guidance changes (quarterly earnings of MSFT, GOOG, META, AMZN)

## Conclusion

Everus Construction Group is a high-quality business benefiting from structural AI infrastructure demand, with execution capability validated by four consecutive massive earnings beats and expanding margins during rapid growth. The competitive moat from union labor access and electrical infrastructure expertise is real and widening.

The problem is the price. At $168.32, the stock trades above every published analyst target, at 36x forward earnings on a construction business, with 17% revenue concentration in a single customer that appeared in the past year. The upside/downside asymmetry is unfavorable at current levels: bull case offers 13-25% upside while bear case risks 32-44% downside if the data center cycle moderates or customer concentration crystallizes.

HOLD with a growth thesis intact. The underlying business deserves premium multiples among specialty contractors, but the current price assumes sustained 30%+ growth and flawless execution with no margin for error. A re-entry point in the $115-130 range would offer compelling risk/reward for a position sized at 2-3%.
