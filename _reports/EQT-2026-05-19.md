## Executive Summary

EQT Corporation is the largest natural gas producer in the United States, operating an integrated upstream-midstream platform concentrated in the Appalachian Basin. Following the Equitrans Midstream merger (July 2024) and a Q1 2026 that produced $1.83B in free cash flow and $2.1B of debt reduction, the company has materially de-risked its balance sheet and is approaching its long-term $5B net debt target. At approximately 12.0x forward earnings and a robust FCF yield, EQT trades at a discount to its peer median P/E of 15x despite superior scale, integration, and growth visibility from LNG offtake contracts and potential data center power demand.

The stock's recent pullback from its March 2026 high of $68.24 to $57.45 (current) reflects softening near-term natural gas price expectations — next-quarter estimates have declined 13.8% over the past 30 days. However, this price weakness creates an improved entry point given that full-year 2026 estimates remain essentially stable (+1.4% revision over 30 days) and the structural demand story (LNG + data centers) extends well beyond current-quarter gas pricing. The company's integrated structure, with midstream revenues providing a counter-cyclical cushion, reduces the commodity beta that historically made pure-play gas producers uninvestable through price cycles.

## Company Overview

EQT operates across three segments: Upstream (natural gas production of ~2,000 Bcfe/year across the Marcellus and Utica shales), Gathering (2,945 miles of pipeline infrastructure), and Transmission (including the Mountain Valley Pipeline with 2.0 Bcf/day capacity). The company holds 28.0 Tcfe of proved reserves across ~2.3 million gross acres, providing 30+ years of drilling inventory at current pace. The July 2024 Equitrans merger transformed EQT from a commodity-dependent E&P into an integrated producer with annuity-like midstream cash flows, materially reducing the risk profile.

Key strategic assets include: 4.3 Bcf/day firm pipeline takeaway capacity, 4.5 MTPA LNG offtake agreements commencing 2030-2031, and two firm sales agreements to deliver and sell up to 1.2 Bcf/day using MVP Mainline capacity targeting 2027, subject to unsatisfied conditions related to the Transco Southeast Supply Enhancement project.

## Financial Analysis

**Income Statement:** FY2025 total operating revenues were $8.6B (including midstream contributions post-merger), with net income of $2.04B ($3.31 EPS). Q1 2026 demonstrated substantial earnings power at elevated gas prices: $3.38B revenue (+57% YoY), $1.49B net income (adjusted EPS of $2.33, beating consensus by approximately 8%).

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow of $5.13B in FY2025 and $1.83B FCF in Q1 2026 alone (a single-quarter record). The FCF margin of approximately 32% in FY2025 reflects the operational leverage of the integrated model. Capital expenditures came in below guidance in Q1 2026 ($608M vs. full-year guidance of $2.65-2.85B), suggesting discipline in deployment.

**Balance Sheet:** Net debt declined from $7.8B (Dec 2025) to $5.7B (Mar 2026) — a $2.1B reduction in one quarter. Debt/equity of approximately 0.33 as of year-end 2025 (improving to approximately 0.24 by Q1 2026) reflects a conservatively leveraged balance sheet for a midstream-integrated company. Fitch upgraded the credit rating to BBB (from BBB-) following Q1 results. The long-term target of $5B net debt is now within 1-2 quarters of achievement, which management has indicated would trigger increased share repurchases.

**Unit Economics:** LOE of $0.09/Mcfe is among the lowest in the industry. Average realized price was $3.19/Mcfe in FY2025 and approximately $5/Mcfe in Q1 2026 (reflecting winter premiums). The company's hedging position is deliberately light — collars covering 2.5 Bcf/d in Q1 with a $4.25 floor/$6.29 ceiling, declining to 0.1 Bcf/d by Q1 2027 — indicating management conviction in sustained pricing above their breakeven.

**Liquidity Concern:** Current ratio of 0.76 as of December 31, 2025 (declining to approximately 0.66 by March 31, 2026) is technically below 1, but this is a function of capital structure management (utilizing revolving credit facilities rather than holding idle cash). EQT disclosed approximately $3.8B of available liquidity as of Q1 2026. With $5.1B annual operating cash flow and ample revolver capacity, this is a structural feature of the business model rather than a liquidity risk.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-Term (2026-2027):**

- Production guidance of 2,275–2,375 Bcfe for 2026 with efficiency gains from combo-development
- 90-day current-year estimate revisions of +59.3% confirm improving fundamentals

**Medium-Term (2027-2030):**
- Data center power demand: EQT has entered two firm sales agreements to deliver and sell up to 1.2 Bcf/day using MVP Mainline capacity, targeting 2027 in-service. These agreements are subject to unsatisfied conditions related to the Transco Southeast Supply Enhancement project's in-service date, and pricing terms have not been publicly disclosed. If realized, these volumes could represent a significant incremental revenue stream.
- MVP Boost project (0.6 Bcf/day additional capacity, mid-2028 in-service, $400-540M cost) extends the infrastructure moat
- Olympus Energy acquisition (completed July 2025) adds 1,768 Bcfe proved reserves and production capacity

**Long-Term (2030+):**
- LNG offtake of 4.5 MTPA + 2.0 MTPA tolling under 20-year contracts provides multi-decade demand visibility, though 1.0 MTPA of the offtake and the 2.0 MTPA tolling commitment relate to projects that had not reached final investment decision as of the latest 10-K filing, creating timing and realization risk
- Secular growth in U.S. LNG exports (structural displacement of coal globally, European energy security requirements) supports sustained gas demand above historical levels

**Key Headwind:** FY2027 estimates have declined 15.2% over 90 days, reflecting uncertainty about gas price sustainability beyond the current winter premium period. The forward gas curve implies lower prices by late 2026/2027, creating a risk that current-year strength doesn't fully repeat.

## Valuation Assessment

At $57.45, EQT trades at:
- Approximately 12.0x forward P/E (on $4.79 FY2026 consensus)
- A robust FCF yield based on Q1 2026's $1.83B quarterly free cash flow; adjusting for seasonality, $4-5B annual FCF seems achievable at current pricing, implying an annualized FCF yield in the low-to-mid teens percentage range
- A discount of approximately 20% to peer median P/E of 15.0x
- 16% below 52-week high of $68.24
- 18% below analyst mean target of $70.00

The discount to peers is partially justified by EQT's pure-gas exposure (vs. oil-weighted peers with more stable price dynamics), but the magnitude appears excessive given: (1) the integrated model providing midstream cash flow cushion, (2) the deleveraging trajectory enabling buybacks, and (3) the contracted LNG demand providing visibility that pure-play gas peers lack.

Applying a 12x multiple to FY2026 consensus EPS of $4.79 yields $57.50 (roughly current price). Applying the peer median 15x yields $72 (consistent with analyst consensus). The gap between these reflects the market's skepticism about FY2026 earnings sustainability — a skepticism that the structural demand catalysts (LNG, data centers) should progressively erode over the next 12 months.

## Competitive Landscape

EQT's competitive advantages are structural and widening:

1. **Scale:** Largest U.S. gas producer with the lowest unit operating costs ($0.09/Mcfe LOE). Scale provides negotiating leverage in LNG contracts, pipeline capacity, and service costs.

2. **Vertical Integration:** Management characterizes EQT as the only large-scale integrated natural gas producer-midstream company in the U.S. This eliminates third-party infrastructure dependency, synchronizes development timing, and captures margin across the value chain.

3. **Infrastructure Control:** 4.3 Bcf/day firm takeaway + MVP Mainline (2.0 Bcf/day through 2044) provides market access that competitors cannot replicate. The MVP took over a decade and billions in overruns to complete — this infrastructure is effectively irreplaceable.

4. **Data Center Positioning:** EQT's Appalachian location provides proximity to the PJM grid and the Virginia data center corridor. Combined with integrated pipeline infrastructure, this creates a potential ability to serve gas-fired power generation for hyperscaler facilities, though the firm sales agreements remain subject to conditions.

5. **TSR Track Record:** Based on the company's proxy statement, a $100 investment in EQT grew to $448.69 versus $323.98 for the peer group over the measured period — sustained operational outperformance.

Competitive risk comes primarily from potential pipeline capacity additions by Appalachian peers (Range Resources, Antero), though permitting timelines and capital requirements create multi-year barriers. Longer-term, renewable energy displacement of gas-fired generation remains a secular threat, though near-term baseload requirements and intermittency challenges extend gas's relevance through 2040+.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Natural Gas Price Volatility (HIGH):** NYMEX ranged from $2.65 to $9.86/MMBtu in 2025. While the integrated model dampens volatility, a sustained move below $2.50/MMBtu would compress FCF significantly and potentially trigger production curtailments (as occurred in 2024 with 107 Bcfe curtailed). The declining hedge book means more earnings exposure to spot pricing by 2027.

**2. Estimate Revision Deceleration:** Next-quarter estimates down 13.8% (30d) and next-year estimates down 15.2% (90d). While current-year remains stable, the shift from positive to negative forward revisions could cap multiple expansion in the near term.

**3. Geographic Concentration:** 100% Appalachian Basin exposure creates vulnerability to regional supply-demand imbalances, basis blow-outs (Appalachian gas typically trades $0.40-0.50 below Henry Hub), and state-level regulatory changes in PA/WV/OH.

**4. Securities Class Action:** EQT recognized approximately $134M in net expense related to the Securities Class Action in 2025. The underlying $167.5M settlement was approved by the court on November 4, 2025, and paid in full during Q3 2025 (with approximately $16M in insurance recoveries). While this specific settlement is resolved, other related shareholder matters remained pending. The primary financial impact has been absorbed.

**5. LNG Commitment Timing Risk:** Of the 4.5 MTPA offtake beginning 2030, 1.0 MTPA relates to a project that had not reached FID. The 2.0 MTPA tolling commitment also relates to a project that had not reached FID. Delays or cancellations would reduce the contracted demand visibility that underpins part of the growth thesis.

**6. MVP Firm Sales Conditionality:** The 1.2 Bcf/day firm sales agreements are subject to unsatisfied conditions related to the Transco Southeast Supply Enhancement project. EQT excluded these volumes from its schedule of total gross commitments. Failure to satisfy conditions would remove a key demand catalyst.

## Conclusion

EQT represents a compelling risk/reward at current levels. The stock trades at an approximately 20% discount to peers despite superior scale, integration, and contracted growth visibility. The Q1 2026 record results confirmed the earnings power of the integrated model at elevated gas prices, and the deleveraging trajectory positions the company to return substantial capital to shareholders over the next 12-18 months.

The primary risk is natural gas price reversion, which estimates are beginning to embed for 2027. However, the combination of LNG export demand growth, potential data center power requirements, and EQT's declining hedge book (reflecting management confidence) suggests that the forward curve may underestimate structural demand support. At approximately 12.0x forward earnings with a clear path to capital returns and multiple expansion catalysts, the stock offers asymmetric upside relative to downside risk.

The specific catalyst that differentiates this from a generic "cheap E&P" thesis is the data center demand channel: firm sales agreements for up to 1.2 Bcf/day on MVP targeting 2027, subject to conditions, represent a potential structural demand anchor that could improve EQT's demand visibility. As conditions are satisfied and demand materializes through contract updates and construction milestones over the next 12 months, the market should progressively re-rate the stock toward its peer median multiple.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** Corrected FY2025 revenue ($8.6B vs. originally stated $23.8B), forward P/E (12.0x vs. 10.9x), reduced peer discount (20% vs. 28%), and reframed MVP firm sales agreements as conditional/unpriced collectively reduce the magnitude of the valuation discount and weaken the certainty of the data-center catalyst that underpins the target price.
