## Executive Summary

Chewy is the largest pure-play online pet retailer in the U.S. with $12.6B in annual revenue, 21.3M active customers, and an Autoship program where customers who have placed at least one Autoship order now account for 83.3% of net sales. The company has undergone decisive profitability improvement: adjusted EBITDA grew from $368M in FY2023 to $719M in FY2025 and is guided to $900-930M in FY2026. Free cash flow of $562M (+24% YoY) at a 4.5% FCF margin demonstrates strong cash generation.

The stock has declined 51% from its 52-week high of $48.62 to $23.56, driven primarily by persistent block selling from controlling shareholder BC Partners (which has liquidated $2B+ in shares over 18 months) and broader rotation out of mid-cap consumer names. Operating and cash flow fundamentals have not deteriorated: estimates are rising (+4.0-4.5% over 90 days), active customer growth is accelerating (+4.0% vs. +2.0% prior year), and the margin expansion thesis is tracking ahead of plan. The stock now trades at approximately 10x FY2026E EV/EBITDA and 15x forward adjusted earnings for a business with high Autoship-customer engagement, which represents a potential mispricing created by technical selling pressure rather than fundamental impairment.

The primary risks are BC Partners' continued overhang, tariff exposure on China-sourced private brand and non-consumable products, and the structural challenge of thin GAAP margins in a competitive category. These risks are adequately compensated at current levels where the FCF yield exceeds 5%.

## Company Overview

Chewy operates approximately 190,000 products from ~4,000 brands through its e-commerce platform. Revenue is distributed across consumables (70%), pet health/specialty (16%), hard goods (11%), and other (3%). The company's competitive advantage centers on three pillars: (1) the Autoship subscription flywheel creating predictable, highly visible revenue with high retention, (2) exceptional customer service driving brand loyalty and organic acquisition, and (3) an expanding healthcare ecosystem that includes the #1 pet pharmacy in America, 18 veterinary clinics (with the pending Modern Animal acquisition expected to increase this to 47), CarePlus insurance/wellness plans, and PracticeHub software serving ~20,000 veterinary practices (~50% of U.S. vet clinics).

The pet industry in the U.S. is approximately $157B and growing at ~4% CAGR, with online penetration at 41% and rising. Pet spending has historically been recession-resistant (grew 12% during 2008-2010). Chewy is outgrowing the category at 8-9% guided revenue growth, gaining market share.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** FY2023 $10.1B → FY2024 $11.1B (+10.4%) → FY2025 $12.6B (+6.2% reported; +8.3% adjusting for the 53rd week) → FY2026 guided $13.6-13.75B (+8-9%). Growth is broad-based: Autoship customer sales grew 11.8% to $10.5B, pet health & specialty grew 11.8%, and hard goods grew 13.4%.

**Margin expansion — the central investment thesis:**
- Gross margin: 28.4% → 29.2% → 29.8%, expanding ~60bp annually. Drivers include private brand penetration, sponsored ads revenue (high-margin), mix shift toward healthcare, and fulfillment automation.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 3.3% → 4.8% → 5.7% → guided 6.6-6.8% in FY2026. The 100bp annual expansion trajectory is well-supported by identifiable levers.
- Operating income swung from -$24M to +$254M over two years.

**Cash flow generation is strong:**
- OCF: $692M in FY2025 (+16% YoY)
- FCF: $562M (+24% YoY) with declining capex as % of revenue
- FCF conversion vs. GAAP net income: 252% ($562M/$223M). However, this elevated ratio partly reflects $298M of share-based compensation expense flowing through OCF as a non-cash add-back. While FCF is real and growing, the conversion ratio overstates underlying cash earnings quality relative to economic compensation costs.

**Balance sheet:** $860M cash, zero drawn debt, $783M available on the ABL facility (extended to 2030). The sub-1.0 current ratio (0.88x) reflects the negative working capital model typical of e-commerce — customers pay immediately while vendors are paid on 30-40 day terms. With ~$879M in cash and marketable securities plus $783M in unused ABL capacity and no outstanding borrowings, there is no liquidity concern.

**Capital allocation:** $943M in buybacks in FY2024, $263M in FY2025, with $250M remaining authorized. Share count has decreased despite $311M in annual SBC, demonstrating net dilution is being managed. The SmartPak acquisition ($175M) for equine health/nutrition was funded from cash.

**GAAP net income context:** GAAP net income declined 43.3% to $222.8M in FY2025 from $392.7M in FY2024, and net margin contracted to 1.8% from 3.3%. This decline was largely driven by the normalization of income tax expense following a one-time deferred-tax valuation allowance release that benefited FY2024. Operating and FCF fundamentals improved while GAAP net income declined due to this tax normalization.

## Growth Analysis

Growth prospects rest on five identifiable vectors:

1. **Autoship penetration continues to accelerate:** From 76.2% to 83.3% of sales over three years. Chewy defines Autoship customer sales as all sales from customers who placed at least one Autoship order in the prior 364 days, including their non-Autoship purchases. Each percentage point gained represents higher retention, lower CAC, and greater revenue visibility. The program grew 11.8% in FY2025 vs. 6.2% total revenue growth, indicating it is the primary growth engine.

2. **Healthcare ecosystem:** Pet health & specialty grew 11.8% and represents only 16% of revenue today. The addressable market for pet healthcare is ~$35B. With 18 vet clinics (expanding to 47 upon closing of the Modern Animal acquisition, adding over $125M in annualized run-rate revenue), the #1 pharmacy, 20,000 practices on PracticeHub, and CarePlus insurance, Chewy is building an integrated healthcare platform that management has described as offering higher margins than core retail, though the company does not report segment-level margins.

3. **Sponsored advertising:** Still early-stage but analogous to Amazon's advertising flywheel — essentially pure-margin revenue from brand partners. This directly flows to gross margin expansion.

4. **International:** Canada launched in 2023; additional markets under evaluation. The U.S. playbook is replicable.

5. **SmartPak/adjacencies:** The equine market ($3B+) represents a new addressable category with similar subscription economics.

Estimate revisions confirm the positive momentum: current-year EPS estimates rose 4.0% over 90 days ($1.56 → $1.62), and next-year estimates rose 4.5% ($1.88 → $1.97). These are modest revisions but consistently positive, with no downward pressure from any of the 14-16 covering analysts.

## Valuation Assessment

At $23.56 with ~416.7M total economic shares (240.2M Class A + 176.5M Class B, both sharing equally in economics), Chewy's market cap is approximately $9.8B. Net of ~$879M cash and marketable securities and with no outstanding debt, EV is approximately $8.9B.

**Current multiples:**
- EV/FY2026 EBITDA: ~$8.9B / $915M midpoint = **9.7x**
- Forward adjusted P/E: $23.56 / $1.62 FY2026E = **14.5x** (or $23.56 / $1.97 FY2027E = **12.0x**)
- FCF yield: $562M / $9.8B = **5.7%** (FY2025); implied FY2026 FCF of $650-700M = **6.6-7.1%**

**Why the market may be wrong — specific thesis:** BC Partners has sold $2B+ in stock over 18 months through a series of secondary offerings and conversions, creating persistent supply that has coincided with a significant price decline. SEC filings confirm a $500M company repurchase concurrent with a June 2024 secondary, a September 2024 secondary with $300M repurchase, a December 2024 secondary with $50M repurchase, a June 2025 secondary (approximately $1B+) with $100M repurchase, and an October 2025 conversion/sale of 13.28M shares. While the precise causal attribution between these sales and the stock decline cannot be proven definitively, the correlation between large block sales and price weakness is consistent with technical selling pressure. As BC Partners' remaining economic interest decreases, this supply pressure should abate.

**Peer comparison:** The peer median P/E is 20.9x. Chewy at 14.5x forward P/E represents a 31% discount to peers despite superior growth (8-9% vs. TSCO at ~6%, ULTA at ~4%), high Autoship-customer engagement (83.3% of sales vs. near-zero subscription mix for peers), and expanding margins. Chewy's discount to the peer group appears attributable to the BC Partners overhang and controlled-company governance rather than fundamental weakness.

**Consensus targets:** Analyst median target of approximately $41.00 implies 74% upside from current levels. Even the low target of $28.00 implies 19% upside. The consensus view (20 out of 25 analysts at Buy/Strong Buy, zero Sells) reflects widespread recognition that the stock is undervalued.

The forward P/E of 47x shown in the profile data appears to use trailing GAAP EPS of $0.52 rather than forward adjusted estimates; this overstates the valuation by a factor of 3x.

## Competitive Landscape

Chewy's competitive position is strengthening on multiple dimensions:

- **Autoship creates switching costs:** 83.3% of net sales comes from Autoship customers, with delivery cadences, product preferences, and pet profiles locked in. Customer lifetime value compounds over time.
- **Healthcare integration:** Chewy's 10-K identifies competition from online retailers, omnichannel retailers, traditional retailers, and the veterinary channel. Chewy's breadth of integrated offerings — spanning online pharmacy, vet clinics, insurance, and PracticeHub — appears differentiated relative to any single competitor, though competitive dynamics continue to evolve.
- **Active customer growth accelerating:** +4.0% in FY2025 vs. +2.0% prior year, indicating share gains despite a mature U.S. pet population.
- **Net sales per active customer rising:** $591 (+2.2%), demonstrating wallet share expansion.
- **Fulfillment advantage:** 80%+ overnight delivery capability with declining fulfillment cost per order as automation investments scale.

The structural risk is Amazon's scale and capability to undercut on pricing. Chewy's specialization, customer service (consistently rated best-in-class), and Autoship stickiness have proven defensible over 7+ years of head-to-head competition. Chewy's revenue has more than doubled since IPO while maintaining share against Amazon.

## Risk Assessment

**1. BC Partners Overhang (MODERATE — trajectory uncertain):** Still holds >40% economic and >80% voting power. Continued block sales create supply pressure. SEC filings show sales accelerated to a ~$1B+ offering in June 2025 and a 13.28M share conversion/sale in October 2025. While the eventual resolution of this overhang is itself a catalyst, the pace and timing of future sales remain uncertain.

**2. Tariff Exposure (MODERATE):** The 10-K states that "a significant portion of suppliers for our private brand business and our non-consumable business are located in China." While the company does not quantify the precise COGS exposure, hard goods represent 11% of revenue and private brands span multiple categories. The company is diversifying supply chains but this creates near-term margin headwinds of uncertain magnitude.

**3. Controlled Company Governance (MODERATE):** Dual-class structure with BC Partners controlling >80% of voting power. Public shareholders have limited influence. This depresses the appropriate multiple but is partially priced.

**4. Thin GAAP Margins (LOW-MODERATE):** GAAP operating margin of 2.0% leaves limited buffer for execution missteps. The trajectory is strongly positive and the adjusted margin profile (5.7% EBITDA, guided to 6.6-6.8%) provides adequate cushion.

**5. SBC Dilution (LOW-MODERATE):** $311M annually (43% of adj. EBITDA). Managed via buybacks but represents ongoing economic dilution. Net share count is declining, which mitigates this concern. The gap between GAAP net income ($223M) and adjusted net income ($541M) is largely driven by SBC and related tax adjustments.

**6. Macroeconomic Sensitivity (LOW):** Pet spending is historically recession-resistant. Chewy's consumable-heavy mix (70% of sales) is non-discretionary for pet owners. Trade-down risk exists but manifests as mix shift, not volume decline.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($36-41, +53-74%):** EBITDA margin expansion continues at 100bp/year, reaching 7.5-8.0% by FY2027. Healthcare segment grows 15-20% annually (boosted by Modern Animal acquisition adding 29 clinics and $125M+ run-rate revenue) and re-rates the multiple. BC Partners completes exit or reduces stake below 20%, removing overhang. Valuation re-rates to 13-14x EV/EBITDA (still below peer median). Math: $1.0B FY2027 EBITDA × 13x + $1B net cash = $14.0B equity value / ~390M shares = ~$35.90/share. At 14x: $15.0B / 390M = ~$38.50/share.

**Bear Case ($18-20, -15% to -23%):** Consumer weakness accelerates trade-down in pet spending, compressing gross margins. Tariffs increase COGS materially. BC Partners conducts another large block sale at distressed levels. Revenue growth decelerates to 4-5% and EBITDA margin expansion stalls at 6%. Valuation compresses to 8x EV/EBITDA. Math: $800M EBITDA × 8x + $500M cash = $6.9B → $16.70/share.

**Base Case ($29-32, +23-36%):** Revenue grows 8% in FY2026-27. EBITDA margin reaches 6.8% in FY2026 and 7.5% in FY2027. BC Partners selling decelerates. Valuation recovers to 11-12x EV/EBITDA (still discounted for governance). EV/EBITDA method: $950M FY2027E EBITDA × 11.5x + $900M cash = $11.8B equity value / ~414M shares = ~$28.60; with buybacks reducing to ~400M shares: ~$29.50. P/E method: 16x FY2027E adjusted EPS of $1.97 = $31.50. The base-case range of $29-32 reflects a blend of both approaches.

## Conclusion

Chewy at $23.56 represents a potentially mispriced asset where the market may be conflating technical selling pressure (BC Partners overhang) with fundamental deterioration. Operating and cash flow fundamentals are at or near all-time highs. At 10x forward EV/EBITDA and 15x forward adjusted P/E for a business with high Autoship-customer engagement and identifiable margin expansion levers, the stock offers favorable risk/reward with a base case of +23-36% over 12-18 months and downside limited to -15-23% in a stressed scenario.

The recommendation is BUY with 2-3% portfolio sizing, acknowledging the Consumer Discretionary sector gate: positive sequential customer growth (+4.0% YoY, accelerating), FCF yield >5%, and estimate revisions positive over 90 days (+4.0-4.5%) all satisfy the elevated challenge requirements. The primary near-term risk is continued BC Partners selling, which could extend the timeline for value realization but does not impair the underlying asset.

These corrections modestly reduce the magnitude of the thesis but do not change the directional recommendation.
