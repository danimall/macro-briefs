**Research Date: June 12, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Cboe Global Markets is one of the highest-quality assets in the exchange complex. Its profit pool is built around proprietary, exclusively-licensed index derivatives (SPX options and the VIX volatility products), which carry ~$0.94 revenue per contract versus ~$0.08 for commoditized multi-listed equity options. On a net-revenue basis (revenues less pass-through cost of revenues), Options generates roughly 64% of the total at ~77-79% operating margin. The first quarter of 2026 was strong: net revenue rose 29% to $728.9M, operating income rose 43% to $505.6M (margin 69.4% from 62.6%), and adjusted EPS climbed 48% to $3.70. Consensus EPS estimates have moved up 15-16% over the past 90 days for both the current and next fiscal year. New CEO Craig Donohue is executing a portfolio simplification — exiting Japan, CEDX, crypto-spot, and selling the Australia/Canada venues to TMX for ~$300M — alongside a ~20% workforce reduction targeting $40-50M of annualized pre-tax savings.

Despite this, the recommendation is HOLD, and the reasoning is specific. First, a disciplined exchange/financial-sector BUY requires a bear-case drawdown of 15% or less; CBOE has already demonstrated far more, falling from $371 on May 19 to $276 on June 2 (a 24% decline in two weeks) as the volatility regime shifted. Second, the near-term options market is pricing acute imminent stress — 1-week ATM implied volatility of 68.6% against 27.6% trailing realized volatility, in steep backwardation, at the 81st percentile of its own chain — signaling a binary event the market is actively hedging into. Third, the headline Q1 growth is cyclically and non-recurringly flattered: a $314M uncertain-tax-position resolution cut the effective rate to 25.2% from 28.4%. With the VIX back near 16, forward earnings power is less certain than the +43% operating-income line implies.

The fundamentals support a constructive 12-month view, but the current setup — elevated near-term event risk, cyclically peaked volumes, and a price that has whipsawed ~24% in a fortnight — argues for patience rather than initiation. The thesis upgrades cleanly if forward quarters show net-revenue growth holding above 15% at a normalized VIX and the term structure resolves without a downside event.

## Company Overview

Cboe operates the largest U.S. options exchange complex (Cboe, C2, BZX, EDGX), the third-largest U.S. equities exchange operator, one of Europe's largest equities venues, a pan-European clearinghouse (Cboe Clear Europe), the BIDS block-trading ATS, and the fast-growing Data Vantage market-data and analytics business. The defining asset is the exclusive license to list and trade S&P 500 index options and the VIX complex, a structure Cboe has built since launching the first listed options exchange in 1973 and creating the VIX in the 1990s.

The five reportable segments by Q1 2026 reported revenue were Options ($668M, 53%), North American Equities ($416M, 33%), Europe & Asia Pacific ($119M, 9%), Futures ($39M, 3%), and Global FX ($30M, 2%). Reported revenue overstates the relevance of the equities business because much of it is pass-through. On a net basis, Options dominates the economics. The business is best understood as a high-margin index-derivatives and data engine with a lower-margin equities transaction layer attached, where the binary risk is licensing rather than competition.

## Financial Analysis

The reported top line is distorted and should not be used for screening. Total Q1 2026 revenue rose only 7% to $1,272.8M because the SEC's Section 31 fee rate dropped to $0/million effective May 2025, mechanically erasing ~$153M from both revenue and cost of revenue with zero margin impact. The meaningful figure is net revenue of $728.9M. Net income rose 54% to $385.7M with diluted EPS of $3.66 (adjusted $3.70).

Growth was broad across volume drivers: index options ADV +29%, total options ADV +18%, European equities ADNV +25%, U.S. matched shares +20%, Global FX ADNV +36%, and Data Vantage +19%. Margin expansion was not confined to Options — North American Equities operating margin jumped to 59.8% from 46.9%, Europe & Asia Pacific to 46.8% from 34.3%, and Global FX to 60.5% from 48.4%, reflecting both the portfolio cleanup and cost discipline. Adjusted operating margin reached 72.4%.

Two items require normalization. The $314M uncertain-tax-position release (paid $299M in Q1) flatters reported and adjusted earnings. And the headline operating cash flow of $1,960M is dominated by $1,878M of Cboe Clear Europe clearing-member margin and default-fund movement, which nets to a balance-sheet liability rather than owner cash. Underlying free cash flow is robust given near-zero capex (~$19M/quarter), but anyone modeling off raw operating cash flow will overstate generation dramatically.

The balance sheet is strong. Cash stood at $2,134M against total debt of $1,443M, all fixed-rate Senior Notes (3.65% due January 2027, 1.625% due 2030, 3.00% due 2032). Net leverage is well under 1x adjusted EBITDA, which itself grew from $987M (2021) to $1,646M (2025). Stockholders' equity of $5,374M is roughly 80% intangible (goodwill $3,142M plus intangibles $1,275M), which is normal for a roll-up exchange operator. The dividend was raised 14% to $0.72/quarter; buybacks remain modest (~$45M in Q1, with $569M authorization remaining), leaving dry powder for M&A. The $400M revolver and €1.2B Cboe Clear Europe facility both terminate within ~12 months and the $650M 3.65% notes mature January 2027, but refinancing risk is low given the credit profile.

## Growth Analysis

The structural growth story rests on three legs. First, the 0DTE (zero-days-to-expiration) SPX options boom is a secular tailwind specific to Cboe's proprietary franchise, generating volume even in calm markets and partly de-coupling index-options activity from the VIX level. Second, Data Vantage is recurring, subscription-like revenue with a growing international contribution that diversifies away from volatility-dependent transaction revenue. Third, the cost-out program ($20-25M realized in 2026) and the shedding of sub-scale international and crypto-spot units should expand margins as savings flow through and dilutive units exit — neither the TMX sale nor the realignment is yet in the reported numbers, as both are subsequent events in the 10-Q.

Estimate revisions confirm positive earnings momentum: current-year consensus EPS moved from $11.92 to $13.75 over 90 days (+15.3%), and next-year from $12.71 to $14.72 over 90 days (+15.8%). The earnings surprise record (three beats, one narrow miss in four quarters, including a 10.3% beat in Q1) supports confidence in forward estimates. These revisions are positive but fall short of the +30% threshold that signals a trough-to-recovery re-rating, and a meaningful share of the Q1 outperformance reflects volatility-driven volume rather than durable structural growth. With the VIX near 16 after the early-2026 return of risk-on sentiment, the highest-margin index and VIX volumes face a cyclical headwind even as 0DTE provides a partial floor.

## Valuation Assessment

At $295.90 against next-year consensus EPS of $14.72, CBOE trades at roughly 20x forward earnings. That is a clear discount to the peer set: NDAQ at 26.3x, MSCI at 35.2x, MCO at 32.4x, COIN at 53.2x, with a peer median P/E of 32.4x. The trailing P/E is ~25x. The discount is partly earned — the comparison peers (MSCI, MCO, FactSet) carry higher-margin, more purely recurring data/index models, whereas a meaningful slice of Cboe's profit is volatility-cyclical transaction revenue. Adjusting for that, ~20x forward is a reasonable, not cheap, multiple for a near-monopoly derivatives franchise with ~70% operating margins, a strong balance sheet, and a credible self-help cost story.

The valuation does not offer the asymmetric upside that a BUY requires, primarily because the forward EPS denominator is cyclically elevated. The 90-day estimate increase rode a high-volatility Q1 and a non-recurring tax release; on normalized volatility, the sustainable earnings base is somewhat lower than the $14.72 figure implies, which means the "20x forward" framing understates the true through-cycle multiple. Analyst targets bracket the picture: low $273, mean $326.93, median $335, high $382. The current price sits near the low end, consistent with a market that is repricing for a lower-volatility 2026 rather than extrapolating Q1's pace.

## Competitive Landscape

The competitive position is bifurcated. In index derivatives, Cboe's SPX and VIX franchise is effectively a monopoly within the exclusive-license structure, and the relevant risk is the license itself, not a competitor. In Europe, Cboe is gaining ground — equities share rose to 25.5% from 24.8% and Cboe Clear Europe cleared share to 40.2% — now that the loss-making CEDX has been decommissioned. Data Vantage is widening its moat as a recurring data product.

The weak side is the commoditized layer. Multi-listed options share slipped to 22.3% from 25.0% and total options share to 29.1% from 31.1%, reflecting genuine fee competition from Nasdaq, NYSE, and MIAX. U.S. equities (~9.8% share, down slightly) is low-margin and pressured by the SEC's reduced access-fee cap and a new options regulatory-fee model that management explicitly flags as a headwind. Net, the trajectory of the profit pool is improving — the high-margin, monopolistic, structurally-tailwinded segments are growing while the commoditized parts are being de-emphasized or losing low-value share — which supports a competitive-position score in the 7 range.

## Risk Assessment

**Licensing concentration.** The top forward-looking risk factor is the loss of the right to exclusively list and trade certain index options and futures. The entire premium-margin franchise depends on the S&P 500 / VIX licensing relationship. A non-renewal or adverse change would be existential. This is a binary tail, not a normally-priced risk.

**Volatility dependence.** Index and VIX volumes correlate with market volatility. The VIX near 16 raises the probability that the highest-margin volumes soften relative to the elevated-vol Q1, even with 0DTE providing a partial offset.

**CAT funding litigation.** The original Consolidated Audit Trail funding model was vacated by the 11th Circuit in July 2025; a revised model was SEC-approved in March 2026 but immediately challenged by Citadel Securities and the ASA on March 24, 2026. Cboe carries ~$97M of net long-term CAT notes receivable with a $30M credit-loss allowance; an adverse outcome could leave Plan Participants bearing costs and unable to collect.

**Execution and governance.** The Q1 10-Q added/modified the human-capital risk factor specifically to address the ~20% workforce reduction and back-to-back C-suite transitions (new CEO, new COO after Chris Isaacson's March 2026 retirement). The 8-K cadence shows an unusually high level of officer/director changes across 2023-2026. A whistleblower suit (Craine v. Cboe, filed January 2026) alleging SOX retaliation tied to "accounting and operational control issues" is low-probability but touches financial-reporting integrity and warrants monitoring; Cboe states its outside investigation found the allegations meritless and that controls remained effective.

**Clearinghouse tail.** Cboe Clear Europe holds ~$3.4B of member collateral; a major clearing-member default could expose Cboe's 25-50% skin-in-the-game capital. Low probability, high severity.

### Options Market Signal

The options market is the most actionable near-term signal and it is cautionary. One-week ATM implied volatility is 68.6% against 27.6% trailing realized — a 41-point spread that places near-term IV at the 81st percentile of its own chain, in steep backwardation (68.6% near-term versus 32.4% three-month). The market is pricing a discrete, imminent event rather than diffuse uncertainty: the 1-month and 3-month tenors sit at a calm ~32%, so the stress is concentrated in the next several sessions. Put/call skew is balanced (0.8%) and short interest is low (3.0% of float, 2.9 days to cover), so this is not a crowded-bearish setup; it reads as event hedging into a binary. The put/call OI ratio rose from 0.51 to 0.69 as the stock fell 17.3% since the prior snapshot. Do not initiate ahead of the near-term resolution; let the front-end vol clear first.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** Sustained or recurring volatility keeps SPX and VIX volumes elevated; 0DTE continues its secular ramp and partly de-couples index activity from the VIX level; Data Vantage compounds at high-teens with a re-rating toward data-peer multiples; the cost-out flows through to margin; and the portfolio simplification (TMX sale closing, Japan/CEDX/crypto-spot exits) lifts consolidated margins. On ~$15.00-15.50 of normalized EPS at ~24-25x, the stock approaches the $382 analyst high. The strong balance sheet and dividend hike provide downside support and optionality for accretive M&A.

**Bear case.** A prolonged low-volatility regime (VIX persistently near 16) dampens the highest-margin index/VIX volumes; multi-listed options and U.S. equities share erosion continues under fee-cap pressure; the CAT litigation resolves adversely; or, in the tail, the SPX/VIX licensing relationship is disrupted. Normalizing the non-recurring tax benefit and the volatility-elevated Q1 leaves sustainable EPS materially below the $14.72 consensus, and the multiple compresses toward the low end of history. The realized 24% drawdown from the May high to early June demonstrates that the bear scenario already moves the stock well beyond the 15% threshold that a disciplined exchange-sector BUY would require.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth_hold).** A 12-month appreciation thesis exists, but the entry is poor and the conditions for an exchange/financial-sector BUY are not met.

This HOLD is maintained despite an overall score (6.6) above the usual neutral band, and the rebuttal is concrete and falsifiable:

1. **Bear-case downside already exceeds the 15% BUY ceiling.** The stock fell from $371 (May 19) to $276 (June 2), a 24% decline in two weeks, on a volatility-regime shift — demonstrating that the realistic adverse scenario blows through 15%. *Falsifiable:* if the stock holds above ~$251 (15% below the current $295.90) through the next two earnings cycles in a normalizing-vol environment, this concern is disproven.

2. **The near-term options term structure prices an imminent binary.** 68.6% 1-week IV versus 27.6% realized, steep backwardation, 81st-percentile IV. *Falsifiable:* if the near-dated expiry passes and the term structure reverts to contango without a >5% downside move, the entry-timing concern resolves.

3. **Forward earnings power is cyclically and non-recurringly inflated.** Q1 net revenue +29% and operating income +43% coincided with a $314M tax-reserve release that cut the effective rate to 25.2% from 28.4%, while the VIX has since fallen to ~16. *Falsifiable:* if Q2/Q3 2026 net-revenue growth holds above 15% YoY at a normalized (~16) VIX, the cyclicality concern is disproven and an upgrade is warranted.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):** (a) Q2/Q3 net-revenue growth sustained above 15% YoY with the VIX near 16, confirming the growth is structural rather than volatility-borrowed; (b) near-term IV term structure normalizes to contango and the stock retraces to ~$270-280 (a sub-19x forward multiple), restoring asymmetry with a bear case inside 15%; (c) cost-savings realization visibly lifting consolidated operating margin above ~72% on a clean (non-vol-spike) quarter.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):** (a) an adverse development in the SPX/VIX licensing relationship; (b) an adverse CAT funding ruling that impairs the ~$97M notes receivable and imposes incremental participant costs; (c) two consecutive quarters of net-revenue contraction with continued multi-listed share erosion below ~20%.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next earnings (Q2 2026, ~late July/early August) is the natural checkpoint, with the near-dated FOMC and the term-structure resolution as the immediate input.

**Reference targets (12-month):** Base case ~$325 (~22x normalized ~$14.75 EPS with partial cost-out flow-through), +10% from current. Bull case ~$382 (analyst high; continued elevated vol + full cost-out + Data Vantage re-rate), +29%. Bear case ~$251 (low-vol regime + share erosion + multiple compression), -15%, with realized downside potentially worse as the early-June action showed.

## Conclusion

Cboe is a structurally advantaged exchange operator with a strong balance sheet, a near-monopoly volatility franchise, a credible new-CEO simplification strategy, and rising estimates. The current setup argues for patience: the near-term options market is hedging an imminent binary, Q1's headline growth was amplified by a one-time tax release, and the realistic bear-case drawdown — already demonstrated at ~24% in real time — exceeds the threshold that would justify initiating in this sector. The recommendation is HOLD with a constructive 12-month bias. The cleaner entry comes either on a pullback toward $270-280 with a calmer term structure, or on confirmation that net-revenue growth holds above 15% at a normalized VIX.
