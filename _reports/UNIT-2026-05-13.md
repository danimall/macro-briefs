## Executive Summary

Uniti Group is a post-merger fiber infrastructure and broadband services company formed from the August 2025 combination of the old Uniti Group (fiber REIT) and Windstream (rural telecom). The combined entity owns ~240,000 fiber route miles serving over 1 million customers across 47 states, with a strategic focus on fiber-to-the-home expansion in secondary U.S. markets.

The investment case rests on a straightforward premise: fiber subscriber growth (consumer +26% YoY, infrastructure +13% YoY) will eventually outpace legacy telecom revenue decline (-7% pro forma YoY), while the massive fiber footprint attracts hyperscaler demand and potential acquirers (TPG, T-Mobile reportedly interested). The problem is execution risk under extreme financial constraints — $10.7B in debt against $1.45B EBITDA (7.3x leverage), $785M in annual interest expense consuming 54% of EBITDA, and years of negative free cash flow ahead during the fiber build-out. The company's EPS estimates are deteriorating rapidly (-287% revision over 90 days for the current quarter), it has missed earnings in 3 of the last 4 quarters, and the stock trades above analyst consensus targets suggesting speculative premium.

At $11.33, the stock trades at an apparent 2.7x trailing P/E — but this figure reflects a one-time $1.68B accounting gain from the merger settlement. On forward estimates, the company will lose approximately $1.85 per share in FY2026. The M&A speculation and record Q1 fiber adds explain the 48% price appreciation over 12 months, but the balance sheet creates binary risk that justifies avoidance.

## Company Overview

Uniti Group operates three segments following its August 2025 merger completion:

**Kinetic (56% of revenue):** Incumbent local exchange carrier (ILEC) operations providing residential broadband (fiber and DSL), business services, and wholesale transport across 18 states and ~1,400 markets. Q1 2026 revenue: $548M with 43% contribution margins. The segment is experiencing a managed transition from legacy DSL to fiber-to-the-home, with 535,000 fiber subscribers and 1.9 million fiber-equipped households.

**Uniti Solutions (19% of revenue):** Enterprise-focused CLEC providing managed cloud communications, UCaaS, SD-WAN, and security services to mid-market and large enterprise customers. Q1 2026 revenue: $192M with 50% contribution margins. This segment faces structural headwinds from TDM-to-IP migration and customer churn.

**Fiber Infrastructure (30% of revenue):** Wholesale dark fiber, wavelength transport, and colocation services primarily serving hyperscalers, carriers, and content providers. Q1 2026 revenue: $295M with 65% contribution margins. This segment benefits directly from AI/data center demand.

The company is headquartered in Little Rock, Arkansas, employs 8,632 people (23% unionized), and no longer qualifies as a REIT. No common dividend is paid.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Trajectory:** FY2025 reported revenue was $2.23B, representing only a partial year post-merger (August-December). Pro forma full-year revenue was $3.79B, which declined 7% YoY driven by legacy DSL, TDM, and voice attrition. Q1 2026 revenue of $987.5M annualizes to ~$3.95B, consistent with the $3.61-3.66B guidance (which accounts for continued legacy decline). The company expects total revenue growth to inflect positive by 2027.

**Profitability:** Adjusted EBITDA of $441.6M in Q1 2026 (45% margin) annualizes to approximately $1.77B — above the $1.43-1.48B guidance range, suggesting either seasonality or one-time items in Q1. The guided EBITDA implies the business generates real operating cash flow, but interest expense ($785M guided) consumes over half.

**Cash Flow:** Operating cash flow was $260.9M in Q1 2026 against $349.2M in capex, producing -$88M in free cash flow for the quarter. Annualized, the company burns approximately $350-450M in free cash flow during the fiber build phase, which management expects to last through at least 2029.

**Balance Sheet — The Central Problem:**
- Total debt: ~$10.7B (up from $9.5B at YE 2025 due to refinancing)
- Net leverage: ~7.3x Adjusted EBITDA
- Interest coverage: 1.85x (EBITDA/interest expense)
- Stockholders' equity: $319.7M
- Debt/equity: 30.65x
- Cash: $982.6M (elevated from recent issuances, deployment pending)
- Altman Z-Score: 0.29 (extreme distress zone)
- $575M preferred stock with 11% escalating dividend (Elliott-held)

The company has actively refinanced its capital structure, extending maturities (nearest significant maturity now 2031-2033) and reducing blended rates slightly. However, new issuances at 7.50-8.625% in a rising rate environment demonstrate the cost of capital for a 7x-levered issuer. Annual interest expense grew 18% YoY and will approach $800M in FY2026.

**Earnings Track Record:** The company missed EPS estimates in 3 of the last 4 quarters, with misses of -267%, -138%, and -252% (the most recent beat was a modest 6.1%). Current quarter EPS estimate has been revised down 287% over 90 days (from -$0.13 to -$0.49). Next year's estimate dropped 150% over 90 days. This negative earnings momentum is among the most severe observable in the coverage universe.

## Growth Analysis

**Structural Growth Drivers:**
- Consumer fiber subscribers growing at ~39,000 gross adds per quarter (record Q1 2026)
- Fiber-equipped households expanding by ~88,000/quarter toward 3.5M target by 2029
- Fiber penetration at 29% with 40%+ long-term target
- Hyperscaler demand for dark fiber in Tier 2/3 markets (AI/data center buildout)
- BEAD awards totaling $184.3M across 7 states
- RDOF funding of $522.8M over 10 years

**Structural Decline Headwinds:**
- DSL subscriber losses accelerating as fiber overlays cannibalize
- TDM/voice revenue declining 44% YoY
- Business segment experiencing customer churn
- Pro forma total revenue declining 7% YoY
- Competitive encroachment from cable, fixed wireless, and fiber overbuilders

**The Growth Math:** Kinetic consumer fiber revenue grew 26% YoY on a $506M base (pro forma), adding roughly $130M. But legacy DSL/TDM revenue across all segments declined by substantially more, producing the net -7% revenue trajectory. The company projects revenue growth inflection by 2027 — this requires fiber revenue growth to accelerate while legacy decline stabilizes. Given that DSL/TDM decline is structural and likely to accelerate (not stabilize), meeting this target requires aggressive fiber subscriber acquisition and pricing.

**AI Infrastructure Upside:** The Fiber Infrastructure segment reported $1.6M in new bookings MRR in Q1 (third-highest ever) driven by hyperscaler demand for high-count fiber to data centers. The segment's 65% contribution margins and growing backlog are attractive, but at $295M quarterly revenue, it requires years of compounding to offset other declines.

## Valuation Assessment

**Headline Metrics Are Misleading:** The trailing P/E of 2.7x reflects a $1.68B one-time gain from settlement of pre-existing relationships during the merger. On forward estimates, the company will lose $1.85/share in FY2026 and $1.50/share in FY2027 — there is no meaningful P/E to compute.

**EV/EBITDA Framework:** At current market cap ($2.75B) plus net debt (~$9.7B), enterprise value is approximately $12.5B. Against guided EBITDA midpoint ($1.45B), this implies 8.6x EV/EBITDA. For a highly leveraged fiber infrastructure company with declining total revenue but a growing fiber subscriber base, 8.6x falls within the 7-10x range typical for leveraged telecom.

**Comparable Context:** Direct peers are limited. Lumen Technologies (LUMN, 9B market cap, negative P/E) is probably the closest analog — another restructured telecom pivoting from legacy to fiber. Cogent (CCOI, negative P/E, -70% from 52-week high) faces its own challenges. The company's fiber assets in isolation would likely command a premium multiple (12-15x EBITDA), but the legacy revenue drag and extreme leverage compress the consolidated valuation.

**M&A Floor:** Reports of TPG and T-Mobile acquisition interest (April 2026) provide a potential valuation floor. A takeout at 9-10x EBITDA ($13-14.5B EV) would imply equity value of $3.3-4.8B, or $13.60-$19.80 per share. However, the $10.7B debt assumption required makes any deal complex, and no formal process has been announced.

**Analyst Consensus:** Mean target $9.94, median $9.0 — both below current price of $11.33. Range: $6.00-$16.00. Distribution is 2 Buy, 5 Hold, 1 Sell, 1 Strong Sell. The stock is trading above consensus, which typically signals market pricing of M&A premium or retail momentum.

## Competitive Landscape

Uniti occupies a differentiated position as an integrated fiber infrastructure + broadband services provider in secondary U.S. markets. This positioning offers both advantages and vulnerabilities:

**Advantages:**
- Dense fiber presence in markets underserved by national carriers
- Combined infrastructure + services model enables cost synergies unavailable to pure-play operators
- Hyperscaler relationships through Fiber Infrastructure segment
- Government funding eligibility (BEAD/RDOF) for rural deployments
- 240,000 route miles provide significant barriers to entry in served markets

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Cable operators (Charter, Altice) are dominant competitors with deeper pockets and broader product bundles
- T-Mobile fixed wireless has captured meaningful share in rural markets at lower price points
- Electric cooperatives are deploying fiber in overlapping territories
- Only ~20% of Uniti households face no competitive broadband alternative
- Union labor costs (23% workforce unionized) reduce flexibility

The competitive position is stable within its existing footprint but faces mounting pressure at the margins. The company's ~29% fiber penetration suggests room for growth among existing premises, but converting DSL customers to fiber is not guaranteed — some will switch to wireless alternatives rather than remaining with the same provider at a higher price point.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks (Not Fully Priced):**

1. **Refinancing Risk in Rising Rate Environment:** With recent issuances at 7.50-8.625%, Uniti's blended cost of debt will continue rising at each refinancing event. A 100bp increase in blended rates would add ~$107M in annual interest expense, consuming incremental EBITDA gains.

2. **Capital Markets Access:** The fiber build strategy requires continued access to debt and equity markets for years. Any disruption — credit market dislocation, ratings downgrade, or loss of market confidence — could force a capital structure restructuring.

3. **RDOF Compliance:** The company disclosed non-compliance with 40% milestones in some states (January 2026 FCC notification). Potential loss of the remaining ~$522.8M in RDOF support would require alternative capital sourcing for planned deployments.

4. **Elliott Preferred Overhang:** The $575M preferred stock with escalating 11% dividend creates a growing cash drain ($63M+ annually, increasing) and potential dilution event. Elliott's board representation and ~25% common ownership creates governance concentration.

5. **Legacy Revenue Decline Acceleration:** If DSL/TDM attrition accelerates beyond current rates (driven by competitive entry or economic weakness), the revenue growth inflection could be delayed beyond 2027, requiring additional capital.

**Options Market Signal:**

Near-term ATM IV at 76.5% vs. 53.2% historical volatility implies the options market is pricing significant event risk (23.3pp premium). The term structure is in backwardation (76.5% near-term vs. 53.9% at 3 months), consistent with near-term binary event risk. The put/call volume ratio of 0.04 and OI ratio of 0.04 indicate extreme call-heavy positioning — speculative interest rather than hedging demand. Combined with 13.6% short interest (5 days to cover), the options market reflects a speculative squeeze dynamic rather than fundamental conviction.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($14-16):** M&A materializes at 9-10x EBITDA. Fiber subscriber growth accelerates beyond plan, demonstrating the combined entity's value proposition to acquirers. Hyperscaler demand for dark fiber exceeds expectations. Revenue growth inflects positive by early 2027. Interest rates decline, reducing refinancing costs and improving equity value. At $16, the equity would imply ~10x EV/EBITDA on 2026 guidance.

**Bear Case ($4-6):** Credit market dislocation forces covenant violations or prevents refinancing. Legacy revenue decline accelerates. Fiber subscriber growth slows due to competition. No M&A materializes. Company forced into restructuring or deeply dilutive equity raise. At $5, the equity would imply ~7x EV/EBITDA but with a broken capital structure narrative.

**Base Case ($8-10):** Fiber growth continues but cannot fully offset legacy decline within the timeframe equity holders need. M&A speculation fades without concrete offer. Leverage remains elevated as FCF stays negative. Stock reverts toward analyst consensus ($9-10) as momentum fades.

## Short Candidate Assessment

**Catalyst:** No single dated event provides a clean catalyst, but several converging pressures create timing: (1) Q2 2026 earnings (likely August 2026) — given the 3-of-4 miss track record and -20% estimate revisions in the last 30 days, another miss is probable; (2) Broader credit market stress — if spreads widen meaningfully, a 7.3x levered issuer will face immediate equity repricing; (3) If M&A reports do not advance to formal process by Q3 2026, the speculative premium unwinds.

**Timing window:** 2-4 months. The M&A premium currently embedded in the stock (trading above all analyst consensus targets except one) will dissipate if no formal process is announced by Q3 earnings.

**Short interest and squeeze risk:** At 13.6% SI (15.8M shares) with 5 days to cover, squeeze risk is moderate. The call-heavy options positioning (P/C OI 0.04) suggests retail speculation that could amplify short-term moves. Institutional ownership concentration (Elliott ~25%) limits free float further. This is shortable but requires tight risk management.

**Borrow and options signal:** Options IV at 76.5% (85th percentile) makes puts expensive. The backwardation structure suggests near-term puts are overpriced relative to longer-dated protection. A 3-month put or put spread would capture the credit risk catalyst at lower carry cost (53.9% IV). Borrow is likely available but costly.

**Downside target and risk/reward:** Current price $11.33 → bear case $6 = 47% downside. Upside risk to $13 (near 52-week high of $12.39) = 15% upside risk. Risk/reward: approximately 3:1 favorable on a 3-month horizon. Stop-loss at $13.50 (above 52-week high).

**Verdict: SHORT CANDIDATE** — Three converging catalysts (credit market stress, earnings miss probability, M&A premium unwind), favorable 3:1 risk/reward, and 13.6% SI below the 25% "crowded" threshold. Suggested sizing: 1-2% of portfolio via 3-month puts or put spreads to manage the elevated IV cost. Stop-loss at $13.50.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**For AVOID:**
- **Re-entry criteria:** (1) Formal M&A process announced with specific bidder and timeline — this would convert from speculative to actionable. (2) Net leverage below 5.0x (requires either significant EBITDA growth or debt reduction), demonstrating the capital structure can survive without constant refinancing. (3) Two consecutive quarters of positive free cash flow, proving the fiber build-out is inflecting toward self-funding. (4) Stock pulls back to $6-7 range with confirmed EBITDA growth, offering genuine asymmetry on the long side.
- **Timeline:** The structural headwinds (leverage, negative FCF, legacy decline) persist for at least 18-24 months. The fiber growth story requires patience through 2027-2028 before the economics potentially self-fund. Monitor quarterly for revenue growth inflection and any formal M&A developments.

## Conclusion

Uniti Group presents a structurally interesting fiber infrastructure story trapped inside a deeply stressed balance sheet. The fiber assets are genuinely valuable — 240,000 route miles, growing hyperscaler demand, 26% consumer subscriber growth, and government funding support. Under different capitalization, this would be an attractive infrastructure investment.

At 7.3x leverage, $785M in annual interest expense, negative free cash flow for the foreseeable future, and rapidly deteriorating EPS estimates, the equity is a leveraged bet on continued capital markets access and eventual revenue growth inflection. The stock trading above analyst consensus (current $11.33 vs. median target $9.00) suggests the market is pricing M&A speculation that may not materialize. Without a formal offer, the equity reverts to fundamental value determined by cash flow generation capacity — which is negative for years. AVOID.
