## Executive Summary

Hanmi Financial Corporation is a $900M market cap Korean-American community bank executing a textbook NIM recovery cycle. Q1 2026 delivered $0.75 EPS (up 27.6% YoY), driven by a 36bp expansion in net interest margin to 3.38% as deposit costs repriced lower while loan yields held firm. The bank simultaneously eliminated all FHLB borrowings, reduced nonperforming assets by 38%, and continued share buybacks at prices well below current levels.

The stock has rallied 32% over the trailing twelve months, from ~$22.85 to $30.17, largely reflecting this fundamental improvement. At 12.0x trailing P/E and ~9.6x current-year estimates ($3.16 consensus), valuation is a discount to peer median P/E of 14.0x. However, the specific risk that prevents an upgrade to BUY is the combination of: (1) CRE concentration at 60.9% of loans with material hospitality ($865M) and office ($487M) exposure in an environment where the 30Y Treasury above 5% compresses CRE valuations and raises refinancing risk; (2) the bank's acute sensitivity to the uncertain rate path — a 300bp rate decline reduces NII by 22% in year two, and Warsh's first FOMC creates genuine policy ambiguity; and (3) tariff risk to its Korean-American trade-finance customer base, which is idiosyncratic and difficult to hedge.

The thesis is constructive: NIM expansion likely continues through 2026 as $2.19B in time deposits mature and reprice lower. But the risk/reward at $30.17 (1.08x TBV) is balanced rather than asymmetric. The stock needs to either demonstrate sustained loan growth beyond NIM expansion, or pull back to $26-27 (where buyback activity occurred), to present a compelling entry.

## Company Overview

Hanmi Financial Corporation is the holding company for Hanmi Bank, a California-headquartered community bank with 34+ branches serving primarily Korean-American entrepreneurs and businesses. Total assets stand at $7.87B with a loan portfolio of $6.5B dominated by commercial real estate (60.9%), C&I lending (17.6%), residential mortgages (15.3%), and equipment financing (6.0%).

The bank operates in a distinctive niche, providing trade finance, SBA lending, and international banking services to Korean-American businesses engaged in cross-border commerce. This niche provides relationship-based deposit gathering advantages and SBA lending premiums (Q1 2026: $32.5M sold at 7.88% premium) but concentrates risk in a specific demographic and geographic (California) footprint.

## Financial Analysis

**Profitability Recovery:** Net income bottomed at $62.2M in FY2024 and recovered to $76.1M in FY2025. Q1 2026's $22.6M annualizes to ~$90M, indicating continued momentum. The primary driver is NIM expansion: cost of deposits fell 33bps YoY to 2.26% while loan yields only declined 5bps to 5.90%.

**Capital Strength:** CET1 at 12.20% and total risk-based capital at 15.22% provide substantial buffers above well-capitalized minimums (6.5% and 10% respectively). The bank holds a 6.45% capital conservation buffer at the bank level. Zero FHLB borrowings and $1.9B+ available borrowing capacity demonstrate exceptional liquidity.

**Credit Quality:** Nonperforming assets declined 38% to $12.4M (0.16% of assets) after a $9.7M recovery on a previously nonaccrual CRE office loan. Net charge-offs were 16bps annualized portfolio-wide, though equipment financing ran at 2.39% — this segment is in managed run-off ($486M → $393M over five quarters).

**Capital Return:** Dividend of $1.12/year (3.7% yield) plus buybacks at $25.89/share in Q1 2026. Combined return yield of 5-6% annualized with 2.15M shares still authorized for repurchase.

**Free Cash Flow:** FY2025 operating cash flow of $206M against minimal capex ($2.3M) = $204M FCF on a $900M market cap. FCF yield exceeds 20%, reflecting the light capital intensity of community banking.

## Growth Analysis

**NIM Tailwind:** The most visible growth driver is continued deposit repricing. With $2.19B in time deposits maturing through the remainder of 2026 and current marginal deposit costs declining, NIM has room to expand further if rates remain stable or decline gradually. The bank's 57% variable-rate loan composition supports yield maintenance in a flat-to-rising rate environment.

**C&I Loan Growth:** Average C&I loans grew $227M (+28% YoY) in Q1 2026, representing meaningful diversification away from CRE. This is a positive structural shift that reduces regulatory concentration risk.

**SBA Lending:** Consistent gain-on-sale income provides a non-interest income stream that benefits from government program demand.

**Constraints:** Total loan growth has been modest (low single-digit percent). This is a margin recovery story, not a high-growth franchise. Consensus estimates reflect this: FY2026E $3.16 (+26% YoY from the trough), FY2027E $3.42 (+8%). After the initial recovery normalization, growth returns to mid-single-digits.

**Estimate Revisions:** Positive but modest — current quarter estimates up 4.8% over 30 days and 5.8% over 90 days, current year up 5.1% over 30 days. Directionally encouraging but well below the 30%+ threshold that signals a major earnings revision cycle.

## Valuation Assessment

At $30.17, HAFC trades at:
- 12.0x trailing P/E ($2.51 TTM EPS)
- 9.6x FY2026E P/E ($3.16 consensus)
- 8.8x FY2027E P/E ($3.42 consensus)
- 1.08x tangible book value (~$28.16 TBV/share)
- 3.7% dividend yield

Versus peer median P/E of 14.0x and dividend yield of 3.3%, HAFC appears inexpensive on earnings metrics. However, several factors partially explain the discount:
1. CRE concentration at 60.9% of loans exceeds regulatory comfort zones and warrants a discount
2. Geographic concentration in California (single-state risk)
3. Niche customer base (Korean-American businesses) creates demographic concentration
4. The 14.0x peer median includes CFFN (13.0x, a thrift) and TRST (14.8x, higher quality) — the relevant comparable is CPF (11.6x), another Pacific-focused community bank

The 2-3 turn P/E discount to broader peer median is partially warranted by concentration risks. At 9.6x forward earnings with positive revision momentum, there is modest upside to fair value (~12x forward = $38), but this requires rate cooperation and no CRE credit deterioration — conditions that are uncertain.

Analyst consensus targets range from $31.50 to $35.00 (median $32.50), implying 8-16% upside from current levels. At a $900M market cap with only 4 covering analysts, price targets should be weighted modestly.

## Competitive Landscape

Hanmi occupies a defensible niche as one of the largest Korean-American community banks. Its competitive advantages include:
- Deep relationships within the Korean-American business community
- Trade finance and international banking capabilities that generic community banks lack
- SBA lending expertise with consistent gain-on-sale economics
- C&I lending growth diversifying the revenue base

Competitive threats:
- Hope Bancorp (HOPE), Preferred Bank (PFBC), and Pacific Premier compete directly for Korean-American banking relationships in California
- Larger banks (JPM, WFC) increasingly target ethnic-community banking with dedicated divisions
- The niche is stable but not expanding rapidly — Korean-American business formation rates are tied to immigration policy and California economic conditions

The C&I diversification is a positive trajectory indicator. The equipment financing pullback removes a lower-quality income source. Overall, competitive position is stable and niche-protected, but not demonstrably improving at a rate that distinguishes HAFC from peers.

## Risk Assessment

**CRE Concentration (Primary Risk):** At 60.9% of loans, CRE exposure is the dominant risk. Hospitality ($865M, 13.2%) is cyclical and vulnerable to consumer spending weakness. Office ($487M, 7.4%) faces secular headwinds. A $21.2M CRE retail loan was downgraded to Special Mention in Q1 2026, signaling early-stage credit migration.

**Rate Sensitivity (Asymmetric):** The bank benefits from stable-to-higher rates but faces material NII compression in a rate-down scenario. The rate path is genuinely uncertain, creating binary risk for the NIM recovery thesis.

**Tariff Risk (Idiosyncratic):** HAFC specifically disclosed tariff risk to Korean-American businesses engaged in international trade. This is unique among community banks and directly relevant given current trade policy uncertainty with East Asian partners.

**Deposit Repricing Risk:** While maturing time deposits are a NIM tailwind today, they also represent rollover risk if competitive conditions force the bank to offer higher rates to retain deposits.

**Professional Fee Surge (+89% YoY):** Attributed to "various corporate initiatives" — this language is often associated with M&A exploration (as acquirer or target). If HAFC is exploring a merger, execution risk increases. If it's a potential target, this could represent upside optionality.

**Earnings Volatility:** The 4-quarter track record shows 2 beats and 2 misses, including a -19.6% miss in Q2 2025. This inconsistency reduces confidence in forward estimates.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($35-38, +16-26%):**
- NIM continues expanding toward 3.60%+ as deposits reprice through 2026
- C&I loan growth accelerates, reducing CRE concentration below 55%
- Credit quality remains benign; equipment financing run-off eliminates loss drag
- Rate environment stays supportive (3.50-3.75% Fed Funds)
- Multiple re-rates toward peer median (12-13x forward) on consistent earnings delivery
- Potential M&A premium (professional fee surge signals)

**Bear Case ($23-25, -17-24%):**
- CRE credit deterioration accelerates as long-term rates stay above 5% and commercial property values decline
- Hospitality exposure becomes impaired if consumer spending contracts materially in H2 2026
- Aggressive rate cuts compress NIM back toward 3.0%
- Tariff implementation impacts Korean-American trade finance customers
- Deposit competition forces rate increases, offsetting time-deposit maturities

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers to BUY:**
1. Stock pulls back to $26-27 range (where management itself was buying), representing <9x forward earnings and ~0.95x TBV
2. Two consecutive quarters of NIM above 3.50% with no credit deterioration (demonstrates durability beyond mechanical repricing)
3. Confirmation of strategic activity (M&A announcement that demonstrates value creation)

**Downgrade triggers to AVOID:**
1. Nonperforming assets rise above 0.50% of total assets (from current 0.16%)
2. NIM contracts below 3.10% (reversal of the recovery thesis)
3. Net charge-offs exceed 50bps annualized on the total portfolio for two consecutive quarters

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected July 2026) is the next natural checkpoint. Rate path clarity after the next FOMC meeting will inform whether the NIM recovery thesis extends or reverses.

## Conclusion

Hanmi Financial is a well-managed community bank executing a clear NIM recovery with conservative capital management and improving credit quality. The fundamental story is sound. However, at $30.17 — after a 32% rally and trading at 1.08x TBV — the risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric. The specific concerns that prevent a BUY are quantifiable: (1) 60.9% CRE concentration with $865M hospitality exposure heading into a potential consumer downturn; (2) asymmetric rate sensitivity where the bear case (NII -22% in 300bp decline) is worse than the bull case (incremental NIM expansion from already-elevated 3.38%); and (3) the stock has already re-rated from ~8x to ~12x trailing P/E, capturing much of the easy multiple expansion.

The 3.7% dividend yield provides carry while waiting for either a more attractive entry or clearer evidence that growth extends beyond mechanical NIM recovery. HOLD with a growth orientation — worth owning at a lower price or with additional operational confirmation.
