**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Otis Worldwide is the global leader in elevator and escalator service, maintaining approximately 2.5 million units through 37,000 field mechanics across 200+ countries. The company has effectively completed its transformation into a service-dominant business. Service now generates 91% of segment operating profit at 25%+ margins, while New Equipment serves primarily as a portfolio feeder. The stock has declined 29% from its 52-week high of $101.42 to $72.05, driven by three converging headwinds: China New Equipment volume declines exceeding 20% per year, a Q1 2026 earnings miss with concerning Service margin compression (-160bps), and negative estimate revision trends (-3.7% current year over 90 days).

At ~19x forward earnings ($4.19 consensus FY26E), Otis trades below its historical average and below the industrial peer median of 27.4x. This discount reflects genuine near-term uncertainty: if Service margins continue deteriorating, the premium-multiple thesis breaks. However, the 30% constant-currency modernization backlog growth, $200M in fully-realized UpLift restructuring savings, and the structural durability of a 2.5-million-unit installed base provide a floor. The central analytical question is whether Q1's margin compression was a temporary investment cycle (WeMaintain, digital infrastructure) or a signal that cost inflation is eroding the service moat's economics.

The stock is not actionable as a BUY today given: (1) negative earnings momentum across all periods, (2) two consecutive quarterly EPS misses, (3) meaningful insider selling in February 2026, and (4) no identifiable near-term catalyst to reverse the estimate trend. It requires evidence of Service margin stabilization in Q2 2026 results before upgrading.

## Company Overview

Otis Worldwide operates the world's largest elevator and escalator service portfolio, spun off from United Technologies (now RTX) in April 2020. New Equipment installations (35% of revenue, 3.3% margin in Q1 2026) convert into multi-decade service contracts (65% of revenue, 25%+ margin). The company employs 72,000 people including 45,000 field professionals, operates through 1,400+ branches globally, and maintains approximately 1.1 million connected units via its Otis ONE IoT platform.

Geographic mix is 71% international, with approximately 45% of workforce in Asia Pacific, 34% in EMEA, and 21% in the Americas. China represents roughly one-fifth of global New Equipment sales and over 50% of global NE unit volume, making the ongoing Chinese property crisis directly material to segment performance.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue trajectory:** $13.7B (2022) → $14.2B (2023) → $14.3B (2024) → $14.4B (2025). Organic growth has been anemic, with China NE declines offsetting Service growth. Q1 2026 showed +6% reported growth (+1% organic), with guidance for FY26 of $15.1-$15.3B.

**Profitability:** Gross margins have expanded from 29.5% (2023) to 30.3% (2025) due to mix shift toward Service. However, Q1 2026 revealed a concerning reversal: Service margins compressed 160bps to 23.0% (from 24.6% prior year) while New Equipment margins deteriorated to just 3.3%. Adjusted EPS of $0.89 missed consensus by $0.02.

**Cash generation:** FCF of $1.44B in 2025 on minimal capex ($152M, ~1% of revenue). FY26 guidance of $1.60-$1.65B implies improving conversion. The business is genuinely asset-light.

**Balance sheet:** Negative stockholders' equity of -$5.4B (structural from spin-off and buybacks, not operational distress). Total debt of ~$7.96B with net debt of $6.86B. Current ratio of 0.85. Average cost of debt rising from 2.1% (2023) to 2.8% (2025) due to refinancing — issued $500M at 5.131% in September 2025 to replace 2.056% notes. Interest expense was $196M in 2025, well-covered by $1.6B OCF.

**Capital return:** $400M in buybacks in Q1 2026 alone against $413M OCF — the company is funding returns partially from cash reserves. Shares outstanding declining from 420M (2022) to 388M (Q1 2026). Dividend of $647M annually, approximately 2.4% yield.

**Key concern:** Q1 2026 capital returns of $563M ($400M buybacks + $163M dividends) exceeded operating cash flow of $413M. This pace is unsustainable without either FCF improvement or incremental leverage.

## Growth Analysis

**Service segment (the growth engine):** +7% organic (2024), +5% organic (2025), +5% organic (Q1 2026). This is steady mid-single-digit growth from the world's largest installed base. The key accelerant is modernization — backlog up 30% at constant currency in Q1 2026, reflecting an aging global building stock.

**New Equipment (the drag):** -6% organic (2024), -7% organic (2025), -5% organic (Q1 2026). China declines of 20%+ per year are the primary driver. Americas NE orders grew +20% in Q1 at constant currency, partially offsetting. The segment is being managed for portfolio contribution (feeding future service contracts) rather than profitability.

**Full-year guidance:** Organic sales growth of low-to-mid single digits; adjusted EPS of $4.20-$4.24; adjusted FCF of $1.60-$1.65B.

**Estimate revisions are negative across all periods:** Current quarter -6.6% over 90 days; current year -3.7% over 90 days; next year -2.4% over 90 days. Consensus is moving against the company.

**Long-term structural growth drivers:** Urbanization, aging installed base requiring modernization, IoT/digital services enabling premium pricing, and emerging market penetration. These are real but slow-moving and do not address near-term margin concerns.

However, the discount is not unwarranted given: (1) negative estimate revisions, (2) two consecutive misses, (3) China structural headwind with no visibility on recovery, and (4) Service margin deterioration. The analytical question is whether this is a cyclical trough (creating opportunity) or the beginning of multiple compression reflecting permanently lower growth expectations.

**Bull case valuation:** If Service margins stabilize and modernization accelerates, a 22x multiple on $4.72 FY27E = $104 (44% upside).

**Bear case valuation:** If Service margins continue compressing and China NE doesn't bottom, 14x on $4.00 EPS = $56 (22% downside).

The asymmetry is mildly favorable (44% up vs. 22% down), but insufficient confidence in the bull case to recommend a position today.

## Competitive Landscape

Otis's competitive position is its strongest attribute. The 2.5-million-unit service portfolio creates a density advantage: more units per geographic area means lower cost per service visit, faster response times, and higher technician utilization. This is a genuine network effect in physical services.

Each new unit installed today generates 30-50 years of service revenue. Even as NE volume declines in China, Otis has been growing its net service portfolio by converting existing third-party-maintained units and gaining share through its digital offerings.

The WeMaintain acquisition (majority stake, Q1 2026) signals investment in AI-enabled service delivery — predictive maintenance, reduced callbacks, premium pricing for connected services. KONE and Schindler are pursuing similar strategies, making it table stakes rather than a differentiator today.

Independent service providers collectively hold ~50% of global service units, representing both a threat (price competition on basic maintenance) and an opportunity (consolidation target, digital differentiation enabling share gains).

**Trajectory:** Improving. Modernization backlog +30% confirms Otis is capturing aging-building upgrades from its installed base. Net portfolio unit growth remains positive. The competitive position score reflects the structural strength of the franchise despite near-term financial headwinds.

## Risk Assessment

**1. China New Equipment structural decline:** No clear floor visible. Chinese property starts remain depressed with limited fiscal stimulus targeting residential construction. Each year of 20%+ decline permanently reduces the future Service portfolio contribution from China NE, though the existing installed base remains.

**2. Service margin compression (critical watch):** The Q1 2026 decline of 160bps was attributed to growth investments (WeMaintain, digital, repair capacity) and cost inflation. If margins don't recover by Q3, the market will question whether the 25%+ service margin is sustainable.

**3. Tariffs and trade disruption:** ~$20M annual impact flagged; manageable at company level but creating margin pressure at the thin NE segment.

**4. Capital allocation aggression:** At elevated net debt levels, further leverage increases create vulnerability if FCF disappoints. Average debt cost rising.

**5. Estimate revisions accelerating downward:** Wolfe Research's May 8 downgrade specifically cited "weaker-than-expected first half" and "earnings visibility concerns."

## Conclusion

Otis Worldwide possesses a genuinely high-quality service franchise — the world's largest elevator maintenance portfolio with predictable recurring revenue, minimal capex requirements, and structural growth from urbanization and building aging. At ~19x forward earnings, the stock is trading at a historically wide discount to both its own history and industrial peers, reflecting legitimate near-term concerns about China exposure, NE margin erosion, and the first signs of Service profitability pressure.

The score trajectory decline from 6.6 to 5.6 over the prior month reflected the emergence of these concerns, which remain unresolved. The current 5.68 overall score confirms the trend hasn't reversed — negative estimate revisions, insider selling, and an absent near-term catalyst keep this stock in limbo. The modernization backlog and digital investments (WeMaintain, Otis ONE) provide genuine medium-term optionality, but the market needs proof that Service margins can recover before re-rating the stock.

HOLD with uncertain rationale. The thesis for owning Otis long-term (installed base compounding, digital services, modernization wave) remains structurally intact. The thesis for buying today is premature — two more quarters of data are needed to confirm whether Q1 was a trough or a trend. Wait for Q2 Service margin confirmation before adding.
