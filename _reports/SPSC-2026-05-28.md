**Research Date: May 28, 2026**


## Executive Summary

SPS Commerce operates a cloud-based supply chain network connecting 54,600+ recurring revenue customers through EDI and supply chain automation. The company has achieved 100 consecutive quarters of revenue growth and generates industry-leading cash flows (20% FCF margins) on 96% recurring revenue with 69% gross margins.

Revenue growth decelerated from 18% (FY2025) to 6% (Q1 2026), ARPU declined for the first time, operating income fell 5% YoY, and the stock has lost 64% from its 52-week high. The proximate cause is the $210M Carbon6 acquisition (February 2025), which added 8,500 low-ARPU Amazon marketplace customers facing "revenue recovery headwinds." Simultaneously, multiple C-suite departures (including the CFO), an ERP system transition, and zero insider buying at multi-year lows suggest internal uncertainty exceeds what management is communicating publicly.

The valuation at ~13x FCF and ~2.5x revenue is historically cheap for a business of this quality — significantly below the peer median P/E of 57.9x. The market is either correctly pricing a structural growth impairment or overreacting to a temporary acquisition integration challenge. Given the magnitude of the decline (>60%), the absence of insider buying, and the lack of clear evidence that Carbon6 headwinds are abating, the burden of proof remains on the bull case.

## Company Overview

SPS Commerce provides a multi-tenant cloud platform enabling electronic data interchange (EDI) between retailers, brands, distributors, manufacturers, and logistics providers. The business model leverages network effects: each new participant increases the value for all existing members. Companies connect once to SPS and can transact with thousands of trading partners without maintaining separate integration logic.

**Revenue Composition:** Fulfillment (89% of recurring revenue), Analytics (8%), and Assortment/Relationship Management (3%). Revenue is 96% recurring, subscription-based, with contracts cancellable on 30-90 days' notice.

**Customer Base:** 54,600 recurring revenue customers with no concentration risk (largest customer <1% of revenue). Operations span the U.S. (83% of revenue), Australia, Canada, Netherlands, Poland, and Ukraine.

**Channel Strategy:** Partnerships with Microsoft, NetSuite, Oracle, SAP, and Sage generate leads as ERP vendors bundle supply chain connectivity into their sales motions.

## Growth Analysis

The growth story has two distinct phases:

**Pre-Carbon6 (through FY2025):** Consistent 18-19% organic + acquisition-driven growth. Customer count expanding at ~20% annually. ARPU rising 8% through upsells and cross-sells.

**Post-Carbon6 (Q1 2026 onward):** Growth collapsed to 6%. ARPU declined 2% — the first reported decline — as 8,500 low-ARPU Amazon marketplace sellers diluted the average. The "Amazon revenue recovery headwinds" cited by management suggest Carbon6's core product (revenue recovery tools for 3P sellers) is facing secular challenges as Amazon adjusts marketplace policies.

**Forward Estimates:**
- Current year (FY2026): $4.73 EPS (consensus, revised up 5.4% over 90 days)
- Next year (FY2027): $5.22 EPS (revised up 3.1% over 30 days)

The positive estimate revisions are modest — up ~5% over 90 days, not the 30%+ signal that would indicate the market is systematically underestimating recovery. The current quarter estimate actually declined 1.6% over 90 days.

**Growth Catalysts:**
1. $19.99/month platform fee — new pricing lever introduced to offset ARPU dilution
2. International expansion (currently only 17% of revenue)
3. AI-enhanced supply chain analytics
4. Continued 1P customer acquisition through channel partners

**Growth Risks:**
1. Carbon6/3P customers may churn at higher rates given 30-day contracts
2. Tariff uncertainty reducing retail supply chain investment
3. Retail bankruptcies reducing addressable customer base
4. AI-native competitors could bypass traditional EDI infrastructure

## Valuation Assessment

At $53.77, SPSC trades at:
- 22x trailing P/E (EPS $2.46 trailing)
- ~2.5x FY2026 revenue ($800M)
- ~8.7x FY2025 Adjusted EBITDA ($231M)
- ~13x FY2025 FCF ($152M)
- 11.4x FY2026 consensus EPS ($4.73)

**Peer Comparison:** The peer median P/E is 57.9x. SPSC at 11.4x on consensus FY2026 EPS represents an extraordinary discount to peers — though peers are generally growing faster.

**Why the discount might be deserved:** If 6-7% becomes the new normal, a 2.5x revenue multiple and ~13x FCF multiple is reasonable for a slow-growth infrastructure software business. At 6% growth, SPSC resembles a legacy IT services company more than a SaaS compounder.

**Why the discount might be excessive:** The underlying 1P business (46,900 customers) is likely still growing in the low-teens organically. The Carbon6 drag may stabilize or be sold/wound down. At $152M in FCF + $300M in buyback capacity against a $2B market cap, the company can retire 7-8% of shares annually, creating meaningful per-share compounding even at low growth rates.

**Forward P/E on FY2026 consensus:** 11.4x. For a 96% recurring revenue business with 69% gross margins and 20% FCF margins, this implies the market expects either (a) growth to decelerate further, (b) margins to compress, or (c) a material risk event. Even a 50% discount to peer median (29x) would imply ~$137, representing 155% upside.

The analyst consensus median target is $60 (range $55-$103), suggesting limited near-term upside expectation but confirming downside is limited from current levels.

## Competitive Landscape

**Moat Assessment:** SPS Commerce's competitive advantage derives from network density — the number of pre-built connections and trading partners on the platform. This creates a virtuous cycle: retailers require suppliers to be EDI-compliant → suppliers join SPS → more retailers choose SPS because their supply base is already connected.

**Competitive Position (Stable to Slightly Weakening):**
- Market remains "fragmented and rapidly evolving" per 10-K
- SAP, Oracle, and other ERP vendors could internalize EDI functionality
- AI-native supply chain tools (e.g., from hyperscalers) could disintermediate traditional EDI
- The shift to 3P/marketplace (Carbon6) moves the company away from its core moat into a more competitive market where Amazon itself sets the rules

**Network Effect Durability:** For the core 1P business, the network effect remains intact. No single competitor has matched SPS's breadth of pre-built connections. However, the growth opportunity is increasingly in the 3P/marketplace segment where SPS has less differentiation.

## Risk Assessment

**Critical Risks:**

1. **Carbon6 Deterioration (High Probability, High Impact):** If Amazon marketplace policies continue shifting unfavorably for 3P sellers, Carbon6's customer base could churn. At 30-day cancellable contracts and lower ARPU, these customers are both less valuable and less sticky than the core base. Impairment of the $136M in Carbon6-related goodwill is plausible.

2. **Executive Turnover (Elevated Uncertainty):** Six 8-K filings with Item 5.02 (officer departures) in 18 months. CFO departed. New CCO hired. ERP transition creating material internal control changes. This level of leadership flux during a business transition adds execution risk.

3. **Further Growth Deceleration:** The 2026 guidance ($796-$802M) implies no acceleration from Q1's 6% rate. If macro weakness intensifies (tariff uncertainty, consumer spending weakness), retail supply chain investment could contract.

4. **No Insider Conviction Signal:** Zero open-market insider purchases despite the stock at 60%+ discount from highs. The company is buying back shares aggressively at the corporate level, but no executive is personally buying. This asymmetry suggests insiders may lack confidence in near-term recovery or see risk not yet in public estimates.

5. **Ukraine Operations:** Development staff in Ukraine creates ongoing geopolitical and operational continuity risk.

**Mitigating Factors:**
- Balance sheet is fortress-quality ($154M cash, minimal debt)
- 96% recurring revenue provides floor
- No customer concentration
- $300M buyback provides EPS support

## Investment Thesis

- Carbon6 headwinds prove temporary; 3P customer base stabilizes and begins growing
- Core 1P business reaccelerates to low-teens growth as tariff uncertainty resolves
- $19.99 platform fee drives ARPU recovery
- Aggressive buybacks at ~$55 retire 10%+ of float over 18 months
- Multiple re-rates from 11x to 16-18x forward earnings as growth visibility improves
- Math: 16x $5.22 FY2027 EPS = $83.50

**Bear Case ($35-$42, -22% to -35%):**
- Carbon6 churns accelerate; goodwill impairment taken
- Core 1P growth decelerates to 3-4% as macro weakness hits retail
- AI alternatives begin displacing traditional EDI (longer-term)
- Revenue stagnates at $800M; margins compress further
- Multiple contracts to 8-9x depressed FCF
- Math: 9x $130M impaired FCF = $35/share

**Base Case ($60-$70, +12% to +30%):**
- Growth stabilizes at 6-8% through FY2027
- Carbon6 becomes neutral (neither accretive nor impairing)
- Buybacks retire 7-8% of shares annually
- Margins stabilize near current levels once transition costs roll off
- Math: 13x $5.00 normalized EPS = $65

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
1. Consecutive quarterly revenue growth acceleration above 10% YoY for two quarters
2. ARPU returning to positive growth (demonstrating Carbon6 dilution has been absorbed)
3. Any insider open-market purchase by CEO or new CFO at current levels
4. Analyst estimate revisions exceeding +15% over 30 days for FY2027

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
1. Revenue growth decelerating below 4% YoY in any reported quarter
2. Goodwill impairment charge taken on Carbon6 assets
3. Net customer losses reported for the first time
4. Operating margins falling below 10% (currently 12.8%)

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August 2026) will show whether the 6% growth rate is stabilizing or deteriorating further. Q3 2026 (late October) will indicate whether the $19.99 platform fee is driving any ARPU recovery.

## Conclusion

SPS Commerce presents a quality business at a challenged moment. The financial fundamentals — cash generation, balance sheet, recurring revenue — are strong. The stock at 11-13x earnings is cheap relative to quality. But the growth deceleration is real, the Carbon6 acquisition appears to be underperforming, executive turnover is elevated, and no insider is buying with personal capital.

Whether 6% growth is a temporary trough or the new structural reality cannot be determined with confidence today. The positive estimate revision trend (FY2026 up 5.4% over 90 days) suggests consensus expects stabilization, but the magnitude is too modest to trigger a conviction call.

At current levels, downside appears limited (fortress balance sheet, aggressive buybacks, sticky customer base), but catalysts for upside are not yet visible in data. HOLD with reassessment at Q2 2026 earnings.
