**Research Date: July 26, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Uniti Group is a fundamentally different company than it was a year ago. On August 1, 2025, the fiber-infrastructure REIT merged with Windstream, its former primary tenant, in a reverse merger that eliminated single-tenant concentration risk, terminated REIT status (no dividend), and internalized the fiber assets Uniti had been leasing out. What trades today is a vertically integrated, three-segment telecom operator with ~240,000 fiber route miles across 47 states, carrying ~$10.7 billion of debt against roughly $320 million of book equity and a $2.6 billion market cap. The equity is a leveraged call option on the Kinetic fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) buildout.

The reported financials are misleading in a way that materially changes the investment case. The trailing 2.6x P/E and the ~$1.3B FY2025 net income are artifacts of a one-time ~$1.68B gain from "settlement of preexisting relationships" in the merger accounting. The underlying business runs at a net loss (–$70.3M in Q1 2026, –$0.34/share to common after preferred dividends) and generates negative free cash flow because capex (~$349M in Q1) exceeds operating cash flow (~$261M). The correct lens is Adjusted EBITDA of ~$1.77B annualized against ~$10.7B of debt (roughly 6x leverage) and ~$750M of annual interest expense. Any model built on GAAP profitability will be wrong.

We rate UNIT a HOLD with an uncertain rationale. The bull case — Kinetic FTTH penetration compounding, hyperscaler/AI dark-fiber demand, and Elliott's activist alignment — is real but requires years of continued negative-FCF capex and orderly refinancing of a $7.36B "thereafter" maturity wall at 7.5–9%+ coupons. The bear case — legacy revenue eroding ~8% pro forma faster than fiber can offset it, an Altman Z-Score near 0.29 (distress zone), a self-reported RDOF build-milestone miss, and an expensive $575M preferred stepping from 11% to 16% — is equally concrete. The stock has more than doubled off its August 2025 low near $6, and the score trajectory has drifted up (4.5 → 4.4 → 4.8), but the fundamental risk/reward remains genuinely two-sided.

## Company Overview

Uniti operates three segments post-merger:

- **Kinetic (~55% of revenue)** — the ILEC residential/business/wholesale broadband franchise and the entire equity thesis. This is the FTTH buildout converting a legacy copper/DSL rural-and-suburban footprint to fiber. As of Q1 2026, Kinetic had passed 1.9M premises (41% of its footprint) at a 29% fiber penetration rate with 564,000 fiber subscribers. The remaining ~59% of the footprint is the growth runway.
- **Uniti Solutions (~19%)** — CLEC managed services (SASE, UCaaS, SD-WAN) for mid-market/enterprise, a legacy business being deliberately shrunk toward profitability. Revenue fell 21% pro forma on the TDM wind-down.
- **Fiber Infrastructure (~26%)** — the legacy Uniti wholesale/dark-fiber/wave-transport business serving carriers, content providers, and hyperscalers. This segment benefits from AI/cloud fiber demand, evidenced by a Q1 2026 hyperscaler dark-fiber IRU deal, but revenue recognition is lumpy.

The strategic logic of the merger was sound: Old Uniti's dominant risk was that Windstream represented ~65% of lease revenue and had gone through bankruptcy in 2019–2020. Merging eliminated that tenant-default exposure. Leadership continuity favored the Old Uniti (accounting acquirer) side: CEO Kenny Gunderman and CFO Paul Bullington are both from the Old Uniti side.

## Financial Analysis

Financial health is precarious, and the screening metrics are actively misleading.

**The GAAP headline is noise.** The FY2025 net income (and the implied 58% net margin / 343% ROE in the screening data) reflect the one-time merger gain, not operating earnings. On a normalized basis the company loses money.

**Q1 2026 actuals:** Revenue $987.5M (up sharply YoY on merger consolidation); net loss to common $85.8M after a $15.5M preferred dividend; Adjusted EBITDA $441.6M.

**The organic trend is decline, masked by consolidation.** On a pro forma (apples-to-apples) basis, Q1 2026 vs Q1 2025:
- Service revenue –8% (–$75.4M)
- Kinetic consumer –4%, business –7%, wholesale –24%
- Uniti Solutions –21% (deliberate TDM wind-down)
- Fiber subscriber revenue +26% — the mix shift is working, but off a small base
- Total revenue was roughly flat (+1%) only because of a one-time $71.4M hyperscaler IRU dark-fiber sale recognized as a sales-type lease. Strip that out and revenue declined.

**Cash flow is the crux.** Operating cash flow was $260.9M in Q1 2026, but capex was $349.2M (mostly Kinetic fiber), producing negative free cash flow. This is the intended model — spend now to build a fiber annuity — but it is funded by debt, and it will persist for years.

**Leverage is extreme.** Debt rose from ~$9.5B at 12/31/2025 to $10.68B at 3/31/2026, against ~$320M of book equity. Interest expense runs ~$188M/quarter (~$750M+ annualized), much of it high-coupon (8.25%, 8.625%). The Altman Z-Score of ~0.29 sits in bankruptcy-model distress territory. This is partly a function of an asset-heavy infrastructure balance sheet, but the leverage is genuinely at the high end even for the category.

**Liquidity is adequate near-term.** $982.6M cash plus ~$862M revolver availability at 3/31/2026, with management asserting sufficient liquidity for 12+ months. The near-term maturity ladder is manageable ($316M in 2027, $580M in 2028, $1.12B in 2029), but $7.36B matures "thereafter" at prevailing rates. The refinancing machine is running continuously: an inaugural $960.1M Kinetic ABS in January 2026 (5.2–7.7% coupons, cheaper than corporate unsecured), a $1.0B term loan in October 2025, and $1.0B of 8.625% notes in February 2026. Securitizing FTTH contracts through bankruptcy-remote ABS structures is the key financing innovation lowering the marginal cost of the buildout.

## Growth Analysis

The growth story is entirely Kinetic FTTH. The runway is the remaining ~59% of the footprint plus rising penetration on already-passed homes, and fiber subscriber revenue growth demonstrates the model works at the unit level.

The problem is arithmetic. Fiber growth is not yet fast enough to offset legacy erosion. This is a race between the fiber annuity ramping and the copper/DSL/wholesale/TDM base declining. The company will spend heavily and burn free cash flow until the fiber base is large enough to flip the aggregate trend positive — a multi-year proposition.

Two secondary drivers help: (1) hyperscaler/AI demand for long-haul and dark fiber (the Q1 IRU is one data point, lumpy but structurally supported by broader data-center buildout demand), and (2) government subsidies — RDOF ($522.8M over 10 years), BEAD provisional awards ($184.3M / ~58,000 locations across 7 states, contracts expected to begin signing Q2 2026), and state USF. The subsidies come with build obligations and Buy-America strings, and Uniti has already stumbled on execution (see Risk Assessment).

Estimate revisions are mixed and net negative on the longer horizon. Near-term (30-day) revisions are marginally positive (current quarter +1.1%, current year +0.2%), but the 90-day trend is negative across every window (current quarter –16.8%, next year –32.8%). All estimates remain losses (current-year EPS –$1.83, next-year –$1.62). This is not the +30% upward revision velocity that flags an earnings inflection; it is a company where consensus has been walking losses wider even as the near-term stabilizes.

## Valuation Assessment

Standard multiples do not apply. The 2.6x trailing/forward P/E is meaningless — it divides the market cap by a one-time merger gain. The company has no earnings and no dividend.

The correct frame is enterprise value against Adjusted EBITDA and against the fiber-buildout option value. Enterprise value is roughly $13.3B ($2.6B equity + ~$10.7B debt), or ~7.5x EV/EBITDA. That is not obviously cheap for a business with declining organic revenue, negative free cash flow, and ~6x net leverage. Peer comparison is unhelpful here: CCOI and LUMN both show negative P/Es (also loss-making), and IRDM at 50x is a different (satellite) business. The Alternative Carriers "peer median" P/E of 50.4x carries no signal for a company like this.

The equity is best understood as a levered call option. With ~$10.7B of debt against a ~$2.6B equity cushion, modest changes in enterprise value are magnified into equity moves. If FTTH scales (more premises, higher penetration, ARPU growth) and refinancing stays orderly, the equity re-rates substantially. If legacy erosion outpaces fiber or credit conditions tighten, the equity is disproportionately exposed. This is a symmetric, high-variance payoff, not an asymmetric value opportunity.

We caution explicitly against reading the compressed headline multiple as "cheap." A low reported P/E on a one-time gain flags a value trap rather than a value opportunity, and there is no independent catalyst here that reprices the equity higher in a way the market has clearly missed. The stock has already re-rated from ~$6 (August 2025) to ~$11, so much of the "the merger de-risked the tenant" repricing has occurred.

## Competitive Landscape

Competitive pressure is intensifying. Uniti's rural/suburban FTTH footprint is under attack from cable (DOCSIS upgrades), fixed wireless (T-Mobile/Verizon 5G home internet), and satellite (Starlink) — all structurally lower-cost and less regulated, with no carrier-of-last-resort obligation. Government-funded competitors via RDOF and BEAD are targeting the same locations.

The bull counterpoint is that incumbent copper telcos (Uniti/Kinetic, Frontier, Lumen, Brightspeed) are all racing to overbuild themselves with fiber before these alternatives permanently capture rural broadband, and FTTH is a genuinely superior physical-layer product. Uniti's 29%-and-rising penetration on passed homes is evidence the product wins where it is deployed.

On trajectory — the dimension that matters most — the picture is mixed. Fiber is gaining, but the aggregate franchise is still shrinking, and the competitive set is structurally advantaged on cost. We score competitive position below neutral.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Leverage and refinancing (dominant risk).** Chronic capital-markets dependence, high-coupon paper, and the $7.36B "thereafter" maturity wall. Cross-default provisions ($75M threshold) link the complex. A higher-for-longer rate environment directly raises the cost of every refinancing.
2. **Secular legacy decline.** The fiber buildout is a race against copper/TDM/wholesale erosion.
3. **RDOF milestone miss (live regulatory flag).** Uniti self-reported missing the 40% build-completion milestone by 12/31/2025 in several states due to permitting delays, notified the FCC 1/15/2026, and stated it "will cooperate on any noncompliance measures." This introduces penalty/clawback risk and warrants monitoring in upcoming filings.
4. **$575M preferred stock.** 11% dividend for 6 years, stepping to a 16% cap (~$15.5M/quarter), senior to common, with an Elliott-affiliate forced-repurchase feature after year 10 and cash-or-stock settlement optionality (dilution risk).
5. **Merger integration.** Early-stage, complex, synergies unproven.
6. **Assumed pension.** $103.6M obligation with $20.6M required 2026 contributions, rate/market sensitive.
7. **Lead-clad copper cable litigation/regulatory exposure**, explicitly flagged.
8. **Controlling shareholder / governance.** Elliott owns ~25.2% (insiders ~36.1%) with board designation rights — sophisticated and equity-aligned, but with disclosed conflicts (Crown Castle stake, corporate-opportunity waiver) and a freely-tradable overhang.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term ATM IV is 50.9% versus 52.5% one-year historical volatility — fair, with no rich/cheap edge. The put/call skew of 10.0% and put/call volume ratio of 1.50 indicate elevated put demand, consistent with the 14.2% short interest and cautious institutional positioning. ATM IV has collapsed from 426.3% (June 18 snapshot) to 50.9%; that prior spike reflected a specific event window that has resolved, and current implied volatility is back to a normal, event-free level. The nearest-expiry implied move is ±11.1%, and the term structure is flat, meaning the market sees uncertainty evenly distributed rather than concentrated around a single dated catalyst. For a BUY thesis this elevated put skew would be a divergence to flag; for our HOLD it reinforces the two-sided read.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case.** The Kinetic FTTH buildout compounds: penetration rises above 29%, more of the remaining footprint gets passed, ARPU grows, and fiber subscriber revenue crosses over to offset legacy decline. Cheaper ABS financing progressively lowers the cost of capital and refinancing proceeds orderly. Hyperscaler/AI dark-fiber demand adds episodic upside. Elliott, as the activist owner who engineered the merger, drives a further value-creating catalyst (M&A, strategic sale, or refinancing engineering). In this world the levered equity re-rates toward the high analyst target of $16.

**Bear case.** Legacy erosion outpaces fiber growth, keeping aggregate revenue in decline and free cash flow negative for longer than the balance sheet can comfortably absorb. Credit markets tighten or rates stay high, making the maturity wall expensive or difficult to refinance. RDOF noncompliance triggers penalties or clawbacks. The preferred step-up and Elliott's freely-tradable overhang pressure common equity. In this world the equity — the thin residual claim behind the debt — falls toward the low analyst target of $6, near the August 2025 lows.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (uncertain rationale).** The setup is a high-variance, symmetric levered bet, not a confident directional call in either direction.

**Reassessment timeline:** Next quarterly earnings (Q2 2026) and each subsequent print, with particular attention to pro forma revenue trajectory and RDOF/FCC developments.

**Upgrade triggers (toward BUY):**
- Pro forma aggregate service revenue inflects to flat or positive as fiber growth overtakes legacy decline, with sustained fiber-sub net adds.
- Free cash flow inflects toward breakeven, or a clear, funded path to positive FCF is demonstrated (e.g., continued cheap ABS execution reducing the refinancing burden and capex intensity peaking).
- Estimate revisions turn broadly positive across all windows, especially next-year EPS improving from –$1.62.
- Clustered open-market insider buying — currently absent — signaling internal conviction.

**Downgrade triggers (toward AVOID):**
- A refinancing failure, credit downgrade, or covenant stress on the maturity wall; or ABS market access closing.
- RDOF noncompliance escalating to material penalties or funding clawback.
- Pro forma revenue decline accelerating beyond ~8%, or fiber sub-add growth decelerating.
- A forced or dilutive resolution of the preferred, or a large Elliott stake sale into the market.

## Conclusion

Uniti is a leveraged equity call option on the Kinetic fiber buildout, wrapped in a balance sheet that leaves little margin for error. The reported profitability is a merger-accounting artifact; the real business loses money, burns free cash flow, and carries ~6x leverage against a distressed Altman Z-Score. The fiber thesis is genuine — penetration and fiber revenue growth show the product wins — but it is a multi-year, capital-intensive race against legacy erosion and refinancing risk, with a live RDOF compliance flag and an expensive senior preferred ahead of common.

The score trajectory has improved modestly (to 4.8 last, this read 4.35 as we normalize the growth and management assessments against the negative 90-day revision trend and absence of insider conviction buying), and the merger did remove the tenant-concentration risk that defined Old Uniti. But nothing in the current data warrants either a BUY (no earnings inflection, no upward revision velocity, no valuation asymmetry, elevated put demand) or an active AVOID (adequate near-term liquidity, a real fiber option, a sophisticated aligned owner). The call is HOLD, sized as a speculative position for investors comfortable with binary balance-sheet outcomes, and reassessed at each print against the specific triggers above.
