**Research Date: June 19, 2026**

## Executive Summary

Interactive Brokers is among the highest-quality franchises in the brokerage industry. It runs the lowest-cost, most automated global trading and custody platform, serving active traders, RIAs, and professional firms across 150+ markets in 30+ countries. The operating record is exceptional: net income grew from $1.64B in 2021 to $4.36B in 2025 (roughly a 28% CAGR), adjusted pre-tax margin expanded from 67% to 77%, and the leading operating indicators are accelerating: May 2026 daily average revenue trades (DARTs) rose 47% YoY, ending client equity reached $937B (+49% YoY), and the firm added more than one million net new accounts in 2025. The franchise debate is settled; the live question is price.

At $96.00 the stock trades at roughly 41x trailing and ~33x forward (FY27 consensus EPS $2.88) earnings, against a same-sector peer median P/E of 19.0. The stock has appreciated about 82% over the trailing year and ~33% in the first half of 2026, moving past the sell-side. The consensus mean price target is $88.27 and the median is $90, both below the current price. The high target of $109 implies +13.5% upside; the low of $64 implies -33% downside. That reward:risk asymmetry, combined with a forward EPS growth rate (~15% FY26→FY27) that does not support a 33x multiple on a PEG basis (~2.2), is the reason this is a hold rather than a buy at current levels.

We rate IBKR HOLD with a growth-hold rationale. We want to own the compounding, and the operating momentum makes an AVOID indefensible, but we will not chase the stock above consensus targets when a material share of record earnings is rate-dependent net interest income and the multiple offers no cushion against any deceleration. The composite score of 6.5 reflects a fortress balance sheet and strong growth offset by a valuation that prices in continued perfection.

## Company Overview

Interactive Brokers Group is a technology-led electronic broker. Three revenue engines drive results:

- **Net interest income (NII)** — the largest profit contributor. IBKR earns spread on margin loans to clients, interest on segregated client cash, and securities lending. This makes earnings directly levered to both the level of short-term rates and the size of client margin/cash balances.
- **Commissions** — transaction revenue scaled to trading volume (DARTs).
- **Other fees and services** — market data, FX, the IBKR debit card, and ancillary services.

The model is asset-light with very high incremental margins because the platform is automated. The firm is structured as an "Up-C": the public Class A company holds a minority economic interest in operating entity IBG LLC, with founder/Chairman Thomas Peterffy and insiders holding the majority via Class B interests. This is why GAAP net income attributable to common holders is far smaller than Group pretax income, and why per-share figures require care. A 4-for-1 forward split executed in mid-2025 explains the ~$96 nominal price and the apparent discontinuity in historical share counts. CEO Milan Galik is a long-tenured, internally promoted engineer; Peterffy retains effective voting control.

## Financial Analysis

The financial profile is the strongest dimension of the story.

| FY (Dec 31) | Net Income | Adj. Pre-Tax Margin |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | $1.64B | 67% |
| 2022 | $1.84B | 67% |
| 2023 | $2.81B | 71% |
| 2024 | $3.41B | 72% |
| 2025 | $4.36B | 77% |

Net income grew ~2.7x over four years while the margin expanded 10 points. That is rare operating leverage: incremental revenue from new accounts and higher balances drops to the bottom line at very high rates because the platform is fixed-cost and automated. Total shareholder return over the measurement period indexed to 434 versus a peer group at 196.

As a broker-dealer, IBKR carries a large balance sheet (client cash, margin receivables, securities borrowed/loaned) but runs it conservatively. The firm maintains excess regulatory capital well above requirements and operates a real-time-margined book that has avoided losses in past industry blowups. Cash generation is high; capital return is modest (the dividend yields ~0.37%), reflecting the founder's preference to retain capital for growth and regulatory cushion. The retained capital is funding account growth that compounds NII and commissions.

The earnings-quality caveat is concentration in rate-sensitive NII. Record 2024–2025 results were materially boosted by the high-rate environment. The structural risk sits further out: a sustained rate-cut cycle would compress the largest profit lever even if account and volume growth continues.

The earnings-surprise record supports confidence in near-term estimates: three beats and one narrow miss over the last four quarters (Q4 2025 +7.8%, Q3 2025 +3.1%, Q2 2025 +8.2%, Q1 2026 -2.2%). The recent quarter's small miss is the first crack in the beat streak and worth monitoring, though it is within noise.

## Growth Analysis

Growth is durable and currently accelerating, which is what separates this from a cyclical peak story.

- **DARTs of 4.969M in May 2026, +47% YoY and +17% MoM**, extraordinary for a firm of this scale.
- **Ending client equity of $937B, +49% YoY and +8% MoM.**
- **Over one million net new accounts added in 2025**, the leading indicator that feeds future NII and commissions.

Account growth is the engine: new accounts bring cash and margin balances that compound NII, and trading activity that compounds commissions. These metrics are accelerating at a company already earning $4.4B, and the main de-rating risk (growth deceleration) is not visible in the leading indicators.

Estimate revisions are positive but modest: current-year EPS revised +2.8% over 90 days to $2.51, next-year +5.0% to $2.88. This is a steady upward grind, not the +30% velocity that flags a trough-to-recovery recovery. The operating metrics (40%+ growth) far outrun the EPS growth estimates (~15% FY26→FY27) because consensus bakes in NII normalization. The market is paying a premium multiple for operating momentum that the EPS line, as modeled, does not fully reflect.

IBKR trades at roughly 2x the peer median. The premium is partly justified, because IBKR's 28% net income CAGR and 77% margin dwarf the full-service brokers carrying branch and advisor cost structures, but a PEG of ~2.2 (33x forward / ~15% forward EPS growth) is not a margin of safety. Robinhood is the only listed peer at a comparable multiple, and it trades 39% below its 52-week high.

The sell-side, after watching the stock run, is not chasing it: the consensus mean target of $88.27 and median of $90 sit below the current $96. The target range runs $64 (-33%) to $109 (+13.5%), a reward:risk of roughly 1:2.4. The recently published bull-case initiation (Wolfe at Outperform) is offset by a hold initiation explicitly grounded in valuation. Both sides agree the franchise is excellent; the disagreement is entirely about price.

Applying cycle-adjusted logic does not rescue the multiple. There is no trough here: earnings are at a cyclical and structural high (rate-boosted NII plus record activity). The stock is being valued on peak-rate earnings, the opposite of a depressed base. Treating analyst targets as floors is inappropriate at >30x forward; at premium multiples, targets reflect the same growth extrapolation as the price rather than independent confirmation of upside.

## Competitive Landscape

IBKR's competitive position is strong and improving, which supports the long-term ownership case even as price caps the near-term entry.

- **Cost and technology leadership.** Few competitors offer genuine multi-market, multi-currency global access; this is the basis for the "only platform with a true global footprint" thesis.
- **Professional/active-trader and RIA focus** rather than mass-retail gamification, a structurally higher-balance, more profitable client base than Robinhood's, and a more efficient cost structure than Schwab's or Morgan Stanley's.
- **Operating-leverage moat.** The automated platform converts growth into profit far more efficiently than full-service brokers, evidenced by the 77% pre-tax margin.

The trajectory is favorable: IBKR is taking deposit and trading share, benefiting from the globalization of retail investing and the shift to mobile. The principal industry headwind is shared by the whole sector: the rate cycle inflated NII broadly and would deflate it on cuts. On a company-specific basis, the moat is widening, not compressing.

## Risk Assessment

1. **Interest-rate sensitivity (dominant).** A large share of profit is NII on client cash and margin balances. A sustained rate-cut cycle is the specific catalyst that would compress earnings and de-rate a 41x multiple. This is the variable a standardized data pull would miss.
2. **Valuation de-rating risk.** At ~33x forward with consensus targets below price, any growth deceleration or earnings miss would hit the multiple hard. The narrow Q1 2026 miss (-2.2%, ending a beat streak) is an early item to watch.
3. **Market-activity dependence.** Commissions and margin balances move with volatility and asset prices; a prolonged bear market or volatility collapse would pressure both lines and client equity.
4. **Governance / controlled-company structure.** The Up-C / Class B structure leaves public holders with limited governance influence; they ride alongside the founder rather than steering.
5. **Multi-jurisdictional regulatory exposure** across 30+ countries (SEC, FINRA, CFTC, plus EU/UK/Asia regimes), with fine and operational-restriction risk.
6. **Filing housekeeping.** The recurring 10-K/A amendment pattern appears administrative (Part III / Item 9A controls language) rather than a restatement red flag, consistent with prior years, but the original 10-K's internal-controls disclosure warrants a direct read to confirm no material weakness.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($109+).** Operating momentum proves durable: DARTs and client equity continue compounding at 30–50% YoY, the rate path holds NII near record levels, and account growth sustains the flywheel. Margins hold at 77%+, the multiple persists, and consensus estimates revise upward toward the +20% growth that would justify a 33x multiple. The franchise quality (global cost leadership, asset-light operating leverage) re-rates IBKR as a structural compounder. The high analyst target of $109 (+13.5%) frames this scenario.

**Bear Case ($64–70).** Rates begin a cutting cycle, compressing the NII profit base; growth in DARTs and client equity decelerates from extraordinary to merely good; the beat streak (already nicked in Q1 2026) gives way to misses. A 41x trailing multiple on normalizing peak earnings compresses toward the peer cohort (17–19x), driving a 25–33% drawdown. The low analyst target of $64 (-33%) frames this scenario. This requires growth deceleration that is not yet visible in the leading indicators.

The base case sits between: continued strong-but-decelerating growth, a gradual multiple normalization as EPS growth (~15%) reasserts itself over operating-metric growth (40%+), and a stock that tracks roughly sideways-to-modestly-higher around the $88–96 zone where consensus targets cluster.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Recommendation: HOLD (growth-hold). Reassessment timeline: next earnings (Q2 2026, expected late July) and the monthly DART/client-equity prints.**

This is a hold rather than a buy for three concrete, falsifiable reasons. First, the consensus mean ($88.27) and median ($90) price targets are below the current price of $96; the sell-side does not see upside from here after the run, and the high target of $109 offers only +13.5% against a low of $64 (-33%), a reward:risk near 1:2.4. Second, the stock trades at ~33x forward / PEG ~2.2 versus a peer median of 19x, while forward EPS growth (FY26 $2.51 → FY27 $2.88, +15%) does not support that multiple on a growth-adjusted basis. Third, a large share of record earnings is rate-dependent NII; NII compression is the specific catalyst a 41x trailing multiple is least able to absorb.

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- A pullback to the high-$70s to low-$80s (~28–30x forward), restoring a margin of safety while operating momentum holds.
- Next-year EPS estimates accelerating past +20% revision velocity (currently +5.0%/90d), with consensus targets revising above $105, which would convert the operating momentum into EPS growth that justifies the multiple.
- Confirmation that NII is structurally insulated (e.g., balance growth offsetting rate normalization), removing the dominant earnings-quality risk.

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- DARTs or client-equity growth decelerating to single digits YoY for two consecutive months, signaling the flywheel is slowing while the multiple still prices acceleration.
- A second consecutive EPS miss after the Q1 2026 -2.2% print, indicating deteriorating visibility.
- A confirmed material weakness in the Item 9A internal-controls disclosure, or escalation of the Vice Chairman's open-market selling into broad C-suite liquidation.

## Conclusion

Interactive Brokers is one of the best businesses in financial services: a global, automated, cost-leading platform compounding accounts and client equity at 40%+ with a 77% pre-tax margin and a fortress, conservatively run balance sheet. Nothing in the current data argues against owning it for the long-term compounding story. The constraint is entirely price. At ~41x trailing / ~33x forward, 2x the peer median, with consensus targets sitting below the current quote and a reward:risk skewed toward downside, the stock has run past where the franchise quality alone justifies adding. A material portion of record earnings rests on rate-dependent NII, which becomes the de-rating catalyst if the rate path turns.

The score has hovered around 6.8–7.1 with one BUY (May 7) that correctly preceded a ~12% rally, followed by a HOLD that captured the franchise quality while flagging valuation. The improving financial-health and stabilizing growth dimensions are confirmed by the latest data; the management dimension is dampened by genuine Vice Chairman open-market selling (~$30M+ in January 2026), partly offset by small steady director buying. Our composite of 6.5 reflects an excellent business priced for continued perfection. **HOLD; accumulate on a pullback toward the high-$70s to low-$80s, where the risk/reward turns clearly favorable.**
