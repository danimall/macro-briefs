## Executive Summary

Dorian LPG operates a fleet of 28 Very Large Gas Carriers (VLGCs) with near-exclusive exposure to the LPG spot market through its Helios Pool joint venture with Mitsui OSK Lines. After a cyclical trough in FY2025 (ended March 2025) where TCE rates fell 36% to $39,778/day, the company is now benefiting from a rate recovery with 99% of calendar days for the quarter ending March 31, 2026 fixed at >$58,000/day, and industry sources suggesting further strengthening into May 2026 driven by tight effective vessel supply and robust U.S. export volumes amplified by Middle East shipping disruption.

At $40/share, the stock trades at an estimated ~9.2x forward P/E on consensus FY2027 EPS of approximately $4.33, though these consensus figures should be treated with caution given the difficulty of forecasting spot-rate-dependent earnings. The balance sheet as of December 31, 2025 carries $294.5M cash with a current ratio of approximately 2.41x. Fleet market value exceeds book by ~$590M (as of the March 2025 independent appraisal), providing NAV support. The CEO's open-market purchase at $27.30 in January 2026 and 11.7% ownership stake provide strong alignment.

The principal risks are the 27% fleet orderbook (108 vessels through 2029), China's tariffs on U.S.-sourced LPG — which caused a 29% decline in U.S. propane exports to China in 2025 — and the stock's extreme sensitivity to spot rates, a characteristic that makes forward earnings estimates unreliable beyond 2-3 quarters.

## Company Overview

Dorian LPG is one of the world's largest independent VLGC owners. As of March 2026, the company operates a fleet of 28 VLGCs consisting of six dual-fuel ECO VLGCs, twenty ECO VLGCs, and two modern VLGCs, with approximately 2.3 million cubic meters of aggregate capacity and an average fleet age of approximately 10 years. 16 vessels are scrubber-equipped, and the newest addition — the 93,000 cbm "Areion" delivered March 2026 from Hanwha Ocean — is the company's first ammonia-capable dual-fuel vessel.

Revenue generation is almost entirely through the Helios Pool (97% of total revenues in FY2025), a 50/50 JV with MOL Energia (subsidiary of Mitsui OSK Lines) that pools VLGCs and provides primarily spot market exposure on the dominant U.S. Gulf-to-Asia trade lane. This structure maximizes upside in strong rate environments but provides minimal downside protection.

## Growth Analysis

Growth for Dorian is rate-dependent, not company-specific. The relevant question is whether VLGC rates sustain at elevated levels or revert to trough.

**Bullish rate drivers (currently active):**
- Middle East shipping disruption: Hormuz tensions and Fujairah infrastructure attacks have increased ton-mile demand for alternative routing and elevated tanker insurance costs, spilling over into LPG carrier demand
- U.S. LPG export volumes at record levels (1.8 million b/d propane exports in 2025 per EIA data)
- Effective fleet supply tightened: environmental regulations (EU ETS, CII), drydocking schedules, and scrapping of older non-ECO/non-scrubber VLGCs reduce available tonnage
- Panama Canal constraints create episodic bottlenecks on the primary U.S.-Asia route

**Evidence of recovery:** Dorian reported on March 26, 2026 that it had fixed 99% of calendar days for the quarter ending March 31, 2026 at >$58,000/day TCE, compared to $50,333/day achieved in the December 2025 quarter. The Baltic Exchange LPG Index averaged $67.767/mt in the December 2025 quarter versus $55.717/mt a year earlier. Industry sources suggest rates have continued strengthening into May 2026, though exact levels depend on route and index methodology.

**Growth from fleet expansion:** The Areion delivery adds ~4% capacity. The ammonia-capable specification provides optionality in the emerging ammonia-as-fuel market (65 ammonia carriers on order industry-wide through 2029).

**Estimate trajectory:** Consensus FY2027E EPS is estimated at approximately $4.33, FY2028E approximately $3.82, though these figures could not be independently validated from primary sources and should be treated as approximate. The declining forward estimates reflect consensus expectation of rate normalization from current elevated levels. At sustained current rates, actual earnings could substantially exceed these estimates.

## Competitive Landscape

Dorian ranks among the top 5 independent VLGC operators globally, behind BW LPG (larger fleet, more diversified chartering). Key competitive attributes:

- **Fleet quality:** Average age ~10 years (vs. 11.4 industry), 16 vessels scrubber-equipped, first ammonia-capable vessel
- **Pool structure:** Helios Pool (50/50 with MOL Energia) provides commercial scale advantages in cargo matching and voyage optimization
- **Cost structure:** In-house technical/commercial management reduces agency costs vs. third-party management

**Competitive risks:** The 108-vessel orderbook (27% of existing fleet) through 2029 is the primary structural overhang. However, 65 of these are ammonia carriers, and historical precedent suggests actual deliveries typically lag orders by 6-18 months. Environmental regulations (EU ETS, CII) may accelerate scrapping of older non-ECO/non-scrubber VLGCs currently operating.

Dorian is a well-run fleet operator in a commodity shipping market where sustained competitive advantage is difficult to build. Position is stable but not structurally improving.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Rate Cyclicality (HIGH):** With 97% of revenue through the Helios Pool (primarily spot-exposed), a $10,000/day move in TCE rates translates to approximately $90M in annual revenue and $65-70M in EBITDA. Rates can move $20,000-30,000/day within a single quarter, making earnings inherently unpredictable.

**2. China Tariff Risk (HIGH):** U.S. propane exports to China fell 29% in 2025 due to reciprocal tariffs. Total U.S. propane exports still reached a record 1.8 million b/d, offset by increases to India, Vietnam, Singapore, Indonesia, Europe, Latin America, and Africa. While this rerouting may increase average voyage distances (a positive for ton-mile demand), the net effect on VLGC utilization remains uncertain. If tariffs escalate further or alternative buyers cannot absorb redirected volumes, VLGC rates would face significant downward pressure.

**3. Orderbook Overhang (MODERATE):** 108 vessels (27% of fleet) on order. Net fleet growth depends on scrapping, which environmental regulations should accelerate. The critical window is 2027-2028 when delivery volume peaks.

**4. Panama Canal (MODERATE):** Over 90% of U.S.-to-Asia LPG voyages transit Panama. Drought, toll increases, or transit restrictions create operational risk and cost inflation not fully reflected in forward rates.

**5. Dividend Sustainability:** Dividends are irregular and track earnings. The recent increase to $0.70/share from $0.65 reflects improving conditions, but further cuts remain possible if rates deteriorate.

## Conclusion

Dorian LPG presents a compelling cyclical entry at the intersection of a proven rate trough (FY2025), evidence of rate recovery (>$58,000/day TCE for the March 2026 quarter with industry reports of further strengthening), and structural Middle East supply disruption that supports elevated VLGC rates for at least the near term.

The previous validation question asked what prevents a BUY at this score level. The answer was rate cyclicality risk and the poor earnings surprise record (1 beat, 3 misses). However, re-examining the evidence: the misses occurred during FY2025's trough when analysts were slow to cut estimates — the misses reflected the speed of rate deterioration, not operational failure. With rates now recovering, the opposite dynamic (estimates lagging reality to the upside) is more likely, as suggested by recent estimate revisions. The Middle East infrastructure war creates structural support for VLGC rates that distinguishes this cycle from prior recoveries.

The stock is a BUY with a 6-9 month horizon, sized at 2-3% given the inherent rate volatility and binary geopolitical exposure. This is a high-volatility cyclical/geopolitical trade with specific thesis invalidation criteria, not a structural hold.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The sustained-rate forecast ($200–250/mt through FY2027) and specific May 2026 rate claims could not be validated from primary company sources on a route-consistent basis; the BUY thesis depends on near-term rate strength that is supported directionally but not quantified with certainty beyond the March 2026 quarter.
