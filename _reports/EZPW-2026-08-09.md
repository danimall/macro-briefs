## Executive Summary

EZCORP is delivering the best operating results in its modern history. Nine-month FY2026 revenue rose 33.1% to $1,247.6M, operating income 63% to $183.3M, and diluted EPS 53% to $1.65. The company has beaten consensus in each of the last four quarters (by 13.3%, 31.0%, 38.7% and 15.8%), consolidated Simple Management Group (SMG) from January 2026, and grown its store base from 1,360 at fiscal year-end 2025 to 1,549 at June 30, 2026. Same-store pawn loans outstanding rose 13% in the U.S. and 35% in Latin America (28% constant currency) in the June quarter. Net debt of roughly $219M against LTM EBITDA of ~$256M is 0.85x, and EBIT covers interest 6.7x.

The analytical problem is composition. Of the $181.7M nine-month gross-profit increase, $48.6M came from newly consolidated SMG. Of the remaining $133.1M, jewelry scrap gross profit contributed $34.7M — 26% — driven almost entirely by margin rather than volume: U.S. scrap gross margin went from 15.0% in FY2024 to 26.7% in FY2025 to 35.1% for the nine months. Adding an estimated ~$15.6M from merchandise gross-margin expansion, much of which is jewelry-inventory-linked, puts the plausibly gold-driven share of the ex-SMG increase near 38%. This margin does not require gold to fall in order to compress: the spread exists because inventory acquired at lower gold prices is being scrapped at higher prices. A gold *plateau* closes that spread mechanically over two to three quarters. Reported (but unverified in any filing) management commentary from the August 5 call points to a normalized scrap margin of 15%-20%, exactly where the line item sat in FY2024.

At $27.90 the shares are 24.8% below the May 7 high of $37.13 and roughly 21% below early-July levels, yet still up 82.6% over twelve months. Estimates have moved *up* modestly (FY2026 +2.5%, FY2027 +2.0% over 90 days), so the drawdown is not estimate-driven. On the numbers that matter — 14.0x LTM if-converted EPS of ~$1.99, 12.8x FY2027 consensus of $2.19, ~8.9x LTM EV/EBITDA — the stock is priced roughly in line with a 12.0x consumer-finance peer median on earnings that include a commodity premium. Strip the scrap excess back toward mid-teens margins and normalized EPS is closer to $1.50-1.65, which puts the same price at 17-18x. Probability-weighting a $19 bear, $29.50 base and $39.50 bull gives an expected value of roughly $28.85, about 3% above spot with no dividend. That is a HOLD, and the classification is genuinely uncertain rather than confidently constructive: the earnings-quality split cannot be resolved from the filings, the convertible settlement election is undisclosed, and SMG purchase accounting is preliminary.

## Company Overview

EZCORP is the second-largest pawn operator in the United States by store count, second-largest for-profit operator in Mexico, and largest in Guatemala. It advances non-recourse cash against tangible personal property, earns pawn service charges (PSC) at 12%-25% monthly in the U.S. and 12%-21% in Latin America, and monetizes forfeited collateral through retail resale or gold/stone scrapping. Roughly 8,500 employees; headquartered in Rollingwood/Austin, Texas.

Three reported segments since Q2 FY2026: U.S. Pawn (560 stores), Latin America Pawn (881 stores across Mexico, Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras), and SMG (108 stores, principally "La Familia Pawn and Jewelry" and "CashWiz" in Florida, Puerto Rico and 11 other countries), consolidated from January 2, 2026. Latin America is 57% of stores but roughly 26% of gross profit, because average monthly pawn loans per store are $111 versus $439 in the U.S. The company also holds 43.5% of ASX-listed Cash Converters International, carried at $26.5M against a $64.1M quoted value.

The industry is large, mature, highly fragmented, with a long tail of one-to-three-store independents, which underpins the acquisition thesis. EZCORP bought 52 stores in FY2025 and 64 in the first nine months of FY2026, plus SMG's 105.

## Financial Analysis

**Income statement.** LTM revenue is $1,584.4M (FY2025 $1,274.3M plus the nine-month delta of $310.1M), LTM operating income $220.3M, LTM net income attributable to EZCORP $158.3M. LTM EBITDA is approximately $256.4M using ~$36.2M of D&A. Q3 FY2026 alone: revenue $418.7M (+34.7%), gross profit $246.2M (+34%), operating income $54.8M (+52%), net income $38.2M, diluted EPS $0.48.

**The basic-to-diluted gap is the central capital-structure fact.** Q3 basic EPS was $0.62 against diluted $0.48; nine-month basic $2.14 against diluted $1.65. The gap is the $230M of 3.750% convertible notes due December 2029, which convert at approximately $11.23 per share into 20.477M shares. Those shares are already in reported diluted EPS on an if-converted basis, with $5.609M of nine-month after-tax interest added back to the numerator. Any peer comparison on basic EPS (~$2.60 LTM, implying a 10.8x multiple) credits the earnings while ignoring the shares. The correct trailing multiple is 14.0x on ~$1.99 of if-converted diluted EPS, which is exactly what the market screen reports, confirming the basis.

**Balance sheet.** At June 30, 2026: total assets $2,166.2M, total liabilities $998.5M, EZCORP equity $1,164.6M. Cash $311.0M (down from $469.5M at fiscal year-end, largely funding SMG and El Bufalo) plus $15.7M restricted. Gross debt $530.0M: $300M 7.375% senior notes due 2032 and the $230M 2029 converts. Net debt $219.0M — 0.85x LTM EBITDA, or roughly 1.5x lease-adjusted including $291.3M of operating lease liabilities at an 8.23% discount rate. In compliance with all 2032 note covenants. No revolver disclosed as drawn.

**Cash generation, honestly measured.** Nine-month operating cash flow was $130.4M (+33%) against $27.8M of capex, producing conventional FCF of $102.6M. For a pawnbroker that overstates owner's cash, because loan-book growth sits in investing: loans made $(948.3)M, repaid $535.6M, principal recovered through collateral sales $358.7M, a net lending outflow of $(54.0)M. Deducting that leaves roughly $48.6M of nine-month owner's cash. The loan book grew $79.7M (PLO $307.5M to $387.2M) and inventory ~$68M. The business self-funds its growth but does not throw off surplus cash at anything like the headline FCF rate. Nine-month figures should not be annualized: fiscal Q1 (Oct-Dec) is the seasonally strongest pretax quarter and fiscal Q3 (Apr-Jun) the weakest, and the period contains $(70.8)M of payables/accrued outflows reflecting payment timing.

**Balance-sheet quality caveats.** Goodwill of $475.4M plus intangibles of $124.7M equals $600.1M, or 27.7% of assets, against tangible equity of roughly $564.5M. A recomputed public-company Altman Z on the June balance sheet with LTM flows lands near 3.07 (conventional "safe" zone), materially above screen-published grey-zone readings that mis-scale EBIT and use the older September balance sheet. Altman Z is a weak instrument for a lender whose current assets are a loan book and whose liabilities include large capitalized store leases; the 5.6x current ratio is pawn accounting, not exceptional liquidity.

Financial health scores 6.5 — modest leverage, adequate coverage, real cash conversion, offset by falling cash, intangibles above half of tangible book, and growth that consumes capital.

## Growth Analysis

Three engines are running simultaneously, and they are not equally durable.

**Store growth (durable).** 1,360 to 1,549 stores in nine months, split between acquisition (64 legacy plus SMG's 105 plus El Bufalo's 12) and de novo (27). This is straightforward roll-up arithmetic in a fragmented industry and should continue, funded by cash and the 2032 notes.

**Core pawn demand (durable, macro-supported).** U.S. same-store PLO +13% and PSC +13%; Latin America same-store PLO +35% and PSC +26% constant currency. Demand for small-ticket collateralized credit — the countercyclical part of the story — is currently working, and the operating data corroborate it, though the filings do not let this be cleanly separated from the gold-appraisal effect that inflates the same PLO metric.

**Gold-linked margin (not durable).** The scrap margin sequence — 15.0%, 26.7%, 35.1% — is a commodity-cycle signature. A sensitivity: nine-month consolidated scrap sales of $177.7M at a 33.5% blended margin produced $59.6M of gross profit; at 17.5% the same volume produces $31.1M, a $28.5M reduction equal to roughly 16% of nine-month pretax income of $176.7M, before any PLO or volume effect. Annualized and after tax at ~26%, that is roughly $0.34 per if-converted share, about 17% of LTM diluted EPS, and merchandise-margin normalization could add another $0.10-0.15.

There is a second, less-noticed drag. Collateral mix has shifted toward jewelry (U.S. 66% to 69%, Latin America 36% to 49%) and monthly yield on PLO has fallen (U.S. 14% to 13%, Latin America 16% to 15%), because U.S. statutory PSC rates decline as transaction size rises. Higher gold prices raise average loan size on jewelry collateral, so PLO grows faster than PSC — Latin America nine-month same-store PLO +34% against PSC +22%. Part of the gap is a timing artifact (period-end balance versus period flow, plus 67 unseasoned new stores), but part is structural yield dilution. Extrapolating PSC from "record PLO" overshoots.

Consensus reflects some of this: FY2026 $2.04 and FY2027 $2.19 imply only 7.4% growth next year despite full-year SMG consolidation and continued store additions. Revisions are positive but modest (+2.5% and +2.0% over 90 days), nothing close to the estimate-velocity threshold that signals a mispriced acceleration. Growth prospects score 6.0.

## Competitive Landscape

Scale in a fragmented market is EZCORP's principal advantage, and it is being pressed: 14% store growth year over year, aged general merchandise down 90bps to 1.9% in the U.S. and below 1% in Latin America, with inventory turnover steady at 2.0x and 3.1x. Stable turns alongside expanding merchandise margins is evidence of real merchandising improvement rather than pure commodity windfall. The EZ+ Rewards program (6.9 million members) is management's stated differentiator, but the filings disclose no retention or basket-size uplift, so it remains an assertion.

Trajectory is mixed at the unit level. Yields are compressing on jewelry mix. Latin America same-store store expenses rose 28% (17% constant currency) in Q3 against same-store PSC of +26% (+16% cc), so that segment's margin expansion came from merchandise and scrap rather than operating leverage; if gold normalizes, minimum-wage-driven labor inflation continues without the cushion. SMG carries lower margins than legacy U.S. Pawn (32.3% merchandise, 27.9% scrap versus 38.6% and 35.1%) and is far more gold-dependent (scrap is 32.6% of SMG revenue versus 16.0% for U.S. Pawn), so the acquisition dilutes both margin and earnings quality. In Mexico, competitors include government-affiliated non-profit foundations, an unusual structural feature that caps pricing.

Competitive position scores 6.0: improving on scale and inventory discipline, deteriorating modestly on yield and mix.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Convertible settlement, live this quarter.** The 10-Q states the 130%-of-conversion-price condition was met for at least 20 of the 30 consecutive trading days ended June 30, 2026, making the 2029 notes convertible at holders' option during the quarter ending September 30, 2026. No settlement election, redemption notice, or hedge/capped-call arrangement was found in the documents reviewed. Mechanics: share settlement issues 20.477M shares (33.3% of existing shares; existing holders diluted to 75.0% ownership) but is **neutral to diluted EPS**, because those shares and the interest add-back are already in the reported diluted figure. Cash settlement would require roughly $572M at $27.97 conversion value against $311M of cash — feasible only with ~$260M of new borrowing, which would be modestly EPS-accretive (replacing 14x equity with ~6% after-tax debt) but would take net leverage higher and end the acquisition program. The 2025 notes settled predominantly in stock, which is the base case. The real exposure is to M&A capacity and the balance sheet, not to reported EPS; headline "25% dilution" framing obscures that distinction. Constraining that further: only ~9.1M of 100M authorized Class A shares were unreserved at fiscal year-end 2025, and raising the authorization requires a vote held entirely by one person.

**2. Gold reversal or plateau.** Four channels operate at once per the 10-K: sales, sales margin, PLO and PSC. Quantified above. The unverified 15%-20% normalized scrap-margin commentary should not be treated as company guidance, but FY2024's actual 15.0% establishes that mid-teens is the historical norm.

**3. SMG.** Purchase price allocation is preliminary (tradenames, payroll liabilities, deferred taxes). Goodwill of $123.4M plus $60.9M of indefinite-lived tradenames were recognized against **negative** net identifiable assets of $(36.1)M. The acquired CEO departed in April 2026, four months after closing, and EZCORP assumed direct operational management. Founders/SMG and El Bufalo were excluded from the June 30 disclosure-controls assessment under first-year guidance, representing 15% of consolidated assets and 11% of quarterly revenue. Approximately $206M of total EZCORP capital is committed against six months of pretax income of $14.7M. This is the clearest impairment-risk cluster on the balance sheet.

**4. Geographic concentration into hurricane season.** Texas (45%) and Florida (17%) are more than 62% of U.S. stores, with SMG adding Puerto Rico. The 10-K notes business-interruption losses would be only partially insured. August through October is the exposure window.

**5. Unaccrued contingencies.** A Latin American subsidiary faces a withholding-tax assertion with reasonably possible loss of zero to ~$10M and no accrual. The AlphaCredit/Grupo Finmart pre-closing tax indemnity remains live with $8.0M in escrow since 2019 and a statute of limitations purportedly extended to February 2027.

**6. Governance.** Phillip E. Cohen beneficially owns all Class B voting stock; every publicly traded share is non-voting. Bylaws permit the voting stockholder to appoint or remove officers and require unanimous director approval for board action — a veto that runs both ways but leaves minority holders with no mechanism to influence capital allocation. Five of seven directors are independent and all committees are fully independent, which is a real mitigant. Current proxy compensation data was not retrievable, leaving pay-for-performance alignment unverified.

Risk factors score 4.5: multiple identifiable, dated, unpriced-or-partially-priced binaries.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case ($39.50, +42%).** Gold holds or extends its structural bid, so scrap margins stay near 30% through FY2027 while the acquired-inventory spread persists. Consumer stress deepens, driving PLO and PSC higher on the countercyclical channel. SMG's 108 stores are re-margined toward legacy U.S. levels under direct management, and full-year consolidation plus continued 60-100 store annual additions carry FY2027 diluted EPS to roughly $2.55. Four consecutive beats averaging ~25% suggest consensus of $2.19 is again too low. At 15.5x that is $39.50, near the low end of the published analyst range. The converts settle in stock, removing the overhang without cash cost.

**Bear case ($19.00, -32%).** Gold plateaus. The acquisition-cost spread closes and scrap margin reverts to 17.5%, removing roughly $38M of annualized pretax profit; merchandise margins give back part of their 160-440bp gains; jewelry appraisal values compress, so PLO falls even as loan demand holds. Latin America loses its margin cushion while same-store expenses keep growing 17% constant currency. SMG's preliminary allocation is finalized with a write-down against $184M of non-amortizing intangibles. FY2027 diluted EPS lands at $1.65, and the multiple compresses to 11.5x on lower earnings quality — $19.00, still well above the $15.12 fifty-two-week low.

**Base case ($29.50, +5.7%).** Gold holds near current levels but the inventory spread narrows, taking scrap margin to the mid-20s. Store growth and full-year SMG offset most of the margin give-back, producing FY2027 diluted EPS of roughly $2.10, slightly below consensus. At 14.0x that is $29.40; adding the ~$0.46 per share of unrecognized Cash Converters value gives $29.50. An EV/EBITDA cross-check at 9.5x on ~$240M of normalized EBITDA supports $27-29, so the base case sits at the upper end of two independent methods rather than in the middle.

Probability-weighting 30% bear / 45% base / 25% bull gives $28.85, or roughly 3% above spot with no dividend.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Rating: HOLD (uncertain).** Existing holders have a self-funding business at 0.85x net leverage with genuine same-store momentum; there is no reason to force an exit. New capital has no entry edge at $27.90 against a $29.50 base case and a $19.00 bear.

**Upgrade triggers (to ACCUMULATE or BUY):**
1. **Price at $22-23 or below with core metrics intact** — specifically same-store PLO growth still positive and PSC growth within 500bps of PLO growth. At $22.50 the base case offers ~31% upside and the stock trades near 11x normalized EPS, which would clear the bar.
2. **Disclosed scrap-margin floor.** A filing or company release quantifying a normalized scrap-margin range at or above 25%, or two consecutive quarters of scrap margin above 28% with gold flat, would remove the largest earnings-quality discount.
3. **Convertible resolution in stock plus a materially larger buyback.** Share settlement disclosed via 8-K, followed by an authorization increase above the current $42M remaining (2.4% of market cap), would convert the overhang into a capital-return catalyst.

**Downgrade triggers (to REDUCE or AVOID):**
1. **Scrap margin below 20% in any single quarter combined with sequential PLO decline** — that combination confirms the multi-channel gold reversal and would put FY2027 EPS on the bear path.
2. **SMG goodwill or tradename impairment**, or finalization of the purchase price allocation materially adverse to the $123.4M/$60.9M carrying values.
3. **Cash settlement of the converts requiring more than ~$200M of new borrowing**, taking net leverage above 2.0x and ending the acquisition program.
4. **Latin America same-store expense growth exceeding same-store PSC growth for two consecutive quarters on a constant-currency basis** (Q3 was 17% versus 16%, already marginal).

**Reassessment timeline.** Two dated checkpoints. First, disclosure of the convertible settlement outcome for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, via 8-K or in the FY2026 10-K. Second, FY2026 fourth-quarter and full-year results, expected mid-November 2026, which will provide the first clean read on scrap margin post-commentary, the finalized SMG allocation, and the seasonally weakest quarter's expense trajectory. Consensus for the current quarter is $0.44 and for the next quarter $0.60; both have drifted down ~0.4-2.6% over 90 days even as full-year estimates rose, which is itself a signal worth watching.

## Conclusion

EZCORP has executed well. Store count is up 14% year over year, same-store loans are growing double digits on both sides of the border, aged inventory is at multi-year lows with stable turns, leverage is under 1x, and the company has beaten consensus four quarters running. The countercyclical demand thesis is being supported by the operating data, though the filings do not let it be cleanly separated from the gold-appraisal effect.

The issue is what the market is paying for. At 14.0x LTM if-converted EPS of $1.99 the stock looks reasonable against a 12.0x peer median, but roughly $0.34-0.49 of that EPS sits in scrap and merchandise margins that were half as wide in FY2024 and that compress mechanically if gold simply stops rising. Normalize, and the same price is 17-18x. Layer on a convertible that became holder-convertible this quarter with no disclosed settlement election, $184M of non-amortizing intangibles on a unit whose CEO left four months after closing and whose purchase accounting remains preliminary, more than 62% of U.S. stores in two hurricane-exposed states entering peak season, and a public float with no votes, and the case for paying up is thin.

The 24.8% drawdown from the May high is not a bargain created by an irrational market; estimates rose over the same window, and the specific new information disclosed on August 5 (convert convertibility, SMG management change, scrap-margin commentary) explains a meaningful part of the move. Overall score 5.6, down marginally from 5.7 in June and continuing the deceleration from 6.9 in May, driven not by the lower price but by the accumulating evidence that the earnings base is more commodity-dependent and the capital structure more contingent than the headline growth rate suggests. HOLD, with a genuine willingness to upgrade at $22-23 or on a disclosed scrap-margin floor.
