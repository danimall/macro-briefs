## Executive Summary

Apellis Pharmaceuticals ceased to exist as an independent public company on May 14, 2026, when Biogen completed its acquisition at $41.00 per share in cash plus one contingent value right (CVR) per share representing up to $4 in additional payments tied to SYFOVRE global net sales milestones. Approximately 82.4% of shares were tendered, and the remaining shares were acquired via short-form merger under Section 251(h) of the DGCL. The stock was delisted from Nasdaq upon deal closure.

The current quoted price of ~$41.03-$41.06 reflects post-closure settlement mechanics and residual market microstructure effects, not a live tradeable security. Volumes have collapsed from 90 million shares on the deal announcement day (March 31, 2026) to minimal levels. There is no equity investment thesis for APLS. The only remaining economic interest for former shareholders is the CVR, which is a contractual right administered outside of public equity markets.

## Company Overview

Apellis was a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on complement-driven diseases, targeting C3, the central protein of the complement cascade. Its two marketed products were SYFOVRE (pegcetacoplan injection for geographic atrophy secondary to AMD) and EMPAVELI (systemic pegcetacoplan for PNH and C3 glomerulopathy). The company grew revenue from $75M in 2022 to over $1B in 2025, achieved its first profitable year in 2025, and posted a $0.15 EPS beat in Q1 2026 (vs. -$0.38 consensus) just days before the acquisition closed.

## Financial Analysis

The financial history is now purely retrospective:

- **Revenue growth:** $75M (2022) → $397M (2023) → $781M (2024) → $1.004B (2025). 28% YoY growth in 2025.
- **Profitability inflection:** Operating income turned positive in 2025 at $55M after losses of -$595M (2022), -$517M (2023), and -$165M (2024).
- **Balance sheet at closure:** $466M cash, $455M total debt (primarily convertible notes), current ratio of 3.1x.
- **Free cash flow:** First positive FCF of $45M in 2025 on minimal capex.

Biogen effectively acquired the company at the profitability crossover point, capturing the value of the revenue ramp while the stock remained depressed from prior commercial execution concerns and safety signal headwinds for SYFOVRE.
