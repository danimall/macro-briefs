## Executive Summary

Intercontinental Exchange operates the world's preeminent energy futures franchise (Brent crude), the New York Stock Exchange, six clearing houses holding $81.2B in margin deposits, a $2B fixed income data business, and the largest integrated mortgage technology platform in the U.S. The company delivered record adjusted EPS of $6.95 in 2025 and beat Q1 2026 estimates by approximately 3.1%, with Q1 2026 adjusted EPS of $2.35 versus consensus of $2.28.

The stock trades at $154.75. Based on consensus FY2026 EPS estimates and available forward P/E data, ICE trades at approximately 23x forward earnings — a discount to peers that typically range from 21x to 29x forward earnings. This discount has persisted as the stock sold off alongside broader risk assets despite the company's fundamental positioning improving: elevated volatility drives exchange volumes, the SEC Treasury clearing mandate creates a potential structural volume growth opportunity, and the Black Knight integration showed its first full-year GAAP profitability in FY2025. The primary risks — approximately $20.4B in debt (as of Q1 2026), the $1.6B Polymarket investment, and mortgage rate sensitivity — are real but quantifiable and partially offset by $4.2B in annual adjusted free cash flow.

At current levels, ICE offers attractive upside: base case fair value of $197 (27% above current) on moderate re-rating, supported by strong operational execution, accelerating revenue growth, and a business that mechanically benefits from the volatility and clearing mandates that define the current macro environment.

## Company Overview

ICE is a diversified financial infrastructure company operating through three segments:

**Exchanges (55% of net revenue, 74% operating margin):** 13 regulated exchanges and 6 clearing houses trading energy, agricultural, financial, and equity derivatives plus cash equities on NYSE. Brent crude futures remain the global benchmark, and open interest grew 17% YoY in 2025, suggesting growing structural engagement across ICE's markets.

**Fixed Income & Data Services (24% of net revenue, 39% GAAP margin):** Evaluated pricing on 3+ million securities across 150 countries, CDS clearing, connectivity (ICE Global Network), and analytics. Annual Subscription Value reached $1.99B, growing 8.3% YoY — a high-visibility recurring revenue stream.

**Mortgage Technology (21% of net revenue, FY2025 turned GAAP-profitable):** End-to-end residential mortgage platform (origination via Encompass, closing via MERS/Simplifile, servicing via MSP). Acquired through the $11.8B Black Knight deal in September 2023. The segment posted its first full-year GAAP operating profit ($14M, 1% margin) in FY2025 after absorbing $269M→$104M→$66M in declining integration costs, though quarterly profitability has not yet been proven durable — Q1 2026 showed a GAAP operating loss of $(13)M, an improvement from $(27)M in Q1 2025.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue and Earnings Trajectory:**
- FY2025 net revenue: $9.931B (+7% YoY)
- FY2025 GAAP operating income: $4.9B (+14% YoY)
- FY2025 GAAP diluted EPS: $5.77 (+21% YoY)
- FY2025 adjusted diluted EPS: $6.95 (+14% YoY)
- Q1 2026 adjusted EPS: $2.35 (beat consensus $2.28 by approximately 3.1%)

**Margins:** Adjusted operating margin expanded to 60% in 2025 from 59% in 2024 and 2023. The Exchange segment runs at 75% adjusted margin — among the highest in financial services. Operating leverage is visible: 7% revenue growth produced only 1% opex growth.

**Free Cash Flow:** $4.187B adjusted FCF in 2025 (+16% YoY). On an $89.5B market cap, this implies a 4.7% adjusted FCF yield. FCF/Net Income is well above 100%, indicating high-quality earnings conversion.

**Debt:** As of December 31, 2025, total debt stood at $19.6B, down from $20.4B at year-end 2024. However, by March 31, 2026, total debt had risen back to approximately $20.4B, including $1.75B in commercial paper borrowings during Q1 — driven in part by funding the $600M Polymarket Series E investment. The senior notes carry a weighted average cost of 3.7% with approximately 13-year average maturity, providing long-duration stability. Commercial paper ($1.8B outstanding at quarter-end) carries shorter maturities (weighted average 24 days) at 4.1%, backed by revolver capacity. Interest expense fell from $910M to $803M in FY2025 as debt was reduced during that year. At current EBITDA (~$6.6B adjusted), net debt/EBITDA is approximately 3.0x — elevated for an infrastructure company but manageable given the cash flow profile.

**Capital Returns Resumed:** $1.3B in buybacks in 2025 after a two-year pause for deleveraging. New $3.0B authorization for 2026 ($2.5B remaining as of March 31, 2026). Dividend raised 8% to $2.08/year (~1.3% yield). ICE returned $2.4B to shareholders in FY2025; theoretical annual return capacity based on adjusted FCF is approximately $4-5B, though actual returns will depend on competing uses including debt reduction and investments.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term (FY2026-2027):** The company has guided for continued revenue and earnings growth with expense discipline. Consensus estimates suggest meaningful EPS growth in FY2026, with upward revisions following strong Q1 results.

**Structural growth drivers:**

1. **Volatility regime:** The current geopolitical and macro environment produces elevated exchange trading volumes. Q1 2026 revenue grew ~20% YoY, driven by heightened volatility. Structural increases in rate and equity volatility benefit ICE's derivative volumes on a sustained basis.

2. **Treasury clearing mandate:** SEC approved ICE Clear Credit's expansion to clear U.S. Treasury securities in February 2026. Cash clearing is operationally live; repo clearing expected Q4 2026. The SEC mandate requires central clearing of eligible cash-market Treasury transactions by December 31, 2026, and eligible repo transactions by June 30, 2027. This represents a potential structural volume growth opportunity, though ICE has cautioned there is "no assurance" the service will achieve meaningful market adoption given competitive dynamics (DTCC's FICC is the incumbent).

3. **Mortgage Technology recovery potential:** Current mortgage origination volumes are at multi-decade lows due to 7%+ rates. ICE's Mortgage Technology segment generates $871M in recurring servicing revenue regardless of origination cycles. When rates eventually decline, origination volumes represent upside with minimal incremental cost. Management has indicated that mortgage revenues are directly tied to origination volumes and that lower rates would benefit transaction-based revenues.

4. **Data ASV compounding:** $1.99B growing 8.3% implies $2.15B+ in 2026. Fixed income data demand is structural — driven by passive investing growth, regulatory compliance requirements, and AI applications requiring clean reference data.

5. **Polymarket optionality:** The $1.6B investment positions ICE in prediction markets — a nascent asset class. If prediction markets achieve regulatory acceptance and scale, ICE's exchange/clearing infrastructure creates a natural path to monetization. Assign near-zero probability-weighted value today but acknowledge the optionality.

## Competitive Landscape

**Exchanges:** Brent crude remains the unchallenged global benchmark. CME competes in WTI, but the current environment has elevated Brent's pricing relevance. Open interest growth of 17% YoY indicates structural position-building. NYSE's listing franchise (~70% of S&P 500 companies, $10.1T in listed ETF AUM) faces modest competition from Nasdaq but retains pricing power.

**Fixed Income & Data:** ASV growth of 8.3% indicates market share gains in a structural growth market. Competitors (Bloomberg, Refinitiv/LSEG) operate at similar scale but ICE Clear Credit's leading position in CDS clearing (supporting 700+ single-name CDS reference entities and 180+ index CDS instruments) creates data advantages that feed back into pricing services.

**Mortgage Technology:** ICE Encompass has approximately 50%+ market share in mortgage origination. The network effect — lenders, servicers, investors, and title companies all connected through ICE platforms — creates enormous switching costs. No competitor offers a comparable end-to-end solution.

**Trajectory:** Competitive position is improving. The Treasury clearing expansion adds a new competitive surface. The data business is gaining share. The Mortgage Technology segment's FY2025 GAAP inflection — while still requiring quarterly confirmation — demonstrates that Black Knight integration has preserved the competitive franchise.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Polymarket Investment ($1.6B deployed, up to $2B commitment) — MEDIUM-HIGH**
As of March 31, 2026, the carrying value of ICE's Series D and E preferred investments in Polymarket was approximately $2.0B (after $389M in upward fair-value adjustments recorded in Q1 2026), representing approximately 14% fully diluted ownership. Prediction markets face unresolved regulatory questions. If CFTC/SEC classification becomes adversarial, or if Polymarket faces a reputational scandal, ICE could face write-downs and franchise damage. ICE's 10-K explicitly warns of "increased reputational risks from investments in companies like Polymarket." The investment carries impairment risk, regulatory uncertainty, and reputational risk.

**2. Elevated Leverage — MEDIUM-HIGH**
While ICE reduced debt by $0.8B during FY2025, Q1 2026 saw $716M in net commercial paper borrowings, partially to fund the Polymarket investment. The company has guided toward continued debt reduction, but the path is not as linear as FY2025 alone would suggest.

**3. Mortgage Technology Rate Sensitivity — MEDIUM**
Origination volumes remain suppressed at 7%+ mortgage rates. If rates rise further (plausible in current inflation environment), transaction-based mortgage revenues face continued headwinds. Q1 2026 reverted to a $(13)M GAAP operating loss — better than $(27)M in Q1 2025, but quarterly profitability has not yet been sustained. Mitigant: $871M in recurring servicing revenue provides a floor.

**4. EMIR 3.0 Active Account Requirement — MEDIUM**
EU regulation could force some clearing activity from ICE Clear Europe to EU-domiciled CCPs. Impact potentially $50-100M in revenue at risk. ICE operates clearing houses in both jurisdictions, partially mitigating.

**5. AI Commoditization of Data Products — LOW-MEDIUM**
Clients could use AI to develop in-house pricing capabilities, reducing demand for ICE's evaluated pricing services. Timeline is long (years, not quarters), and ICE's data moat deepens with network effects and proprietary transaction data.

- FY2026 consensus EPS achieved or modestly beaten

## Conclusion

ICE represents one of the highest-quality financial infrastructure franchises available at a discount to peers. The valuation discount (~23x vs. ~26x median) persists despite ICE growing faster than most peers and directly benefiting from the elevated volatility regime.

The primary concerns — leverage that rose in Q1 2026 rather than declining, Mortgage Technology that has not yet proven quarterly GAAP profitability, and the Polymarket investment — are legitimate and warrant a more modest confidence level than six months ago. However, these risks are being penalized in the stock price disproportionately to their probable impact on intrinsic value. Structural vol tailwinds, Treasury clearing optionality, and strong underlying cash generation support maintaining the BUY recommendation with the acknowledgment that conviction is lower than six months ago.

At approximately 23x forward earnings with a 4.7% adjusted FCF yield and direct mechanical benefit from elevated volatility and clearing mandates, ICE offers attractive risk/reward. The stock should re-rate toward $195-200 over 12 months as the market prices in sustained vol benefits and continued integration execution.

> **[POST-REVIEW FLAG]** The peer valuation discount (previously stated as 29%) is smaller than originally framed based on available forward P/E data (~12-13% discount), and Q1 2026 leverage increased rather than decreased, which collectively reduce confidence in the $197-$223 target range without materially invalidating the directional thesis.
