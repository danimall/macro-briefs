**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Everpure (formerly Pure Storage) is a differentiated enterprise storage platform company benefiting from AI-driven data demand, a validated hyperscaler design win, and an accelerating subscription transition. FY2026 revenue grew 15.6% to $3.66B with $616M in free cash flow (16.8% FCF margin), and remaining performance obligations surged 40% YoY to $3.7B — the strongest leading indicator in the company's history. The recent 1touch acquisition and rebrand signal strategic ambition beyond hardware toward a data management platform.

The critical near-term overhang is a 300-900% surge in semiconductor component costs that prompted 70% average price increases in Q1 FY2027. While industry-wide (Dell, HPE, NetApp all raising prices), this creates margin compression risk and potential demand destruction that the market has not fully resolved. At $88.35 with a forward P/E of ~24x on $2.31 current-year EPS (or ~123x on trailing), the stock has recovered significantly from its early-2026 trough of ~$57, pricing in much of the optimistic scenario. The risk/reward at this level is approximately symmetric — substantial upside exists if margins hold and the hyperscaler ramps further, but material downside if component costs erode profitability or the macro environment slows enterprise IT spending.

The stock merits holding for its 12-month growth trajectory, but the specific risk preventing a BUY is margin compression certainty in Q1-Q2 FY2027 combined with hyperscaler customer concentration — if the unnamed hyperscaler (likely Meta) pulls back orders while Everpure holds committed NAND purchase obligations, the downside is significant and concentrated.

## Company Overview

Everpure provides an integrated storage and data management platform spanning on-premises, hybrid cloud, and public cloud environments. The company differentiates through proprietary DirectFlash Modules that bypass SSD firmware, delivering 5-10x better power/space efficiency. Its Evergreen Architecture eliminates forklift hardware upgrades, enabling a subscription-based consumption model that generates recurring revenue.

**Revenue composition (FY2026):** Product revenue $1.97B (54%) / Subscription services $1.69B (46%)
**Customer base:** 14,500+ customers including 64% of Fortune 500
**Employees:** 6,400
**Key catalysts:** Hyperscaler design win (unnamed, likely Meta), AI workload demand (FlashBlade//EXA), 1touch acquisition (data intelligence)

- Net income: $188M (+76% YoY)

**Balance Sheet:**

The balance sheet is a clear strength. Zero net debt, $1.5B cash, and $616M annual FCF generation provides substantial buffer against the component cost headwind. The Altman Z-Score of 1.86 (grey zone) is misleading — the "leverage" is primarily deferred revenue from subscription contracts, not financial debt.

**Forward Estimates:**
- FY2027E EPS: $2.31 (current year), implying forward P/E of ~24x
- FY2028E EPS: $2.84, implying ~31x forward on out-year estimates
- Estimate revisions: +1.4% for current year and +2.9% for next year over 90 days — modestly positive but not the aggressive revision pattern that typically precedes sustained re-rating

## Growth Analysis

Three growth vectors converge:

1. **Hyperscaler Ramp:** The unnamed hyperscaler design win exceeded shipment forecasts in FY2026 with "more shipments anticipated in FY2027." This single customer likely drove a meaningful portion of the revenue acceleration. The 40% RPO surge suggests additional large-deal commitments.

2. **AI/Data Infrastructure Demand:** FlashBlade//EXA targets GPU-intensive AI workloads. Enterprise AI adoption requires massive, fast storage for training data, checkpointing, and inference pipelines. The 1touch acquisition adds data governance capabilities that position Everpure's platform as the foundation for AI-ready data estates.

3. **Subscription Transition:** Evergreen//One and //Flex models convert hardware capex into recurring subscription revenue. While ARR growth decelerated from 21% to 16%, the 40% RPO growth indicates large multi-year deal signing that will convert to revenue over time.

**Counterpoint:** The component cost environment creates a revenue-margin paradox. Wedbush expects a revenue beat in Q1 FY2027 partly because 70% higher prices mechanically inflate top-line growth — but at lower margins. Investors must distinguish between organic demand-driven growth and cost pass-through-driven growth.

## Valuation Assessment

At $88.35, Everpure trades at:
- ~24x FY2027E EPS ($2.31)
- ~123x trailing P/E
- ~5.7x EV/Revenue (based on ~$22.4B market cap less ~$1.5B net cash = ~$20.9B EV / $3.66B revenue)
- ~2.7% FCF yield ($616M FCF / $22.4B market cap)

The forward P/E of 24x on current-year estimates for a company growing revenue 15-16% with 70% gross margins is reasonable for high-quality enterprise tech, but not cheap. The PEG ratio on current-year growth is approximately 1.5x (24x / 16% growth) — fair but not compelling.

The stock has recovered from $57 (March 2026 lows) to $88.35, a 55% recovery. This recovery prices in the 1touch acquisition (+11.6% day of announcement), stabilizing estimates, and the general AI infrastructure bid. The consensus median target of $90 is essentially where the stock trades today.

The bull case to higher prices requires margin stability through the component cycle and sustained hyperscaler demand. If both materialize, FY2028E EPS of $2.84 at 30-35x (growth premium maintained) yields $85-99. If the hyperscaler relationship expands and margins recover, upside to $105+ exists. But margin compression or hyperscaler pullback creates downside to $63-70.

## Competitive Landscape

Everpure competes against Dell EMC, NetApp, HPE, IBM, and cloud providers. Key differentiators:

- **DirectFlash Modules** use less raw NAND per TB than SSD-based competitors — a structural advantage when NAND is scarce and expensive
- **Evergreen Architecture** eliminates forklift upgrades, creating switching costs
- **Purity software** spans all hardware, enabling unified management
- **Industry-leading NPS of 84** reflects customer satisfaction and retention
- **Net Dollar Retention of 113%** (though declining from 117%)

The company states it is gaining market share, international growth at 25% outpaces the market, and the hyperscaler win validates technology at scale. However, competitors have longer-term supply contracts and may be better positioned during the component shortage. The 300TB DirectFlash Module (18x hard drive capacity) is a density advantage that is difficult to replicate.

## Risk Assessment

**1. Component Cost Inflation (HIGH — specific and quantified):** Semiconductor costs surged 300-900%. The 70% price increase may cause demand destruction, and margins will compress in H1 FY2027. CEO Giancarlo stated this "will last far longer than the COVID-era disruption." Lead times extend beyond 2028.

**2. Hyperscaler Customer Concentration (HIGH — binary):** A single unnamed customer drives significant revenue and capex decisions. If this customer reduces orders, Everpure carries committed NAND purchase obligations that become stranded. No second hyperscaler design win has been announced.

**3. Subscription Transition Deceleration:** ARR growth slowed from 21% to 16%. NDR declined from 117% to 113%. If the subscription model loses momentum, the platform valuation premium erodes.

**4. SBC Dilution:** $482M in SBC (13% of revenue) is high. Share buybacks ($343M) partially offset but don't fully neutralize dilution.

**5. Tariff Exposure:** International component sourcing creates tariff vulnerability. Price increases partly address this, but incremental tariffs could necessitate further pricing actions in a slowing demand environment.

**Specific risk preventing BUY recommendation:** The combination of (a) confirmed margin compression in Q1-Q2 FY2027, (b) concentrated hyperscaler dependency with committed purchase obligations, and (c) the stock trading at consensus analyst targets after a 55% recovery means the risk/reward is approximately symmetric at current levels. The margin compression is confirmed by management guidance. Whether demand holds at 70% higher prices won't be available until Q1 FY2027 results (expected late August/early September 2026). Buying today means accepting 3 months of uncertainty on the single most important variable.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($95-105, +8-19%):**
- 70% price increases hold without demand destruction; revenue beats estimates on pass-through
- Hyperscaler ramp continues/expands; second hyperscaler win announced
- RPO conversion accelerates revenue growth to 18-20% in FY2027
- Component costs eventually normalize (H2 FY2028), restoring margins
- Platform re-rating: market values Everpure as data management platform (30-35x earnings) vs. hardware vendor (20-22x)

**Bear Case ($63-70, -21-29%):**
- 70% price increases cause demand destruction; enterprise IT budgets contract in stagflationary environment
- Hyperscaler pulls back orders; Everpure stuck with committed NAND purchases
- Margins compress 300-500bps with no near-term recovery
- Subscription deceleration continues; NDR falls below 110%
- Multiple contracts to 18-20x forward on margin compression fears

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Q1 FY2027 results (expected August/September 2026) showing product gross margins declining <300bps from FY2026 levels — proving price increases are holding without margin destruction
2. Announcement of a second hyperscaler design win or expansion of existing relationship beyond current commitments
3. Stock pullback to $72-75 (forward P/E <21x on current estimates) creating asymmetric upside

**Downgrade triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. Q1 FY2027 product gross margins declining >500bps, indicating demand destruction from price increases
2. Hyperscaler shipment delay or contract renegotiation announcement
3. RPO growth decelerating to <20% (from current 40%), suggesting large deal pipeline exhaustion

**Reassessment timeline:** Q1 FY2027 earnings (August/September 2026) is the definitive data point. The margin impact from component costs will be quantified, demand elasticity at 70% higher prices will be visible, and hyperscaler shipment trajectory will update.

## Conclusion

Everpure is a high-quality enterprise storage platform company with differentiated technology, strong FCF generation, and powerful growth indicators. The HOLD recommendation reflects confirmed margin compression entering Q1-Q2 FY2027 combined with the stock having already recovered to consensus targets. The growth thesis is intact and the company deserves holding for its 12-month trajectory, but buying at $88 requires accepting 3 months of uncertainty on margin durability at 70% higher prices while the stock sits at analyst consensus. Q1 FY2027 earnings will either validate the bull case (margins hold → re-rate to $95-105) or the bear case (demand destruction → retrace to $63-70).
