**Research Date: May 27, 2026**


## Executive Summary

Cohu is a semiconductor test and inspection equipment company at an early cyclical recovery. After revenue fell 44% from 2022 peak ($813M) to 2025 trough ($453M), Q1 FY2026 posted $125.1M (+29% YoY) with gross margins expanding to 46.3%. The demand catalyst is AI infrastructure requiring more complex power semiconductor testing (GaN, SiC), higher pin-count contactors, and advanced packaging inspection — all areas where Cohu has positioned products.

The fundamental recovery is real, but the stock has moved aggressively to price it. At $55.40, COHU has appreciated approximately 212% from its May 2025 low of $17.74 and trades at a $2.6B market cap against trailing losses. Forward estimates imply $0.58 EPS for FY2026 (95x forward P/E) and $1.46 for FY2027 (38x). Normalized mid-cycle earnings power ($3-4/share at $650-700M revenue) supports a higher stock price eventually, but the current valuation embeds substantial execution on auto/industrial recovery, sustained AI spending, and no macro disruption — with limited margin of safety at present levels.

Compounding the valuation concern: $287.5M convertible notes are deeply in-the-money with unhedged dilution above $41.02, coordinated insider selling in April-May 2026 at $44-49, elevated short interest (16.2% of float), and IV in backwardation indicating the options market prices near-term risk. The setup favors waiting for a pullback rather than chasing the move.

## Company Overview

Cohu, Inc. (founded 1947) supplies semiconductor test automation, inspection/metrology equipment, automated test equipment (ATE), interface solutions, AI-driven software analytics, and related services. The company serves IDMs, OSATs, and fabless semiconductor companies across 31 countries with 25,000+ installed systems at 280+ manufacturing facilities.

Revenue mix has shifted toward recurring streams: 60% of FY2025 revenue came from interface solutions (consumable contactors), spares, services, and software, up from 49% in FY2023. This structural improvement reduces earnings volatility and supports higher-quality valuation multiples over time.

The serviceable addressable market is approximately $3 billion. Cohu competes primarily with Advantest and Teradyne (both significantly larger), along with Hon Precision and smaller regional competitors.

## Growth Analysis

The growth thesis rests on three pillars:

1. **AI/Power Semiconductor Test Demand:** GaN and SiC power devices for AI data centers require new test solutions with advanced thermal management. Cohu has booked multiple orders (~$5M) for DiamondX systems specifically for this application. AI-related test intensity (more complex devices requiring longer test times and higher pin counts) directly benefits Cohu's recurring interface business.

2. **Cyclical Recovery in Auto/Industrial:** These end markets remain depressed (mobile -54% from 2022-2024, auto/industrial weak on inventory digestion). Any normalization would add meaningful volume on the existing cost base. Management upgraded FY2026 outlook in May.

3. **Recurring Revenue Model Maturation:** At 60% recurring revenue, each percentage point of installed base growth creates durable, high-margin annuity income. The shift from 49% to 60% recurring over two years is structural.

Backlog grew 20% ($138M → $165M) from Dec 2024 to Dec 2025. Estimate revisions show FY2027 EPS rising 18.7% over 30 days and 14.3% over 90 days — positive momentum but below the +30% threshold that would constitute a strong BUY signal.

Annualized Q1 run-rate ($500M) with sequential growth implies FY2026 revenue of $520-550M. If auto/industrial recovers, revenue could approach $600-700M within 12-18 months, where the operating leverage model generates meaningful earnings ($3-4/share based on FY2022 margins).

## Valuation Assessment

At $55.40 per share and $2.6B market cap:

- **Trailing P/E:** Negative (losses)
- **Forward P/E (FY2026E $0.58):** 95.5x
- **Forward P/E (FY2027E $1.46):** 37.9x
- **Normalized mid-cycle P/E:** ~$3.50/share earnings at $650M revenue → 15.8x (attractive, but 12-18 months away)
- **EV/Revenue (LTM):** ~5.3x
- **P/B:** ~3.0x (adjusted for net cash)

The valuation is pricing a full recovery that hasn't been delivered yet. At 38x FY2027 estimates, the stock trades at a premium to peers (PLAB at 22x) and requires significant execution. The market is assigning ~$55 to a business earning $0.01/share last quarter.

Against peers: ICHR and UCTT are similarly negative-earnings semi-cap plays trading at comparable or cheaper valuations. PLAB (profitable, 22x P/E) offers a more favorable risk/reward profile within the subsector.

The 212% appreciation from the 52-week low is driven by genuine fundamental improvement (revenue +29% YoY, margin expansion, order wins). At 95x current-year earnings, the price reflects the recovery thesis without providing meaningful margin of safety if the timeline extends or if auto/industrial recovery disappoints.

**Convertible Note Dilution:** The $287.5M in notes convert at $27.18. Capped calls offset dilution only to $41.02. At $55.40, there is unhedged dilution exposure. On conversion, this adds approximately 2.5-3M equivalent dilutive shares above what the capped calls offset, representing ~5-6% incremental dilution to existing shareholders. This is a structural headwind to per-share value that worsens as the stock rises above $41.

## Competitive Landscape

Cohu occupies a mid-tier position in semiconductor test equipment — #3 globally in test handlers behind Advantest and Teradyne, with a meaningful position in test interfaces (contactors and probe cards). The company's competitive differentiation centers on:

- **Portfolio breadth:** Few competitors offer handlers, ATE, interfaces, inspection, and software analytics from a single supplier
- **Recurring revenue model:** 60% recurring provides earnings stability uncommon in capital equipment
- **Active thermal control:** Critical technology for testing high-power AI chips at temperature

Competitive pressures include: (1) Advantest/Teradyne have 5-10x Cohu's R&D budgets; (2) Chinese competitors are developing lower-cost alternatives, encouraged by export controls to reduce US equipment dependency; (3) market share in ATE is limited (niche RF/mixed-signal focus).

The competitive position is stable to improving — AI/power testing creates a greenfield opportunity where Cohu's thermal technology is advantaged, and the recurring interface business has natural switching costs once qualified into a customer's test cell.

## Risk Assessment

**Primary Risks:**

1. **Cycle doesn't fully recover:** Auto/industrial may stay weak longer than modeled. If FY2027 revenue comes in at $530M rather than $600M+, EPS could be $0.80-1.00 rather than $1.46, and the stock at 55-70x would face multiple compression.

2. **Convertible dilution:** If notes convert in cash/shares, ~10.58M shares could enter the float (22.5% dilution). Management choices around settlement will be critical.

3. **Goodwill impairment:** A charge would be non-cash but psychologically negative and could indicate the segment's competitive position is weaker than assumed.

4. **Customer concentration:** Top 10 = 60% of revenue. Loss of a major customer or capex delay would create significant quarterly volatility.

5. **Geopolitical:** 90% international revenue. China export controls are encouraging domestic substitution. Iran conflict added to risk factors in Q1 2026.

6. **Jabil manufacturing dependency:** Single-source for ATE systems. Supply disruption risk concentrated.

**Options Market Signal:**

ATM IV is 79.9% versus 49% historical volatility — a 30.9pp premium indicating the market prices significant near-term uncertainty. The term structure is in backwardation (79.9% near-term vs 73.7% at 6 months). P/C volume ratio at 0.04 and OI at 0.03 indicate extremely low put demand — seemingly contradicting the elevated IV. The most likely interpretation: the elevated IV reflects post-earnings residual premium (last earnings April 30) plus the 16.2% short interest creating borrow/locate costs embedded in option pricing. The backwardation may also reflect summer seasonality risk in semiconductor orders.

Short interest at 16.2% with 5.5 days to cover represents meaningful bearish institutional positioning. This creates both squeeze potential on positive catalysts and validation that sophisticated investors see the valuation as stretched.

## Investment Thesis

**Base Case ($50-58, -10% to +5%):**
Revenue tracks to $530-560M FY2026, $600-650M FY2027. EPS roughly in-line with consensus ($0.50-0.65 FY2026, $1.30-1.60 FY2027). Stock consolidates around current levels, supported by earnings growth but capped by convertible dilution and full valuation. Upside limited without positive surprise.

## Conclusion

Cohu represents a genuine semiconductor cycle recovery story with structural improvements (60% recurring revenue, AI/power testing positioning, fortress balance sheet). The fundamental trajectory is positive and the business should generate meaningful earnings as revenue normalizes toward $600-700M.

However, at $55.40 the stock has already priced in substantial recovery. Coordinated insider selling ($2.7M+ by CEO/CFO in April-May at $44-49), 16.2% short interest, deeply in-the-money convertible notes creating unhedged dilution above $41, and options IV in backwardation all represent caution signals. The risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetrically attractive — the bull case (+17-35%) is roughly symmetric with the bear case (-31-46%) from here.

The appropriate posture is to maintain exposure for those already long, but wait for a pullback to the $38-42 range (or material positive estimate revision acceleration) before initiating new positions.
