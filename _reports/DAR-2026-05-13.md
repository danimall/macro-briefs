## Executive Summary

Darling Ingredients is executing a clear earnings inflection after a brutal two-year margin compression driven by the DGD renewable diesel joint venture's policy transition and commodity price volatility. Q1 2026 net income recovered to $134.3M vs. a ($26.2M) loss a year earlier, and consensus FY2026 EPS estimates have surged +96% over 90 days to $4.72. The company generates significantly more cash than GAAP earnings suggest — $1.06B in operating cash flow on $63M of net income in FY2025 — providing genuine downside cushion.

The stock has responded accordingly, rising 78% from its 52-week low of $29.15 to $62.51. At ~13.2x forward FY2026 EPS, the valuation is reasonable but no longer cheap given $3.9B in debt, DGD's ongoing policy sensitivity, and a track record of 3 consecutive quarterly misses prior to Q1 2026. The 12/12 analyst Buy consensus with a $76.42 mean target suggests 22% further upside, but the market is pricing in continued recovery that still requires DGD margin normalization to sustain.

The position warrants holding for investors already exposed, with the next 1-2 quarters of earnings confirmation needed before the risk/reward supports new capital at these levels.

## Company Overview

Darling Ingredients is the world's largest processor of animal by-products (rendering) and a major producer of renewable energy from waste fats. The company operates 260+ facilities across 15+ countries, processing approximately 15% of the world's animal agricultural by-products and producing ~30% of global collagen supply through its Rousselot brand.

**Three segments:**
- **Feed Ingredients** ($4.0B, FY2025): Core rendering operations — converting animal by-products and used cooking oil into non-food grade fats, protein meals, pet food ingredients, and organic fertilizers.
- **Food Ingredients** ($1.5B): Collagen, gelatin, natural casings, heparin, food-grade fats (Rousselot brand).
- **Fuel Ingredients** ($601M): Primarily the 50/50 DGD Joint Venture with Valero (1.2B gallon/year renewable diesel/SAF capacity), plus European biogas operations.

The integrated model — collecting waste fats, processing them, and supplying them to DGD — creates a feedstock cost advantage relative to standalone renewable diesel producers.

## Competitive Landscape

**Competitive Position: Strong and Improving**

Darling's competitive moat rests on three pillars:

1. **Collection Infrastructure:** The extensive fleet, rail network, and processing facilities across North America create barriers to entry. Raw material logistics in rendering are inherently local/regional, and scale provides density economics. Competitors cannot replicate this network without decades of investment and relationship-building.

2. **Vertical Integration:** The collect-process-supply chain to DGD provides a feedstock cost advantage vs. standalone renewable diesel producers. With the OBBBA mandating North American-sourced feedstocks starting 2026, Darling's domestic collection infrastructure becomes an even more critical competitive advantage.

3. **Collagen Leadership:** Rousselot's ~30% global collagen market share, further consolidated by the Tessenderlo JV, provides pricing power in a structurally growing market.

The OBBBA feedstock requirement disadvantages foreign-sourced renewable diesel feedstocks. Multiple competing renewable diesel producers have idled capacity due to margin compression — reducing industry supply while Darling's integrated model provides more resilience.

**Risks to position:** Increasing competition for used cooking oil collection from biofuel companies; consolidation in meatpacking reducing access to raw materials for independent renderers; potential for large meatpackers to vertically integrate into renewable fuels.

## Risk Assessment

**1. DGD Policy Dependency (HIGH):** The JV swung from $298M net income to ($68M) loss in one year based on regulatory transitions. While CFPC extension to 2029 provides medium-term visibility, renewable fuel policy remains politically sensitive. Any weakening of RIN values, LCFS credits, or CFPC structure would immediately impair DAR's earnings trajectory.

**2. Leverage (MODERATE-HIGH):** Altman Z-Score of 1.91. The $500M 2027 maturity must be refinanced in an elevated rate environment. If commodity prices turn against DAR simultaneously with a DGD margin compression, the balance sheet provides limited cushion.

**3. Customer Concentration:** DGD alone represents $1.2B in revenue (the largest customer). Top 10 customers constitute ~40% of revenue. The JV structure creates mutual dependency, but any disruption to DGD operations directly impairs DAR's top line.

**4. Commodity Volatility:** Protein meal prices, fat prices, and renewable fuel credit values all swing independently. FY2025 demonstrated that simultaneous adverse moves can compress margins rapidly despite volume stability.

**5. Goodwill Vulnerability:** One reporting unit ($563M goodwill) is flagged at only 20-30% above carrying value. Another commodity downturn could trigger material write-downs.

**6. Executive Turnover:** 5+ Item 5.02 filings (officer departures) between Sept 2024 and Feb 2026 is elevated and warrants monitoring.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (to BUY):**
- Stock pulls back to $52-55 range (11-12x FY2026 EPS) on market-wide weakness without fundamental deterioration — would offer ~40% upside to consensus targets.
- Two consecutive quarters of DGD margin >$0.50/gallon, demonstrating policy-driven recovery is sustained rather than one-quarter aberration.
- Management initiates meaningful debt reduction ($200M+ in FY2026), de-risking the balance sheet narrative.

**Downgrade Triggers (to AVOID):**
- Q2 2026 EPS misses by >15%, breaking the inflection narrative and suggesting Q1 was a one-off.
- DGD margins revert below $0.25/gallon for a full quarter, indicating the policy tailwind from RVO finalization has not materialized.
- Any goodwill impairment charge or covenant concern disclosure.
- Stock appreciation to $75+ without corresponding earnings upgrades beyond current $4.72 consensus (would imply 16x+ on uncertain earnings with high leverage).

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected late July/early August). This is the critical confirmation quarter — one quarter of inflection is insufficient to validate the thesis given 3 consecutive prior misses.

## Conclusion

Darling Ingredients presents a genuine fundamental recovery supported by exceptional estimate revision velocity and strong underlying cash generation. The integrated rendering-to-renewable-fuel model benefits from regulatory tailwinds (OBBBA, RVO finalization), and the collagen business provides a structurally growing diversification anchor. However, the stock has already priced in much of this recovery at 78% above its 52-week low. At 13.2x forward earnings with high leverage, meaningful DGD execution risk, and only one quarter of earnings confirmation after three consecutive misses, the current entry point does not offer sufficient asymmetry for new capital.

The recommendation is HOLD — maintaining exposure for the growth thesis while awaiting either a pullback to improve risk/reward or Q2 confirmation that the Q1 inflection is durable. The 12/12 analyst Buy consensus and $76.42 mean target suggest the sell-side agrees on direction but may be overly uniform in conviction given the limited confirmation data.
