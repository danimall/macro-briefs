## Executive Summary

Corcept Therapeutics received FDA approval of Lifyorli (relacorilant) for platinum-resistant ovarian cancer on March 25, 2026. This transforms the company from a single-product Cushing's syndrome business facing generic erosion into a multi-indication oncology platform with OS-level data (HR 0.65, p=0.0004). Management raised 2026 revenue guidance to $950M–$1.05B, implying 25-38% growth, with long-term targets of $2B in Cushing's sales and $1B+ from Lifyorli by decade-end.

The investment case rests on commercial execution of a drug launch that has not yet generated meaningful revenue, priced into a stock trading at ~150x trailing earnings. The company reported a net loss of $0.30/share in Q1 2026 due to pre-revenue launch spending ($449M SG&A in 2025, expected higher in 2026). Operating leverage is substantial if Lifyorli ramps, but execution risk is non-trivial in a competitive ovarian cancer landscape, compounded by an unresolved DOJ investigation since 2021, active securities class action litigation, and finalized patent loss on Korlym that opens the door to additional generic entrants.

The stock has rallied from its $28.66 low (52-week) to $60.22, recovering approximately half its decline from the $91 high. At the current price, the market is partially crediting the Lifyorli opportunity while still discounting the legal overhang and launch execution uncertainty. The 12.9% short interest with 9.6 days to cover reflects meaningful institutional skepticism. I view this as a growth hold — the directional thesis is positive, but the specific risk constellation (litigation, DOJ, launch execution against a ~150x P/E) prevents conviction at this entry point.

## Company Overview

Corcept Therapeutics is a commercial-stage pharmaceutical company specializing in cortisol modulation. The company's sole marketed product through 2025 was Korlym (mifepristone) for hyperglycemia secondary to Cushing's syndrome, generating $761M in 2025 revenue. Following FDA approval of Lifyorli in March 2026, the company now has a second marketed product targeting platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

The pipeline extends further: miricorilant in Phase 2b for MASH, dazucorilant in development for ALS (showing 84% reduction in death risk despite missing primary endpoint), and multiple Phase 2 expansions of relacorilant into pancreatic, endometrial, cervical, and prostate cancers. The company holds >1,000 proprietary selective cortisol modulators across four structural series.

## Financial Analysis

**Revenue Growth:** Revenue grew from $402M (2022) to $761M (2025), a 24% CAGR. Q1 2026 revenue was $165M (+4.9% YoY), depressed by distributor transition and pre-Lifyorli timing. Management's 2026 guidance of $950M–$1.05B represents a meaningful acceleration.

**Profitability Compression:** Operating income fell from $137M (2024) to $45M (2025) despite 13% revenue growth. The driver was SG&A expansion from $280M to $449M — a deliberate pre-investment in oncology commercial infrastructure ahead of the Lifyorli launch. Q1 2026 showed a $31.8M net loss ($0.30/share), the company's first quarterly loss in years. R&D spending was $255M in 2025.

**Gross Margins:** At 98.3%, Corcept operates one of the highest gross margin profiles in pharmaceuticals. COGS is trivially low (~1.7% of revenue). Incremental Lifyorli revenue will flow almost entirely to gross profit, creating extraordinary operating leverage once revenue ramps past the fixed SG&A base.

**Balance Sheet:** $532M in cash and investments (Dec 2025), approximately $515M at Q1 2026-end. Zero debt. Current ratio 2.92. Altman Z-Score 4.69. The company is fully self-funding with no need for external capital. Share repurchases totaled $173M in 2025 ($245.9M in total cash deployed for share acquisition activities).

**Cash Flow:** FCF margin was 18.6% in 2025 despite the SG&A surge. The business generates sufficient operating cash flow to fund both the launch investment and buybacks simultaneously.

**Earnings Trajectory:** Trailing EPS of $0.82 (FY2025) produces the ~150x P/E. Forward consensus: $0.48 (FY2026), rising to $1.63 (FY2027). The FY2026 estimate implies continued compression from launch spending. The FY2027 estimate reflects analyst expectations for operating leverage as Lifyorli scales — at $60, this implies ~37x forward P/E on FY2027 earnings.

## Growth Analysis

**Near-term (2026):** The $950M–$1.05B guidance implies $189M–$289M in incremental revenue over 2025's $761M. Some comes from continued Korlym volume growth (disease awareness programs expanding the diagnosed Cushing's population), and the remainder from early Lifyorli revenues.

**Pipeline Optionality:**
- **Cushing's CRL Resolution:** If relacorilant secures eventual Cushing's approval, it would cannibalize Korlym volume but at potentially higher pricing power (selective mechanism, no abortion-drug stigma, differentiated safety profile). The path forward is uncertain after the Dec 2025 CRL.
- **ALS (dazucorilant):** The 84% reduction in death risk (p=0.0009) is remarkable, even though the primary endpoint was missed. Phase 3 planned for 2026. Orphan designation + Fast Track. Small patient population but extreme unmet need and pricing power.
- **MASH (miricorilant):** Phase 2b enrollment complete. Multi-billion dollar market. Competitive with Madrigal's resmetirom but differentiated mechanism.
- **Oncology expansion:** Phase 2 trials in pancreatic, endometrial, cervical cancers. EU MAA submitted for ovarian cancer (decision expected Q4 2026).

**Estimate Revisions:** The revision picture is mixed. Current quarter EPS revised down 29.5% over 30 days (to $0.04), reflecting launch investment. Next quarter revised up 54.3% (to $0.25), suggesting analysts expect revenue ramp. Current year up 17.6% over 30 days. However, FY2027 estimates have been cut 49.9% over 90 days — from $3.25 to $1.63 — reflecting the post-CRL reality that the Cushing's relacorilant indication won't contribute revenues on the prior timeline. This downward revision tempers the bull case timeline.

On FY2027 consensus of $1.63, the forward P/E is approximately 37x — elevated relative to peers (ALKS at 40.6x, APLS at 38.4x, INDV at 19.2x; peer median 38.4x) but not extreme given the growth profile.

The valuation challenge: CORT's current earnings power ($0.48 consensus for FY2026) reflects a trough due to launch investment, not normalized profitability. The company was earning $1.34/share in 2024 before the SG&A surge. If Lifyorli achieves even $200M in steady-state revenue at 98% gross margins against an already-built commercial infrastructure, the operating leverage is substantial.

**Bull case valuation:** $1.63 FY2027 EPS × 45x (premium for high-growth pharma with pipeline optionality) = $73. If Lifyorli exceeds expectations and ALS advances, FY2028 EPS could reach $2.50+ → $100+ target.

**Bear case valuation:** If Lifyorli launch disappoints and Korlym erosion accelerates, FY2027 EPS could be $0.80-1.00 with a de-rating to 25x = $20-25.

At $60, the stock prices in significant success on Lifyorli but not full long-term pipeline value. The 49.9% downward revision in FY2027 estimates over 90 days suggests the consensus is still adjusting to the CRL impact on the Cushing's timeline.

## Competitive Landscape

**Cushing's Syndrome:**
Corcept faces permanent generic competition on Korlym following the finalized Teva patent loss (Feb 2026). Sun Pharmaceutical and Hikma have settlement agreements allowing generic launches. The company's strategy — grow the diagnosed population while defending volume through its own authorized generic — is sound but guarantees continued price erosion. Recordati's Isturisa broadened to all Cushing's in April 2025, adding competitive pressure.

**Ovarian Cancer:**
Lifyorli enters a competitive landscape: bevacizumab, PARP inhibitors (olaparib, niraparib, rucaparib), checkpoint inhibitors, and mirvetuximab soravtansine (Elahere). The differentiation is mechanism (cortisol modulation enhancing chemo sensitivity) and the demonstrated OS benefit (4.1 month median OS improvement). The combination with nab-paclitaxel offers a manageable safety profile. The key competitive question is whether oncologists adopt a novel mechanism from a company with no oncology track record versus familiar agents from established oncology companies.

**Platform Moat:**
Corcept's >1,000 proprietary cortisol modulators and deep cortisol biology expertise create a genuine scientific moat. The company is the only firm with a marketed cortisol modulator and the only one pursuing cortisol modulation across multiple therapeutic areas simultaneously.

## Risk Assessment

**1. DOJ Investigation (Since Nov 2021):** Over four years of investigation into Korlym promotion practices, payments to healthcare professionals, and reimbursement. The company states it is not currently a "defendant" but is cooperating. Resolution could range from a declination (favorable) to substantial penalties and a Corporate Integrity Agreement (materially adverse). The duration suggests the investigation is non-trivial.

**2. Securities Class Action:** Filed alleging management concealed FDA warnings about insufficient Cushing's relacorilant data while publicly promoting pipeline readiness. Class period Oct 31, 2024 – Dec 30, 2025. Lead plaintiff deadline was April 21, 2026. This creates ongoing uncertainty and potential financial liability.

**3. Lifyorli Launch Execution:** The company has never launched an oncology product. The platinum-resistant ovarian cancer market is competitive with established players. Specialty distribution is complex. Market access (payer coverage, NCCN guidelines) will take time. The $449M+ SG&A base is a sunk cost if launch disappoints.

**4. Korlym Generic Erosion:** With the patent loss finalized and additional generics potentially entering, price erosion on Korlym could accelerate beyond the 17.7% already experienced in 2025. If volume growth cannot offset pricing declines, Cushing's revenue could peak sooner than management's $2B target implies.

**5. Short Interest at 12.9%:** With 9.6 days to cover, meaningful institutional capital is positioned for further downside. This likely reflects skepticism about Lifyorli launch execution and/or expectation that legal risks will materialize.

EU approval arrives Q4 2026. The Cushing's CRL is resolved through additional analysis or a limited trial, opening the relacorilant Cushing's market ($2B+ opportunity). ## Conclusion

Corcept Therapeutics presents a compelling growth story with the Lifyorli approval transforming its fundamental profile. The pristine balance sheet ($515M+ cash, zero debt), ~98% gross margins, and multi-indication pipeline create genuine optionality. However, the current valuation prices in meaningful launch success before a single quarter of Lifyorli revenue is reported, while three specific asymmetric risk factors — DOJ investigation (4+ years unresolved), active securities litigation, and 12.9% short interest — create a risk profile that prevents a BUY recommendation at $60.

At ~37x FY2027 consensus earnings, the stock is priced for a launch that goes well, yet the company has never commercialized an oncology product, the DOJ investigation could produce a material adverse outcome at any time, and the 49.9% downward revision in FY2027 estimates over 90 days demonstrates that the consensus itself is unstable. A stock priced for success with binary downside risks from legal catalysts that cannot be timed or sized warrants a growth hold, because the risk/reward at this specific price does not offer asymmetric upside once the legal overhang is sized properly.

HOLD with a 12-month positive trajectory contingent on Lifyorli commercial proof-of-concept in Q2-Q3 2026 earnings.
