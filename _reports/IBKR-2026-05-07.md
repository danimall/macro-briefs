## Executive Summary

Interactive Brokers is a technology-driven global electronic brokerage with financial characteristics more resembling a high-margin software platform than a traditional financial services firm. The company generated a 79% pretax margin in Q3 2025 with only 3,131 employees serving 4.13 million accounts across 160+ exchanges in 37 countries. Customer equity reached $757.5B (+40% YoY), with total DARTs at 3.62 million (+34% YoY). This operating leverage means incremental volume growth flows nearly entirely to the bottom line.

The investment case centers on three converging drivers: (1) strong growth in global derivatives trading volume — options, futures, and event contracts — driven by rising retail participation and institutional hedging demand; (2) the company's unique position as a primary beneficiary of elevated trading activity, with commission revenue scaling directly with volume; and (3) continued robust account growth (31% YoY as of Q1 2026) compounding the asset base from which net interest income, margin lending, and securities lending revenues are generated. At approximately 27x FY2027 consensus EPS (with estimates trending higher), the valuation is demanding but justified by the quality and durability of the growth profile relative to peers trading at 15-17x with structurally lower growth trajectories.

The primary risk preventing a higher-conviction position is valuation after a significant rally over the past year, combined with a unique ownership structure where public shareholders access only 26.3% of the operating entity's economics. However, accelerating account growth, rising estimates, and macro tailwinds from volatility and rate uncertainty support continued appreciation over a 12-month horizon.

## Company Overview

Interactive Brokers Group, Inc. is a holding company whose primary asset is approximately 26.3% ownership of IBG LLC, the operating entity that conducts all brokerage activities. The remaining 73.7% is held by IBG Holdings LLC, controlled by founder Thomas Peterffy and affiliates. This structure, while unusual, has been in place since the 2007 IPO, with IBG Inc.'s share gradually increasing from 10% to 26.3% through periodic conversions.

The business provides automated trade execution, clearing, and settlement across stocks, options, futures, forex, bonds, mutual funds, ETFs, precious metals, cryptocurrencies, and forecast contracts. The platform connects to more than 170 electronic exchanges and market centers in 40 countries and 29 currencies, offering professional-grade tools at the lowest margin rates in the industry (benchmark rate + small spread, minimum 0.75%).

IBKR's competitive position derives from 47+ years of proprietary technology development that enables extraordinary automation. Revenue per employee runs approximately $2.1M annually — multiples above traditional brokerages — reflecting the capital-light, technology-first model that requires minimal human intervention for trade processing.

**Stock Split:** On April 15, 2025, IBKR announced its intention to effect a 4-for-1 stock split. Holders of record on June 16, 2025 received three additional shares distributed after market close on June 17, 2025, with split-adjusted trading beginning June 18, 2025. All historical per-share data has been retroactively adjusted.

**S&P 500 Inclusion:** August 2025.

This approximately 30% account growth rate has been sustained for multiple years, driven by international expansion (IBKR is a leading choice for non-US investors seeking global market access), increasing advertising spend (+$27M in the first 9 months of 2025), and structural growth in derivatives trading globally. Whether this rate can be maintained going forward is a key assumption in the bull case.

**Securities Lending Surge:**
Net securities lending income nearly doubled YoY to an estimated $314M in Q3 2025 (from $156M). Rising short-selling activity, increased market volatility, and higher notional values of customer securities all contribute. This revenue stream has minimal capital requirements and scales directly with customer asset growth. However, full-year 2025 net interest income grew 13% overall, suggesting the securities lending surge may moderate relative to its Q3 2025 peak.

**Macro Tailwinds:**
The current macro environment — geopolitical uncertainty, Fed governance transition, elevated rate volatility, expanding derivatives usage — has been supportive of IBKR's model. Trading volume growth has been robust across asset classes, though whether this represents a durable structural shift or a cyclical peak remains debatable.

**Estimate Revisions:**
Consensus EPS estimates have been trending modestly higher, consistent with the company's continued operational outperformance. Given the operating leverage, even modest volume outperformance relative to consensus would produce meaningful EPS beats.

## Competitive Landscape

IBKR's competitive moat rests on three pillars:

1. **Technology Stack (47 Years of Development):** The proprietary platform executes, clears, and settles trades with minimal human intervention. This cannot be replicated — competitors would need decades and billions to match the global connectivity and automation.

2. **Global Exchange Access:** More than 170 electronic exchanges and market centers in 40 countries and 29 currencies. Among retail-accessible brokers, IBKR's global reach is unmatched based on exchange connectivity and currency coverage. For international investors, IBKR is effectively a primary option for direct US market access at institutional-grade pricing.

3. **Cost Leadership:** Lowest margin rates in the industry, highest interest paid on customer cash, and commission rates competitive with zero-commission brokers when accounting for execution quality and payment-for-order-flow avoidance. The automation allows IBKR to maintain these rates profitably at 77-79% margins.

**Competitive Trajectory — Improving:**
- Account growth accelerating (31% YoY as of Q1 2026)
- Revenue per employee widening vs. peers
- Product breadth expanding (ForecastEx, crypto, bonds)
- International dominance deepening (200+ countries served)
- Options/derivatives share growing structurally

## Risk Assessment

**Rate Sensitivity (Moderate-High):** Net interest income comprises 57% of revenue. A 100bp Fed rate cut cycle would reduce NII by approximately $440M annually (25bp sensitivity: $77M USD + $33M non-USD = $110M per 25bp). However, the growing balance sheet has historically offset NIM compression — total NII grew 11% YoY in the first nine months of 2025 despite rate cuts.

**Ownership Structure (Permanent):** Public shareholders access only 26.3% of IBG LLC economics. While this creates governance limitations, it also provides significant founder alignment — Peterffy's indirect economic ownership of approximately 67.5% of IBG LLC (through Holdings' ~73.7% stake, of which Peterffy controls ~91.6%) means his wealth creation is closely tied to shareholder value creation. He retains voting control through the Holdings structure. The gradual conversion (10% → 26.3% since 2007) provides a path to normalization over the next 10-15 years.

**Volume Cyclicality:** A sustained low-volatility, low-volume environment would pressure commission revenue. However, the diversity of asset classes (170+ exchanges, multiple products) provides natural hedging — when equity volumes decline, forex or derivatives often increase as hedging activity rises.

**Regulatory Complexity:** Operations across 40 countries create ongoing compliance burden. The OFAC settlement ($11.8M) illustrates this risk, though the amount was immaterial to the business.

**Valuation Risk:** After a significant rally over the past year, the stock trades near its 52-week high. A broad equity correction or sustained decline in trading volumes could create a meaningful drawdown in the near term.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term IV at 43.1% versus historical vol of 36.9% indicates the options market is pricing elevated uncertainty. The low put/call OI ratio (0.24) and volume ratio (0.39) indicate no institutional hedging buildup. ## Addressing the BUY Threshold

The specific evidence supporting BUY at the current price:

1. **Elevated trading activity beneficiary.** Each increase in market volatility and trading activity historically corresponds to DART growth for IBKR. The current macro setup — geopolitical binary outcomes, credit stress, Fed transition — has been creating sustained elevated trading activity, as evidenced by Q1 2026 DARTs of 4.37M.

2. **The trailing P/E overstates current valuation.** Trailing EPS of $2.22 understates run-rate earnings given recent growth. The company is growing revenue 20%+ with 77-79% pretax margins. On a forward basis, ~27x FY2027E ($2.75, potentially rising) is a more accurate representation. For a business growing accounts at ~30% annually with 77-79% margins and no debt, 27x forward is reasonable — it is below the PEG threshold of 1.0 (27x / 31% growth).

3. **Estimate revisions are positive.** Consensus estimates have been trending higher, and the company has consistently delivered operational results that meet or exceed expectations.

The risk that moderates conviction: valuation is not cheap, and the stock has rallied significantly. Position sizing should reflect this — 2% rather than 3-4% allocation — but the fundamental quality and macro tailwinds justify owning the name at these levels.

## Conclusion

Interactive Brokers is a structural compounder favorably positioned as the world's most automated brokerage, serving the fastest-growing segment (active/derivatives traders), benefiting from an environment of elevated trading activity that supports volume growth. The 77-79% pretax margin, ~31% account growth, $13.7B excess capital, and zero debt create a financial profile with very few peers in any sector. The valuation at ~27x FY2027E is demanding but not extreme for a PEG below 1.0, and rising estimates provide a path to continued appreciation. BUY with 2% position sizing, acknowledging the limited margin of safety at current levels.
