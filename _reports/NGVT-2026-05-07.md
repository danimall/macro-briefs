## Executive Summary

Ingevity Corporation is a specialty materials company executing an aggressive strategic transformation, shedding non-core assets to focus on its two strongest businesses: Performance Materials (activated carbon for automotive emissions control) and Pavement Technologies. The core Performance Materials segment generates 54% EBITDA margins, representing a genuinely differentiated business with pricing power tied to regulatory compliance requirements. The company has already divested its Industrial Specialties and Road Markings businesses, with the Advanced Polymer Technologies (APT) segment actively marketed for sale targeting year-end 2026.

The stock has appreciated ~99% from its 52-week low of $36.18 to $77.87, driven by the strategic review completion, activist engagement, and strong Q1 2026 execution ($1.15 adjusted EPS, a 45% beat). However, the appreciation now prices in much of the transformation upside, leaving limited asymmetry at current levels. At approximately 16x FY2026E earnings (using $4.80 consensus), valuation is reasonable relative to specialty chemicals peers (median ~21x), but the discount appropriately reflects ICE vehicle secular risk, a $95M BASF litigation payment due in Q2 2026, and execution uncertainty around the APT sale.

The investment case depends on two critical factors: (1) whether Performance Materials can sustain 50%+ margins and grow through content-per-vehicle increases even as EV adoption accelerates, and (2) whether the APT divestiture generates sufficient proceeds to meaningfully de-lever and fund buybacks. We rate NGVT a HOLD with a positive growth orientation — the transformation thesis is credible but current price captures most of the near-term catalyst value.

## Company Overview

Ingevity was spun off from WestRock in May 2016 and has since evolved through acquisitions and divestitures. The company's most valuable asset is its Performance Materials segment ($607M revenue, 52% of continuing operations), which manufactures chemically activated carbon from hardwood sawdust primarily for automotive gasoline vapor emission control systems. These products are mandated by emission regulations globally — OEMs cannot sell vehicles without functioning evaporative emission control systems, creating a non-discretionary demand profile.

The Pavement Technologies business ($401M, 34% of revenue) provides warm-mix asphalt additives (Evotherm), emulsifiers, and pavement preservation products. This business benefits from US infrastructure spending tailwinds and sustainability trends favoring lower-emission construction processes.

The APT segment ($160M, 14% of revenue) — caprolactone chemistry produced at a single UK plant — is actively being sold after management concluded it lacks strategic fit and competitive positioning against Chinese alternatives.

## Financial Analysis

### Revenue and Profitability

Continuing operations revenue has been modestly declining: $1,216M (2023) → $1,200M (2024) → $1,168M (2025), reflecting APT weakness and the wind-down of non-core product lines. However, Q1 2026 showed 4% YoY growth to $258M, suggesting stabilization in the core businesses.

The headline GAAP picture is distorted by ~$600M in cumulative impairments over 2024-2025:
- 2024: $306.6M goodwill impairment (Performance Chemicals)
- 2025: $183.8M goodwill impairment (APT) + $109.3M long-lived asset impairment (Road Markings)

Adjusted EBITDA from continuing operations tells the real story: $373M (32% margin) in 2025, up from $353M in 2023. Performance Materials alone generated $327M at a 53.8% EBITDA margin — world-class for specialty materials.

### Cash Flow

Free cash flow generation is the company's strongest financial attribute:
- 2025 Operating CF: $331.2M (vs. $128.6M in 2024, which was depressed by a $100M CTO contract termination payment)
- 2025 CapEx: $57.7M
- 2025 FCF: $273.5M (23.4% margin)
- 2026 CapEx guidance: $40-60M (mostly maintenance)

### Balance Sheet

Total debt stands at approximately $1.26B against $78M cash, yielding net debt of ~$1.18B. Net leverage per credit agreement is 2.7x — well within the 4.0x covenant. Interest coverage at 5.5x is comfortable. The revolving credit facility was amended/extended in March 2026 (per 8-K filing), removing near-term maturity risk. The $550M Senior Notes are due 2028.

Stockholders' equity has been reduced to just $29.7M at year-end 2025 — an optical concern driven entirely by the impairment charges, not operational deterioration. The Altman Z-Score of 0.7 is misleading for the same reason; this business generates $370M+ EBITDA with $474M of undrawn revolver capacity.

### Capital Allocation

Management returned $56.3M via buybacks in 2025 (at an average price of $52.67) and has $297.5M remaining under a $500M authorization. No dividend is paid. Debt reduction of $192.3M on the revolver in 2025 demonstrates de-leveraging priority.

## Growth Analysis

### Near-Term Drivers (2026-2027)

1. **APT divestiture proceeds** (targeting year-end 2026): Carrying value ~$393M, though realizable value is uncertain given the goodwill write-off. Even at 50% of carrying value, proceeds would provide ~$200M for debt reduction or buybacks.

2. **BASF litigation resolution**: The ~$95.4M payment in Q2 2026 is a one-time drag that removes an overhang once completed.

3. **Stranded cost elimination**: ~$15M of costs associated with divested businesses to be removed by 2027, directly improving margins.

4. **Infrastructure spending**: Multi-year tailwind for Pavement Technologies from the US infrastructure bill.

### Medium-Term Growth (2027-2030)

5. **Content-per-vehicle growth**: Stricter emission standards globally (particularly China and US Tier 3) increase activated carbon content requirements per vehicle. Management estimates this can offset modest volume declines from EV adoption through 2030.

6. **Battery/energy storage applications**: R&D investment in hard carbon and nanotubes for next-generation batteries. Early-stage but provides optionality against ICE decline.

### Secular Headwind

Performance Materials derives the majority of total company EBITDA from ICE/hybrid vehicles. While North American truck/SUV content growth provides insulation through 2028-2030, the long-term trajectory is definitively negative in Europe and uncertain globally. This caps the growth multiple the market will assign.

### Forward Estimates

FY2026 guidance: $1.1-1.2B revenue, $380-400M Adjusted EBITDA, $4.70-$5.20 Adjusted EPS. Consensus at $4.80 for FY2026 and $5.92 for FY2027. Estimate revisions are modestly negative (-4.2% over 90 days for current year), suggesting no positive momentum catalyst.

## Valuation Assessment

At $77.87 per share with 35.2M shares outstanding, market cap is $2.6B. Enterprise value is approximately $3.8B ($2.6B + $1.18B net debt).

**Key multiples:**
- P/E (FY2026E at $4.80): 16.2x
- P/E (FY2027E at $5.92): 13.2x
- EV/EBITDA (FY2026E at $390M midpoint): 9.7x
- FCF Yield (normalized $250-270M): 9.6-10.4%

**Peer comparison:** Specialty chemicals peers trade at a median P/E of 21.1x and EV/EBITDA of 12-14x. NGVT's discount reflects the ICE secular overhang, leverage, and transformation execution risk. Minerals Technologies (MTX) at 15.3x P/E is the closest comp with similar size and cyclicality.

The current valuation appears fair. The ~16x forward P/E appropriately discounts the ICE risk versus the peer median of 21x. At $77.87, the stock trades near the analyst consensus mean target of $80.50. For meaningful upside, one needs to underwrite either: (a) successful APT sale at attractive terms + aggressive buybacks compressing the share count, or (b) proof that Performance Materials can grow revenue through content gains despite EV adoption. Neither is yet confirmed.

## Competitive Landscape

### Performance Materials — Wide Moat, Secular Threat

Ingevity dominates the activated carbon market for automotive emission control. The competitive moat rests on:
- Decades of proven performance data (OEMs face massive recall costs from emission failures)
- Proprietary chemically activated hardwood carbon technology
- Five global manufacturing plants creating geographic proximity to customers
- Regulatory mandates creating non-discretionary demand (EPA Tier 3, Euro 6d+)
- Top 10 customers represent 90% of segment revenue — high switching costs once qualified

Competitors (Norit/Cabot, Kuraray, Chinese producers) exist but struggle to match the consistency and qualification data. BASF's entry as a competitor post-litigation adds a credible threat long-term.

### Pavement Technologies — Differentiated but Niche

Evotherm warm-mix technology has sustainability advantages (lower mixing temperatures, more recycled content). The business is relationship-driven with state DOTs and contractors. Growth is tied to infrastructure spending cycles rather than competitive displacement.

### Overall Assessment

The competitive position is improving as portfolio simplification eliminates underperforming segments (APT faced Chinese competition with no moat; Industrial Specialties was commoditized). Post-transformation, nearly all of Ingevity's revenue will come from businesses with genuine differentiation. The 54% EBITDA margin in Performance Materials is the proof point.

## Risk Assessment

### 1. ICE/Hybrid Vehicle Production Decline (Structural, High Impact)
Performance Materials' revenue is directly tied to ICE and hybrid vehicle production volumes. North America provides insulation (trucks/SUVs = more content, EV adoption slower), but Europe is already weakening. A faster-than-expected EV adoption curve would erode the company's largest profit center. Timeline: material impact likely 2028-2032.

### 2. BASF Litigation Cash Drain ($95.4M, Q2 2026)
The Federal Circuit upheld the treble damages verdict in February 2026. Payment of ~$95.4M is expected in Q2 2026, with potential additional attorneys' fees. This reduces near-term capital allocation flexibility.

### 3. APT Sale Execution
If the APT sale fails or yields minimal proceeds (the goodwill write-off implies the business is worth less than the $183.8M paid for it), the company retains a ~$160M revenue business at a single UK plant with rising Chinese competition and tariff headwinds.

### 4. Customer Concentration
Top 10 customers = 90% of Performance Materials revenue. Loss of a single major automotive parts supplier relationship would be highly impactful.

### 5. Leverage in a Rising-Rate Environment
~$415M of variable-rate debt is exposed to current elevated rates. While interest coverage is adequate at 5.5x, the cost of debt is a meaningful drag on equity returns until leverage is reduced below 2.0x.

### Options Market Signal

Near-term implied volatility is significantly elevated at 55.9% versus 42.1% historical vol (13.8pp rich), with the term structure in steep backwardation (55.9% 1-week vs. 30.8% 6-month). This signals the market is pricing a near-term event or heightened uncertainty. The P/C OI ratio moved from 0.98 to 1.08 alongside a 4.9% price decline on May 7 — modest put accumulation. The call-heavy skew at -6.4% suggests some speculative upside positioning remains, potentially related to the APT sale process or further transformation announcements.

The elevated near-term IV is consistent with a stock in transformation — event risk around divestiture announcements, earnings, or BASF payment timing creates episodic volatility. This is not a favorable entry timing signal for new positions.

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case ($87-95; +12% to +22%)

- APT sale closes at $200-300M, enabling accelerated buybacks and de-leveraging to <2.0x
- Performance Materials demonstrates organic revenue growth from content-per-vehicle gains
- FY2027 EPS reaches $6.50-7.00 as stranded costs are eliminated and buybacks reduce share count
- Market re-rates toward peer multiples (18-20x forward P/E) once simplification is complete
- Battery/energy storage R&D produces commercial partnerships, providing narrative bridge past ICE decline

### Bear Case ($55-60; -23% to -29%)

- APT sale fails or yields sub-$100M proceeds
- China tariff escalation impacts Performance Materials' Asia-Pacific revenue (~20% of segment)
- Automotive production downturn reduces volumes 10-15% (recession scenario)
- EV adoption accelerates, analysts cut long-term growth estimates, multiple compresses to 10-12x
- BASF payment + potential attorneys' fees ($95M + $10-20M) constrains buyback capacity through 2027

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade triggers (to BUY):**
- APT sale announced at >$200M proceeds, with clear de-leveraging and buyback plan
- FY2026 EPS tracking above $5.20 (high end of guidance) with FY2027 consensus rising above $6.50
- Stock pulls back to $65-68 range (creating >15% upside to fair value)

**Downgrade triggers (to AVOID):**
- APT sale process fails or yields <$100M
- Performance Materials revenue declines >5% organically for two consecutive quarters
- Management cuts FY2026 guidance below $4.50 EPS
- Net leverage exceeds 3.5x or credit facility covenant pressure emerges

**Reassessment timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (August 2026) — should provide BASF payment impact, APT sale process update, and mid-year Performance Materials demand visibility.

## Conclusion

Ingevity is a well-managed specialty materials company executing a credible transformation to focus on its highest-quality assets. The Performance Materials business generates exceptional margins and has a defensible competitive position for the next 5-7 years. However, the stock's 99% appreciation from its 52-week low has substantially reduced the margin of safety, and the forward catalyst path (APT sale, litigation resolution, buyback execution) is identified but not yet delivered.

At 16x forward earnings with modestly declining estimate revisions, the risk/reward is balanced rather than asymmetric. The ICE secular overhang, customer concentration, and near-term cash drains from BASF litigation create enough uncertainty to prevent a more constructive rating. We maintain a HOLD recommendation with a positive growth orientation — the transformation is directionally correct, but investors should wait for either a pullback to the mid-$60s or tangible proof of post-transformation earnings power (likely H1 2027) before adding aggressively.
