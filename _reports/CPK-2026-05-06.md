## Executive Summary

Chesapeake Utilities is a mid-cap gas utility operating across Florida, the Delmarva Peninsula, and Ohio, with a growth profile meaningfully above its peer group (9-10% EPS CAGR vs. 5-7% sector norm). The company's regulated energy segment (~87% of operating income) benefits from Florida population in-migration, multiple infrastructure riders that reduce regulatory lag, and a $47M FCG rate case filed in April 2026 that represents the single largest near-term earnings catalyst. At $125.83, the stock trades at 21.1x trailing earnings — a modest premium to the 19.6x peer median — with analyst targets ranging from $142 to $160.

The core tension in CPK's investment case is timing. The FCG rate case, if approved at even 60-70% of the request, could triple after-tax earnings from that subsidiary and meaningfully accelerate the path toward the $7.75-$8.00 EPS target by 2028. However, the stock has delivered 3 misses in 4 quarters, the CFO is departing after 18 years, insider selling was concentrated in late 2025, and options markets are pricing near-term stress (34.8% IV vs. 20.1% HV in steep backwardation). The growth thesis is intact and visible, but entering ahead of confirmed catalysts at a peer premium carries execution risk that argues for patience.

## Company Overview

Chesapeake Utilities is a diversified energy delivery company formed in 1947. Operations span two segments:

**Regulated Energy (~87% operating income):** Natural gas distribution serving ~343,000 customers (Delmarva and Florida), natural gas transmission (Eastern Shore, Peninsula Pipeline, Aspire Energy Express), and electric distribution (~33,500 Florida customers). The November 2023 acquisition of Florida City Gas ($923M) added ~116,000 customers and positioned CPK as a major Florida gas utility.

**Unregulated Energy (~13% operating income):** Propane operations (~92,400 customers), Aspire Energy (2,800 miles of Ohio pipeline), Marlin Gas Services (virtual CNG/RNG/LNG pipeline), Eight Flags CHP generation, and renewable natural gas production facilities.

The company operates in structurally growing service territories — Florida net in-migration drives 2-4% annual customer additions — with a vertically integrated model spanning transmission and distribution that allows internal project creation.

## Financial Analysis

- 2025 Adjusted EPS: $6.01 (+11.5%)

Revenue grew from $681M (2022) to $930M (2025), a CAGR of 11%, driven by the FCG acquisition and organic growth. Operating margin reached 27.5% in 2025, with net margin at 15.1%.

**Capital Structure:** The company maintains a 50/50 debt-to-equity capitalization ($1,462M long-term debt vs. $1,599M equity). Interest expense has roughly doubled from $37M to $72.5M due to FCG acquisition debt. $200M in senior notes were issued at 5.04% in late 2025. The revolver has $287M available.

**Cash Flow Gap:** Operating cash flow of $234M covers only ~50% of $449-471M annual capex. The difference is funded through continuous equity issuance ($123M DRIP/ATM in 2025, ~$60M expected in 2026), debt issuance, and revolver draws. Shares outstanding have grown 36% since 2022 (17.7M to ~24.0M). This dilution is an ongoing cost of the growth model.

**Earnings Quality Concern:** The recent track record shows 3 misses in 4 quarters, with the Q3 2025 miss at -12.1%. Q1 2026's 3.6% beat is encouraging but insufficient to re-establish consistent delivery.

## Growth Analysis

CPK's growth visibility is exceptionally high for a utility. The identified projects/initiatives table shows incremental adjusted gross margin growing from $91.2M (2025) to $135.6M (2026) to $156.3M (2027) — a 49% increase in 2026 from known, largely approved projects.

**Key Growth Drivers:**
1. **FCG Rate Case ($47M request, filed April 2026):** FCG earned only $5.2M net income on $1.13B assets in 2025. Interim rates of ~$16M expected Q3 2026. Even partial approval (60-70%) would add $28-33M revenue, potentially tripling FCG's after-tax earnings contribution.
2. **Infrastructure Riders:** GUARD ($205M/10yr), SAFE ($255M/10yr), SPP storm hardening, Capital Cost Surcharges — all provide rate base growth outside general rate cases.
3. **Florida Customer Additions:** 2-4% annual growth from population in-migration.
4. **Peninsula Pipeline Expansions:** Multiple new communities (St. Cloud/Twin Lakes, Wildlight, Newberry, Boynton Beach).
5. **Worcester Resiliency Upgrade:** $17.1M annual margin upon Q1 2027 completion (delayed from mid-2026, costing $0.10 EPS in 2026).
6. **Duncan Plains Data Center Pipeline:** Small ($1.5M annual margin) but strategically significant for gas-to-data-center demand thesis.

**2028 EPS Guidance:** $7.75-$8.00 implies 9-10% annual growth from 2025's $6.01. Forward consensus: $6.50 for 2026, $7.33 for 2027.

**Revision Trends:** Current year estimate is down 3.0% over 90 days ($6.70 → $6.50), reflecting the Worcester delay and potentially softer weather assumptions. Next year is flat (+0.5%). Estimate momentum is negative.

## Valuation Assessment

At $125.83 and trailing EPS of $5.97 (as reported) or $6.01 (adjusted), CPK trades at ~21.1x earnings. The peer median is 19.6x.

**Premium Justified?** CPK grows EPS at 9-10% vs. 5-7% for typical gas utilities. A 1.5-2.0x growth premium would support 22-24x on trailing earnings ($132-$144 range), suggesting the stock is roughly fairly valued at the low end of a premium range but not compellingly cheap.

**Forward Valuation:**
- On $6.50 FY2026E: 19.4x forward P/E (at peer median)
- On $7.33 FY2027E: 17.2x forward P/E (below peer median)
- On $7.88 FY2028E midpoint: 16.0x (meaningful discount)

**Dividend:** 2.17% yield vs. 3.4% peer median. The lower yield reflects the growth-oriented capital allocation (heavy reinvestment vs. payout).

**Analyst Targets:** $142-$160 range, with $149.25 mean. This implies 13-27% upside, which is unusual consensus for a utility and reflects confidence in the growth trajectory.

The stock trades at forward multiples that compress rapidly as earnings growth materializes. However, the recent downward estimate revisions (-3.0% on current year) and inconsistent beat rate create execution uncertainty that prevents assigning a full growth premium today.

## Competitive Landscape

**Structural Advantages:**
- Vertically integrated transmission + distribution in Florida allows internal project generation
- Multiple infrastructure riders reduce regulatory lag to near-zero for approved programs
- Florida demographic tailwinds are structural and multi-decade
- RNG positioning provides defensibility against gas utility existential concerns

**Position Trajectory:** Improving. The FCG acquisition created the second-largest gas utility in Florida. Peninsula Pipeline's transmission capacity is being built ahead of demand, creating barriers for competitors. The data center pipeline opportunity (Duncan Plains) represents early positioning in a theme that could become material.

**Competitive Risks:** Limited direct competition in franchise service territories (regulated monopoly). The primary competitive risk is substitution — electrification of heating and cooking — but Florida's warm climate means gas demand is weighted toward water heating, cooking, and industrial use rather than space heating, making electrification less threatening.

## Risk Assessment

**Near-Term Risks:**
1. **FCG Rate Case Outcome (H2 2026-H1 2027):** If the Florida PSC approves significantly less than the $47M request (as seen in recent Delaware/Maryland cases where allowed ROEs came in well below requests), earnings accretion delays.
2. **Weather Reversion:** 2025 was significantly colder than normal (Delmarva +188 HDD, Ohio +763 HDD). Normal weather in 2026 creates $5-10M gross margin headwind.
3. **Tariff Impact on Capex:** With $450-500M annual capital spending on steel pipe, meters, and transformers, tariffs could increase project costs. Recovery through regulators adds lag.
4. **CFO Departure:** Beth Cooper's exit after 18 years removes institutional knowledge during a period of heavy capital deployment and ERP implementation.

**Medium-Term Risks:**
5. **Capital Market Access:** Negative free cash flow requires continuous equity/debt issuance. Market disruption would impair growth execution.
6. **ERP Implementation:** $75M SAP S/4HANA project (go-live Q2 2027) is large and complex for a company of CPK's size.
7. **Dilution:** ~$60M annual equity issuance continues to dilute existing shareholders by 1-2% annually.

### Options Market Signal

This pricing structure indicates the market expects a near-term resolution of uncertainty — likely related to the May 7 Q1 earnings release (which has passed) and potentially the FCG rate case progression. The call-heavy put/call skew (-6.6%) suggests any directional positioning is mildly bullish, not defensive. The IV spike from 26.8% to 34.8% over the past week (+7.9pp) alongside only -1.5% price decline suggests event-driven uncertainty rather than fundamental deterioration.

## Investment Thesis

**Bull Case ($145-$160, 15-27% upside):**
- FCG rate case approved at 70%+ of request → $20-25M annual earnings uplift
- Worcester Resiliency completes on time in Q1 2027 → $17.1M margin
- Florida customer growth accelerates to 3-4%
- 2028 EPS hits $8.00 at 20x = $160
- Data center pipeline demand becomes a material growth vector

**Bear Case ($108-$115, 9-14% downside):**
- FCG rate case gets significantly trimmed (30-40% approval)
- Weather normalizes, reducing 2026 margins by $8-10M
- Tariffs increase capex costs 10-15%, compressing near-term returns
- ERP implementation faces delays/overruns
- Stock de-rates to peer median (19.6x on $5.97 = $117)

**Specific Reasons Against BUY at Current Price:**

CPK has missed earnings estimates in 3 of the past 4 quarters. The Q3 2025 miss was -12.1%. Current year estimates have been revised down 3.0% over 90 days. For a utility priced at a premium to peers (21.1x vs. 19.6x), this execution record creates unacceptable downside asymmetry. A utility trading above peer multiples needs consistent delivery to justify the premium. The next two quarters ($2.38 Q2 2026, $1.11 Q3 2026) are consensus estimates that have been falling. If CPK misses again, the stock de-rates toward or below peer multiples (19.6x × $6.50 = $127, essentially flat from here). The reward for being early is approximately +13-27% to analyst targets; the risk of another miss at a premium multiple is -9-14% to bear case. This is roughly 1.5:1 risk/reward — insufficiently asymmetric for BUY given the recent miss pattern.

**Falsifiable trigger for upgrade:** If CPK beats Q2 2026 estimates AND current year estimate revisions turn positive (back toward $6.70+), the execution concern is invalidated and the stock merits BUY given forward growth visibility.

## Investment Horizon & Exit Criteria

**Upgrade Triggers (HOLD → BUY):**
1. Two consecutive quarterly beats that re-establish delivery credibility (Q2 and Q3 2026)
2. FCG interim rate approval at ≥$12M (75% of request) in Q3 2026, confirming the rate case catalyst
3. Current year EPS estimate revisions turn positive (move from $6.50 back toward $6.65+)

**Downgrade Triggers (HOLD → AVOID):**
1. FCG rate case approved at <50% of request ($23M or less)
2. Management revises 2028 EPS target below $7.50
3. Q2 or Q3 2026 miss by >5%, indicating growth execution failure
4. Debt-to-capitalization exceeds 55% without corresponding earnings uplift

**Reassessment Timeline:** Q2 2026 earnings (expected August 2026) is the natural decision point. FCG interim rate order (expected Q3 2026) provides the second key data point.

## Conclusion

Chesapeake Utilities has a genuinely differentiated growth story for a gas utility — 9-10% EPS growth, visible project pipeline, Florida demographic tailwinds, and a major rate case catalyst in the FCG filing. The forward valuation (17.2x FY2027E, 16.0x FY2028E) is attractive if management delivers. The problem is recent execution: 3 misses in 4 quarters, falling estimates, CFO departure, and insider selling. For a stock at a peer premium, these create an asymmetry that favors waiting for confirmation over early entry. The growth thesis is correct; the entry timing is premature. HOLD with clear upgrade path tied to Q2 execution and FCG rate case progress.
