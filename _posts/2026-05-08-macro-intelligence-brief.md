### Momentum Factor Reversal: 5-Year Extreme Signals Crowding Risk

MarketWatch reports momentum stocks experienced one of their biggest reversals in five years. This is genuine novelty — it doesn't connect directly to existing world model themes but creates near-term risk for concentrated positions.

The mechanism: momentum factor strategies (systematic funds buying recent winners, shorting recent losers) had become overcrowded in AI/tech winners. When enough participants are long the same positions, any catalyst (peace deal hopes → energy rotation, or simply rebalancing) triggers coordinated selling. The reversal was amplified by the peace deal news causing a brief energy-to-tech rotation that then reversed when the deal collapsed.

Implications for portfolio: AI positions remain fundamentally strong (7+ demand confirmations) but face short-term mechanical selling pressure from factor rebalancing. This is noise, not signal — hold through it. The danger is if the momentum reversal coincides with a genuine fundamental disappointment (NVDA earnings miss), which would amplify the selloff.

### Dollar Weakness During Conflict: 12th De-Dollarization Signal

The dollar heading for a second straight weekly decline despite active US-Iran military clashes is historically anomalous. In prior US military conflicts (Iraq 2003, Libya 2011, Syria 2017), the dollar strengthened on safe-haven flows. This reversal suggests the market is pricing structural dollar deterioration rather than temporary geopolitical risk.

Combined with the existing 11 de-dollarization data points in the world model (central bank gold purchases, BRICS settlement mechanisms, de-pegging announcements, etc.), this constitutes a 12th independent signal. The structural thesis: markets perceive that the conflict itself is damaging US credibility rather than reinforcing it. This supports gold ($4,750) and commodities denominated in dollars.

Combined with McDonald's weak Q2 guidance, NY Fed lower-income study, and 16 prior signals, the consumer thesis heading into May 19-21 earnings (HD, TGT, WMT) has the strongest evidentiary base of any macro call in this series.
