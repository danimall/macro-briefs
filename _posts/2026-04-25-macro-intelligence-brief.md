## Executive Summary

The macro picture shifted in three specific ways since yesterday's brief. First, Hormuz traffic collapsed to five ships in 24 hours — the closest to complete shutdown since the conflict began — while simultaneously the first concrete diplomatic venue materialized with Witkoff/Kushner traveling to Pakistan and Turkey offering to participate in Hormuz demining. The operational deterioration is at its worst precisely when the diplomatic specificity is at its best, creating an asymmetric setup. Second, the enterprise software impairment thesis graduated from confirmed pattern to sector-wide event: ServiceNow's 16% drop, now accompanied by a broad selloff in CRM, WDAY, and ORCL, represents the first conflict-driven revenue damage to propagate across an entire sector. Third, the options market normalized sharply — SPY near-term IV collapsed to 11.1% (below HV of 12.5%) — creating the 6th equity vol normalization cycle of the conflict. Two genuinely new signals emerged: DOJ reclassifying marijuana to Schedule III (material for cannabis-adjacent equities) and FT reporting that defense stocks are giving back gains on production bottlenecks (first counter-signal to the defense MAXIMUM CONVICTION thesis).

## New Developments

### Hormuz Near-Total Shutdown Coincides with First Specific Diplomatic Venue

Five ships in 24 hours through Hormuz represents the most severe operational disruption of the conflict. The Pentagon's announcement that the blockade is "going global" confirms the pattern: ceasefire labels have produced zero operational improvement across 10 cycles.

This week's diplomatic signals differ qualitatively from the prior 9 failures in specificity. Prior signals were vague ("winding down," "a lot of progress," "very complete"). This week introduces: (1) a named negotiating venue (Pakistan), (2) named US envoys with travel confirmed (Witkoff, Kushner), (3) a concrete verification mechanism offered by a third party (Turkey's demining), and (4) a specific claim that "Iran will make an offer." Per analyst lessons, specificity increases the probability weight assigned to diplomatic signals, but the 0-for-9 track record means this still warrants a high prior of failure.

Assign 20-25% probability to this diplomatic track producing operational change within 10 days (up from the generic 10-15% for prior vague signals). Even at 20-25%, the base case remains sustained blockade with periodic openings (40-45%).

Reuters (Tier 1) confirms European and African crude prices hit fresh records while US crude was buffered by domestic supply. This structural competitive advantage for US refiners (MPC, VLO, PSX), producers (COP, FANG, EOG), and LNG exporters persists as long as Hormuz remains disrupted, and IEA's 2-year recovery timeline means the infrastructure damage alone maintains the divergence well beyond any ceasefire.

### DOJ Marijuana Reclassification: Schedule I to Schedule III

This is genuinely novel and has no antecedent in prior briefs. The 280E tax elimination is the single most impactful policy change for cannabis economics — it immediately improves after-tax profitability by 20-40% for most operators. Banking access removes the all-cash operational model that has elevated costs. Institutional investment eligibility (mutual funds, ETFs, pensions prohibited from Schedule I exposure) opens a new capital pool.

I lack deep company-level coverage of pure-play cannabis operators in the tracked universe. The most direct tracked exposure is IIPR (cannabis REIT) and MO (Altria's Cronos investment). Both warrant monitoring but this is a single data point — the implementation timeline, FDA specifics, and state-level regulatory responses will determine the magnitude. Not assigning directional conviction yet; flagging for dedicated analysis.

### Defense Production Bottleneck: First Counter-Signal

FT (Tier 2) reports defense stocks retreating as production bottlenecks and munitions funding uncertainty weigh on near-term sentiment. This is the first counter-signal to the defense MAXIMUM CONVICTION thesis, which has been supported by 5+ independent data points (US dual-theater budget, RTX beat, Germany military strategy, Japan warship export, global defense spending expansion).

One counter-signal vs. 5+ bullish confirmations does not warrant changing conviction per analyst lessons. Production bottlenecks actually extend revenue recognition timelines for firms with record backlogs — HII's $53.1B backlog and LMT's $193.6B backlog become more valuable, not less, if production is constrained, because competitors cannot steal share through faster delivery. The appropriate response is to monitor for reinforcement (a second quarter of bottleneck-driven guidance reductions would be material) and view any pullback as a buying opportunity.

### Iran War Energy Shock Drives Solar Demand Revival

Reuters (Tier 1) reports that energy security concerns from the conflict are accelerating renewable energy investment more than climate concerns alone. European rooftop solar demand is specifically reviving. Energy executives at CERAWeek indicated this shift.

This creates a partial offset to Trump's 2027 budget proposal cutting renewable energy research. The international demand catalyst is real but primarily benefits Chinese solar manufacturers who dominate panel production. For tracked names, ENPH benefits most directly from European rooftop microinverter demand. The nuclear baseload thesis (CEG) benefits more from energy security framing than intermittent solar, strengthening the CEG/FSLR pair trade's long leg.
