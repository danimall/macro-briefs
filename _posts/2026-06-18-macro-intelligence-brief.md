## New Developments

### Warsh's Inaugural FOMC Resolves Hawkish on Guidance

The prior briefs framed June 16-17 as a binary on guidance tone, not the move, and counseled no pre-positioning. The guidance resolved hawkish: the FOMC held at 3.50-3.75% but projected a hike later this year, with officials split between no cuts and hikes (Federal Reserve, Tier 1; Reuters, Tier 1; corroborated across CNBC and FT). The mechanism the world model assumed was inverted: a politically-inflation-tolerant chair guiding hawkish, rather than the dovish-surprise asymmetry that justified the now-expired TLT June-18 call concentration. Warsh's framing emphasized restructuring Fed operations and "listening more to market signals," which reduces forward-guidance reliability and is itself a communication-regime change to monitor.

The causal chain into assets: hawkish projection plus falling oil plus strong retail sales means no growth scare to force cuts but a higher projected discount rate. This de-rates high-duration tech at the margin into the AI net-share-supply wave, supports a "US exceptionalism" dollar bid that pressures EM, and compounds the structural long-end pressure (AI-debt supply, gold-over-Treasuries, carry-unwind). The clean beneficiary is the exchanges: reduced guidance reliability under a new chair raises rate-path uncertainty and hedging volume, and KBW upgraded CME the same day arguing the perpetual-futures competitive fear is overblown. The dovish-Warsh TLT bet, which the prior briefs correctly called stranded, expires worthless against this guide. The calibration document shows our score is anti-predictive on alpha and BUYs add no selection edge, so this is a directional read on positioning, not a high-conviction long anywhere.

### Accenture Down ~14% — Hardest AI-Displacement Confirmation Yet at a Bellwether

Accenture fell roughly 14% pre-market on a weaker-than-expected Q4 revenue forecast, with the market and management framing generative AI as disrupting the core IT-consulting model, even as it booked $4.18B in new cybersecurity work (FT, Tier 2; SeekingAlpha corroboration). This is the cleanest earnings-level confirmation of the AI-displacement thesis the world model has tracked, and it differs from the seat-based SaaS displacement (WDAY/CRM) and the infra-wins/application-impaired bifurcation: it is headcount-based professional-services revenue being structurally undercut by AI productivity.

This validates the ACN short leg of the LMT-vs-ACN and BRK.B-vs-ACN CORE pairs directly. The read-through extends to IT-services peers facing the same displacement (CTSH, EPAM, IBM consulting), and reinforces the AVOID on DXC. It cuts the opposite way for the infra-wins leg: if AI is displacing the services layer, the value accrues to the platforms providing the AI (GOOG, MSFT, TSM). The calibration document is relevant here: Information Technology is the only sector with positive beta-stripped alpha (+1.83pp), and Communication Services/IT-services are neutral-to-negative, so ACN is better expressed as a short/pair leg than any IT-services name as a long. The cybersecurity bookings confirm security remains the relative bright spot in IT spend, consistent with the PANW thesis.

### Memory-Chip Tightness Lifts Micron; Apple Flags Price Hikes; Intel-Apple Foundry Report Unconfirmed

MarketWatch reports memory-chip demand outpacing supply near-term, lifting Micron and pushing Korean and Taiwanese memory makers to record highs, while Apple's Tim Cook flagged "unavoidable" price hikes to cover chip costs (MarketWatch, Tier 2). This is the hardware-side counter-evidence to the demand-economics worry: AI compute demand is real enough to tighten memory supply, the Jevons/inference leg. It does not resolve the demand-economics question (Databricks margin compression, OpenAI usage-plateau hypothesis remain open), but it is genuine confirmation that silicon demand is not decelerating.

Separately, Trump said Intel will partner with Apple on US chip design and manufacturing, sending Intel up ~9%. The claim originates from a political statement, not a corporate filing, so the single-source discipline applies: monitor, do not establish conviction until corporate confirmation. If confirmed, it is a US-reshoring milestone and a marginal competitive read-through against TSM, though TSM's process lead and customer diversification keep it the cleanest AI expression. Apple's price-hike flag confirms hardware cost inflation passing through to consumers, both a margin-defense signal and an inflation read-through into the hawkish-Fed picture.
