## Executive Summary

The prior brief's central thesis — that Monday's HD earnings would trigger a credit cascade — has been partially falsified. This removes the most immediate catalyst for credit spread widening and shifts the cascade probability from 65-75% back toward 55-65%. ## New Developments

### Japan-China Treasury Selling: From Theoretical to Confirmed

The CNBC report that Japan and China are leading a foreign government retreat from US Treasurys is the most important new information since last brief. This was previously a theoretical risk (Japan 30Y at 4% creating repatriation incentive). It is now reported as active selling, with China at 18-year holding lows.

The mechanism: Japan holds ~$1.1T and China ~$750B in UST. If even 5-10% of combined holdings ($90-185B) are sold over 6-12 months, this represents substantial supply pressure on the long end that operates independently of domestic inflation or Fed policy. The Fed cannot offset this via forward guidance or rate adjustments — it's an exogenous supply shock to the Treasury market.

Investment implications are severe for duration-sensitive assets. The prior brief's estimate of 10Y breaking 5% at 45-55% probability within 60 days should be upgraded toward 55-60% given confirmed selling flow adds to inflation-driven yield increases.

This strengthens: exchange/volatility beneficiaries (CME, CBOE, ICE), energy overweight (as supply constraint on oil parallels supply constraint on Treasuries — both structural, not cyclical), and short-duration positioning. It weakens: REITs, utilities at current yields, homebuilders, long-duration growth at premium multiples.

### Inflation at 6% Forecast: The Rate Hike Constraint Tightens

Top economic forecasters projecting Q2 inflation at 6% (CNBC) represents an acceleration from the current 3.8% CPI / 6% PPI regime. If CPI approaches 6%, Warsh faces a binding constraint: the bond market has already moved (30Y at 5.12%) and Yardeni's "bond vigilante" framework suggests a July rate hike may be required simply to maintain institutional credibility.

The causal chain: PCE energy already surged 11.56% MoM in March → PPI at 6% (confirmed) → CPI passthrough with 1-3 month lag → if May CPI prints 4.5%+ (released mid-June), Warsh's inaugural FOMC on June 16-17 faces immediate pressure. The Kalshi 38% probability on a 2026 rate hike appears reasonable; our estimate of 35-45% remains well-calibrated.

### HD Beat: Credit Cascade Catalyst Weakened, Not Eliminated

Home Depot's beat with maintained guidance directly challenges the credit cascade's most imminent trigger. However, the SSS miss + consumers pulling back on large projects + mortgage rates at 9-month highs collectively suggest a bifurcated consumer: necessities continue, discretionary collapses.

The key question shifts to Target (Tuesday) and Walmart (this week). TGT's customer base is lower-income and more discretionary-weighted than HD's Pro-heavy mix. Estimates revised UP 4.8% over 90 days with the new CEO promising comp growth every quarter of FY2027. A TGT miss would be particularly damaging to sentiment given the positive revision trend and demonstrate that HD's resilience is customer-segment-specific rather than economy-wide.

Credit cascade probability revision: 65-75% → 55-65%. The fundamental stress signals (4 institutional failures, negative real wages, PPI 6%) remain intact, but the conversion trigger now requires TGT/WMT miss OR a non-consumer event.
