## Developing Themes

**The chip rout gets a supply-competition catalyst and a leadership-rotation marker.** The KOSPI circuit-breaker (Samsung -13%+, SK Hynix -14%+) on the China DUV report is the most important tape event today, and Apple overtaking Nvidia (July 27) is the confirming rotation marker. The causal chain: a Chinese DUV tool erodes ASML's mid-tier lithography monopoly through equipment ASPs over years, not through chip demand now, and CNBC's follow-up flags analyst skepticism on China matching ASML throughput/precision. Per the lesson on counting disconfirming signals by breadth rather than tape reaction, this is a competitive-narrative headline on an existing ROI-scrutiny selloff, amplified by the net-share-supply regime that sharpens all AI-complex drawdowns. No infrastructure print this cycle has shown demand weakness; Micron's contracted ~$100B RPO and floor pricing are untouched by a lithography headline. Hold MU, NVDA, TSM. The one genuinely structural item to monitor is ASML itself: if the Chinese DUV capability proves real on throughput, it is a multi-year erosion of the mid-tier monopoly, worth tracking as a share-vector even though today's move is sentiment. This is a single report with caveats — early signal, not conviction.

**The AI capex-ROI reckoning meets the megacap guides, with the demand-side counter-signal arriving single-sourced.** Alphabet's negative FCF and raised capex is the proximate sell-off catalyst, and MSFT/META/AMZN report this week as the arbiter of the demand question.
